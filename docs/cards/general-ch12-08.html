Conventional Flow & Electron Flow

通用 Ch 12 · Conventional Flow & Electron Flow · 原文 PDF 第 456 页

1. 原文

Today’s technician will find that there are two competing schools of thought and analytical practices regarding the flow of electricity. The two are called the conventional current theory and the electron theory.

Conventional Flow

Of the two, the conventional current theory was the first to be developed and, through many years of use, this method has become ingrained in electrical publications. The theory was initially advanced by Benjamin Franklin who reasoned that current flowed out of a positive source into a negative source or an area that lacked an abundance of charge. The notation assigned to the electric charges was positive (+) for the abundance of charge and negative (−) for a lack of charge. It then seemed natural to visualize the flow of current as being from the positive (+) to the negative (−).

Later discoveries were made that proved just the opposite is true. Electron flow is what actually happens when an abundance of electrons flow out of the negative (−) source to an area that lacks electrons or the positive (+) source. Both conventional flow and electron flow are used in industry. Many publications in current use employ both electron flow and conventional flow methods. From the practical standpoint of the technician troubleshooting a system, it makes little to no difference which way current is flowing as long as it is used consistently in the analysis. The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) officially defines current flow using electron theory (negative to positive).

2. 中文翻译

今天的技术员会发现，关于电流的流动存在两种相互竞争的理论观点和分析做法。这两者分别称为常规电流理论和电子理论。

常规电流

在这两者之中，常规电流理论是先发展起来的，经过多年使用，这种方法已在电气出版物中根深蒂固。该理论最初由本杰明·富兰克林提出，他推断电流从正极电源流出，流入负极电源或缺少大量电荷的区域。给电荷规定的标记是：电荷多时为正（+），电荷少时为负（−）。于是，把电流的流动设想为从正（+）到负（−）就显得很自然了。

后来的发现证明事实恰好相反。电子流才是实际发生的情况：大量电子从负（−）电源流出，流向缺少电子的区域或正（+）电源。常规电流和电子流在工业中都有使用。目前使用的许多出版物同时采用电子流和常规电流两种方法。从技术员对系统进行故障排查的实用角度看，只要分析中始终一致，电流朝哪个方向流动几乎甚至完全没有差别。美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）正式采用电子理论（从负极到正极）来定义电流流向。

4. 语法解析

① will find + there be 宾语从句

原句：Today’s technician will find that there are two competing schools of thought and analytical practices regarding the flow of electricity. 拆解：主句 Today’s technician will find that…；that 引导宾语从句，从句为 there be 句型，真正主语是 two competing schools of thought and analytical practices；regarding… 为介词短语作后置定语；competing 为现在分词作前置定语。 要点：there be 作宾语从句时 there 保留；regarding 是「关于」的正式介词，相当于 about，不能用作分词谓语。schools of thought 是「学派、观点派别」，不是学校。

② the first + 不定式作后置定语 + 插入语

原句：Of the two, the conventional current theory was the first to be developed and, through many years of use, this method has become ingrained in electrical publications. 拆解：Of the two 为范围状语；第一个分句 the conventional current theory was the first to be developed，不定式 to be developed 作 the first 的后置定语；and 连接第二个分句，through many years of use 为插入状语，谓用现在完成 has become ingrained。 要点：the first 后接不定式表示「第一个被……的」，不用 being developed；用 and 连接两个独立分句时要各自带主语，这里由 the conventional current theory 换成 this method 以避免重复。become ingrained in 是「在……中根深蒂固」。

③ 定语从句 + reasoned that 宾语从句

原句：The theory was initially advanced by Benjamin Franklin who reasoned that current flowed out of a positive source into a negative source or an area that lacked an abundance of charge. 拆解：主句为被动 The theory was initially advanced by Benjamin Franklin；who 引导定语从句修饰 Franklin；reasoned 后接 that 宾语从句，从句谓语 flowed out of… into…，宾语有两个并列：a negative source 与 an area that lacked…，其中 that 引导定语从句修饰 area。 要点：flow out of A into B 是「从 A 流出进入 B」的方向搭配；lacked 用一般过去时表示当时的判断，与主句时态呼应。that lacked… 的先行词是 area 而非 source，靠语义和就近原则判断。

④ 定语从句与先行词被分隔

原句：Later discoveries were made that proved just the opposite is true. 拆解：主干 Later discoveries were made；that proved… 为定语从句修饰 discoveries，因谓语短而后置，与先行词被 was made 隔开；proved 后接省略 that 的宾语从句 just the opposite is true。 要点：主谓结构较短时，定语从句常被移到谓语之后，形成「先行词 + 被动谓语 + that 从句」的分隔现象，读句时要把 that 与先行词挂钩；just the opposite 是「恰恰相反的事」。

⑤ it makes no difference + which 从句 + as long as

原句：From the practical standpoint of the technician troubleshooting a system, it makes little to no difference which way current is flowing as long as it is used consistently in the analysis. 拆解：From the practical standpoint of… 为状语，troubleshooting a system 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 technician；主句 it makes little to no difference，it 为形式主语，真正主语是 which way current is flowing；as long as 引导条件状语从句。 要点：make no/little difference 是「没有／几乎没有差别」的固定搭配；little to no 是程度递进的压缩表达（几乎没有到完全没有）。which way 引出的从句用陈述语序，且 which way 是「往哪个方向」而不是「哪个方法」。
