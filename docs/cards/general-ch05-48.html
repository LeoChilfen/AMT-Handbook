Conduction

1. 原文

Heat transfer always takes place by areas of high heat energy migrating to areas of low heat energy. Heat transfer by conduction requires that there be physical contact between an object that has a large amount of heat energy and one that has a smaller amount of heat energy.

Everyone knows from experience that the metal handle of a heated pan can burn the hand. A plastic or wood handle, however, remains relatively cool even though it is in direct contact with the pan. The metal transmits the heat more easily than the wood because it is a better conductor of heat.

Different materials conduct heat at different rates. Some metals are much better conductors of heat than others.

Aluminum and copper are used in pots and pans because they conduct heat very rapidly. Woods and plastics are used for handles because they conduct heat very slowly.

Figure 5-27 illustrates the different rates of conduction of various metals. Of those listed, silver is the best conductor and lead is the poorest. As mentioned previously, copper and aluminum are used in pots and pans because they are good conductors. It is interesting to note that silver, copper, and aluminum are also excellent conductors of electricity.

Liquids are poorer conductors of heat than metals. Notice that the ice in the test tube shown in Figure 5-28 is not melting rapidly even though the water at the top is boiling.

The water conducts heat so poorly that not enough heat reaches the ice to melt it.

Gases are even poorer conductors of heat than liquids. It is possible to stand quite close to a stove without being burned because air is such a poor conductor. Since conduction is a process whereby the increase in molecular energy is passed along by actual contact, gases are poor conductors.

At the point of application of the heat source, the molecules become violently agitated. These molecules strike adjacent molecules causing them to become agitated. This process continues until the heat energy is distributed evenly throughout the substance. The gases are much poorer conductors of heat because molecules are farther apart in gases than in solids.

2. 中文翻译

热传递总是通过高热能区域向低热能区域迁移来实现。以传导方式进行热传递，要求一个热能多的物体与一个热能少的物体之间存在物理接触。

凭经验谁都知道，受热锅具的金属手柄会烫手。而塑料或木质手柄即使与锅直接接触，也仍然相对凉爽。金属比木材更容易传热，因为它是更好的导热体。

不同材料传热的速率不同。有些金属的导热性远远优于其他金属。

铝和铜被用于制作锅具，因为它们传热非常快。木材和塑料被用于制作手柄，因为它们传热非常慢。

图 5-27 示出各种金属不同的导热速率。在所列出者中，银是最好的导体，铅是最差的。如前所述，铜和铝用于锅具是因为它们是良导体。值得注意的是，银、铜和铝同时也是极好的导电体。

液体是比金属差得多的导热体。请注意，图 5-28 所示试管中的冰并没有迅速融化，尽管上部的水已经沸腾。

水的导热性太差，传到冰处的热量不足以使冰融化。

气体是比液体更差的导热体。人可以站得离炉子相当近而不被烫伤，因为空气的导热性极差。既然传导是这样一种过程——分子能量的增加靠实际接触逐层传递，那么气体自然是差的导热体。

在热源作用的位置，分子被剧烈扰动。这些分子撞击相邻分子，使它们也被扰动。这一过程持续进行，直到热能在整个物质中均匀分布为止。气体是差得多的导热体，因为气体中分子之间的间距比固体中大。

（此处为图内文字，略：图 5-27 为各种金属导热率的刻度条，标有铅 0.08、铁 0.18、镍 0.22、镁 0.37、铝 0.57、铜 0.94、银 1.00；图 5-28 标有水、冰、蒸气、沸水以及防止冰上浮的金属环）导热性差的材料被用来阻止热量传递，称为绝热体（heat insulators）。锅上或电烙铁上的木质手柄就起绝热体的作用。

某些材料，例如细拉的玻璃纤维或石棉，导热性特别差。因此这些材料被用于多种类型的隔热。

4. 语法解析

① 虚拟语气：requires that there be physical contact

原句：Heat transfer by conduction requires that there be physical contact between an object that has a large amount of heat energy and one that has a smaller amount of heat energy.

拆解：主语 Heat transfer by conduction；谓语 requires；宾语从句 that there be physical contact …。require 后的 that 从句用虚拟语气，动词用原形，所以是 there be 而不是 there is。between A and B 中，A ＝ an object that has a large amount …, B ＝ one (＝ an object) that has a smaller amount …。

要点：表示「要求、建议、命令」的动词（require, demand, suggest, recommend, insist）后接 that 从句时，谓语用 should + 动词原形，should 常省略，于是出现 requires that there be / requires that he do 这类形式。one 代替前面出现过的 an object，避免重复，后面照样带定语从句。

② so … that 结果状语从句

原句：The water conducts heat so poorly that not enough heat reaches the ice to melt it.

拆解：主句 The water conducts heat so poorly；so + 副词 poorly 后接 that 引导的结果状语从句 not enough heat reaches the ice；to melt it 是不定式作结果状语，it 指 the ice。

要点：so + adj./adv. + that 表「如此……以至于」，与 such + 名词 + that 相对应。enough 修饰名词时前置（enough heat），修饰形容词时才后置（good enough）。it 不能换成 one，因为指的是同一块冰；one 只用于指同类中的另一个。

③ whereby 引导的定语从句 + 被动语态

原句：Since conduction is a process whereby the increase in molecular energy is passed along by actual contact, gases are poor conductors.

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句；从句主干 conduction is a process；whereby（＝by which）引导定语从句修饰 process，从句内 the increase in molecular energy 作主语，is passed along 为被动谓语，by actual contact 是方式状语。

要点：whereby 是正式书面语的关系副词，等于 by which，多用于定义过程的句子。pass along 是「逐个传递下去」，与被动 is passed along 一起表示「靠接触一层层传下去」。主语中心词是 the increase（增加量），所以谓语用单数 is，不要被后面的 molecular energy 带偏。
