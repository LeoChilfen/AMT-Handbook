Fluid Pressure

1. 原文

The pressure exerted on the bottom of a container by a liquid is determined by the height of the liquid and not by the shape of the container. This can be seen in Figure 5-41, where three different shapes and sizes of containers are full of colored water. Even though they are different shapes and have different volumes of liquid, each one has a height of 231 inches. Each one would exert a pressure on the bottom of 8.34 psi because of this height. The container on the left, with a surface area of 1 in 2, contains a volume of 231 in 3 (one gallon). One gallon of water weighs 8.34 lb, which is why the pressure on the bottom is 8.34 psi.

Still thinking about Figure 5-41, if the pressure was measured half way down, it would be half of 8.34, or 4.17 psi. In other words, the pressure is adjustable by varying the height of the column. Pressure based on the column height of a fluid is known as static pressure. With liquids, such as gasoline, it is sometimes referred to as a head of pressure. For example, if a carburetor needs to have 2 psi supplied to its inlet, or head of pressure, this could be accomplished by having the fuel tank positioned the appropriate number of inches higher than the carburetor.

As identified in the previous paragraph, pressure due to the height of a fluid column is known as static pressure. When a fluid is in motion, and its velocity is converted to pressure, that pressure is known as ram. When ram pressure and static pressure are added together, the result is known as total pressure. In the inlet of a gas turbine engine, for example, total pressure is often measured to provide a signal to the fuel metering device or to provide a signal to a gauge on the flight deck.

2. 中文翻译

液体对容器底部所施加的压力，由液体的高度决定，而不是由容器的形状决定。这从图 5-41 中可以看出：图中三个形状和尺寸不同的容器都装满了着色水。尽管形状不同、液体体积也不同，但每个容器的高度都是 231 英寸。由于这一高度，每个容器对底部施加的压力都是 8.34 psi。左侧容器的底面积为 1 in2，容纳体积为 231 in3（1 加仑）。1 加仑水重 8.34 lb，这就是底部压力为 8.34 psi 的原因。

仍以图 5-41 为例，如果在半高处测量压力，则为 8.34 的一半，即 4.17 psi。换句话说，压力可以通过改变液柱高度来调节。由流体柱高度决定的压力称为静压。对汽油这类液体，有时也称其为压头。例如，如果化油器进口需要供给 2 psi（即 2 psi 压头），只需把燃油箱安装在比化油器高出相应英寸数的位置即可实现。

如上一段所述，由流体柱高度产生的压力称为静压。当流体处于运动状态、其速度转化为压力时，该压力称为冲压。把冲压与静压相加，结果称为全压。例如在燃气涡轮发动机的进气道中，常测量全压，用来向燃油计量装置提供信号，或向驾驶舱上的仪表提供信号。

4. 语法解析

① is determined by … and not by …（被动 + 对照否定）

原句：The pressure exerted on the bottom of a container by a liquid is determined by the height of the liquid and not by the shape of the container.

拆解：主语 The pressure，后接 exerted on the bottom of a container by a liquid（过去分词短语作后置定语，其中 by a liquid 是施动者）；谓语 is determined by 引出被动句；and not by … 是并列的否定介词短语，与第一个 by 形成对照。

要点：手册说明「由 A 决定而非由 B 决定」时，用 and not by 把两个 by 短语并列，比写成 but not 更紧凑。注意 exerted 与 determined 都是过去分词，一个在主语后作定语、一个在谓语里作被动核心，形式相同但功能不同——先找到 is 才能分清哪个是谓语。

② which is why …（which 指代前句、表原因的定语从句）

原句：One gallon of water weighs 8.34 lb, which is why the pressure on the bottom is 8.34 psi.

拆解：主句 One gallon of water weighs 8.34 lb；逗号后 which 指代整个主句所述事实（不是某个名词），is why 后接表语从句 the pressure on the bottom is 8.34 psi。

要点：which 指代前面整句话时，只能放在逗号之后引导非限制性定语从句，且谓语用单数 is。which is why 是手册解释因果的常用句式，译作「这就是……的原因」；同类还有 which explains why / which means that。

③ When a fluid is in motion, … is known as ram（时间状语从句 + 术语定义被动）

原句：When a fluid is in motion, and its velocity is converted to pressure, that pressure is known as ram.

拆解：When 引出的状语部分含两个并列被动分句（a fluid is in motion 是系表结构，its velocity is converted to pressure 是 be + 过去分词）；主句 that pressure is known as ram。

要点：is known as / is referred to as / is called 是本节给术语下定义的三兄弟，都表示「称为」，后面直接接名词（不加 to be）。velocity is converted to pressure 用被动，因为「速度」不是施动者而是被转换的对象。in motion 是固定短语，表状态，不要写成 in moving。
