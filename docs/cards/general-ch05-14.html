1. 原文

Sliding friction is the resistance to motion offered by an object sliding over a surface. It pertains to friction produced after the object has been set in motion, and is always less than starting friction. The amount of sliding resistance is dependent on the nature of the surface of the object, the surface over which it slides, and the normal force between the object and the surface. This resistive force may be computed by using the following formula:

F = mN In the formula above, “F” is the resistive force due to friction expressed in pounds; “N” is the force exerted on or by the object perpendicular (normal) to the surface over which it slides; and “m” (mu) is the coefficient of sliding friction. On a horizontal surface, N is equal to the weight of the object in pounds. The area of the sliding object exposed to the sliding surface has no effect on the results. A block of wood, for

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词作后置定语 + after 从句用现在完成时

原句：It pertains to friction produced after the object has been set in motion, and is always less than starting friction.

拆解：主句主语 It（指滑动摩擦），并列谓语 pertains to … and is always less than …；friction produced after … 中 produced 是过去分词作后置定语（= which is produced）；after 从句用现在完成时 has been set in motion，表示「先被起动，然后才有摩擦」。

要点：after / when / once 引导的时间从句用现在完成时强调动作先于主句完成，这是技术说明里常见的时态配合。set in motion 是固定搭配「使……起动」，被动形式为 be set in motion。

② be dependent on + 三项并列 + 介词前置的定语从句

原句：The amount of sliding resistance is dependent on the nature of the surface of the object, the surface over which it slides, and the normal force between the object and the surface.

拆解：主语 The amount of sliding resistance；谓语 is dependent on；后面并列三个宾语：the nature of the surface of the object、the surface over which it slides、the normal force between the object and the surface；其中 over which it slides 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，修饰 the surface。

要点：be dependent on / depend on 后接名词或名词短语，不接 that 从句。介词提到 which 之前是正式书面语的标志（over which = which … over），口语里也可说 the surface which it slides over，但手册体一律前置。

③ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + have no effect on

原句：The area of the sliding object exposed to the sliding surface has no effect on the results.

拆解：主语中心词 The area；of the sliding object 与 exposed to the sliding surface 都是后置定语，其中 exposed to … 是过去分词短语（= which is exposed to …）；谓语 has no effect on；宾语 the results。

要点：exposed to 用过去分词，因为面积是「被接触、被暴露」的；have no effect on 是「对……没有影响」的固定搭配，介词用 on。原文紧接着用木板宽面与窄面滑动的比较来印证这一点，结论是接触面积不进入摩擦公式。

④ 情态被动 + by + 动名词

原句：This resistive force may be computed by using the following formula:

拆解：主语 This resistive force；谓语 may be computed（情态动词 + be + 过去分词）；by using the following formula 是方式状语，介词 by 后接动名词。

要点：表示「通过做某事」时，by 后必须用 -ing，不能用不定式；by using 也可换成 with the following formula（用某工具），区别在于 by 强调手段、with 强调工具。手册里「可用以下公式计算」几乎固定为 may be computed by using / can be calculated with。
