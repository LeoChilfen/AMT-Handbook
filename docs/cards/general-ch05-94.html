There are a number of different shapes, known as planforms that a wing can have. A wing in the shape of a rectangle is very common on small general aviation airplanes. An elliptical shape or tapered wing can also be used, but these do not have as desirable a stall characteristic. For airplanes that operate at high subsonic speeds, sweptback wings are common, and for supersonic flight, a delta shape might be used.

The aspect ratio of a wing is the relationship between its span, or a wingtip to wingtip measurement, and the chord of the wing. If a wing has a long span and a very narrow chord, it is said to have a high aspect ratio. A higher aspect ratio produces less drag for a given flight speed, and is typically found on glider type aircraft.

The angle of incidence of a wing is the angle formed by the intersection of the wing chord line and the horizontal plane passing through the longitudinal axis of the aircraft. Many airplanes are designed with a greater angle of incidence at the root of the wing than at the tip, and this is referred to as washout. This feature causes the inboard part of the wing to stall before the outboard part, which helps maintain aileron control during the initial stages of a wing stall.

2. 中文翻译

……升力就会被破坏。当这种情况发生时，就出现了称为失速（stall）的状态。

机翼可以有多种不同的平面形状，称为平面形状（planforms）。矩形机翼在小型通用航空飞机上很常见。椭圆形机翼或锥形（渐缩形）机翼也可以采用，但它们的失速特性不如前者理想。对于在高亚声速下运行的飞机，后掠翼（sweptback wings）很常见；而对于超声速飞行，则可能采用三角形机翼。

机翼的展弦比（aspect ratio）是指机翼的翼展（span，即从一侧翼尖到另一侧翼尖的度量）与机翼弦长之间的关系。如果机翼翼展长而弦长很窄，就说它具有大的展弦比。在给定飞行速度下，较大的展弦比产生的阻力较小，这种机翼常见于滑翔机类航空器。

机翼的安装角（angle of incidence）是机翼弦线与穿过飞机纵轴的水平面相交所形成的角度。许多飞机在机翼根部设计的安装角大于翼尖处的安装角，这称为扭转（washout）。这一特征使机翼内侧部分先于外侧部分失速，有助于在机翼失速的初始阶段保持副翼操纵。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作后置定语：known as a stall

原句：When this occurs, a condition known as a stall takes place.

拆解：When this occurs 是时间状语从句，this 指上一句「升力被破坏」；主句主语 a condition，known as a stall 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 condition；谓语是 takes place，为不及物动词短语。

要点：take place（发生）没有被动式，写成 is taken place 是常见错误，同类的还有 occur、happen、arise。known as 是「被称为」的紧凑后置定语，等于 which is known as；本句可还原为 a condition which is known as a stall takes place。

② For 短语前置 + that 引导的定语从句

原句：For airplanes that operate at high subsonic speeds, sweptback wings are common, and for supersonic flight, a delta shape might be used.

拆解：For airplanes that operate at high subsonic speeds 是介词短语作状语，其中 that operate at high subsonic speeds 是定语从句修饰 airplanes；主句 sweptback wings are common；and 连接第二分句 for supersonic flight, a delta shape might be used，同样以 for 短语前置。

要点：为了对比两种速度范围，两个分句都用「For + 对象」开头，形成对称结构。might 表示「可能采用」，比 may 语气更弱、更不确定，适合描述设计上的可选方案。介词短语前置后要加逗号，读句时先抓主句的主谓（sweptback wings are common）。

③ 比较结构中的省略 + be referred to as

原句：Many airplanes are designed with a greater angle of incidence at the root of the wing than at the tip, and this is referred to as washout.

拆解：主句 Many airplanes are designed with a greater angle of incidence at the root of the wing；than at the tip 是比较结构，省略了重复的 the angle of incidence（还原为 than the angle of incidence at the tip）；and 连接第二分句 this is referred to as washout，this 指前面整个设计特征。

要点：比较结构中相同成分常被省略，只留 than at the tip，读时要把省略部分补回来才不会歧义；greater 前用 a 而非 the，是比较级的泛指用法。be designed with（设计上带有）是手册常用搭配，不要写成 be designed by。
