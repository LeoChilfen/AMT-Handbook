Electronic Flight Information Systems

1. 原文

In an effort to increase the safety of operating complicated aircraft, computers and computer systems have been incorporated. Flight instrumentation and engine and airframe monitoring are areas particularly well suited to gain advantages from the use of computers. They contribute by helping to reduce instrument panel clutter and focusing the pilot’s attention only on matters of imminent importance.

“Glass flight deck” is a term that refers to the use of flat-panel display screens in flight deck instrumentation. In reality, it also refers to the use of computer-produced images that have replaced individual mechanical gauges. Moreover, computers and computer systems monitor the processes and components of an operating aircraft beyond human ability while relieving the pilot of the stress from having to do so.

Computerized electronic flight instrument systems have additional benefits. The solid-state nature of the components increases reliability. Also, microprocessors, data buses, and LCDs all save space and weight. The following systems have been developed and utilized on aircraft for a number of years. New systems and computer architecture are sure to come in the future.

Electronic Flight Instrument System (EFIS)

The flight instruments were the first to adopt computer technology and utilize flat screen, multifunctional displays (MFD). EFIS uses dedicated signal generators to drive two independent displays in the center of the basic T. The attitude indicator and directional gyro are replaced by cathode ray tubes (CRT) used to display EADI and EHSI presentations. These enhanced instruments operate alongside ordinary mechanic and electric instruments with limited integration. Still, EADI and EHSI technology is very desirable, reducing workload and panel scan with the added safety provided by integration of navigation information as described.

Early EFIS systems have analog technology, while newer models may be digital systems. The signal generators receive information from attitude and navigation equipment. Through a display controller, the pilot can select the various mode or screen features wishing to be displayed. Independent dedicated pilot and copilot systems are normal. A third, backup symbol generator is available to assume operation should one of the two primary units fail.

Electronic depiction of ADI and HSI information is the core purpose of an EFIS system. Its expanded size and capabilities over traditional gauges allow for integration of even more flight instrument data. A vertical airspeed scale is typically displayed just left of the attitude field. This is in the same relative position as the airspeed indicator in an analog basic T instrument panel. To the right of the attitude field, many EFIS systems display an altitude and vertical speed scale. Since most EFIS EADI depictions include the inclinometer, normally part of the turn coordinator, all of the basic flight instruments are depicted by the EFIS display.

Electronic Centralized Aircraft Monitor (ECAM)

The pilot’s workload on all aircraft includes continuous monitoring of the flight instruments and the sky outside of the aircraft. It also includes vigilant scrutiny for proper operation of the engine and airframe systems. On transport category aircraft, this can mean monitoring numerous gauges in addition to maneuvering the aircraft. The electronic centralized aircraft monitoring (ECAM) system is designed to assist with this duty.

The basic concept behind ECAM (and other monitoring systems) is automatic performance of monitoring duties for the pilot. When a problem is detected, or a failure occurs, the primary display, along with an aural and visual cue, alerts the pilot. Corrective action that needs to be taken is displayed, as well as suggested action due to the failure. By performing system monitoring automatically, the pilot is free to fly the aircraft until a problem occurs.

Early ECAM systems only monitor airframe systems. Engine parameters are displayed on traditional full-time flight deck gauges. Later model ECAM systems incorporate engine displays, as well as airframe.

An ECAM system has two CRT monitors. In newer aircraft, these may be LCD. The left or upper monitor, depending on the aircraft panel layout, displays information on system status and any warnings associated corrective actions. This is done in a checklist format. The right or lower monitor displays accompanying system information in a pictorial form, such as a diagram of the system being referred to on The ECAM monitors are typically powered by separate signal generators. Aircraft data inputs are fed into two flight warning computers. Analog inputs are first fed through a system data analog converter and then into the warning computers. The warning computers process the information and forward information to the signal generators to illuminate the monitors. [Figure 10-120 ]

There are four basic modes to the ECAM system: flight phase, advisory, failure related, and manual. The flight phase mode is normally used. The phases are: preflight, takeoff, climb, cruise, descent, approach, and post landing. Advisory and failure–related modes will appear automatically as the situation requires. When an advisory is shown on the primary monitor, the secondary monitor will automatically display the system schematic with numerical values. The same is true for the failure-related mode, which takes precedent over all other modes regardless of which mode is selected at the time of the failure. Color coding is used on the displays to draw attention to matters in order of importance. Display modes are selected via a separate ECAM control panel shown in Figure 10-121 .

The manual mode of an ECAM is set by pressing one of the synoptic display buttons on the control panel. This allows the display of system diagrams. A failure warning or advisory event will cancel this view.

ECAM flight warning computers self-test upon startup. The signal generators are also tested. A maintenance panel allows for testing annunciation and further testing upon demand. BITE stands for built-in test equipment. It is standard for monitoring systems to monitor themselves as well as the aircraft systems. All of the system inputs to the flight warning computers can also be tested for continuity from this panel, as well as inputs and outputs of the system data analog converter. Any individual system faults will be listed on the primary display as normal. Faults in the flight warning computers and signal generators will annunciate on the maintenance panel. Follow the manufacturer’s guidelines when testing ECAM and related systems.

Engine Indicating & Crew Alerting System (EICAS)

An engine indicating and crew alerting system (EICAS) performs many of the same functions as an ECAM system. The objective is still to monitor the aircraft systems for the pilot. All EICASs display engine, as well as airframe, parameters.

Traditional gauges are not utilized, other than as a standby combination engine gauge in case of total system failure.

EICAS is also a two-monitor, two-computer system with a display select panel. Both monitors receive information from the same computer. The second computer serves as a standby. Digital and analog inputs from the engine and airframe systems are continuously monitored. Caution and warning lights, as well as aural tones, are incorporated.

EICAS provides full time primary engine parameters (EPR, N1, EGT) on the top, primary monitor. Advisories and warning are also shown there. Secondary engine parameters and nonengine system status are displayed on the bottom screen. The lower screen is also used for maintenance diagnosis when the aircraft is on the ground. Color coding is used, as well as message prioritizing.

The display select panel allows the pilot to choose which computer is actively supplying information. It also controls the display of secondary engine information and system status displays on the lower monitor. EICAS has a unique feature that automatically records the parameters of a failure event to be reviewed afterwards by maintenance personnel. Pilots that suspect a problem may be occurring during flight can press the event record button on the display select panel. This also records the parameters for that flight period to be studied later by maintenance. Hydraulic, electrical, environmental, performance, and APU data are examples of what may be EICAS uses BITE for systems and components. A maintenance panel is included for technicians. From this panel, when the aircraft is on the ground, push-button switches display information pertinent to various systems for analysis.

2. 中文翻译

为提高复杂飞机的运行安全性，计算机与计算机系统被引入飞机。飞行仪表以及发动机和机体监控，是特别适合借助计算机获益的领域。计算机的贡献在于帮助减少仪表板上的杂乱，并使飞行员的注意力只集中在当下紧要的事项上。

“玻璃座舱”一词，指的是在驾驶舱仪表中使用平板显示屏。实际上，它还指使用计算机生成的图像取代一个个机械式仪表。此外，计算机与计算机系统能以超出人的能力的方式，监控运行中飞机的各种过程和部件，同时免去飞行员必须亲自监控的压力。

计算机化的电子飞行仪表系统还有额外的好处。元件的固态特性提高了可靠性。此外，微处理器、数据总线和 LCD 都能节省空间与重量。以下系统已在飞机上研制并使用多年。未来必定还会出现新的系统和计算机架构。

电子飞行仪表系统（EFIS）

飞行仪表是最先采用计算机技术、使用平板多功能显示器（MFD）的。EFIS 使用专用信号发生器驱动位于“基本 T”布局中央的两台独立显示器。姿态指示器和航向陀螺由阴极射线管（CRT）取代，用来显示 EADI 和 EHSI 画面。这些增强型仪表与普通机械式和电动式仪表并存工作，集成程度有限。尽管如此，EADI 和 EHSI 技术仍非常可取，它减轻了工作负荷、减少了仪表板扫视，并因前文所述导航信息的集成而提高了安全性。

早期 EFIS 系统采用模拟技术，而较新的型号可能是数字系统。信号发生器从姿态和导航设备接收信息。飞行员通过显示控制器选择希望显示的各种模式或画面特征。通常设有相互独立的机长和副驾驶专用系统。还有第三台备份符号发生器，当两台主组件之一失效时可接替工作。

以电子方式描绘 ADI 和 HSI 信息，是 EFIS 系统的核心目的。相比传统仪表，其扩大的尺寸和能力可以集成更多的飞行仪表数据。垂直的空速刻度通常显示在姿态显示区的左侧，其相对位置与模拟式“基本 T”仪表板中的空速表相同。在姿态显示区右侧，许多 EFIS 系统显示高度和垂直速度刻度。由于多数 EFIS 的 EADI 画面都包含通常属于转弯协调器的倾斜仪，因此全部基本飞行仪表都由 EFIS 显示器来描绘。

电子中央飞机监控系统（ECAM）

所有飞机上飞行员的工作负荷，都包括持续监控飞行仪表和机外天空。还包括警惕地审视发动机和机体系统是否正常工作。在运输类飞机上，这意味着除操纵飞机之外还要监控大量仪表。电子中央飞机监控（ECAM）系统就是为协助完成这项职责而设计的。

ECAM（以及其他监控系统）的基本理念，是代替飞行员自动执行监控职责。当检测到问题或发生失效时，主显示器连同音响和视觉提示一起向飞行员告警。需要采取的修正措施会显示出来，因失效而建议采取的措施也会显示。由于系统监控自动进行，飞行员可以专心驾驶飞机，直到出现问题为止。

早期 ECAM 系统只监控机体系统，发动机参数由驾驶舱内传统的常显仪表显示。后来型号的 ECAM 系统则把发动机显示与机体显示一并纳入。

ECAM 系统有两台 CRT 显示器，在较新的飞机上可能是 LCD。左侧或上方的显示器（视飞机仪表板布局而定）以检查单格式显示系统状态信息以及任何警告和相应的修正措施。右侧或下方的显示器以图形形式显示相应的系统信息，例如所涉及系统的示意图。ECAM 显示器通常由各自独立的信号发生器供电。飞机数据输入送入两台飞行警告计算机。模拟输入先经过系统数据模拟转换器，再进入警告计算机。警告计算机处理这些信息，并把信息转发给信号发生器，以点亮显示器。[图 10-120]

ECAM 系统有四种基本模式：飞行阶段、咨询、失效相关和人工。通常使用飞行阶段模式。这些阶段为：飞行前、起飞、爬升、巡航、下降、进近和着陆后。咨询模式和失效相关模式会视情况需要自动出现。当主显示器上出现咨询信息时，辅助显示器会自动显示带数值的系统示意图。失效相关模式也是如此，该模式优先于所有其他模式，无论失效发生时选中的是哪种模式。显示器上使用颜色编码，按重要程度依次引起注意。显示模式通过单独的 ECAM 控制面板选择，见图 10-121。

按下控制面板上的某个系统简图显示按钮，即可设置 ECAM 的人工模式。这样就能显示各系统简图。一旦出现失效警告或咨询事件，该视图即被取消。

ECAM 的飞行警告计算机在启动时进行自检，信号发生器也要测试。维护面板可用于测试通告显示，并可按需进行进一步测试。BITE 代表机内测试设备。监控系统既监控飞机系统、也监控自身，这是标准做法。从该面板还可以测试送入飞行警告计算机的所有系统输入的导通性，以及系统数据模拟转换器的输入和输出。任何单个系统故障都会照常列在主显示器上。飞行警告计算机和信号发生器的故障则会在维护面板上通告。测试 ECAM 及相关系统时，应遵循制造商的指导。

发动机指示与机组告警系统（EICAS）

发动机指示与机组告警系统（EICAS）执行许多与 ECAM 系统相同的功能。其目的仍是为飞行员监控飞机系统。所有 EICAS 都显示发动机参数，同时也显示机体参数。

除作为系统完全失效时的备用组合发动机仪表外，不再使用传统仪表。

EICAS 同样是双显示器、双计算机并带显示选择面板的系统。两台显示器都从同一台计算机接收信息，第二台计算机作为备用。发动机和机体系统的数字与模拟输入被连续监控。系统中还设有注意灯和警告灯以及音响信号。

EICAS 在上方的主显示器上常显主要发动机参数（EPR、N1、EGT），咨询信息和警告也显示在那里。次要发动机参数和非发动机系统状态显示在下方屏幕上。飞机在地面时，下方屏幕还用于维护诊断。显示中使用了颜色编码以及信息优先级排序。

显示选择面板让飞行员选择由哪台计算机实际提供信息，它还控制下方显示器上次要发动机信息和系统状态显示的显示。EICAS 有一项独特功能，能自动记录失效事件的参数，供维修人员事后查阅。怀疑飞行中可能正在出现问题的飞行员，可以按下显示选择面板上的事件记录按钮，这也会记录该飞行时段的参数，供维修人员日后研究。液压、电气、环境、性能以及 APU 数据，都是可能被记录的内容。EICAS 对系统和部件使用 BITE。系统配有供技术人员使用的维护面板。飞机在地面时，通过该面板上的按钮开关可显示各系统的相关信息以供分析。

4. 语法解析

① 目的状语 + 现在完成时被动语态

原句：In an effort to increase the safety of operating complicated aircraft, computers and computer systems have been incorporated. 拆解：In an effort to increase the safety of operating complicated aircraft 为目的状语，其中 operating 为动名词修饰 aircraft；主句主语 computers and computer systems，谓语 have been incorporated 为现在完成时的被动语态。 要点：in an effort to do 相当于“为了做……”，不能按字面译成“在努力中”。现在完成被动强调“已被引入”这一结果，施动者（设计者）不必出现。

② that 引导的定语从句

原句：“Glass flight deck” is a term that refers to the use of flat-panel display screens in flight deck instrumentation. 拆解：主语 “Glass flight deck”，系动词 is，表语 a term；that refers to the use of flat-panel display screens in flight deck instrumentation 为定语从句修饰 term，关系词 that 在从句中作主语，谓语 refers to。 要点：term 是抽象名词，后面的 that 从句是定语从句而非同位语从句——that 在从句中充当主语，从句缺它不完整。refer to 在此意为“指”，不是“参考”。

③ 分词短语作插入语

原句：The left or upper monitor, depending on the aircraft panel layout, displays information on system status and any warnings associated corrective actions. 拆解：主语 The left or upper monitor，逗号间 depending on the aircraft panel layout 为现在分词短语作插入语（补充条件）；谓语 displays，宾语 information，on system status and any warnings... 为介词短语作后置定语。 要点：插入语用两个逗号隔开，删去后主谓仍然完整，这是识别插入语的可靠办法。depending on 的逻辑主语是说话人/读者，属悬垂分词，科技英语中常见。

④ 非限制性定语从句 + regardless of 宾语从句

原句：The same is true for the failure-related mode, which takes precedent over all other modes regardless of which mode is selected at the time of the failure. 拆解：主句 The same is true for the failure-related mode；which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 mode，从句谓语 takes precedent over；regardless of 后接 which 引导的宾语从句，作让步状语。 要点：takes precedent over 意为“优先于”，precedent 此处不译“先例”。regardless of 是介词短语，后接名词或从句均可；which mode 是从句的引导词，不是疑问句。

⑤ since 原因状语从句 + 同位语插入

原句：Since most EFIS EADI depictions include the inclinometer, normally part of the turn coordinator, all of the basic flight instruments are depicted by the EFIS display. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（既然/由于），从句主语 most EFIS EADI depictions，谓语 include；normally part of the turn coordinator 为同位语，补充说明 inclinometer；主句为被动语态 are depicted by。 要点：since 在此表原因，不表时间，可据主句是结论性陈述来判断。同位语紧跟在被说明的名词后，用逗号隔开，不要误当成 include 的并列宾语。

⑥ should 倒装表示虚拟条件

原句：A third, backup symbol generator is available to assume operation should one of the two primary units fail. 拆解：主句主语 A third, backup symbol generator，谓语 is available，to assume operation 为目的状语；should one of the two primary units fail 为省略 if 的倒装条件从句，相当于 if one of the two primary units should fail。 要点：should 提到主语前即构成虚拟条件，常用于科技说明中的“万一……”。从句谓语用动词原形 fail，不能加 -s；主句用陈述语气 is available，说明这是常规配置而非假设结果。
