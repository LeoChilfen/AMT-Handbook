Heat Energy and Thermal Efficiency

通用 Ch 05 · Heat Energy and Thermal Efficiency · 原文 PDF 第 169 页

1. 原文

Thermal efficiency is the relationship between the potential for power contained in a specific heat source, and how much usable power is created when that heat source is used. The formula for calculating thermal efficiency is:

Horsepower Produced ÷ Potential Horsepower in Fuel For example, consider the piston engine used in a small general aviation airplane, which typically consumes 0.5 lb of fuel per hour for each horsepower it creates. Imagine that the engine is creating 200 hp. If we multiply 0.5 by the horsepower of 200, we find the engine is consuming 100 lb of fuel per hour, or 1.67 lb per minute. Earlier in this chapter, one horsepower was found to be 33,000 ft-lb of work per minute.

The potential horsepower in the fuel burned for this example engine would be:

Hp = 33,000 ft-lb/min 1.67 lb/minute × 18,900 BTU/lb × 778 ft lb/BTU Hp = 744 The example engine is burning enough fuel that it has the potential to create 744 horsepower, but it is only creating 200.

The thermal efficiency of the engine would be:

Thermal Efficiency = Hp Produced ÷ Hp in Fuel = 200 ÷ 744 = .2688 or 26.88% More than 70 percent of the energy in the fuel is not being used to create usable horsepower. The wasted energy is in the form of friction and heat. A tremendous amount of heat is given up to the atmosphere and not used inside the engine to create power.

2. 中文翻译

热效率是指某一特定热源所含的功率潜力，与该热源被利用时实际产生的可用功率之间的关系。计算热效率的公式为：

Horsepower Produced ÷ Potential Horsepower in Fuel 例如，考虑小型通用航空飞机所用的活塞发动机，它每产生 1 马力通常每小时消耗 0.5 lb 燃油。设想该发动机正在产生 200 hp。若用 0.5 乘以 200 马力，可知该发动机每小时消耗 100 lb 燃油，即每分钟 1.67 lb。本章前面已得出，1 马力等于每分钟 33,000 ft-lb 的功。

这台示例发动机所烧燃油中的潜在马力为：

Hp = 33,000 ft-lb/min 1.67 lb/minute × 18,900 BTU/lb × 778 ft lb/BTU（原书为分式，OCR 压平成一行，实为 Hp = [1.67 lb/minute × 18,900 BTU/lb × 778 ft lb/BTU] ÷ 33,000 ft-lb/min）Hp = 744 这台示例发动机所烧的燃油足以产生 744 马力的潜在功率，但它实际只产生 200 马力。

该发动机的热效率为：

Thermal Efficiency = Hp Produced ÷ Hp in Fuel = 200 ÷ 744 = .2688 or 26.88% 燃油中 70% 以上的能量并未用于产生可用马力。被浪费的能量以摩擦和热的形式存在。大量的热被排入大气，而没有在发动机内部用于产生功率。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作定语 + 非限制性定语从句

原句：For example, consider the piston engine used in a small general aviation airplane, which typically consumes 0.5 lb of fuel per hour for each horsepower it creates.

拆解：祈使句 consider + 宾语 the piston engine；used in a small general aviation airplane 是过去分词短语作后置定语；which typically consumes … 是非限制性定语从句，which 指 the piston engine；it creates 是省略关系代词的定语从句，修饰 each horsepower。

要点：used in … 是「用于……的」，不是谓语。which 前有逗号，属于补充说明该发动机的耗油特性，不能译成限定关系。for each horsepower it creates 中 it creates 省略了 that，读时先抓 consumes 这个主句谓语，再处理两个从句。

② 被动语态 + 不定式补足：was found to be

原句：Earlier in this chapter, one horsepower was found to be 33,000 ft-lb of work per minute.

拆解：主语 one horsepower；谓语 was found to be（一般过去时被动 + 不定式）；33,000 ft-lb of work per minute 作 to be 的表语。

要点：find sb/sth to be … 变被动后为 sb/sth is found to be …，表示「被认定／查明为」。同类还有 is shown to be、is considered to be。这里的 to be 不能省，省略后句子结构不成立。Earlier in this chapter 是「本章前面」，指回前文的结论。

③ 现在进行时被动：is not being used to create usable horsepower

原句：More than 70 percent of the energy in the fuel is not being used to create usable horsepower.

拆解：主语 More than 70 percent of the energy in the fuel（百分数 + of + 不可数名词，谓语用单数）；谓语 is not being used 是现在进行时的被动语态；to create usable horsepower 是目的状语。

要点：is being used ＝「正在被使用」，与 is used（一般现在时，表常态）不同，这里强调「此刻并未被利用」。百分数作主语时谓语单复数取决于 of 后面的名词：70 percent of the energy is …，但 70 percent of the engines are …。usable 由 use 加 -able 构成，意为「可用的」。
