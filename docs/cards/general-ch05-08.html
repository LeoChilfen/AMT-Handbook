1. 原文

Potential energy is defined as being energy at rest, or energy that is stored. Potential energy may be classified into three groups: (1) energy due to position, (2) energy due to distortion of an elastic body, and (3) energy which produces work

Examples of the first group are water in an elevated reservoir or an airplane raised off the ground with jacks; a stretched bungee cord on a Piper Tri- Pacer or compressed spring are examples of the second group; and energy in aviation gasoline, food, or storage batteries are examples of the third group.

To calculate the potential energy of an object due to its position, as in height, the following formula is used:

Potential Energy = Weight × Height A calculation based on this formula will produce an answer that has units of foot-pounds (ft-lb) or inch-pounds (in-lb), which are the same units that apply to work. Work, which is covered later in this chapter, is described as a force being applied over a measured distance, with the force being pounds and the distance being feet or inches. Potential energy and work have a lot in common.

Example: A Boeing 747 weighing 450,000 pounds needs to be raised 4 feet in the air so maintenance can be done on the landing gear. How much potential energy does the airplane possess because of this raised position?

Potential Ener gy = Weight × Height PE = 450,000 lb × 4 ft PE = 1,800,000 ft-lb As previously mentioned, aviation gasoline possesses potential energy because of its chemical nature. Gasoline has the potential to release heat energy, based on its British thermal unit (BTU) content. One pound of aviation gas contains 18,900 BTU of heat energy, and each BTU is capable of 778 ft-lb of work. So, when we multiply 778 by 18,900, we find that one pound of aviation gas is capable of 14,704,200 ft-lb of work. Imagine the potential energy in the completely serviced fuel tanks of an airplane.

要计算物体因其位置（例如高度）而具有的势能，使用下列公式：

Potential Energy = Weight × Height 按此公式计算得到的结果，其单位为英尺-磅（ft-lb）或英寸-磅（in-lb），与适用于功的单位相同。功（本章后文将讨论）被描述为在量度的距离上施加的力，其中力以磅计、距离以英尺或英寸计。势能与功有很多共同之处。

例：一架重 450,000 磅的波音 747 需要被举高 4 英尺，以便对起落架进行维修。由于这一被举高的位置，该飞机具有多少势能？

Potential Ener gy = Weight × Height PE = 450,000 lb × 4 ft PE = 1,800,000 ft-lb（原文「Potential Ener gy」为 OCR 断词，即 Potential Energy。）如前所述，航空汽油因其化学性质而具有势能。汽油具有释放热能的潜力，这取决于其英国热量单位（BTU）含量。一磅航空汽油含 18,900 BTU 的热能，而每 BTU 能做出 778 ft-lb 的功。因此，把 778 乘以 18,900，就可得出：一磅航空汽油能够做出 14,704,200 ft-lb 的功。想一想一架燃油加注完毕的飞机，其油箱里蕴藏着多大的势能。

4. 语法解析

① define as being … or …

原句：Potential energy is defined as being energy at rest, or energy that is stored.

拆解：is defined as being 是被动定义句式，as 后接动名词 being，再带表语 energy at rest；or energy that is stored 是对 energy at rest 的同义改写，that is stored 为定语从句。

要点：be defined as being 与 be defined as 意思相同，加 being 使定义更侧重「处于某种状态」，在技术文本中常见。or 在此表「或者说」，前后是同一概念的不同表述，不是选择关系。at rest 是固定短语，不加冠词。

② 嵌套定语从句：that has units … which are the same units that …

原句：A calculation based on this formula will produce an answer that has units of foot-pounds (ft-lb) or inch-pounds (in-lb), which are the same units that apply to work.

拆解：主句 A calculation … will produce an answer；based on this formula 是过去分词短语后置修饰 calculation；that has units … 是定语从句修饰 answer；which are the same units that apply to work 是非限制性定语从句，其中又含 that apply to work 修饰 units。

要点：定语从句可以层层嵌套，读到 which 时先回找最近的先行词 units，再判断关系。apply to 意为「适用于」，不是「申请」。the same … that … 指「同一个」，与 the same … as …（同类）语义不同。

③ when 从句 + find that 宾语从句

原句：So, when we multiply 778 by 18,900, we find that one pound of aviation gas is capable of 14,704,200 ft-lb of work.

拆解：when 引导时间状语从句，multiply A by B 是固定搭配；主句 we find that …，that 引导宾语从句，从句中 be capable of + 名词表示「有能力做出」。

要点：multiply A by B（A 乘以 B）与 divide A by B（A 除以 B）方向相反，是计算类句子最易出错处。be capable of doing 与 can do 同义，但更书面，且 of 后必须接动名词。
