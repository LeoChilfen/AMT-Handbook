1. 原文

Pressure is the amount of force acting on a specific amount of surface area. The force is typically measured in pounds and the surface area in square inches, making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi. If a 100-lb weight was placed on top of a block with a surface area of 10 in 2, the average weight distribution would be 10 lb for each of the square inches (100 ÷ 10), or 10 psi. When atmospheric pressure is being measured, in addition to psi, other means of pressure measurement can be used. These include inches or millimeters of mercury, and millibars. Standard day atmospheric pressure is equal to 14.7 psi, 29.92 inches of mercury ("Hg), 760 millimeters of mercury (mm hg), or 1013.2 millibars. The test tube is then turned upside down and placed in an open container of mercury, and the top is uncovered. Gravity acting on the mercury in the test tube will try to make the mercury run out. Atmospheric pressure pushing down on the mercury in the open container tries to make the mercury stay in the test tube. At some point these two forces, gravity and atmospheric pressure, will equal out and the mercury will stabilize at a certain height in the test tube. Under standard day atmospheric conditions, the air in a 1-in2 column extending all the way to the top of the atmosphere would weigh 14.7 lb. A 1 in2 column of mercury, 29.92 inches tall, would also weigh 14.7 lb. That is why 14.7 psi is equal to 29.92 "Hg.

A gauge pressure (psig) is a reading that refers to when an instrument, such as an oil pressure gauge, fuel pressure gauge, or hydraulic system pressure gauge, displays pressure which is over and above ambient. When the oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump is not turning, and there is no pressure being created, the gauge will read zero.

Absolute Pressure

A gauge that includes atmospheric pressure in its reading is measuring what is known as absolute pressure, or psia. Absolute pressure is equal to gauge pressure plus atmospheric pressure. If someone hooked up a psia indicating instrument to an engine’s oil system, the gauge would read atmospheric pressure when the engine was not running. Since this would not make good sense to the typical operator, psia gauges are not used in this type of application. For the manifold pressure on a piston engine, a psia gauge does make good sense. Manifold pressure on a piston engine can read anywhere from less than atmospheric pressure if the engine is not supercharged, to more than atmospheric if it is supercharged. The only gauge that has the flexibility to show this variety of readings is the absolute pressure gauge. A manifold pressure gauge has a readout that ranges from 10 "Hg to 35 "Hg. Remember that 29.92 "Hg is standard day atmospheric.

Differential Pressure

Differential pressure, or psid, is the difference between pressures being read at two different locations within a system. For example, in a turbine engine oil system the pressure is read as it enters the oil filter, and as it leaves the filter. These two readings are sent to a transmitter which powers a light located on the flight deck. Across anything that poses a resistance to flow, like an oil filter, there will be a drop in pressure. If the filter starts to clog, the pressure drop will become greater, eventually causing the advisory light on the flight deck to come on. There is a differential pressure gauge for the pressurization system on a Boeing 737. In this case, the difference in pressure is between the inside and the outside of the airplane. If the pressure difference becomes too great, the structure of the airplane could become overstressed.

2. 中文翻译

压力是作用在一定表面积上的力的大小。力通常用磅来度量，表面积用平方英寸来度量，因此压力的单位是磅每平方英寸，即 psi。如果把一个 100 lb 的重物放在表面积为 10 in2 的物块上，平均的重量分布就是每平方英寸 10 lb（100 ÷ 10），即 10 psi。测量大气压力时，除 psi 以外还可以采用其他压力计量方式，其中包括英寸汞柱或毫米汞柱，以及毫巴。标准日大气压力等于 14.7 psi、29.92 英寸汞柱（"Hg）、760 毫米汞柱（mm Hg）或 1013.2 毫巴。这些计量单位之间的关系如下：

表压力（psig）指的是仪表读数高出环境压力的那部分压力，例如滑油压力表、燃油压力表或液压系统压力表所显示的就是这种压力。当滑油泵、燃油泵或液压泵停止转动、不再产生压力时，该表的读数为零。

绝对压力

把大气压力计入读数的仪表，测量的是所谓绝对压力，即 psia。绝对压力等于表压力加大气压力。如果把一块指示 psia 的仪表接到发动机的滑油系统上，那么发动机不运转时该表会指示大气压力。由于这在一般操作者看来并不合理，所以这类场合不使用 psia 表。对于活塞式发动机的进气压力来说，psia 表就非常合理。活塞式发动机的进气压力，在发动机不增压时可以低于大气压力，增压时又可以高于大气压力。唯有绝对压力表才有这种灵活显示多种读数的能力。进气压力表的读数范围为 10 "Hg 到 35 "Hg。请记住，29.92 "Hg 就是标准日大气压力。

压差

压差，或称 psid，是系统内两个不同位置所测得压力之差。例如在燃气涡轮发动机的滑油系统中，要在滑油进入油滤时和流出油滤时分别测量压力。这两个读数被送往一个变送器，由它点亮驾驶舱里的一盏灯。流体流过任何对流动构成阻力的东西（如油滤）时，压力都会下降。如果油滤开始堵塞，压降就会变得更大，最终使驾驶舱里的警告灯亮起。波音 737 的增压系统装有压差表。在这里，压差指的是机内与机外之间的压力差。若压差变得过大，飞机结构就可能承受过大的应力。

4. 语法解析

① 并列分句中的省略

原句：The force is typically measured in pounds and the surface area in square inches, making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi.

拆解：第一分句为“主语 + is measured + in pounds”的被动结构；and 之后的第二分句只剩“the surface area + in square inches”，省略了重复的 is measured。句末 making … 为现在分词短语，作前面整个并列结构的结果状语。

要点：英语常用“助动词/系动词右省略”避免重复，翻译时要把省略的谓语补出来（“表面积则用平方英寸度量”），否则容易误读成两个并列名词短语。

② 现在分词短语作结果状语

原句：The force is typically measured in pounds and the surface area in square inches, making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi.

拆解：making 的逻辑主语是前面整个句子所述的做法；make + 宾语（the units of pressure）+ 名词短语宾补（pounds per square inch or psi），表示“使……成为……”。

要点：make 后接名词宾补时不加 to、不加 as；翻译一般处理成“因此……即为……”，“使……成为……”。

③ 与事实相反的虚拟条件句

原句：If someone hooked up a psia indicating instrument to an engine’s oil system, the gauge would read atmospheric pressure when the engine was not running.

拆解：if 从句用一般过去时 hooked up，主句用 would + 动词原形 read，构成对现在情况的虚拟；句末 when the engine was not running 为时间状语从句，用过去时是为与虚拟语气保持一致。

要点：技术文章常用虚拟语气做“假设性推演”，汉语译作“如果把……接上，那么……就会……”，不要译成过去发生的事。

④ 定语从句 + 不定式后置定语

原句：The only gauge that has the flexibility to show this variety of readings is the absolute pressure gauge.

拆解：主干是 The only gauge … is the absolute pressure gauge；that has the flexibility 为关系代词 that 引导的定语从句，修饰 the only gauge；to show this variety of readings 为不定式，作 flexibility 的后置定语。

要点：主语后带了长定语从句，谓语 is 仍与单数主语 The only gauge 一致；翻译宜先译定语从句再落到主句判断，避免“只有仪表……是仪表”的拗口。

⑤ there be 结构 + 被动意义的 being done

原句：When the oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump is not turning, and there is no pressure being created, the gauge will read zero.

拆解：when 引导时间状语从句，内含两个由 and 并列的分句：前一个是“主语 + is not turning”的进行时，后一个是 there is + no pressure + being created。being created 为现在分词被动式，作 pressure 的后置定语，表示“正在被产生”的动作。

要点：there be 句型中不能再出现谓语动词，故“有压力正在产生”必须写成 there is pressure being created；主句用一般将来时 will read 表示常态结果。

⑥ why 引导的表语从句

原句：That is why 14.7 psi is equal to 29.92 "Hg.

拆解：That 为主语，is 为系动词，why 引导的从句作表语；why 在从句中作原因状语，从句本身是“主语 + 系动词 + 表语”的完整陈述句，语序不倒装。

要点：why 表语从句与 because 表语从句方向相反：That is why …（结果是……）／That is because …（原因是……）；从句用陈述语序，不可写成 why is 14.7 psi equal to …。
