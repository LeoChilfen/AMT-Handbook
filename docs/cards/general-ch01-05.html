Safety Around Hazardous Materials

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Hazardous Materials · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

1. 原文

Material safety diamonds are important with regard to shop safety. These diamond-shaped labels are a simple and quick way to determine the risk of hazardous material within the associated container and, if used properly with the tags, indicate what personal safety equipment to use. The most observable portion of the Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) (formerly known as Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)) label is the risk diamond. It is a four-color segmented diamond that represents flammability (red), reactivity (yellow), health (blue), and special hazard (white). In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4. Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous. The special hazard segment contains a word or abbreviation to represent the specific hazard. Some examples are RAD for radiation, ALK for alkali materials, Acid for acidic materials, and CARC for carcinogenic materials. The letter W with a line through it stands for high reactivity to water. [Figure 1-2]

The SDS is a more detailed version of the chemical safety issues. These forms have the detailed breakdown of the chemicals, including formulas and action to take if personnel come in contact with the chemicals. All sheets have the same information requirements; however, the exact location of the information on the sheet may vary depending on the SDS manufacturer. These forms are necessary for a safe shop that meets all the requirements of the governing safety body, the U.S. Department of Labor Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA).

2. 中文翻译

材料安全菱形标识对车间安全十分重要。这类菱形标签是判断相关容器内危险材料风险的简便快捷方法，若与标签配合正确使用，还能指明应使用何种个人防护装备。安全数据表（SDSs，旧称材料安全数据表 MSDS）标签中最显眼的部分是风险菱形。它是一个四色分区的菱形，分别代表可燃性（红）、反应性（黄）、健康危害（蓝）和特殊危险（白）。在可燃性、反应性和健康三个区块中，标有 0 至 4 的数字。0 表示对使用者危害很小或没有危害，而 4 表示该材料危害很大。特殊危险区块含有一个词或缩写，表示具体危险。例如 RAD 表示放射性、ALK 表示碱性材料、Acid 表示酸性材料、CARC 表示致癌材料。字母 W 加一道斜线表示遇水高度反应。[图 1-2]

SDS 是化学品安全问题的更详细版本。这些表格载有化学品的详细分解资料，包括化学式以及人员接触化学品后应采取的处置措施。所有表格的信息要求相同；但信息在表格上的确切位置可能因 SDS 编制厂商而异。对于符合主管安全机构——美国劳工部职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）——全部要求的安全车间而言，这些表格是必需的。

4. 语法解析

① 并列谓语 + 插入的条件状语 + what + 不定式宾语从句

原句：These diamond-shaped labels are a simple and quick way to determine the risk of hazardous material within the associated container and, if used properly with the tags, indicate what personal safety equipment to use. 拆解：主语 These diamond-shaped labels + 并列谓语 are… and indicate…；to determine… 为不定式作后置定语修饰 way；if used properly with the tags 为插入的条件状语（省略 they are），插在两个谓语之间；what personal safety equipment to use 为宾语从句（疑问词 + 不定式）。 要点：if used properly… 是“if they are used properly…”的省略，被动语态省掉的主语与主句主语一致时才可如此处理。what + 名词 + to do 表示“该用哪一个……”，疑问词后不倒装；这种名词性从句常作 indicate、determine、know 的宾语。易错点：第二个谓语 indicate 与 are 并列共用主语，不要把 if 从句当成它的条件的一部分而漏读主句。

② 句首地点状语 + there be 存在句

原句：In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4. 拆解：介词短语 In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks 置于句首作地点状语 + there is 存在句 + 真正主语 a number + from 0 to 4 后置定语。 要点：存在句的谓语与 be 后的真正主语一致，此处 a number 为单数，故用 there is，不能因为前面列了三个区块就用 are。from 0 to 4 是“0 到 4”的区间表达，作定语后置；作状语时也常写成 from 0 to 4 / between 0 and 4。

③ while 表对比 + 宾语从句

原句：Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous. 拆解：前半 Zero represents little or no hazard to the user（主谓宾）；while 引导对比状语从句（= 而……）+ 4 means + that 引导的宾语从句 the material is very hazardous。 要点：while 在此表对比对照（“而”），不是“当……时”；判断依据是前后两个分句主语不同、语义相反。little or no 是“很少或没有”的固定搭配，little 修饰不可数名词 hazard（此处作“危害”解为不可数）。represent 直接接名词（represent a hazard），mean 后接 that 从句，两个动词的句型不能互换。

④ 分号 + however 连接两个独立分句

原句：All sheets have the same information requirements; however, the exact location of the information on the sheet may vary depending on the SDS manufacturer. 拆解：分号连接两个独立分句；however 为连接副词，后跟逗号；第二分句主语 the exact location（of the information on the sheet 为后置定语）+ 谓语 may vary + 现在分词短语 depending on the SDS manufacturer 作状语。 要点：两个完整句之间用“分号 + however + 逗号”是书面语的规范写法，不能只用逗号（那是逗号粘连的错误）。depend on 的逻辑主语是 location（位置取决于厂商）；vary 是不及物动词“不同、有差异”，不接宾语。易错点：however 不是连词，不能像 but 那样只加逗号连接两句。
