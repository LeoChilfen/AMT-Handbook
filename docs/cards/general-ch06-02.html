Requirements for Aircraft Weighing

通用 Ch 06 · Requirements for Aircraft Weighing · 原文 PDF 第 213 页

1. 原文

Every aircraft type certificated by the FAA receives a weight and balance report as part of its required aircraft records before leaving the factory for delivery to its new owner. The weight and balance report identifies the empty weight of the aircraft and the location at which the aircraft balances, known as the center of gravity (CG). The weight and balance report must include an equipment list showing weights and moment arms of all required and optional items of equipment included in the certificated empty weight. If the manufacturer chooses to do so, it can weigh every aircraft it produces and issue the weight and balance report based on that weighing. As an alternative, the manufacturer is permitted to weigh an agreed upon percentage of a particular model of aircraft produced, perhaps 10 to 20 percent, and apply the average to all the aircraft.

After the aircraft leaves the factory and is delivered to its owner, the requirement for placing the aircraft on scales and reweighing it varies depending on the type of aircraft and how it is used. For a small, GA airplane being used privately, such as a Cessna 172, there is no FAA requirement that it be periodically reweighed; but after each annual, the mechanic must ensure that the weight and balance data in the aircraft records is correct. Additionally, there is an FAA requirement that the airplane always have a current and accurate weight and balance report. If the weight and balance report for an aircraft is lost, the aircraft must be weighed and a new report must be created. When an aircraft has undergone extensive repair, major alteration, or has new equipment installed, such as a radio or a global positioning system, a new weight and balance report must be created. The equipment installer may place the airplane on scales and weigh it after the installation, which is an acceptable way of creating the new report. If the installer knows the exact weight and location of the new equipment, it is also possible to create a new report by doing a series of mathematical calculations.

Over time, almost all aircraft tend to gain weight. Examples of how this can happen include an airplane being repainted without the old paint being removed and the accumulation of dirt, grease, and oil in parts of the aircraft that are not easily accessible for cleaning. When new equipment is installed, and its weight and location are mathematically accounted for, some miscellaneous weight might be overlooked, such as wire and hardware. For this reason, even if the FAA does not require it, it is a good practice to periodically place an aircraft on scales and confirm its actual empty weight and empty weight center of gravity (EWCG).

Some aircraft are required to be weighed and have their CG calculated on a periodic basis, typically every 3 years. Examples of aircraft that fall under this requirement are:

  1. Air taxi and charter twin-engine airplanes operating under Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 135, section 135.185(a).
  1. Airplanes with a seating capacity of 20 or more passengers or a maximum payload of 6,000 pounds or more, as identified in 14 CFR part 125, section 125.91(b).

2. 中文翻译

每一种由 FAA 合格审定的航空器，在离开工厂交付新机主之前，都会取得一份重量与平衡报告，作为其必备航空器记录的一部分。重量与平衡报告载明该航空器的空重，以及该航空器取得平衡的位置，即重心（CG）。重量与平衡报告必须包含一份设备清单，列出计入合格审定空重的全部必备与选装设备各自的重量和力臂。如果制造商选择这样做，可以对它生产的每一架航空器称重，并据以出具重量与平衡报告。作为替代方案，制造商可以对某一已投产型号按约定比例（例如 10% 到 20%）称重，再把这批的平均值套用到该型号的所有航空器上。

航空器出厂并交付机主之后，把它放上秤台重新称重的要求，因航空器类型和使用方式而异。对于私人使用的小型 GA 飞机，例如塞斯纳 172，FAA 并不要求定期重新称重；但每次年检之后，维修人员必须确保航空器记录中的重量与平衡数据是正确的。此外，FAA 还要求飞机始终持有一份现行且准确的重量与平衡报告。如果一架航空器的重量与平衡报告丢失，就必须对该航空器称重并重新出具报告。当航空器经过大范围修理、重大改装，或加装了新设备（例如电台或全球定位系统）时，必须重新出具重量与平衡报告。设备安装者可以在安装完成后把飞机放上秤台称重，这也是出具新报告的一种可接受方式。如果安装者知道新设备的准确重量和位置，也可以通过一系列计算来出具新报告。

随着时间推移，几乎所有航空器都会增重。造成增重的情形包括：飞机重新喷漆时没有去除旧漆；以及不易清洁的部位积聚了灰尘、油脂和滑油。加装新设备、并以计算方式计入其重量和位置时，一些零散重量可能被漏算，例如导线和五金件。因此，即使 FAA 并不要求，定期把航空器放上秤台核对实际空重与空重重心（EWCG），仍是一种良好做法。

有些航空器必须定期称重并计算重心，通常每 3 年一次。属于这一要求范围的航空器例如：

  1. 按《联邦法规汇编》第 14 编（14 CFR）第 135 部第 135.185(a) 节运行的空中出租与包机双发飞机。
  1. 按 14 CFR 第 125 部第 125.91(b) 节所界定，座位数达到 20 个或以上，或最大业载达到 6,000 磅或以上的飞机。

4. 语法解析

① before + 动名词 + as part of 短语

原句：Every aircraft type certificated by the FAA receives a weight and balance report as part of its required aircraft records before leaving the factory for delivery to its new owner. 拆解：主语 every aircraft type，过去分词短语 certificated by the FAA 作后置定语；谓语 receives，宾语 a weight and balance report；as part of its required aircraft records 为介词短语作状语，说明这份报告的身份；before leaving the factory for delivery to its new owner 为 before + 动名词短语作时间状语，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致（航空器）。 要点：before 作介词时后接动名词，若从句主语与主句不同则须改为 before it leaves…；as part of 表「作为……的一部分」，不译成「像……一样」；for delivery to 表示目的，delivery 为名词化表达，译文宜顺译为「交付」而非「为了交付给」。

② 现在分词与过去分词并用的后置定语

原句：The weight and balance report must include an equipment list showing weights and moment arms of all required and optional items of equipment included in the certificated empty weight. 拆解：谓语 must include，宾语 an equipment list；showing weights and moment arms… 为现在分词短语作后置定语，说明清单的内容（主动关系，清单显示数据）；of all required and optional items of equipment 为介词短语修饰 weights and moment arms；included in the certificated empty weight 为过去分词短语修饰 items of equipment（被动关系，设备被计入空重）。 要点：分词后置定语的关键是先判断主动/被动：清单「显示」数据用 showing，设备「被计入」用 included；一个名词短语可层层嵌套，读长句时从右往左逐层还原（重量与力臂 ← 全部设备 ← 计入空重的），翻译时按中文习惯把定语前置为「列出……重量和力臂的清单」。

③ 省略关系代词的定语从句 + 情态动词并列谓语

原句：If the manufacturer chooses to do so, it can weigh every aircraft it produces and issue the weight and balance report based on that weighing. 拆解：if 引导条件状语从句，choose to do so 中 do so 替代前文所述的做法；主句主语 it（指制造商），情态动词 can 后并列两个动词原形 weigh 与 issue；every aircraft 后接省略关系代词 that/which 的定语从句 it produces；based on that weighing 为过去分词短语作状语，说明报告的依据。 要点：定语从句的关系代词在从句中作宾语时可省略，此处 produces 的宾语就是被省略的 that；can 后并列两个动词时只需保留一个 can，第二个动词用原形；based on 是「以……为依据」，不宜译作「基于……的基础之上」（语义重复）。

④ requirement 后接同位语从句用虚拟语气

原句：For a small, GA airplane being used privately, such as a Cessna 172, there is no FAA requirement that it be periodically reweighed; but after each annual, the mechanic must ensure that the weight and balance data in the aircraft records is correct. 拆解：句首 for a small, GA airplane being used privately 为状语，being used privately 为被动分词短语作后置定语；there is no FAA requirement that… 为 there be 句型，that 从句是 requirement 的同位语从句，谓语用虚拟语气 (should) be reweighed；分号后 but 引出并列分句：after each annual 为时间状语，the mechanic must ensure that… 中 that 从句作 ensure 的宾语。 要点：requirement、suggestion、recommendation 等同位语从句中的谓语用「(should) + 动词原形」，所以是 be reweighed 而不是 is reweighed；但 ensure、make sure 后的宾语从句用陈述语气（is correct），两类从句不要混用。

⑤ 形式主语 it + by doing 方式状语

原句：If the installer knows the exact weight and location of the new equipment, it is also possible to create a new report by doing a series of mathematical calculations. 拆解：if 引导条件状语从句，宾语为 the exact weight and location of the new equipment；主句 it is also possible to create a new report，it 为形式主语，真正的主语是不定式短语 to create a new report；by doing a series of mathematical calculations 为介词 + 动名词作方式状语。 要点：possible 不能以人作主语，不能说 the installer is possible to…，必须用 it is possible for sb. to do 或 it is possible to do；by + 动名词表示手段、方法，与 with + 名词（借助工具）区分；also 在句中表「还、此外」，位置在 be 动词之后。

⑥ 动名词复合结构 + without 独立结构

原句：Examples of how this can happen include an airplane being repainted without the old paint being removed and the accumulation of dirt, grease, and oil in parts of the aircraft that are not easily accessible for cleaning. 拆解：主语 examples of how this can happen（of + how 引导的从句作定语）；谓语 include，宾语为并列两项：an airplane being repainted without the old paint being removed（being repainted 为被动动名词，逻辑主语是 an airplane；without the old paint being removed 为 without + 名词 + 现在分词构成的独立结构）与 the accumulation of dirt, grease, and oil in parts of the aircraft that are not easily accessible for cleaning（that 引导定语从句修饰 parts）。 要点：being + 过去分词表示被动的动作正在进行或发生，可作动名词，此处不能换成 to be repainted；without + 名词 + doing/done 是常见的独立结构，表示「在……没有发生的情况下」；include 后并列两项时只在最后一项前用 and，中间的逗号分隔并列名词 dirt, grease, and oil 属同一层的列举。
