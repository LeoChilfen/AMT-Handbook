Torsion

通用 Ch 05 · Torsion · 原文 PDF 第 163 页

1. 原文

Torsion is the stress an object experiences when it is twisted, which is what happens when torque is applied to a shaft. Torsion is made up of two other stresses: tension and compression. When a shaft is twisted, tension is experienced at a diagonal to the shaft and compression acts 90 degrees to the tension. [Figure 5-20]

The turbine shaft on a turbofan engine, which connects to the compressor in order to drive it, is under a torsion stress.

The turbine blades extract energy from the high velocity air as a force in pounds. This force in pounds acts along the length from the blades to the center of the shaft, and creates the torque that causes rotation. [Figure 5-21]

2. 中文翻译

扭转是物体被扭拧时承受的应力，也就是对轴施加扭矩时所发生的情形。扭转由另外两种应力构成：拉力和压力。当轴被扭拧时，沿与轴线成对角的方向出现拉力，压力则与拉力成 90 度方向作用。（图 5-20）

涡扇发动机上的涡轮轴与压气机相连、用以驱动压气机，该轴承受的就是扭转应力。

涡轮叶片从高速气流中提取能量，表现为以磅为单位的力。这个以磅计的力沿叶片到轴心的长度方向作用，产生使轴旋转的扭矩。（图 5-21）

4. 语法解析

① the stress an object experiences … which is what happens（省略关系代词 + which 指代整句）

原句：Torsion is the stress an object experiences when it is twisted, which is what happens when torque is applied to a shaft.

拆解：主句 Torsion is the stress；an object experiences 是省略了关系代词 that/which 的定语从句（关系代词作 experiences 的宾语）；when it is twisted 是时间状语从句；which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面「物体被扭拧」这件事；what happens 为表语从句，其后又带 when torque is applied to a shaft。

要点：关系代词在定语从句中作宾语时可以省略，这是技术英语压缩句子的常用手段。which 前有逗号时指代前面整个情况，不能译成「哪个」。what happens 中的 what 相当于 the thing that，不能换成 that。

② which connects to the compressor in order to drive it（非限制性定语从句插在主谓之间）

原句：The turbine shaft on a turbofan engine, which connects to the compressor in order to drive it, is under a torsion stress.

拆解：主语 The turbine shaft，on a turbofan engine 为后置定语；which connects to the compressor in order to drive it 是非限制性定语从句，插在主谓之间；谓语 is under a torsion stress。

要点：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，只补充信息，去掉后主句仍成立。in order to drive it 表目的，it 指 the compressor。读长句时先跳过两个逗号之间的从句，抓住主谓 is。

③ and creates the torque that causes rotation（并列谓语 + 限制性定语从句）

原句：This force in pounds acts along the length from the blades to the center of the shaft, and creates the torque that causes rotation.

拆解：主语 This force in pounds；并列谓语 acts along … 与 creates …；that causes rotation 是限制性定语从句，修饰 the torque。

要点：逗号 + and 后的第二个谓语仍与 acts 共用一个主语。in pounds 是「以磅计」的后置定语，不是「在磅里」。causes rotation = 使轴转动，rotation 是 rotate 的名词形式。
