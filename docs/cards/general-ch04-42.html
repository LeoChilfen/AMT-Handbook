1. 原文

Section line symbols show the kind of material from which the part is to be constructed. The material may not be indicated symbolically if its exact specification is shown elsewhere on the drawing. In this case, the more easily drawn symbol for cast iron is used for the sectioning, and the material specification is listed in the bill of materials or indicated in a note. 2. 中文翻译

剖面线符号表示该零件要用何种材料制作。如果材料的准确规范已在图纸的其他位置给出，则材料可以不采用符号表示。在这种情况下，剖面就使用较易绘制的铸铁符号，而材料规范则列入材料清单，或以附注形式标出。图 4-27 示出几种标准（原文至此中断）。

4. 语法解析

① the kind of material from which the part is to be constructed（介词 + which + be to do）

原句：Section line symbols show the kind of material from which the part is to be constructed.

拆解：主语 Section line symbols，谓语 show，宾语 the kind of material；from which the part is to be constructed 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，修饰 material；从句中 the part 为主语，is to be constructed 是「be to do」的被动形式，表按图纸规定「将要/应当被制作」。

要点：be to do 在技术文件中表「按规定应当」，比 will 更具规范性；其被动 is to be done 常见于「待制、待装」的表述。介词 from 不能省，因为 construct 与 material 的搭配是 construct … from …（用……制作）。

② if its exact specification is shown elsewhere on the drawing（if 条件句 + 被动）

原句：The material may not be indicated symbolically if its exact specification is shown elsewhere on the drawing.

拆解：主句 The material may not be indicated symbolically（情态动词 may + 否定 + 被动，表「可以不……」）；if 引导条件状语从句，从句主语 its exact specification，谓语 is shown 为被动，elsewhere on the drawing 是地点状语。

要点：may not be done 在此不是「禁止」，而是「可以不（不必）」——手册里 may not 与 must not 语气截然不同，读图时要分清。its 指 material（材料自身的规范），不是指 drawing，代词回指须按语义确定。

③ the more easily drawn symbol for cast iron（比较级副词修饰过去分词作前置定语）

原句：In this case, the more easily drawn symbol for cast iron is used for the sectioning, and the material specification is listed in the bill of materials or indicated in a note.

拆解：In this case 是条件承接状语；前半句主语 the more easily drawn symbol for cast iron（more easily 修饰过去分词 drawn，整个短语作 symbol 的前置定语），谓语 is used for the sectioning；后半句 the material specification 与 listed in the bill of materials、indicated in a note 构成被动并列。

要点：「比较级副词 + 过去分词」可整体前置作定语：more easily drawn symbol（更易绘制的符号）、better finished surface（加工更好的表面）。翻译时宜还原为「较易绘制的……」。后半句两个被动谓语由 or 连接，表两种可选做法，译作「列入……或以……标出」。
