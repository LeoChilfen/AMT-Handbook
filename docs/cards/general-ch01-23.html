Tie-Down Procedures for Helicopters

通用 Ch 01 · Tie-Down Procedures for Helicopters · 原文 PDF 第 32 页

1. 原文

Helicopters, like other aircraft are secured to prevent structural damage that can occur from high-velocity surface winds.

Helicopters are to be secured in hangars, when possible. If not, they must be tied down securely. Helicopters that are tied down can usually sustain winds up to approximately 65 mph. If at all possible, helicopters are evacuated to a safe area if tornadoes or hurricanes are anticipated. For added protection, helicopters can be moved to a clear area so that they are not damaged by flying objects or falling limbs from surrounding trees.

If high winds are anticipated with the helicopter parked in the open, tie down the main rotor blades. Detailed instructions for securing and mooring each type of helicopter can be found in the applicable maintenance manual. Typical mooring procedures are as follows:

  1. Face the helicopter in the direction that the highest forecast wind or gusts are anticipated.
  1. Spot the helicopter slightly more than one rotor span distance from other aircraft.
  1. Place wheel chocks ahead of and behind all wheels (where applicable). On helicopters equipped with skids, retract the ground handling wheels, lower the helicopter to rest on the skids, and install wheel position lock pins or remove the ground-handling wheels. Secure ground-handling wheels inside the aircraft or inside the hangar or storage buildings. Do not leave them unsecured on the flight line.
  1. Align the blades and install tie-down assemblies as prescribed by the helicopter manufacturer.

[Figure 1-14] Tie straps snugly without strain, and during wet weather, provide some slack to avoid the possibility of the straps shrinking, causing undue stress on the aircraft and/or its rotor system(s).

  1. Fasten the tie-down ropes or cables to the forward and aft landing gear cross tubes and secure to ground stakes or tie-down rings.

Procedures for Securing Weight-Shift-Control There are many types of weight-shift-controlled aircraft— engine powered and non-powered. These types of aircraft are very susceptible to wind damage. The wings can be secured in a similar manner as a conventional aircraft in light winds.

In high winds, the mast can be disconnected from the wing and the wing placed close to the ground and secured. This type of aircraft can also be partially disassembled or moved into a hangar for protection.

Procedures for Securing Powered Parachutes When securing powered parachutes, pack the parachute in a bag to prevent the chute from filling with air from the wind and dragging the seat and engine. The engine and seat can also be secured if needed.

直升机与其他航空器一样要加以系留，以防高速地面风造成的结构损坏。

直升机在可能时应收入机库；若无法入库，则必须牢靠系留。已系留的直升机通常能承受约 65 mph 的风。如预计有龙卷风或飓风，只要有一点可能，就把直升机转移到安全区域。为进一步保护，可把直升机移到空旷处，以免被飞起的物体或周围树木折断落下的枝干砸坏。

……以系留直升机。（承接上段的残句）

典型的系泊程序如下：

  1. 使直升机机头朝向预报风力或阵风最大的方向。
  1. 直升机与其他航空器之间留出略大于一个旋翼直径（rotor span）的距离。
  1. 在所有机轮的前后放置轮挡（如适用）。对装有滑橇的直升机，收起着地移动轮，把直升机放低至滑橇着地，并装上机轮位置锁定销或拆下地面移动轮。地面移动轮要收存于航空器内或机库、储存建筑内；不得将其不加固定地留在停机坪上。
  1. 按直升机制造商的规定对齐桨叶并安装系留组件。

（图注残留后接正文）系留带要系紧但不使其受拉力；潮湿天气应留出少量松弛，以免带子收缩而对航空器和／或其旋翼系统产生过大应力。

  1. 把系留绳索或钢索固定在前、后起落架横管上，并系在地桩或系留环上。

重量转移操纵式航空器的固定程序：重量转移操纵式（weight-shift-control）航空器种类很多，有动力驱动的，也有无动力的。这类航空器极易受风害。小风时，其机翼可以比照常规航空器的方式固定。

大风时，可将桅杆与机翼脱开，把机翼放低贴近地面并加以固定。这类航空器也可以部分分解，或推入机库保护。

动力伞的固定程序：固定动力伞时，要把伞衣收进伞包，防止伞衣被风灌满而拖动座椅与发动机。必要时，座椅与发动机也可另行固定。

4. 语法解析

① be to + 被动不定式：are to be secured

原句：Helicopters are to be secured in hangars, when possible.

拆解：主语 Helicopters；are to be secured 是「be to + 不定式被动」结构，表按规定应当做某事；when possible 是省略式时间／条件状语（when it is possible 的省略）。

要点：be to do 在手册里不表将来，而表「应当、必须」，接近 must / should，但语气更像「按程序规定」。被动式 are to be secured 把「谁去固定」隐去，突出「必须处于固定状态」。同类：The aircraft is to be inspected daily（航空器应每日检查）。

② 被动语态 + up to 数值范围

原句：Helicopters that are tied down can usually sustain winds up to approximately 65 mph.

拆解：主语 Helicopters 带定语从句 that are tied down（被动，已系留的）；谓语 can usually sustain（情态动词 + 动词原形）；宾语 winds 由 up to approximately 65 mph 作后置修饰，表示上限。

要点：up to + 数值 是技术文本表达「最高可达」的固定搭配，译作「最高约 65 mph」，不要译成「直到」。that are tied down 是限制性定语从句，翻译时前置为「已系留的直升机」比逗号拆句更顺。

③ so that 引导的目的状语从句

原句：For added protection, helicopters can be moved to a clear area so that they are not damaged by flying objects or falling limbs from surrounding trees.

拆解：For added protection 是目的性状语前置；主句 helicopters can be moved to a clear area 为情态动词被动；so that 引导目的从句，从句内 they 指直升机，are not damaged by… 又是被动，by 引出施动者 flying objects 与 falling limbs。

要点：so that 从句用 can/may 表「能够」，用 not 表「避免」，中文常译「以免、以便不」。注意 so that 与 so…that（如此……以至于）的区别：此处 so that 后面没有形容词，是纯目的从句。可迁移：Tie the blades so that they cannot flap（系好桨叶以免摆动）。
