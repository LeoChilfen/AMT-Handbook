Tie-Down Procedures for Helicopters

通用 Ch 01 · Tie-Down Procedures for Helicopters · 原文 PDF 第 32 页

1. 原文

Helicopters, like other aircraft are secured to prevent structural damage that can occur from high-velocity surface winds. Helicopters are to be secured in hangars, when possible. If not, they must be tied down securely. Helicopters that are tied down can usually sustain winds up to approximately 65 mph. If at all possible, helicopters are evacuated to a safe area if tornadoes or hurricanes are anticipated. For added protection, helicopters can be moved to a clear area so that they are not damaged by flying objects or falling limbs from surrounding trees.

If high winds are anticipated with the helicopter parked in the open, tie down the main rotor blades. Detailed instructions for securing and mooring each type of helicopter can be found in the applicable maintenance manual. [Figure 1-13] Methods of securing helicopters vary with weather conditions, the to secure helicopters.

Typical mooring procedures are as follows:

  1. Face the helicopter in the direction that the highest forecast wind or gusts are anticipated.
  1. Spot the helicopter slightly more than one rotor span distance from other aircraft.
  1. Place wheel chocks ahead of and behind all wheels (where applicable). On helicopters equipped with skids, retract the ground handling wheels, lower the helicopter to rest on the skids, and install wheel position lock pins or remove the ground-handling wheels. Secure ground-handling wheels inside the aircraft or inside the hangar or storage buildings. Do not leave them unsecured on the flight line.
  1. Align the blades and install tie-down assemblies as prescribed by the helicopter manufacturer. [Figure 1-14] Tie straps snugly without strain, and during wet weather, provide some slack to avoid the possibility of the straps shrinking, causing undue stress on the aircraft and/or its rotor system(s).
  1. Fasten the tie-down ropes or cables to the forward and aft landing gear cross tubes and secure to ground stakes or tie-down rings.

2. 中文翻译

直升机与其他飞机一样，需要固定以防高速地面风造成结构损伤。条件允许时，直升机应停入机库固定。若不能入库，必须牢固系留。已系留的直升机通常能承受约 65 mph 的风。如预计有龙卷风或飓风，应尽可能把直升机撤到安全区域。为进一步保护，可把直升机移到空旷区域，以免被飞来的物体或周围树上掉落的枝干砸伤。

如预计有大风而直升机停在露天，应系留主旋翼桨叶。各型直升机的固定与系泊详细说明见相应的维护手册。[图 1-13] 直升机的固定方法随天气状况、……（原文此处断句缺失，略）。

典型的系泊程序如下：

  1. 使直升机朝向预报风力最大或阵风最强的方向。
  1. 直升机与其他飞机的停放间距应略大于一个旋翼跨度。
  1. 在所有机轮的前后放置轮挡（适用时）。对装滑橇的直升机，收起地面移动轮，把直升机放低到滑橇着地，并装上轮位锁定销或拆下地面移动轮。把地面移动轮固定在机内，或存放在机库、库房内。不得把它们不加固定地留在停机坪上。
  1. 按要求对齐桨叶，并按直升机制造商的规定安装系留组件。[图 1-14] 把系带系紧但不使其受张力；潮湿天气应留出一些松量，以免系带收缩，对机体和/或旋翼系统产生过大应力。
  1. 把系留绳或钢索拴在前、后起落架横管上，并固定到地桩或系留环上。

4. 语法解析

① 介词 like 与不定式目的状语

原句：Helicopters, like other aircraft are secured to prevent structural damage that can occur from high-velocity surface winds. 拆解：主语 Helicopters + 插入语 like other aircraft（介词短语）+ 被动谓语 are secured + 目的状语 to prevent structural damage；that 引导定语从句修饰 damage。 要点：like 是介词“像”，不能引导从句（引导从句要用 as）；不定式 to prevent 表目的，比 for preventing 更常用于手册。

② 定语从句 + up to 表上限

原句：Helicopters that are tied down can usually sustain winds up to approximately 65 mph. 拆解：主语 Helicopters + that are tied down 定语从句 + 情态谓语 can usually sustain + 宾语 winds + up to approximately 65 mph 后置修饰。 要点：up to 表“上限”，与 approximately 连用是“约到…为止”；定语从句用被动 are tied down，强调“已系留”这一状态而非动作。

③ at all 加强语气与条件从句的时态

原句：If at all possible, helicopters are evacuated to a safe area if tornadoes or hurricanes are anticipated. 拆解：If at all possible 是省略式条件状语（= if it is at all possible）；主句 helicopters are evacuated to a safe area（被动）；句末 if tornadoes or hurricanes are anticipated 为条件从句，用一般现在时表将来。 要点：at all 用来加强语气（“只要有一点可能”）；条件从句中不用 will，用一般现在时代替将来时。

④ leave + 宾语 + 过去分词

原句：Do not leave them unsecured on the flight line. 拆解：否定祈使句 Do not leave + 宾语 them + 宾语补足语 unsecured（过去分词）+ 地点状语 on the flight line。 要点：leave + 宾语 + 分词/形容词 表示“使…处于某种状态”，unsecured 是补语不是定语；注意 unsecured 与 secured 语义相反，别混。
