1. 原文

A proportion is a statement of equality between two or more ratios. For example, 3/4 = 6/8 or 3:4 = 6:8

This proportion is read as, “3 is to 4 as 6 is to 8.”

Extremes and Means

The first and last terms of the proportion (the 3 and 8 in this example) are called the extremes. The second and third terms (the 4 and 6 in this example) are called the means. In any proportion, the product of the extremes is equal to the product of the means.

In the proportion 2:3 = 4:6, the product of the extremes, 2 × 6, is 12; the product of the means, 3 × 4, is also 12. An inspection of any proportion shows this to be true.

Solving Proportions

Normally when solving a proportion, three quantities are known, and the fourth is unknown. To solve for the unknown, multiply the two numbers along the diagonal and then divide by the third number.

Example: Solve for X in the proportion given below.

65/80 = X/100

First, multiply 65 × 100: 65 × 100 = 6500

Next, divide by 80: 6500 ÷ 80 = 81.25

Therefore, X = 81.25.

Example: An airplane flying 300 miles used 24 gallons of gasoline. How many gallons will it need to travel 750 miles?

The ratio here is: “miles to gallons;” therefore, the proportion is set up as:

300/24 = 750/G

Solve for G: (750 × 24) ÷ 300 = 60

Therefore, to fly 750 miles, 60 gallons of gasoline is required.

2. 中文翻译

比例是两个或多个比之间的相等关系式。例如，3 4 = 6 8 或 3:4 = 6:8（原文此处分数被抽平，即 3⁄4 = 6⁄8）

这个比例读作「3 比 4 等于 6 比 8」。

外项与内项

比例的第一项和最后一项（本例中的 3 和 8）称为外项。第二项和第三项（本例中的 4 和 6）称为内项。在任何比例中，外项之积等于内项之积。

在比例 2:3 = 4:6 中，外项之积 2 × 6 为 12；内项之积 3 × 4 也是 12。对任何比例作一番查看，都会表明这一点成立。

解比例

解比例时通常已知三个量，第四个量未知。要求出未知量，把对角线上的两个数相乘，再除以第三个数。

例：求下面所给比例中的 X。

65 80 = X100

首先，把 65 × 100 相乘：65 × 100 = 6500

接着，除以 80：6500 ÷ 80 = 81.25

因此，X = 81.25。

例：一架飞机飞行 300 英里用了 24 加仑汽油。它飞行 750 英里需要多少加仑？

这里的比是「英里比加仑」，因此比例式列为：

（原文此处为整页分数—小数—毫米换算表的抽排数据，原文无正文。）Miles Gallons 300 24 = 750 G

解出 G：（750 × 24）÷ 300 = 60

因此，飞行 750 英里需要 60 加仑汽油。

4. 语法解析

① 定义句 + between 短语作定语

原句：A proportion is a statement of equality between two or more ratios.

拆解：主语 A proportion；系动词 is；表语 a statement of equality；between two or more ratios 是介词短语作定语修饰 equality。

要点：定义术语时用「X is a statement of … between A and B」，between 用来说明相等关系发生在哪两者之间。two or more + 复数名词表示「两个及以上」，可译作「两个或多个」。

② 被动语态 + 括号补充说明

原句：The first and last terms of the proportion (the 3 and 8 in this example) are called the extremes.

拆解：主语 The first and last terms of the proportion；括号内 the 3 and 8 in this example 是对主语的补充说明（同位语性质）；谓语 are called 为被动语态，the extremes 作主语补足语。

要点：be called + 名词是下定义的被动句式；括号里的内容不是句子成分，而是解释前文所指，翻译时放在「即……」的位置。the first and last 两个形容词共用 terms，注意 the 的位置。

③ be equal to + 状语前置

原句：In any proportion, the product of the extremes is equal to the product of the means.

拆解：In any proportion 是条件状语置于句首；主句主语 the product of the extremes，系动词 is，表语 equal to the product of the means。

要点：be equal to 中的 to 是介词，不能省；表示「任何……」用 in any + 单数名词，泛指一类情形。of 短语层层限定 product，理解时要按「……的积」拆开。

④ when + 动名词（省略主谓）

原句：Normally when solving a proportion, three quantities are known, and the fourth is unknown.

拆解：Normally 是状语；when solving a proportion 是时间状语，when 后省略了主语与 be（when one is solving）；主句由两个分句并列：three quantities are known 与 the fourth is unknown，均为被动或系表结构。

要点：when + 动名词表示「在做……时」，其逻辑主语须与主句主语一致；此处主句主语是 three quantities，形式上并不一致，属于技术英语中常见的悬垂用法，翻译时补出「解比例时」即可。the fourth 用序数词指代「第四个量」。

⑤ 目的状语 + 祈使句 + 顺序连接

原句：To solve for the unknown, multiply the two numbers along the diagonal and then divide by the third number.

拆解：To solve for the unknown 是目的状语；主句为祈使句，谓语由 multiply … and then divide … 两个动词并列构成；along the diagonal 是方式状语，by the third number 引出除数。

要点：说明算法步骤时常用「祈使句 + and then + 祈使句」，表示先后操作。solve for 意为「解出（未知量）」，multiply A by B、divide A by B 中 by 都引出乘数或除数。

⑥ be read as + as … as 结构

原句：This proportion is read as, “3 is to 4 as 6 is to 8.”

拆解：主语 This proportion；谓语 is read as 为被动；引号内 3 is to 4 as 6 is to 8 是 as … as … 的等比结构，第一个 as 后接被比对象，第二个 as 引出比较基准。

要点：读比例式的固定说法是「A is to B as C is to D」，即「A 比 B 等于 C 比 D」。is to 中的 to 是介词；as … as … 在此不表「和……一样」，而表两对比值相等，注意与比较级结构区分。
