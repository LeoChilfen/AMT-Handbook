Airworthiness Limitations

通用 Ch 02 · Airworthiness Limitations · 原文 PDF 第 83 页

1. 原文

The ICA must contain a separate and clearly distinguishable section titled “Airworthiness Limitations.” Within this section are mandatory replacement times, structural inspection interval, and related inspection procedures.

All of this is included in the initial release of documents when the aircraft is delivered. However, over the course of the life of an aircraft, various modifications can and often do occur. Whether these are as simple as a new cabin to galley sliding door, or as complex as a navigation related STC, any major alteration requires that this type of maintenance data be provided to the owner, so that subsequent maintenance, inspection, and repair can be properly accomplished. As aircraft and their systems become more and more complex, and society continues its preoccupation with litigation for every incident, it is imperative that the technician have the right information, that it is current, and that they have the proper tools, including those required for any special inspection, and correct replacement parts. If any one of these items is required, and the technician does not have it accessible, they are in violation of 14 CFR sections 65.81(b), 43.13(a), and 43.16 if they attempt to return the aircraft to service.

Manufacturers may provide this required information in a variety of different manuals:

• Operating Instructions—The Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) or the Pilot’s Operating Handbook (POH)

provides the pilot with the necessary information to properly operate the aircraft. These manuals are usually listed in the aircraft TCDS, and therefore are a required item for the aircraft to be considered airworthy. Note that the AFM is generally serial number specific, whereas the POH is model specific.

2. 中文翻译

ICA 必须包含一个独立且清晰可辨、标题为「适航限制」的部分。该部分列有强制的更换时限、结构检查间隔以及相关的检查程序。

以上内容在航空器交付时的初始文件发布中即已包含。然而，在航空器的整个寿命期内，各种改装都可能、而且经常发生。无论这些改装简单到只是把客舱隔板改成通往厨房的滑动门，还是复杂到是一项与导航有关的 STC，任何重大更改都要求把这类维修数据提供给机主，以便后续的维修、检查和修理能够正确实施。随着航空器及其系统变得越来越复杂，加上社会对每一起事件都热衷于诉讼，技师必须掌握正确的信息、信息必须现行有效、并且拥有适当的工具（包括任何特殊检查所需的工具）和正确的替换件，这一点至关重要。如果上述任何一项是必需的，而技师在无法获取的情况下仍试图让航空器恢复使用，即违反了 14 CFR 第 65.81(b)、43.13(a) 和 43.16 节。

制造商可以用多种不同的手册提供这些必需的信息：

• 使用说明——飞机飞行手册（AFM）或飞行员使用手册（POH）

为飞行员提供正确操作航空器所必需的信息。这些手册通常列在航空器 TCDS 中，因此是航空器被视为适航所必需的项目。注意：AFM 一般针对具体序号，而 POH 针对具体型号。

4. 语法解析

① Within this section are …（介词短语前置引起的完全倒装）

原句：Within this section are mandatory replacement times, structural inspection interval, and related inspection procedures.

拆解：介词短语 Within this section 提到句首，句子完全倒装：谓语 are + 三个并列主语 mandatory replacement times / structural inspection interval / related inspection procedures。

要点：这是「状语 + be + 主语」的倒装，作用是让长串并列主语排在后面、便于展开。正常语序为 Three items are within this section；翻译时先还原成「该部分之内是……」再逐项译文。主语为三项并列，所以用 are 而不是 is。

② it is imperative that the technician have …（形式主语 + 虚拟语气从句）

原句：As aircraft and their systems become more and more complex, and society continues its preoccupation with litigation for every incident, it is imperative that the technician have the right information, that it is current, and that they have the proper tools, including those required for any special inspection, and correct replacement parts.

拆解：As 引导两个并列状语从句（become more and more complex / continues its preoccupation with …）；主句 it is imperative + 三个并列 that 从句，it 为形式主语；第一个 that 从句中的 have 用动词原形，是虚拟语气。

要点：it is imperative that 后接 (should) + 动词原形，因此写 have 而不是 has，这是考试与写作的高频错点。第二个 that 从句的 it 指 information，与第一句内容衔接，三个从句层层递进：信息正确、信息现行、工具与备件齐全。more and more + 形容词是比较级叠用，表「越来越……」。

③ they are in violation of … if they attempt …（泛指单数 they + 条件从句）

原句：If any one of these items is required, and the technician does not have it accessible, they are in violation of 14 CFR sections 65.81(b), 43.13(a), and 43.16 if they attempt to return the aircraft to service.

拆解：前两个 if／and 条件从句并列（is required 为被动，does not have it accessible 为主动）；主句 they are in violation of …；末尾 if they attempt to return the aircraft to service 是附加条件；they 指代单数的 the technician。

要点：法规英文为避免 he/she 的性别偏向，常用 they 指代单数 technician，谓语也随之用 are，这在正式文本中已被接受。have it accessible 是 have + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补（使……处于可取用状态）。in violation of 是固定搭配，不能说 in violation to。

④ are a required item for the aircraft to be considered airworthy（for + 名词 + 不定式的复合结构）

原句：These manuals are usually listed in the aircraft TCDS, and therefore are a required item for the aircraft to be considered airworthy.

拆解：两个并列谓语 are usually listed（被动）与 are a required item；therefore 是连接副词，说明因果；for the aircraft to be considered airworthy 是「for + 名词 + 不定式」的复合结构，作 required item 的限定，其中不定式用被动语态。

要点：for sb/sth to do sth 可作定语或状语，此处相当于「对航空器而言要被认定为适航」；to be considered 用被动，因为航空器是「被认定」的对象。be listed in 表示「被列入（清单、数据单）」，介词用 in。
