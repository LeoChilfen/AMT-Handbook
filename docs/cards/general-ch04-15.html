Drawing Sketches

1. 原文

A sketch is a simple rough drawing that is made rapidly and without much detail. Sketches may take many forms— from a simple pictorial presentation to a multi-view orthographic projection.

Just as aircraft technicians need not be highly skilled in creating drawings, they need not be accomplished artists. However, in many situations, they need to prepare a drawing to present an idea for a new design, a modification, or a repair method. The medium of sketching is an excellent way of accomplishing this.

The rules and conventional practices for making mechanical drawings are followed to the extent that all views needed to portray an object accurately are shown in their proper relationship. It is also necessary to observe the rules for correct line use and dimensioning.

Sketching Techniques

To make a sketch, first determine what views are necessary to portray the object. Then block in the views using light construction lines. Next, complete the details, darken the object outline, and sketch extension and dimension lines. Complete the drawing by adding notes, dimensions, title, date, and when necessary, the sketcher’s name. The steps in making a sketch of an object are illustrated.

Depending on the complexity of the sketch, basic shapes may be drawn in freehand or by use of templates. If the sketch is quite complicated or the technician is required to make frequent sketches, use of a variety of templates and other drafting tools is highly recommended.

A sketch is frequently drawn for repairs or for use in manufacturing a replacement part. Such a sketch must provide all necessary information to those who must make the repair or manufacture the part.

The degree that a sketch is complete depends on its intended use. Obviously, a sketch used only to represent an object pictorially need not be dimensioned. If a part is to be manufactured from the sketch, it should show all the necessary construction details.

2. 中文翻译

草图是一种简单粗略的图，画得快，细节不多。草图可以采取多种形式——从简单的立体表现到多视图正投影图。

正如飞机技术人员不必精于绘图，他们也不必是熟练的画家。然而，在许多情况下，他们需要画一张图来表达新设计、改装或修理方法的设想。草图这种手段是完成这一任务的极好方式。

绘制机械图的规则和习惯做法要遵守到这样的程度：为准确表现物体所需的全部视图都按其正确的相互关系给出。同时，还必须遵守正确用线和尺寸标注的规则。

草图的画法

画草图时，先确定需要哪些视图才能表现该物体。然后用浅色作图线勾出各视图的轮廓。接着完成细节，加深物体轮廓，并画出尺寸界线和尺寸线。最后加上注释、尺寸、标题、日期，必要时加上绘图者姓名，草图即告完成。画物体草图的各个步骤已示于图中。

视草图的复杂程度，基本图形可以徒手画出，也可以借助样板绘制。如果草图相当复杂，或技术人员需要经常画草图，则强烈建议使用各种样板和其他制图工具。

草图常常是为修理或为制造替换件而画的。这样的草图必须向必须进行修理或制造该零件的人提供全部必要信息。

草图的完整程度取决于它的用途。显然，仅用于以立体方式表示物体的草图不需要标注尺寸。如果零件要照草图制造，则草图应给出全部必要的结构细节。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句修饰定义词

原句：A sketch is a simple rough drawing that is made rapidly and without much detail. 拆解：主语 A sketch + 系动词 is + 表语 a simple rough drawing；that 引导定语从句修饰 drawing，从句谓语为被动 is made，后接两个状语 rapidly 与 without much detail。 要点：定义句式「A is a B that…」在技术文本里用来给术语下定义，that 从句是定义的核心信息，翻译时宜还原成「……的图」。注意 and 连接的是两个状语而非两个并列谓语；rough 在此指「粗略的」，不是「粗糙、粗糙的加工」。

② just as 引导的类比从句

原句：Just as aircraft technicians need not be highly skilled in creating drawings, they need not be accomplished artists. 拆解：Just as 引导比较（类比）状语从句，从句主语 aircraft technicians + 谓语 need not be + 表语 highly skilled in creating drawings；主句 they need not be accomplished artists，两处 need not be 结构平行。 要点：just as A, B 意为「正如 A 一样，B 也……」，主从句的谓语形式通常保持一致以形成对照。need 在此是情态动词，否定式为 need not（不加 do）；accomplished 是形容词「有造诣的」，与动词 accomplish 的过去分词形式同形，要靠句法位置区分。

③ to the extent that 引导的程度状语从句

原句：The rules and conventional practices for making mechanical drawings are followed to the extent that all views needed to portray an object accurately are shown in their proper relationship. 拆解：主语 The rules and conventional practices（for making mechanical drawings 后置定语）+ 被动谓语 are followed + to the extent 程度状语，that 从句说明程度的具体内容；从句主语 all views（needed to portray an object accurately 为过去分词短语作后置定语）+ 被动谓语 are shown + 方式状语 in their proper relationship。 要点：to the extent that 意为「到了……的程度」，从句是对程度的展开说明，不能译成「到那个范围」。needed to do 是过去分词短语作后置定语（= that are needed），不是谓语，找谓语时要跳过它。

④ 祈使句 + 不定式目的状语 + 宾语从句

原句：To make a sketch, first determine what views are necessary to portray the object. 拆解：To make a sketch 为目的状语前置；主句为祈使句 determine（省略主语 you），first 为状语；what views are necessary to portray the object 为 what 引导的宾语从句作 determine 的宾语，其中 what 修饰 views。 要点：祈使句在操作步骤说明中最常见，翻译时补出「（你）先……」即可；what + 名词 引导的从句中 what 是限定词而非疑问词，语序为陈述语序（what views are necessary），不能写成 what are views。necessary to do 表示「做某事所必需的」。

⑤ depending on 独立分词短语作状语

原句：Depending on the complexity of the sketch, basic shapes may be drawn in freehand or by use of templates. 拆解：Depending on… 为现在分词短语作条件状语，其逻辑主语与主句主语 basic shapes 并不一致（实为泛指的技术人员），属悬垂分词；主句 basic shapes + 情态被动 may be drawn + 两个并列方式状语 in freehand 与 by use of templates。 要点：depending on 已固化成介词性短语，意为「取决于、视……而定」，这类悬垂用法被广泛接受，不必强行改写成 dependent on。in freehand 中 freehand 是名词作介词宾语（徒手）；by use of = by using，注意 use 在此是名词。

⑥ the degree that 定语从句 + depend on 搭配

原句：The degree that a sketch is complete depends on its intended use. 拆解：主语 The degree + that 引导的同位语/定语从句 a sketch is complete（说明 degree 的内容）；谓语 depends on + 宾语 its intended use。 要点：the degree (to which) that… 中 that 从句用来说明程度，现代英语常省略 to which，直接 the degree that…；depends on 的 on 不可省。intended use 是「预期用途」，是技术文件里判断要求的常用依据词，翻译时要落在「看它打算怎么用」上。
