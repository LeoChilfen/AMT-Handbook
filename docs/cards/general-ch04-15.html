Reference and Dash Numbers

通用 Ch 04 · Reference and Dash Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

1. 原文

Reference numbers that appear in the title block refer you to the numbers of other prints. When more than one detail is shown on a drawing, dash numbers are used. Both parts would have the same drawing number plus an individual number, such as 40267-1 and 40267-2.

In addition to appearing in the title block, dash numbers may appear on the face of the drawing near the parts they identify. Dash numbers are also used to identify right-hand and left-hand parts.

In aircraft, many parts on the left side are like the corresponding parts on the right side but in reverse. The left- hand part is always shown in the drawing. The right-hand part is called for in the title block. Above the title block a notation is found, such as: 470204-1LH shown; 470204-2RH opposite. Both parts carry the same number, but the part called for is distinguished by a dash number. Some prints have odd numbers for left-hand parts and even numbers for right-hand parts.

2. 中文翻译

标题栏中出现的参考编号（reference number）把读者引向其他图纸的编号。当一张图纸上表示不止一个零件（detail）时，就使用短横编号（dash number）。两个零件都使用同一个图纸编号，再各加一个单独编号，例如 40267-1 和 40267-2。

除出现在标题栏内之外，短横编号也可能出现在图面上、靠近其所标识的零件处。短横编号还用于标识右手件和左手件。

在航空器上，左侧的许多零件与右侧对应零件相同，只是方向相反。图纸上总是画出左手件，而标题栏中指定需要的是右手件。标题栏上方有一处附注，例如：470204-1LH shown; 470204-2RH opposite（图示 470204-1LH；470204-2RH 为其对称件）。两个零件编号相同，但所指定的零件用短横编号加以区分。有些图纸用奇数编号表示左手件、偶数编号表示右手件。

4. 语法解析

① Reference numbers that appear in the title block refer you to the numbers of other prints（定语从句 + refer sb to sth）

原句：Reference numbers that appear in the title block refer you to the numbers of other prints.

拆解：主语 Reference numbers 后接 that 引导的限制性定语从句；谓语 refer；宾语 you；to the numbers of other prints 是介词短语，与 refer 构成 refer sb to sth。

要点：refer to 单独使用是「提到、参考」，refer sb to sth 才是「让某人去查阅某物」。手册中交叉引用常用 See …／Refer to … 表达。定语从句修饰物时用 that 或 which 均可，技术文本偏好 that，且从句谓语与先行词复数一致（appear）。

② When more than one detail is shown on a drawing, dash numbers are used（when 从句 + 主谓一致 + 被动）

原句：When more than one detail is shown on a drawing, dash numbers are used.

拆解：When 引导时间／条件状语从句，从句主语 more than one detail 视为单数，谓语用 is shown；主句 dash numbers are used 为被动。

要点：「more than one + 单数名词」作主语时谓语用单数，而 more than two details 用复数，这是最易错的语法点之一。be used 后不直接接动作，要表达「用于做某事」须用 be used to do 或 be used for doing。

③ The right-hand part is called for in the title block（短语动词的被动形式）

原句：The right-hand part is called for in the title block.

拆解：主语 The right-hand part，谓语 is called for 为短语动词 call for 的被动形式；in the title block 是地点状语。

要点：call for 意为「需要、要求、指定」，变成被动后介词 for 必须留在动词后面，不能写成 is called。left-hand／right-hand 作前置定语时必须带连字符；说「在左侧」时则写 on the left-hand side。图纸上只画左手件、标题栏指定右手件，是航空器成对零件的通行做法。
