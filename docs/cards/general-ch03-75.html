Conventional (U.S. or English) System

通用 Ch 03 · Conventional (U.S. or English) System · 原文 PDF 第 122 页

1. 原文

Our conventional (U.S. or English) system of measurement is part of our cultural heritage from the days when the thirteen colonies were under British rule. It started as a collection of Anglo-Saxon, Roman, and Norman-French weights and measures. For example, the inch represents the width of the thumb and the foot is from the length of the human foot.

Tradition holds that King Henry I decreed that the yard should be the distance from the tip of his nose to the end of his thumb.

Since medieval times, commissions appointed by various English monarchs have reduced the chaos of measurement by setting specific standards for some of the most important units. Some of the conventional units of measure are: inches, feet, yards, miles, ounces, pints, gallons, and pounds. Because the conventional system was not set up systematically, it contains a random collection of conversions. For example, 1 mile = 5,280 feet and 1 foot = 12 inches.

2. 中文翻译

我们的传统（美制，或称英制）计量体系是文化遗产的一部分，它来自十三个殖民地处于英国统治之下的那个年代。它最初是盎格鲁-撒克逊、罗马和诺曼-法国度量衡的混合体。例如，英寸代表拇指的宽度，英尺则取自人脚的长度。

传统上认为，亨利一世国王曾下令，码应为他鼻尖到他拇指末端的距离。

自中世纪以来，由历代英国君主委任的委员会为一些最重要的单位设定了具体标准，从而减少了计量上的混乱。部分传统计量单位有：英寸、英尺、码、英里、盎司、品脱、加仑和磅。由于传统体系并非系统性地建立，其中包含的换算关系是零散拼凑的。例如，1 英里 = 5,280 英尺，1 英尺 = 12 英寸。

4. 语法解析

① when 引导的定语从句修饰 the days

原句：Our conventional (U.S. or English) system of measurement is part of our cultural heritage from the days when the thirteen colonies were under British rule.

拆解：主干是 Our … system … is part of our cultural heritage；from the days 是后置的介词短语修饰 heritage；when the thirteen colonies were under British rule 是关系副词 when 引导的定语从句，修饰 the days。

要点：when 引导定语从句时，先行词必须是表时间的名词（the days / the time / the period）；不能用 which 代替 when，除非写成 in which。be part of 前不加冠词，表示「是……的一部分」。

② hold that 加 decree that should…：从句套从句

原句：Tradition holds that King Henry I decreed that the yard should be the distance from the tip of his nose to the end of his thumb.

拆解：Tradition holds that … 中 holds 意为「认为、主张」，后接宾语从句；该宾语从句内部又套一个 that 从句 King Henry I decreed that …；should be 是规定语气。

要点：hold that 是「主张、认定」的正式说法，主语常为 tradition / theory / view 等。decree / require / stipulate / mandate 等表规定的动词后常用 should + 动词原形，美式英语里也常省略 should 直接接原形 be。两层 that 都不能省，否则句子层次会塌掉。

③ since + 现在完成时，以及过去分词后置定语

原句：Since medieval times, commissions appointed by various English monarchs have reduced the chaos of measurement by setting specific standards for some of the most important units.

拆解：Since medieval times 是时间状语，配合现在完成时 have reduced；commissions 后接过去分词短语 appointed by various English monarchs 作后置定语；by setting specific standards … 是方式状语，介词 by 后接动名词。

要点：since + 时间点、后接现在完成时，强调「从那时起一直到现在」的变化；若用一般过去时就把「延续到现在」的意思丢了。appointed by … 是过去分词短语作定语（= which were appointed by …）；by doing 表手段，注意 by 后必须用 -ing。
