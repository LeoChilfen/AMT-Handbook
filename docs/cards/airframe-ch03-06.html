Determining Fabric Condition—Repair or Recover?

机体 Ch 03 · Determining Fabric Condition—Repair or Recover? · 原文 PDF 第 146 页

1. 原文

Re-covering an aircraft with fabric is a major repair and should only be undertaken when necessary. Often a repair to the present fabric is sufficient to keep the aircraft airworthy. The original manufacturer’s recommendations or the covering process STC should be consulted for the type of repair required for the damage incurred by the fabric covering. AC 43.13-1 also gives guidelines and acceptable practices for repairing cotton fabric, specifically when stitching is concerned.

Often a large area that needs repair is judged in reference to the overall remaining lifespan of the fabric on the aircraft. For example, if the fabric has reached the limit of its durability, it is better to re-cover the entire aircraft than to replace a large damaged area when the remainder of the aircraft would soon need to be re-covered.

On aircraft with dope-based covering systems, continued shrinkage of the dope can cause the fabric to become too tight. Overly tight fabric may require the aircraft to be re-covered rather than repaired because excess tension on fabric can cause airframe structural damage. Loose fabric flaps in the wind during flight, affecting weight distribution and unduly stressing the airframe. It may also need to be replaced because of damage to the airframe.

Another reason to re-cover rather than repair occurs when dope coatings on fabric develop cracks. These cracks could expose the fabric beneath to the elements that can weaken it. Close observation and field testing must be used to determine if the fabrics are airworthy. If not, the aircraft must be re-covered. If the fabric is airworthy and no other problems exist, a rejuvenator can be used per manufacturer’s instructions. This product is usually sprayed on and softens the coatings with very powerful solvents. Plasticizers in the rejuvenator become part of the film that fills in the cracks. After the rejuvenator dries, additional coats of aluminum-pigmented dope must be added and then final topcoats applied to finish the job. While laborious, rejuvenating a dope finish over strong fabric can save a great deal of time and money. Polyurethane-based finishes cannot be rejuvenated.

2. 中文翻译

用蒙布重新蒙布（re-cover）飞机属于重大修理，只有在必要时才应进行。通常，对现有蒙布做一次修补就足以保持飞机适航。对于蒙布因损伤所需的修理类型，应查阅原制造厂的建议或蒙布工艺的 STC。AC 43.13-1 也给出了修理棉布蒙布的指导原则和可接受做法，尤其是在涉及缝合时。

需要修补的大面积区域，往往要参照飞机上蒙布的整体剩余寿命来判断。例如，如果蒙布已达到其耐久性极限，那么与其更换一大块损伤区域、而飞机其余部分很快也要重新蒙布，不如把整架飞机重新蒙布。

在采用涂布漆（dope）蒙布系统的飞机上，涂布漆的持续收缩会使蒙布变得过紧。过紧的蒙布可能需要整机重新蒙布而不是修补，因为蒙布上过大的张力会造成机体结构损伤。松动的蒙布在飞行中会随风拍打，影响重量分布并使机体承受过大应力。蒙布也可能因机体结构损伤而需要更换。

需要重新蒙布而非修补的另一个原因，是蒙布上的涂布漆出现裂纹。这些裂纹会使下面的蒙布暴露于可能使其强度下降的环境因素之中。必须通过仔细观察和现场测试来判断蒙布是否适航。如果不适航，飞机必须重新蒙布。如果蒙布适航且不存在其他问题，可按制造厂说明使用再生剂（rejuvenator）。这种产品通常喷涂使用，用很强的溶剂软化涂层。再生剂中的增塑剂成为涂膜的一部分，把裂纹填满。再生剂干燥后，必须再涂几道铝粉着色的涂布漆，然后涂最后的面漆以完成作业。虽然费工，但在强度尚好的蒙布上对涂布漆精整层做再生处理，可以节省大量时间和费用。聚氨酯类精整层不能再生处理。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词主语 + 并列谓语中的被动不定式

原句：Re-covering an aircraft with fabric is a major repair and should only be undertaken when necessary. 拆解：动名词短语 Re-covering an aircraft with fabric 作主语；第一个谓语 is a major repair（系表结构），第二个谓语 should only be undertaken 为被动语态，与 is 并列共用一个主语；when necessary 是省略形式的时间状语从句（when it is necessary）。 要点：and 连接的两个谓语共用同一个动名词主语，不要把 a major repair 误当成第二个谓语的主语。should only be undertaken 用被动式，说明“重新蒙布”是被执行的动作；when necessary 是 when (it is) necessary 的省略，中文译作“只有在必要时”。

② 定语从句把主语和谓语隔开

原句：Often a large area that needs repair is judged in reference to the overall remaining lifespan of the fabric on the aircraft. 拆解：主语 a large area + that needs repair 定语从句（that 作从句主语）+ 谓语 is judged（被动语态）+ in reference to... 作方式状语。 要点：主语和谓语之间插了定语从句，is judged 要越过从句才能找到；in reference to 意为“参照、对照”，不是“关于”。被动语态省略了施动者（由维修人员判断），中文可转为主动“要参照……来判断”。

③ 介词短语前置 + 动名词主语 + cause sb. to do

原句：On aircraft with dope-based covering systems, continued shrinkage of the dope can cause the fabric to become too tight. 拆解：On aircraft with dope-based covering systems 为范围状语前置；主语 continued shrinkage of the dope（continued 为过去分词定语）；谓语 can cause；宾语 the fabric；宾补 to become too tight。 要点：cause sb./sth. to do sth. 的补足语用不定式；continued 是定语，不是谓语。句首 On aircraft... 限定适用范围，翻译时应放到句首“在采用涂布漆蒙布系统的飞机上”。

④ require sb. to do 的被动式 + rather than 并列

原句：Overly tight fabric may require the aircraft to be re-covered rather than repaired because excess tension on fabric can cause airframe structural damage. 拆解：主语 Overly tight fabric；谓语 may require；宾语 the aircraft；宾补 to be re-covered（不定式被动式），rather than repaired 为并列的被动不定式省略形式（rather than be repaired）；because 引导原因状语从句。 要点：require 后接不定式复合结构，逻辑上 the aircraft 是“被重新蒙布”的，所以用 to be re-covered；rather than 后接与前项同形成分，故仍用过去分词 repaired。because 从句说明“为什么不能只修”，是全句判断的依据。

⑤ 省略形式的让步状语 + 动名词主语

原句：While laborious, rejuvenating a dope finish over strong fabric can save a great deal of time and money. 拆解：While laborious 为省略形式的让步状语（while it is laborious），其逻辑主语是后面的动名词短语 rejuvenating a dope finish over strong fabric；谓语 can save；宾语 a great deal of time and money。 要点：while 引导的省略句，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，这里指“再生处理”这件事本身。a great deal of 修饰不可数名词，time and money 共用这一量词。译文用“虽然……却……”保留让步关系。
