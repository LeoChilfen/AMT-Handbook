Section 43.1—Applicability

通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.1—Applicability · 原文 PDF 第 64 页

1. 原文

Paragraph (a) states quite clearly that aircraft (whether U.S.- or foreign-registered operating under 14 CFR part 121 or 135) and component parts thereof must be maintained in accordance with the rules set forth in this part. Although paragraph b states quite clearly the type of aircraft that this part does not apply to, it seems to have led to considerable confusion within the aviation industry. If an aircraft is flying with a Special Airworthiness—Experimental Certificate (FAA Form 8130-7, Special Airworthiness Certificate—pink color certificate) and that is the only airworthiness certificate this aircraft has ever had, then 14 CFR part 43 does not apply.

Conversely, sometimes during maintenance (especially STC modification—the STC is addressed later in this chapter), it becomes necessary to temporarily place the aircraft into Special Airworthiness—Experimental. This is done to show compliance with federal regulations. These aircraft must still be maintained in accordance with 14 CFR part 43, because the aircraft had a different kind of airworthiness (in this example a Standard) prior to being issued the Special Airworthiness Certificate.

2. 中文翻译

第 (a) 款非常明确地规定：航空器（无论是在美国登记，还是在外国登记并按 14 CFR 第 121 部或第 135 部运行）及其零部件，都必须按照本部所列规则进行维修。第 (b) 款虽然也非常明确地规定了本部不适用于哪些类型的航空器，但这一款似乎在航空业内引起了相当大的困惑。如果一架航空器正以特殊适航证——实验类（FAA 表格 8130-7，特殊适航证——粉色证书）飞行，并且这是该航空器有史以来唯一持有过的适航证，那么 14 CFR 第 43 部不适用。

反过来，有时在维修过程中（尤其是 STC 改装——STC 将在本章后文讨论），有必要临时将航空器转入特殊适航证——实验类。这样做是为了证明其符合联邦规章。这类航空器仍必须按照 14 CFR 第 43 部进行维修，因为在获发该特殊适航证之前，它持有的是另一类适航证（在此例中为标准适航证）。

4. 语法解析

① 情态被动 + set forth 过去分词后置定语

原句：Paragraph (a) states quite clearly that aircraft (whether U.S.- or foreign-registered operating under 14 CFR part 121 or 135) and component parts thereof must be maintained in accordance with the rules set forth in this part.

拆解：主干 Paragraph (a) states that …；that 引导宾语从句，从句主语为 aircraft and component parts thereof；括号内 whether … 为插入的让步/选择说明；从句谓语 must be maintained 为情态被动；in accordance with the rules 为方式状语；set forth in this part 是过去分词短语作 rules 的后置定语。

要点：法规句子常把限定条件塞进括号，读时先剥离括号抓主干。in accordance with（依照）是法规固定搭配，比 according to 更正式；set forth 用过去分词而非 which are set forth，是文件语体的省略写法。

② if 条件句 + then 呼应 + 现在进行时表状态

原句：If an aircraft is flying with a Special Airworthiness—Experimental Certificate (FAA Form 8130-7, Special Airworthiness Certificate—pink color certificate) and that is the only airworthiness certificate this aircraft has ever had, then 14 CFR part 43 does not apply.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，从句由 and 并列两个分句（is flying with … / that is the only airworthiness certificate …）；this aircraft has ever had 是省略了关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 certificate；主句 14 CFR part 43 does not apply 用 then 与前文呼应。

要点：真实条件句“主将从现”的规则在这里表现为“主句用一般现在时表法规效力”。is flying with 用进行时并不强调“正在飞”，而是描述“处于持有该证运行的状态”。apply 是不及物动词，说 does not apply to sb./sth.，不能说 does not apply sb.。

③ 不定式表目的 + 被动语态

原句：This is done to show compliance with federal regulations.

拆解：This 指代上句“临时把航空器转入实验类”这一做法；is done 为一般现在时被动；to show compliance with federal regulations 为不定式短语作目的状语。

要点：技术文本常用 This is done to … 承接上文解释“为什么这么做”，比重复动名词更简洁。show compliance with（证明符合……）是审定与维修文件的高频搭配，同义表达还有 demonstrate compliance with。

④ seem to have done（完成式不定式）

原句：Although paragraph b states quite clearly the type of aircraft that this part does not apply to, it seems to have led to considerable confusion within the aviation industry.

拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句，从句中 that this part does not apply to 是定语从句修饰 the type of aircraft（介词 to 留在句末）；主句 it seems to have led to …，it 指代前面提到的该款规定；to have led 为完成式不定式，表示该影响已经发生并延续到现在。

要点：seem to have + 过去分词表示“看来已经……”，用于对已发生事实的推测；若用 seem to lead 则指现在或将来的倾向。注意 apply to 的介词 to 在定语从句中不能省略，这是改写到书面语时最易丢的一处。
