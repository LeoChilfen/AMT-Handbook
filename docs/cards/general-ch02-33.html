Appendix D—Scope and Detail of Items To Be Included in Annual and 100-Hour Inspections Some important items to consider in this appendix are:

  1. The list of items and areas to be inspected are exactly the same for an annual as a 100-hour inspection.

The difference between the inspections is in who is authorized to perform and approve the aircraft for RTS following the inspection. Refer to 14 CFR part 65, section 65.95(a)(2) that states that an IA must perform an annual inspection.

  1. The aircraft and engine must be cleaned prior to conducting the inspection.
  1. Any miscellaneous item not covered in the detailed list provided must also be inspected for improper installation and operation.
  1. There are eight specific areas identified for detailed inspection. They are the fuselage hull group, cabin/ flight deck group, engine/nacelle group, landing gear group, wing/center section group, empennage assembly, propeller group, and the radio group.

2. 中文翻译

附录 D——年度检查和 100 小时检查所含项目的内容范围与详细程度 本附录中需要考虑的几个重要事项是：

  1. 年度检查与 100 小时检查所需检查的项目和区域清单完全相同。

这两种检查的区别在于：检查之后由谁有权实施该检查、并批准航空器恢复使用（RTS）。参见 14 CFR 第 65 部 § 65.95(a)(2)，该条规定年度检查必须由持有检查授权（IA）的人员实施。

  1. 在实施检查之前，必须清洁航空器和发动机。
  1. 对于所提供详细清单未涵盖的任何其他杂项项目，也必须检查其安装与工作是否正常。
  1. 共确定了八个需要详细检查的特定区域，即机身（机体）组、客舱/驾驶舱组、发动机/短舱组、起落架组、机翼/中翼段组、尾翼组件、螺旋桨组和无线电组。

4. 语法解析

① prior to + 动名词作时间状语

原句 The aircraft and engine must be cleaned prior to conducting the inspection.

拆解 主句为被动结构 must be cleaned；prior to 是复合介词，后接动名词短语 conducting the inspection 构成时间状语，说明清洁先于检查。

要点 prior to 比 before 正式，常见于规章与技术文件，后接名词或动名词、不接从句；must be cleaned 表强制；翻译时时间状语前置更合中文语序（「检查前必须先清洁」）。

② There be 存在句 + 过去分词后置定语

原句 There are eight specific areas identified for detailed inspection.

拆解 There are 引出存在句，真正主语是 eight specific areas，后接过去分词短语 identified for detailed inspection 作后置定语；for 引出用途。

要点 identified 是过去分词定语而非谓语，译「被确定用于……的八个区域」；这类存在句翻译时可直接用「有八个……」；数字 eight 与下文八项区域对应，须保留。

③ 介词后接 who 引导的宾语从句

原句 The difference between the inspections is in who is authorized to perform and approve the aircraft for RTS following the inspection.

拆解 主语 The difference between the inspections，系动词 is 后接 in + 宾语从句 who is authorized to …；从句内为被动 be authorized to do，perform and approve 两个动词共用 to；following the inspection 为时间状语。

要点 介词后接 who 从句，译「区别在于由谁……」，不要拆成独立的「谁被授权」；be authorized to do 是「有资格做」，与 approve the aircraft for RTS（批准放行）须分清动作主体；following 在此是介词「在……之后」，不是分词。
