Fueling Hazards

通用 Ch 01 · Fueling Hazards · 原文 PDF 第 48 页

1. 原文

The volatility of aviation fuels creates a fire hazard that has plagued aviators and aviation engine designers since the beginning of powered flight. V olatility is the ability of a liquid to change into a gas at a relatively low temperature. In its liquid state, aviation fuel does not burn. It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state that the liquid fuel changes that is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard.

Static electricity is a byproduct of one substance rubbing against another. Fuel flowing through a fuel line causes a certain amount of static electricity. The greatest static electricity concern around aircraft is that during flight, the aircraft moving through the air causes static electricity to build in the airframe. If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling, the static electricity in the airframe attempts to return to the ground through the fuel line from the servicing unit. The spark caused by the static electricity can ignite any vaporized fuel.

Breathing the vapors from fuel can be harmful and must be limited. Any fuel spilled on the clothing or skin must be removed as soon as possible.

2. 中文翻译

航空燃油的挥发性带来火灾危险，这一危险自动力飞行诞生以来就一直困扰着飞行员和航空发动机设计者。挥发性是指液体在相对较低的温度下转变为气体的能力。在其液态下，航空燃油并不燃烧。因此，正是液态燃油转变而成的蒸气或气态，不仅可用于为飞机提供动力，同时也是一种火灾危险。

静电是一种物质与另一种物质摩擦产生的副产物。燃油流经燃油管路时会产生一定量的静电。飞机周围最值得关注的静电问题是：飞行中飞机在空气中运动，会使静电荷在机体上积聚。如果这些静电在加油前未被消散，机体上的静电就会试图通过勤务设备的燃油管路回到地面。静电产生的火花可以引燃任何已汽化的燃油。

吸入燃油蒸气有害健康，必须加以限制。任何溅到衣物或皮肤上的燃油都必须尽快清除。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句与现在完成时

原句：The volatility of aviation fuels creates a fire hazard that has plagued aviators and aviation engine designers since the beginning of powered flight. 拆解：主语 The volatility of aviation fuels + 谓语 creates + 宾语 a fire hazard + 定语从句 that has plagued…（现在完成时）+ since the beginning of powered flight（时间状语）。 要点：since + 时间点必须与现在完成时连用，表示「从那时起一直」；定语从句的先行词是 a fire hazard，从句谓语用单数 has。

② 强调句与嵌套定语从句

原句：It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state that the liquid fuel changes that is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard. 拆解：It is + 被强调成分 the vapor or gaseous state + 定语从句 that the liquid fuel changes（修饰 the vapor or gaseous state，that 作 changes 的宾语）+ 强调句的 that + is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard。 要点：句中出现两个 that，第一个属于定语从句（that 作宾语，相当于 which），第二个才是强调句的结构词；not only … but also 连接两个并列表语，翻译时可拆成「不仅……而且……」。

③ 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：Fuel flowing through a fuel line causes a certain amount of static electricity. 拆解：主语 Fuel + 现在分词短语 flowing through a fuel line 作后置定语（= which flows…）+ 谓语 causes + 宾语 a certain amount of static electricity。 要点：现在分词短语作定语时与被修饰名词是主动关系（燃油自己流动），若用过去分词则表被动；a certain amount of 修饰不可数名词，谓语用单数 causes。

④ 条件从句中的被动语态与 prior to

原句：If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling, the static electricity in the airframe attempts to return to the ground through the fuel line from the servicing unit. 拆解：if 条件从句（主语 that static electricity + 谓语 is not dissipated 被动 + prior to refueling 时间状语）+ 主句（主语 the static electricity in the airframe + 谓语 attempts to return + through… 路径状语 + from the servicing unit 来源状语）。 要点：prior to = before，是正式书面语，后接名词或动名词；attempt to do 表示「试图做」，不能接 attempt doing；主句用一般现在时表一般规律。
