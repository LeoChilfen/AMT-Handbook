Defueling

通用 Ch 01 · Defueling · 原文 PDF 第 50 页

1. 原文

Defueling procedures differ with different types of aircraft.

Before defueling an aircraft, check the maintenance/service manual for specific procedures and cautions. Defueling can be accomplished by gravity defueling or by pumping the fuel out of the tanks. When the gravity method is used, it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel. When the pumping method is used, care must be taken not to damage the tanks, and the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel.

General precautions when defueling are:

• Ground the aircraft and defueling equipment.

• Turn off all electrical and electronic equipment.

• Have the correct type of fire extinguisher available.

• Wear eye protection.

2. 中文翻译

抽放燃油的程序因航空器类型不同而不同。

抽放燃油之前，应查阅维修／勤务手册中的具体程序和注意事项。抽放燃油可以用重力放油的方式，也可以把燃油从油箱中泵出。采用重力法时，必须有收集燃油的手段。采用泵抽法时，必须注意不要损坏油箱，并且抽出的燃油不得与合格燃油混合。

抽放燃油时的一般注意事项如下：

• 给航空器和抽放燃油设备接地。

• 关闭所有电气和电子设备。

• 备好适用类型的灭火器。

• 佩戴护目镜。

4. 语法解析

① differ with different types of aircraft（不及物动词 + 介词搭配）

原句：Defueling procedures differ with different types of aircraft.

拆解：主语 Defueling procedures（动名词 defueling 作前置定语）；谓语 differ 为不及物动词；with different types of aircraft 表示「随……而不同」。

要点：differ with 是「随……而异」，differ from 是「与……不同」，本句取前者。同类写法：vary with type、depend on the aircraft type。主语是复数 procedures，所以 differ 用原形。

② it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel（形式主语 + 动名词定语）

原句：When the gravity method is used, it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel.

拆解：When the gravity method is used 为时间状语从句（被动）；主句 it is necessary to have …，it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to have a method of collecting the fuel；of collecting 为介词 + 动名词作后置定语修饰 method。

要点：it is necessary to do 是形式主语句型，比 to have … is necessary 更常见。method of + -ing 为固定搭配。状语从句用被动 is used，避免点明「谁使用」重力法。

③ care must be taken not to damage the tanks（take care 的被动式）

原句：When the pumping method is used, care must be taken not to damage the tanks, and the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel.

拆解：When 引导时间状语从句（被动）；主句是两个并列分句：care must be taken not to damage the tanks（take care not to do 的被动形式，not 置于不定式之前），以及 the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel（情态否定 + 被动）；removed 为过去分词作前置定语。

要点：主动式 take care not to do 变被动后成为 care must be taken not to do，否定词放在 to 之前，不能写成 must be taken to not damage。cannot 在此表「不得、不允许」。removed fuel（抽出的油）与 good fuel（合格油）不得混装，这是防止交叉污染的关键要求。
