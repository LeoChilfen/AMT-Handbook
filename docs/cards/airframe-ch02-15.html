Rotary-Wing Aircraft Assembly & Rigging

机体 Ch 02 · Rotary-Wing Aircraft Assembly & Rigging · 原文 PDF 第 86 页

1. 原文

The flight control units located in the flight deck of all helicopters are very nearly the same. All helicopters have either one or two of each of the following: collective pitch control, throttle grip, cyclic pitch control, and directional control pedals. Basically, these units do the same things, regardless of the type of helicopter on which they are installed; however, the operation of the control system varies greatly by helicopter model.

Rigging the helicopter coordinates the movements of the flight controls and establishes the relationship between the main rotor and its controls, and between the tail rotor and its controls. Rigging is not a difficult job, but it requires great precision and attention to detail. Strict adherence to rigging procedures described in the manufacturer’s maintenance manuals and service instructions is a must. Adjustments, clearances, and tolerances must be exact.

Rigging of the various flight control systems can be broken down into the following three major steps:

  1. Placing the control system in a specific position— holding it in position with pins, clamps, or jigs, then adjusting the various linkages to fit the immobilized control component.
  1. Placing the control surfaces in a specific reference position—using a rigging jig, a precision bubble protractor, or a spirit level to check the angular difference between the control surface and some fixed surface on the aircraft.
  1. Setting the maximum range of travel of the various components—this adjustment limits the physical movement of the control system.

After completion of the static rigging, a functional check of the flight control system must be accomplished. The nature of the functional check varies with the type of helicopter and system concerned, but usually includes determining that:

  1. The direction of movement of the main and tail rotor blades is correct in relation to movement of the pilot’s controls.
  1. The operation of interconnected control systems (engine throttle and collective pitch) is properly coordinated.
  1. The range of movement and neutral position of the pilot’s controls are correct.
  1. The maximum and minimum pitch angles of the main rotor blades are within specified limits. This includes checking the fore-and-aft and lateral cyclic pitch and collective pitch blade angles.
  1. The tracking of the main rotor blades is correct.
  1. In the case of multirotor aircraft, the rigging and movement of the rotor blades are synchronized.
  1. When tabs are provided on main rotor blades, they are correctly set.
  1. The neutral, maximum, and minimum pitch angles and coning angles of the tail rotor blades are correct.
  1. When dual controls are provided, they function correctly and in synchronization.

Upon completion of rigging, a thorough check should be made of all attaching, securing, and pivot points. All bolts, nuts, and rod ends should be properly secured and safetied as specified in the manufacturers’ maintenance and service instructions.

2. 中文翻译

所有直升机驾驶舱内的飞行操纵装置几乎相同。所有直升机都装有下列装置中的一种或两种：总距操纵、油门握把、周期变距操纵和方向操纵脚蹬。基本上，无论装在何种直升机上，这些装置的作用都相同；但操纵系统的工作方式随机型不同差异很大。

直升机校装是协调各飞行操纵装置的运动，并建立主旋翼与其操纵机构之间、尾桨与其操纵机构之间的关系。校装并非难事，但要求高度精确并注重细节。必须严格遵守制造商维护手册和服务说明中所述的校装程序。调整量、间隙和公差都必须准确无误。

各种飞行操纵系统的校装可分为以下三个主要步骤：

  1. 将操纵系统置于特定位置——用销钉、夹块或工装将其固定在位，然后调整各连杆机构，使之与固定不动的操纵部件相配合。
  1. 将操纵面置于特定基准位置——使用校装工装、精密气泡量角器或水平仪，检查操纵面与飞机上某固定面之间的角度差。
  1. 设定各部件的最大的行程范围——这一调整限制操纵系统的实际运动量。

静校装完成后，必须对飞行操纵系统进行功能检查。功能检查的性质因直升机和系统类型而异，但通常包括确认以下各项：

  1. 主旋翼和尾桨桨叶的运动方向与飞行员操纵装置的运动方向对应正确。
  1. 相互关联的操纵系统（发动机油门与总距）的工作协调正确。
  1. 飞行员操纵装置的运动范围和中间位置正确。
  1. 主旋翼桨叶的最大和最小桨距角在规定极限内。这包括检查前后向和横向周期变距桨距角以及总距桨距角。
  1. 主旋翼桨叶的锥体（tracking）正确。
  1. 对于多旋翼飞机，各旋翼桨叶的校装与运动是同步的。
  1. 若主旋翼桨叶装有调整片，其设定正确。
  1. 尾桨桨叶的中间、最大和最小桨距角以及锥角正确。
  1. 若装有双重操纵，其工作正确且同步。

校装完成后，应对所有连接点、固定点和枢轴点作彻底检查。所有螺栓、螺母和杆端应按制造商维护和服务说明的规定正确固定并打保险。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词作主语 + 并列谓语 + 并列介词短语

原句：Rigging the helicopter coordinates the movements of the flight controls and establishes the relationship between the main rotor and its controls, and between the tail rotor and its controls. 拆解：Rigging the helicopter 为动名词短语作主语；谓语由 and 连接 coordinates 与 establishes 两个第三人称单数动词；第一个宾语 the movements of the flight controls；第二个宾语 the relationship，后接两个并列的 between...and... 介词短语作后置定语。 要点：动名词短语作主语视为单数，故谓语用 coordinates/establishes。两个 between 短语并列，第二个省略了 relationship 的重复；翻译时要把“主旋翼与其操纵机构之间”和“尾桨与其操纵机构之间”分开表述。

② 过去分词短语作后置定语 + is a must

原句：Strict adherence to rigging procedures described in the manufacturer’s maintenance manuals and service instructions is a must. 拆解：主语中心词 adherence，to rigging procedures 为介词短语作后置定语；described in... 为过去分词短语修饰 procedures；谓语 is，表语 a must（名词化的情态表达）。 要点：主语后挂了两层定语，谓语 is 直到句末才出现，读句先抓“adherence ... is a must”。a must 是名词，意为“必须做的事”，不能换成 must 情态动词。

③ vary with + include determining that 宾语从句

原句：The nature of the functional check varies with the type of helicopter and system concerned, but usually includes determining that: 拆解：主语 The nature of the functional check，谓语 varies with（随……而变），宾语 the type of helicopter and system concerned；but 转折后承前省略主语，谓语 includes，宾语为动名词短语 determining that...，that 引导宾语从句（内容由后文列举）。 要点：concerned 是后置形容词，意为“相关的”，修饰 system。include 后接动名词 determining，不能接不定式；冒号说明 that 从句的内容将在下文逐条列出。

④ 被动语态 + 介词短语后置修饰

原句：Upon completion of rigging, a thorough check should be made of all attaching, securing, and pivot points. 拆解：Upon completion of rigging 为时间状语；主句为被动语态，主语 a thorough check，谓语 should be made，of all attaching, securing, and pivot points 为介词短语，说明 check 的对象。 要点：make a check of sth. 变被动后，介词短语 of... 与主语 check 被谓语隔开，形成“check should be made of...”的搭配，不要误把 of 短语当成状语。attaching、securing、pivot 三个并列定语共同修饰 points。

⑤ 被动语态并列 + as specified 省略结构

原句：All bolts, nuts, and rod ends should be properly secured and safetied as specified in the manufacturers’ maintenance and service instructions. 拆解：主语 All bolts, nuts, and rod ends；谓语为被动语态 should be secured and safetied 两个过去分词并列；as specified in... 为方式状语从句的省略形式（as is specified）。 要点：safety 作动词意为“打保险”，与 secure（固定）并列。as specified 是科技英语高频省略结构，完整形式 as it is specified，译为“按……的规定”。
