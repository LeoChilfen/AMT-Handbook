Alternative Method of Compliance

通用 Ch 02 · Alternative Method of Compliance · 原文 PDF 第 77 页

1. 原文

Different approaches or techniques that are not specified in an AD can, after FAA approval, be used to correct an unsafe condition on an aircraft or aircraft product. Although the alternative was not known at the time the AD was issued, an alternative method may be acceptable to accomplish the intent of the AD. A compliance time that differs from the requirements of the AD can also be approved if the revised time period and approved alternative method provides an acceptable level of safety as the requirements of the AD.

2. 中文翻译

未在 AD 中规定的不同做法或技术，经 FAA 批准后，可用于纠正航空器或航空器产品上的不安全状况。尽管在 AD 发布之时并不知道该替代方案，但为达成 AD 的意图，某种替代方法仍可能被接受。如果修订后的时间期限与经批准的替代方法能够达到与 AD 要求相当的可接受安全水平，则与 AD 要求不同的符合期限也可以获得批准。

4. 语法解析

① 主谓之间插入状语 + 情态被动

原句：Different approaches or techniques that are not specified in an AD can, after FAA approval, be used to correct an unsafe condition on an aircraft or aircraft product.

拆解：主语 Different approaches or techniques，后接 that are not specified in an AD 定语从句；谓语为 can be used，中间插入 after FAA approval 作条件状语；to correct an unsafe condition on an aircraft or aircraft product 是目的不定式。

要点：把 after FAA approval 插在 can 与 be used 之间，是为了突出「经批准」这一前提，阅读时应先跳过插入语抓 can be used，再回头补上条件。or 连接的两个名词作主语时谓语按复数处理（此处 can 无形态区分），但意义上是「做法或技术」任一种均可。

② Although 让步从句 + at the time (when) 省略

原句：Although the alternative was not known at the time the AD was issued, an alternative method may be acceptable to accomplish the intent of the AD.

拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句；at the time the AD was issued 中 the time 后省略了关系副词 when（也可用 that），构成定语从句；主句 an alternative method may be acceptable，to accomplish the intent of the AD 是目的状语。

要点：the time / the moment / the day 后的定语从句常省略 when，译作「在……之时」。may be acceptable 比 is acceptable 语气委婉，符合「可能被接受、需个案批准」的咨询性表述。although 从句与主句之间不能加 but。

③ 条件从句中的省略式比较结构

原句：A compliance time that differs from the requirements of the AD can also be approved if the revised time period and approved alternative method provides an acceptable level of safety as the requirements of the AD.

拆解：主语 A compliance time，后接 that differs from the requirements of the AD 定语从句；谓语 can also be approved 为被动；if 引导条件从句，从句主语是 the revised time period and approved alternative method（并列），谓语 provides 用单数；as the requirements of the AD 是省略式比较，与 an acceptable level of safety 对应。

要点：句末 as the requirements of the AD 属于省略写法，补全后相当于 as acceptable a level of safety as the requirements of the AD，读时把前一个 as 补回来就不会觉得别扭。provides 用单数，是因为两个并列主语被视为同一个方案整体，这类「意义一致」在技术文本中常见。
