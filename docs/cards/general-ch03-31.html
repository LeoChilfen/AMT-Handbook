Addition of Positive & Negative Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Addition of Positive & Negative Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 107 页

1. 原文

The sum (addition) of two positive numbers is positive. The sum (addition) of two negative numbers is negative. The sum of a positive and a negative number can be positive or negative, depending on the values of the numbers. A good way to visualize a negative number is to think in terms of debt. If you are in debt by $100 (or, −100) and you add $45 to your account, you are now only $55 in debt (or −55).

Therefore: −100 + 45 = −55.

Example: The weight of an aircraft is 2,000 pounds. A radio rack weighing 3 pounds and a transceiver weighing 10 pounds 80 N are removed from the aircraft. What is the new weight? For weight and balance purposes, all weight removed from an aircraft is given a minus sign, and all weight added is given a plus sign.

2,000 + −3 + −10 = 2,000 + −13 = 1,987 Therefore, the new weight is 1,987 pounds.

2. 中文翻译

两个正数之和（加法运算）为正。两个负数之和（加法运算）为负。一个正数与一个负数之和既可能为正，也可能为负，这取决于这两个数的数值大小。把负数直观地想象成负债是个好办法。如果你负债 $100（即 −100），然后又往账户里加上 $45，那么你现在只负债 $55（即 −55）。

因此：−100 + 45 = −55。

例：一架航空器的重量为 2,000 磅。从该航空器上拆下一台重 3 磅的无线电设备架和一部重 10 磅 80 N 的收发机。新的重量是多少？就载重与平衡而言，凡是从航空器上拆下的重量都记负号，凡是增加的重量都记正号。

2,000 + −3 + −10 = 2,000 + −13 = 1,987 因此，新的重量为 1,987 磅。

4. 语法解析

① 不定式：to visualize 作定语，to think 作表语

原句：A good way to visualize a negative number is to think in terms of debt.

拆解：主语 A good way，后置定语 to visualize a negative number（不定式修饰 way，说明「哪一种方法」）；系动词 is；表语由不定式 to think in terms of debt 充当。

要点：「做某事的方法」说 a way to do sth. 或 a way of doing sth.，不写 a way to doing。主语带不定式时，表语也用不定式以求得结构对称（to visualize … is to think …），这是英文定义句常见写法。in terms of 意为「从……的角度、以……来衡量」，技术文本高频。

② 过去分词短语作后置定语 + 被动语态

原句：For weight and balance purposes, all weight removed from an aircraft is given a minus sign, and all weight added is given a plus sign.

拆解：For weight and balance purposes 为目的状语；两个并列分句的主语分别是 all weight removed from an aircraft 与 all weight added，其中 removed from an aircraft、added 均为过去分词短语作后置定语（相当于 that is removed / that is added）；谓语 is given 为被动语态。

要点：单个分词作定语放名词前（added weight），带介词短语的分词短语必须放名词后（weight removed from an aircraft）。用现在分词还是过去分词，看名词是主动还是被动：重量是「被拆下」，故用 removed；同理 the weight added（被加上去的重量）。

③ 分词短语作状语：depending on

原句：The sum of a positive and a negative number can be positive or negative, depending on the values of the numbers.

拆解：主句 The sum … can be positive or negative；depending on the values of the numbers 是现在分词短语作状语，逻辑主语是前面整个判断，表示「取决于……」。

要点：depending on 是固定的分词短语，不能写成 depend on —— 那样句子会多出一个没有主语的谓语（悬垂结构）。同类还有 according to、considering、given that。译成中文时通常提前：「……取决于这两个数的数值」。
