Safety Around Helicopters

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Helicopters · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

Every type of helicopter has different features. These differences must be learned to avoid damaging the helicopter or injuring the technician. When approaching a helicopter while the blades are turning, adhere to the following guidelines to ensure safety.

• Observe the rotor head and blades to see if they are level. This allows maximum clearance when approaching the helicopter.

• Approach the helicopter in view of the pilot.

• Never approach a helicopter carrying anything with a vertical height that the blades could hit. This could cause blade damage and injury to the individual.

• Never approach a single-rotor helicopter from the rear.

The tail rotor is invisible when operating.

• Never go from one side of the helicopter to the other by going around the tail. Always go around the nose of the helicopter.

When securing the rotor on helicopters with elastomeric

bearings, check the maintenance manual for the proper method. Using the wrong method could damage the bearing.

2. 中文翻译

每一种直升机都有各自不同的特点。必须学会这些差异，才能避免损坏直升机或伤及技师。桨叶正在旋转时接近直升机，应遵守下列准则以确保安全。

• 观察旋翼头和桨叶，看它们是否处于水平位置。这样接近直升机时可获得最大的间隙。

• 在飞行员视野内接近直升机。

• 绝不要携带竖直高度可能被桨叶打到的物品接近直升机。这可能造成桨叶损坏并伤及人员。

• 绝不要从后方接近单旋翼直升机。

尾桨在运转时是看不见的。

• 绝不要绕经尾部从直升机的一侧走到另一侧。始终应绕机头通过。

在装有弹性轴承的直升机上固定旋翼时，应查阅维修手册，了解正确的方法。

使用错误的方法可能损坏轴承。

4. 语法解析

① These differences must be learned to avoid damaging the helicopter（情态被动 + 动名词作宾语）

原句：These differences must be learned to avoid damaging the helicopter or injuring the technician.

拆解：主语 These differences，谓语 must be learned（情态被动）；to avoid … 是目的状语，avoid 后接两个并列动名词 damaging 与 injuring。

要点：avoid 后必须接动名词，不能接不定式（avoid to damage 是典型错误）；or 连接两个动名词，共用 damaging 的宾语结构。手册中「must be learned」译作「必须掌握/学会」，不必补出施事者。

② Observe the rotor head and blades to see if they are level（不定式 + if 引导的宾语从句）

原句：Observe the rotor head and blades to see if they are level.

拆解：祈使句 Observe + 并列宾语 the rotor head and blades；to see 是目的状语；see 后接 if 引导的宾语从句 if they are level，they 指旋翼头和桨叶。

要点：if 在动词后除「如果」外还可表「是否」，相当于 whether，此处译「看它们是否水平」。level 在此是形容词「水平的」，不是名词「水平面」；主系表结构 are level 不能加 -ly。

③ Never approach a helicopter carrying anything with a vertical height that the blades could hit（现在分词作定语 + 定语从句）

原句：Never approach a helicopter carrying anything with a vertical height that the blades could hit.

拆解：祈使句 Never approach a helicopter；carrying anything 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰接近者（隐含主语 you），with a vertical height 修饰 anything；that the blades could hit 是定语从句，修饰 height。

要点：现在分词短语的逻辑主语要与句子主语（隐含的 you）一致，此句意为「你携带……接近」，若改成 carried 则变成「被携带的直升机」，语义完全不同。定语从句 that … hit 中 that 作 hit 的宾语，不能换成 where；作者强调「竖直高度」是因为桨叶扫掠面在水平方向，高度才是关键。

④ Using the wrong method could damage the bearing（动名词作主语 + could 表推测）

原句：Using the wrong method could damage the bearing.

拆解：主语是动名词短语 Using the wrong method，谓语 could damage（情态动词 + 动词原形），宾语 the bearing。

要点：动名词作主语表「做某事这件事」，谓语用单数；could 在此不是过去时，而是表示「有可能」，语气比 can 委婉，常用来陈述后果。对比 Using the wrong method damages the bearing（陈述必然结果），用 could 更符合手册提示风险的口气。
