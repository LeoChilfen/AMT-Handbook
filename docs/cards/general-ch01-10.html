Safety Around Helicopters

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Helicopters · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

Every type of helicopter has different features. These differences must be learned to avoid damaging the helicopter or injuring the technician. When approaching a helicopter while the blades are turning, adhere to the following guidelines to ensure safety.

• Observe the rotor head and blades to see if they are level. This allows maximum clearance when approaching the helicopter.

• Approach the helicopter in view of the pilot.

• Never approach a helicopter carrying anything with a vertical height that the blades could hit. This could cause blade damage and injury to the individual.

• Never approach a single-rotor helicopter from the rear.

The tail rotor is invisible when operating.

• Never go from one side of the helicopter to the other by going around the tail. Always go around the nose of the helicopter.

When securing the rotor on helicopters with elastomeric bearings, check the maintenance manual for the proper method. Using the wrong method could damage the bearing.

2. 中文翻译

每种直升机都有各自不同的特点。必须了解这些差异，以免损坏直升机或伤及维修人员。在旋翼仍在转动时接近直升机，应遵守下列准则以确保安全。

• 观察旋翼桨毂与桨叶是否处于水平状态。这样在接近直升机时才能获得最大的间隙。

• 在飞行员的视线范围内接近直升机。

• 绝不要携带任何竖直高度可能被桨叶打到的物品接近直升机。这可能造成桨叶损坏，并使人受伤。

• 绝不要从后方接近单旋翼直升机。

尾桨在运转时是看不见的。

• 绝不要绕过尾部从直升机的一侧走到另一侧。始终应从机头一侧绕行。

在为装有弹性体轴承的直升机固定旋翼时，应查阅维修手册确认正确的方法。方法错误可能损坏轴承。

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词的被动语态

原句：These differences must be learned to avoid damaging the helicopter or injuring the technician. 拆解：主语 These differences；谓语 must be learned（情态动词 + 被动语态）；to avoid damaging… or injuring… 为目的状语，avoid 后接两个并列动名词。 要点：must be + 过去分词表示“必须被（人）掌握”，强调动作由人执行而非主语自己完成；avoid 后只能接动名词，不能接 to do。

② 时间状语叠加 + 祈使句

原句：When approaching a helicopter while the blades are turning, adhere to the following guidelines to ensure safety. 拆解：When approaching… 为省略主语的时间状语（相当于 When you approach…）；while the blades are turning 为同时性时间状语从句；主句 adhere to… 是祈使句，to ensure safety 表目的。 要点：when + 动名词的逻辑主语须与主句一致，否则要补出主语；while 用进行时强调桨叶“正在转”这一持续状态；adhere to 是“遵守”，不要与 stick to（粘住）混用。

③ 定语从句的嵌套修饰

原句：Never approach a helicopter carrying anything with a vertical height that the blades could hit. 拆解：祈使句 Never approach + 宾语 a helicopter；carrying anything 为现在分词短语作后置定语；with a vertical height 为介词短语修饰 anything；that the blades could hit 为定语从句修饰 height，that 在从句中作 hit 的宾语。 要点：现在分词作后置定语表主动、伴随；长定语层层后置时，that 从句只修饰紧邻的 height，翻译要按“可能被桨叶打到的竖直高度”重组语序。

④ by + 动名词表方式

原句：Never go from one side of the helicopter to the other by going around the tail. 拆解：from… to… 表位移的起点与终点；by going around the tail 为方式状语（by + 动名词）；go around 为短语动词。 要点：by + 动名词表示“通过做某事”，与 by + 交通工具（by bus）不同；never 置于句首构成绝对禁止的祈使句，手册中应译作“绝不要”。
