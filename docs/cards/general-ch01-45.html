Oxygen Hazards

通用 Ch 01 · Oxygen Hazards · 原文 PDF 第 47 页

1. 原文

Gaseous oxygen is chemically stable and is nonflammable.

However, combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity in an oxygen-rich atmosphere. In addition, oxygen combines with oil, grease, or bituminous material to form a highly-explosive mixture that is sensitive to compression or impact. Physical damage to, or failure of, oxygen containers, valves, or plumbing can result in an explosive rupture with extreme danger to life and property.

It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems. In addition to aggravating the fire hazard and because of its low temperature (it boils at −297 °F), liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) if it comes in contact with the skin.

2. 中文翻译

气态氧化学性质稳定，且不可燃。

然而在富氧环境中，可燃物质着火更快、燃烧更猛烈。此外，氧与滑油、润滑脂或沥青质材料结合，会形成对压缩或撞击敏感的高爆炸性混合物。氧气瓶、阀门或管路受到物理损伤或发生失效，可能导致爆炸性破裂，对生命和财产造成极大危险。

在处置氧气时遵守最高标准的清洁要求、并且只允许合格且经授权的人员对航空器气态氧系统实施勤务，这一点至关重要。除加剧火灾危险之外，由于温度极低（其沸点为 −297 °F），液氧接触皮肤会造成严重「灼伤」（冻伤）。

4. 语法解析

① combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity（并列谓语 + 两个比较级）

原句：However, combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity in an oxygen-rich atmosphere.

拆解：主语 combustible materials；两个并列谓语 ignite（状语 more rapidly 为比较级）与 burn（介词短语 with greater intensity 表程度比较级）；in an oxygen-rich atmosphere 为条件／范围状语。

要点：两个比较级一个用副词、一个用介词短语，翻译时不必都译成「更……」，合成「更快、更猛烈」更自然。ignite 及物、不及物两用，此处不及物，指可燃物自身着火。

② It is imperative that … be observed and that … be permitted（虚拟语气从句作真正主语）

原句：It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正主语是两个并列的 that 从句；从句谓语用动词原形 be observed、be permitted，即 (should) + 动词原形的虚拟语气；in handling oxygen 为介词短语状语；permit sb. to do 结构中 to service 为不定式。

要点：It is imperative／essential／necessary that … (should) + 动词原形是规范文本高频句型。写成 is observed 在口语中虽能听到，正式文件仍用 be observed。第二个 that 不可省略，否则并列关系不清。

③ In addition to aggravating …, liquid oxygen causes severe “burns”（介词 + 动名词 + 分词/形容词表语）

原句：In addition to aggravating the fire hazard and because of its low temperature (it boils at −297 °F), liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) if it comes in contact with the skin.

拆解：In addition to aggravating … 为介词短语（to 是介词，后接动名词）作补充状语；because of its low temperature 为原因状语，括号内是补充数据；主句 liquid oxygen causes severe “burns”；if it comes in contact with the skin 为条件状语从句。

要点：in addition to 后接名词或 -ing，in addition（副词）后才接句子。come in contact with 是固定搭配，不能写成 contact with。括号内的 −297 °F 是必要数据，翻译时应保留。
