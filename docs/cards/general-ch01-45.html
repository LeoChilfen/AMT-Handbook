Ground Air Heating and Air Conditioning

通用 Ch 01 · Ground Air Heating and Air Conditioning · 原文 PDF 第 46 页

1. 原文

Most airport gates have facilities that can provide heated or cooled air. The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations that connect to the aircraft’s ventilation system by use of a large hose. Portable heating and air conditioning units can also be moved close to the aircraft and connected by a duct that provides air to keep the cabin temperature comfortable.

2. 中文翻译

大多数机场登机口都配有可提供加热或冷却空气的设施。制冷或加热空气的机组是永久性安装设备，通过一根粗大的软管连接到飞机的通风系统。便携式加热与空调机组也可以移到飞机附近，并用风管连接，向飞机供气以保持客舱温度舒适。

4. 语法解析

① that 引导的定语从句

原句：Most airport gates have facilities that can provide heated or cooled air. 拆解：主语 Most airport gates + 谓语 have + 宾语 facilities + 定语从句 that can provide heated or cooled air（that 在从句中作主语，can provide 为情态动词 + 动词原形，heated / cooled 为过去分词作前置定语）。 要点：that 作从句主语时不能省略；heated or cooled air 中两个过去分词并列修饰 air，表示「被加热或冷却的空气」，与主动的 heating / cooling 设备形成对照，注意区分 -ed 与 -ing 定语。

② 并列的情态动词被动语态

原句：Portable heating and air conditioning units can also be moved close to the aircraft and connected by a duct that provides air to keep the cabin temperature comfortable. 拆解：主语 Portable heating and air conditioning units + 谓语 can also be moved（情态动词被动）+ and (can be) connected（并列的第二个被动，重复的 can be 省略）+ by a duct 方式状语 + that provides air 定语从句 + to keep… 目的状语。 要点：两个被动动词共用一个情态动词时，第二个常只留过去分词，这是手册英语压缩句子的常见手段；还原成 can be moved … and can be connected … 即可看懂。by a duct 引出的是手段而非施动者。

③ by use of 引导的方式状语

原句：The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations that connect to the aircraft’s ventilation system by use of a large hose. 拆解：主语 The units（+ that cool or heat the air 定语从句）+ 系动词 are + 表语 permanent installations（+ that connect to… 定语从句）+ by use of a large hose 方式状语。 要点：by use of = by using = 通过使用……，介词 by 后只能接名词或动名词，不能接从句；句中两个 that 从句分别修饰 units 和 installations，阅读时先找出「主—系—表」主干再挂从句。
