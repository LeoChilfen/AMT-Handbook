Auxiliary Power Units (APUs)

通用 Ch 01 · Auxiliary Power Units (APUs) · 原文 PDF 第 38 页

1. 原文

APUs are generally smaller turbine engines that provide compressed air for starting engines, cabin heating and cooling, and electrical power while on the ground. Their operation is normally simple. By turning a switch on and up to the start position (spring loaded to on position), the engine starts automatically. During start, the exhaust gas temperature must be monitored. APUs are at idle at 100 percent rpm with no load. After the engine reaches its operating rpm, it can be used for cooling or heating the cabin and for electrical power. It is normally used to start the main engines.

2. 中文翻译

APU 通常是较小的涡轮发动机，在地面为起动发动机提供压缩空气，并为客舱供暖、制冷和供电。它的操作一般很简单。把电门先接通、再向上扳到起动位（该位置为弹簧加载，松手后回到接通位），发动机便自动起动。起动过程中必须监视排气温度。APU 空载时以 100% 转速处于慢车状态。发动机达到工作转速后，即可用于客舱制冷或供暖，以及供电。它通常用来起动主发动机。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + 三个并列宾语 + while 短语省略

原句：APUs are generally smaller turbine engines that provide compressed air for starting engines, cabin heating and cooling, and electrical power while on the ground.

拆解：主干 APUs are smaller turbine engines；that 引导定语从句修饰 engines，从句谓语 provide，宾语是三个并列名词短语 compressed air、cabin heating and cooling、electrical power；for starting engines 是 compressed air 的用途定语；while on the ground 是时间状语，省略了 they are。

要点：三个并列宾语之间用逗号 + and，第一个 for 管住后面全部（for starting engines, cabin heating and cooling, and electrical power），翻译时不要只把 for 挂到第一个宾语上。while + 形容词/介词短语是「省略主语和 be」的常见状语。

② by doing 表手段 + 插入的过去分词短语

原句：By turning a switch on and up to the start position (spring loaded to on position), the engine starts automatically.

拆解：By turning a switch on and up to the start position 是方式状语（by + 动名词），turn on 与 turn up 是短语动词，宾语 a switch 插在中间（turn a switch on / up）；括号内 spring loaded to on position 是过去分词短语作插入定语，修饰 the start position；主句 the engine starts automatically。

要点：短语动词带宾语时，代词宾语必须放中间（turn it on），名词宾语可前可后；这里 a switch 放中间是为了让 on and up 连读清楚。spring loaded 是「弹簧加载的」，作定语时用过去分词形式。

③ 情态动词被动（无施动者）

原句：During start, the exhaust gas temperature must be monitored.

拆解：During start 是时间状语；主语 the exhaust gas temperature，谓语 must be monitored 是情态动词 + 被动语态，未出现 by 短语（施动者泛指操作者）。

要点：手册里「必须监视某参数」习惯用 must be monitored 而非 you must monitor，因为规定针对的是所有操作者。翻译时补出泛指的「（操作者）必须监视」，不要译成「排气温度自己必须被监视」这种生硬被动。

④ after 从句 + be used for 的并列介词宾语

原句：After the engine reaches its operating rpm, it can be used for cooling or heating the cabin and for electrical power.

拆解：After 引导时间状语从句（主语 the engine，谓语 reaches，宾语 its operating rpm）；主句主语 it（指 APU），谓语 can be used 是情态动词被动，for cooling or heating the cabin 与 for electrical power 是并列的目的状语（两个 for 并列）。

要点：be used for + 动名词（cooling/heating）与 be used for + 名词（electrical power）都成立，但并列时两个 for 都要写出以保持平行。after 从句用一般现在时表将来，是时间状语从句的常规时态。
