Appendix E—Altimeter System Test and Inspection

通用 Ch 02 · Appendix E—Altimeter System Test and Inspection · 原文 PDF 第 70 页

1. 原文

This is commonly referred to as “the 411 test.” Refer to 14 CFR part 91, section 91.411 that requires that no person may operate an aircraft in controlled airspace under IFR unless the aircraft has had this test completed successfully within the preceding 24 months.) This section requires detailed testing of the static pressure system, the altimeter, and the automatic pressure altitude reporting equipment, and that the test information be recorded in the maintenance logs and on the altimeter.

2. 中文翻译

这一测试通常被称为「411 测试」。参见 14 CFR 第 91 部 § 91.411，该条要求：除航空器已在之前 24 个月内成功完成该测试外，任何人不得在管制空域内按仪表飞行规则（IFR）运行航空器。）本节要求对静压系统、高度表以及自动气压高度报告设备进行详细测试，并要求将测试信息记录在维修记录本上以及高度表上。

4. 语法解析

① that 定语从句内嵌 unless 条件

原句 Refer to 14 CFR part 91, section 91.411 that requires that no person may operate an aircraft in controlled airspace under IFR unless the aircraft has had this test completed successfully within the preceding 24 months.

拆解 祈使句 Refer to … 引出条文号；第一个 that 引导定语从句修饰 section 91.411，第二个 that 引导 requires 的宾语从句；宾语从句主干为 no person may operate an aircraft，后接 in controlled airspace、under IFR 两个状语，unless 引出唯一例外。

要点 两个 that 层级不同，翻译要先拆成「该条要求……」再引出条件；no person may … unless … 是法规否定句的固定搭配，译「除非……否则任何人不得……」；in controlled airspace under IFR 是两个限定叠加，不能只译一个。

② has had this test completed 的完成含义

原句 unless the aircraft has had this test completed successfully within the preceding 24 months

拆解 has had 是 have 的现在完成时（has + 过去分词 had），构成 have sth. done 结构：this test 为宾语，completed successfully 为过去分词短语作宾语补足语；within the preceding 24 months 为时间状语。

要点 has had this test completed 不能按「有」直译，意为「已使该测试完成」，即「已完成该测试」；现在完成时强调到目前仍然有效的状态，译「在此前 24 个月内已成功完成」；preceding 是「之前的」，指从当下往前推算的 24 个月。

③ requires 后接并列宾语与虚拟语气从句

原句 This section requires detailed testing of the static pressure system, the altimeter, and the automatic pressure altitude reporting equipment, and that the test information be recorded in the maintenance logs and on the altimeter.

拆解 requires 后接两个并列宾语：名词短语 detailed testing of A, B, and C，以及 that 从句 the test information be recorded …；从句谓语用原形 be，属要求类动词后的虚拟语气。

要点 requires 后 that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形，be recorded 即此用法，译文加「须」即可；detailed testing of 后三项以逗号 + and 收尾，须保持三项完整；on the altimeter 指在高度表上作记录，与 in the logs 形成对照。
