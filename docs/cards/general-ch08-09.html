Treatment of Titanium & Titanium Alloys

通用 Ch 08 · Treatment of Titanium & Titanium Alloys · 原文 PDF 第 345 页

1. 原文

Attack on titanium surfaces is generally difficult to detect. Titanium is, by nature, highly corrosion resistant, but it may show deterioration from the presence of salt deposits and metal impurities, particularly at high temperatures. Therefore, the use of steel wool, iron scrapers, or steel brushes for cleaning or for the removal of corrosion from titanium parts is prohibited.

If titanium surfaces require cleaning, hand polishing with aluminum polish or a mild abrasive is permissible if fiber brushes only are used and if the surface is treated following cleaning with a suitable solution of sodium dichromate. Wipe the treated surface with dry cloths to remove excess solution, but do not use a water rinse.

2. 中文翻译

钛表面受到的侵蚀通常难以发现。钛本身具有很高的耐腐蚀性，但盐沉积物和金属杂质的存在可能使其出现劣化，在高温条件下尤其如此。因此，禁止用钢丝绵、铁刮刀或钢丝刷来清洁钛零件或去除其腐蚀。

如果钛表面需要清洁，可以用铝抛光剂或细磨料手工抛光，但前提是只能使用纤维刷，并且在清洁后要用合适的重铬酸钠溶液对表面进行处理。处理后用干布擦拭表面以去除多余的溶液，但不得用水冲洗。

4. 语法解析

① 形容词 + 不定式（主动形式表被动意义）

原句：Attack on titanium surfaces is generally difficult to detect. 拆解：主语 Attack（on titanium surfaces 为介词短语作后置定语）+ 系动词 is + 表语 generally difficult，后接不定式 to detect 作状语；to detect 的逻辑宾语就是句子的主语 attack，但句中不出现。 要点：difficult、easy、hard、impossible 等形容词后接不定式时，主动形式表达被动含义，不能写成 is difficult to be detected；译成中文常把宾语提前，说「侵蚀难以被发现」。同类结构还有 the corrosion is hard to see。

② 插入语 + but 并列复合句

原句：Titanium is, by nature, highly corrosion resistant, but it may show deterioration from the presence of salt deposits and metal impurities, particularly at high temperatures. 拆解：逗号之间的 by nature 是插入语，说明「从本质上讲」，不充当句子成分；but 连接两个并列分句，前一分句为主系表结构，后一分句为「主语 it + 情态动词 may + 谓语 show + 宾语 deterioration + 介词短语 from…作原因状语」，句末 particularly at high temperatures 为补充状语。 要点：插入语放在系动词与表语之间时，前后必须用逗号隔开，阅读时可先跳过它抓住 is highly corrosion resistant 这一主干；corrosion resistant 是「名词 + 形容词」构成的复合形容词，作表语，不能写成 corrosion-resistantly。

③ 长主语 + 被动语态（主语与谓语被后置定语隔开）

原句：Therefore, the use of steel wool, iron scrapers, or steel brushes for cleaning or for the removal of corrosion from titanium parts is prohibited. 拆解：主语中心词是 the use，of steel wool, iron scrapers, or steel brushes 为后置定语，for cleaning or for the removal of corrosion from titanium parts 为两个并列的目的状语；真正的谓语是被动语态 is prohibited，与主语之间隔了二十多个词。 要点：手册类文本习惯把「禁止什么」写成 sth is prohibited，而不写 we prohibit sth；阅读时必须越过 of/for 引导的长定语去找谓语，否则容易把 steel brushes 误当成主语。同类被动表达还有 is not permitted、must not be used。

④ 条件从句的并列与省略

原句：If titanium surfaces require cleaning, hand polishing with aluminum polish or a mild abrasive is permissible if fiber brushes only are used and if the surface is treated following cleaning with a suitable solution of sodium dichromate. 拆解：句首 If titanium surfaces require cleaning 为条件状语从句；主句主语是动名词短语 hand polishing（with aluminum polish or a mild abrasive 为方式状语），谓语是 is permissible；后面由 and 连接两个并列的 if 从句，第二个 if 从句中 the surface 是主语、is treated 是被动谓语，following cleaning 为时间状语。 要点：两个并列条件从句各自保留 if，不能只写一个 if 管两个分句；fiber brushes only 中的 only 后置是为了强调「只能用纤维刷」，不能移到句首；is permissible 表示「是允许的」，语气比 may be used 更客观。

⑤ but 连接的祈使句与否定祈使句

原句：Wipe the treated surface with dry cloths to remove excess solution, but do not use a water rinse. 拆解：but 连接两个祈使句：前一句为肯定祈使句 Wipe…，to remove excess solution 为目的状语；后一句为否定祈使句 do not use a water rinse，do not 后接动词原形 use。 要点：操作步骤常用祈使句，主语 you 不出现；否定祈使句由 do not + 动词原形构成，不能写成 do not to use 或 not use。中文说明书里对应「用干布擦拭……，但不得用水冲洗」，否定词要放在动词前。
