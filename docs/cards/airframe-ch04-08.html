Forming Process

机体 Ch 04 · Forming Process · 原文 PDF 第 218 页

1. 原文

Before a part is attached to the aircraft during either manufacture or repair, it has to be shaped to fit into place.

This shaping process is called forming and may be a simple process, such as making one or two holes for attaching; it may be a complex process, such as making shapes with complex curvatures. Forming, which tends to change the shape or contour of a flat sheet or extruded shape, is accomplished by either stretching or shrinking the material in a certain area to produce curves, flanges, and various irregular shapes. Since the operation involves altering the shape of the stock material, the amount of shrinking and stretching almost entirely depends on the type of material used. Fully annealed (heated and cooled) material can withstand considerably more stretching and shrinking and can be formed at a much smaller bend radius than when it is in any of the tempered conditions.

When aircraft parts are formed at the factory, they are made on large presses or by drop hammers equipped with dies of the correct shape. Factory engineers, who designate specifications for the materials to be used to ensure the finished part has the correct temper when it leaves the machines, plan every part. Factory draftsmen prepare a layout for each part.

Forming processes used on the flight line and those practiced in the maintenance or repair shop cannot duplicate a manufacturer’s resources, but similar techniques of factory metal working can be applied in the handcrafting of repair parts.

Forming usually involves the use of extremely light-gauge alloys of a delicate nature that can be readily made useless by coarse and careless workmanship. A formed part may seem outwardly perfect, yet a wrong step in the forming procedure may leave the part in a strained condition. Such a defect may hasten fatigue or may cause sudden structural failure.

Of all the aircraft metals, pure aluminum is the most easily formed. In aluminum alloys, ease of forming varies with the temper condition. Since modern aircraft are constructed chiefly of aluminum and aluminum alloys, this section deals with the procedures for forming aluminum or aluminum alloy parts with a brief discussion of working with stainless steel, magnesium, and titanium.

Most parts can be formed without annealing the metal, but if extensive forming operations, such as deep draws (large folds) or complex curves, are planned, the metal should be in the dead soft or annealed condition. During the forming of some complex parts, operations may need to be stopped and the metal annealed before the process can be continued or completed. For example, alloy 2024 in the “0” condition can be formed into almost any shape by the common forming operations, but it must be heat treated afterward.

2. 中文翻译

在制造或修理过程中把零件装到飞机上之前，必须先把它加工成能装到位的外形。

这种成形加工称为成形（forming），可能很简单，如为安装钻一两个孔；也可能很复杂，如加工出带复杂曲面的形状。成形倾向于改变平板或挤压型材的形状或轮廓，其做法是在某一区域内拉伸或收缩材料，以形成曲面、弯边（flange）和各种不规则形状。由于这一操作要改变原材料的形状，收缩与拉伸的量几乎完全取决于所用材料的种类。完全退火（加热后冷却）的材料能承受大得多的拉伸与收缩，并能在比任何回火状态小得多的弯曲半径下成形。

在工厂成形飞机零件时，零件是在配备形状正确模具的大型压力机或落锤上制成的。工厂工程师为每个零件做规划，他们规定所用材料的规范，以确保成品零件在离开机器时具有正确的回火状态。工厂制图员为每个零件绘制布局图。

航线现场使用的成形工艺，以及维修或修理车间里实行的工艺，都无法复制制造商的资源条件，但工厂金属加工的类似技术仍可应用于修理件的手工制作。

成形通常涉及使用极薄、性质娇贵的合金，粗糙马虎的操作很容易把它们弄废。一个成形件外表看起来可能完美，但成形过程中走错一步，就可能使零件处于受应力的状态。这类缺陷会加速疲劳，或引起突然的结构破坏。

在所有飞机金属中，纯铝最容易成形。在铝合金中，成形的难易程度随回火状态而变化。由于现代飞机主要用铝和铝合金制造，本节讲述铝及铝合金零件的成形工艺，并简要讨论不锈钢、镁和钛的加工。

大多数零件不必退火即可成形，但如果计划进行大量成形操作，如深拉（大折弯）或复杂曲面，金属应处于全软或退火状态。在成形某些复杂零件时，可能需要停下来把金属退火，然后才能继续或完成这道工序。例如，2024 合金在“O”状态下几乎可用普通成形操作加工成任何形状，但之后必须进行热处理。

4. 语法解析

① 时间状语从句 + have to be done

原句：Before a part is attached to the aircraft during either manufacture or repair, it has to be shaped to fit into place. 拆解：Before 引导时间状语从句 a part is attached to the aircraft（被动语态），during either manufacture or repair 为嵌在从句里的时间状语；主句 it has to be shaped（被动），to fit into place 作目的状语。 要点：either manufacture or repair 用 either...or... 连接两个名词，前面共用介词 during；has to be shaped 字面是“必须被加工”，中文宜转成主动的“必须先把它加工成形”。

② 分号连接两个对称分句 + 举例

原句：This shaping process is called forming and may be a simple process, such as making one or two holes for attaching; it may be a complex process, such as making shapes with complex curvatures. 拆解：前一分句有两个并列谓语 is called forming 与 may be a simple process，such as making... 用动名词短语举例；分号后是结构平行的第二分句 it may be a complex process，同样用 such as 引出动名词。 要点：such as 后接动名词 making，不能接句子；两句用分号而非 and 连接，是为了强调“简单/复杂”的对照，翻译时保留对仗句式最自然。

③ 非限制性定语从句 + 被动谓语 + by 动名词

原句：Forming, which tends to change the shape or contour of a flat sheet or extruded shape, is accomplished by either stretching or shrinking the material in a certain area to produce curves, flanges, and various irregular shapes. 拆解：主语 Forming 后接 which 引导的非限制性定语从句，谓语 tends to change，宾语 the shape or contour；主句谓语 is accomplished 为被动；by either stretching or shrinking... 为方式状语（by + 动名词），to produce... 为目的状语。 要点：which 从句用逗号隔开，删去后主句仍完整；by 是介词，所以用 stretching/shrinking 而不是动词原形。way 类的“方式”在此由 by + 动名词承担，不要与 in a certain area（范围状语）混为一层。

④ 原因状语从句 + 动名词宾语 + 主谓一致

原句：Since the operation involves altering the shape of the stock material, the amount of shrinking and stretching almost entirely depends on the type of material used. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句，involves 后接动名词 altering 作宾语；主句主语是 the amount of shrinking and stretching，中心词 amount 为单数，谓语 depends on，宾语 the type of material，used 为过去分词后置修饰 material。 要点：involve doing sth. 是固定搭配，不能换成 involve to do；主语虽含 and，但中心词是 amount，故谓语用单数 depends。used 修饰的是 material，不是 type。

⑤ 比较级 + 被动 + than 引导的从句

原句：Fully annealed (heated and cooled) material can withstand considerably more stretching and shrinking and can be formed at a much smaller bend radius than when it is in any of the tempered conditions. 拆解：主语 Fully annealed material，括号内 heated and cooled 为解释性过去分词；两个并列谓语 can withstand 与 can be formed（被动）；considerably more 修饰宾语，at a much smaller bend radius 为方式状语；than when it is in any of the tempered conditions 为比较状语从句。 要点：than 后面接的是 when 从句而非名词短语，比较的是“退火态”与“任何回火态下”的成形能力；much、considerably 只能修饰比较级，不能修饰原级。

⑥ 主句被长定语从句隔开

原句：Factory engineers, who designate specifications for the materials to be used to ensure the finished part has the correct temper when it leaves the machines, plan every part. 拆解：主语 Factory engineers 后接 who 引导的非限制性定语从句：谓语 designate，宾语 specifications；to be used 为不定式被动作 materials 的后置定语；to ensure... 为目的状语，其后 the finished part has the correct temper 为省略 that 的宾语从句，when 引导时间状语从句；主句谓语是句末的 plan，宾语 every part。 要点：真正的谓语 plan 被长达二十余词的定语从句推到最后，读句时先抓“Factory engineers ... plan every part”这条骨架。ensure 后的宾语从句省略了 that，has 的主语是 the finished part 而不是 engineers。
