Trim Controls

机体 Ch 02 · Trim Controls · 原文 PDF 第 82 页

1. 原文

Included in the trim controls are the trim tabs, servo tabs, balance tabs, and spring tabs. Trim tabs are small airfoils recessed into the trailing edges of the primary control surfaces. Trim tabs can be used to correct any tendency of the aircraft to move toward an undesirable flight attitude. Their purpose is to enable the pilot to trim out any unbalanced condition which may exist during flight, without exerting any pressure on the primary controls.

Servo tabs, sometimes referred to as flight tabs, are used primarily on the large main control surfaces. They aid in moving the main control surface and holding it in the desired position. Only the servo tab moves in response to movement by the pilot of the primary flight controls.

Balance tabs are designed to move in the opposite direction of the primary flight control. Thus, aerodynamic forces acting on the tab assist in moving the primary control surface.

Spring tabs are similar in appearance to trim tabs, but serve an entirely different purpose. Spring tabs are used for the same purpose as hydraulic actuators—to aid the pilot in moving the primary control surface.

2. 中文翻译

配平操纵装置包括配平片、伺服片、平衡片和弹簧片。配平片是凹陷嵌装在主操纵面后缘内的小翼型。配平片可用于修正飞机趋向不良飞行姿态的任何趋势。其用途是使飞行员能够消除飞行中可能存在的任何不平衡状态，而无需在主操纵装置上施加任何压力。

伺服片有时称为飞行片，主要用于大型主操纵面。它们帮助移动主操纵面，并将其保持在所需的位置。只有伺服片会响应飞行员对主飞行操纵装置的移动而移动。

平衡片设计成与主飞行操纵装置反向移动。因此，作用在平衡片上的气动力协助移动主操纵面。

弹簧片在外观上与配平片相似，但用途完全不同。弹簧片的用途与液压作动筒相同——帮助飞行员移动主操纵面。

4. 语法解析

① 完全倒装（表语前置）

原句：Included in the trim controls are the trim tabs, servo tabs, balance tabs, and spring tabs. 拆解：正常语序应为 The trim tabs, servo tabs, balance tabs, and spring tabs are included in the trim controls；为了承接上文的“配平操纵装置”，把过去分词短语 Included in the trim controls 提到句首作表语，主谓完全倒装，are 在主语之前。 要点：这类倒装常见于技术说明文的列举句。识别方法是句首出现过去分词、后面紧跟 be 动词和名词主语；翻译时还原成“配平操纵装置包括……”。

② 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：Trim tabs are small airfoils recessed into the trailing edges of the primary control surfaces. 拆解：主干 Trim tabs are small airfoils，系表结构；recessed into the trailing edges of the primary control surfaces 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 airfoils，说明配平片的安装方式（凹陷嵌入）。 要点：recessed 与 airfoils 是被动关系（被嵌入），故用过去分词；into 强调嵌入到后缘内部，而不是贴在表面。the trailing edges 用复数，因为每个主操纵面各有一条后缘。

③ 表语为不定式 + enable sb. to do + which 定语从句 + without doing

原句：Their purpose is to enable the pilot to trim out any unbalanced condition which may exist during flight, without exerting any pressure on the primary controls. 拆解：主干 Their purpose is to enable the pilot to trim out...，不定式作表语；enable 后接宾语 the pilot 和不定式宾补 to trim out；which may exist during flight 为定语从句修饰 condition；without exerting... 为伴随/条件状语，修饰 trim out 这一动作。 要点：enable sb. to do 中 to 不可省；without 后接动名词，逻辑主语仍是 the pilot（飞行员不必施力）。这句解释了配平片与主操纵装置的分工。

④ only 置于句首修饰主语 + in response to 嵌套 of 短语

原句：Only the servo tab moves in response to movement by the pilot of the primary flight controls. 拆解：Only 修饰主语 the servo tab，置于句首但不引起倒装；谓语 moves；in response to movement 为方式/原因状语，by the pilot 说明是谁的移动，of the primary flight controls 再修饰 movement，层层后置。 要点：only 修饰主语时句子不倒装（区别于 only + 状语置于句首的情况）。of the primary flight controls 管的是 movement，即“飞行员对主飞行操纵装置的移动”，不是“飞行员的操纵装置”。

⑤ how 引导的宾语从句中含并列被动谓语

原句：Figure 2-13 indicates how each trim tab is hinged to its parent primary control surface, but is operated by an independent control. 拆解：主句 Figure 2-13 indicates，how 引导宾语从句；从句主语 each trim tab，两个并列的被动谓语 is hinged to... 与 but is operated by...，后者省略了相同的主语。 要点：but 连接的第二个谓语与第一个共用主语 each trim tab，形成“铰接在同一操纵面上、却由独立机构驱动”的对照，这正是配平片的工作原理要点。
