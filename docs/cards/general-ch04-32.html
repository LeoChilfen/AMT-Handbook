Lines and Their Meanings

1. 原文

Every drawing is composed of lines. Lines mark the boundaries, edges, and intersection of surfaces. Lines are used to show dimensions and hidden surfaces and to indicate centers. Obviously, if the same kind of line is used to show these variations, a drawing becomes a meaningless collection of lines. For this reason, various kinds of standardized lines are used on aircraft drawings. [Figure 4-24] Examples of correct line uses are shown in Figure 4-25.

Most drawings use three widths, or intensities, of lines:

thin, medium, or thick. These lines may vary somewhat on different drawings, but there is a noticeable difference between a thin and a thick line, with the width of the medium line somewhere between the two.

2. 中文翻译

任何图样都由线条构成。线条标示表面的边界、棱边和交线。线条用来表示尺寸和不可见表面，也用来指示中心。显然，如果同一种线条被用来表示这些不同的含义，图样就成了一堆毫无意义的线条。因此，航空器图样上使用各种标准化线型。正确用线的示例见图 4-25。

大多数图样使用三种线宽（或称线条粗细）：

即细、中、粗。这些线宽在不同图样上可能略有出入，但细线与粗线之间差别明显，中线的宽度则介于两者之间。

4. 语法解析

① Lines are used to show dimensions and hidden surfaces and to indicate centers（被动 + 并列目的不定式）

原句：Lines are used to show dimensions and hidden surfaces and to indicate centers.

拆解：被动 are used + 两个并列的目的不定式 to show … and to indicate …；第一个不定式后接两个并列宾语 dimensions、hidden surfaces。

要点：两个目的不定式并列时，第二个 to 通常保留，以免与前面并列的宾语混淆。be used to do（被用来做）与 used to do（过去常常）、be used to doing（习惯于）三者在形式上接近，靠 be 和 to 后的成分区分。

② if the same kind of line is used …, a drawing becomes a meaningless collection of lines（真实条件句 + become 作系动词）

原句：Obviously, if the same kind of line is used to show these variations, a drawing becomes a meaningless collection of lines.

拆解：Obviously 是评注性状语，修饰整句；if 引导真实条件从句，从句谓语为被动 is used to show；主句 a drawing becomes + 名词短语 a meaningless collection of lines，become 在此是系动词。

要点：become / remain / seem 等系动词后接名词或形容词作表语。条件从句用一般现在时表将来，主句不用 will。the same kind of line 用单数，强调「同一种线型」这一类别。

③ there is a noticeable difference …, with the width of the medium line somewhere between the two（there be + with 复合结构省略分词）

原句：These lines may vary somewhat on different drawings, but there is a noticeable difference between a thin and a thick line, with the width of the medium line somewhere between the two.

拆解：but 连接两个并列分句，第二分句为 there be 句型；between a thin and a thick line 是范围状语；with the width of the medium line somewhere between the two 是 with 复合结构，相当于 being somewhere between the two，being 被省略。

要点：with 复合结构中若表状态而非动作，可省略 being，如 with the door open。somewhat 是程度副词，修饰 vary 表示「略有」。the two 指前面提到的细线与粗线两种线宽，是「the + 数词」的指代用法。
