FAA Form 337—Major Repair and Alteration

通用 Ch 02 · FAA Form 337—Major Repair and Alteration · 原文 PDF 第 88 页

1. 原文

Refer to the current issue of AC 43.9-1, Instructions for Completion of FAA Form 337 for help completing FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration (Airframe, Powerplant, Propeller, or Appliance). [Figure 2-22]

As the name clearly states, this form is to be used whenever major repairs or alterations are accomplished on an aircraft.

The only exception would be that 14 CFR part 43, Appendix B, allows for a certificated repair station to RTS an aircraft after a major repair by using a signed and dated work order and a signed maintenance release.

• Information in item 1 comes directly from the aircraft dataplate, except for the tail number. That is to be compared to the aircraft registration form.

• Information in item 2 reflects the name and address listed on AC Form 8050-3, Certificate of Registration.

• Item 3 is used when there is no existing approved data for the intended repair or alteration. In that case, the technician can request that the local FSDO Principal Maintenance Inspector (PMI) review the data and then grant a field approval, shown by completing and signing this area. In many cases, this block is blank because the technician has found, used, and made reference to data already approved by the FAA.

• Item 4—If the repair or alteration is being done to the aircraft airframe, no entry is required since the data is identical to that in item 1. However, if the repair or alteration is being done to an engine, a propeller, or other appliance, entries must include the appropriate make, model, and serial number information.

• Item 5 should have “X” marked in either the “Repair” or the “Alteration” column.

• Item 6—Enter appropriate data as specified and check the proper box in B. The technician is encouraged to carefully read the preprinted statement in subparagraph D prior to signing this section.

• Item 7 must be completed by the IA or authorized individual from the repair station.

• Item 8 (on the reverse side) is for the description of the work accomplished. It must include a reference to the approved data used to conduct the required

The form must be completed at least in duplicate, with the original provided to the owner/operator and a copy to the local FSDO within 48 hours of completing the maintenance and RTS. If the FAA Form 337 is used to document additional fuel tanks in the cabin or cargo, then an additional copy must be signed and in the aircraft at all times. Maintenance facilities and mechanics are encouraged to make a copy for their own records.

2. 中文翻译

关于如何填写 FAA 表格 337《重大修理与改装（机身、动力装置、螺旋桨或机载设备）》，请参阅现行版 AC 43.9-1《FAA 表格 337 填写说明》。

顾名思义，凡在航空器上实施了重大修理或改装，就应使用该表格。

唯一的例外是：14 CFR 第 43 部附录 B 允许持证修理站在完成重大修理后，改用一份签署并注明日期的工作单，加一份签署的维修放行单来恢复使用（RTS）。

• 第 1 项的信息直接取自航空器数据铭牌，唯独尾号例外；尾号须与航空器登记证核对。

• 第 2 项填写 AC 表格 8050-3《登记证》上载明的姓名（名称）与地址。

• 第 3 项用于拟实施的修理或改装尚无现成批准数据的情形。此时技师可请当地飞行标准地区办公室（FSDO）的主管维修检查员（PMI）审核数据，然后签署批准「现场批准」，方式就是填写并签署该区域。多数情况下此栏为空，因为技师已经找到、使用了 FAA 已批准的数据并加以引用。

• 第 4 项——如果修理或改装是对航空器机身实施的，则无需填写，因为数据与第 1 项相同。但如果修理或改装是对发动机、螺旋桨或其他机载设备实施的，则必须填写相应的制造厂、型号和序号信息。

• 第 5 项应在「修理」或「改装」栏之一标注「X」。

• 第 6 项——按要求填入相应数据，并在 B 中勾选正确的方框。建议技师在签署本节之前仔细阅读 D 小节中预先印制的声明。

• 第 7 项必须由授权检查员（IA）或修理站的授权人员填写。

• 第 8 项（位于表格背面）用于描述所完成的工作，必须包含对实施该项工作所用批准数据的引用（原文此处被截断）。

该表至少一式两份：在维修完成并恢复使用后 48 小时内，正本交机主/运营人，副本交当地 FSDO。如果 FAA 表格 337 用于记录客舱或货舱中的附加燃油箱，则还必须另签一份副本并始终随航空器携带。鼓励维修单位和维修技师自留一份副本存档。

4. 语法解析

① be to be used + whenever 从句：规章里的「须」

原句：As the name clearly states, this form is to be used whenever major repairs or alterations are accomplished on an aircraft.

拆解：As the name clearly states 是 as 引导的方式状语从句（意为「顾名思义」）；主句 this form is to be used 中 be + 不定式表示「应当、须」；whenever 引导时间状语从句，从句用被动 are accomplished。

要点：be to + 动词原形在规章类文本里表示强制，语气接近 must 但更书面。注意 is to be used 是「被使用」，不能写成 is to use。同类结构：is to be completed、is to be retained。

② 并列谓语共用情态动词

原句：If the FAA Form 337 is used to document additional fuel tanks in the cabin or cargo, then an additional copy must be signed and in the aircraft at all times.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，从句为被动 is used to document；主句由 then 引出，谓语是两个并列成分 must be signed 与 must be in the aircraft，后者省略了重复的 must。

要点：读长句时要把省略的 must 补回去——第二个谓语是「本副本必须始终在航空器上」，不是「副本在航空器上」这种陈述。翻译时须补齐「必须」二字，否则会丢掉强制含义。

③ because 从句 + 现在完成时 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：In many cases, this block is blank because the technician has found, used, and made reference to data already approved by the FAA.

拆解：In many cases 是状语；主句 this block is blank；because 引导原因状语从句，从句谓语是三个并列的现在完成时 has found、has used、has made reference to，共用助动词 has；already approved by the FAA 是过去分词短语作 data 的后置定语，相当于 which was already approved by the FAA。

要点：现在完成时在此表示「已经做完、且对眼下有影响」——正因为已有批准数据可用，第 3 项才空着。make reference to 是「引用、参阅」的固定搭配，维修记录里也常写 referenced data。
