Safety Around Hazardous Materials

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Hazardous Materials · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

1. 原文

Material safety diamonds are important with regard to shop safety. These diamond-shaped labels are a simple and quick way to determine the risk of hazardous material within the associated container and, if used properly with the tags, indicate what personal safety equipment to use. The most observable portion of the Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) (formerly known as Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)) label is the risk diamond. It is a four-color segmented diamond that represents flammability (red), reactivity (yellow), health (blue), and special hazard (white). In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4. Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous. The special hazard segment contains a word or abbreviation to represent the specific hazard. Some examples are RAD for radiation, ALK for alkali materials, Acid for acidic materials, and CARC for carcinogenic materials. The letter W with a line through it stands for high reactivity to water. [Figure 1-2]

The SDS is a more detailed version of the chemical safety issues. These forms have the detailed breakdown of the chemicals, including formulas and action to take if personnel come in contact with the chemicals. All sheets have the same information requirements; however, the exact location of the information on the sheet may vary depending on the SDS manufacturer. These forms are necessary for a safe shop that meets all the requirements of the governing safety body, the U.S. Department of Labor Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA).

2. 中文翻译

材料安全菱形标识对车间安全十分重要。这些菱形标签是判断容器内危险材料风险的一种简单快捷的方法；若与标牌配合使用得当，还能指示应当使用哪些个人安全防护装备。安全数据表（SDS，旧称材料安全数据表 MSDS）标识中最显眼的部分是风险菱形。它是一个四色分区的菱形，分别代表可燃性（红色）、反应性（黄色）、健康（蓝色）和特殊危害（白色）。在可燃性、反应性和健康三个区块中，标有 0 到 4 的数字。0 表示对使用者几乎无危害或没有危害，而 4 表示该材料危害很大。特殊危害区含有一个词或缩写，用以表示特定的危害。例如 RAD 表示辐射，ALK 表示碱性材料，Acid 表示酸性材料，CARC 表示致癌材料。带一条斜线的字母 W 表示对水具有高反应性。[Figure 1-2]

安全数据表（SDS）是化学品安全问题更详细的版本。这类表格详细列出化学品的成分，包括化学式，以及人员接触化学品后应采取的措施。所有 SDS 表格所要求的信息内容都相同；但是信息在表格上的具体位置可能因 SDS 生产厂商不同而有所差异。这些表格是使车间达到主管安全机构——美国劳工部职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）——全部要求所必需的。

4. 语法解析

① while 引导的对比 + that 宾语从句

原句：Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous. 拆解：前半句 Zero represents little or no hazard to the user 为主谓宾结构；while 是并列连词，表对比「而」；4 means that… 中 that 引导宾语从句，从句内 the material is very hazardous 为主系表。 要点：while 在这里不是「当……时」，而是对比连词，相当于 whereas。技术文本常用它把两个等级、两种状态并排对照。注意 little or no 这种「否定叠加」的固定说法，意为「几乎没有或完全没有」。

② that 定语从句 + 并列宾语

原句：It is a four-color segmented diamond that represents flammability (red), reactivity (yellow), health (blue), and special hazard (white). 拆解：It 指代上文的 risk diamond；主句为主系表 It is a four-color segmented diamond；that represents… 是定语从句修饰 diamond，从句谓语 represents 带四个并列宾语 flammability、reactivity、health、special hazard，括号里的颜色是同位补充。 要点：that 引导限制性定语从句，紧跟先行词 diamond。四个并列宾语之间用逗号加 and 收尾，括号内容只是注释，不改变句子主干。汉译时定语从句常拆成「它是一个……的菱形，分别代表……」这样的独立分句。

③ 分号 + however 连接副词 + depend on 搭配

原句：All sheets have the same information requirements; however, the exact location of the information on the sheet may vary depending on the SDS manufacturer. 拆解：分号连接两个独立分句；however 是连接副词，不能像 but 那样直接连接两个分句，所以前面用分号、后面加逗号；后半句主语 the exact location of the information on the sheet 为「名词 + of 短语 + on 短语」的多层后置定语；depending on… 是现在分词短语作状语。 要点：易错点在标点——however 连接两个句子时必须用「; however,」或另起一句，写成「, however,」会造成逗号粘连（comma splice）。vary depending on 是固定搭配，意为「随……而不同」。

④ including 介词短语 + 不定式后置定语 + if 条件从句

原句：These forms have the detailed breakdown of the chemicals, including formulas and action to take if personnel come in contact with the chemicals. 拆解：主句 These forms have the detailed breakdown of the chemicals 为主谓宾；including… 是介词短语（含现在分词形式）作补充说明，相当于「其中包括」；action to take 中不定式 to take 作后置定语修饰 action；if personnel come in contact with… 是条件状语从句，修饰 to take 这个动作。 要点：including 不是分词作状语，而是介词化的补充说明成分，可换成 such as。action to take 是「名词 + 不定式」表示「要采取的动作」，不定式与被修饰名词是动宾关系（take action）。条件从句用一般现在时表将来，是主将从现的典型。
