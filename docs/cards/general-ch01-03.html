Fire Safety

通用 Ch 01 · Fire Safety · 原文 PDF 第 23 页

1. 原文

Anytime current flows, whether during generation or transmission, a by-product is heat. The greater the current flow, the greater the amount of heat created. When this heat becomes too great, protective coatings on wiring and other electrical devices can melt, causing shorting. That in turn leads to more current flow and greater heat. This heat can become so great that metals can melt, liquids vaporize, and flammable substances ignite.

An important factor in preventing electrical fires is to keep the area around electrical work or electrical equipment clean, uncluttered, and free of all unnecessary flammable substances. Ensure that all power cords, wires, and lines are free of kinks and bends that can damage the wire. Never place wires or cords where they may be walked on or run over by other equipment. When several wires inside a power cord are broken, the current passing through the remaining wires increases. This generates more heat than the insulation coatings on the wire are designed to withstand and can lead to a fire. Closely monitor the condition of electrical equipment. Repair or replace damaged equipment before further use.

2. 中文翻译

只要有电流流动，无论在发电还是在输电过程中，都会产生一种副产物——热。电流越大，产生的热量越多。当这种热量过大时，导线及其他电气装置上的防护涂层会熔化，从而造成短路。这又会导致更大的电流和更高的温度。热量可能大到使金属熔化、液体汽化、可燃物质着火。

防止电气火灾的一个重要因素是：保持电气作业处或电气设备周围的区域清洁、无杂物，并清除一切不必要的可燃物质。确保所有电源线、导线和线路没有会损伤导线的扭结和弯折。绝不要把导线或电源线放在可能被踩踏或被其他设备碾过的地方。当一根电源线内有多根导线断裂时，通过其余导线的电流会增大。这产生的热量超过导线绝缘涂层的设计承受能力，可能引发火灾。密切监视电气设备的状况。在继续使用之前，修复或更换已损坏的设备。

4. 语法解析

① the + 比较级，the + 比较级（越……越……）

原句：The greater the current flow, the greater the amount of heat created. 拆解：前半 The greater the current flow 为程度状语从句（省略引导词），后半 the greater the amount of heat created 为主句；created 为过去分词后置定语，修饰 heat（= that is created）。 要点：“the + 比较级 …, the + 比较级 …”表示“越……越……”，前后两个 the 都不能省略，也不能省成 greater… greater…。后半句常省略谓语（本句省略 is）：the greater the amount of heat (is) created。created 是分词作定语，不是谓语。

② 现在分词短语作结果状语

原句：When this heat becomes too great, protective coatings on wiring and other electrical devices can melt, causing shorting. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句 + 主句 protective coatings… can melt + 现在分词短语 causing shorting 作结果状语。 要点：现在分词作结果状语，表示主句动作自然引发的结果，可改写成 which causes shorting。易错点：causing 的逻辑主语是前面整件事（涂层熔化），不是 coatings 主动“去导致”；另外 on wiring and other electrical devices 是修饰 coatings 的后置定语，不要读成 on wiring 与 other devices 两个并列成分。

③ That 指代前句 + in turn 承接因果链

原句：That in turn leads to more current flow and greater heat. 拆解：主语 That（指代上句“涂层熔化造成短路”这一事实）+ 插入语 in turn + 谓语 leads to + 两个并列宾语 more current flow 与 greater heat。 要点：that 在此指代前文整句内容，不是某个名词，翻译成“这（又）……”。in turn 意为“进而、反过来”，用来串起技术文本里的因果链；lead to 后接名词或动名词，不能接 that 从句（要接从句须用 lead to the result that…）。

④ 现在分词后置定语 + 不及物动词 increase

原句：When several wires inside a power cord are broken, the current passing through the remaining wires increases. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句（inside a power cord 为介词短语后置定语修饰 wires）+ 主句主语 the current + 现在分词短语 passing through the remaining wires 作后置定语 + 谓语 increases（不及物）。 要点：现在分词短语紧贴名词后作定语，等于 that passes through…，它与主句谓语 increases 是两回事，读句时先划掉分词短语再找谓语。increase 此处为不及物动词“增大”，后面不接宾语；remaining 意为“其余的、剩下的”。
