Fire Safety

通用 Ch 01 · Fire Safety · 原文 PDF 第 23 页

1. 原文

Anytime current flows, whether during generation or transmission, a by-product is heat. The greater the current flow, the greater the amount of heat created. When this heat becomes too great, protective coatings on wiring and other electrical devices can melt, causing shorting. That in turn leads to more current flow and greater heat. This heat can become so great that metals can melt, liquids vaporize, and flammable substances ignite.

An important factor in preventing electrical fires is to keep the area around electrical work or electrical equipment

clean, uncluttered, and free of all unnecessary flammable substances. Ensure that all power cords, wires, and lines are free of kinks and bends that can damage the wire. Never place wires or cords where they may be walked on or run over by other equipment. When several wires inside a power cord are broken, the current passing through the remaining wires increases. This generates more heat than the insulation coatings on the wire are designed to withstand and can lead to a fire. Closely monitor the condition of electrical equipment. Repair or replace damaged equipment before further use.

2. 中文翻译

只要有电流流动，无论是在发电还是在输电过程中，都会产生一种副产品——热。电流越大，产生的热量就越多。当热量过大时，导线及其他电气设备上的防护涂层会熔化，从而造成短路。短路反过来又引起更大的电流和更多的热量。这种热量可能大到使金属熔化、液体汽化、可燃物质被引燃的程度。

预防电气火灾的一个重要因素，是保持电气作业区或电气设备周围区域清洁、不杂乱，并且不存放任何不必要的可燃物质。要确保所有电源线、导线和线路没有会损伤导线的扭结和弯折。绝不要把导线或软线放在可能被踩踏或被其他设备碾过的地方。当一根电源线内部的多根导线断裂时，通过剩余导线的电流就会增大。这产生的热量超出导线绝缘层的设计承受能力，可能导致火灾。要密切监视电气设备的状况。损坏的设备必须在继续使用之前修复或更换。

4. 语法解析

① The greater the current flow, the greater the amount of heat created（the + 比较级，the + 比较级）

原句：The greater the current flow, the greater the amount of heat created.

拆解：两个「the + 比较级」分句构成「越……就越……」结构；前句 The greater the current flow 相当于条件，后句 The greater the amount of heat created 中 created 是过去分词，作后置定语修饰 heat。

要点：这种句型前后都要用 the，且比较级提前、谓语常省略（本句两句都省略了 is）。科技文中用来表达正相关关系非常经济，可迁移为 The higher the temperature, the faster the reaction。注意不要写成 The greater current flow, the greater heat，the 和比较级缺一不可。

② … can melt, causing shorting（现在分词短语作结果状语）

原句：When this heat becomes too great, protective coatings on wiring and other electrical devices can melt, causing shorting.

拆解：When 引导时间状语从句；主句主语 protective coatings，谓语 can melt；causing shorting 是现在分词短语作结果状语，逻辑主语就是主句主语（涂层熔化这件事）。

要点：分词作状语时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致；此处「造成短路」的正是「熔化」这一动作，所以用主动形式的 causing，而不是 caused。表结果时还可用 thus / thereby + 分词替换，含义相同但更书面。

③ This heat can become so great that …（so … that 结果状语从句）

原句：This heat can become so great that metals can melt, liquids vaporize, and flammable substances ignite.

拆解：so + 形容词 great + that 引导结果状语从句；从句内是三个并列的主谓结构 metals can melt、liquids vaporize、flammable substances ignite，后两个承前省略了情态动词 can。

要点：so … that 与 such … that 的分工是：so 修饰形容词或副词，such 修饰名词短语。从句中省略相同的情态动词、只保留实义动词是英语的常用压缩手段，翻译时要补回「能/会」，否则中文读起来像陈述事实而不是描述可能性。

④ This generates more heat than the insulation coatings … are designed to withstand（more … than 引导比较从句）

原句：This generates more heat than the insulation coatings on the wire are designed to withstand and can lead to a fire.

拆解：主句 This generates more heat；than 引导比较从句 the insulation coatings on the wire are designed to withstand，withstand 的宾语就是前面的 more heat（比较对象在从句中缺位）；and can lead to a fire 与 generates 并列，共用主语 This。

要点：than 后面接的是完整从句而不是名词短语，这是比较结构最常见的误读点——不要译成「比绝缘层」，而要还原成「超出绝缘层所能承受的热量」。withstand 后省略了宾语 heat，这种「比较对象在从句中留空」的用法在技术文中极多。
