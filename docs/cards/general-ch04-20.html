1. 原文

Notes are added to drawings for various reasons. Some of these notes refer to methods of attachment or construction. Others give alternatives, so that the drawing can be used for different styles of the same object. Still others list modifications that are available. Notes may be found alongside the item that they refer to. If the notes are lengthy, they may be placed elsewhere on the drawing and identified by letters or numbers.

Notes are used only when the information cannot be conveyed in the conventional manner or when it is desirable to avoid crowding the drawing. Figure 4-4E illustrates one method of depicting notes.

When the note refers to a specific part, a light line with an arrowhead leads from the note to the part. If it applies to more than one part, the note is worded to eliminate ambiguity as to the parts it pertains to. If there are several notes, they are generally grouped together and numbered consecutively.

2. 中文翻译

图纸上加注附注（notes）有各种原因。有些附注说明连接方法或构造方法；有些给出可选方案，使同一张图纸能用于同一物件的不同型式；还有些列出可以采用的改装。附注可以写在它所针对的项目旁边。如果附注较长，也可以放在图纸上的其他位置，并用字母或数字加以标识。

只有在信息无法用常规方式表达，或需要避免图面过于拥挤时，才使用附注。图 4-4E 表示了一种加注附注的方法。

当附注针对某一特定零件时，用一条带箭头的细线从附注引到该零件。如果附注适用于一个以上零件，则其措辞要消除关于所指零件的歧义。如果附注有几条，通常把它们集中在一起并连续编号。

4. 语法解析

① Notes are used only when … or when it is desirable to avoid crowding the drawing（only when 双条件 + 形式主语 it）

原句：Notes are used only when the information cannot be conveyed in the conventional manner or when it is desirable to avoid crowding the drawing.

拆解：主句 Notes are used 为被动；only 限定后面两个由 or 连接的并列 when 从句；第一从句含被动 cannot be conveyed；第二从句用 it 作形式主语，真正主语是 to avoid crowding the drawing。

要点：only when 意为「只有在……时才」，两个 when 从句用 or 连接，译作「或者」。it is desirable to do 中 it 是形式主语，不能换成 that。avoid 后必须接动名词，avoid to crowd 是典型错误；同类动词还有 consider、suggest、finish。

② Some of these notes refer to methods of attachment or construction（主语的部分限定 + refer to）

原句：Some of these notes refer to methods of attachment or construction.

拆解：主语 Some of these notes；谓语 refer to；宾语 methods of attachment or construction，两个 of 短语并列修饰 methods。

要点：Some of these notes 作主语时谓语用复数（refer），因为 some 指代复数名词。refer to 在此意为「涉及、指的是」，与本册第 15 节 refer sb to sth「让某人参见」用法不同，注意按上下文判断。

③ the note is worded to eliminate ambiguity as to the parts it pertains to（be worded to + as to + 句末介词）

原句：If it applies to more than one part, the note is worded to eliminate ambiguity as to the parts it pertains to.

拆解：If 引导条件从句，it 指 the note，applies to 意为「适用于」；主句 the note is worded 为被动，to eliminate ambiguity 是目的不定式；as to the parts (that) it pertains to 是介词短语，内含省略关系词的定语从句。

要点：be worded 意为「措辞为、行文表述为」，word 在此作动词。as to 引出「关于……」的限定，比 about 正式。pertain to 的 to 是介词，定语从句中关系词作介词宾语时可省略并把介词留在句尾（the parts it pertains to），这是技术英语的常态，翻译时须译回「它所指的那些零件」。
