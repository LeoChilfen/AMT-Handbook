Converting Decimal Numbers to Fractions

1. 原文

To change a decimal number to a fraction, “read” the decimal out loud, and then write it into a fraction just as it is read as shown below.

Example: One oversized rivet has a diameter of 0.52 inches.

Convert 0.52 to a fraction. The decimal 0.52 is read as “fifty-two hundredths.” 0.52 = 52 “fifty-two” 100 “hundredths” In the above fraction of 52⁄100, we can divide 4 into each number resulting in a fraction of 13⁄25.

A dimension often appears in a maintenance manual or on a blueprint as a decimal instead of a fraction. To use the dimension, it may need to be converted to a fraction. An aviation mechanic frequently uses a steel rule that is calibrated in units of 1⁄64 of an inch. To change a decimal to the nearest equivalent common fraction, multiply the decimal by 64. The product of the decimal and 64 is the numerator of the fraction and 64 is the denominator. Reduce the fraction, if needed.

Example: The width of a hex head bolt is 0.3123 inches.

Convert the decimal 0.3123 to a common fraction to decide which socket would be the best fit for the bolt head. First, multiply the 0.3123 decimal by 64:

0.3123 × 64 = 19.9872 Next, round the product to the nearest whole number:

Use this whole number (20) as the numerator and 64 as the denominator: 20⁄64.

Now, reduce 20⁄64 to 5⁄16 as 4 is common to both the numerator and denominator. Therefore, the correct socket would be the 5⁄16 inch socket (20⁄64 reduced).

Example: When accurate holes of uniform diameter are required for aircraft structures, they are first drilled approximately 1⁄64 inch undersized and then reamed to the final desired diameter. What size drill bit should be selected for the undersized hole if the final hole is reamed to a diameter of 0.763 inches? First, multiply the 0.763 decimal by 64.

0.763 × 64 = 48.832 Next, round the product to the nearest whole number:

Use this number (49) as the numerator and 64 as the denominator: 49⁄64 is the closest fraction to the final reaming diameter of 0.763 inches. To determine the drill size for the initial undersized hole, subtract 1⁄64 inch from the finished hole size.

49 – 1 = 48 = 3 64 64 64 4 Therefore, a 3⁄4-inch drill bit should be used for the initial undersized holes.

2. 中文翻译

要把小数化为分数，就“读出”这个小数，然后按读出的说法把它写成分数，如下所示。

例：某个尺寸过大的铆钉直径为 0.52 英寸。

把 0.52 化为分数。小数 0.52 读作“五十二个百分之一（fifty-two hundredths）”。0.52 = 52 “fifty-two” 100 “hundredths” 在上面的分数 52⁄100 中，可以对分子和分母都除以 4，得到分数 13⁄25。

尺寸在维修手册或蓝图上常常以小数而非分数出现。要使用该尺寸，可能需要把它换算成分数。航空维修人员经常使用以 1⁄64 英寸为刻度单位的钢直尺。要把小数换算成最接近的常用分数，就用 64 乘该小数。该小数与 64 的积作分数的分子，64 作分母。如有必要，再把分数约分。

例：某六角头螺栓的头部宽度为 0.3123 英寸。

把小数 0.3123 化为常用分数，以确定哪种套筒与螺栓头最配合。首先，用 64 乘小数 0.3123：

0.3123 × 64 = 19.9872 接着，把积四舍五入到最接近的整数：

以这个整数（20）作分子、64 作分母：20⁄64。

现在把 20⁄64 约为 5⁄16，因为 4 是分子和分母的公因数。因此，正确的套筒应为 5⁄16 英寸套筒（即约分后的 20⁄64）。

例：当航空器结构上需要直径一致且精确的孔时，先钻出比最终直径小约 1⁄64 英寸的孔，再铰到所需的最终直径。若最终铰成的孔径为 0.763 英寸，钻这个偏小的孔应选用多大的钻头？首先，用 64 乘小数 0.763。

0.763 × 64 = 48.832 接着，把积四舍五入到最接近的整数：

以这个数（49）作分子、64 作分母：49⁄64 是最接近最终铰孔直径 0.763 英寸的分数。要确定初钻偏小孔的钻头尺寸，就从完工孔径中减去 1⁄64 英寸。

49 – 1 = 48 = 3 64 64 64 4 因此，初钻偏小孔时应使用 3⁄4 英寸钻头。

4. 语法解析

① just as it is read / as shown below（方式从句 + 过去分词短语）

原句：To change a decimal number to a fraction, “read” the decimal out loud, and then write it into a fraction just as it is read as shown below.

拆解：To change a decimal number to a fraction 为目的状语；主句为祈使句，“read” ... out loud 与 write it into a fraction 两个动作并列；just as it is read 是方式状语从句，it 回指 the decimal；as shown below 是过去分词短语作方式补充。

要点：just as 表“正如、按照”，后接完整从句；as shown below 中 as 是关系代词性质，完整形式为 as is shown below，省略 is 后成为固定插入语。write it into a fraction 用介词 into 表“转换成”；read out loud 是“出声念”，out loud 不能换成 loudly（后者指音量大）。

② a steel rule that is calibrated in units of 1⁄64 of an inch（定语从句 + 被动）

原句：An aviation mechanic frequently uses a steel rule that is calibrated in units of 1⁄64 of an inch.

拆解：主句 An aviation mechanic frequently uses a steel rule；that 引导定语从句修饰 a steel rule，从句谓语 is calibrated 为被动，in units of 1⁄64 of an inch 说明刻度单位。

要点：be calibrated in units of ... 意为“以……为单位刻度”，介词用 in；calibrate 在机务语境里是“校准”，此处指直尺的刻度标定。that 从句修饰物时可用 which 替换，但紧接逗号引导非限制性定语从句时只能用 which。frequently 是频率副词，位置在主谓之间。

③ they are first drilled … and then reamed to …（when 从句 + 并列被动 + 主语补足语）

原句：When accurate holes of uniform diameter are required for aircraft structures, they are first drilled approximately 1⁄64 inch undersized and then reamed to the final desired diameter.

拆解：When 引导时间／条件状语从句，主语 accurate holes of uniform diameter，谓语 are required 为被动；主句 they are first drilled ... and then reamed ... 两个过去分词共用一个 are；undersized 是主语补足语，说明钻孔后的状态；to the final desired diameter 是 reamed 的结果状语。

要点：并列被动句中重复的 be 动词可只保留第一个（are drilled ... and reamed），中文要把“钻”和“铰”两个动作都译出。undersized 是形容词作补足语，跟在 drilled 后说明尺寸状态，不能用 undersize 名词。ream to + 尺寸 表示“铰到某一尺寸”，介词 to 表结果，与 cut to length、grind to size 同一框架。
