Types of Fuel and Identification

1. 原文

Two types of aviation fuel in general use are aviation gasoline, also known as A VGAS, and turbine fuel, also known as JET A fuel.

Aviation gasoline (A VGAS) is used in reciprocating engine aircraft. Currently, there are three grades of fuel in general use: 80/87, 100/130, and 100LL (low lead). A fourth grade, 115/145, is in limited use in the large reciprocating-engine aircraft. The two numbers indicate the lean mixture and rich mixture octane rating numbers of the specific fuel. In other words, with 80/87 A VGAS, the 80 is the lean mixture rating and 87 is the rich mixture rating number. To avoid confusing the types of A VGAS, it is generally identified as grade 80, 100, 100LL, or 115. A VGAS can also be identified by a color code. The color of the fuel needs to match the color band on piping and fueling equipment. [Figure 1-31]

Turbine fuel/jet fuel is used to power turbojet and turbo- shaft engines. Three types of turbine fuel generally used in civilian aviation are JET A and JET A-1, made from kerosene, and JET B, a blend of kerosene and A VGAS.

While jet fuel is identified by the color black on piping and fueling equipment, the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored.

Before mixing A VGAS and turbine fuel, refer to the Type Certificate Data Sheet for the respective powerplant. Adding jet fuel to A VGAS causes a decrease in the power developed by the engine and could cause damage to the engine (through detonation) and loss of life. Adding A VGAS to jet fuel can cause lead deposits in the turbine engine and can lead to reduced service life.

2. 中文翻译

目前普遍使用的航空燃油有两类：航空汽油（又称 AVGAS）和涡轮燃油（又称 JET A 燃油）。

航空汽油（AVGAS）用于活塞式发动机航空器。目前普遍使用的有三个牌号：80/87、100/130 和 100LL（低铅）。第四个牌号 115/145 在大型活塞发动机航空器上少量使用。两个数字分别表示该燃油的贫油混合气辛烷值和富油混合气辛烷值。换言之，对 80/87 的 AVGAS 来说，80 是贫油混合气评级，87 是富油混合气评级。为避免混淆 AVGAS 的类型，通常按牌号 80、100、100LL 或 115 来标识。AVGAS 还可以用颜色代码识别。燃油的颜色必须与管路及加油设备上的色带一致。

涡轮燃油／喷气燃油用于驱动涡轮喷气发动机和涡轮轴发动机。民用航空中普遍使用的涡轮燃油有三种：由煤油制得的 JET A 和 JET A-1，以及由煤油与 AVGAS 混合而成的 JET B。

喷气燃油在管路和加油设备上以黑色标识，而喷气燃油本身的实际颜色可以是无色或草黄色。

在混合 AVGAS 与涡轮燃油之前，应查阅相应动力装置的型号合格证数据单。向 AVGAS 中加入喷气燃油会降低发动机输出的功率，并可能（因爆震）损坏发动机，甚至造成人员伤亡。向喷气燃油中加入 AVGAS 会在涡轮发动机内形成铅沉积，并可能导致使用寿命缩短。

4. 语法解析

① aviation gasoline, also known as A VGAS, and turbine fuel …（过去分词短语作同位语）

原句：Two types of aviation fuel in general use are aviation gasoline, also known as A VGAS, and turbine fuel, also known as JET A fuel.

拆解：主语 Two types of aviation fuel in general use（in general use 为后置定语）；系动词 are；表语是两个并列名词短语 aviation gasoline 与 turbine fuel，各带 also known as 引导的同位说明。

要点：also known as（缩写 a.k.a.）是过去分词短语作同位语，译作「又称、亦称」。两个表语被插入成分隔开，读句时先抓 are … and … 两个中心词，再补同位成分。

② To avoid confusing …, it is generally identified as …（目的不定式 + avoid doing）

原句：To avoid confusing the types of A VGAS, it is generally identified as grade 80, 100, 100LL, or 115.

拆解：To avoid confusing … 为目的状语（不定式），avoid 后接动名词 confusing；主句 it is identified as … 为被动，it 指代前文的 AVGAS，不是形式主语。

要点：avoid 后只能接 -ing（avoid to confuse 是错的）。to avoid 置于句首表目的，可译为「为避免……」。as 在此是「作为……来标识」，不是「因为」。

③ While jet fuel is identified by the color black …（while 表对比）

原句：While jet fuel is identified by the color black on piping and fueling equipment, the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored.

拆解：While 引导对比状语从句（「虽然、而」），is identified by the color black 为被动；主句主语 the actual color of jet fuel，谓语 can be clear or straw colored 为系表结构。

要点：技术文本里 while 常表对比而非时间。identify by 是「以……作为标识」，标识色（黑）与实际油色（无色或草黄）是两回事，别混。straw colored 指「草黄色」，不是直译的「稻草色」。

④ Adding jet fuel to A VGAS causes a decrease …（动名词作主语）

原句：Adding jet fuel to A VGAS causes a decrease in the power developed by the engine and could cause damage to the engine (through detonation) and loss of life.

拆解：主语是动名词短语 Adding jet fuel to A VGAS；谓语为并列的 causes 与 could cause；a decrease in the power developed by the engine 中 developed by the engine 为过去分词短语作后置定语；damage to the engine 与 loss of life 并列作 cause 的宾语。

要点：动名词短语作主语时谓语用第三人称单数（causes）。a decrease in …（在……方面下降）不同于 decrease of；cause 的两个宾语 damage、loss 前分别带 to 与无 to，注意搭配：damage to sth、loss of life。
