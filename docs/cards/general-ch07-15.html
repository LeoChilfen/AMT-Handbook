Safetying Methods

1. 原文

To ensure fasteners do not separate from their nuts or holding ends, various safetying methods are used in aircraft from heavy aircraft to gliders to recreational aircraft. Safetying is the process of securing all aircraft, bolts, nuts, screws, pins, and other fasteners so that they do not work loose due to vibration. A familiarity with the various methods and means of safetying equipment on an aircraft is necessary to perform maintenance and inspection.

There are various methods of safetying aircraft parts. The most widely used methods are safety wire, cotter pins, lock washers, snap rings, and special nuts, such as self-locking nuts, pal nuts, and jam nuts. Some of these nuts and washers have been previously described in this chapter.

The three main types of pins used in aircraft structures are the taper pin, flathead pin, and cotter pin. Pins are used in shear applications and for safetying. Roll pins are finding increasing uses in aircraft construction.

Taper Pins Plain and threaded taper pins (AN385 and AN386) are used in joints that carry shear loads and where absence of play is essential. The plain taper pin is drilled and usually safetied with wire. The threaded taper pin is used with a taper pin washer (AN975) and shear nut (safetied with a cotter pin or safety clip) or self-locking nut.

Flathead Pin Commonly called a Clevis pin, the flathead pin (MS20392) is used with tie rod terminals and in secondary controls, which are not subject to continuous operation. The pin is customarily installed with the head up so that if the cotter pin fails or works out, the pin remains in place.

Cotter Pins The AN380 cadmium-plated, low-carbon steel cotter pin is used for safetying bolts, screws, nuts, other pins, and in various applications where such safetying is necessary. The AN381 corrosion-resistant steel cotter pin is used in locations where nonmagnetic material is required or in locations where resistance to corrosion is desired.

Roll Pins The roll pin is a pressed fit pin with chamfered ends.

2. 中文翻译

为确保紧固件不会从螺母或固定端上脱开，从重型飞机到滑翔机再到娱乐用飞机，都采用各种打保险方法。打保险（safetying）就是把飞机上的螺栓、螺母、螺钉、销等紧固件全部锁定，使其不因振动而松脱的过程。要执行维修和检查工作，必须熟悉飞机上设备打保险的各种方法和手段。

给飞机零件打保险有多种方法。最常用的方法是保险丝、开口销、锁紧垫圈、卡簧（snap ring）以及特殊螺母，例如自锁螺母、盖形螺母（pal nut）和锁紧螺母（jam nut）。其中一些螺母和垫圈已在本章前面介绍过。

飞机结构中使用的销主要有三种：锥销、平头销和开口销。销用于承受剪切的部位，也用于打保险。卷销在飞机制造中的使用正日益增多。

锥销　光锥销和螺纹锥销（AN385 和 AN386）用于承受剪切载荷、且必须没有游隙的接头。光锥销上钻有孔，通常用保险丝打保险。螺纹锥销与锥销垫圈（AN975）以及剪切螺母（用开口销或保险夹打保险）或自锁螺母配合使用。

平头销　平头销（MS20392）通常称为叉耳销（Clevis pin），与拉杆端接头配合使用，也用于不连续工作的次要操纵系统。该销通常头部朝上安装，这样即使开口销失效或脱出，销仍能保持在原位。

开口销　AN380 镀镉低碳钢开口销用于给螺栓、螺钉、螺母和其他销打保险，也用于各种需要此类保险的场合。AN381 耐蚀钢开口销用于要求非磁性材料的部位，或要求耐腐蚀的部位。

卷销　卷销是两端倒角的压配销。

4. 语法解析

① 目的不定式 + ensure 的宾语从句 + 被动语态

原句：To ensure fasteners do not separate from their nuts or holding ends, various safetying methods are used in aircraft from heavy aircraft to gliders to recreational aircraft. 拆解：句首 To ensure… 为目的状语，ensure 后接省略 that 的宾语从句（主语 fasteners，谓语 do not separate，from their nuts or holding ends 为分离的对象）；主句 various safetying methods are used in aircraft（被动）；from heavy aircraft to gliders to recreational aircraft 为范围状语，用连续 from…to…to… 列举机型范围。 要点：ensure 后接 that 从句时常省略 that，从句用陈述语序，不能写成 do fasteners not separate；separate from 是固定搭配「与……分离」。目的状语置于句首、用逗号与主句隔开，翻译时通常前置成「为确保……」。

② the process of doing + so that 结果状语 + due to 原因短语

原句：Safetying is the process of securing all aircraft, bolts, nuts, screws, pins, and other fasteners so that they do not work loose due to vibration. 拆解：主干 Safetying is the process；of securing… 为介词短语作后置定语，说明 process 的内容；so that 引导结果状语从句（they do not work loose）；due to vibration 为原因短语，修饰 work loose。 要点：定义术语常用「X is the process of doing…」句式，of 后必须用动名词；so that 在此表「从而、结果」，与表目的的 in order that 略有区别；work loose 是「（螺栓）逐渐松脱」的固定搭配，loose 作形容词补语，不是副词。

③ with 复合结构 + so that 目的状语 + 内含 if 条件从句

原句：The pin is customarily installed with the head up so that if the cotter pin fails or works out, the pin remains in place. 拆解：主干 The pin is customarily installed（被动）；with the head up 为 with 复合结构（with + 宾语 + 副词/形容词），表安装时的状态；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句内又含 if 条件从句 if the cotter pin fails or works out，主句为 the pin remains in place。 要点：with + 名词 + up/down 是描述安装姿态的常见结构；so that 从句里再嵌 if 从句，翻译时先译条件「即使开口销失效或脱出」，再译结果「销仍留在原位」；works out 意为「（销）逐渐脱出」，work 在此为不及物动词。remain in place 比 stay 更正式。

④ 并列定语从句 that… and where… 共用先行词

原句：Plain and threaded taper pins (AN385 and AN386) are used in joints that carry shear loads and where absence of play is essential. 拆解：主语 Plain and threaded taper pins (AN385 and AN386)；谓语 are used（被动）+ in joints 地点状语；joints 后接两个并列定语从句：that carry shear loads（关系代词 that 作主语）和 where absence of play is essential（关系副词 where 作地点状语，先行词仍是 joints）。 要点：两个定语从句由 and 并列、共用同一先行词，第二个从句用 where 而不用 which，因为 joints 在此表「部位」；absence of play 意为「无游隙」，play 是机械间隙的术语。翻译时两个从句可合并成「承受剪切载荷、且必须没有游隙的接头」。

⑤ 现在进行时表发展趋势 + find use 搭配

原句：Roll pins are finding increasing uses in aircraft construction. 拆解：主语 Roll pins + 谓语 are finding（现在进行时）+ 宾语 increasing uses + in aircraft construction 范围状语。 要点：进行时在此不表「此刻正在」，而表一种持续发展的趋势，译作「正日益增多」；find use(s) 是「得到应用」的固定搭配，use 在此为可数名词，故用复数 uses；increasing 修饰 uses（越来越多的用途），不修饰 find。
