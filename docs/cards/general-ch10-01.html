1. 原文

Techniques/Practices

Before starting an inspection, be certain all plates, access doors, fairings, and cowling have been opened or removed and the structure cleaned. When opening inspection plates and cowling, and before cleaning the area, take note of any oil or other evidence of fluid leakage.

In order to conduct a thorough inspection, a great deal of paperwork and/or reference information must be accessed and studied before proceeding to the aircraft to conduct the inspection. The aircraft logbooks must be reviewed to provide background information and a maintenance history of the particular aircraft. The appropriate checklist or checklists must be utilized to ensure that no items are forgotten or overlooked during the inspection. Also, many additional publications must be available, either in hard copy or in electronic format, to assist in the inspections. These additional publications may include information provided by the aircraft and engine manufacturers, appliance manufacturers, parts vendors, and the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA).

2. 中文翻译

检查技术与做法

在开始检查之前，要确认所有盖板、检修门、整流罩和发动机罩都已打开或拆下，结构也已清洁。打开检查盖板和发动机罩时，以及在清洁该区域之前，要留意有无滑油或其他液体渗漏的迹象。

为了进行彻底的检查，必须先查阅并研究大量文件资料和（或）参考资料，然后才能前往飞机实施检查。必须查阅飞机履历本，以获得该具体飞机的背景信息和维修历史。必须使用相应的检查单（一份或多份），以确保检查过程中没有任何项目被遗忘或遗漏。此外，还必须备有大量补充出版物（纸质或电子格式）来辅助检查。这些补充出版物可能包括飞机制造商、发动机制造商、机载设备制造商、零部件供应商以及美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）提供的信息。

4. 语法解析

① 祈使句 + be certain 引导的省略 that 从句

原句：Before starting an inspection, be certain all plates, access doors, fairings, and cowling have been opened or removed and the structure cleaned. 拆解：before starting an inspection 是「介词 + 动名词」构成的时间状语；主句为祈使句，谓语用动词原形 be；be certain 后接省略 that 的宾语从句，从句主语 all plates…cowling 为复数，谓语用现在完成时被动语态 have been opened or removed，并与 the structure cleaned 并列，后者省略了相同的 has been。 要点：be certain (that)… 意为「务必确保」，语气比 make sure 书面；并列结构后半的助动词省略是维修文件中的常见紧缩写法，回读时要把 cleaned 还原成 has been cleaned，否则容易误判为主动语态。

② 目的状语前置 + 情态动词被动语态

原句：In order to conduct a thorough inspection, a great deal of paperwork and/or reference information must be accessed and studied before proceeding to the aircraft to conduct the inspection. 拆解：in order to conduct a thorough inspection 为目的状语；主句主语是 a great deal of paperwork and/or reference information（a great deal of 修饰不可数名词，谓语按单数处理），谓语为 must be accessed and studied 两个并列的被动结构；before proceeding to the aircraft 是时间状语，to conduct the inspection 是目的状语。 要点：in order to 比单用 to 更强调目的；a great deal of 后接不可数名词，谓语用单数，不要与 a number of（接可数复数）混淆；before + 动名词表示「在做……之前」，逻辑主语须与主句一致。

③ when 分词短语与祈使句并列

原句：When opening inspection plates and cowling, and before cleaning the area, take note of any oil or other evidence of fluid leakage. 拆解：when opening… 与 before cleaning… 两个状语短语由 and 连接，共同修饰祈使句谓语 take note of；opening、cleaning 均为现在分词，其逻辑主语是听话的维修人员（主动关系）；take note of 后接宾语 any oil or other evidence of fluid leakage。 要点：when + 现在分词 = when you open，不能写成 when open（那样分词逻辑主语就与主句不一致）；take note of 是固定搭配，意为「留意、记下」，与 take notice of 相近但更强调记录。

④ 被动语态 + 不定式表目的

原句：The aircraft logbooks must be reviewed to provide background information and a maintenance history of the particular aircraft. 拆解：主语 the aircraft logbooks，谓语 must be reviewed（情态动词 + 被动语态）；to provide… 为不定式作目的状语，provide 后接并列宾语 background information 和 a maintenance history，of the particular aircraft 限定后者。 要点：技术文本中「动作执行者不重要」时普遍用被动，翻译时可补出泛指主语「（维修人员）必须查阅」；particular 在此意为「该具体（飞机）」，不是「特别的」。

⑤ 被动语态 + ensure that 宾语从句

原句：The appropriate checklist or checklists must be utilized to ensure that no items are forgotten or overlooked during the inspection. 拆解：主句为 must be utilized 的被动结构；to ensure that… 为不定式作目的状语，that 引导宾语从句；从句主语 no items 为否定主语，谓语是 are forgotten or overlooked 的被动并列；during the inspection 为时间状语。 要点：ensure that 从句中用一般现在时表将来，不用 will；forgotten 与 overlooked 语义相近但有别——forget 是「漏掉没做」，overlook 是「看见了却忽略」，检查单的意义正在于同时防住这两种失误。

⑥ 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：These additional publications may include information provided by the aircraft and engine manufacturers, appliance manufacturers, parts vendors, and the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA). 拆解：主语 These additional publications，谓语 may include，宾语 information 由过去分词短语 provided by… 后置修饰，相当于 which is provided by…；by 后并列四个信息来源：aircraft and engine manufacturers、appliance manufacturers、parts vendors、the FAA。 要点：information provided by = 由……提供的信息，过去分词短语作定语比定语从句简洁，是技术文献的高频结构；并列项之间的逗号与 and 要按「最后两项用 and 连接」的规则还原层次，避免把 parts vendors 误当成 appliance manufacturers 的同位语。
