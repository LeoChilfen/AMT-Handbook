1. 原文

In calculating work done, the actual resistance overcome is measured. This is not necessarily the weight of the object being moved. [Figure 5-5] A 900-lb load is being pulled a distance of 200 ft. This does not mean that the work done (force × distance) is 180,000 ft-lb (900 lb × 200 ft). This is

because the person pulling the load is not working against the total weight of the load, but rather against the rolling friction of the cart, which may be no more than 90 lb.

Friction is an important aspect of work. Without friction, it would be impossible to walk. One would have to shove oneself from place to place, and would have to bump against some obstacle to stop at a destination. Yet friction is a liability as well as an asset, and requires consideration when dealing with any moving mechanism.

In experiments relating to friction, measurement of the applied forces reveals that there are three kinds of friction.

One force is required to start a body moving, while another is required to keep the body moving at constant speed. Also, after a body is in motion, a definitely larger force is required to keep it sliding than to keep it rolling.

2. 中文翻译

计算所做的功时，所测的是实际被克服的阻力。这并不一定是被移动物体的重量。（图 5-5）一个 900 lb 的载荷被拉动 200 ft，并不意味着所做的功（力 × 距离）就是 180,000 ft-lb（900 lb × 200 ft）。之所以不是这样，

是因为拉载荷的人所对抗的并不是载荷的总重量，而是推车的滚动摩擦，后者可能不超过 90 lb。

摩擦是功的一个重要方面。没有摩擦就无法行走：人只能把自己从一个地方蹭到另一个地方，到了目的地还得撞上某个障碍物才能停下来。然而摩擦既是有利因素，也是不利因素，在处理任何运动机构时都需要加以考虑。

在与摩擦有关的实验中，对所施加的力的测量表明，摩擦有三种。

使物体起动需要一种力，而使物体保持匀速运动需要另一种力。此外，物体运动起来之后，使它保持滑动所需的力明显大于使它保持滚动所需的力。

因此，三种摩擦可分类如下：

（1）起动摩擦或称静摩擦；（2）滑动摩擦；以及（3）——原文在此处截断，第（3）类滚动摩擦见后续卡片。

4. 语法解析

① 含蓄虚拟条件：Without friction, it would be impossible to walk

原句：Without friction, it would be impossible to walk.

拆解：Without friction 是介词短语，相当于条件从句 If there were no friction；主句 it would be impossible to walk 用 would + 动词原形，其中 it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to walk。

要点：without / but for / otherwise 都能引出「与事实相反」的含蓄条件，主句要用 would / could / might + 动词原形。翻译时补出「如果没有……」即可，不要译成「没有摩擦，将不可能走路」这种半虚拟口气。

② not … but rather 对比结构 + 非限制性定语从句

原句：because the person pulling the load is not working against the total weight of the load, but rather against the rolling friction of the cart, which may be no more than 90 lb.

拆解：because 承接上一段结尾的 This is，引出原因；从句主语 the person pulling the load（现在分词短语作后置定语）；谓语是 is not working … but rather (working) …，not … but rather 构成对比；which may be no more than 90 lb 是非限制性定语从句，指代 the rolling friction of the cart。

要点：not … but rather 意为「不是……而是……」，rather 后面可省略与前句重复的动词，阅读时要把 working 补回来。no more than 强调「至多不过」，与 not more than（不超过）语气不同，此处暗示「小得可以忽略」。原文因插图插排把这一句拆在两个自然段里，此处引的是第二段开头那一截。

③ while 引导的对比 + start/keep + 宾语 + 现在分词

原句：One force is required to start a body moving, while another is required to keep the body moving at constant speed.

拆解：两个分句都是 be required to do 的被动结构；to start a body moving 与 to keep the body moving 都是 start / keep + 宾语 + 现在分词（分词作宾语补足语）；while 在此作对比连词，意为「而」。

要点：start / keep / set 后接「宾语 + 现在分词」表示使某物进入或保持某种运动状态（start the body moving、keep it rolling），不能换成不定式。while 引导对比时前后结构通常对称，译作「而……」比「当……时」更贴切。

④ that 引导的宾语从句 + there be 句型

原句：In experiments relating to friction, measurement of the applied forces reveals that there are three kinds of friction.

拆解：In experiments relating to friction 是状语（relating to 现在分词短语作 experiments 的后置定语）；主语 measurement of the applied forces；谓语 reveals；that there are three kinds of friction 是宾语从句，从句内部为 there be 句型。

要点：reveal / show / indicate / find 后接 that 从句是技术文献陈述实验结论的常见句式。there be 在从句中保持 there are 的语序，不要写成 there have。
