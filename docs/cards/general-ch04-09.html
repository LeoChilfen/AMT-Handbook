Bill of Material

通用 Ch 04 · Bill of Material · 原文 PDF 第 131 页

1. 原文

A list of the materials and parts necessary for the fabrication or assembly of a component or system is often included on the drawing. The list is usually in ruled columns that AA BB SECTION A-A SECTION B-B provide the part number, name of the part, material the part is to be constructed of, the quantity required, and the source of the part or material. On drawings that do not have a bill of material, the data may be indicated directly on the drawing. On assembly drawings, each item is identified by a number in a circle or square. An arrow connecting the number with the item assists in locating it in the bill of material.

2. 中文翻译

制造或装配某个组件或系统所需的材料和零件清单，常常附在图纸上。该清单通常列在带格线的栏内（原文此处夹有「SECTION A-A」「SECTION B-B」等剖视标注的零散文字），依次给出零件编号、零件名称、制造该零件所用的材料、所需数量以及零件或材料的来源。在没有材料清单的图纸上，这些数据可以直接标注在图纸上。在装配图上，每个项目都用一个圆圈或方框内的编号来标识。用箭头把编号与该项目相连，有助于在材料清单中找到它。

4. 语法解析

① 长主语 + necessary for 后置定语 + 被动

原句：A list of the materials and parts necessary for the fabrication or assembly of a component or system is often included on the drawing. 拆解：主语为 A list…，of the materials and parts 为后置定语，necessary for… 为形容词短语进一步修饰 parts；谓语 is often included 为被动，often 插在 be 与过去分词之间；on the drawing 为地点状语。 要点：主语中心词是 A list，故谓语用单数 is；necessary for 后接名词或动名词；用 on 是因为图纸上的信息“附着于”纸面。

② On 介词短语前置 + that 定语从句 + 情态被动

原句：On drawings that do not have a bill of material, the data may be indicated directly on the drawing. 拆解：On drawings… 为地点状语前置，drawings 后接 that 引导的定语从句；主句主语 the data；谓语为情态被动 may be indicated。 要点：that 从句修饰紧邻的 drawings（没有清单的图纸），不要误读为修饰 material；directly 修饰 indicated，强调“直接标在图上”。

③ 被动 + by 引出手段 + 介词短语作定语

原句：On assembly drawings, each item is identified by a number in a circle or square. 拆解：On assembly drawings 为范围状语；主语 each item；谓语 is identified 为被动；by a number 表手段；in a circle or square 为介词短语作后置定语修饰 number。 要点：identify A by B 的被动式是 A is identified by B；by 表“用……（手段）”，而编号位于圆圈“里”要用 in，两个介词分工不同。

④ 现在分词短语作后置定语 + assist in + 动名词

原句：An arrow connecting the number with the item assists in locating it in the bill of material. 拆解：主语 An arrow + connecting the number with the item（现在分词短语作后置定语）；谓语 assists in；locating it in the bill of material 为动名词短语作介词宾语；it 指 the item。 要点：connect A with B 表示“把 A 与 B 连起来”；assist in doing 中的 in 可省略，但不能接不定式；代词 it 的指代要回看最近的名词。
