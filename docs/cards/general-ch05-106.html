Flight Control Surfaces

通用 Ch 05 · Flight Control Surfaces · 原文 PDF 第 192 页

1. 原文

The purpose of flight controls is to allow the pilot to maneuver the airplane, and to control it from the time it starts the takeoff roll until it lands and safely comes to a halt. Flight controls are typically associated with the wing and the vertical and horizontal stabilizers, because these are the parts of the airplane that flight controls most often attach to. In flight, and to some extent on the ground, flight controls provide the airplane with the ability to move around one or more of the three axes.

Flight controls function by changing the shape or aerodynamic characteristics of the surface they are attached to.

2. 中文翻译

飞行操纵装置的作用，是让飞行员能够操纵飞机，并在从开始起飞滑跑直到着陆、安全停住的全过程中对飞机实施控制。飞行操纵装置通常与机翼以及垂直安定面和水平安定面相关联，因为这些正是飞行操纵装置最常附装的飞机部件。飞行中（在地面上也在一定程度上），飞行操纵装置使飞机具备绕三个轴中的一个或多个轴运动的能力。

飞行操纵装置通过改变其所附装翼面的形状或空气动力特性来起作用。

4. 语法解析

① allow sb to do + from the time … until …

原句：The purpose of flight controls is to allow the pilot to maneuver the airplane, and to control it from the time it starts the takeoff roll until it lands and safely comes to a halt.

拆解：主语 The purpose of flight controls，表语为不定式 to allow；allow sb to do 后接两个并列不定式 to maneuver 与 to control；from the time (that) it starts … until it lands … 是时间状语，两个 it 都指飞机。

要点：from the time … until … 是「从……起直到……」的固定框架，the time 后省略了关系词 that/when。allow sb to do 不能省 to（× allow sb do），这一点与 let/make 相反。

② because 从句 + 介词悬空的定语从句

原句：Flight controls are typically associated with the wing and the vertical and horizontal stabilizers, because these are the parts of the airplane that flight controls most often attach to.

拆解：主句为被动 are associated with；because 引导原因状语从句；从句中 these are the parts of the airplane 后接 that … attach to 定语从句，that 作介词 to 的宾语，介词留在句尾。

要点：attach to 的介词 to 留在从句末尾，是英语的「介词悬空」；正式书面语也可写 to which flight controls most often attach。be associated with 意为「与……相关联／配套」，不是「与……交往」。the wing and the vertical and horizontal stabilizers 是三个并列成分，注意 vertical 与 horizontal 共用 stabilizers。

③ by + 动名词表方式

原句：Flight controls function by changing the shape or aerodynamic characteristics of the surface they are attached to.

拆解：主语 Flight controls，谓语 function（起作用，不及物）；by changing … 是方式状语，by + 动名词表示「通过……方式」；they are attached to 是省略关系词的定语从句修饰 the surface。

要点：by + -ing 是技术文本说明工作原理最常用的结构，译作「通过……来……」。the surface (which/that) they are attached to 中关系词作宾语故可省略，但介词 to 必须保留。function 作动词时不及物，不能写成 is functioned。
