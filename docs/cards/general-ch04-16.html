Universal Numbering System

1. 原文

The universal numbering system provides a means of identifying standard drawing sizes. In the universal numbering system, each drawing number consists of six or seven digits. The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing. The number 1 indicates a drawing of 8½" × 11"; number 2 indicates an 11" × 17" drawing; number 4 represents a drawing of 17" × 22"; and 5 indicates a width of between 17 and 36 inches but on a continuous roll. Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E. The letter A is 8½" × 11", B is 11" × 17", C is 17" × 22", D is 22" × 34" and E is 34"

× 44". There are additional letters, such as D1 at 24" × 36", E1 at 30" × 42" and additional sizes unique to even larger formats but generally reserved for inter-company operations.

4. 语法解析

① The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing（并列谓语 + 主谓一致）

原句：The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing.

拆解：主语 The first digit 带两个性质不同的并列谓语：系动词 is（表语为 1, 2, 4, or 5）和实义动词 indicates；always 修饰 be。

要点：一个主语带 is 和实义动词两个谓语时，实义动词仍要与主语保持一致（digit 为单数，故加 -s 写作 indicates）。列举若干可能取值时，最后一项前用 or 表示「其中之一」；数值列举用逗号分隔，不用顿号式并列。always 位于 be 之后、实义动词之前。

② Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E（被动 + with 独立结构）

原句：Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E.

拆解：主干 Letters are also used 为被动；括号内 and becoming more prevalent 与 are used 共用主语 Letters 并省略 are；with the most common letters being A through E 是 with + 名词 + 现在分词的独立结构，作补充说明。

要点：with + 宾语 + 分词（being／having／drawn）是技术英语常用的补充说明手段，译作「其中……」。括号内属并列省略，正式行文可补全为 and are becoming more prevalent。A through E 表示「A 到 E（含 E）」，美式用 through，英式多用 to。

③ Many firms have modified this basic system to conform to their needs（现在完成时 + 目的不定式）

原句：Many firms have modified this basic system to conform to their needs.

拆解：主语 Many firms，谓语 have modified 为现在完成时（强调已完成的改动及其现存结果）；to conform to their needs 是目的状语。

要点：现在完成时在这里表示「已经改了，所以现行体系与原来不同」，不能与明确的过去时间状语（如 in 1990）连用。conform to 是「符合、遵照」的固定搭配，也可用 conform with，但不能写成 conform their needs（conform 不及物）。
