Tie-Down Procedures for Ski Planes

1. 原文

Ski planes are tied down, if the securing means are available, in the same manner as land planes. Ski-equipped airplanes can be secured on ice or in snow by using a device called a dead-man. A dead-man is any item at hand, such as a piece of pipe, log, and so forth, that a rope is attached to and buried in a snow or ice trench. Using caution to keep the free end of the rope dry and unfrozen, snow is packed in the trench. If available, pour water into the trench; when it is frozen, tie down the aircraft with the free end of the rope.

If this is not possible, the seaplane could be anchored in a sheltered area away from the wind and waves.

2. 中文翻译

滑雪飞机在具备固定手段时，按与陆上飞机相同的方式系留。装有雪橇的飞机在冰面或雪地上，可用一种称为“死锚”（dead-man）的装置固定。死锚是随手可得的任何物件，例如一段管子、圆木等，把绳子系在其上并埋入雪沟或冰沟中。注意保持绳子的自由端干燥、不结冰，把雪填入沟内压实。如有条件，向沟内浇水；水冻结后，用绳子的自由端把飞机系留好。

4. 语法解析

① 插入的条件状语从句

原句：Ski planes are tied down, if the securing means are available, in the same manner as land planes. 拆解：主语 Ski planes + 被动谓语 are tied down + 插入的条件状语从句 if the securing means are available（两侧用逗号隔开）+ 方式状语 in the same manner as land planes。 要点：if 从句插在谓语与状语之间时必须两侧加逗号，否则读者会把 in the same manner 误当成从句的一部分；the same…as 是同级比较，as 后接名词。

② 定语从句中的悬空介词与谓语省略

原句：A dead-man is any item at hand, such as a piece of pipe, log, and so forth, that a rope is attached to and buried in a snow or ice trench. 拆解：主句 A dead-man is any item；at hand 为后置定语；such as a piece of pipe, log, and so forth 为插入举例；that 引导定语从句修饰 item，从句中 a rope is attached to（介词 to 留在句末）与 (is) buried 并列。 要点：that 从句里 to 的宾语就是先行词 item——这叫“悬空介词”；第二个被动谓语省略了 is，靠 and 支撑；不要把 that 误当成 such as 的一部分。

③ 现在分词作状语 + keep + 宾语 + 形容词

原句：Using caution to keep the free end of the rope dry and unfrozen, snow is packed in the trench. 拆解：句首 Using caution to keep… 是现在分词短语作方式状语；主句 snow is packed in the trench 为被动；keep the free end of the rope dry and unfrozen 是 keep + 宾语 + 形容词（宾语补足语）。 要点：分词状语的逻辑主语是“人”，而主句主语是 snow，中文翻译要补出“（人）注意…”；keep sth dry / unfrozen 用形容词作宾补，不能用副词。

④ when + 分词的省略式状语

原句：Caution must be used when moving an aircraft that has been secured in this manner to ensure that a ski is not still frozen to the ground. 拆解：主句 Caution must be used（被动）；when moving an aircraft… 是“连词 + 现在分词”的省略式时间状语（= when one is moving…）；that 引导定语从句修饰 aircraft；to ensure that… 是不定式作目的状语，后接宾语从句。 要点：when / while + 分词 可省去主语和 be，是科技文本的常见压缩；ensure that 从句用一般现在时表示对常规状况的判断。
