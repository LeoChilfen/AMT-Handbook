14 CFR Part 33—Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines

通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 33—Airworthiness Standards: Aircraft Engines · 原文 PDF 第 58 页

1. 原文

Engines Each of the four preceding 14 CFR regulations require that the engine used in the aircraft must be type certificated. Title 14 CFR part 33 details the requirements for both reciprocating and turbine style aircraft engines. It not only specifies the design and construction requirements, but also the block test requirements that subject the engine to extremely demanding testing in order to prove its capability of enduring the stresses of powering the aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

发动机（Engines）。前面四部 14 CFR 法规（parts 23、25、27、29）都要求：航空器上所使用的发动机必须取得型号合格证。14 CFR part 33 详细规定了往复式与涡轮式航空器发动机的要求。它不仅规定设计与构造要求，还规定台架试车（block test）要求——使发动机经受极为苛刻的试验，以证明其具备承受为航空器提供动力时的各种应力的能力。

4. 语法解析

① require that the engine … must be type certificated（require that 宾语从句）

原句：Engines Each of the four preceding 14 CFR regulations require that the engine used in the aircraft must be type certificated.

拆解：主语 Each of the four preceding 14 CFR regulations；谓语 require；that 引导宾语从句 the engine … must be type certificated；used in the aircraft 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 the engine。

要点：require that 从句在正式英语中多用虚拟语气 (should) be type certificated，原文改用 must be，强制语气更强，属手册常见写法。each of + 复数名词作主语时规范语法用单数谓语（requires），原文用复数 require 属就近一致的宽松处理。

② not only specifies …, but also the block test requirements that subject the engine to …（not only … but also 并列 + subject sb to sth）

原句：It not only specifies the design and construction requirements, but also the block test requirements that subject the engine to extremely demanding testing in order to prove its capability of enduring the stresses of powering the aircraft.

拆解：not only … but also … 连接两个并列宾语（the design and construction requirements 与 the block test requirements）；that subject the engine to … 是定语从句修饰 block test requirements；in order to prove … 表目的。

要点：subject A to B 意为「使 A 承受 B」，此处 subject 是动词，重音在第二音节，不要与名词 subject（科目、主题）混淆。not only 置于句首要引起主句部分倒装，此处 not only 在句中故不倒装。enduring 是动名词，作 of 的宾语。

③ details the requirements for both reciprocating and turbine style aircraft engines（both … and … 并列）

原句：Title 14 CFR part 33 details the requirements for both reciprocating and turbine style aircraft engines.

拆解：details 是第三人称单数动词，意为「详细规定」；for both A and B 是介词短语修饰 requirements；both … and … 连接两个形容词性修饰成分，共同修饰 aircraft engines。

要点：detail 作动词时重音在第一音节（名词同形），表示逐一列出细节，与 describe（概括描述）有别。both … and … 要求前后结构对称：此处 reciprocating 与 turbine style 均为定语，若写成 both reciprocating engines and turbine engines 则中心词重复但更清楚。
