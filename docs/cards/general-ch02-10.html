Light Sport Aircraft (LSA)

通用 Ch 02 · Light Sport Aircraft (LSA) · 原文 PDF 第 90 页

1. 原文

The light sport aircraft (LSA) category includes gliders, airplanes, gyroplanes, powered parachutes, weight-shift and lighter-than-air aircraft. There are two general types of LSAs: Special (SLSA) and Experimental (ELSA). The SLSA are factory built and the ESLA are kit-built. This new category of aircraft was added to the regulations in 2004. (Refer to 14 CFR sections 21.190, 65.107, and 91.327, all dated July 27, 2004.)

Just as industry standard specifications have replaced many of the military standards to define products that are destined to be part of the Department of Defense (DoD) inventory, so too have industry standards come into the FAA sights for documenting certain information. Quality is one example. The Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) has developed AS 9100 and AS 9110 as auditing standards for aerospace facilities and specifically repair stations. Likewise, ISO 9001 is being adopted by the FAA as a system of measuring their performance. Therefore, it was logical that when the FAA looked to develop the standards for this newest category of aircraft, they again looked to industry, and this time it was the American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM).

The ASTM developed a comprehensive list of consensus standards for use by manufacturers, regulators, maintenance facilities, LSA owners, and service providers. It is unique that these standards are the first ones in over 100 years to solely address the issue of recreational aircraft use. It is also the first complete set of industry consensus standards covering the design, manufacture, and use of recreational aircraft that was developed by a non-government agency. The ASTM committee that developed these LSA standards did so to ensure the quality of products and services to support both the national and the international regulatory structures for LSAs. Over 20 standards have been generated, and more are being developed to cover this diversity of aircraft. This handbook only incorporates a review of F2483-05, “Standard Practice for Maintenance and the Development of Maintenance Manuals for Light Sport Aircraft (LSA)” a six-page document comprised of the following 12 sections:

  1. Scope
  1. Referenced Documents
  1. Terminology
  1. Significance and Use
  1. Aircraft Maintenance Manual
  1. Line Maintenance, Repairs, and Alterations
  1. Heavy Maintenance, Repairs, and Alterations
  1. Overhaul
  1. Major Repairs and Alterations
  1. Task-Specific Training
  1. Safety Directives
  1. Keywords

The scope of that document is basically twofold:

• To provide guidelines for the qualification necessary to accomplish various levels of maintenance on LSA.

• To provide the content and structure of maintenance manuals for aircraft and their components that are operated as LSAs.

Some additional definitions from section 3, Terminology, that help to better explain the LSA concepts are:

• Annual condition inspection—defined as a detailed inspection accomplished once a year in accordance with instructions provided in the maintenance manual supplied with the LSA. The purpose of this inspection is to look for any wear, corrosion, or damage that would cause the LSA not to be in condition for safe operation.

• Heavy maintenance—any maintenance, inspection, repair, or alteration a manufacturer has designated that requires specialized training, equipment, or facilities.

• Line maintenance—any repair, maintenance, scheduled checks, servicing, inspections, or alterations not considered heavy maintenance that are approved by the manufacturer and is specified in the manufacturer’s maintenance manual.

• LSA repairman–inspection—a U.S. FAA-certified LSA repairman with an inspection rating per 14 CFR part 65. This person is authorized to perform the 100- hour/annual inspection of the aircraft that they own.

• LSA repairman–maintenance—a U.S. FAA-certified LSA repairman with a maintenance rating per 14 CFR part 65. This person is allowed to perform the required maintenance and can also accomplish the 100-hour/ annual inspection.

• Major repair, alteration, or maintenance—any repair, alteration, or maintenance where instructions to complete the task are excluded from the maintenance manual.

• Minor repair, alteration, or maintenance—any repair, alteration, or maintenance where instructions to complete the task are included in the maintenance manual.

The 100-hour inspection is the same as the annual inspection, except for the interval of time. The requirements for whether or not the 100-hour inspection is applicable are exactly the same as the criteria for the standard 100-hour/annual required of non-LSA aircraft.

Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)

Although these manuals do not require any FAA approval, the regulations do require that the manual be developed in accordance with industry standards. This ASTM sets that standard by requiring:

• General specifications to be listed, include capacities, servicing, lubrication, and ground handling

• An inspection checklist for the annual condition or 100-hour inspection

• A description of and the instructions for the maintenance, repair, and overhaul of the LSA engine

• A description of and the instructions for the maintenance, repair, and alteration of the aircraft’s primary structure

Other items that maintenance procedures must be provided for are:

• Instruments and avionics

• Painting and coatings

The Inspection, Repair, and Alterations section must specifically list any special tools and parts needed to complete the task, as well as the type of maintenance action (line, heavy, or overhaul) necessary to accomplish the activity. Directly associated with that information is the requirement to specify the level of certification needed to do the job (i.e., LSA repairman, A&P, or repair station). The manual may refer to existing FAA ACs.

Line Maintenance, Repairs, & Alterations

The minimum level of certification necessary to accomplish line maintenance is LSA inspection. Some typical tasks considered to be line maintenance are:

• 100-hour/annual condition inspection

• Servicing of fluids

• Removing and replacing components when instructions to do so are provided in the maintenance manual – Batteries – Fuel pump – Exhaust – Spark plugs and wires – Floats and skis

• Repair or alteration of components when specific instructions are provided in the maintenance manual – Patching a hole in the fabric – Installation of a strobe light kit

Heavy maintenance, repairs, and alterations must be accomplished by either a certified mechanic (A or P or A&P) or an LSA repairman—maintenance who has received additional “task specific” training. Some examples of this would be the removal and replacement of complete engine, cylinder, piston and valve assemblies; primary flight controls; and landing gear.

Heavy repair of components or structure can be accomplished when instructions are provided in the maintenance manual or other service directed instructions. A few examples of this activity are:

• Repainting of control surfaces

• Structural repairs

• Recovering of a dope and fabric

Heavy alterations of components can be accomplished when instructions are provided in the maintenance manual or other service directed instructions. Examples of this activity are initial installation of skis and installation of new additional pitot static instruments.

Overhaul of components can be performed only by the manufacturer (or someone authorized to perform) of the LSA or the component to be overhauled. An overhaul manual is required and must be a separate manual from the manufacturer’s maintenance manual. Items typically considered for overhaul are engines, carburetors, starters, generators, alternators, and instruments.

Major Repairs & Alterations

Another major difference between LSA maintenance and traditional aircraft maintenance is that FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration, is not required to document major repairs and alterations. Instead, any major repair or alteration that is accomplished after the LSA has gone through production acceptance testing must be evaluated relative to the applicable ASTM requirements. After this evaluation has been accomplished (either by the manufacturer or an entity approved by them), a written affidavit must be provided attesting that the LSA still meets the requirements of the applicable ASTMs.

The manufacturer (or other approved entity) must provide written instructions defining the level of certification necessary to perform the maintenance and also include any ground test or flight testing necessary to verify that the LSA complies with the original LSA acceptance test standards, and is in condition for safe operation. Proper documentation of this maintenance activity is required to be entered in the LSA records and is also defined by the manufacturer.

Task specific training is not required to be FAA approved. This is solely the responsibility of the manufacturer. Some examples of this are an engine manufacturer’s overhaul school or the EAA Sport Air fabric covering school.

Safety directives are issued against an LSA or component and are not issued by the FAA, but rather by the original aircraft manufacturer. Note: If the LSA includes a product that is TC’d by the FAA, the manufacturer is required to issue a safety directive. Typical instructions within a safety directive include:

• List of tools required for the task

• List of parts needed

• Type of maintenance (line, heavy, overhaul)

• Level of certification needed

• Detailed instructions and diagrams

• Inspection and test methods

Safety directives are mandatory, except for experimental use LSAs.

2. 中文翻译

轻型运动航空器（LSA）类别包括滑翔机、飞机、自转旋翼机、动力降落伞、重量移动操纵航空器和轻于空气的航空器。LSA 有两大通用类型：特殊类（SLSA）和实验类（ELSA）。SLSA 为工厂制造，ELSA 为套件自制。这一新类别于 2004 年被纳入法规。（参见 14 CFR 第 21.190 条、第 65.107 条和第 91.327 条，均为 2004 年 7 月 27 日版本。）

正如工业标准规范已取代许多军用标准来界定即将进入国防部（DoD）物资目录的产品一样，工业标准也同样进入了 FAA 的视野，被用来记载某些信息。质量即是一例。美国汽车工程师学会（SAE）制定了 AS 9100 和 AS 9110，作为航空航天设施特别是维修站的审核标准。同样，FAA 也在采用 ISO 9001 作为衡量其绩效的体系。因此，当 FAA 着手为这一最新类别的航空器制定标准时，他们合乎逻辑地再次求助于工业界，而这一次是美国材料与试验协会（ASTM）。

ASTM 制定了一份供制造商、监管机构、维修单位、LSA 所有人和服务提供者使用的完整共识标准清单。其独特之处在于，这些标准是 100 多年来首批专门针对休闲类航空器使用问题的标准。它也是首套由非政府机构制定的、涵盖休闲类航空器设计、制造和使用的完整行业共识标准。制定这些 LSA 标准的 ASTM 委员会之所以这样做，是为了确保产品与服务质量，以支撑 LSA 的国内和国际监管体系。目前已产生 20 多项标准，还有更多标准正在制定，以覆盖这一类别航空器的多样性。本手册只收录了对 F2483-05《轻型运动航空器（LSA）维修与维修手册制定的标准做法》的评述，该文件共六页，由以下 12 节组成：

  1. 适用范围
  1. 引用文件
  1. 术语
  1. 意义与用途
  1. 航空器维修手册
  1. 外场维修、修理与改装
  1. 深度维修、修理与改装
  1. 大修

  1. 任务专项培训

  1. 关键词

该文件的范围基本上有两个方面：

• 为完成 LSA 各级维修所必需的资格提供指南。

• 为作为 LSA 运行的航空器及其部件的维修手册提供内容与结构。

第 3 节「术语」中还有一些有助于更好解释 LSA 概念的定义：

• 年度状态检查——定义为每年一次、按 LSA 随附维修手册中给出的说明实施的详细检查。此项检查的目的是查找任何会导致 LSA 不具备安全运行状态的磨损、腐蚀或损伤。

• 深度维修——制造商指定的、需要专门培训、设备或设施的任何维修、检查、修理或改装。

• 外场维修——不属于深度维修、经制造商批准并在制造商维修手册中规定的任何修理、维修、计划性检查、勤务、检查或改装。

• LSA 维修人员（检查类）——按 14 CFR part 65 取得检查等级的美国 FAA 认证 LSA 维修人员。此人被授权对其自有的航空器实施 100 小时／年度检查。

• LSA 维修人员（维修类）——按 14 CFR part 65 取得维修等级的美国 FAA 认证 LSA 维修人员。此人可以实施所要求的维修，也可以完成 100 小时／年度检查。

• 重大修理、改装或维修——完成任务所需说明未包含在维修手册中的任何修理、改装或维修。

• 轻微修理、改装或维修——完成任务所需说明已包含在维修手册中的任何修理、改装或维修。

除时间间隔不同外，100 小时检查与年度检查相同。100 小时检查是否适用，其判定要求与非 LSA 航空器所要求的标准 100 小时／年度检查的判据完全相同。

航空器维修手册（AMM）

虽然这些手册不需要 FAA 批准，但法规确实要求手册按工业标准制定。ASTM 通过以下要求确立了该标准：

• 应列出一般性规范，包括容量、勤务、润滑和地面操作

• 年度状态检查或 100 小时检查用的检查单

• 对 LSA 发动机维修、修理和大修的说明及描述

• 对航空器一次结构维修、修理和改装的说明及描述

维修程序还必须涵盖的其他项目有：

• 仪表与航空电子设备

• 喷漆与涂层

「检查、修理与改装」一节必须具体列出完成任务所需的任何专用工具和零件，以及完成该活动所必需的维修类别（外场、深度或大修）。与这些信息直接相关的要求是：规定完成该工作所需的证书等级（即 LSA 维修人员、A&P 或维修站）。手册可以引用现有的 FAA AC。

外场维修、修理与改装

完成外场维修所需的最低证书等级是 LSA 检查类。以下是一些被视为外场维修的典型任务：

• 100 小时／年度状态检查

• 油液勤务

• 在维修手册给出说明时拆装部件——蓄电池——燃油泵——排气管——火花塞与点火线——浮筒与雪橇

• 在维修手册给出具体说明时修理或改装部件——修补蒙布上的破洞——加装频闪灯组件

深度维修、修理和改装必须由持证机务人员（A 照、P 照或 A&P）或接受过额外「任务专项」培训的 LSA 维修人员（维修类）完成。这方面的例子包括整体发动机、气缸、活塞与气门组件的拆装，一次飞行操纵系统的拆装，以及起落架的拆装。

当维修手册或其他服务指导性说明给出说明时，可以进行部件或结构的深度修理。这类活动的几个例子是：

• 操纵面的重新喷漆

• 结构修理

• 涂布／蒙布结构的重新蒙皮

当维修手册或其他服务指导性说明给出说明时，可以进行部件的重大改装。这类活动的例子有首次安装雪橇，以及加装新的皮托静压仪表。

部件大修只能由 LSA 或待大修部件的制造商（或经其授权者）实施。必须有大修手册，且该手册必须与制造商的维修手册分开成册。通常考虑大修的项目有发动机、化油器、起动机、发电机、交流发电机和仪表。

重大修理与改装

LSA 维修与传统航空器维修的另一个重大差别是：重大修理和改装不需要用 FAA Form 337《重大修理与改装》来记录。取而代之的是，LSA 通过生产验收测试后所实施的任何重大修理或改装，都必须对照适用的 ASTM 要求进行评估。评估完成后（由制造商或其批准的实体实施），必须提供书面宣誓声明，证明该 LSA 仍然满足适用 ASTM 的要求。

制造商（或其他经批准的实体）必须提供书面说明，规定实施维修所需的证书等级，并包含为验证该 LSA 符合原 LSA 验收测试标准且处于安全运行状态所必需的任何地面试验或飞行试验。此类维修活动必须按规定记入 LSA 记录，具体规定同样由制造商给出。

任务专项培训不需要 FAA 批准，这完全是制造商的责任。这方面的例子有发动机制造商的大修学校，或 EAA 运动航空蒙布培训学校。

安全指令针对 LSA 或其部件发布，并非由 FAA 发布，而是由原航空器制造商发布。注：如果 LSA 包含 FAA 已颁发 TC 的产品，则制造商必须发布安全指令。安全指令中的典型内容有：

• 完成任务所需工具的清单

• 所需零件的清单

• 维修类别（外场、深度、大修）

• 所需的证书等级

• 详细说明与示意图

• 检查与试验方法

除实验类 LSA 外，安全指令是强制性的。

4. 语法解析

① 长列举中的并列层次与共用中心词

原句：The light sport aircraft (LSA) category includes gliders, airplanes, gyroplanes, powered parachutes, weight-shift and lighter-than-air aircraft.

拆解：主语 The light sport aircraft (LSA) category；谓语 includes；宾语为五项并列：gliders、airplanes、gyroplanes、powered parachutes、weight-shift and lighter-than-air aircraft。最后一项中 weight-shift 与 lighter-than-air 是两个前置定语，共用中心词 aircraft。

要点：英语列举中 and 的层级很关键：前四项之间只有逗号，最后一项内部也有 and，读者要判断出「weight-shift」与「lighter-than-air」是共同修饰 aircraft 的两个定语，而不是把 weight-shift 单独当作一类。翻译时应补出中心词（「重量移动操纵航空器和轻于空气的航空器」），否则中文会读成五类之外还有一类。缩写 LSA 用括号紧跟全称首次出现处，后文即可独立使用。

② 破折号定义 + 过去分词与定语从句的双重后置

原句：Line maintenance—any repair, maintenance, scheduled checks, servicing, inspections, or alterations not considered heavy maintenance that are approved by the manufacturer and is specified in the manufacturer’s maintenance manual.

拆解：主干只有 Line maintenance 与破折号后的名词短语，省略了 is defined as；not considered heavy maintenance 为过去分词短语后置修饰前面的六项列举；that are approved by the manufacturer and is specified… 为定语从句，其中 are… 与 is… 两个谓语共用一个先行词。

要点：破折号在这里等于「即、也就是」，用于给出术语定义，是技术手册常见的省略句式（完整形式为 Line maintenance is any repair…）。that 从句里 are approved（复数，指六项活动）与 is specified（单数，指 line maintenance 这个类别）数不一致，是原文把「活动」与「类别」混指造成的，翻译时不必强行统一，按「经制造商批准并在维修手册中规定的……」处理即可。such 类定义句翻译时保留「——」能凸显术语与释义的分界。

③ for whether or not… 作介词宾语 + the same as 比较

原句：The requirements for whether or not the 100-hour inspection is applicable are exactly the same as the criteria for the standard 100-hour/annual required of non-LSA aircraft.

拆解：主语 The requirements + 后置修饰 for whether or not the 100-hour inspection is applicable（介词 for 后接 whether 从句，不能用 if 从句）；谓语 are exactly the same as；比较对象 the criteria（for the standard 100-hour/annual required of non-LSA aircraft，其中 required of 为过去分词短语后置定语）。

要点：介词后只能用 whether 不能用 if，且 whether or not 可紧连也可拆开（whether the inspection is applicable or not）。the same as 是固定比较结构，as 后接名词短语；若接从句须写 the same as what…。required of non-LSA aircraft 中 of 表示「对……的要求」，不是被动 by，翻译为「对非 LSA 航空器所要求的」。

④ 表语从句中的同位语与被动不定式

原句：Another major difference between LSA maintenance and traditional aircraft maintenance is that FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration, is not required to document major repairs and alterations.

拆解：主语 Another major difference（between A and B 为后置修饰）+ 系动词 is + that 引导的表语从句；从句主语 FAA Form 337，其后 Major Repair and Alteration 为同位语（解释该表格名称），谓语 is not required to document…（被动 + 不定式）。

要点：that 表语从句不能省略 that，否则句子会被读成 difference is FAA Form 337（把表格当成差异本身）。同位语用逗号夹在主语与谓语之间，翻译时用「FAA Form 337《重大修理与改装》」的书名号处理。be required to do 在此是「（规定上）必须用……来记载」，document 作动词意为「记录、记载」，不是名词「文件」。

⑤ Although 让步从句 + do 强调 + require that … be 虚拟

原句：Although these manuals do not require any FAA approval, the regulations do require that the manual be developed in accordance with industry standards.

拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句（these manuals do not require any FAA approval，do 为助动词构成否定）；主句 the regulations do require that the manual be developed in accordance with industry standards，其中 do 为强调助动词，that 从句谓语用原形 be（虚拟语气）。

要点：主句的 do require 与从句的 do not require 形成「手册不需要——法规却要求」的对照，这个 do 是强调用法，朗读时重读，翻译可用「确实要求」体现。require that sb/sth be done 是规章英语的标准虚拟句式，不能写成 is developed。in accordance with 是「依照」，比 according to 更正式，规章文本一律用它。
