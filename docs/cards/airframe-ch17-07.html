Lavatory Smoke Detectors

机体 Ch 17 · Lavatory Smoke Detectors · 原文 PDF 第 988 页

1. 原文

Airplanes that have a passenger capacity of 20 or more are equipped with a smoke detector system that monitors the lavatories for smoke. Smoke indications provide a warning light in the flight deck or provide a warning light or audible warning at the lavatory and at flight attendant stations that would be readily detected by a flight attendant. Each lavatory must have a built-in fire extinguisher that discharges automatically. The smoke detector is located in the ceiling of the lavatory.

Lavatory Smoke Detector System

Refer to Figure 17-23. The lavatory smoke detector is powered by the 28-volt DC left/right main DC bus. If there is smoke in the sensing chamber of the smoke detector, the alarm LED (red) comes on. The timing circuit makes an intermittent ground. The warning horn and lavatory call light operate intermittently. The smoke detection circuit makes a ground for the relay. The energized relay makes a ground signal for the overhead electronics unit (OEU) in the central monitoring systems (CMS). This interface gives these indications: lavatory master call light flashes, cabin system control panel (CSCP) and cabin area control panel (CACP) pop-up window shows, and the lavatory call chime operates. Push the lavatory call reset switch or the smoke detector interrupt switch to cancel the smoke indications. If there is still smoke in the lavatory, the alarm LED (red) stays on. All smoke indications go away automatically when the smoke is gone.

Lavatory Fire Extinguisher System

The lavatory compartment is outfitted with a fire extinguisher bottle to extinguish fires in the waste compartment. The fire extinguisher is a bottle with two nozzles. The bottle contains pressurized Halon 1301 or equivalent fire extinguishing agent. When the temperature in the waste compartment reaches approximately 170 °F, the solder that seals the nozzles melt and the Halon is discharged. Weighing the bottle is often the only way to determine if the bottle is empty or full.

2. 中文翻译

载客量为 20 人及以上的飞机装有烟雾探测系统，用于监测盥洗室内是否有烟。烟雾指示会在驾驶舱点亮警告灯，或在盥洗室及乘务员站位给出警告灯或音响警告，使乘务员易于察觉。每个盥洗室都须装有自动喷放的固定式灭火器。烟雾探测器装在盥洗室顶板上。

盥洗室烟雾探测器系统

参见图 17-23。盥洗室烟雾探测器由 28 伏直流左/右主直流汇流条供电。若烟雾探测器的感应腔内有烟，报警 LED（红色）点亮。定时电路产生间歇接地。警告喇叭与盥洗室呼叫灯间歇工作。烟雾探测电路为继电器提供接地。继电器通电后，为中央监控系统（CMS）中的顶部电子组件（OEU）提供接地信号。该接口给出下列指示：盥洗室主呼叫灯闪烁，客舱系统控制面板（CSCP）和客舱区域控制面板（CACP）弹出窗口显示，盥洗室呼叫谐音响起。按压盥洗室呼叫复位电门或烟雾探测器中断电门可取消烟雾指示。若盥洗室内仍有烟，报警 LED（红色）保持点亮。烟散去后，所有烟雾指示自动消失。

盥洗室灭火系统

盥洗室隔间装有灭火瓶，用于扑灭废弃物舱内的火。该灭火器是一个带两个喷嘴的瓶子，内装加压的 Halon 1301 或等效灭火剂。当废弃物舱内温度达到约 170 °F 时，密封喷嘴的焊料熔化，哈龙随即喷放。称量瓶重往往是判断瓶内是空是满的唯一办法。

4. 语法解析

① 两个 that 定语从句叠加

原句：Airplanes that have a passenger capacity of 20 or more are equipped with a smoke detector system that monitors the lavatories for smoke. 拆解：主语 Airplanes 后接第一个 that 从句（that have a passenger capacity of 20 or more）作定语；谓语 are equipped with，宾语 a smoke detector system；第二个 that 从句（that monitors the lavatories for smoke）修饰 system。 要点：第一个 that 从句插在主语与谓语之间，把“飞机—装—系统”这条骨架隔开，读句时须先跳过它。monitor sth. for sth. 意为“监测某物以发现某物”，此处即监测盥洗室以发现烟。

② 并列谓语 + that 定语从句的修饰范围

原句：Smoke indications provide a warning light in the flight deck or provide a warning light or audible warning at the lavatory and at flight attendant stations that would be readily detected by a flight attendant. 拆解：主语 Smoke indications，由 or 并列两个谓语 provide... 与 provide...；第二个 provide 的宾语是 a warning light or audible warning；at the lavatory and at flight attendant stations 为地点状语；that would be readily detected by a flight attendant 为定语从句。 要点：that 从句紧跟 stations，形式上修饰 stations，但语义上限定的是前面的“警告灯或音响警告”，即这些指示须能被乘务员轻易察觉。or 在此连接两个等同的谓语结构，不是连接两个句子。

③ 祈使句 + 不定式目的状语

原句：Push the lavatory call reset switch or the smoke detector interrupt switch to cancel the smoke indications. 拆解：Push 为祈使句谓语，省略主语 you；宾语 the lavatory call reset switch or the smoke detector interrupt switch 由 or 连接两个可选电门；to cancel the smoke indications 为不定式作目的状语。 要点：技术手册大量使用祈使句给出操作步骤，中文常译成“按压……可……”。句末不定式表目的，说明按压电门要达到的结果，不要误读成宾语补足语。

④ when 时间状语从句 + that 定语从句 + and 并列两个谓语

原句：When the temperature in the waste compartment reaches approximately 170 °F, the solder that seals the nozzles melt and the Halon is discharged. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 the temperature，in the waste compartment 为后置定语，谓语 reaches，宾语 approximately 170 °F；主句主语 the solder，that seals the nozzles 为定语从句，谓语 melt 与后面的 the Halon is discharged 由 and 并列。 要点：主句由两个分句并列而成，前一个是主动语态（焊料熔化），后一个是被动语态（哈龙被喷放），主语不同，不可把 is discharged 当成 melt 的并列谓语。melt 在此用原形，与主语 the solder 搭配，属原文用法。

⑤ 动名词作主语 + if 宾语从句

原句：Weighing the bottle is often the only way to determine if the bottle is empty or full. 拆解：Weighing the bottle 为动名词短语作主语，谓语 is，表语 the only way，to determine... 为不定式作后置定语；if the bottle is empty or full 为宾语从句，作 determine 的宾语。 要点：动名词作主语时谓语用单数 is。determine if... 中的 if 不是“如果”而是“是否”，相当于 whether，这类宾语从句不能用 that 替换。the only way to do 是“做……的唯一办法”的固定搭配。
