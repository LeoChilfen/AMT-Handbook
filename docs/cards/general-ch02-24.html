maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alteration

通用 Ch 02 · maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alteration · 原文 PDF 第 66 页

1. 原文

There are seven different persons listed in this section who may sign RTS documentation:

  1. Certificated mechanic or holder of an inspection authorization (IA).
  1. Holder of a repair station certificate.
  1. Holder of an air carrier certificate.
  1. Certificated private pilot.
  1. Repairman certificated with a maintenance rating for light sport aircraft (LSA) only.
  1. Certificated sport pilot for preventive maintenance on an aircraft owned and or operated by them.

Note that although a certificated repairman is authorized to work on a product undergoing maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alterations (refer to 14 CFR part 43, section 43.3), they are not authorized to approve that product for RTS. They must make the appropriate maintenance record entry per the requirements of 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9 or 43.11.

Section 43.9—Content, form and disposition of maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, and alteration records (except inspection performed in accordance with parts 91 and 125, and sections 135.411(a)(1) and 135.419 of this chapter)

The first observation is that this section specifically excludes inspection entries (those are covered in 14 CFR part 43, section 43.11). This section deals exclusively with maintenance record entries.

The next observation is that the list of maintenance actions includes “preventive maintenance.” As stated in the explanation of 14 CFR part 43, section 43.3, a certificated pilot is authorized to perform preventive maintenance on the aircraft they own or operate. Therefore, remember that the pilot must make a record entry of the preventive maintenance they have accomplished. There are three distinct issues to be addressed in the maintenance entry and they answer the questions of “what?, when?, and who?” • What—a description of the work performed • When—the date the work was completed • Who—the name of the person who did the work if other than the person who approves the RTS —the signature, certificate number, and type of certificate of the person who is approving the work for RTS Note: Frequently, logbooks have a statement entered that ends something like this: “ … and is hereby returned to service. Joe Fixer A&P, Certificate #123456789.” As this section of the regulation currently reads, that part of the record entry is not required. Title 14 of the CFR part 43, section 43.9 clearly states that “the signature constitutes the approval for return to service only for the work performed.” Furthermore, the technician is only signing off the work they have done. Later, 14 CFR part 43, section 43.11 explains that an inspection write-up usually carries a broader scope of responsibility. This section is very clear that the entry completed in accordance with this section only holds the technician responsible for the service maintenance action they entered.

If the maintenance accomplished was a major repair or alteration, the work must be documented on FAA Form 337 and requires supporting approved data. If the maintenance action was a major repair and it was done by a certificated repair station, a signed copy of the completed customer work order accompanied by a signed maintenance release may be used in lieu of the FAA Form 337.

2. 中文翻译

本节列出了七类可以签署恢复使用（RTS）文件的人员：

  1. 持证维修技师，或持有检查授权（IA）者。
  1. 维修站证书持有人。
  1. 航空承运人证书持有人。
  1. 持证私用驾驶员。
  1. 仅限获轻型运动航空器（LSA）维修等级的持证维修人员。
  1. 持证运动驾驶员，可就其拥有和/或运行的航空器实施预防性维修。

需要注意：持证维修人员虽被授权对处于维修、预防性维修、重造或改装中的产品施工（见 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.3 条），但无权批准该产品恢复使用。他们必须按 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 条或第 43.11 条的要求，做出相应的维修记录条目。

第 43.9 条——维修、预防性维修、重造与改装记录的内容、格式与保存（依第 91 部、第 125 部以及本章第 135.411(a)(1) 条和第 135.419 条实施的检查除外）。

第一个要点是：本条明确把检查记录排除在外（检查记录由 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.11 条规定）。本条只处理维修记录条目。

第二个要点是：维修动作清单中包括“预防性维修”。正如对 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.3 条的解释所述，持证驾驶员被授权对其拥有或运行的航空器实施预防性维修。因此要记住：驾驶员必须就其完成的预防性维修做出记录条目。维修条目须处理三个不同问题，即回答“做了什么？什么时候做？谁做的？”：• 做了什么——对所做工作的描述；• 什么时候——工作完成的日期；• 谁做的——若施工人不是批准恢复使用的人，则写明施工人姓名；再写明批准该工作恢复使用的人的签名、证书编号与证书种类。注：日志本中常见这样的结尾：“……并特此恢复使用。Joe Fixer A&P，证书号 123456789。”按本条现行文本，记录条目中并不要求写这一部分。14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 条明确规定：“签名仅对所完成的工作构成恢复使用的批准。”此外，技师只是为自己做过的工作签字放行。后文 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.11 条说明，检查记录通常承担更宽的责任范围。本条非常明确：依本条做出的条目，只让技师对其填报的维修动作负责。

如果所完成的维修属于重大修理或改装，则工作必须记录在 FAA 表格 337 上，并需有批准数据支持。如果维修动作是重大修理且由持证维修站完成，则可用经签署的完整客户工单副本连同经签署的维修放行文件，代替 FAA 表格 337。

4. 语法解析

① although 让步从句中的 be authorized to（肯定与否定并列）

原句：Note that although a certificated repairman is authorized to work on a product undergoing maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alterations (refer to 14 CFR part 43, section 43.3), they are not authorized to approve that product for RTS.

拆解：祈使句 Note that … 引出宾语从句；从句内 although 引导让步状语从句，is authorized to work on … 为被动 + 不定式，product undergoing maintenance 是“名词 + 现在分词短语”作后置定语；主句 they are not authorized to approve that product for RTS 与之形成“能做/不能做”的对照；括号内为交叉引用。

要点：they 在此指前文的 a certificated repairman（单数），是当代英语用复数代词指代单数不定性别的常见做法，翻译时按“他们／其”处理即可。be authorized to do 与 be authorized to approve 配对使用，凸显“有权施工”不等于“有权放行”，这是本条最关键的区分。

② As … currently reads（as 引导的方式状语）

原句：As this section of the regulation currently reads, that part of the record entry is not required.

拆解：As 引导方式状语从句，意为“按照……目前的表述”；主语 this section of the regulation，谓语 reads（此处 read 作不及物动词，指“条文表述为”）；主句 that part of the record entry is not required 为一般现在时被动。

要点：read 在法规语境中指“条文写的是……”，如 the section reads “…”（该条行文为……）。as … reads 是解读规章时的固定说法，提示所引内容是现行文本，可能随修订变化；不要误译为“正如该条所读”。

③ if 条件句 + 情态被动 + in lieu of

原句：If the maintenance action was a major repair and it was done by a certificated repair station, a signed copy of the completed customer work order accompanied by a signed maintenance release may be used in lieu of the FAA Form 337.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，内部由 and 并列 was a major repair 与 was done by … 两个谓语（后者为被动）；主句主语 a signed copy of the completed customer work order；accompanied by a signed maintenance release 是过去分词短语作后置定语；谓语 may be used 为情态被动；in lieu of 引导替代对象。

要点：in lieu of = instead of，是法规里的正式替代说法，后接名词。主句主语被两个过去分词定语拉长（signed、completed、accompanied），翻译时先译中心词“客户工单副本”，再把修饰语依次补上。
