Strain

通用 Ch 05 · Strain · 原文 PDF 第 164 页

1. 原文

If the stress acting on an object is great enough, it can cause the object to change its shape or to become distorted. One characteristic of matter is that it tends to be elastic, meaning it can be forced out of shape when a force is applied and then return to its original shape when the force is removed. When an object becomes distorted by an applied force, the object is said to be strained.

On turbine engine test cells, the thrust of the engine is typically measured by what are called strain gages. When the force, or thrust, of the engine is pulling out against the strain gages, the amount of distortion is measured and then translated into the appropriate thrust reading.

A deflecting beam style of torque wrench uses the strain on the drive end of the wrench and the resulting distortion of the beam to indicate the amount of torque on a bolt or nut. [Figure 5-24]

2. 中文翻译

如果作用在物体上的应力足够大，就能使物体改变形状或发生畸变。物质的一个特性是它趋向于弹性，即受力时可被压得变形，力卸除后又恢复原状。当物体因受力而畸变时，就说该物体产生了应变。

在涡轮发动机试车台上，发动机的推力通常用所谓的应变片来测量。当发动机的力（推力）向外拉伸应变片时，测出变形量，再换算成相应的推力读数。

挠性梁式扭矩扳手利用扳手驱动端的应变以及梁随之产生的挠曲，来指示螺栓或螺母上的扭矩大小。（图 5-24）

4. 语法解析

① the stress acting on an object（现在分词作后置定语 + cause sb to do）

原句：If the stress acting on an object is great enough, it can cause the object to change its shape or to become distorted.

拆解：If 从句主语 the stress，acting on an object 为现在分词短语作后置定语；主句 it can cause the object to change … or to become …，两个并列不定式作宾补。

要点：acting on 是主动分词，表示「作用于物体的」应力，不是被动。cause sb to do 的 to 必须保留；change its shape 与 become distorted 是近义并列。

② One characteristic of matter is that …（表语从句 + meaning 分词状语）

原句：One characteristic of matter is that it tends to be elastic, meaning it can be forced out of shape when a force is applied and then return to its original shape when the force is removed.

拆解：is that … 为表语从句；meaning … 是现在分词短语作解释性状语（= which means）；从句内 can be forced 与 (can) return 并列，各带一个 when 时间状语。

要点：meaning 这种用法在技术英语里很常见，可译为「即／也就是说」。force 在此是动词「迫使」，force sth out of shape = 使……变形。第二个并列谓语 return 前省略了 can。

③ measured by what are called strain gages（介词 + what 从句）

原句：On turbine engine test cells, the thrust of the engine is typically measured by what are called strain gages.

拆解：On turbine engine test cells 为地点状语；主语 the thrust of the engine，谓语 is typically measured 被动；by what are called strain gages 中 what 引导介词宾语从句，what = the devices that。

要点：介词后不能直接接 that 从句，要用 what；what are called … =「所谓的……」。measure by = 用……测量。thrust 与 force 在这里同义。

④ the object is said to be strained（be said to be + 过去分词）

原句：When an object becomes distorted by an applied force, the object is said to be strained.

拆解：When 从句；主句 the object is said to be strained，is said 为被动，to be strained 是主语补足语。

要点：be said to be 是「据说／被称为」，技术定义常用「is said to be + 形容词／过去分词」。strained 已形容词化，表示「处于应变状态的」，不是被动动作。
