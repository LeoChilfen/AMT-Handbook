Aircraft Hydraulic Systems

机体 Ch 12 · Aircraft Hydraulic Systems · 原文 PDF 第 680 页

1. 原文

The word “hydraulics” is based on the Greek word for water and originally meant the study of the physical behavior of water at rest and in motion. Today, the meaning has been expanded to include the physical behavior of all liquids, including hydraulic fluid. Hydraulic systems are not new to aviation. Early aircraft had hydraulic brake systems. As aircraft became more sophisticated, newer systems with hydraulic power were developed.

Hydraulic systems in aircraft provide a means for the operation of aircraft components. The operation of landing gear, flaps, flight control surfaces, and brakes is largely accomplished with hydraulic power systems. Hydraulic system complexity varies from small aircraft that require fluid only for manual operation of the wheel brakes to large transport aircraft where the systems are large and complex. To achieve the necessary redundancy and reliability, the system may consist of several subsystems. Each subsystem has a power generating device (pump), reservoir, accumulator, heat exchanger, filtering system, etc. System operating pressure may vary from a couple hundred pounds per square inch (psi) in small aircraft and rotorcraft to 5,000 psi in large transports. Hydraulic systems have many advantages as power sources for operating various aircraft units; they combine the advantages of light weight, ease of installation, simplification of inspection, and minimum maintenance requirements. Hydraulic operations are also almost 100 percent efficient, with only negligible loss due to fluid friction.

2. 中文翻译

“液压”（hydraulics）一词源自希腊语中表示“水”的词，原意是研究水在静止和运动状态下的物理行为。如今，这一含义已扩展到包括所有液体的物理行为，液压油（hydraulic fluid）也在其中。液压系统对航空来说并不新鲜。早期的飞机就装有液压刹车系统。随着飞机日益精密复杂，采用液压动力的更新系统也相继发展起来。

飞机上的液压系统为飞机部件的操纵提供了一种手段。起落架、襟翼、飞行操纵面和刹车的操纵，很大程度上是由液压动力系统完成的。液压系统的复杂程度各不相同：从小型飞机只需要液压油来手动操作机轮刹车，到大型运输机系统庞大而复杂。为获得必要的冗余和可靠性，系统可能由若干子系统组成。每个子系统都有动力产生装置（泵）、油箱、蓄压器、热交换器、过滤系统等。系统工作压力，从中小型飞机和旋翼机的几百磅每平方英寸（psi），到大型运输机的 5,000 psi 不等。作为操纵各种飞机部件的动力源，液压系统有许多优点：它兼具重量轻、易于安装、检查简便和维护要求最低等优势。液压操作的效率也几乎达到 100%，仅因液体摩擦而有可忽略的损失。

4. 语法解析

① 并列谓语与句尾后置定语

原句：The word “hydraulics” is based on the Greek word for water and originally meant the study of the physical behavior of water at rest and in motion. 拆解：主语 The word “hydraulics”；谓语由并列的两部分构成——is based on（被动结构，介词 on 引出来源）和 originally meant（一般过去时）；宾语 the study of the physical behavior of water 后接 at rest and in motion 作后置定语，说明“静止”与“运动”两种状态。 要点：and 连接的是两个并列谓语（is based on / meant），不是两个宾语，所以句子只有一套主谓。be based on 在这里是“源自”，不是“建立在……之上”的动作。at rest and in motion 修饰 water，译作“水在静止和运动状态下的”才通顺。

② vary from A to B 两端各带定语从句

原句：Hydraulic system complexity varies from small aircraft that require fluid only for manual operation of the wheel brakes to large transport aircraft where the systems are large and complex. 拆解：主语 Hydraulic system complexity，谓语 varies，from A to B 表示变化范围；A 为 small aircraft，由 that 引导的定语从句修饰（that 在从句中作主语）；B 为 large transport aircraft，由 where 引导的定语从句修饰（where = in which，指“在这类飞机上”）。 要点：from ... to ... 的两个端点各挂一个定语从句，句子因此拉得很长，读到 to large transport aircraft 才回到主句框架。that 从句缺主语、where 从句缺地点状语，可据此判断关系词。中文宜拆成“小型飞机……，而大型运输机……”的对比句式。

③ 句首不定式作目的状语

原句：To achieve the necessary redundancy and reliability, the system may consist of several subsystems. 拆解：句首 To achieve ... 为不定式短语作目的状语，其逻辑主语就是主句主语 the system；主句主语 the system，情态动词 may + 动词原形 consist of，宾语 several subsystems。 要点：不定式作目的状语置于句首时，通常用逗号与主句隔开。consist of 是不及物动词短语，没有被动形式，不能写成 be consisted of——这是技术写作里的高频错误。

④ 分号连接的两个独立分句

原句：Hydraulic systems have many advantages as power sources for operating various aircraft units; they combine the advantages of light weight, ease of installation, simplification of inspection, and minimum maintenance requirements. 拆解：分号连接两个并列分句。前句主干 Hydraulic systems have many advantages，as power sources 说明“作为动力源”，for operating various aircraft units 说明用途；后句主语 they 指代液压系统，谓语 combine，宾语 the advantages of 后接四个并列成分。 要点：分号前后都是能独立成句的完整分句，不能用逗号代替分号。of 后面四项的语法形式并不整齐（三个名词 + 一个名词短语），翻译时统一处理成“重量轻、易于安装、检查简便、维护要求低”。

⑤ with 短语作伴随状语

原句：Hydraulic operations are also almost 100 percent efficient, with only negligible loss due to fluid friction. 拆解：主句为主系表结构 are ... efficient，程度状语 almost 100 percent 修饰 efficient；逗号后 with only negligible loss 为 with 介词短语作伴随状语，due to fluid friction 为形容词短语后置修饰 loss。 要点：这里的 with 短语补充说明“效率高到什么程度”，不构成独立分句，不能改写成并列句。due to 是复合介词，后接名词短语表原因，不能接句子。negligible 是“小到可忽略”，并不等于“没有”。
