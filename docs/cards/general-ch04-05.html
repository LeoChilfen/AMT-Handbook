Types of Drawings

通用 Ch 04 · Types of Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 126 页

1. 原文

Drawings must give information such as size and shape of the object and all its parts, specifications for material to be used, how the material is to be finished, how the parts are to be assembled, and any other information essential to making and assembling the object. Drawings may be divided into three classes: detail, assembly, and installation.

2. 中文翻译

图纸必须给出诸如此类的信息：物品及其全部零件的尺寸与形状、所用材料的技术要求、材料如何加工（表面处理）、零件如何装配，以及制作和装配该物品所必需的任何其他信息。图纸可分为三类：零件图（detail drawing）、装配图（assembly drawing）和安装图（installation drawing）。

3. 专业词汇

英文 词性 中文 本节语境
specification n. 技术要求、规格 specifications for material to be used，规定用何种材料
finish v. 加工、表面处理 how the material is to be finished，指材料的加工与光洁处理方式
assemble v. 装配 how the parts are to be assembled，把零件组合成组件
detail drawing n. 零件图 三类图纸之一，描述单个零件（详见下一节）
assembly drawing n. 装配图 三类图纸之一，描述由两个及以上零件组成的物件
installation drawing n. 安装图 三类图纸之一，给出装机后最终位置的信息
essential to adj. phr. 对……必不可少 any other information essential to making and assembling the object

4. 语法解析

① Drawings must give information such as …（长宾语列举 + such as）

原句：Drawings must give information such as size and shape of the object and all its parts, specifications for material to be used, how the material is to be finished, how the parts are to be assembled, and any other information essential to making and assembling the object.

拆解：主干 Drawings must give information；such as 后并列五个成分：size and shape …、specifications for material to be used、how the material is to be finished、how the parts are to be assembled、any other information essential to …。其中两个 how 从句是名词性从句，作 such as 的并列项。

要点：such as 引出的列举并不穷尽，但末项前仍用 and 收尾。material to be used 中不定式被动 to be used 后置作定语。such as 后并列名词性从句属手册常见的重列举结构，读时先抓主干，再按逗号切分五个项。

② is to be finished / are to be assembled（be to do 表规定与安排）

原句：specifications for material to be used, how the material is to be finished, how the parts are to be assembled

拆解：be to + 动词原形表示「按计划、按要求应当」；此处用被动 is to be finished 与 are to be assembled，因为 material 与 parts 是动作的承受者。

要点：be to do 在技术文件中常等于 shall be done，表规定而非单纯将来。注意 to 后是 be + 过去分词，漏掉 be 写成 is to finished 是常见错误。单复数随主语变化：material 用 is，parts 用 are。

③ essential to making and assembling（介词 to + 动名词）

原句：any other information essential to making and assembling the object

拆解：essential to 是「形容词 + 介词」固定搭配，to 为介词，后接并列动名词 making and assembling；整个 essential to … 短语作后置定语修饰 information。

要点：essential to / necessary for / important to 后接名词或动名词，不接不定式，写成 essential to make 是常见错误。形容词短语修饰名词须后置，译成汉语则前置：「对制作和装配该物品必不可少的其他信息」。
