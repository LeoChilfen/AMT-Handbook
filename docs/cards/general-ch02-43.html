Types of Airworthiness Directives (AD)

通用 Ch 02 · Types of Airworthiness Directives (AD) · 原文 PDF 第 76 页

1. 原文

Three types of ADs are issued:

• Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM), followed by a Final Rule • Final Rule; Request for Comments • Emergency ADs The standard AD process is to issue an NPRM followed by a Final Rule. After an unsafe condition is discovered, a proposed solution is published as an NPRM and solicits public comment on the proposed action. After the comment period closes, the final rule is prepared, taking into account all substantive comments received with the rule perhaps being changed as warranted by the comments. The preamble to the final rule AD provides response to the substantive comments or states there were no comments received.

In certain cases, the critical nature of an unsafe condition may warrant the immediate adoption of a rule without prior notice and solicitation of comments. This is an exception to the standard process. If time for the terminating action to be accomplished is too short to allow for public comment (that is, less than 60 days), then a finding of impracticability is justified for the terminating action, and it can be issued as an immediately adopted rule. The immediately adopted rule is published in the Federal Register with a request for comments. The Final Rule AD may be changed later if substantive comments are received.

An Emergency AD is issued when an unsafe condition exists that requires immediate action by an owner/operator. The intent of an Emergency AD is to rapidly correct an urgent safety deficiency. An Emergency AD may be distributed by fax, letter, or other methods. It is issued and effective to only the people who actually receive it. This is known as “actual notice.” All known owners and operators of affected U.S.-registered aircraft, or those aircraft that are known to have an affected product installed, are sent a copy of an Emergency AD. To make the AD effective to all persons, a follow up publication of the Final Rule AD in the Federal Register is critical. This Final Rule AD must be identical to the Emergency AD and is normally published in the Federal Register within 30 days of the Emergency AD issue.

2. 中文翻译

发布的 AD 有三种：

• 拟议规则制定通知（NPRM），其后为最终规则 • 最终规则；征求评论 • 紧急 AD。标准的 AD 程序是先发布 NPRM，再发布最终规则。发现不安全状况后，拟议的解决方案以 NPRM 形式公布，并就拟议措施征求公众评论。评论期结束后编制最终规则，编制时考虑收到的所有实质性评论，规则可能按评论的要求作相应修改。最终规则 AD 的前言部分对实质性评论作出答复，或说明未收到评论。

在某些情况下，不安全状况的严重性质可能使得有必要不经事先通知、不征求意见而立即通过规则。这是对标准程序的例外。如果完成终止性措施的时间过短，无法安排公众评论（即少于 60 天），则可以就该终止性措施作出不可行性认定，并以立即通过的规则形式发布。立即通过的规则在《联邦公报》上公布，同时征求评论。若收到实质性评论，该最终规则 AD 可在此后修改。

当存在需要机主/运营人立即采取行动的不安全状况时，发布紧急 AD。紧急 AD 的意图是迅速纠正紧急的安全缺陷。紧急 AD 可以通过传真、信函或其他方式分发，其发布和生效只针对实际收到它的人，这称为「实际通知」。所有已知受影响的美国登记航空器的机主和运营人，或已知装有受影响产品的航空器的机主和运营人，都会收到一份紧急 AD。为使 AD 对所有人生效，必须在《联邦公报》上后续公布最终规则 AD，这一点至关重要。该最终规则 AD 必须与紧急 AD 完全一致，通常在紧急 AD 发布后 30 天内在《联邦公报》上公布。

4. 语法解析

① 现在分词短语作状语 + with 复合结构

原句：After the comment period closes, the final rule is prepared, taking into account all substantive comments received with the rule perhaps being changed as warranted by the comments.

拆解：After the comment period closes 是时间状语从句；主句 the final rule is prepared 为被动；taking into account all substantive comments received 是现在分词短语作状语，说明编制规则时的方式；received 是过去分词后置修饰 comments；with the rule perhaps being changed as warranted by the comments 是 with 复合结构（with + 名词 + 现在分词被动式），as warranted by the comments 中 as 后接过去分词，意为「按评论所需」。

要点：这种「被动主句 + 现在分词状语」在技术文本中很常见，taking into account 的逻辑主语跟主句主语走，译作「……时兼顾/考虑……」。with 复合结构常译成「同时……可能……」。本句信息密度很高，先抓主干 the final rule is prepared，再逐层挂上三个附加成分，比顺译更清楚。

② too…to… 与 for + 名词 + 不定式的被动式

原句：If time for the terminating action to be accomplished is too short to allow for public comment (that is, less than 60 days), then a finding of impracticability is justified for the terminating action, and it can be issued as an immediately adopted rule.

拆解：If 引导条件从句，从句主语是 time，for the terminating action to be accomplished 是「for + 名词 + 不定式被动」结构，作 time 的后置定语；too short to allow for public comment 是 too…to… 结构；主句由 then 引出两个并列分句：a finding of impracticability is justified for the terminating action 与 it can be issued as an immediately adopted rule。

要点：too…to… 本身含否定意义（太……以致不能……），不要再加 not，也不要译成「太短而允许评论」。for + 名词 + to do 是表达「让某事完成所需的时间」的常用结构，此处用被动 to be accomplished 因为措施是被完成的。

③ 时间状语从句中夹带定语从句

原句：An Emergency AD is issued when an unsafe condition exists that requires immediate action by an owner/operator.

拆解：主句 An Emergency AD is issued 为被动；when 引导时间状语从句；that requires immediate action by an owner/operator 是定语从句，先行词是 an unsafe condition。

要点：定语从句被 when 从句隔在后面，that 要回溯到 an unsafe condition，而不是 an Emergency AD。require sth by sb 表示「某事需要某人来做」，介词用 by；译作「需要机主/运营人立即采取行动的不安全状况」时，中文习惯把定语放到名词前面。
