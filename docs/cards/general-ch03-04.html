Division of Whole Numbers

1. 原文

Division is the process of finding how many times one number (called the divisor) is contained in another number (called the dividend). The result is the quotient, and any amount left over is called the remainder.

quotient divisor dividend Example: 218 landing gear bolts need to be divided between 7 aircraft. How many bolts will each aircraft receive?

Place Value Ten Thousands Thousands Hundreds Tens Ones 3 5 2 6 9 1 2 7 4 9 35 shown as 269 shown as 12,749 shown as 31 7 218 − 21 8 − 7 1 In this case, there are 31 bolts for each of the seven aircraft with one extra remaining.

2. 中文翻译

除法是这样一种过程：求一个数（称为除数）中包含另一个数（称为被除数）多少次。结果称为商，剩下的量称为余数。

例：现需把 218 个起落架螺栓分配给 7 架航空器。每架航空器将分到多少个螺栓？

31　7 218 − 21 8 − 7 1　在这种情况下，七架航空器每架分到 31 个螺栓，还剩 1 个。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作后置定语：called the divisor

原句：Division is the process of finding how many times one number (called the divisor) is contained in another number (called the dividend).

拆解：表语 the process 后接 of finding …（介词 + 动名词）；finding 的宾语是宾语从句 how many times one number is contained in another number；两个括号里的 called the divisor / called the dividend 都是过去分词短语作后置定语，可还原为 which is called …；is contained in 是被动。

要点：called + 名词 = 「称为……的」，是最省事的术语引入方式，等于 which is called。注意 contain 在此不是「包含容器」而是「含有」，be contained in 译作「被包含在……中」，不要译成「被装着」。

② need to be + 过去分词

原句：218 landing gear bolts need to be divided between 7 aircraft.

拆解：主语 218 landing gear bolts（复数），谓语 need to be divided（need + 不定式的被动式），between 7 aircraft 表示分配的范围。

要点：need to be done 是技术文本里表示「需要被处理」的常用句式，主语是动作的承受者；也可写 need dividing（need + 动名词，主动形式表被动含义）。divide A between B 指在若干对象之间分配，between 后用复数。

③ with 复合结构作伴随状语：with one extra remaining

原句：In this case, there are 31 bolts for each of the seven aircraft with one extra remaining.

拆解：there are 存在句；for each of the seven aircraft 是范围状语；with one extra remaining 是 with + 名词 + 现在分词构成的复合结构，作伴随状语，remaining 与 one extra 是主动关系。

要点：with 复合结构里，名词与后面的分词若是主动关系用 -ing（remaining），被动关系用 -ed（with the work done）。译成中文常处理成「还剩下……」的并列小句，比硬译「带着一个多余的剩余着」自然。
