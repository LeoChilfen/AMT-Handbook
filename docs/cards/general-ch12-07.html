Electromagnetism

1. 原文

In 1820, the Danish physicist, Hans Christian Oersted, discovered that the needle of a compass brought near a current carrying conductor would be deflected. When the current flow stopped, the compass needle returned to its original position. This important discovery demonstrated a relationship between electricity and magnetism that led to the electromagnet and to many of the inventions on which modern industry is based.

Oersted discovered that the magnetic field had no connection with the conductor in which the electrons were flowing, because the conductor was made of nonmagnetic copper. The electrons moving through the wire created the magnetic field around the conductor. Since a magnetic field accompanies a charged particle, the greater the current flow, the greater the magnetic field. A series of concentric circles around the conductor represent the field, which if all the lines were shown would appear more as a continuous cylinder of such circles around the conductor.

As long as current flows in the conductor, the lines of force remain around it. If a small current flows through the conductor, there will be a line of force extending out to circle A. If the current flow is increased, the line of force increases in size to circle B, and a further increase in current expands it to circle C. As the original line (circle) of force expands from circle A to B, a new line of force appears at circle A. As the current flow increases, the number of circles of force increases, expanding the outer circles farther from the surface of the current carrying conductor.

If the current flow is a steady nonvarying direct current, the magnetic field remains stationary. When the current stops, the magnetic field collapses and the magnetism around the conductor disappears.

A compass needle is used to demonstrate the direction of the magnetic field around a current carrying conductor. A compass needle is positioned at right angles to, and approximately one inch from, a current carrying conductor. If no current were flowing, the North seeking end of the compass needle would point toward the earth’s magnetic pole. When current flows, the needle lines itself up at right angles to a radius drawn from the conductor. Since the compass needle is a small magnet, with lines of force extending from South to North inside the metal, it turns until the direction of these lines agrees with the direction of the lines of force around the conductor. As the compass needle is moved around the conductor, it maintains itself in a position at right angles to the conductor, indicating that the magnetic field around a current carrying conductor is circular. When the direction of current flow through the conductor is reversed, the compass needle points in the opposite direction, indicating the magnetic field has reversed its direction.

A method used to determine the direction of the lines of force when the direction of the current flow is known is shown in with the thumb pointing in the direction of current flow, the fingers will be wrapped around the conductor in the same direction as the lines of the magnetic field. This is called the left-hand rule.

Although it has been stated that the lines of force have direction, this should not be construed to mean that the lines have motion in a circular direction around the conductor. Although the lines of force tend to act in a clockwise or counterclockwise direction, they are not revolving around the conductor.

Since current flows from negative to positive, many illustrations indicate current direction with a dot symbol on the end of the conductor when the electrons are flowing toward and a plus sign when the current is flowing away from the observer.

When a wire is bent into a loop and an electric current flows through it, the left-hand rule remains valid.

If the wire is coiled into two loops, many of the lines of force become large enough to include both loops. go through the loops in the same direction, circle around the outside of the two coils, and come in at the opposite end.

When a wire contains many such loops, it is called a coil. The lines of force form a pattern through all the loops causing a high concentration of flux lines through the center of the coil.

In a coil made from loops of a conductor, many of the lines of force are dissipated between the loops of the coil. By placing a soft iron bar inside the coil, the lines of force are concentrated in the center of the coil, since soft iron has a greater permeability than air. This combination of an iron core in a coil of wire loops, or turns, is called an electromagnet, since the poles (ends) of the coil possess the characteristics of a bar magnet.

The addition of the soft iron core does two things for the current carrying coil. First, the magnetic flux is increased. Second, the flux lines are more highly concentrated.

When direct current flows through the coil, the core becomes magnetized with the same polarity (location of North and South poles) as the coil would have without the core. If the current is reversed, the polarity is also reversed.

The polarity of the electromagnet is determined by the left-hand rule in the same manner as the polarity of the coil without the core was determined. If the coil is grasped in the left hand in such a manner that the fingers curve around the coil in the direction of electron flow (minus to plus), the thumb points in the direction of the North pole.

The strength of the magnetic field of the electromagnet can be increased by either increasing the flow of current or the number of loops in the wire. Doubling the current flow approximately doubles the strength of the field. In a similar manner, doubling the number of loops approximately doubles magnetic field strength. Finally, the type of metal in the core is a factor in the field strength of the electromagnet.

A soft iron bar is attracted to either pole of a permanent magnet and, likewise, is attracted by a current carrying coil. The lines of force extend through the soft iron, magnetizing it by induction and pulling the iron bar toward the coil. If the bar is free to move, it is drawn into the coil to a position near the center where the field is strongest.

Electromagnets are used in electrical instruments, motors, generators, relays, and other devices. Some electromagnetic devices operate on the principle that an iron core held away from the center of a coil is rapidly pulled into a center position when the coil is energized. This principle is used in the solenoid, also called solenoid switch or relay, in which the iron core is spring-loaded off center and moves to complete a circuit when the coil is energized.

2. 中文翻译

1820 年，丹麦物理学家汉斯·克里斯蒂安·奥斯特发现，把罗盘针移近载流导体，针会发生偏转。电流停止流动后，罗盘针回到原来的位置。这一重要发现证明了电与磁之间的关系，这种关系促成了电磁铁的发明，也促成了现代工业所赖以建立的许多发明。

奥斯特发现，磁场与电子在其中流动的导体本身没有关系，因为该导体是用非磁性的铜制成的。穿过导线的电子在导体周围产生了磁场。由于磁场伴随着带电粒子，所以电流越大，磁场越强。围绕导体的一系列同心圆表示这个场；如果把所有力线都画出来，看起来就更像围绕导体的一个由这类圆构成的连续圆柱。

只要电流在导体中流动，力线就保持在其周围。如果有一个小电流通过导体，就会有一条力线一直扩展到圆 A。如果电流增大，力线就增大到圆 B，电流再增大，就把它扩展到圆 C。当原来那条力线（圆）从圆 A 扩展到 B 时，新的力线在圆 A 处出现。随着电流增大，力线圈数增加，并把外圈的圆扩展到离载流导体表面更远的地方。

如果电流是稳定不变的直流电，磁场保持静止。电流停止时，磁场塌缩，导体周围的磁性消失。

罗盘针用来演示载流导体周围磁场的方向。把罗盘针放置在载流导体的垂直方向上，距导线约一英寸。如果没有电流流过，罗盘针的指北端会指向地球的磁极。电流流过时，针会自行排列成与从导体引出的半径成直角的方向。由于罗盘针是一个小磁铁，其力线在金属内部由南极延伸到北极，因此它转动到这些线的方向与导体周围力线的方向一致为止。把罗盘针绕导体移动时，它始终保持在与导体成直角的位置，这表明载流导体周围的磁场是圆形的。当通过导体中的电流方向反转时，罗盘针指向相反方向，表明磁场也反转了方向。

用来在已知电流方向时判定力线方向的一种方法如图所示：拇指指向电流方向，其余四指则沿磁场力线的同一方向环绕导体。这称为左手定则。

尽管前面说力线有方向，但这不应被理解为力线沿圆周方向绕导体运动。虽然力线倾向于按顺时针或逆时针方向起作用，但它们并不绕导体旋转。

由于电流从负极流向正极，许多图示用导体端部的圆点符号表示电子向观察者流来，用加号表示电流背离观察者流去。

当把导线弯成一个环并通以电流时，左手定则仍然适用。

如果把导线绕成两个环，许多力线会大到足以包含两个环。这些力线沿同一方向穿过各个环，绕过两个线圈的外侧，再从相反的一端进入。

当一根导线包含许多这样的环时，就称为线圈。力线穿过所有环形成一种图案，使线圈中心有很高的磁通线集中度。

在用导线环制成的线圈中，许多力线在线圈各环之间散失掉。在线圈内放置一根软铁棒，力线就会集中在线圈中心，因为软铁的磁导率比空气大。这种把铁芯放在导线环（或称匝）线圈中的组合称为电磁铁，因为线圈的端部（磁极）具有条形磁铁的特性。

加入软铁芯对载流线圈有两方面作用。第一，磁通增加。第二，磁通线更加高度集中。

当直流电流过线圈时，铁芯被磁化，其极性与没有铁芯时线圈的极性（北极与南极的位置）相同。如果电流反向，极性也反向。

电磁铁的极性用左手定则判定，判定方式与判定无铁芯线圈极性时相同。如果用左手握住线圈，使四指沿电子流动方向（从负极到正极）绕线圈弯曲，拇指所指方向就是北极的方向。

电磁铁磁场的强度可以通过增大电流或增加导线环数来增强。电流加倍，磁场强度大约也加倍。同样，环数加倍，磁场强度大约也加倍。最后，铁芯所用金属的种类也是影响电磁铁磁场强度的因素。

软铁棒被永磁铁的两极吸引，同样也被载流线圈吸引。力线穿过软铁，通过感应使软铁磁化，并把铁棒拉向线圈。如果铁棒可以自由移动，它就会被吸进线圈，停在磁场最强的中心附近。

电磁铁用于电气仪表、电动机、发电机、继电器及其他装置中。有些电磁装置的工作原理是：把铁芯保持在偏离线圈中心的位置，线圈通电时铁芯会迅速被拉向中心位置。这一原理用于螺线管，也称电磁开关或继电器；其中铁芯靠弹簧保持在偏心位置，线圈通电时铁芯移动以接通电路。

4. 语法解析

① that 宾语从句 + 过去分词后置定语 + would

原句：In 1820, the Danish physicist, Hans Christian Oersted, discovered that the needle of a compass brought near a current carrying conductor would be deflected. 拆解：主句 In 1820… discovered that…；the Danish physicist, Hans Christian Oersted 为同位语；that 引导宾语从句，从句主语 the needle of a compass 带过去分词短语 brought near… 作后置定语；从句谓语 would be deflected 为过去将来时的被动。 要点：brought near… 修饰 needle 而不是 compass，判断依据是语义「被移近导体的针」；would 在此表过去的预测或反复出现的结果，不表虚拟。current carrying 是名词 + 动名词构成的复合定语，相当于 which carries current。

② 定语从句 + on which 前置介词

原句：This important discovery demonstrated a relationship between electricity and magnetism that led to the electromagnet and to many of the inventions on which modern industry is based. 拆解：主句 This important discovery demonstrated a relationship…；that led to… 为定语从句修饰 relationship，其中 led to 后接两个并列宾语 the electromagnet 与 many of the inventions；on which modern industry is based 为修饰 inventions 的定语从句，on 提前。 要点：be based on 的介词 on 提到关系词前即成为 on which，正式书面语常用；翻译时还原语序「现代工业所赖以建立的发明」。两个 to 并列不能省第二个，否则会把 the inventions 误当成 led to 的唯一宾语。

③ which 非限制性定语从句中插入条件状语从句

原句：A series of concentric circles around the conductor represent the field, which if all the lines were shown would appear more as a continuous cylinder of such circles around the conductor. 拆解：主句 A series of… represent the field；which 引导非限制性定语从句，从句中又插入 if all the lines were shown 条件状语从句，主句谓语 would appear，which 在从句中作主语。 要点：定语从句内嵌条件从句时语序是「which + if 从句 + 主句谓语」，读句时先把 which 与 would appear 连起来；were shown 是虚拟条件句的过去式形式，表示与现在事实相反的假设，主句用 would + 动词原形。

④ 虚拟条件句（were + would do）

原句：If no current were flowing, the North seeking end of the compass needle would point toward the earth’s magnetic pole. 拆解：If 引导的虚拟条件从句用 were flowing（过去进行形式的虚拟），主句用 would point；the North seeking end 为复合定语修饰 end。 要点：与现在事实相反的假设，从句用过去式（be 动词一律用 were），主句用 would/could/might + 原形；这里用 were flowing 强调「假设正在流动」，比 were 更具体。North seeking 是「指北的」，名词 + 现在分词构成复合形容词。

⑤ with 独立主格 + until 状语从句

原句：Since the compass needle is a small magnet, with lines of force extending from South to North inside the metal, it turns until the direction of these lines agrees with the direction of the lines of force around the conductor. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句；with lines of force extending… 为 with 复合结构（独立主格），lines of force 与 extending 是主动关系；主句 it turns until…，until 引导时间状语从句，agrees with 为从句谓语。 要点：with + 名词 + 现在分词表主动、进行，若为被动关系则用过去分词（with the work done）；until 从句用一般现在时表将来，不用 will。agree with 在此是「与……一致」，不是「同意某人」。

⑥ 动名词短语作主语

原句：Doubling the current flow approximately doubles the strength of the field. 拆解：主语是动名词短语 Doubling the current flow，谓语是 doubles（第三人称单数，与动名词短语整体一致），宾语 the strength of the field，approximately 为副词作状语。 要点：同一个 double 在句中先作动名词（加倍）后作谓语动词（使……加倍），是典型的词性转换；动名词作主语时谓语用单数，不能因为后面有 the current flow 就误用复数。
