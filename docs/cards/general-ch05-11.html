1. 原文

The study of machines, both simple and complex, can be seen as a study of the energy of mechanical work. This is true because all machines transfer input energy, or the work done on the machine, to output energy, or the work done by the machine.

Work, in the mechanical sense of the term, is done when a resistance is overcome by force acting through a measurable distance. Two factors are involved: (1) force and (2) movement through a distance. As an example, suppose a small aircraft is stuck in the snow. Two men push against it for a period of time, but the aircraft does not move. According to the technical definition, no work had been done when the men were pushing against the aircraft. By definition, work is accomplished only when an object is displaced some distance against a resistive force. To calculate work, the following formula is used:

Work = Force (F) × distance (d)

In the English system, the force will be identified in pounds and the distance either in feet or inches, so the units will be foot-pounds or inch-pounds. Notice these are the same units that were used for potential and kinetic energy.

In the metric system, the force is identified in newtons (N)

and the distance in meters, with the resultant units being joules. One pound of force is equal to 4.448 N and one meter is equal to 3.28 feet. One joule is equal to 0.74 ft-lb.

Example: How much work is accomplished by jacking a 150,000-lb Airbus A-320 airplane a vertical height of 4 ft?

Work = Force × Distance = 150,000 lb × 4 ft = 600,000 ft-lb Example: How much work is accomplished when a tow tractor is hooked up to a tow bar and a Boeing 737-800 airplane weighing 130,000 lb is pushed 80 ft. into the hangar?

The force on the tow bar is 5,000 lb.

Work = Force × Distance = 5,000 lb × 80 ft = 400,000 ft-lb In this last example, notice the force does not equal the weight of the airplane. This is because the airplane is being moved horizontally and not lifted vertically. In almost all cases, it takes less work to move something horizontally than it does to lift it vertically. Most people can push their car a short distance if it runs out of gas, but they cannot get under their car and lift it off the ground.

2. 中文翻译

对简单机械与复杂机械的研究，可以看作是对机械功所涉及的能量所作的研究。之所以如此，是因为所有机械都是把输入能量（即外界对机械所做的功）转换为输出能量（即机械对外所做的功）。

按“功”在力学意义上的定义，当力作用经过一段可测量的距离而克服了某一阻力时，就说做了功。这里涉及两个因素：（1）力；（2）经一段距离的位移。举例来说，假设一架小型航空器陷在雪里，两个人推了一段时间，但航空器没有移动。按技术定义，这两个人推航空器的过程并没有做功。按定义，只有当物体克服阻力被移动了一段距离，才算完成了功。计算功时使用以下公式：

功 = 力（F）× 距离（d）

在英制中，力用磅表示，距离用英尺或英寸表示，因此单位是英尺-磅或英寸-磅。注意，这与前面势能和动能所用的单位相同。

在公制中，力以牛顿（N）表示，

距离以米表示，所得的单位是焦耳。1 磅力等于 4.448 N，1 米等于 3.28 英尺。1 焦耳等于 0.74 ft-lb。

例：用千斤顶把一架重 150,000 lb 的 Airbus A-320 飞机垂直顶升 4 ft，所做的功是多少？

功 = 力 × 距离 = 150,000 lb × 4 ft = 600,000 ft-lb 例：把牵引车接上牵引杆，将一架重 130,000 lb 的 Boeing 737-800 飞机推入机库 80 ft，所做的功是多少？

牵引杆上的力为 5,000 lb。

功 = 力 × 距离 = 5,000 lb × 80 ft = 400,000 ft-lb 在后一个例子中要注意，力并不等于飞机的重量。这是因为飞机是被水平推动的，而不是被垂直举升的。几乎所有情况下，水平移动物体都比垂直举升它所需的功更少。多数人在汽车没油时能把它推出一段距离，却无法钻到车下把它举离地面。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + 插入语：can be seen as

原句：The study of machines, both simple and complex, can be seen as a study of the energy of mechanical work.

拆解：主语 The study of machines；both simple and complex 是插入的同位说明（补充 machines 的范围），去掉后句子仍完整；谓语 can be seen as（情态动词 + be + 过去分词 + as 短语）。

要点：技术文本常用「A can be seen as B」把新概念挂到已知概念上，译作「A 可以看作 B」。注意 as 后接名词，不接从句；同类表达还有 may be regarded as / is considered to be。插入语两侧逗号必须成对，写作时容易漏掉后一个逗号。

② when 引导的时间状语从句 + 分词短语作定语

原句：Work, in the mechanical sense of the term, is done when a resistance is overcome by force acting through a measurable distance.

拆解：主句 Work … is done 为被动；when 从句 a resistance is overcome by force 也是被动，by 引出施动者 force；acting through a measurable distance 是现在分词短语作 force 的后置定语（= which acts through …）；in the mechanical sense of the term 是插入的状语，限定「力学意义」。

要点：reading 长句时先剥掉两处插入成分（in the mechanical sense / acting through …），主干就是 Work is done when a resistance is overcome。分词短语作定语时与被修饰词是主动关系，所以用 acting 而不是 acted。

③ 过去分词短语作后置定语：done on / done by

原句：This is true because all machines transfer input energy, or the work done on the machine, to output energy, or the work done by the machine.

拆解：because 引导原因状语从句；从句主干 transfer input energy … to output energy；or the work done on the machine 与 or the work done by the machine 分别是 input energy、output energy 的同位语；done on / done by 是过去分词短语作 work 的后置定语。

要点：on 与 by 一字之差决定功的方向：the work done on the machine 是外界对机械做功（输入），the work done by the machine 是机械对外做功（输出）。过去分词表被动（功是被做的），写成 doing 就变成「做功的机械」，意思完全不同。
