Equipment Change & Aircraft Alteration

通用 Ch 06 · Equipment Change & Aircraft Alteration · 原文 PDF 第 233 页

1. 原文

When the equipment in an aircraft is changed, such as the installation of a new radar system or ground proximity warning system, or the removal of a radio or seat, the weight and balance of an aircraft changes. An alteration performed on an aircraft, such as a cargo door being installed or a reinforcing plate being attached to the spar of a wing, also changes the weight and balance of an aircraft. Any time the equipment is changed or an alteration is performed, the new empty weight and EWCG must be determined. This can be accomplished by placing the aircraft on scales and weighing it, or by mathematically calculating the new weight and balance. The mathematical calculation is acceptable if the exact weight and arm of all the changes are known.

Ballast is used in an aircraft to attain the desired CG balance, when the CG is not within limits or is not at the location desired by the operator. It is usually located as far aft or as far forward as possible to bring the CG within limits, while using a minimum amount of weight.

Temporary Ballast

Temporary ballast, in the form of lead bars, heavy canvas bags of sand, or lead shot, is often carried in the baggage compartments to adjust the balance for certain flight conditions. The bags are marked “Ballast XX Pounds– Removal Requires Weight and Balance Check.” Temporary ballast must be secured so it cannot shift its location in flight, and the structural limits of the baggage compartment must not be exceeded. All temporary ballast must be removed before the aircraft is weighed.

In evaluating the ballast calculation shown above, the following key points should be recognized.

• The loaded weight of the aircraft, as identified in the formula, is what the airplane weighed when the CG was out of limits.

• The distance the CG is out of limits is the difference between the CG location and the CG limit, in this case 99.6" minus 99".

• The affected limit identified in the formula is the CG limit which has been exceeded. If the CG is too far aft, it is the aft limit that has been exceeded.

• The aft limit for this example is 99", and the ballast is being placed in the baggage compartment at an arm of 60". The difference between the two is 39", the quantity divided by in the formula. Viewed as a first-class lever problem, the calculation takes the form shown below. A ballast weight of 46.68 lb on the left side of the lever multiplied by the arm of 39" (99 minus 60) would equal the aircraft weight of 3,034 lb multiplied by the distance the CG is out of limits, which is 0.6" (99.6 minus 99).

Permanent Ballast

If a repair or alteration causes the aircraft CG to fall outside of its limit, permanent ballast can be installed. Usually, permanent ballast is made of blocks of lead painted red and marked “Permanent Ballast–Do Not Remove.” It should be attached to the structure so that it does not interfere with any control action, and attached rigidly enough that it cannot be dislodged by any flight maneuvers or rough landing. The installation of permanent ballast results in an increase in the aircraft empty weight, and it reduces the useful load.

Three things must be known to determine the amount of ballast needed to bring the CG within limits: the amount the CG is out of limits, the distance between the location of the ballast, and the limit that is affected. If an airplane with an empty weight of 1,876 lb has been altered so its EWCG is +32.2, and CG range for weights up to 2,250 lb is +33.0 to +46.0, permanent ballast must be installed to move the EWCG from +32.2 to +33.0. There is a bulkhead at fuselage station 228 strong enough to support the ballast. To determine the amount of ballast needed, use this formula:

Aircraft empty wt. × dist. out of limits Ballast weight = Dist. between ballast and desired CG 1,876 lb × 0.8" = Ballast weight of 46.68 lb at an arm of 60"

2. 中文翻译

当飞机上的设备发生变化，例如加装新的雷达系统或近地警告系统，或者拆除一部无线电或一个座椅时，飞机的重量与平衡就会改变。对飞机实施改装，例如加装一扇货舱门或在机翼大梁上固定一块加强板，同样会改变飞机的重量与平衡。每当设备发生变化或实施改装时，都必须确定新的空重和空重重心。这可以通过把飞机置于秤上称重，或通过数学计算得出新的重量与平衡来完成。如果已知所有变化的准确重量和力臂，数学计算是可以接受的。

临时配重

临时配重通常以铅条、装满砂子的厚帆布袋或铅丸的形式装在行李舱内，用于在特定飞行条件下调整平衡。袋子标有“配重 XX 磅——拆除前需做重量与平衡检查”。临时配重必须固定牢靠，使其在飞行中不会移动位置，并且不得超过行李舱的结构限制。给飞机称重之前，必须拆除所有临时配重。

永久配重

如果修理或改装使飞机的重心落到限制之外，可以安装永久配重。永久配重通常是用涂成红色、标有“永久配重——不得拆除”的铅块制成。它应固定在结构上，使其不妨碍任何操纵动作，并且固定得足够牢靠，不会被任何飞行动作或粗暴着陆震脱。安装永久配重会增加飞机的空重，并减少有效载重。

要确定把重心带入限制范围所需的配重量，必须知道三件事：重心超出限制的量、配重安装位置与受影响限制之间的距离。如果一架空重 1,876 lb 的飞机经过改装后空重重心为 +32.2，而重量不超过 2,250 lb 时的重心范围为 +33.0 至 +46.0，则必须安装永久配重，把空重重心从 +32.2 移到 +33.0。机身站位 228 处有一个隔框，强度足以支撑该配重。要确定所需配重的量，使用以下公式：

4. 语法解析

① 时间状语从句 + 情态动词被动语态

原句：Any time the equipment is changed or an alteration is performed, the new empty weight and EWCG must be determined. 拆解：Any time 引导时间状语从句，从句内是两个并列的被动结构 is changed / is performed；主句主语 the new empty weight and EWCG，谓语 must be determined（情态动词 + 被动语态）。 要点：Any time 相当于「每当……」，比 when 更强调「只要发生就必须处理」，是手册规定句的常用开头，后面不加 that。情态被动 must be determined 隐去执行者，只强调「必须被确定」。注意主句主语是「空重和空重心」两项并列，译文不必补主语，写成「必须确定新的空重和空重心」即可。

② by + 动名词并列作方式状语

原句：This can be accomplished by placing the aircraft on scales and weighing it, or by mathematically calculating the new weight and balance. 拆解：主语 This + 情态被动 can be accomplished；两个 by + 动名词短语并列作方式状语：by placing … and weighing it / by mathematically calculating …；mathematically 为副词修饰 calculating。 要点：介词 by 表示「通过做某事」时后面只能接动名词，不能接不定式，易错写成 by place 或 by to place。两条途径用 or 并列时第二个 by 通常保留，读起来界限清楚；weighing it 中的 it 回指 the aircraft。

③ need to be done + whether 宾语从句

原句：The weight of the equipment needs to be identified with a plus or minus to signify whether it is being installed or removed. 拆解：主语 The weight of the equipment；谓语 needs（need 此处为实义动词、第三人称单数）+ 不定式被动 to be identified；with a plus or minus 为方式状语；to signify 为目的状语，后接 whether 引导的宾语从句，从句用现在进行被动 is being installed / removed。 要点：need to be + 过去分词表示「需要被……」，语气比 must be 弱，说的是客观需要而非规章强制。表示「是否」的宾语从句用 whether 比 if 正式，尤其在书面技术文本中。is being installed 是现在进行被动，强调「正在被装上或拆下」。

④ 情态被动 + so 目的状语从句 + 并列分句

原句：Temporary ballast must be secured so it cannot shift its location in flight, and the structural limits of the baggage compartment must not be exceeded. 拆解：两个并列主句，均为情态被动：must be secured / must not be exceeded；第一句带 so 引导的目的状语从句 it cannot shift its location in flight，so 后省略了 that。 要点：so（that）引导目的状语从句时 that 常省略，从句中多用 can/cannot。否定情态被动的语序是 must not be + 过去分词，不能写成 must be not exceeded。两个被动句译成中文时都可转为主动说法：「必须固定牢靠」「不得超过行李舱的结构限制」。

⑤ 与过去事实相反的虚拟语气

原句：If the ballast could have been placed farther forward, such as being bolted to the engine firewall, less ballast would have been needed. 拆解：If 引导虚拟条件从句，谓语 could have been placed（情态动词 + have been + 过去分词）；主句谓语 would have been needed；such as being bolted to the engine firewall 为插入举例，being bolted 是动名词的被动形式。 要点：这是「过去本可以却没有做到」的虚拟：配重实际上没有装得更靠前，所以用了更多的配重。主从句都用 would/could + have + 过去分词。such as 后接名词或动名词，不能接完整句子。易错点是把 could have been placed 读成「已经被放置」，其实它表示「本来能够被放置」。

⑥ cause sth to do + 条件状语从句

原句：If a repair or alteration causes the aircraft CG to fall outside of its limit, permanent ballast can be installed. 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，从句为 cause + 宾语 the aircraft CG + 不定式 to fall（宾语补足语）；主句 permanent ballast can be installed 为情态被动。 要点：cause 的固定搭配是 cause sb/sth to do sth，to 不能省略。outside of 在美式技术文本中常见，意思等于 outside。主句用 can be installed 表「可以安装」，是允许或可能，而不是 must be installed。翻译时条件从句宜前置：「若修理或改装致使飞机重心落到限制之外……」。
