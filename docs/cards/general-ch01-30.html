Turbofan Engines

1. 原文

Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft, the turbine-powered aircraft does not require a preflight run-up unless it is necessary to investigate a suspected malfunction.

Before starting, all protective covers and air inlet duct covers are removed. If possible, head the aircraft into the wind to obtain better cooling, faster starting, and smoother engine performance. It is especially important that the aircraft be headed into the wind if the engine is to be trimmed.

The run-up area around the aircraft is cleared of both personnel and loose equipment. The turbofan engine intake and exhaust hazard areas are illustrated in Figure 1-17 .

Exercise care to ensure that the run-up area is clear of all items, such as nuts, bolts, rocks, shop towels, or other loose debris. Many very serious accidents have occurred involving personnel in the vicinity of turbine engine air inlets. Use extreme caution when starting turbine aircraft.

Check the aircraft fuel sumps for water or ice. Inspect the engine air inlet for general condition and the presence of foreign objects. Visually inspect the fan blades, forward compressor blades, and the compressor inlet guide vanes for nicks and other damage. If possible, check the fan blades for free rotation by turning the fan blades by hand. All engine controls must be operational. Check engine instruments and warning lights for proper operation.

2. 中文翻译

与往复式发动机航空器不同，涡轮动力航空器不要求做航前试车，除非需要查证某个怀疑存在的故障。

起动之前，拆下所有防护罩与进气道堵盖。如有可能，使机头迎风，以获得更好的冷却、更快的起动和更平稳的发动机工作。如果要对发动机进行配平（trim），机头迎风就尤为重要。

航空器周围的试车区域要清空人员与松散设备。涡扇发动机的进气与排气危险区见图 1-17。

要注意确保试车区内没有任何杂物，如螺帽、螺栓、石块、抹布或其他松散碎屑。曾发生过多起涉及涡轮发动机进气口附近人员的重大事故。起动涡轮航空器时必须极度谨慎。

检查航空器燃油集油槽有无水或冰。检查发动机进气口的总体状况与有无外来物。目视检查风扇叶片、前级压气机叶片以及压气机进口导流叶片有无缺口及其他损伤。如有可能，用手转动风扇叶片以检查其能否自由转动。所有发动机操纵机构都必须工作正常。检查发动机仪表与警告灯工作是否正常。

4. 语法解析

① It is important that… + 虚拟语气（be headed）

原句：It is especially important that the aircraft be headed into the wind if the engine is to be trimmed.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句谓语用动词原形 be headed（前面省略了 should），构成「It is important / necessary / essential + that + 主语 + (should) + 动词原形」的虚拟语气；if the engine is to be trimmed 是条件状语从句，其中 be to 表「打算、将要」。

要点：表示必要性、重要性的评价性形容词后面的 that 从句要用虚拟语气，美式英语直接把 should 省掉，所以看到 the aircraft be headed 不要以为漏了 is。head 作动词是「使机头朝向」，be headed into the wind 被动形式表状态。若写成 the aircraft is headed，就变成陈述事实的语气，规范性消失。

② Unlike + 名词 / unless 否定的条件从句

原句：Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft, the turbine-powered aircraft does not require a preflight run-up unless it is necessary to investigate a suspected malfunction.

拆解：Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft 是介词短语作状语，表对比；主干 the turbine-powered aircraft does not require a preflight run-up；unless 引导否定条件从句（相当于 if not），从句内 it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to investigate a suspected malfunction。

要点：unlike 是介词，后面只能跟名词或名词短语，不能直接跟句子（对比 different from）。unless 本身已含否定，不能再加 not，× unless it is not necessary。it is necessary to do 中 it 是形式主语，这是英语避免「to do 作主语太长」的惯用手法，中文照常译「需要做……」。

③ 现在完成时 + 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：Many very serious accidents have occurred involving personnel in the vicinity of turbine engine air inlets.

拆解：主语 Many very serious accidents，谓语 have occurred（现在完成时，强调已发生且与现状相关）；involving personnel in the vicinity of turbine engine air inlets 是现在分词短语后置修饰 accidents，说明这些事故涉及哪些人；in the vicinity of 意为「在……附近」。

要点：现在分词短语作定语相当于 which involve，用主动形式是因为事故「涉及」人员。occur 是不及物动词，没有被动语态，× have been occurred 是典型错误。in the vicinity of 比 near 更书面，是手册语体的标志；这类抽象名词 + 介词短语的搭配要整块记。
