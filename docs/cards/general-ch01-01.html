Shop Safety

通用 Ch 01 · Shop Safety · 原文 PDF 第 23 页

1. 原文

Keeping the shop, hangars, and flight line clean is essential to safety and efficient maintenance. The highest standards of orderly work arrangements and cleanliness must be observed during the maintenance of aircraft. Where continuous work shifts are established, the outgoing shift removes and properly stores personal tools, rollaway boxes, work stands, maintenance stands, hoses, electrical cords, hoists, crates, and boxes that were needed for the work to be accomplished. Signs are posted to indicate dangerous equipment or hazardous conditions. Additionally, there are signs that provide the location of first aid and fire equipment. Safety lanes, pedestrian walkways, and fire lanes are painted around the perimeter inside the hangars. This is a safety measure to prevent accidents and to keep pedestrian traffic out of work areas.

Safety is everyone’s business. However, technicians and supervisors must watch for their own safety and for the safety of others working around them. Communication is key to ensuring everyone’s safety. If other personnel are conducting their actions in an unsafe manner, communicate with them, reminding them of their safety and that of others around them.

2. 中文翻译

保持车间、机库和停机坪的清洁，对安全和高效维修至关重要。维修飞机期间，必须遵守最高标准的作业秩序与清洁要求。凡实行连续轮班的场所，交班班组应撤收并妥善存放本次作业所需的个人工具、移动工具柜、工作梯、维修平台、软管、电线、吊具、板条箱和箱子。现场设置标志牌，标示危险设备或危险状况；此外还设有标志牌，指明急救设备和消防器材的位置。机库内侧四周地面漆划安全通道、人行通道和消防通道。这是一项安全措施，用以防止事故，并使行人远离作业区。

安全是每个人的事。但技术人员和主管必须既注意自身安全，也注意周围一同作业的其他人员的安全。沟通是确保所有人安全的关键。如果其他人员的行为方式不安全，就应主动与他们沟通，提醒其注意自身及周围人员的安全。

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词被动语态表示“必须被遵守”

原句：The highest standards of orderly work arrangements and cleanliness must be observed during the maintenance of aircraft. 拆解：主语 The highest standards（of orderly work arrangements and cleanliness 为介词短语后置定语）+ 谓语 must be observed（情态动词 + 被动语态）+ 时间状语 during the maintenance of aircraft。 要点：手册类文本用 must be + 过去分词表示“（由人）必须做到”，施动者不出现，把重点放在要求本身。易错点：standards 在此为复数，谓语用 must be 而非 must is；observe 在此不译“观察”，而是“遵守、奉行”，与 rules、standards、procedures 搭配时都作此义，可迁移到 must be observed / strictly observed。

② where 引导条件状语从句

原句：Where continuous work shifts are established, the outgoing shift removes and properly stores personal tools, rollaway boxes, work stands, maintenance stands, hoses, electrical cords, hoists, crates, and boxes that were needed for the work to be accomplished. 拆解：Where 引导条件状语从句（= 在……的场所/情况下）+ 主句主语 the outgoing shift + 并列谓语 removes and properly stores + 长宾语列表 + that were needed for the work to be accomplished 为定语从句修饰前面全部物品。 要点：技术文本中 where 常作“凡……之处、在……情况下”的条件连词，既不是疑问词，也不是定语从句引导词，翻译时译成“凡……的场所”最顺。易错点：主句两个谓语共用一个主语 the outgoing shift，removes 与 stores 时态、人称须一致；that 从句的先行词是一整串并列名词，不只最后一个 boxes。

③ there be 存在句 + that 定语从句

原句：Additionally, there are signs that provide the location of first aid and fire equipment. 拆解：Additionally 为连接副词 + there are signs 存在句 + that provide the location of first aid and fire equipment 为关系代词 that 引导的定语从句，修饰 signs。 要点：存在句的真正主语在 be 之后，谓语与 signs 一致用 are；that 在从句中作主语，不可省略，也不能换成 what。provide the location of… 是“标示/给出……的位置”，与上句 Signs are posted to indicate… 属同一语义场的两种说法。

④ 不定式作目的状语 + keep 复合结构

原句：This is a safety measure to prevent accidents and to keep pedestrian traffic out of work areas. 拆解：主语 This + 系动词 is + 表语 a safety measure + 两个并列不定式 to prevent accidents 与 to keep pedestrian traffic out of work areas 作目的状语（说明该措施用来做什么）。 要点：表语名词后接不定式，说明“该事物用于做……”，不要误判为定语从句省略。keep + 宾语 + out of + 地点是“使……不进入/远离……”的复合宾语结构，keep pedestrian traffic out of work areas 可整体记成一个可迁移句式：keep sth. out of sth.。
