Compression

1. 原文

Compression is a force that tries to crush an object. An excellent example of compression is when a sheet metal airplane is assembled using the fastener known as a rivet. The rivet passes through a hole drilled in the pieces of aluminum, and then a rivet gun on one side and a bucking bar on the other apply a force. This applied force tries to crush the rivet and makes it expand to fill the hole and securely hold the aluminum pieces together. [Figure 5-19]

2. 中文翻译

压缩（力）是一种试图把物体压扁的力。压缩的一个极好例子，是用称为铆钉的紧固件来组装钣金飞机。铆钉穿过铝板上钻出的孔，随后一侧的铆枪与另一侧的顶铁施加作用力。这一施加的力试图压扁铆钉，使其膨胀填满孔，并把铝板牢固地夹持在一起。（图 5-19）

3. 专业词汇

英文 词性 中文 本节语境
compression n. 压缩（力）；受压 本节指试图把物体压扁的力，与 tension 相对
sheet metal n. 钣金、薄板金属 用铆钉连接的飞机蒙皮与结构件

aluminum n. 铝、铝材 the pieces of aluminum 指被连接的铝制板件

4. 语法解析

① An excellent example of compression is when …（when 引导表语从句）

原句：An excellent example of compression is when a sheet metal airplane is assembled using the fastener known as a rivet.

拆解：主语 An excellent example of compression，系动词 is，when 从句作表语；从句内 is assembled 为被动，using … 是方式状语，known as a rivet 为过去分词短语后置修饰 fastener。

要点：「is when + 完整从句」在技术手册里很常见，可理解为「……的情形就是……」。更书面的写法是 is the assembly of … using …。known as =「被称为」，相当于 which is known as。

② The rivet passes through a hole drilled in the pieces of aluminum（过去分词作后置定语 + 并列分句）

原句：The rivet passes through a hole drilled in the pieces of aluminum, and then a rivet gun on one side and a bucking bar on the other apply a force.

拆解：and 连接两个分句；前一分句 passes through a hole，drilled in the pieces of aluminum 后置修饰 hole；后一分句主语为并列的 a rivet gun … and a bucking bar …，谓语 apply。

要点：drilled 是「被钻出的」（被动、已完成），不是主动的「钻孔」。on one side / on the other (side) 是省略 side 的对称说法。后一分句主语是两项，谓语用原形 apply。

③ makes it expand to fill the hole and securely hold …（make + 宾语 + 不带 to 的不定式）

原句：This applied force tries to crush the rivet and makes it expand to fill the hole and securely hold the aluminum pieces together.

拆解：主语 This applied force，并列谓语 tries to crush 与 makes；makes it expand 为 make + 宾语 + 动词原形；to fill 与 (to) hold 并列，表示膨胀的目的与结果。

要点：make/let/have 后的宾补不定式不带 to，但变成被动时要还原：it is made to expand。tries to crush 中的 to 则不能省。applied 是过去分词作定语，意为「所施加的」。
