Extinguishing Engine Fires

通用 Ch 01 · Extinguishing Engine Fires · 原文 PDF 第 36 页

1. 原文

In all cases, a fireguard should stand by with a CO 2 fire extinguisher while the aircraft engine is being started. This is a necessary precaution against fire during the starting procedure. The fireguard must be familiar with the induction system of the engine so that in case of fire, they can direct the CO2 into the air intake of the engine to extinguish it. A fire could also occur in the exhaust system of the engine from liquid fuel being ignited in the cylinder and expelled during the normal rotation of the engine.

If an engine fire develops during the starting procedure, the operator should continue cranking to start the engine and extinguish the fire. If the engine does not start and the fire continues to burn, discontinue the start attempt. The fireguard then extinguishes the fire using the available equipment. The fireguard must observe all safety practices at all times while standing by during the starting procedure.

2. 中文翻译

无论何种情况，起动航空器发动机时，消防警戒员都应手持 CO₂ 灭火器在旁待命。这是起动过程中防火的必要预防措施。消防警戒员必须熟悉该发动机的进气系统，以便万一起火时能把 CO₂ 喷入发动机进气口将火扑灭。发动机排气系统也可能起火，原因是液状燃油在气缸内被点燃、并在发动机正常转动过程中被排出。

若在起动过程中发生发动机起火，操作者应继续盘转以起动发动机，从而把火吹灭。若发动机不能起动而火继续燃烧，则中止起动尝试，由消防警戒员使用可用器材灭火。消防警戒员在起动过程中待命期间，必须始终遵守各项安全规程。

4. 语法解析

① while + 现在进行时的被动语态：is being started

原句：In all cases, a fireguard should stand by with a CO 2 fire extinguisher while the aircraft engine is being started.

拆解：In all cases 是条件性状语（「无论何种情况」）；主语 a fireguard，谓语 should stand by（情态动词 + 动词短语）；with a CO2 fire extinguisher 表携带、伴随；while 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在进行时的被动语态 is being started。

要点：is being started 的结构是 is + being + 过去分词，表示「正在被起动」，是这一时态的唯一正确写法，常见错误是写成 is being start。stand by 意为「在旁待命、守候」。本句用 should 而非 must，语气上略留余地，但在手册语境中仍是应照办的要求。

② be familiar with + so that 目的从句 + in case of

原句：The fireguard must be familiar with the induction system of the engine so that in case of fire, they can direct the CO2 into the air intake of the engine to extinguish it.

拆解：谓语 must be familiar with（固定搭配，with 后接所熟悉的对象）；so that 引导目的状语从句；从句内 in case of fire 是前置的条件状语，主谓为 they can direct the CO2 into the air intake of the engine，最后 to extinguish it 是不定式表目的。

要点：be familiar with sb/sth 固定用 with，不能用 to。in case of + 名词 意为「万一发生……」，比 if there is 更书面简洁，是应急类条文的常用式（in case of fire / in case of emergency）。句中的 they 是单数泛指的性别中立代词，指那名消防警戒员。

③ 条件从句（一般现在时）+ 主句祈使句

原句：If the engine does not start and the fire continues to burn, discontinue the start attempt.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，从句内两个谓语并列——does not start（一般现在时否定）与 continues to burn（continue + 不定式作宾语）；主句是祈使句 discontinue the start attempt（动词原形 + 宾语）。

要点：条件从句用一般现在时表将来，主句用祈使句，是手册程序条文的典型搭配，不能写成 If the engine will not start。discontinue 比 stop 正式，是手册里「中止」的标准用词，与后文 discontinue the start attempt 呼应；the start attempt 指「这一次起动尝试」。
