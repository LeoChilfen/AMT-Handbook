14 CFR Part 23—Airworthiness Standards:

Normal, Utility, Acrobatic, and Commuter Category Airplanes Aircraft certificated under 14 CFR part 23 represent the greatest portion of what the industry refers to as “general aviation.” These aircraft vary from the small two-place piston engine, propeller-driven trainers that are frequently used for flight training, to turbine-powered corporate jets used to transport business executives. Seating capacity is limited to nine or less on all aircraft, except the commuter aircraft where the maximum passenger seating is 19, excluding the pilot and copilot seats.

14 CFR part 23——《适航标准》：

正常类、实用类、特技类和通勤类飞机。按 14 CFR part 23 取得合格证的航空器，构成了业界所称「通用航空」的最大部分。这类航空器的范围，从常用于飞行训练的小型双座活塞式螺旋桨教练机，一直到用于运送企业高管的涡轮动力公务机。所有这类航空器的座位数均限制在 9 个及以下，但通勤类飞机除外，其最大旅客座位数为 19 个，不含正、副驾驶座位。

4. 语法解析

① Note that … although it has minor application in recurrent airworthiness（宾语从句 + 让步状语从句）

原句：Note that part 21’s greatest significance is in the original airworthiness phase, although it has minor application in recurrent airworthiness.

拆解：祈使句 Note that 后接宾语从句；从句为「主语 + 系动词 + 表语（in the original airworthiness phase）」结构；although 引导让步状语从句，与主句形成「主要／次要」对比。

要点：significance is in … 译「意义在于……」，不要按字面译成「意义是在……里」。greatest significance 与 minor application 是刻意对照，翻译时保留这层比较，否则读者会误以为 part 21 对持续适航毫无作用。

② When an aircraft is delivered new …, it comes with maintenance manuals that define …（时间状语从句 + 定语从句）

原句：When an aircraft is delivered new from the manufacturer, it comes with maintenance manuals that define the inspection and maintenance actions necessary to maintain the aircraft in airworthy condition.

拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，从句为被动语态 is delivered，new 作主语补足语；主句 it comes with maintenance manuals，it 指 the aircraft；that define … 是定语从句修饰 manuals；necessary to maintain … 是形容词短语后置修饰 actions。

要点：be delivered new 是「以新机状态交付」，new 说明交付时的状态，属主语补足语，不能当副词理解。come with 意为「随附、附带」。necessary to do 作后置定语，等于 which are necessary to do。

③ must have, as part of the STC documentation, a complete set of …（插入状语隔开谓语与宾语）

原句：Also, any STC modification that was developed after 1981 must have, as part of the STC documentation, a complete set of instructions for continued airworthiness (ICA).

拆解：主语 any STC modification + that was developed after 1981（定语从句）；谓语 must have；宾语 a complete set of instructions for continued airworthiness (ICA)；as part of the STC documentation 是插入的状语，插在谓语与宾语之间。

要点：长宾语被状语隔开时，先抓 must have a complete set of … 的骨架，再看插入成分。must 表法规强制要求，译「必须」，不能弱化成 should（应）。that was developed 用被动，是因为改装由申请人开发，改装本身是被开发的对象。
