Empennage

1. 原文

The empennage of an aircraft is also known as the tail section. Most empennage designs consist of a tail cone, fixed aerodynamic surfaces or stabilizers, and movable aerodynamic surfaces.

The tail cone serves to close and streamline the aft end of most fuselages. The cone is made up of structural members like those of the fuselage; however, cones are usually of lighter construction since they receive less stress than the fuselage.

The other components of the typical empennage are of heavier construction than the tail cone. These members include fixed surfaces that help stabilize the aircraft and movable surfaces that help to direct an aircraft during flight. The fixed surfaces are the horizontal stabilizer and vertical stabilizer. The movable surfaces are usually a rudder located at the aft edge of the vertical stabilizer and an elevator located at the aft edge the horizontal stabilizer.

The structure of the stabilizers is very similar to that which is used in wing construction. Figure 1-48 shows a typical vertical stabilizer. Notice the use of spars, ribs, stringers, and skin like those found in a wing. They perform the same functions shaping and supporting the stabilizer and transferring stresses. Bending, torsion, and shear created by air loads in flight pass from one structural member to another. Each member absorbs some of the stress and passes the remainder on to the others. Ultimately, the spar transmits any overloads to the fuselage. 2. 中文翻译

飞机的尾翼（empennage）又称尾段（tail section）。多数尾翼设计由尾锥、固定的空气动力面（即安定面）以及可动的空气动力面组成。

尾锥用于封闭大多数机身的后端，并使其流线化。尾锥由与机身类似的结构件构成；不过尾锥的构造通常较轻，因为它承受的应力比机身小。

典型尾翼的其他部件比尾锥构造更重。这些构件包括帮助稳定飞机的固定面，以及帮助在飞行中操纵飞机方向的舵面。固定面是水平安定面和垂直安定面。可动面通常包括位于垂直安定面后缘的方向舵，以及位于水平安定面后缘的升降舵。

安定面的结构与机翼构造中所用的结构非常相似。图1-48展示了一个典型的垂直安定面。注意其所用的翼梁、翼肋、桁条和蒙皮与机翼中的相同。它们执行相同的功能：使安定面成形并支撑安定面，以及传递应力。飞行中空气载荷产生的弯曲、扭转和剪切，从一个结构件传递到另一个结构件。每个构件吸收一部分应力，并把其余部分传给其他构件。最终，翼梁把任何过载传给机身。水平安定面也是按这种方式构造的。方向舵和升降舵是飞行操纵面，也属于本章下一节所要讨论的尾翼的一部分。

4. 语法解析

① 分号连接两个独立分句 + since 原因状语从句

原句：The cone is made up of structural members like those of the fuselage; however, cones are usually of lighter construction since they receive less stress than the fuselage. 拆解：前一分句为被动语态 is made up of，like those of the fuselage 是介词短语作定语（those 代指 structural members）；分号后用 however 转折，主句为主系表 cones are of lighter construction，since 引导原因状语从句，从句中 less stress than the fuselage 为比较结构。 要点：however 是副词而非连词，不能像 but 那样直接连接两个句子，所以前面必须用分号或句号。be of + 抽象名词（of lighter construction）相当于形容词用法，表示“具有……的性质”。

② 两个 that 定语从句并列修饰不同先行词

原句：These members include fixed surfaces that help stabilize the aircraft and movable surfaces that help to direct an aircraft during flight. 拆解：主干为 These members include fixed surfaces and movable surfaces；两个 that 分别引导定语从句修饰其前的 surfaces，help 后接省略 to 的不定式 stabilize / direct 作宾语。 要点：两个 that 从句是并列关系，各自管住自己前面的名词，不能把第二个 that 误当成第一个从句的延续。help do sth. 中不定式不带 to，但 help to do 也可以，本文两种写法都出现。

③ that which 结构避免重复先行词

原句：The structure of the stabilizers is very similar to that which is used in wing construction. 拆解：主句为主系表 The structure ... is similar to that；that 是指示代词作介词 to 的宾语，指代前面的 the structure；which 引导定语从句修饰 that，从句为被动语态 is used in wing construction。 要点：这里 that 不是定语从句的关系词，而是被 which 从句修饰的代词，构成“that which = the one which”。若写成 the structure which is used... 会重复，故用 that 替代。

④ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + 并列主语

原句：Bending, torsion, and shear created by air loads in flight pass from one structural member to another. 拆解：主语是并列的三个名词 Bending, torsion, and shear；created by air loads in flight 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰这三个名词；谓语 pass，from one structural member to another 为介词短语作状语。 要点：主语和谓语之间插入了分词定语，pass 才是谓语，不要误把 created 当谓语。created 与主语是被动关系（载荷“被产生”），故用过去分词而非现在分词。

⑤ 过去分词作后置定语 + 并列宾语

原句：The movable surfaces are usually a rudder located at the aft edge of the vertical stabilizer and an elevator located at the aft edge the horizontal stabilizer. 拆解：主语 The movable surfaces，系动词 are，表语为并列的两个名词 a rudder 和 an elevator；两个 located at... 均为过去分词短语作后置定语，分别修饰 rudder 和 elevator。 要点：located 的逻辑主语分别是 rudder 和 elevator，属被动关系。原文第二个 at the aft edge 后漏了 of（OCR 缺词），阅读时应按 at the aft edge of the horizontal stabilizer 理解。
