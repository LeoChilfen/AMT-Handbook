Lift and Newton’s Third Law

通用 Ch 05 · Lift and Newton’s Third Law · 原文 PDF 第 187 页

1. 原文

Newton’s third law identifies that for every force there is an equal and opposite reacting force. In addition to Bernoulli’s principle, Newton’s third law can also be used to explain the lift being created by a wing. As the air travels around a wing and leaves the trailing edge, the air is forced to move in a downward direction. Since a force is required to make something change direction, there must be an equal and opposite reacting force.

In this case, the reacting force is what we call lift. In order to calculate lift based on Newton’s third law, Newton’s second law and the formula “Force = Mass × Acceleration” would be used. The mass would be the weight of air flowing over the wing every second, and the acceleration would be the change in velocity the wing imparts to the air.

The lift on the wing as described by Bernoulli’s principle, and lift on the wing as described by Newton’s third law, is

2. 中文翻译

牛顿第三定律指出，每一个力都有一个大小相等、方向相反的反作用力。除伯努利原理之外，牛顿第三定律也可以用来解释机翼所产生的升力。当空气绕过机翼并从后缘流出时，气流被迫向下偏转。既然要使某物改变方向就必须施加一个力，那就必然存在一个大小相等、方向相反的反作用力。

在本例中，这个反作用力就是我们所说的升力。要依据牛顿第三定律计算升力，需要用到牛顿第二定律以及公式「Force = Mass × Acceleration」。这里的质量是每秒流过机翼的空气重量，加速度则是机翼赋予空气的速度变化量。

4. 语法解析

① 介词短语前置 + there be 存在句

原句：Newton’s third law identifies that for every force there is an equal and opposite reacting force.

拆解：主语 Newton’s third law，谓语 identifies，后接 that 引导的宾语从句；从句内 for every force 是表示范围的介词短语，前置到句首，然后是 there is an equal and opposite reacting force 存在句。

要点：there be 句型的正常语序是 there is + 名词 + 状语，把 for every force 提到句首是为了与「每一个力」形成对照，读起来更紧凑。identifies that … 意为「指出、确认」，比 says 正式。equal and opposite 是力学中的固定搭配，指大小相等、方向相反，不能只译「相等」。

② In order to + 被动语态 would be used

原句：In order to calculate lift based on Newton’s third law, Newton’s second law and the formula “Force = Mass × Acceleration” would be used.

拆解：In order to calculate lift based on Newton’s third law 是目的状语（based on … 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 lift 的计算依据）；主句主语是并列的 Newton’s second law and the formula（两项并列），谓语 would be used 为被动结构，would 表示按前述方法「应当会用到」。

要点：in order to 与 so as to 同义，但 in order to 可置于句首，so as to 不可。would be used 用被动的理由是执行者（我们）不言自明，重点在「用哪条定律」。注意 be 在 would be used 中是不定式形式，不随主语单复数变化，所以主语是一项还是两项都写 would be used；真正需要跟主语一致的是 have/has、is/are 这类形式。

③ Since 原因从句 + require sb to do / make sb do 的使役区别

原句：Since a force is required to make something change direction, there must be an equal and opposite reacting force.

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句为被动 a force is required to make something change direction；主句 there must be an equal and opposite reacting force 是 there be + 情态动词 must，表示必然推断。

要点：require 后接不定式（require a force to do），make 后接不带 to 的不定式（make something change），两个使役结构的差别是常见考点：make、let、have 后省略 to，而 require、cause、force 后保留 to。there must be 译作「必然存在」，是推理句的典型说法，比 there is 多一层逻辑必然性。

④ 省略关系代词的定语从句：the change in velocity the wing imparts to the air

原句：The mass would be the weight of air flowing over the wing every second, and the acceleration would be the change in velocity the wing imparts to the air.

拆解：and 连接两个并列分句，都用 would be 表推断；第一分句中 flowing over the wing every second 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 air；第二分句中 the change in velocity 后紧跟 the wing imparts to the air，这是省略了关系代词 that/which 的定语从句（= the change in velocity that the wing imparts to the air）。

要点：impart sth to sb 意为「把某物赋予某人／某物」，这里 impart the change in velocity to the air。定语从句省略关系代词时，关系词在从句中作宾语，读句时若把 the wing 误当成主句新主语就会断错句。翻译时按「机翼赋予空气的速度变化量」把定语提前，符合汉语习惯。
