Aircraft Lighting Systems

机体 Ch 09 · Aircraft Lighting Systems · 原文 PDF 第 516 页

1. 原文

Aircraft lighting systems provide illumination for both exterior and interior use. Lights on the exterior provide illumination for such operations as landing at night, inspection of icing conditions, and safety from midair collision. Interior lighting provides illumination for instruments, flight decks, cabins, and other sections occupied by crewmembers and passengers. Certain special lights, such as indicator and warning lights, indicate the operation status of equipment.

Exterior Lights

Position, anticollision, landing, and taxi lights are common examples of aircraft exterior lights. Some lights are required for night operations. Other types of exterior lights, such as wing inspection lights, are of great benefit for specialized flying operations.

Position Lights

Aircraft operating at night must be equipped with position lights that meet the minimum requirements specified by Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations. A set of position lights consist of one red, one green, and one white light. [Figures 9-174 and 9-175]

On some types of installations, a switch in the flight deck provides for steady or flashing operation of the position lights. On many aircraft, each light unit contains a single lamp mounted on the surface of the aircraft. Other types of position light units contain two lamps and are often streamlined into the surface of the aircraft structure. The green light unit is always mounted at the extreme tip of the right wing. The red unit is mounted in a similar position on the left wing. The white unit is usually located on the vertical stabilizer in a position where it is clearly visible through a wide angle from the rear of the aircraft. Figure 9-176 illustrates a schematic diagram of a position light circuit. Position lights are also known as navigation lights.

There are, of course, many variations in the position light circuits used on different aircraft. All circuits are protected by fuses or circuit breakers, and many circuits include flashing and dimming equipment. Small aircraft are usually equipped with a simplified control switch and circuitry. In some cases, one control knob or switch is used to turn on several sets of lights; for example, one type utilizes a control knob, the first movement of which turns on the position lights and the instrument panel lights. Further rotation of the control knob increases the intensity of only the panel lights. A flasher unit is seldom included in the position light circuitry of very light aircraft but is used in small twin-engine aircraft. Traditional position lights use incandescent light bulbs. LED lights have been introduced on modern aircraft because of their good visibility, high reliability, and low power consumption.

Anticollision Lights

An anticollision light system may consist of one or more lights. They are rotating beam lights that are usually installed on top of the fuselage or tail in such a location that the light does not affect the vision of the crewmember or detract from the visibility of the position lights. Large transport type aircraft use an anticollision light on top and one on the bottom of the aircraft. Figure 9-177 shows a typical anticollision light installation in a vertical stabilizer.

An anticollision light unit usually consists of one or two rotating lights operated by an electric motor. The light may be fixed but mounted under rotating mirrors inside a protruding red glass housing. The mirrors rotate in an arc, and the resulting flash rate is between 40 and 100 cycles per minute. Newer aircraft designs use a LED type of anticollision light. The anticollision light is a safety light to warn other aircraft, especially in congested areas.

A white strobe light is a second type of anti-collision light that is also common. Usually mounted at the wing tips and, possibly, at empennage extremities, strobe lights produce an extremely bright intermittent flash of white light that is highly visible. The light is produced by a high voltage discharge of a capacitor. A dedicated power pack houses the capacitor and supplies voltage to a sealed xenon-filled tube. The xenon ionizes with a flash when the voltage is applied. A strobe light is shown in Figure 9-174 .

Landing & Taxi Lights

Landing lights are installed in aircraft to illuminate runways during night landings. These lights are very powerful and are directed by a parabolic reflector at an angle providing a maximum range of illumination. Landing lights of smaller aircraft are usually located midway in the leading edge of each wing or streamlined into the aircraft surface. Landing lights for larger transport category aircraft are usually located in the leading edge of the wing close to the fuselage. Each light may be controlled by a relay, or it may be connected directly into the electric circuit. On some aircraft, the landing light is mounted in the same area with a taxi light. A sealed beam, halogen, or high intensity xenon discharge lamp is used.

Taxi lights are designed to provide illumination on the ground while taxiing or towing the aircraft to or from a runway, taxi strip, or in the hangar area. Taxi lights are not designed to provide the degree of illumination necessary for landing lights. On aircraft with tricycle landing gear, either single or multiple taxi lights are often mounted on the non-steerable part of the nose landing gear. They are positioned at an oblique angle to the center line of the aircraft to provide illumination directly in front of the aircraft and also some illumination to the right and left of the aircraft’s path. On some aircraft, the dual taxi lights are supplemented by wingtip clearance lights controlled by the same circuitry. Taxi lights are also mounted in the recessed areas of the wing leading edge, often in the same area with a fixed landing light.

Many small aircraft are not equipped with any type of taxi light, but rely on the intermittent use of a landing light to illuminate taxiing operations. Still other aircraft utilize a dimming resistor in the landing light circuit to provide reduced illumination for taxiing. A typical circuit for taxi lights is shown in Figure 9-180 .

Some large aircraft are equipped with alternate taxi lights located on the lower surface of the aircraft, aft of the nose radome. These lights, operated by a separate switch from the main taxi lights, illuminate the area immediately in front of and below the aircraft nose.

Wing Inspection Lights

Some aircraft are equipped with wing inspection lights to illuminate the leading edge of the wings to permit observation of icing and general condition of the se areas in flight. These lights permit visual detection of ice formation on wing leading edges while flying at night. They are usually controlled through a relay by an on/off toggle switch in the flight deck. Some wing inspection light systems may include or be supplemented by additional lights, sometimes called nacelle lights, that illuminate adjacent areas, such a cowl flaps or the landing gear. These are normally the same type of lights and can be controlled by the same circuits.

Interior Lights

Aircraft are equipped with interior lights to illuminate the cabin. Often white and red light settings are provided. Commercial aircraft have a lighting system that illuminates the main cabin, an independent lighting system so that passengers can read when the cabin lights are off, and an emergency lighting system on the floor of the aircraft to aid passengers during an emergency.

Maintenance & Inspection of Lighting Systems

Inspection of an aircraft’s lighting system normally includes checking the condition and security of all visible wiring, connections, terminals, fuses, and switches. A continuity light or meter can be used in making these checks, since the cause of many troubles can often be located by systematically testing each circuit for continuity.

2. 中文翻译

飞机照明系统为飞机外部和内部提供照明。外部灯光为夜间着陆、结冰状况检查、防止空中相撞等作业提供照明。内部照明为仪表、驾驶舱、客舱以及其他由机组和乘客占用的区域提供照明。某些专用灯，如指示灯和警告灯，则指示设备的工作状态。

外部灯光

位置灯、防撞灯、着陆灯和滑行灯是飞机外部灯光的常见例子。有些灯是夜间运行所必需的。其他类型的外部灯光，如机翼检查灯，对专门飞行作业大有裨益。

位置灯

夜间运行的飞机必须装备符合《联邦法规汇编》第14篇（Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations）所规定最低要求的位置灯。一组位置灯由一盏红灯、一盏绿灯和一盏白灯组成。[图9-174和图9-175]

在某些型别的安装中，驾驶舱内的一个开关可使位置灯以常亮或闪光方式工作。在许多飞机上，每个灯具只装一盏灯，安装在飞机表面。其他型式的位置灯组件装有两盏灯，通常以流线型嵌入机体结构表面。绿灯组件始终装在右翼的最外侧翼尖；红灯组件装在左翼的相应位置。白灯通常装在垂直安定面上，其位置应保证从飞机后方在很宽的角度范围内都能清楚看到。图9-176所示为位置灯电路的原理图。位置灯也称为航行灯。

当然，不同飞机所用的位置灯电路有许多变化。所有电路都由保险丝或断路器保护，许多电路还包含闪光和调光装置。小型飞机通常装有一套简化的控制开关和电路。某些情况下，用一个控制旋钮或开关即可点亮几组灯；例如，有一种型式使用一个控制旋钮，其第一次转动点亮位置灯和仪表板灯，继续转动旋钮则只增大仪表板灯的亮度。很轻的飞机其位置灯电路中很少装闪光器，但小型双发飞机则使用。传统位置灯使用白炽灯泡。现代飞机因LED灯可见性好、可靠性高、功耗低而开始采用。

防撞灯

防撞灯系统可由一盏或多盏灯组成。它们是旋转光束灯，通常装在机身或尾部的上方，其位置应使灯光不影响机组的视力，也不削弱位置灯的可见性。大型运输类飞机在机身上方和下方各装一盏防撞灯。图9-177所示为垂直安定面上典型的防撞灯安装。

一个防撞灯组件通常由一盏或两盏由电动机驱动的旋转灯组成。灯也可以是固定的，但安装在凸出的红色玻璃罩内旋转镜的下方。镜子在弧形范围内旋转，由此产生的闪光频率为每分钟40至100次。较新的飞机设计采用LED型防撞灯。防撞灯是一种安全灯，用于警示其他飞机，尤其是在飞机密集区域。

白色频闪灯（strobe light）是第二种同样常见的防撞灯。频闪灯通常装在翼尖，也可能装在尾翼端部，产生极亮的间歇白光闪烁，可见性很高。这种光由电容器的高压放电产生。专用的电源组件内装电容器，并向密封的充氙灯管供电。施加电压时氙气电离并发出闪光。图9-174所示为一只频闪灯。

着陆灯与滑行灯

飞机装设着陆灯，用于在夜间着陆时照亮跑道。这类灯功率很大，由抛物面反射镜定向，其角度可提供最大的照明距离。小型飞机的着陆灯通常位于每个机翼前缘的中部，或以流线型嵌入飞机表面。较大的运输类飞机的着陆灯，通常位于靠近机身处的机翼前缘。每盏灯可由继电器控制，也可直接接入电路。有些飞机上，着陆灯与滑行灯装在同一区域。所用灯型为封闭光束灯、卤素灯或高强度氙放电灯。

滑行灯用于在飞机滑行或牵引进出跑道、滑行道或在机库区域时提供地面照明。滑行灯的设计并不提供着陆灯所需的那种照明强度。在前三点起落架的飞机上，单盏或多盏滑行灯常装在前起落架的不可转向部分。它们相对飞机中心线成斜角安装，以照亮飞机正前方，同时为飞机航迹的左右两侧提供部分照明。有些飞机上，双滑行灯由同一电路控制的翼尖间隙灯补充。滑行灯也装在机翼前缘的凹陷区域内，常与固定式着陆灯装在同一部位。

许多小型飞机不装任何型式的滑行灯，而是靠间歇使用着陆灯来为滑行作业照明。还有一些飞机在着陆灯电路中使用一个降压电阻，为滑行提供减弱的照明。图9-180所示为典型的滑行灯电路。

有些大型飞机装有辅助滑行灯，位于飞机下表面、机头雷达罩之后。这些灯由与主滑行灯分开的开关控制，照亮机头正前方和下方的区域。

机翼检查灯

有些飞机装有机翼检查灯，用于照亮机翼前缘，以便在飞行中观察这些区域的结冰情况和总体状态。这些灯使飞机在夜间飞行时能目视发现机翼前缘上的结冰。它们通常由驾驶舱内的通/断拨杆开关通过继电器控制。有些机翼检查灯系统可能包含或辅以额外的灯（有时称为短舱灯），用于照亮相邻区域，如整流罩鱼鳞板或起落架。这些通常为同一型式的灯，可由同一电路控制。

内部灯光

飞机装有内部灯光以照亮客舱。通常设有白光和红光两种设置。商用飞机有一套照亮主客舱的照明系统、一套在客舱灯关闭时供乘客阅读的独立照明系统，以及一套位于飞机地板上、在紧急情况下帮助乘客的应急照明系统。

照明系统的维护与检查

飞机照明系统的检查通常包括检查所有可见导线、连接、接线端、保险丝和开关的状况与牢固程度。做这些检查时可使用通断测试灯或仪表，因为许多故障的原因往往可以通过逐条系统地对电路进行通断测试来确定。

4. 语法解析

① 现在分词后置定语 + that 定语从句 + 过去分词定语三重叠加

原句：Aircraft operating at night must be equipped with position lights that meet the minimum requirements specified by Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations. 拆解：主语 Aircraft + 现在分词短语 operating at night 作后置定语；谓语 must be equipped with（被动）；宾语 position lights 后接 that 引导的定语从句，从句宾语 the minimum requirements 又带过去分词短语 specified by... 作后置定语。 要点：三个定语分属不同层次：operating 修饰 Aircraft，that 从句修饰 position lights，specified 修饰 requirements。逐层剥开才能看清主干“Aircraft must be equipped with lights”。

② such ... that 结果状语从句嵌在定语从句中

原句：They are rotating beam lights that are usually installed on top of the fuselage or tail in such a location that the light does not affect the vision of the crewmember or detract from the visibility of the position lights. 拆解：主句 They are rotating beam lights；that 引导定语从句修饰 lights，从句为被动 are installed...in such a location；such...that 引出结果状语从句，从句主语 the light，两个并列谓语 does not affect 与 (does not) detract from。 要点：这里的 that 出现两次，第一个是定语从句的关系代词，第二个是 such...that 的结果连词，作用完全不同。detract from 与 affect 共用否定词 not，第二个 not 被省略。

③ where 引导的定语从句 + 被动语态 + 介词短语嵌套

原句：The white unit is usually located on the vertical stabilizer in a position where it is clearly visible through a wide angle from the rear of the aircraft. 拆解：主句为被动 is located，后接两个介词短语 on the vertical stabilizer 与 in a position；where 引导定语从句修饰 position，从句为主系表 it is visible，clearly 与 through a wide angle、from the rear 分别作程度、方式与地点状语。 要点：where 的先行词是 position 而不是 stabilizer，判断依据是“在宽角度下可见”说的是位置。through a wide angle 表示观察的角度范围，不是“穿过”。

④ 分号连接 + of which 非限制性定语从句

原句：In some cases, one control knob or switch is used to turn on several sets of lights; for example, one type utilizes a control knob, the first movement of which turns on the position lights and the instrument panel lights. 拆解：分号前是被动句 is used to turn on...；分号后 for example 引出例证，主句 one type utilizes a control knob，逗号后 the first movement of which 引导非限制性定语从句，从句谓语 turns on 带两个并列宾语。 要点：of which 是“which 的所有格”形式，等于 whose first movement，但先行词是物时更常用 of which。非限制性从句用逗号隔开，去掉后主句仍完整。

⑤ 并列谓语 + 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：These lights are very powerful and are directed by a parabolic reflector at an angle providing a maximum range of illumination. 拆解：主语 These lights 带两个并列谓语 are very powerful（系表）和 are directed by a parabolic reflector（被动）；at an angle 为方式状语；现在分词短语 providing a maximum range of illumination 作后置定语修饰 angle。 要点：providing 的逻辑主语是 angle 而不是 lights——是“角度”提供了最大照射距离。并列谓语共用同一主语，第二个谓语前的 be 不能省，因为时态语态都需完整。

⑥ since 引导原因状语从句 + 动名词作介词宾语

原句：A continuity light or meter can be used in making these checks, since the cause of many troubles can often be located by systematically testing each circuit for continuity. 拆解：主句为被动 can be used，in making these checks 为介词+动名词短语作状语；since 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 the cause of many troubles，谓语 can be located（被动），by systematically testing... 为方式状语，动名词 testing 带宾语 each circuit。 要点：since 在此表原因（“因为”），不是“自从”。介词 by 后必须用动名词 testing，不能用不定式；for continuity 说明测试的项目。
