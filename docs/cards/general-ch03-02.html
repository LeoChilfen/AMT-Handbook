Addition of Whole Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Addition of Whole Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 97 页

1. 原文

Addition is the process where the value of one number is added to the value of another. The result is called the sum. When working with whole numbers, it is important to understand the principle of the place value. The place value in a whole number is the value of the position of each individual digit within the entire number. For example, in the number 512, the 5 is in the hundreds column, the 1 is in the tens column, and the 2 is in the ones column. Examples of place values of three whole numbers are shown in Figure 3-1.

When adding several whole numbers, such as 4,314, 122, 93,132, and 10, align them into columns according to place value and then add.

4,314 122 93,132 + 10 97,578 Therefore, 97,578 is the sum of the four whole numbers.

2. 中文翻译

加法是这样一种过程：把一个数的值加到另一个数的值上。结果称为和。处理整数时，理解位值原理很重要。整数中的位值，是指每一个单个数字在这整个数中所处位置代表的值。例如在 512 这个数中，5 在百位列，1 在十位列，2 在个位列。三个整数的位值示例见图 3-1。

对几个整数做加法（例如 4,314、122、93,132 和 10）时，要按位值把它们对齐成列，然后再相加。

4,314　122　93,132　+ 10　97,578　因此，97,578 就是这四个整数之和。

4. 语法解析

① where 引导的定语从句：the process where …

原句：Addition is the process where the value of one number is added to the value of another.

拆解：主语 Addition，系动词 is，表语 the process；where 引导定语从句修饰 process，相当于 in which；从句内部是被动结构 is added to，add A to B 的被动式。

要点：where 在定义句中常不表地点而表「在……过程中」，译作「……的过程」即可。注意 add A to B 用介词 to，不是 add A with B；被动时保留 to：A is added to B。

② When + 现在分词（省略主语）+ it 作形式主语

原句：When working with whole numbers, it is important to understand the principle of the place value.

拆解：When working with whole numbers 是时间状语从句的省略形式，完整为 When one works with whole numbers，从句主语与主句泛指的主语一致时连同 be 一起省略；主句 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to understand the principle of the place value。

要点：技术文本大量用 When + 现在分词开头，比 When you work … 简洁。it is important to do sth 中的 it 不可省；若要说明「对谁重要」，写成 it is important for the mechanic to do sth。

③ 三个并列分句 + 介词短语作表语

原句：For example, in the number 512, the 5 is in the hundreds column, the 1 is in the tens column, and the 2 is in the ones column.

拆解：For example 是插入性状语，in the number 512 是范围状语；后面三个分句结构相同：数词主语 + is + in the … column（介词短语作表语），最后用 and 连接。

要点：三个分句共用一个地点状语 in the number 512，读的时候先把它记在心里，后面的 the 5 / the 1 / the 2 都是指这个数里的某一位。位值表达固定用序数词 + column：hundreds column（百位）、thousands column（千位）。
