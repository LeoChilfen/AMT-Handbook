Nondestructive Inspection (NDI) of Composites

1. 原文

Visual Inspection

A visual inspection is the primary inspection method for in-service inspections. Visible damage may include scorches, stains, dents, penetration, abrasions, or chips in the composite surface. Once damage is detected, the affected area needs to be inspected closer using flashlights, magnifying glasses, mirrors, and borescopes. These tools are used to magnify defects that otherwise might not be seen easily and to allow visual inspection of areas that are not readily accessible. Resin starvation, resin richness, wrinkles, ply bridging, discoloration (due to overheating, lightning strike, etc.), impact damage by any cause, foreign matter, blisters, and disbonding are some of the discrepancies that can be detected with a visual inspection. Visual inspection cannot find internal flaws in the composite, such as delaminations, disbonds, and matrix crazing. More sophisticated NDI techniques are needed to detect these types of defects.

Audible Sonic Testing (Coin Tapping)

Sometimes referred to as audio, sonic, or coin tap, this technique makes use of frequencies in the audible range (10 Hz to 20 Hz). A surprisingly accurate method in the hands of experienced personnel, tap testing is perhaps the most common technique used for the detection of delamination and/or disbond. The method is accomplished by tapping the inspection area with a solid round disc or lightweight hammer-like device and listening to the response of the structure to the hammer. A clear, sharp, ringing sound is indicative of a well-bonded solid structure, while a dull or thud-like sound indicates a discrepant area.

The tapping rate needs to be rapid enough to produce enough sound for any difference in sound tone to be discernable to the ear. Tap testing is effective on thin skin to stiffener bondlines, honeycomb sandwich with thin face sheets, or even near the surface of thick laminates, such as rotorcraft blade supports. Again, inherent in the method is the possibility that changes within the internal elements of the structure might produce pitch changes that are interpreted as defects, when in fact they are present by design. This inspection should be accomplished in as quiet an area as possible and by experienced personnel familiar with the part’s internal configuration. This method is not reliable for structures with more than four plies. It is often used to map out the damage on thin honeycomb facesheets.

Automated Tap Test

This test is very similar to the manual tap test except that a solenoid is used instead of a hammer. The solenoid produces multiple impacts in a single area. The tip of the impactor has a transducer that records the force versus time signal of the impactor. The magnitude of the force depends on the impactor, the impact energy, and the mechanical properties of the structure. The impact duration (period) is not sensitive to the magnitude of the impact force; however, this duration changes as the stiffness of the structure is altered. Therefore, the signal from an unflawed region is used for calibration, and any deviation from this unflawed signal indicates the existence of damage.

Ultrasonic Inspection

Ultrasonic inspection has proven to be a very useful tool for the detection of internal delaminations, voids, or inconsistencies in composite components not otherwise discernable using visual or tap methodology. There are many ultrasonic techniques; however, each technique uses sound wave energy with a frequency above the audible range. A high-frequency (usually several MHz) sound wave is introduced into the part and may be directed to travel normal to the part surface, or along the surface of the part, or at some predefined angle to the part surface. You may need to try different directions to locate the flow. The introduced sound is then monitored as it travels its assigned route through the part for any significant change. Ultrasonic sound waves have properties similar to light waves. When an ultrasonic wave strikes an interrupting object, the wave or energy is either absorbed or reflected back to the surface. The disrupted or diminished sonic energy is then picked up by a receiving transducer and converted into a display on an oscilloscope or a chart recorder. The display allows the operator to evaluate the discrepant indications comparatively with those areas known to be good. To facilitate the comparison, reference standards are established and utilized to calibrate the ultrasonic equipment.

The repair technician must realize that the concepts outlined here work fine in the repetitious manufacturing environment, but are likely to be more difficult to implement in a repair environment given the vast number of different composite components installed on the aircraft and the relative complexity of their construction. The reference standards would also have to take into account the transmutations that take place when a composite component is exposed to an in-service environment over a prolonged period or has been the subject of repair activity or similar restorative action. The four most common ultrasonic techniques are discussed next.

Through Transmission Ultrasonic Inspection

Through transmission ultrasonic inspection uses two transducers, one on each side of the area to be inspected. The ultrasonic signal is transmitted from one transducer to the other transducer. The loss of signal strength is then measured by the instrument. The instrument shows the loss as a percent of the original signal strength or the loss in decibels. The signal loss is compared to a reference standard. Areas with a greater loss than the reference standard indicate a defective area.

Pulse Echo Ultrasonic Inspection

Single-side ultrasonic inspection may be accomplished using pulse echo techniques. In this method, a single search unit is working as a transmitting and a receiving transducer that is excited by high voltage pulses. Each electrical pulse activates the transducer element. This element converts the electrical energy into mechanical energy in the form of an ultrasonic sound wave. The sonic energy travels through a Teflon® or methacrylate contact tip into the test part. A waveform is generated in the test part and is picked up by the transducer element. Any change in amplitude of the received signal, or time required for the echo to return to the transducer, indicates the presence of a defect. Pulse echo inspections are used to find delaminations, cracks, porosity, water, and disbonds of bonded components. Pulse echo does not find disbonds or defects between laminated skins and honeycomb core.

Ultrasonic Bond Tester Inspection

Low-frequency and high-frequency bond testers are used for ultrasonic inspections of composite structures. These bond testers use an inspection probe that has one or two transducers. The high-frequency bond tester is used to detect delaminations and voids. It cannot detect a skin-to-honeycomb core disbond or porosity. It can detect defects as small as 0.5-inch in diameter. The low-frequency bond tester uses two transducers and is used to detect delamination, voids, and skin to honeycomb core disbands. This inspection method does not detect which side of the part is damaged, and cannot detect defects smaller than 1.0-inch.

Phased Array Inspection

Phased array inspection is one of the latest ultrasonic instruments to detect flaws in composite structures. It operates under the same principle of operation as pulse echo, but it uses 64 sensors at the same time, which speeds up the process.

Radiography

Radiography, often referred to as X-ray, is a very useful NDI method because it essentially allows a view into the interior of the part. This inspection method is accomplished by passing X-rays through the part or assembly being tested while recording the absorption of the rays onto a film sensitive to X-rays. The exposed film, when developed, allows the inspector to analyze variations in the opacity of the exposure recorded onto the film, in effect creating a visualization of the relationship of the component’s internal details. Since the method records changes in total density through its thickness, it is not a preferred method for detecting defects such as delaminations that are in a plane that is normal to the ray direction. It is a most effective method, however, for detecting flaws parallel to the X-ray beam’s centerline. Internal anomalies, such as delaminations in the corners, crushed core, blown core, water in core cells, voids in foam adhesive joints, and relative position of internal details, can readily be seen via radiography. Most composites are nearly transparent to X-rays, so low energy rays must be used. Because of safety concerns, it is impractical to use around aircraft. Operators should always be protected by sufficient lead shields, as the possibility of exposure exists either from the X-ray tube or from scattered radiation. Maintaining a minimum safe distance from the X-ray source is always essential.

Thermography

Thermal inspection comprises all methods in which heat-sensing devices are used to measure temperature variations for parts under inspection. The basic principle of thermal inspection consists of measuring or mapping of surface temperatures when heat flows from, to, or through a test object. All thermographic techniques rely on differentials in thermal conductivity between normal, defect free areas, and those having a defect. Normally, a heat source is used to elevate the temperature of the part being examined while observing the surface heating effects. Because defect free areas conduct heat more efficiently than areas with defects, the amount of heat that is either absorbed or reflected indicates the quality of the bond. The type of defects that affect the thermal properties include debonds, cracks, impact damage, panel thinning, and water ingress into composite materials and honeycomb core. Thermal methods are most effective for thin laminates or for defects near the surface.

Neutron Radiography

Neutron radiography is a nondestructive imaging technique that is capable of visualizing the internal characteristics of a sample. The transmission of neutrons through a medium is dependent upon the neutron cross sections for the nuclei in the medium. Differential attenuation of neutrons through a medium may be measured, mapped, and then visualized. The resulting image may then be utilized to analyze the internal characteristics of the sample. Neutron radiography is a complementary technique to X-ray radiography. Both techniques visualize the attenuation through a medium. The major advantage of neutron radiography is its ability to reveal light elements such as hydrogen found in corrosion products and water.

Moisture Detector

A moisture meter can be used to detect water in sandwich honeycomb structures. A moisture meter measures the radio frequency (RF) power loss caused by the presence of water. The moisture meter is often used to detect moisture in nose radomes. Figure 7-30 provides a comparison of NDI testing equipment.

2. 中文翻译

目视检查

目视检查是使用中检查的主要方法。可见损伤可能包括复合材料表面的烧焦、污渍、凹坑、穿透、磨损或崩边。一旦发现损伤，就需要用手电筒、放大镜、反光镜和内窥镜对受影响区域做更细致的检查。这些工具用来放大原本不易看清的缺陷，并使难以接近的区域也能进行目视检查。贫树脂、富树脂、皱褶、铺层架桥、变色（由过热、雷击等引起）、任何原因造成的冲击损伤、异物、起泡和脱粘，都是目视检查可以发现的一些异常。目视检查无法发现复合材料内部的缺陷，例如分层、脱粘和基体龟裂。要检出这类缺陷，需要更复杂的无损检测（NDI）技术。

可听声波检测（敲击法）

这种技术有时称为音频法、声波法或硬币敲击法，利用的是人耳可听范围内的频率（10 Hz 至 20 Hz）。在经验丰富的人员手中，敲击检测的准确程度令人惊讶，它也许是检测分层和/或脱粘最常用的技术。方法是用实心圆盘或轻质锤状器具敲击待检区域，听结构对锤击的响应。清脆、尖锐、有回响的声音说明胶接良好、结构密实；而沉闷或“咚”的一声则表明存在异常区域。

敲击的速率要足够快，才能产生足够的声响，使音调上的任何差异都能被耳朵分辨。敲击检测对薄蒙皮与加强件之间的胶接线、薄面板的蜂窝夹层，甚至厚层合板的近表面区域（例如旋翼桨叶支撑件）都有效。同样，这种方法本身固有的问题是：结构内部构件的变化也可能产生音调变化，被误判为缺陷，而实际上这些变化是设计上本来就有的。这项检查应在尽可能安静的区域进行，并由熟悉该部件内部构造的有经验人员实施。对于超过四层的结构，此方法并不可靠。它常用于在薄蜂窝面板上标绘损伤范围。

自动敲击检测

该检测与手动敲击检测非常相似，只是用电磁铁（螺线管）代替了锤子。电磁铁在同一区域产生多次冲击。冲击器的端头装有一个换能器，记录冲击器的力—时间信号。力的大小取决于冲击器、冲击能量以及结构的力学性能。冲击持续时间（周期）对冲击力的大小不敏感；然而，当结构的刚度改变时，这一持续时间会变化。因此，用无缺陷区域的信号进行校准，凡偏离该无缺陷信号的，就表明存在损伤。

超声检测

对于目视法或敲击法无法分辨的复合材料内部的分层、空洞或不连续，超声检测已被证明是非常有用的工具。超声技术有很多种，但每种技术使用的都是频率高于可听范围的声波能量。把一束高频（通常为几兆赫）声波引入零件，可使其垂直于零件表面传播，或沿零件表面传播，或与零件表面成某个预定角度传播。为了找到缺陷，你可能需要尝试不同的方向。然后对引入的声波按其既定路径穿过零件的过程进行监测，看是否有显著变化。超声波的特性与光波相似。当超声波遇到阻碍物时，波或能量要么被吸收，要么被反射回表面。被打断或减弱的声能被接收换能器拾取，并转换成示波器或图表记录仪上的显示。操作人员可借助显示，把异常指示与已知良好的区域进行对比评估。为便于对比，需要建立参考试块并用来校准超声设备。

修理技术人员必须认识到，这里概述的概念在重复性的制造环境中行之有效，但在修理环境中实施起来很可能更为困难，因为飞机上安装的复合材料部件种类繁多，其构造也相对复杂。参考试块还必须考虑到复合材料部件长期暴露于使用环境后、或经历过修理活动或类似修复措施后所发生的变化。下面讨论四种最常见的超声技术。

穿透法超声检测

穿透法超声检测使用两个换能器，分别置于待检区域的两侧。超声信号从一个换能器发射到另一个换能器。仪器随即测量信号强度的损失，并以原始信号强度的百分比或分贝数显示该损失。信号损失与参考试块进行对比。损失大于参考试块的区域即为缺陷区域。

脉冲回波超声检测

单面超声检测可用脉冲回波技术完成。这种方法中，单个探头同时作为发射换能器和接收换能器，由高压脉冲激励。每个电脉冲激励换能器元件，该元件把电能转换成超声波形式的机械能。声能经特氟龙（Teflon®）或甲基丙烯酸酯接触头传入被检零件。零件中产生波形，由换能器元件拾取。接收信号幅度的任何变化，或回波返回换能器所需时间的任何变化，都表明存在缺陷。脉冲回波检测用于查找分层、裂纹、孔隙、水分以及胶接件的脱粘。脉冲回波检测无法发现层合蒙皮与蜂窝芯之间的脱粘或缺陷。

超声胶接检测仪检测

低频和高频胶接检测仪用于复合材料结构的超声检测。这类检测仪使用带一个或两个换能器的检测探头。高频胶接检测仪用于检测分层和空洞，它无法检测蒙皮与蜂窝芯之间的脱粘或孔隙，可检出直径小至 0.5 英寸的缺陷。低频胶接检测仪使用两个换能器，用于检测分层、空洞以及蒙皮与蜂窝芯之间的脱粘。这种检测方法无法判定部件是哪一侧受损，也无法检出小于 1.0 英寸的缺陷。

相控阵检测

相控阵检测是用于检测复合材料结构缺陷的最新超声仪器之一。它的工作原理与脉冲回波相同，但同时使用 64 个传感器，从而加快了检测过程。

射线照相

射线照相常被称为 X 射线检测，是一种非常有用的无损检测方法，因为它实质上相当于让检查者看到部件内部。该方法让 X 射线穿过被检零件或组件，同时把射线的吸收情况记录在对 X 射线敏感的胶片上。曝光后的胶片经显影，检查者便可分析胶片所记录曝光不透明度的变化，实际上就是把部件内部细节的关系可视化。由于该方法记录的是沿厚度方向的总密度变化，对于位于与射线方向垂直平面内的缺陷（例如分层），它并不是首选方法。然而，对于平行于 X 射线束中心线的缺陷，它却是最有效的方法。内部异常，例如转角处的分层、压溃的芯材、吹破的芯材、蜂窝芯格中的水、泡沫胶接接头中的空洞以及内部细节的相对位置，都可以通过射线照相清晰地看到。大多数复合材料对 X 射线几乎透明，因此必须使用低能射线。出于安全考虑，在飞机周围使用并不现实。操作人员应始终有足够的铅屏蔽保护，因为X射线管或散射辐射都可能造成照射。始终与 X 射线源保持最小安全距离是至关重要的。

热成像

热学检测包括所有使用热敏装置测量被检零件温度变化的方法。热学检测的基本原理是：当热量从试验对象流出、流入或穿过试验对象时，测量或测绘其表面温度。所有热成像技术都依赖于无缺陷的正常区域与有缺陷区域之间导热率的差异。通常用热源提高被检零件的温度，同时观察表面受热效果。由于无缺陷区域导热比有缺陷区域更有效，被吸收或被反射的热量多少就表明了胶接质量。影响热学性能的缺陷类型包括脱粘、裂纹、冲击损伤、壁板减薄，以及水分侵入复合材料和蜂窝芯。热学方法对薄层合板或近表面缺陷最为有效。

中子射线照相

中子射线照相是一种无损成像技术，能够使样件的内部特征可视化。中子穿过介质的透射取决于介质中原子核的中子截面。中子穿过介质时产生的差异衰减可以被测量、测绘，然后成像。所得图像随后可用于分析样件的内部特征。中子射线照相是 X 射线照相的补充技术。两种技术都是把穿过介质的衰减成像。中子射线照相的主要优点在于，它能够显现轻元素，例如腐蚀产物和水中所含的氢。

水分检测仪

水分仪可用于检测夹层蜂窝结构中的水。水分仪测量水存在所引起的射频（RF）功率损耗。水分仪常用于检测机头雷达罩内的水分。图 7-30 给出了无损检测设备的对比。

4. 语法解析

① Once 引导的时间状语从句 + 方式状语

原句：Once damage is detected, the affected area needs to be inspected closer using flashlights, magnifying glasses, mirrors, and borescopes. 拆解：Once damage is detected 为时间状语从句，用被动语态；主句主语 the affected area，谓语 needs to be inspected（need 后接不定式被动式）；closer 为程度状语；using... 为现在分词短语作方式状语。 要点：once 在此是连词“一旦”，不是副词“一次”。needs to be inspected 是“需要被检查”，主语 area 是动作的承受者，所以用不定式被动式；closer 修饰 inspect，不是修饰 area。

② 长并列主语 + 定语从句

原句：Resin starvation, resin richness, wrinkles, ply bridging, discoloration (due to overheating, lightning strike, etc.), impact damage by any cause, foreign matter, blisters, and disbonding are some of the discrepancies that can be detected with a visual inspection. 拆解：主语为九个名词短语的并列，从 Resin starvation 到 disbonding；括号内 due to... 说明变色成因；谓语 are，表语 some of the discrepancies；that can be detected... 为定语从句修饰 discrepancies，with a visual inspection 为方式状语。 要点：并列主语很长，谓语 are 直到最后才出现，阅读时先数清并列项、再找谓语。that 从句修饰的是 discrepancies（异常），不是最后一个并列项 disbonding；被动语态 can be detected 保留了情态动词。

③ except that 引导的名词性从句

原句：This test is very similar to the manual tap test except that a solenoid is used instead of a hammer. 拆解：主句 This test is very similar to the manual tap test，be similar to 为固定搭配；except that 引导名词性从句作例外说明，从句为被动 a solenoid is used，instead of a hammer 为替代状语。 要点：except that 后必须接完整从句（有自己的主语和谓语），而 except for 后接名词短语，二者不可混用。is used 的被动语态强调“用的是电磁铁”而非“电磁铁做了什么”。

④ Since 原因状语从句 + 双重定语从句

原句：Since the method records changes in total density through its thickness, it is not a preferred method for detecting defects such as delaminations that are in a plane that is normal to the ray direction. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句，records 为谓语、changes in total density 为宾语；主句 it is not a preferred method for detecting defects；such as delaminations 为举例，后接 that are in a plane 定语从句，其中又套 that is normal to the ray direction 定语从句修饰 plane。 要点：两个 that 从句分属不同层次：第一个修饰 delaminations，第二个修饰 plane，中间的 in a plane 是第一个从句的表语。not a preferred method 是“不是首选”，不是“不能”，语气要译准。

⑤ Because 原因状语从句 + that 定语从句

原句：Because defect free areas conduct heat more efficiently than areas with defects, the amount of heat that is either absorbed or reflected indicates the quality of the bond. 拆解：Because 引导原因状语从句，conduct heat 为动宾，more efficiently than... 为比较状语；主句主语 the amount of heat，后接 that is either absorbed or reflected 定语从句；谓语 indicates，宾语 the quality of the bond。 要点：either... or... 在定语从句内并列两个过去分词，说明热量“不是被吸收就是被反射”。主句谓语 indicates 与主语中心词 amount 保持一致，of heat 只是修饰语。

⑥ 被动语态 + 三个并列方向状语

原句：A high-frequency (usually several MHz) sound wave is introduced into the part and may be directed to travel normal to the part surface, or along the surface of the part, or at some predefined angle to the part surface. 拆解：主语 A high-frequency sound wave，括号内为补充说明；谓语为 is introduced 与 may be directed 两个被动式的并列；to travel... 为不定式作主语补足语；后面三个 or 引出并列的方向状语。 要点：may be directed to travel 中的 to travel 说明声波“被引导去传播”，不是目的状语。三个方向状语由 or 并列，分别表示垂直、沿表面和成预定角度，翻译时保持三项并列结构。
