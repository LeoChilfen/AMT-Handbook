Care of Drafting Instruments

通用 Ch 04 · Care of Drafting Instruments · 原文 PDF 第 146 页

1. 原文

Good drawing instruments are expensive precision tools.

Reasonable care given to them during their use and storage can prolong their service life.

T-squares, triangles, and scales should not be used or placed where their surfaces or edges may be damaged. Use a drawing board only for its intended purpose and not in a manner that can mar the working surface.

Compasses, dividers, and pens provide better results with less annoyance, if they are correctly shaped and sharpened and are not damaged by careless handling. Store drawing instruments in a place where they are not likely to be damaged by contact with other tools or equipment. Protect compass and divider points by inserting them into a piece of soft rubber or similar material. Never store ink pens without first cleaning and drying them thoroughly.

2. 中文翻译

好的绘图仪器是昂贵的精密工具。

在使用和存放期间给予适当爱护，可以延长其使用寿命。

丁字尺、三角板和比例尺不应使用或放置在其表面或边缘可能受损的地方。绘图板只应按规定用途使用，不得以会损伤其工作面的方式使用。

圆规、分规和绘图笔如果形状正确、磨得锋利，并且没有因搬弄不当而受损，使用起来效果更好、麻烦更少。绘图仪器应存放在不会因与其他工具或设备接触而受损的地方。要保护圆规和分规的针尖，可把它们插入一块软橡胶或类似材料中。墨线笔绝不能未经彻底清洗并干燥就收存。

4. 语法解析

① Reasonable care given to them … can prolong their service life（过去分词短语作后置定语）

原句：Reasonable care given to them during their use and storage can prolong their service life.

拆解：主语中心词是 care，过去分词短语 given to them during their use and storage 作后置定语（= which is given to them …）；谓语是 can prolong，宾语 their service life；them 与 their 都指绘图仪器。

要点：带状语的分词短语修饰名词时一律后置，译成汉语则前置：「在使用和存放期间给予它们的适当爱护」。care 在此是不可数名词，故用单数谓语 can（不写 cares）。give care to 是「爱护、照管」，不是「给护理」。

② should not be used or placed where …（情态否定被动 + where 状语从句）

原句：T-squares, triangles, and scales should not be used or placed where their surfaces or edges may be damaged.

拆解：主语并列三项，谓语 should not be used or placed 为情态否定被动，两个过去分词共用；where 引导地点状语从句，说明「在哪些地方不行」，从句内 may be damaged 又是被动。

要点：should not be done 表示「不应被……」，是规范性的劝阻，比 must not 语气缓和。地点从句里用 may 表示「有可能」，即某处存在损坏风险就不该放。注意 where 从句修饰的是 placed 这个动作，而不是被动的对象。

③ if they are correctly shaped and sharpened and are not damaged …（条件从句中的并列被动）

原句：Compasses, dividers, and pens provide better results with less annoyance, if they are correctly shaped and sharpened and are not damaged by careless handling.

拆解：主句 Compasses, dividers, and pens provide better results with less annoyance；if 引导条件从句，从句主语 they 后并列三个被动谓语：are correctly shaped、are (correctly) sharpened、are not damaged by careless handling。

要点：三个被动谓语共用主语与 be 动词，最后一个用否定式。by careless handling 中的 by 引出原因（因搬弄不当而），不是被动语态的执行者，译作「由于」更顺。主句与从句用逗号隔开，是因为从句在后、带补充说明性质。

④ Never store … without first cleaning and drying them thoroughly（否定祈使 + without + 动名词）

原句：Never store ink pens without first cleaning and drying them thoroughly.

拆解：Never 引导否定祈使句，动词 store；without 是介词，后接并列动名词 first cleaning and drying，宾语 them 指 ink pens；thoroughly 修饰两个动名词。

要点：Never … without … 是「绝不要在没有……的情况下……」的双重否定句式，语气最强，通常译作「绝不能未经……就……」。without 后必须用 -ing，不能用动词原形；first 在此是「先（做某事）」，位置在动名词前。
