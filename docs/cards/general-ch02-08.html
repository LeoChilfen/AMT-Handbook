Airworthiness Certificates

In addition to the registration certificate that indicates the ownership of an aircraft, an airworthiness certificate indicates the airworthiness of the aircraft. AC 21-12, Application for U.S. Airworthiness Certificate, FAA Form 8130-6, is a comprehensive guide for the completion of the application form for this certificate. There are two certificates: standard

• Recommended overhaul periods

• Details for an inspection program that identifies both the frequency and the extent of the inspections necessary to provide for continued airworthiness

• Troubleshooting information

• The order and method for proper removal and replacement of parts

• Procedures for system testing during ground operations

• Diagrams for structural access plates

• Details for application of special inspection techniques

• Information concerning the application of protective treatments after inspection

• Information relative to the structural fasteners

• List of any special tools needed

Airworthiness Limitations

The ICA must contain a separate and clearly distinguishable section titled “Airworthiness Limitations.” Within this section are mandatory replacement times, structural inspection interval, and related inspection procedures.

All of this is included in the initial release of documents when the aircraft is delivered. However, over the course of the life of an aircraft, various modifications can and often do occur. Whether these are as simple as a new cabin to galley sliding door, or as complex as a navigation related STC, any major alteration requires that this type of maintenance data be provided to the owner, so that subsequent maintenance, inspection, and repair can be properly accomplished. As aircraft and their systems become more and more complex, and society continues its preoccupation with litigation for every incident, it is imperative that the technician have the right information, that it is current, and that they have the proper tools, including those required for any special inspection, and correct replacement parts. If any one of these items is required, and the technician does not have it accessible, they are in violation of 14 CFR sections 65.81(b), 43.13(a), and 43.16 if they attempt to return the aircraft to service.

Manufacturers may provide this required information in a variety of different manuals:

• Operating Instructions—The Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) or the Pilot’s Operating Handbook (POH) provides the pilot with the necessary information to properly operate the aircraft. These manuals are usually listed in the aircraft TCDS, and therefore are a required item for the aircraft to be considered airworthy. Note that the AFM is generally serial number specific, whereas the POH is model specific.

Aircraft Registration

Aircraft must be registered in the United States if the aircraft is not registered under the laws of a foreign country and is owned by either a citizen of the United States, a foreign citizen lawfully admitted to the United States, or a corporation organized in and doing business under U.S. laws and primarily based in the United States. This registration is accomplished by using FAA Form 8050-1, Aircraft Registration Application. The aircraft registration form is available online at www.faa.gov. The aircraft owner can mail in completed copy, and keep a copy of the form as temporary authority to operate the aircraft after the fee and evidence of ownership have been mailed or delivered to the Registry. When carried in the aircraft with an appropriate current airworthiness certificate or a special flight permit, a copy of this completed application provides authority to operate the aircraft in the United States for up to 90 days.

Radio Station License

A radio station license is required if the aircraft is equipped with radios, and the aircraft is planned to be flown outside the boundaries of the United States. A radio station license is not required for aircraft that are operated domestically. (A major change occurred on February 8, 1996, when the telecommunications Act of 1996 was signed into law.)

The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) formerly required that any communication transmitter installed in aircraft be licensed. These FCC licenses were valid for 5 years. This is not an FAA requirement. FAA inspectors who conducted ramp inspections and detected an expired radio station license were not required to notify the FCC, nor could they issue a violation to the owner/operator. Simply informing the operator of the expired radio station license was their only responsibility.

FSGA 96-06, a Flight Standards Information Bulletin (FSIB) for General Aviation (FSGA) titled “Elimination of Aircraft Radio Station Licenses” became effective on July 8, 1996. Although that FSIB had an effectivity of only 1 year, the elimination of the requirement for aircraft used only in domestic operations continues.

FAA Form 337—Major Repair and Alteration

Refer to the current issue of AC 43.9-1, Instructions for Completion of FAA Form 337 for help completing FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration (Airframe, Powerplant, Propeller, or Appliance).

As the name clearly states, this form is to be used whenever major repairs or alterations are accomplished on an aircraft. The only exception would be that 14 CFR part 43, Appendix B, allows for a certificated repair station to RTS an aircraft after a major repair by using a signed and dated work order and a signed maintenance release.

• Information in item 1 comes directly from the aircraft dataplate, except for the tail number. That is to be compared to the aircraft registration form.

• Information in item 2 reflects the name and address listed on AC Form 8050-3, Certificate of Registration.

• Item 3 is used when there is no existing approved data for the intended repair or alteration. In that case, the technician can request that the local FSDO Principal Maintenance Inspector (PMI) review the data and then grant a field approval, shown by completing and signing this area. In many cases, this block is blank because the technician has found, used, and made reference to data already approved by the FAA.

• Item 4—If the repair or alteration is being done to the aircraft airframe, no entry is required since the data is identical to that in item 1. However, if the repair or alteration is being done to an engine, a propeller, or other appliance, entries must include the appropriate make, model, and serial number information.

• Item 5 should have “X” marked in either the “Repair”

or the “Alteration” column.

• Item 6—Enter appropriate data as specified and check the proper box in B. The technician is encouraged to carefully read the preprinted statement in subparagraph D prior to signing this section.

• Item 7 must be completed by the IA or authorized individual from the repair station.

• Item 8 (on the reverse side) is for the description of the work accomplished. It must include a reference to the approved data used to conduct the required maintenance.

The form must be completed at least in duplicate, with the original provided to the owner/operator and a copy to the local FSDO within 48 hours of completing the maintenance and RTS. If the FAA Form 337 is used to document additional fuel tanks in the cabin or cargo, then an additional copy must be signed and in the aircraft at all times. Maintenance facilities and mechanics are encouraged to make a copy for their own records.

适航证书

除表明航空器所有权的登记证书外，适航证书表明航空器的适航性。AC 21-12《美国适航证书申请》（申请表为 FAA Form 8130-6）是填写该证书申请表的完整指南。该证书分两类：标准类

• 建议的大修周期

• 检查大纲的详细内容，既规定检查频次，也规定为保持持续适航所必需的检查范围

• 故障排除信息

• 正确拆卸与更换部件的顺序和方法

• 地面运行期间的系统测试程序

• 结构检查口盖的示意图

• 应用特殊检查技术的详细说明

• 检查后施加防护处理的有关信息

• 有关结构紧固件的信息

• 所需专用工具的清单

适航限制

ICA 必须包含一个单独且清晰可辨的章节，标题为「适航限制」。该章节内载有强制性更换时间、结构检查间隔及相关检查程序。

所有这些内容都包含在航空器交付时最初发布的文件中。然而在航空器的寿命期内，各种改装可能会、而且经常会发生。无论这些改装简单到只是新增一扇客舱通厨房的滑门，还是复杂到与导航相关的 STC，任何重大改装都要求向所有人提供这类维修数据，以便后续的维修、检查和修理能够正确地完成。随着航空器及其系统日益复杂，社会又持续痴迷于为每一起事件打官司，技术人员必须掌握正确的信息、信息必须是最新的，并且必须拥有合适的工具（包括实施任何特殊检查所需的工具）和正确的替换件。如果上述任何一项是必需的而技术人员无法取得，那么他们若试图让航空器恢复使用，就违反了 14 CFR 第 65.81(b) 条、第 43.13(a) 条和第 43.16 条。

制造商可以用多种不同的手册提供这些必需的信息：

• 使用说明——飞机飞行手册（AFM）或驾驶员操作手册（POH）为驾驶员提供正确操作航空器所必需的信息。这些手册通常列入航空器的 TCDS，因而是航空器被视为适航所必需的随机项目。注意：AFM 一般按出厂序号编制，而 POH 按型号编制。

航空器登记

如果航空器未在外国法律下登记，且其所有人为美国公民、合法获准进入美国的外国公民，或依美国法律组建并在美国经营、主要基地设在美国的公司，则该航空器必须在美国登记。登记通过 FAA Form 8050-1《航空器登记申请表》办理。该登记表可在 www.faa.gov 在线获取。所有人可将填妥的副本邮寄提交，并保留一份表格副本，作为在费用和所有权证明已邮寄或递交登记处之后运行航空器的临时授权。当该填妥申请表的副本与相应的现行适航证书或特别飞行许可一同随机携带时，即构成在美国境内运行航空器最长 90 天的授权。

无线电台执照

如果航空器装有无线电设备，且计划飞出美国境外，则需要无线电台执照。仅在美国境内运行的航空器不需要无线电台执照。（1996 年 2 月 8 日《1996 年电信法》签署成为法律时发生过一次重大变化。）

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）以前要求，凡安装在航空器上的通信发射机都必须取得执照。这些 FCC 执照的有效期为 5 年。这不是 FAA 的要求。FAA 检查员在执行停机坪检查时发现无线电台执照过期，无须通知 FCC，也不能对所有人／运营人开具违规单；他们唯一的责任只是把执照过期的情况告知运营人。

FSGA 96-06 是一份面向通用航空（FSGA）的飞行标准信息通告（FSIB），标题为《取消航空器无线电台执照》，1996 年 7 月 8 日生效。虽然该 FSIB 的有效期只有 1 年，但仅在美国境内运行的航空器免除此项要求这一做法延续至今。

FAA Form 337——重大修理与改装

填写 FAA Form 337《重大修理与改装（机体、动力装置、螺旋桨或附件）》时，请参阅现行版本的 AC 43.9-1《FAA Form 337 填写说明》。

顾名思义，凡对航空器实施重大修理或改装，都必须使用本表格。唯一的例外是：14 CFR part 43 附录 B 允许持证维修站在完成重大修理后，使用已签名并注明日期的工作单和已签名的维修放行单让航空器恢复使用。

• 第 1 项的信息直接取自航空器数据铭牌，但机尾号除外——机尾号应与航空器登记表核对。

• 第 2 项的信息反映 AC Form 8050-3《登记证书》上所载的名称和地址。

• 当拟实施的修理或改装没有现成的已批准数据时，使用第 3 项。此时技术人员可以请当地飞行标准地区办公室（FSDO）的主任维修检查员（PMI）审查数据，然后给予现场批准，其标志是填写并签署该栏。很多情况下该栏为空白，因为技术人员已找到、使用了 FAA 已批准的数据并作了引用。

• 第 4 项——如果修理或改装的对象是航空器机体，则无需填写，因为该数据与第 1 项相同。但如果修理或改装的对象是发动机、螺旋桨或其他附件，则必须填写相应的制造厂、型号和出厂序号信息。

• 第 5 项应在「修理」

或「改装」栏内标「X」。

• 第 6 项——按规定的数据填写，并勾选 B 项中相应的方框。建议技术人员在签署本节之前仔细阅读 D 小段中的预先印制声明。

• 第 7 项必须由 IA（经授权的检查员）或维修站的授权人员填写。

• 第 8 项（位于背面）用于描述所完成的工作，必须包含对实施该项维修所用已批准数据的引用。

本表至少须填写两份：正本交所有人／运营人，一份副本在完成维修并恢复使用后 48 小时内交当地 FSDO。如果用 FAA Form 337 记录客舱或货舱内的附加油箱，则还须签署额外一份副本并始终随机携带。维修单位和机务人员宜自留一份副本备查。

4. 语法解析

① titled 引出的分词短语与引号内的标题

原句：The ICA must contain a separate and clearly distinguishable section titled “Airworthiness Limitations.”

拆解：主干 The ICA must contain a separate and clearly distinguishable section；titled… 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 section；引号内是章节的确切标题。

要点：titled 是 title 的过去分词，意为「标题为…的」，与 section 构成被动关系，不能用 titling。并列形容词 separate and clearly distinguishable 中 clearly 修饰 distinguishable，位置不能移到 separate 前。原文用引号标出章节名，翻译时保留为「适航限制」，不要译成「适航限制部分」而冲淡其专有标题性质。

② 条件从句的并列与被动动词的省略

原句：A radio station license is required if the aircraft is equipped with radios, and the aircraft is planned to be flown outside the boundaries of the United States.

拆解：主句 A radio station license is required；if 引导的条件状语从句由两个并列分句构成：the aircraft is equipped with radios 与 the aircraft is planned to be flown outside the boundaries of the United States，第二个分句重复主语 aircraft 以保持清晰。

要点：are required 与 is required 的差别在于主语单复数：licence 单数用 is；后半句 the aircraft 单复数同形，故用 is。be planned to be flown 是「被动 + 被动不定式」，字面为「被计划被飞行」，汉语按逻辑主语处理即可（「计划飞往…」）。判定条件是两个条件同时成立（装有电台 + 飞出国境），不能译成「或」。

③ requires that … be 的虚拟语气

原句：14 CFR section 91.203(a)(2) requires that either the pink copy of the application or the actual certificate of registration be on board the aircraft during its operation.

拆解：主语 14 CFR section 91.203(a)(2) + 谓语 requires + that 引导的宾语从句；从句主语为 either A or B 结构（A = the pink copy of the application，B = the actual certificate of registration），从句谓语用原形 be。

要点：require、demand、insist 等表要求的动词后接 that 从句时，从句谓语用动词原形（虚拟语气），因此是 be on board 而不是 is on board；改成 require sb to do 时则为 requires the copy or the certificate to be on board。be on board 是「随机携带」的固定说法，介词 on 不可换 in the aircraft——航空语境里 on board 就是机上。

④ The only exception would be that… 与 allows for sb to do

原句：The only exception would be that 14 CFR part 43, Appendix B, allows for a certificated repair station to RTS an aircraft after a major repair by using a signed and dated work order and a signed maintenance release.

拆解：主句 The only exception would be + that 引导的表语从句；从句主干 allows for a certificated repair station to RTS an aircraft，after a major repair 为时间状语，by using… 为方式状语（两个并列宾语 a signed and dated work order 与 a signed maintenance release）。

要点：would be 用虚拟/委婉语气，因为「唯一例外」是假设性的规定而非事实描述。allow for sb to do 与 allow sb to do 都成立，前者多一层「容许…的存在」意味，规章文本常见 allow for。RTS 在此处被当作动词用（to RTS an aircraft），是行业里把缩写动词化的典型写法，翻译时还原为「让…恢复使用」。a signed and dated work order 两个分词并列修饰名词，语序固定为 signed and dated。

⑤ 主句在前、if 从句在后 + either…or… 的三项列举

原句：Aircraft must be registered in the United States if the aircraft is not registered under the laws of a foreign country and is owned by either a citizen of the United States, a foreign citizen lawfully admitted to the United States, or a corporation organized in and doing business under U.S. laws and primarily based in the United States.

拆解：主句 Aircraft must be registered in the United States；if 从句含两个并列谓语：is not registered under the laws of a foreign country 与 is owned by either A, B, or C；either…or… 之后并列三项名词，均为带后置修饰的短语（lawfully admitted…、organized in and doing business under…、primarily based in…）。

要点：either 后接三项以上时与 or 搭配仍成立（either A, B, or C），比 whether…or… 更简洁。Aircraft 单复数同形，此处泛指「航空器」这一类，谓语用 must be 不受影响；若专指某架则加 the。organized in and doing business under U.S. laws 是两个分词共用一个介词宾语，翻译时拆成「依美国法律组建并在美国经营」两动作；primarily based 的 primarily 修饰 based，不能译成「主要的基地」。
