Chemical Cleaners

通用 Ch 08 · Chemical Cleaners · 原文 PDF 第 354 页

1. 原文

Chemical cleaners must be used with great care in cleaning assembled aircraft. The danger of entrapping corrosive materials in faying surfaces and crevices counteracts any advantages in their speed and effectiveness. Any materials used must be relatively neutral and easy to remove. It is emphasized that all residues must be removed. Soluble salts from chemical surface treatments, such as chromic acid or dichromate treatment, liquefy and promote blistering in the paint coatings.

Phosphoric-citric Acid

A phosphoric-citric acid mixture (Type I) for cleaning aluminum surfaces is available and is ready to use as packaged. Type II is a concentrate that must be diluted with mineral spirits and water. Wear rubber gloves and goggles to avoid skin contact. Any acid burns may be neutralized by copious water washing, followed by treatment with a diluted solution of baking soda (sodium bicarbonate).

Baking soda may be used to neutralize acid deposits in lead-acid battery compartments and to treat acid burns from chemical cleaners and inhibitors.

2. 中文翻译

在清洁装配好的飞机时，必须极其小心地使用化学清洁剂。腐蚀性材料滞留在贴合面和缝隙中的危险，抵消了其在速度和有效性方面的任何优势。所用任何材料都必须相对中性且易于去除。这里强调，必须去除所有残留物。化学表面处理（如铬酸或重铬酸盐处理）产生的可溶性盐会液化并促使漆层起泡。

磷酸-柠檬酸

用于清洁铝表面的磷酸-柠檬酸混合液（Ⅰ型）已有供应，按包装即可使用。Ⅱ型是浓缩液，必须用矿物油精和水稀释。要戴橡胶手套和护目镜，避免皮肤接触。任何酸灼伤都可以用大量水冲洗来中和，然后用小苏打（碳酸氢钠）稀溶液处理。

小苏打可用于中和铅酸蓄电池舱内的酸沉积物，以及处理化学清洁剂和缓蚀剂造成的酸灼伤。

4. 语法解析

① 情态被动 + 动名词状语

原句：Chemical cleaners must be used with great care in cleaning assembled aircraft. 拆解：主语 Chemical cleaners，谓语 must be used（情态被动），with great care 为方式状语，in cleaning assembled aircraft 为范围状语（动名词短语）。 要点：in + 动名词表示“在做……时/在……方面”，与 when cleaning 语义接近但更正式；assembled 为过去分词作前置定语。

② of + 动名词作后置定语

原句：The danger of entrapping corrosive materials in faying surfaces and crevices counteracts any advantages in their speed and effectiveness. 拆解：主语 The danger（of entrapping… 为其后置定语），谓语 counteracts，宾语 any advantages；in their speed and effectiveness 为范围状语，their 指化学清洁剂。 要点：of 后接动名词说明“危险”的具体内容；counteract 意为“抵消”，是及物动词，不能加 against。

③ It is emphasized that 形式主语

原句：It is emphasized that all residues must be removed. 拆解：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句 all residues must be removed（情态被动）。 要点：It is + 过去分词 + that 是技术文本中常见的强调句式，翻译时可译为“这里强调……”，不必拘泥于“它被强调”。

④ such as 插入语 + 并列谓语

原句：Soluble salts from chemical surface treatments, such as chromic acid or dichromate treatment, liquefy and promote blistering in the paint coatings. 拆解：主语 Soluble salts（from chemical surface treatments 为后置定语），such as… 为插入举例；谓语 liquefy 与 promote 并列，promote blistering 为“促使起泡”。 要点：such as 举例时用逗号隔开，去掉后句子仍完整；liquefy 是不及物动词，promote 是及物动词，两者并列时注意宾语只属于后者。

⑤ may be neutralized by + followed by

原句：Any acid burns may be neutralized by copious water washing, followed by treatment with a diluted solution of baking soda (sodium bicarbonate). 拆解：主语 Any acid burns，谓语 may be neutralized（情态被动），by copious water washing 为方式状语；followed by… 为过去分词短语作补充说明，逻辑主语是前面的处理过程。 要点：followed by 在此表示“随后进行”，不是“被跟随”；a diluted solution of… 是“某物的稀溶液”，diluted 修饰 solution。
