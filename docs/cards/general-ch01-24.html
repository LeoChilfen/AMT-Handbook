Procedures for Securing Weight-Shift-Control

通用 Ch 01 · Procedures for Securing Weight-Shift-Control · 原文 PDF 第 33 页

1. 原文

There are many types of weight-shift-controlled aircraft— engine powered and non-powered. These types of aircraft are very susceptible to wind damage. The wings can be secured in a similar manner as a conventional aircraft in light winds. In high winds, the mast can be disconnected from the wing and the wing placed close to the ground and secured. This type of aircraft can also be partially disassembled or moved into a hangar for protection.

2. 中文翻译

重量转移操纵飞机有多种类型——有动力的和无动力的都有。这类飞机极易受风害。轻风时，机翼可参照常规飞机的方式固定。大风时，可把桅杆与机翼脱开，将机翼放低贴近地面并加以固定。这类飞机也可以部分分解，或移入机库保护。

4. 语法解析

① There be 存在句 + 破折号后的省略定语

原句：There are many types of weight-shift-controlled aircraft— engine powered and non-powered. 拆解：There be 存在句（There are many types of weight-shift-controlled aircraft）；破折号后 engine powered and non-powered 是省略式后置定语（= which are engine powered and non-powered）。 要点：破折号引出补充说明，省略了 which are；powered 与 non-powered 构成对照，non- 前缀表否定。

② in a similar manner as 与 in + 天气名词

原句：The wings can be secured in a similar manner as a conventional aircraft in light winds. 拆解：主语 The wings + 情态被动 can be secured + 方式状语 in a similar manner as a conventional aircraft + 条件状语 in light winds。 要点：in a similar manner as 中 as 后直接接名词，严格写法是 in a similar manner to that of…，手册里常简化；in light winds 用 in 表“在…条件下”。

③ 并列被动谓语的省略

原句：In high winds, the mast can be disconnected from the wing and the wing placed close to the ground and secured. 拆解：句首 In high winds 为条件状语；主句由三个并列被动谓语构成：can be disconnected from the wing、and (can be) placed close to the ground、and secured，后两个省略了 can be。 要点：并列谓语省略助动词要靠 and 提示，阅读时要自己补出；close to the ground 是形容词短语作 placed 的补足语（表结果状态）。
