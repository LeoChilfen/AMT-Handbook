Four Forces of Flight

通用 Ch 05 · Four Forces of Flight · 原文 PDF 第 186 页

1. 原文

During flight, there are four forces acting on an airplane.

These forces are lift, weight, thrust, and drag. [Figure 5-53]

Lift is the upward force created by the wing, weight is the pull of gravity on the mass, thrust is the force created by the airplane’s propeller or turbine engine, and drag is the friction caused by the air flowing around the airplane.

All four of these forces are measured in pounds. Any time the forces are not in balance, something about the airplane’s condition is changing. The possibilities are as follows:

  1. When an airplane is accelerating, it has more thrust than drag.
  1. When an airplane is decelerating, it has less thrust than drag.
  1. When an airplane is at a constant velocity, thrust and drag are equal.

Lift Weight Drag Thrust 4. When an airplane is climbing, it has more lift than weight.

  1. When an airplane is descending, it has more weight than lift.
  1. When an airplane is at a constant altitude, lift and weight are equal.

2. 中文翻译

飞行过程中，有四个力作用在飞机上。

这四个力是升力、重力、推力和阻力（图 5-53）。

升力是由机翼产生的向上的力；重力是地球对质量施加的拉力；推力是飞机的螺旋桨或涡轮发动机产生的力；阻力则是空气绕飞机流动时产生的摩擦。

这四个力都以磅为计量单位。只要这些力不处于平衡状态，飞机的状态就在发生某种变化。可能的情况如下：

  1. 当飞机加速时，推力大于阻力。
  1. 当飞机减速时，推力小于阻力。
  1. 当飞机以恒定的速度飞行时，推力与阻力相等。

（此处为图内文字，略）4. 当飞机爬升时，升力大于重力。

  1. 当飞机下降时，重力大于升力。
  1. 当飞机在恒定高度飞行时，升力与重力相等。

4. 语法解析

① There be + 现在分词作后置定语

原句：During flight, there are four forces acting on an airplane.

拆解：During flight 是时间状语；there are 为存在句；four forces 是真正的主语（结构主语）；acting on an airplane 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 forces，相当于 which act on an airplane。

要点：there be 句型中，be 后面的名词才是语义主语，所以是 there are four forces（复数），不能写 there is。现在分词作定语表示主动、进行的动作（力在作用于飞机），过去分词则表示被动、完成；选用 acting 而非 acted 是因为力是施动方。同类：there are several factors affecting performance。

② 过去分词短语作后置定语：created by / caused by

原句：Lift is the upward force created by the wing, weight is the pull of gravity on the mass, thrust is the force created by the airplane’s propeller or turbine engine, and drag is the friction caused by the air flowing around the airplane.

拆解：四个分句用逗号并列，最后以 and 连接；每句都是「主语 + is + 名词 + 过去分词短语」，created by the wing、created by the airplane’s propeller or turbine engine、caused by the air … 都作后置定语；the air flowing around the airplane 中 flowing 是现在分词修饰 air。

要点：本节用同一定义句式把四个力逐一界定：「X is the force 过去分词短语」，是技术定义的标准写法。翻译时把后置定语前置为「由……产生的……」。注意 drag 的施动结构是 the friction caused by the air，即空气是致因，不是被摩擦的对象。

③ Any time 引导时间状语从句 + 现在进行时

原句：Any time the forces are not in balance, something about the airplane’s condition is changing.

拆解：Any time 在此起连词作用，引导时间状语从句（= whenever the forces are not in balance）；主句 something about the airplane’s condition is changing 用现在进行时，表示正在持续发生的变化。

要点：any time / every time / each time 都可以直接引导从句，不加 that（加了也不算错，但少见）。主句用进行时而不用一般现在时，是为了强调「失衡即意味着状态在变」这一持续过程。in balance 是固定搭配，说 out of balance 表示失衡，不能说 in unbalance。

④ more … than 比较结构 + when 从句的进行时

原句：When an airplane is accelerating, it has more thrust than drag.

拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在进行时 is accelerating 表示正在进行的动作；主句 it has more thrust than drag 是 more … than … 比较结构，比较对象是 thrust 与 drag（同类相比，都是力）。

要点：比较双方必须同类，more thrust than drag = 推力比阻力大，不能写成 more thrust than drag is。手册用六个并列条目把「加速／减速／匀速／爬升／下降／平飞」与力的比较一一对应，背诵时可成对记忆：thrust vs drag 管速度，lift vs weight 管高度。
