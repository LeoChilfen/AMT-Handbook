1. 原文

Mathematics is woven into many areas of everyday life. Performing mathematical calculations with success requires an understanding of the correct methods, procedures, practice, and review of these principles. Mathematics may be thought of as a set of tools. The aviation mechanic needs these tools to successfully complete the maintenance, repair, installation, or certification of aircraft equipment.

Many examples of using mathematical principles by the aviation mechanic are available. Tolerances in turbine engine components are critical, making it necessary to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch. Because of these close tolerances, it is important that the aviation mechanic can make accurate measurements and mathematical calculations. An aviation mechanic working on aircraft fuel systems also uses mathematical principles to calculate volumes and capacities of fuel tanks. The use of fractions and surface area calculations are required to perform sheet metal repair on aircraft structures.

2. 中文翻译

数学渗透在日常生活的许多领域。要成功地进行数学计算，需要理解正确的方法、步骤，并加以练习和对这些原理的复习。数学可以看作是一套工具。航空维修技师需要这些工具，才能顺利完成飞机设备的维修、修理、安装或认证工作。

航空维修技师运用数学原理的例子有很多。涡轮发动机部件的公差要求非常严格，必须测量到万分之一英寸以内。由于这些公差很紧，航空维修技师能够进行精确的测量和数学计算就十分重要。从事飞机燃油系统工作的航空维修技师也要运用数学原理来计算油箱的容积和容量。对飞机结构进行钣金修理时，需要用到分数和表面积计算。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词短语作主语

原句：Performing mathematical calculations with success requires an understanding of the correct methods, procedures, practice, and review of these principles. 拆解：主语是动名词短语 Performing mathematical calculations with success（with success 作方式状语）；谓语 requires 为第三人称单数，与动名词主语一致；宾语 an understanding 后接 of 引出的四个并列名词 methods、procedures、practice、review。 要点：动名词短语作主语时谓语一律用单数，这是最容易出错的地方（易误写成 require）。宾语后的 of 短语把四个抽象名词串成一条，翻译时按汉语习惯展开为「理解……并加以练习和复习」，不要逐个名词硬译成「……的理解」。

② 情态动词被动 + think of … as

原句：Mathematics may be thought of as a set of tools. 拆解：主语 Mathematics + 情态被动 may be thought of + as a set of tools（介词短语作主语补足语）；主动形式为 We may think of mathematics as a set of tools。 要点：think of A as B 变被动后，as B 仍留在动词后面作主语补足语，不能改成 is thought as 或 is thought to be a set of tools（后者虽可接受但改变了原意侧重）。科技英语常用这一句式给概念下比喻性定义。

③ 现在分词短语作结果状语

原句：Tolerances in turbine engine components are critical, making it necessary to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch. 拆解：主句 Tolerances … are critical（in turbine engine components 为后置定语）；逗号后 making it necessary to measure … 为现在分词短语作结果状语，其中 it 是形式宾语，真正宾语是 to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch。 要点：make it necessary to do 中的 it 不可省略，也不能换成 that；分词短语的逻辑主语是前面整件事（公差很紧），不是 tolerances 这个名词本身。同类结果状语还有 thus requiring、thereby causing。

④ it 作形式主语 + that 从句

原句：Because of these close tolerances, it is important that the aviation mechanic can make accurate measurements and mathematical calculations. 拆解：Because of these close tolerances 为原因状语（介词短语，不是从句）；it 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 引导的主语从句 the aviation mechanic can make accurate measurements and mathematical calculations。 要点：It is important that … 是手册说明必要性的高频框架，that 从句用陈述语序，不能用倒装；注意区分 Because of + 名词（本句）与 Because + 从句，前者不能接句子。

⑤ 被动语态 + 不定式作目的状语（主谓一致陷阱）

原句：The use of fractions and surface area calculations are required to perform sheet metal repair on aircraft structures. 拆解：主语中心词是 The use（of fractions and surface area calculations 为后置定语）；谓语 are required 为被动语态；to perform sheet metal repair on aircraft structures 为不定式作目的状语。 要点：原文这里按「分数和表面积计算」两项共用而用了 are，属于技术文本中常见的就近一致写法；考试或写作中应按语法严格用 is。翻译时以「需要用到分数和表面积计算」处理，既保留原意又符合汉语表达。
