1. 原文

Mathematics is woven into many areas of everyday life.

Performing mathematical calculations with success requires an understanding of the correct methods, procedures, practice, and review of these principles. Mathematics may be thought of as a set of tools. The aviation mechanic needs these tools to successfully complete the maintenance, repair, installation, or certification of aircraft equipment.

Many examples of using mathematical principles by the aviation mechanic are available. Tolerances in turbine engine components are critical, making it necessary to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch. Because of these close tolerances, it is important that the aviation mechanic can make accurate measurements and mathematical calculations.

An aviation mechanic working on aircraft fuel systems also uses mathematical principles to calculate volumes and capacities of fuel tanks. The use of fractions and surface area calculations are required to perform sheet metal repair on aircraft structures.

2. 中文翻译

数学融入了日常生活的许多方面。

要想成功地完成数学计算，需要理解正确的方法与步骤，并经过练习和对这些原理的复习。数学可以看作是一套工具。航空维修人员需要这些工具，才能顺利完成航空器设备的维修、修理、安装或认证。

航空维修人员运用数学原理的例子很多。涡轮发动机部件的公差极为关键，因此测量必须精确到万分之一英寸。由于公差如此之紧，航空维修人员能够做出精确的测量和数学计算就十分重要。

从事航空器燃油系统工作的航空维修人员，同样运用数学原理来计算燃油箱的容积和容量。对航空器结构进行钣金修理时，需要用到分数和表面积计算。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态：is woven into（be + 过去分词）

原句：Mathematics is woven into many areas of everyday life.

拆解：主语 Mathematics，谓语 is woven into（weave 的过去分词 woven），into many areas of everyday life 是介词短语作状语。

要点：这是比喻句，weave 原义「编织」，be woven into 即「被织进……之中」，译文取「融入」更自然。注意 weave 的三态是 weave – wove – woven，不要误写成 weaved；同类隐喻还有 be built into / be embedded in。

② 动名词短语作主语：Performing … requires an understanding of …

原句：Performing mathematical calculations with success requires an understanding of the correct methods, procedures, practice, and review of these principles.

拆解：主语是动名词短语 Performing mathematical calculations，with success 作方式状语；谓语 requires 用第三人称单数；宾语 an understanding of 后接四个并列成分 the correct methods, procedures, practice, and review of these principles。

要点：动名词短语作主语时谓语一律用单数，这是中文母语者最常错的点（易写成 require）。理解「主语是一个动作」就不难：会算 = 需要懂方法。同类：Keeping the shop clean is essential to safety.

③ 现在分词短语作结果状语：making it necessary to …

原句：Tolerances in turbine engine components are critical, making it necessary to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch.

拆解：主句 Tolerances … are critical；逗号后 making it necessary to measure … 是现在分词短语作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面整句；其中 it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 to measure within a ten-thousandth of an inch，necessary 是宾语补足语。

要点：make it necessary to do sth 是手册高频句型，it 不能换成 that，也不能省。现在分词短语放句末表「因而导致」，比 and therefore 更紧凑；同理还有 making it possible to / making it difficult to。
