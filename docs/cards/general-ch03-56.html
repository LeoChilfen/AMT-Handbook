Square

1. 原文

A square is a four-sided figure with all sides of equal length and opposite sides are parallel to each other. [Figure 3-15] All angles are right angles. A right angle is a 90° angle. The formula for the area of a square is:

area = length × width = l × w Since the length and the width of a square are the same value, the formula for the area of a square can also be written as:

area = side × side = s2 Example: What is the area of a square access plate whose side measures 25 inches? First, determine the known value and substitute it in the formula.

a = l × w = 25 inches × 25 inches = 625 square inches

2. 中文翻译

正方形是有四条边、四边等长且对边彼此平行的图形。[图 3-15] 它的所有角都是直角。直角即 90° 角。正方形的面积公式为：

面积 = 长 × 宽 = l × w 由于正方形的长与宽数值相同，正方形的面积公式也可以写作：

面积 = 边 × 边 = s2 例：一块边长 25 英寸的正方形检修口盖板，面积是多少？首先，确定已知数值并把它代入公式。

a = l × w = 25 英寸 × 25 英寸 = 625 平方英寸

4. 语法解析

① since 引导原因状语从句 + 情态被动

原句：Since the length and the width of a square are the same value, the formula for the area of a square can also be written as:

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（此处 since = because）；主句主语 the formula for the area of a square，谓语 can also be written（情态动词被动）；as 后接改写后的公式形式。

要点：技术文本中的 since 多表原因，不表「自从」，判断依据是从句与主句存在因果。can be written as 是「可写作……」的固定句式，as 后面跟的是同一对象的不同表达形式。

② with 结构中混用短语与从句（原文瑕疵）

原句：A square is a four-sided figure with all sides of equal length and opposite sides are parallel to each other.

拆解：主语 A square；系动词 is；表语 a four-sided figure；with 后接 all sides of equal length（名词 + 形容词短语）与 opposite sides are parallel to each other（完整主谓小句）两部分，二者结构不一致。

要点：规范写法应把后半改为 with ... opposite sides parallel to each other，去掉 are。阅读时不必纠结结构，按「四边等长、对边平行」理解即可；自己写定义句时，with 后面要保持同一结构。

③ whose 引导的定语从句

原句：What is the area of a square access plate whose side measures 25 inches?

拆解：疑问句，What 作表语；主语 the area of a square access plate；whose side measures 25 inches 为 whose 引导的定语从句修饰 plate，whose 相当于 of which。

要点：whose 表示先行词的所属关系，可指人也可指物，不可换成 who's。measure 在此为不及物动词「量得、尺寸为」，whose side measures 25 inches 即「边长 25 英寸」，中文习惯译成前置定语。
