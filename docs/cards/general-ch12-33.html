1. 原文

To understand why solid-state devices function as they do, it is necessary to examine the composition and nature of semiconductors. The two most common materials used for semiconductors are germanium and silicon. The essential characteristic of these elements is that each atom has four valence electrons to share with adjacent atoms in forming bonds. While both elements are used in semiconductor construction, silicon is preferred in most modern applications due to its ability to operate over a wider range of temperatures. The nature of a bond between two silicon atoms is such that each atom provides one electron to share with the other. The two electrons shared are in fact shared equally between the two atoms. This form of sharing is known as a covalent bond. Such bonds are very stable and hold the two atoms together very tightly requiring much energy to break this bond. In this case, all of the outer electrons are used to make covalent bonds with other silicon atoms. In this condition, because all of the outer shell atoms are used, silicon takes on the characteristic of a good insulator, due to the fact that there are no open positions available for electrons to migrate through the orbits.

For the silicon crystal to conduct electricity, there must be some means available to allow some electrons to move from place to place within the crystal, regardless of the covalent bonds present between the atoms. One way to accomplish this is to introduce an impurity, such as arsenic or phosphorus, into the crystal structure, which either provides an extra electron or create a vacant position in the outer shell for electrons AC coil Field coil Field coil Commutator Permanent magnet rotor Filter AC outputDC input A B G R A B to pass though. The method used to create this condition is called doping.

Doping is the process by which small amounts of additives called impurities are added to the semiconductor material to increase their current flow by adding a few electrons or a few holes. Once the material is doped, it then falls into one of two categories: the N-type semiconductor and the P-type semiconductor.

An N-type semiconductor material is one that is doped with an N-type or a donor impurity. Elements such as phosphorus, arsenic, and antimony are added as impurities and have five outer electrons to share with other atoms. This causes the semiconductor material to have an excess electron. Due to the surplus of electrons, the electrons are then considered the majority current carriers. This electron can easily be moved with only a small applied electrical voltage. Current flow in an N-type silicon material is similar to conduction in a copper wire. That is, with voltage applied across the material, electrons will move through the crystal towards the positive terminal just like current flows in a copper wire.

A P-type semiconductor is one that is doped with a P-type or an acceptor impurity. Elements such as boron, aluminum, and gallium have only three electrons in the valence shell to share with the silicon atom. Those three electrons form covalent bonds with adjacent silicon atoms. However, the expected fourth bond cannot be formed and a complete connection is impossible here, leaving a “hole” in the structure of the crystal. There is an empty place where an electron would naturally go, and often an electron moves into that space.

PN Junctions & the Basic Diode

A single type of semiconductor material by itself is not very useful. Useful applications are developed only when a single component contains both P-type and N-type materials. The semiconductor diode is also known as a PN junction diode. This is a two-element semiconductor device that makes use of the rectifying properties of a PN junction to convert alternating current into direct current by permitting current flow in one direction only.

illustrates the electrical characteristics of an unbiased diode, which means that no external voltage is applied. The P-side in the illustration is shown to have many holes, while the N-side shows many electrons. The electrons on the N-side tend to diffuse out in all directions. When an electron enters the P region, it becomes a minority carrier. By definition, a minority carrier is an electron or hole, whichever is the less dominant carrier in a semiconductor device. In P-type materials, electrons are the minority carrier and in N-type material, the hole is considered the minority carrier. With so many holes around the electron, the electron soon drops into a hole. When this occurs, the hole then disappears, and the conduction band electron becomes a valence electron.

Each time an electron crosses the PN junction, it creates a pair of ions. The circled plus signs and the circled negative signs are the positive and negative ions, respectively. These ions are fixed in the crystal and do not move around like electrons or holes in the conduction band. Thus, the depletion zone constitutes a layer of a fixed charge. An electrostatic field, represented by a small battery, is established across the junction between the oppositely charged ions.

The junction barrier is an electrostatic field, which has been created by the joining of a section of N-type and P-type material. Because holes and electrons must overcome this field to cross the junction, the electrostatic field is usually called a barrier. Because there is a lack or depletion of free electrons and holes in the area around the barrier, this area is called the depletion region. As the diffusion of electrons and holes across the junction continue, the strength of the electrostatic field increases until it is strong enough to prevent electrons or holes from crossing over. At this point, a state of equilibrium exists, and there is no further movement across the junction. The electrostatic field created at the junction by the ions in the depletion zone is called a barrier.

Forward Biased Diode

When an external voltage is applied to a PN junction, it is called bias. In a forward biased PN junction or diode, the negative voltage source is connected to the N-type material and the positive voltage source is connected to the P-type material. In this configuration, the current can easily flow. If a battery is used to bias the PN junction and it is connected in such a way that the applied voltage opposes the junction field, it has the effect of reducing the junction barrier and consequently aids in the current flow through the junction.

The electrons move toward the junction and the right end of the diode becomes slightly positive. This occurs because electrons at the right end of the diode move toward the junction and leave positively charged atoms behind. The positively charged atoms then pull electrons into the diode from the negative terminal of the battery.

When electrons on the N-type side approach the junction, they recombine with holes. Basically, electrons are flowing into the right end of the diode, while the bulk of the electrons in the N-type material move toward the junctions. The left edge of this moving front of electrons disappears by dropping into holes at the junction. In this way, there is a continuous current of electrons from the battery moving toward the junction.

Reverse Biased Diode

When the battery is turned around, then the diode is reverse biased and current does not flow. The most noticeable effect seen is the widened depletion zone.

The applied battery voltage is in the same direction as the depletion zone field. Because of this, holes and electrons tend to move away from the junction. Simply stated, the negative terminal attracts the holes away from the junction, and the positive terminal attracts the electrons away from the barrier. Therefore, the result is a wider depletion zone. This action increases the barrier width because there are more negative ions on the P-side of the junction and more positive ions on the N-side of the junction. This increase in the number of ions at the junction prevents current flow across the barrier by the majority carriers.

To summarize, the important thing to remember is that these PN junction diodes offer very little resistance to current when the diode is forward biased. 2. 中文翻译

要理解固态器件为何能这样工作，就必须考察半导体的组成与性质。用于半导体的两种最常见材料是锗和硅。这些元素的基本特性是：每个原子都有四个价电子，可与相邻原子的电子共享以形成键。虽然这两种元素都用于半导体的制造，但硅能在更宽的温度范围内工作，因而在大多数现代应用中更受青睐。两个硅原子之间键的性质是：每个原子提供一个电子与另一个原子共享。共享的这两个电子实际上在两个原子之间均等共享。这种共享形式称为共价键。这种键非常稳定，把两个原子结合得很紧，需要很大的能量才能将其断开。在这种情况下，所有外层电子都用于与其他硅原子形成共价键。在这种状态下，由于全部外层壳电子都被占用，硅便表现出良好绝缘体的特性，因为没有可供电子在各轨道间迁移的空位。

要使硅晶体导电，必须有某种途径让一些电子在晶体内从一处移动到另一处，而不管原子之间存在的共价键。实现这一点的一种方法是把杂质（例如砷或磷）引入晶体结构，这些杂质或者提供一个额外电子，或者在外层壳中造成一个空位，供电子通过。造成这种状态的方法称为掺杂。

掺杂就是把少量称为杂质的添加物加入半导体材料、通过增加少量电子或少量空穴来提高其电流流动能力的过程。材料一经掺杂，就归入以下两类之一：N 型半导体和 P 型半导体。

N 型半导体材料是掺杂了 N 型（施主）杂质的材料。加入磷、砷、锑等元素作为杂质，它们有五个外层电子可与其他原子共享。这使半导体材料多出一个电子。由于电子过剩，电子便被视为多数载流子。这个电子只需施加很小的电压就能轻易移动。N 型硅材料中的电流流动类似于铜导线中的导电。也就是说，在材料两端施加电压时，电子会穿过晶体向正极方向运动，就像电流在铜导线中流动一样。

P 型半导体是掺杂了 P 型（受主）杂质的半导体。硼、铝、镓等元素的价壳层中只有三个电子可与硅原子共享。这三个电子与相邻硅原子形成共价键。然而，预期的第四个键无法形成，这里不可能形成完整连接，于是在晶体结构中留下一个“空穴”。那里有一个电子本来会去占据的空位，而电子常常会移入该空间。

……然而，填补空穴的电子在填满这个空位时在身后留下了一个共价键，而它在移动时又留下另一个空穴。然后另一个电子可能移入那个特定的空穴，又留下另一个空穴。随着这一过程持续进行，空穴看起来就像正电荷一样在整个晶体中移动。这种半导体材料称为 P 型硅材料。要注意的是，最左侧所示的一个空穴把下一个价电子吸引进该空位，这又在右边相邻位置产生另一个称为空穴的空位。这个空位又吸引下一个价电子。这种空穴与电子的交换持续进行，可以从两种方式来理解。第一种方式是把这种流动看成电子的运动。电子通过一系列空穴从右向左移动。同样，第二种方式把空穴的运动看成是从左向右迁移。这种观点通常称为空穴运动。结构中的价电子沿着箭头所示的路径前进。而空穴的移动方向则与电子相反。

PN 结与基本二极管

单一类型的半导体材料本身用处不大。只有当单个元件同时含有 P 型和 N 型材料时，才能开发出有用的应用。半导体二极管也称为 PN 结二极管。这是一种两元素半导体器件，它利用 PN 结的整流特性，只允许电流沿一个方向流动，从而把交流电转换为直流电。

……说明了未加偏置二极管的电学特性，即不施加任何外部电压。图中 P 侧显示有许多空穴，而 N 侧显示有许多电子。N 侧的电子倾向于向各个方向扩散。当电子进入 P 区时，它就成为少数载流子。按定义，少数载流子是在半导体器件中占次要地位的电子或空穴。在 P 型材料中，电子是少数载流子；在 N 型材料中，空穴被视为少数载流子。由于电子周围有如此多的空穴，电子很快落入一个空穴。发生这种情况时，空穴随之消失，导带电子则变成价电子。

每当一个电子穿过 PN 结，就会产生一对离子。带圈的加号和带圈的减号分别表示正离子和负离子。这些离子固定在晶体中，不会像导带中的电子或空穴那样四处移动。因此，耗尽区构成一层固定电荷。在带相反电荷的离子之间，跨越结建立起一个静电场，图中用一个小电池表示。

结垒是一种静电场，由 N 型材料与 P 型材料接合而形成。由于空穴和电子必须克服这个场才能穿过结，这个静电场通常称为势垒。由于势垒周围区域缺少或耗尽了自由电子和空穴，该区域称为耗尽区。随着电子和空穴穿越结的扩散持续进行，静电场的强度不断增强，直到强到足以阻止电子或空穴穿过。此时便处于平衡状态，不再有跨越结的运动。由耗尽区离子在结处建立的静电场称为势垒。

正向偏置二极管

当外部电压加在 PN 结上时，称为偏置。在正向偏置的 PN 结或二极管中，负电压源接 N 型材料，正电压源接 P 型材料。在这种接法下，电流可以很容易地流动。如果用电池给 PN 结加偏置，并且接线方式使外加电压与结电场方向相反，其效果就是降低结垒，从而有助于电流通过结。

电子向结移动，二极管右端变得略带正电。这是因为二极管右端的电子向结移动，把带正电的原子留在原处。带正电的原子于是从电池负极把电子拉入二极管。

当 N 型侧的电子接近结时，它们与空穴复合。从根本上说，电子流入二极管的右端，而 N 型材料中的大部分电子向结移动。这个运动的电子前锋的左边缘在结处落入空穴而消失。这样，从电池出发的电子便形成连续电流流向结。

当电子撞击结时，它们就变成价电子。一旦成为价电子，它们就能通过 P 型材料中的空穴移动。当价电子在 P 型材料中从右向左移动时，空穴也发生类似的运动，即从 P 型材料的左侧移向右侧。一旦价电子到达二极管的末端，它便流回电池的正极。

  1. 电子离开电池负极，进入二极管的右端（N 型材料）。
  1. 电子随后穿过 N 型材料。
  1. 电子接近结，发生复合，成为价电子。
  1. 电子现在作为价电子穿过 P 型材料。
  1. 电子随后离开二极管，流回电池正极。

反向偏置二极管

当电池反接时，二极管就处于反向偏置，电流不流动。最明显可见的效果是耗尽区变宽。

所加电池电压的方向与耗尽区电场方向相同。因此，空穴和电子倾向于离开结。简单地说，负端把空穴拉离结，正端把电子拉离势垒。结果便是耗尽区变宽。这一作用使势垒宽度增大，因为结的 P 侧负离子更多、N 侧正离子更多。结处离子数量的这种增加阻止多数载流子越过势垒流动。

总之，要记住的重要一点是：这些 PN 结二极管在正向偏置时对电流的阻力很小；而最大电阻出现在……

……当二极管反向偏置时。

4. 语法解析

① 形式主语 it + 不定式作目的状语

原句：To understand why solid-state devices function as they do, it is necessary to examine the composition and nature of semiconductors. 拆解：To understand why solid-state devices function as they do 为不定式短语作目的状语，其中 why 引导宾语从句作 understand 的宾语，as they do 为方式状语从句；主句 it 为形式主语，真正的主语是 to examine the composition and nature of semiconductors，is necessary 为系表结构。 要点：it 作形式主语时真正主语后置，避免头重脚轻，是技术说明文的常见句式，可译为「有必要……」；as they do 中 do 替代前文动词，表示「按它们实际工作的方式」；目的状语的不定式置于句首时用逗号与主句隔开，其逻辑主语就是后面的动作执行者。

② while 让步 + 过去分词被动 + due to 短语

原句：While both elements are used in semiconductor construction, silicon is preferred in most modern applications due to its ability to operate over a wider range of temperatures. 拆解：While 引导让步状语从句（主语 both elements，谓语 are used 被动，状语 in semiconductor construction）；主句主语 silicon，谓语 is preferred（被动），状语 in most modern applications，原因状语 due to its ability to operate over a wider range of temperatures，其中 to operate 为 ability 的同位语性定语。 要点：while 在此表让步「虽然」，与主句的「更受青睐」形成对比；be used in 表「用于某领域」，而 be used for 表「用于某目的」；due to 为介词短语，后接名词 ability，不能接句子；ability 常接不定式作定语，译为「……的能力」。

③ such that 表程度或结果

原句：The nature of a bond between two silicon atoms is such that each atom provides one electron to share with the other. 拆解：主语 The nature（of a bond between two silicon atoms 为后置定语），系动词 is，表语 such，that 引导结果/程度状语从句（主语 each atom，谓语 provides，宾语 one electron，不定式 to share with the other 作目的/后置修饰）。 要点：such that 连接主句与从句，表示「如此……以至于」，such 是表语而非修饰名词的形容词，故不能写成 such a nature that；从句谓语用一般现在时表客观事实；to share with the other 中 the other 指另一个原子，不可省。

④ 现在分词短语作结果状语

原句：However, the expected fourth bond cannot be formed and a complete connection is impossible here, leaving a “hole” in the structure of the crystal. 拆解：However 为转折副词；主句由 and 连接两个被动/系表分句（the expected fourth bond cannot be formed；a complete connection is impossible here）；逗号后 leaving a “hole” in the structure of the crystal 为现在分词短语作结果状语。 要点：leaving 的逻辑主语是前面整件事（第四个键无法形成），而非某个具体名词，译为「从而留下……」；现在分词作结果状语与主句之间必须有逗号，若用 to leave 则变成目的，语义相反；hole 加引号表示借喻，指空位而非物理孔洞。

⑤ 定语从句 + 不定式目的 + by 动名词

原句：This is a two-element semiconductor device that makes use of the rectifying properties of a PN junction to convert alternating current into direct current by permitting current flow in one direction only. 拆解：主句 This is a two-element semiconductor device；that 引导定语从句修饰 device，从句谓语 makes use of，宾语 the rectifying properties of a PN junction；不定式 to convert alternating current into direct current 作目的状语；by permitting current flow in one direction only 为方式状语，介词 by 后接动名词。 要点：make use of 意为「利用」，比 use 更正式；convert A into B 是「把 A 转换为 B」的固定搭配，介词用 into 不用 to；by + 动名词表示手段，翻译时译作「通过……」，不能写成 by permit；定语从句 that 作主语不可省略。
