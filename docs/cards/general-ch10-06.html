1. 原文

In an effort to standardize the format in which maintenance information is presented in aircraft maintenance manuals, Air Transport Association (now Airlines for America)issued specifications for Manufacturers’ Technical Data. The original specification was called ATA Spec 100. Over the years, Spec 100 has been continuously revised and updated. Eventually, ATA Spec 2100 was developed for electronic documentation. These two specifications evolved into one document called ATA iSpec 2200, developed and managed by the ATA e-Business Program, a consensus-based industry standards organization administered by Airlines for America (A4A). As a result of this standardization, maintenance technicians can always find information regarding a particular system in the same section of an aircraft maintenance manual, regardless of manufacturer. For example, if seeking information about the electrical system on any aircraft, that information is always found in section (chapter) 24. The ATA iSpec 2200 divides the aircraft into systems, such as air conditioning, that covers the basic air conditioning system (ATA 21). Numbering in each major system provides an arrangement for breaking the system down into several subsystems. Late model aircraft, both over and under the 12,500-pound designation, have their parts manuals and maintenance manuals arranged according to the ATA- coded system. The following abbreviated table of ATA System, Subsystem, and Titles is included for familiarization purposes.

Keep in mind that not all aircraft have all these systems installed. Small and simple aircraft have fewer systems than larger, more complex aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

为使飞机维修手册中呈现维修信息的格式标准化，美国航空运输协会（Air Transport Association，现为 Airlines for America）发布了《制造商技术数据》规范。最初的规范称为 ATA Spec 100。多年来，Spec 100 不断修订和更新。最终，又为电子文档制定了 ATA Spec 2100。这两项规范合并成一份名为 ATA iSpec 2200 的文件，由 ATA e-Business Program 制定和管理，该机构是一个以共识为基础的行业标准组织，由 Airlines for America（A4A）管理。由于这种标准化，维修技术人员总能在飞机维修手册的同一章节中找到有关某一特定系统的信息，而不论制造商是谁。例如，若要查找任何飞机上电气系统的信息，该信息总是在第 24 节（章）中找到。ATA iSpec 2200 把飞机划分为若干系统，例如空调，涵盖基本空调系统（ATA 21）。每个主要系统内的编号提供了将该系统分解为若干子系统的编排方式。较新机型的飞机，无论高于还是低于 12,500 磅这一界限，其零件手册和维修手册都按 ATA 编码体系编排。以下 ATA 系统、子系统与标题的简表，供熟悉之用。

要记住，并非所有飞机都装设了上述全部系统。小型简单飞机装设的系统比更大、更复杂的飞机少。

4. 语法解析

① 结果状语 + 后置的 regardless of 短语

原句：As a result of this standardization, maintenance technicians can always find information regarding a particular system in the same section of an aircraft maintenance manual, regardless of manufacturer. 拆解：as a result of this standardization 为结果/原因状语；主句主语 maintenance technicians，谓语为 can always find，宾语 information 由 regarding a particular system 后置修饰；in the same section of an aircraft maintenance manual 为地点状语；regardless of manufacturer 是形容词性短语作让步状语，后置于句末。 要点：as a result of 表示「由于……的结果」，比 because of 更强调因果链条；regardless of + 名词 = 不论……，后置时用逗号与主句隔开，意思是「与制造商无关」；information regarding = 有关……的信息。

② 定语从句的先行词判断（就近修饰）

原句：The ATA iSpec 2200 divides the aircraft into systems, such as air conditioning, that covers the basic air conditioning system (ATA 21). 拆解：主句 divides the aircraft into systems 中 divide…into 为「把……划分为」；such as air conditioning 为插入的举例；that covers… 为定语从句，其先行词是紧邻的 air conditioning（单数），故谓语用 covers。 要点：that 从句的先行词按就近原则确定，若误认为修饰 systems，就会以为 covers 是主谓不一致的错误；ATA 21 是系统编号，读作「ATA 二十一章」，本章后文各系统编号依此类推。

③ 过去分词短语 + 同位语补充说明

原句：These two specifications evolved into one document called ATA iSpec 2200, developed and managed by the ATA e-Business Program, a consensus-based industry standards organization administered by Airlines for America (A4A). 拆解：主语 These two specifications，谓语 evolved into（演变为），宾语 one document 由 called ATA iSpec 2200 后置修饰；developed and managed by… 为过去分词短语，进一步修饰该文件；a consensus-based industry standards organization 是 the ATA e-Business Program 的同位语，administered by… 为过去分词修饰 organization。 要点：evolve into 表示「逐步演变为」，强调过程，不同于 change into；同位语用逗号隔开，作用是把长名称拆成两层解释；同一个名词后可叠加多个后置修饰语，翻译时按由近及远逐层还原。

④ if + 现在分词的条件状语 + 被动语态

原句：For example, if seeking information about the electrical system on any aircraft, that information is always found in section (chapter) 24. 拆解：if seeking… 为 if 引导的省略式条件状语（= if you are seeking…），seek 与主语是主动关系故用现在分词；主句 that information is always found 为被动结构，in section (chapter) 24 为地点状语，section 后的括号说明它相当于「章」。 要点：if + 现在分词要求分词逻辑主语与主句主语一致；find information in section 24 用主动，转成被动即 information is found in section 24，被动句保留了「信息在何处找到」的焦点。

⑤ 插入的并列介词短语 + have sth done

原句：Late model aircraft, both over and under the 12,500-pound designation, have their parts manuals and maintenance manuals arranged according to the ATA- coded system. 拆解：主语 Late model aircraft 后插入 both over and under the 12,500-pound designation 作补充说明；谓语是 have sth done 结构——have their parts manuals and maintenance manuals arranged（使其手册按……编排），arranged 为过去分词作宾语补足语；according to the ATA-coded system 为方式状语。 要点：both over and under 是「既高于又低于」的并列，意为「无论是否超过」，不是「两者都超过」；12,500-pound 是「数词 + 名词 + 连字符」构成的复合形容词，名词用单数；have sth done 表示「让某事处于某种状态」，与 have sb do（让某人做）不同；原文 ATA- coded 在连字符后多一个空格，属版面抽取留下的排版现象，语义即 ATA-coded。

⑥ 祈使句 + that 宾语从句 + 部分否定

原句：Keep in mind that not all aircraft have all these systems installed. 拆解：keep in mind 为祈使句，后接 that 引导的宾语从句；从句中 not all 构成部分否定（并非全部），谓语 have 后接宾语 all these systems 和过去分词 installed 作宾语补足语（have sth installed）。 要点：not all = 「并非所有」，与 none（全都不）意思不同，这是 reading 中最易误读的否定范围问题；keep in mind that… 是「记住……」的固定说法；have all these systems installed 中的 installed 表示系统已被装设的状态。
