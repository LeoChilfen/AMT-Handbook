Taxi Signals

1. 原文

Many ground accidents have occurred as a result of improper technique in taxiing aircraft. Although the pilot is ultimately responsible for the aircraft until the engine is stopped, a taxi signalman can assist the pilot around the flight line. In some aircraft configurations, the pilot’s vision is obstructed while on the ground. The pilot cannot see obstructions close to the wheels or under the wings and has little idea of what is behind the aircraft. Consequently, the pilot depends upon the taxi signalman for directions. Figure 1-23 shows a taxi signalman indicating his readiness to assume guidance of the aircraft by extending both arms at full length above his head, palms facing each other.

The standard position for a signalman is slightly ahead of and in line with the aircraft’s left wingtip. As the signalman faces the aircraft, the nose of the aircraft is on the left.

[Figure 1-24] The signalman must stay far enough ahead of the wingtip to remain in the pilot’s field of vision. It is a good practice to perform a foolproof test to be sure the pilot can see all signals. If the signalman can see the pilot’s eyes, the pilot can see the signals.

Figure 1-24 shows the standard aircraft taxiing signals published in the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA)

Aeronautical Information Manual (AIM). There are other standard signals, such as those published in Advisory Circular 00-34, as revised, and by the International Standards (ICAO)

Annex 2, Appendix 1 and the Armed Forces. Furthermore, operation conditions in many areas may call for a modified set of taxi signals. The signals shown in Figure 1-24 represent a minimum number of the most commonly used signals.

Whether this set of signals or a modified set is used is not the most important consideration, as long as each flight operational center uses a suitable, agreed-upon set of signals.

Figure 1-25 illustrates some of the most commonly used helicopter operating signals.

The taxi signals to be used must be studied until the taxi signalman can execute them clearly and precisely. The signals are to be given in such a way that the pilot cannot confuse their meaning. Remember that the pilot receiving the signals is always some distance away and often look out and down from a difficult angle. Thus, the signalman’s hands must be kept well separated, and signals are to be over-exaggerated rather than risk making indistinct signals. If there is any doubt about a signal, or if the pilot does not appear to be following the signals, use the “stop” sign and begin the series of signals again.

The signalman is to always try to give the pilot an indication of the approximate area that the aircraft is to be parked.

The signalman must glance behind himself or herself often when walking backward to prevent backing into a propeller or tripping over a chock, fire bottle, tie-down line, or other obstruction.

Taxi signals are usually given at night with the aid of illuminated wands attached to flashlights. [Figure 1-26] Night signals are made in the same manner as day signals with the exception of the stop signal. The stop signal used at night is the “emergence stop” signal. This signal is made by crossing the wands to form a lighted “X” above and in front of the head.

2. 中文翻译

许多地面事故都是由于滑行航空器时操作手法不当造成的。虽然直到发动机停车为止，航空器最终都由飞行员负责，但滑行信号员仍能在停机坪周围协助飞行员。在某些航空器构型下，飞行员在地面时视线受阻：既看不到靠近机轮或机翼下方的障碍物，也几乎不知道航空器后方的情况。因此，飞行员要靠滑行信号员指示方向。图 1-23 示出一名滑行信号员把两臂伸直举过头顶、掌心相对，表示已准备好接替引导航空器。

信号员的标准站位是在航空器左翼尖稍前方、与翼尖成一条直线处。信号员面向航空器时，机头位于其左侧。

信号员必须走在翼尖前方足够远处，以便始终处于飞行员的视野之内。有一项简单可靠的做法可以验证飞行员能否看清全部信号：如果信号员能看到飞行员的眼睛，飞行员就能看到信号。

采用这一套信号还是经修改的一套信号，并不是最重要的考虑，只要每个飞行运行中心使用一套合适且事先商定的信号即可。

所要使用的滑行信号必须反复演练，直到滑行信号员能够清楚、准确地做出这些信号。信号应以飞行员不会误解其含义的方式给出。要记住，接收信号的飞行员总在一定距离之外，而且常常要从困难的角度向外、向下观察。因此，信号员的双手必须保持充分分开，信号宁可做得夸张些，也不要冒险做出含混不清的信号。如果对某个信号有疑问，或者飞行员似乎没有在按信号行动，就使用「停止」信号，然后从头再做一遍这一系列信号。

信号员应始终设法向飞行员指示航空器大致要停放的区域。

信号员倒退行走时必须经常回头看一眼身后，以免撞上螺旋桨，或被轮挡、灭火瓶、系留索或其他障碍物绊倒。

滑行信号通常在夜间借助装在电筒上的发光指挥棒给出。除「停止」信号外，夜间信号的做法与白天信号相同。夜间使用的停止信号是「紧急停止」信号，做法是在头顶上方偏前处把两根指挥棒交叉成一个发光的「X」。

4. 语法解析

① whether 主语从句嵌套在长句中

原句：Whether this set of signals or a modified set is used is not the most important consideration, as long as each flight operational center uses a suitable, agreed-upon set of signals.

拆解：Whether…is used 是主语从句（whether…or… 并列），其中第一个 is 属于从句，第二个 is 才是主句谓语；as long as 引导条件状语从句。

要点：主语从句较长时，容易把从句谓语误当成主句谓语，看到两个 is 要先分清层级。as long as 表「只要」，后接一般现在时表将来，不接 will。

② be to be done 表规定 + such…that 结果状语

原句：The signals are to be given in such a way that the pilot cannot confuse their meaning.

拆解：are to be given 是「be to do」的被动式，表规定；in such a way that… 为 such…that… 结构，that 从句说明方式所导致的结果。

要点：in such a way that 译作「以这样的方式……，以至于……」。are to be 表「应当被」，不要误读成将来时；本句可改写为 must be given，语气相近而更直白。

③ as a result of + 动名词短语

原句：Many ground accidents have occurred as a result of improper technique in taxiing aircraft.

拆解：现在完成时 have occurred 表示「迄今为止已经发生」；as a result of 是复合介词，后接名词短语 improper technique；in taxiing aircraft 中 taxiing 是动名词，aircraft 是它的宾语。

要点：as a result of（由于）后接名词或动名词，不能接从句；要接句子就用 because。taxiing aircraft 是「滑行航空器（这件事）」，不是「正在滑行的航空器」。

④ when + 现在分词（省略主语）+ prevent doing

原句：The signalman must glance behind himself or herself often when walking backward to prevent backing into a propeller or tripping over a chock, fire bottle, tie-down line, or other obstruction.

拆解：when walking backward 是时间状语从句的省略形式（when he or she is walking backward）；to prevent… 是目的状语；prevent 后接两个并列动名词 backing into… 与 tripping over…。

要点：prevent 后直接接动名词（prevent doing），要点出对象则用 prevent sb from doing。when + 现在分词省略主语是常见写法，译作「在……时」；本句四个并列障碍物逐项译出，不要漏项。
