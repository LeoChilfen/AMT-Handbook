Aviation-related regulations that have occurred from 1926– 1966 are reflected in Figure 2-1. Just as aircraft continue to evolve with ever improving technology, so do the regulations, publications, forms, and records required to design, build, and maintain them.

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) regulations that govern today’s aircraft are found in Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR). [Figure 2-2] There are five volumes under Title 14, Aeronautics and Space. The first three volumes containing 75 active regulations address the Federal Aviation Administration. The fourth volume deals with the Office of the Secretary of the Department of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings) and Commercial Space Transportation, while the fifth volume addresses the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) and Air Transportation System Stabilization.

4. 语法解析

① are reflected in Figure 2-1（被动语态 + 定语从句）

原句：Aviation-related regulations that have occurred from 1926– 1966 are reflected in Figure 2-1.

拆解：主语 Aviation-related regulations 后接 that 引导的定语从句（that have occurred from 1926–1966 修饰 regulations）；谓语 are reflected 为被动语态；in Figure 2-1 是地点状语。

要点：手册说明图表内容时固定用 is/are reflected in Figure X、are shown in Figure X，动作承受者（regulations）作主语、制图者省略。从句用现在完成时 have occurred，表示「在这一期间发生过的」直到现在仍有意义的动作，不能用一般过去时 occurred 直接替换。

② Just as …, so do …（比较结构 + 部分倒装）

原句：Just as aircraft continue to evolve with ever improving technology, so do the regulations, publications, forms, and records required to design, build, and maintain them.

拆解：Just as 引导方式状语从句；主句 so do + 主语为部分倒装，do 代替前面出现过的 evolve，避免重复；required to design, build, and maintain them 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰四个并列名词。

要点：just as A…, so B… 译「正如 A……，B 也……」，so 后的助动词必须与所代替的谓语时态一致（evolve 为一般现在时，故用 do；若前面是 has evolved 则用 so has B）。ever improving 是「不断改进的」，ever 起加强作用，不译「曾经」。

③ while 连接的对比并列句

原句：The fourth volume deals with the Office of the Secretary of the Department of Transportation (Aviation Proceedings) and Commercial Space Transportation, while the fifth volume addresses the National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) and Air Transportation System Stabilization.

拆解：while 连接两个结构对称的分句，表前后对比；deals with 与 addresses 是同义替换，均表「涉及、处理」。

要点：技术文本中 while 常表「而、然而」的对比，而非「当……时」，判断依据是前后两句是否在比较两件事。写作时用同义动词轮换（deal with / address / cover）可避免重复，但翻译成中文时不必跟着换词，都译「涉及」即可。
