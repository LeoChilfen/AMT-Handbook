1. 原文

Two gears with teeth on their outer edges, as shown in Figure 5-13, act like a first class lever when one gear drives the other. The gear with the input force is called the drive gear, and the other is called the driven gear. The effort arm is the diameter of the driven gear, and the resistance arm is the diameter of the drive gear.

Notice that the two gears turn in opposite directions: the bottom one clockwise and the top one counterclockwise. The gear on top is 9 inches in diameter and has 45 teeth, and the gear on the bottom is 12 inches in diameter and has 60 teeth.

Imagine that the blue gear is driving the yellow one, which makes the blue the drive and the yellow the driven. The mechanical advantage in terms of force would be the effort arm divided by the resistance arm, or 9 ÷ 12, which is 0.75.

This would actually be called a fractional disadvantage, because there would be less force out than force in. The mechanical advantage in terms of distance, in rpm in this case, would be 12 ÷ 9, or 1.33.

This analysis tells us that when a large gear drives a small one, the small one turns faster and has less available force. In order to be a force gaining machine, the small gear needs to turn the large one. When the terminology reduction gearbox is used, such as a propeller reduction gearbox, it means that there is more rpm going in than is coming out. The end result

In this example, the sun gear has 28 teeth, each planet gear has 22 teeth, and the ring gear has 82 teeth. To figure how much gear reduction is taking place, the number of teeth on the ring gear is divided by the number of teeth on the sun gear.

In this case, the gear reduction is 2.93, meaning the engine has an rpm 2.93 times greater than the propeller. [Figure 5-16]

2. 中文翻译

两个外缘带齿的齿轮（如图 5-13 所示），在一个齿轮驱动另一个齿轮时，其作用相当于一根第一类杠杆。承受输入力的齿轮称为主动齿轮，另一个称为从动齿轮。施力臂是从动齿轮的直径，阻力臂是主动齿轮的直径。

注意这两个齿轮的转向相反：下方的一个顺时针转，上方的一个逆时针转。上方的齿轮直径为 9 in、有 45 个齿；下方的齿轮直径为 12 in、有 60 个齿。

设想由蓝色齿轮驱动黄色齿轮，那么蓝色的是主动轮、黄色的是从动轮。就力而言，机械利益为施力臂除以阻力臂，即 9 ÷ 12 = 0.75。

这种情况实际上应称为分数式不利，因为输出力小于输入力。就距离（本例中即转速 rpm）而言，机械利益为 12 ÷ 9 = 1.33。

这一分析说明：当大齿轮驱动小齿轮时，小齿轮转得更快，可用的力也更小。要成为增力机械，必须由小齿轮驱动大齿轮。使用「减速齿轮箱」这一说法时（例如螺旋桨减速齿轮箱），意思是输入的转速大于输出的转速。最终结果……（原文在此处被版面打断）

（此处为图内文字，略）……它带动行星齿轮旋转，并最终带动齿圈转动。

在本例中，太阳齿轮有 28 个齿，每个行星齿轮有 22 个齿，齿圈有 82 个齿。要算出减速比是多少，用齿圈的齿数除以太阳齿轮的齿数。

本例中减速比为 2.93，意思是发动机的转速是螺旋桨转速的 2.93 倍。

4. 语法解析

① 插入语 as shown in Figure 5-13

原句：Two gears with teeth on their outer edges, as shown in Figure 5-13, act like a first class lever when one gear drives the other.

拆解：主语 Two gears with teeth on their outer edges（with 短语作后置定语）；as shown in Figure 5-13 为插入语（as + 过去分词，= as it is shown），前后加逗号；谓语 act like a first class lever；when one gear drives the other 为时间状语从句。

要点：as shown in Figure X 是图文引述的固定说法，as 后省略了 it is。act like 意为「起作用像」，与 act as（充当某物）语气不同。主语与谓语被插入语隔开时，先跳过插入语找谓语 act，再回读。

② 非限制性 which 指代整句 + make 复合宾语

原句：Imagine that the blue gear is driving the yellow one, which makes the blue the drive and the yellow the driven.

拆解：祈使句 Imagine 后接 that 宾语从句；which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整个事实（蓝色齿轮驱动黄色齿轮）；makes the blue the drive and the yellow the driven 是 make + 宾语 + 名词补语，两组并列。

要点：非限制性 which 可指代整个主句，译「这就使得……」。the drive / the driven 是「the + 名词化成分」，意为「主动的一方、从动的一方」，句末省略了 gear。one 用来代替前面出现过的 gear，避免重复。

③ more … than 中 than 兼作从句主语

原句：When the terminology reduction gearbox is used, such as a propeller reduction gearbox, it means that there is more rpm going in than is coming out.

拆解：When… 引导时间状语从句，其中 the terminology reduction gearbox is used 为被动；such as… 为举例插入；主句 it means that… 中 that 从句是 there be 结构；more rpm going in 中 going in 为现在分词后置定语；than is coming out 为比较从句，than 在从句中作主语（= than the rpm which is coming out）。

要点：than 后常省略主语，形成 than is / than are 结构，译「输入的转速多于输出的转速」。rpm 单复数同形，此处按不可数处理，故用 is。such as 举例时前面必须有被举例的名词（reduction gearbox），不能单独用于句首。
