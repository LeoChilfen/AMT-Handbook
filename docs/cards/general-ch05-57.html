Differential Pressure

通用 Ch 05 · Differential Pressure · 原文 PDF 第 173 页

1. 原文

Differential pressure, or psid, is the difference between pressures being read at two different locations within a system. For example, in a turbine engine oil system the pressure is read as it enters the oil filter, and as it leaves the filter. These two readings are sent to a transmitter which powers a light located on the flight deck. Across anything that poses a resistance to flow, like an oil filter, there will be a drop in pressure. If the filter starts to clog, the pressure drop will become greater, eventually causing the advisory light on the flight deck to come on.

Figure 5-36 shows a differential pressure gauge for the pressurization system on a Boeing 737. In this case, the difference in pressure is between the inside and the outside of the airplane. If the pressure difference becomes too great, the structure of the airplane could become overstressed.

2. 中文翻译

压差（psid）是系统内两个不同位置所测得压力之间的差值。例如，在涡轮发动机的滑油系统中，要在滑油进入滑油滤时和离开滑油滤时分别测取压力。这两个读数被送往一个变送器，由变送器给驾驶舱内的一盏灯供电。任何对流动构成阻力的部件（如滑油滤），其两侧都会有压力下降。若滤子开始堵塞，压降就会增大，最终使驾驶舱内的提示灯亮起。

图 5-36 所示为波音 737 增压系统的压差表。在此情况下，压差是航空器机内与机外之间的压力差。若压差变得过大，航空器的结构就可能受力过度。

4. 语法解析

① 现在分词被动式作后置定语（pressures being read）

原句：Differential pressure, or psid, is the difference between pressures being read at two different locations within a system.

拆解：主语 Differential pressure，or psid 为同位语；系动词 is；表语 the difference；between pressures being read at two different locations within a system 中，being read 是现在分词被动式作 pressures 的后置定语（= which are being read），at two different locations 与 within a system 层层限定位置。

要点：being read 强调「正在被测取」，比过去分词 read（已测完）更贴合仪表实时读数的语境。between 是介词，后面只能接名词或名词短语，不能接从句；两处位置之间的差用 between，三者以上用 among。

② 定语从句 + 过去分词后置定语（located on …）

原句：These two readings are sent to a transmitter which powers a light located on the flight deck.

拆解：主句 These two readings are sent to a transmitter（被动）；which powers a light 是定语从句修饰 transmitter；located on the flight deck 是过去分词短语作 a light 的后置定语（= which is located on …）。

要点：located、mounted、installed 这类过去分词后置定语在机务文本中极为常见（a gauge located on the panel、a filter mounted on the engine），相当于省略了 which is，翻译时前置作「位于……的」即可。sent to 后接接收者，与 sent by（由……发出）方向相反。

③ 分词短语作结果状语 + cause … to do

原句：If the filter starts to clog, the pressure drop will become greater, eventually causing the advisory light on the flight deck to come on.

拆解：If 引导真实条件从句，start to clog 为「开始堵塞」；主句 the pressure drop will become greater；eventually causing … 是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中 cause + 宾语 the advisory light + 不定式 to come on（宾补）；on the flight deck 修饰 light。

要点：逗号后的现在分词短语表「由此导致的结果」，其逻辑主语是前面整句所述情形，不是某个具体名词。cause sb./sth. to do 中的 to 不能省（与 make / have / let 后省 to 不同）。advisory light 是「提示灯、告诫灯」，不是「咨询灯」；come on 指灯「亮起」，不是「过来」。
