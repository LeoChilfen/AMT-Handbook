Securing Heavy Aircraft

通用 Ch 01 · Securing Heavy Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

1. 原文

The normal tie-down procedure for heavy aircraft can be accomplished with rope or cable tie-down. The number of tie-downs are governed by anticipated weather conditions.

Most heavy aircraft are equipped with surface control locks that are engaged or installed when the aircraft is secured. Since the method of locking controls vary on different types of aircraft, check the manufacturer’s instructions for proper installation or engaging procedures. If high winds are anticipated, control surface battens can also be installed to prevent damage. Figure 1-11 illustrates four common tie- down points on heavy aircraft.

The normal tie-down procedure for heavy aircraft includes the following:

  1. Head aircraft into prevailing wind whenever possible.
  1. Install control locks, all covers, and guards.
  1. Chock all wheels fore and aft. [Figure 1-12]
  1. Attach tie-down reels to aircraft tie-down loops, tie- down anchors, or tie-down stakes. Use tie-down stakes for temporary tie-down only. If tie-down reels are not available, 1⁄4" wire cable or 1 1⁄2" manila line may be used.

2. 中文翻译

重型飞机的常规系留程序可用绳索或钢索完成。系留点的数量由预计的天气状况决定。

大多数重型飞机装有操纵面锁，在飞机系留时予以啮合或装上。由于不同机型的操纵面锁定方法各不相同，应查阅制造商说明以确认正确的安装或啮合程序。如预计有大风，还可加装操纵面压板以防损伤。图 1-11 示出了重型飞机上四个常见的系留点。

重型飞机的常规系留程序包括下列内容：

  1. 尽可能使机头迎向主导风。
  1. 装好操纵面锁、全部堵盖和护罩。
  1. 在全部机轮的前后打轮挡。[图 1-12]
  1. 把系留绞盘接到飞机系留环、系留地锚或系留桩上。系留桩只用于临时系留。如无系留绞盘，可用 1⁄4" 钢丝绳或 1 1⁄2" 马尼拉绳。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + by 引出施动者（并注意主谓一致）

原句：The number of tie-downs are governed by anticipated weather conditions. 拆解：主语 The number of tie-downs（of 短语作后置定语）+ 被动谓语 are governed + 状语 by anticipated weather conditions（过去分词作定语）。 要点：原文用 are 与单数主语 The number 不一致，规范写法应为 is governed；抄录时保留原样，但要知道这是原文的疏漏。by + 名词 引出施动者，是技术被动句的常见收尾。

② 定语从句套时间状语从句

原句：Most heavy aircraft are equipped with surface control locks that are engaged or installed when the aircraft is secured. 拆解：主句 Most heavy aircraft are equipped with surface control locks（被动）；that 引导定语从句修饰 locks，从句内 are engaged or installed 为并列被动谓语，when 引导时间状语从句。 要点：be equipped with 意为“装有”，不是“被装备”；定语从句里再套时间状语从句，是规章文本“层层限定”的典型写法，翻译时要按中文习惯拆开。

③ Since 引导原因 + 祈使句主句

原句：Since the method of locking controls vary on different types of aircraft, check the manufacturer’s instructions for proper installation or engaging procedures. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（主语 the method of locking controls，谓语 vary）；主句是祈使句 check the manufacturer’s instructions；for proper installation or engaging procedures 说明查说明书的目的，installation 与 engaging 并列作介词宾语。 要点：“Since + 原因, 祈使句”构成“因为…所以请…”的要求句；installation 是名词、engaging 是动名词，并列时要保证词性一致（此处都作介词宾语，合法）。

④ only 的位置与限定范围

原句：Use tie-down stakes for temporary tie-down only. 拆解：祈使句 Use tie-down stakes for temporary tie-down only；only 位于句末，限定的是状语 for temporary tie-down。 要点：only 位置不同则限定对象不同——句末 only 强调“仅限临时系留”；若写成 Use only tie-down stakes… 就变成“只许用系留桩”，语义跑偏。
