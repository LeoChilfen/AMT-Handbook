Safety Around Compressed Gases

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Compressed Gases · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

1. 原文

Compressed air, like electricity, is an excellent tool when it is under control. A typical nitrogen bottle set is shown in Figure 1-1. The following “dos and don’ts” apply when working with or around compressed gases:

• Inspect air hoses frequently for breaks and worn spots.

Unsafe hoses must be replaced immediately.

• Keep all connections in a “no-leak condition.”

• Maintain in-line oilers, if installed, in operating condition.

• Ensure the system has water sumps installed and drained at regular intervals.

• Filter air used for paint spraying to remove oil and water.

• Never use compressed air to clean hands or clothing.

Pressure can force debris into the flesh leading to infection.

• Never spray compressed air in the area of other personnel.

• Straighten, coil, and properly store air hoses when not in use.

• Many accidents involving compressed gases occur during aircraft tire mounting. To prevent possible personal injury, use tire dollies and other appropriate devices to mount or remove heavy aircraft tires.

When inflating tires on any type of aircraft wheels, always use tire cage guards. Extreme caution is required to avoid over inflation of high-pressure tires because of possible personal injury. Use pressure regulators on high-pressure air bottles to eliminate the possibility of over inflation of tires. Tire cages are not required when adjusting pressure in tires installed on an aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

压缩空气和电一样，在受控状态下是极好的工具。典型的氮气瓶组见图 1-1。在使用压缩气体或在其附近作业时，应遵守以下「该做与不该做」事项：

• 经常检查空气软管有无破损和磨损部位。

不安全的软管必须立即更换。

• 保持所有接头处于「无泄漏状态」。

• 如装有管路油雾器，应使其保持可用状态。

• 确保系统装有集水器，并按固定间隔排放。

• 对用于喷漆的空气进行过滤，以去除油和水。

• 绝不要用压缩空气吹手或吹衣服。

压力会把碎屑压入皮肉，导致感染。

• 绝不要在其他人员所在区域喷射压缩空气。

• 不使用时，将空气软管理直、盘卷并妥善存放。

• 许多涉及压缩气体的事故发生在航空器轮胎安装过程中。为防止可能的人身伤害，安装或拆卸沉重的航空器轮胎时应使用轮胎小车及其他合适的器具。

给任何类型航空器机轮上的轮胎充气时，务必使用轮胎防护笼。为避免高压轮胎充气过量，必须格外谨慎，因为可能造成人身伤害。在高压气瓶上使用减压器，以消除轮胎充气过量的可能。在航空器上已安装的轮胎上调整气压时，不需要轮胎防护笼。

4. 语法解析

① 插入语 + when 引导的条件状语从句

原句：Compressed air, like electricity, is an excellent tool when it is under control. 拆解：主语 Compressed air + 插入语 like electricity（介词短语作比较，用两个逗号隔开）+ 系动词 is + 表语 an excellent tool + when 引导的时间/条件状语从句（it 指代 compressed air）。 要点：like 在此是介词「像……一样」，不是动词，不能作谓语；插入语不影响主谓一致，主语仍是单数 Compressed air，故用 is。when 从句在安全类文本中常表条件「在受控时」。

② 祈使句 + if installed 省略结构 + 宾语补足语

原句：Maintain in-line oilers, if installed, in operating condition. 拆解：祈使句谓语 Maintain + 宾语 in-line oilers + 插入的条件状语 if installed（省略 they are）+ 宾语补足语 in operating condition。 要点：if + 过去分词是「如已安装」的省略式；maintain sth in … condition 是「使某物保持……状态」的固定搭配，介词 in 不能换成 to 或 at。

③ 现在分词短语作结果状语

原句：Pressure can force debris into the flesh leading to infection. 拆解：主语 Pressure + 谓语 can force + 宾语 debris + 方向状语 into the flesh + 现在分词短语 leading to infection 作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面的整个动作。 要点：force sth into sth 表示「把某物压入某处」；leading to 的逻辑主语不是 pressure，而是「压力把碎屑压入皮肉」这件事，因此用主动式 leading，不能用 led。

④ when + 现在分词 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：Tire cages are not required when adjusting pressure in tires installed on an aircraft. 拆解：主语 Tire cages + 谓语 are not required（被动）+ when adjusting pressure in tires（when + 现在分词，省略与主句一致的 you are）+ installed on an aircraft（过去分词短语作 tires 的后置定语）。 要点：when + doing 的时间状语只在分词逻辑主语与主句一致时才可以省略主语；installed 过去分词短语后置修饰 tires，相当于 which are installed on an aircraft。
