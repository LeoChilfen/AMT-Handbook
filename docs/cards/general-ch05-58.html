1. 原文

The simple structure of gases makes them readily adaptable to mathematical analysis from which has evolved a detailed theory of the behavior of gases. This is called the kinetic theory of gases. The theory assumes that a body of gas is composed of identical molecules which behave like minute elastic spheres, spaced relatively far apart and continuously in motion.

The degree of molecular motion is dependent upon the temperature of the gas. Since the molecules are continuously striking against each other and against the walls of the container, an increase in temperature with the resulting increase in molecular motion causes a corresponding increase in the number of collisions between the molecules.

The increased number of collisions results in an increase in pressure because a greater number of molecules strike against the walls of the container in a given unit of time.

If the container were an open vessel, the gas would expand and overflow from the container. However, if the container is sealed and possesses elasticity, such as a rubber balloon, the increased pressure causes the container to expand. For instance, when making a long drive on a hot day, the pressure in the tires of an automobile increases, and a tire which appeared to be somewhat “soft” in cool morning temperature may appear normal at a higher midday temperature.

2. 中文翻译

气体的结构简单，因而易于进行数学分析，由此发展出了关于气体行为的详细理论。这一理论称为气体动理论。该理论假定：一团气体由相同的分子组成，这些分子如同微小的弹性球体，彼此间隔较远，并处于持续运动之中。

分子运动的剧烈程度取决于气体的温度。由于分子不断相互碰撞并撞击容器壁，温度升高以及由此引起的分子运动加剧，会使分子之间的碰撞次数相应增加。

碰撞次数增加导致压力升高，因为在给定的单位时间内有更多分子撞击容器壁。

若容器是敞口的，气体就会膨胀并从容器中溢出。但是，如果容器是密封的并且具有弹性（例如橡胶气球），压力升高就会使容器膨胀。例如，在炎热天气长途行车时，汽车轮胎内的压力会升高，早晨气温较低时显得有些「软」的轮胎，到中午气温较高时就可能显得正常。

4. 语法解析

① 介词 + which 引导定语从句，从句完全倒装

原句：The simple structure of gases makes them readily adaptable to mathematical analysis from which has evolved a detailed theory of the behavior of gases.

拆解：主句 The simple structure of gases makes them readily adaptable to mathematical analysis（make + 宾语 + 形容词宾补）；from which has evolved a detailed theory of the behavior of gases 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，从句为完全倒装，正常语序是 a detailed theory … has evolved from which。

要点：倒装的原因是主语 a detailed theory 后面还带 of 短语，太长，倒装后句子更平衡；读时可还原为 a detailed theory of the behavior of gases has evolved from that analysis。which 指代前面的 mathematical analysis，from 不能丢，evolve from 是「由……发展而来」。

② 与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句

原句：If the container were an open vessel, the gas would expand and overflow from the container.

拆解：If 从句用 were（be 动词虚拟式，所有人称一律用 were），主句用 would + 动词原形 expand and overflow；from the container 表示溢出方向或来源。

要点：这是「假设容器是敞口的」的虚拟条件，与下一句「然而如果是密封的」构成对照。技术文本用虚拟，是为了在讨论假设情形时不与实际情况混淆。注意从句用 were 而非 was，主句用 would 而非 will。

③ 长定语：定语从句与分词、介词短语并列后置

原句：The theory assumes that a body of gas is composed of identical molecules which behave like minute elastic spheres, spaced relatively far apart and continuously in motion.

拆解：主句 The theory assumes + that 引导的宾语从句；从句主干 a body of gas is composed of identical molecules；which behave like minute elastic spheres 是定语从句；spaced relatively far apart 与 (continuously) in motion 是并列的后置成分，共同说明 molecules 的状态。

要点：be composed of（由……组成）是固定被动搭配，不能用 be composed by。spaced 是过去分词表「被隔开」的状态，and continuously in motion 中的 in motion 是介词短语，与前一分词并列，相当于省略了 being；译成中文时两者都按「彼此间隔较远、处于持续运动中」并列处理。
