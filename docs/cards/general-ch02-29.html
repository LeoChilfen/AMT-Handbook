Section 43.16—Airworthiness Limitations

通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.16—Airworthiness Limitations · 原文 PDF 第 68 页

1. 原文

The technician performing inspection or maintenance actions on an aircraft must be certain they have all appropriate data available. Each person performing an inspection or other maintenance specified in an Airworthiness Limitations section of a manufacturer's maintenance manual or Instructions for Continued Airworthiness shall perform the inspection or other maintenance in accordance with that section, or in accordance with operations specifications approved by the Administrator under part 121 or 135, or an inspection program approved under 14 CFR part 91, section 91.409(e).

ICAs, as required by 14 CFR part 21, section 21.50, must also be consulted when available. Since 1998, the FAA has required ICAs to be generated for all major alterations that are accomplished by the field approval process. This section specifies that the technician is responsible to perform inspections or maintenance specified in an airworthiness limitation in accordance with all the preceding instructions.

2. 中文翻译

对航空器实施检查或维修动作的技师，必须确认自己手头备有全部适用数据。凡实施制造商维护手册或持续适航文件中“适航限制”部分所规定的检查或其他维修的人员，都必须按照该部分实施该检查或其他维修；或者按照依第 121 部或第 135 部经局方批准运行规范实施；或者按照依 14 CFR 第 91 部第 91.409(e) 条批准的检查方案实施。

按 14 CFR 第 21 部第 21.50 条的要求，凡有 ICA 可用时也必须查阅。自 1998 年起，FAA 要求对通过现场批准程序完成的所有重大改装编制 ICA。本条明确，技师有责任按照前述各项指示，实施适航限制所规定的检查或维修。

4. 语法解析

① 现在分词后置定语 + shall 表义务 + 三个 in accordance with 并列

原句：Each person performing an inspection or other maintenance specified in an Airworthiness Limitations section of a manufacturer's maintenance manual or Instructions for Continued Airworthiness shall perform the inspection or other maintenance in accordance with that section, or in accordance with operations specifications approved by the Administrator under part 121 or 135, or an inspection program approved under 14 CFR part 91, section 91.409(e).

拆解：主语 Each person，后接现在分词短语 performing … 作后置定语，其中 specified in … 又是过去分词短语修饰 an inspection or other maintenance；谓语 shall perform（shall 在法规中表强制义务）；宾语为 the inspection or other maintenance；后面三个 in accordance with 短语并列，approved by … 与 approved under … 均为过去分词作后置定语。

要点：法规中的 shall 一律译作“应当、必须”，不表将来。三个并列依据用 or 连接，意味着“满足其一即可”，不要译成“同时”。注意第三个并列项省略了 in accordance with，只保留 an inspection program，靠前面的介词短语共享结构。

② Since + 现在完成时 + require sth. to be done

原句：Since 1998, the FAA has required ICAs to be generated for all major alterations that are accomplished by the field approval process.

拆解：Since 1998 作时间状语，主句用现在完成时 has required，表示从 1998 年起持续至今；required ICAs to be generated 为 require + 宾语 + 不定式被动；that are accomplished by the field approval process 是定语从句修饰 all major alterations。

要点：since + 时间点必须配现在完成时（或现在完成进行时），不能配一般过去时。require sth. to be done 是“要求某事被做”的正式表达，比 require sb. to do 更常用于无明确施动者的规章条文。accomplished 在此意为“完成、实施”，与 perform 同义。

③ specifies that 宾语从句 + be responsible to do + 过去分词后置定语

原句：This section specifies that the technician is responsible to perform inspections or maintenance specified in an airworthiness limitation in accordance with all the preceding instructions.

拆解：主句 This section specifies that …；宾语从句主语 the technician，系表结构 is responsible to perform …；perform 的宾语 inspections or maintenance 后接过去分词短语 specified in an airworthiness limitation 作后置定语；in accordance with all the preceding instructions 为方式状语。

要点：be responsible to do sth. 表“有责任做某事”，与 be responsible for doing sth. 语义相近但后者更常见于描述职责范围。注意 specified 紧跟在 maintenance 之后，修饰的是“检查或维修”，而不是“技师”；英语定语后置造成的中文语序重组是本句最大难点。
