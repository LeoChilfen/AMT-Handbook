Block and Tackle

通用 Ch 05 · Block and Tackle · 原文 PDF 第 160 页

1. 原文

A block and tackle is made up of multiple pulleys, some of them fixed and some movable. In Figure 5-12, the block and tackle is made up of four pulleys, the top two being fixed and the bottom two being movable. Viewing the figure from right to left, notice there are four ropes supporting the weight and a fifth rope where the effort is applied. The number of weight supporting ropes determines the mechanical advantage of a block and tackle, so in this case the mechanical advantage is four. If the weight was 200 lb, it would require a 50 lb effort to lift it.

2. 中文翻译

滑车组由多个滑轮组成，其中一些是定滑轮，一些是动滑轮。在图 5-12 中，该滑车组由四个滑轮组成：上面两个是定滑轮，下面两个是动滑轮。从右向左看图可以看到，有四根绳索支承着重物，第五根绳索用于施加拉力。支承重物的绳索数目决定滑车组的机械利益，因此本例中机械利益为 4。若重物为 200 lb，则只需 50 lb 的施力即可将其提起。

4. 语法解析

① 独立主格：名词 + being + 形容词

原句：In Figure 5-12, the block and tackle is made up of four pulleys, the top two being fixed and the bottom two being movable.

拆解：主句 the block and tackle is made up of four pulleys（be made up of 表示「由……组成」）；其后的 the top two being fixed and the bottom two being movable 是独立主格结构，由两个「名词 + being + 形容词」并列，作补充说明。

要点：独立主格没有连词，只靠逗号挂靠主句，其逻辑主语与主句主语不同（the top two 指滑轮）。汉语译成「上面两个是定滑轮，下面两个是动滑轮」。若改写成并列句，须补 and they are…。同类：the first two being driven.

② 分词状语 + 祈使句 + there be + where 从句

原句：Viewing the figure from right to left, notice there are four ropes supporting the weight and a fifth rope where the effort is applied.

拆解：Viewing… 为现在分词短语作状语（时间／方式）；notice 后接省略 that 的宾语从句 there are four ropes … and a fifth rope …；supporting the weight 为现在分词后置定语；where the effort is applied 为定语从句修饰 a fifth rope。

要点：祈使句 notice… 是手册引导读者看图的惯用语，可译「注意／可以看到」。a fifth rope 用序数词表示「第五根、另一根」，与前面的 four ropes 形成对照，不可译成「一根第五绳」。notice 后的 that 常省略，但主句与从句都是完整结构，读时要找到宾语从句的起点。

③ The number of + 复数名词作主语

原句：The number of weight supporting ropes determines the mechanical advantage of a block and tackle, so in this case the mechanical advantage is four.

拆解：主语 The number of weight supporting ropes，中心词是单数 number，故谓语用 determines；weight supporting 为名词 + 分词构成的前置定语（= ropes that support the weight）；so 引导结果并列句。

要点：the number of + 复数名词 → 谓语用单数；a number of + 复数名词 → 谓语用复数，这是高频考点。weight-supporting ropes 书面常加连字符，作形容词用时不加 -s。so in this case… 译「因此本例中……」。
