1. 原文

The Pythagorean Theorem is named after the ancient Greek mathematician, Pythagoras (~500 B.C.). This theorem is used to find the third side of any right triangle when two sides are known. The Pythagorean Theorem states that a 2 + b 2 = c 2.

[Figure 3-32] Where “c” = the hypotenuse of a right triangle, “a” is one side of the triangle and “b” is the other side of the triangle.

Example: What is the length of the longest side of a right triangle, given the other sides are 7 inches and 9 inches?

The longest side of a right triangle is always side “c,” the hypotenuse. Use the Pythagorean Theorem to solve for the length of side “c” as follows:

a 2 + b2 = c2 7 2 + 92 = c2 49 + 81 = c2 130 = c2 c = 130 = 11.4 inches Therefore, side “c” = 11.4 inches.

Example: The cargo door opening in a military airplane is a rectangle that is 5 1⁄2 feet tall by 7 feet wide. A section of square steel plate that is 8 feet wide by 8 feet tall by 1 inch thick must fit inside the airplane. Can the square section of steel plate fit through the cargo door? It is obvious that the square steel plate will not fit horizontally through the cargo door. The steel plate is 8 feet wide and the cargo door is only 7 feet wide. However, if the steel plate is tilted diagonally, will it fit through the cargo door opening?

The diagonal distance across the cargo door opening can be calculated using the Pythagorean Theorem where “a” is the cargo door width, “b” is the cargo door height, and “c” is the diagonal distance across the cargo door opening.

4. 语法解析

① be used to do 表用途 + when 条件从句

原句：This theorem is used to find the third side of any right triangle when two sides are known.

拆解：主句是被动结构 is used to find，be used to do 表示「被用来做某事」，此处 to 是不定式符号；when two sides are known 是时间／条件状语从句，从句内部也是被动。

要点：be used to do（用来做）与 be used to doing（习惯于做）只差一个 -ing，意思完全不同，本节是前者；另有 be used to + 名词「被用于……」。when 从句用被动是因为重点在「两条边已知」这个状态，不必点明谁已知。

② given 引导的条件状语

原句：Example: What is the length of the longest side of a right triangle, given the other sides are 7 inches and 9 inches?

拆解：主句是特殊疑问句 What is the length of …；given the other sides are 7 inches and 9 inches 是 given 引导的条件状语，相当于 if / assuming (that)。

要点：given 在这里不是 give 的过去分词「被给予」，而是介词化的「已知、假定」，后接从句时常省略 that；数学题面里的 given… 就是「已知……」。译成中文时习惯上把它提到句首：「已知另两条边为 7 英寸和 9 英寸，求……」。

③ It 作形式主语 + that 从句

原句：It is obvious that the square steel plate will not fit horizontally through the cargo door.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 从句；obvious 作表语；horizontally 是方式副词修饰 fit。

要点：It is + adj. + that 从句是技术说明里常用的评价性句式，避免主语过长造成头重脚轻。fit through 表示「穿过（开口）」，比 fit in 更强调从孔洞中通过；horizontally、diagonally 这类方式副词通常放在动词之后。
