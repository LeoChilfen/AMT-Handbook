Extinguishing Engine Fires

通用 Ch 01 · Extinguishing Engine Fires · 原文 PDF 第 36 页

1. 原文

In all cases, a fireguard should stand by with a CO 2 fire extinguisher while the aircraft engine is being started. This is a necessary precaution against fire during the starting procedure. The fireguard must be familiar with the induction system of the engine so that in case of fire, they can direct the CO2 into the air intake of the engine to extinguish it. A fire could also occur in the exhaust system of the engine from liquid fuel being ignited in the cylinder and expelled during the normal rotation of the engine. If an engine fire develops during the starting procedure, the operator should continue cranking to start the engine and extinguish the fire. If the engine does not start and the fire continues to burn, discontinue the start attempt. The fireguard then extinguishes the fire using the available equipment. The fireguard must observe all safety practices at all times while standing by during the starting procedure.

2. 中文翻译

无论何种情况，起动飞机发动机时，消防警戒员（fireguard）都应手持 CO2 灭火器守候在旁。这是起动过程中防火的必要预防措施。消防警戒员必须熟悉发动机的进气系统，以便起火时能把 CO2 喷入发动机进气道把火扑灭。发动机排气系统也可能起火：燃油在气缸内被点燃后，在发动机正常转动过程中被排出而燃烧。如果起动过程中发生发动机起火，操作者应继续盘转、把发动机起动着，从而把火吹灭。如果发动机没能起动而火继续燃烧，就中止这次起动尝试。随后由消防警戒员用现场可用的器材把火扑灭。守候期间，消防警戒员必须始终遵守各项安全操作规程。

4. 语法解析

① while 从句中的被动进行时

原句：In all cases, a fireguard should stand by with a CO 2 fire extinguisher while the aircraft engine is being started.

拆解：In all cases 是条件/范围状语；主句主语 a fireguard，谓语 should stand by（情态动词 + 短语动词），with a CO 2 fire extinguisher 是伴随状语；while 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在进行时的被动语态 is being started。

要点：is being started 表示「正在被起动」，强调与主句动作同时进行；若写成 is started 就变成「（每次）被起动」的惯常意味，与「守候在旁」的同时性不符。被动进行时的格式固定为 be + being + 过去分词。

② so that 目的从句 + in case of 插入

原句：The fireguard must be familiar with the induction system of the engine so that in case of fire, they can direct the CO2 into the air intake of the engine to extinguish it.

拆解：主句 The fireguard must be familiar with…（be familiar with 是固定搭配）；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句内 in case of fire 是条件状语插在句首，主语 they，谓语 can direct，宾语 the CO2，into the air intake 是方向状语，to extinguish it 是目的不定式。

要点：so that 表目的时从句常带 can/could/may；in case of + 名词 = 「万一发生……」，比 in case + 从句更书面。they 在此指代 a fireguard 这一职务（单数概念），现代手册常用 they 避免 he/she 之累。

③ 动名词复合结构（被动式）作介词宾语

原句：A fire could also occur in the exhaust system of the engine from liquid fuel being ignited in the cylinder and expelled during the normal rotation of the engine.

拆解：主干 A fire could occur；in the exhaust system of the engine 是地点状语；from liquid fuel being ignited… and expelled… 是原因状语，介词 from 的宾语是动名词复合结构「liquid fuel + being ignited / (being) expelled」，燃油与点燃是被动关系，故用 being + 过去分词；during… 是时间状语。

要点：介词后接「名词 + -ing」时，名词是 -ing 的逻辑主语；被动关系必须用 being done（不能用 doing）。这里 being ignited 与 (being) expelled 并列，后一个 being 可承前省略，考试里常考这处省略。

④ 现在分词短语作方式状语

原句：The fireguard then extinguishes the fire using the available equipment.

拆解：主语 The fireguard，状语 then，谓语 extinguishes，宾语 the fire；using the available equipment 是现在分词短语作方式状语，相当于 by using…。

要点：using 的逻辑主语就是句子的主语 the fireguard（主动关系），所以用 -ing 而非 -ed；这种分词状语比 by using 更简洁，但改写时不要丢掉「手段」含义而误读成时间状语。
