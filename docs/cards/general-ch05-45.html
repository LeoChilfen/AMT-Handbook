1. 原文

Heat is a form of energy. It is produced only by the conversion of one of the other forms of energy. Heat may also be defined as the total kinetic energy of the molecules of any substance.

Some forms of energy which can be converted into heat energy are as follows:

• Mechanical Energy—this includes all methods of producing increased motion of molecules such as friction, impact of bodies, or compression of gases.

• Electrical Energy—electrical energy is converted to heat energy when an electric current flows through any form of resistance such as an electric iron, electric light, or an electric blanket.

• Chemical Energy—most forms of chemical reaction convert stored potential energy into heat. Some examples are the explosive effects of gunpowder, the burning of oil or wood, and the combining of oxygen and grease.

• Radiant Energy—electromagnetic waves of certain frequencies produce heat when they are absorbed by the bodies they strike such as x-rays, light rays, and infrared rays.

• Nuclear Energy—energy stored in the nucleus of atoms is released during the process of nuclear fission in a nuclear reactor or atomic explosion.

• Sun—all heat energy can be directly or indirectly traced to the nuclear reactions occurring in the sun.

When a gas is compressed, work is done and the gas becomes warm or hot. Conversely, when a gas under high pressure is allowed to expand, the expanding gas becomes cool. In the first case, work was converted into energy in the form of heat; in the second case heat energy was expended. Since heat is given off or absorbed, there must be a relationship between heat energy and work. Also, when two surfaces are rubbed together, the friction develops heat. However, work was required to cause the heat, and by experimentation, it has been shown that the work required and the amount of heat produced by friction is proportional. Thus, heat can be regarded as a form of energy.

According to this theory of heat as a form of energy, the molecules, atoms, and electrons in all bodies are in a continual state of motion. In a hot body, these small particles possess relatively large amounts of kinetic energy, but in cooler bodies they have less. Because the small particles are given motion, and hence kinetic energy, work must be done to slide one body over the other. Mechanical energy apparently is transformed, and what we know as heat is really kinetic energy of the small molecular subdivisions of matter.

2. 中文翻译

热是能量的一种形式。它只能由其他形式能量中的某一种转换而产生。热也可以定义为任何物质全部分子动能的总和。

可以转换为热能的能量形式如下：

• 机械能——包括一切使分子运动加剧的产生方式，例如摩擦、物体撞击或气体压缩。

• 电能——当电流通过任何形式的电阻时，电能就转换为热能，例如电熨斗、电灯或电热毯。

• 化学能——大多数化学反应会把储存的势能转换为热。例如火药的爆炸效应、油或木材的燃烧，以及氧与润滑脂的结合。

• 辐射能——某些频率的电磁波被其所照射的物体吸收时会产生热，例如 X 射线、光线和红外线。

• 核能——储存在原子核中的能量，在核反应堆的核裂变过程中或原子爆炸中释放出来。

• 太阳——一切热能都可以直接或间接追溯到太阳中发生的核反应。

气体被压缩时要作功，气体随之变温或变热。反之，若让高压气体膨胀，膨胀中的气体就会变冷。前一种情况下，功被转换为热能形式；后一种情况下，热能则被消耗掉。既然热被放出或被吸收，热能与功之间就必定存在某种关系。此外，把两个表面相互摩擦时，摩擦会产生热。但要产生这些热必须作功；实验表明，所需的功与摩擦产生的热量成正比。因此，热可以被看作能量的一种形式。

按照这种「热是能量的一种形式」的理论，一切物体中的分子、原子和电子都处于持续运动状态。在热的物体中，这些微小粒子具有相对较多的动能；在较冷的物体中则较少。由于这些微小粒子被赋予运动、从而获得动能，所以要使一个物体在另一物体上滑动就必须作功。机械能显然发生了转换，而我们所说的热，实际上就是物质中更小分子单元的动能。

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词 + 被动：may also be defined as

原句：Heat may also be defined as the total kinetic energy of the molecules of any substance.

拆解：主语 Heat；谓语 may be defined（情态动词 + be + 过去分词）；as the total kinetic energy of the molecules of any substance 是主语补足语，说明「被定义为什么」。

要点：be defined as 是给术语下定义的固定句式，as 后面接名词短语，不能换成 like。may 在此表示「也可以（这样理解）」，并非「可能」。手册里下定义常用 is defined as / may be defined as / refers to 三种说法，注意区分：refers to 后面直接跟名词，不带 as。

② 过去分词后置定语与主谓一致：the work required and the amount of heat produced

原句：However, work was required to cause the heat, and by experimentation, it has been shown that the work required and the amount of heat produced by friction is proportional.

拆解：and 连接两个并列分句。后一分句里 it has been shown that … 是「形式主语 + 现在完成时被动 + 主语从句」；从句主语是 the work required and the amount of heat produced by friction，required 与 produced by friction 都是过去分词短语作后置定语；谓语用 is proportional，把两者当作一个整体来看。

要点：it has been shown that 是「已证明」的客观表述，比 we have shown 更适合手册语体。required 是「所需的」，不是谓语。原文谓语用单数 is，因为作者把「所需的功与产生的热量」视为一个比例关系整体；用 and 连接两个不同事物时通常用复数 are，遇到这种原文写法照译即可，不必改数。

③ since 引导原因状语从句 + there must be 存在句

原句：Since heat is given off or absorbed, there must be a relationship between heat energy and work.

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（「既然」）；主句是 there must be 存在句，主语 a relationship，between … and … 作后置定语修饰 a relationship。

要点：since 表原因时语气比 because 弱，常译「既然」，也可表时间「自从」，要靠上下文分辨。there must be 是 there be 加情态动词，表示推断「必定存在」。give off 是「放出（热、气味、光）」，此处用被动 is given off，与 is absorbed 并列。

④ 目的状语与结果表达：work must be done to slide one body over the other

原句：Because the small particles are given motion, and hence kinetic energy, work must be done to slide one body over the other.

拆解：Because 引导原因状语从句，从句内 are given motion 为被动，and hence kinetic energy 是省略成分（＝and hence are given kinetic energy）；主句 work must be done，to slide one body over the other 是不定式作目的状语。

要点：hence 是副词「因而」，能这样直接跟一个名词短语，是因为它承接前面的 are given，省略了重复的动词。work must be done 是被动语态，表示「必须作功」而不点明谁作。slide A over B 是「使 A 在 B 上滑动」，over 不能换成 on。
