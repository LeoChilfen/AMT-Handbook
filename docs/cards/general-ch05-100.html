Static Stability

通用 Ch 05 · Static Stability · 原文 PDF 第 190 页

1. 原文

The initial response that an airplane displays after its equilibrium is disrupted is referred to as its static stability. If the static stability is positive, the airplane will tend to return to its original position after the disruptive force is removed.

If the static stability is negative, the airplane will continue to move away from its original position after the disruptive force is removed. If an airplane with negative static stability has the nose pitch up because of wake turbulence, the tendency will be for the nose to continue to pitch up even after the turbulence goes away. If an airplane tends to remain in a displaced position after the force is removed, but does not continue to move toward even greater displacement, its static stability is described as being neutral.

2. 中文翻译

飞机的平衡被打破之后所表现出的最初反应，称为它的静稳定性（static stability）。如果静稳定性为正（positive），那么在破坏力被移除后，飞机倾向于回到原来的位置。

如果静稳定性为负（negative），那么在破坏力被移除后，飞机会继续偏离原来的位置。如果一架具有负静稳定性的飞机因尾流湍流而机头上仰，那么即使湍流消失，机头也会继续上仰。如果飞机在力被移除后倾向于停留在偏离后的位置，但并不继续朝更大的偏离方向运动，则其静稳定性被称为中性（neutral）。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + 时间状语从句套在名词后

原句：The initial response that an airplane displays after its equilibrium is disrupted is referred to as its static stability.

拆解：主语 The initial response；that an airplane displays 是定语从句修饰 response；after its equilibrium is disrupted 是时间状语从句，紧跟 displays，说明「在平衡被打破之后」；主句谓语 is referred to as its static stability（被动 + as 补语）。

要点：一个名词后接连两个从句容易误读：先读 response → that an airplane displays（飞机所表现出的）→ after...（在平衡被打破之后）。be referred to as（被称为）后接名词。disrupt 与 disturb 同义，注意此处用的是被动 is disrupted。

② 条件句 + tend to do + 时间从句

原句：If the static stability is positive, the airplane will tend to return to its original position after the disruptive force is removed.

拆解：If 条件从句用一般现在时；主句 the airplane will tend to return to its original position；tend to 后接动词原形 return；after the disruptive force is removed 是时间状语从句，用被动。

要点：tend to do（倾向于做）是描述稳定性与趋势的高频搭配，不能写 tend doing。after 从句用一般现在时被动表将来（「主将从现」的延伸），is removed 不能写成 will be removed。

③ if 从句中的并列谓语 + be described as being

原句：If an airplane tends to remain in a displaced position after the force is removed, but does not continue to move toward even greater displacement, its static stability is described as being neutral.

拆解：If 从句的主语 an airplane 后接两个并列谓语 tends to remain... 与 does not continue to move...，用 but 连接，表示转折、限定；after the force is removed 是时间状语从句；主句 its static stability is described as being neutral。

要点：一个 if 从句里并列两个谓语时，第二个谓语用助动词 does 构成否定（does not continue），不必重复 tends。even greater（更大的）中 even 修饰比较级，起加强作用；toward 表方向。be described as being + 形容词／名词中，being 强调状态。
