1. 原文

  1. Administrative 2. Airworthiness Certification 3. Airworthiness Operation Since 1958, these rules have typically been referred to as “FARs,” short for Federal Aviation Regulations. However, another set of regulations, Title 48, is titled “Federal Acquisitions Regulations,” and this has led to confusion with the use of the acronym “FAR.” Therefore, the FAA began to refer to specific regulations by the term “14 CFR part XX.” Most regulations and the sections within are odd numbered, because the FAA realized in 1958 when the Civil Aeronautics Regulations were recodified into the Federal Aviation Regulations that it would be necessary to add regulations later.

Over the years, the FAA has sometimes seen the need to issue Special Federal Aviation Regulations (SFAR). [Figure 2-3]

These are frequently focused very specifically on a unique situation and are usually given a limited length of time for effectiveness. Note that the SFAR number is purely a sequential number and has no relevance to the regulation it is addressing or attached to.

The remainder of this handbook focuses only on those regulations relative to airworthiness certification. There are 30 of these listed in Figure 2-4 , and they are shown graphically in Figure 2-5. A significant benefit of this chart is the visual effect showing the interaction of the regulation with other regulations and the placement of the regulation relative to its impact on airworthiness. It is fundamentally important that the definition of the term “airworthy” be clearly understood.

Only recently did the FAA actually define the term “airworthy” in a regulation. (Refer to the 14 CFR part 3 excerpt following this paragraph.) Prior to this definition in part 3, the term could be implied from reading part 21, section 21.183. The term was defined in other non-regulatory FAA publications, and could also be implied from the text found in block 5 of FAA Form 8100-2, Standard Airworthiness Certificate. This certificate is required to be visibly placed on board each civil aircraft.

(Refer to “Forms” presented later in this chapter.)

2. 中文翻译

  1. 行政类（Administrative）；2. 适航审定类（Airworthiness Certification）；3. 适航运行类（Airworthiness Operation）。自 1958 年起，这些规则通常被称为「FARs」，即《联邦航空法规》（Federal Aviation Regulations）的缩写。然而，另一套法规即第 48 编名为《联邦采购法规》（Federal Acquisitions Regulations），这就使「FAR」这一缩写的使用产生了混淆。因此，FAA 开始用「14 CFR part XX」来指称具体法规。多数法规及其中的条款编号为奇数，因为 FAA 在 1958 年把《民用航空法规》重新编纂为《联邦航空法规》时就意识到，日后必然需要增补法规。

多年来，FAA 有时认为有必要发布《特殊联邦航空法规》（SFAR）。[图 2-3]

这类法规往往非常具体地针对某一特殊情形，并且通常规定了有限的有效期限。需要注意，SFAR 的编号纯粹是顺序号，与它所针对或所附的法规没有任何关联。

本手册余下部分只讨论与适航审定有关的法规。图 2-4 列出了其中 30 部，图 2-5 则以图示方式给出。这张图的一大价值在于其视觉效果：它显示出该法规与其他法规之间的相互作用，以及该法规在影响适航性方面的位置。从根本上说，必须清晰理解「airworthy」（适航）这一术语的定义。

直到不久前，FAA 才真正在一部法规中对「airworthy」一词作出定义。（参见紧接本段之后的 14 CFR part 3 摘录。）在 part 3 给出这一定义之前，该术语只能从 part 21 第 21.183 节中读出其含义。该术语曾在 FAA 的其他非法规性出版物中作过定义，也可以从 FAA 表格 8100-2《标准适航证》第 5 栏的文字中读出。按规定，每架民用航空器上都须在醒目位置放置该合格证。

（参见本章后文「表格（Forms）」一节。）

4. 语法解析

① have typically been referred to as（现在完成时被动 + since 状语）

原句：Since 1958, these rules have typically been referred to as “FARs,” short for Federal Aviation Regulations.

拆解：Since 1958 是现在完成时的时间状语；谓语 have been referred to as 为「现在完成时 + 被动」；short for Federal Aviation Regulations 是形容词短语作同位补充说明，不是句子成分的谓语部分。

要点：refer to A as B「把 A 称为 B」，被动式为 A is referred to as B，B 前通常不加冠词。since + 时间点要求主句用现在完成时，写成 since 1958 … are referred to 是常见错误。short for 常紧跟在缩写后作插入说明。

② and this has led to confusion（并列句 + this 指代整句）

原句：However, another set of regulations, Title 48, is titled “Federal Acquisitions Regulations,” and this has led to confusion with the use of the acronym “FAR.”

拆解：However 表转折；another set of regulations 后接同位语 Title 48；is titled 为被动；and this has led to … 中 this 是代词，指代前面「第 48 编也叫 FAR」这一整个事实；with the use of 是介词短语状语。

要点：科技英语常用 this / which 指代前面一整句话而非单个名词，译成中文时要补出「这一情况／这」才能成立。lead to「导致」，后接名词；接动词须用 lead to doing。

③ are odd numbered, because … that it would be necessary to add regulations later（原因状语从句 + 形式主语）

原句：Most regulations and the sections within are odd numbered, because the FAA realized in 1958 when the Civil Aeronautics Regulations were recodified into the Federal Aviation Regulations that it would be necessary to add regulations later.

拆解：主句是系表结构（are odd numbered）；because 从句的骨架是 the FAA realized … that …，realized 与 that 从句之间插入了 when 引导的时间状语从句；that 从句中 it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to add regulations later。

要点：长句里 when 从句夹在动词和 that 之间，容易被误读成定语从句；先抓住 realized that 的骨架，再回头补时间信息。odd numbered 是「编号为奇数」，不是「奇怪的编号」；the sections within 中 within 是副词后置，等于 the sections within them。
