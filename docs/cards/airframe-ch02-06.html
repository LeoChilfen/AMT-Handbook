Thrust & Drag

机体 Ch 02 · Thrust & Drag · 原文 PDF 第 77 页

1. 原文

An aircraft in flight is the center of a continuous battle of forces. Actually, this conflict is not as violent as it sounds, but it is the key to all maneuvers performed in the air. There is nothing mysterious about these forces; they are definite and known. The directions in which they act can be calculated, and the aircraft itself is designed to take advantage of each of them. In all types of flying, flight calculations are based on the magnitude and direction of four forces: weight, lift, drag, and thrust.

An aircraft in flight is acted upon by four forces:

  1. Gravity or weight—the force that pulls the aircraft toward the earth. Weight is the force of gravity acting downward upon everything that goes into the aircraft, such as the aircraft itself, crew, fuel, and cargo.
  1. Lift—the force that pushes the aircraft upward. Lift acts vertically and counteracts the effects of weight.
  1. Thrust—the force that moves the aircraft forward. Thrust is the forward force produced by the powerplant that overcomes the force of drag.
  1. Drag—the force that exerts a braking action to hold the aircraft back. Drag is a backward deterrent force and is caused by the disruption of the airflow by the wings, fuselage, and protruding objects.

These four forces are in perfect balance only when the aircraft is in straight-and-level unaccelerated flight.

The forces of lift and drag are the direct result of the relationship between the relative wind and the aircraft. The force of lift always acts perpendicular to the relative wind, and the force of drag always acts parallel to and in the same direction as the relative wind. These forces are actually the components that produce a resultant lift force on the wing.

Weight has a definite relationship with lift, and thrust with drag. These relationships are quite simple, but very important in understanding the aerodynamics of flying. As stated previously, lift is the upward force on the wing perpendicular to the relative wind. Lift is required to counteract the aircraft’s weight, caused by the force of gravity acting on the mass of the aircraft. This weight force acts downward through a point called the center of gravity (CG). The CG is the point at which all the weight of the aircraft is considered to be concentrated. When the lift force is in equilibrium with the weight force, the aircraft neither gains nor loses altitude. If lift becomes less than weight, the aircraft loses altitude. When the lift is greater than the weight, the aircraft gains altitude.

Wing area is measured in square feet and includes the part blanked out by the fuselage. Wing area is adequately described as the area of the shadow cast by the wing at high noon. Tests show that lift and drag forces acting on a wing are roughly proportional to the wing area. This means that if the wing area is doubled, all other variables remaining the same, the lift and drag created by the wing is doubled. If the area is tripled, lift and drag are tripled.

Drag must be overcome for the aircraft to move, and movement is essential to obtain lift. To overcome drag and move the aircraft forward, another force is essential. This force is thrust. Thrust is derived from jet propulsion or from a propeller and engine combination. Jet propulsion theory is based on Newton’s third law of motion (page 2-3) . The turbine engine causes a mass of air to be moved backward at high velocity causing a reaction that moves the aircraft forward.

In a propeller/engine combination, the propeller is actually two or more revolving airfoils mounted on a horizontal shaft. The motion of the blades through the air produces lift similar to the lift on the wing, but acts in a horizontal direction, pulling the aircraft forward.

Before the aircraft begins to move, thrust must be exerted. The aircraft continues to move and gain speed until thrust and drag are equal. In order to maintain a steady speed, thrust and drag must remain equal, just as lift and weight must be equal for steady, horizontal flight. Increasing the lift means that the aircraft moves upward, whereas decreasing the lift so that it is less than the weight causes the aircraft to lose altitude. A similar rule applies to the two forces of thrust and drag. If the revolutions per minute (rpm) of the engine is reduced, the thrust is lessened, and the aircraft slows down. As long as the thrust is less than the drag, the aircraft travels more and more slowly until its speed is insufficient to support it in the air.

Likewise, if the rpm of the engine is increased, thrust becomes greater than drag, and the speed of the aircraft increases. As long as the thrust continues to be greater than the drag, the aircraft continues to accelerate. When drag equals thrust, the aircraft flies at a steady speed.

The relative motion of the air over an object that produces lift also produces drag. Drag is the resistance of the air to objects moving through it. If an aircraft is flying on a level course, the lift force acts vertically to support it while the drag force acts horizontally to hold it back. The total amount of drag on an aircraft is made up of many drag forces, but this handbook considers three: parasite drag, profile drag, and induced drag.

Parasite drag is made up of a combination of many different drag forces. Any exposed object on an aircraft offers some resistance to the air, and the more objects in the airstream, the more parasite drag. While parasite drag can be reduced by reducing the number of exposed parts to as few as practical and streamlining their shape, skin friction is the type of parasite drag most difficult to reduce. No surface is perfectly smooth. Even machined surfaces have a ragged uneven appearance when inspected under magnification. These ragged surfaces deflect the air near the surface causing resistance to smooth airflow. Skin friction can be reduced by using glossy smooth finishes and eliminating protruding rivet heads, roughness, and other irregularities.

Profile drag may be considered the parasite drag of the airfoil. The various components of parasite drag are all of the same nature as profile drag.

The action of the airfoil that creates lift also causes induced drag. Remember, the pressure above the wing is less than atmospheric pressure, and the pressure below the wing is equal to or greater than atmospheric pressure. Since fluids always move from high pressure toward low pressure, there is a spanwise movement of air from the bottom of the wing outward from the fuselage and upward around the wing tip. This flow of air results in spillage over the wing tip, thereby setting up a whirlpool of air called a “vortex.”

The air on the upper surface has a tendency to move in toward the fuselage and off the trailing edge. This air current forms a similar vortex at the inner portion of the trailing edge of the wing. These vortices increase drag because of the turbulence produced, and constitute induced drag.

Just as lift increases with an increase in AOA, induced drag also increases as the AOA becomes greater. This occurs because, as the AOA is increased, the pressure difference between the top and bottom of the wing becomes greater. This causes more violent vortices to be set up, resulting in more turbulence and more induced drag.

2. 中文翻译

飞行中的飞机是一连串力相互角逐的中心。其实，这种“角逐”并不像听起来那样激烈，但它是一切空中机动的关键。这些力并没有什么神秘之处；它们是确定且已知的。它们作用的方向可以计算，而飞机本身的设计正是要利用其中的每一个力。在所有类型的飞行中，飞行计算都以四个力的大小和方向为基础：重力、升力、阻力与推力。

飞行中的飞机受到四个力的作用：

  1. 重力或称重量——把飞机拉向地球的力。重量是重力向下作用于飞机上一切物体的力，这些物体包括飞机本身、机组、燃油和货物。
  1. 升力——把飞机向上推的力。升力垂直作用，抵消重量的作用。
  1. 推力——使飞机向前运动的力。推力是动力装置产生的、克服阻力的前向力。
  1. 阻力——起制动作用、把飞机拖住的力。阻力是向后的阻滞力，由机翼、机身和突出物扰乱气流而产生。

只有当飞机处于直线、水平、无加速度飞行时，这四个力才完全平衡。

升力和阻力是相对气流与飞机之间关系的直接结果。升力总是垂直于相对气流作用，而阻力则总是平行于相对气流、且与相对气流方向相同。这些力实际上是在机翼上产生合升力的分力。

重量与升力、推力与阻力之间都有确定的关系。这些关系相当简单，但对理解飞行空气动力学非常重要。如前所述，升力是机翼上垂直于相对气流的向上力。需要升力来抵消飞机的重量，而重量是由重力作用于飞机的质量而产生的。这个重力向下作用于一个称为重心（CG）的点。重心是飞机全部重量被视为集中的点。当升力与重量平衡时，飞机既不上升也不下降。如果升力小于重量，飞机就下降；当升力大于重量时，飞机就上升。

机翼面积以平方英尺计，并且包括被机身遮蔽的那一部分。机翼面积可以恰当地描述为机翼在正午时分投下的阴影面积。试验表明，作用在机翼上的升力和阻力大致与机翼面积成正比。这意味着，如果机翼面积加倍，在其余变量不变的情况下，机翼产生的升力和阻力也加倍；如果面积增至三倍，升力和阻力也增至三倍。

飞机要运动就必须克服阻力，而运动又是获得升力所必需的。要克服阻力并使飞机向前运动，还需要另一个力，这就是推力。推力来自喷气推进，或来自螺旋桨与发动机的组合。喷气推进理论基于牛顿第三运动定律（第2-3页）。涡轮发动机使一股空气高速向后运动，产生反作用力把飞机推向前方。

在螺旋桨与发动机的组合中，螺旋桨实际上是两个或多个装在水平轴上的旋转翼型。桨叶在空气中运动产生升力，与机翼上的升力相似，但作用方向是水平的，把飞机向前拉。

在飞机开始运动之前，必须先施加推力。飞机会持续运动和加速，直到推力与阻力相等。为了保持稳定速度，推力与阻力必须保持相等，正如要保持稳定的水平飞行，升力与重量必须相等一样。增大升力意味着飞机向上运动，而减小升力使其小于重量则使飞机下降。同样的规则适用于推力与阻力这两个力。如果发动机的每分钟转数（rpm）降低，推力便减小，飞机随之减速。只要推力小于阻力，飞机就会越飞越慢，直到其速度不足以维持在空中飞行。

同样，如果发动机的 rpm 增大，推力就变得大于阻力，飞机的速度随之增大。只要推力持续大于阻力，飞机就持续加速。当阻力等于推力时，飞机以稳定速度飞行。

空气相对于物体运动而产生升力的同时，也产生阻力。阻力是空气对在其中运动的物体的抵抗。如果飞机在水平航线上飞行，升力垂直作用以支撑飞机，而阻力水平作用把飞机拖住。飞机上的总阻力由许多阻力合成，但本手册讨论其中三种：废阻力、型面阻力和诱导阻力。

废阻力由许多不同阻力组合而成。飞机上任何外露物体都会对空气产生一定的阻力，气流中的物体越多，废阻力越大。虽然可以通过把外露部件减到尽可能少并使其外形流线化来减小废阻力，但表面摩擦是废阻力中最难减小的一种。没有哪个表面是绝对光滑的。即使经过机加工的表面，在放大镜下观察也有粗糙不平的外观。这些粗糙表面使靠近表面的空气发生偏转，对平滑气流形成阻力。使用光洁的涂饰面，并消除突出的铆钉头、粗糙度和其他不平整，可以减小表面摩擦。

型面阻力可以看作是翼型的废阻力。废阻力的各个组成部分在性质上都与型面阻力相同。

翼型产生升力的作用同时也引起诱导阻力。要记住，机翼上方的压力低于大气压力，而机翼下方的压力等于或大于大气压力。由于流体总是从高压流向低压，空气便沿展向从机翼下表面向外、绕过翼尖向上流动。这种气流造成气流从翼尖外溢，从而形成一个称为“涡”的空气旋涡。

上表面的空气有向机身内侧、朝后缘流出的趋势。这股气流在机翼后缘靠内侧形成类似的涡。这些涡因产生的湍流而增大阻力，构成诱导阻力。

正如升力随迎角（AOA）增大而增大一样，诱导阻力也随着 AOA 变大而增大。这是因为，随着 AOA 增大，机翼上下表面的压力差变大。这便形成更剧烈的涡，产生更多湍流和更大的诱导阻力。

4. 语法解析

① 介词短语前置 + 被动语态 + 冒号列举

原句：In all types of flying, flight calculations are based on the magnitude and direction of four forces: weight, lift, drag, and thrust. 拆解：句首 In all types of flying 为范围状语，提前交代适用场合；主干是 flight calculations are based on...，被动语态；宾语为 the magnitude and direction of four forces，冒号后 weight, lift, drag, and thrust 是对 four forces 的同位列举。 要点：be based on 后面跟的是两个并列名词（magnitude and direction），of four forces 只管 direction 与 magnitude 共同的对象；冒号后的四项不是新句子成分，翻译时用“即/：”引出即可，不要另起一句。

② only when 引导的限制性条件从句

原句：These four forces are in perfect balance only when the aircraft is in straight-and-level unaccelerated flight. 拆解：主句 These four forces are in perfect balance 为系表结构；only when 引导时间/条件状语从句，从句中 the aircraft is in straight-and-level unaccelerated flight 也是系表结构，straight-and-level unaccelerated 三个形容词并列修饰 flight。 要点：only when 放在主句之后仍表示“只有在……时才”，语义上限制的是整个主句，不是修饰 balance；中文要把“只有……才”这层限制译出来，不能译成“当……时，四力平衡”。

③ 两个分句的对称比较 + 介词短语并列

原句：The force of lift always acts perpendicular to the relative wind, and the force of drag always acts parallel to and in the same direction as the relative wind. 拆解：and 连接两个结构对称的分句，主语分别是 The force of lift 与 the force of drag，谓语同为 acts；前一分句状语是 perpendicular to the relative wind，后一分句是 parallel to 与 in the same direction as 两个介词短语共用一个宾语 the relative wind。 要点：parallel to 和 in the same direction as 共用 the relative wind，第二个介词短语省略了重复的宾语；perpendicular to 与 parallel to 是一对反义搭配，是理解升力、阻力方向的关键。

④ 被动语态 + 过去分词作后置定语

原句：Wing area is adequately described as the area of the shadow cast by the wing at high noon. 拆解：主干 Wing area is described as...，被动语态，as 引出主语补足语；the area of the shadow 为中心名词，过去分词短语 cast by the wing at high noon 作后置定语修饰 shadow，cast 的过去分词与原形同形。 要点：cast 在这里是“投（影）”，不是“铸造”；过去分词短语紧跟 shadow，说明是“机翼投下的”影子。翻译时把定语还原成动词：“机翼在正午投下的阴影面积”。

⑤ cause sth. to do + 现在分词结果状语 + 定语从句

原句：The turbine engine causes a mass of air to be moved backward at high velocity causing a reaction that moves the aircraft forward. 拆解：主干 The turbine engine causes a mass of air to be moved...，cause + 宾语 + 不定式作宾补，因 air 是被移动的，故用被动式 to be moved；causing a reaction 为现在分词短语作结果状语，其逻辑主语是整个前面的事件；a reaction 后接 that 定语从句。 要点：causes ... to be moved 与 causing a reaction 两个 -ing/-ed 形式功能不同：前者是宾补，后者是状语。causing 的逻辑主语不是 engine，而是“空气被向后加速”这一过程。

⑥ because 从句中插入 as 从句

原句：This occurs because, as the AOA is increased, the pressure difference between the top and bottom of the wing becomes greater. 拆解：主句 This occurs；because 引导原因状语从句，从句内部又插入 as the AOA is increased 这一时间/条件从句，主句为 the pressure difference ... becomes greater，between the top and bottom of the wing 作后置定语修饰 pressure difference。 要点：because 后面紧跟逗号再跟 as 从句，容易误读成 because of；这里的 as 表示“随着”，与 because 分工明确：because 管整句因果，as 管条件变化过程。
