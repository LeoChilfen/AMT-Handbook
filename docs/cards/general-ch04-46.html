Drawing Sketches

1. 原文

A sketch is a simple rough drawing that is made rapidly and without much detail. Sketches may take many forms— from a simple pictorial presentation to a multi-view orthographic projection.

Just as aircraft technicians need not be highly skilled in creating drawings, they need not be accomplished artists.

However, in many situations, they need to prepare a drawing to present an idea for a new design, a modification, or a repair method. The medium of sketching is an excellent way of accomplishing this.

The rules and conventional practices for making mechanical drawings are followed to the extent that all views needed to portray an object accurately are shown in their proper relationship. It is also necessary to observe the rules for correct line use and dimensioning. [Figures 4-24 and 4-25]

2. 中文翻译

草图（sketch）是一种快速绘制、细节不多的简略图。草图可以采取多种形式——从简单的立体图表现，到多视图正投影图。

正如航空器维修技师不必在绘图上技艺高超一样，他们也不必是训练有素的画家。

然而在许多情况下，他们需要准备一张图，用来表达新设计、改装方案或修理方法的构想；而草图这种手段正是完成此事的极佳方式。

绘制机械图的规则与惯例应予以遵循，其程度以「准确表现物体所需的全部视图都按正确的位置关系画出」为准。同时，还必须遵守正确用线与尺寸标注的规则。（图 4-24 和图 4-25）

4. 语法解析

① that is made rapidly and without much detail（定语从句 + 被动）

原句：A sketch is a simple rough drawing that is made rapidly and without much detail.

拆解：主句 A sketch is a simple rough drawing；that 引导定语从句修饰 drawing，从句谓语为被动 is made，后接两个状语 rapidly 与 without much detail。

要点：定义术语时手册惯用「A is a … that is + 过去分词」的句式，把「谁来做」隐去，突出动作方式。注意 rough 在此不是「粗糙、劣质」，而是「简略、不加细节」，与后文 without much detail 呼应。

② Just as …, they need not be …（just as 引导比较状语从句）

原句：Just as aircraft technicians need not be highly skilled in creating drawings, they need not be accomplished artists.

拆解：Just as 引导方式／比较状语从句，意为「正如……一样」；主句 they need not be accomplished artists 与从句结构平行（need not be + 表语）。

要点：just as A …, B … 表示两类情况的类比，前后常用相同的谓语形式以保持对称。need not be 是情态动词的否定，不是 don't need to be 的等义替换：前者是「不必是」，后者偏「不需要去做」。

③ to the extent that …（程度状语从句 + 过去分词后置定语）

原句：The rules and conventional practices for making mechanical drawings are followed to the extent that all views needed to portray an object accurately are shown in their proper relationship.

拆解：主句为被动 The rules … are followed；to the extent that 引出程度状语从句，说明「遵循到什么程度」；从句主语 all views 后接过去分词短语 needed to portray an object accurately 作后置定语，谓语是 are shown。

要点：to the extent that 相当于「达到……的程度」，是技术文本限定适用范围的高频结构，比 so … that 更书面。needed to … 是「被需要的」，用过去分词而非 needing，因为 views 是「被需要」的一方。读长句时先找到 are followed，再顺着 to the extent that 切出从句。
