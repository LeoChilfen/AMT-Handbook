1. 原文

A solid having the shape of a can, a length of pipe, or a barrel is called a cylinder. [Figure 3-26] The ends of a cylinder are identical circles. The formula for the volume of a cylinder is:

volume = π × radius2 × height of the cylinder = π r2 × h One of the most important applications of the volume of a cylinder is finding the piston displacement of a cylinder in a reciprocating engine. Piston displacement is the total volume (in cubic inches, cubic centimeters, or liters) swept by all of the pistons of a reciprocating engine as they move in one revolution of the crankshaft. The formula for piston displacement is given as:

Piston Displacement = π × (bore divided by 2)2 × stroke × (# cylinders)

Conversion Factors The bore of an engine is the inside diameter of the cylinder.

The stroke of the engine is the length the piston travels inside the cylinder. [Figure 3-27]

Example: Find the piston displacement of one cylinder in a multi-cylinder aircraft engine. The engine has a cylinder bore of 5.5 inches and a stroke of 5.4 inches. First, substitute the known values in the formula.

v = π × r2 × h = (3.1416) × (5.5 ÷ 2)2 × (5.4)

v = 23.758 × 5.4 = 128.29 cubic inches The piston displacement of one cylinder is 128.29 cubic inches. For an eight-cylinder engine, then the total engine displacement would be:

Total displacement for 8 cylinders = 8 × 128.29 = 1026.32 cubic inches of displacement

4. 语法解析

① A solid having the shape of … is called a cylinder（分词短语作后置定语 + 被动）

原句：A solid having the shape of a can, a length of pipe, or a barrel is called a cylinder.

拆解：主语 A solid 后接现在分词短语 having the shape of … 作后置定语（= that has the shape of …）；谓语 is called 为被动语态，a cylinder 是主语补足语。

要点：having 在此不是进行时，而是定语，可还原为 which has。手册给术语下定义时常用「... is called a X」，译作「称为」即可，不必译成「被叫做」。注意 having 的逻辑主语就是 A solid。

② swept by all of the pistons … as they move（过去分词后置定语 + as 时间状语从句）

原句：Piston displacement is the total volume (in cubic inches, cubic centimeters, or liters) swept by all of the pistons of a reciprocating engine as they move in one revolution of the crankshaft.

拆解：主语 Piston displacement，系动词 is，表语 the total volume；swept by … 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 volume；括号内 in cubic inches … 说明计量单位；as they move … 是 as 引导的时间状语从句。

要点：swept by 相当于 which is swept by，含被动义；译成「被全部活塞扫过的总容积」时可直接用「扫过」带宾语，更顺。as 在此表示「当……时」，不是「因为」；one revolution of the crankshaft 是「曲轴一转」，不要译成「一次革命」。

③ the length the piston travels（省略关系代词的定语从句）

原句：The stroke of the engine is the length the piston travels inside the cylinder.

拆解：主语 The stroke of the engine，系动词 is，表语 the length；the piston travels inside the cylinder 是省略了关系代词 that/which 的定语从句，修饰 the length（travel 在此作及物动词，意为「走过……距离」）。

要点：定语从句的关系代词作宾语时可以省略，这是技术文本里很常见的省略；读到时要在 the length 后补一个 that。travel 通常作不及物动词，但表示「走过某段距离」时可及物，如 travel 5 miles。
