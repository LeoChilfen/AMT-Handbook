Section 43.12—Maintenance Records: Falsification, Reproduction, or Alteration

通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.12—Maintenance Records: Falsification, Reproduction, or Alteration · 原文 PDF 第 67 页

1. 原文

The aviation community relies heavily on trust and honesty in both oral and written communication. The maintenance log entries described in 14 CFR part 43, sections 43.9 and 43.11 provide the documentation trail relied upon by aircraft owners, pilots, and technicians regarding the aircraft’s maintenance history. Falsification of these records is potentially dangerous to the personnel who rely on the accuracy of these records.

This section identifies that fraudulent entries are unacceptable.

If someone commits such an act, that action is the basis for suspension or revocation of the appropriate certificate, authorization, or approval. A technician who is encouraged by their employer, or by anyone else, to falsify records in any way should remember this comment: “Companies come and go, but my signature lasts a lifetime. I will not use it inappropriately.”

2. 中文翻译

航空界在口头与书面沟通中都高度依赖信任与诚实。14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 条和第 43.11 条所述的维修记录条目，构成了航空器所有人、驾驶员和技师了解该航空器维修历史所依赖的文件链。伪造这些记录，对依赖其准确性的人员具有潜在危险。

本条明确指出：虚假条目是不可接受的。

如果有人实施了此类行为，该行为即构成暂停或吊销相应执照、授权或批准的依据。技师若被其雇主或任何其他人怂恿以任何方式伪造记录，应当记住这句话：“公司来来去去，而我的签名却要用一辈子。我不会不当使用它。”

4. 语法解析

① 名词化主语 + who 定语从句 + be dangerous to

原句：Falsification of these records is potentially dangerous to the personnel who rely on the accuracy of these records.

拆解：主语为名词化的 Falsification of these records；系动词 is + 表语 potentially dangerous；to the personnel 为对象状语；who rely on the accuracy of these records 是定语从句修饰 the personnel，从句谓语 rely on 后接名词短语作宾语。

要点：把动作名词化（falsification 而非 someone falsifies）是法规与技术写作的典型手法，可使陈述客观化、便于加限定语。be dangerous to sb. 的介词固定用 to，不用 for；potentially 修饰形容词而非整个句子。

② if 条件句 + be the basis for

原句：If someone commits such an act, that action is the basis for suspension or revocation of the appropriate certificate, authorization, or approval.

拆解：If 引导真实条件状语从句，用一般现在时 commits（主语 someone，第三人称单数）；主句 that action is the basis for …，for 后接 suspension or revocation，of the appropriate certificate, authorization, or approval 限定被停被吊的对象。

要点：be the basis for（是……的依据）是法规中说明处罚依据的固定表达，同义还有 constitutes grounds for。注意 action 与 act 的呼应用词变化，译文用“该行为”承接最自然。suspension 与 revocation 是两个不同层级的处罚，翻译不能合并为“吊销”。

③ 定语从句割裂主谓 + should 表义务

原句：A technician who is encouraged by their employer, or by anyone else, to falsify records in any way should remember this comment:

拆解：主语 A technician 后接 who 引导的定语从句（内含被动 is encouraged by … to falsify，by their employer, or by anyone else 为并列施动者），谓语 should remember 被定语从句推后；this comment 后接冒号引出引语。

要点：定语从句过长时，主谓会被拉开很远，读句要先越过从句找到 should remember 这个谓语，否则容易误把 encouraged 当成主句动词。encourage sb. to do sth. 的被动式是 be encouraged to do，此处插入了 by 短语，顺序为 be encouraged by sb. to do sth.。should 在此表“应当”，是劝告性义务，语气弱于 must。
