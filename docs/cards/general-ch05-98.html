Axes of an Aircraft

通用 Ch 05 · Axes of an Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 190 页

1. 原文

An airplane in flight is controlled around one or more of three axes of rotation. These axes of rotation are the longitudinal, lateral, and vertical. On the airplane, all three axes intersect at the center of gravity. As the airplane pivots on one of these axes, it is in essence pivoting around the center of gravity (CG).

The center of gravity is also referred to as the center of rotation.

On the brightly colored airplane shown in Figure 5-60, the three axes are shown in the colors red (vertical axis), blue (longitudinal axis), and orange (lateral axis). The flight control that makes the airplane move around the axis is shown in a matching color.

The rudder, in red, causes the airplane to move around the vertical axis and this movement is described as being a yaw.

The elevator, in orange, causes the airplane to move around the lateral axis and this movement is described as being a pitch. The ailerons, in blue, cause the airplane to move around the longitudinal axis and this movement is described as being a roll.

2. 中文翻译

飞行中的飞机是绕三个轴中的一个或多个轴进行操纵的。这三个转轴是纵轴、横轴和立轴。在飞机上，三个轴都相交于重心（center of gravity）。当飞机绕其中某一轴转动时，本质上就是绕重心（CG）转动。

重心也称为转动中心（center of rotation）。

在图 5-60 所示涂有鲜艳颜色的飞机上，三个轴分别用红色（立轴）、蓝色（纵轴）和橙色（横轴）表示。使飞机绕该轴运动的飞行操纵面，也用与之匹配的颜色表示。

方向舵（rudder）为红色，使飞机绕立轴运动，这种运动称为偏航（yaw）。

升降舵（elevator）为橙色，使飞机绕横轴运动，这种运动称为俯仰（pitch）。副翼（ailerons）为蓝色，使飞机绕纵轴运动，这种运动称为横滚（roll）。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + in flight 作后置定语

原句：An airplane in flight is controlled around one or more of three axes of rotation.

拆解：主语 An airplane，in flight 是介词短语作后置定语（= which is in flight）；谓语 is controlled 为被动；around one or more of three axes of rotation 是方向状语，one or more of three 表示「三个中的某一个或几个」。

要点：被动语态省略了施动者（飞行员），因为手册关心的是「飞机如何被操纵」而非谁在操纵。in flight 这类「介词 + 名词」短语常后置作定语，相当于定语从句，如 a wing in motion。one or more of + 复数名词是固定表达；注意 one of + 复数名词作主语时谓语用单数。

② as 从句 + in essence 插入语

原句：As the airplane pivots on one of these axes, it is in essence pivoting around the center of gravity (CG).

拆解：As the airplane pivots on one of these axes 是时间／伴随状语从句；主句 it is in essence pivoting around the center of gravity，in essence（本质上）是插入状语，插在助动词 is 与现在分词之间；pivots on 与 pivoting around 是同义换用。

要点：pivot on／around 都表示「绕……转动」，on 更强调「以……为支点」。in essence 是句子副词，可置于句首或助动词之后；句中用现在进行时 is pivoting，表示「所做的其实正是……」的解释性语气。

③ 插入语 + be described as being

原句：The rudder, in red, causes the airplane to move around the vertical axis and this movement is described as being a yaw.

拆解：主语 The rudder，in red 是插入的介词短语（说明图中颜色）；谓语 causes，宾语 the airplane，宾语补足语 to move around the vertical axis；and 连接第二分句 this movement is described as being a yaw，as being 后接名词作补语。

要点：be described as（被描述为）后可直接跟名词，也可用 as being + 名词，being 强调「作为一种状态」。as 后不能用 like。causes the airplane to move 再次印证 cause sb to do 结构；这类「颜色对应」的说明句在插图说明中很常见，翻译时保留「为红色」即可。
