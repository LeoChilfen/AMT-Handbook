1. 原文

The use of vacuum tubes in aircraft electrical and electronic systems has rapidly declined due to the many advantages of using transistors. However, some systems still employ vacuum tubes in special applications, and possibly some older model aircraft still in service are equipped with devices that use vacuum tubes. While these components may still be in service, their infrequent occurrence does not warrant a detailed discussion.

Originally, vacuum tubes were developed for radio work. They are used in radio transmitters as amplifiers for controlling voltage and current, as oscillators for generating audio and radio frequency signals, and as rectifiers for converting AC into DC. While there are many types of vacuum tubes for a variety of applications, the most common types fall into one of the following families: (1) diode, (2) triode, (3) tetrode, and (4) pentode. Each of these vacuum tube types operates on the following fundamental principles.

When a piece of metal is heated, the speed of the electrons in the metal is increased. If the metal is heated to a high enough temperature, the electrons are accelerated to the point where some of them actually leave the surface of the metal. In a vacuum tube, electrons are supplied by a piece of metal called a cathode, which is heated by an electric current. Within limits, the hotter the cathode, the greater the number of electrons it gives off or emits.

To increase the number of electrons emitted, the cathode is usually coated with special chemical compounds. If an external field does not draw the emitted electrons away, they form about the cathode into a negatively-charged cloud called the space charge. The accumulation of negative electrons near the emitter repels others coming from the emitter. The emitter, if insulated, becomes positive because of the loss of electrons. This establishes an electrostatic field between the cloud of negative electrons and the now positive cathode. A balance is reached when only enough electrons flow from the cathode to the area surrounding it to supply the loss caused by diffusion of the space charge.

2. 中文翻译

由于使用晶体管具有诸多优点，真空管在飞机电气与电子系统中的应用已迅速减少。然而，一些系统在特殊应用中仍使用真空管，而且可能还有一些仍在服役的老型号飞机装备了使用真空管的装置。虽然这些元件可能仍在服役，但它们出现得并不频繁，不值得详细讨论。

真空管最初是为无线电工作而研制的。在无线电发射机中，它们用作放大器以控制电压和电流，用作振荡器以产生音频和射频信号，还用作整流器以把交流电转换为直流电。尽管真空管种类繁多、应用各异，但最常见的类型都属于以下系列之一：（1）二极管，（2）三极管，（3）四极管，（4）五极管。这些真空管类型都按以下基本原理工作。

当一块金属被加热时，金属中电子的速度会增大。如果把金属加热到足够高的温度，电子就会被加速到其中一部分实际离开金属表面的程度。在真空管中，电子由一块称为阴极的金属提供，阴极由电流加热。在一定范围内，阴极越热，它释放（发射）的电子数量就越多。

为增加发射的电子数量，阴极通常涂覆有特殊的化合物。如果外部电场没有把已发射的电子拉走，它们就会在阴极周围形成一团带负电的云，称为空间电荷。发射体附近负电子的积聚排斥从发射体出来的其他电子。发射体如果被绝缘，就会因失去电子而带正电。这在负电子云与现在带正电的阴极之间建立起一个静电场。当只有恰好足够的电子从阴极流向其周围区域、以补充空间电荷扩散所造成的损失时，就达到了平衡。

4. 语法解析

① 现在完成时 + due to 介词短语表原因

原句：The use of vacuum tubes in aircraft electrical and electronic systems has rapidly declined due to the many advantages of using transistors. 拆解：主语 The use（of vacuum tubes in aircraft electrical and electronic systems 为多层后置定语），谓语 has rapidly declined（现在完成时，副词 rapidly 置于助动词与实义动词之间），due to the many advantages of using transistors 为介词短语作原因状语。 要点：due to 后接名词或动名词，不能接从句，表原因时比 because of 更正式，手册中常用于说明趋势的成因；主语中心词是 use 而不是 vacuum tubes，所以谓语用单数 has；of using transistors 中 using 为动名词，介词 of 后不能接动词原形。

② while 引导让步状语从句

原句：While these components may still be in service, their infrequent occurrence does not warrant a detailed discussion. 拆解：While 引导让步状语从句（主语 these components，情态动词 may，系动词 still be，表语 in service）；主句主语 their infrequent occurrence，谓语 does not warrant，宾语 a detailed discussion。 要点：while 既可表时间「当……时」，也可表让步「虽然」，此处因主从句语义相对可判定为让步，译为「虽然……但……」；warrant 意为「值得、使有必要」，后接名词，不说 warrant to discuss；主句用 does 而非 do，因为主语 occurrence 为单数。

③ the + 比较级，the + 比较级

原句：Within limits, the hotter the cathode, the greater the number of electrons it gives off or emits. 拆解：Within limits 为前置状语；两个 the + 比较级分句并列，the hotter the cathode 表条件，the greater the number of electrons 表结果；it gives off or emits 为省略关系词的定语从句，修饰 electrons，其中 it 指阴极。 要点：这是「越……越……」的固定句式，两个 the 都不能省；后半句的表语 the greater 后还要接被比较的名词短语 the number of electrons，谓语 be 被省略；定语从句用 gives off or emits 双动词并列，说明「释放/发射」是同一动作的两种说法。

④ 不定式作目的状语 + 被动语态

原句：To increase the number of electrons emitted, the cathode is usually coated with special chemical compounds. 拆解：To increase the number of electrons emitted 为不定式短语作目的状语，其中 emitted 为过去分词作后置定语修饰 electrons；主句主语 the cathode，谓语 is coated（被动语态），with special chemical compounds 为方式状语。 要点：目的状语置于句首时用逗号与主句隔开，其逻辑主语是主句主语，翻译时常补出「为了……」；be coated with 是固定搭配「涂覆有……」，不能换成 coated by（那是「被某人涂」）；过去分词 emitted 单独后置修饰名词，等于 that are emitted，比用定语从句更简洁。
