Finding the Least Common Denominator

通用 Ch 03 · Finding the Least Common Denominator · 原文 PDF 第 98 页

1. 原文

To add or subtract fractions, they must have a common denominator. In math, the least common denominator (LCD)

is generally used. One way to find the LCD is to list the multiples of each denominator and then choose the smallest number that they all have in common (can be divided by).

Example: Add 1⁄5 + 1⁄10 by finding the LCD.

Multiples of 5 are: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, and so on. Multiples of 10 are: 10, 20, 30, 40, and so on. Notice that 10, 20, and 30 are in both lists, but 10 is the smallest or LCD. The advantage of finding the LCD is that the final answer should be in the simplest form.

A common denominator can also be found for any group of fractions by multiplying all the denominators together. This number is not always the LCD, but it can still be used to add or subtract fractions.

Example: Add 2⁄3 + 3⁄5 + 4⁄7 by finding a common denominator.

A common denominator can be found by multiplying the denominators 3 × 5 × 7 to get 105.

( 2 + 3 + 4 )=( 70 + 63 + 60 )= 193 = 1 88 3 5 7 105 105 105 105 105

2. 中文翻译

要对分数做加法或减法，它们必须有相同的分母。在数学中，最小公分母（LCD）

一般是采用的对象。求 LCD 的一种方法是：列出每个分母的倍数，然后选出它们共有（即都能被它整除）的最小的那个数。

例：用求 LCD 的方法计算 1⁄5 + 1⁄10。

5 的倍数是：5、10、15、20、25 等等。10 的倍数是：10、20、30、40 等等。可以看到，10、20 和 30 在两个数列里都出现，但 10 是最小的，也就是 LCD。求 LCD 的好处在于，最终的答案应当就是最简形式。

对任何一组分数，也可以把所有分母相乘来求一个公分母。这个数不一定就是 LCD，但它仍然可以用来做分数的加法或减法。

例：用求公分母的方法计算 2⁄3 + 3⁄5 + 4⁄7。

把分母 3 × 5 × 7 相乘，得到 105，就求出了公分母。

(2⁄3 + 3⁄5 + 4⁄7) = (70⁄105 + 63⁄105 + 60⁄105) = 193⁄105 = 1 88⁄105

4. 语法解析

① one way to do … is to do …（主语用不定式，表语也用不定式）

原句：One way to find the LCD is to list the multiples of each denominator and then choose the smallest number that they all have in common (can be divided by).

拆解：主语 One way to find the LCD（不定式 to find the LCD 作后置定语修饰 way）；谓语 is；表语是不定式 to list … and then choose …；the smallest number 后接定语从句 that they all have in common，关系代词 that 作 have 的宾语，可省略。

要点：English 里「做某事的方法是做另一事」固定用 One way to do A is to do B，两头都用不定式，中间不能改成动名词。that they all have in common 中 have … in common 是「有共同点」，宾语被提前成关系代词，所以句尾只剩 in common。

② 情态动词被动 + by doing 表方式

原句：A common denominator can also be found for any group of fractions by multiplying all the denominators together.

拆解：主语 A common denominator，谓语 can also be found（情态动词 + be + 过去分词）；for any group of fractions 表示对象；by multiplying all the denominators together 是 by + 动名词短语，表示方式手段。

要点：can be found by doing 译作「可以通过……求得」，是手册讲操作步骤的标准句式。by 后必须接动名词，不能接不定式；multiply … together 是「把……相乘」的固定搭配，together 不能省。

③ The advantage of … is that …（that 引导表语从句）

原句：The advantage of finding the LCD is that the final answer should be in the simplest form.

拆解：主语 The advantage of finding the LCD（of + 动名词作后置定语）；系动词 is；that 引导表语从句 the final answer should be in the simplest form，从句内部 should be 表示「应当会」。

要点：advantage 后固定用 of doing，不用 to do。that 引导表语从句时通常不省，尤其在书面语里；should 在此不表义务而表预期结果，译作「应当就是」。反面表达是 The disadvantage of … is that …。
