Fluid Mechanics

通用 Ch 05 · Fluid Mechanics · 原文 PDF 第 176 页

1. 原文

By definition, a fluid is any substance that is able to flow if it is not in some way confined or restricted. Liquids and gases are both classified as fluids, and often act in a very similar way. One significant difference comes into play when a force is applied to these fluids. In this case, liquids tend to be incompressible and gases are highly compressible.

Many of the principles that aviation is based on, such as the theory of lift on a wing and the force generated by a hydraulic system, can be explained and quantified by using the laws of fluid mechanics.

2. 中文翻译

按定义，流体是任何能够流动的物质，前提是它没有以某种方式被约束或限制住。液体和气体都归入流体，且往往表现出非常相似的性状。当对这些流体施加一个力时，就会显现出一处显著差异：在这种情况下，液体倾向于不可压缩，而气体则高度可压缩。

航空所依托的许多原理，例如机翼升力理论和液压系统产生的力，都可以用流体力学的定律来解释和量化。

4. 语法解析

① a fluid is any substance that is able to flow if …（定义句式 + 从句嵌套）

原句：By definition, a fluid is any substance that is able to flow if it is not in some way confined or restricted.

拆解：By definition 是状语；主句为「主语 a fluid + 系动词 is + 表语 any substance」；that is able to flow 是定语从句修饰 substance，if it is not in some way confined or restricted 这个条件从句又嵌在定语从句内部，it 回指 substance；in some way 是方式状语，confined or restricted 是两个近义过去分词并列。

要点：手册下定义惯用「A is any substance that …」，比 A means … 更正式。注意定语从句里不能用 can flow（情态动词不能单独作从句谓语），必须换成 be able to flow。confined or restricted 用一对近义词并列，是技术文本避免歧义的加固手段，中译「被约束或限制」保留两词即可。

② liquids tend to be incompressible and gases are highly compressible（对照结构与措辞分寸）

原句：In this case, liquids tend to be incompressible and gases are highly compressible.

拆解：In this case 指「对流体施力」这一情形，作状语；两个并列分句以 and 连接：liquids tend to be incompressible 与 gases are highly compressible；tend to 后接动词原形 be，highly 是程度副词修饰 compressible。

要点：两个分句用词刻意不对称——tend to be 留有余地（「倾向于不可压缩」），are highly compressible 是强判断（「高度可压缩」），译时不能把前者写成「液体不可压缩」，那样就把手册的分寸写没了。可迁移：tend to corrode（易腐蚀）、tend to creep（易蠕变）。

③ Many of the principles … can be explained and quantified by using …（分隔主语 + 介词悬空 + 并列被动）

原句：Many of the principles that aviation is based on, such as the theory of lift on a wing and the force generated by a hydraulic system, can be explained and quantified by using the laws of fluid mechanics.

拆解：主语 Many of the principles；that aviation is based on 是定语从句，介词 on 留在从句末尾（介词悬空），先行词是 principles；such as … 是插入的同位语举例，其中 the force generated by a hydraulic system 用过去分词短语作后置定语；谓语 can be explained and quantified 为并列被动；by using the laws of fluid mechanics 是方式状语。

要点：读这个句子要先跳过 that 从句和 such as 插入语，直接看到 can be explained and quantified，否则会被中间十几个词带偏。be based on 的 on 不可省略，写成 that aviation is based 是典型错误；by + 动名词表示手段，与 through / by means of 同义。
