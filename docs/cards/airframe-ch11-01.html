1. 原文

With the mechanics of flight secured, early aviators began the tasks of improving operational safety and functionality of flight. These were developed in large part through the use of reliable communication and navigation systems. Today, with thousands of aircraft aloft at any one time, communication and navigation systems are essential to safe, successful flight. Continuing development is occurring. Smaller, lighter, and more powerful communication and navigation devices increase situational awareness on the flight deck. Coupled with improved displays and management control systems, the advancement of aviation electronics is relied upon to increase aviation safety.

Clear radio voice communication was one of the first developments in the use of electronics in aviation. Navigational radios soon followed. Today, numerous electronic navigation and landing aids exist. Electronic devices also exist to assist with weather, collision avoidance, automatic flight control, flight recording, flight management, public address, and entertainment systems.

Avionics in Aviation Maintenance

Avionics is a conjunction of the words aviation and electronics. It is used to describe the electronic equipment found in modern aircraft. The term “avionics” was not used until the 1970s. For many years, aircraft had electrical devices, but true solid-state electronic devices were only introduced in large numbers in the 1960s.

Airframe and engine maintenance is required on all aircraft and is not likely to ever go away. Aircraft instrument maintenance and repair also has an inevitable part in aviation maintenance. The increased use of avionics in aircraft over the past 50 years has increased the role of avionics maintenance in aviation. However, modern, solid-state, digital avionics are highly reliable. Mean times between failures are high, and maintenance rates of avionics systems compared to mechanical systems are likely to be lower.

The first decade of avionics proliferation saw a greater increase in the percent of cost of avionics compared to the overall cost of an aircraft. In some military aircraft with highly refined navigation, weapons targeting, and monitoring systems, it hit a high estimate of 80 percent of the total cost of the aircraft. Currently, the ratio of the cost of avionics to the cost of the total aircraft is beginning to decline. This is due to advances in digital electronics and numerous manufacturers offering highly refined instrumentation, communication, and navigation systems that can be fitted to nearly any aircraft. New aircraft of all sizes are manufactured with digital glass flight decks, and many owners of older aircraft are retrofitting digital avionics to replace analog instrumentation and radio navigation equipment.

The airframe and powerplant (A&P) maintenance technician needs to be familiar with the general workings of various avionics. Maintenance of the actual avionics devices is often reserved for the avionics manufacturers or certified repair stations. However, the installation and proper operation of these devices and systems remains the responsibility of the field technician. This chapter discusses some internal components used in avionics devices. It also discusses a wide range of common communication and navigational aids found on aircraft. The breadth of avionics is so wide that discussion of all avionics devices is not possible.

History of Avionics

The history of avionics is the history of the use of electronics in aviation. Both military and civil aviation requirements contributed to the development. The First World War brought about an urgent need for communications. Voice communications from ground-to-air and from aircraft-to-aircraft were established. The development of aircraft reliability and use for civilian purposes in the 1920s led to increased instrumentation and set in motion the need to conquer blind flight—flight without the ground being visible. Radio beacon direction finding was developed for en route navigation. Toward the end of the decade, instrument navigation combined with rudimentary radio use to produce the first safe blind landing of an aircraft.

In the 1930s, the first all radio-controlled blind-landing was accomplished. At the same time, radio navigation using ground-based beacons expanded. Instrument navigation certification for airline pilots began. Low and medium frequency radio waves were found to be problematic at night and in weather. By the end of the decade, use of high-frequency radio waves was explored and included the advent of high-frequency radar.

In the 1940s, after two decades of development driven by mail carrier and passenger airline requirements, World War II injected urgency into the development of aircraft radio communication and navigation. Communication radios, despite their size, were essential on board aircraft. Very high frequencies were developed for communication and navigational purposes. Installation of the first instrument landing systems for blind landings began mid-decade and, by the end of the decade, the very high-frequency omni-directional range (VOR) navigational network was instituted. It was also in the 1940s that the first transistor was developed, paving the way for modern, solid-state electronics.

Civilian air transportation increased over the ensuing decades. Communication and navigation equipment was refined. Solid-state radio development, especially in the 1960s, produced a wide range of small, rugged radio and navigational equipment for aircraft. The space program began and added a higher level of communication and navigational necessity. Communication satellites were also launched. The Cold War military build-up caused developments in guidance and navigation and gave birth to the concept of using satellites for positioning.

In the 1970s, concept-validation of satellite navigation was introduced for the military and Block I global positioning system (GPS) satellites were launched well into the 1980s. Back on earth, the long-range navigation system (LORAN) was constructed. Block II GPS satellites were commissioned in the mid-80s and GPS became operational in 1990 with the full 24-satellite system operational in 1994.

In the new millennium, the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) assessed the national airspace system (NAS) and traffic projections for the future. Gridlock is predicted by 2022. Therefore, a complete overhaul of the NAS, including communication and navigational systems, has been developed and undertaken. The program is called NextGen. It uses the latest technologies to provide a more efficient and effective system of air traffic management. Heavily reliant on global satellite positioning of aircraft in flight and on the ground, NextGen combines GPS technology with automatic dependent surveillance broadcast technology (ADS-B) for traffic separation. A large increase in air system capacity is the planned result. Overhauled ground facilities accompany the technology upgrades mandated for aircraft. NextGen implementation has started and is currently scheduled through the year 2025.

For the past few decades, avionics development has increased at a faster pace than that of airframe and powerplant development. This is likely to continue in the near future. Improvements to solid-state electronics in the form of micro- and nano-technologies continue to this day. Trends are toward lighter, smaller devices with remarkable capability and reliability. Integration of the wide range of communication and navigational aids is a focus.

2. 中文翻译

随着飞行机理的确立，早期飞行员开始着手改善飞行的运行安全性与功能。这在很大程度上是通过使用可靠的通信与导航系统实现的。如今，任何时候都有数以千计的飞机在空中，通信与导航系统对于安全、成功的飞行不可或缺。相关研制仍在持续进行。更小、更轻、更强的通信与导航设备提高了驾驶舱内的情景意识。再加上改进后的显示器与管理控制系统，航空电子技术的进步被寄望于进一步提升航空安全。

清晰的无线电语音通信是电子技术在航空中最早的应用之一。导航无线电随后出现。如今已有大量电子导航与着陆辅助设备。此外还有用于气象、防撞、自动飞行控制、飞行记录、飞行管理、旅客广播与娱乐系统的电子装置。

航空维修中的航空电子

航空电子（avionics）是 aviation（航空）与 electronics（电子）两词的缩合，用来描述现代飞机上的电子设备。“avionics”这一术语直到 20 世纪 70 年代才被使用。多年来飞机上一直装有电气设备，但真正的固态电子器件直到 20 世纪 60 年代才大量引入。

所有飞机都需要机体与发动机维修，这种需求大概永远不会消失。飞机仪表的维护与修理在航空维修中也占有不可避免的一席之地。过去 50 年间飞机上航空电子使用量的增加，提升了航空电子维修在航空业的地位。不过，现代固态数字航空电子设备的可靠性很高：平均故障间隔时间长，与机械系统相比，航空电子系统的维修率很可能更低。

在航空电子迅速普及的第一个十年，航空电子成本占飞机总成本的比例出现了较大增长。在一些配备高度精良的导航、武器瞄准与监控系统的军用飞机上，这一比例最高估计达到飞机总成本的 80%。目前，航空电子成本与飞机总成本之比已开始下降。这归因于数字电子技术的进步，以及众多制造商提供几乎可安装于任何飞机的高度精良的仪表、通信与导航系统。各种尺寸的新飞机都按数字玻璃座舱制造，许多老龄飞机的机主也在加装数字航空电子设备，以取代模拟仪表与无线电导航设备。

机体与发动机（A&P）维修技师需要熟悉各种航空电子设备的一般工作原理。航空电子装置本体的维修往往保留给航空电子制造商或持证修理站，但这些装置的安装与正确操作仍然是现场技师的责任。本章讨论航空电子装置中使用的一些内部元件，也讨论飞机上广泛存在的常见通信与导航辅助设备。航空电子的范围如此之广，不可能对所有航空电子装置都加以讨论。

航空电子的历史

航空电子的历史就是电子技术在航空中应用的历史。军用与民用航空的需求都推动了这一发展。第一次世界大战带来了对通信的迫切需求，地空之间与飞机之间的语音通信得以建立。20 世纪 20 年代飞机可靠性的提高及其在民用方面的使用，导致仪表设备增多，并启动了征服盲目飞行——即看不见地面的飞行——的需求。无线电指向标测向技术被开发用于航路导航。到 20 年代后期，仪表导航与初级无线电使用相结合，实现了飞机首次安全的盲目着陆。

20 世纪 30 年代，首次全无线电控制的盲目着陆得以实现。与此同时，使用地面指向标的无线电导航得到扩展。航空公司飞行员的仪表导航认证开始。人们发现低频与中频无线电波在夜间和恶劣天气下存在问题。到 30 年代末，人们探索了高频无线电波的使用，并随之出现了高频雷达。

20 世纪 40 年代，在邮政与客运航空公司需求推动了两十年发展之后，第二次世界大战为飞机无线电通信与导航的发展注入了紧迫性。通信电台尽管体积庞大，却是机上必备。为通信与导航目的开发了甚高频。首批用于盲目着陆的仪表着陆系统于 40 年代中期开始安装；到 40 年代末，甚高频全向信标（VOR）导航网络建立起来。也正是在 20 世纪 40 年代，第一只晶体管问世，为现代固态电子技术铺平了道路。

此后数十年，民用航空运输不断增长，通信与导航设备得到改进。固态无线电技术的发展，尤其是在 20 世纪 60 年代，为飞机生产出种类繁多的小型、坚固的无线电与导航设备。太空计划开始，对通信与导航提出了更高层次的需求，通信卫星也发射升空。冷战时期的军事扩充推动了制导与导航的发展，并催生了利用卫星进行定位的构想。

20 世纪 70 年代，卫星导航的概念验证被引入军方，第一代（Block I）全球定位系统（GPS）卫星一直发射到 80 年代。在地面上，远程导航系统（LORAN）建成。第二代（Block II）GPS 卫星于 80 年代中期投入使用，GPS 于 1990 年投入运行，1994 年由 24 颗卫星组成的完整系统全面运行。

进入新千年，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）评估了国家空域系统（NAS）以及未来的交通量预测。预计到 2022 年将出现交通拥堵。因此，对 NAS（包括通信与导航系统）的全面改造已被制定并着手实施。该计划称为 NextGen。它利用最新技术提供更高效、更有效的空中交通管理体系。NextGen 在很大程度上依赖对飞行中与地面上飞机的全球卫星定位，将 GPS 技术与广播式自动相关监视（ADS-B）技术结合用于交通间隔。计划的结果是空中系统容量大幅增加。在地面设施改造的同时，也对飞机强制实施技术升级。NextGen 的实施已经开始，目前计划持续到 2025 年。

过去几十年，航空电子的发展速度一直快于机体与动力装置的发展。这一趋势在不久的将来很可能延续。以微米与纳米技术为形式的固态电子改进至今仍在继续。趋势是更轻、更小的装置，却具备非凡的能力与可靠性。对各种通信与导航辅助设备的整合是一个重点。

4. 语法解析

① with 复合结构作原因状语

原句：With the mechanics of flight secured, early aviators began the tasks of improving operational safety and functionality of flight. 拆解：句首 with + 名词 the mechanics of flight + 过去分词 secured 构成 with 复合结构，作原因状语；主句主语 early aviators，谓语 began，宾语 the tasks，of improving... 为介词短语作后置定语说明 tasks 的内容。 要点：mechanics of flight 与 secure 是被动关系（飞行机理“被确立”），所以用过去分词 secured 而非 securing。with 复合结构不是完整分句，不能单独成句；译成中文时常处理成“随着……确立”这样的时间/原因状语。

② 过去分词短语作状语 + 被动语态

原句：Coupled with improved displays and management control systems, the advancement of aviation electronics is relied upon to increase aviation safety. 拆解：句首 Coupled with... 为过去分词短语作状语（be coupled with 的变体，意为“与……相结合”）；主句主语 the advancement of aviation electronics，谓语 is relied upon（被动），不定式 to increase aviation safety 作目的状语。 要点：coupled with 的逻辑主语是主句主语 the advancement，二者是被动关系，故用过去分词。rely upon sb./sth. to do 变被动后，不定式仍保留在谓语后面，别误读成主语补足语。

③ 强调句 It was ... that + 分词结果状语

原句：It was also in the 1940s that the first transistor was developed, paving the way for modern, solid-state electronics. 拆解：It was + 被强调的时间状语 in the 1940s + that 引出其余部分（the first transistor was developed 为被动语态）；逗号后 paving the way for... 为现在分词短语作结果状语。 要点：去掉 It was ... that 后剩下 Also in the 1940s the first transistor was developed，句子仍完整，可见是强调句而非形式主语。paving 的逻辑主语是整个前面的主句内容（晶体管问世这件事），不是 transistor。

④ 形容词短语作状语 + combine A with B

原句：Heavily reliant on global satellite positioning of aircraft in flight and on the ground, NextGen combines GPS technology with automatic dependent surveillance broadcast technology (ADS-B) for traffic separation. 拆解：句首 Heavily reliant on... 为形容词短语作状语，说明主语 NextGen 的特点；介词 on 后接两个并列宾语 global satellite positioning of aircraft in flight 与 on the ground（省略重复的 positioning）；主句谓语 combines，宾语 GPS technology，with 引出第二宾语 ADS-B，for traffic separation 为目的状语。 要点：be reliant on 省略 be 后只剩形容词短语，是书面语常见的状语句首结构，逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致。combine A with B 意为“把 A 与 B 结合”，不要译成“结合成”。

⑤ 过去分词后置定语 + 比较结构

原句：Mean times between failures are high, and maintenance rates of avionics systems compared to mechanical systems are likely to be lower. 拆解：并列句。前一分句主语 Mean times between failures，系动词 are，表语 high；后一分句主语 maintenance rates of avionics systems，compared to mechanical systems 为过去分词短语作后置定语（相当于 when they are compared to...），谓语 are likely to be，表语 lower。 要点：compared to 引出的比较对象插在主语和谓语之间，容易把 systems 误当成主语。be likely to be lower 是“很可能是更低的”，不是“可能降低”。

⑥ 主语带长定语 + 现在完成时

原句：The increased use of avionics in aircraft over the past 50 years has increased the role of avionics maintenance in aviation. 拆解：主语中心词 use，前置定语 increased，后置定语 of avionics in aircraft 与 over the past 50 years；谓语 has increased（现在完成时，与 over the past 50 years 呼应）；宾语 the role of avionics maintenance，in aviation 为范围状语。 要点：increased 在主语里是形容词“增加后的”，在谓语里是动词“增加了”，同一个词前后身兼两职，读时先切出主语边界再找谓语。over the past 50 years 是现在完成时的标志性状语。
