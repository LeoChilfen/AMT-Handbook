Electric Ground Power Units

1. 原文

Ground support electrical APUs vary widely in size and type. However, they can be generally classified by towed, stationary, or self-propelled items of equipment. Some units are mainly for in-hangar use during maintenance. Others are designed for use on the flight line, either at a stationary gate area or towed from aircraft to aircraft. The stationary type can be powered from the electrical service of the facility.

The movable type ground power unit (GPU) generally has an onboard engine that turns a generator to produce power.

Some smaller units use a series of batteries. The towed power units vary in size and range of available power.

The smallest units are simply high-capacity batteries used to start light aircraft. These units are normally mounted on wheels or skids and are equipped with an extra-long electrical line terminated in a suitable plug-in adapter.

Larger units are equipped with generators. Providing a wider range of output power, these power units are normally designed to supply constant-current, variable voltage DC electrical power for starting turbine aircraft engines and constant-voltage DC for starting reciprocating aircraft engines. Normally somewhat top-heavy, large towed power units are towed at restricted speeds, and sharp turns are avoided. An example of a large power unit is shown in Self-propelled power units are normally more expensive than the towed units and, in most instances, supply a wider range of output voltages and frequencies. The stationary power unit, shown in Figure 1-28, is capable of supplying DC power in varying amounts, as well as 115/200-volt, 3-phase, 400-cycle AC power continuously for 5 minutes.

When using ground electrical power units, it is important to position the unit to prevent collision with the aircraft being serviced, or others nearby, in the event the brakes on the unit fail. It must be parked so that the service cable is extended to near its full length away from the aircraft being serviced, but not so far that the cable is stretched or undue stress is placed on the aircraft electrical receptacle.

Observe all electrical safety precautions when servicing an aircraft. Additionally, never move a power unit when service cables are attached to an aircraft or when the generator system is engaged.

2. 中文翻译

地面保障用电动辅助电源设备在尺寸和类型上差别很大。不过，通常可按拖行式、固定式或自行式设备来分类。有些机组主要用于维修期间的机库内作业。另一些则设计为外场使用，或在固定机位使用，或从一架航空器拖到另一架航空器。固定式机组可由设施的供电系统供电。

可移动式地面电源车（GPU）一般自带发动机，由发动机驱动发电机发电。

有些较小的机组使用一组蓄电池。拖行式电源机组在尺寸和可提供的功率范围上各不相同。

最小的机组其实就是用于起动轻型航空器的大容量蓄电池。这类机组通常装在轮子或滑撬上，配有一根超长电缆，电缆末端为合适的插接式转接头。

较大的机组配有发电机。这些电源机组能提供更宽的输出功率范围，通常设计用于为起动涡轮航空器发动机提供恒流、变压的直流电，为起动活塞航空器发动机提供恒压直流电。大型拖行式电源机组通常有些头重脚轻，拖行时须限速，并避免急转弯。大型电源机组的实例见图（原文此处图号残缺）。自行式电源机组通常比拖行式机组贵，而且在多数情况下可提供更宽的输出电压和频率范围。图 1-28 所示的固定式电源机组既能输出不同大小的直流电，也能连续 5 分钟提供 115/200 伏、三相、400 赫兹的交流电。

使用地面电源机组时，重要的是选好机组的停放位置，以使机组刹车万一失效时不会撞上正在被勤务的航空器或附近的其他航空器。停放时必须把供电电缆从被勤务航空器处拉出到接近全长，但又不能远到把电缆绷紧，或给航空器电源插座施加过大的应力。

对航空器进行勤务时，要遵守所有用电安全注意事项。此外，当供电电缆连接在航空器上，或发电机系统已接合时，绝不可移动电源机组。

4. 语法解析

① when + 动名词 + it 形式主语

原句：When using ground electrical power units, it is important to position the unit to prevent collision with the aircraft being serviced, or others nearby, in the event the brakes on the unit fail.

拆解：When using… 是时间状语从句的省略形式（when you are using…）；it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to position the unit…；being serviced 是现在分词被动作后置定语，修饰 the aircraft；in the event (that) 引导条件状语从句。

要点：it is important to do 是高频句型，译作「重要的是……」。in the event (that) 意为「万一、如果发生」，比 if 更书面。being serviced 后置时表示「正在被勤务的」，不要译成「勤务了」。

② so that 结果状语 + not so…that…

原句：It must be parked so that the service cable is extended to near its full length away from the aircraft being serviced, but not so far that the cable is stretched or undue stress is placed on the aircraft electrical receptacle.

拆解：so that 引导结果状语从句；but not so far that… 是 so…that 的否定形式，that 从句说明「远到何种程度就会出问题」；or 连接两个并列的被动分句 the cable is stretched 与 undue stress is placed on…。

要点：not so…that… 意为「不至于……以致……」，翻译时要体现「防止」而不是「结果」。全句核心是「近到够长、远到不绷紧」的平衡，两个方向都要译出。

③ 分词 providing 作状语 + be designed to + for 动名词

原句：Providing a wider range of output power, these power units are normally designed to supply constant-current, variable voltage DC electrical power for starting turbine aircraft engines and constant-voltage DC for starting reciprocating aircraft engines.

拆解：Providing… 是现在分词短语作状语，逻辑主语为 these power units；are designed to supply 后接两个并列宾语（…DC electrical power 与 …DC），二者各由 for + 动名词短语修饰。

要点：for + 动名词表用途（for starting = 用于起动），比 to start 更强调「用途」。constant-current, variable voltage 与 constant-voltage 两组复合定语须按原序译准，不可互换。

④ never + 祈使句 + 两个并列 when 从句

原句：Additionally, never move a power unit when service cables are attached to an aircraft or when the generator system is engaged.

拆解：never + 祈使句表强禁止；两个 when 时间状语从句由 or 并列；两个从句都是 be + 过去分词被动语态。

要点：never + 祈使句是维修手册最常见的禁令写法，译「绝不可……」。are attached / is engaged 是被动语态，中文说「电缆接着」「系统接合着」更自然。
