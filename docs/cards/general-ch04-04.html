Care & Use of Drawings

通用 Ch 04 · Care & Use of Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 126 页

1. 原文

Drawings should be handled carefully as they are both expensive and valuable. Open drawings slowly and carefully to prevent tearing of the paper. When the drawing is open, smooth out the fold lines instead of bending them backward.

To protect drawings from damage, never spread them on the floor or lay them on a surface covered with tools or other objects that may make holes in the paper. Hands should be free of oil, grease, or other unclean matter that can soil or smudge the print.

Never make notes or marks on a print, as they may confuse others and lead to incorrect work. Only authorized individuals are permitted to make notes or changes on prints, and they must sign and date any changes they make.

When finished with a drawing, fold and return it to its proper place. Prints are folded originally in an appropriate size for filing. Care should be taken so that the original folds are always used.

2. 中文翻译

图纸应小心取放，因为它们既昂贵又贵重。打开图纸时应缓慢、小心，以免撕破纸张。图纸打开后，应把折痕抚平，而不是朝反方向弯折。

为防止图纸受损，绝不要把图纸铺在地上，也不要放在堆有工具或其他可能把纸扎出洞的物品的表面上。手应洁净，不带油、油脂或其他可能弄脏或污损复制图的脏物。

绝不要在复制图上写笔记或做标记，因为这会让他人困惑并导致错误的作业。只有经授权的人员才可以在复制图上写笔记或作更改，而且他们必须在自己所作的任何更改上签名并注明日期。

图纸用完后，应折叠好并放回它应在的位置。复制图原本就是按适合归档的尺寸折好的。应注意始终沿用原来的折痕。

4. 语法解析

① should be + 过去分词 + as 原因状语从句

原句：Drawings should be handled carefully as they are both expensive and valuable. 拆解：主句为情态被动 should be handled，carefully 为方式副词；as 引导原因状语从句，从句为系表结构 are both expensive and valuable（both…and… 连接两个并列表语）。 要点：should 在此表规章性建议而非“某人的义务”；as 表原因时语气比 because 弱且常后置；both…and… 不能改写成 both…as well as。

② 祈使句 + 不定式目的状语 + prevent + 动名词

原句：Open drawings slowly and carefully to prevent tearing of the paper. 拆解：Open drawings 为祈使句（隐含主语 you），slowly and carefully 为方式状语；to prevent tearing of the paper 为不定式目的状语；prevent 后接动名词 tearing。 要点：prevent 后接动名词或用 prevent sb from doing，不能接不定式；说明操作目的时手册几乎都用“祈使句 + to do”结构。

③ instead of + 动名词表示取舍

原句：When the drawing is open, smooth out the fold lines instead of bending them backward. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句（open 为形容词作表语）；主句为祈使句 smooth out the fold lines；instead of bending them backward 为介词短语作状语，bending 为动名词。 要点：instead of 后须接名词或动名词，不能接句子或不定式；只有 instead 单独使用时才是副词。译文里“而不是”要放在动作之后。

④ be free of + that 定语从句

原句：Hands should be free of oil, grease, or other unclean matter that can soil or smudge the print. 拆解：主语 Hands；系表 should be free of…；that 引导定语从句修饰 oil, grease, or other unclean matter（并列三项整体作先行词），从句谓语为 can soil or smudge。 要点：be free of/from 表示“没有、不含”，是技术文本的常用搭配；定语从句修饰并列名词时通常指整体，故从句谓语用复数形式 can。

⑤ be permitted to do + 并列分句 + 省略关系代词

原句：Only authorized individuals are permitted to make notes or changes on prints, and they must sign and date any changes they make. 拆解：前一分句为被动 are permitted to make…（不定式作主语补足语），Only 修饰主语；and 引出并列分句，they 复指 authorized individuals；any changes they make 中省略了关系代词 that/which。 要点：permit 的被动结构是 sb be permitted to do，不能写成 be permitted doing；定语从句省略关系代词时，从句必须有自己的主语（they）。

⑥ 被动语态的时态与 originally 的位置

原句：Prints are folded originally in an appropriate size for filing. 拆解：主语 Prints；谓语为一般现在时被动 are folded；originally 为副词作状语，位于 be 与过去分词之间；in an appropriate size for filing 为方式或结果状语。 要点：副词插在 be 与过去分词之间是英语的常见语序（are carefully noted 同理）；一般现在时被动在此表习惯性做法，不指某一次折叠。
