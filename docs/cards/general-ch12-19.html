Alternating Current (AC) & Voltage

通用 Ch 12 · Alternating Current (AC) & Voltage · 原文 PDF 第 485 页

1. 原文

Alternating current (AC) has largely replaced direct current (DC) in commercial power systems for a number of reasons. It can be transmitted over long distances more readily and more economically than DC, since AC voltages can be increased or decreased by means of transformers.

Because more and more units are being operated electrically in airplanes, the power requirements are such that a number of advantages can be realized by using AC. Space and weight can be saved since AC devices, especially motors, are smaller and simpler than DC devices. In most AC motors, no brushes are required, and commutation trouble at high altitude is eliminated. Circuit breakers operate satisfactorily under load at high altitudes in an AC system, whereas arcing is so excessive on DC systems that circuit breakers must be replaced frequently. Finally, most airplanes using a 24-volt DC system have special equipment that requires a certain amount of 400-cycle AC current.

AC and DC Compared

Many of the principles, characteristics, and effects of AC are similar to those of DC. Similarly, there are a number of differences. DC flows constantly in only one direction with a constant polarity. It changes magnitude only when the circuit is opened or closed, as shown in the DC waveform. AC changes direction at regular intervals, increases in value at a definite rate from zero to a maximum positive strength, and decreases back to zero; then it flows in the opposite direction, similarly increasing to a maximum negative value, and again decreasing to zero. DC and AC waveforms are compared in the figure.

Since AC constantly changes direction and intensity, the following two effects (to be discussed later) take place in AC circuits that do not occur in DC circuits:

  1. Inductive reactance
  1. Capacitive reactance

2. 中文翻译

交流电（AC）在商业电力系统中已在很大程度上取代了直流电（DC），原因有多方面。交流电比直流电更容易、更经济地实现远距离输送，因为交流电压可以借助变压器升高或降低。

由于飞机上以电力驱动的设备越来越多，用电需求使得采用交流电可以获得若干好处。交流装置（尤其是电动机）比直流装置更小、更简单，因而可以节省空间和重量。大多数交流电动机不需要电刷，高空换向故障也随之消除。在交流系统中，断路器在高空带载分断仍能正常工作；而在直流系统中电弧过大，断路器必须频繁更换。最后，大多数采用 24 伏直流系统的飞机都装有专门设备，需要一定量的 400 周波交流电。

交流电与直流电的比较

交流电的许多原理、特性和效应与直流电相似。同样，两者也存在许多差异。直流电始终只沿一个方向流动，极性恒定。它只在电路接通或断开时才改变大小，如直流波形所示。交流电则按固定间隔改变方向，其值以确定的速率从零增至正向最大值，再降回零；随后它沿相反方向流动，同样增至负向最大值，然后再次降回零。图中对直流波形与交流波形作了比较。

由于交流电不断改变方向和大小，交流电路中会出现以下两种（后文将讨论的）效应，而直流电路中不会出现：

  1. 感抗
  1. 容抗

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词的被动语态 + 并列分句

原句：In most AC motors, no brushes are required, and commutation trouble at high altitude is eliminated. 拆解：In most AC motors 为地点状语；and 连接两个被动分句：no brushes are required（否定主语 + be + 过去分词）、commutation trouble at high altitude is eliminated（主语 commutation trouble，at high altitude 为后置修饰，谓语 is eliminated）。 要点：技术说明文用被动语态，是因为动作执行者（设计者、维护者）不重要或无从指出；no + 复数名词作主语时谓语用复数 are，不可写成 is required。译成汉语时通常补出「不需要」「被消除」，不能照搬「被……」。

② 情态动词被动 + 双比较级 + since 原因从句

原句：It can be transmitted over long distances more readily and more economically than DC, since AC voltages can be increased or decreased by means of transformers. 拆解：主句 It can be transmitted（情态动词 + 被动语态），over long distances 为距离状语，more readily and more economically 为两个比较级副词并列，than DC 引出比较对象；since 引导原因状语从句，从句中 AC voltages 作主语，can be increased or decreased 为并列的情态被动，by means of transformers 为方式状语。 要点：多音节副词用 more 构成比较级，两个比较级用 and 并列后共用一个 than；since 在此表「因为」而不是「自从」，判断依据是后面跟完整的原因分句；by means of 是「借助、通过」的固定搭配，技术文本中出现频率极高。

③ 一个主语带多个并列谓语 + 分号衔接 + 分词状语

原句：AC changes direction at regular intervals, increases in value at a definite rate from zero to a maximum positive strength, and decreases back to zero; then it flows in the opposite direction, similarly increasing to a maximum negative value, and again decreasing to zero. 拆解：分号前主语 AC 带三个并列谓语 changes、increases、decreases；分号后另起一分句 it flows，后面接两个现在分词短语 similarly increasing…、again decreasing… 作伴随说明，其逻辑主语都是 it（即 AC）。 要点：多个谓语共用一个主语时，只在最后一项前加 and，前面用逗号分隔；分号连接两个关系紧密的独立分句，正好对应「正半周—负半周」的对照，比句号更紧凑。分词短语前有逗号表示补充说明，不要误读成并列谓语。

④ 分隔定语从句 + 不定式被动作后置定语

原句：Since AC constantly changes direction and intensity, the following two effects (to be discussed later) take place in AC circuits that do not occur in DC circuits: 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（主语 AC，谓语 changes，宾语 direction and intensity，constantly 为状语）；主句主语 the following two effects，括号内 to be discussed later 为不定式被动作后置定语，谓语 take place，in AC circuits 为地点状语；that do not occur in DC circuits 是定语从句，先行词是 effects，被 in AC circuits 隔开。 要点：定语从句与先行词被介词短语隔开，称为分隔定语从句，理解时必须回指 effects 而不是紧邻的 circuits；take place 是不及物动词短语，没有被动形式，不能写 be taken place；冒号在此引出下文的列举，翻译时保留冒号即可。

⑤ whereas 对比从句 + so…that 结果从句

原句：Circuit breakers operate satisfactorily under load at high altitudes in an AC system, whereas arcing is so excessive on DC systems that circuit breakers must be replaced frequently. 拆解：前一分句主语 Circuit breakers，谓语 operate，satisfactorily 为状语，under load / at high altitudes / in an AC system 三个介词短语依次修饰；whereas 引导对比状语从句，从句中 arcing 作主语，is 为系动词，so excessive 为表语，that 引导结果状语从句，从句谓语 must be replaced 为情态被动。 要点：whereas 表对比，相当于汉语的「而」，比 but 更正式，技术文本常用于并列两种系统的优劣；so + 形容词 + that 引导结果从句，so 后面只能跟形容词或副词，不能跟名词（跟名词要用 such）；两个分句里的 circuit breakers 指同一类器件，翻译时第二处可省略不重复。
