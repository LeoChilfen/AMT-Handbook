Revision Block

通用 Ch 04 · Revision Block · 原文 PDF 第 132 页

1. 原文

Revisions to a drawing are necessitated by changes in dimensions, design, or materials. The changes are usually listed in ruled columns either adjacent to the title block or at one corner of the drawing. All changes to approved drawings must be carefully noted on all existing prints of the drawing.

When drawings contain such corrections, attention is directed to the changes by lettering or numbering them and listing those changes against the symbol in a revision block. [Figure 4-4D]

The revision block contains the identification symbol, the date, the nature of the revision, the authority for the change, and the name of the draftsman who made the change.

To distinguish the corrected drawing from its previous version, many firms are including, as part of the title block, a space for entering the appropriate symbol to designate that the drawing has been changed or revised.

2. 中文翻译

图纸的修订由尺寸、设计或材料的变更引起。这些变更通常列在标题栏旁或图纸一角的分栏表内。已批准图纸的所有更改，都必须在该图纸的所有现存印件上仔细注明。

当图纸含有此类更正时，通过对更改加注字母或数字编号、并在修订栏中把这些更改与相应符号对应列出，以提醒注意这些更改。（图 4-4D）

修订栏包含识别符号、日期、修订性质、更改依据，以及做出该更改的制图员姓名。

为了把已更正的图纸与其前一版本区分开，许多公司在标题栏内留出一处空格，用于填入相应符号，以表明该图纸已经更改或修订。

4. 语法解析

① Revisions to a drawing are necessitated by changes in dimensions, design, or materials（被动 + by 引出原因）

原句：Revisions to a drawing are necessitated by changes in dimensions, design, or materials.

拆解：主语 Revisions to a drawing（to 引出修订所针对的对象）；谓语 are necessitated 为被动；by changes … 引出原因；in dimensions, design, or materials 是 changes 的后置定语。

要点：necessitate 是及物动词「使成为必要」，常用被动 be necessitated by，译「由……引起／因……而必要」，比 need 正式得多。revision to a drawing 用 to 而不用 of，表示「针对图纸所作的修订」。

② All changes to approved drawings must be carefully noted on all existing prints（情态被动 + 副词位置）

原句：All changes to approved drawings must be carefully noted on all existing prints of the drawing.

拆解：主语 All changes to approved drawings；谓语 must be noted 为「情态动词 + 被动」；carefully 是方式副词，插在 be 与过去分词之间；on all existing prints 是地点状语。

要点：副词在被动语态中的标准位置是 be 之后、过去分词之前（must be carefully noted），放句首或句末都会改变强调。note 作动词意为「注明、记下」，此处对象是印件。两个 all 分别限定 changes 与 prints，译成中文时两个「所有」都要保留。

③ To distinguish the corrected drawing from its previous version …（目的不定式 + distinguish A from B）

原句：To distinguish the corrected drawing from its previous version, many firms are including, as part of the title block, a space for entering the appropriate symbol to designate that the drawing has been changed or revised.

拆解：句首 To distinguish … 是目的状语；主句 many firms are including a space（现在进行时表当前趋势）；as part of the title block 是插入语；for entering the appropriate symbol 是后置定语修饰 space；to designate that … 是目的不定式，that 从句作 designate 的宾语。

要点：distinguish A from B 是「把 A 与 B 区分开」，不能说 distinguish A and B。句首目的不定式与主句之间必须用逗号隔开，且其逻辑主语应与主句主语一致（此处是 firms）。as part of 作插入语位置灵活，翻译时常提前到句首。
