Division of Decimal Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Division of Decimal Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 102 页

1. 原文

Division of decimal numbers is performed the same way as whole numbers, unless the divisor is a decimal.

quotient divisor dividend When the divisor is a decimal, it must be changed to a whole number before dividing. To do this, move the decimal in the divisor to the right until there are no decimal places. At the same time, move the decimal point in the dividend to the right the same number of places. Then divide. The decimal in the quotient is placed directly above the decimal in the dividend.

Example: Divide 0.144 by 0.12 1.2 0.12 0.144 = 12. 14.4 – 12 24 – 24 0 Move the decimal in the divisor (0.12) two places to the right.

The result is 12.0. Next, move the decimal in the dividend (0.144) two places to the right. The result is 14.4. Now divide.

Example: The wing area of an airplane is 262.6 square feet and its span is 40.4 feet. Find the mean chord of its wing using the formula: area ÷ span = mean chord.

6.5 40.4 262.6 = 404. 2626.0 – 2424 2020 – 2020 0 Move the decimal in the divisor (40.4) one place to the right. Next, move the decimal in the dividend (262.6) one place to the right. Then divide. The mean chord length is 6.5 feet.

2. 中文翻译

小数除法与整数除法的做法相同，除非除数是小数。

商（quotient）、除数（divisor）、被除数（dividend）——当除数是小数时，必须先把它化为整数再相除。做法是把除数的小数点向右移，直到没有小数位；同时把被除数的小数点也向右移相同的位数；然后相除。商的小数点直接点在被除数小数点的正上方。

例：用 0.12 除 0.144 1.2 0.12 0.144 = 12. 14.4 – 12 24 – 24 0 把除数（0.12）的小数点向右移两位。

结果为 12.0。接着把被除数（0.144）的小数点向右移两位，结果为 14.4。现在相除。

例：某机翼翼面积为 262.6 平方英尺，翼展为 40.4 英尺。用公式 面积 ÷ 翼展 = 平均弦长，求该机翼的平均弦长。

6.5 40.4 262.6 = 404. 2626.0 – 2424 2020 – 2020 0 把除数（40.4）的小数点向右移一位。接着把被除数（262.6）的小数点向右移一位，然后相除。平均弦长为 6.5 英尺。

4. 语法解析

① … is performed the same way as whole numbers, unless …（被动 + unless 真实条件句）

原句：Division of decimal numbers is performed the same way as whole numbers, unless the divisor is a decimal.

拆解：主语 Division of decimal numbers（名词化）；谓语 is performed 为被动；the same way as whole numbers 是方式状语；unless 引导否定条件状语从句（= if the divisor is not a decimal）。

要点：unless 等于 if not，本身已含否定，从句里不能再加 not。the same way as 后接名词或从句，与 the same ... that 区别在于 as 侧重“照同样的方式”。is performed 用被动是因为动作执行者（读者）不言自明，全文都在描述运算步骤。

② When the divisor is a decimal, it must be changed to a whole number（when 从句 + must be + before doing）

原句：When the divisor is a decimal, it must be changed to a whole number before dividing.

拆解：When 引导时间／条件状语从句；主句 it must be changed to a whole number 为情态被动，it 回指 the divisor；before dividing 是“before + 动名词”，省略与主句相同的主语。

要点：change A to/into B 是“把 A 变成 B”，介词用 to 或 into，不能用 change A as B。before + 动名词 前的主语若与主句主语一致才可省略，否则要写 before it is divided。must be changed 强调“必须被改变”，是本段规则的语气标志。

③ move … to the right until there are no decimal places（until 从句 + there be）

原句：To do this, move the decimal in the divisor to the right until there are no decimal places.

拆解：To do this 为目的状语，回指上句；主句为祈使句 move ... to the right；until 引导时间状语从句，从句是 there be 结构，表示“直到不存在小数位为止”。

要点：until 从句用一般现在时表将来意义的“直到……为止”，不用 will。there are no decimal places 是 there be 的否定形式，no 比 not any 更书面。the decimal 是 the decimal point 的省略说法，本段多次出现，读时按“小数点”理解。
