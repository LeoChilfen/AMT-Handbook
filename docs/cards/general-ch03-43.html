1. 原文

The Functions of Numbers chart found in Figure 3-10 is included in this chapter for convenience in making computations. Each column in the chart is listed below, with new concepts explained.

• Square root of N ( N)

• Cube root of N (3 N)

• Circumference of a circle with diameter = N.

Circumference is the linear measurement of the distance around a circle. The circumference is calculated by multiplying the diameter of the circle by 3.1416 (3.1416 is the number referred to as pi, which has the symbol π). If the diameter of a circle is 10 inches, then the circumference would be:

10 × 3.1416 = 31.4160.

• Area of a circle with diameter = N. Area of a circle is the number of square units of measurement contained in the circle with a diameter of N. The area of a circle equals π multiplied by the radius squared. This is calculated by the formula: A = π × r2. Remember that the radius is equal to one-half of the diameter.

Example: A flight deck instrument gauge has a round face that is 3 inches in diameter. What is the area of the face of the gauge? From Figure 3-10 for N = 3, the answer is 7.0686 square inches. This is calculated by:

If the diameter of the gauge is 3 inches, then the radius = D⁄2 = 3⁄2 = 1.5 inches.

Area = π × r2 = 3.1416 × 1.5 2 = 3.1416 × 2.25 = 7.0686 square inches.

2. 中文翻译

本章收录了图 3-10 中的「数字函数表」（Functions of Numbers chart），以便于进行计算。该表的每一列都在下面逐一列出，并对其中的新概念加以说明。

• N 的平方根（ N）

• N 的立方根（3 N）

• 直径为 N 的圆的周长。

周长是对圆一周长度的线性度量。周长的计算方法是：把圆的直径乘以 3.1416（3.1416 就是被称为 pi 的数，其符号为 π）。若圆的直径为 10 英寸，则周长为：

10 × 3.1416 = 31.4160。

• 直径为 N 的圆的面积。圆的面积是直径为 N 的圆内所包含的平方单位数。圆的面积等于 π 乘以半径的平方。其计算公式为：A = π × r2。注意，半径等于直径的一半。

例：某飞行甲板仪表有一个直径 3 英寸的圆形表盘。该表盘的面积是多少？查图 3-10，当 N = 3 时，答案为 7.0686 平方英寸。计算过程如下：

若仪表表盘的直径为 3 英寸，则半径 = D⁄2 = 3⁄2 = 1.5 英寸。

面积 = π × r2 = 3.1416 × 1.5 2 = 3.1416 × 2.25 = 7.0686 平方英寸。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：The Functions of Numbers chart found in Figure 3-10 is included in this chapter for convenience in making computations.

拆解：主语 The Functions of Numbers chart；found in Figure 3-10 为过去分词短语作后置定语（= which is found …）；谓语 is included 为被动；for convenience in making computations 是介词短语作目的状语，其中 in + 动名词。

要点：中心词 chart 是单数，谓语必须用 is included，不要被就近的 Figure 3-10 带偏。for convenience in doing sth 是手册常用套语，译「以便于……」，in 后接动名词。

② with 复合结构（with + 宾语 + 过去分词）

原句：Each column in the chart is listed below, with new concepts explained.

拆解：主句 Each column … is listed below 为被动；with new concepts explained 是 with 复合结构：new concepts 为 with 的宾语，explained 为过去分词作宾语补足语，整个结构作伴随状语。

要点：with + 宾语 + 过去分词表示「（某物）被……的状态」，过去分词含被动义；若宾语是动作发出者则用现在分词（with the engine running）。此处译作「并对其中的新概念加以说明」。

③ by + 动名词表方法；multiply A by B

原句：The circumference is calculated by multiplying the diameter of the circle by 3.1416 (3.1416 is the number referred to as pi, which has the symbol π).

拆解：主语 The circumference；is calculated 为被动谓语；by multiplying … 是「介词 by + 动名词」表方法；multiplying 的宾语是 the diameter of the circle，by 3.1416 属于 multiply 的固定搭配；括号内 referred to as pi 是过去分词短语作定语，which 引导非限制性定语从句。

要点：同一句里两个 by 作用不同：第一个 by 引出方法（通过……来算），第二个 by 是 multiply A by B 的固定介词（把 A 乘以 B）。非限制性定语从句用 which，不能用 that 替换；referred to as 意为「被称为」。
