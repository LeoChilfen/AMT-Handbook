Engine Starting and Operation

1. 原文

The following instructions cover the starting procedures for reciprocating, turboprop, turbofan, and APU. These procedures are presented only as a general guide for familiarization with typical procedures and methods. Detailed instructions for starting a specific type of engine can be found in the manufacturer’s instruction book.

Before starting an aircraft engine:

  1. Position the aircraft to head into the prevailing wind to ensure adequate airflow over the engine for cooling purposes.
  1. Make sure that no property damage or personal injury occurs from the propeller blast or jet exhaust.
  1. If external electrical power is used for starting, ensure that it can be removed safely, and it is sufficient for the total starting sequence. 4. During any and all starting procedures, a “fireguard” equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher shall be stationed in an appropriate place. A fireguard is someone familiar with aircraft starting procedures. The fire extinguisher should be a CO 2 extinguisher of at least 5-pound capacity. The appropriate place is adjacent to the outboard side of the engine, in view of the pilot, and also where they can observe the engine/ aircraft for indication of starting problems.
  1. If the aircraft is turbine-engine powered, the area in front of the jet inlet must be kept clear of personnel, property, and/or debris (FOD).
  1. These “before starting” procedures apply to all aircraft powerplants.
  1. Follow manufacturer’s checklists for start procedures and shutdown procedures.

2. 中文翻译

以下说明涵盖活塞式、涡轮螺旋桨、涡轮风扇发动机及 APU 的起动程序。这些程序只作为熟悉典型程序与方法的通用指南。具体型别发动机的起动详细说明见制造商的使用说明书。

起动航空发动机之前：

  1. 使飞机机头迎向主导风，以确保发动机表面有足够气流用于冷却。
  1. 确认螺旋桨尾流或喷气尾流不会造成财产损失或人员伤害。
  1. 如使用外部电源起动，应确认该电源能被安全断开，且足以完成整个起动过程。4. 在任何起动过程中，都必须有一名配备合适灭火器的“消防警戒员”驻守适当位置。消防警戒员是熟悉飞机起动程序的人员。灭火器应为容量至少 5 磅的 CO₂ 灭火器。适当位置指发动机外侧、在飞行员视野之内，且能观察发动机/飞机以发现起动异常迹象的地方。
  1. 如飞机装涡轮发动机，进气口前方的区域必须保持无人员、无物品和/或无外来物（FOD）。
  1. 这些“起动前”程序适用于所有飞机动力装置。
  1. 起动程序和停车程序应遵循制造商的检查单。

4. 语法解析

① present…as… 与 only 的限定

原句：These procedures are presented only as a general guide for familiarization with typical procedures and methods. 拆解：主语 These procedures + 被动谓语 are presented + only as a general guide（方式状语）+ for familiarization with typical procedures and methods（目的状语）。 要点：present sth as sth = 把…作为…给出；only 限定“仅作为指南”，是免责语气，翻译不能漏。

② for + 动名词作后置定语

原句：Detailed instructions for starting a specific type of engine can be found in the manufacturer’s instruction book. 拆解：主语 Detailed instructions + for starting a specific type of engine（介词 + 动名词作后置定语）+ 情态被动 can be found + 地点状语 in the manufacturer’s instruction book。 要点：“for + 动名词”作后置定语说明用途；can be found in 是手册“见某处”的固定句式，比 you can find 更客观。

③ shall 表强制规定与过去分词后置定语

原句：During any and all starting procedures, a “fireguard” equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher shall be stationed in an appropriate place. 拆解：句首 During any and all starting procedures 为时间状语；主语 a “fireguard” + 过去分词短语 equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher 作后置定语；谓语 shall be stationed（shall 表强制规定 + 被动）；地点状语 in an appropriate place。 要点：shall 在技术规范中表“必须”，强制性高于 should；equipped with… 后置定语等于 who is equipped with…；any and all 强调“任何一次都不例外”。

④ keep + 宾语 + clear of

原句：If the aircraft is turbine-engine powered, the area in front of the jet inlet must be kept clear of personnel, property, and/or debris (FOD). 拆解：If the aircraft is turbine-engine powered 为条件从句（被动）；主句 the area + in front of the jet inlet 后置定语 + must be kept（被动）+ clear of personnel, property, and/or debris 形容词短语作主语补足语。 要点：keep + 宾语 + clear of… 意为“使…保持无…”；clear 不能换成 clean（clean 指清洁，clear 指“没有…”）；and/or 表示“和/或”。
