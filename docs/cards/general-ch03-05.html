1. 原文

A fraction is a number written in the form N⁄D where N is called the numerator and D is called the denominator. The fraction bar between the numerator and denominator shows that division is taking place.

17 , 2 , 5Some examples of fractions are: 18 3 8 The denominator of a fraction cannot be a zero. For example, the fraction 2⁄0 is not allowed, because dividing by zero is undefined.

An improper fraction is a fraction in which the numerator is equal to or larger than the denominator. For example, 4⁄4 or 15⁄8 are examples of improper fractions.

2. 中文翻译

分数是写成 N⁄D 形式的数，其中 N 称为分子，D 称为分母。分子与分母之间的分数线表示所进行的是除法。

分数的例子有 17⁄18、2⁄3、5⁄8。分数的分母不能为零。例如，2⁄0 这个分数是不允许的，因为除以零是没有定义的。

假分数是分子等于或大于分母的分数。例如，4⁄4 或 15⁄8 都是假分数。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作后置定语 + where 定语从句

原句：A fraction is a number written in the form N⁄D where N is called the numerator and D is called the denominator.

拆解：主语 A fraction，系动词 is，表语 a number；written in the form N⁄D 是过去分词短语作后置定语（= which is written …）；where 引导定语从句修饰 the form N⁄D，从句内是两个并列的被动结构 is called the numerator 和 is called the denominator。

要点：written 在此是被动含义「被写成」，不能换成 writing。一个句子里「过去分词定语 + where 从句」连着出现是定义类文本的典型写法，翻译时按中文习惯拆成两小句：「……的数，其中 N 称为……」。

② that 引导的宾语从句 + 现在进行时表「正在发生」

原句：The fraction bar between the numerator and denominator shows that division is taking place.

拆解：主语 The fraction bar，后接 between … and … 作后置定语；谓语 shows；that 引导宾语从句 division is taking place，从句用现在进行时。

要点：show that … 是「表明、说明」，that 在宾语从句中不作成分但不可省。is taking place 用进行时，强调分数线一画出来，除法这个动作就「正在进行」，而不是「将要发生」；take place 是不及物动词短语，没有被动式。

③ in which 引导的定语从句 + 并列比较结构

原句：An improper fraction is a fraction in which the numerator is equal to or larger than the denominator.

拆解：表语 a fraction 后接 in which 引导的定语从句（in which = where）；从句主语 the numerator，系动词 is，表语 equal to or larger than the denominator——equal to 与 larger than 两个比较结构用 or 并列，共用 than 之后的 the denominator。

要点：in which 比 where 更正式，指代前面表示抽象概念的先行词（a fraction）。equal to or larger than 要读成「等于或大于」两个条件之一，不可理解成「等于那个更大的」。同类：less than or equal to（小于或等于）。
