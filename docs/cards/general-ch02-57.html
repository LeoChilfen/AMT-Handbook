Airworthiness Certificates

通用 Ch 02 · Airworthiness Certificates · 原文 PDF 第 83 页

1. 原文

In addition to the registration certificate that indicates the ownership of an aircraft, an airworthiness certificate indicates the airworthiness of the aircraft. AC 21-12, Application for U.S. Airworthiness Certificate, FAA Form 8130-6, is a comprehensive guide for the completion of the application form for this certificate. There are two certificates: standard • Recommended overhaul periods • Details for an inspection program that identifies both the frequency and the extent of the inspections necessary to provide for continued airworthiness • Troubleshooting information • The order and method for proper removal and replacement of parts • Procedures for system testing during ground operations • Diagrams for structural access plates • Details for application of special inspection techniques • Information concerning the application of protective treatments after inspection • Information relative to the structural fasteners • List of any special tools needed

2. 中文翻译

除表明航空器所有权的登记证之外，适航证表明航空器的适航性。AC 21-12《美国适航证申请》（FAA 表格 8130-6）是填写该证申请表的综合性指南。原文至此只写到「有两类证书：标准类」即被截断（「与特殊类」一句见下一张卡片开头），其后串入的条目依次为：• 建议的翻修周期 • 检查大纲的详细内容，须同时标明为保持持续适航所必需的检查频次与范围 • 排故信息 • 正确拆卸与更换部件的顺序与方法 • 地面运行期间的系统测试程序 • 结构接近盖板图示 • 特殊检查技术应用的详细说明 • 检查后施加防护处理的有关信息 • 与结构紧固件相关的资料 • 所需专用工具的清单

4. 语法解析

① In addition to the registration certificate that indicates …（介词短语作状语 + 定语从句）

原句：In addition to the registration certificate that indicates the ownership of an aircraft, an airworthiness certificate indicates the airworthiness of the aircraft.

拆解：In addition to … 是介词短语作状语，其中 that indicates the ownership of an aircraft 是定语从句修饰 the registration certificate；主句 an airworthiness certificate indicates the airworthiness of the aircraft。

要点：in addition to 中的 to 是介词，后面只能接名词或动名词，不能接句子（要接句子得写 in addition to the fact that …）。主句和从句都用 indicate，形成「登记证证所有权／适航证证适航性」的对称，读时注意第二个 indicates 的宾语是 airworthiness 而非 ownership。

② AC 21-12, …, FAA Form 8130-6, is …（同位语说明同一文件）

原句：AC 21-12, Application for U.S. Airworthiness Certificate, FAA Form 8130-6, is a comprehensive guide for the completion of the application form for this certificate.

拆解：主语 AC 21-12；其后两个由逗号隔开的同位语 Application for U.S. Airworthiness Certificate 与 FAA Form 8130-6 指同一份文件；谓语 is；表语 a comprehensive guide for the completion of the application form for this certificate。

要点：文件编号后紧跟「名称 + 表格号」的同位语，是 FAA 文书的典型写法，中文宜用书名号加括号处理。注意两个 for 层级不同：guide for the completion（关于填写）与 form for this certificate（用于该证的表格）。completion of the application form 里的 of 表动宾关系。

③ There are two certificates: standard …（there be 存在句 + 冒号列举）

原句：There are two certificates: standard

拆解：There be 存在句，真正的主语是 two certificates，故用 are；冒号后列举类别，standard 与（原文下一页起首的）special 并列。该句在原文中被截断，后半句见相邻章节。

要点：There be 的谓语与后面的名词一致（two certificates → are），不要按 there 单复数判断。冒号在技术文本里常用来引出列举，译成「分为……类」比直译「有」更顺。遇到原文截断时，译文照实处理，不要自行补全成「标准类与特殊类」的完整句子而不作说明。
