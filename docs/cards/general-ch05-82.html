Absolute Humidity

通用 Ch 05 · Absolute Humidity · 原文 PDF 第 185 页

1. 原文

Absolute humidity is the actual amount of the water vapor in a mixture of air and water. It is expressed either in grams per cubic meter or pounds per cubic foot. The amount of water vapor that can be present in the air is dependent upon the temperature and pressure. The higher the temperatures, the more water vapor the air is capable of holding, assuming constant pressure. When air has all the water vapor it can hold at the prevailing temperature and pressure, it is said to be saturated.

2. 中文翻译

绝对湿度是空气与水混合物中水蒸气的实际含量。它可以用克每立方米或磅每立方英尺表示。空气中能够存在的水蒸气量取决于温度和压力。在压力恒定的条件下，温度越高，空气能够容纳的水蒸气越多。当空气在当时的温度和压力下已含有它所能容纳的全部水蒸气时，就称该空气为饱和。

4. 语法解析

① either … or 并列介词短语 + 被动语态

原句：It is expressed either in grams per cubic meter or pounds per cubic foot.

拆解：It 指 absolute humidity；is expressed 为被动；either … or … 连接两个介词短语 in grams per cubic meter 与（in）pounds per cubic foot，第二个 in 被省略。

要点：either … or … 要求两边结构对称，这里对称的是「介词 + 单位」；省略第二个 in 是技术文献的常见经济写法。注意 grams per cubic meter 用复数 grams，而 pounds per cubic foot 用复数 pounds，都是「每……」的表达，不要写成 gram per cubic meters。

② When 状语从句 + 省略关系代词的定语从句 + be said to be

原句：When air has all the water vapor it can hold at the prevailing temperature and pressure, it is said to be saturated.

拆解：When 引导时间状语从句；从句中 all the water vapor it can hold 是省略了关系代词 that 的定语从句（= all the water vapor that it can hold）；主句 it is said to be saturated 为「主语 + is said + to be + 表语」，it 指 air。

要点：先行词被 all 修饰时关系代词只能用 that，口语和手册中常直接省略，读句时要能把 it can hold 认出来是定语而不是第二个主句。be said to be 译作「被称为／据说是」，比 people say it is 更正式；同类还有 is considered to be、is known as。

③ the + 比较级, the + 比较级 + 独立分词结构

原句：The higher the temperatures, the more water vapor the air is capable of holding, assuming constant pressure.

拆解：The higher the temperatures 与 the more water vapor the air is capable of holding 构成「the + 比较级 …, the + 比较级 …」结构，意为「越……就越……」；后一分句的正常语序是 the air is capable of holding more water vapor；assuming constant pressure 是现在分词短语作条件状语。

要点：这个句型两个分句都用陈述语序，但前一分句常省略谓语（the higher the temperatures = the higher the temperatures are）。翻译要按「温度越高，……越多」的汉语语序重排。assuming (that) … 是技术文本中引出前提的固定说法，相当于 if we assume，注意 assuming 的逻辑主语须与主句一致。
