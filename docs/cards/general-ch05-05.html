Density

1. 原文

The density of a substance is its weight per unit volume.

The unit volume selected for use in the English system of measurement is 1 cubic foot (ft 3). In the metric system, it is 1 cubic centimeter (cm3). Therefore, density is expressed in pounds per cubic foot (lb⁄ft 3) or in grams per cubic centimeter (g⁄cm 3).

To find the density of a substance, its weight and volume must be known. Its weight is then divided by its volume to find the weight per unit volume. For example, the liquid which fills a certain container weighs 1,497.6 lb. The container is 4 ft long, 3 ft wide and 2 ft deep. Its volume is 24 ft 3 (4 ft. × 3 ft. × 2 ft.). If 24 ft3 of liquid weighs 1,497.6 lb, then 1 ft3 weighs 1,497.6 ÷ 24, or 62.4 lb. Therefore, the density of the liquid is 62.4 lb/ft3. This is the density of water at 4 °C (Centigrade) and is usually used as the standard for comparing densities of other substances. In the metric system, the density of water is 1 g⁄cm3. The standard temperature of 4 °C is used when measuring the density of liquids and solids. Changes in temperature will not change the weight of a substance, but will change the volume of the substance by expansion or contraction, thus changing its weight per unit volume.

The procedure for finding density applies to all substances; however, it is necessary to consider the pressure when finding the density of gases. Pressure is more critical when measuring the density of gases than it is for other substances. The density of a gas increases in direct proportion to the pressure exerted on it. Standard conditions for the measurement of the densities of gases have been established at 0 °C for temperature and a pressure of 76 cm of mercury (Hg), which is the average pressure of the atmosphere at sea level. Density is computed based on these conditions for all gases.

2. 中文翻译

物质的密度（density）是其单位体积的重量。

英制测量中所选用的单位体积是 1 立方英尺（ft3）。公制中则是 1 立方厘米（cm3）。因此，密度用磅每立方英尺（lb/ft3）或克每立方厘米（g/cm3）表示。

要求出某物质的密度，必须已知其重量和体积。然后用体积去除重量，即得单位体积的重量。例如，充满某一容器的液体重 1,497.6 lb。该容器长 4 ft、宽 3 ft、深 2 ft，其体积为 24 ft3（4 ft. × 3 ft. × 2 ft.）。若 24 ft3 的液体重 1,497.6 lb，则 1 ft3 重 1,497.6 ÷ 24，即 62.4 lb。因此，该液体的密度为 62.4 lb/ft3。这就是水在 4 °C（摄氏）时的密度，通常用作比较其他物质密度的标准。在公制中，水的密度为 1 g/cm3。测量液体和固体的密度时采用 4 °C 这一标准温度。温度变化不会改变物质的重量，但会因膨胀或收缩而改变物质的体积，从而改变其单位体积的重量。

求密度的方法适用于所有物质；但在求气体的密度时，必须考虑压力。测量气体密度时，压力比在其他物质中更为关键。气体的密度与施加于其上的压力成正比增加。测量气体密度的标准条件已确定为：温度 0 °C、压力 76 cm 汞柱（Hg），即海平面处大气的平均压力。所有气体的密度均按此条件计算。

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词被动：must be known

原句：To find the density of a substance, its weight and volume must be known.

拆解：To find the density of a substance 是目的状语；主句主语 its weight and volume，谓语 must be known（情态动词 + be + 过去分词）。

要点：技术文本说明「必须知道什么」时用被动，把「谁知道」隐去；must be known 不能写成 must know。同类还有 must be measured（必须测量）、should be recorded（应记录）。主语虽是并列两项，must be 的形式不随主语变化，这也是情态被动的便利之处。

② if 条件句 + or 引出同位解释

原句：If 24 ft3 of liquid weighs 1,497.6 lb, then 1 ft3 weighs 1,497.6 ÷ 24, or 62.4 lb.

拆解：If 引导真实条件从句，主句用一般现在时 weighs；or 62.4 lb 是对前面 1,497.6 ÷ 24 的同位解释，意为「也就是 62.4 lb」。

要点：科技英语里 or 常不表选择而表「即、也就是」，前面用逗号隔开，把算式与结果并列。真实条件句遵循「主将从现」，此处主句陈述客观事实，故用现在时。注意 lb 是单复数同形的单位缩写。

③ thus + 现在分词作结果状语

原句：Changes in temperature will not change the weight of a substance, but will change the volume of the substance by expansion or contraction, thus changing its weight per unit volume.

拆解：主语 Changes in temperature，两个并列谓语 will not change … but will change …；by expansion or contraction 是方式状语；thus changing its weight per unit volume 是现在分词短语作结果状语。

要点：thus / thereby + -ing 表示「从而……」，是技术文本描述因果链的常用收尾。分词短语的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致（此处指温度变化），否则要改用从句。注意 weight 与 volume 在此都作不可数名词，不加 -s。
