Camber

通用 Ch 05 · Camber · 原文 PDF 第 188 页

1. 原文

The camber of a wing is the curvature which is present on top and bottom surfaces. The camber on the top is much more pronounced, unless the wing is a symmetrical airfoil, which has the same camber top and bottom. The bottom of the wing, more often than not, is relatively flat. The increased camber on top is what causes the velocity of the air to increase and the static pressure to decrease. The bottom of the wing has less velocity and more static pressure, which is why the wing generates lift.

2. 中文翻译

机翼的弯度（camber）是指机翼上、下表面所呈现的曲率。上表面的弯度要明显得多，除非该机翼是对称翼型（symmetrical airfoil）——对称翼型上、下两面的弯度相同。机翼的下表面往往是相当平坦的。正是上表面弯度的增大，使气流速度增大、静压降低。机翼下表面的气流速度较小、静压较大，机翼之所以能产生升力，原因就在于此。

3. 专业词汇

英文 词性 中文 本节语境
camber n. 弯度 机翼上、下表面呈现的曲率，本节讨论的核心量
curvature n. 曲率、弯曲程度 用来解释 camber 的同义换用词
symmetrical airfoil n. phr. 对称翼型 上、下表面弯度相同的翼型，是「上表面弯度更大」这一规律的例外

4. 语法解析

① unless 引导的否定条件从句

原句：The camber on the top is much more pronounced, unless the wing is a symmetrical airfoil, which has the same camber top and bottom.

拆解：主句 The camber on the top is much more pronounced；unless the wing is a symmetrical airfoil 是条件状语从句，相当于 if the wing is not a symmetrical airfoil；which has the same camber top and bottom 是非限制性定语从句，补充说明 symmetrical airfoil。

要点：unless 本身含否定意义（「除非、如果不」），从句里不能再加 not，写成 unless the wing is not... 会语义反转，是常见错误。非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开、只作补充说明，这里的 which 指翼型，不能换成 that。

② what 引导的表语从句 + cause sb to do

原句：The increased camber on top is what causes the velocity of the air to increase and the static pressure to decrease.

拆解：主语 The increased camber on top（上表面增大了的弯度），increased 是过去分词作定语；系动词 is；what causes... 是 what 引导的表语从句，what 在从句中作主语；causes 后接两个「宾语 + 不定式」：the velocity of the air to increase 与 the static pressure to decrease，用 and 并列。

要点：what 在此等于 the thing that，既起连接作用又在从句里充当成分，不能换成 that。cause 的结构是 cause sth to do sth（使某物做某事），不能写 cause sth do；可对比 make sth do（不带 to）、allow sth to do。译文用「正是……的原因」比直译「是……的东西」更合技术语体。

③ which is why … 非限制性定语从句表结果

原句：The bottom of the wing has less velocity and more static pressure, which is why the wing generates lift.

拆解：主句 The bottom of the wing has less velocity and more static pressure；which 指代整个主句所述事实，在非限制性定语从句中作主语；is 的表语是 why the wing generates lift 这一表语从句。

要点：which is why... 是技术写作中表示因果的紧凑说法，相当于 and this is the reason why...；不要写成 which is because...。less 与 more 直接修饰不可数名词 velocity、pressure，用原级比较，不加 -er；注意 velocity 在此是不可数用法。
