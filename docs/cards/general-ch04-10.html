Other Drawing Data

1. 原文

Revision Block

Revisions to a drawing are necessitated by changes in dimensions, design, or materials. The changes are usually listed in ruled columns either adjacent to the title block or at one corner of the drawing. All changes to approved drawings must be carefully noted on all existing prints of the drawing.

When drawings contain such corrections, attention is directed to the changes by lettering or numbering them and listing those changes against the symbol in a revision block. The revision block contains the identification symbol, the date, the nature of the revision, the authority for the change, and the name of the draftsman who made the change.

To distinguish the corrected drawing from its previous version, many firms are including, as part of the title block, a space for entering the appropriate symbol to designate that the drawing has been changed or revised.

Notes

Notes are added to drawings for various reasons. Some of these notes refer to methods of attachment or construction. Others give alternatives, so that the drawing can be used for different styles of the same object. Still others list modifications that are available. Notes may be found alongside the item that they refer to. If the notes are lengthy, they may be placed elsewhere on the drawing and identified by letters or numbers. Notes are used only when the information cannot be conveyed in the conventional manner or when it is desirable to avoid crowding the drawing.

When the note refers to a specific part, a light line with an arrowhead leads from the note to the part. If it applies to more than one part, the note is worded to eliminate ambiguity as to the parts it pertains to. If there are several notes, they are generally grouped together and numbered consecutively.

Zone Numbers

Zone numbers on drawings are like the numbers and letters printed on the borders of a map. They help locate a point. To find a point, mentally draw horizontal and vertical lines from the letters and numerals specified; the point where these lines intersect is the area sought.

Use the same method to locate parts, sections, and views on large drawings, particularly assembly drawings. Parts numbered in the title block can be located on the drawing by finding the numbers in squares along the lower border. Zone numbers read from right to left.

Station Numbers & Location Identification on Aircraft

A numbering system is used on large assemblies for aircraft to locate stations, such as fuselage frames. Fuselage station 185 indicates a location that is 185 inches from the datum of the aircraft. The measurement is usually taken from the nose or zero station, but in some instances, it may be taken from the firewall or some other point chosen by the manufacturer. Just as forward and aft locations on aircraft are made by reference to the datum, locations left and right of the aircraft’s longitudinal axis are made by reference to the buttock line and are called butt stations. Vertical locations on aircraft are made in reference to the waterline.

The same station numbering system is used for wing and stabilizer frames. The measurement is taken from the centerline or zero station of the aircraft.

Allowances & Tolerances

When a given dimension on a print shows an allowable variation, the plus (+) number indicates the maximum, and the minus (−) number the minimum allowable variation. The sum of the plus and minus allowance numbers is called tolerance. For example, using 0.225 + 0.0025 − 0.0005, the plus and minus numbers indicate the part is acceptable if it is not more than 0.0025 larger than the 0.225 given dimension, or not more than 0.0005 smaller than the 0.225 dimension. Tolerance in this example is 0.0030 (0.0025 max plus 0.0005 min).

If the plus and minus allowances are the same, you will find them presented as 0.225 ± 0.0025. The tolerance would then be 0.0050. Allowance can be indicated in either fractional or decimal form. When very accurate dimensions are necessary, decimal allowances are used. Fractional allowances are sufficient when precise tolerances are not required. Standard tolerances of –0.010 or −1⁄32 may be given in the title block of many drawings, to apply throughout the drawing.

Finish Marks

Finish marks are used to indicate the surface that must be machine finished. Such finished surfaces have a better appearance and allow a closer fit with adjoining parts. During the finishing process, the required limits and tolerances must be observed. Do not confuse machined finishes with those of paint, enamel, chromium plating, and similar coating.

Scale

Some drawings are made the same size as the drawn part; reflecting a scale of 1:1. Other scales may be used. However, when drawings are made on a computer, drawing sizes may be easily increased (zoom in) or decreased (zoom out). Some electronic printers have the same capability. Furthermore, when a 1:1 copy of a print is made, the copy size may differ slightly from that of the original. For accurate information, refer to the dimensions shown on the drawing.

Application

When shown near or in the title block, application may refer to a specific aircraft, assembly, sub-assembly or unique application. For example, the title block indicates the bracket assembly is for a Roll Servo installation for an S-Tec Auto Pilot installation. If this drawing pertained to a B95 Aircraft equipped with an Aero- Tech air conditioning system and the bracket illustrated was unique to that installation, the title block would provide that application information. The title block may indicate Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed.

2. 中文翻译

修订栏

尺寸、设计或材料的变更会使图纸需要修订。这些变更通常列在标题栏旁或图纸一角的带格线栏内。已批准图纸的所有变更，都必须在该图纸所有现存复制图上仔细注明。

当图纸含有这类更正时，用字母或数字对变更加以标注，并在修订栏中把这些变更列在相应符号之后，以引导读者注意。修订栏包含识别符号、日期、修订性质、变更依据以及作出该变更的绘图员姓名。

为把更正后的图纸与前一版本区分开，许多公司在标题栏中留出一栏，用来填写相应符号，以标明该图纸已被更改或修订。

注释

在图纸上加注注释有各种原因。其中一些注释说明连接或构造的方法。另一些给出可选方案，使该图纸可用于同一物体的不同样式。还有一些列出可采用的改装。注释可能就放在其所指项目的旁边。如果注释较长，可以放在图纸的其他位置，并用字母或数字标识。只有在信息无法以常规方式表达，或为避免图纸过于拥挤时，才使用注释。

当注释针对某个特定零件时，用一条带箭头的细线把注释引向该零件。如果注释适用于一个以上的零件，则注释的措辞应消除关于其所指零件的含糊。如果有若干条注释，通常把它们归在一起并连续编号。

分区编号

图纸上的分区编号就像地图边框上印的数字和字母。它们有助于确定某一点的位置。要找某一点，就从指定的字母和数字出发，在想象中画出水平和垂直的线，这些线的交点即为所要找的区域。

用同样的方法可以在大型图纸上、尤其是装配图上确定零件、剖视图和视图的位置。在标题栏中编了号的零件，可以通过在图纸下边框的方格中找到相应编号来定位。分区编号从右向左读。

站位编号与飞机上的位置识别

大型飞机装配件采用编号体系来标定站位，例如机身框架。机身站位 185 表示距飞机基准面 185 英寸的位置。该测量通常从机头或零站位量起，但在某些情况下也可能从防火墙或制造商选定的其他点量起。正如飞机上的前后位置是以基准面为参考确定的，飞机纵轴线左右两侧的位置则以对称中心线（buttock line）为参考确定，称为对称站位（butt station）。飞机上的垂直位置则以水线为参考确定。

机翼和安定面框架也使用同样的站位编号体系，其测量从飞机的中心线或零站位量起。

留量与公差

当图纸上某一给定尺寸标出允许的变动量时，正（+）数表示允许变动的最大值，负（−）数表示允许变动的最小值。正负留量数值之和称为公差。例如，对于 0.225 + 0.0025 − 0.0005，正负数值表示：只要该零件比给定的 0.225 尺寸大不超过 0.0025，或比 0.225 尺寸小不超过 0.0005，即为合格。本例中的公差为 0.0030（最大 0.0025 加最小 0.0005）。

如果正负留量相同，就会发现它们写成 0.225 ± 0.0025 的形式，此时公差为 0.0050。留量可以用分数或小数形式表示。当需要非常精确的尺寸时，使用小数留量；当不要求精确公差时，分数留量就足够了。许多图纸会在标题栏中给出 −0.010 或 −1⁄32 的标准公差，以适用于整张图纸。

加工标记

加工标记用于标明必须进行机加工的表面。这样的精加工表面外观更好，并可与相邻零件配合得更紧密。在加工过程中，必须遵守规定的极限和公差。不要把机加工表面与油漆、瓷漆、镀铬及类似涂层所形成的表面混淆。

比例

有些图纸按所绘零件的实际大小绘制，即采用 1:1 的比例。也可以使用其他比例。不过，图纸在计算机上绘制时，图纸尺寸可以方便地放大或缩小。有些电子打印机也具有同样的能力。此外，制作复制图的 1:1 副本时，副本尺寸可能与原件略有差异。要获得准确信息，应参照图纸上标注的尺寸。

应用

当应用信息出现在标题栏附近或其中时，可能指某一特定飞机、装配件、分组件或特定用途。例如，标题栏表明该支架组件用于 S-Tec 自动驾驶仪的滚转伺服机构（Roll Servo）安装。如果该图纸涉及一架装有 Aero-Tech 空调系统的 B95 飞机，且所示支架为该安装所独有，标题栏就会给出该应用信息。标题栏可注明：Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed。

4. 语法解析

① 名词化主语 + 被动 necessitated by

原句：Revisions to a drawing are necessitated by changes in dimensions, design, or materials. 拆解：主语 Revisions to a drawing（to a drawing 为后置定语）；谓语 are necessitated 为被动；by changes in dimensions, design, or materials 引出原因，in… 修饰 changes。 要点：necessitate 为及物动词“使成为必需”，常用被动 be necessitated by；主语用名词化形式（Revisions）更书面，翻译时还原为“因……而需要修订”更自然。

② must be + 副词插入 + 过去分词

原句：All changes to approved drawings must be carefully noted on all existing prints of the drawing. 拆解：主语 All changes to approved drawings（approved 为过去分词作前置定语）；谓语 must be carefully noted（carefully 插在 be 与过去分词之间）；on all existing prints of the drawing 为地点状语。 要点：情态被动中副词的位置在 be 与过去分词之间；approved（被动）与 existing（主动）两个分词作前置定语，含义相反，注意区分。

③ direct attention to + by + 并列动名词

原句：When drawings contain such corrections, attention is directed to the changes by lettering or numbering them and listing those changes against the symbol in a revision block. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句；主句为被动 attention is directed to the changes（direct attention to 的被动式）；by 引出两个并列动名词 lettering or numbering them 与 listing those changes…；against the symbol 表示“对照该符号”。 要点：direct attention to sth 的被动式须把 attention 提到句首；by 后的并列动名词共用 by 时第二个不再重复；list A against B 表示“把 A 列在 B 之旁”。

④ only when 引导的两个并列从句 + it 形式主语

原句：Notes are used only when the information cannot be conveyed in the conventional manner or when it is desirable to avoid crowding the drawing. 拆解：主句为被动 Notes are used；only when…or when… 为两个并列的时间状语从句；第二个从句中 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to avoid crowding the drawing。 要点：only 修饰 when 从句，强调“唯有在……时”；or 连接的两个 when 从句须结构平行；avoid 后接动名词 crowding。

⑤ 句首不定式表目的 + 分词后置定语 + where 定语从句

原句：To find a point, mentally draw horizontal and vertical lines from the letters and numerals specified; the point where these lines intersect is the area sought. 拆解：To find a point 为不定式目的状语；前一分句为祈使句 mentally draw…，from the letters and numerals specified 中 specified 为过去分词作后置定语；分号后主句 the point where these lines intersect is the area sought，where 引导定语从句，sought 为过去分词作后置定语。 要点：单个过去分词（specified、sought）作后置定语相当于定语从句的省略形式（which are specified / which is sought）；分号前后是两个独立分句，不能用逗号连接。

⑥ find + 宾语 + 过去分词作宾语补足语

原句：If the plus and minus allowances are the same, you will find them presented as 0.225 ± 0.0025. 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句（are the same 为系表）；主句主语 you，谓语 will find，宾语 them（指 allowances），presented as 0.225 ± 0.0025 为过去分词短语作宾语补足语。 要点：find sth done 表示“发现某事被（以某种形式）呈现”，宾语与补足语是被动关系；presented as 意为“以……形式表示”。
