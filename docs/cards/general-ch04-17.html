Drawing Standards

通用 Ch 04 · Drawing Standards · 原文 PDF 第 131 页

1. 原文

Drawing standards cover such items as paper sizes, notes, numbering systems, geometric dimensions and tolerances, abbreviations, welding symbols, roughness symbols, and electrical symbols. These standards cover metric and inch measurements, as well as computer-drafting standards.

Different standards for drawings are used in industry and some of the more common ones are published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the American National Standards Institute (ANSI).

2. 中文翻译

制图标准涵盖的项目包括纸张幅面、附注、编号体系、几何尺寸与公差、缩写、焊接符号、表面粗糙度符号以及电气符号。这些标准既涉及米制与英制计量，也涉及计算机辅助制图标准。

工业界使用不同的制图标准，其中较为通用的几套由国际标准化组织（ISO）和美国国家标准学会（ANSI）发布。

4. 语法解析

① Drawing standards cover such items as paper sizes, notes, numbering systems …（such … as 举例结构）

原句：Drawing standards cover such items as paper sizes, notes, numbering systems, geometric dimensions and tolerances, abbreviations, welding symbols, roughness symbols, and electrical symbols.

拆解：主语 Drawing standards，谓语 cover；宾语 such items as …，其中 such … as 引出对 items 的举例；as 后是一长串并列名词短语，最后一项前加 and。

要点：such … as 列举的只是部分例子，译「诸如……之类」；口语中的 such as 不能换成 like 用于正式技术文件。若要表示全部列举，则应改用 all of the following。cover 在此意为「涵盖、包括」，不是「覆盖」；同类的 include／comprise 更强调构成。

② These standards cover metric and inch measurements, as well as computer-drafting standards（as well as 的附加并列）

原句：These standards cover metric and inch measurements, as well as computer-drafting standards.

拆解：主谓宾 These standards cover … measurements；as well as 引出附加成分 computer-drafting standards；该附加成分不改变主语的数。

要点：A as well as B 的语义重心在 A，是「除 A 之外还有 B」，因此谓语与 A 保持一致，这一点与 and 不同。as well as 不作「和」的等价替换，尤其在主语位置：Tom as well as his friends is … 用单数。metric and inch 是美式技术文本的固定并提方式（英式常说 metric and imperial）。

③ some of the more common ones are published by ISO and ANSI（ones 替代 + 被动）

原句：Different standards for drawings are used in industry and some of the more common ones are published by the International Organization for Standardization (ISO) and the American National Standards Institute (ANSI).

拆解：and 连接两个并列分句；前句 are used 为被动；后句主语 some of the more common ones（ones 替代前文的 standards），谓语 are published 为被动；by 后接两个发布机构。

要点：one／ones 用来替代前文出现过的可数名词，避免重复（standards → ones），前面可带限定词 the more common。publish 用于标准、规范时译「发布、颁布」，不用「出版」。两个机构名缩写写在括号内，是技术文献首次提及全称时的标准做法。
