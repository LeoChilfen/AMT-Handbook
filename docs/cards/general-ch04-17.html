1. 原文

Graphs and charts are frequently used to convey information graphically or information given certain conditions. They often utilize values shown on the “x” and “y” axes that can be projected up and across to arrive at a specific result. Also, when data is entered into a computer database, software programs can create a variety of different bar graphs, pie charts, and so forth, to graphically represent that data.

Reading & Interpreting Graphs & Charts

When interpreting information shown on graphs and charts, it is extremely important that all the notes and legend information be carefully understood to eliminate any misinterpretation of the information presented.

A nomogram is a graph that usually consists of three sets of data. Knowledge of any two sets of data enables the interpreter to obtain the value for the third unknown corresponding value. One type of nomogram consists of three parallel scales graduated for different variables, so that when a straight edge connects any two values, the third can be read directly. Other types may use values on the “x” and “y” axes of a graph with the third corresponding value determined by the intersection of the “x” and “y” values with one of a series of curved lines. The illustration is an example of a nomogram that shows the relationship between aviation fuels, specific weight, and temperature.

2. 中文翻译

曲线图和图表常用来以图形方式传达信息，或传达在特定条件下的信息。它们通常利用“x”轴和“y”轴上标出的数值，把这些数值向上、横向投影，从而得出某一具体结果。此外，当数据录入计算机数据库后，软件程序可以生成各种不同的柱形图、饼图等等，以图形方式表示这些数据。

阅读与解读曲线图和图表

在解读曲线图和图表上所示的信息时，极其重要的一点是：所有注释和图例信息都必须仔细理解，以消除对所呈现信息的任何误读。

诺模图是一种通常由三组数据组成的曲线图。只要知道其中任意两组数据，解读的人就能求出第三组未知的对应值。有一种诺模图由三条按不同变量刻度的平行标尺组成，用一把直尺把任意两个值连起来，第三值便可直接读出。另一些类型则用曲线图上“x”轴和“y”轴的数值，第三组对应值由“x”“y”值与一族曲线中某一条的交点确定。图中所示即为一例诺模图，它表示航空燃油、比重和温度之间的关系。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + 目的不定式 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：Graphs and charts are frequently used to convey information graphically or information given certain conditions. 拆解：主语 Graphs and charts + 被动谓语 are used + 目的状语 to convey…；convey 后接两个并列宾语 information 与 information given certain conditions（过去分词短语作后置定语，= information that is given certain conditions）。 要点：to convey 的不定式符号 to 后接动词原形，与 be used 的被动完成体无关；given certain conditions 是「在给定条件下」的紧凑表达，翻译时展开成「在某条件给定时的信息」。并列的两个宾语信息量不同，第二个带分词短语，读句时不要把它误当谓语。

② it 作形式主语 + 虚拟语气

原句：When interpreting information shown on graphs and charts, it is extremely important that all the notes and legend information be carefully understood to eliminate any misinterpretation of the information presented. 拆解：When + 动名词短语作时间状语（shown on graphs and charts 为过去分词修饰 information，presented 同为后置定语）；主句 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句；从句中 be carefully understood 是动词原形，属虚拟语气（should be 省略 should）。 要点：it is important/necessary that… 后接 (should) + 动词原形，是正式书面语的规定性表达；不能写成 is 或 are。eliminate 的不定式 to eliminate 在从句中作目的状语，其宾语 misinterpretation 带 of 短语说明误读的对象。

③ enable sb to do 结构

原句：Knowledge of any two sets of data enables the interpreter to obtain the value for the third unknown corresponding value. 拆解：主语 Knowledge（of any two sets of data 后置定语）+ 谓语 enables + 宾语 the interpreter + 宾语补足语 to obtain…；obtain 的宾语是 the value for the third unknown corresponding value（四个词层层修饰 value）。 要点：enable sb to do 意为「使某人能够做某事」，宾语后必须用不定式作补足，不能直接接动词（✗ enables the interpreter obtain）。主语是抽象名词 knowledge 而非人，翻译时按「只要知道……，就能……」处理最顺。

④ so that 结果从句中套 when 时间从句

原句：One type of nomogram consists of three parallel scales graduated for different variables, so that when a straight edge connects any two values, the third can be read directly. 拆解：主句 One type of nomogram consists of + 宾语 three parallel scales（graduated for different variables 过去分词后置定语）；so that 引导结果状语从句，从句本身又以 when 引导的时间状语从句开头；主句 the third can be read directly 省略了 value。 要点：consist of 是「由……组成」，无被动形式（✗ is consisted of），是易错点。so that 在此表结果而非目的，译为「因此、这样就能」；the third 是序数词名词化，指代前面出现的第三个值，翻译时补出「第三个值」。

⑤ with + 名词 + 过去分词（独立结构）

原句：Other types may use values on the “x” and “y” axes of a graph with the third corresponding value determined by the intersection of the “x” and “y” values with one of a series of curved lines. 拆解：主语 Other types + 谓语 may use + 宾语 values（on the “x” and “y” axes of a graph 后置定语）；with the third corresponding value determined by… 为 with 复合结构（with + 名词 + 过去分词）作伴随状语，determined by 的宾语是 the intersection（of…with… 两个介词短语分别说明交点的双方）。 要点：with + 宾语 + 过去分词表示被动关系（值「被」确定），若用 determining 则变成主动，语义反转。intersection of A with B 是「A 与 B 的交点」的固定搭配；the third corresponding value 中 third 与 corresponding 同时修饰 value，顺序不可随意调换。
