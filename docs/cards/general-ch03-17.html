Multiplication of Decimal Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Multiplication of Decimal Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 101 页

1. 原文

To multiply decimal numbers, vertical alignment of the decimal point is not required. Instead, align the numbers to the right in the same way that whole numbers are multiplied (with no regard to the decimal points or place values) and then multiply. The last step is to place the decimal point in the correct place in the answer. To do this, count the number of decimal places in each of the numbers, add the total, and then assign that number of decimal places to the result.

Example: To multiply 0.2 × 6.03, arrange the numbers vertically and align them to the right. Multiply the numbers, ignoring the decimal points for now.

6.03 × 0.2 1206 (ignore the decimal points, for now)

After multiplying the numbers, count the total number of decimal places in both numbers. For this example, 6.03 has 2 decimal places and 0.2 has 1 decimal place. Together there are a total of 3 decimal places. The decimal point for the answer is placed 3 decimal places from the right. Therefore, the answer is 1.206.

6.03 2 decimal places × 0.2 1 decimal place 1.206 3 decimal places Example: Using the formula watts = amperes × voltage, what is the wattage of an electric drill that uses 9.45 amperes from a 120-volt source? Align the numbers to the right and multiply.

After multiplying the numbers, count the total number of decimal places in both numbers. For this example, 9.45 has 2 decimal places and 120 has no decimal place. Together there are 2 decimal places. The decimal point for the answer is placed 2 decimal places from the right. Therefore, the answer is 1,134.00 watts, or simplified to 1,134 watts.

9.45 2 decimal places × 120 no decimal place 000 1890 + 945 1,134.00 2 decimal places

2. 中文翻译

乘小数时，不需要把小数点竖直对齐。相反，要像整数相乘那样把各数右对齐（暂不考虑小数点和位值），然后相乘。最后一步是把小数点点在得数的正确位置上。做法是：数出每个数的小数位数，把位数相加，再给结果保留同样多位小数。

例：计算 0.2 × 6.03，把两数竖直排列并右对齐。先不管小数点，把两数相乘。

6.03 × 0.2 1206（暂时忽略小数点）

相乘之后，数出两数小数位数的总和。本例中 6.03 有 2 位小数，0.2 有 1 位小数，合计 3 位小数。得数的小数点从右边起数 3 位点上。因此，答案为 1.206。

6.03 2 decimal places × 0.2 1 decimal place 1.206 3 decimal places

例：按公式 瓦特 = 安培 × 电压，一台从 120 伏电源取用 9.45 安培的电钻，其功率是多少瓦？把各数右对齐后相乘。

相乘之后，数出两数小数位数的总和。本例中 9.45 有 2 位小数，120 没有小数位，合计 2 位小数。得数的小数点从右边起数 2 位点上。因此，答案为 1,134.00 瓦，简写为 1,134 瓦。

9.45 2 decimal places × 120 no decimal place 000 1890 + 945 1,134.00 2 decimal places

4. 语法解析

① vertical alignment of the decimal point is not required（名词化主语 + 否定被动）

原句：To multiply decimal numbers, vertical alignment of the decimal point is not required.

拆解：目的状语 To multiply decimal numbers；主语 vertical alignment of the decimal point（名词化短语）；谓语 is not required 为被动。

要点：把动词 align 名词化成 alignment 后，句子主语变长、谓语变短，是技术文本提升信息密度的常见手法；译成中文要还原成动词“不需要对齐”。not required 是“不必”，不等于 must not（禁止），二者在规范里差别很大。require 的被动后接 of 短语时表“需要某物”。

② Instead, align the numbers to the right in the same way that …（转折副词 + 方式从句）

原句：Instead, align the numbers to the right in the same way that whole numbers are multiplied (with no regard to the decimal points or place values) and then multiply.

拆解：Instead 是转折副词，承接上句“不需要竖直对齐”；主句为祈使句，两个并列谓语 align 与 multiply；in the same way that 引导方式定语从句，说明“同……的做法”；括号内 with no regard to 补充条件。

要点：instead 放句首时后用逗号，表示与前文相反的做法，译“相反、而是”。the same way that / the same way as 都可接从句，但 that 后是完整句子。括号在英文技术文本中常用来加限定条件，翻译时可保留括号，不要合并进正文而丢掉语气。

③ To do this, count …, add …, and then assign …（目的指代 + 三个祈使动词并列）

原句：To do this, count the number of decimal places in each of the numbers, add the total, and then assign that number of decimal places to the result.

拆解：To do this 回指上句“把小数点点在正确位置”；主句为祈使句，count、add、assign 三个动词并列，and then 标出先后顺序；assign A to B 为“把 A 分配给 B”。

要点：To do this / To do so 用来回指前一句提到的动作，避免重复整句，译作“要做到这一点”。步骤描述里逗号加 and 连接三个以上动词时，最后一个 and 前的逗号不可省（牛津逗号）。assign ... to ... 与 add ... to ...、apply ... to ... 一样，都是“动词 + 宾语 + to + 对象”的固定框架。
