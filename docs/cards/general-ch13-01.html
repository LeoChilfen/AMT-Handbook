1. 原文

Since Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 65 was covered only briefly in Chapter 2, Regulations, Maintenance Forms, Records, and Publications, it is discussed in greater detail in this chapter. This chapter discusses the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) regulation governing the certification of airmen other than flight crew members. This chapter is based on the material contained in 14 CFR part 65, which has the following subparts:

• Subpart B—Air Traffic Control Tower Operators

• Subpart C—Aircraft Dispatchers

• Subpart D—Mechanics

• Subpart E—Repairmen

• Subpart F—Parachute Riggers This chapter only focuses on the certification of maintenance technicians and, therefore, subparts B, C, E, and F are not addressed. The FAA certificates two separate categories of maintenance technicians: mechanic and repairman. The fundamental difference between these two is that the mechanic certificate is transportable, is issued to the technician based upon their training and knowledge, and is not dependent on the technician’s location. Although the repairman certificate is also based upon the training and knowledge of the technician, it is specifically issued to that technician while they are employed at a distinct location of a specific company. This certificate carries a literal address where the technician is authorized to work using their repairman skills. When the technician is no longer employed there, the repairman certificate must be returned to the Flight Standards District Office (FSDO) that issued it.

2. 中文翻译

由于《联邦法规汇编》第 14 编（14 CFR）第 65 部在第 2 章《法规、维修表格、记录与出版物》中只作了简略介绍，本章将对其作更详细的讨论。本章讨论的是美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）关于除飞行机组成员以外的航空人员合格证的法规。本章以 14 CFR 第 65 部所载内容为基础，该部包含以下各分部分：

• B 分部分——空中交通管制塔台管制员

• C 分部分——航空器签派员

• D 分部分——维修人员

• E 分部分——修理人员

• F 分部分——降落伞装具员 本章只聚焦于维修技术人员的合格证，因此 B、C、E、F 四个分部分不予讨论。FAA 颁发两类彼此独立的维修技术人员证件：维修人员和修理人员。两者的根本区别在于：维修人员合格证是可携带的，依据技术人员的培训情况和知识水平颁发给该技术人员，不取决于技术人员所在的地点。修理人员合格证虽然同样依据技术人员的培训情况和知识水平，但它是在该技术人员受雇于某家特定公司的某一特定地点期间专门颁发给该技术人员的。这张合格证上写有一个具体地址，即该技术人员获准运用其修理人员技能工作的地点。当该技术人员不再受雇于该地点时，修理人员合格证必须交还给为其颁发证件的飞行标准地区办公室（FSDO）。

4. 语法解析

① since 引导原因状语从句 + 主句被动语态

原句：Since Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 65 was covered only briefly in Chapter 2, Regulations, Maintenance Forms, Records, and Publications, it is discussed in greater detail in this chapter. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 65（层层后置定语修饰 Title 14），谓语 was covered（被动），状语 only briefly in Chapter 2；主句 it is discussed in greater detail in this chapter 中 it 指代前面的 part 65，谓语为一般现在时被动。 要点：Since 在此表原因而非时间，判断依据是从句与主句存在因果、且主句用现在时；it 回指前文内容，翻译时不能译成「它」了事，应还原为「第 65 部」。手册类文本常用被动，把「谁讨论」隐去。

② 非限制性定语从句 which 指代整部法规

原句：This chapter is based on the material contained in 14 CFR part 65, which has the following subparts: 拆解：主句 This chapter is based on the material（contained in 14 CFR part 65 为过去分词短语作后置定语）；逗号后 which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 14 CFR part 65，而非 material。 要点：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，只能译成并列分句（「该部包含……」），不能译成「……的」。which 的先行词要看逻辑：material 不会「包含各分部分」，part 65 才会。冒号引出后续列举，译文保留冒号。

③ 表语从句中三个并列谓语的省略

原句：The fundamental difference between these two is that the mechanic certificate is transportable, is issued to the technician based upon their training and knowledge, and is not dependent on the technician’s location. 拆解：主干 The fundamental difference between these two is + that 引导的表语从句；从句主语 the mechanic certificate，后接三个并列谓语 is transportable、is issued、is not dependent，第二个 is 前省略了重复的主语。 要点：表语从句用 that 引导且 that 不可省，这是区分同位语从句与定语从句的标志之一。三个 is 并列时后两个不能省略 be，否则句子失去谓语；based upon 为过去分词短语作状语，表依据。

④ when 引导时间状语从句 + 定语从句嵌套

原句：When the technician is no longer employed there, the repairman certificate must be returned to the Flight Standards District Office (FSDO) that issued it. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句（is employed 为被动，there 指前述特定地点）；主句 the repairman certificate must be returned to the FSDO（情态被动）+ that issued it 定语从句修饰 FSDO，that 在从句中作主语。 要点：主句用 must be + 过去分词，规定的是义务而非事实，译文用「必须交还」。定语从句 that 指物的先行词，不能用 who；issued it 的 it 回指 certificate，译文宜还原为「证件」以免歧义。

⑤ although 让步状语从句与 while 时间状语从句并用

原句：Although the repairman certificate is also based upon the training and knowledge of the technician, it is specifically issued to that technician while they are employed at a distinct location of a specific company. 拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句（is based upon 被动）；主句 it is specifically issued to that technician（被动 + 状语 specifically）+ while they are employed... 时间状语从句，they 指该技术人员。 要点：although 与 but 不能同现，汉语「虽然……但是……」直译时最易犯这个错。while 此处表「在……期间」，与表对比的 while 不同，要看从句谓语是否为持续性状态。被动结构 is issued to sb 中的 to 不能换成 for。
