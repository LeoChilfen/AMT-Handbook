14 CFR Part 183—Representatives of the Administrator

通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 183—Representatives of the Administrator · 原文 PDF 第 63 页

1. 原文

As the aviation industry grows and the design, manufacture, and testing of aircraft gets more complex, the FAA faces both budget constraints and personnel shortages. As early as 1962, the FAA began a program to allow private sector persons in various areas of industry to be “designees” or “representatives of the FAA Administrator.” These people are NOT FAA employees, but rather are designated by the FAA to act on their behalf. Regular doctors may serve as “aviation medical examiners,” skilled pilots can become “pilot examiners,” and experienced airframe and/or powerplant mechanics can become “designated mechanic examiners (DME)” to administer the oral and practical portion of the FAA testing.

Other lesser known designees are the designated engineering

representatives (DER), the designated manufacturing inspection representatives (DMIR), and the designated airworthiness representatives (DAR).

• DERs approve data based upon their engineering training and their knowledge of FAA regulations.

• DMIRs make conformity inspections only at their employer. They are similar to “designated repairmen” because they are only authorized to inspect parts at their employers’ facility.

• DARs conduct aircraft certification and aircraft inspection functions on behalf of the FAA depending on specific functions they are authorized. They may perform work for either manufacturing facilities or maintenance entities depending on their designation.

2. 中文翻译

随着航空业的发展、航空器在设计、制造与试验上日趋复杂，FAA 同时面临预算约束与人员短缺。早在 1962 年，FAA 便开始推行一项计划，允许工业界各领域的私营部门人员充任“指定代表”（designee）或“FAA 局长的代表”。这些人并非 FAA 雇员，而是由 FAA 指定、代其行使职能。普通医生可以担任“航空体检医师”，技术娴熟的驾驶员可以成为“驾驶员考试员”，有经验的机体和/或动力装置维修技师则可以成为“指定维修技师考试员（DME）”，负责实施 FAA 考试中的口试与实际操作部分。

其他较少为人所知的指定代表包括指定工程代表（DER）、指定制造检验代表（DMIR）与指定适航代表（DAR）。

• 指定工程代表依据其工程训练与对 FAA 规章的掌握来批准数据。

• 指定制造检验代表仅在其雇主处实施符合性检查。他们类似“指定维修人员”，因为只被授权在雇主的设施内检查零件。

• 指定适航代表代表 FAA 实施航空器审定与航空器检查职能，具体取决其被授权的职能。依据指定范围的不同，他们既可为制造设施服务，也可为维修单位服务。

4. 语法解析

① As 引导的伴随变化状语从句 + 变化系动词

原句：As the aviation industry grows and the design, manufacture, and testing of aircraft gets more complex, the FAA faces both budget constraints and personnel shortages.

拆解：As 引导时间/伴随状语从句，从句内并列两个谓语 grows 与 gets more complex；gets 在此为变化系动词（“变得”），后接形容词比较级作表语；主句 the FAA faces both … and … 为简单主谓宾。

要点：技术文本里的 as 常表“随着……”，与表示原因的 as 要区分：此处从句与主句是同步变化关系，译作“随着”。注意 gets 的主语是 the design, manufacture, and testing 这一整体概念，看似复数其实作单数，所以用 gets 而非 get。

② not … but rather … 对比结构 + be designated to do

原句：These people are NOT FAA employees, but rather are designated by the FAA to act on their behalf.

拆解：not … but rather … 连接两个表语部分：are NOT FAA employees 与 are designated by the FAA to act on their behalf；后者为被动语态，to act on their behalf 是不定式作目的状语，说明“被指定去做什么”。

要点：but rather 比 but 语气更强，用于纠正读者可能的误解，法律与技术文本中常见。be designated by sb. to do sth.（由某人指定去做某事）与 be authorized to do、be certified to do 属同一类“被动 + 不定式”结构，是适航文件里的高频句式。

③ because 原因状语从句 + be authorized to do

原句：They are similar to “designated repairmen” because they are only authorized to inspect parts at their employers’ facility.

拆解：主句 They are similar to “designated repairmen”；because 引导原因状语从句；从句谓语 are only authorized to inspect 为“be + 副词 + 过去分词 + 不定式”，only 修饰 authorized，限定授权范围。

要点：be authorized to do sth. 不能写成 be authorized doing；only 的位置决定限定对象——此处 only authorized to inspect parts at their employers’ facility 意为“授权范围仅限于雇主厂内”。类似的还有 be limited to doing、be restricted to doing，后接动名词。
