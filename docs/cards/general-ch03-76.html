1. 原文

The metric system, also known as the International System of Units (SI), is the dominant language of measurement used today. Its standardization and decimal features make it well- suited for engineering and aviation work.

The metric system was first envisioned by Gabriel Mouton, Vicar of St. Paul’s Church in Lyons, France. The meter is the unit of length in the metric system, and it is equal to one ten-millionth of the distance from the equator to the North Pole. The liter is the unit of volume and is equal to one cubic decimeter. The gram is the unit of mass and is equal to one cubic centimeter of water.

All of the metric units follow a consistent naming scheme, which consists of attaching a prefix to the unit. For example, since kilo stands for 1,000, one kilometer equals 1,000 meters.

Centi is the prefix for one hundredth, so one meter equals one hundred centimeters. Milli is the prefix for one thousandths and one gram equals one thousand milligrams. Refer to Figure 3-33 for the names and definitions of metric prefixes.

2. 中文翻译

公制，又称国际单位制（SI），是当今计量领域的主导语言。其标准化与十进制特征使其非常适合工程和航空工作。

公制最早由法国里昂圣保罗教堂的牧师加布里埃尔·穆顿提出构想。米是公制中的长度单位，等于从赤道到北极距离的一千万分之一。升是体积单位，等于一立方分米。克是质量单位，等于一立方厘米水的质量。

所有公制单位都遵循一致的命名方案，即在单位前加一个词头。例如，由于 kilo 表示 1,000，所以一公里等于 1,000 米。

Centi 是表示百分之一的词头，因此一米等于一百厘米。Milli 是表示千分之一的词头，一克等于一千毫克。公制词头的名称与定义见图 3-33。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作插入语：also known as…

原句：The metric system, also known as the International System of Units (SI), is the dominant language of measurement used today.

拆解：主干是 The metric system is the dominant language of measurement；also known as … 是过去分词短语作插入语，用一对逗号与主干隔开，对主语作补充说明；used today 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 language。

要点：这类插入语可还原为非限制性定语从句 which is also known as …。be known as（被称为）与 be known for（因……而闻名）要分清。used today 中 today 是副词，前面不加介词；也可理解为 (that is) used today 的省略。

② make + 宾语 + 形容词作宾补

原句：Its standardization and decimal features make it well- suited for engineering and aviation work.

拆解：主语 Its standardization and decimal features；谓语 make；宾语 it（指公制）；well-suited for engineering and aviation work 是形容词短语作宾语补足语，构成 make + 宾语 + 形容词的复合结构。

要点：make 后接形容词作宾补时不需要 be，不能说 make it is well-suited。be suited for 与 be suitable for 都表示「适用于」。原文 well- suited 中间的空格是 PDF 排版断字留下的，规范写法为 well-suited。

③ 非限制性定语从句 + consist of doing

原句：All of the metric units follow a consistent naming scheme, which consists of attaching a prefix to the unit.

拆解：主句 All of the metric units follow a consistent naming scheme；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 naming scheme；consist of 后接动名词 attaching 作宾语。

要点：consist of 中的 of 是介词，后面只能接名词或 -ing，不能接不定式；同义表达有 be composed of、be made up of。非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，翻译时另起一句「该方案……」更合乎中文习惯。
