Foreign Object Damage (FOD)

通用 Ch 01 · Foreign Object Damage (FOD) · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

Foreign object damage (FOD) is any damage to aircraft, personnel, or equipment caused by any loose object. These loose objects can be anything, such as broken runway concrete, shop towels, safety wire, etc. To control FOD, keep ramp and operation areas clean, have a tool control program, and provide convenient receptacles for used hardware, shop towels, and other consumables.

Never leave tools or other items around the intake of a turbine engine. The modern gas turbine engine creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine that causes any loose object to be drawn into the engine. The exhaust of these engines can propel loose objects great distances with enough force to damage anything that is hit. The importance of a FOD program cannot be overstressed when a technician considers the cost of engines, components, or a human life.

2. 中文翻译

外来物损伤（FOD）是指任何松散物体对飞机、人员或设备造成的任何损伤。这些松散物体可以是任何东西，例如破碎的跑道混凝土、车间抹布、保险丝等。要控制 FOD，应保持机坪和作业区域清洁，建立工具管控制度，并为用过的紧固件、车间抹布及其他消耗品提供便利的回收容器。

绝不要把工具或其他物品留在燃气涡轮发动机进气口附近。现代燃气涡轮发动机在发动机前方形成低压区，会把任何松散物体吸入发动机。这类发动机的排气能把松散物体抛出很远，其力量足以损坏被击中的任何东西。当技术人员考虑到发动机、部件或一条人命的代价时，FOD 计划的重要性再怎么强调也不为过。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：Foreign object damage (FOD) is any damage to aircraft, personnel, or equipment caused by any loose object. 拆解：主语 Foreign object damage (FOD) + 系动词 is + 表语 any damage（to aircraft, personnel, or equipment 为后置定语）+ 过去分词短语 caused by any loose object 作后置定语。 要点：caused by… 是过去分词短语后置修饰 damage（= which is caused by…），不是谓语，划掉后主句是 FOD is any damage。to aircraft, personnel, or equipment 用逗号并列三个名词，最后一个前加 or，是正式书面语的列举方式。易错点：不要把 is any damage 读成“是任何损伤”就完，后置定语才是定义的限定条件。

② that 定语从句修饰哪个名词 + cause sb./sth. to do

原句：The modern gas turbine engine creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine that causes any loose object to be drawn into the engine. 拆解：主句 … creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine + that 引导的定语从句修饰 a low-pressure area；从句谓语 causes + 宾语 any loose object + 不定式被动 to be drawn 作宾语补足语。 要点：that 从句紧跟的是 area（不是 engine），因为“吸入外来物”的是低压区而非发动机本体。cause sth. to do sth. 的被动形式是 cause sth. to be done，to 不能省略（× cause it be drawn）。draw 的过去分词是 drawn，与动词原形同形变化易混：draw–drew–drawn。

③ propel + 宾语 + 距离 + enough…to do

原句：The exhaust of these engines can propel loose objects great distances with enough force to damage anything that is hit. 拆解：主语 The exhaust + 谓语 can propel + 宾语 loose objects + 名词短语 great distances 作距离状语 + with enough force 方式状语 + 不定式 to damage anything 为后置定语修饰 force + that is hit 为定语从句修饰 anything。 要点：propel + 宾语 + 距离（great distances）是“把……推出很远”的固定搭配，距离前不加介词；enough + 名词 + to do 表示“足以……的力量”，enough 修饰名词时前置。anything that is hit 中 that 不可换成 what，因为 anything 是先行词、从句缺主语。

④ cannot be overstressed 的否定 + 程度构式

原句：The importance of a FOD program cannot be overstressed when a technician considers the cost of engines, components, or a human life. 拆解：主句 The importance of a FOD program cannot be overstressed（情态动词否定 + 被动语态）+ when 引导时间状语从句（considers + 三个并列宾语）。 要点：cannot be overstressed 字面是“不能被过分强调”，实际含义是“再怎么强调也不过分”，同类构式还有 cannot be overemphasized、cannot be too careful。这类“否定词 + 过度义动词”不能按字面直译，否则意思正好相反。when 从句在此表“当……时/考虑到……”，译成“考虑到”更合中文习惯。
