Foreign Object Damage (FOD)

通用 Ch 01 · Foreign Object Damage (FOD) · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

Foreign object damage (FOD) is any damage to aircraft, personnel, or equipment caused by any loose object. These loose objects can be anything, such as broken runway concrete, shop towels, safety wire, etc. To control FOD, keep ramp and operation areas clean, have a tool control program, and provide convenient receptacles for used hardware, shop towels, and other consumables.

Never leave tools or other items around the intake of a turbine engine. The modern gas turbine engine creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine that causes any loose object to be drawn into the engine. The exhaust of these engines can propel loose objects great distances with enough force to damage anything that is hit. The importance of a FOD program cannot be overstressed when a technician considers the cost of engines, components, or a human life.

2. 中文翻译

外来物损伤（FOD）是指任何松散物体对航空器、人员或设备造成的任何损伤。这些松散物体可以是任何东西，例如破损的跑道混凝土、车间抹布、保险丝等。为控制 FOD，应保持停机坪和作业区清洁，建立工具管控制度，并为用过的紧固件、车间抹布及其他消耗品提供方便的收纳容器。

绝不要把工具或其他物品遗落在涡轮发动机进气道附近。现代燃气涡轮发动机在发动机前方形成一个低压区，会把任何松散物体吸入发动机。这类发动机的排气能把松散物体推出很远，其力量足以损坏被击中的任何东西。当技师考虑到发动机、部件乃至一条人命的代价时，FOD 计划的重要性怎么强调都不为过。

4. 语法解析

① … any damage to aircraft, personnel, or equipment caused by any loose object（过去分词短语作后置定语）

原句：Foreign object damage (FOD) is any damage to aircraft, personnel, or equipment caused by any loose object.

拆解：主干 Foreign object damage (FOD) is any damage；to aircraft, personnel, or equipment 是 damage 的后置定语，caused by any loose object 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which is caused by …。

要点：damage to 是固定搭配（对……的损伤），介词不能换成 of。过去分词后置定语表被动，与现在分词后置定语表主动正好相对，比较：damage caused by FOD（被 FOD 造成的损伤）与 FOD causing damage（FOD 造成损伤）。中文翻译时通常把定语提到名词前面。

② … a low-pressure area in front of the engine that causes any loose object to be drawn into the engine（定语从句 + cause sb to do）

原句：The modern gas turbine engine creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine that causes any loose object to be drawn into the engine.

拆解：主句 The modern gas turbine engine creates a low-pressure area in front of the engine；that 引导定语从句修饰 a low-pressure area（不是 engine）；从句谓语 causes，宾语 any loose object，宾补 to be drawn into the engine（被动不定式）。

要点：定语从句紧跟的名词不一定就是先行词，要按逻辑判断——「吸入物体」的是低压区而不是发动机本身，所以 that 修饰 area。cause sb/sth to do 是「使……做」，宾语与不定式之间是主谓关系；此处物体是被吸入，故用被动形式 to be drawn。

③ The importance … cannot be overstressed（cannot + over- 的「再……也不为过」句型）

原句：The importance of a FOD program cannot be overstressed when a technician considers the cost of engines, components, or a human life.

拆解：主语 The importance of a FOD program，谓语 cannot be overstressed（情态动词否定 + 被动）；when 引导时间状语从句，consider 后接三个并列宾语。

要点：cannot be overstressed / cannot be overemphasized 是固定说法，字面是「不能被过分强调」，实际意思是「怎么强调都不为过」，不能直译成否定。同类还有 cannot be overstated（不能夸大）、cannot be too careful（越小心越好）；这类「否定 + 过度词」的句式要整体记忆。
