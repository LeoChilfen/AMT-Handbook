1. 原文

Pressure is the amount of force acting on a specific amount of surface area. The force is typically measured in pounds and the surface area in square inches, making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi. If a 100-lb weight was placed on top of a block with a surface area of 10 in 2, the average weight distribution would be 10 lb for each of the square inches (100 ÷ 10), or 10 psi.

When atmospheric pressure is being measured, in addition to psi, other means of pressure measurement can be used.

These include inches or millimeters of mercury, and millibars.

Standard day atmospheric pressure is equal to 14.7 psi, 29.92 inches of mercury ("Hg), 760 millimeters of mercury (mm hg), or 1013.2 millibars. The relationship between these units of measure is as follows:

1 psi = 2.04 "Hg 1 psi = 51.7 mm Hg 1 psi = 68.9 millibars The concept behind measuring pressure in inches of mercury

The test tube is then turned upside down and placed in an open container of mercury, and the top is uncovered. Gravity acting on the mercury in the test tube will try to make the mercury run out. Atmospheric pressure pushing down on the mercury in the open container tries to make the mercury stay in the test tube. At some point these two forces, gravity and atmospheric pressure, will equal out and the mercury will stabilize at a certain height in the test tube.

Under standard day atmospheric conditions, the air in a 1-in2 column extending all the way to the top of the atmosphere would weigh 14.7 lb. A 1 in2 column of mercury, 29.92 inches tall, would also weigh 14.7 lb. That is why 14.7 psi is equal to 29.92 "Hg. Figure 5-33 demonstrates this point.

2. 中文翻译

压力是作用在一定面积表面上的力的大小。力通常以磅计量，表面积以平方英寸计量，由此得出压力的单位为磅每平方英寸，即 psi。若把一个 100 lb 的重物放在表面积为 10 in 2 的物块上，则重量平均分布为每平方英寸 10 lb（100 ÷ 10），即 10 psi。（原文 in 2 为 OCR 压平的 in²。）

测量大气压力时，除 psi 之外，还可采用其他压力计量方式。

这些方式包括英寸汞柱或毫米汞柱，以及毫巴。

4. 语法解析

① 现在分词短语作结果状语 + make 的复合宾语

原句：The force is typically measured in pounds and the surface area in square inches, making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi.

拆解：两个并列分句：The force is typically measured in pounds 与 the surface area (is measured) in square inches（后一分句省略了重复的 is measured）；making the units of pressure pounds per square inch or psi 是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中 make + 宾语 the units of pressure + 名词宾补 pounds per square inch。

要点：逗号后的 making 表示前面事实自然导致的结果，逻辑主语是整句而并非某个名词。make 后接名词作宾补时不加 to be，写成 making the units … to be … 属于多余。in pounds / in square inches 中的 in 表计量单位，与 in inches of mercury 用法相同。

② 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：Gravity acting on the mercury in the test tube will try to make the mercury run out.

拆解：主语 Gravity，后接 acting on the mercury in the test tube（现在分词短语作后置定语，= which acts on …）；谓语 will try；宾语 to make the mercury run out，其中 make + 宾语 the mercury + 动词原形 run out，不定式符号 to 省略。

要点：acting on … 不是谓语，真正的谓语是 will try，阅读时不要被分词截断。这种「名词 + 分词短语」在技术文本里替代定语从句，句子更紧凑。make sb./sth. do 中的 do 必须用原形；run out 在此指「流出、跑光」，不是「用完」。

③ 并列被动语态与步骤顺序词

原句：The test tube is then turned upside down and placed in an open container of mercury, and the top is uncovered.

拆解：两个并列分句。前一分句主语 The test tube 共用两个被动谓语 is turned 与 (is) placed，后者承前省略 is 和主语；后一分句主语 the top，谓语 is uncovered（被动）；and 连接两个分句，then 表示步骤的先后。

要点：并列谓语共用一个 be 时，后面那个只留过去分词（is turned … and placed …），这是省略而非漏词，阅读时不要误判为过去式动词。turn upside down（倒转）、place in …（放入）、uncover（打开、揭开）是实验步骤描述的高频动词，中文按「倒转—放入—打开」的顺序照译即可。the top 此处指试管口，不是「顶部」。
