Purpose & Function of Aircraft Drawings

通用 Ch 04 · Purpose & Function of Aircraft Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 125 页

1. 原文

Drawings and prints are the link between the engineers who design an aircraft and the workers who build, maintain, and repair it. A print may be a copy of a working drawing for an aircraft part or group of parts, or for a design of a system or group of systems. They are made by placing a tracing of the drawing over a sheet of chemically-treated paper and exposing it to a strong light for a short period of time. When the exposed paper is developed, it turns blue where the light has penetrated the transparent tracing. The inked lines of the tracing, having blocked out the light, show as white lines on a blue background. With other types of sensitized paper, prints may have a white background with colored lines or a colored background with white lines.

Drawings created using computers may be viewed on the computer monitor or printed out in “hard copy” by use of an ink jet or laser printer. Larger drawings may be printed by use of a plotter or large format printer. Large printers can print drawings up to 42 inches high with widths up to 600 inches by use of continuous roll paper.

2. 中文翻译

图纸和复制图是设计飞机的工程师与制造、维修和修理飞机的人员之间的纽带。复制图可以是一份飞机零件或零件组的加工图副本，也可以是某个系统或系统组设计的副本。它们的制作方法是：把描好的图纸覆盖在一张经化学处理的纸上，用强光短时间曝光。曝光后的纸经显影，凡光线透过透明描图的地方即变为蓝色。描图上的墨线挡住了光，因而在蓝色背景上呈现为白色线条。使用其他类型的感光纸时，复制图可能是白色背景配彩色线条，或彩色背景配白色线条。

用计算机绘制的图纸可以在计算机显示器上查看，也可以用喷墨或激光打印机打印成“硬拷贝”。较大的图纸可以用绘图机或大幅面打印机打印。大型打印机使用连续卷纸，可打印高达 42 英寸、宽度达 600 英寸的图纸。

4. 语法解析

① 并列定语从句 + 介词 between 的并列宾语

原句：Drawings and prints are the link between the engineers who design an aircraft and the workers who build, maintain, and repair it. 拆解：主语 Drawings and prints；系表 are the link；between…and… 引出两个并列名词短语作介词宾语，各带一个 who 引导的定语从句；句末 it 指代 an aircraft。 要点：between 的宾语是两个短语而非单个词，翻译时要把 who 从句前置到名词前（“设计飞机的工程师”）；it 为单数代词，只能指 aircraft，不能指 drawings。

② by + 并列动名词表示制作方法

原句：They are made by placing a tracing of the drawing over a sheet of chemically-treated paper and exposing it to a strong light for a short period of time. 拆解：主句为被动 They are made；by 引出方式，后接两个并列动名词 placing… 与 exposing…（第二项省略了 by）；it 指 a sheet of chemically-treated paper；for a short period of time 为时间状语。 要点：介词 by 后必须用动名词，并列第二项可省 by，但不能改成不定式；expose sth to sth（使……接受……的照射）是固定搭配。

③ when 时间状语从句 + where 地点状语从句

原句：When the exposed paper is developed, it turns blue where the light has penetrated the transparent tracing. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，过去分词 exposed 作前置定语修饰 paper；主句 it turns blue 为系表结构；where 引导地点状语从句，主语 the light，谓语 has penetrated（现在完成时）。 要点：turn 在此为系动词，后接形容词表颜色变化；where 从句说明“在光线透过的地方”变蓝，不能译成“……的”这样的定语。

④ 完成式分词短语作原因状语

原句：The inked lines of the tracing, having blocked out the light, show as white lines on a blue background. 拆解：主语 The inked lines of the tracing；having blocked out the light 为完成式现在分词短语作原因状语（该动作先于主句谓语发生）；谓语 show 在此表“显现”，as white lines 为结果状语。 要点：完成式分词（having done）表示其动作先于主句谓语，此处逻辑主语是 the inked lines、为主动关系，故用 -ing 而非 -ed；block out 意为“遮住”。

⑤ 情态被动 + or 并列 + by use of 表手段

原句：Drawings created using computers may be viewed on the computer monitor or printed out in “hard copy” by use of an ink jet or laser printer. 拆解：主语 Drawings 带过去分词短语 created using computers 作后置定语（using computers 修饰 created）；谓语为 may be viewed 与 may be printed out 的并列（第二项省略 may be）；by use of… 为方式状语。 要点：两个被动谓语共用情态动词时，第二项常省略 may be，阅读时要自行补出；by use of 相当于 by using，属正式书面表达。

⑥ 名词后接两个表示尺寸上限的修饰成分

原句：Large printers can print drawings up to 42 inches high with widths up to 600 inches by use of continuous roll paper. 拆解：主语 Large printers；谓语 can print；宾语 drawings 后接 up to 42 inches high（数量状语）与 with widths up to 600 inches（with 复合结构）两个修饰成分；by use of continuous roll paper 为方式状语。 要点：up to 表示“最高达”，前面不加 about；with 短语补充另一维度的尺寸，翻译时宜拆成“高度……、宽度……”两个小句。
