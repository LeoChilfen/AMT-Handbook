Safety Around Airplanes

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Airplanes · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

As with the previously mentioned items, it is important to be aware of propellers. Technicians cannot assume the pilot of a taxiing aircraft can see them and must stay within the pilot’s view while on the ramp area. Turbine engine intakes and exhaust can also be very hazardous areas. Smoking or open flames are not permitted anywhere near an aircraft in operation. Be aware of aircraft fluids that can be detrimental to skin. When operating support equipment around aircraft, be sure to allow space between it and the aircraft, and secure it so it cannot roll into the aircraft. All items in the area of operating aircraft must be stowed properly.

2. 中文翻译

与前面提到的各项一样，必须注意螺旋桨。技术人员不能想当然地认为滑行中飞机的飞行员能看到自己，在机坪区域作业时必须始终处于飞行员的视野之内。燃气涡轮发动机的进气道和排气口同样可能是十分危险的区域。飞机运行时，其附近任何地方都不允许吸烟或出现明火。要注意那些对皮肤有害的飞机用液体。在飞机周围操作地面保障设备时，务必让设备与飞机之间留出间距，并将其固定好，使其不会滑向飞机。正在运行的飞机周围区域内的所有物品都必须妥善存放。

4. 语法解析

① 省略 that 的宾语从句 + 并列谓语 + while 省略

原句：Technicians cannot assume the pilot of a taxiing aircraft can see them and must stay within the pilot’s view while on the ramp area. 拆解：主语 Technicians + 谓语 1 cannot assume + 省略 that 的宾语从句（the pilot of a taxiing aircraft can see them）+ and 谓语 2 must stay within the pilot’s view + while on the ramp area 为时间状语（省略 they are）。 要点：assume 后的宾语从句常省略 that，cannot assume (that) the pilot… can see them 的从句里又套了一个情态动词 can，读句时先找出 assume 的从句边界。两个谓语不能混为一句：technicians 既“不能想当然”，又“必须留在视野内”。while on the ramp area = while they are on the ramp area，省略后主语须与主句一致；注意句末 their/them 都指 technicians。

② 动名词与名词并列作主语 + 被动语态

原句：Smoking or open flames are not permitted anywhere near an aircraft in operation. 拆解：主语 Smoking or open flames（动名词与名词短语并列）+ 谓语 are not permitted（被动语态）+ 地点状语 anywhere near an aircraft（in operation 为后置定语修饰 aircraft）。 要点：动名词 smoking 与名词短语 open flames 并列作主语，谓语用复数 are，不能因为第一个成分是动名词就用 is。anywhere near… 是“在……附近任何地方”的强调说法。in operation 是“正在运行/使用中”的固定表达，作后置定语时必须放在名词之后。

③ When + 现在分词（省略）+ so that 目的从句

原句：When operating support equipment around aircraft, be sure to allow space between it and the aircraft, and secure it so it cannot roll into the aircraft. 拆解：When operating… 为时间状语（省略 you are）+ 祈使句 be sure to allow space between it and the aircraft + and secure it（并列祈使）+ so (that) 引导目的/结果状语从句 it cannot roll into the aircraft。 要点：When + 现在分词是 When you are operating… 的省略，祈使句的隐含主语是 you，所以可以省。be sure to do 表示“务必做”，比 must 委婉。so (that) 引导目的从句时常用 can/could/cannot，本句 so it cannot roll… 表示“使其不能滑向飞机”。between it and the aircraft 中 it 指 support equipment，代词指代要按就近原则判断。

④ 现在分词作定语 + must be done

原句：All items in the area of operating aircraft must be stowed properly. 拆解：主语 All items（in the area of operating aircraft 为后置定语）+ 谓语 must be stowed（情态动词 + 被动语态）+ 副词 properly 作状语。 要点：in the area of operating aircraft 中 operating 是现在分词作定语修饰 aircraft（“正在运行的飞机”），整个介词短语修饰 items，不要读成“运行中的区域”。must be stowed 用被动，强调物品“必须被人收好”，施动者不出现。properly 修饰 stowed，位置在句末，不能插到 must 与 be 之间随意改写。
