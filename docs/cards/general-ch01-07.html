Hearing Protection

通用 Ch 01 · Hearing Protection · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

1. 原文

The flight line is a place of dangerous activity. Technicians who perform maintenance on the flight line must constantly be aware of what is going on around them. The noise on a flight line comes from many places. Aircraft are only one source of noise. There are auxiliary power units (APUs), fuel trucks, baggage handling equipment, and so forth. Each has its own frequency of sound. Combined all together, the noise on the ramp or flight line can cause hearing loss.

There are many types of hearing protection available. Hearing protection can be external or internal. Earmuffs or headphones are considered external protection. The internal type of hearing protection fits into the auditory canal. Both types reduce the sound level reaching the eardrum and reduce the chances of hearing loss.

Hearing protection is essential when working with pneumatic drills, rivet guns, or other loud tools. Even short duration exposure to these sounds can cause hearing loss because of their high frequency. Continued exposure will cause hearing loss.

2. 中文翻译

停机坪（外场）是进行危险作业的场所。在停机坪上执行维修的技术人员必须时刻注意自己周围正在发生什么。停机坪上的噪声来源很多。飞机只是一个噪声源。现场还有辅助动力装置（APU）、加油车、行李装卸设备等等。每一种都有各自的噪声频率。所有这些声音叠加在一起，机坪或停机坪上的噪声就可能造成听力损伤。

可用的听力防护用品有很多类型。听力防护可分为外部式和内部式。耳罩或头戴式耳机属于外部防护。内部式听力防护用品则塞入耳道内。两种类型都能降低到达耳膜的声音强度，从而降低听力损伤的几率。

使用气动钻、铆钉枪或其他高噪声工具时，听力防护是必需的。这类声音频率高，即使短时间接触也可能造成听力损伤。持续接触则会造成听力损伤。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + what 引导的宾语从句

原句：Technicians who perform maintenance on the flight line must constantly be aware of what is going on around them. 拆解：主语 Technicians + who 引导的定语从句（who perform maintenance on the flight line）+ 谓语 must constantly be aware of + what 引导的宾语从句（what is going on around them）。 要点：be aware of 后接名词或 what 从句，不能接 that 从句（× be aware of that…）。what 在从句中作主语，相当于 the things that，因此从句里不能再有先行词。副词 constantly 插在情态动词 must 与 be 之间，位置固定，不能放到句末。

② 过去分词短语作状语 + 不可数名词短语

原句：Combined all together, the noise on the ramp or flight line can cause hearing loss. 拆解：过去分词短语 Combined all together 作状语（逻辑主语是主句主语 the noise）+ 主句主语 the noise（on the ramp or flight line 为后置定语）+ 谓语 can cause + 宾语 hearing loss。 要点：过去分词作状语表示“经……处理后”，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，否则就是悬垂分词。hearing loss 是不可数名词短语，前面不加 a、也不加复数（× a hearing loss / hearing losses 在泛指时不用）。combine all together 中 all 与 together 叠用是英语的强调习惯。

③ because of 与 because 的区分

原句：Even short duration exposure to these sounds can cause hearing loss because of their high frequency. 拆解：主语 Even short duration exposure（to these sounds 为后置定语，exposure to 为固定搭配）+ 谓语 can cause + 宾语 hearing loss + because of their high frequency 为原因状语（介词短语）。 要点：because of 后接名词短语，because 后接从句，二者不可互换，这是考试与写作的高频错点。exposure to sth. 是“暴露于……”，介词固定用 to。their high frequency 中 their 指 these sounds；short duration 直接作定语修饰 exposure，是“名词 + 名词”的复合定语。

④ 并列谓语 + 现在分词后置定语

原句：Both types reduce the sound level reaching the eardrum and reduce the chances of hearing loss. 拆解：主语 Both types + 并列谓语 reduce the sound level… and reduce the chances…；第一个宾语 the sound level 后接现在分词短语 reaching the eardrum 作后置定语。 要点：reaching the eardrum 是修饰 the sound level 的后置定语（= that reaches…），不要误读成谓语；两个 reduce 并列共用主语，第二个 reduce 前省略了主语与情态。chances of + 名词/动名词表示“……的可能性”，比 possibility 更口语，复数是习惯用法。
