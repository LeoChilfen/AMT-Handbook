1. 原文

Types of Fuel and Identification

Two types of aviation fuel in general use are aviation gasoline, also known as A VGAS, and turbine fuel, also known as JET A fuel.

Aviation gasoline (A VGAS) is used in reciprocating engine aircraft. Currently, there are three grades of fuel in general use: 80/87, 100/130, and 100LL (low lead). A fourth grade, 115/145, is in limited use in the large reciprocating-engine aircraft. The two numbers indicate the lean mixture and rich mixture octane rating numbers of the specific fuel. In other words, with 80/87 A VGAS, the 80 is the lean mixture rating and 87 is the rich mixture rating number. To avoid confusing the types of A VGAS, it is generally identified as grade 80, 100, 100LL, or 115. A VGAS can also be identified by a color code. The color of the fuel needs to match the color band on piping and fueling equipment.

Turbine fuel/jet fuel is used to power turbojet and turbo- shaft engines. Three types of turbine fuel generally used in civilian aviation are JET A and JET A-1, made from kerosene, and JET B, a blend of kerosene and A VGAS. While jet fuel is identified by the color black on piping and fueling equipment, the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored.

Before mixing A VGAS and turbine fuel, refer to the Type Certificate Data Sheet for the respective powerplant. Adding jet fuel to A VGAS causes a decrease in the power developed by the engine and could cause damage to the engine (through detonation) and loss of life. Adding A VGAS to jet fuel can cause lead deposits in the turbine engine and can lead to reduced service life.

Contamination Control

Contamination is anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there. The types of contamination found in aviation fuel include water, solids, and microbial growths. The control of contamination in aviation fuel is extremely important, since contamination can lead to engine failure or stoppage and the loss of life. The best method of controlling contamination is to prevent its introduction into the fuel system. Some forms of contamination can still occur inside the fuel system. However, the filter, separators, and screens remove most of the contamination.

Water in aviation fuels generally take two forms: dissolved (vapor) and free water. The dissolved water is not a major problem until, as the temperature lowers, it becomes free water. This then poses a problem if ice crystals form, clogging filters and other small orifices.

Free water can appear as water slugs or entrained water. Water slugs are concentrations of water. This is the water that is drained after fueling an aircraft. Entrained water is suspended water droplets. These droplets may not be visible to the eye but give the fuel a cloudy look. The entrained water settles out in time.

Solid contaminants are insoluble in fuel. The more common types are rust, dirt, sand, gasket material, lint, and fragments of shop towels. The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair.

Microbiological growths are a problem in jet fuel. There are a number of varieties of micro-organisms that can live in the free water in jet fuel. Some variations of these organisms are airborne, others live in the soil. The aircraft fuel system becomes susceptible to the introduction of these organisms each time the aircraft is fueled. Favorable conditions for the growth of micro-organisms in the fuel are warm temperatures and the presence of iron oxide and mineral salts in the water. The best way to prevent microbial growth is to keep the fuel dry.

The effects of micro-organisms are:

• Formation of slime or sludge that can foul filters, separators, or fuel controls.

• Emulsification of the fuel.

• Corrosive compounds that can attack the fuel tank’s structure. In the case of a wet wing tank, the tank is made from the aircraft’s structure. They can also have offensive odors.

Fueling Hazards

The volatility of aviation fuels creates a fire hazard that has plagued aviators and aviation engine designers since the beginning of powered flight. V olatility is the ability of a liquid to change into a gas at a relatively low temperature. In its liquid state, aviation fuel does not burn. It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state that the liquid fuel changes that is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard.

Static electricity is a byproduct of one substance rubbing against another. Fuel flowing through a fuel line causes a certain amount of static electricity. The greatest static electricity concern around aircraft is that during flight, the aircraft moving through the air causes static electricity to build in the airframe. If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling, the static electricity in the airframe attempts to return to the ground through the fuel line from the servicing unit. The spark caused by the static electricity can ignite any vaporized fuel.

Breathing the vapors from fuel can be harmful and must be limited. Any fuel spilled on the clothing or skin must be removed as soon as possible.

Fueling Procedures

The proper fueling of an aircraft is the responsibility of the owner/operator. This does not, however, relieve the person doing the fueling of the responsibility to use the correct type of fuel and safe fueling procedures. There are two basic procedures when fueling an aircraft. Smaller aircraft are fueled by the over-the-wing method. This method uses the fuel hose to fill through fueling ports on the top of the wing. The method used for larger aircraft is the single point fueling system. This type of fueling system uses receptacles in the bottom leading edge of the wing to fill all the tanks. This decreases the time it takes to refuel the aircraft, limits contamination, and reduces the chance of static electricity igniting the fuel. Most pressure fueling systems consist of a pressure fueling hose and a panel of controls and gauges that permit one person to fuel or defuel any or all fuel tanks of an aircraft. Each tank can be filled to a predetermined level.

Prior to fueling, the person fueling must check the following:

  1. Ensure all aircraft electrical systems and electronic devices, including radar, are turned off. 2. Do not carry anything in the shirt pockets. These items could fall into the fuel tanks.
  1. Ensure no flame-producing devices are carried by anyone engaged in the fueling operation. A moment of carelessness could cause an accident.
  1. Ensure that the proper type and grade of fuel is used. Do not mix A VGAS and JET fuel.
  1. Ensure that all the sumps have been drained.
  1. Wear eye protection. Although generally not as critical as eye protection, other forms of protection, such as rubber gloves and aprons, can also protect the skin from the effects of spilled or splashed fuel.
  1. Do not fuel aircraft if there is danger of other aircraft in the vicinity blowing dirt in the direction of the aircraft being fueled. Blown dirt, dust, or other contaminants can enter an open fuel tank, contaminating the entire contents of the tank. 8. Do not fuel an aircraft when there is lightning within 5 miles.
  1. Do not fuel an aircraft within 500 feet of operating ground radar.

When using mobile fueling equipment:

  1. Approach the aircraft with caution, positioning the fuel truck so that if it is necessary to depart quickly, no backing needed.
  1. Set the hand brake of the fuel truck, and chock the wheels to prevent rolling.
  1. Ground the aircraft and then ground the truck. Next, ground or bond them together by running a connecting wire between the aircraft and the fuel truck. This may be done by three separate ground wires or by a “Y”

cable from the fuel truck.

  1. Ensure that the grounds are in contact with bare metal or are in the proper grounding points on the aircraft. Do not use the engine exhaust or propeller as grounding points. Damage to the propeller can result, and there is no way of quickly ensuring a positive bond between the engine and the airframe.
  1. Ground the nozzle to the aircraft, then open the fuel tank.
  1. Protect the wing and any other item on the aircraft from damage caused by spilled fuel or careless handling of the nozzle, hose, or grounding wires.
  1. Check the fuel cap for proper installation and security before leaving the aircraft.
  1. Remove the grounding wires in the reverse order. If the aircraft is not going to be flown or moved soon, the aircraft ground wire can be left attached.

When fueling from pits or cabinets, follow the same procedures as when using a truck. Pits or cabinets are usually designed with permanent grounding, eliminating the need to ground the equipment. However, the aircraft still must be grounded, and then the equipment must be grounded to the aircraft as it was with mobile equipment.

Defueling

Defueling procedures differ with different types of aircraft. Before defueling an aircraft, check the maintenance/service manual for specific procedures and cautions. Defueling can be accomplished by gravity defueling or by pumping the fuel out of the tanks. When the gravity method is used, it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel. When the pumping method is used, care must be taken not to damage the tanks, and the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel.

General precautions when defueling are:

• Ground the aircraft and defueling equipment.

• Turn off all electrical and electronic equipment.

• Have the correct type of fire extinguisher available.

• Wear eye protection.

2. 中文翻译

燃油的类型与识别

通用航空燃料主要有两类：航空汽油（AVGAS）和涡轮燃料（也称 JET A 燃油）。

航空汽油（AVGAS）用于活塞发动机飞机。目前通用的有三个牌号：80/87、100/130 和 100LL（低铅）。第四个牌号 115/145 在大型活塞发动机飞机上少量使用。两个数字分别表示该燃油的贫混合比和富混合比辛烷值。换言之，对 80/87 AVGAS，80 是贫混合比辛烷值，87 是富混合比辛烷值。为避免混淆各型 AVGAS，通常以牌号 80、100、100LL 或 115 来标识。AVGAS 也可用色标识别。燃油颜色必须与管路和加油设备上的色带一致。

涡轮燃料/喷气燃料用于驱动涡轮喷气和涡轴发动机。民用航空常用的三种涡轮燃料中，JET A 和 JET A-1 由煤油制成，JET B 则是煤油与 AVGAS 的混合体。喷气燃料在管路和加油设备上以黑色标识，而喷气燃料本身可为无色或草黄色。

在混合 AVGAS 与涡轮燃料之前，应查阅相应动力装置的型号合格证数据表（TCDS）。向 AVGAS 中加入喷气燃料会降低发动机的输出功率，并可能因爆震损坏发动机、造成人员死亡。向喷气燃料中加入 AVGAS 会在涡轮发动机内形成铅沉积，导致使用寿命缩短。

污染控制

污染是指燃油中本不该存在的任何物质。航空燃油中发现的污染类型包括水、固体物和微生物生长物。航空燃油的污染控制极为重要，因为污染会导致发动机失效或停车，乃至人员死亡。控制污染最好的办法是防止其进入燃油系统。燃油系统内部仍可能出现某些形式的污染，但滤芯、分离器和滤网能去除大部分污染。

航空燃油中的水一般有两种形态：溶解水（蒸气态）和游离水。溶解水在温度降低转变为游离水之前并不构成大问题。此时若形成冰晶，堵塞滤芯及其他细小孔口，才会成为问题。

游离水可呈水塞或夹带水形态。水塞是水的聚集。这是给飞机加油后放出的水。夹带水是悬浮的水滴。这些水滴可能肉眼不可见，但会使燃油呈现浑浊。夹带水会随时间沉降析出。

固体污染物不溶于燃油。较常见的类型有铁锈、污垢、砂粒、垫片材料、棉绒和擦布碎屑。燃油控制器及其他燃油相关机构的配合公差很紧，小到人发直径 1/20 的颗粒就能使其损坏或堵塞。

微生物生长物是喷气燃料中的一个问题。有多种微生物能在喷气燃料的游离水中存活。这类生物有的来源于空气，有的生活在土壤中。飞机每次加油，其燃油系统都有可能被这些微生物带入。微生物在燃油中生长的有利条件是温度较高，且水中存在氧化铁和矿物盐。防止微生物生长的最好办法是保持燃油干燥。

微生物的影响有：

• 形成黏液或油泥，堵塞滤芯、分离器或燃油控制器。

• 使燃油乳化。

• 产生腐蚀性化合物，侵蚀油箱结构。就整体油箱（wet wing tank）而言，油箱本身就是由飞机结构构成的。这些微生物还会产生难闻的气味。

加油危害

航空燃油的挥发性造成了自动力飞行之初就困扰飞行员和航空发动机设计者的火灾危险。挥发性是指液体在相对较低温度下转变为气体的能力。航空燃油在液态下不会燃烧。因此，正是液态燃油转变而成的蒸气态或气态，既可用于驱动飞机，又构成火灾危险。

静电是两种物质相互摩擦的副产物。燃油流经油管会产生一定量的静电。飞机周围最值得关注的静电问题，是飞行中飞机穿越空气使机体上积聚静电。如果该静电在加油前未被释放，机体上的静电就会试图通过加油设备的油管回到地面。静电产生的火花可点燃任何已蒸发的燃油。

吸入燃油蒸气有害健康，必须加以限制。溅到衣服或皮肤上的燃油必须尽快清除。

加油程序

正确地为飞机加油是机主/运营人的责任。但这并不免除实际加油人员使用正确油料和安全加油程序的责任。为飞机加油有两种基本方式。小型飞机采用翼上加油法。该方法用油软管通过机翼顶部的加油口加注。较大飞机采用单点加油系统。此类加油系统利用机翼下前缘的受油插座为所有油箱加注。这可缩短加油所需时间、限制污染，并降低静电引燃燃油的可能性。大多数压力加油系统由一根压力加油软管和一块控制与仪表板组成，可使一人为飞机的任一或全部油箱加油或抽油。每个油箱都可加到预定油量。

加油前，加油人员必须检查以下各项：

  1. 确认飞机所有电气系统和电子设备（包括雷达）均已关闭。2. 衬衣口袋里不得装任何物品，这些物品可能掉入油箱。
  1. 确认参与加油作业的任何人都未携带产生火焰的装置。一时的疏忽就可能酿成事故。
  1. 确认使用正确类型和牌号的燃油，不得混用 AVGAS 与喷气燃料。
  1. 确认所有放沉淀活门均已排放。
  1. 佩戴护目镜。其他防护用品（如橡胶手套和围裙）通常虽不如护目镜关键，但也能保护皮肤免受溢出或溅出燃油的影响。
  1. 如果附近其他飞机有可能把尘土吹向正在加油的飞机，不得为该机加油。被吹起的尘土、灰尘或其他污染物会进入敞开的油箱，污染整箱燃油。8. 5 英里范围内有闪电时，不得为飞机加油。
  1. 不得在距正在工作的地面雷达 500 英尺以内为飞机加油。

使用移动式加油设备时：

  1. 小心接近飞机，停放加油车的位置应保证需要快速撤离时无需倒车。
  1. 拉紧加油车的手制动，并垫上轮挡防止溜动。
  1. 先为飞机接地，再为加油车接地。然后通过一根连接线把飞机与加油车连接起来接地或搭接。这可以用三根独立的接地线完成，也可以用加油车引出的“Y”形

电缆完成。（原文此处因版面被切成两块，本块为上一块句子的延续）

  1. 确认接地线接触的是裸金属或飞机上正确的接地点。不得以发动机排气管或螺旋桨作为接地点：这可能损坏螺旋桨，而且无法迅速确认发动机与机体之间已可靠搭接。
  1. 为加油枪接地到飞机上，然后打开油箱。
  1. 保护机翼及机上其他部件，避免因燃油溢出或加油枪、软管、接地线操作不当而受损。
  1. 离开飞机前检查油箱盖是否安装正确、是否牢固。
  1. 按相反顺序拆除接地线。如果飞机短时间内不飞行也不移动，机体接地线可以保留不拆。

从地井或加油柜加油时，遵循与使用加油车相同的程序。地井或加油柜通常设计为永久接地，无需再为设备接地。但飞机仍必须接地，然后仍须像移动设备那样把设备与飞机连接接地。

放油

抽油程序因飞机类型而异。为飞机抽油前，应查阅维修/勤务手册中的具体程序和注意事项。抽油可采用重力放油或用泵把燃油从油箱抽出。采用重力法时，必须有收集燃油的办法。采用泵抽法时，须注意不要损坏油箱，且抽出的燃油不得与好油混合。

抽油时的一般注意事项：

• 为飞机和抽油设备接地。

• 关闭所有电气和电子设备。

• 备好正确类型的灭火器。

• 佩戴护目镜。

4. 语法解析

① 情态动词的被动语态

原句：Any fuel spilled on the clothing or skin must be removed as soon as possible. 拆解：主语 Any fuel，后接过去分词短语 spilled on the clothing or skin 作后置定语；谓语 must be removed 是「情态动词 + be + 过去分词」的被动结构；as soon as possible 作状语修饰 removed。 要点：手册里的禁令与要求常用 must be + 过去分词，句子主语是动作的承受者而不是执行者。易错点：不要把 spilled 误当成谓语——它只是修饰 fuel 的定语，真正的谓语是 must be removed。可迁移：the fuel drained after fueling（放出的油）、any item left in the tank（留在油箱里的物品）都是这种「名词 + 过去分词短语」的压缩定语。

② 状语从句的省略与祈使句主句

原句：When fueling from pits or cabinets, follow the same procedures as when using a truck. 拆解：When fueling from pits or cabinets 是时间状语从句，完整形式为 When you are fueling…，主语与主句一致且含 be，故省略「主语 + be」；主句 follow the same procedures 是祈使句；as when using a truck 是 as 引导的比较状语从句，同样省略了 you are。 要点：「When + 现在分词」是技术手册交代操作前提的标准写法，省略后仍保留主动关系，所以只能用 fueling，不能写成 fueled。as when… 中的 as 是连词，表示「与……时相同」，不能换成 like。注意不要把 When fueling 读成介词短语——它自带隐含主语。

③ as…as 比较结构与分数表达

原句：The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair. 拆解：主语 The close tolerances，of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms 为介词短语作后置定语；谓语 can be damaged or blocked 是情态动词 + 被动语态，or 并列两个过去分词；by particles 引出施动者；as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair 是形容词原级比较结构，作 particles 的后置修饰。 要点：as + 形容词原级 + as 表示「小到/大到某种程度」，用于强调量级而非比较两个对象。易错点：1⁄20 the diameter 是「分数 + the + 名词」的固定语序，中间不加 of，也不说 1⁄20 of diameter；同类还有 twice the length、half the weight。can be damaged or blocked 再次印证情态动词被动语态在描述风险时的通用性。

④ 现在分词作伴随状语 + so that 目的从句内嵌 if 条件从句

原句：Approach the aircraft with caution, positioning the fuel truck so that if it is necessary to depart quickly, no backing needed. 拆解：主句是祈使句 Approach the aircraft with caution；positioning the fuel truck 是现在分词短语作伴随状语，逻辑主语是隐含的 you；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句内部又套了一个 if it is necessary to depart quickly 条件从句；no backing needed 是 there is no backing needed 的省略形式（口语化的 there 存在句省略）。 要点：一句里出现「祈使句 + 分词状语 + so that + if」的多层嵌套，是手册讲操作要领的典型句式。要点在于分清层次：positioning 修饰主句动作，so that 说明目的，if 只在目的从句内部设条件。no backing needed 属于省略写法，正式改写为 no backing is needed；阅读时不要把 needed 当成谓语动词。

⑤ that 引导的表语从句 + 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：The greatest static electricity concern around aircraft is that during flight, the aircraft moving through the air causes static electricity to build in the airframe. 拆解：主语 The greatest static electricity concern，around aircraft 为介词短语作后置定语；系动词 is 后接 that 引导的表语从句。从句内部：主语 the aircraft，moving through the air 是现在分词短语作后置定语（= which moves through the air）；谓语 causes，宾语 static electricity，宾语补足语 to build in the airframe（不定式）。 要点：causes + 宾语 + to do 是「致使」结构，to 不能省略，也不能改成 causes…building。现在分词短语作后置定语表示主动、进行，与过去分词作定语（如 the damaged tank）形成对照。表语从句只能用 that 引导，不能用 what；that 在从句中不充当成分，只起连接作用。
