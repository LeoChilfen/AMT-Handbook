Lithium-Ion Batteries

通用 Ch 12 · Lithium-Ion Batteries · 原文 PDF 第 535 页

1. 原文

Lithium-ion batteries are the primary type of battery for many consumer type of equipment, such as cell phones, battery-powered tools, and computers, but now they are also being used in commercial and military aircraft. The FAA has certified lithium-ion batteries to be used on aircraft and one of the first aircraft to utilize the lithium-ion battery is the Boeing 787. The three primary functional components of a lithium-ion battery are the positive and negative electrodes and electrolyte. Generally, the negative electrode of a conventional lithium-ion cell is made from carbon. The positive electrode is a metal-oxide, and the electrolyte is a lithium salt in an organic solvent. The electrochemical roles of the electrodes reverse between anode and cathode, depending on the direction of current flow through the cell. Lithium-ion batteries can be dangerous under some conditions and can pose a safety hazard since they contain, unlike other rechargeable batteries, a flammable electrolyte and are also kept pressurized. Under certain conditions, they can overheat and a fire can occur. The Boeing 787 aircraft utilizes two large 32V 8 cell lithium-ion batteries. These batteries are much lighter and more powerful than Ni-Cad batteries used in similar-sized aircraft. These batteries can produce 150 A for airplane power up.

2. 中文翻译

锂离子电池是许多消费类设备——例如手机、电动工具和计算机——的主要电池类型，但如今它们也被用于商用和军用飞机。FAA 已认证锂离子电池可用于飞机，而最早采用锂离子电池的飞机之一是波音 787。锂离子电池的三个主要功能部件是正极、负极和电解液。通常，常规锂离子单体电池的负极由碳制成。正极是一种金属氧化物，电解液是溶解在有机溶剂中的锂盐。电极的电化学角色在阳极与阴极之间反转，取决于通过单体电池的电流方向。锂离子电池在某些情况下可能很危险并构成安全隐患，因为与其他可充电电池不同，它们含有可燃的电解液，而且还处于加压状态。在某些情况下，它们会过热并可能起火。波音 787 飞机使用两块大型 32V 8 单体锂离子电池。这些电池比同尺寸飞机所使用的镍镉电池轻得多、功率也大得多。这些电池可为飞机通电提供 150 A。

4. 语法解析

① 现在完成时 + 不定式被动 + one of the first …

原句：The FAA has certified lithium-ion batteries to be used on aircraft and one of the first aircraft to utilize the lithium-ion battery is the Boeing 787. 拆解：and 连接两个并列分句。前一分句主语 The FAA，谓语 has certified（现在完成时），宾语 lithium-ion batteries，宾语补足语 to be used on aircraft（不定式被动）；后一分句主语 one of the first aircraft，不定式 to utilize the lithium-ion battery 作后置定语，系动词 is，表语 the Boeing 787。 要点：certify sth to be used 表示「认证某物可供……使用」，被认证对象作宾语、使用方式用不定式被动，不能写成 to use；one of the first + 复数名词后接不定式作定语，谓语用单数 is，因为主语中心词是 one 而非 aircraft。

② since 引导原因状语从句 + 插入语 + 并列谓语

原句：Lithium-ion batteries can be dangerous under some conditions and can pose a safety hazard since they contain, unlike other rechargeable batteries, a flammable electrolyte and are also kept pressurized. 拆解：主句有两个并列谓语 can be dangerous … 和 can pose a safety hazard；since 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 they，谓语为 contain … 与 are also kept pressurized 两个并列成分；unlike other rechargeable batteries 为插入语，把 contain 与其宾语 a flammable electrolyte 隔开。 要点：since 表原因时译作「因为、由于」，语气比 because 弱、比 as 正式，技术说明文常用；插入语由两个逗号夹住，翻译时宜提前为「与其他……不同」；are kept pressurized 是被动语态，指电池「被保持加压状态」，不能译成主动的「加压」。

③ 现在分词短语作状语

原句：The electrochemical roles of the electrodes reverse between anode and cathode, depending on the direction of current flow through the cell. 拆解：主句主语 The electrochemical roles（of the electrodes 为后置定语），谓语 reverse，状语 between anode and cathode；逗号后 depending on … 为现在分词短语作伴随/条件状语，其逻辑主语是主句主语 roles，即「角色取决于……」。 要点：现在分词状语的逻辑主语须与主句主语一致；介词 on 后接名词短语，若接动作须用动名词；同类表达还有 varying with、according to；不要写成 depended on，过去分词表被动，与这里「角色主动取决于」的逻辑相反。

④ much 修饰比较级 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：These batteries are much lighter and more powerful than Ni-Cad batteries used in similar-sized aircraft. 拆解：主语 These batteries，系动词 are，两个并列的比较级表语 much lighter 和 more powerful，than 引导比较状语；much 修饰比较级表程度；used in similar-sized aircraft 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 Ni-Cad batteries（相当于 that are used …）。 要点：修饰比较级用 much、far、even、a lot，不能用 very；比较级本身已含「更」，不能再叠加 more；of 后置的过去分词与被修饰名词是被动关系（电池「被使用」），若换成 using 则主被动颠倒；similar-sized 是「形容词 + 名词 + -ed」的复合定语。
