1. 原文

The terminology, theory, and concepts of weight and balance that applies to airplanes also applies to weight-shift control aircraft and powered parachutes. Weight is still weight, and the balance point is still the balance point. However, there are a few differences that need to be discussed. Before reading about the specifics of weight and balance on weight-shift control aircraft and powered parachutes, be sure to read about their aerodynamic characteristics in Chapter 5, Physics.

Weight-Shift Control Aircraft

Weight-shift control aircraft and powered parachutes do not fall under the same Code of Federal Regulations that govern certified airplanes and helicopters and, therefore, do not have TCDS or the same type of FAA-mandated weight and balance reports. Weight and balance information and guidelines are left to the individual owners and the companies with which they work in acquiring this type of aircraft. Overall, the industry that is supplying these aircraft is regulating itself well, and the safety record is good for those aircraft being operated by experienced pilots.

Some trikes have only one seat and a fuel tank, so the only variables for a flight are amount of fuel and weight of the pilot. Some trikes have two seats and a small storage bin in addition to the fuel tank.

The most significant factor affecting the weight and balance of a trike is the weight of the pilot; if the aircraft has two seats, the weight of the passenger must be considered. The trike acts somewhat like a single main rotor helicopter because the weight of the aircraft is hanging like a pendulum under the wing. When the trike is in flight, the weight of the aircraft is hanging from the wing attach point. The weight of the engine and fuel is behind this point, the passenger is almost directly below this point, and the pilot is forward of this point. The balance of the aircraft is determined by how all these weights compare.

The wing attach point, with respect to the wing keel, is an adjustable location. The attach point can be loosened and moved slightly forward or slightly aft, depending on the weight of the occupants. For example, if the aircraft is flown by a person that weighs more, the attach point can be moved a little farther aft, bringing the wing forward, to compensate for the change in CG.

Powered Parachutes

Powered parachutes have many of the same characteristics as weight-shift aircraft when it comes to weight and balance. They have the same limited loading, with only one or two seats and a fuel tank. They also act like a pendulum, with the weight of the aircraft hanging beneath the inflated wing (parachute). The point at which the inflated wing attaches to the structure of the aircraft is adjustable to compensate for pilots and passengers of varying weights. With a very heavy pilot, the wing attach point would be moved forward to prevent the aircraft from being too nose heavy.

重量转移操纵飞机

重量转移操纵飞机以及动力伞，都不受管辖持证飞机和直升机的同一部《联邦法规》约束，因此没有型号合格证数据表（TCDS），也没有 FAA 强制要求的同类型重量与平衡报告。重量与平衡的信息和指南，交由航空器所有者本人以及协助他们购置这类航空器的公司负责。总体而言，提供这类航空器的行业自我管理良好，由有经验的驾驶员驾驶时，这类航空器的安全记录良好。

对三角翼重量与平衡影响最大的因素是驾驶员体重；如果该航空器有两个座位，还必须考虑旅客体重。三角翼的行为有点像单旋翼直升机，因为航空器的重量像单摆一样悬挂在机翼下方。三角翼飞行时，航空器的重量悬挂在机翼连接点上。发动机和燃油的重量在此点之后，旅客几乎就在此点正下方，驾驶员则在此点之前。航空器的平衡由所有这些重量相互比较的结果决定。

机翼连接点相对于机翼龙骨是一个可调整的位置。可以松开连接点，根据乘员体重将其稍微前移或稍后移。例如，如果驾驶该航空器的人体重较大，可以把连接点再往后移一点，使机翼前移，以补偿重心的变化。

动力伞

就重量与平衡而言，动力伞具有与重量转移操纵航空器许多相同的特性。它们的装载同样有限，只有一两个座位和一个油箱。它们同样像单摆一样工作，航空器的重量悬挂在充气机翼（伞）下方。充气机翼与航空器结构的连接点可以调整，以补偿体重各异的驾驶员和旅客。如果驾驶员很重，应把机翼连接点前移，以防航空器过于头重。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + 主谓一致（原文有瑕疵）

原句：The terminology, theory, and concepts of weight and balance that applies to airplanes also applies to weight-shift control aircraft and powered parachutes. 拆解：主语为三项并列 The terminology, theory, and concepts；of weight and balance 为后置定语；that applies to airplanes 为定语从句修饰前面的名词；主句谓语 also applies。 要点：原文中定语从句和主句都用单数 applies，是受靠近的 concepts 影响而写错；按语法，先行词 concepts 为复数，从句应为 that apply。读手册要能识别这种原文瑕疵，理解时仍按复数含义处理。that 在从句中作主语，不能省略。

② Before + 动名词 + 祈使句

原句：Before reading about the specifics of weight and balance on weight-shift control aircraft and powered parachutes, be sure to read about their aerodynamic characteristics in Chapter 5, Physics. 拆解：Before + 动名词短语作时间状语，其逻辑主语与主句祈使句隐含的 you 一致；主句 be sure to read about … 为祈使句；in Chapter 5, Physics 为状语，Physics 是章名的同位语。 要点：before 作介词时后接动名词，不能接不定式，易错写成 before to read。be sure to do 意为「务必做」，比 must 委婉。Chapter 5, Physics 这种「编号 + 名称」同位写法在英文技术书中常见，译作「第 5 章“物理学”」。

③ 定语从句 + 并列否定谓语

原句：and powered parachutes do not fall under the same Code of Federal Regulations that govern certified airplanes and helicopters and, therefore, do not have TCDS or the same type of FAA-mandated weight and balance reports. 拆解：主语由上文承接，与 powered parachutes 并列；谓语为两个并列否定式 do not fall under … and … do not have …；that govern certified airplanes and helicopters 为定语从句修饰 the same Code of Federal Regulations；therefore 为插入的连接副词。 要点：两个 do not 靠 and 并列，第二个不能省略，否则会读成「有 TCDS」。fall under 意为「属于……的管辖范围」。the same … that … 是固定搭配，表示「管辖……的同一部（法规）」。

④ by + how 宾语从句

原句：The balance of the aircraft is determined by how all these weights compare. 拆解：主语 The balance of the aircraft + 被动谓语 is determined + by + how 引导的宾语从句，作介词 by 的宾语；从句中 all these weights 为主语，compare 为不及物动词。 要点：介词后可以接 how、what、whether 等引导的从句，但不能直接接 that 从句。compare 在此为不及物动词「相比较」，不是 compare A with B 的及物用法。翻译时把 how 从句提前：「由这些重量相互比较的结果决定」。

⑤ 介词短语作插入语

原句：The wing attach point, with respect to the wing keel, is an adjustable location. 拆解：主干 The wing attach point is an adjustable location；with respect to the wing keel 为介词短语作插入语，用两个逗号隔开，说明「就机翼龙骨而言」。 要点：with respect to 意为「相对于、就……而言」，是技术文本高频短语，可移到句首而句意不变。插入语会打断主谓，阅读时先跳过逗号内容抓住 is。adjustable 后常接 for，表示「为……而可调」。
