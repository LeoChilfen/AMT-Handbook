1. 原文

The concept of power involves the previously discussed topic of work, which was a force being applied over a measured distance, but adds one more consideration—time.

In other words, how long it takes to accomplish the work.

If someone asked the average person if they could lift one million pounds 5 feet off the ground, the answer most assuredly would be no. This person would probably assume that they are to lift it all at once. What if they are given 365 days to lift it, and could lift small amounts of weight at a time? The work involved would be the same, regardless of how long it took to lift the weight, but the power required is different. If the weight is to be lifted in a shorter period of time, it will take more power. The formula for power is as follows:

Power = Force × distance ÷ time The units for power will be foot-pounds per minute, foot- pounds per second, inch-pounds per minute or second, and possibly mile-pounds per hour. The units depend on how distance and time are measured.

Many years ago, there was a desire to compare the power of the newly evolving steam engine to that of horses.

People wanted to know how many horses the steam engine was equivalent to. The value we know currently as one horsepower (hp) was developed, and it is equal to 550 foot- pounds per second (ft-lb/s) because of this. It was found that the average horse could lift a weight of 550 lb, one foot off the ground, in one second. The values we use today, in order to convert power to horsepower, are as follows:

1 hp = 550 ft-lb/s 1 hp = 33,000 ft-lb/min.

1 hp = 375 mile pounds per hour (mi-lb/hr.)

1 hp = 746 watts (electricity conversion)

To convert power to horsepower, divide the power by the appropriate conversion based on the units being used.

Example: What power would be needed, and also horsepower, to raise the GE-90 turbofan engine into position to install it on a Boeing 777-300 airplane? The engine weighs 19,000 lb, and it must be lifted 4 ft in 2 minutes.

Power = Force × distance ÷ time = 19,000 lb × 4 ft ÷ 2 min.

Hp = 38,000 ft-lb/min. ÷ 33,000 ft-lb/min.

Hp = 1.15 The hoist that will be used to raise this engine into position will need to be powered by an electric motor because the average person will not be able to generate 1.15 hp in their arms for the necessary 2 minutes.

2. 中文翻译

功率这一概念涉及前面讨论过的功——功是力在一定距离上的作用——但还要再加一个因素：时间。

换句话说，就是完成该功需要多长时间。

如果问一个普通人能否把一百万磅举离地面 5 英尺，答案多半是「不能」。这个人大概会以为要一次把它全部举起来。可如果给他 365 天来举，并且每次只举一小部分重量呢？无论举这个重量花多长时间，所做的功都一样，但所需的功率不同。如果要在更短的时间内把重量举起，就需要更大的功率。功率的公式如下：

功率 = 力 × 距离 ÷ 时间 功率的单位是英尺-磅每分、英尺-磅每秒、英寸-磅每分或每秒，也可能是英里-磅每小时。单位取决于距离和时间如何计量。

许多年前，人们希望把当时新出现的蒸汽机的功率与马的功率作比较。

人们想知道一台蒸汽机相当于多少匹马。于是产生了我们今天所知的「一马力（hp）」这一数值，正因如此，它等于 550 英尺-磅每秒（ft-lb/s）。当时发现，一匹普通的马能在 1 秒内把 550 lb 的重物举离地面 1 英尺。如今把功率换算为马力所用的数值如下：

1 hp = 550 ft-lb/s 1 hp = 33,000 ft-lb/min.

1 hp = 375 mile pounds per hour (mi-lb/hr.)

1 hp = 746 watts (electricity conversion)

把功率换算为马力时，根据所用单位除以相应的换算系数。

例：要把 GE-90 涡扇发动机吊起到位、装到 Boeing 777-300 飞机上，需要多大的功率，合多少马力？该发动机重 19,000 lb，必须在 2 分钟内吊起 4 ft。

功率 = 力 × 距离 ÷ 时间 = 19,000 lb × 4 ft ÷ 2 min.

Hp = 38,000 ft-lb/min. ÷ 33,000 ft-lb/min.

Hp = 1.15 用来把该发动机吊起到位的起重设备必须由电动机驱动，因为普通人的双臂无法在所需的 2 分钟内输出 1.15 hp。

4. 语法解析

① 与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句 + 宾语从句中的 if

原句：If someone asked the average person if they could lift one million pounds 5 feet off the ground, the answer most assuredly would be no.

拆解：外层 If someone asked … 是虚拟条件从句（与现在／将来事实相反，用一般过去时 asked）；主句 the answer most assuredly would be no 用 would + 动词原形；内层 if they could lift … 是 asked 的宾语从句，此处 if = whether，不是条件从句，因此用 could 而非 will。

要点：一个句子里出现两个 if，功能完全不同：前一个是条件，后一个是「是否」。虚拟条件句的公式是「从句过去时，主句 would + 原形」；be 动词在从句中习惯用 were（If someone were …）。

② regardless of + 名词性从句，过去分词作后置定语

原句：The work involved would be the same, regardless of how long it took to lift the weight, but the power required is different.

拆解：前一分句主语 The work involved（involved 为过去分词后置定语 = that is involved）；would be the same 用 would 表推测性判断；regardless of how long it took to lift the weight 是让步状语，of 后接 how 引导的宾语从句，took 用过去时与虚拟语气一致；but 后 the power required 同样是过去分词后置定语。

要点：regardless of 后只能接名词、动名词或名词性从句，不能直接接句子（不写 regardless of it took …）。involved / required / discussed 这类单个过去分词修饰名词时常后置，相当于省略了 which is。

③ it 作形式主语 + that 从句，插入成分

原句：It was found that the average horse could lift a weight of 550 lb, one foot off the ground, in one second.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 从句；从句主干 the average horse could lift a weight of 550 lb；one foot off the ground 是插入的距离补语，说明举升高度；in one second 是时间状语。

要点：It was found / It is estimated / It has been shown that … 是技术文献引出结论的固定被动句式，译作「据发现／据估算」。插入成分 one foot off the ground 用逗号隔开，读句时先跳过它抓主干，再回填「举多高」。

④ 名词性从句作介词宾语

原句：The units depend on how distance and time are measured.

拆解：主语 The units；谓语 depend on；宾语是 how 引导的名词性从句 how distance and time are measured（从句内部为被动语态，语序仍为陈述语序）。

要点：介词后的疑问词从句必须用陈述语序，不能写成 how are distance and time measured。depend on / rely on / be based on 后接 how、what、whether 从句是技术说明的常见结构，表示「取决于……如何……」。
