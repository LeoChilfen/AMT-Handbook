Oxygen Servicing Equipment

通用 Ch 01 · Oxygen Servicing Equipment · 原文 PDF 第 46 页

1. 原文

Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper types of servicing equipment to be used. Two personnel are required to service an aircraft with gaseous oxygen. One person is stationed at the control valves of the servicing equipment, and one person is stationed where they can observe the pressure in the aircraft system. Communication between the two people is required in the event of an emergency.

Do not service aircraft with oxygen during fueling, defueling, or other maintenance work that could provide a source of ignition. Oxygen servicing of aircraft is to be accomplished outside hangars.

Oxygen used on aircraft is available in two types: gaseous and liquid. The type to use on any specific aircraft depends on the type of equipment in the aircraft. Gaseous oxygen is stored in large steel cylinders, while liquid oxygen (commonly referred to as LOX) is stored and converted into a usable gas in a liquid oxygen converter.

Oxygen is commercially available in three general types:

aviator’s breathing, industrial, and medical. Only oxygen marked “Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen” that meets Federal Specification BB-0-925A, Grade A, or its equivalent is to be used in aircraft breathing oxygen systems. Industrial oxygen may contain impurities that could cause the pilot, crew, and/ or passengers to become sick. Medical oxygen, although pure, contains water that can freeze in the cold temperatures found at the altitudes where oxygen is necessary.

2. 中文翻译

在对任何航空器实施勤务之前，应查阅该型航空器的维修手册，确定应当使用何种类型的勤务设备。用气态氧对航空器实施勤务需要两人配合：一人守在勤务设备的控制阀处，一人守在能够观察航空器系统压力读数的位置。出现紧急情况时，两人之间必须保持通信联络。

不得在加油、抽放燃油或其他可能产生点火源的维修作业期间对航空器充氧。航空器的充氧作业必须在机库外进行。

航空器上使用的氧气有气态和液态两种。某一具体型号的航空器用哪一种，取决于该机上所装氧气设备的类型。气态氧储存于大型钢瓶中；液氧（通常简称 LOX）储存于液氧转换器中，并在其中转变为可用的气体。

氧气在商业上一般分为三大类：

即飞行员呼吸用氧、工业用氧和医用氧。只有标注「Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen」（飞行员呼吸用氧）、符合联邦规范 BB-0-925A A 级或其等效规范的氧气，才可用于航空器的呼吸用氧系统。工业氧可能含有杂质，会使飞行员、机组成员和／或乘客身体不适。医用氧虽然纯净，但其中含有水分，在必须供氧的高空低温环境下会结冰。

4. 语法解析

① Before servicing any aircraft, consult …（介词 + 动名词 + 目的不定式）

原句：Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper types of servicing equipment to be used.

拆解：Before servicing any aircraft 是「介词 + 动名词」构成的时间状语；主句为祈使句 consult …；to determine … 为目的不定式；to be used 为不定式被动，作后置定语修饰 equipment。

要点：before 后接 -ing 而不是动词原形（before to service 是错的）。to be used 表示「待使用的、应当使用的」，比 which are to be used 简洁。手册的祈使句常省略主语 you，翻译时应补出「应……」。

② One person is stationed at …, and one person is stationed where …（并列 + where 状语从句）

原句：One person is stationed at the control valves of the servicing equipment, and one person is stationed where they can observe the pressure in the aircraft system.

拆解：两个并列分句，谓语都是 be stationed（驻守、值守的被动结构）；where they can observe … 是 where 引导的地点状语从句，前面的名词没有先行词，因此不是定语从句。

要点：station 作动词时常用被动 be stationed at／in，表示「被安排守在某处」。此处 they 指「那一个人」，正式文本可写 he or she。翻译时两个分句宜说成「一人……，一人……」，对应原文的一一分工。

③ Only oxygen marked … is to be used（be to do 表强制要求）

原句：Only oxygen marked “Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen” that meets Federal Specification BB-0-925A, Grade A, or its equivalent is to be used in aircraft breathing oxygen systems.

拆解：主语中心词 oxygen；marked … 为过去分词短语作后置定语；that meets … or its equivalent 为定语从句，先行词是 oxygen（含 marked 短语整体），从句谓语 meets 用单数；谓语 is to be used 表「必须／应当被使用」。

要点：be to do 在规范文本里表强制要求，接近 must。marked 与 that meets 两层定语叠加，译成中文按「标注……且符合……的氧气」顺下来。or its equivalent 是「或其等效规范」，不能漏译。
