1. 原文

The following procedures are typical of those used to start many turbine engines. There are, however, wide variations in the starting procedures used for turbine engines, and no attempts are to be made to use these procedures in the actual starting of an engine. These procedures are presented only as a general guide for familiarization with typical procedures and methods. In the starting of all turbine engines, refer to the detailed procedures contained in the applicable manufacturer’s instructions or their approved equivalent. Most turbofan engines can be started by either air turbine or electrical starters. Air-turbine starters use compressed air from an external source as discussed earlier. Fuel is turned on either by moving the start lever to “idle/start” position or by opening a fuel shutoff valve. This time interval, if exceeded, indicates a malfunction has occurred and the start must be discontinued. Most turbofan engine controls consist of a power lever, reversing levers, and start levers. Newer aircraft have replaced the start levers with a fuel switch. [Figure 1-18] Turbofan engines also use all the normal instruments speeds, (percent of total rpm) exhaust gas temperature, fuel flow, oil pressure, and temperature. An instrument that measures the amount of thrust being delivered is the engine pressure ratio. This measures the ratio between the inlet pressures to the outlet pressure of the turbine. The following procedures are useful only as a general guide and are included to show the sequence of events in starting a turbofan engine.

  1. If the engine is so equipped, place the power lever in the “idle” position.
  1. Turn the fuel boost pump(s) switch on.
  1. A fuel inlet pressure indicator reading ensures fuel is being delivered to engine fuel pump inlet.
  1. Turn engine starter switch on. Note that the engine rotates to a preset limit. Check for oil pressure.
  1. Turn ignition switch on. (This is usually accomplished by moving the start lever toward the “on” position. A micro switch connected to the leveler turns on the ignition.)
  1. Move the start lever to “idle” or “start” position, this starts fuel flow into the engine. 7. Engine start (light off) is indicated by a rise in exhaust gas temperature.
  1. If a two-spool engine, check rotation of fan or N1.
  1. Check for proper oil pressure.
  1. Turn engine starter switch off at proper speeds.
  1. After engine stabilizes at idle, ensure that none of the engine limits are exceeded.
  1. Newer aircraft drop off the starter automatically.

2. 中文翻译

下列程序是起动许多涡轮发动机时的典型做法。不过，各型涡轮发动机所用的起动程序差别很大，切勿把这些程序用于发动机的实际起动。列出这些程序只是作为熟悉典型程序和方法的通用指南。起动任何涡轮发动机时，都应查阅相应制造商说明或其经批准等效文件中所载的详细程序。大多数涡扇发动机既可用空气涡轮起动机起动，也可用电动起动机起动。空气涡轮起动机使用来自外部气源的压缩空气，前文已述及。接通燃油有两种方式：把起动手柄移到「idle/start」位，或打开燃油关断活门。若使用空气涡轮起动机，（原文此处为图 1-18 发动机仪表板标注文字残片，略）则发动机应在接通燃油后的预定时间内「点着火」（light off）。这一时间间隔一旦超过，就说明发生了故障，必须中止起动。大多数涡扇发动机的操纵装置由功率杆、反推杆和起动手柄组成。较新的飞机已用燃油电门取代起动手柄。［图 1-18］涡扇发动机还使用全部常规仪表：转速（原文作 speeds，以占额定转速的百分比表示）、排气温度、燃油流量、滑油压力和温度。测量所输出推力大小的仪表是发动机压力比（EPR）。它测量的是涡轮进口压力与出口压力之比。下列程序只作通用指南，用来说明涡扇发动机起动的事件顺序。

  1. 若发动机装有该装置，把功率杆置于「慢车（idle）」位。
  1. 接通燃油增压泵电门。
  1. 燃油进口压力表有读数，即表明燃油已供到发动机燃油泵进口。
  1. 接通发动机起动机电门。注意发动机将被带到预定的转速上限。检查滑油压力。
  1. 接通点火电门。（通常是把起动手柄移向「开」位来实现；连杆上连接的微动开关随即接通点火。）
  1. 把起动手柄移到「慢车」或「起动」位，燃油随即流入发动机。7. 排气温度上升，即表明发动机已起动（点着火）。
  1. 若是双转子发动机，检查风扇或 N1 的转动。
  1. 检查滑油压力是否正常。
  1. 在规定转速下关断发动机起动机电门。
  1. 发动机在慢车稳定后，确认各项发动机限制均未超出。
  1. 较新的飞机起动机自动脱开。

3. 专业词汇

英文 词性 中文 本节语境
start lever n. 起动手柄 移到「idle/start」位即同时接通燃油并触发点火
light off n./v. 点着火、点燃 排气温度开始上升即标志 light off，有时间限制
exhaust gas temperature n. 排气温度 判断是否点着火、是否超限的首要参数
engine pressure ratio (EPR) n. 发动机压力比 用涡轮进出口压力之比来表示所输出的推力
fuel shutoff valve n. 燃油关断活门 打开它就等于向发动机供油，是另一种供油方式
boost pump n. 增压泵 起动第二步打开，保证燃油泵进口有压力
two-spool engine n. 双转子发动机 需分别检查风扇（N1）与高压转子的转动
micro switch n. 微动开关 装在手柄连杆上，手柄一动即接通点火

4. 语法解析

① there be + be to（义务）并列长句

原句：There are, however, wide variations in the starting procedures used for turbine engines, and no attempts are to be made to use these procedures in the actual starting of an engine.

拆解：前半句是 there be 存在句，主语 wide variations，in the starting procedures 是范围状语，used for turbine engines 是过去分词短语后置修饰 procedures，however 是插入语；and 连接后半句，主语 no attempts，谓语 are to be made 是 be to 结构表义务/禁止，to use these procedures… 是 attempt 的定语（attempts to do）。

要点：be to do 在规章、手册中表「应当/不得」，no attempts are to be made = 「不得试图」；如果写成 no attempts are made 就只是陈述事实，失掉禁止语气。there be 句的真正主语在后面，谓语随 variations 用复数。

② either…or 连接两个 by 短语（平行结构）

原句：Fuel is turned on either by moving the start lever to “idle/start” position or by opening a fuel shutoff valve.

拆解：主干 Fuel is turned on 是被动语态；either…or… 连接两个方式状语 by moving… 与 by opening…，两者结构完全对称（by + 动名词 + 宾语）；to “idle/start” position 是 move 的方向状语。

要点：either…or 必须连接同类成分（这里是两个 by 短语），不能一个接名词、一个接从句；两个 by 都不能省，否则平行被破坏。这种「两种可选手段」的句式在程序手册里极常见，翻译用「或……或……」/「既……也可……」。

③ 定语从句 + 现在分词被动式作定语

原句：An instrument that measures the amount of thrust being delivered is the engine pressure ratio.

拆解：主语 An instrument 带 that 引导的定语从句；从句谓语 measures，宾语 the amount of thrust，being delivered 是现在分词被动式作后置定语修饰 thrust（正在被输出的推力）；主句谓语 is，表语 the engine pressure ratio。

要点：being + 过去分词表示「正在被……」，与过去分词 delivered（已输出/被输出，无进行意味）有别；thrust being delivered 是「当时正在输出的推力」，正是 EPR 的定义语境。表语倒装（主语较长时把表语放句末）使句子平衡。

④ 插入的条件从句省略 + 省略 that 的宾语从句

原句：This time interval, if exceeded, indicates a malfunction has occurred and the start must be discontinued.

拆解：主语 This time interval 后插入 if exceeded，是 if it is exceeded 的省略（主语与主句相同、谓语含 be，故一并省略）；主句谓语 indicates 后接两个并列的省略 that 的宾语从句：a malfunction has occurred 与 the start must be discontinued。

要点：状语从句省略的条件是「主语与主句一致 + 谓语含 be」，if exceeded 不能还原成 if exceeding。indicates 后省略 that 是常见现象，但并列的两个宾语从句各自要有完整主谓；第二个从句用 must be discontinued，被动语态强调「起动必须被中止」。
