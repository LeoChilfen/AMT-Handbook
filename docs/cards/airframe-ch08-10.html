Common Paint Troubles

机体 Ch 08 · Common Paint Troubles · 原文 PDF 第 407 页

1. 原文

Common problems that may occur during the painting of almost any project but are particularly noticeable and troublesome on the surfaces of an aircraft include poor adhesion, blushing, pinholes, sags and/or runs, “orange peel,” fisheyes, sanding scratches, wrinkling, and spray dust.

Poor Adhesion

• Improper cleaning and preparation of the surface to be finished.

• Application of the wrong primer.

• Incompatibility of the topcoat with the primer.

• Improper thinning of the coating material or selection of the wrong grade reducer.

• Improper mixing of materials.

• Contamination of the spray equipment and/or air supply.

Correction for poor adhesion requires a complete removal of the finish, a determination and correction of the cause, and a complete refinishing of the affected area.

Blushing

Blushing is the dull milky haze that appears in a paint finish. It occurs when moisture is trapped in the paint. Blushing forms when the solvents quickly evaporate from the sprayed coating, causing a drop in temperature that is enough to condense the water in the air. It usually forms when the humidity is above 80 percent. Other causes include:

• Incorrect temperature (below 60 °F or above 95 °F).

• Incorrect reducer (fast drying) being used.

• Excessively high air pressure at the spray gun.

If blushing is noticed during painting, a slow-drying reducer can sometimes be added to the paint mixture, and then the area resprayed. If blushing is found after the finish has dried, the area must be sanded down and repainted.

Pinholes

Pinholes are tiny holes, or groups of holes, that appear in the surface of the finish as a result of trapped solvents, air, or moisture. Examples include:

• Contaminants in the paint or air lines.

• Poor spraying techniques that allow excessively heavy or wet paint coats, which tend to trap moisture or solvent under the finish.

• Use of the wrong thinner or reducer, either too fast by quick drying the surface and trapping solvents or too slow and trapping solvents by subsequent topcoats.

If pinholes occur during painting, the equipment and painting technique must be evaluated before continuing. When dry, sand the surface smooth and then repaint.

Sags & Runs

Sags and runs are usually caused by applying too much paint to an area, by holding the spray gun too close to the surface, or moving the gun too slowly across the surface. Other causes include:

• Too much reducer in the paint (too thin).

• Incorrect spray gun setting of air-paint mixture.

Sags and runs can be avoided by following the recommended thinning instructions for the coatings being applied and taking care to use the proper spray gun techniques, especially on vertical surfaces and projected edges. Dried sags and runs must be sanded out and the surface repainted.

Orange Peel

“Orange peel” refers to the appearance of a bumpy surface, much like the skin of an orange. It can be the result of a number of factors with the first being the improper adjustment of the spray gun. Other causes include:

• Not enough reducer (too thick) or the wrong type reducer for the ambient temperature.

• Material not uniformly mixed.

• Forced drying method, either with fans or heat, is too quick.

• Too little flash time between coats.

• Spray painting when the ambient or substrate temperature is either too hot or too cold.

Light orange peel can be wet sanded or buffed out with polishing compound. In extreme cases, it has to be sanded smooth and resprayed.

Fisheyes

Fisheyes appear as small holes in the coating as it is being applied, which allows the underlying surface to be seen. Usually, it is due to the surface not being cleaned of all traces of silicone wax. If numerous fisheyes appear when spraying a surface, stop spraying and clean off all the wet paint. Then, thoroughly clean the surface to remove all traces of silicone with a silicone wax remover.

The most effective way to eliminate fisheyes is to ensure that the surface about to be painted is clean and free from any type of contamination. A simple and effective way to check this is referred to as a water break test. Using clean water, spray, pour, or gently hose down the surface to be painted. If the water beads up anywhere on the surface, it is not clean. The water should flatten out and cover the area with an unbroken film.

If the occasional fisheye appears when spraying, wait until the first coat sets up and then add a recommended amount of fisheye eliminator to the subsequent finish coats. Fisheyes may appear during touchup of a repair. A coat of sealer may help, but a complete removal of the finish may be the only solution.

One last check before spraying is to ensure that the air compressor has been drained of water, the regulator cleaned, and the system filters are clean or have been replaced so that this source of contamination is eliminated.

Sanding Scratches

Sanding scratches appear in the finish paint when the surface has not been properly sanded and/or sealed prior to spraying the finish coats. This usually shows up in nonmetal surfaces. Composite cowling, wood surfaces, and plastic fairings must be properly sanded and sealed before painting. The scratches may also appear if an overly rapid quick-drying thinner is used.

The only fix after the finish coat has set up is to sand down the affected areas using a finer grade of sandpaper, follow with a recommended sealer, and then repaint.

Wrinkling

Wrinkling is usually caused by trapped solvents and unequal drying of the paint finish due to excessively thick or solvent- heavy paint coats. Fast reducers can also contribute to wrinkling if the sprayed coat is not allowed to dry thoroughly. Thick coatings and quick-drying reducers allow the top surface of the coating to dry, trapping the solvents underneath. If another heavy coat is applied before the first one dries, wrinkles may result. It may also have the effect of lifting the coating underneath, almost with the same result as a paint stripper.

Rapid changes in ambient temperatures while spraying may cause an uneven release of the solvents, causing the surface to dry, shrink, and wrinkle. Making the mistake of using an incompatible thinner, or reducer, when mixing the coating materials may cause not only wrinkles but other problems as well. Wrinkled paint must be completely removed and the surface refinished.

Spray Dust

Spray dust is caused by the atomized spray particles from the gun becoming dry before reaching the surface being painted, thus failing to flow into a continuous film. This may be caused by:

• Incorrect spray gun setting of air pressure, paint flow, or spray pattern.

• Spray gun being held too far from the surface.

• Material being improperly thinned or the wrong reducers being used with the finish coats.

The affected area needs to be sanded and recoated.

2. 中文翻译

几乎所有项目在喷漆过程中都可能出现一些常见问题，而在飞机表面上，这些问题尤其明显、尤其麻烦，包括附着力差、发白、针孔、下坠和/或流挂、“橘皮”、鱼眼、砂纸痕、起皱以及干喷（漆尘）。

附着力差

• 待涂表面的清洁和预处理不当。

• 使用了错误的底漆。

• 面漆与底漆不相容。

• 涂料稀释不当，或选错了减薄剂等级。

• 材料混合不当。

• 喷涂设备和/或供气被污染。

附着力差的纠正办法是：彻底除去旧涂层，查明并纠正原因，然后对受影响区域重新做完整涂装。

发白

发白是漆膜中出现的暗淡乳白色雾状。它是水分被封在漆膜中造成的。当溶剂从喷好的涂层中迅速蒸发、使温度降到足以让空气中的水汽凝结时，就会形成发白。它通常在湿度高于 80% 时出现。其他原因包括：

• 温度不当（低于 60 °F 或高于 95 °F）。

• 使用了不正确的（快干型）减薄剂。

• 喷枪处气压过高。

如果喷漆过程中发现发白，有时可以往漆里加入慢干减薄剂，然后对该区域重新喷涂。如果漆膜干燥后才发现发白，则必须把该区域打磨掉重新喷涂。

针孔

针孔是漆膜表面上出现的细小孔洞或成群的孔洞，由被封闭的溶剂、空气或水分造成。例如：

• 油漆或空气管路中的污染物。

• 喷涂手法不当，造成漆层过厚或过湿，容易把水分或溶剂封在漆膜下面。

• 使用了错误的稀释剂或减薄剂：干燥过快会令表面迅速干燥而把溶剂封住，干燥过慢则会被后续面漆把溶剂封住。

如果喷漆过程中出现针孔，必须先评估设备和喷涂手法，再继续施工。干燥后把表面打磨平整，然后重新喷涂。

下坠与流挂

下坠和流挂通常是某一区域喷漆过多、喷枪距表面太近，或喷枪在表面上移动太慢造成的。其他原因包括：

• 漆中减薄剂过多（太稀）。

• 喷枪的空气—涂料混合比设定不正确。

只要按所喷涂料推荐的稀释说明操作，并注意采用正确的喷枪手法，尤其是在立面和突出边缘上，下坠和流挂是可以避免的。已经干固的下坠和流挂必须打磨掉并重新喷涂该表面。

橘皮

“橘皮”指表面呈凹凸麻点状，很像橘子皮。它可能是多种因素造成的，首先是喷枪调节不当。其他原因包括：

• 减薄剂不足（太稠），或所用减薄剂类型与环境温度不匹配。

• 材料搅拌不均匀。

• 强制干燥手段（用风扇或加热）太快。

• 道与道之间的闪干时间太短。

• 在环境温度或基材温度过高或过低时喷涂。

轻微的橘皮可以湿磨或用抛光膏抛掉。严重时必须打磨平整后重新喷涂。

鱼眼

鱼眼是涂层施工过程中出现的小孔，能看见下面的表面。通常是因为表面没有把硅酮蜡的痕迹清除干净。如果喷某个表面时出现大量鱼眼，应停止喷涂，把湿漆全部擦掉。然后用去硅酮蜡清洗剂彻底清洁表面，除去所有硅酮痕迹。

消除鱼眼最有效的办法，是确保即将喷涂的表面清洁、不含任何污染。一种简单有效的检查方法叫水膜破裂试验（water break test）。用清水喷洒、浇淋或轻轻冲洗待喷表面。如果水在表面任何地方聚成珠状，说明表面不干净；水应摊平并形成连续水膜覆盖该区域。

如果喷涂时偶尔出现鱼眼，可等第一道漆开始凝固定型，然后在后续面漆中加入推荐用量的鱼眼消除剂。修理后的补漆过程中也可能出现鱼眼。涂一道封闭漆（sealer）可能有帮助，但彻底除去旧涂层或许是唯一的办法。

喷漆前的最后一项检查，是确认空气压缩机已排过水、调压器已清洁、系统滤芯已清洁或更换，以消除这一污染来源。

砂纸痕

砂纸痕是在喷面漆之前表面打磨和/或封闭不到位时，出现在面漆上的痕迹。这通常出现在非金属表面上。复合材料整流罩、木质表面和塑料整流包皮在喷漆前必须正确打磨并封闭。使用干燥过快的稀释剂也可能导致砂纸痕。

面漆凝固之后唯一的补救办法，是用更细的砂纸把受影响区域打磨掉，再涂推荐的封闭漆，然后重新喷涂。

起皱

起皱通常是由被封闭的溶剂以及漆膜干燥不均造成的，而后者源于漆层过厚或含溶剂过多。如果喷上的漆层没有充分干燥，快干减薄剂也会促成起皱。厚涂层配快干减薄剂，会让涂层表层先干，把溶剂封在下面。如果在第一道未干时又喷一道厚漆，就可能起皱。它还可能把下层的涂层咬起，效果几乎与脱漆剂相同。

喷涂过程中环境温度骤变，可能使溶剂释放不均，导致表面干燥、收缩而起皱。调配涂料时误用不相容的稀释剂或减薄剂，不仅会引起起皱，还会带来其他问题。起皱的漆必须彻底清除并重新涂装表面。

干喷（漆尘）

漆尘是喷枪雾化的漆粒在到达被涂表面之前就已变干，因而无法流平成连续漆膜造成的。其原因可能是：

• 喷枪的气压、出漆量或雾形设定不正确。

• 喷枪距表面太远。

• 材料稀释不当，或面漆配用了错误的减薄剂。

受影响区域需要打磨并重新喷涂。

4. 语法解析

① 时间状语从句 + 分词短语作结果状语（含定语从句）

原句：Blushing forms when the solvents quickly evaporate from the sprayed coating, causing a drop in temperature that is enough to condense the water in the air. 拆解：主句 Blushing forms；when 引导时间状语从句，主语 the solvents，谓语 evaporate，from the sprayed coating 为来源状语；逗号后 causing a drop in temperature 为现在分词短语作结果状语；that is enough to condense the water in the air 为定语从句修饰 drop。 要点：causing 的逻辑主语是前面整个“溶剂快速蒸发”这件事，不是 Blushing。定语从句中 enough to do 表示“足以做……”，to condense 的逻辑主语仍是 a drop in temperature。

② 同位语插入 + 定语从句含 as a result of

原句：Pinholes are tiny holes, or groups of holes, that appear in the surface of the finish as a result of trapped solvents, air, or moisture. 拆解：主语 Pinholes，系动词 are，表语 tiny holes；or groups of holes 是同位成分，对 tiny holes 作补充说明；that appear in the surface of the finish 为定语从句修饰 holes；as a result of ... 为原因状语，trapped 为过去分词作前置定语。 要点：or 引导的同位语夹在中心词与定语从句之间，定语从句仍然修饰 tiny holes。as a result of 后接名词短语，不能接句子；trapped solvents 指“被封住的溶剂”，不是“陷阱”。

③ 被动语态 + by doing 方式状语并列

原句：Sags and runs can be avoided by following the recommended thinning instructions for the coatings being applied and taking care to use the proper spray gun techniques, especially on vertical surfaces and projected edges. 拆解：主语 Sags and runs，谓语 can be avoided（情态动词 + 被动）；by 后接两个并列动名词短语 following ... 和 taking care to use ...，作方式状语；being applied 为现在分词被动式作后置定语修饰 coatings；especially on ... 为补充说明的地点状语。 要点：by 后面必须是动名词，不能接不定式。并列的两个动名词共享同一个 by，第二个前面不再重复。being applied 指“正在施工的”涂层，与前面的 drying 阶段区分。

④ with 复合结构 + 动名词作介词宾语

原句：It can be the result of a number of factors with the first being the improper adjustment of the spray gun. 拆解：It 回指上句的“橘皮”现象；can be the result of a number of factors 为系表结构；with the first being the improper adjustment of the spray gun 是 with 复合结构，the first 为宾语，being ... 为现在分词补足语，作伴随说明。 要点：with 复合结构中宾语与补足语是主动关系用 being（此处 the first = the first factor，是“是”的关系）。the first 是省略了 factor 的序数词名词化用法，翻译时要补出“第一个因素”。

⑤ as 引导的时间从句 + which 指代整句

原句：Fisheyes appear as small holes in the coating as it is being applied, which allows the underlying surface to be seen. 拆解：主句 Fisheyes appear as small holes in the coating（as 为介词“作为”）；as it is being applied 为时间状语从句，it 指 the coating，is being applied 为现在进行时被动；which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面“涂层上出现小孔”这一整件事。 要点：句中两个 as 用法完全不同：第一个是介词，第二个是连词“当……时”。which 前有逗号，说明它修饰整个主句而不是紧邻的 coating。
