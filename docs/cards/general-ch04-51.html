Graphs & Charts

通用 Ch 04 · Graphs & Charts · 原文 PDF 第 147 页

1. 原文

Graphs and charts are frequently used to convey information graphically or information given certain conditions. They often utilize values shown on the “x” and “y” axes that can be projected up and across to arrive at a specific result. Also, when data is entered into a computer database, software programs can create a variety of different bar graphs, pie charts, and so forth, to graphically represent that data.

2. 中文翻译

曲线图与图表（graphs and charts）常用于以图形方式传达信息，或传达在一定条件下才成立的信息。它们往往利用“x”轴和“y”轴上标出的数值，把这些数值向上、横向投影，即可得出特定结果。此外，数据录入计算机数据库之后，软件程序可以生成各种不同的条形图、饼图等，以图形方式表示这些数据。

3. 专业词汇

4. 语法解析

① are frequently used to convey information … or information given certain conditions（被动 + 目的不定式 + 并列宾语）

原句：Graphs and charts are frequently used to convey information graphically or information given certain conditions.

拆解：主干是 Graphs and charts are frequently used；to convey … 是不定式作目的状语；convey 后并列两个宾语 information（被 graphically 修饰）与 information（被过去分词短语 given certain conditions 修饰）。

要点：同一个词 information 出现两次，第二次带过去分词定语，是技术文本为避免歧义而重复名词的写法。given certain conditions 意为「在给定条件下」，given 是「给定的」而非「给予」；也可理解为省略 which are 的定语从句。

② values shown on the “x” and “y” axes that can be projected …（过去分词定语 + that 从句的修饰对象）

原句：They often utilize values shown on the “x” and “y” axes that can be projected up and across to arrive at a specific result.

拆解：主句 They often utilize values；shown on the “x” and “y” axes 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 values；that can be projected up and across to arrive at a specific result 是定语从句，同样修饰 values（而不是紧邻的 axes）。

要点：定语从句紧跟名词时未必修饰该名词，要靠语义判断——能被「向上、横向投影」的是数值，不是坐标轴。up and across 指沿纵轴向上、沿横轴横向引线，两条线相交即得结果，这正是查图的动作。

③ when data is entered …, software programs can create …（时间状语从句 + 不定式表目的）

原句：Also, when data is entered into a computer database, software programs can create a variety of different bar graphs, pie charts, and so forth, to graphically represent that data.

拆解：when 引导时间状语从句，从句为被动 is entered into a computer database；主句 software programs can create a variety of different bar graphs, pie charts, and so forth；句末 to graphically represent that data 为不定式作目的状语。

要点：when 从句用一般现在时表经常性情况，主句用 can 表能力。insert／enter data into … 是固定搭配，介词用 into 不用 in。to graphically represent 是「分裂不定式」（副词插在 to 与动词之间），技术文本中可接受，正式写作也可改为 to represent … graphically。
