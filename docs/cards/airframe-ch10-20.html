Instrument Housings & Handling

机体 Ch 10 · Instrument Housings & Handling · 原文 PDF 第 595 页

1. 原文

Various materials are used to protect the inner workings of aircraft instruments, as well as to enhance the performance of the instrument and other equipment mounted in the immediate vicinity. Instrument cases can be one piece or multipiece. Aluminum alloy, magnesium alloy, steel, iron, and plastic are all common materials for case construction.

Electric instruments usually have a steel or iron alloy case to contain electromagnetic flux caused by current flow inside.

Despite their rugged outward appearance, all instruments, especially analog mechanical instruments, should be handled with special care and should never be dropped. A crack in an airtight instrument case renders it unairworthy. Ports should never be blown into and should be plugged until the instrument is installed. Cage all gyro instruments until mounted in the instrument panel. Observe all cautions written on the instrument housing and follow the manufacturer’s instruction for proper handling and shipping, as well as installation.

2. 中文翻译

人们使用各种材料来保护飞机仪表的内部机构，同时也用以提升仪表及紧邻安装的其他设备的性能。仪表壳体可以是单件式，也可以是多件式。铝合金、镁合金、钢、铁和塑料都是制造壳体的常用材料。

电动仪表通常采用钢或铁合金壳体，以约束内部电流流动所产生的电磁通量。

尽管外表看起来坚固耐用，所有仪表，尤其是模拟式机械仪表，都应特别小心地拿放，绝不可摔落。气密仪表壳体一旦出现裂纹，即判定为不适航。绝不可向接口内吹气，在仪表安装之前应将这些接口堵住。所有陀螺仪表在装到仪表板之前都应处于锁扣（caging）状态。注意仪表壳体上标注的所有告诫，并遵循制造商关于正确拿放、运输和安装的说明。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + as well as 并列不定式 + 过去分词定语

原句：Various materials are used to protect the inner workings of aircraft instruments, as well as to enhance the performance of the instrument and other equipment mounted in the immediate vicinity. 拆解：主语 Various materials，谓语 are used（被动）；to protect ... 与 as well as 后的 to enhance ... 为两个并列的不定式作目的状语；mounted in the immediate vicinity 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 equipment。 要点：as well as 连接的是两个不定式，不是两个名词，所以 enhance 前也带 to。mounted 的逻辑主语是 equipment，设备“被安装”，故用过去分词。

② 不定式目的状语 + 过去分词短语

原句：Electric instruments usually have a steel or iron alloy case to contain electromagnetic flux caused by current flow inside. 拆解：主语 Electric instruments，谓语 have，宾语 a steel or iron alloy case；to contain electromagnetic flux 为不定式作目的状语；caused by current flow inside 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 flux。 要点：to contain 修饰 have 的目的，不是修饰 case 的定语。caused by 引出 flux 的来源，inside 是副词，指壳体内。

③ Despite 让步 + 插入同位语 + 并列被动谓语

原句：Despite their rugged outward appearance, all instruments, especially analog mechanical instruments, should be handled with special care and should never be dropped. 拆解：Despite their rugged outward appearance 为让步状语；主语 all instruments，especially analog mechanical instruments 为插入的同位语；谓语为两个并列的被动结构 should be handled with special care 与 should never be dropped。 要点：despite 是介词，后接名词短语，不能接从句（不能写 despite they look rugged）。两个谓语共用一个主语，第二个谓语省略了主语与情态动词的重复。

④ render + 宾语 + 形容词宾补

原句：A crack in an airtight instrument case renders it unairworthy. 拆解：主语 A crack，in an airtight instrument case 为后置定语修饰 crack；谓语 renders，宾语 it（指该仪表），宾补 unairworthy（形容词）。 要点：render sth. + adj. 意为“使……变得……”，此处 it 指仪表而非裂纹。这句是被动判断句式的主动表达，译成“即判定为不适航”。

⑤ 被动语态 + 介词后置 + until 时间状语从句

原句：Ports should never be blown into and should be plugged until the instrument is installed. 拆解：主语 Ports，两个并列的被动谓语 should never be blown into 与 should be plugged；blown into 的介词 into 留在句末（介词后置）；until the instrument is installed 为时间状语从句。 要点：blow into the ports 变成被动后介词 into 不能丢，形成“be blown into”这种句末介词结构。until 从句表示“直到……为止”，与否定含义的 never 连用时要译成“在安装之前都不要”。

⑥ 祈使句并列 + 过去分词定语

原句：Observe all cautions written on the instrument housing and follow the manufacturer’s instruction for proper handling and shipping, as well as installation. 拆解：两个并列的祈使句：Observe all cautions ... 与 follow the manufacturer’s instruction ...；written on the instrument housing 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 cautions；for proper handling and shipping, as well as installation 为介词短语修饰 instruction。 要点：祈使句以动词原形开头，主语 you 省略。written 修饰 cautions（被写在壳体上的告诫），不是修饰 housing。
