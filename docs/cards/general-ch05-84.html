Dew Point

通用 Ch 05 · Dew Point · 原文 PDF 第 186 页

1. 原文

The dew point is the temperature to which humid air must be cooled at constant pressure to become saturated. If the temperature drops below the dew point, condensation occurs.

People who wear eyeglasses have experience going from cold outside air into a warm room and having moisture collect quickly on their glasses. This happens because the glasses were below the dew point temperature of the air in the room.

The air immediately in contact with the glasses was cooled below its dew point temperature, and some of the water vapor was condensed out. This principle is applied in determining the dew point. A vessel is cooled until water vapor begins to condense on its surface. The temperature at which this occurs is the dew point.

2. 中文翻译

露点是湿空气在定压下必须被冷却到才能达到饱和的温度。如果温度降到露点以下，就会发生凝结。

戴眼镜的人都有这样的经历：从寒冷的室外空气进入温暖的房间，镜片上会很快聚起水汽。这是因为镜片的温度低于室内空气的露点温度。

与镜片直接接触的那层空气被冷却到其露点温度以下，其中一部分水蒸气便凝结析出。测定露点正是应用了这一原理。将一个容器冷却，直到水蒸气开始在其表面凝结。发生凝结时的温度就是露点。

4. 语法解析

① 介词 + which 引导的定语从句：to which

原句：The dew point is the temperature to which humid air must be cooled at constant pressure to become saturated.

拆解：主语 the dew point，系动词 is，表语 the temperature；to which humid air must be cooled 是「介词 + which」定语从句，修饰 temperature（还原为 humid air must be cooled to the temperature）；at constant pressure 是条件状语；to become saturated 是目的状语。

要点：cool 表「冷却到某温度」时搭配介词 to，所以定语从句用 to which，不能用 in which 或 which。判断介词的方法是把先行词放回从句——cooled to the temperature。注意 to which 后面是完整语序（主语 humid air + 谓语 must be cooled），不提前倒装。

② have experience doing sth + 并列动名词

原句：People who wear eyeglasses have experience going from cold outside air into a warm room and having moisture collect quickly on their glasses.

拆解：People 后接 who wear eyeglasses 定语从句；谓语 have experience 后接两个并列动名词短语作宾语：going from cold outside air into a warm room 与 having moisture collect quickly on their glasses；have sth do/doing 表示「使某事发生」。

要点：have experience (in) doing sth 意为「有做某事的经历」，介词 in 常省略，因此要用 -ing 而非 to do。第二个动名词 having moisture collect 中的 have 是使役用法，宾语 moisture 后面跟不带 to 的不定式 collect。注意 go from … into … 这一固定搭配，不能写成 go from … to into …。

③ 被动语态 + in + 动名词表「在……过程中」

原句：This principle is applied in determining the dew point.

拆解：主语 This principle，谓语 is applied 为一般现在时被动；in determining the dew point 中 in 是介词，后接动名词，整个短语作方式状语，意为「在测定露点的过程中」。

要点：be applied in doing sth 表示「被应用于做某事」，in 后必须接动名词，写成 in determine 是常见错误。若强调用途而非过程，也可用 be used to determine（to 为不定式符号，后接动词原形），两种结构的 to 词性不同，不能混用。
