Fire Safety

通用 Ch 01 · Fire Safety · 原文 PDF 第 27 页

1. 原文

Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components requires the use of electrical tools that can produce sparks, heat-producing tools and equipment, flammable and explosive liquids, and gases. As a result, a high potential exists for fire to occur. Measures must be taken to prevent a fire from occurring and to have a plan for extinguishing it.

The key to fire safety is knowledge of what causes a fire, how to prevent it, and how to put it out. This knowledge must be instilled in each technician, emphasized by their supervisors through sound safety programs, and occasionally practiced.

Airport or other local fire departments can normally be called upon to assist in training personnel and helping to establish fire safety programs for the hangar, shops, and flight line.

2. 中文翻译

对航空器及其部件执行维修，需要使用会产生火花的电动工具、发热的工具和设备、易燃易爆的液体和气体。因此，发生火灾的可能性很高。必须采取措施防止火灾发生，并制定一旦起火如何扑灭的预案。

消防安全的关键在于了解火灾的成因、如何预防火灾以及如何扑灭火灾。这些知识必须灌输给每一位技师，由主管通过健全的安全计划加以强调，并适时组织演练。

机场消防部门或其他当地消防部门通常可以请来协助培训人员，并帮助机库、车间和停机坪建立消防安全计划。

4. 语法解析

① Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components requires the use of …（动名词作主语 + 长宾语）

原句：Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components requires the use of electrical tools that can produce sparks, heat-producing tools and equipment, flammable and explosive liquids, and gases.

拆解：主语是动名词短语 Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components；谓语 requires；宾语 the use of 后面接四项并列名词：electrical tools、heat-producing tools and equipment、flammable and explosive liquids、gases；其中 that can produce sparks 是定语从句，只修饰 electrical tools。

要点：动名词短语作主语，谓语用单数 requires。长列举中定语从句紧跟的是第一项 electrical tools，不要误以为它修饰后面所有项。the use of 后接名词短语，比直接用 use 更书面；翻译时可把「需要使用……」处理成动词短语，读起来更顺。

② Measures must be taken to prevent a fire from occurring and to have a plan for extinguishing it（被动 + 并列不定式目的状语）

原句：Measures must be taken to prevent a fire from occurring and to have a plan for extinguishing it.

拆解：主语 Measures，谓语 must be taken（情态被动）；后面两个 to do 并列作目的状语：to prevent a fire from occurring 和 to have a plan for extinguishing it；prevent … from doing 是固定搭配，extinguishing 是介词 for 后的动名词。

要点：measure 作「措施」讲时常用复数，take measures 是固定搭配，被动形式即 measures must be taken。prevent sb/sth from doing 中的 from 在美式英语里有时省略，但正式文本一般保留。for + 动名词（a plan for extinguishing it）表示用途，不能换成 to extinguish。

③ … knowledge of what causes a fire, how to prevent it, and how to put it out（介词后并列的名词性结构）

原句：The key to fire safety is knowledge of what causes a fire, how to prevent it, and how to put it out.

拆解：主语 The key to fire safety，系动词 is，表语 knowledge；of 后面并列三个成分：what 引导的宾语从句 what causes a fire，以及两个「疑问词 + 不定式」结构 how to prevent it、how to put it out。

要点：what causes a fire 是从句（有主谓），how to prevent it 是短语（无疑问主语），两者都能作介词宾语，但不能写成 how can prevent it。put out 是「扑灭」的动词短语，代词宾语 it 必须插在中间（put it out），不能写成 put out it；这一点在口语化技术表述中经常考。
