Fire Safety

通用 Ch 01 · Fire Safety · 原文 PDF 第 27 页

1. 原文

Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components requires the use of electrical tools that can produce sparks, heat-producing tools and equipment, flammable and explosive liquids, and gases. As a result, a high potential exists for fire to occur. Measures must be taken to prevent a fire from occurring and to have a plan for extinguishing it.

The key to fire safety is knowledge of what causes a fire, how to prevent it, and how to put it out. This knowledge must be instilled in each technician, emphasized by their supervisors through sound safety programs, and occasionally practiced. Airport or other local fire departments can normally be called upon to assist in training personnel and helping to establish fire safety programs for the hangar, shops, and flight line.

2. 中文翻译

对飞机及其部件进行维修，需要使用可能产生火花的电动工具、发热的工具与设备，以及易燃、易爆的液体和气体。因此，发生火灾的可能性很大。必须采取措施防止火灾发生，并制定灭火预案。

消防安全的要点，在于掌握火灾的成因、预防办法和扑救方法。这些知识必须灌输给每一名维修人员，由主管通过完善的安全计划加以强调，并定期进行演练。通常可以请机场消防队或其他当地消防部门协助培训人员，并帮助机库、车间和停机坪建立消防安全计划。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词作主语 + 定语从句

原句：Performing maintenance on aircraft and their components requires the use of electrical tools that can produce sparks, heat-producing tools and equipment, flammable and explosive liquids, and gases. 拆解：动名词短语 Performing maintenance… 作主语，谓语用单数 requires；宾语 the use of… 后并列四个成分，that can produce sparks 为定语从句，只修饰 electrical tools。 要点：动名词主语视为单数，谓语加 -s；长句里定语从句只修饰紧邻的名词，后面的并列项不受其限制，这是阅读时最容易错判修饰范围的地方。

② 被动语态 + 并列目的状语

原句：Measures must be taken to prevent a fire from occurring and to have a plan for extinguishing it. 拆解：主语 Measures + 被动谓语 must be taken + 两个并列的不定式目的状语 to prevent… and to have…。 要点：prevent sth from doing 是固定搭配，正式文本中 from 不省略；并列不定式第二个 to 保留，表示两个彼此独立的目的。

③ 表语从句与“疑问词 + 不定式”

原句：The key to fire safety is knowledge of what causes a fire, how to prevent it, and how to put it out. 拆解：主语 The key（to fire safety 为介词短语作后置定语）+ 系动词 is + 表语 knowledge；of 后并列三个成分：what causes a fire（宾语从句，what 作主语）、how to prevent it、how to put it out（疑问词 + 不定式）。 要点：what 引导名词性从句时兼作从句成分；how to do 是不定式短语，不能换成 how do。三者并列都作 of 的宾语。

④ 并列被动谓语的省略

原句：This knowledge must be instilled in each technician, emphasized by their supervisors through sound safety programs, and occasionally practiced. 拆解：主语 This knowledge 后接三个并列谓语：must be instilled in…、must be emphasized by…、must be practiced，后两个承前省略 must be。 要点：并列谓语省略相同助动词是正式文体的压缩手段，阅读时要把 must be 补回每一项；instill sth in sb（把……灌输给某人）与 emphasize by（通过……强调）都是固定搭配。
