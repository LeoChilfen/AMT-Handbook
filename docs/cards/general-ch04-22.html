Station Numbers & Location Identification on Aircraft

通用 Ch 04 · Station Numbers & Location Identification on Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 132 页

1. 原文

Aircraft A numbering system is used on large assemblies for aircraft to locate stations, such as fuselage frames. Fuselage station 185 indicates a location that is 185 inches from the datum of the aircraft. The measurement is usually taken from the nose or zero station, but in some instances, it may be taken from the firewall or some other point chosen by the manufacturer.

Just as forward and aft locations on aircraft are made by

reference to the datum, locations left and right of the aircraft’s longitudinal axis are made by reference to the buttock line and are called butt stations. Vertical locations on aircraft are made in reference to the waterline.

The same station numbering system is used for wing and stabilizer frames. The measurement is taken from the centerline or zero station of the aircraft. Figure 4-10 shows use of the fuselage stations (FS), waterline locations (WL), and left and right buttock line locations (RBL and LBL).

2. 中文翻译

航空器的大型部件采用一套编号体系来确定站位（station）位置，例如机身框。机身站位（fuselage station）185 表示距航空器基准面（datum）185 英寸的位置。该尺寸通常从机头或零站位量起，但在某些情况下也可能从防火隔板（firewall）或制造厂选定的其他基准点量起。

正如航空器上的前后位置是以基准面为参照确定的，

航空器左右方向的位置则以站位线（buttock line）为参照，称为侧站位（butt stations）。航空器上的垂直位置则以水线（waterline）为参照确定。

机翼和安定面框也使用同一套站位编号体系，其尺寸从航空器的中心线或零站位量起。图 4-10 表示机身站位（FS）、水线位置（WL）以及左、右侧站位线位置（LBL 和 RBL）的用法。

4. 语法解析

① Fuselage station 185 indicates a location that is 185 inches from the datum（定语从句 + 度量表达）

原句：Fuselage station 185 indicates a location that is 185 inches from the datum of the aircraft.

拆解：主语 Fuselage station 185，谓语 indicates；宾语 a location 后接 that 引导的定语从句；从句为系表结构，185 inches 作度量状语，与 from the datum 搭配。

要点：表示「距某处多远」用「数字 + 单位 + from + 基准」，不加 for 或 of。station 185 这种「名词 + 数字」的编号写法不加连字符，读作 station one eighty-five。of the aircraft 修饰 datum，说明基准面属于该航空器。

② locations … are made by reference to the buttock line and are called butt stations（并列被动 + by reference to）

原句：locations left and right of the aircraft’s longitudinal axis are made by reference to the buttock line and are called butt stations.

拆解：本句是原文第三段中的后半句（前半句 Just as forward and aft locations on aircraft are made by 因分页断行而另起一段，与前句共用同一骨架）。主语 locations，后置定语 left and right of the aircraft’s longitudinal axis 说明是哪一类位置；谓语为两个并列被动 are made … and are called …；by reference to 引出参照对象。

要点：by reference to 是固定短语「以……为参照」，写作 by referring to 也可以，但前者更书面、更常见于规范。just as …, … 的对称句式用来把新概念挂到已知概念上，读时抓住两组相同的骨架即可。后置定语 left and right of … 放在名词后，不能改成 left and right locations of。

③ The measurement is usually taken from the nose or zero station, but … it may be taken from the firewall（被动 + 转折并列 + 分词后置定语）

原句：The measurement is usually taken from the nose or zero station, but in some instances, it may be taken from the firewall or some other point chosen by the manufacturer.

拆解：由 but 连接两个并列分句，均为被动：is usually taken（一般现在时被动，usually 位于 is 之后）与 may be taken（情态被动）；chosen by the manufacturer 是过去分词短语后置修饰 point。

要点：take a measurement from … 是「从……量取尺寸」的固定搭配，被动形式为 the measurement is taken from。in some instances 意为「在某些情况下」，与 in some cases 同义。过去分词短语 chosen by … 代替定语从句 which is chosen by …，使句子更紧凑，这类压缩在手册中极为常见。
