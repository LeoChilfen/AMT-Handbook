Air Transport Association ATA iSpec 2200

通用 Ch 02 · Air Transport Association ATA iSpec 2200 · 原文 PDF 第 80 页

1. 原文

To standardize the technical data and maintenance activities on large and therefore complex aircraft, the ATA e-Business Program has established a classification of maintenance related actions. These are arranged with sequential numbers assigned to ATA chapters. These chapters are consistent regardless of the large aircraft that is being worked on.

2. 中文翻译

为统一大型——因而也是复杂的——航空器上的技术数据与维修活动，ATA e-Business Program 建立了一套维修相关行动的划分体系。这些行动按分配给各 ATA 章的连续编号编排。无论正在维修的是哪一种大型航空器，这些章节都是一致的。

4. 语法解析

① To standardize … , the ATA e-Business Program has established …（不定式作目的状语）

原句：To standardize the technical data and maintenance activities on large and therefore complex aircraft, the ATA e-Business Program has established a classification of maintenance related actions.

拆解：句首 To standardize … 为不定式短语作目的状语，用逗号与主句隔开；主句主语 the ATA e-Business Program，谓语 has established（现在完成时），宾语 a classification of maintenance related actions。

要点：目的状语放句首是为了先交代「为什么这么做」，技术说明文很常见。therefore 在此是插入的副词，逻辑上修饰 complex——「大型、因而复杂」，不要机械译成「因此」并挂到句尾。has established 用现在完成时，表示该体系建立后一直沿用至今。

② sequential numbers assigned to ATA chapters（过去分词短语作后置定语）

原句：These are arranged with sequential numbers assigned to ATA chapters.

拆解：主语 These（指上句的 actions）；谓语 are arranged（被动）；with … 是方式状语；assigned to ATA chapters 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 sequential numbers（= which are assigned to ATA chapters）。

要点：assign 的固定搭配是 assign A to B（把 A 分配给 B），被动即 be assigned to，因此这里必须用 assigned 而不是 assigning。长句里先找谓语 are arranged，再判断 with 后面是「伴随」还是「方式」，可避免把 assigned 误读成谓语。

③ regardless of the large aircraft that is being worked on（让步状语 + 进行时被动定语从句）

原句：These chapters are consistent regardless of the large aircraft that is being worked on.

拆解：主句 These chapters are consistent；regardless of … 是介词短语作让步状语；that is being worked on 是定语从句修饰 the aircraft，用现在进行时的被动语态，介词 on 留在句末。

要点：regardless of 后接名词或名词短语，不能直接跟句子（跟句子要用 regardless of the fact that …）。be being + 过去分词表示「正在被……」；work on 的宾语被 that 提前后，介词 on 必须留在句尾，不能省略。
