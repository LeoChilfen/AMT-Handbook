Stitch Lines

通用 Ch 04 · Stitch Lines · 原文 PDF 第 142 页

1. 原文

Stitch lines are used to indicate the stitching or sewing lines on an article and consists of a series of very short dashes, approximately half the length of dash or hidden lines, evenly spaced. Long lines of stitching may be indicated by a series of stitch lines connected by phantom lines.

4. 语法解析

① a series of very short dashes, approximately half the length of …（同位语式后置修饰）

原句：Stitch lines are used to indicate the stitching or sewing lines on an article and consists of a series of very short dashes, approximately half the length of dash or hidden lines, evenly spaced.

拆解：主语 Stitch lines；并列谓语 are used to indicate … 与 consists of …；a series of very short dashes 是 of 的宾语；approximately half the length of dash or hidden lines 是名词短语，作 dashes 的同位补充说明（省略了 which are）；evenly spaced 为过去分词短语，进一步修饰 dashes。

要点：a series of + 复数名词作主语时谓语用单数（a series of dashes consists of …）。本句主语为复数 Stitch lines，谓语却写作 consists，属原文的笔误，规范写法应为 consist——读手册时要能识别这类印刷错误，不要照抄进维修文件。

② approximately half the length of dash or hidden lines（数量表达省略 of）

原句：… approximately half the length of dash or hidden lines …

拆解：half the length of … 是「……长度的一半」，half 前可加 approximately / about 表约数；of 后 dash or hidden lines 两个名词共用冠词省略结构，意为「（普通）划线或虚线的划长」。

要点：英语数量表达中 half / twice / three times 直接置于 the + 名词前，不加 of，如 half the length、twice the diameter；若说 half of the length 则 half 为名词。译成中文时「约为……的一半」最自然，不必逐字对应。

③ may be indicated by a series of stitch lines connected by phantom lines（情态被动 + 过去分词短语作定语）

原句：Long lines of stitching may be indicated by a series of stitch lines connected by phantom lines.

拆解：主语 Long lines of stitching，谓语 may be indicated by …（情态动词 + 被动，引出手段）；a series of stitch lines 是 by 的宾语；connected by phantom lines 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 stitch lines。

要点：may be + 过去分词表「可以（被）……」，是手册给出可选画法的标准句式，与 must be（必须）形成语气差别。connected by … 与前面的 indicated by … 同为 by 短语但功能不同：前者表手段（被……表示），后者表施动者（被……连接），读时应按位置判断归属。
