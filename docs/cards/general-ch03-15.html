Computing Volume of Three-Dimensional Solids

通用 Ch 03 · Computing Volume of Three-Dimensional Solids · 原文 PDF 第 116 页

1. 原文

Three-dimensional solids have length, width, and height. There are many three-dimensional solids, but the most common are rectangular solids, cubes, cylinders, spheres, and cones. V olume is the amount of space within a solid. V olume is expressed in cubic units. Cubic inches or cubic centimeters are used for small spaces and cubic feet or cubic meters for larger spaces.

Rectangular Solid

A rectangular solid is a three-dimensional solid with six rectangular-shaped sides. The volume is the number of cubic units within the rectangular solid. The formula for the volume of a rectangular solid is:

volume = length × width × height = l × w × h

Example: A rectangular baggage compartment measures 5 feet 6 inches in length, 3 feet 4 inches in width, and 2 feet 3 inches in height. How many cubic feet of baggage will it hold? First, substitute the known values into the formula.

v = l × w × h = 5'6" × 3'4" × 2'3" = 5.5 ft × 3.33 ft × 2.25 ft = 41.25 cubic feet

Cube

A cube is a solid with six square sides. A cube is just a special type of rectangular solid. It has the same formula for volume as does the rectangular solid, which is volume = length × width × height = L × W × H. Because all of the sides of a cube are equal, the volume formula for a cube can also be written as:

volume = side × side × side = S^3

Example: A large, cube-shaped carton contains a shipment of smaller boxes inside of it. Each of the smaller boxes is 1 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft. The measurement of the large carton is 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft. How many of the smaller boxes are in the large carton? First, substitute the known values into the formula.

v = l × w × h = 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 cubic feet of volume in the large carton

Since each of the smaller boxes has a volume of 1 cubic foot, the large carton holds 27 boxes.

Cylinder

A solid having the shape of a can, a length of pipe, or a barrel is called a cylinder. The ends of a cylinder are identical circles. The formula for the volume of a cylinder is:

volume = π × radius^2 × height of the cylinder = π r^2 × h

One of the most important applications of the volume of a cylinder is finding the piston displacement of a cylinder in a reciprocating engine. Piston displacement is the total volume (in cubic inches, cubic centimeters, or liters) swept by all of the pistons of a reciprocating engine as they move in one revolution of the crankshaft. The formula for piston displacement is given as:

Piston Displacement = π × (bore divided by 2)^2 × stroke × (# cylinders)

The bore of an engine is the inside diameter of the cylinder. The stroke of the engine is the length the piston travels inside the cylinder.

Example: Find the piston displacement of one cylinder in a multi-cylinder aircraft engine. The engine has a cylinder bore of 5.5 inches and a stroke of 5.4 inches. First, substitute the known values in the formula.

v = π × r2 × h = (3.1416) × (5.5 ÷ 2)^2 × (5.4) v = 23.758 × 5.4 = 128.29 cubic inches

The piston displacement of one cylinder is 128.29 cubic inches. For an eight-cylinder engine, then the total engine displacement would be:

Total displacement for 8 cylinders = 8 × 128.29 = 1026.32 cubic inches of displacement

Sphere

A solid having the shape of a ball is called a sphere. A sphere has a constant diameter. The radius (r) of a sphere is one-half of the diameter (d). The formula for the volume of a sphere is given as:

V = 4⁄3 × π × radius^3 = 4⁄3 × π × r^3 or v = 1⁄6 × πd^3

Example: A pressure tank inside the fuselage of a cargo aircraft is in the shape of a sphere with a diameter of 34 inches. What is the volume of the pressure tank?

v = 4⁄3 × π × radius^3 = 4⁄3 × (3.1416) × (34⁄2)^3 = 1.33 × 3.1416 × 173 = 1.33 × 3.1416 × 4,913 v = 20,528.125 cubic inches

Cone

A solid with a circle as a base and with sides that gradually taper to a point is called a cone. The formula for the volume of a cone is given as:

v = 1⁄3 × π × radius^2 × height = 1⁄3 × π × r^2 × h

Units of Volume

Since all volumes are not measured in the same units, it is necessary to know all the common units of volume and how they are related to each other. For example, the mechanic may know the volume of a tank in cubic feet or cubic inches, but when the tank is full of gasoline, they are interested in how many gallons it contains.

2. 中文翻译

三维立体有长、宽、高。三维立体有很多种，最常见的是长方体、立方体、圆柱体、球体和圆锥体。体积是立体内部所含空间的大小。体积用立方单位表示。小空间用立方英寸或立方厘米，较大的空间用立方英尺或立方米。

长方体

长方体是有六个矩形面的三维立体。体积是长方体内部所含立方单位的数目。长方体的体积公式为：

volume = length × width × height = l × w × h 这个长方体的尺寸是 3 英尺 × 2 英尺 × 2 英尺。该立体的体积为 3 ft × 2 ft × 2 ft = 12 立方英尺。

例：某行李舱长 5 英尺 6 英寸、宽 3 英尺 4 英寸、高 2 英尺 3 英寸，它能装多少立方英尺的行李？先把已知值代入公式。

v = l × w × h = 5'6" × 3'4" × 2'3" = 5.5 ft × 3.33 ft × 2.25 ft = 41.25 立方英尺

立方体

立方体是有六个正方形面的立体。立方体只是长方体的一种特例。它的体积公式与长方体相同，即 volume = length × width × height = L × W × H。由于立方体各边都相等，其体积公式也可以写成：

volume = side × side × side = S3（原文 S3 中的 3 为上标，即 S 的立方）

例：一个大立方体纸箱里装着若干小盒子。每个小盒子为 1 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft，大纸箱的尺寸为 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft。大纸箱里有多少个小盒子？先把已知值代入公式。

v = l × w × h = 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 立方英尺，这是大纸箱的容积。

由于每个小盒子的体积为 1 立方英尺，这个大纸箱可装 27 个盒子。

圆柱体

形状像罐头、一段管子或木桶的立体称为圆柱体。圆柱体的两端是完全相同的圆。圆柱体的体积公式为：

volume = π × radius2 × height of the cylinder = π r2 × h

Piston Displacement = π × (bore divided by 2)2 × stroke × (# cylinders)（原文此处括号外的 2 为上标，即求平方）

v = π × r2 × h = (3.1416) × (5.5 ÷ 2)2 × (5.4) v = 23.758 × 5.4 = 128.29 立方英寸

8 个气缸的总排量 = 8 × 128.29 = 1026.32 立方英寸

圆锥

以圆为底、侧面逐渐收拢到一点的立体称为圆锥体。圆锥体的体积公式为：

v = 1⁄3 × π × radius2 × height = 1⁄3 × π × r2 × h

体积单位

由于各种体积并不都用同一单位计量，必须了解所有常用的体积单位以及它们彼此之间的换算关系。例如，维修人员可能知道某个油箱的容积是若干立方英尺或立方英寸，但当油箱装满汽油时，他们关心的是它能装多少加仑。

4. 语法解析

① There be 存在句 + but 并列（表语前置）

原句：There are many three-dimensional solids, but the most common are rectangular solids, cubes, cylinders, spheres, and cones. 拆解：前半句 There are many three-dimensional solids 为存在句；but 连接后半句，主语 the most common（最常见的几种）承前省略了 three-dimensional solids，谓语 are，表语由五个并列名词充当。 要点：the most common 后面省略中心词是英语常见的“形容词名词化”用法，翻译时要补出“立体”；并列项超过三个时，最后两项之间用 and，前面用逗号。

② be expressed in + 计量单位

原句：Volume is expressed in cubic units. 拆解：主语 Volume，谓语 is expressed（被动语态），in cubic units 为介词短语，表示计量的单位。 要点：表示“用某单位表示”固定用 be expressed in，介词不能用 by 或 with；cubic units 是复数泛指，不加冠词。

③ 现在分词短语作后置定语

原句：A solid having the shape of a can, a length of pipe, or a barrel is called a cylinder. 拆解：主语为 A solid，其后 having the shape of a can, a length of pipe, or a barrel 是现在分词短语作后置定语，三个并列宾语共用 having；谓语 is called（被动），表语 a cylinder。 要点：现在分词短语作定语相当于 which has…，因短语较长而后置；have the shape of 是“具有……的形状”，翻译时不必逐字译成“有形状”。

④ the same … as + 倒装 + 非限制性定语从句

原句：It has the same formula for volume as does the rectangular solid, which is volume = length × width × height = L × W × H. 拆解：It 指立方体；the same formula for volume as does the rectangular solid 中 as 引导比较结构，为强调用倒装语序 does the rectangular solid（等于 the rectangular solid does）；, which is … 为非限制性定语从句，which 指 the same formula。 要点：the same … as 表示“与……相同”，as 后可用倒装，也可写成 as the rectangular solid does；which 引导的非限制性从句前必须有逗号，且不能用 that。

⑤ since 原因从句 + it 形式主语 + 部分否定

原句：Since all volumes are not measured in the same units, it is necessary to know all the common units of volume and how they are related to each other. 拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句为被动结构 all volumes are not measured in the same units（all…not 是部分否定，意为“并非所有体积都用同一单位计量”）；主句 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to know 后的两个并列宾语 all the common units of volume 与 how they are related to each other。 要点：all … not 表示部分否定而非全部否定，全部否定要用 none；be related to each other 是“彼此相关”的固定说法，how 从句作 know 的宾语。

⑥ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + as 引导时间状语从句

原句：Piston displacement is the total volume (in cubic inches, cubic centimeters, or liters) swept by all of the pistons of a reciprocating engine as they move in one revolution of the crankshaft. 拆解：主语 Piston displacement，系动词 is，表语 the total volume；圆括号内的 in cubic inches… 补充计量单位；swept by all of the pistons … 为过去分词短语后置修饰 volume；as they move in one revolution of the crankshaft 为 as 引导的时间状语从句。 要点：过去分词 swept 与 volume 是被动关系（容积被活塞扫过），故用过去分词而非现在分词；as 在此表示“当……时”，与表示原因的 since 不同。
