1. 原文

Acceleration is defined as the rate of change of velocity. If the velocity of an object is increased from 20 mph to 30 mph, the object has been accelerated. If the increase in velocity is 10 mph in 5 seconds, the rate of change in velocity is 10 mph in 5 seconds, or 2 mph per second. If this were multiplied

by 1.467, it could also be expressed as an acceleration of 2.93 feet per second per second (fps/s). By comparison, the acceleration due to gravity is 32.2 fps/s.

To calculate acceleration, the following formula is used.

Acceleration (A) = Time (t)

Velocity Final (Vf) − Velocity Initial (Vi)

Example: An Air Force F-15 fighter is cruising at 400 mph.

The pilot advances the throttles to full afterburner and accelerates to 1,200 mph in 20 seconds. What is the average acceleration in mph/s and fps/s?

A = tVf − Vi A = 201200 − 400 A = 40 mph⁄s, or multiplying by 1.467, 58.7 fps⁄s In the example just shown, the acceleration was found to be 58.7 fps/s. Since 32.2 fps/s is equal to the acceleration due to gravity, divide the F-15’s acceleration by 32.2 to find out how many G forces the pilot is experiencing. In this case, it would be 1.82 Gs.

2. 中文翻译

加速度定义为速度的变化率。如果物体的速度从 20 mph 增大到 30 mph，该物体就经历了加速。如果在 5 秒内速度增量是 10 mph，则速度变化率为 5 秒 10 mph，即每秒 2 mph。如果把这一数值

乘以 1.467，也可以表示为 2.93 英尺每二次方秒（fps/s）的加速度。作为对比，重力加速度为 32.2 fps/s。

计算加速度时使用下面的公式。

「Acceleration (A) = Time (t)」（加速度（A）＝时间（t）；说明：原文公式为分式，OCR 压平后分子、分母分行排版，分子为末速度减初速度，分母为时间）

「Velocity Final (Vf) − Velocity Initial (Vi)」（末速度（Vf）− 初速度（Vi）；按分式读作 A =（Vf − Vi）／t）

例题：一架空军 F-15 战斗机以 400 mph 巡航。

飞行员把油门推到全加力，在 20 秒内加速到 1,200 mph。以 mph/s 和 fps/s 计，平均加速度是多少？

「A = tVf − Vi　A = 201200 − 400　A = 40 mph⁄s」——上式中的分式与上下标已被 OCR 压平，按 A =（Vf − Vi）／t 代入，即 A =（1200 − 400）／20 = 40 mph/s，乘以 1.467 得 58.7 fps/s。在刚才的例子中，求得加速度为 58.7 fps/s。由于 32.2 fps/s 等于重力加速度，把 F-15 的加速度除以 32.2，即可求出飞行员承受多少个 G。本例中为 1.82 G。

4. 语法解析

① the object has been accelerated（现在完成时被动）

原句：If the velocity of an object is increased from 20 mph to 30 mph, the object has been accelerated.

拆解：If 从句用被动 is increased；主句 has been accelerated 为现在完成时被动（has been + 过去分词），强调到说话时已经完成的加速。

要点：现在完成被动 = has/have been + 过去分词。accelerate 既可及物也可不及物，此处用被动表示「被加速」，与 the object accelerates（物体自行加速）不同。

② it could also be expressed as an acceleration of 2.93 fps/s（虚拟条件句）

原句：it could also be expressed as an acceleration of 2.93 feet per second per second (fps/s).

拆解：主句 it could also be expressed 为「情态动词 + 被动」，as an acceleration of … 是方式补语。条件从句 If this were multiplied by 1.467 在上一段末尾，原文因 OCR 分段而断开。

要点：与现在事实相反的虚拟：if + 过去式（be 一律用 were），主句 would/could + 动词原形。技术文本用它表示「若作此换算，则可以表示为」，语气比直接用 can be 更委婉。multiply by = 乘以。

③ To calculate acceleration, the following formula is used（不定式作目的状语 + 被动）

原句：To calculate acceleration, the following formula is used.

拆解：To calculate acceleration 为不定式短语作目的状语，置于句首；主句 the following formula is used 为被动。

要点：目的状语前置时常用逗号与主句隔开。the following formula 是「下式」的固定说法，不能写成 the next formula。被动句省略了执行者（读者／我们）。

④ divide the F-15’s acceleration by 32.2 to find out how many G forces …（since 原因从句 + 祈使句 + 宾语从句）

原句：Since 32.2 fps/s is equal to the acceleration due to gravity, divide the F-15’s acceleration by 32.2 to find out how many G forces the pilot is experiencing.

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句；主句为祈使句 divide … by …；to find out 为目的状语；how many G forces the pilot is experiencing 为 find out 的宾语从句，从句用现在进行时。

要点：divide A by B 是「A 除以 B」，注意与 divide A into B（把 A 分成 B）区分。due to gravity 是形容词短语后置修饰 acceleration。G forces 中的 G 读作 gravity。
