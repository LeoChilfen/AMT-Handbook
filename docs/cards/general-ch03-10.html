Division of Fractions

1. 原文

Division of fractions does not require a common denominator.

To divide fractions, first change the division symbol to multiplication. Next, invert the second fraction. Then, multiply the fractions.

Example: Divide 7⁄8 by 4⁄3 ( 7 ÷ 4 ) = ( 7 × 3 ) = ( 7 × 3) = 21 8 3 8 4 8 × 4 32 Example: In Figure 3-2, the center of the hole is in the center of the plate. Find the distance that the center of the hole is from the edges of the plate. To find the answer, the length and width of the plate should each be divided in half. First, change the mixed numbers to improper fractions:

5 7⁄16 inches = 87⁄16 inches 3 5⁄8 inches = 29⁄8 inches Then, divide each improper fraction by 2 to find the center of the plate.

87 ÷ 2 = 87 × 1 = 87 inches 16 1 16 2 32

29 ÷ 2 = 29 × 1 = 29 inches 8 1 8 2 16 Finally, convert each improper fraction to a mixed number:

87 = 87 ÷ 32 = 2 23 inches 32 32

29 = 29 ÷ 16 = 1 13 inches 16 16

Therefore, the distance to the center of the hole from each of the plate edges is 2 23⁄32 inches and 1 13⁄16 inches.

2. 中文翻译

分数的除法不需要公分母。

要做分数的除法，首先把除号改成乘号。接着，把第二个分数颠倒过来。然后，把这两个分数相乘。

例：用 7⁄8 除以 4⁄3　(7⁄8 ÷ 4⁄3) = (7⁄8 × 3⁄4) = (7 × 3)⁄(8 × 4) = 21⁄32　例：在图 3-2 中，孔的中心位于板件的中心。求孔中心到板件各边缘的距离。要求出答案，应把板件的长度和宽度各分成两半。首先，把带分数化成假分数：

5 7⁄16 英寸 = 87⁄16 英寸　3 5⁄8 英寸 = 29⁄8 英寸　然后，把每个假分数除以 2，以求出板件的中心。

87⁄16 ÷ 2⁄1 = (87⁄16 × 1⁄2) = 87⁄32 英寸

29⁄8 ÷ 2⁄1 = (29⁄8 × 1⁄2) = 29⁄16 英寸　最后，把每个假分数化成带分数：

87⁄32 = 87 ÷ 32 = 2 23⁄32 英寸

29⁄16 = 29 ÷ 16 = 1 13⁄16 英寸

因此，孔中心到板件各边缘的距离分别是 2 23⁄32 英寸和 1 13⁄16 英寸。

4. 语法解析

① 一般现在时否定句：does not require

原句：Division of fractions does not require a common denominator.

拆解：主语 Division of fractions（of 短语作后置定语）；谓语 does not require，因主语是不可数名词 Division 用第三人称单数，否定借 does；宾语 a common denominator。

要点：这句和前面加法、减法两节形成对照：分数加减 must have a common denominator，乘除 does not require one。注意 require 是及物动词，后面直接跟宾语，不能说 require for；同类否定表述还有 needs no common denominator。

② 情态动词被动 + each 作同位语插入：should each be divided

原句：To find the answer, the length and width of the plate should each be divided in half.

拆解：To find the answer 是目的状语；主语 the length and width of the plate；谓语 should each be divided（should + each + be + 过去分词），each 作主语的同位语插在 should 与 be 之间；in half 是方式状语。

要点：each 放在情态动词之后、be 之前是固定位置，写成 should be each divided 就错了。divide sth in half 表示「把……分成两半」，介词用 in，不是 into half。主语是并列的 length and width，用复数谓语，each 只是强调「各自」。

③ that 引导的定语从句：the distance that … is from …

原句：Find the distance that the center of the hole is from the edges of the plate.

拆解：祈使句 Find the distance；that 引导定语从句修饰 distance，that 在从句中作 from 的宾语（还原为 the center of the hole is [the distance] from the edges）；从句主语 the center of the hole，系动词 is，from the edges of the plate 是介词短语。

要点：be … from 表示「距……多远」，距离值被提前成先行词后，从句看似缺表语，其实由 that 承担。同类：the distance (that) the hole is from the edge；也可写成 the distance from the hole to the edge，用名词短语更省事。
