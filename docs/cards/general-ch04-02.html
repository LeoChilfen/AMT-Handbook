1. 原文

From the early days of aviation, development of aircraft, aircraft engines, and other components relied heavily on aircraft drawings. For most of the 20 th century, drawings were created on a drawing “board” with pen or pencil and paper. With the introduction and advancement of computers in the later decades of the 20 th century, the way drawings are created changed dramatically. Computers were used not only to create drawings, but they were being used to show items in “virtual reality,” from any possible viewing angle. Further development of computer software programs allowed for assembling of separately created parts to check for proper fit and possible interferences. Additionally, with nearly instantaneous information sharing capability through computer networking and the Internet, it became much easier for designers to share their work with other designers and manufacturers virtually anytime, anywhere in the world.

Using new computer-controlled manufacturing techniques, it became possible to design a part and have it precisely manufactured without ever having shown it on paper. New terms and acronyms became commonplace. The more common of these terms are:

• Computer Graphics—drawing with the use of a computer • Computer Aided Design (CAD)—where a computer is used in the design of a part or product • Computer Aided Design Drafting (CADD)—where a computer is used in the design and drafting process • Computer Aided Manufacturing (CAM)—where a computer is used in the manufacturing of a part or product • Computer Aided Engineering (CAE)—where a computer is used in the engineering of a part or product As computer hardware and software continue to evolve, a greater amount of CAE is completed in less time, at lower cost. In addition to product design, some of the other uses of CAE are product analysis, assembly, simulations, and maintenance information. [Figure 4-1]

CATIA, ProEngineer, Solid Works, and Unigraphics are some of the more popular CAD software packages used for aircraft design and manufacturing. Most airframe manufacturers use CATIA software to design their aircraft. The complete aircraft is designed and assembled in the software package before it is manufactured. Drawings of all parts of the aircraft are available and can be accessed using the computer software.

Drawings are no longer limited to 1, 2, or 3 views. Drawings from every angle can easily be accessed by using the computer model of the part or product. Technicians can access drawings and aircraft manuals on laptops or even mobile devices when performing maintenance on the shop floor.

2. 中文翻译

自航空业的早期起，航空器、航空发动机及其他部件的研制就严重依赖飞机图纸。在 20 世纪的大部分时间里，图纸是在绘图「板」上用钢笔或铅笔加纸张绘制出来的。随着 20 世纪后期计算机的出现与进步，图纸的绘制方式发生了剧烈变化。计算机不仅被用来绘制图纸，还被用来以「虚拟现实」的方式、从任何可能的观察角度展示物件。计算机软件的进一步发展，使得可以把分别创建的零件装配起来，以检查配合是否合适、是否存在干涉。此外，借助计算机网络与互联网近乎即时的信息共享能力，设计者可以几乎在任何时间、世界任何地点，更轻松地与其他设计者及制造商共享其成果。

采用新的计算机控制制造技术后，可以设计出一个零件，并让它在从未以纸面形式出示过的情况下被精确制造出来。新术语与首字母缩略词随之变得平常。其中较常见的术语有：

• 计算机图形学（Computer Graphics）——借助计算机绘图 • 计算机辅助设计（CAD）——用计算机进行零件或产品的设计 • 计算机辅助设计制图（CADD）——用计算机进行设计与制图过程 • 计算机辅助制造（CAM）——用计算机进行零件或产品的制造 • 计算机辅助工程（CAE）——用计算机完成零件或产品的工程工作 随着计算机硬件与软件不断演进，越来越多的 CAE 工作能在更短时间内、以更低成本完成。除产品设计外，CAE 的其他用途还包括产品分析、装配、仿真以及维修信息。[图 4-1]

CATIA、ProEngineer、Solid Works 和 Unigraphics 是用于航空器设计与制造的一些较流行的 CAD 软件包。大多数机体制造商使用 CATIA 软件设计其航空器。整架航空器在制造之前，先在软件包中完成设计与装配。航空器所有零件的图纸都可以获取，并可用计算机软件调阅。

图纸不再局限于 1 个、2 个或 3 个视图。借助零件或产品的计算机模型，可以轻松调阅从各个角度得到的视图。技师在车间现场执行维修时，可以在笔记本电脑甚至移动设备上查阅图纸和航空器手册。

4. 语法解析

① not only … but … 的并列

原句：Computers were used not only to create drawings, but they were being used to show items in “virtual reality,” from any possible viewing angle.

拆解：not only 置于不定式短语 to create drawings 之前，but 后接完整分句 they were being used to show …；后一分句是过去进行时被动（were being used），from any possible viewing angle 是角度状语。

要点：not only 在句首并连接分句时才需要倒装；本句 not only 只引导不定式短语，故不倒装。were being used 强调当时正在形成的用法，翻译不必硬译进行意味，用「还被用来」即可。not only … but 中的 but 后不强制加 also。

② Using …, it became possible to …（分词状语 + 形式主语 + have sth done）

原句：Using new computer-controlled manufacturing techniques, it became possible to design a part and have it precisely manufactured without ever having shown it on paper.

拆解：Using … 是现在分词短语作方式状语；it 为形式主语，真正主语是并列的不定式 to design a part and have it … manufactured；have it manufactured 是 have + 宾语 + 过去分词（使役）；without ever having shown 是介词 + 完成式动名词。

要点：have sth done 表示「让某事被做」，done 与宾语是被动关系，不能写成 have it manufacture。介词 without 后用 having done 强调该动作先于主句发生，本句即「制造先于图纸出示」。computer-controlled 是「名词 + 过去分词」构成的复合形容词。

③ no longer + be limited to

原句：Drawings are no longer limited to 1, 2, or 3 views.

拆解：are limited to 是被动结构，to 为介词，后接名词短语 1, 2, or 3 views；no longer 是否定副词短语，位于 be 动词之后。

要点：be limited to 的 to 是介词，接名词或动名词，不接动词原形。no longer 的位置固定在 be 动词或助动词之后、实义动词之前；若要改用 not … any longer，则须放到句末。
