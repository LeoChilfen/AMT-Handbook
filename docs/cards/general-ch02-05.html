14 CFR Part 1—Definitions and Abbreviations

通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 1—Definitions and Abbreviations · 原文 PDF 第 53 页

1. 原文

This section is a very comprehensive, but certainly not all inclusive, list of definitions that both pilots and mechanics must become familiar with. Many regulations often provide additional definitions that are unique to their use and interpretation in that specific part. Title 14 CFR part 1, section 1.2, Abbreviations and Symbols, tends to be highly focused on those abbreviations related to flight.

2. 中文翻译

本节是一份相当全面、但肯定并非包罗全部的定义清单，飞行员和维修技师都必须熟悉。许多法规常常还会给出各自独有的附加定义，这些定义只在该部法规的使用与解释中有效。14 CFR 第 1 编第 1.2 节「缩写与符号」，则偏重于集中列出与飞行有关的那些缩写。

4. 语法解析

① … a list of definitions that both pilots and mechanics must become familiar with（定语从句 + 介词后置）

原句：This section is a very comprehensive, but certainly not all inclusive, list of definitions that both pilots and mechanics must become familiar with.

拆解：主语 This section，系动词 is，表语为 a … list of definitions；两个逗号之间 but certainly not all inclusive 是插入的并列形容词短语，与 comprehensive 共同修饰 list；that … 是定语从句修饰 definitions，介词 with 留在从句末尾（即 become familiar with the definitions）。

要点：become familiar with 的宾语被关系代词 that 提前，介词 with 留在句末，这是正式书面语的典型用法，口语才说 definitions which pilots are familiar with 时把介词前置（with which）。插入语用两个逗号隔开，中文宜拆成「但肯定并不包罗全部」的独立短语。

② definitions that are unique to their use and interpretation in that specific part（be unique to + 指示代词 that）

原句：Many regulations often provide additional definitions that are unique to their use and interpretation in that specific part.

拆解：主句 Many regulations provide additional definitions；that … 引导定语从句修饰 definitions；be unique to 后接名词短语 their use and interpretation；in that specific part 是范围状语。

要点：be unique to sth 意为「为……所独有」，不能用 be unique for。their 指代 regulations（法规各自的），不是 definitions。此处 that 是指示代词「该、那个」，不是引导从句的连词，判断办法是看它后面是否直接跟名词。

③ tends to be highly focused on those abbreviations related to flight（tend to + 系表结构 + 过去分词后置定语）

原句：Title 14 CFR part 1, section 1.2, Abbreviations and Symbols, tends to be highly focused on those abbreviations related to flight.

拆解：主语很长，Title 14 CFR part 1、section 1.2、Abbreviations and Symbols 三者层层同位；谓语为 tends to be highly focused on；related to flight 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 those abbreviations。

要点：tend to do 表「往往、倾向于」，用于描述文本特点而非绝对规律，语气比 always 弱。related to / associated with / pertaining to 都作后置定语，相当于 which are related to，科技文中出现频率极高。
