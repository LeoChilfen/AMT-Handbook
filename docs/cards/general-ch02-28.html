Section 43.15—Additional Performance Rules for Inspections

通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.15—Additional Performance Rules for Inspections · 原文 PDF 第 68 页

1. 原文

This section presents general comments concerning the responsibility of conducting an inspection and then provides details of three separate conditions. They are rotorcraft, annual and 100-hour inspections, and progressive inspections.

  1. Rotorcraft—If a rotorcraft is being inspected, specific items, such as rotor transmissions and drive shafts, must be inspected.
  1. Annual and 100-hour inspections—When performing an annual or 100-hour inspection, a checklist must be used. This checklist may be a personal one or one from the manufacturer. Either way, it must include the scope and detail of the inspection in Appendix D.

Specific engine performance is also required to be tested (or monitored) as part of RTS for an annual or 100-hour inspection. This applies whether the aircraft is reciprocating or turbine powered.

  1. Progressive inspection—If a progressive inspection is being conducted, it must be preceded by a complete aircraft inspection. ( Note: A progressive inspection is the result of breaking down the large task of conducting a major inspection into smaller tasks that can be accomplished periodically without taking the aircraft out of service for an extended period of time.)

Two new definitions are also presented: “routine” and “detailed.” A routine inspection is a visual examination or check of the item, but no disassembly is required.

A detailed inspection is a thorough examination of the item, including disassembly. The overhaul of a component is considered to be a detailed inspection.

If the aircraft is away from the station where inspections are normally conducted, an appropriately rated mechanic, a certificated repair station, or the manufacturer of the aircraft may perform inspections in accordance with the procedures and using the forms of the person who would otherwise perform the inspection.

2. 中文翻译

本条先就实施检查的责任提出总体说明，然后分别详述三种情形，即旋翼航空器、年度检查与 100 小时检查、渐进式检查。

  1. 旋翼航空器——如果检查对象是旋翼航空器，必须检查特定项目，例如旋翼传动装置与传动轴。
  1. 年度检查与 100 小时检查——实施年度检查或 100 小时检查时，必须使用检查单。该检查单可以是自编的，也可以是制造商提供的。无论哪种，都必须包含附录 D 所规定的检查范围与详细程度。

作为年度检查或 100 小时检查恢复使用的一部分，还要求对特定的发动机性能进行测试（或监控）。无论航空器采用往复式还是涡轮式动力装置，这一要求都适用。

  1. 渐进式检查——如果实施渐进式检查，必须先做一次完整的航空器检查。（注：渐进式检查是把“做一次大检查”这一大任务拆分为若干较小任务的结果，这些小任务可以周期性地完成，而不必让航空器长时间停场。）

本条还给出两个新定义：“常规”与“详细”。常规检查是对项目进行目视检查或查看，但不要求分解。

详细检查是对项目进行彻底检查，包括分解。部件的翻修即视为详细检查。

如果航空器不在通常实施检查的站点，具备相应等级的维修技师、持证维修站或航空器制造商，可以按照本应实施该检查者的程序、使用其表格来实施检查。

4. 语法解析

① if 条件句 + 现在进行时被动 + be preceded by

原句：If a progressive inspection is being conducted, it must be preceded by a complete aircraft inspection.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，谓语 is being conducted 为现在进行时被动，表示“正在实施”；主句 it must be preceded by a complete aircraft inspection 为情态被动，preceded by 引出“在它之前必须先做的事”。

要点：be preceded by（在……之前先有……）是技术文本说明先后次序的正式表达，主语是后发生的事。条件从句用进行时被动，是因为“实施检查”是一个持续过程；若写成 is conducted，则偏指“每次实施”，语义差别不大但语体不同。注意 must be preceded by 不能写成 must precede by。

② but 连接的并列分句 + 被动

原句：A routine inspection is a visual examination or check of the item, but no disassembly is required.

拆解：前一分句 A routine inspection is a visual examination or check of the item 为主系表结构；but 连接后一分句 no disassembly is required，为一般现在时被动，no 否定主语。

要点：用 no + 名词作主语（no disassembly is required）比 Disassembly is not required 语气更强，强调“完全不需要”。定义类句子常用“X is a … , but no … is required”的对照句式，翻译时保留“但不要求……”的转折最清楚。

③ where 定语从句 + 分词状语 + who would otherwise do

原句：If the aircraft is away from the station where inspections are normally conducted, an appropriately rated mechanic, a certificated repair station, or the manufacturer of the aircraft may perform inspections in accordance with the procedures and using the forms of the person who would otherwise perform the inspection.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，其中 where inspections are normally conducted 是定语从句修饰 the station；主句主语为三项并列（mechanic / repair station / manufacturer），谓语 may perform；in accordance with the procedures 与 using the forms … 为并列方式状语（一个介词短语、一个分词短语）；who would otherwise perform the inspection 是定语从句修饰 the person。

要点：otherwise 在此意为“本来会（做这件事）的”，说明“按原检查方的程序和表格办事”，是法规中界定替代执行方式的关键词。并列状语一个用介词短语、一个用分词，是英语避免重复的常见写法，中文需补成“按照……程序并使用……表格”。
