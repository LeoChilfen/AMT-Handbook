Suspected Unapproved Parts (SUP)

通用 Ch 02 · Suspected Unapproved Parts (SUP) · 原文 PDF 第 74 页

1. 原文

There are four types of aircraft parts:

  1. Good parts with good paperwork.
  1. Good parts with bad paperwork.
  1. Bad parts with “good” (bogus) paperwork.
  1. Bad parts with bad paperwork.

The first of those listed represents properly authorized parts that, when properly installed, are approved parts, and the aircraft can be returned to service. The last of those listed represent unauthorized and unapproved parts. The technician should be alert for these and must never install them on an aircraft.

The center two categories of parts represent suspected unapproved parts. If either the physical part or the paperwork associated with the part is questionable, it is best to contact the shop foreman, shift supervisor, or the assigned quality individual to discuss your concerns. Suspected unapproved parts (SUPs) should be segregated and quarantined until proper disposition can be determined. Contacting the manufacturer of the product is a good way to start gathering the facts concerning the product in question. Refer to the current version of AC 21-29, Detecting and Reporting Suspected Unapproved Parts, for additional information. Current contact information for submitting a SUP Notification can be found at www.faa.gov.

2. 中文翻译

飞机零件共有四种类型：

  1. 资料齐全的合格零件。
  1. 资料有问题的合格零件。
  1. 资料“齐全”（实为伪造）的不合格零件。
  1. 资料不全的不合格零件。

上述第一类指经正当授权的零件，正确安装后即为合格零件，飞机可以恢复使用。最后一类则是未经授权、未获批准的零件。技术人员应当对这两类保持警觉，绝不可把它们装上飞机。

中间的两类属于可疑不合格零件。如果实物零件或与该零件相关的资料存在疑问，最好与车间主任、值班主管或指定的质量人员联系，讨论你的疑虑。可疑不合格零件（SUPs）应予隔离、封存，直至能够确定适当的处置方式。联系产品的制造商，是着手收集该可疑产品相关事实的好办法。更多信息请参阅现行版本的 AC 21-29《检测与报告可疑不合格零件》。提交 SUP 通知的最新联系信息可在 www.faa.gov 上查到。

4. 语法解析

① 过去分词后置定语 + 插入的时间状语从句

原句：The first of those listed represents properly authorized parts that, when properly installed, are approved parts, and the aircraft can be returned to service. 拆解：主干 The first of those listed represents properly authorized parts；listed 为过去分词作后置定语修饰 The first。that 引导定语从句修饰 parts，从句的主语 that 之后插入省略形式的时间状语 when properly installed（相当于 when they are properly installed），从句谓语是 are approved parts；and 连接第二个并列分句 the aircraft can be returned to service。 要点：读到 that 后不要急着找谓语，先跳过插入的 when 从句，谓语是 are。when + 过去分词是省略结构，省略了与从句主语相同的主语及 be 动词；技术文本常借此避免重复，翻译时补成「正确安装后」。

② either…or 作主语 + it is best to do

原句：If either the physical part or the paperwork associated with the part is questionable, it is best to contact the shop foreman, shift supervisor, or the assigned quality individual to discuss your concerns. 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，主语为 either the physical part or the paperwork（associated with the part 为过去分词短语作后置定语），谓语 is questionable；主句 it is best to contact…，it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to contact…；to discuss your concerns 作目的状语。 要点：either A or B 作主语时谓语按就近一致，此处靠近的是单数 paperwork，故用 is；若换成长在前的复数名词则用 are。associated with 意为「与……相关的」，不要译成「被联想」。it is best to do 比 you must do 更委婉，适合给建议。

③ 情态被动 + until 引导的时间状语从句

原句：Suspected unapproved parts (SUPs) should be segregated and quarantined until proper disposition can be determined. 拆解：主语 SUPs + 情态被动谓语 should be segregated and quarantined（两个过去分词并列）+ until 引导的时间状语从句，从句主语 proper disposition，谓语 can be determined（带情态的被动）。 要点：until 从句用 can be determined 而非 will be determined，表示「到能够确定为止」这一条件性时点；segregated 与 quarantined 是近义并列，前者强调物理分开、后者强调封存受控，翻译时两个动作都要保留，不能只译一个。

④ 动名词作主语 + way to do + in question

原句：Contacting the manufacturer of the product is a good way to start gathering the facts concerning the product in question. 拆解：主语是动名词短语 Contacting the manufacturer of the product；谓语 is；表语 a good way；to start gathering the facts 为不定式作 way 的后置定语；concerning the product in question 为介词短语作定语修饰 facts，in question 又后置修饰 product。 要点：way 后接不定式（a way to do sth）而非 of doing；in question 意为「正被讨论的、存疑的」，不是「在问题中」。动名词短语作主语时谓语用单数 is。

⑤ 并列谓语 + must never 的语序

原句：The technician should be alert for these and must never install them on an aircraft. 拆解：并列的两个谓语：should be alert for these（系表结构，alert 作表语）+ must never install them on an aircraft（情态动词 + 否定副词 + 动词原形）。 要点：never 必须放在情态动词之后、实义动词之前，写成 must never install，不能写成 must install never；be alert for 是固定搭配「对……保持警惕」。两个谓语共用主语 The technician，时态不必一致（一个是状态描述，一个是行为禁止）。
