Subtraction of Mixed Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Subtraction of Mixed Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 100 页

1. 原文

To subtract mixed numbers, find a common denominator for the fractions. Subtract the fractions from each other. It may be necessary to borrow from the larger whole number when subtracting the fractions. Subtract the whole numbers from each other. The final step is to combine the final whole number with the final fraction.

Example: What is the length of the grip of the bolt shown in Figure 3-3? The overall length of the bolt is 3 1⁄2 inches, the shank length is 3 1⁄8 inches, and the threaded portion is 15⁄16 inches long. To find the grip, subtract the length of the threaded portion from the length of the shank.

31⁄8 inches – 15⁄16 inches = grip length To subtract, start with the fractions. Borrowing is necessary because 5⁄16 is larger than 1⁄8 (or 2⁄16). From the whole number 3, borrow 1, which is actually 16⁄16. After borrowing, the first mixed number is now 2 18⁄16. This is because, 3 1⁄8 = 3 2⁄16 = 2 + 1 + 2⁄16 = 2 + 16⁄16 + 2⁄16 = 2 18⁄16.

3 1 – 1 5 = 3 2 – 1 5 = 2 18 – 1 5 = 113 8 16 16 16 16 16 16 Therefore, the grip length of the bolt is 1 13⁄16 inches.

(Note: The value for the overall length of the bolt was given in the example, but it was not needed to solve the problem. This type of information is sometimes referred to as a “distracter,” because it distracts from the information needed to solve the problem.)

2. 中文翻译

减带分数时，先为各分数找出公分母，再把各分数相减。减分数时，可能需要从较大的整数部分借 1。然后把各整数部分相减。最后一步是把最终的整数与最终的分数合并。

例：图 3-3 所示螺栓的夹持长度是多少？螺栓的总长为 3 1⁄2 英寸，杆部长 3 1⁄8 英寸，螺纹部分长 15⁄16 英寸。求夹持长度，就是从杆部长度中减去螺纹部分的长度。

3 1⁄8 英寸 – 15⁄16 英寸 = 夹持长度 相减时先从分数部分入手。必须借位，因为 5⁄16 大于 1⁄8（即 2⁄16）。从整数 3 中借 1，这个 1 实际上就是 16⁄16。借位之后，第一个带分数变为 2 18⁄16。这是因为：3 1⁄8 = 3 2⁄16 = 2 + 1 + 2⁄16 = 2 + 16⁄16 + 2⁄16 = 2 18⁄16。

3 1 – 1 5 = 3 2 – 1 5 = 2 18 – 1 5 = 113 8 16 16 16 16 16 16 因此，该螺栓的夹持长度为 1 13⁄16 英寸。

（注：例题给出了螺栓的总长，但解题并不需要这一数据。这类信息有时被称为“干扰项”，因为它会把注意力从解题所需的信息上引开。）

4. 语法解析

① It may be necessary to borrow … when subtracting the fractions（形式主语 + when + 动名词）

原句：It may be necessary to borrow from the larger whole number when subtracting the fractions.

拆解：it 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面的不定式 to borrow from the larger whole number；may be necessary 为“情态动词 + be + 形容词”；when subtracting the fractions 是“when + 动名词”构成的时间状语。

要点：it 作形式主语把长不定式后置，是技术文本的常规写法，可换成 Borrowing may be necessary，但语气与侧重不同。when + 动名词等于 when you subtract，前提是从句与主句主语一致；若主语不同则必须写完整的 when 从句（如 when the fraction is larger）。borrow from 的介词用 from，意为“从……借”。

② Borrowing is necessary because 5⁄16 is larger than 1⁄8（动名词主语 + 原因从句 + 比较级）

原句：Borrowing is necessary because 5⁄16 is larger than 1⁄8 (or 2⁄16).

拆解：主语 Borrowing 为动名词；系动词 is + 表语 necessary；because 引导原因状语从句，从句谓语 is larger than 是比较级结构。

要点：动名词作主语时谓语用单数（is）。比较级 larger than 后接的是被比较对象，同质才能比；本例把 1⁄8 换算成 2⁄16 才能与 5⁄16 直接比较，这也是括号里 or 2⁄16 的作用。中文说“A 大于 B”，英文固定为 A is larger/greater than B。

③ is sometimes referred to as a “distracter”（被动 + 过去分词短语作定语）

原句：This type of information is sometimes referred to as a “distracter,” because it distracts from the information needed to solve the problem.

拆解：主语 This type of information；谓语 is referred to as，即“be + 过去分词 + as”，as 后接补语；because 引导原因从句，从句中 needed to solve the problem 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 the information。

要点：refer to A as B（把 A 称为 B）变被动后成为 A is referred to as B，as 不可省，也不能换成 like。distract 的搭配是 distract from（使人从……分心），与形容词 distracter 同源。过去分词短语作定语要后置（information needed），现在分词短语则表主动或进行，注意区分。
