1. 原文

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with lines, angles, figures, and certain assumed properties in space. Applied geometry, as used in drawings, makes use of these properties to accurately and correctly represent objects graphically. In the past, draftsmen utilized a variety of instruments with various scales, shapes, and curves to make their drawings. Today, computer software graphics programs show drawings at nearly any scale, shape, and curve imaginable, outdating the need for additional instruments.

Several methods are used to illustrate objects graphically.

The most common are orthographic projections, pictorial drawings, diagrams, and flowcharts.

2. 中文翻译

几何学是数学的一个分支，研究直线、角、图形以及空间中某些假定成立的性质。制图中所讲的应用几何（applied geometry），正是利用这些性质以图形方式准确、正确地表示物体。过去，绘图员要使用比例尺、曲线板、曲线尺等种类繁多的仪器来绘图。如今，计算机软件绘图程序能以几乎任何可以想象的比例、形状和曲线来显示图样，使额外仪器的需求变得过时。

以图形方式表示物体可采用多种方法。

最常用的是正投影图、立体图、示意图和流程图。

4. 语法解析

① the branch of mathematics that deals with …（定语从句 + 主谓一致）

原句：Geometry is the branch of mathematics that deals with lines, angles, figures, and certain assumed properties in space.

拆解：主语 Geometry + 系动词 is + 表语 the branch of mathematics；that deals with … 是定语从句修饰 the branch，从句谓语 deals 用单数第三人称。

要点：deal with 在此意为「研究、论述」，不是「处理问题」。定语从句的谓语与先行词 the branch（单数）一致，不受 of mathematics 影响——与主谓一致里「看中心词」的规则相同。

② Applied geometry, as used in drawings, makes use of these properties to … represent objects（as 引导的省略插入语 + 目的不定式）

原句：Applied geometry, as used in drawings, makes use of these properties to accurately and correctly represent objects graphically.

拆解：主语 Applied geometry；as used in drawings 是插入的省略结构，完整为 as it is used in drawings；谓语 makes use of；to represent … 是目的不定式，accurately and correctly 两个副词修饰 represent。

要点：as used in … 是技术文本的标准写法，译「如……中所用的」。make use of = 利用，注意不是 make use of to do 之外另有 make use to；目的不定式前的两个副词用 and 连接，位置在 to 之后、动词之前。

③ …, outdating the need for additional instruments（现在分词短语作结果状语）

原句：Today, computer software graphics programs show drawings at nearly any scale, shape, and curve imaginable, outdating the need for additional instruments.

拆解：主语 computer software graphics programs，谓语 show；at nearly any scale, shape, and curve imaginable 是方式／范围状语，imaginable 后置修饰 curve；outdating … 是现在分词短语作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面整句所述事实。

要点：逗号后的现在分词短语常表结果（「从而使……过时」），逻辑主语是整句内容而非句首名词，这一点与定语用分词要区分开。imaginable、possible、available 等 -able 形容词修饰名词时习惯后置。outdate 由 out- + date 构成，作动词表示「使过时」。
