1. 原文

Welding can be traced back to the Bronze Age, but it was not until the 19th century that welding as we know it today was invented. Some of the first successful commercially manufactured aircraft were constructed from welded steel tube frames.

As the technology and manufacturing processes evolved in the aircraft and aerospace industry, lighter metals, such as aluminum, magnesium, and titanium, were used in their construction. New processes and methods of welding these metals were developed. This chapter provides some of the basic information needed to understand and initiate the various welding methods and processes.

Traditionally, welding is defined as a process that joins metal by melting or hammering the work pieces until they are united together. With the right equipment and instruction, almost anyone with some basic mechanical skill, dexterity, and practice can learn to weld.

There are three general types of welding: gas, electric arc, and electric resistance. Each type of welding has several variations, some of which are used in the construction of aircraft. Additionally, there are some new welding processes that have been developed in recent years that are highlighted for the purpose of information.

This chapter addresses the welding equipment, methods, and various techniques used during the repair of aircraft and fabrication of component parts, including the processes of brazing and soldering of various metals.

2. 中文翻译

焊接的历史可以追溯到青铜时代，但直到19世纪，我们今天所理解的焊接才被发明出来。一些最早成功实现商业化生产的飞机，就是用焊接钢管构架制造的。

随着航空与航天工业的技术和制造工艺不断演进，铝、镁、钛等更轻的金属被用于飞机制造。焊接这些金属的新工艺和新方法也随之开发出来。本章提供理解和入门各种焊接方法与工艺所需的一些基础知识。

传统上，焊接被定义为一种通过熔化或锤击工件、直至其结合为一体来连接金属的工艺。只要设备合适并有人指导，几乎任何具备基本机械技能、手部灵巧度并经过练习的人都能学会焊接。

焊接通常分为三大类：气焊、电弧焊和电阻焊。每一类又有若干变种，其中一些用于飞机制造。此外，近年来还开发出一些新的焊接工艺，本章也专门介绍，以供参考。

本章讲述飞机修理和零部件制造过程中所用的焊接设备、方法以及各种技术，还包括各种金属的钎焊与软钎焊工艺。

4. 语法解析

① not until 的强调句结构

原句：Welding can be traced back to the Bronze Age, but it was not until the 19th century that welding as we know it today was invented. 拆解：前半句为被动语态 can be traced back to；but 转折后是 It was not until ... that ... 强调句型，被强调的时间状语是 not until the 19th century；that 之后是真正的主谓 welding ... was invented，其中 as we know it today 是插入的定语从句修饰 welding。 要点：not until 提到句首用强调句时，后面要用 that 而不用 when；中文习惯译成“直到……才……”，否定落在“直到”之前，不要译成“不到19世纪不发明”。as we know it today 是插入成分，读句时先跳过它抓主干。

② 多层前置修饰的名词短语

原句：Some of the first successful commercially manufactured aircraft were constructed from welded steel tube frames. 拆解：主语中心词 Some of the first ... aircraft，aircraft 前依次是 successful（形容词）、commercially（副词修饰 manufactured）、manufactured（过去分词）三层前置定语；谓语为被动 were constructed from，介词 from 引出材料来源。 要点：英语前置定语按“限定词—副词—分词—名词”顺序堆叠，中文要拆成“……最早成功实现商业化生产的飞机”。construct from 表示“用……建造”，与 be made of 语义相近但更强调工艺来源。

③ 时间状语从句 + 插入语 + 被动主句

原句：As the technology and manufacturing processes evolved in the aircraft and aerospace industry, lighter metals, such as aluminum, magnesium, and titanium, were used in their construction. 拆解：As 引导时间状语从句，从句主语为并列的 the technology and manufacturing processes，谓语 evolved；主句主语 lighter metals 后接 such as 引出的同位语列举，谓语为被动 were used，in their construction 作范围状语。 要点：such as 引出的列举用逗号隔开、插在主谓之间，是典型的插入成分，去掉后句子依然完整。their 指代前面的 aircraft，代词还原是读懂这类长句的关键。

④ 被动定义句 + until 时间状语从句

原句：Traditionally, welding is defined as a process that joins metal by melting or hammering the work pieces until they are united together. 拆解：主句为被动 is defined as a process；that 引导定语从句修饰 process，从句谓语 joins，by melting or hammering 为方式状语，until they are united together 为时间状语从句，其中 they 指 work pieces。 要点：define A as B 变被动后是 A is defined as B；定语从句中的 until 从句属于从句内部，不要误读成修饰主句。unite 用被动 united 是因为工件是“被结合”的一方。

⑤ with 短语作条件状语

原句：With the right equipment and instruction, almost anyone with some basic mechanical skill, dexterity, and practice can learn to weld. 拆解：句首 With the right equipment and instruction 为介词短语作条件状语，相当于“如果有……”；主语 almost anyone 后又带一个 with 短语作后置定语，谓语 can learn，宾语 to weld 为不定式。 要点：两个 with 短语功能不同：句首的作状语，紧贴 anyone 的作定语。翻译时前者可译成“只要……”，后者译成“具备……的人”，否则中文会连成“和……的人”造成歧义。
