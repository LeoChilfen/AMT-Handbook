Universal Numbering System

通用 Ch 04 · Universal Numbering System · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

1. 原文

The universal numbering system provides a means of identifying standard drawing sizes. In the universal numbering system, each drawing number consists of six or seven digits. The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing. The number 1 indicates a drawing of 8½" × 11"; number 2 indicates an 11" × 17" drawing; number 4 represents a drawing of 17" × 22"; and 5 indicates a width of between 17 and 36 inches but on a continuous roll. Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E. The letter A is 8½" × 11", B is 11" × 17", C is 17" × 22", D is 22" × 34" and E is 34" × 44". There are additional letters, such as D1 at 24" × 36", E1 at 30" × 42" and additional sizes unique to even larger formats but generally reserved for inter-company operations. The remaining digits identify the drawing. Many firms have modified this basic system to conform to their needs. The letter or number depicting the standard drawing size may be prefixed to the number, separated from it by a dash. Other numbering systems provide a separate box preceding the drawing number for the drawing size identifier. In another modification of this system, the part number of the depicted assembly is assigned as the drawing number.

2. 中文翻译

通用编号体系提供了一种识别标准图纸尺寸的方法。在通用编号体系中，每个图纸编号由六位或七位数字组成。第一位数字总是 1、2、4 或 5，表示图纸的尺寸。数字 1 表示 8½" × 11" 的图纸；数字 2 表示 11" × 17" 的图纸；数字 4 表示 17" × 22" 的图纸；数字 5 则表示宽度在 17 至 36 英寸之间、但采用连续卷筒供纸的图纸。也使用字母（而且越来越普遍），最常见的字母是 A 到 E。字母 A 为 8½" × 11"，B 为 11" × 17"，C 为 17" × 22"，D 为 22" × 34"，E 为 34" × 44"。还有一些附加字母，例如 24" × 36" 的 D1、30" × 42" 的 E1，以及更大的幅面所特有的其他尺寸，但这些一般只用于公司之间的往来。余下的数字用于识别图纸。许多公司已对这一基本体系作了修改，以适合自身的需要。表示标准图纸尺寸的字母或数字可以加一短横，置于编号之前。其他编号体系则在图纸编号之前另设一个单独的框，用于填写图纸尺寸标识。这一体系的另一种改法，是把所绘组件的零件编号指定为图纸编号。

4. 语法解析

① provide a means of + 动名词

原句：The universal numbering system provides a means of identifying standard drawing sizes. 拆解：主语 The universal numbering system；谓语 provides；宾语 a means（单复数同形）；of identifying… 为介词短语作后置定语修饰 means。 要点：means 表“手段”时单复数同形（a means / two means），不能写成 mean；a means of 后接动名词。

② 介词短语前置 + consist of

原句：In the universal numbering system, each drawing number consists of six or seven digits. 拆解：In the universal numbering system 为范围状语前置；主语 each drawing number；谓语 consists of；宾语 six or seven digits。 要点：consist of 用主动形式表被动意义，没有 be consisted of；each + 单数名词作主语时谓语用单数 consists。

③ 并列谓语 + or 引导的择一列举

原句：The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing. 拆解：主语 The first digit；两个并列谓语 is… 与 indicates…；表语为四个并列数字（最后一项前用 or 表择一）；indicates 后接宾语 the size of the drawing。 要点：并列谓语共用同一主语时，第二个谓语前必须有 and；or 用于择一列举，比 and 的取舍意味更明确。

④ 插入语 + with 复合结构

原句：Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E. 拆解：主句为被动 Letters are also used；括号内 and becoming more prevalent 为插入成分（承前省略 are）；with the most common letters being A through E 为 with 复合结构（宾语 + 现在分词），作伴随状语。 要点：with 复合结构中，宾语与分词为主动关系用 -ing（being），被动关系用 -ed；括号插入语在技术文本中常补趋势信息，可译作“而且越来越普遍”。

⑤ there be + such as 列举 + 分词短语后置定语

原句：There are additional letters, such as D1 at 24" × 36", E1 at 30" × 42" and additional sizes unique to even larger formats but generally reserved for inter-company operations. 拆解：There be 句型表存在；such as 后并列 D1、E1 与 additional sizes 三项；unique to even larger formats 与 reserved for inter-company operations 为两个过去分词短语并列作后置定语修饰 sizes。 要点：such as 后列举应与前面的中心词同类；unique to 表示“……所独有的”，reserved for 表示“保留给……的”，两短语由 but 转折连接。

⑥ 被动语态 + as 补语 + 前置过去分词定语

原句：In another modification of this system, the part number of the depicted assembly is assigned as the drawing number. 拆解：In another modification of this system 为范围状语；主语 the part number of the depicted assembly（depicted 为过去分词作前置定语）；谓语 is assigned 为被动；as the drawing number 为介词短语作主语补足语。 要点：assign A as B（把 A 指定为 B）的被动式是 A is assigned as B；单个过去分词作前置定语（depicted assembly）要与分词短语作后置定语区分开。
