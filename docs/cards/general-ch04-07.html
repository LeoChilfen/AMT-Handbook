Assembly Drawing

通用 Ch 04 · Assembly Drawing · 原文 PDF 第 126 页

1. 原文

An assembly drawing is a description of an object made up of two or more parts. [Figure 4-4] It describes the object’s size and shape. Its primary purpose is to show the relationship of the various parts. An assembly drawing is usually more complex than a detail drawing and is often accompanied by detail drawings of various parts.

2. 中文翻译

装配图是对由两个或两个以上零件组成的物件的描述。[图 4-4] 它描述该物件的尺寸与形状。其主要用途是表示各零件之间的相互关系。装配图通常比零件图复杂，并且往往附有各零件的零件图。

4. 语法解析

① made up of two or more parts（过去分词短语作后置定语）

原句：An assembly drawing is a description of an object made up of two or more parts.

拆解：主干 An assembly drawing is a description of an object；made up of two or more parts 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 an object；be made up of 意为「由……组成」。

要点：make up 与 be made up of 方向相反：前者是「（若干部分）组成……」，后者是「由……组成」。此处省略了 which is，还原为 an object which is made up of … 更清楚。two or more 是「两个或更多」，不是「两个以上」的排除义。

② Its primary purpose is to show …（不定式作表语）

原句：Its primary purpose is to show the relationship of the various parts.

拆解：主语 Its primary purpose，系动词 is，表语为不定式 to show …；of the various parts 是 relationship 的后置定语。

要点：purpose、aim、function 作主语时，表语常用不定式，用动名词（is showing）不自然。relationship of A to B 表示「A 与 B 的关系」；后文安装图用 with relation to，可对照记忆。

③ is often accompanied by（被动语态 + by 引出伴随出现的事物）

原句：An assembly drawing is usually more complex than a detail drawing and is often accompanied by detail drawings of various parts.

拆解：两个并列谓语：is more complex than a detail drawing（比较结构）与 is usually … accompanied by detail drawings（被动）；of various parts 修饰 detail drawings。

要点：accompany 的被动 be accompanied by 后接「伴随出现的事物」，不是「陪同的人」。比较级 more complex than 后须接同类名词，不能省成 than a detail。usually、often 等频率副词位于 be 之后、实义动词之前。
