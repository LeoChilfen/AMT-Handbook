1. 原文

The exchange of ideas is essential to everyone, regardless of their vocation or position. This exchange is usually carried on by oral or written word; but under some conditions, the use of these alone is impractical. The aviation industry discovered that it could not depend entirely upon written or spoken words for the exchange of ideas, because misunderstanding and misinterpretation arose frequently. A written description of an object can be changed in meaning just by misplacing a comma, and the meaning of an oral description can be completely changed by using a wrong word. To avoid these possible errors, drawings are used to describe objects. For this reason, drawing is the draftsman’s language.

Drawing, in the aviation industry, is a method of conveying ideas concerning the construction or assembly of objects.

This is done with the help of lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols. It is important that the aviation mechanic who is to make or assemble the object understands the meaning of the different lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols that are used in a drawing. (See the “Lines and Their Meanings” section of this chapter.)

2. 中文翻译

思想的交流对每个人都不可或缺，无论其从事何种职业、处于何种地位。这种交流通常依靠口头或书面语言进行；但在某些情况下，仅靠这两种方式并不现实。航空业发现，它不能完全依靠书面或口头语言来交流思想，因为误解与曲解频繁发生。对一件物品的书面描述，只要错放一个逗号，含义就可能改变；而口头描述的含义，只要用错一个词，就可能被彻底改变。为避免这些可能的错误，人们使用图纸来描述物品。因此，图纸是绘图员的语言。

在航空业中，图纸是一种传达有关物品构造或装配之构想的方法。

这项工作要借助线条、注释、缩写和符号来完成。重要的是，负责制作或装配该物品的航空维修技师要理解图纸中所用各种线条、注释、缩写和符号的含义。（参见本章「线条及其含义」一节。）

4. 语法解析

① It is important that … understands …（形式主语 + 主语从句 + 定语从句）

原句：It is important that the aviation mechanic who is to make or assemble the object understands the meaning of the different lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols that are used in a drawing.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句主语 the aviation mechanic 后接 who is to make or assemble the object 定语从句，谓语 understands 保持第三人称单数；句末 that are used in a drawing 是修饰那四种图面要素的定语从句。

要点：主语被定语从句拉长后，谓语仍要与中心词 aviation mechanic 一致，写成 understand 就错了。be to do 在此表「按职责将要/应当做」，不是单纯将来时。It is important that 后接陈述语气，手册文体不加 should。

② can be changed … by misplacing（情态被动 + by doing 方式状语）

原句：A written description of an object can be changed in meaning just by misplacing a comma, and the meaning of an oral description can be completely changed by using a wrong word.

拆解：两个并列分句都用「can + be + 过去分词」；in meaning 是方面状语，just 强调「仅仅」；by misplacing a comma 与 by using a wrong word 都是 by + 动名词，表示致变的手段。

要点：by 后接动名词而非不定式，是「因……而改变」的固定写法。in meaning 是「在含义上」，不能译成「在意思里」。两分句结构对称，翻译时保留对照。

③ 抽象名词作主语：misunderstanding and misinterpretation arose

原句：The aviation industry discovered that it could not depend entirely upon written or spoken words for the exchange of ideas, because misunderstanding and misinterpretation arose frequently.

拆解：主句谓语 discovered 后接 that 宾语从句；从句中 could not depend entirely upon 是「情态动词 + 否定 + 副词 + 动词短语」，for the exchange of ideas 说明依赖的目的；because 引导原因状语从句，其主语是两个并列抽象名词。

要点：技术英语常把「人们误解」写成「误解产生」（misunderstanding arose），主语由人换成抽象名词，语气更客观。arise 是不及物动词，没有被动式，不能写 be arisen；对比及物动词 raise（需带宾语）。
