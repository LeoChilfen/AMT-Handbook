1. 原文

The exchange of ideas is essential to everyone, regardless of their vocation or position. This exchange is usually carried on by oral or written word; but under some conditions, the use of these alone is impractical. The aviation industry discovered that it could not depend entirely upon written or spoken words for the exchange of ideas, because misunderstanding and misinterpretation arose frequently. A written description of an object can be changed in meaning just by misplacing a comma, and the meaning of an oral description can be completely changed by using a wrong word. To avoid these possible errors, drawings are used to describe objects. For this reason, drawing is the draftsman’s language.

Drawing, in the aviation industry, is a method of conveying ideas concerning the construction or assembly of objects. This is done with the help of lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols. It is important that the aviation mechanic who is to make or assemble the object understands the meaning of the different lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols that are used in a drawing. (See the “Lines and Their Meanings” section of this chapter.)

2. 中文翻译

思想交流对每个人都必不可少，无论其职业或职位如何。这种交流通常借助口头或书面文字进行；但在某些情况下，单靠这两种方式并不现实。航空业发现，它不能完全依赖书面或口头文字来交流思想，因为误解和曲解经常产生。对一件物体的书面描述，只要把逗号放错位置，含义就可能改变；而口头描述只要用错一个词，含义就可能完全走样。为避免这些可能的差错，人们用图纸来描述物体。正因如此，制图是绘图员的语言。

在航空业中，图纸是传达有关物体制造或装配构想的一种方法。这是借助线条、注释、缩写和符号来完成的。负责制作或装配该物体的航空维修人员，必须理解图纸中所用各种线条、注释、缩写和符号的含义。（参见本章“线条及其含义”一节。）

4. 语法解析

① 让步性状语 regardless of 引导的介词短语

原句：The exchange of ideas is essential to everyone, regardless of their vocation or position. 拆解：主语 The exchange of ideas（of ideas 为后置定语）+ 系表结构 is essential to everyone + regardless of their vocation or position 为介词短语作让步状语，置于句末。 要点：regardless of 后接名词或名词短语，相当于“不论……”，比 although 从句简洁，是规章与说明文的高频结构；它后面不能直接接句子，要接从句须改用 regardless of the fact that 或 although。

② 分号连接的两个并列分句（转折对比）

原句：This exchange is usually carried on by oral or written word; but under some conditions, the use of these alone is impractical. 拆解：前一分句为被动语态 is carried on by…（carry on 为短语动词，介词 by 引出方式）；分号后 but 引出转折分句，主语 the use of these alone，系表 is impractical。 要点：carry on 的宾语 exchange 被提到了主语位置，故用被动；these alone 指代前面的 oral or written word，翻译时须还原为“单靠这两种方式”。分号连接两个关系紧密的独立分句，比用句号更能体现对照关系。

③ that 宾语从句 + because 原因状语从句

原句：The aviation industry discovered that it could not depend entirely upon written or spoken words for the exchange of ideas, because misunderstanding and misinterpretation arose frequently. 拆解：主句 The aviation industry discovered + that 引导宾语从句（主语 it，谓语 could not depend upon，宾语 written or spoken words，for the exchange of ideas 为目的状语）；逗号后 because 引导原因状语从句，主语为并列的 misunderstanding and misinterpretation，谓语 arose（arise 的过去式）。 要点：depend upon/on 后接名词，不能接不定式；arise 为不及物动词，过去式 arose、过去分词 arisen，易与及物动词 raise 混淆。原因从句后置时通常用逗号与主句隔开。

④ by + 动名词表示致变手段

原句：A written description of an object can be changed in meaning just by misplacing a comma, and the meaning of an oral description can be completely changed by using a wrong word. 拆解：两个并列分句均为“情态动词 can + be + 过去分词”的被动结构；by misplacing a comma 与 by using a wrong word 为介词 by + 动名词，表示致变的手段；in meaning 为方面状语。 要点：by doing 表示“通过做某事”，是技术文本说明手段的常用结构；此处主语 description 是“被改变”的对象，故用被动，若写成 by misplace 则语法错误。

⑤ 不定式作目的状语置于句首

原句：To avoid these possible errors, drawings are used to describe objects. 拆解：句首 To avoid these possible errors 为不定式短语作目的状语；主句为被动语态 drawings are used，其后 to describe objects 说明用途。 要点：不定式位于句首表目的时须用逗号与主句隔开；be used to do 表示“被用来做”，与 be used to doing（习惯于）形近义远，是常见易错点。

⑥ It is important that 句型 + 嵌套定语从句

原句：It is important that the aviation mechanic who is to make or assemble the object understands the meaning of the different lines, notes, abbreviations, and symbols that are used in a drawing. 拆解：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句主语 the aviation mechanic 后接 who is to make or assemble the object 定语从句，谓语 understands（第三人称单数，不可用 understand）；宾语 the meaning of… 后又带 that are used in a drawing 定语从句，修饰四个并列名词。 要点：主语从句的谓语要用陈述语气，此处 understands 与 mechanic 一致；be to do 在此表按职责应当做的事；句中一前一后两个定语从句，翻译时宜把 who 从句前置作定语、把 that 从句后置。
