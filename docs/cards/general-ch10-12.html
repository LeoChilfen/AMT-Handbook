Inspection of Welds

1. 原文

A discussion of welds in this chapter is confined to judging the quality of completed welds by visual means. Although the appearance of the completed weld is not a positive indication of quality, it provides a good clue about the care used in making it. A properly designed joint weld is stronger than the base metal that it joins. The characteristics of a properly welded joint are discussed in the following paragraphs.

A good weld is uniform in width; the ripples are even and well feathered into the base metal and show no burn due to overheating. The weld has good penetration and is free of gas pockets, porosity, or inclusions. The edges of the bead are not in a straight line, yet the weld is good since penetration is excellent.

Penetration is the depth of fusion in a weld. Thorough fusion is the most important characteristic contributing to a sound weld. Penetration is affected by the thickness of the material to be joined, the size of the filler rod, and how it is added. In a butt weld, the penetration should be 100 percent of the thickness of the base metal. On a fillet weld, the penetration requirements are 25 to 50 percent of the thickness of the base metal.

To assist further in determining the quality of a welded joint, several examples of incorrect welds are discussed in the following paragraphs.

The weld in the figure was made too rapidly. The long and pointed appearance of the ripples was caused by an excessive amount of heat or an oxidizing flame. If the weld were cross-sectioned, it would probably disclose gas pockets, porosity, and slag inclusions. The figure illustrates a weld that has improper penetration and cold laps caused by insufficient heat. It appears rough and irregular, and its edges are not feathered into the base metal.

The puddle tends to boil during the welding operation if an excessive amount of acetylene is used. This often leaves slight bumps along the center and craters at the finish of the weld. Cross-checks are apparent if the body of the weld is sound. If the weld were cross-sectioned, pockets and porosity are visible.

A bad weld has irregular edges and considerable variation in the depth of penetration. It often has the appearance of a cold weld.

2. 中文翻译

本章对焊缝的讨论仅限于用目视方法判断已完成焊缝的质量。虽然完工焊缝的外观并不能可靠地说明质量，但它能很好地提示焊接时的用心程度。设计得当的接头焊缝比它所连接的母材更强。正确焊接接头应具备的特征将在以下各段中讨论。

良好的焊缝宽度均匀；焊波均匀，并很好地熔入母材，没有因过热而产生的烧伤。焊缝熔深良好，没有气孔、疏松或夹杂物。焊道边缘并不成一条直线，但由于熔深极好，焊缝仍然是合格的。

熔深是焊缝中熔合的深度。完全熔合是使焊缝致密可靠的最重要特征。熔深受被连接材料的厚度、填充焊条的粗细以及其添加方式的影响。在对接焊缝中，熔深应达到母材厚度的 100%。在角焊缝中，熔深要求为母材厚度的 25% 至 50%。

为进一步帮助判断焊接接头的质量，以下各段将讨论若干不正确焊缝的实例。

图中的焊缝焊接速度过快。焊波长而尖的外观，是热量过多或氧化焰造成的。如果把该焊缝切开看横截面，很可能会发现有气孔、疏松和夹渣。图中还示意了另一种焊缝，其熔深不当，并因热量不足而产生冷搭。它看起来粗糙、不规则，边缘没有熔入母材。

如果乙炔用量过多，焊接过程中熔池容易沸腾。这常常在焊缝中心线上留下轻微的凸起，并在焊缝收尾处留下弧坑。如果焊缝本体致密，就会出现横裂纹。如果把该焊缝切开，可以看到气孔和疏松。

劣质焊缝边缘不规则，熔深深度变化相当大。它常常呈现出冷焊的外观。

4. 语法解析

① be confined to + 动名词

原句：A discussion of welds in this chapter is confined to judging the quality of completed welds by visual means. 拆解：主语 A discussion of welds in this chapter；谓语 is confined to（被动语态）；to 是介词，后接动名词短语 judging the quality of completed welds；by visual means 作方式状语。 要点：be confined to 中的 to 属介词，后面只能接名词或动名词，不能接动词原形；by … means 表示「用……手段」，means 单复数同形；同族搭配 be limited to / be restricted to 也是「介词 to」。

② although 让步从句 + 代词 it 回指

原句：Although the appearance of the completed weld is not a positive indication of quality, it provides a good clue about the care used in making it. 拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句；主句主语 it 回指 the appearance of the completed weld；a good clue about the care 中 about 短语作 clue 的定语；used in making it 为过去分词短语后置修饰 the care。 要点：英语里 although 不能与 but 连用，译成中文时可加「但」；主句的 it 指代前面整个名词短语，译文要还原成「外观」才通顺；a positive indication of quality 直译「质量的正面指标」，宜译作「可靠说明质量的依据」。

③ 比较级 + 定语从句

原句：A properly designed joint weld is stronger than the base metal that it joins. 拆解：主语 A properly designed joint weld（副词 + 过去分词作前置定语）；系动词 is；表语 stronger；than 引导比较状语；the base metal 后接 that 引导的定语从句，that 在从句中作 joins 的宾语。 要点：than 后面是比较对象，不能省略；比较对象带定语从句时，从句主语用 that／it 回指，不要重复整串名词；properly designed 这类「副词 + 过去分词」组合要放在名词前，不能后置。

④ 虚拟条件句（were + would）

原句：If the weld were cross-sectioned, it would probably disclose gas pockets, porosity, and slag inclusions. 拆解：If 引导条件从句，用 were cross-sectioned（被动语态的虚拟形式）；主句 it would probably disclose …，为 would + 动词原形。 要点：表示与现在事实相反或对情况的推测时，从句的 be 动词一律用 were；主句用 would／could／might + 原形；disclose 本义「使显露」，此处译「（切开后）发现有」；三个并列宾语共用 disclose，最后一个宾语前才用 and。

⑤ 不定式目的状语前置 + 主句被动

原句：To assist further in determining the quality of a welded joint, several examples of incorrect welds are discussed in the following paragraphs. 拆解：句首 To assist further in determining … 为目的状语；主句 several examples of incorrect welds are discussed 为被动语态，in the following paragraphs 作地点／范围状语。 要点：assist in doing sth 中的 in 可以省略，但 assist sb to do sth 不用 in，两种搭配别混；目的状语放句首时用逗号与主句隔开；被动语态下动作发出者（作者）不必出现，这是技术英文的常见写法。
