1. 原文

The light sport aircraft (LSA) category includes gliders, airplanes, gyroplanes, powered parachutes, weight-shift and lighter-than-air aircraft. There are two general types of LSAs:

Special (SLSA) and Experimental (ELSA). The SLSA are factory built and the ESLA are kit-built. This new category of aircraft was added to the regulations in 2004. (Refer to 14 CFR sections 21.190, 65.107, and 91.327, all dated July 27, 2004.)

SAMPLE Just as industry standard specifications have replaced many of the military standards to define products that are destined to be part of the Department of Defense (DoD) inventory, so too have industry standards come into the FAA sights for documenting certain information. Quality is one example.

The Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) has developed AS 9100 and AS 9110 as auditing standards for aerospace facilities and specifically repair stations. Likewise, ISO 9001 is being adopted by the FAA as a system of measuring their performance. Therefore, it was logical that when the FAA looked to develop the standards for this newest category of aircraft, they again looked to industry, and this time it was the American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM).

The ASTM developed a comprehensive list of consensus standards for use by manufacturers, regulators, maintenance facilities, LSA owners, and service providers. It is unique that these standards are the first ones in over 100 years to solely address the issue of recreational aircraft use. It is also the first complete set of industry consensus standards covering the design, manufacture, and use of recreational aircraft that was developed by a non-government agency. The ASTM committee that developed these LSA standards did so to ensure the quality of products and services to support both the national and the international regulatory structures for LSAs.

Over 20 standards have been generated, and more are being developed to cover this diversity of aircraft. This handbook only incorporates a review of F2483-05, “Standard Practice for Maintenance and the Development of Maintenance Manuals for Light Sport Aircraft (LSA)” a six-page document comprised of the following 12 sections:

  1. Scope 2. Referenced Documents 3. Terminology 4. Significance and Use 5. Aircraft Maintenance Manual 6. Line Maintenance, Repairs, and Alterations 7. Heavy Maintenance, Repairs, and Alterations 8. Overhaul 9. Major Repairs and Alterations 10. Task-Specific Training 11. Safety Directives 12. Keywords The scope of that document is basically twofold:

• To provide guidelines for the qualification necessary to accomplish various levels of maintenance on LSA.

• To provide the content and structure of maintenance manuals for aircraft and their components that are operated as LSAs.

Some additional definitions from section 3, Terminology, that help to better explain the LSA concepts are:

• Annual condition inspection—defined as a detailed inspection accomplished once a year in accordance with instructions provided in the maintenance manual supplied with the LSA. The purpose of this inspection is to look for any wear, corrosion, or damage that would cause the LSA not to be in condition for safe operation.

• Heavy maintenance—any maintenance, inspection, repair, or alteration a manufacturer has designated that requires specialized training, equipment, or facilities.

• Line maintenance—any repair, maintenance, scheduled checks, servicing, inspections, or alterations not considered heavy maintenance that are approved by the manufacturer and is specified in the manufacturer’s maintenance manual.

• LSA repairman–inspection—a U.S. FAA-certified LSA repairman with an inspection rating per 14 CFR part 65. This person is authorized to perform the 100- hour/annual inspection of the aircraft that they own.

• LSA repairman–maintenance—a U.S. FAA-certified LSA repairman with a maintenance rating per 14 CFR part 65. This person is allowed to perform the required maintenance and can also accomplish the 100-hour/ annual inspection.

• Major repair, alteration, or maintenance—any repair, alteration, or maintenance where instructions to complete the task are excluded from the maintenance manual.

• Minor repair, alteration, or maintenance—any repair, alteration, or maintenance where instructions to complete the task are included in the maintenance manual.

The 100-hour inspection is the same as the annual inspection, except for the interval of time. The requirements for whether or not the 100-hour inspection is applicable are exactly the same as the criteria for the standard 100-hour/annual required of non-LSA aircraft.

正如工业标准规范已取代许多军用标准、用以界定进入国防部（DoD）库存的产品一样，工业标准也进入了 FAA 的视野，被用于记录某些信息。质量就是一例。

美国汽车工程师学会（SAE）制定了 AS 9100 和 AS 9110，作为航空航天设施、特别是修理站的审核标准。同样，ISO 9001 也正被 FAA 采纳为衡量其绩效的体系。因此，当 FAA 着手为这一最新的航空器类别制定标准时，他们合乎逻辑地再次求诸工业界，这一次是美国材料与试验协会（ASTM）。

ASTM 制定了一份全面的共识性标准清单，供制造厂、监管机构、维修单位、LSA 机主和服务提供者使用。其独特之处在于：这些标准是 100 多年来首批专门针对休闲类航空器使用问题的标准；它也是首套由非政府机构制定的、涵盖休闲类航空器设计、制造和使用的完整行业共识标准。制定这些 LSA 标准的 ASTM 委员会之所以这样做，是为了确保产品与服务的质量，以支撑 LSA 的国内与国际监管体系。

目前已制定 20 多项标准，且还有更多标准正在制定中，以覆盖此类航空器的多样性。本手册只纳入了对 F2483-05《轻型运动航空器（LSA）维修与维修手册制定的标准做法》的评述；该文件共 6 页，由以下 12 节组成：

  1. 范围 2. 引用文件 3. 术语 4. 意义与用途 5. 飞机维修手册 6. 航线维修、修理与改装 7. 重维修、修理与改装 8. 翻修 9. 重大修理与改装 10. 任务专项培训 11. 安全指令 12. 关键词 该文件的范围基本上是两方面：

• 为完成 LSA 各级维修所需的资质提供指南。

• 为作为 LSA 运行的航空器及其部件的维修手册提供内容与结构。

第 3 节「术语」中的另外一些定义，有助于更好地理解 LSA 的概念：

• 年度状态检查（annual condition inspection）——指按 LSA 随附维修手册中给出的说明，每年实施一次的详细检查。该检查的目的是查找任何会导致 LSA 不处于安全运行状态的磨损、腐蚀或损伤。

• 重维修（heavy maintenance）——制造厂指定的、需要专门培训、设备或设施的任何维修、检查、修理或改装。

• 航线维修（line maintenance）——不属于重维修的任何修理、维修、定期检查、勤务、检查或改装，且须经制造厂批准并在制造厂维修手册中作出规定。

• LSA 检查类维修人员（LSA repairman–inspection）——按 14 CFR 第 65 部取得检查等级的美国 FAA 持证 LSA 维修人员。此人有权对其本人拥有的航空器实施 100 小时／年度检查。

• LSA 维修类维修人员（LSA repairman–maintenance）——按 14 CFR 第 65 部取得维修等级的美国 FAA 持证 LSA 维修人员。此人可实施所需的维修工作，也可完成 100 小时／年度检查。

• 重大修理、改装或维修——完成任务所需的说明未被维修手册收录的任何修理、改装或维修。

• 轻微修理、改装或维修——完成任务所需的说明已包含在维修手册中的任何修理、改装或维修。

100 小时检查与年度检查相同，只是时间间隔不同。100 小时检查是否适用的判定标准，与非 LSA 航空器所要求的常规 100 小时／年度检查的判定标准完全相同。

4. 语法解析

① Just as …, so too … 关联结构 + 倒装

原句：Just as industry standard specifications have replaced many of the military standards to define products that are destined to be part of the Department of Defense (DoD) inventory, so too have industry standards come into the FAA sights for documenting certain information.

拆解：Just as 引导方式状语从句，从句中 to define products 是目的状语，that are destined to be part of … 是修饰 products 的定语从句；主句由 so too 引出并倒装：have industry standards come（助动词 have 提到主语之前）。

要点：just as …, so too … 是「正如……，……也同样……」的固定关联结构，主句必须倒装，语序为 so too + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词。destined to be 意为「注定成为」，不是被动语态的「被指定」。

② It 作形式主语 + 序数词 + 不定式

原句：It is unique that these standards are the first ones in over 100 years to solely address the issue of recreational aircraft use.

拆解：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；in over 100 years 是范围状语；to solely address … 是不定式短语作 the first ones 的后置定语。

要点：the first/last/only + 名词 + to do 是固定搭配，表示「第一个做某事的」。solely 插在 to 与动词之间属于分裂不定式，正式文本中可接受，译作「专门、仅仅」。

③ the same as 同级比较 + except for

原句：The 100-hour inspection is the same as the annual inspection, except for the interval of time.

拆解：the same as 构成同级比较，as 后接被比较的名词 the annual inspection；except for 引出唯一的例外项 the interval of time。

要点：the same as 后接名词短语，the same … that 后接从句，两者不可混用。except for 用于「整体相同、只有某一点例外」，比 except 更适合此处，译作「只是……不同」最自然。
