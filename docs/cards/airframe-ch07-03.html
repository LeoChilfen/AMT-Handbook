Manufacturing & In-Service Damage

1. 原文

Manufacturing Defects

Manufacturing defects include:

Manufacturing damage includes anomalies, such as porosity, microcracking, and delaminations resulting from processing discrepancies. It also includes such items as inadvertent edge cuts, surface gouges and scratches, damaged fastener holes, and impact damage. Examples of flaws occurring in manufacturing include a contaminated bondline surface or inclusions, such as prepreg backing paper or separation film, that is inadvertently left between plies during layup. Inadvertent (nonprocess) damage can occur in detail parts or components during assembly or transport or during operation.

A part is resin rich if too much resin is used, for nonstructural applications this is not necessarily bad, but it adds weight. A part is called resin starved if too much resin is bled off during the curing process or if not enough resin is applied during the wet layup process. Resin-starved areas are indicated by fibers that show to the surface. The ratio of 60:40 fiber to resin ratio is considered optimum. Sources of manufacturing defects include:

• Improper cure or processing • Mislocation of holes or details Damage can occur at several scales within the composite material and structural configuration. This ranges from damage in the matrix and fiber to broken elements and failure of bonded or bolted attachments. The extent of damage controls repeated load life and residual strength and is critical to damage tolerance.

Fiber Breakage

Fiber breakage can be critical because structures are typically designed to be fiber dominant (i.e., fibers carry most of the loads). Fortunately, fiber failure is typically limited to a zone near the point of impact and is constrained by the impact object size and energy. Only a few of the service-related events listed in the previous section could lead to large areas of fiber damage.

Matrix Imperfections

Matrix imperfections usually occur on the matrix-fiber interface or in the matrix parallel to the fibers. These imperfections can slightly reduce some of the material properties but are seldom critical to the structure, unless the matrix degradation is widespread. Accumulation of matrix cracks can cause the degradation of matrix-dominated properties. For laminates designed to transmit loads with their fibers (fiber dominant), only a slight reduction of properties is observed when the matrix is severely damaged. Matrix cracks, or microcracks, can significantly reduce properties dependent on the resin or the fiber-resin interface, such as interlaminar shear and compression strength. Micro-cracking can have a very negative effect on properties of high-temperature resins. Matrix imperfections may develop into delaminations, which are a more critical type of damage.

Delamination & Debonds

Delaminations form on the interface between the layers in the laminate. Delaminations may form from matrix cracks that grow into the interlaminar layer or from low-energy impact. Debonds can also form from production nonadhesion along the bondline between two elements and initiate delamination in adjacent laminate layers. Under certain conditions, delaminations or debonds can grow when subjected to repeated loading and can cause catastrophic failure when the laminate is loaded in compression. The criticality of delaminations or debonds depend on:

• Number of delaminations at a given location.

• Location—in the thickness of laminate, in the structure, proximity to free edges, stress concentration region, geometrical discontinuities, etc.

• Loads—behavior of delaminations and debonds depend on loading type. They have little effect on the response of laminates loaded in tension. Under compression or shear loading, however, the sublaminates adjacent to the delaminations or debonded elements may buckle and cause a load redistribution mechanism that leads to structural failure.

Combinations of Damages

In general, impact events cause combinations of damages. High-energy impacts by large objects (e.g., turbine blades) may lead to broken elements and failed attachments. The resulting damage may include significant fiber failure, matrix cracking, delamination, broken fasteners, and debonded elements. Damage caused by low-energy impact is more contained, but may also include a combination of broken fibers, matrix cracks, and multiple delaminations.

Flawed Fastener Holes

Improper hole drilling, poor fastener installation, and missing fasteners may occur in manufacturing. Hole elongation can occur due to repeated load cycling in service.

In-Service Defects

In-service defects include:

• Environmental degradation • Cracks from local overload Many honeycomb structures, such as wing spoilers, fairings, flight controls, and landing gear doors, have thin face sheets which have experienced durability problems that could be grouped into three categories: low resistance to impact, liquid ingression, and erosion. These structures have adequate stiffness and strength but low resistance to a service environment in which parts are crawled over, tools dropped, and service personnel are often unaware of the fragility of thin-skinned sandwich parts. Damages to these components, such as core crush, impact damages, and disbonds, are quite often easy to detect with a visual inspection due to their thin face sheets. However, they are sometimes overlooked or damaged by service personnel who do not want to delay aircraft departure or bring attention to their accidents, which might reflect poorly on their performance record. Therefore, damages are sometimes allowed to go unchecked, often resulting in growth of the damage due to liquid ingression into the core. Nondurable design details (e.g., improper core edge close-outs) also lead to liquid ingression.

The repair of parts due to liquid ingression can vary depending on the liquid, most commonly water or Skydrol (hydraulic fluid). Water tends to create additional damage in repaired parts when cured unless all moisture is removed from the part. Most repair material systems cure at temperatures above the boiling point of water, which can cause a disbond at the skin-to-core interface wherever trapped water resides. For this reason, core drying cycles are typically included prior to performing any repair. Some operators take the extra step of placing a damaged but unrepaired part in the autoclave to dry to preclude any additional damage from occurring during the cure of the repair. Skydrol presents a different problem. Once the core of a sandwich part is saturated, complete removal of Skydrol is almost impossible. The part continues to weep the liquid even in cure until bondlines can become contaminated and full bonding does not occur. Removal of contaminated core and adhesive as part of the repair is highly recommended.

Erosion capabilities of composite materials have been known to be less than that of aluminum and, as a result, their application in leading-edge surfaces has been generally avoided. However, composites have been used in areas of highly complex geometry, but generally with an erosion coating. The durability and maintainability of some erosion coatings are less than ideal. Another problem, not as obvious as the first, is that edges of doors or panels can erode if they are exposed to the air stream. This erosion can be attributed to improper design or installation/fit-up. On the other hand, metal structures in contact or in the vicinity of these composite parts may show corrosion damage due to inappropriate choice of aluminum alloy, damaged corrosion sealant of metal parts during assembly or at splices, or insufficient sealant and/or lack of glass fabric isolation plies at the interfaces of spars, ribs, and fittings.

Corrosion

Many fiberglass and Kevlar ® parts have a fine aluminum mesh for lightning protection. This aluminum mesh often corrodes around the bolt or screw holes. The corrosion affects the electrical bonding of the panel, and the aluminum mesh needs to be removed and new mesh installed to restore the electrical bonding of the panel.

Ultraviolet (UV) light affects the strength of composite materials. Composite structures need to be protected by a top coating to prevent the effects of UV light. Special UV primers and paints have been developed to protect composite materials.

2. 中文翻译

制造缺陷

制造缺陷包括：

制造损伤包括由工艺偏差引起的孔隙、微裂纹和分层等异常。它还包括非有意的边缘切伤、表面凿痕与划痕、受损的紧固件孔以及冲击损伤。制造过程中出现的缺陷实例包括胶接面污染，或铺层时无意间夹在层间的夹杂物，例如预浸料背纸或隔离膜。非有意的（非工艺性）损伤可能在装配、运输或使用过程中发生于零件或部件上。

如果树脂用量过多，零件即为富树脂；对非结构用途而言这未必是坏事，但会增加重量。如果在固化过程中排出的树脂过多，或在湿法铺层过程中施加的树脂不足，零件就称为贫树脂。贫树脂区域的标志是纤维显露至表面。纤维与树脂之比为 60:40 被认为是最佳比例。制造缺陷的来源包括：

• 固化或工艺不当 • 孔或细节件位置错误
损伤可在复合材料内部及结构构型的多个尺度上发生，从基体与纤维中的损伤，到构件断裂以及胶接或螺接连接失效。损伤的程度决定重复载荷寿命和剩余强度，对损伤容限至关重要。

纤维断裂

纤维断裂可能成为关键问题，因为结构通常按纤维主导设计（即纤维承担大部分载荷）。所幸纤维失效通常局限于撞击点附近的区域，并受撞击物尺寸与能量的制约。上一节所列的使用相关事件中，只有少数几种会导致大面积纤维损伤。

基体缺陷

基体缺陷通常出现在基体—纤维界面上，或出现在与纤维平行的基体内部。这些缺陷会略微降低某些材料性能，但除非基体退化大面积扩展，否则很少对结构构成关键影响。基体裂纹的累积会导致基体主导性能的退化。对于按纤维传载设计的层合板（纤维主导），即使基体严重损伤，性能也只观察到轻微下降。基体裂纹（或称微裂纹）会显著降低依赖树脂或纤维—树脂界面的性能，例如层间剪切强度和压缩强度。微裂纹对高温树脂的性能有非常不利的影响。基体缺陷可能发展成分层，而分层是更为关键的一类损伤。

分层与脱粘

分层在层合板各层之间的界面上形成。分层可能由伸入层间层的基体裂纹发展而来，也可能由低能量冲击造成。脱粘也可能由生产过程中两个构件之间胶接线上的不粘合形成，并在相邻的层合板层中引发分层。在一定条件下，分层或脱粘在承受重复载荷时会扩展，并在层合板承受压缩载荷时导致灾难性失效。分层或脱粘的关键程度取决于：

• 给定位置处的分层数量。

• 位置——在层合板厚度中的位置、在结构中的位置、与自由边缘的接近程度、应力集中区、几何不连续处等。

• 载荷——分层与脱粘的表现取决于载荷类型。它们对承受拉伸载荷的层合板的响应影响很小。但在压缩或剪切载荷下，与分层或脱粘构件相邻的子层合板可能屈曲，并引起载荷重分布机制，从而导致结构失效。

损伤的组合

一般而言，冲击事件会造成多种损伤的组合。大型物体（例如涡轮叶片）的高能量冲击可能导致构件断裂和连接失效。由此产生的损伤可能包括显著的纤维失效、基体开裂、分层、紧固件断裂以及构件脱粘。低能量冲击造成的损伤范围较为局限，但也可能包括断纤维、基体裂纹和多处分层的组合。

紧固件孔缺陷

制造中可能出现钻孔不当、紧固件安装不良和紧固件缺失。使用中反复的载荷循环可能造成孔拉长。

使用中缺陷

使用中缺陷包括：

• 环境退化 • 局部过载引起的裂纹
许多蜂窝结构，如机翼扰流板、整流罩、飞行操纵面和起落架舱门，其面板很薄，曾出现耐久性问题，可归为三类：抗冲击能力低、液体侵入和侵蚀。这些结构具有足够的刚度和强度，但对使用环境的抵抗能力低——在这种环境中，零件会被人爬踩、工具会掉落，而维修人员往往并未意识到薄蒙皮夹层件的脆弱性。这类部件的损伤，如芯材压溃、冲击损伤和脱粘，由于面板很薄，通常很容易通过目视检查发现。然而，有时这些损伤会被忽视，或被那些不愿延误飞机起飞、也不愿让自己的事故引起注意的维修人员弄伤——因为那可能影响他们的绩效记录。因此，损伤有时被放任不查，往往因液体侵入芯材而导致损伤扩展。不耐久的设计细节（例如芯材边缘收口不当）也会导致液体侵入。

因液体侵入而对部件进行修理，做法因液体不同而异，最常见的是水或 Skydrol（液压油）。除非把部件中的水分全部去除，否则水在修理后的部件固化时往往会造成额外损伤。大多数修理材料体系的固化温度高于水的沸点，这会在存有滞留水的部位造成蒙皮—芯材界面的脱粘。因此，在进行任何修理之前，通常都要安排芯材干燥循环。有些运营方还多走一步，把已损伤但尚未修理的部件放入热压罐中干燥，以避免修理固化期间产生额外损伤。Skydrol 带来的则是另一种问题。夹层件的芯材一旦被 Skydrol 浸透，几乎不可能将其彻底清除。即使在固化过程中，部件仍会不断渗出液体，直到胶接面受到污染而无法实现完全粘合。强烈建议在修理中切除受污染的芯材和胶粘剂。

复合材料的抗侵蚀能力已知不如铝，因此一般避免将其用于前缘表面。不过，复合材料已用于几何形状高度复杂的部位，但通常带有抗侵蚀涂层。某些抗侵蚀涂层的耐久性和可维护性并不理想。另一个不那么明显的问题是，舱门或壁板的边缘如果暴露在气流中，也会发生侵蚀。这种侵蚀可归因于设计不当或安装/配合不当。另一方面，与这些复合材料部件接触或邻近的金属结构，可能因铝合金选择不当、装配时或对接处金属件防腐密封胶破损，或翼梁、翼肋和接头界面处密封胶不足和/或缺玻璃布隔离层而出现腐蚀损伤。

腐蚀

许多玻璃钢和 Kevlar® 部件带有一层细密的铝网用于防雷击。这层铝网常在螺栓孔或螺钉孔周围腐蚀。腐蚀会影响壁板的电搭接，需要去除铝网并安装新网，以恢复壁板的电搭接。

紫外线（UV）会影响复合材料的强度。复合材料结构需要面漆保护，以防紫外线的影响。人们已研制出专用的紫外线底漆和涂料来保护复合材料。

4. 语法解析

① such as 插入语 + that 定语从句

原句：Examples of flaws occurring in manufacturing include a contaminated bondline surface or inclusions, such as prepreg backing paper or separation film, that is inadvertently left between plies during layup. 拆解：主语 Examples of flaws，occurring in manufacturing 为现在分词短语作后置定语；谓语 include，宾语为 a contaminated bondline surface or inclusions 的并列；such as... 为插入语举例；that is inadvertently left... 为定语从句修饰 inclusions，during layup 为时间状语。 要点：that 从句紧跟的是 inclusions，不是紧邻的 separation film，因为 such as 结构只是插入举例，不改变中心词。从句用被动 is left，说明夹杂物是“被留下”的，主语是 inclusions。

② 并列条件状语从句 + 被动语态

原句：A part is called resin starved if too much resin is bled off during the curing process or if not enough resin is applied during the wet layup process. 拆解：主句 A part is called resin starved 为被动语态，resin starved 作主语补足语；if 引导两个并列的条件状语从句，分别用 is bled off 和 is applied 的被动式，during... 为时间状语。 要点：or 连接的是两个完整的 if 从句，第二个 if 不能省，否则会读成“树脂不足”与“排胶过多”共用一个条件。主语补足语 resin starved 是名词化的形容词短语，说明零件被称作什么状态。

③ 介词短语前置 + 被动句

原句：For laminates designed to transmit loads with their fibers (fiber dominant), only a slight reduction of properties is observed when the matrix is severely damaged. 拆解：句首 For laminates... 为范围状语，designed to transmit loads... 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 laminates；主句为被动 only a slight reduction of properties is observed；when 引导时间状语从句，其中 the matrix 为主语、is damaged 为被动谓语。 要点：介词短语提到句首后，主句主语 only a slight reduction 直到逗号后才出现，容易被误当成 For 的宾语。designed 的逻辑主语是 laminates，与主句主语无关。

④ 定语从句 + 并列谓语

原句：Under compression or shear loading, however, the sublaminates adjacent to the delaminations or debonded elements may buckle and cause a load redistribution mechanism that leads to structural failure. 拆解：句首 Under compression or shear loading 为条件状语；主语 the sublaminates，adjacent to... 为形容词短语作后置定语；谓语为 may buckle and cause 的并列；宾语 a load redistribution mechanism 后接 that 定语从句。 要点：buckle 与 cause 共用一个情态动词 may，是并列谓语而非两个分句。that 从句修饰 mechanism，说明载荷重分布“导致”结构失效这一后果，而不是修饰 cause。

⑤ 多层定语从句 + 冒号列举

原句：Many honeycomb structures, such as wing spoilers, fairings, flight controls, and landing gear doors, have thin face sheets which have experienced durability problems that could be grouped into three categories: low resistance to impact, liquid ingression, and erosion. 拆解：主语 Many honeycomb structures，such as... 为插入举例；谓语 have，宾语 thin face sheets；which have experienced... 为定语从句修饰 face sheets，其中又套 that could be grouped into three categories 定语从句修饰 problems；冒号后为三项并列同位语，说明三类问题。 要点：两个定语从句层层嵌套，which 指 face sheets，that 指 problems，不能互换。冒号后的三项与 three categories 同位，中文用“可归为三类：……”对应，冒号保留。

⑥ 省略的被动状语 + unless 条件

原句：Water tends to create additional damage in repaired parts when cured unless all moisture is removed from the part. 拆解：主句 Water tends to create additional damage in repaired parts，tends to do 为谓语结构；when cured 为省略式时间状语从句，完整形式是 when it is cured，it 指 parts；unless 引导否定条件状语从句，用被动 is removed。 要点：when cured 中省略了主语和 be 动词，只剩过去分词，逻辑主语是 parts 而不是 water——判断省略成分要回到主句找被修饰的名词。unless 相当于 if...not，中文译“除非……否则……”。
