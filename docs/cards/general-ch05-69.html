1. 原文

Since sound is a wave motion in matter, it can best be understood by first considering water waves, like a series of circular waves travel away from the disturbance of an object thrown into a pool. In Figure 5-47 such waves are seen from a top perspective, with the waves traveling out from the center. In the cross-section perspective in Figure 5-47, notice that the water waves are a succession of crests and troughs. The wavelength is the distance from the crest of one wave to the crest of the next. Water waves are known as transverse waves because the motion of the water molecules is up and down, or at right angles to the direction in which the waves are traveling. This can be seen by observing a cork on the water, bobbing up and down as the waves pass by.

Sound travels through matter in the form of longitudinal wave motions. These waves are called longitudinal waves because the particles of the medium vibrate back and forth longitudinally in the direction of propagation. Because air is elastic, this disturbance is transmitted progressively in an outward direction from the tine in the form of a compression wave.

When the tine returns and moves in an inward direction, the air in front of the tine is rarefied so that its momentary pressure is reduced below that at other points in the surrounding medium. This disturbance is transmitted in the form of a rarefaction, or expansion, wave and follows the compression wave through the medium. The progress of any wave involves two distinct motions: (1) The wave itself moves forward with constant speed, and (2) simultaneously, the particles of the medium that convey the wave vibrate harmonically. Examples of harmonic motion are the motion of a clock pendulum, the balance wheel in a watch, and the piston in a reciprocating engine.

2. 中文翻译

由于声音是物质中的一种波动，最好先考察水波来理解它：如同向池中投入一个物体后，一圈圈圆形波从扰动处向外传开。在图 5-47 中，这样的波以俯视角度呈现，波从中心向外传播。在图 5-47 的剖视图中可以看到，水波是一连串的波峰和波谷。波长是从一个波的波峰到下一个波峰的距离。水波称为横波，因为水分子的运动是上下方向，即与波的传播方向成直角。观察水面上的一块软木随波经过而上下起伏，就能看出这一点。

声音以纵波运动的形式在物质中传播。这些波称为纵波，因为介质质点沿传播方向前后作纵向振动。〔图 5-48〕当音叉的叉股向外运动时，紧靠叉股前方的空气被压缩，其

当叉股返回并向内运动时，叉股前方的空气变得稀薄，其瞬时压力低于周围介质中其他各点。这一扰动以稀疏波（膨胀波）的形式传递，并在介质中跟随压缩波前进。任何波的推进都包含两种不同的运动：(1) 波本身以恒定速度向前行进；(2) 与此同时，传递波的介质质点作谐振动。谐振动的例子有钟摆的运动、手表中的摆轮以及往复式发动机中的活塞。

4. 语法解析

① Since …, it can best be understood by first considering …（原因从句 + 方式状语）

原句：Since sound is a wave motion in matter, it can best be understood by first considering water waves, like a series of circular waves travel away from the disturbance of an object thrown into a pool.

拆解：Since 引导原因状语从句（「既然、由于」）；主句 it can best be understood by first considering water waves 中，by + 动名词表示方式，best 是副词修饰 understood；like … 引出类比的对象，其中 thrown into a pool 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 an object。

要点：Since 作「由于」时语气比 because 轻，常置于句首，译「既然／由于」；作「自从」时则引导时间从句，靠句意区分。后半句 like a series of circular waves travel … 用的是 like 直接接从句，属手册原文的不规范写法，规范英语应为 such as a series of circular waves that travel … 或 as a series of circular waves travel …，读时不必为它困惑。

② These waves are called … because …（被动定名 + 原因从句）

原句：These waves are called longitudinal waves because the particles of the medium vibrate back and forth longitudinally in the direction of propagation.

拆解：主句 These waves are called longitudinal waves（be called + 主语补足语）；because 引导原因从句，从句主语 the particles of the medium，谓语 vibrate，状语 back and forth（方式）与 longitudinally（方向）、in the direction of propagation（方向细化）。

要点：be called + 名词（不加 as、不加 to be）是给事物命名的固定被动式。注意 longitudinally 与 in the direction of propagation 语义重叠，是手册用「副词 + 介词短语」双重强调方向的写法，中译可合并为「沿传播方向前后振动」。

③ …, with the waves traveling out from the center（with 复合结构）

原句：In Figure 5-47 such waves are seen from a top perspective, with the waves traveling out from the center.

拆解：主句 such waves are seen from a top perspective（被动，from a top perspective 是观察角度）；with the waves traveling out from the center 是「with + 名词 + 现在分词」复合结构（独立主格），作伴随状语；traveling 的逻辑主语是 the waves，不是主句主语 such waves 之外的东西。

要点：with + 名词 + 分词 是技术图注和过程描述里极常用的伴随状语，译「……，同时／而……」。用现在分词表示主动、正在进行（traveling），若换成过去分词则表被动完成（with the valve closed）。注意此处主句是被动语态，with 结构的逻辑主语与主句主语可以不同，这正是独立主格的用处。
