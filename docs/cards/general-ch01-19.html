Securing Light Aircraft

通用 Ch 01 · Securing Light Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

1. 原文

Light aircraft are most often secured with ropes tied only at the aircraft tie-down rings provided for securing purposes.

Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut, since this practice can bend a strut if the rope slips to a point where there is no slack.

Since manila rope shrinks when wet, about 1 inch (1") of slack needs to be provided for movement. Too much slack, however, allows the aircraft to jerk against the ropes. Tight tie-down ropes put inverted flight stresses on the aircraft and many are not designed to take such loads.

A tie-down rope holds no better than the knot. Anti-slip knots, such as the bowline, are quickly tied and are easy to untie.

[Figure 1-10] Aircraft not equipped with tie-down fittings must be secured in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions. Ropes are to be tied to outer ends of struts on high-wing monoplanes and suitable rings provided where structural conditions permit, if the manufacturer has not already provided them.

2. 中文翻译

轻型航空器最常用绳索系留，绳索只系在航空器专为系留设置的系留环上。

绝不能把绳索系在升力撑杆上，因为绳索一旦滑到没有余量的位置，这种做法就可能把撑杆压弯。

由于马尼拉绳受潮后会收缩，应留出约 1 英寸（1"）的松弛量供航空器活动。但松弛量过大，又会让航空器猛地拽动绳索。系留绳绷得过紧会给航空器施加倒飞应力，而许多航空器并非按承受这类载荷设计的。

系留绳的牢固程度取决于绳结。防滑绳结（如单套结）打起来快，也容易解开。

[图 1-10] 未装系留接头的航空器，必须按制造厂说明进行系留。对于上单翼飞机，如果制造厂尚未提供合适的系留环，绳索应系在撑杆外端以及结构条件允许处所设的合适系环上。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：Light aircraft are most often secured with ropes tied only at the aircraft tie-down rings provided for securing purposes.

拆解：主干 Light aircraft are secured with ropes；tied only at the aircraft tie-down rings 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 ropes；provided for securing purposes 又是过去分词短语修饰 rings。

要点：两个过去分词短语都没有 that/which，是典型的「分词作定语」，可还原为 which are tied … / which are provided …。only 强调「只许系在系留环上」，位置紧贴 tied at，起限定作用；be secured with 表示「用……固定」。

② since 原因从句 + if 条件从句 + where 定语从句

原句：Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut, since this practice can bend a strut if the rope slips to a point where there is no slack.

拆解：主句 Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut（be to + 被动，表禁止）；since 引导原因状语从句 this practice can bend a strut；从句内又套 if 条件从句 the rope slips to a point，point 后接 where there is no slack 定语从句。

要点：never 与 be to 连用表示「绝对不得」；this practice 指代前面「把绳子系在撑杆上」的做法。where 引导的定语从句修饰表示「地点/程度」的先行词 point，此处指「绳子滑到没有余量那一步」。多层从句时先抓主句，再逐层剥离 since → if → where。

③ 比较结构：no better than

原句：A tie-down rope holds no better than the knot.

拆解：主语 A tie-down rope，谓语 holds（此处意为「保持得住、吃得住力」）；no better than the knot 是比较结构，no better than = 并不比……更好。

要点：no better than 是「和……一样差/一样不可靠」的强调说法，全句意为「系留绳牢不牢，全看绳结」，即绳结是薄弱环节。hold 在此不是「握住」而是「受力不失效」，与 The knot holds. 同义；no 比 not 语气更强，常含贬义。
