4. 语法解析

① like a knife cutting through butter（like + 名词 + 现在分词）

原句：When a shear stress is applied to an object, the force tries to cut or slice through, like a knife cutting through butter.

拆解：When 从句用被动 is applied；主句 the force tries to cut or slice through；like a knife cutting through butter 为介词短语作方式状语，cutting 是现在分词，相当于 as a knife cuts through butter。

要点：slice through 意为「切开、切穿」。like 后不是句子而是「名词 + 分词」结构时，动词只能用分词形式，不能写成 like a knife cuts。

② A clevis bolt, which … , has shear stress acting on it（非限制性定语从句 + have sth doing）

原句：A clevis bolt, which is often used to secure a cable to a part of the airframe, has shear stress acting on it.

拆解：主语 A clevis bolt，which … 为非限制性定语从句补充说明，谓语 has，宾语 shear stress，acting on it 为现在分词短语作宾补（have sth doing）。

要点：主谓被长从句隔开时，先找逗号后的 has。have + 宾语 + 现在分词表示「让……持续作用／处于某种状态」，与 have sth done（让别人做）不同。secure A to B = 把 A 固定到 B 上。

③ the fork tries to slide off the eye by cutting through the clevis bolt（by + 动名词表方式）

原句：When the cable is put under tension, the fork tries to slide off the eye by cutting through the clevis bolt.

拆解：When 从句用被动 is put under tension；主句 the fork tries to slide off the eye；by cutting through the clevis bolt 为介词短语作方式状语。

要点：by + 动名词表示「通过……的方式」，cutting 的逻辑主语是 the fork，不是 the cable。slide off 是「滑脱、滑离」，off 是介词，后接 the eye。

④ This bolt would be designed to take very high shear loads（情态 + 被动表设计意图）

原句：This bolt would be designed to take very high shear loads.

拆解：主语 This bolt，谓语 would be designed（情态动词 + be + 过去分词），to take … 为目的状语。

要点：would be designed to 是手册说明「按设计应能……」的常见说法，would 不表过去，而表设计意图／推定。take loads 意为「承受载荷」，与 carry loads 同义。
