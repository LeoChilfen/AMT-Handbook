Safety Around Compressed Gases

通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Compressed Gases · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

1. 原文

Compressed air, like electricity, is an excellent tool when it is under control. A typical nitrogen bottle set is shown in Figure 1-1. The following “dos and don’ts” apply when working with or around compressed gases:

• Inspect air hoses frequently for breaks and worn spots.

Unsafe hoses must be replaced immediately.

• Keep all connections in a “no-leak condition.” • Maintain in-line oilers, if installed, in operating condition.

• Ensure the system has water sumps installed and drained at regular intervals.

• Filter air used for paint spraying to remove oil and water.

• Never use compressed air to clean hands or clothing.

Pressure can force debris into the flesh leading to infection.

• Never spray compressed air in the area of other personnel.

• Straighten, coil, and properly store air hoses when not in use.

• Many accidents involving compressed gases occur during aircraft tire mounting. To prevent possible personal injury, use tire dollies and other appropriate devices to mount or remove heavy aircraft tires.

When inflating tires on any type of aircraft wheels, always use tire cage guards. Extreme caution is required to avoid over inflation of high-pressure tires because of possible personal injury. Use pressure regulators on high-pressure air bottles to eliminate the possibility of over inflation of tires.

Tire cages are not required when adjusting pressure in tires installed on an aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

压缩空气和电一样，在受控状态下是一种极好的工具。图 1-1 所示为典型的一套氮气瓶组。使用压缩气体或在其周围作业时，应遵守以下“应做与不应做”的事项：

• 经常检查空气软管有无破损和磨损处。

不安全的软管必须立即更换。

• 所有接头必须保持“无泄漏状态”。• 已装设的管路油雾器（in-line oiler）应保持工作状态。

• 确保系统装有集水器（water sump），并定期排放。

• 对喷漆用气进行过滤，去除其中的油和水。

• 绝不要用压缩空气吹洗手或衣物。

压力会把碎屑压入皮肉，导致感染。

• 绝不要在其他人员所在的区域喷射压缩空气。

• 空气软管不使用时，应理顺、盘卷并妥善存放。

• 许多涉及压缩气体的事故发生在航空器轮胎安装过程中。为防止可能的人身伤害，安装或拆卸重型航空器轮胎时应使用轮胎托架（tire dolly）及其他适当的器具。

给任何类型航空器机轮上的轮胎充气时，都必须使用轮胎防爆笼。必须极为谨慎，避免高压轮胎充气过度，因为可能造成人身伤害。高压气瓶上应使用调压器，以消除轮胎充气过度的可能。

在航空器上已装好的轮胎上调压时，不需要使用防爆笼。

4. 语法解析

① When inflating tires … , always use tire cage guards（时间状语从句的省略形式）

原句：When inflating tires on any type of aircraft wheels, always use tire cage guards.

拆解：When inflating 是 When you are inflating 的省略形式，从句主语与主句祈使句隐含的主语（操作者）一致，故只保留现在分词；主句是祈使句 always use tire cage guards。

要点：状语从句主语与主句主语一致且从句含 be 动词时，可省略主语和 be，留下 when/while/if + 分词，这是手册英语压缩句子的常用手段。反过来，若从句主语与主句主语不同，就不能省略，例如 When the wheel is hot, do not … 不能写成 When hot, do not …（主语不同）。

② To prevent possible personal injury, use tire dollies …（不定式短语作目的状语）

原句：To prevent possible personal injury, use tire dollies and other appropriate devices to mount or remove heavy aircraft tires.

拆解：句首 To prevent possible personal injury 是不定式短语作目的状语；主句为祈使句 use + 并列宾语；句末 to mount or remove heavy aircraft tires 也是目的状语，说明器具的用途。

要点：to do 置于句首时几乎总表目的，可译作「为……」；句中再出现一个 to do 时要注意区分：前一个管「为什么做」，后一个管「用来做什么」。表示目的还可用 in order to / so as to，但否定目的必须用 in order not to / so as not to，不能直接用 to not do。

③ Many accidents involving compressed gases occur …（现在分词短语作后置定语）

原句：Many accidents involving compressed gases occur during aircraft tire mounting.

拆解：主语 Many accidents，involving compressed gases 是现在分词短语作后置定语（相当于 which involve compressed gases），谓语 occur，during aircraft tire mounting 是时间状语。

要点：involving 与 involved 要分清：involving 表主动「涉及……的」，involved 表被动「被卷入的、有关的」，the personnel involved 指「涉事人员」，而 accidents involving gases 指「涉及气体的事故」。这类 -ing 后置定语在技术文中比定语从句更常见，翻译时常要提前成「涉及压缩气体的事故」。

④ Unsafe hoses must be replaced immediately（情态动词 + 被动语态）

原句：Unsafe hoses must be replaced immediately.

拆解：主语 Unsafe hoses，谓语 must be replaced（情态动词 + be + 过去分词），immediately 作状语。

要点：手册条文常用「情态动词 + 被动」把责任主体隐去，只强调「必须被怎样处理」，译作「必须立即更换」即可，不必补出「由谁更换」。同类句式本节还有 must be in the immediate area、must be replaced；注意 must be 后面接过去分词，接动词原形（must be replace）是典型错误。
