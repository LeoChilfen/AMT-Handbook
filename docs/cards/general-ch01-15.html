Inspection of Fire Extinguishers

通用 Ch 01 · Inspection of Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 29 页

1. 原文

Fire extinguishers need to be checked periodically utilizing a checklist. If a checklist is unavailable, check the following as a minimum:

• Proper location of appropriate extinguisher

• Safety seals unbroken

• All external dirt and rust removed

• Gauge or indicator in operable range

• No nozzle obstruction

• No obvious damage Airport or other local fire departments can usually help in preparing or providing extinguisher checklists. In addition, these fire departments can be helpful in answering questions and assisting in obtaining repairs to or replacement of fire extinguishers.

2. 中文翻译

灭火器需要借助检查单定期检查。如果没有检查单，至少应检查以下各项：

• 适用的灭火器放在规定位置

• 保险封条未破损

• 已清除所有外部污垢和锈迹

• 压力表或指示器处于可工作范围

• 喷嘴无堵塞

• 无明显损伤（原文此处项目符号与下段文字在 PDF 中连排，未分段：机场或其他当地消防部门通常可以协助编制或提供灭火器检查单。此外，这些消防部门还可帮助解答问题，并协助安排灭火器的修理或更换。）

4. 语法解析

① need to be done + 分词方式状语

原句：Fire extinguishers need to be checked periodically utilizing a checklist. 拆解：主语 Fire extinguishers + 谓语 need + 不定式被动 to be checked；periodically 为频率副词；utilizing a checklist 为现在分词短语作方式状语。 要点：need 后接不定式被动，表示“（东西）需要被……”，与 need doing（同义口语式）可互换；utilize 在此等于 use，是手册常用的正式说法。

② if 条件从句 + 祈使主句

原句：If a checklist is unavailable, check the following as a minimum: 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句（主系表 is unavailable）+ 祈使句主句 check the following；as a minimum 为方式状语，意为“至少”。 要点：条件从句与祈使主句搭配，是说明书的标准句式；the following 指代后面列举的项目，冒号引出清单；as a minimum 不可写成 at minimum 以外的不规范形式。

③ help in + 动名词

原句：Airport or other local fire departments can usually help in preparing or providing extinguisher checklists. 拆解：主语 Airport or other local fire departments + 情态动词 can + 副词 usually + 谓语 help + in preparing or providing…（介词 in 后接并列动名词）。 要点：help in doing sth 比 help do sth 更强调“在……过程中提供帮助”；or 连接两个动名词须保持形式平行；主语含 or 时谓语单复数按就近原则处理，此处用复数与就近的 departments 一致。

④ be helpful in doing + 并列名词短语

原句：In addition, these fire departments can be helpful in answering questions and assisting in obtaining repairs to or replacement of fire extinguishers. 拆解：In addition 为连接性状语；主语 these fire departments + 系动词 can be + 表语 helpful + in answering… and assisting in…（并列动名词短语）。 要点：be helpful in doing 与 help in doing 同义，但更强调“有用”；assist in doing 也是固定搭配；obtaining repairs to or replacement of 中 to 和 of 各自搭配前面的名词，不能合并成一个介词。
