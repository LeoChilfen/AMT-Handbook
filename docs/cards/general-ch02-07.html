1. 原文

Air Transport Association ATA iSpec 2200

To standardize the technical data and maintenance activities on large and therefore complex aircraft, the ATA e-Business Program has established a classification of maintenance related actions. These are arranged with sequential numbers assigned to ATA chapters. These chapters are consistent regardless of the large aircraft that is being worked on.

Manufacturers’ Published Data

The original equipment manufacturer (OEM) is usually the best source of information for the operation of and maintenance on a particular product. If the product is a TC’d or STC’d item, 14 CFR part 21, section 21.50 requires the holder of the design approval to provide one set of complete ICAs. Additional requirements for ICAs are specified in sections 23.1529, 25.1529, 27.1529 and 29.1529. These sections further refer the reader to 14 CFR part 23, Appendix A; part 25, Appendix H; part 27, Appendix A; and part 29, Appendix A. Regardless of the appendix referred to, the requirements in the appendix for the ICA are as follows:

• General: The aircraft ICA must contain instructions for continued airworthiness for each engine, propeller, or appliance and the interface of those appliances and products with the aircraft.

• Format: The ICA must be in the form of a manual or manuals appropriate to the data being provided.

• Content: The manual contents must be in English and must include the following:

• Introductory information, including an explanation of the airplane’s features and data as necessary to perform maintenance or preventive maintenance

• A description of the aircraft and its systems, including engine, propeller, and appliances

• Basic operating information describing how the aircraft and its components are controlled

• Servicing information with such detail as servicing parts, tank capacities, types of fluid to be used, applicable pressures for the various systems, access panels for inspection and servicing, lubrication points, and types of lubricants to be used

The maintenance instructions must include the following data:

• Recommended schedule for cleaning, inspecting, adjusting, testing, and lubricating the various parts

• Applicable wear tolerances

• Recommended overhaul periods

• Details for an inspection program that identifies both the frequency and the extent of the inspections necessary to provide for continued airworthiness

• Troubleshooting information

• The order and method for proper removal and replacement of parts

• Procedures for system testing during ground operations

• Diagrams for structural access plates

• Details for application of special inspection techniques

• Information concerning the application of protective treatments after inspection

• Information relative to the structural fasteners

• List of any special tools needed

Airworthiness Limitations

The ICA must contain a separate and clearly distinguishable section titled “Airworthiness Limitations.” Within this section are mandatory replacement times, structural inspection interval, and related inspection procedures.

All of this is included in the initial release of documents when the aircraft is delivered. However, over the course of the life of an aircraft, various modifications can and often do occur. Whether these are as simple as a new cabin to galley sliding door, or as complex as a navigation related STC, any major alteration requires that this type of maintenance data be provided to the owner, so that subsequent maintenance, inspection, and repair can be properly accomplished. As aircraft and their systems become more and more complex, and society continues its preoccupation with litigation for every incident, it is imperative that the technician have the right information, that it is current, and that they have the proper tools, including those required for any special inspection, and correct replacement parts. If any one of these items is required, and the technician does not have it accessible, they are in violation of 14 CFR sections 65.81(b), 43.13(a), and 43.16 if they attempt to return the aircraft to service.

Manufacturers may provide this required information in a variety of different manuals:

• Operating Instructions—The Airplane Flight Manual (AFM) or the Pilot’s Operating Handbook (POH) provides the pilot with the necessary information to properly operate the aircraft. These manuals are usually listed in the aircraft TCDS, and therefore are a required item for the aircraft to be considered airworthy. Note that the AFM is generally serial number specific, whereas the POH is model specific.After 1978, the POHs generally took on both roles.

• Maintenance Manuals—These manuals are often referred to as Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) or Component Maintenance Manual (CMM).

The AMM is focused on the entire aircraft and provides the “big picture” for the maintenance technician. It provides information concerning the maintenance, including troubleshooting and repair, of the aircraft and systems on the aircraft.

The CMM, on the other hand, is focused on a specific item or component, such as hydraulic pump, generator, or thrust reverser. It provides the bench mechanic with detail troubleshooting information and usually serves as an overhaul manual giving details for disassembly, cleaning, inspection, repair as necessary, reassembly, and testing in accordance with approved standards and technical data accepted by the Administrator. Refer to 14 CFR part 43 section 43.2(a). When maintenance is done according to the CMM, the technician must always include the appropriate references in the maintenance record entry required by 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9 or 43.11.

Service Bulletins (SB)

Throughout the life of a product (whether TC’d or not), manufacturing defects, changes in service, or design improvements often occur. When that happens, the OEM frequently uses an SB to distribute the information to the operator of the aircraft. SBs are good information and should be strongly considered by the owner for implementation to the aircraft. However, SBs are not required unless they are referred to in an AD note or if compliance is required as a part of the authorized inspection program. Refer to section 14 CFR part 39, 39.27.

Structural Repair Manual (SRM)

As the name implies, this manual carries detail information for the technician concerning an aircraft’s primary and secondary structure, criteria for evaluating the severity of the detected damage, determining the feasibility of a repair, and alignment/inspection information. This manual is usually a separate manual for large aircraft. On small aircraft, this information is often included in the AMM.

2. 中文翻译

航空运输协会 ATA iSpec 2200

为使大而复杂的航空器上的技术数据与维修活动实现标准化，ATA 电子商务计划（ATA e-Business Program）对与维修相关的行动作了分类。这些行动按分配给 ATA 章节的顺序编号编排。无论正在维修的是哪种大型航空器，这些章节都是一致的。

制造商发布的数据

原始设备制造商（OEM）通常是特定产品运行与维修信息的最佳来源。如果该产品是取得 TC 或 STC 的项目，14 CFR part 21 第 21.50 条要求设计批准持有人提供一整套完整的 ICA。ICA 的其他要求规定在第 23.1529、25.1529、27.1529 和 29.1529 条中。这些条款进一步指引读者参阅 14 CFR part 23 附录 A、part 25 附录 H、part 27 附录 A 和 part 29 附录 A。无论引用的是哪个附录，附录中对 ICA 的要求如下：

• 总则：航空器 ICA 必须包含每台发动机、螺旋桨或附件的持续适航说明，以及这些附件和产品同航空器接口处的持续适航说明。

• 格式：ICA 必须采用与所提供数据相适应的、一册或多册手册的形式。

• 内容：手册内容必须使用英文，并且必须包括下列内容：

• 介绍性信息，包括为实施维修或预防性维修所必需的飞机特点与数据的说明

• 对航空器及其系统的说明，包括发动机、螺旋桨和附件

• 基本使用信息，说明航空器及其各部件是如何操纵的

• 勤务信息，其详细程度应涵盖勤务部件、油箱容量、所用油液种类、各系统的适用压力、用于检查和勤务的检查口盖、润滑点以及所用润滑剂种类。

维修说明必须包括下列数据：

• 清洁、检查、调整、测试和润滑各部件所建议的周期

• 适用的磨损公差

• 建议的大修周期

• 检查大纲的详细内容，既规定检查频次，也规定为保持持续适航所必需的检查范围

• 故障排除信息

• 正确拆卸与更换部件的顺序和方法

• 地面运行期间的系统测试程序

• 结构检查口盖的示意图

• 应用特殊检查技术的详细说明

• 检查后施加防护处理的有关信息

• 有关结构紧固件的信息

• 所需专用工具的清单

适航限制

ICA 必须包含一个单独且清晰可辨的章节，标题为「适航限制」。该章节内载有强制性更换时间、结构检查间隔及相关检查程序。

所有这些内容都包含在航空器交付时最初发布的文件中。然而在航空器的寿命期内，各种改装可能会、而且经常会发生。无论这些改装简单到只是新增一扇客舱通厨房的滑门，还是复杂到与导航相关的 STC，任何重大改装都要求向所有人提供这类维修数据，以便后续的维修、检查和修理能够正确地完成。随着航空器及其系统日益复杂，社会又持续痴迷于为每一起事件打官司，技术人员必须掌握正确的信息、信息必须是最新的，并且必须拥有合适的工具（包括实施任何特殊检查所需的工具）和正确的替换件。如果上述任何一项是必需的而技术人员无法取得，那么他们若试图让航空器恢复使用，就违反了 14 CFR 第 65.81(b) 条、第 43.13(a) 条和第 43.16 条。

制造商可以用多种不同的手册提供这些必需的信息：

• 使用说明——飞机飞行手册（AFM）或驾驶员操作手册（POH）为驾驶员提供正确操作航空器所必需的信息。这些手册通常列入航空器的 TCDS，因而是航空器被视为适航所必需的随机项目。注意：AFM 一般按出厂序号编制，而 POH 按型号编制。1978 年以后，POH 一般同时承担这两种角色。

• 维修手册——这类手册常被称为航空器维修手册（AMM）或部件维修手册（CMM）。

AMM 着眼于整架航空器，为维修技术人员提供「全局图景」。它提供有关航空器及航空器各系统维修（包括故障排除和修理）的信息。

CMM 则着眼于某个特定项目或部件，例如液压泵、发电机或反推力装置。它为车间机械员提供详细的故障排除信息，通常也兼作大修手册，给出按局方认可的标准和技术数据（参见 14 CFR part 43 第 43.2(a) 条）进行分解、清洁、检查、视情修理、重新装配和试验的详细说明。当按 CMM 实施维修时，技术人员必须始终在 14 CFR part 43 第 43.9 条或第 43.11 条所要求的维修记录条目中写入相应的引用出处。

服务通告（SB）

在产品整个寿命期内（无论是否取得 TC），制造缺陷、使用中的变化或设计改进时有发生。出现这种情况时，OEM 通常使用 SB 将信息分发给航空器运营人。SB 是很好的信息，所有者应认真考虑在航空器上执行。但是，除非在 AD 通告中被引用，或作为经批准的检查大纲的一部分被要求符合，SB 并非强制。参见 14 CFR part 39 第 39.27 条。

结构修理手册（SRM）

顾名思义，该手册为技术人员提供关于航空器一次结构和二次结构的详细信息，包括评估所发现损伤严重程度的准则、判定修理可行性的方法，以及校装／检查信息。对大型航空器，该手册通常是单独一册；对小型航空器，这些信息往往并入 AMM。

4. 语法解析

① 目的状语前置与现在完成时

原句：To standardize the technical data and maintenance activities on large and therefore complex aircraft, the ATA e-Business Program has established a classification of maintenance related actions.

拆解：句首不定式短语 To standardize… 作目的状语并前置加逗号；主干为 the ATA e-Business Program + has established（现在完成时）+ 宾语 a classification（of maintenance related actions 为 of 短语后置定语）。

要点：不定式前置是为了把「为什么做这件事」放在句首做铺垫，主干因此后移；若改成 In order to… 意思不变，但正式文体更常省略 in order。用现在完成时 has established 是因为该分类体系建立后一直沿用至今，强调结果对现在有效；用一般过去时则只陈述历史动作、会暗示可能已废止。

② regardless of 引出的让步与定语从句里的被动

原句：These chapters are consistent regardless of the large aircraft that is being worked on.

拆解：主干 These chapters are consistent；regardless of the large aircraft 为介词短语作让步状语；that is being worked on 为定语从句修饰 aircraft，从句为现在进行时被动。

要点：aircraft 单复数同形，此处指「某一架正在被维修的大型航空器」，故从句谓语用单数 is。regardless of 是复合介词，后接名词短语而非句子；要接句子须换成 regardless of the fact that… 或改用 whatever aircraft is being worked on。介词 on 留在从句末尾（work on the aircraft），是不可拆的短语动词。

③ require sb to do 与形容词化的缩写

原句：If the product is a TC’d or STC’d item, 14 CFR part 21, section 21.50 requires the holder of the design approval to provide one set of complete ICAs.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句；主句主语 14 CFR part 21, section 21.50 + 谓语 requires + 宾语 the holder（of the design approval 后置）+ 宾补 to provide one set of complete ICAs。

要点：require 的常用句型是 require sb to do sth，不能用 require sb doing 或 require that sb does（后者须用虚拟语气 require that sb provide）。TC’d、STC’d 是行业内的形容词化写法，即「已取得 TC / STC 的」，读作 TC'd（tee-see'd），正式文件里对应表述是 certificated under a TC。法规引用中 part 与 section 之间用逗号分隔，翻译时保留编号、只译连接词。

④ 时间状语从句 + 不定式表目的 + distribute sth to sb

原句：When that happens, the OEM frequently uses an SB to distribute the information to the operator of the aircraft.

拆解：When that happens 为时间状语从句；主句 the OEM frequently uses an SB + 目的状语 to distribute the information to the operator of the aircraft，其中 distribute 后为 distribute sth to sb 的双宾结构。

要点：that 回指前句所述缺陷、变化或改进发生的情形，不译作具体的「那件事」更自然。to distribute 表目的而非结果：SB 被「用来」分发信息，若换成 distributing 则变成使用 SB 时附带发生的行为。distribute 的搭配是 distribute A to B，介词不能用 for 或 among 混用（among 表示在群体内分发，此处对象是单一运营人，用 to）。

⑤ 插入语 on the other hand 与 such as 列举

原句：The CMM, on the other hand, is focused on a specific item or component, such as hydraulic pump, generator, or thrust reverser.

拆解：主语 The CMM 与谓语 is focused on 之间插入 on the other hand，两侧用逗号隔开；宾语 a specific item or component 后接 such as 引出的举例，列举三项。

要点：on the other hand 是与前段 AMM 对照的连接语，必须用两个逗号夹住，不能只留一侧。be focused on 是「以…为对象」的固定搭配，介词 on 不可换成 in 或 at。such as 之后原文未加冠词（hydraulic pump, generator…），表示「这一类中的实例」而非特指某台，翻译时用「例如液压泵、发电机或反推力装置」即可，不要补出「一台」。
