Approved Materials

机体 Ch 03 · Approved Materials · 原文 PDF 第 141 页

1. 原文

There are a variety of approved materials used in aircraft fabric covering and repair processes. In order for the items to legally be used, the FAA must approve the fabric, tapes, threads, cords, glues, dopes, sealants, coatings, thinners, additives, fungicides, rejuvenators, and paints for the manufacturer, the holder of an STC, or a field approval.

Fabric

A Technical Standard Order (TSO) is a minimum performance standard issued by the FAA for specified materials, parts, processes, and appliances used on civil aircraft. For example, TSO-C15d, Aircraft Fabric, Grade A, prescribes the minimum performance standards that approved aircraft fabric must meet. Fabric that meets or exceeds the TSO can be used as a covering. Fabric approved to replace Grade-A cotton, such as polyester, must meet the same criteria. TSO-C15d also refers to another document, Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) Aerospace Material Specification (AMS) 3806D, which details properties a fabric must contain to be an approved fabric for airplane cloth. Lighter weight fabrics typically adhere to the specifications in TSO-C14b, which refers to SAEAMS 3804C.

When a company is approved to manufacture or sell an approved aviation fabric, it applies for and receives a Parts Manufacturer Approval (PMA). Currently, only a few approved fabrics are used for aircraft coverings, such as the polyester fabrics Ceconite™, Stits/Polyfiber™, and Superflite™. These fabrics and some of their characteristics are shown in Figure 3-4 . The holders of the PMA for these fabrics have also developed and gained approval for the various tapes, chords, threads, and liquids that are used in the covering process. These approved materials, along with the procedures for using them, constitute the STCs for each particular fabric covering process. Only the approved materials can be used. Substitution of other materials is forbidden and results in the aircraft being unairworthy.

Other Fabric Covering Materials

The following is an introduction to the supplemental materials used to complete a fabric covering job per manufacturer’s Anti-chafe tape is used on sharp protrusions, rib caps, metal seams, and other areas to provide a smoother surface to keep the fabric from being torn. It is usually self-adhesive cloth tape and is applied after the aircraft is cleaned, inspected, and primed, but before the fabric is installed.

Anti-Chafe Tape

Reinforcing Tape

Reinforcing tape is most commonly used on rib caps after the fabric covering is installed to protect and strengthen the area for attaching the fabric to the ribs.

Rib Bracing

Rib bracing tape is used on wing ribs before the fabric is installed. It is applied spanwise and alternately wrapped around a top rib cap and then a bottom rib cap progressing from rib to rib until all are braced. Lacing the ribs in this manner holds them in the proper place and alignment during the covering process.

Surface Tape

Surface tape, made of polyester material and often pre - shrunk, is obtained from the STC holder. This tape, also known as finishing tape, is applied after the fabric is installed. It is used over seams, ribs, patches, and edges. Surface tape can have straight or pinked edges and comes in various widths. For curved surfaces, bias cut tape is available, which allows the tape to be shaped around a radius.

Rib Lacing Cord

Rib lacing cord is used to lace the fabric to the wing ribs. It must be strong and applied as directed to safely transfer in-flight loads from the fabric to the ribs. Rib lacing cord is available in a round or flat cross-section. The round cord is easier to use than the flat lacing, but if installed properly, the flat lacing results in a smoother finish over the ribs.

Sewing Thread

Sewing of polyester fabric is rare and mostly limited to the creation of prefitted envelopes used in the envelope method covering process. When a fabric seam must be made with no structure underneath it, a sewn seam could be used. Polyester threads of various specifications are used on polyester fabric. Different thread is specified for hand sewing versus machine sewing. For hand sewing, the thread is typically a three-ply, uncoated polyester thread with a 15-pound tensile strength. Machine thread is typically four-ply polyester with a 10-pound tensile strength.

Special Fabric Fasteners

Each fabric covering job involves a method of attaching the fabric to wing and empennage ribs. The original manufacturer’s method of fastening should be used. In addition to lacing the fabric to the ribs with approved rib lacing cord, special clips, screws, and rivets are employed on some aircraft. The first step in using any of these fasteners is to inspect the holes into which they fit. Worn holes may have to be enlarged or re-drilled according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Use of approved fasteners is mandatory. Use of unapproved fasteners can render the covering job unairworthy if substituted. Screws and rivets often incorporate the use of a plastic or aluminum washer. All fasteners and rib lacing are covered with finishing tape once installed to provide a smooth finish and airflow.

Grommets

Grommets are used to create reinforced drain holes in the aircraft fabric. Usually made of aluminum or plastic, they are glued or doped into place on the fabric surface. Once secured, a hole is created in the fabric through the center of the grommet. Often, this is done with a hot soldering pencil that also heat seals the fabric edge to prevent raveling. Seaplane grommets have a shield over the drain hole to prevent splashed water from entering the interior of the covered structure and to assist in siphoning out any water from within. Drain holes using these grommets must be made before the grommets are put in place. Note that some drain holes do not require grommets if they are made through two layers of fabric.

Inspection Rings

The structure underneath an aircraft covering must be inspected periodically. To facilitate this in fabric-covered aircraft, inspection rings are glued or doped to the fabric. They provide a stable rim around an area of fabric that can be cut to allow viewing of the structure underneath. The fabric remains uncut until an inspection is desired. The rings are typically plastic or aluminum with an approximately three-inch inside diameter. Spring clip metal panel covers can be fitted to close the area once the fabric inside the inspection ring has been cut for access. The location of the inspection rings are specified by the manufacturer. Additional rings are sometimes added to permit access to important areas that may not have been fitted originally with inspection access.

Primer

The airframe structure of a fabric covered aircraft must be cleaned, inspected, and prepared before the fabric covering process begins. The final preparation procedure involves priming the structure with a treatment that works with the adhesive and first coats of fabric sealant that are to be utilized. Each STC specifies which primers, or if a wood structure, which varnishes are suitable. Most often, two-part epoxy primers are used on metal structure and two-part epoxy varnishes are used on wood structure. Utilize the primer specified by the manufacturer’s or STC’s instructions.

Fabric Cement

Modern fabric covering systems utilize special fabric cement to attach the fabric to the airframe. There are various types of cement. In addition to good adhesion qualities, flexibility, and long life, fabric cements must be compatible with the primer and the fabric sealer that are applied before and after the cement.

Fabric Sealer

Fabric sealer surrounds the fibers in the fabric with a protective coating to provide adhesion and keep out dirt and moisture. The sealer is the first coat applied to the polyester fabric after it is attached to the airframe and heat shrunk to fit snugly. Dope-based fabric coating systems utilize non-tautening nitrate dope as the primary fabric sealant. The application of tautening dope may cause the fabric to become too taut resulting in excess stress on the airframe that could damage it. Nondope coating systems use proprietary sealers that are also nontautening.

Fillers

After the fabric sealer is applied, a filler is used. It is sprayed on in a number of cross coats as required by the manufacturer or the fabric covering process STC. The filler contains solids or chemicals that are included to block UV light from reaching the fabric. Proper fill coating is critical because UV light is the single most destructive element that causes polyester fabric to deteriorate. Dope-based processes use butyrate dope fillers while other processes have their own proprietary formulas. When fillers and sealers are combined, they are known as fabric primers. Aluminum pastes and powders, formerly added to butyrate dope to provide the UV protection, have been replaced by premixed formulas.

Topcoats

Once the aircraft fabric has been installed, sealed, and fill-coat protected, finishing or topcoats are applied to give the aircraft its final appearance. Colored butyrate dope is common in dope-based processes, but various polyurethane topcoats are also available. It is important to use the topcoat products and procedures specified in the applicable STC to complete an airworthy fabric re-covering job.

The use of various additives is common at different stages when utilizing the above products. The following is a short list of additional products that facilitate the proper application of the fabric coatings. Note again that only products approved under a particular STC can be used. Substitution of similar products, even though they perform the same basic function, is not allowed.

• A catalyst accelerates a chemical reaction. Catalysts are specifically designed for each product with which they are mixed. They are commonly used with epoxies and polyurethanes.

• A thinner is a solvent or mixture of solvents added to a product to give it the proper consistency for application, such as when spraying or brushing.

• A retarder is added to a product to slow drying time. Used mostly in dope processes and topcoats, a retarder allow more time for a sprayed coating to flow and level, resulting in a deeper, glossier finish. It is used when the working temperature is elevated slightly above the ideal temperature for a product. It also can be used to prevent blushing of a dope finish when high humidity conditions exist.

• An accelerators contains solvents that speed up the drying time of the product with which it is mixed. It is typically used when the application working temperature is below that of the ideal working temperature. It can also be used for faster drying when airborne contaminants threaten a coating finish.

• Rejuvenator, used on dope finishes only, contains solvents that soften coatings and allow them to flow slightly. Rejuvenator also contains fresh plasticizers that mix into the original coatings. This increases the overall flexibility and life of the coatings.

• Fungicide and mildewicide additives are important for organic fabric covered aircraft because fabrics, such as cotton and linen, are hosts for fungus and mildew. Since fungus and mildew are not concerns when using polyester fabric, these additives are not required. Modern coating formulas contain premixed anti-fungal agents, providing sufficient insurance against the problem of fungus or mildew.

2. 中文翻译

用于飞机织物蒙皮和修理的批准材料种类很多。这些物品要合法使用，FAA 必须针对制造商、STC 持有人或现场批准，对织物、带材、缝线、绳、胶粘剂、涂料、密封剂、涂层、稀释剂、添加剂、杀菌剂、复原剂和油漆予以批准。

织物

技术标准规定（TSO）是 FAA 针对民用飞机上使用的特定材料、零件、工艺和设备颁发的最低性能标准。例如，TSO-C15d《飞机织物，A 级》规定了经批准的航空织物必须满足的最低性能标准。达到或超过该 TSO 的织物可用作蒙皮。获准替代 A 级棉布的织物（如聚酯织物）必须满足同样的准则。TSO-C15d 还引用另一份文件——美国汽车工程师学会（SAE）航空航天材料规范（AMS）3806D，其中详列了织物要成为经批准的飞机蒙皮布所必须具备的性能。较轻的织物通常符合 TSO-C14b 的规定，该标准引用 SAE AMS 3804C。

公司获准制造或销售经批准的航空织物时，要申请并获得零件制造商批准（PMA）。目前用于飞机蒙皮的批准织物只有少数几种，如聚酯织物 Ceconite™、Stits/Polyfiber™ 和 Superflite™。这些织物及其部分特性见图 3-4。这些织物的 PMA 持有人还研制并取得了蒙皮工艺中所用各种带材、绳、缝线和液体的批准。这些批准材料连同其使用程序，构成每种具体蒙皮工艺的 STC。只能使用批准的材料。替换为其他材料是被禁止的，并会导致飞机不适航。

其他蒙皮材料

以下按制造商（规程）介绍用于完成蒙皮作业的辅助材料。防磨带（anti-chafe tape）用于尖锐凸起、翼肋帽、金属接缝及其他部位，以提供更平滑的表面，避免织物被划破。它通常是自粘布带，在飞机清洁、检查并涂底漆之后、安装织物之前贴上。

防磨带

加强带

加强带最常用于织物蒙皮装好之后的翼肋帽处，用以保护和加强将织物固定到翼肋上的区域。

翼肋支撑

翼肋支撑带在安装织物之前用于机翼翼肋。它沿展向铺设，交替绕过上翼肋帽再绕下翼肋帽，逐肋推进直到全部支撑完毕。以这种方式把翼肋系住，可在蒙皮过程中使翼肋保持在正确位置和对齐状态。

表面带

表面带由聚酯材料制成，通常经过预收缩，从 STC 持有人处购得。这种带又称修饰带（finishing tape），在织物装好后使用，用于接缝、翼肋、补片和边缘处。表面带可为直边或锯齿边，有多种宽度。对于曲面，可用斜裁带，它能顺应半径弯曲成形。

翼肋系绳

翼肋系绳用于把织物系到机翼翼肋上。它必须足够结实，并按规定施工，以把飞行载荷从织物安全传递到翼肋。翼肋系绳有圆形和扁平两种截面。圆绳比扁带易用，但若施工得当，扁带在翼肋上形成的表面更平滑。

缝线

聚酯织物的缝合很少见，主要限于制作套筒法（envelope method）蒙皮所用的预成形套。当必须在无结构支撑处做织物接缝时，可采用缝合接缝。聚酯织物使用多种规格的聚酯缝线。手缝与机缝使用不同的线。手缝通常用三股、无涂层的聚酯线，抗拉强度 15 磅；机缝通常用四股聚酯线，抗拉强度 10 磅。

专用织物紧固件

每项蒙皮作业都涉及把织物固定到机翼和尾翼翼肋上的方法。应使用原制造商的固定方法。除用批准的翼肋系绳把织物系到翼肋上之外，某些飞机还使用专用卡子、螺钉和铆钉。使用任何这类紧固件的第一步，是检查它们所装入的孔。磨损的孔可能需要按制造商说明扩孔或重新钻孔。必须使用批准的紧固件；若以未批准的紧固件替代，会使蒙皮作业不适航。螺钉和铆钉常配用塑料或铝制垫圈。所有紧固件和翼肋系结在装好后都要用修饰带覆盖，以获得平滑表面和气流。

索环

索环（grommet）用于在飞机织物上制作加强的排水孔。它通常由铝或塑料制成，用胶粘或涂料固定在织物表面。固定后，从索环中心在织物上开孔。这常用热的电烙铁完成，同时把织物边缘热封以防脱散。水上飞机索环在排水孔上方有护罩，防止溅起的水进入所蒙结构内部，并有助于把内部的水虹吸排出。使用这类索环的排水孔必须在索环安装之前开好。注意，若排水孔穿过两层织物，则有些排水孔不需要索环。

检查环

飞机蒙皮下方的结构必须定期检查。为便于在蒙布飞机上进行检查，检查环用胶粘或涂料固定在织物上。它们在织物上提供一圈稳固的边缘，该区域可剪开以查看下方结构。在需要检查之前，织物保持不剪开。检查环通常为塑料或铝制，内径约 3 英寸。在检查环内的织物剪开以进入后，可装弹簧卡式金属盖板将其封住。检查环的位置由制造商规定。有时还加装检查环，以便进入原厂未设检查口的重点部位。

底漆

蒙布飞机的机体结构在蒙皮作业开始前必须清洁、检查并预处理。最后的预处理工序是给结构涂底漆，所用处理剂须与将要使用的胶粘剂和头几道织物密封剂相容。每份 STC 都规定适用哪些底漆，或（若为木质结构）哪些清漆。最常用的是金属结构用双组分环氧底漆、木质结构用双组分环氧清漆。应使用制造商或 STC 说明所规定的底漆。

织物胶

现代蒙皮体系使用专用织物胶把织物固定到机体上。胶有多种类型。除良好的粘附性、柔韧性和长寿命外，织物胶还必须与在它之前和之后施工的底漆和织物密封剂相容。

织物密封剂

织物密封剂以保护性涂层包裹织物纤维，提供粘附并阻隔脏污和潮气。密封剂是聚酯织物固定到机体并热收缩贴合后施工的第一道涂层。涂料型（dope-based）蒙皮体系用不绷紧的硝酸盐涂料作为主要织物密封剂。使用绷紧型涂料可能使织物过度绷紧，对机体产生过大应力而损坏结构。非涂料涂层体系使用同样是自身不绷紧的专用密封剂。

填充料

涂过织物密封剂之后使用填充料。按制造商或蒙皮工艺 STC 的要求，以若干道交叉涂层喷涂。填充料中含有固体分或化学物质，用以阻挡紫外线到达织物。正确的填充涂覆至关重要，因为紫外线是导致聚酯织物老化最主要的破坏因素。涂料型工艺使用丁酸盐涂料填充料，其他工艺则各有自己的配方。填充料与密封剂合用时称为织物底漆（fabric primers）。过去加入丁酸盐涂料以提供紫外线防护的铝膏和铝粉，现已被预混配方取代。

面漆

飞机织物安装、密封并做完填充保护后，涂饰面漆（topcoat）以赋予飞机最终外观。涂料型工艺常用着色的丁酸盐涂料，但也有各种聚氨酯面漆可供选择。要完成适航的织物重新蒙皮作业，必须使用相应 STC 规定的面漆产品和程序。

使用上述产品时，在不同阶段常使用各种添加剂。以下简要列出有助于正确施工织物涂层的其他产品。再次强调，只能使用特定 STC 批准的产品。即使功能相同，也不允许用类似产品替代。

• 催化剂（catalyst）加速化学反应。催化剂是针对与其混合的每种产品专门设计的，常用于环氧和聚氨酯。

• 稀释剂（thinner）是加入产品中的溶剂或混合溶剂，使其达到适合刷涂或喷涂等施工的稠度。

• 缓干剂（retarder）加入产品中以减慢干燥时间。它主要用于涂料工艺和面漆，使喷涂的涂层有更多时间流平，从而获得更深、更光亮的表面。当施工温度略高于产品理想温度时使用。在高湿条件下，它还可用来防止涂料面层发白（blushing）。

• 促干剂（accelerator）含有溶剂，可加快与其混合产品的干燥时间。通常在施工温度低于理想工作温度时使用；当空气中有污染物威胁涂层表面质量时，也可用它加快干燥。

• 复原剂（rejuvenator）仅用于涂料面层，含有可软化涂层并使其略微流平的溶剂。复原剂还含有新的增塑剂，混入原有涂层中，从而提高涂层的整体柔韧性和寿命。

• 杀菌剂和防霉剂添加剂对有机织物蒙皮飞机很重要，因为棉布、亚麻布等织物是真菌和霉菌的宿主。使用聚酯织物时不存在真菌和霉菌问题，因此不需要这些添加剂。现代涂层配方含有预混的抗真菌剂，足以防止真菌或霉菌问题。

4. 语法解析

① in order for ... to do 复合结构 + 超长并列宾语

原句：In order for the items to legally be used, the FAA must approve the fabric, tapes, threads, cords, glues, dopes, sealants, coatings, thinners, additives, fungicides, rejuvenators, and paints for the manufacturer, the holder of an STC, or a field approval. 拆解：句首 In order for the items to legally be used 为不定式复合结构作目的状语（for 引出不定式的逻辑主语 the items）；主句主语 the FAA，谓语 must approve，宾语为十三个并列名词，for the manufacturer, the holder of an STC, or a field approval 为对象状语，说明为谁批准。 要点：for the items to be used 中 items 是 use 的逻辑宾语，所以用被动式 to be used；legally 修饰不定式而非谓语。长并列宾语的最后一项前有 and，翻译时不必逐项罗列次序，但要保证“批准谁、批准什么”两层关系不混。

② 同位语 + 非限制性定语从句 + 省略关系词的定语从句

原句：TSO-C15d also refers to another document, Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) Aerospace Material Specification (AMS) 3806D, which details properties a fabric must contain to be an approved fabric for airplane cloth. 拆解：主句主语 TSO-C15d，谓语 refers to，宾语 another document；Society of Automotive Engineers... 3806D 为同位语，说明该文件的全名；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 3806D；从句宾语 properties 后接省略了关系词 that/which 的定语从句 a fabric must contain，to be an approved fabric 为目的状语。 要点：document 与后面的规范名之间没有 of，是同位关系而非所属关系。properties 后的定语从句省略了作宾语的关系词，这是英语中最常见的省略；不能把 a fabric 误读成 properties 的同位语。

③ along with 插入语与主谓一致

原句：These approved materials, along with the procedures for using them, constitute the STCs for each particular fabric covering process. 拆解：主语 These approved materials，along with the procedures for using them 为插入的伴随介词短语，谓语 constitute（复数形式），宾语 the STCs，for each particular fabric covering process 为后置定语。 要点：along with、together with、as well as 引导的短语不改变主谓一致，谓语仍与前面的主语 materials 一致，故用 constitute 而非 constitutes。这一点与 and 连接并列主语有本质区别。

④ 被动语态 + after / but before 并列时间状语

原句：It is usually self-adhesive cloth tape and is applied after the aircraft is cleaned, inspected, and primed, but before the fabric is installed. 拆解：It 指代上文的防磨带，两个并列谓语 is（系动词，表语 self-adhesive cloth tape）和 is applied（被动）；after 与 but before 各引导一个时间状语从句，两个从句都是被动语态，且 after 从句内三个过去分词并列（cleaned, inspected, and primed）。 要点：but before 与前面的 after 构成对照，翻译时宜译成“在……之后、……之前”，而不是把 but 译成“但是”。三个过去分词共用 is，不能理解为三个独立动作的先后。

⑤ because 原因状语从句 + 定语从句

原句：Proper fill coating is critical because UV light is the single most destructive element that causes polyester fabric to deteriorate. 拆解：主句为主系表结构（Proper fill coating is critical）；because 引导原因状语从句，从句也是主系表结构，表语 the single most destructive element 后接 that 引导的定语从句，cause sth. to do 为复合宾语结构。 要点：the single most destructive 中 single 起强调作用（“唯一最……”），不是数量“一个”；cause ... to deteriorate 的宾语是 polyester fabric，不能用被动含义理解成织物自己老化。

⑥ 时间状语从句 + be known as

原句：When fillers and sealers are combined, they are known as fabric primers. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，用被动语态 are combined；主句 they are known as fabric primers，be known as 表示“被称为”，as 后为身份/名称。 要点：be known as 后接名称，be known for 后接特征，二者不可混用。主句的 they 指 fillers and sealers 两者合并后的产物，所以谓语用复数、名称用复数 primers。
