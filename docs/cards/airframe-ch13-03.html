Landing Gear System Maintenance

机体 Ch 13 · Landing Gear System Maintenance · 原文 PDF 第 755 页

1. 原文

The moving parts and dirty environment of the landing gear make this an area of regular maintenance. Because of the stresses and pressures acting on the landing gear, inspection, servicing, and other maintenance becomes a continuous process. The most important job in the maintenance of the aircraft landing gear system is thorough accurate inspections. To properly perform inspections, all surfaces should be cleaned to ensure that no trouble spots are undetected.

Periodically, it is necessary to inspect shock struts, trunnion and brace assemblies and bearings, shimmy dampers, wheels, wheel bearings, tires, and brakes. Landing gear position indicators, lights, and warning horns must also be checked for proper operation. During all inspections and visits to the wheel wells, ensure all ground safety locks are installed.

Other landing gear inspection items include checking emergency control handles and systems for proper position and condition. Inspect landing gear wheels for cleanliness, corrosion, and cracks. Check wheel tie bolts for looseness. Examine anti-skid wiring for deterioration. Check tires for wear, cuts, deterioration, presence of grease or oil, alignment of slippage marks, and proper inflation. Inspect landing gear mechanism for condition, operation, and proper adjustment. Lubricate the landing gear, including the nose wheel steering. Check steering system cables for wear, broken strands, alignment, and safetying. Inspect landing gear shock struts for such conditions as cracks, corrosion, breaks, and security. Where applicable, check brake clearances and wear.

Various types of lubricant are required to lubricate points of friction and wear on landing gear. Specific products to be used are given by the manufacturer in the maintenance manual. Lubrication may be accomplished by hand or with a grease gun. Follow manufacturer’s instructions. Before applying grease to a pressure grease fitting, be sure the fitting is wiped clean of dirt and debris, as well as old hardened grease. Dust and sand mixed with grease produce a very destructive abrasive compound. Wipe off all excess grease while greasing the gear. The piston rods of all exposed strut cylinders and actuating cylinders should be clean at all times.

Periodically, wheel bearings must be removed, cleaned, inspected, and lubricated. When cleaning a wheel bearing, use the recommended cleaning solvent. Do not use gasoline or jet fuel. Dry the bearing by directing a blast of dry air between the rollers. Do not direct the air so that it spins the bearing as without lubrication, this could cause the bearing to fly apart resulting in injury. When inspecting the bearing, check for defects that would render it unserviceable, such as cracks, flaking, broken bearing surfaces, roughness due to impact pressure or surface wear, corrosion or pitting, discoloration from excessive heat, cracked or broken bearing cages, and scored or loose bearing cups or cones that would affect proper seating on the axle or wheel. If any discrepancies are found, replace the bearing with a serviceable unit. Bearings should be lubricated immediately after cleaning and inspection to prevent corrosion.

To lubricate a tapered roller bearing, use a bearing lubrication tool or place a small amount of the approved grease on the palm of the hand. Grasp the bearing with the other hands and press the larger diameter side of the bearing into the grease to force it completely through the space between the bearing rollers and the cone. Gradually turn the bearing so that all of the rollers have been completely packed with grease.

Landing Gear Rigging & Adjustment

Occasionally, it becomes necessary to adjust the landing gear switches, doors, linkages, latches, and locks to ensure proper operation of the landing gear system and doors. When landing gear actuating cylinders are replaced and when length adjustments are made, over-travel must be checked. Over-travel is the action of the cylinder piston beyond the movement necessary for landing gear extension and retraction. The additional action operates the landing gear latch mechanisms.

A wide variety of aircraft types and landing gear system designs result in procedures for rigging and adjustment that vary from aircraft to aircraft. Uplock and downlock clearances, linkage adjustments, limit switch adjustments, and other adjustments must be confirmed by the technician in the manufacturer’s maintenance data before taking action. The following examples of various adjustments are given to convey concepts, rather than actual procedures for any particular aircraft.

Adjusting Landing Gear Latches

The adjustment of various latches is a primary concern to the aircraft technician. Latches are generally used in landing gear systems to hold the gear up or down and/or to hold the gear doors open or closed. Despite numerous variations, all latches are designed to do the same thing. They must operate automatically at the proper time, and they must hold the unit in the desired position. A typical landing gear door latch is examined below. Many gear up latches operate similarly. Clearances and dimensional measurements of rollers, shafts, bushings, pins, bolts, etc., are common.

On this particular aircraft, the landing gear door is held closed by two latches. To have the door locked securely, both latches must grip and hold the door tightly against the aircraft structure. The principle components of each latch mechanism are shown in Figure 13-47. They are a hydraulic latch cylinder, a latch hook, a spring-loaded crank-and-lever linkage with sector, and the latch roller.

When hydraulic pressure is applied, the cylinder operates the linkage to engage (or disengage) the hook with (or from) the roller on the gear door. In the gear-down sequence, the hook is disengaged by the spring load on the linkage. In the gear-up sequence, when the closing door is in contact with the latch hook, the cylinder operates the linkage to engage the latch hook with the door roller. Cables on the landing gear emergency extension system are connected to the sector to permit emergency release of the latch rollers. An uplock switch is installed on, and actuated by, each latch to provide a gear up indication in the flight deck.

With the gear up and the door latched, inspect the latch roller for proper clearance as shown in Figure 13-48A . On this installation, the required clearance is 1 ⁄ 8 ± 3 ⁄ 32-inch. If the roller is not within tolerance, it may be adjusted by loosening its mounting bolts and raising or lowering the latch roller support. This is accomplished via the elongated holes and serrated locking surfaces of the latch roller support and serrated plate.

Gear Door Clearances

Landing gear doors have specific allowable clearances between the doors and the aircraft structure that must be maintained. Adjustments are typically made at the hinge installations or to the connecting links that support and move the door. On some installations, door hinges are adjusted by placing a serrated hinge with an elongated mounting hole in the proper position in a hinge support fitting. Using serrated washers, the mounting bolt is torqued to hold the position. Figure 13-49 illustrates this type of mounting, which allows linear adjustments via the elongated hole.

The distance landing gear doors open or close may depend upon the length of the door linkage. Rod end adjustments are common to fit the door. Adjustments to door stops are also a possibility. The manufacturer’s maintenance manual specifies the length of the linkages and gives procedure for adjusting the stops. Follow all specified procedures that are accomplished with the aircraft on jacks and the gear retracted. Doors that are too tight can cause structural damage. Doors that are too loose catch wind in flight, which could cause wear and potential failure, as well as parasite drag.

Drag & Side Brace Adjustment

Each landing gear has specific adjustments and tolerances per the manufacturer that permit the gear to function as intended. A common geometry used to lock a landing gear in the down position involves a collapsible side brace that is extended and held in an over-center position through the use of a locking link. Springs and actuators may also contribute to the motion of the linkage. Adjustments and tests are needed to ensure proper operation.

Figure 13-50 illustrates a landing gear on a small aircraft with such a side brace. It consists of an upper and lower link hinged at the center that permits the brace to jackknife during retraction of the gear. The upper end pivots on a trunnion attached to structure in the wheel well overhead. The lower end is attached to the shock strut. A locking link is incorporated between the upper end of the shock strut and the lower drag link. It is adjustable to provide the correct amount of over-center travel of the side brace links. This locks the gear securely in the down position to prevent collapse of the gear.

To adjust the over-center position of the side brace locking link, the aircraft must be placed on jacks. With the landing gear in the down position, the lock link end fitting is adjusted so that the side brace links are held firmly over-center. When the gear is held inboard six inches from the down and locked position and then released, the gear must free fall into the locked down position.

In addition to the amount the side brace links are adjusted to travel over center, down lock spring tension must also be checked. This is accomplished with a spring scale. The tension on this particular gear is between 40 and 60 pounds. Check the manufacturer’s maintenance data for each aircraft to ensure correct tensions exist and proper adjustments are made.

Landing Gear Retraction Test

The proper functioning of a landing gear system and components can be checked by performing a landing gear retraction test. This is also known as swinging the gear. The aircraft is properly supported on jacks for this check, and the landing gear should be cleaned and lubricated if needed. The gear is then raised and lowered as though the aircraft were in flight while a close visual inspection is performed. All parts of the system should be observed for security and proper operation. The emergency back-up extension system should be checked whenever swinging the gear.

Retraction tests are performed at various times, such as during annual inspection. Any time a landing gear component is replaced that could affect the correct functioning of the landing gear system, a retraction test should follow when adjustments to landing gear linkages or components that affect gear system performance are made. It may be necessary to swing the gear after a hard or overweight landing. It is also common to swing the gear while attempting to locate a malfunction within the system. For all required retraction tests and the specific inspection points to check, consult the manufacturer’s maintenance manual for the aircraft in question as each landing gear system is unique.

The following is a list of general inspection items to be performed while swinging the gear:

  1. Check the landing gear for proper extension and retraction.
  1. Check all switches, lights, and warning devices for proper operation.
  1. Check the landing gear doors for clearance and freedom from binding.
  1. Check landing gear linkage for proper operation, adjustment, and general condition.
  1. Check the alternate/emergency extension or retraction systems for proper operation.
  1. Investigate any unusual sounds, such as those caused by rubbing, binding, chafing, or vibration.

2. 中文翻译

起落架的运动部件和脏污环境，使其成为需要经常维护的区域。由于起落架承受着各种应力和压力，检查、勤务和其他维护工作就成了一项持续不断的过程。飞机起落架系统维护中最重要的工作，是彻底而准确的检查。为正确实施检查，所有表面都应清洁干净，以确保不遗漏任何故障点。

需要定期检查减震支柱、耳轴与撑杆组件及其轴承、减摆器、机轮、轮轴承、轮胎和刹车。起落架位置指示器、指示灯和警告喇叭也必须检查其工作是否正常。在所有检查和进入轮舱作业期间，要确保所有地面安全锁都已安装到位。

起落架的其他检查项目还包括：检查应急操纵手柄和系统是否处于正确位置、状态是否良好。检查起落架机轮的清洁度、腐蚀和裂纹情况。检查轮毂连接螺栓是否松动。检查防滞系统线路有无老化变质。检查轮胎的磨损、割伤、老化、有无油脂污染、打滑标记是否对齐以及充气压力是否适当。检查起落架机构的状态、工作和调整是否正确。对起落架进行润滑，包括前轮转弯机构。检查转弯系统钢索的磨损、断股、走向对齐和安全打保险情况。检查起落架减震支柱有无裂纹、腐蚀、断裂以及固定是否牢靠。适用时，检查刹车间隙和磨损。

润滑起落架上的摩擦点和磨损点需要使用多种类型的润滑剂。应使用何种具体产品，由制造商在维护手册中规定。润滑可用手工或注油枪完成，须遵循制造商的说明。在向压力注油嘴加注润滑脂之前，务必把油嘴上的污物、碎屑以及陈旧的硬结油脂擦拭干净。灰尘和砂粒与润滑脂混合，会形成破坏性极强的研磨性混合物。给起落架加脂时，应擦去所有多余的润滑脂。所有外露的支柱筒和作动筒活塞杆应始终保持清洁。

必须定期拆下轮轴承，进行清洗、检查和润滑。清洗轮轴承时，应使用推荐的清洗溶剂，不得使用汽油或喷气燃油。用干燥气流吹过滚子之间来吹干轴承。不要使气流吹得轴承旋转，因为在无润滑的情况下，这可能使轴承散架飞出而造成人身伤害。检查轴承时，要查找会使其不适用的缺陷，例如裂纹、剥落、轴承工作面破损、由冲击压力或表面磨损造成的粗糙、腐蚀或点蚀、过热变色、保持架开裂或断裂，以及划伤或松动的轴承外圈/内锥，这些都会影响其在轮轴或机轮上的正确座合。若发现任何缺陷，应更换为可用件。轴承清洗和检查后应立即润滑，以防腐蚀。

润滑圆锥滚子轴承时，可使用轴承润滑工具，或把少量经批准的润滑脂放在手掌上。用另一只手抓住轴承，将轴承直径较大的一侧压入润滑脂中，使润滑脂完全挤过滚子与内锥之间的空隙。逐渐转动轴承，直到所有滚子都填满润滑脂。

起落架校装与调整

有时需要调整起落架的开关、舱门、连杆、锁扣和锁具，以确保起落架系统和舱门工作正常。当更换起落架作动筒以及进行长度调整时，必须检查超行程。超行程是指作动筒活塞超出起落架收放所需行程之外的运动。这部分附加运动用来操纵起落架的锁扣机构。

机型繁多、起落架系统设计各异，使得校装与调整程序因机而异。上锁和下锁间隙、连杆调整、限位开关调整以及其他调整，技术员在动手之前都必须先在制造商的维护资料中加以确认。下面给出的各种调整示例旨在说明概念，而不是针对某一具体飞机的实际程序。

调整起落架锁扣

各种锁扣的调整是飞机技术员的一项主要工作。起落架系统中的锁扣一般用于把起落架固定在收上或放下位置，和/或把起落架舱门固定在打开或关闭位置。尽管形式多样，所有锁扣的设计目的都相同：它们必须在适当的时机自动动作，并把所锁定的部件保持在所需位置。下面分析一种典型的起落架舱门锁扣，许多起落架收上锁扣的工作原理与之类似。滚轮、轴、衬套、销、螺栓等的间隙和尺寸测量是常见的检查内容。

在这种特定飞机上，起落架舱门由两个锁扣保持关闭。要使舱门可靠锁定，两个锁扣都必须把舱门紧紧压住并固定在飞机结构上。每个锁扣机构的主要部件如图 13-47 所示，包括液压锁扣作动筒、锁钩、带扇形件的弹簧曲柄连杆机构以及锁扣滚轮。

施加液压时，作动筒操纵连杆使锁钩与舱门上的滚轮啮合（或脱开）。在起落架放下顺序中，锁钩靠连杆上的弹簧载荷脱开。在起落架收上顺序中，当正在关闭的舱门接触到锁钩时，作动筒操纵连杆使锁钩与舱门滚轮啮合。起落架应急放下系统的钢索连接到扇形件上，以便应急释放锁扣滚轮。每个锁扣上都装有一个上锁开关，并由锁扣本身驱动，用以在驾驶舱内提供起落架收上指示。

在起落架收上、舱门锁定的状态下，按图 13-48A 所示检查锁扣滚轮的间隙是否合适。该安装要求的间隙为 1/8 ± 3/32 英寸。如果滚轮不在公差范围内，可松开其安装螺栓、升高或降低锁扣滚轮支架进行调整。这是通过锁扣滚轮支架和锯齿板上的长圆孔与锯齿锁定面实现的。

起落架舱门间隙

起落架舱门与飞机结构之间有特定的允许间隙，必须予以保持。调整通常在铰链安装处或支撑并带动舱门的连接连杆上进行。在某些安装中，舱门铰链的调整方法是：把带长圆安装孔的锯齿铰链放到铰链支撑接头中的正确位置，然后用锯齿垫圈，按规定力矩拧紧安装螺栓以保持该位置。图 13-49 示出了这种安装方式，它可通过长圆孔进行直线调整。

起落架舱门开启或关闭的距离可能取决于舱门连杆的长度。常用杆端接头调整来使舱门配合到位，也可能需要调整舱门限位。制造商的维护手册规定了连杆长度，并给出了调整限位的程序。凡规定需将飞机顶起、起落架收上进行的程序，都要遵照执行。舱门过紧会造成结构损伤；舱门过松会在飞行中兜风，从而引起磨损和潜在故障，还会产生废阻。

阻力撑杆与侧撑杆调整

每个起落架都有制造商规定的特定调整量和公差，使起落架能按设计意图工作。把起落架锁定在放下位置的常见几何布局，采用可折收的侧撑杆，通过锁定连杆使其伸展并保持在过中心位置。弹簧和作动器也可能参与连杆的运动。需要进行调整和测试以确保工作正常。

图 13-50 示出了小型飞机上装用这种侧撑杆的起落架。它由上下两根在中部铰接的连杆组成，收放起落架时允许撑杆折叠。上端绕一个耳轴转动，耳轴固定在轮舱顶部的结构上；下端连接到减震支柱。在减震支柱上端与下阻力连杆之间装有锁定连杆，它可调整，以提供侧撑杆连杆正确的过中心行程量，从而把起落架可靠锁定在放下位置，防止起落架折倒。

调整侧撑杆锁定连杆的过中心位置时，必须把飞机顶在千斤顶上。在起落架放下状态下调整锁定连杆端接头，使侧撑杆连杆牢固地保持在过中心位置。将起落架从放下锁定位置向内侧扳离六英寸后松开，起落架必须能自由落到放下锁定位置。

除了侧撑杆连杆越过中心的调整量之外，还必须检查下位锁弹簧张力。这用弹簧秤来测量。该起落架的张力在 40 至 60 磅之间。对每种飞机都应查阅制造商的维护资料，以确保张力正确、调整得当。

起落架收放试验

起落架系统及各部件的功能是否正常，可通过做起落架收放试验来检查，这也称为“摆动起落架”（swinging the gear）。做此检查时，飞机应正确地架在千斤顶上，起落架必要时应予清洁和润滑。然后像飞机在空中一样收放起落架，同时进行仔细的目视检查。应观察系统所有部件是否固定牢靠、工作正常。每次摆动起落架时，都应检查应急备份放下系统。

收放试验在多种时机进行，例如年度检查期间。任何时候更换了可能影响起落架系统正常工作的起落架部件，或对影响起落架系统性能的连杆、部件做了调整之后，都应随后进行收放试验。在重着陆或超重着陆之后，可能需要摆动起落架。在查找系统内部故障时，摆动起落架也很常见。所有要求的收放试验及具体检查点，都应查阅相应飞机的制造商维护手册，因为每套起落架系统都各具特点。

以下是摆动起落架时应进行的一般检查项目：

  1. 检查起落架收放是否正常。
  1. 检查所有开关、指示灯和警告装置工作是否正常。
  1. 检查起落架舱门的间隙以及有无卡滞。
  1. 检查起落架连杆的工作、调整和总体状况。
  1. 检查备用/应急收放系统工作是否正常。
  1. 查明任何异常声响，例如由摩擦、卡滞、磨碰或振动引起的声响。

4. 语法解析

① 目的状语前置 + 被动语态 + ensure that 从句

原句：To properly perform inspections, all surfaces should be cleaned to ensure that no trouble spots are undetected. 拆解：不定式短语 To properly perform inspections 置于句首作目的状语；主句为被动语态 all surfaces should be cleaned，should 表义务；to ensure that... 为目的状语，其中 that 引导宾语从句，从句用被动语态 no trouble spots are undetected。 要点：句首不定式用逗号与主句隔开，说明“为了做检查”而非句子主语的动作；两个被动结构的主语不同——are cleaned 的主语是 all surfaces，are undetected 的主语是 trouble spots，读时不要混为一谈。

② 过去分词后置定语 + 主谓一致

原句：Dust and sand mixed with grease produce a very destructive abrasive compound. 拆解：主语为并列名词 Dust and sand，mixed with grease 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰主语；谓语 produce 用复数形式，与并列主语一致；宾语 a very destructive abrasive compound 带两个前置定语。 要点：mixed 的逻辑主语是 dust and sand，构成被动关系（“被油脂混合”），所以用过去分词而非现在分词。主语中心词是并列的 Dust and sand，中间插入的 mixed with grease 不改变谓语数，故用 produce。

③ 否定祈使句 + so that 结果状语 + as 引导原因

原句：Do not direct the air so that it spins the bearing as without lubrication, this could cause the bearing to fly apart resulting in injury. 拆解：主句是否定祈使句 Do not direct the air；so that 引导结果状语从句 it spins the bearing；as 在此作连词引导原因状语从句（as = because），省略了主语和谓语的一部分；逗号后 this could cause... 另起一分句说明后果，resulting in injury 为现在分词短语作结果状语。 要点：这里 as 不是“作为”也不是“当……时”，而是“因为”，是维修手册中常见的因果用法；so that 在此表“以至于”，与表目的的 so that 要靠语境区分。分词结果状语 resulting in... 的逻辑主语是整个前一分句所述情况。

④ 时间状语从句省略 + 长列举 + 两个定语从句

原句：When inspecting the bearing, check for defects that would render it unserviceable, such as cracks, flaking, broken bearing surfaces, roughness due to impact pressure or surface wear, corrosion or pitting, discoloration from excessive heat, cracked or broken bearing cages, and scored or loose bearing cups or cones that would affect proper seating on the axle or wheel. 拆解：When inspecting the bearing 为时间状语从句，省略了主语和 be 动词（when you are inspecting）；主句为祈使句 check for defects；that would render it unserviceable 是定语从句修饰 defects；such as 引出七个并列名词短语作举例；句末 that would affect proper seating... 是第二个定语从句，修饰 bearing cups or cones。 要点：such as 的列举一直延伸到句末，中间的逗号和 and 都属列举内部，不要误读成主句的新成分；两个 that 从句分别修饰 defects 和 cups or cones，靠“就近”判断先行词。render it unserviceable 为 render + 宾语 + 宾补结构。

⑤ 系表结构 + 形容词短语后置定语

原句：Over-travel is the action of the cylinder piston beyond the movement necessary for landing gear extension and retraction. 拆解：主语 Over-travel，系动词 is，表语 the action of the cylinder piston；beyond the movement... 为介词短语作后置定语，修饰 the action；necessary for landing gear extension and retraction 为形容词短语后置修饰 the movement。 要点：necessary 作定语时习惯后置（something necessary / the movement necessary for...），因为被修饰语带有较长的介词短语。beyond 在这里表示“超出……之外”，是全句理解的关键。

⑥ 时间状语从句的被动 + 主句情态动词

原句：When the gear is held inboard six inches from the down and locked position and then released, the gear must free fall into the locked down position. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 the gear，谓语为并列的被动结构 is held... and then released；inboard six inches from... 为程度/距离状语；主句 the gear must free fall into...，must 表强制要求，free fall 为动词短语。 要点：从句中 is held 与 released 共用一个主语 the gear，第二个被动省略了 is；inboard 在此作副词，表示“向机内侧”。主句 must 表达的是维护手册中的验收判据，而非推测。
