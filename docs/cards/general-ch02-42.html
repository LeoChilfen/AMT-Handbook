The AC Numbering System

1. 原文

There are three parts to an AC number, as in 25-42-C.

• The first part of the number identifies the subject matter area of the AC. This corresponds to the part of the FAA’s regulations. In the above example, this would be part 25.

• The second part of the number, beginning with the dash, is a sequential number within each subject area.

In the above example, this would be the 42nd AC relating to part 25.

• The third part of the number is a letter assigned by the originating office showing the revision sequence if an AC is revised. The first version of an AC does not have a revision letter. In the above example, this is third revision, as designated by the “C.” [Figure 2-12]

Airworthiness Directives (AD)

In accordance with 14 CFR part 39, the FAA issues ADs in response to deficiencies and/or unsafe conditions found in aircraft, engines, propellers, or other aircraft parts. ADs require that the relevant problem must be corrected on all aircraft or aircraft parts using the same design. ADs are initiated as either proposed, corrective, or final (telegraphic) via the Federal Register. The Federal Register is the official daily publication of the United States Government. It is the printed method of informing the public of laws that are enacted or will be enacted.

Electronic versions of ADs are available from the Federal Register and from the Regulatory and Guidance Library. You can search by manufacturer, model, or AD number. All ADs are “incorporated by reference” into part 39 and are considered final. ADs must be followed to remain in compliance with the FAA. Once an AD has been issued, a person/company is authorized to use the affected aircraft or part only if it has been corrected in accordance with the AD.

2. 中文翻译

AC 编号由三部分组成，如 25-42-C 所示。

• 第一部分标明该 AC 的主题领域，与 FAA 规章的「部」相对应。上例中即第 25 部。

• 第二部分从连字符开始，是各主题领域内的顺序号。

上例中，这就是与第 25 部有关的第 42 份 AC。

• 第三部分是由发文办公室指定的一个字母，用以表示 AC 被修订后的修订次序。AC 的第一版不带修订字母。上例中的「C」表示已是第三次修订。

适航指令（AD）

依据 14 CFR 第 39 部，FAA 针对在航空器、发动机、螺旋桨或其他航空器部件中发现的缺陷和/或不安全状况发布 AD。AD 要求：凡采用同一设计的航空器或航空器部件，都必须纠正相关问题。AD 以拟议规则、纠正性规则或最终（电报）规则的形式，通过《联邦公报》启动。《联邦公报》是美国政府的官方每日出版物，是以印刷方式告知公众已经颁布或将要颁布的法律的途径。

AD 的电子版本可从《联邦公报》以及监管与指导库获取，可以按制造商、型号或 AD 编号检索。所有 AD 都「通过引用并入」第 39 部，并被视为最终规则。必须遵守 AD 才能保持对 FAA 的符合性。AD 一经发布，个人或公司只有按该 AD 完成纠正后，才被授权使用受影响的航空器或部件。

4. 语法解析

① There are three parts to an AC number（there be + 介词 to 表归属）

原句：There are three parts to an AC number, as in 25-42-C.

拆解：There be 存在句，真正的主语是 three parts；to an AC number 是介词短语后置修饰 parts，说明「属于一个 AC 编号的」；as in 25-42-C 是插入的举例。

要点：表示「某事物有几个部分」既可用 An AC number has three parts，也可用 there are three parts to sth，后者更强调结构本身。to 在此表归属关系，不要随意换成 for。as in 是「如……所示、例如」的固定插入语。

② 两个分词短语同时后置修饰 a letter

原句：The third part of the number is a letter assigned by the originating office showing the revision sequence if an AC is revised.

拆解：主语 The third part of the number；系动词 is；表语 a letter；assigned by the originating office 是过去分词短语作后置定语（表被动），showing the revision sequence 是现在分词短语作后置定语（表主动）；if an AC is revised 是条件状语从句。

要点：assigned 与 showing 都修饰 a letter，按逻辑先后排列——先说「由谁指定」，再说「表示什么」。过去分词表被动、现在分词表主动，不能写成 which assigned。读长定语时先剥掉两个分词短语，句干就是 The third part is a letter。

③ require that 从句中的 must be corrected

原句：ADs require that the relevant problem must be corrected on all aircraft or aircraft parts using the same design.

拆解：主句 ADs require；that 引导宾语从句，从句为被动结构 must be corrected；on all aircraft or aircraft parts 是状语；using the same design 是现在分词短语后置修饰 parts。

要点：require that 从句在英文正式文本中仍常用 must，而不是虚拟式 (should) be corrected，看到 require that … must 不要以为是重复多余。using the same design 是适用范围的关键限定：只要采用同一设计，不论哪一架，都必须纠正。
