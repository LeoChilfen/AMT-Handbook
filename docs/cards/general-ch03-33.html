Multiplication of Positive & Negative Numbers

1. 原文

The product of two positive numbers is always positive. The product of two negative numbers is always positive. The product of a positive and a negative number is always negative.

3 × 6 = 18 −3 × 6 = −18 −3 × −6 = 18 3 × −6 = −18 Division of Positive & Negative Numbers The quotient of two positive numbers is always positive. The quotient of two negative numbers is always positive. The quotient of a positive and negative number is always negative.

6 ÷ 3 = 2 −6 ÷ 3 = −2 −6 ÷ −3 = 2 6 ÷ −3 = −2

4. 语法解析

① 抽象名词作主语 + of 短语作定语（平行结构）

原句：The product of two positive numbers is always positive.

拆解：主语 The product，of two positive numbers 为后置定语，限定是「哪两个数的积」；系动词 is；表语 positive；always 是频度副词，插在 is 与 positive 之间。

要点：数学表述偏爱「The product / quotient / sum / difference of A is B」这种名词化句式，把运算结果提到主语位置。本节六个判断句结构完全一致，只替换 product 与 quotient、正负结论，读熟一句即可推出其余，不必逐句分析。

② 频度副词 always 的位置

原句：The quotient of two positive numbers is always positive.

拆解：主语 The quotient of two positive numbers；谓语 is always positive，always 位于系动词之后、表语之前。

要点：频度副词（always / usually / never）的位置规律是「be 动词后、实义动词前」：可说 is always positive，不能说 always is positive；若谓语是实义动词则说 always equals。此处 always 有「无一例外」的强调意味，是符号法则的典型措辞。

③ 并列形容词前的冠词省略

原句：The product of a positive and a negative number is always negative.

拆解：a positive and a negative number 中，两个形容词各自带冠词，后面共享中心词 number，实际指「一个正数和一个负数」两个数。

要点：a X and Y N（如 a positive and negative number）通常理解为「一个既 X 又 Y 的 N」，即同一个事物；要表达两个数就必须补出第二个冠词 a。本节上文正是 a positive and a negative number，可对照记忆 —— 数学与维修文件里这类冠词差别会直接改变题意。
