1. 原文

Though not a drawing, a digital image created by a digital camera can be extremely helpful to aviation maintenance technicians in evaluating and sharing information concerning the airworthiness or other information about aircraft. Digital images can be rapidly transmitted over the World Wide Web as attachments to e-mail messages. Images of structural fatigue cracks, failed parts, or other flaws, as well as desired design and paint schemes, are just a few examples of the types of digital images that might be shared by any number of users over the Internet. Figure 4-33 is a digital image of damage to a composite structure taken with a simple digital camera.

2. 中文翻译

4. 语法解析

① Though not a drawing, a digital image created by … can be extremely helpful（让步状语省略 + 分词定语）

原句：Though not a drawing, a digital image created by a digital camera can be extremely helpful to aviation maintenance technicians in evaluating and sharing information concerning the airworthiness or other information about aircraft.

拆解：Though not a drawing 是让步状语从句的省略形式，补全为 Though it is not a drawing；主语 a digital image 后接过去分词短语 created by a digital camera 作后置定语；谓语 can be extremely helpful；to aviation maintenance technicians 引出对象；in evaluating and sharing … 是「介词 in + 动名词」，说明在哪些方面有帮助。

要点：连词后直接跟名词或形容词，说明主语与 be 动词一起被省略（though not a drawing / when necessary / if possible）。be helpful in doing sth. 中的 in 是介词，不能写成 to do；be helpful to sb. 中的 to 才是「对某人」。

② a measurement scale, or other object, such as a coin, can be placed …（主语 + 插入语 + 被动）

原句：To provide information about the extent of the damage, a measurement scale, or other object, such as a coin, can be placed near the area of concern before the picture is taken.

拆解：To provide information about the extent of the damage 为不定式作目的状语；主语 a measurement scale 后用逗号插入 or other object, such as a coin 作补充说明；谓语 can be placed 为情态被动；near the area of concern 是地点状语；before the picture is taken 是时间状语从句。

要点：逗号夹住的插入成分读图时先跳过，抓主干「a measurement scale can be placed」再回填细节，否则容易把 can be placed 的主语错认成 coin。before 从句用一般现在时表将来（before the picture is taken，不用 will be taken）。

③ Images of …, as well as …, are just a few examples of the types of digital images that might be shared（长主语 + 定语从句）

原句：Images of structural fatigue cracks, failed parts, or other flaws, as well as desired design and paint schemes, are just a few examples of the types of digital images that might be shared by any number of users over the Internet.

拆解：主语中心词是 Images（复数），of … 与 as well as … 都是附加成分，故谓语用 are；表语 just a few examples of the types of digital images；that might be shared by any number of users over the Internet 是定语从句修饰 digital images。

要点：as well as 连接成分时，谓语与前面那个主语一致（Images … are），这一点与 and 不同。might 在此表「可能」，比 can 更留余地；by any number of users 是被动句里的执行者，译作「由任意数量的用户」。

④ can be rapidly transmitted … as attachments to e-mail messages（情态被动 + as 短语表形式）

原句：Digital images can be rapidly transmitted over the World Wide Web as attachments to e-mail messages.

拆解：主语 Digital images，谓语 can be transmitted 为情态被动，rapidly 与 over the World Wide Web 作状语，as attachments to e-mail messages 是介词短语，说明以什么形式传输。

要点：as 在此表「作为、以……的形式」，不是「当……时」。attachment to 是固定搭配，介词用 to（附属于邮件）。技术文本陈述功能时惯用 can be + 过去分词，把「谁能做」隐去，只强调该功能可以实现。
