Helicopter Aerodynamics

通用 Ch 05 · Helicopter Aerodynamics · 原文 PDF 第 199 页

1. 原文

The helicopter, as we know it today, falls under the classification known as rotorcraft. Rotorcraft is also known as rotary wing aircraft, because instead of their wing being fixed like it is on an airplane, the wing rotates. The rotating wing of a rotorcraft can be thought of as a lift producing device, like the wing of an airplane, or as a thrust producing device, like the propeller on a piston engine.

2. 中文翻译

我们今天所知的直升机，属于称为旋翼航空器（rotorcraft）的这一类别。旋翼航空器又称旋转翼航空器，因为它的「机翼」不像飞机那样固定，而是旋转的。旋翼航空器的旋转翼既可以看作产生升力的装置，如同飞机的机翼；也可以看作产生推力的装置，如同活塞发动机上的螺旋桨。

3. 专业词汇

英文 词性 中文 本节语境

rotary wing aircraft n. phr. 旋转翼航空器 rotorcraft 的另一种叫法，强调翼是旋转的

4. 语法解析

① as we know it today 插入语 + 过去分词作定语

原句：The helicopter, as we know it today, falls under the classification known as rotorcraft.

拆解：主语 The helicopter 与谓语 falls 之间插入 as we know it today（as 引导的方式状语从句，it 指 the helicopter）；谓语 falls under the classification；known as rotorcraft 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 classification。

要点：as we know it 是固定插入语「就我们今天所知」，其中 it 必须与主语同指。fall under 意为「归入（某类）」，是技术分类句的常用动词；known as 是压缩的定语从句（= which is known as），翻译时并入「称为……」即可。

② because 从句 + instead of + 动名词复合结构

原句：Rotorcraft is also known as rotary wing aircraft, because instead of their wing being fixed like it is on an airplane, the wing rotates.

拆解：主句 Rotorcraft is also known as rotary wing aircraft；because 引导原因状语从句，从句内 instead of their wing being fixed like it is on an airplane 是介词 + 动名词复合结构（their wing being fixed）作对比状语，like it is on an airplane 是方式状语从句；从句主句是 the wing rotates。

要点：instead of 后接名词性成分，动词要用 -ing 并自带逻辑主语（their wing being fixed）；写成 instead of their wing is fixed 是常见错误。like it is on an airplane 中的 it 指 their wing，like 在此是连词，口语色彩较重，正式文体可用 as。

③ be thought of as … or as … 并列

原句：The rotating wing of a rotorcraft can be thought of as a lift producing device, like the wing of an airplane, or as a thrust producing device, like the propeller on a piston engine.

拆解：主语 The rotating wing of a rotorcraft；谓语 can be thought of as … or as …，两个 as 短语并列，共用 can be thought of；每个 as 短语后都有 like … 举例说明。

要点：think of A as B（把 A 看作 B）变被动后 as 不能丢，即 A can be thought of as B。or 连接两个并列的 as 短语时，第二个 as 前的 thought of 承前省略，读句子时要把它补回来。lift producing / thrust producing 是「名词 + 现在分词」构成的复合形容词。
