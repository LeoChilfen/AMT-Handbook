Type Certificate Data Sheets (TCDS)

通用 Ch 02 · Type Certificate Data Sheets (TCDS) · 原文 PDF 第 80 页

1. 原文

The TCDS is a formal description of the aircraft, engine, or propeller. It lists limitations and information required for type certification including airspeed limits, weight limits, thrust limitations, and so forth.

TCDSs and specifications set forth essential factors and other conditions that are necessary for U.S. airworthiness certification. Aircraft, engines, and propellers that conform to a U.S. TC are eligible for U.S. airworthiness certification when found to be in a condition for safe operation and ownership requisites are fulfilled. [Figure 2-16]

TCDSs were originated and first published in January 1958.

Title 14 of the CFR part 21, section 21.41 indicates they are part of the TC. As such, a TCDS is evidence the product has been type certificated. Generally, TCDSs are compiled from details supplied by the TC holder; however, the FAA may request and incorporate additional details when conditions warrant. [Figure 2-17]

Under federal law, no civil aircraft registered in the United States can operate without a valid airworthiness certificate.

This certificate must be approved and issued by the FAA; and it is only issued if the aircraft and its engines, propellers, and appliances are found to be airworthy and meet the requirements of an FAA-approved TC. The FAA issues a TC when a new aircraft, engine, propeller, and so forth, is found to meet safety standards set forth by the FAA. The TCDS lists the specifications, conditions, and limitations that the airworthiness requirements were met under for the specified product, such as engine make and model, fuel type, engine limits, airspeed limits, maximum weight, minimum crew, and so forth. TCDSs are issued and revised as necessary to accommodate new models or other major changes in the certified product. TCDSs are categorized by TC holder and product type.

2. 中文翻译

TCDS 是对航空器、发动机或螺旋桨的正式说明。它列出型号合格审定所要求的限制与信息，包括空速限制、重量限制、推力限制等等。

TCDS 与规范（specifications）规定了美国适航审定所必需的基本要素和其他条件。符合美国 TC 的航空器、发动机和螺旋桨，在被认定处于可安全运行的状态、且所有权方面的必要条件已满足时，即有资格取得美国适航证。[图 2-16]

TCDS 于 1958 年 1 月创设并首次发布。

《联邦法规》第 14 编（14 CFR）第 21 部第 21.41 节指出，它们属于 TC 的一部分。因此，TCDS 是产品已完成型号合格审定的证据。一般而言，TCDS 依据 TC 持有人提供的细节编制；但在情况需要时，FAA 可要求提供并纳入补充细节。[图 2-17]

依据联邦法律，在美国登记的民用航空器，若无有效适航证，不得运行。

该证必须由 FAA 批准并颁发；只有当航空器及其发动机、螺旋桨和设备经认定适航，并符合经 FAA 批准的 TC 的要求时，才会颁发。当新航空器、发动机、螺旋桨等被认定符合 FAA 规定的安全标准时，FAA 即颁发 TC。TCDS 列出该特定产品满足适航要求所依据的规范、条件和限制，例如发动机厂牌与型号、燃油类型、发动机限制、空速限制、最大重量、最少机组人数等等。TCDS 在需要时为适应新的型号或已审定产品的其他重大更改而颁发和修订。TCDS 按 TC 持有人和产品类别分类。

4. 语法解析

① when found to be … and ownership requisites are fulfilled（省略 + 并列的状语从句）

原句：Aircraft, engines, and propellers that conform to a U.S. TC are eligible for U.S. airworthiness certification when found to be in a condition for safe operation and ownership requisites are fulfilled.

拆解：主句主语为 Aircraft, engines, and propellers，被 that conform to a U.S. TC 这个定语从句修饰；谓语是 are eligible for；when 引导时间／条件状语从句，从句内是两个并列分句——(they are) found to be in a condition for safe operation 与 ownership requisites are fulfilled，前一分句省略了主语和 be。

要点：when + 过去分词是法规文本的典型省略结构，还原后为 when they are found to be …；find sth to be + 形容词的被动式即 be found to be。容易踩的坑是把 found 当成主动动词，译成「找到」。后一分句换回主动语态（requisites are fulfilled），说明省略只在主语相同的分句里才发生。

② must be approved and issued by …（情态动词被动 + 条件限制）

原句：This certificate must be approved and issued by the FAA; and it is only issued if the aircraft and its engines, propellers, and appliances are found to be airworthy and meet the requirements of an FAA-approved TC.

拆解：分号连接两个并列句。前句 must be approved and issued 为「情态动词 + be + 过去分词」，执行者用 by the FAA 点出；后句 only … if … 引导唯一条件，条件从句里 are found to be airworthy 与 meet the requirements 是并列谓语。

要点：must be + 过去分词表示「规定上必须被……」，此时把施动者放在 by 后；only if 比 if 强，强调别无他途。注意并列的第二个谓语 meet 前面不再重复 are，即 are found … and meet …，读时不要误以为漏了 be。

③ no … without …（双重否定表强制条件）

原句：Under federal law, no civil aircraft registered in the United States can operate without a valid airworthiness certificate.

拆解：主语 no civil aircraft（= not any civil aircraft），registered in the United States 为过去分词短语作后置定语（= which is registered …）；谓语 can operate；without a valid airworthiness certificate 是介词短语，作隐含的条件状语。

要点：no … without … 是「没有……就不得……」的强式表达，直译成「没有航空器能在无适航证时运行」很别扭，中文宜转成肯定条件句「若无有效适航证，不得运行」。可迁移：No aircraft may be returned to service without an approved maintenance record.

④ As such, a TCDS is evidence (that) …（同位语从句省略 that）

原句：As such, a TCDS is evidence the product has been type certificated.

拆解：As such 为句首连接性状语，意为「因此、就此而言」；主句 a TCDS is evidence；the product has been type certificated 是同位语从句，说明 evidence 的内容，连词 that 被省略；从句用现在完成时被动。

要点：evidence / fact / proof 后的同位语从句常省略 that，但句意仍是「……的证据」；has been type certificated 用现在完成时，强调审定状态延续至今。type certificate 作动词时写成 type-certificate 或 type certificate 均可，此处原文用 type certificated。
