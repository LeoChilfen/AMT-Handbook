Microfilm & Microfiche

通用 Ch 04 · Microfilm & Microfiche · 原文 PDF 第 147 页

1. 原文

The practice of recording drawings, parts catalogs, and maintenance and overhaul manuals on microfilms was utilized extensively in the past. Microfilm is available as regular 16 mm or 35 mm film. Since 35 mm film is larger, it provides a better reproduction of drawings. Microfiche is a card with pages laid out in a grid format. Microfilm and microfiche require use of special devices for both reading and printing the information.

Most modern aircraft manufacturers have replaced microfilm and microfiche with digital storage methods utilizing CDs, DVDs, and other data storage devices. A great deal of service and repair information for older aircraft has been transferred to digital storage devices. However, there may still be a need to access information using the old methods. A well-equipped shop should have available, both the old microfilm and microfiche equipment, as well as new computer equipment.

2. 中文翻译

过去，把图纸、零件目录以及维修和大修手册记录在缩微胶片上的做法曾被广泛采用。缩微胶片有常规的 16 mm 或 35 mm 胶片规格。由于 35 mm 胶片尺寸较大，它对图纸的复制效果更好。缩微平片是一张卡片，其上的页面按网格格式排布。

缩微胶片和缩微平片都需要使用专门的设备来阅读和打印信息。大多数现代飞机制造商已用光盘、DVD 及其他数据存储设备等数字存储方式，取代了缩微胶片和缩微平片。大量老式飞机的使用和修理信息已转入数字存储设备。然而，可能仍然需要用老办法来获取信息。设备齐全的修理车间应当同时备有老式的缩微胶片和缩微平片设备以及新的计算机设备。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词复合结构作 of 宾语 + 被动语态

原句：The practice of recording drawings, parts catalogs, and maintenance and overhaul manuals on microfilms was utilized extensively in the past. 拆解：主语 The practice（of recording… 后置定语，of 的宾语是动名词 recording + 三个并列宾语）；谓语为被动 was utilized + 状语 extensively 与 in the past。 要点：the practice of doing 意为「做某事的做法」，of 后必须用动名词，不能接动词原形。长主语后面先找到 was utilized 这个被动谓语再回头拆修饰关系；record…on… 是「把……记录在……上」的搭配，介词 on 不可改为 in。

② with + 名词 + 过去分词

原句：Microfiche is a card with pages laid out in a grid format. 拆解：主语 Microfiche + 系动词 is + 表语 a card；with pages laid out in a grid format 为 with 复合结构作后置定语（with + 名词 pages + 过去分词 laid out），in a grid format 为方式状语。 要点：laid out 是过去分词，表示页面「被排布」，若换成 laying out 则主动、语义错误。这种 with 结构在技术英语里几乎是「附带的特征/状态」的标准表达，可译为「其上的页面按……排布」。注意 microfiche 与 microfilm 只差后缀，前者是平片、后者是卷片。

③ replace A with B + 现在分词作后置定语

原句：Most modern aircraft manufacturers have replaced microfilm and microfiche with digital storage methods utilizing CDs, DVDs, and other data storage devices. 拆解：主语 Most modern aircraft manufacturers + 现在完成时谓语 have replaced + 宾语 microfilm and microfiche + 状语 with digital storage methods（utilizing… 为现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 methods）。 要点：replace A with B 是「用 B 取代 A」，A、B 位置不可颠倒，是本句最易错的搭配。have replaced 用现在完成时，强调到目前已经完成的替换；utilizing 的逻辑主语是 methods，表「这些方式利用……」，不要误读成 manufacturers 的动作。

④ 兼有让步条件的 however + there be 存在句

原句：However, there may still be a need to access information using the old methods. 拆解：However 为转折副词，后接 there be 存在句 there may still be a need；to access information 为不定式作 need 的后置定语；using the old methods 为现在分词短语作方式状语。 要点：a need to do 是「做某事的需要」，need 后接不定式而非动名词；there may be 表示对现在情况的推测，与 there is 相比语气留有余地。using… 的逻辑主语是省略的泛指人称，不是 need。

⑤ both…as well as 的并列

原句：A well-equipped shop should have available, both the old microfilm and microfiche equipment, as well as new computer equipment. 拆解：主语 A well-equipped shop + 谓语 should have + 宾语补足语 available（前置，正常语序为 have both…available）+ 两个并列宾语 both the old microfilm and microfiche equipment 与 as well as new computer equipment。 要点：have sth available 意为「备有某物（可随时取用）」，available 作宾语补足语，位置可在宾语前也可在后。strictly 来说 both…as well as 属叠用（both…and 与 as well as 重复），但手册原文如此，翻译按「既……又……」处理；遇到这类不规范的并列，理解语义即可，不必模仿写作。
