Boundary Layer Control

1. 原文

One way of keeping the boundary layer air under control, or lessening its negative effect, is to make the wing’s surface as smooth as possible and to keep it free of dirt and debris.

As the friction between the air and the surface of the wing increases, the boundary layer thickens and becomes more turbulent and eventually a wing stall occurs. With a smooth and clean wing surface, the onset of a stall is delayed and the wing can operate at a higher angle of attack. One of the reasons ice forming on a wing can be such a serious problem is because of its effect on boundary layer air. On a high-speed airplane, even a few bugs splattered on the wing’s leading edge can negatively affect boundary layer air.

Other methods of controlling boundary layer air include wing leading edge slots, air suction through small holes on the wing’s upper surface, and the use of devices called vortex generators.

A wing leading edge slot is a duct that allows air to flow from the bottom of the wing, through the duct, to the top of the wing. As the air flows to the top of the wing, it is directed along the wing’s surface at a high velocity and helps keep the boundary layer from becoming turbulent and separating from the wing’s surface.

V ortex generators are used on airplanes that fly at high subsonic speed, where the velocity of the air on the top of the wing can reach Mach 1. As the air reaches Mach 1 velocity, a shock wave forms on the top of the wing, and the subsequent shock wave causes the air to separate from the wing’s upper surface. V ortex generators are short airfoils, arranged in pairs, located on the wing’s upper surface. They are positioned such that they pull high-energy air down into the boundary layer region and prevent airflow separation.

2. 中文翻译

使边界层气流得到控制、或者说减轻其负面影响的一种方法，是尽可能把机翼表面做得光滑，并保持其没有污物和碎屑。

随着空气与机翼表面之间的摩擦增大，边界层会增厚、变得更加湍流，最终发生机翼失速。机翼表面光滑洁净时，失速的发生被推迟，机翼可以在更大的迎角下工作。机翼上结冰之所以会成为如此严重的问题，原因之一就在于冰对边界层气流的影响。在高速飞机上，即使只有几只昆虫撞在机翼前缘上，也会对边界层气流产生不利影响。

控制边界层气流的其他方法包括：机翼前缘缝口（leading edge slots）、通过机翼上表面小孔进行的吸气（air suction），以及使用称为涡流发生器（vortex generators）的装置。

机翼前缘缝口是一条通道，允许空气从机翼下表面经该通道流到机翼上表面。当空气流到机翼上表面时，会以很高的速度沿机翼表面被引导，有助于使边界层不变成湍流、不从机翼表面分离。

涡流发生器用于以高亚声速飞行的飞机，这类飞机机翼上表面的气流速度可以达到 Mach 1。当气流达到 Mach 1 的速度时，机翼上表面会形成激波（shock wave），随后出现的激波使空气从机翼上表面分离。涡流发生器是成对布置在机翼上表面的短小翼型。它们的安装位置使其把高能气流向下引入边界层区域，从而防止气流分离。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词作介词宾语 + 不定式作表语

原句：One way of keeping the boundary layer air under control, or lessening its negative effect, is to make the wing’s surface as smooth as possible and to keep it free of dirt and debris.

拆解：主语 One way；of keeping... or lessening... 是「介词 of + 两个并列动名词短语」作后置定语，说明是什么方法；谓语 is；表语是两个并列不定式 to make... 和 to keep...，用 and 连接。

要点：way of doing sth（做某事的方法）中 of 后必须接动名词，不能写 way of do。两个不定式并列作表语时，第二个 to 不能省。as + 形容词 + as possible（尽可能……）中间只能用原级，不能写成 as smoother as possible；keep sth free of...（使某物不沾染……）是手册常用搭配。

② 定语从句省略关系词 + the reason is because of

原句：One of the reasons ice forming on a wing can be such a serious problem is because of its effect on boundary layer air.

拆解：主语 One of the reasons；ice forming on a wing can be such a serious problem 是省略了关系词（why／that）的定语从句，修饰 reasons；谓语 is；表语是 because of its effect on boundary layer air。ice forming 是「名词 + 现在分词」的复合结构。

要点：严格语法中 the reason is because... 属语义重复，正式写作宜用 the reason is that...；但手册的讲解性段落里并不少见，理解时按「原因在于……」处理即可。ice forming（正在结冰）不等于 ice is forming；such a + 形容词 + 名词表示「如此……的」。

③ 被动语态 + help (to) do + keep … from doing

原句：As the air flows to the top of the wing, it is directed along the wing’s surface at a high velocity and helps keep the boundary layer from becoming turbulent and separating from the wing’s surface.

拆解：As 引导时间状语从句；主句主语 it（指空气）后接两个并列谓语 is directed...（被动）与 helps keep...；helps 后接省略 to 的不定式 keep，keep 再带「宾语 + from + 动名词」：the boundary layer from becoming... and separating...。

要点：help 后接不定式时 to 可省略（help do／help to do），但第三人称仍是 helps keep，不能写成 helps keeps。keep sb/sth from doing sth（使……不发生）里的 from 不能省，省掉就变成「让……一直 doing」，意思正好相反。is directed along... 是省略施动者的被动语态，因为施动者（缝口这条通道）已在上一句点明；同段 arranged in pairs、located on the wing’s upper surface 也同属过去分词短语作后置定语，表示「被布置、被安装」。

④ such that 引导结果状语从句

原句：They are positioned such that they pull high-energy air down into the boundary layer region and prevent airflow separation.

拆解：They are positioned 是被动主句；such that 引导结果状语从句，从句主语 they（指涡流发生器），两个并列谓语 pull...down into... 与 prevent...。

要点：such that 在此相当于 so that，表「使得……」的结果，语气更正式，常与 be positioned／be designed／be arranged 等被动结构搭配。pull...down into... 中 down 是方向副词、into 引出目标区域；prevent airflow separation 也可写成 prevent airflow from separating。
