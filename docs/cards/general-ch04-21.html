Zone Numbers

通用 Ch 04 · Zone Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 132 页

1. 原文

Zone numbers on drawings are like the numbers and letters printed on the borders of a map. They help locate a point.

To find a point, mentally draw horizontal and vertical lines from the letters and numerals specified; the point where these lines intersect is the area sought. Figure 4-4F shows the zone numbers on a drawing.

Use the same method to locate parts, sections, and views on large drawings, particularly assembly drawings. Parts numbered in the title block can be located on the drawing by finding the numbers in squares along the lower border.

Zone numbers read from right to left.

2. 中文翻译

图纸上的分区编号（zone numbers）就像印在地图边框上的数字和字母一样，用来帮助确定某一点的位置。

要找出某一点，就按指定的字母和数字在脑中画出水平线和垂直线；两线的交点即为所要找的区域。图 4-4F 表示图纸上的分区编号。

用同样的方法可以在大幅图纸（尤其是装配图）上查找零件、剖视图和视图的位置。凡在标题栏中给出编号的零件，都可以沿图纸下边框在方格中找出相应编号，从而在图上定位。

分区编号自右向左读。

4. 语法解析

① Zone numbers on drawings are like the numbers and letters printed on the borders of a map（be like + 过去分词后置定语）

原句：Zone numbers on drawings are like the numbers and letters printed on the borders of a map.

拆解：主语 Zone numbers on drawings；系动词 are + 介词短语 like … 作表语；printed on the borders of a map 是过去分词短语后置修饰 the numbers and letters。

要点：be like 是「像、类似」，只作比较，不等于等同（若写 are the numbers 就变成「就是」了）。过去分词短语作定语含被动义（被印在边框上的），现在分词则是主动，注意 printed 与 printing 的区别。类比说明（like …）在手册中常用于把陌生概念挂到熟悉概念上。

② the point where these lines intersect is the area sought（where 定语从句 + 单个过去分词后置）

原句：To find a point, mentally draw horizontal and vertical lines from the letters and numerals specified; the point where these lines intersect is the area sought.

拆解：分号前是祈使句，To find a point 为目的状语，from the letters and numerals specified 中 specified 为过去分词后置定语；分号后主句主语 the point，where 引导定语从句修饰 point，表语 the area sought 中 sought 也是过去分词后置定语。

要点：where 引导的定语从句修饰表地点或抽象位置的名词，相当于 at which，此处不能换成 which。the area sought = the area that is sought，「所要找的区域」；单个过去分词后置代替定语从句是技术文本的简洁写法，同类还有 the value required、the view indicated。

③ Parts numbered in the title block can be located … by finding the numbers（情态被动 + by doing 表手段）

原句：Parts numbered in the title block can be located on the drawing by finding the numbers in squares along the lower border.

拆解：主语 Parts（带过去分词后置定语 numbered in the title block）；谓语 can be located 为「情态动词 + 被动」；by finding … 是方式状语（by + 动名词）；along the lower border 是后置定语修饰 squares。

要点：by + 动名词表手段、方法，译「通过……（来）」；注意与 with（用工具）、through（经由某个过程）区分。can be located by doing 是说明书里典型的「操作步骤」句型，可套用于 can be identified by、can be checked by。
