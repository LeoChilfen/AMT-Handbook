1. 原文

Section 65.91, Inspection Authorization

An A&P mechanic who has held their certificate for at least 3 years, and has been active for the last 2 years, may submit application using FAA Form 8610-1, Mechanic’s Application for Inspection Authorization, to the FAA for consideration as an IA. In addition to the preceding time requirements, the IA candidate must have:

• A fixed base of operation where they can be located in person or by phone during a normal working week but it need not be the place where they will exercise their inspection authority.

• Available equipment, facilities, and inspection data necessary to properly inspect the airframe, powerplants, propellers, or any related part or appliance they are approving for return to service.

The applicant who meets all the above criteria must then pass a written test (or computerized version of the test) to determine their ability to inspect the airworthiness of an aircraft following either a major repair or alteration action or the performance of an annual or progressive inspection. The minimum passing score for the computer test is 70 percent. If the applicant fails the test, retesting cannot be attempted until a minimum of 90 days have elapsed from the failure date. Unlike the A&P test, there is no reduction in this time if the applicant receives additional training.

Section 65.92, Inspection Authorization: Duration

An IA certificate expires on March 31 of each odd-numbered year, but may only be exercised during the time the technician holds a currently effective mechanic certificate. The IA ceases to be effective if:

• The technician surrenders it, or it is suspended or revoked.

• The technician no longer has a fixed base of operations.

• The technician no longer has the required facilities, equipment, or inspection data available.

Whenever the certificate is suspended or revoked, the technician must return it to the Administrator when requested by the FAA to do so.

Section 65.93, Inspection Authorization: Renewal

An IA certificate may be renewed in one of the following ways each year the technician is seeking renewal:

• The performance of at least one annual inspection for each 90 days the technician has held the IA rating.

• The performance of the inspections of at least two major repairs or alterations for each 90 days the technician has held the IA rating. ( Note: The inspections can be counted regardless of the approval or disapproval of the work.)

• The performance (or supervision) and approval of at least one progressive inspection.

• The attendance and successful completion of a refresher course (acceptable to the Administrator) that is at least 8 hours of instruction. This can be either a single day seminar or a combination of individual classes acceptable to the Administrator. Some seminars are sponsored by the FAA FSDO and are free; others are low cost. Private industry also frequently conducts one-day sessions and usually charge for their efforts. Regardless of who is conducting the seminar, it is usually an excellent way to accomplish renewal, learn about new issues, and develop a network among peers.

• Passed an oral test by an FAA inspector to determine that the applicant's knowledge of applicable regulations and standards is current.

Because all IA certificates expire in the first quarter of each calendar year (March 31), and the regulation states that anyone holding an IA for less than 90 days need not meet the preceding renewal requirements, no renewal is required for someone who received the IA during the first quarter of the calendar year.

The technician with IA should note that regulations clearly state the number of annual inspections (four) and major repair or alteration inspections (eight) are required for each 90-day period prior to March 31st. This does not mean in each previous 90-day period the technician must have conducted either an annual or two major repair or alteration inspections, but rather their cumulative number by March 31st. Therefore, an IA could actually go 11 months without performing any inspection activity relative to renewal. Then in March, they could conduct all four necessary annual inspections, or all eight 337-related inspections. However, the regulations do not provide for the mixing of any of these renewal activities (i.e., two annual inspections and four Major Repair and Alteration forms).

Another method of renewal is to meet with the FAA-assigned FSDO inspector who will determine that the applicant possesses current knowledge of the applicable regulations and standards. Although this is often considered the renewal method of last resort, it should not be considered a negative experience. If the IA has been performing their activities in a professional manner throughout the year, this session can be considered a professional follow-up or consultation. Proper IA-to-FSDO inspector interaction can be enhanced with such a meeting.

Section 65.95, Inspection Authorization: Privileges and Limitations

The IA may perform an annual inspection or perform or supervise a progressive inspection. They may also approve for return to service any aircraft-related part or appliance that has undergone a major repair or alteration (except aircraft maintained in accordance with a continuous airworthiness program operated under 14 CFR part 121).

The IA must keep their certificate available for inspection by any one of the following persons:

• Authorized representative of the NTSB

• Any federal, state, local, or law enforcement officer If the holder of an IA moves their fixed base of operation, they must notify in writing the FSDO responsible for the location they are moving to before beginning to exercise the privileges of an IA. Although it is not required, good business etiquette and professional responsibility would suggest that a similar letter be written to the responsible FAA Principal Maintenance Inspector (PMI) at the FSDO in the area they are leaving.

2. 中文翻译

第 65.91 条 检验授权

持有 A&P 合格证满 3 年、且在最近 2 年内一直处于从业状态的维修人员，可使用 FAA 表格 8610-1《维修人员检验授权申请书》向 FAA 提出申请，作为 IA 人选接受审议。除上述时间要求外，IA 候选人还必须具备：

• 一个固定作业基地，在正常工作周内能以本人到场或电话方式联系到，但该地点不必是其行使检验权限的地点。

• 为正确检验其拟批准放行的机体、动力装置、螺旋桨或任何相关零件或附件所必需的可用设备、设施和检验数据。

符合上述全部条件的申请人随后必须通过一次笔试（或该考试的计算机化版本），以测定其在完成大修或改装之后、或在实施年度检查或渐进式检查之后检验航空器适航性的能力。计算机考试的最低及格分数为 70 分。如申请人考试不合格，则自不合格之日起至少满 90 天方可重考。与 A&P 考试不同，即使申请人接受补充培训，这一时限也不缩短。

第 65.92 条 检验授权：有效期

IA 合格证于每个奇数年的 3 月 31 日到期，但只有在技术人员持有当前有效的维修人员合格证期间方可行使。出现下列情形时，IA 即不再有效：

• 技术人员将其交回，或该证被暂停或吊销。

• 技术人员不再拥有固定作业基地。

• 技术人员不再拥有所需的设施、设备或检验数据。

无论何时合格证被暂停或吊销，技术人员都必须在 FAA 要求时将其交回局长。

第 65.93 条 检验授权：续期

IA 合格证可在技术人员寻求续期的每一年，按下列方式之一续期：

• 在技术人员持有 IA 等级的每 90 天内，至少完成一次年度检查。

• 在技术人员持有 IA 等级的每 90 天内，至少完成两次大修或重大改装的检查。（注：无论该工作被批准与否，这些检查均可计入。）

• 实施（或监督）并批准至少一次渐进式检查。

• 参加并成功完成不少于 8 学时授课的复训课程（须为局长认可者）。这可以是单日研讨会，也可以是局长认可的若干单独课程的组合。有些研讨会由 FAA 的 FSDO 主办且免费；另一些收费较低。私营行业也经常举办单日课程，通常要收取费用。无论由谁主办研讨会，这通常都是完成续期、了解新问题并在同行中建立人脉的极好途径。

• 通过 FAA 监察员的口试，以确认申请人对适用法规和标准的了解是当前的。

由于所有 IA 合格证都在每个日历年的第一季度（3 月 31 日）到期，且法规规定持有 IA 不满 90 天者无需满足上述续期要求，因此在日历年的第一季度取得 IA 的人员无需续期。

持有 IA 的技术人员应注意，法规明确规定，在 3 月 31 日之前的每 90 天期间内，所需完成的年度检查（四次）和大修或改装检查（八次）的数量。这并不是说在之前每一个 90 天期间内技术人员都必须完成一次年度检查或两次大修或改装检查，而是指截至 3 月 31 日的累计数量。因此，IA 实际上可以连续 11 个月不进行任何与续期有关的检查活动，然后在 3 月一次性完成全部四次所需的年度检查，或全部八次与 337 表格有关的检查。但是，法规不允许将这些续期活动任意混合（即两次年度检查加四份大修与重大改装表格）。

另一种续期方式是面见 FAA 指派的 FSDO 监察员，由其确认申请人具备对适用法规和标准的当前知识。尽管这常被视为最后的续期手段，但不应把它看成一桩负面经历。如果 IA 全年都以专业方式开展其活动，这次会面可视为一次专业的跟踪回访或咨询。IA 与 FSDO 监察员之间的良好互动，可通过这样的会面得到增进。

第 65.95 条 检验授权：权限与限制

IA 可实施年度检查，或实施、监督渐进式检查。他们还可批准经过大修或改装的任何与航空器相关的零件或附件放行（但按 14 CFR 第 121 部运行的持续适航方案维护的航空器除外）。

IA 必须将其合格证保持在可供下列任一人员检查的状态：

• NTSB 的授权代表

• 任何联邦、州、地方或执法人员 如果 IA 持有人迁移其固定作业基地，则必须在开始行使 IA 权限之前，以书面形式通知负责其迁入地点的 FSDO。尽管并无此要求，但良好的商业礼仪和职业责任感应提示：也应向迁出地 FSDO 的负责 FAA 主管维修监察员（PMI）写一封类似的信。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + 现在分词短语作方式状语

原句：An A&P mechanic who has held their certificate for at least 3 years, and has been active for the last 2 years, may submit application using FAA Form 8610-1, Mechanic’s Application for Inspection Authorization, to the FAA for consideration as an IA. 拆解：主语 An A&P mechanic + who has held... and has been active... 定语从句（两个并列的现在完成时谓语，共用一个 who）；主句谓语 may submit application；using FAA Form 8610-1 为现在分词短语作方式状语；to the FAA 为间接宾语方向，for consideration as an IA 表目的。 要点：using 的逻辑主语是主句主语 mechanic，不是 application，故不能理解为「申请使用表格」。介词短语 to the FAA 与 for consideration 分别表递交对象与目的，两者位置调换会改变语义。并列完成时中第二个 has 不能省，因为主语相同但时态短语各自独立。

② until 引导时间状语从句 + 被动语态

原句：If the applicant fails the test, retesting cannot be attempted until a minimum of 90 days have elapsed from the failure date. 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句；主句 retesting cannot be attempted（情态 + 被动，主语为动名词 retesting）；until 引导时间状语从句，从句主语 a minimum of 90 days，谓语 have elapsed（现在完成时），from the failure date 为时间起点。 要点：not...until 是「直到……才」的固定搭配，主句否定 + until 从句肯定。a minimum of 90 days 作主语时谓语用复数 have，中心词是 days 而非 minimum。条件从句用一般现在时替代将来时，符合主将从现。

③ unlike 介词短语作状语 + there be 存在句

原句：Unlike the A&P test, there is no reduction in this time if the applicant receives additional training. 拆解：Unlike the A&P test 为介词短语作状语，表对比；主句为 there be 存在句 there is no reduction in this time；if the applicant receives additional training 为条件状语从句。 要点：unlike 是介词，后接名词或名词短语，不能接句子（接句子要用 whereas/while）；翻译成「与……不同」而非「不像」。there be 句型的真正主语是 reduction，故用 is；in this time 指前句提到的 90 天等待期。

④ but 并列两个谓语 + during the time 引导定语从句

原句：An IA certificate expires on March 31 of each odd-numbered year, but may only be exercised during the time the technician holds a currently effective mechanic certificate. 拆解：主语 An IA certificate 带两个并列谓语：expires on March 31 of each odd-numbered year 与 may only be exercised during the time...；during the time 后为省略关系副词 when 的定语从句，the technician holds a currently effective mechanic certificate。 要点：第二个分句承前省略主语，但保留了情态动词 may，说明这是「可以行使」的权限条件。the time 后的定语从句省略了 when，因为先行词是表时间的名词且在从句中作时间状语。only 位置贴近被修饰的谓语，译文应把「只有……才」体现出来。

⑤ although 让步从句 + 被动语态

原句：Although this is often considered the renewal method of last resort, it should not be considered a negative experience. 拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句，this is often considered + 主语补足语 the renewal method of last resort；主句 it should not be considered a negative experience（情态 + 被动 + 否定）。 要点：consider 的被动式后直接跟名词作主语补足语，不加 as（加 as 亦可但本句没有）。the method of last resort 意为「最后手段」，of last resort 是固定搭配。译文宜用「不应被看成」，与前半句的「常被视为」形成对照。
