1. 原文

Aeronautical publications are the sources of information for guiding aviation mechanics in the operation and maintenance of aircraft and related equipment. The proper use of these publications greatly aid in the efficient operation and maintenance of all aircraft. These include manufacturers’ SBs, manuals, and catalogs; FAA regulations; ADs; advisory circulars (ACs); and aircraft, engine, and propeller specifications.

Manufacturers’ Service Bulletins/Instructions

Service bulletins or service instructions are two of several types of publications issued by airframe, engine, and component manufacturers. The bulletins may include: purpose for issuing the publication; name of the applicable airframe, engine, or component; detailed instructions for service, adjustment, modification or inspection, and source of parts, if required; and estimated number of man-hours required to accomplish the job.

Maintenance Manual

The manufacturer’s aircraft maintenance manual contains complete instructions for maintenance of all systems and components installed in the aircraft. It contains information for the mechanic who normally works on components, assemblies, and systems while they are installed in the aircraft, but not for the overhaul mechanic. A typical aircraft maintenance manual contains:

• A description of the systems (i.e., electrical, hydraulic, fuel, control)

• Lubrication instructions setting forth the frequency and the lubricants and fluids that are to be used in the various systems

• Pressures and electrical loads applicable to the various systems

• Tolerances and adjustments necessary to proper functioning of the airplane

• Methods of leveling, raising, and towing

• Methods of balancing control surfaces

• Identification of primary and secondary structures

• Frequency and extent of inspections necessary to the proper operation of the airplane

• Special repair methods applicable to the airplane

• Special inspection techniques requiring x-ray, ultrasonic, or magnetic particle inspection

• A list of special tools

The manufacturer’s overhaul manual contains brief descriptive information and detailed step-by-step instructions covering work normally performed on a unit that has been removed from the aircraft. Simple, inexpensive items, such as switches and relays where overhaul is uneconomical, are not covered in the overhaul manual.

Structural Repair Manual

The structural repair manual contains the manufacturer’s information and specific instructions for repairing primary and secondary structures. Typical skin, frame, rib, and stringer repairs are covered in this manual. Also, included are material and fastener substitutions and special repair techniques.

Illustrated Parts Catalog

The illustrated parts catalog presents component breakdowns of structure and equipment in disassembly sequence. Also, included are exploded views or cutaway illustrations for all parts and equipment manufactured by the aircraft manufacturer.

Wiring Diagram Manual

The wiring diagram manual is a collection of diagrams, drawings, and lists that define the wiring and hook up of associated equipment installed on airplanes. The data is organized in accordance with the Air Transport Association ATA iSPec 2200 specification.

Code of Federal Regulations (CFRs)

) The Code of Federal Regulations (CFRs) were established by law to provide for the safe and orderly conduct of flight operations and to prescribe airmen privileges and limitations. A knowledge of the CFRs is necessary during the performance of maintenance, since all work done on aircraft must comply with CFR provisions.

Airworthiness Directives (ADs)

A primary safety function of the FAA is to require correction of unsafe conditions found in an aircraft, aircraft engine, propeller, or appliance when such conditions exist and are likely to exist or develop in other products of the same design. The unsafe condition may exist because of a design defect, maintenance, or other causes. Title 14 of the CFR part 39, Airworthiness Directives, defines the authority and responsibility of the administrator for requiring the necessary corrective action. The ADs are published to notify aircraft owners and other interested persons of unsafe conditions and to prescribe the conditions that the product may continue to be operated. Furthermore, these are federal aviation regulations and must be complied with unless specific exemption is granted.

There are two categories of ADs: 1. Those of an emergency nature requiring immediate compliance upon receipt.

  1. Those of a less urgent nature requiring compliance within a relatively longer period of time. Also, ADs may be a one-time compliance item or a recurring item that requires future inspection on an hourly basis (accrued flight time since last compliance) or a calendar time basis.

The contents of ADs include the aircraft, engine, propeller, or appliance model and serial numbers affected. Also, included are the compliance time or period, a description of the difficulty experienced, and the necessary corrective action.

Type Certificate Data Sheets (TCDS)

The type certificate data sheet (TCDS) describes the type design and sets forth the limitations prescribed by the applicable CFR part. It also includes any other limitations and information found necessary for type certification of a particular model aircraft.

All TCDS are numbered in the upper right corner of each page. This number is the same as the type certificate number. The name of the type certificate holder, together with all of the approved models, appears immediately below the type certificate number. The issue date completes this group. This information is contained within a bordered text box to set it off.

The TCDS is separated into one or more sections. Each section is identified by a Roman numeral followed by the model designation of the aircraft that the section pertains. The category or categories that the aircraft can be certificated in are shown in parentheses following the model number. Also, included is the approval date shown on the type certificate. The data sheet contains information regarding:

  1. Model designation of all engines that the aircraft manufacturer obtained approval for use with this model aircraft.
  1. Minimum fuel grade to be used.
  1. Maximum continuous and takeoff ratings of the approved engines, including manifold pressure (when used), rotations per minute (rpm), and horsepower (hp).
  1. Name of the manufacturer and model designation for each propeller that the aircraft manufacturer obtained approval is shown together with the propeller limits and any operating restrictions peculiar to the propeller or propeller engine combination.
  1. Airspeed limits in both miles per hour (mph) and knots.
  1. Center of gravity (CG) range for the extreme loading conditions of the aircraft is given in inches from the datum. The range may also be stated in percent of mean aerodynamic chord (%MAC) for transport category aircraft.
  1. Empty weight center of gravity (EWCG) range (when established) is given as fore and aft limits in inches from the datum. If no range exists, the word “none” is shown following the heading on the data sheet.
  1. Location of the datum.
  1. Means provided for leveling the aircraft.
  1. All pertinent maximum weights.
  1. Number of seats and their moment arms.
  1. Oil and fuel capacity.
  1. Control surface movements.
  1. Required equipment.
  1. Additional or special equipment found necessary for certification.
  1. Information concerning required placards.

It is not within the scope of this handbook to list all the items that can be shown on the TCDS. Those items listed above serve only to acquaint aviation mechanics with the type of information generally included on the data sheets. TCDS may be many pages in length.

When conducting a required or routine inspection, it is necessary to ensure that the aircraft and all the major items on it are as defined in the TCDS. The inspector ensures that all installed aircraft equipment conforms to the TCDS. This is called a conformity check and verifies that the aircraft conforms to the specifications of the aircraft as it was originally certified. Sometimes alterations are made that are not specified or authorized in the TCDS. When that condition exists, a supplemental type certificate (STC) is issued. STCs are considered a part of the permanent records of an aircraft and should be maintained as part of that aircraft’s logs.

2. 中文翻译

航空出版物是指导航空维修人员开展飞机及相关设备运行与维修工作的信息来源。正确使用这些出版物，对高效地运行和维修所有飞机大有帮助。这些出版物包括制造商的 SB（服务通告）、手册和目录；FAA 法规；AD（适航指令）；咨询通告（AC）；以及飞机、发动机和螺旋桨规范。

制造商服务通告/说明

服务通告或服务说明，是机体、发动机和部件制造商所发布的若干类出版物中的两类。这类通告可能包括：发布该出版物的目的；适用的机体、发动机或部件名称；关于维护、调整、改装或检查的详细说明，以及所需零件的来源（如需要）；完成该项工作所需的估计工时。

维修手册

制造商的飞机维修手册包含对飞机上所装全部系统和部件进行维修的完整说明。它提供的信息面向的是通常在部件、组件和系统仍安装在飞机上时对其进行作业的维修人员，而不是面向翻修人员。一份典型的飞机维修手册包含以下内容：

• 各系统的说明（即电气、液压、燃油、操纵系统）

• 润滑说明，规定各系统所用润滑剂和液体的使用频次与种类

• 适用于各系统的压力和电负载

• 飞机正常工作所必需的容差与调整

• 调平、顶升和牵引的方法

• 操纵面平衡的方法

• 主结构和次结构的识别

• 飞机正常使用所必需的检查频次和范围

• 适用于该飞机的特殊修理方法

• 需要 X 射线、超声或磁粉检测的特殊检查技术

• 专用工具清单

制造商的翻修手册包含简明的描述性信息和详细的逐步说明，涵盖对已从飞机上拆下的部件（单元）通常进行的作业。对于翻修不经济的简单廉价件，如开关和继电器，翻修手册不予收录。

结构修理手册

结构修理手册包含制造商提供的、用于修理主结构和次结构的信息与具体说明。本手册涵盖典型的蒙皮、框架、翼肋和桁条修理。此外还包括材料与紧固件的替换以及特殊修理技术。

图解零件目录

图解零件目录按分解顺序给出结构和设备的部件分解。此外，还收录飞机制造商所制造的全部零件和设备的分解图或剖视图。

线路图手册

线路图手册汇集了说明飞机上所装相关设备布线与接线的图、图样和清单。其数据按美国航空运输协会 ATA iSPec 2200 规范编排。

《联邦法规汇编》（CFRs）

《联邦法规汇编》（CFRs）由法律规定设立，用以保障飞行运行安全有序地进行，并规定飞行人员的权利与限制。在实施维修时必须了解 CFR，因为对飞机所做的所有工作都必须符合 CFR 的规定。

适航指令（ADs）

FAA 的一项主要安全职能，是当飞机、飞机发动机、螺旋桨或机载设备中存在不安全状况、且这类状况很可能在同一设计的其他产品中存在或出现时，要求对这些不安全状况予以纠正。不安全状况可能由设计缺陷、维修或其他原因造成。《联邦法规汇编》第 14 篇第 39 部《适航指令》规定了局长要求采取必要纠正措施的权力与责任。发布 AD 是为了将不安全状况通知飞机所有人和有关方面，并规定该产品可以继续运行的条件。此外，AD 属于联邦航空法规，除获得专门豁免外必须遵守。

AD 分为两类：1. 紧急性质的，收到后须立即符合。

  1. 紧急程度较低的，须在相对较长的时间内符合。此外，AD 可能是一次性符合项目，也可能是重复性项目，要求今后按小时基准（自上次符合以来累计的飞行时间）或日历时间基准进行检查。

AD 的内容包括受影响的飞机、发动机、螺旋桨或机载设备的型号和序号。此外还包括符合时间或期限、对所经历问题的描述，以及必要的纠正措施。

型号合格证数据表（TCDS）

型号合格证数据表（TCDS）描述型号设计，并载明适用 CFR 部所规定的限制。它还包括对某一具体型号飞机进行型号合格审定时认为必要的其他限制和信息。

所有 TCDS 都在每页右上角编号。该编号与型号合格证编号相同。型号合格证持有人的名称连同所有经批准的型号，紧接在型号合格证编号下方。这一组信息以签发日期收尾。这些信息置于带边框的文本框内，以突出显示。

TCDS 分为一节或多节。每一节以一个罗马数字标识，其后是该节所涉飞机的型号名称。该飞机可取得审定所属的类别，以括号形式列在型号编号之后。此外还包括型号合格证上所示的批准日期。该数据表包含以下方面的信息：

  1. 飞机制造商取得批准、可用于该型号飞机的所有发动机的型号名称。
  1. 应使用的最低燃油等级。
  1. 经批准发动机的最大连续功率和起飞功率额定值，包括进气压力（如使用）、每分钟转数（rpm）和马力（hp）。
  1. 对飞机制造商已取得批准的每一副螺旋桨，给出制造商名称和型号名称，并同时给出螺旋桨限制以及该螺旋桨或螺旋桨—发动机组合所特有的任何使用限制。
  1. 以英里/小时（mph）和节两种单位表示的空速限制。
  1. 飞机极限装载条件下的重心（CG）范围，以距基准的英寸数给出。对运输类飞机，该范围也可以平均气动弦长的百分比（%MAC）表示。
  1. 空重重心（EWCG）范围（在已确定时）以距基准的前后限（英寸）给出。如果没有范围，则在数据表该标题之后标出 “none” 一词。
  1. 基准的位置。
  1. 为飞机调平所设置的手段。
  1. 所有相关的最大重量。
  1. 座椅数量及其力臂。
  1. 滑油和燃油容量。
  1. 操纵面活动量。
  1. 必备设备。
  1. 审定中认为必要的附加或特殊设备。
  1. 有关必备标牌的信息。

本手册的范围不包括列出 TCDS 上可能出现的所有项目。上面列出的项目只是为了让航空维修人员了解数据表上通常包含的信息类型。TCDS 的篇幅可能有很多页。

在进行规定检查或例行检查时，必须确认飞机及其上的所有主要项目都符合 TCDS 的规定。检查员要确保所有装机设备都符合 TCDS。这称为符合性检查，用以核实飞机符合其最初取得审定时的规范。有时会进行未在 TCDS 中规定或未获授权的改装。出现这种情况时，应颁发补充型号合格证（STC）。STC 被视为飞机永久记录的一部分，应作为该飞机履历的一部分加以保存。

4. 语法解析

① 主谓一致（原文 aid 的形误）

原句：The proper use of these publications greatly aid in the efficient operation and maintenance of all aircraft. 拆解：主语中心词是 use（单数），of these publications 只是其后置定语，因此谓语应为 aids；原印刷文本写作 aid，属主谓不一致。aid in 后接动名词短语 the efficient operation and maintenance of all aircraft，greatly 修饰谓语。 要点：of 短语作定语时，谓语与 of 前的中心词一致，而不是与 of 后的复数名词一致，这是维修资料阅读与写作最常考的一致性问题；aid in doing = 有助于做某事，与 assist in 同义。

② or 并列主语 + 复数谓语 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：Service bulletins or service instructions are two of several types of publications issued by airframe, engine, and component manufacturers. 拆解：Service bulletins or service instructions 由 or 连接，指同一类事物的两种叫法，谓语用复数 are；two of several types of publications 为表语，issued by… 是过去分词短语后置修饰 publications，等于 which are issued by…。 要点：or 连接并列主语时谓语通常与最近的主语一致（此处两者均为复数，故用 are）；过去分词短语作后置定语时可与定语从句互换，是技术文献压缩句长的常用手段。

③ it 作形式主语 + 不定式真正主语

原句：It is not within the scope of this handbook to list all the items that can be shown on the TCDS. 拆解：it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式短语 to list all the items…；is not within the scope of this handbook 为系表结构；that can be shown on the TCDS 是修饰 items 的定语从句，情态动词 can 表可能性。 要点：within the scope of 意为「在……范围之内」，否定式 not within the scope of 常用来声明「不属于本手册讨论范围」；形式主语句把长不定式后移，避免头重脚轻。

④ 并列不定式作目的状语 + notify sb of sth

原句：The ADs are published to notify aircraft owners and other interested persons of unsafe conditions and to prescribe the conditions that the product may continue to be operated. 拆解：主句为 are published 被动结构；两个并列的不定式 to notify… 和 to prescribe… 作目的状语；notify 的搭配是 notify sb of sth（通知某人某事）；that the product may continue to be operated 是修饰 conditions 的同位/定语从句，从句为被动语态。 要点：notify 后不能直接跟双宾语写成 notify sb sth，必须用 notify sb of sth 或 notify sb that…；两个不定式并列时第二个 to 不能省略（to notify… and to prescribe…），否则会被误读为与前面的并列成分共用。

⑤ 现在分词作定语 + 过去分词 + that 定语从句

原句：The manufacturer’s overhaul manual contains brief descriptive information and detailed step-by-step instructions covering work normally performed on a unit that has been removed from the aircraft. 拆解：谓语 contains 带两个并列宾语 information 和 instructions；covering work… 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 instructions；performed on a unit 为过去分词短语修饰 work；that has been removed from the aircraft 为定语从句修饰 unit。 要点：covering 的逻辑主语是 instructions（说明覆盖哪些内容），用现在分词；performed 与 work 是被动关系，用过去分词——同一句中两种分词并存，判断依据是逻辑主谓关系而非位置；step-by-step 复合形容词作前置定语。

⑥ 并列谓语 + as 引导的方式状语从句

原句：This is called a conformity check and verifies that the aircraft conforms to the specifications of the aircraft as it was originally certified. 拆解：This is called a conformity check 为被动结构，与 verifies that… 共用主语 This，构成并列谓语；that 从句中 conforms to 是「符合」的固定搭配；as it was originally certified 为 as 引导的方式/比较状语从句，说明「按其最初取得审定时的（规范）」。 要点：is called 与 verifies 并列时，时态与主语要一致；conform to 是「符合（规范、标准）」，不要与 comply with 混用但二者语义接近；as 从句在此表「按照……的样子」，不是原因状语。
