Matter

通用 Ch 05 · Matter · 原文 PDF 第 151 页

1. 原文

Matter is the foundation, or the building blocks, for any discussion of physics. According to the dictionary, matter is what all things are made of; whatever occupies space, has mass, and is perceptible to the senses in some way. According to the Law of Conservation, matter cannot be created or destroyed, although it is possible to change its physical state.

When liquid gasoline vaporizes and mixes with air, and then burns, it might seem that this piece of matter has disappeared and no longer exists. Although it no longer exists in the state of liquid gasoline, the matter still exists in the form of the gases given off by the burning fuel.

2. 中文翻译

物质（matter）是讨论任何物理学问题的基石，或者说是其「积木」。按词典的解释，物质是一切事物由之构成的东西；凡是占有空间、具有质量、并能以某种方式被感官察觉的东西，都是物质。根据守恒定律（Law of Conservation），物质既不能被创造，也不能被消灭，尽管可以改变它的物理状态。

当液态汽油汽化并与空气混合、随后燃烧时，看上去这部分物质似乎已经消失、不再存在了。虽然它不再以液态汽油的状态存在，但物质仍以燃烧的燃料所释放出的气体形式继续存在。

4. 语法解析

① what 引导的名词性从句作表语

原句：According to the dictionary, matter is what all things are made of; whatever occupies space, has mass, and is perceptible to the senses in some way.

拆解：分号前 matter is what all things are made of，what 从句作表语，be made of 的介词 of 留在句尾；分号后 whatever occupies space, has mass, and is perceptible to the senses in some way 是 whatever 引导的名词性从句，原文省略了主句（可理解为 matter is），用来补充「物质」的判定条件。

要点：what = the thing(s) that，本身已含先行词，不能再写成 matter is the thing what…。be made of 指「由……制成（看得出材料）」，与 be made from（看不出原料）相对。whatever 相当于 anything that，语气比 what 强，常用来下定义。

② 让步状语从句：although it is possible to change…

原句：According to the Law of Conservation, matter cannot be created or destroyed, although it is possible to change its physical state.

拆解：主句 matter cannot be created or destroyed 为情态被动，or 连接两个过去分词；although 引导让步状语从句，从句中 it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to change its physical state。

要点：although 与 but 不能同时使用。英文用 cannot be created or destroyed 这样的被动并列，译成中文常转成主动的「既不能创造也不能消灭」。it is possible to do 是技术文本的高频句型，比 ... can be done 更强调「可能性」。

③ 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：Although it no longer exists in the state of liquid gasoline, the matter still exists in the form of the gases given off by the burning fuel.

拆解：Although 引导让步从句；主句 the matter still exists in the form of the gases；given off by the burning fuel 是过去分词短语后置修饰 gases，相当于 which are given off by the burning fuel。

要点：分词短语作定语时省略了关系代词和 be 动词，是手册压缩句长的常用手段。given off 与 gases 是被动关系（气体被放出），所以用过去分词；对比 the burning fuel 中的 burning 是现在分词，表主动「正在燃烧的」。
