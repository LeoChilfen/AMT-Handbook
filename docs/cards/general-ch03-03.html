Subtraction of Whole Numbers

通用 Ch 03 · Subtraction of Whole Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 97 页

1. 原文

Subtraction is the process where the value of one number is taken from the value of another. The result is called the difference. When subtracting two whole numbers, such as 3,461 from 97,564, align them into columns according to place value and then subtract.

97,564 – 3,461 94,103 The difference of the two whole numbers is 94,103.

Multiplication of Whole Numbers Multiplication is the process of repeated addition. For example, 4 × 3 is the same as 4 + 4 + 4. The result is called the product.

Example: How many hydraulic system filters do you have if there are 35 cartons in the supply room and each carton contains 18 filters?

18 × 35 90 54 630 Therefore, there are 630 filters in the supply room.

2. 中文翻译

减法是这样一种过程：把一个数的值从另一个数的值中减去。结果称为差。对两个整数做减法（例如从 97,564 中减去 3,461）时，要按位值把它们对齐成列，然后再相减。

97,564 – 3,461　94,103　这两个整数的差是 94,103。

整数的乘法　乘法是重复相加的过程。例如，4 × 3 与 4 + 4 + 4 相同。结果称为积。

例：如果库房里有 35 个纸箱，每个纸箱装 18 个滤芯，那么共有多少个液压系统滤芯？

18 × 35　90　54　630　因此，库房中共有 630 个滤芯。

4. 语法解析

① where 引导的定语从句 + 被动语态：is taken from

原句：Subtraction is the process where the value of one number is taken from the value of another.

拆解：where 引导定语从句修饰 process（= in which）；从句主语 the value of one number，谓语 is taken from（take A from B 的被动），宾语 the value of another，其中 another 后省略了 number。

要点：与加法卡里的 is added to 对照记忆——加减法在英文里分别配 to 和 from：add A to B / take A from B。another 是「另一个（数）」，后面不能再加 number 以外的名词单数，也不要写成 an other。

② 比较结构：is the same as

原句：For example, 4 × 3 is the same as 4 + 4 + 4.

拆解：主语 4 × 3，系动词 is，表语 the same，as 引出比较对象 4 + 4 + 4；the same as 是固定搭配。

要点：the same as 后面接名词或从句，不能写成 the same like。若要说「与……完全相同」，可加 exactly：exactly the same as。技术文本里同类还有 equivalent to / equal to，但 equal to 后接的是数值，the same as 后接的是同类事物。

③ if 引导的条件状语从句 + each 作主语用单数

原句：Example: How many hydraulic system filters do you have if there are 35 cartons in the supply room and each carton contains 18 filters?

拆解：主句是特殊疑问句 How many … do you have；if 引导条件状语从句，从句内部又由 and 连接两个分句：there are 35 cartons …（there be 存在句）和 each carton contains 18 filters（each + 单数名词作主语，谓语用单数 contains）。

要点：each 作限定词时后面必须跟单数可数名词，谓语也用单数，这是本节最易错处；而 all / both 则接复数。题目问「共有多少」，所以答案用 there are 630 filters 而不是 has。
