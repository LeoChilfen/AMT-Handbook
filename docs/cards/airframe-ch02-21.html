1. 原文

Bell Stabilizer Bar System

Arthur M. Young discovered that stability could be increased significantly with the addition of a stabilizer bar perpendicular to the two blades. The stabilizer bar has weighted ends, which cause it to stay relatively stable in the plane of rotation. The stabilizer bar is linked with the swash plate in a manner that reduces the pitch rate. The two blades can flap as a unit and, therefore, do not require lag-lead hinges (the whole rotor slows down and accelerates per turn). Two-bladed systems require a single teetering hinge and two coning hinges to permit modest coning of the rotor disc as thrust is increased. The configuration is known under multiple names, including Hiller panels, Hiller system, Bell-Hiller system, and flybar system.

Offset Flapping Hinge

The offset flapping hinge is offset from the center of the rotor hub and can produce powerful moments useful for controlling the helicopter. The distance of the hinge from the hub (the offset) multiplied by the force produced at the hinge produces a moment at the hub. Obviously, the larger the offset, the greater the moment for the same force produced by the blade.

The flapping motion is the result of the constantly changing balance between lift, centrifugal, and inertial forces. This rising and falling of the blades is characteristic of most helicopters and has often been compared to the beating of a bird’s wing. The flapping hinge, together with the natural flexibility found in most blades, permits the blade to droop considerably when the helicopter is at rest and the rotor is not turning over. During flight, the necessary rigidity is provided by the powerful centrifugal force that results from the rotation of the blades. This force pulls outward from the tip, stiffening the blade, and is the only factor that keeps it from folding up.

Stability Augmentation Systems (SAS)

Some helicopters incorporate stability augmentation systems (SAS) to help stabilize the helicopter in flight and in a hover. The simplest of these systems is a force trim system, which uses a magnetic clutch and springs to hold the cyclic control in the position at which it was released. More advanced systems use electric actuators that make inputs to the hydraulic servos. These servos receive control commands from a computer that senses helicopter attitude. Other inputs, such as heading, speed, altitude, and navigation information may be supplied to the computer to form a complete autopilot system. The SAS may be overridden or disconnected by the pilot at any time. SAS reduces pilot workload by improving basic aircraft control harmony and decreasing disturbances. These systems are very useful when the pilot is required to perform other duties, such as sling loading and search and rescue operations.

Helicopter Vibration

The following paragraphs describe the various types of vibrations. Figure 2-50 shows the general levels into which frequencies are divided.

Extreme Low Frequency Vibration

Extreme low frequency vibration is pretty well limited to pylon rock. Pylon rocking (two to three cycles per second) is inherent with the rotor, mast, and transmission system. To keep the vibration from reaching noticeable levels, transmission mount dampening is incorporated to absorb the rocking.

Low Frequency Vibration

Low frequency vibrations (1/rev and 2/rev) are caused by the rotor itself. 1/rev vibrations are of two basic types: vertical or lateral. A 1/rev is caused simply by one blade developing more lift at a given point than the other blade develops at the same point.

Medium Frequency Vibration

Medium frequency vibration (4/rev and 6/rev) is another vibration inherent in most rotors. An increase in the level of these vibrations is caused by a change in the capability of the fuselage to absorb vibration, or a loose airframe component, such as the skids, vibrating at that frequency.

High Frequency Vibration

High frequency vibrations can be caused by anything in the helicopter that rotates or vibrates at extremely high speeds. A high frequency vibration typically occurs when the tail rotor gears, tail drive shaft or the tail rotor engine, fan or shaft assembly vibrates or rotates at an equal or greater speed than the tail rotor.

Rotor Blade Tracking

Blade tracking is the process of determining the positions of the tips of the rotor blade relative to each other while the rotor head is turning, and of determining the corrections necessary to hold these positions within certain tolerances. The blades should all track one another as closely as possible. The purpose of blade tracking is to bring the tips of all blades into the same tip path throughout their entire cycle of rotation. Various methods of blade tracking are explained below.

Flag & Pole

The flag and pole method, as shown in Figure 2-51, shows the relative positions of the rotor blades. The blade tips are marked with chalk or a grease pencil. Each blade tip should be marked with a different color so that it is easy to determine the relationship of the other tips of the rotor blades to each other. This method can be used on all types of helicopters that do not have jet propulsion at the blade tips. Refer to the applicable maintenance manual for specific procedures.

Electronic Blade Tracker

The most common electronic blade tracker consists of a Balancer/Phazor, Strobex tracker, and Vibrex tester. [Figures 2-52 through 2-54] The Strobex blade tracker permits blade tracking from inside or outside the helicopter while on the ground or inside the helicopter in flight. The system uses a highly concentrated light beam flashing in sequence with the rotation of the main rotor blades so that a fixed target at the blade tips appears to be stopped. Each blade is identified by an elongated retroreflective number taped or attached to the underside of the blade in a uniform location. When viewed at an angle from inside the helicopter, the taped numbers will appear normal. Tracking can be accomplished with tracking tip cap reflectors and a strobe light. The tip caps are temporarily attached to the tip of each blade. The high-intensity strobe light flashes in time with the rotating blades. The strobe light operates from the aircraft electrical power supply. By observing the reflected tip cap image, it is possible to view the track of the rotating blades. Tracking is accomplished in a sequence of four separate steps: ground tracking, hover verification, forward flight tracking, and autorotation rpm adjustment.

Tail Rotor Tracking

The marking and electronic methods of tail rotor tracking are explained in the following paragraphs.

Marking Method

Procedures for tail rotor tracking using the marking method, as shown in Figure 2-55 , are as follows:

• After replacement or installation of tail rotor hub, blades, or pitch change system, check tail rotor rigging and track tail rotor blades. Tail rotor tip clearance shall be set before tracking and checked again after tracking.

• The strobe-type tracking device may be used if available. Instructions for use are provided with the device. Attach a piece of soft rubber hose six inches long on the end of a ½ × ½ inch pine stick or other flexible device. Cover the rubber hose with Prussian blue or similar type of coloring thinned with oil.

Note: Ground run-up shall be performed by authorized personnel only. Start engine in accordance with applicable maintenance manual. Run engine with pedals in neutral position. Reset marking device on underside of tail boom assembly. Slowly move marking device into disc of tail rotor approximately one inch from tip. When near blade is marked, stop engine and allow rotor to stop. Repeat this procedure until tracking mark crosses over to the other blade, then extend pitch control link of unmarked blade one half turn.

Electronic Method

The electronic Vibrex balancing and tracking kit is housed in a carrying case and consists of a Model 177M-6A Balancer, a Model 135M-11 Strobex, track and balance charts, an accelerometer, cables, and attaching brackets.

The Vibrex balancing kit is used to measure and indicate the level of vibration induced by the main rotor and tail rotor of a helicopter. The Vibrex analyzes the vibration induced by out-of-track or out-of-balance rotors, and then by plotting vibration amplitude and clock angle on a chart the amount and location of rotor track or weight change is determined. In addition, the Vibrex is used in troubleshooting by measuring the vibration levels and frequencies or rpm of unknown disturbances.

Rotor Blade Preservation & Storage

Accomplish the following requirements for rotor blade preservation and storage:

• Condemn, demilitarize, and dispose of locally any blade which has incurred nonrepairable damage.

• Tape all holes in the blade, such as tree damage, or foreign object damage (FOD) to protect the interior of the blade from moisture and corrosion.

• Thoroughly remove foreign matter from the entire exterior surface of blade with mild soap and water.

• Protect blade outboard eroded surfaces with a light coating of corrosion preventive or primer coating.

• Protect blade main bolt hole bushing, drag brace retention bolt hole bushing, and any exposed bare metal (i.e., grip and drag pads) with a light coating of corrosion preventive.

• Secure blade to shock-mounted support and secure container lid.

• Place copy of manufacturer’s blade records, containing information required by Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) section 91.417(a)(2)(ii), and any other blade records in a waterproof bag and insert into container record tube.

• Obliterate old markings from the container that pertained to the original shipment or to the original item it contained. Annotate the blade model, part number (P/N) and serial number, as applicable, on the outside of the container.

2. 中文翻译

贝尔稳定杆系统

阿瑟·M·扬（Arthur M. Young）发现，在两片桨叶的垂直方向加装一根稳定杆，可以显著提高稳定性。稳定杆两端带有配重，使其在旋转平面内保持相对稳定。稳定杆与旋转斜盘相连，连接方式可降低桨距变化率。两片桨叶可作为一个整体挥舞，因此不需要摆振铰（整个旋翼每转一圈减速再加速）。双桨叶系统需要一个跷跷板铰和两个锥度铰，以便在拉力增大时允许桨盘适度锥化。这种构型有多个名称，包括 Hiller 舵面、Hiller 系统、Bell-Hiller 系统和飞杆（flybar）系统。

偏置挥舞铰

偏置挥舞铰偏离旋翼毂中心，可产生用于控制直升机的强大力矩。铰到旋翼毂的距离（偏置量）乘以铰处产生的力，即为旋翼毂处产生的力矩。显然，偏置量越大，在桨叶产生同样力的情况下力矩越大。

挥舞运动是升力、离心力和惯性力之间不断变化的平衡的结果。桨叶的这种上下起伏是多数直升机的特征，常被比作鸟翼的扇动。挥舞铰加上多数桨叶固有的自然柔性，使直升机停放、旋翼不转时桨叶可以明显下垂。飞行中，桨叶旋转产生的强大离心力提供了必要的刚度。该力从桨尖向外拉，使桨叶绷紧，是防止桨叶向上折起的唯一因素。

增稳系统（SAS）

有些直升机装有增稳系统（SAS），以帮助在飞行和悬停中保持稳定。这类系统中最简单的是力配平系统，它用电磁离合器和弹簧把周期变距操纵保持在松手时的位置。更先进的系统使用电动舵机，向液压伺服机构输入指令。这些伺服机构接收来自感知直升机姿态的计算机的控制指令。还可以向计算机提供航向、速度、高度和导航信息等其他输入，构成完整的自动驾驶仪系统。飞行员可随时超控或断开 SAS。SAS 通过改善飞机基本操纵协调性和减小扰动来减轻飞行员工作负荷。当飞行员需要执行其他任务（如吊挂载荷和搜索救援）时，这些系统非常有用。

直升机振动

以下各段介绍各种类型的振动。图 2-50 示出了频率划分的一般范围。

极低频振动

极低频振动基本仅限于旋翼塔架摇摆。塔架摇摆（每秒 2 至 3 个循环）是旋翼、旋翼轴和传动系统所固有的。为使振动不致达到可察觉的程度，采用减速器安装减振来吸收这种摇摆。

低频振动

低频振动（1/rev 和 2/rev）由旋翼本身引起。1/rev 振动有两种基本类型：垂直和横向。1/rev 振动仅仅是由于一片桨叶在某一点产生的升力大于另一片桨叶在同一点产生的升力。

中频振动

中频振动（4/rev 和 6/rev）是多数旋翼固有的另一种振动。这类振动水平的升高，是由于机身吸收振动的能力发生变化，或某个机体部件（如滑橇）松动并以该频率振动。

高频振动

高频振动可由直升机上任何以极高速度旋转或振动的部件引起。高频振动通常发生在尾桨齿轮、尾传动轴或尾桨发动机、风扇或轴组件以等于或高于尾桨的速度振动或旋转时。

旋翼桨叶轨迹检查

桨叶轨迹检查是在旋翼头转动时确定各旋翼桨叶桨尖彼此相对位置、并确定将其保持在一定公差范围内所需修正的过程。各桨叶应尽可能相互一致地运动。桨叶轨迹检查的目的是使所有桨叶的桨尖在整个旋转周期中都处于同一桨尖轨迹上。下面介绍各种桨叶轨迹检查方法。

旗杆法

旗杆法如图 2-51 所示，用来显示各旋翼桨叶的相对位置。桨尖用粉笔或油脂铅笔做标记。每片桨尖应使用不同颜色标记，以便容易判断旋翼桨叶各桨尖彼此的关系。该方法可用于所有桨尖不带喷气推进的直升机。具体程序参见适用的维修手册。

电子桨叶轨迹仪

最常见的电子桨叶轨迹仪由平衡器/Phazor、Strobex 轨迹仪和 Vibrex 测试仪组成。[图 2-52 至 2-54] Strobex 桨叶轨迹仪可在地面从直升机内或外、或在飞行中从直升机内进行桨叶轨迹检查。该系统使用一束高度集中的光，按主旋翼桨叶的旋转顺序闪亮，使桨尖处一个固定目标看上去静止不动。每片桨叶用一条细长的反光数字标识，数字带粘在或固定在桨叶下表面同一位置。从直升机内以一定角度观看时，粘上的数字看起来正常。可用桨尖帽反光片和频闪灯完成轨迹检查。桨尖帽临时装在每片桨叶的桨尖上。高强度频闪灯与旋转桨叶同步闪亮，由飞机电源供电。通过观察反射的桨尖帽影像，可以看到旋转桨叶的轨迹。轨迹检查按四个独立步骤依次进行：地面轨迹检查、悬停验证、前飞轨迹检查和自转转速调整。

尾桨轨迹检查

以下各段说明尾桨轨迹检查的标记法和电子法。

标记法

采用标记法进行尾桨轨迹检查的程序如图 2-55 所示，具体如下：

• 更换或安装尾桨毂、桨叶或变距系统后，检查尾桨校装并进行尾桨桨叶轨迹检查。尾桨桨尖间隙应在轨迹检查前设定，并在检查后再检查一次。

• 如有频闪式轨迹检查设备，可以使用，使用说明随设备提供。在一根 ½ × ½ 英寸松木棍或其他柔性装置端部装一段 6 英寸长的软橡胶管，并用油稀释的普鲁士蓝或类似着色剂涂覆橡胶管。

注：地面试车只能由授权人员进行。按适用维修手册启动发动机。在脚蹬处于中立位置的情况下运转发动机。将标记装置重新置于尾梁组件下表面。慢慢把标记装置移入尾桨桨盘，距桨尖约 1 英寸。当近侧桨叶被标记后，停车并让旋翼停止转动。重复此程序，直到轨迹标记转到另一片桨叶上，然后将未标记桨叶的变距操纵拉杆伸长半圈。

电子法

电子 Vibrex 平衡与轨迹检查套件装在一个便携箱内，包括一台 177M-6A 型平衡器、一台 135M-11 型 Strobex、轨迹与平衡图表、一个加速度计、电缆和安装支架。

Vibrex 平衡套件用于测量和指示直升机主旋翼和尾桨引起的振动水平。Vibrex 分析由轨迹不正或平衡不良的旋翼引起的振动，然后把振动幅值和时钟角标绘在图表上，据此确定旋翼轨迹或配重改变的量值和位置。此外，Vibrex 还通过测量未知扰动的振动水平、频率或转速，用于故障排查。

旋翼桨叶的防腐与存放

旋翼桨叶的防腐与存放应满足下列要求：

• 对任何发生不可修复损伤的桨叶，就地报废、作退役处理并处置。

• 用胶带封住桨叶上的所有孔洞，例如树木撞击损伤或外来物损伤（FOD）造成的孔洞，以保护桨叶内部不受潮、不腐蚀。

• 用中性肥皂和水彻底清除桨叶整个外表面的异物。

• 在桨叶外侧受侵蚀表面涂一薄层防腐剂或底漆加以保护。

• 在桨叶主螺栓孔衬套、阻力撑杆固定螺栓孔衬套以及任何裸露金属（即桨根夹和阻力垫）上涂一薄层防腐剂。

• 将桨叶固定在减振支架上，并盖好包装箱盖。

• 将制造商桨叶记录副本（含《联邦法规汇编》第 14 编（14 CFR）第 91.417(a)(2)(ii) 条要求的信息）及任何其他桨叶记录装入防水袋，插入箱内记录管。

• 清除箱体上与原发货或原装物品有关的旧标记。在箱体外部标注桨叶型号、件号（P/N）和序号（如适用）。

4. 语法解析

① that 引导的宾语从句 + 被动语态

原句：Arthur M. Young discovered that stability could be increased significantly with the addition of a stabilizer bar perpendicular to the two blades. 拆解：主句主语 Arthur M. Young，谓语 discovered，that 引导宾语从句；从句主语 stability，谓语 could be increased（带情态动词的被动语态），with the addition of... 表方式，perpendicular to the two blades 为形容词短语后置修饰 a stabilizer bar。 要点：宾语从句的主语是 stability，不是 Arthur M. Young——是“稳定性被提高”。perpendicular to 是“垂直于”，后置定语修饰 stabilizer bar，翻译时应提到名词前：“垂直于两片桨叶的稳定杆”。

② 过去分词作定语 + 主谓分割

原句：The distance of the hinge from the hub (the offset) multiplied by the force produced at the hinge produces a moment at the hub. 拆解：主语中心词 The distance，后接 of the hinge from the hub 与括号同位语 (the offset)；multiplied by the force produced at the hinge 为过去分词短语作后置定语（其中 produced at the hinge 又修饰 force），真正的谓语是 produces，宾语 a moment。 要点：multiplied 容易被误认作谓语，其实它是分词定语，谓语 produces 一直到 force 短语之后才出现。括号内的 the offset 是对 distance 的同位解释，读句时先跳过。

③ 主语后的插入成分 + 时间状语从句

原句：The flapping hinge, together with the natural flexibility found in most blades, permits the blade to droop considerably when the helicopter is at rest and the rotor is not turning over. 拆解：主语 The flapping hinge 后用逗号插入 together with... 短语（补充说明伴随因素），谓语 permits，宾语 the blade，宾补 to droop；when 引导时间状语从句，从句内由 and 并列两个分句。 要点：together with 引出的成分不改变主谓一致，谓语仍随单数主语用 permits。when 从句里 at rest（静止）与 not turning over（不转动）是同义复现，汉语合并成一句即可。

④ 现在分词作结果状语 + 定语从句

原句：This force pulls outward from the tip, stiffening the blade, and is the only factor that keeps it from folding up. 拆解：主语 This force 带两个并列谓语 pulls 与 is；stiffening the blade 为现在分词短语作结果状语；表语 the only factor 后接 that 引导的定语从句，keeps it from folding up 中 from 后接动名词。 要点：stiffening 的逻辑主语就是 this force，表示前一分句造成的自然结果，不是并列谓语。keep sth. from doing 意为“使……不能做”，句末 it 指 the blade。

⑤ of + 动名词的并列 + while 时间状语从句

原句：Blade tracking is the process of determining the positions of the tips of the rotor blade relative to each other while the rotor head is turning, and of determining the corrections necessary to hold these positions within certain tolerances. 拆解：主干为主系表 Blade tracking is the process；of determining... 与 and of determining... 两个介词短语并列，共同修饰 the process；第一个 of 短语内含 while 引导的时间状语从句；necessary to hold... 为形容词短语后置修饰 the corrections。 要点：and 连接的是两个 of 短语，不是两个分句，所以第二个 of 不能省略，否则结构失衡。relative to each other 意为“彼此相对”，修饰 positions；necessary 后置是因为它带有不定式短语。

⑥ 方式状语前置 + 被动句

原句：The Vibrex analyzes the vibration induced by out-of-track or out-of-balance rotors, and then by plotting vibration amplitude and clock angle on a chart the amount and location of rotor track or weight change is determined. 拆解：and 连接两个分句：前一分句主语 The Vibrex，谓语 analyzes，宾语 the vibration 后接过去分词短语 induced by... 作定语；后一分句把方式状语 by plotting... 提到句首，主句为被动结构 the amount and location... is determined。 要点：后一分句正常语序是 the amount and location... is determined by plotting...，作者把 by 短语前置以承接前文的 analyze。主语中心词是 the amount and location，谓语用 is 而非 are，因为两者被视为一体。
