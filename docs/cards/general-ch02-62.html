Making Maintenance Record Entries

通用 Ch 02 · Making Maintenance Record Entries · 原文 PDF 第 89 页

1. 原文

Title 14 of the CFR part 43, sections 43.9 and 43.11 require the technician to make appropriate entries of maintenance actions or inspection results in the aircraft maintenance record. How long those records must be kept is defined in 14 CFR part 91, section 91.417.

Whenever maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alteration work occurs on an aircraft, airframe, aircraft engine, propeller, appliance, or component part, a maintenance record entry must be created. The importance of compliance with this requirement cannot be overemphasized. Complete and organized maintenance logs for an aircraft can have significant (and usually positive) effect during the buy/ sell negotiations of an aircraft. On the other hand, poorly organized and incomplete logs can have a detrimental effect upon the selling price of an aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

《联邦法规汇编》第 14 编（14 CFR）第 43 部第 43.9 节和第 43.11 节要求维修技师在航空器维修记录中对所采取的维修措施或检查结果作出相应记录。这些记录必须保存多久，由 14 CFR 第 91 部第 91.417 节规定。

只要对航空器、机身、航空器发动机、螺旋桨、机载设备或零部件实施了维修、预防性维修、翻修重建或改装，就必须建立一条维修记录。遵守这一要求的重要性，再怎么强调也不为过。一份完整、有条理的航空器维修记录，可以在航空器买卖谈判中产生显著（且通常是正面的）影响；反之，记录杂乱无章、残缺不全，则会对航空器的售价产生不利影响。

4. 语法解析

① require sb. to do sth.

原句：Title 14 of the CFR part 43, sections 43.9 and 43.11 require the technician to make appropriate entries of maintenance actions or inspection results in the aircraft maintenance record.

拆解：主语是 sections 43.9 and 43.11 这个复数并列结构，谓语 require 用原形；require sb. to do sth. 为固定句型；make entries of … in … 意为「在……中作出……的记录」。

要点：主谓一致取决于真正作主语的 sections，而不是前面的 part 43，所以用 require 而非 requires。require 后接宾语 + 不定式；若改用从句，须是 require that … be made 的虚拟语气形式。

② Whenever 从句 + 情态被动

原句：Whenever maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alteration work occurs on an aircraft, airframe, aircraft engine, propeller, appliance, or component part, a maintenance record entry must be created.

拆解：Whenever 引导时间状语从句，从句主语是四个并列修饰语 + work，谓语 occurs；主句 a maintenance record entry must be created 为「情态动词 + be + 过去分词」。

要点：whenever 比 when 更强调「每次发生都如此」，规章常用它表示无例外的条件。主句用被动而把执行者省略，是因为条文约束的是「记录必须存在」这一结果，而不是某个人。

③ cannot be overemphasized 的强调句式

原句：The importance of compliance with this requirement cannot be overemphasized.

拆解：主语 The importance of compliance with this requirement；谓语 cannot be overemphasized 为情态否定被动；over- 前缀加在动词上表示「过度地」。

要点：cannot be overemphasized 字面是「不可能被过分强调」，实际意思是「再怎么强调也不为过」，不能译成「不能被强调」。同类固定说法还有 cannot be overstated、cannot be stressed enough。
