Fueling Hazards

通用 Ch 01 · Fueling Hazards · 原文 PDF 第 48 页

1. 原文

The volatility of aviation fuels creates a fire hazard that has plagued aviators and aviation engine designers since the beginning of powered flight. V olatility is the ability of a liquid to change into a gas at a relatively low temperature. In its liquid state, aviation fuel does not burn. It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state that the liquid fuel changes that is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard.

Static electricity is a byproduct of one substance rubbing against another. Fuel flowing through a fuel line causes a certain amount of static electricity. The greatest static electricity concern around aircraft is that during flight, the aircraft moving through the air causes static electricity to build in the airframe. If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling, the static electricity in the airframe attempts to return to the ground through the fuel line from the servicing unit. The spark caused by the static electricity can ignite any vaporized fuel.

Breathing the vapors from fuel can be harmful and must be limited. Any fuel spilled on the clothing or skin must be removed as soon as possible.

2. 中文翻译

航空燃油的挥发性所带来的火灾危险，自动力飞行诞生以来就一直困扰着飞行员和航空发动机设计者。挥发性是指液体在相对较低的温度下转变为气体的能力。航空燃油在液态下并不燃烧。因此，正是液体燃油所变成的油气（气态）不仅可用于驱动航空器，同时也构成火灾危险。

静电是一种物质与另一种物质摩擦所产生的副产物。燃油流经输油管路时会产生一定量的静电。航空器周围最值得关注的静电问题，是飞行中航空器穿过空气使机体上积聚静电。如果这些静电在加油前没有被释放掉，机体上的静电就会试图经由勤务设备的输油管路回流至地面。静电产生的电火花能够点燃任何已汽化的燃油。

吸入燃油油气有害健康，必须加以限制。任何溅到衣物或皮肤上的燃油都必须尽快清除。

4. 语法解析

① a fire hazard that has plagued … since …（that 定语从句 + since 完成时）

原句：The volatility of aviation fuels creates a fire hazard that has plagued aviators and aviation engine designers since the beginning of powered flight.

拆解：主语 The volatility of aviation fuels；谓语 creates；宾语 a fire hazard 后接 that 引导的定语从句；从句用现在完成时 has plagued，since the beginning of powered flight 为时间状语。

要点：since + 时间点要求主句用现在完成时，这是手册里叙述「长期问题」的常见写法。plague 在此是动词「困扰」，不是名词「瘟疫」。译成「自……以来一直困扰」最贴合。

② It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state … that is not only useful …（强调句）

原句：It is, therefore, the vapor or gaseous state that the liquid fuel changes that is not only useful in powering the aircraft, but also a fire hazard.

拆解：强调句型 It is … that …，被强调的是 the vapor or gaseous state；state 后第一个 that 引导定语从句（the liquid fuel changes that，相当于 changes into that state，介词 into 省略）；第二个 that 是强调句的连接词，其后 is not only useful … but also a fire hazard 为并列表语。

要点：一句里两个 that 功能不同，按「强调句 + 定语从句 + 强调句连接词」拆开读。not only … but also 连接 useful 与 a fire hazard 两个表语。changes 后省略 into 是原文写法，理解时要补上。

③ If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling …（条件句 + prior to）

原句：If that static electricity is not dissipated prior to refueling, the static electricity in the airframe attempts to return to the ground through the fuel line from the servicing unit.

拆解：If 引导条件状语从句，谓语为一般现在时被动 is not dissipated；主句用一般现在时 attempts to return；prior to refueling 为介词短语作时间状语；through the fuel line from the servicing unit 为路径状语。

要点：讲普遍规律时条件句是「主现从现」，而非「主将从现」。prior to 相当于 before，后接名词或 -ing，比 before 正式。dissipate 用被动，是因为动作由接地线完成，施动者不必点明。
