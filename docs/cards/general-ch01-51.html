Fueling Procedures

通用 Ch 01 · Fueling Procedures · 原文 PDF 第 48 页

1. 原文

The proper fueling of an aircraft is the responsibility of the owner/operator. This does not, however, relieve the person doing the fueling of the responsibility to use the correct type of fuel and safe fueling procedures. There are two basic procedures when fueling an aircraft. Smaller aircraft are fueled by the over-the-wing method. This method uses the fuel hose to fill through fueling ports on the top of the wing. The method used for larger aircraft is the single point fueling system. This type of fueling system uses receptacles in the bottom leading edge of the wing to fill all the tanks. This decreases the time it takes to refuel the aircraft, limits contamination, and reduces the chance of static electricity igniting the fuel. Most pressure fueling systems consist of a pressure fueling hose and a panel of controls and gauges that permit one person to fuel or defuel any or all fuel tanks of an aircraft. Each tank can be filled to a predetermined level. These procedures are illustrated in Figures 1-32 and 1-33.

Prior to fueling, the person fueling must check the following:

  1. Ensure all aircraft electrical systems and electronic devices, including radar, are turned off. 2. Do not carry anything in the shirt pockets. These items could fall into the fuel tanks.
  1. Ensure no flame-producing devices are carried by anyone engaged in the fueling operation. A moment of carelessness could cause an accident.
  1. Ensure that the proper type and grade of fuel is used. Do not mix A VGAS and JET fuel.
  1. Ensure that all the sumps have been drained.
  1. Wear eye protection. Although generally not as critical as eye protection, other forms of protection, such as rubber gloves and aprons, can also protect the skin from the effects of spilled or splashed fuel.

Ground wire 7. Do not fuel aircraft if there is danger of other aircraft in the vicinity blowing dirt in the direction of the aircraft being fueled. Blown dirt, dust, or other contaminants can enter an open fuel tank, contaminating the entire contents of the tank. 8. Do not fuel an aircraft when there is lightning within 5 miles.

  1. Do not fuel an aircraft within 500 feet of operating ground radar.

When using mobile fueling equipment:

  1. Approach the aircraft with caution, positioning the fuel truck so that if it is necessary to depart quickly, no backing needed.
  1. Set the hand brake of the fuel truck, and chock the wheels to prevent rolling.
  1. Ground the aircraft and then ground the truck. Next, ground or bond them together by running a connecting wire between the aircraft and the fuel truck. This may be done by three separate ground wires or by a “Y”

cable from the fuel truck.

  1. Ensure that the grounds are in contact with bare metal or are in the proper grounding points on the aircraft. Do not use the engine exhaust or propeller as grounding points. Damage to the propeller can result, and there is no way of quickly ensuring a positive bond between the engine and the airframe.
  1. Ground the nozzle to the aircraft, then open the fuel tank.
  1. Protect the wing and any other item on the aircraft from damage caused by spilled fuel or careless handling of the nozzle, hose, or grounding wires.
  1. Check the fuel cap for proper installation and security before leaving the aircraft.
  1. Remove the grounding wires in the reverse order. If the aircraft is not going to be flown or moved soon, the aircraft ground wire can be left attached.

When fueling from pits or cabinets, follow the same procedures as when using a truck. Pits or cabinets are usually designed with permanent grounding, eliminating the need to ground the equipment. However, the aircraft still must be grounded, and then the equipment must be grounded to the aircraft as it was with mobile equipment.

2. 中文翻译

飞机的正确加油是机主/运营人的责任。但这并不免除实际加油人员使用正确燃油类型和安全加油程序的责任。给飞机加油有两种基本方式。较小的飞机采用翼上加油法。这种方法用加油软管通过机翼顶部的加油口加注。较大飞机采用的方式是单点加油系统。这种加油系统利用机翼下前缘的插座为所有油箱加注。这缩短了给飞机加油所需的时间，限制了污染，并降低了静电引燃燃油的可能性。大多数压力加油系统由一根压力加油软管和一块控制与仪表面板组成，使一人即可为飞机的任一或全部油箱加油或抽油。每个油箱都能加注到预定的油量。这些程序见图 1-32 和图 1-33。

在加油之前，加油人员必须检查以下各项：

  1. 确认所有飞机电气系统和电子设备（包括雷达）均已关闭。2. 不要在衬衫口袋里携带任何物品。这些东西可能掉入油箱。
  1. 确认参与加油作业的任何人都未携带产生火焰的装置。一时的疏忽就可能造成事故。
  1. 确认所用燃油的类型和牌号正确。不要将 AVGAS 与 JET 燃油混合。
  1. 确认所有沉淀槽均已放水。
  1. 佩戴护目镜。其他形式的防护（例如橡胶手套和围裙）虽然一般不如护目重要，但也能保护皮肤免受溢出或飞溅燃油的影响。

4. 语法解析

① relieve sb of sth 与分词后置定语

原句：This does not, however, relieve the person doing the fueling of the responsibility to use the correct type of fuel and safe fueling procedures. 拆解：主语 This + 谓语 does not relieve + 宾语 the person doing the fueling（现在分词短语作后置定语）+ of the responsibility（relieve sb of sth 固定搭配）+ to use…（不定式作 responsibility 的后置定语）；however 为插入语，用逗号隔开。 要点：relieve sb of sth = 免除某人的（责任、负担），介词 of 不能省略；插入语 however 放句中时前后都要加逗号，不影响句子结构。

② 不定式作目的状语与介词短语链

原句：This method uses the fuel hose to fill through fueling ports on the top of the wing. 拆解：主语 This method + 谓语 uses + 宾语 the fuel hose + to fill（目的状语）+ through fueling ports（路径）+ on the top of the wing（后置定语，修饰 ports）。 要点：英语介词短语按由小到大排列（on the top of the wing = 机翼顶部之上），中文通常要反过来译；to fill 的逻辑主语与主句主语一致。

③ consist of 与定语从句

原句：Most pressure fueling systems consist of a pressure fueling hose and a panel of controls and gauges that permit one person to fuel or defuel any or all fuel tanks of an aircraft. 拆解：主语 Most pressure fueling systems + 谓语 consist of + 并列宾语 a pressure fueling hose 和 a panel of controls and gauges + that permit one person to fuel or defuel… 定语从句（修饰 a panel）+ permit sb to do 搭配。 要点：consist of 是及物性的动词短语，没有被动式（不能写 be consisted of）；定语从句 that 的先行词是 a panel（不是 systems），据此决定从句谓语用 permit。

④ there be + 动名词复合结构

原句：Do not fuel aircraft if there is danger of other aircraft in the vicinity blowing dirt in the direction of the aircraft being fueled. 拆解：否定祈使句 Do not fuel aircraft + if 条件从句 there is danger of…（there be 存在句）+ of + 动名词复合结构 other aircraft in the vicinity blowing dirt（other aircraft 是 blowing 的逻辑主语）+ in the direction of the aircraft being fueled（being fueled 为现在分词被动式作后置定语）。 要点：there is danger of + 名词/动名词，of 后不能接 that 从句；the aircraft being fueled = 正在被加油的飞机，being + 过去分词表「正在被……」。
