Contamination Control

1. 原文

Contamination is anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there. The types of contamination found in aviation fuel include water, solids, and microbial growths. The control of contamination in aviation fuel is extremely important, since contamination can lead to engine failure or stoppage and the loss of life. The best method of controlling contamination is to prevent its introduction into the fuel system. Some forms of contamination can still occur inside the fuel system.

However, the filter, separators, and screens remove most of the contamination.

Water in aviation fuels generally take two forms: dissolved (vapor) and free water. The dissolved water is not a major problem until, as the temperature lowers, it becomes free water. This then poses a problem if ice crystals form, clogging filters and other small orifices.

Color Grade Red 80 Green 100 Blue 100LL Purple 115 Free water can appear as water slugs or entrained water.

Water slugs are concentrations of water. This is the water that is drained after fueling an aircraft. Entrained water is suspended water droplets. These droplets may not be visible to the eye but give the fuel a cloudy look. The entrained water settles out in time.

Solid contaminants are insoluble in fuel. The more common types are rust, dirt, sand, gasket material, lint, and fragments of shop towels. The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair.

Microbiological growths are a problem in jet fuel. There are a number of varieties of micro-organisms that can live in the free water in jet fuel. Some variations of these organisms are airborne, others live in the soil. The aircraft fuel system becomes susceptible to the introduction of these organisms each time the aircraft is fueled. Favorable conditions for the growth of micro-organisms in the fuel are warm temperatures and the presence of iron oxide and mineral salts in the water. The best way to prevent microbial growth is to keep the fuel dry.

The effects of micro-organisms are:

• Formation of slime or sludge that can foul filters, separators, or fuel controls.

• Emulsification of the fuel.

• Corrosive compounds that can attack the fuel tank’s structure. In the case of a wet wing tank, the tank is made from the aircraft’s structure. They can also have offensive odors.

2. 中文翻译

污染物是指燃油中本不该存在的任何物质。航空燃油中发现的污染物类型包括水分、固体物和微生物生长物。航空燃油的污染控制极为重要，因为污染可能导致发动机失效或停车，甚至造成人员伤亡。控制污染的最佳方法是防止其进入燃油系统。某些形式的污染仍可能在燃油系统内部产生。

不过，滤芯、分离器和滤网能除去大部分污染物。

航空燃油中的水一般有两种形态：溶解水（以油气形态存在）和游离水。在温度降低、溶解水转变为游离水之前，它并不是主要问题；此后一旦形成冰晶，堵塞滤芯及其他细小孔道，就成为问题。

游离水可以以水塞或被夹带水的形式出现。

水塞是水的聚集。这是给航空器加油后放出的水。被夹带水是悬浮的水滴，这些水滴肉眼可能看不见，但会使燃油呈现浑浊外观。被夹带的水会随时间沉降出来。

固体污染物不溶于燃油。较常见的类型有铁锈、脏物、砂粒、垫片材料、绒毛以及抹布碎屑。燃油控制器及其他燃油相关机构的配合间隙很小，直径仅为头发丝 1/20 的颗粒就能使其损坏或堵塞。

微生物生长物在喷气燃油中是个问题。有若干种微生物能够存活于喷气燃油的游离水中，其中一些随空气传播，另一些存在于土壤中。航空器每次加油时，其燃油系统都可能被引入这些微生物。燃油中微生物生长的有利条件是温度较高，以及水中存在氧化铁和矿物盐。防止微生物生长的最好办法是保持燃油干燥。

微生物的影响包括：

• 形成黏液或油泥，堵塞滤芯、分离器或燃油控制器。

• 使燃油乳化。

• 产生腐蚀性化合物，侵蚀油箱结构；就整体油箱而言，油箱即由航空器结构构成。这些微生物还可能产生难闻气味。

4. 语法解析

① anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there（不定代词 + that 定语从句）

原句：Contamination is anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there.

拆解：主语 Contamination；系动词 is；表语 anything，后接 in the fuel 与 that is not supposed to be there 定语从句；从句谓语用 is（anything 视为单数）。

要点：先行词是 anything 等不定代词时，关系代词用 that 而不用 which。be supposed to 表「本应、应该」，否定形式即「不该存在」；手册用定义句起节，后面再逐项展开，读时注意这种「定义—分类」结构。

② can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as …（并列被动 + as … as 比较）

原句：The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair.

拆解：主语 The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms；谓语 can be damaged or blocked 为并列被动；by particles 引出施动者；as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair 为比较结构作后置定语修饰 particles。

要点：主语中心词是 tolerances（间隙），不是 fuel controls，别读错。as + adj. + as + 数量表「小到……」，中文常译作「直径仅……的颗粒」。damage 与 block 一「损」一「堵」，译文要分开。

③ The best method of controlling … is to prevent …（动名词定语 + 不定式表语）

原句：The best method of controlling contamination is to prevent its introduction into the fuel system.

拆解：主语 The best method of controlling contamination（of + 动名词作后置定语）；表语为不定式 to prevent its introduction into the fuel system。

要点：method of 后接动名词，不接不定式。主语是 method 时表语用不定式，形式上不对称，但英语允许（method is to do）。introduction into 是固定搭配，「引入……之中」；its 指 contamination。
