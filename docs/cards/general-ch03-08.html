Finding a Percentage of a Given Number

This is the most common type of percentage calculation. Here are two methods to solve percentage problems: using algebra or using proportions. Each method is shown next to find a percent of a given number. Example: In a shipment of 80 wingtip lights, 15% of the lights were defective. How many of the lights were defective?

Algebraic Method

15% of 80 lights = N (number of defective lights)

0.15 × 80 = N 12 = N Therefore, 12 defective lights were in the shipment.

Proportion Method

N 80 = 15100 To solve for N: N × 100 = 80 × 15 N × 100 = 1,200 N = 1,200 ÷ 100 N = 12 or N = (80 × 15) ÷ 100 N = 12

Finding What Percentage One Number is of Another

Example: A small engine rated at 12 horsepower is found to be delivering only 10.75 horsepower. What is the motor efficiency expressed as a percent?

Algebraic Method

N% of 12 rated horsepower = 10.75 actual horsepower N% × 12 = 10.75 N% = 10.75 ÷ 12 N% = 0.8958 N = 89.58 Therefore, the motor efficiency is 89.58%.

Proportion Method

10.75 12 = N100 Solve for G: (750 × 24) ÷ 300 = 60 Therefore, to fly 750 miles, 60 gallons of gasoline is required.

Percentage Percentage means “parts out of one hundred.” The percentage sign is “%.” Ninety percent is expressed as 90% (= 90 parts out of 100). The decimal 0.90 equals 90⁄100, or 90 out of 100, or 90%.

Expressing a Decimal Number as a Percentage

To express a decimal number in percent, move the decimal point two places to the right (adding zeroes if necessary) and then affix the percent symbol.

Example: Express the following decimal numbers as a percent:

0.90 = 90% 0.5 = 50% 1.25 = 125% 0.335 = 33.5%

Expressing a Percentage as a Decimal Number

Sometimes it may be necessary to express a percentage as a decimal number. To express a percentage as a decimal number, move the decimal point two places to the left and drop the % symbol.

For example: Express the following percentages as decimal numbers:

90% = 0.90 50% = 0.50 5% = 0.05 150% = 1.5

4. 语法解析

① 最高级 + of 短语

原句：This is the most common type of percentage calculation.

拆解：主语 This；系动词 is；表语 the most common type of percentage calculation，其中 of 短语限定 type 的范围。

要点：最高级前必须加 the；of 短语说明「在……之中」。说明某一类问题最常见、最基本时，这个句式在技术教材的过渡段里很常用。

② Here are 倒装 + 冒号列举

原句：Here are two methods to solve percentage problems: using algebra or using proportions.

拆解：Here are 是倒装结构，真正的主语是 two methods to solve percentage problems（不定式作后置定语）；冒号后 using algebra 与 using proportions 是动名词短语并列，说明两种方法。

要点：Here is/are … 中谓语与后面的名词一致，此处为复数故用 are。冒号引出的并列成分可以是名词，也可以是动名词短语；method 后用 to do 而非 doing 作定语。

③ 目的状语 + 祈使句 + 现在分词伴随状语

原句：To express a decimal number in percent, move the decimal point two places to the right (adding zeroes if necessary) and then affix the percent symbol.

拆解：To express a decimal number in percent 是目的状语；主句为祈使句，谓语 move … and then affix … 并列；two places to the right 是表示距离的状语；括号内 adding zeroes if necessary 是现在分词短语作伴随状语，if necessary 为省略结构（if it is necessary）。

要点：表示「移动小数点几位」用 move the decimal point two places to the right/left。祈使句连用加 then 表示操作顺序；if necessary 是省略的固定表达，不能写成 if is necessary。

④ 形式主语 it + 不定式

原句：Sometimes it may be necessary to express a percentage as a decimal number.

拆解：Sometimes 是时间状语；it 为形式主语；may be necessary 是谓语部分；真正的主语是 to express a percentage as a decimal number。

要点：it is necessary to do sth 是技术说明里表示「有必要做某事」的标准句式，it 不能省。express A as B 意为「把 A 表示为 B」，是换算类表述的核心搭配。

⑤ 过去分词后置定语 + be found to be doing

原句：A small engine rated at 12 horsepower is found to be delivering only 10.75 horsepower.

拆解：主语 A small engine 后接过去分词短语 rated at 12 horsepower 作后置定语；谓语 is found to be delivering only 10.75 horsepower，其中 to be delivering 是动词不定式的进行式，作主语补足语。

要点：rated at … 表示「额定为……」，是设备参数的固定说法。be found to be doing 意为「被发现正处于……状态」；be found to do 与 be found to be doing 的差别在于后者强调当时正在发生。

⑥ 单位作主语的主谓一致 + 名词所有格

原句：Eighty ohms represents 52% of a microphone’s total resistance.

拆解：Eighty ohms 形式上是复数，但作为一个量值，谓语用单数 represents；宾语 52% of a microphone’s total resistance 中 a microphone’s 是名词所有格，修饰 total resistance。

要点：表示度量、金额、距离的量值作主语时，通常视为一个整体，谓语用单数。百分数后接 of + 名词时，谓语与 of 后的名词一致（此处 52% of … resistance 仍随整体），表达「某量的百分之几」。
