Ice Detector System

机体 Ch 15 · Ice Detector System · 原文 PDF 第 883 页

1. 原文

Ice can be detected visually, but most modern aircraft have one or more ice detector sensors that warn the flight crew of icing conditions. An annunciator light comes on to alert the flight crew. In some aircraft models, multiple ice detectors are used, and the ice detection system automatically turns on the WAI systems when icing is detected.

Ice Prevention

Several means to prevent or control ice formation are used in aircraft today: 1. Heating surfaces with hot air. 2. Heating by electrical elements. 3. Breaking up ice formations, usually by inflatable boots. 4. Chemical application.

Equipment is designed for anti-icing or for deicing. Anti-icing equipment is turned on before entering icing conditions and is designed to prevent ice from forming. A surface may be anti-iced by keeping it dry, by heating to a temperature that evaporates water upon impingement, or by heating the surface just enough to prevent freezing, maintaining it running wet. Deicing equipment is designed to remove ice after it begins to accumulate typically on the wings and stabilizer leading edges. Ice may be controlled on aircraft structure by the methods described in Figure 15-5.

2. 中文翻译

冰可以目视发现，但大多数现代飞机装有一个或多个冰探测器传感器，用于向飞行机组告警结冰条件。信号灯会点亮以提醒飞行机组。在某些机型上使用多个冰探测器，一旦探测到结冰，冰探测系统便自动接通机翼防冰（WAI）系统。

防冰

如今飞机上采用多种手段来防止或控制结冰：1. 用热空气加热表面。2. 用电加热元件加热。3. 破坏已形成的冰，通常用可膨胀除冰带。4. 施加化学液体。

设备按防冰或除冰来设计。防冰设备在进入结冰条件之前就接通，用于防止冰的形成。可以使表面保持干燥、加热到水滴撞击后即蒸发的温度，或仅加热到足以防止冻结而让表面保持“湿态流动”，以此实现表面防冰。除冰设备用于在冰开始积聚后将其清除，积冰通常出现在机翼和安定面前缘。飞机结构上的冰可按图15-5所述的方法加以控制。

4. 语法解析

① 并列句 + when 时间从句

原句：In some aircraft models, multiple ice detectors are used, and the ice detection system automatically turns on the WAI systems when icing is detected. 拆解：In some aircraft models 为地点状语；前一分句 multiple ice detectors are used 为被动语态；and 连接后一分句，主语 the ice detection system，谓语 turns on，宾语 the WAI systems；when icing is detected 为时间状语从句，用被动语态。 要点：两个分句主语不同（detectors 与 system），所以 and 后必须重复主语，不能省略。when 从句用一般现在时被动式表习惯性动作，不是将来时。

② 并列谓语 + before doing

原句：Anti-icing equipment is turned on before entering icing conditions and is designed to prevent ice from forming. 拆解：主语 Anti-icing equipment，两个并列谓语 is turned on 与 is designed to；before entering icing conditions 为介词短语作时间状语，before 后接动名词；be designed to do 表示“设计用来做某事”。 要点：before 是介词而非连词，后面只能接动名词 entering；若要接从句须写成 before it enters。and 连接的两个谓语共用一个主语，第二个 is 不能省，否则会被读成与前面并列的另一个主语。

③ by 引出的三个并列方式状语 + 定语从句

原句：A surface may be anti-iced by keeping it dry, by heating to a temperature that evaporates water upon impingement, or by heating the surface just enough to prevent freezing, maintaining it running wet. 拆解：主语 A surface，谓语 may be anti-iced 为“情态动词 + 被动语态”；三个 by + 动名词短语并列作方式状语；第一个 by 短语中 it 指 surface；第二个含 that 引导的定语从句修饰 temperature；第三个中 just enough to prevent freezing 为程度状语，句末 maintaining it running wet 为现在分词短语作结果状语。 要点：三个 by 并列，最后一个前有 or，读时按“或”切分即可，不要误把 or by 当成新的分句。maintaining 的逻辑主语是前面动作的执行者，其宾语 it 仍指 surface，running wet 为宾语补足语。

④ after 从句 + 插入状语

原句：Deicing equipment is designed to remove ice after it begins to accumulate typically on the wings and stabilizer leading edges. 拆解：主句 Deicing equipment is designed to remove ice；after 引导时间状语从句 it begins to accumulate；typically on the wings and stabilizer leading edges 为插入的地点状语，说明积冰的典型部位，逻辑上修饰 accumulate。 要点：typically 插在从句谓语与地点状语之间，容易误读成修饰 remove；结合语义，它说明“冰通常积聚在何处”。after 从句用一般现在时表将来，符合时间状语从句的时态规则。
