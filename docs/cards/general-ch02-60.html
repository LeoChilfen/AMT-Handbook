Radio Station License

通用 Ch 02 · Radio Station License · 原文 PDF 第 88 页

1. 原文

A radio station license is required if the aircraft is equipped with radios, and the aircraft is planned to be flown outside the boundaries of the United States. A radio station license is not required for aircraft that are operated domestically.

(A major change occurred on February 8, 1996, when the telecommunications Act of 1996 was signed into law.)

The Federal Communications Commission (FCC) formerly required that any communication transmitter installed in aircraft be licensed. These FCC licenses were valid for 5 years. This is not an FAA requirement. FAA inspectors who conducted ramp inspections and detected an expired radio station license were not required to notify the FCC, nor could they issue a violation to the owner/operator. Simply informing the operator of the expired radio station license was their only responsibility.

FSGA 96-06, a Flight Standards Information Bulletin (FSIB)

for General Aviation (FSGA) titled “Elimination of Aircraft Radio Station Licenses” became effective on July 8, 1996.

Although that FSIB had an effectivity of only 1 year, the elimination of the requirement for aircraft used only in domestic operations continues.

2. 中文翻译

如果航空器装备了无线电设备，并且计划飞出美国国境之外运行，则需要电台执照。对于仅在美国国内运行的航空器，不需要电台执照。

（1996 年 2 月 8 日发生了一项重大变化——当时《1996 年电信法》被签署成为法律。）

联邦通信委员会（FCC）过去要求，安装在航空器上的任何通信发射机都必须取得执照。这些 FCC 执照的有效期为 5 年。这并不是 FAA 的要求。执行停机坪检查并发现电台执照已过期的 FAA 检查员，无需通知 FCC，也不能向机主/运营人开具违规通知。只是把电台执照已过期一事告知运营人，是他们唯一的职责。

FSGA 96-06，一份飞行标准信息通报（FSIB）

面向通用航空（FSGA）、题为「取消航空器电台执照」的通报，于 1996 年 7 月 8 日生效。

尽管该 FSIB 的有效期仅为 1 年，但取消对仅用于国内运行的航空器的此项要求，至今仍在延续。

4. 语法解析

① nor could they issue …（nor 引导的部分倒装）

原句：FAA inspectors who conducted ramp inspections and detected an expired radio station license were not required to notify the FCC, nor could they issue a violation to the owner/operator.

拆解：主干为 FAA inspectors … were not required to notify the FCC；who conducted … and detected … 是定语从句，两个并列谓语修饰 inspectors；nor 引出并列分句，采用部分倒装：nor + 情态动词 could + 主语 they + 动词原形 issue。

要点：否定句后用 nor／neither 连接另一否定分句时，必须把助动词或情态动词提到主语之前，形成「nor + 助动词 + 主语 + 动词」。此处 they 指 inspectors，不可换成单数 he，因为先行词是复数。to the owner/operator 中的 to 表示「开具（处罚）给某人」，不能省。

② Simply informing … was their only responsibility（动名词短语作主语）

原句：Simply informing the operator of the expired radio station license was their only responsibility.

拆解：主语是动名词短语 Simply informing the operator of the expired radio station license，谓语 was，表语 their only responsibility；simply 为副词，修饰整个动名词短语，inform sb of sth 为固定搭配。

要点：动名词（短语）作主语时谓语用单数，故为 was。inform 的搭配是 inform sb of sth，「把某事告知某人」，不能写成 inform sb sth。本句与上一句构成对照：通知 FCC、开违规都不在其职责内，告知运营人才是。

③ Although … , the elimination … continues（让步状语从句 + 分词后置定语）

原句：Although that FSIB had an effectivity of only 1 year, the elimination of the requirement for aircraft used only in domestic operations continues.

拆解：Although 引导让步状语从句；主句主语 the elimination of the requirement for aircraft …，其中 used only in domestic operations 是过去分词短语后置修饰 aircraft；谓语 continues（一般现在时，第三人称单数）。

要点：although 与 but 不能同时出现，汉语「虽然……但是……」译成英文只能保留其一。used only in domestic operations 是「被使用的」被动含义，故用过去分词而非 using。谓语用一般现在时 continues，正是为了强调该规定「至今仍然有效」这一延续性。
