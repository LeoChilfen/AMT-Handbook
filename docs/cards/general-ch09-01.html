1. 原文

Aircraft fluid lines are usually made of metal tubing or flexible hose. Metal tubing (also called rigid fluid lines) is used in stationary applications and where long, relatively straight runs are possible. They are widely used in aircraft for fuel, oil, coolant, oxygen, instrument, and hydraulic lines. Flexible hose is generally used with moving parts or where the hose is subject to considerable vibration.

Occasionally, it may be necessary to repair or replace damaged aircraft fluid lines. Very often the repair can be made simply by replacing the tubing. However, if replacements are not available, the needed parts may have to be fabricated. Replacement tubing should be of the same size and material as the original tubing. All tubing is pressure tested prior to initial installation and is designed to withstand several times the normal operating pressure to which it is subjected. If a tube bursts or cracks, it is generally the result of excessive vibration, improper installation, or damage caused by collision with an object. All tubing failures should be carefully studied and the cause of the failure determined.

2. 中文翻译

飞机流体管路通常由金属导管或软管制成。金属导管（也称刚性流体管路）用于固定不动的部位，以及可以布置较长、相对平直管段的场合。它们在飞机上广泛用于燃油、滑油、冷却液、氧气、仪表和液压管路。软管一般用于运动部件，或用于承受较大振动的部位。

有时可能需要修理或更换损坏的飞机流体管路。很多时候，只要更换导管就能完成修理。但是，如果没有现成的备件，所需的零件可能必须自行制作。更换用的导管应与原导管的尺寸和材料相同。所有导管在首次装机前都要经过压力试验，并设计成能承受其所受正常工作压力的若干倍。如果导管爆裂或产生裂纹，通常都是振动过大、安装不当或与物体碰撞造成损伤的结果。对所有导管失效都应仔细研究，并查明失效的原因。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + be made of

原句：Aircraft fluid lines are usually made of metal tubing or flexible hose. 拆解：主语 Aircraft fluid lines + 谓语 are usually made of（be + 过去分词 + 介词 of）+ 表语 metal tubing or flexible hose（并列名词短语）。 要点：be made of 强调「由……制成（看得出材质）」，be made from 强调「由……加工而成（材质已改变）」，be made with 强调「用……作配件」；手册介绍器材构成时几乎都用被动语态，动作发出者不出现。

② 括号插入语 + 并列 + where 引导的定语从句

原句：Metal tubing (also called rigid fluid lines) is used in stationary applications and where long, relatively straight runs are possible. 拆解：主干 Metal tubing is used；括号内 also called rigid fluid lines 为过去分词短语作插入语，补充说明同义术语；介词短语 in stationary applications 与 where 引导的定语从句（相当于 in places where…）并列作地点状语。 要点：括号插入语不是句子成分，跳过它才能找到谓语 is used；where 从句前省略了介词 in，属于「地点名词 + where」的压缩写法，翻译时要补出「在……的场合」。

③ by + 动名词表示方式

原句：Very often the repair can be made simply by replacing the tubing. 拆解：主语 the repair + 谓语 can be made（情态被动）+ 方式状语 simply by replacing the tubing（介词 by + 动名词短语）。 要点：by + doing 表示「通过做某事」，是技术文中交代手段的常用结构，不能用 by to do；simply 修饰整个方式状语，译作「只要……就能」。被动语态把「修理」放在主语位置，符合本节说明「修理怎么做」的行文角度。

④ 并列被动 + 倍数表达 + to which 定语从句

原句：All tubing is pressure tested prior to initial installation and is designed to withstand several times the normal operating pressure to which it is subjected. 拆解：两个并列谓语 is pressure tested 与 is designed to withstand…；prior to initial installation 为时间状语；to withstand… 为不定式作目的状语；several times the normal operating pressure 是倍数表达；to which it is subjected 为「介词 + which」引导的定语从句修饰 operating pressure。 要点：several times + 名词 = 「是……的若干倍」，不加 than，与 several times greater than 不同；be subjected to 意为「承受、经受」，介词提到 which 前是正式书面语的要求，切勿漏掉 to。

⑤ 条件从句 + caused by 过去分词短语作后置定语

原句：If a tube bursts or cracks, it is generally the result of excessive vibration, improper installation, or damage caused by collision with an object. 拆解：If 引导条件状语从句（主语 a tube，并列谓语 bursts or cracks）；主句 it is generally the result of…；of 后接三个并列名词短语 excessive vibration、improper installation、damage，其中 caused by collision with an object 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 damage。 要点：caused by 只修饰紧邻的 damage，不修饰前面两项，翻译时要把「由……造成的」放在「损伤」之后；collision with 是固定搭配，不能写成 collision to。

⑥ 并列句中的助动词省略

原句：All tubing failures should be carefully studied and the cause of the failure determined. 拆解：前半句 All tubing failures should be carefully studied；and 后 the cause of the failure determined 承前省略了 should be，完整形式为 the cause of the failure should be determined。 要点：并列句中后一分句与前面重复的助动词、be 动词可以省略，只留过去分词，读的时候要自行补回；这类省略在规章、手册里很常见，漏读会误以为 determined 是谓语动词的过去式。
