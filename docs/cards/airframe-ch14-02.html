Fuel Storage Refilling or Recharging System

机体 Ch 14 · Fuel Storage Refilling or Recharging System · 原文 PDF 第 826 页

1. 原文

Each fuel storage refilling or recharging system must be designed to prevent improper refilling or recharging; prevent contamination of the fuel stored during likely operating conditions; and prevent the occurrence of any hazard to the airplane or to persons during refilling or recharging.

2. 中文翻译

每个燃油储存加注或充装系统的设计，都必须防止加注或充装方式不当；防止在可能的运行条件下所储存的燃油受到污染；并防止在加注或充装期间对飞机或人员造成任何危害。

4. 语法解析

① 三个并列不定式共用 must be designed to

原句：Each fuel storage refilling or recharging system must be designed to prevent improper refilling or recharging; prevent contamination of the fuel stored during likely operating conditions; and prevent the occurrence of any hazard to the airplane or to persons during refilling or recharging. 拆解：主语 Each fuel storage refilling or recharging system（refilling or recharging 作 storage 的并列修饰语）；谓语 must be designed；to 后接三个并列不定式 prevent...、prevent...、prevent...，分号用于分隔含内部逗号或较长的并列项，最后一项前用 and。 要点：第二、三项的 prevent 前省略了 to，这是并列不定式的省略，不能理解为三个独立谓语。分号在这里不是分句界限，全句仍是一个句子，所以三项之间不能用句号断成三句。

② 主句的被动结构 must be designed to do

原句：Each fuel storage refilling or recharging system must be designed to prevent improper refilling or recharging 拆解：主语中心词 system，前置定语为 fuel storage refilling or recharging 这一串名词叠加；谓语为情态动词 + 被动语态 must be designed；不定式 to prevent 作目的状语，宾语为名词化的 improper refilling or recharging。 要点：四个名词直接叠加（fuel / storage / refilling / recharging）是技术英语的典型压缩写法，翻译时须按逻辑拆开：“燃油储存（的）加注或充装系统”。improper 修饰的是操作行为，不是系统。

③ 过去分词 stored 作后置定语 + during 的归属

原句：prevent contamination of the fuel stored during likely operating conditions 拆解：动宾结构 prevent contamination of the fuel；过去分词短语 stored during likely operating conditions 后置修饰 the fuel；during 短语在句法上依附于 stored，说明储存发生的时段。 要点：stored 的逻辑主语是 the fuel，表被动“被储存的燃油”。during likely operating conditions 修饰 stored 而非 contamination，即“在可能的运行条件下储存期间（受到的）污染”；这类介词短语的归属要看语义，不能只看就近。

④ 名词化宾语 + 并列介词宾语

原句：prevent the occurrence of any hazard to the airplane or to persons during refilling or recharging 拆解：谓语 prevent，宾语为名词化结构 the occurrence of any hazard；to the airplane or to persons 为两个并列介词短语，作 hazard 的后置定语；during refilling or recharging 为时间状语，修饰 occurrence。 要点：occurrence of 把“发生危害”这一动作名词化，是法规英语的常见写法，翻译时还原为动词更顺：“防止……造成危害”。or 连接的两个 to 短语必须重复介词 to，否则会误读成 persons during... 连读。
