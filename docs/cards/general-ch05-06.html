1. 原文

It is often necessary to compare the density of one substance with another substance. For this purpose, a standard is needed. Water is the standard that physicists have chosen to use when comparing the densities of all liquids and solids.

For gases, air is most commonly used, but hydrogen is also sometimes used as a standard for gases. In physics, the word “specific” implies a ratio. Thus, specific gravity is calculated by comparing the weight of a definite volume of the given substance with the weight of an equal volume of water. The terms “specific weight” or “specific density” are sometimes used to express this ratio.

The following formulas are used to find the specific gravity of liquids and solids.

Specific Gravity = Weight of an equal volume of waterWeight of the substance or Specific Gravity = Density of waterDensity of the substance The same formulas are used to find the density of gases by substituting air or hydrogen for water.

Specific gravity is not expressed in units, but as pure numbers.

For example, if a certain hydraulic fluid has a specific gravity of 0.8, 1 ft3 of the liquid weighs 0.8 times as much as 1 ft 3 of water: 62.4 times 0.8, or 49.92 lb.

Specific gravity and density are independent of the size of the sample under consideration and depend only upon the substance of which it is made. See Figure 5-1 for typical values of specific gravity for various substances.

A device called a hydrometer is used for measuring specific gravity of liquids. This device consists of a tubular glass float contained in a larger glass tube. [Figure 5-2] The larger glass tube provides the container for the liquid. A rubber suction bulb draws the liquid up into the container. There must be enough liquid raising the float to prevent it from touching the bottom. The float is weighted and has a vertically graduated scale. To determine specific gravity, the scale is read at the surface of the liquid in which the float is immersed. An indication of 1000 is read when the float is immersed in pure water. When immersed in a liquid of greater density, the float rises, indicating a greater specific gravity. For liquids of lesser density, the float sinks, indicating a lower specific gravity.

An example of the use of the hydrometer is to determine the specific gravity of the electrolyte (battery liquid) in an aircraft battery. When a battery is discharged, the calibrated float immersed in the electrolyte will indicate approximately 1150. The indication of a charged battery is between 1275 and 1310. The values 1150, 1275, and 1310 represent 1.150, 1.275, and 1.310. The electrolyte in a discharged battery is 1.15 times denser than water, and in a charged battery 1.275 to 1.31 times denser than water.

2. 中文翻译

常常需要把一种物质的密度与另一种物质作比较。为此需要一个标准。水就是物理学家在比较所有液体和固体的密度时所选用的标准。

对于气体，最常用的是空气，但氢有时也被用作气体的标准。在物理学中，「specific（比）」一词意味着比值。因此，比重是通过把一定体积给定物质的重量与同体积水的重量相比较来计算的。有时也用「specific weight（比重量）」或「specific density（比密度）」这两个术语来表达这一比值。

以下公式用于求液体和固体的比重。

Specific Gravity = Weight of an equal volume of waterWeight of the substance or Specific Gravity = Density of waterDensity of the substance（原文此处两个公式为分式排版，OCR 压平后分子与分母连写在一起，须按分式读：比重 = 该物质重量 ÷ 同体积水的重量；第二式同理，即比重 = 该物质的密度 ÷ 水的密度。）把水替换为空气或氢，即可用同样的公式求出气体的密度。

比重不用单位表示，而用纯数表示。

例如，若某种液压油的比重为 0.8，则 1 ft3 该液体的重量是 1 ft3 水的 0.8 倍：62.4 乘以 0.8，即 49.92 lb。

比重和密度与所考察试样的大小无关，只取决于构成该试样的物质。各种物质的比重典型值参见图 5-1。

测量液体比重所用的装置称为比重计（hydrometer）。该装置由一个管状玻璃浮子构成，浮子装在较大的玻璃管内。较大的玻璃管用作盛装液体的容器。橡胶吸球把液体吸上进入容器。液体必须足够多，能把浮子托起，以免浮子碰到管底。浮子经过配重，并带有竖直刻度。测定比重时，在浮子所浸入的液体液面处读取刻度。浮子浸入纯水时，读数为 1000。浸入密度较大的液体时，浮子上浮，表示比重较大。对于密度较小的液体，浮子下沉，表示比重较小。

比重计的一个应用实例是测定航空器蓄电池中电解液（电池液）的比重。当蓄电池放电时，浸在电解液中的已校准浮子将指示约 1150。充足电的蓄电池，其指示值在 1275 与 1310 之间。1150、1275 和 1310 这些值分别代表 1.150、1.275 和 1.310。放电电池的电解液密度是水的 1.15 倍，充足电时为水的 1.275 至 1.31 倍。

4. 语法解析

① 定语从句 + when + 动名词

原句：Water is the standard that physicists have chosen to use when comparing the densities of all liquids and solids.

拆解：主句 Water is the standard；that physicists have chosen to use 是定语从句，that 作 chosen 的宾语（use 后面省略了 the standard）；when comparing… 是「连词 + 现在分词」构成的时间状语，省略了 they are。

要点：when + 分词要求分词的逻辑主语与主句主语一致（此处是 physicists），否则要写成完整从句。定语从句中 that 作宾语时可以省略；have chosen 用现在完成时，强调「迄今选定、沿用至今」。

② by + 动名词表示方法

原句：Thus, specific gravity is calculated by comparing the weight of a definite volume of the given substance with the weight of an equal volume of water.

拆解：主句 specific gravity is calculated（被动）；by comparing A with B 是方式状语，by 后接动名词；compare 的搭配是 compare A with/to B。

要点：by + -ing 译作「通过……（来做）」，是说明计算或操作方法的固定结构，by 后面不能用不定式。被动句 is calculated 隐去计算者，符合手册文体。a definite volume 与 an equal volume 是「一定体积」与「同体积」的对照，别混。

③ there be + 情态动词 / prevent … from doing

原句：There must be enough liquid raising the float to prevent it from touching the bottom.

拆解：There must be 是 there be 句型加情态动词；enough liquid 是真正的主语；raising the float 是现在分词短语后置修饰 liquid；to prevent it from touching the bottom 是目的状语，prevent A from doing B。

要点：there be 与情态动词连用时形式是 there must be / there should be，不能写成 there must have。prevent … from … 中 from 在口语里可省，但技术文本宜保留；同类搭配有 stop … from doing、keep … from doing。
