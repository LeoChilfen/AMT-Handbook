1. 原文

Understanding the functional design and operation of electrical measuring instruments is very important, since they are used in repairing, maintaining, and troubleshooting electrical circuits. The best and most expensive measuring instrument is of no use unless the technician knows what is

being measured and what each reading indicates. The purpose of the meter is to measure quantities existing in a circuit. For this reason, when a meter is connected to a circuit, it must not change the characteristics of that circuit.

Meters are either self-excited or externally excited. Those that are self-excited operate from a power source within the meter. Externally-excited meters get their power source from the circuit that they are connected to. The most common analog meters in use today are the voltmeter, ammeter, and ohmmeter. All of which operate on the principles of electromagnetism. The fundamental principle behind the operation of the meter is the interaction between magnetic fields created by a current gathered from the circuit in some manner. This interaction is between the magnetic fields of a permanent magnet and the coils of a rotating magnet. The greater the current through the coils of the rotating magnet, the stronger the magnetic field produced. A stronger field produces greater rotation of the coil. While some meters can be used for both DC and AC circuit measurement, only those used as DC instruments are discussed in this section. The meters used for AC, or for both AC and DC, are discussed in the study of AC theory and circuitry.

D’Arsonval Meter Movement

This basic DC type of meter movement—first employed by the French scientist, d’Arsonval, in making electrical measurement—is a current measuring device, which is used in the ammeter, voltmeter, and ohmmeter. The pointer is deflected in proportion to the amount of current through the coil. Basically, both the ammeter and the voltmeter are current measuring instruments, the principal difference being the method in which they are connected in a circuit. While an ohmmeter is also basically a current measuring instrument, it differs from the ammeter and voltmeter in that it provides its own source (self-excited) of power and contains other auxiliary circuits.

Current Sensitivity and Resistance

The current sensitivity of a meter movement is the amount of current required to drive the meter movement to a full-scale deflection. A simple example would be a meter movement that has 1 mA sensitivity. What this indicates is that meter movement requires 1 mA of current to move the needle to a full-scale indication. Likewise, a half-scale deflection requires only 0.5 mA of current. Additionally, what is called movement resistance is the actual DC resistance of the wire used to construct the meter coil.

In a standard d’Arsonval meter, movement may have a current sensitivity of 1 mA and a resistance of 50 Ω. If the meter is going to be used to measure more than 1 mA, then additional circuitry is required to accomplish the task. This additional circuitry is a simple shunt resistor. The purpose of the shunt resistor is to bypass current that exceeds the 1 mA limitation of the meter movement. To illustrate this, assume that the 1 mA meter in question is needed to measure 10 mA. The shunt resistor used should carry 9 mA while the remaining 1 mA is allowed to pass through the meter.

To make meter readings quickly and accurately, it is desirable that the moving pointer overshoot its proper position only a small amount and come to rest after not more than one or two small oscillations. The term “damping” is applied to methods used to bring the pointer of an electrical meter to rest after it has been set in motion. Damping may be accomplished by electrical means, by mechanical means, or by a combination of both.

Electrical Damping

A common method of damping by electrical means is to wind the moving coil on an aluminum frame. As the coil moves in the field of the permanent magnet, eddy currents are set up in the aluminum frame. The magnetic field produced by the eddy currents opposes the motion of the coil. The pointer therefore swings more slowly to its proper position and comes to rest quickly with very little oscillation.

Mechanical Damping

Air damping is a common method of damping by mechanical means. As shown in the figure, a vane is attached to the shaft of the moving element and enclosed in an air chamber. The movement of the shaft is retarded because of the resistance that the air offers to the vane. Effective damping is achieved if the vane nearly touches the walls of the chamber.

A Basic Multirange Ammeter

Building upon the basic meter previously discussed is the more complex and useful multirange meter, which is more practical. The basic idea of a multirange ammeter is to make the meter usable over a wide range of voltages. In order to accomplish this, each range must utilize a different shunt resistance. The example given in this handbook is that of a two-range meter. However, once the basics of a two-range multirange ammeter are understood, the concepts can easily be transferred to the design of meters with many selectable ranges.

The figure shows the schematic of an ammeter with two selectable ranges. This example builds upon the previous 10 mA range meter by adding a 100 mA range. With the switch selected to the 10 mA range, the meter indicates 10 mA when the needle is deflected to full-scale and likewise indicates 100 mA at full-scale when selected to 100 mA. The value of the 100 mA shunt resistor is determined the same way the 10 mA shunt resistor was determined. Recall that the meter movement can only carry 1 mA. This means that in a 100 mA range the remaining current of 99 mA must pass through the shunt resistor.

  1. Always connect ammeter in series with the element

  1. Never connect an ammeter across a source of voltage, such as a battery or generator. Remember that the resistance of an ammeter, particularly on the higher ranges, is extremely low and that any voltage, even a volt or so, can cause very high current to flow through the meter, causing damage to it.
  1. Use a range large enough to keep the deflection less than full-scale. Before measuring a current, form some idea of its magnitude. Then switch to a large enough scale or start with the highest range and work down until the appropriate scale is reached. The most accurate readings are obtained at approximately half-scale deflection. Many milliammeters have been ruined by attempts to measure amperes. Therefore, be sure to read the lettering either on the dial or on the switch positions and choose proper scale before connecting the meter in the circuit.
  1. Observe proper polarity in connecting the meter in the circuit. Current must flow through the coil in a definite direction in order to move the indicator needle up scale. Current reversal because of incorrect connection in the circuit results in a reversed meter deflection and frequently causes bending of the meter needle. Avoid improper meter connections by observing the polarity markings on the meter.

仪表有自励式和外励式两类。自励式仪表依靠仪表内部的电源工作。外励式仪表的电源取自其所接入的电路。当今最常用的模拟式仪表是电压表、电流表和欧姆表，它们都依据电磁学原理工作。仪表工作的基本原理，是由按某种方式从电路取得的电流所建立的磁场之间的相互作用。这种相互作用发生在永久磁铁的磁场与旋转磁铁的线圈之间。流过旋转磁铁线圈的电流越大，产生的磁场越强。磁场越强，线圈的转动越大。虽然有些仪表既能测量直流也能测量交流，但本节只讨论用作直流仪表的那些。用于交流、或同时用于交流和直流的仪表，在交流理论与电路的学习中讨论。

达松瓦尔表头机构

这种基本的直流式表头机构——最早由法国科学家达松瓦尔用于进行电气测量——是一种电流测量装置，用于电流表、电压表和欧姆表。指针的偏转量与通过线圈的电流大小成正比。基本上，电流表和电压表都是电流测量仪器，主要区别在于它们接入电路的方式。欧姆表基本上也是一种电流测量仪器，但它与电流表、电压表的不同之处在于，它自带电源（自励式），并含有其他辅助电路。

电流灵敏度与电阻

表头机构的电流灵敏度，是驱动表头达到满刻度偏转所需的电流量。一个简单的例子是电流灵敏度为 1 mA 的表头。这说明该表头需要 1 mA 的电流才能使指针达到满刻度指示。同样，半刻度偏转只需要 0.5 mA 的电流。此外，所谓机构电阻，是指绕制仪表线圈所用导线的实际直流电阻。

在标准达松瓦尔仪表中，机构可能具有 1 mA 的电流灵敏度和 50 Ω 的电阻。如果要用该仪表测量大于 1 mA 的电流，就需要附加电路来完成这一任务。这种附加电路就是一个简单的分流电阻。分流电阻的作用是旁路掉超过表头 1 mA 限值的电流。为说明这一点，假设上述 1 mA 仪表需要测量 10 mA。所用分流电阻应承载 9 mA，而让其余 1 mA 通过仪表。

为使仪表读数迅速而准确，希望运动指针超出其正确位置的幅度很小，并在不超过一两次小幅摆动之后就停下来。「阻尼」一词用于指使电气仪表的指针在运动起来之后停下来的各种方法。阻尼可以用电气方法、机械方法或二者的组合来实现。

电气阻尼

一种常见的电气阻尼方法，是把动圈绕在铝制框架上。当线圈在永久磁铁的磁场中运动时，铝框架中会产生涡流。涡流产生的磁场阻碍线圈的运动。因此指针较缓慢地摆向正确位置，并很快停下来，摆动很小。

机械阻尼

空气阻尼是一种常见的机械阻尼方法。如图所示，一个翼片固定在运动元件的轴上，并封装在一个空气室内。由于空气对翼片产生阻力，轴的运动受到阻滞。如果翼片几乎贴到空气室壁，就能获得有效的阻尼。

基本的多量程电流表

在前面所讨论的基本仪表基础上，发展出更复杂、更有用的多量程仪表，它们更为实用。多量程电流表的基本思路是使仪表能在很宽的电压范围内使用。为做到这一点，每个量程必须采用不同的分流电阻。本手册所举的例子是一块两量程仪表。不过，一旦理解了两量程多量程电流表的基本原理，这些概念就可以很容易地推广到具有多个可选量程的仪表设计中。

4. 语法解析

① 动名词短语作主语 + since 原因状语从句

原句：Understanding the functional design and operation of electrical measuring instruments is very important, since they are used in repairing, maintaining, and troubleshooting electrical circuits. 拆解：动名词短语 Understanding the functional design and operation of electrical measuring instruments 作主语，谓语 is，表语 very important；since 引导原因状语从句，从句为被动语态 are used in…，后面接三个并列动名词 repairing、maintaining、troubleshooting 作介词 in 的宾语。 要点：动名词短语作主语时谓语用单数 is；since 在此表原因，可换成 because，但不能换成 for；be used in + 动名词表示「用于做某事」，区别于 be used to do。

② when 引导的时间状语从句 + must 否定 + 被动

原句：For this reason, when a meter is connected to a circuit, it must not change the characteristics of that circuit. 拆解：For this reason 为介词短语作状语；when 引导时间状语从句，从句为被动 is connected to a circuit；主句主语 it（指 the meter），谓语 must not change，宾语 the characteristics of that circuit。 要点：must not 表示「禁止、不得」，与 need not（不必）区分明显；时间状语从句常用一般现在时表示将来；characteristics 作「特性」解时常用复数。

③ that 引导的定语从句 + 介词 from 引导的来源状语

原句：Those that are self-excited operate from a power source within the meter. 拆解：主句主语 Those，that 引导定语从句 are self-excited 修饰 those；谓语 operate，from a power source 为来源状语，within the meter 为后置定语修饰 a power source。 要点：those 作先行词时用 that/who 引导定语从句，指物也可用 which；operate from 表示「由……供电/驱动」，区别于 operate on（依据……工作）；within 比 in 更强调「在内部范围之内」。

④ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + required to do

原句：The current sensitivity of a meter movement is the amount of current required to drive the meter movement to a full-scale deflection. 拆解：主语 The current sensitivity of a meter movement，谓语 is，表语 the amount of current；required to drive… 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 current，其中 to drive… 为目的状语。 要点：the amount of + 不可数名词，谓语用单数；required to do 意为「为做……所需的」，是过去分词被动含义；drive…to… 表示「把……驱动到……程度」。

⑤ While 引导的让步状语从句 + in that 引导的原因从句

原句：While an ohmmeter is also basically a current measuring instrument, it differs from the ammeter and voltmeter in that it provides its own source (self-excited) of power and contains other auxiliary circuits. 拆解：While 引导让步状语从句；主句 it differs from the ammeter and voltmeter；in that 引导原因状语从句，从句中 provides 与 contains 为并列谓语，宾语分别为 its own source (self-excited) of power 和 other auxiliary circuits。 要点：句首 While 表「虽然」，与 although 相近，不能用 but 呼应；differ from A in that… 是「与 A 的不同之处在于……」的固定句型；in that 是复合连词，不能拆成介词 in + that 从句理解。
