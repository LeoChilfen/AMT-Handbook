Types of Rotor Systems

机体 Ch 02 · Types of Rotor Systems · 原文 PDF 第 88 页

1. 原文

Fully Articulated Rotor

A fully articulated rotor is found on aircraft with more than two blades and allows movement of each individual blade in three directions. In this design, each blade can rotate about the pitch axis to change lift; each blade can move back and forth in plane, lead and lag; and flap up and down through a hinge independent of the other blades.

Semirigid Rotor

The semirigid rotor design is found on aircraft with two rotor blades. The blades are connected in a manner such that as one blade flaps up, the opposite blade flaps down.

Rigid Rotor

The rigid rotor system is a rare design but potentially offers the best properties of both the fully articulated and semirigid rotors. In this design, the blade roots are rigidly attached to the rotor hub. The blades do not have hinges to allow lead-lag or flapping. Instead, the blades accommodate these motions by using elastomeric bearings. Elastomeric bearings are molded, rubber-like materials that are bonded to the appropriate parts. Instead of rotating like conventional bearings, they twist and flex to allow proper movement of the blades.

2. 中文翻译

全铰接式旋翼

全铰接式旋翼见于装有两片以上桨叶的飞机，它允许每片桨叶在三个方向上运动。在这种设计中，每片桨叶可绕变距轴转动以改变升力；可在旋转平面内前后摆动，即超前与滞后；还可通过一个铰链独立于其他桨叶上下挥舞。

半刚性旋翼

半刚性旋翼设计见于装有两片旋翼桨叶的飞机。桨叶的连接方式使得一片桨叶向上挥舞时，对面的桨叶向下挥舞。

刚性旋翼

刚性旋翼系统是一种少见的设计，但有可能兼具全铰接式与半刚性旋翼的最佳特性。在这种设计中，桨叶根部刚性固定在旋翼毂上。桨叶没有允许超前—滞后或挥舞的铰链，而是采用弹性体轴承来适应这些运动。弹性体轴承是模压成型的类橡胶材料，粘接到相应部件上。它们不像常规轴承那样转动，而是通过扭转和弯曲来允许桨叶正常运动。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态与主动谓语并列

原句：A fully articulated rotor is found on aircraft with more than two blades and allows movement of each individual blade in three directions. 拆解：主语 A fully articulated rotor；第一个谓语为被动语态 is found，状语 on aircraft with more than two blades；and 连接第二个谓语 allows，宾语 movement of each individual blade，in three directions 为范围状语。 要点：and 连接的是两个谓语，主语相同，第二个谓语 allows 为主动语态——同一主语先“被发现于”再“允许”，语态不同但并列。with more than two blades 修饰 aircraft。

② 分号并列三个分句 + 省略主语

原句：In this design, each blade can rotate about the pitch axis to change lift; each blade can move back and forth in plane, lead and lag; and flap up and down through a hinge independent of the other blades. 拆解：In this design 为状语；三个分句用分号并列，第一、二分句各有主语 each blade，第三个分句承前省略主语与情态动词 can，只留 flap；to change lift 为目的状语，lead and lag 为同位解释，independent of the other blades 为形容词短语作状语。 要点：分号在此并列三个平行的运动方式，第三个分句的谓语是 flap，不是 move 的并列。independent of 意为“不受……影响、独立于”，修饰 flap 的方式。

③ such that 结果状语从句 + as 时间状语从句

原句：The blades are connected in a manner such that as one blade flaps up, the opposite blade flaps down. 拆解：主句为被动语态 The blades are connected in a manner；such that 引导结果（方式）状语从句；从句内 as one blade flaps up 为时间状语从句，主句 the opposite blade flaps down。 要点：in a manner such that 意为“以这样一种方式，使得……”，such that 不能拆开理解。as 在这里是“当……时”，不是“因为”，对置桨叶一上一下是连接方式造成的必然结果。

④ but 连接并列谓语 + 副词插入

原句：The rigid rotor system is a rare design but potentially offers the best properties of both the fully articulated and semirigid rotors. 拆解：主语 The rigid rotor system；is a rare design 为第一谓语部分（系表）；but 转折后承前省略主语，potentially 为副词作状语，offers 为第二谓语，宾语 the best properties of both...rotors。 要点：potentially 修饰 offers，表示“有潜力提供”，不是“可能已经具备”。both A and B 结构中 A、B 分别是 the fully articulated (rotors) 与 semirigid rotors，前者省略了 rotors。

⑤ 定语从句被动 + 复合形容词

原句：Elastomeric bearings are molded, rubber-like materials that are bonded to the appropriate parts. 拆解：主语 Elastomeric bearings，系动词 are，表语 molded, rubber-like materials（两个形容词并列作定语）；that 引导定语从句，从句为被动语态 are bonded to the appropriate parts。 要点：molded 是过去分词转化来的形容词“模压成型的”，与 rubber-like 并列，不要读成谓语。bond to 意为“粘接到……上”，是胶接工艺的固定搭配。

⑥ instead of 介词短语 + 不定式目的状语

原句：Instead of rotating like conventional bearings, they twist and flex to allow proper movement of the blades. 拆解：Instead of rotating like conventional bearings 为介词短语作状语，表示对比（instead of + 动名词）；主句主语 they，并列谓语 twist and flex；to allow proper movement of the blades 为不定式作目的状语。 要点：instead of 后接动名词 rotating，其逻辑主语与主句主语 they 一致。twist and flex 是弹性体轴承的两种变形方式：扭转与弯曲，区别于常规轴承的滚动。
