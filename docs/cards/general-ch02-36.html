Subpart A—General

通用 Ch 02 · Subpart A—General · 原文 PDF 第 71 页

1. 原文

As mentioned in the brief overview of the regulation portion earlier in this chapter, this part is actually addressing the operation of the aircraft. For example, 14 CFR part 91, section 91.7(a) states “no person may operate a civil aircraft unless it is in an airworthy condition.” We learned earlier that this term means that the aircraft conforms to its approved type design and is in condition for safe operation. When the pilot performs a preflight inspection, they are making a determination concerning the “condition for safe operation.” The pilot does not usually determine “conformity to type design” unless they perform a review of the maintenance records. However, since that is fundamental to the definition of airworthy, it is still part of their responsibility. Therefore, a professional and ethical technician wants to help the customer understand their responsibilities in maintaining and documenting the airworthiness of the aircraft.

2. 中文翻译

正如本章前面法规部分简要概述中所述，这一部实际上针对的是航空器的运行。例如，14 CFR 第 91 部 § 91.7(a) 规定「除处于适航状态外，任何人不得运行民用航空器」。我们此前已了解到，该术语的意思是航空器符合其经批准的型号设计，并处于可安全运行的状态。当驾驶员实施飞行前检查时，他们正是在就「可安全运行的状态」作出判断。驾驶员通常并不判断「与型号设计的一致性」，除非他们查阅维修记录。不过，由于这一点是适航定义的根本组成部分，它仍然属于驾驶员职责的一部分。因此，一名专业且恪守职业道德的维修技师，会希望帮助客户理解其在保持并记录航空器适航性方面的责任。

4. 语法解析

① 引语中的 no person may … unless

原句 no person may operate a civil aircraft unless it is in an airworthy condition.

拆解 no person 为否定主语，may operate 为谓语，a civil aircraft 为宾语；unless 引导条件状语从句 it is in an airworthy condition，it 回指该航空器。

要点 no person may 是法规中「任何人不得」的固定表达，比 nobody can 正式；unless 从句用一般现在时表条件，译「除非处于适航状态」；in an airworthy condition 是状态介词短语，冠词 an 不可省。

② when 从句配现在进行时表同时性

原句 When the pilot performs a preflight inspection, they are making a determination concerning the “condition for safe operation.”

拆解 When 引导时间状语从句，主句用现在进行时 are making，表示与从句动作同时发生、正在作出的判断；they 为单数泛指代词，回指 the pilot；concerning … 为介词短语修饰 determination。

要点 主句用进行时强调「正在作出判断」的同时性，译「就是在作判断」；they 回指单数 pilot，属性别中性用法，译「驾驶员」即可；concerning 在此是介词「关于」，不是分词。

③ since 引导原因状语从句

原句 since that is fundamental to the definition of airworthy, it is still part of their responsibility.

拆解 since 引导原因状语从句，that 指前文的 conformity to type design；主句 it is still part of their responsibility 中，it 指判断符合性这项工作，their 回指技师。

要点 since 在此是「既然、由于」，不是「自从」，须与时间用法区分；fundamental to 后接名词，译「是……的根本」；still 强调「即便如此仍然」，呼应上文的让步语气。
