1. 原文

As a general rule, only rated pilots and qualified airframe and powerplant (A&P) technicians are authorized to start, run up, and taxi aircraft. All taxiing operations are

performed in accordance with applicable local regulations.

Figure 1-22 contains the standard taxi light signals used by control towers to control and expedite the taxiing of aircraft.

The following section provides detailed instructions on taxi signals and related taxi instructions.

4. 语法解析

① be authorized to do + 三个并列不定式

原句：As a general rule, only rated pilots and qualified airframe and powerplant (A&P) technicians are authorized to start, run up, and taxi aircraft.

拆解：As a general rule 为状语；主语由 only 限定，是 rated pilots 与 qualified A&P technicians 的并列；谓语 are authorized to 后接三个并列不定式 start、run up、taxi，后两个共用 to。

要点：be authorized to do 是「被授权做某事」的固定被动搭配；only 紧贴所限定的主语，不能随意后移。并列不定式只有第一个带 to，读时不要以为后两个是原形动词作谓语。

② 被动语态 + in accordance with

原句：All taxiing operations are performed in accordance with applicable local regulations.

拆解：主语 All taxiing operations，其中 taxiing 是动名词作定语（滑行作业）；谓语 are performed 为被动；in accordance with 是复合介词，后接名词短语 applicable local regulations。

要点：in accordance with（依照）比 according to 更正式，是规章文本的常用搭配。taxi 的动名词拼作 taxiing，注意不要漏写中间的 i。

③ 过去分词作后置定语 + 不定式表目的

原句：Figure 1-22 contains the standard taxi light signals used by control towers to control and expedite the taxiing of aircraft.

拆解：主句 Figure 1-22 contains the standard taxi light signals；used by control towers 是过去分词短语作后置定语；to control and expedite 是不定式作目的状语，两个动词并列；the taxiing of aircraft 中 taxiing 是动名词。

要点：过去分词短语与被修饰名词是被动关系，译作「由塔台使用的」，改成 which are used by 则更啰嗦。the taxiing of aircraft 用名词形式，比 taxi aircraft 更适合放在 expedite 之后作宾语。
