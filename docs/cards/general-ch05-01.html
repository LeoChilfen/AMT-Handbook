1. 原文

Chapter 5 Physical science, which is most often called physics, is a very interesting and exciting topic. For an individual who likes technical things and is a hands-on type of person, physics is an invaluable tool. Physics allows us to explain how engines work, both piston and gas turbine; how airplanes and helicopters fly; and countless other things related to the field of aviation and aerospace. In addition to allowing us to explain the operation of the things around us, it also allows us to quantify them. For example, through the use of physics we can explain what the concept of thrust means for a jet engine, and then follow it up by mathematically calculating the pounds of thrust being created.

Physics is the term applied to an area of knowledge regarding the basic and fundamental nature of matter and energy. It does not attempt to determine why matter and energy behave as they do in their relation to physical phenomena, but rather how they behave. The people who maintain and repair aircraft should have knowledge of basic physics, which is sometimes called the science of matter and energy.

2. 中文翻译

物理科学（physical science）常被直接称为物理学（physics），是一门非常有趣、令人兴奋的学科。对于喜欢技术性内容、属于动手实操型的人来说，物理学是一件无价的工具。物理学使我们能够解释发动机——无论是活塞式还是燃气涡轮式——如何工作，飞机与直升机如何飞行，以及无数与航空、航天领域相关的事物。除了让我们能够解释身边事物的运行原理之外，物理学还让我们能够对它们进行量化。例如，借助物理学，我们既能说明推力（thrust）这一概念对喷气发动机意味着什么，又能进一步用数学方法计算出正在产生的推力磅数。

物理学（physics）这个词被用来指称一个知识领域，它研究物质与能量的基本、根本性质。物理学并不试图确定物质与能量在与各种物理现象的关系中为何如此表现，而是要确定它们如何表现。从事航空器维护与修理的人员应当具备基础物理学的知识，这门学问有时被称为关于物质与能量的科学。

4. 语法解析

① 非限制性定语从句：which is most often called physics

原句：Chapter 5 Physical science, which is most often called physics, is a very interesting and exciting topic.

拆解：主语是 Physical science（承 Chapter 5 的标题而来），which 引导非限制性定语从句补充说明它的常用名，主句谓语是 is；a very interesting and exciting topic 作表语。

要点：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，只补充信息，删去不影响主句成立；此处 which 指前面的 physical science，不能换成 that。手册常在术语首次出现时用这种从句给出同义名，如 the empennage, which is also called the tail section。

② allow sb to do sth 后接并列宾语

原句：Physics allows us to explain how engines work, both piston and gas turbine; how airplanes and helicopters fly; and countless other things related to the field of aviation and aerospace.

拆解：allow + 宾语 us + 不定式 to explain；explain 后带三个并列宾语：两个 how 引导的宾语从句（how engines work / how airplanes and helicopters fly）和一个名词短语 countless other things；both piston and gas turbine 是 engines 的同位语。

要点：allow 的结构是 allow sb to do sth，不能写成 allow sb do sth；被动式则为 be allowed to do。解释原理时英文惯用 how 从句而非 what，译成中文多为「……如何……」。三个并列项用分号隔开，读长句时先数分号再找主谓。

③ not … but rather … 对比结构

原句：It does not attempt to determine why matter and energy behave as they do in their relation to physical phenomena, but rather how they behave.

拆解：否定部分 does not attempt to determine + why 宾语从句；but rather 引出被肯定的对象 how they behave，与前面的 why 形成对照，determine 的宾语由这两个从句共同承担。

要点：not … but rather 是「不是……而是……」的正式说法，rather 可以省略；前后两项必须同型（这里都是 wh- 从句），否则句子失衡。as they do 中的 do 是代动词，代替 behave，避免重复。
