1. 原文

Trim Tabs Trim tabs are small movable surfaces that attach to the trailing edge of flight controls. These tabs can be controlled from the flight deck, and their purpose is to create an aerodynamic force that keeps the flight control in a deflected position. Trim tabs can be installed on any of the primary flight controls.

A very common flight control to find fitted with a trim tab is the elevator. In order to be stable in flight, most airplanes have the center of gravity located forward of the center of lift on the wing. This causes a nose heavy condition, which needs to be balanced out by having the elevator deflect upwards and create a downward force. To relieve the pilot of the need to hold back pressure on the control wheel, a trim tab on the elevator can be adjusted to hold the elevator in a slightly deflected position. An elevator trim tab for a Cessna 182 is shown in Figure 5-71.

Anti-servo Tab Some airplanes, like a Piper Cherokee Arrow, do not have a fixed horizontal stabilizer and movable elevator. The Cherokee uses a moving horizontal surface known as a stabilator. Because of the location of the pivot point for this movable surface, it has a tendency to be extremely sensitive to pilot input. To reduce the sensitivity, a full length anti-servo tab is installed on the trailing edge of the stabilator. As the trailing edge of the stabilator moves down, the anti-servo tab moves down and creates a force trying to raise the trailing edge. With this force acting against the movement of the stabilator, it reduces the sensitivity to pilot input. The anti- servo tab on a Piper Cherokee Arrow is shown in Figure 5-70.

Balance Tab On some airplanes, the force needed to move the flight controls can be excessive. In these cases, a balance tab can be used to generate a force that assists in the movement of the flight control. Just the opposite of anti-servo tabs, balance tabs move in the opposite direction of the flight control’s trailing edge, providing a force that helps the flight control move.

2. 中文翻译

配平片 配平片是附装在飞行操纵面后缘的小型可动翼面。这些调整片可在驾驶舱内操纵，其作用是产生一种空气动力，把操纵面保持在偏转位置上。配平片可安装在任何一种主飞行操纵面上。

升降舵是最常见装设配平片的飞行操纵面。为在飞行中保持稳定，大多数飞机的重心位于机翼升力中心之前。这会造成头重状态，需要通过让升降舵上偏、产生一个向下的力来加以平衡。为使飞行员不必持续对操纵盘保持带杆力，可调整升降舵上的配平片，把升降舵保持在略微偏转的位置。Cessna 182 的升降舵配平片见图 5-71。

抗伺服调整片 有些飞机，如 Piper Cherokee Arrow，并没有固定的水平安定面加可动的升降舵。Cherokee 采用的是称为 stabilator 的可动水平翼面。由于该可动翼面支点位置的原因，它对飞行员的输入有极为敏感的倾向。为降低这种敏感性，在 stabilator 后缘安装了全长的抗伺服调整片。当 stabilator 后缘下偏时，抗伺服调整片随之下偏，并产生一个力图抬起后缘的力。该力与 stabilator 的运动方向相反，从而降低了对飞行员输入的敏感性。Piper Cherokee Arrow 上的抗伺服调整片见图 5-70。

平衡调整片 在某些飞机上，移动飞行操纵面所需的力可能过大。在这种情况下，可用平衡调整片来产生一个协助飞行操纵面运动的力。与抗伺服调整片正好相反，平衡调整片朝操纵面后缘的相反方向运动，从而提供一个帮助操纵面运动的力。

4. 语法解析

① their purpose is to do + keep sth in a … position

原句：These tabs can be controlled from the flight deck, and their purpose is to create an aerodynamic force that keeps the flight control in a deflected position.

拆解：并列句：前一分句为情态动词被动 can be controlled；后一分句 their purpose is to create … 用不定式作表语说明用途；that keeps … 是定语从句修饰 force，其中 keep sth in a … position 是「动词 + 宾语 + 介词短语」的复合结构。

要点：purpose/function/aim 作主语时，表语用不定式 to do 表示「用来做……」。keep + 宾语 + 介词短语/形容词表示「使……保持某状态」，此处 in a deflected position 不能换成 into。from the flight deck 的 from 表示操纵的发出地点。

② have sth done + by having sb do（方式状语）

原句：In order to be stable in flight, most airplanes have the center of gravity located forward of the center of lift on the wing. This causes a nose heavy condition, which needs to be balanced out by having the elevator deflect upwards and create a downward force.

拆解：第一句 In order to be stable in flight 是目的状语，主句为 have the center of gravity located …（have + 宾语 + 过去分词，表示「使……处于某位置」）；第二句中 which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 condition，by having the elevator deflect … 是方式状语，have sb/sth do 为不带 to 的复合结构。

要点：have + 宾语 + 过去分词表示「让某事被做／使处于某状态」，而 have + 宾语 + 动词原形表示「让某人做某事」，两者含义与语态都不同。by + 动名词说明手段，此处 by having … 译作「通过让……」。forward of ＝「在……之前（方）」，是航空文本常用的方位表达，等于 in front of。

③ rather than + 动名词表对比取舍

原句：The servo tab is acting like a balance tab, but rather than assisting the normal force that moves the elevator, it becomes the sole force that makes the elevator move.

拆解：but 连接两个分句；rather than assisting … 是介词短语（rather than + 动名词）作让步／对比状语，插在主句 it becomes the sole force 之前；that moves the elevator 与 that makes the elevator move 均为定语从句。

要点：rather than 后接动名词时强调「而不是（做）某事」，与前面的动作形成对照；接不定式时通常不带 to，接名词时直接用名词。它与 instead of 语义相近，但 rather than 更强调主观取舍。sole 意为「唯一的」，比 only 更书面，常见于 sole source、sole means。
