Water & Waste Tank Ice Prevention

机体 Ch 15 · Water & Waste Tank Ice Prevention · 原文 PDF 第 910 页

1. 原文

Transport type aircraft have water and waste systems on board, and electrical heaters are often used to prevent the formation of ice in the water lines of these systems. Water lines carry water from the potable tanks to the lavatories and galleys. The waste water tanks collect the gray water from the galleys and lavatories. Heater blankets, inline heaters, or heater boots are often used to heat the water supply lines, water tank drain hoses, waste drain lines, waste tank rinse fittings, and drain masts. Thermostats in the water lines supply temperature data to the control unit that turns the electrical heaters on and off. When the temperature falls below freezing, the electrical heaters turn on and stay on until the temperature reaches a safe temperature. 2. 中文翻译

运输类飞机上装有水和废水系统，常采用电加热器防止这些系统的水管内结冰。水管把水从饮用水箱输送到盥洗室和厨房。废水箱收集来自厨房和盥洗室的灰水。加热毯、管路内加热器或加热套常用于加热供水管、水箱排放软管、废水排放管、废水箱冲洗接头以及排放桅杆。水管内的恒温器把温度数据输送给控制组件，由控制组件接通和断开电加热器。当温度降到冰点以下时，电加热器接通，并一直保持接通直至温度达到安全温度。图15-47 是供水管加温系统的示意图，图15-48 示出废水箱与加热毯的位置。

4. 语法解析

① and 连接的两个并列分句，时态与语态不同

原句：Transport type aircraft have water and waste systems on board, and electrical heaters are often used to prevent the formation of ice in the water lines of these systems. 拆解：and 前为主动句，主语 Transport type aircraft，谓语 have，宾语 water and waste systems，on board 为地点状语；and 后为被动句，主语 electrical heaters，谓语 are used，to prevent... 为目的状语。 要点：on board 是“在机上”，作状语而非定语，故置于句末。两句时态都为一般现在时，但语态不同，说明前者陈述配置、后者陈述做法。

② 三项并列主语 + 五项并列宾语

原句：Heater blankets, inline heaters, or heater boots are often used to heat the water supply lines, water tank drain hoses, waste drain lines, waste tank rinse fittings, and drain masts. 拆解：主语为 Heater blankets、inline heaters、heater boots 三项，用 or 连接，谓语 are used 取复数；to heat 为目的状语，后接五个并列宾语，最后两项用 and 连接。 要点：主语用 or 连接时，谓语仍按复数的 are（因为 are 后接 used，此处书面语中就近为 boots 复数）。to heat 后接的是加热对象清单，译文应完整列出五项，不能合并省略。

③ that 引导定语从句，先行词为 control unit

原句：Thermostats in the water lines supply temperature data to the control unit that turns the electrical heaters on and off. 拆解：主语 Thermostats，in the water lines 为后置定语；谓语 supply，宾语 temperature data，to the control unit 为方向状语；that 引导定语从句修饰 control unit，从句谓语 turns...on and off 为“动词 + 宾语 + 副词”结构。 要点：that 从句紧跟 control unit，不能误修饰 thermostats——是控制组件在通断加热器。turn sth. on and off 中代词作宾语时须说 turn it on，名词可前置或后置，此处名词后置。

④ when 与 until 两个时间从句呼应

原句：When the temperature falls below freezing, the electrical heaters turn on and stay on until the temperature reaches a safe temperature. 拆解：When 引导时间状语从句，below freezing 为介词短语表“低于冰点”；主句含两个并列谓语 turn on 与 stay on；until 引导时间状语从句，说明“保持接通”的终点。 要点：until 从句用一般现在时表将来。stay on 中的 on 是副词表“接通状态”，与 turn on 的 on 同源但功能不同，译文“保持接通”要把这一状态译出。safe temperature 与 freezing 形成上下限对照。

⑤ 两个并列分句分别指图，is 与 shows 对应

原句：Figure 15-47 is a schematic of a water supply line heater system, and Figure 15-48 shows the location of the waste water tanks and heater blankets. 拆解：and 连接两个独立分句，第一句为“主语 + be + 表语”结构（a schematic of... 为表语，of 短语说明示意图的内容）；第二句主语 Figure 15-48，谓语 shows，宾语 the location of...。 要点：同类句式在技术英语中反复出现（Figure N is / shows），is 用于“是……图”，shows 用于“示出……”。location of 后接两个并列名词，指“位置”，不可译成“定位”。
