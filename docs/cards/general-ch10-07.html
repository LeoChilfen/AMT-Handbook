Hard or Overweight Landing Inspection

The structural stress induced by a landing depends not only upon the gross weight at the time, but also upon the severity of impact. The hard landing inspection is for hard landings at or below the maximum design landing limits. An overweight landing inspection must be performed when an airplane lands at a weight above the maximum design landing weight. However, because of the difficulty in estimating vertical velocity at the time of contact, it is hard to judge whether or not a landing has been sufficiently severe to cause structural damage. For this reason, a special inspection is performed after a landing is made at a weight known to exceed the design landing weight or after a rough landing, even though the latter may have occurred when the aircraft did not exceed the design landing weight.

Wrinkled wing skin is the most easily detected sign of an excessive load having been imposed during a landing. Another indication easily detected is fuel leakage along riveted seams. Other possible locations of damage are spar webs, bulkheads, nacelle skin and attachments, firewall skin, and wing and fuselage stringers. If none of these areas show adverse effects, it is reasonable to assume that no serious damage has occurred. If damage is detected, a more extensive inspection and alignment check may be necessary.

Severe Turbulence Inspection/Over “G”

” When an aircraft encounters a gust condition, the airload on the wings exceeds the normal wingload supporting the aircraft weight. The gust tends to accelerate the aircraft while its inertia acts to resist this change. If the combination of gust velocity and airspeed is too severe, the induced stress can cause structural damage.

A special inspection is performed after a flight through severe turbulence. Emphasis is placed upon inspecting the upper and lower wing surfaces for excessive buckles or wrinkles with permanent set. Where wrinkles have occurred, remove a few rivets and examine the rivet shanks to determine if the rivets have sheared or were highly loaded in shear.

Through the inspection doors and other accessible openings, inspect all spar webs from the fuselage to the tip. Check for buckling, wrinkles, and sheared attachments. Inspect for buckling in the area around the nacelles and in the nacelle skin, particularly at the wing leading edge. Check for fuel leaks. Any sizeable fuel leak is an indication that an area may have received overloads that have broken the sealant and opened the seams.

Lightning Strike

Although lightning strikes to aircraft are extremely rare, if a strike has occurred, the aircraft is carefully inspected to determine the extent of any damage that might have occurred. When lightning strikes an aircraft, the electrical current must be conducted through the structure and be allowed to discharge or dissipate at controlled locations. These controlled locations are primarily the aircraft’s static discharge wicks, or on more sophisticated aircraft, null field dischargers. When surges of high-voltage electricity pass through good electrical conductors, such as aluminum or steel, damage is likely to be minimal or nonexistent. When surges of high-voltage electricity pass through non-metallic structures, such as a fiberglass radome, engine cowl or fairing, glass or plastic window, or a composite structure that does not have built-in electrical bonding, burning and more serious damage to the structure could occur. Visual inspection of the structure is required. Look for evidence of degradation, burning, or erosion of the composite resin at all affected structures, electrical bonding straps, static discharge wicks, and null field dischargers.

When the aircraft is hit by birds during flight, the external areas of the airplane are inspected in the general area of the bird strike. If the initial inspection shows structural damage, then the internal structure of the airplane must be inspected as well. Also, inspect the hydraulic, pneumatic, and any other systems in the area of the bird strike.

Inspection of aircraft structures that have been subjected to fire or intense heat can be relatively simple if visible damage is present. Visible damage requires repair or replacement. If there is no visible damage, the structural integrity of an aircraft may still have been compromised. Since most structural metallic components of an aircraft have undergone some sort of heat-treatment process during manufacture, an exposure to high heat not encountered during normal operations could severely degrade the design strength of the structure. The strength and airworthiness of an aluminum structure that passes a visual inspection, but is still suspect, can be further determined by use of a conductivity tester. This is a device that uses eddy current and is discussed later in this chapter. Since strength of metals is related to hardness, possible damage to steel structures might be determined by use of a hardness tester, such as a Rockwell C hardness tester.

Like aircraft damaged by fire, aircraft damaged by water can range from minor to severe. This depends on the level of the flood water, whether it was fresh or salt water, and the elapsed time between the flood occurrence and when repairs were initiated. Any parts that were totally submerged are completely disassembled, thoroughly cleaned, dried, and treated with a corrosion inhibitor. Many parts might have to be replaced, particularly interior carpeting, seats, side panels, and instruments. Since water serves as an electrolyte that promotes corrosion, all traces of water and salt must be removed before the aircraft can again be considered airworthy.

Because they operate in an environment that accelerates corrosion, seaplanes must be carefully inspected for corrosion and conditions that promote corrosion. Inspect bilge areas for waste hydraulic fluids, water, dirt, drill chips, and other debris. Additionally, since seaplanes often encounter excessive stress from the pounding of rough water at high speeds, inspect for loose rivets and other fasteners; stretched, bent or cracked skins; damage to the float attach fitting; and general wear and tear on the entire structure.

Aerial Application Aircraft

Two primary factors that make inspecting these aircraft different from other aircraft are the corrosive nature of some of the chemicals used and the typical flight profile. Damaging effects of corrosion may be detected in a much shorter period of time than normal use aircraft. Chemicals may soften the fabric or loosen the fabric tapes of fabric-covered aircraft. Metal aircraft may need to have the paint stripped, cleaned, and repainted and corrosion treated annually. Leading edges of wings and other areas may require protective coatings or tapes. Hardware may require more frequent replacement. During peak use, these aircraft may fly up to 50 cycles (takeoffs and landings) or more in a day, most likely from an unimproved or grass runway. This can greatly accelerate the failure of normal fatigue items. Landing gear and related items require frequent inspections. Because these aircraft operate almost continuously at very low altitudes, air filters tend to become obstructed more rapidly.

重着陆或超重着陆检查

着陆所引起的结构应力，不仅取决于当时的全重，还取决于撞击的剧烈程度。重着陆检查针对的是以最大设计着陆限制或低于该限制着陆的重着陆。当飞机以超过最大设计着陆重量的重量着陆时，必须进行超重着陆检查。然而，由于难以估计接地瞬间的垂直速度，很难判断一次着陆是否严重到足以造成结构损伤。因此，在已知超过设计着陆重量的重量下着陆之后，或在粗暴着陆之后——即使后者发生时航空器并未超过设计着陆重量——都要实施一次特别检查。

(3) 对航空器进行必要分解和正确检查所需的足够机库与设备；以及 (4) 适用于该航空器的现行适当技术资料。

渐进式检查的频次和详细程度，应保证在每个 12 个日历月内对航空器完成一次完整检查，并与制造商的建议、外场使用经验以及该航空器所从事的运行种类相一致。渐进式检查计划必须确保航空器在任何时候都适航，并符合所有适用的 FAA 航空器规范、型号合格证数据表、适航指令和其他经批准的资料。如果中止渐进式检查，所有人或运营人应立即以书面形式将中止一事通知当地 FAA 飞行标准地区办公室。中止之后，按 §91.409(a)(1) 的首次年度检查应在航空器按渐进式检查完成的最后一次完整检查之后 12 个日历月内到期。按 §91.409(b) 的 100 小时检查应在该次完整检查之后 100 小时内到期。就确定年度检查和 100 小时检查何时到期而言，对航空器的完整检查要求按渐进式检查对航空器及其所有部件作详细检查。对航空器的例行检查再加上对若干部件的详细检查，不算一次完整检查。

机翼蒙皮起皱，是着陆过程中施加了过大载荷最容易发现的迹象。另一个容易发现的迹象是铆接缝处的燃油渗漏。其他可能的损伤部位是翼梁腹板、隔框、短舱蒙皮及其连接件、防火墙蒙皮，以及机翼和机身长桁。如果这些部位都没有不良影响，可以合理地认为未发生严重损伤。如果发现了损伤，则可能需要更广泛的检查和结构对合检查。

严重湍流检查／超“G”检查

当航空器遇到阵风时，机翼上的空气动力载荷超过支撑航空器重量的正常机翼载荷。阵风趋向于使航空器加速，而其惯性则阻碍这一变化。如果阵风速度与空速的组合过于严重，诱发的应力就可能造成结构损伤。

在飞越严重湍流之后要实施一次特别检查。重点检查机翼上下表面有无带永久变形的过度鼓胀或起皱。凡出现起皱处，拆下几颗铆钉并检查铆钉杆，判断铆钉是否被剪断，或在剪切中承受了很高的载荷。

通过检查门和其他可触及的开口，检查从机身到翼尖的所有翼梁腹板。检查有无鼓胀、起皱和被剪断的连接件。检查短舱周围区域和短舱蒙皮有无鼓胀，尤其是机翼前缘处。检查有无燃油渗漏。任何明显的燃油渗漏都表明该区域可能承受过超载，使密封胶破裂、接缝张开。

如果在严重湍流期间放下了起落架，应仔细检查周围表面有无铆钉松动、裂纹或鼓胀。轮舱内部可能进一步显示过度阵风的情况。检查机身蒙皮的上下表面。过大的弯矩可能在这些区域留下斜向的皱褶。

雷击

尽管航空器遭雷击极为罕见，但一旦发生雷击，就要仔细检查航空器，以确定可能发生的损伤范围。雷击航空器时，电流必须通过结构传导，并被允许在受控部位放电或消散。这些受控部位主要是航空器的静电放电刷，或在更先进的航空器上是零场放电器。当高压电涌通过铝或钢等良导体时，损伤可能很小或不存在。当高压电涌通过非金属结构时，例如玻璃纤维雷达罩、发动机整流罩或包皮、玻璃或塑料窗，或没有内置电气搭接的复合材料结构，就可能发生烧蚀和更严重的结构损伤。需要对结构进行目视检查。在所有受影响的部位、电气搭接带、静电放电刷和零场放电器上，寻找复合材料树脂劣化、烧蚀或侵蚀的迹象。

当航空器在飞行中遭鸟撞时，要检查飞机外部鸟撞大致区域。如果初步检查显示结构损伤，则还必须检查飞机的内部结构。此外，还要检查鸟撞区域的液压、气动和任何其他系统。

检查遭受火灾或强烈高温的航空器结构，如果存在可见损伤，则相对简单。可见损伤需要修理或更换。如果没有可见损伤，航空器的结构完整性仍可能已受损。由于航空器大多数金属结构件在制造过程中都经过某种热处理，遭受正常使用中不会遇到的高温，可能严重降低结构的设计强度。对通过目视检查但仍然可疑的铝结构，可用导电率测试仪进一步确定其强度和适航性。这是一种利用涡流的装置，本章后面将予以讨论。由于金属的强度与硬度相关，钢结构的可能损伤可以用硬度计确定，例如 Rockwell C 硬度计。

与遭火灾损伤的航空器一样，遭水损伤的航空器也有轻有重。这取决于洪水水位、是淡水还是盐水，以及洪水发生到开始修理之间经过的时间。凡完全浸没的零件都要完全分解、彻底清洁、干燥，并做缓蚀处理。许多零件可能必须更换，尤其是内部地毯、座椅、侧板和仪表。由于水是促进腐蚀的电解质，在航空器重新被认为适航之前，必须清除所有水和盐的痕迹。

由于水上飞机在加速腐蚀的环境中运行，必须仔细检查其腐蚀情况和促进腐蚀的条件。检查舱底区域有无废液压油、水、污物、钻屑和其他杂物。此外，由于水上飞机在高速下击水常承受过大应力，应检查铆钉和其他紧固件是否松动；蒙皮是否被拉伸、弯曲或开裂；浮筒连接接头是否损伤；以及整个结构的一般磨损。

农业喷洒航空器

使这类航空器的检查与其他航空器不同的两个主要因素，是所用某些化学品的腐蚀性和典型的飞行剖面。腐蚀的破坏效应可能在比正常使用的航空器短得多的时间内被发现。化学品可能使蒙布变软，或使蒙布航空器的蒙布带松开。金属航空器可能每年都需要脱漆、清洁、重新喷漆并进行防腐处理。机翼前缘和其他区域可能需要保护涂层或胶带。五金件可能需要更频繁地更换。在使用高峰时，这类航空器一天可飞 50 个循环（起飞和着陆）以上，而且大多从简易跑道或草地跑道起飞。这会大大加速正常疲劳件的失效。起落架及相关部件需要频繁检查。由于这类航空器几乎一直在极低空连续运行，空气滤清器往往更快堵塞。

4. 语法解析

① when 引导的定语从句 + 名词后置介词短语

原句：During the service life of an aircraft, occasions may arise when something out of the ordinary care and use of an aircraft could possibly affect its airworthiness. 拆解：During the service life of an aircraft 为时间状语；主句 occasions may arise；when 引导定语从句修饰 occasions，从句主语 something 后接 out of the ordinary care and use of an aircraft 作后置修饰；could possibly affect 为谓语。 要点：arise 是不及物动词，没有被动形式，别写成 may be arisen；这里的 when 从句修饰先行词 occasions，不是时间状语，译成「……的情况」；something out of … 这类「代词 + 介词短语」结构里，介词短语起定语作用。

② 现在分词后置定语 + shall 的法定义务

原句：Each person performing an annual or 100-hour inspection shall inspect (where applicable) the following components of the landing gear group: 拆解：主语 Each person 后接现在分词短语 performing … 作后置定语；谓语 shall inspect；宾语 the following components of the landing gear group；括号内 where applicable 为插入语，限定适用范围。 要点：shall 在法规文本中表「应当、必须」，比 must 更正式，不能译成「将」；perform an inspection 是固定动宾搭配；where applicable「在适用时」是法规文书的高频插入语，位置灵活。

③ not only … but also 的平行介词短语

原句：The structural stress induced by a landing depends not only upon the gross weight at the time, but also upon the severity of impact. 拆解：主语 The structural stress，过去分词短语 induced by a landing 作后置定语；谓语 depends upon，后面是 not only upon … but also upon … 的平行结构；at the time 作 gross weight 的后置定语。 要点：depend on／upon 意为「取决于」，本身就含被动意味，不能用 be depended on；not only 与 but also 后必须接同类成分，这里是两个 upon 短语；induced by 是「由……引起」的固定分词搭配。

④ because of + 形式主语 it + whether or not 宾语从句

原句：Because of the difficulty in estimating vertical velocity at the time of contact, it is hard to judge whether or not a landing has been sufficiently severe to cause structural damage. 拆解：Because of 引导原因状语，只能接名词短语；主句 it 是形式主语，真正主语为不定式 to judge …；whether or not 引导宾语从句，从句用现在完成时 has been；sufficiently … to cause 是「足够……以致」结构。 要点：because of 后不能接句子，接句子要用 because；difficulty in doing sth 中的 in 不能换成 to do；it is hard to judge 里的 it 不可换成 that，也不能省。

⑤ 条件从句 + 祈使句的维修指令句式

原句：If the landing gear was lowered during a period of severe turbulence, inspect the surrounding surfaces carefully for loose rivets, cracks, or buckling. 拆解：If 引导条件从句，was lowered 为被动；主句是祈使句 inspect …；for loose rivets, cracks, or buckling 说明检查目标；carefully 修饰 inspect。 要点：维修手册大量使用「条件从句 + 祈使句」给出操作步骤，祈使句无主语，译成中文时补「应」；inspect sth for sth 表示「检查某物以查某问题」；三项并列用逗号分隔、最后一项前加 or。
