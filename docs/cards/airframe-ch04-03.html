1. 原文

Drilling holes is a common operation in the airframe repair shop. Once the fundamentals of drills and their uses are learned, drilling holes for rivets and bolts on light metal is not difficult. While a small portable power drill is usually the most practical tool for this common operation in airframe metalwork, sometimes a drill press may prove to be the better piece of equipment for the job.

Portable Power Drills

Portable power drills operate by electricity or compressed air. Pneumatic drill motors are recommended for use on repairs around flammable materials where potential sparks from an electric drill motor might become a fire hazard.

When using the portable power drill, hold it firmly with both hands. Before drilling, be sure to place a backup block of wood under the hole to be drilled to add support to the metal structure. The drill bit should be inserted in the chuck and tested for trueness or vibration. This may be visibly checked by running the motor freely. A drill bit that wobbles or is slightly bent should not be used since such a condition causes enlarged holes. The drill should always be held at right angles to the work regardless of the position or curvatures. Tilting the drill at any time when drilling into or withdrawing from the material may cause elongation (egg shape) of the hole. When drilling through sheet metal, small burrs are formed around the edge of the hole. Burrs must be removed to allow rivets or bolts to fit snugly and to prevent scratching. Burrs may be removed with a bearing scraper, a countersink, or a drill bit larger than the hole. If a drill bit or countersink is used, it should be rotated by hand. Always wear safety goggles while drilling.

Pneumatic Drill Motors

Pneumatic drill motors are the most common type of drill motor for aircraft repair work. They are lightweight and have sufficient power and good speed control. Drill motors are available in many different sizes and models. Most drill motors used for aircraft sheet metal work are rated at 3,000 rpm, but if drilling deep holes or drilling in hard materials, such as corrosion resistant steel or titanium, a drill motor with more torque and lower rpm should be selected to prevent damage to tools and materials.

Right Angle & 45°Drill Motors

Right angle and 45° drill motors are used for positions that are not accessible with a pistol grip drill motor. Most right angle drill motors use threaded drill bits that are available in several lengths. Heavy-duty right angle drills are equipped with a chuck similar to the pistol grip drill motor.

Two Hole

Special drill motors that drill two holes at the same time are used for the installation of nutplates. By drilling two holes at the same time, the distance between the holes is fixed and the holes line up perfectly with the holes in the nutplate.

Drill Press

The drill press is a precision machine used for drilling holes that require a high degree of accuracy. It serves as an accurate means of locating and maintaining the direction of a hole that is to be drilled and provides the operator with a feed lever that makes the task of feeding the drill into the work easier. The upright drill press is the most common of the variety of drill presses available.

When using a drill press, the height of the drill press table is adjusted to accommodate the height of the part to be drilled. When the height of the part is greater than the distance between the drill and the table, the table is lowered. When the height of the part is less than the distance between the drill and the table, the table is raised.

After the table is properly adjusted, the part is placed on the table and the drill is brought down to aid in positioning the metal so that the hole to be drilled is directly beneath the point of the drill. The part is then clamped to the drill press table to prevent it from slipping during the drilling operation. Parts not properly clamped may bind on the drill and start spinning, causing serious cuts on the operator’s arms or body, or loss of fingers or hands. Always make sure the part to be drilled is properly clamped to the drill press table before starting the drilling operation.

The degree of accuracy that it is possible to attain when using the drill press depends to a certain extent on the condition of the spindle hole, sleeves, and drill shank. Therefore, special care must be exercised to keep these parts clean and free from nicks, dents, and warpage. Always be sure that the sleeve is securely pressed into the spindle hole. Never insert a broken drill in a sleeve or spindle hole. Be careful never to use the sleeve-clamping vise to remove a drill since this may cause the sleeve to warp.

The drill speed on a drill press is adjustable. Always select the optimum drill speed for the material to be drilled. Technically, the speed of a drill bit means its speed at the circumference, in surface feet per minute (sfm). The recommended speed for drilling aluminum alloy is from 200 to 300 sfm, and for mild steel is 30 to 50 sfm. In practice, this must be converted into rpm for each size drill. Machinist and mechanic handbooks include drill rpm charts or drill rpm may be computed by use of the formula: CS × 4 = rpmD CS = The recommended cutting speed in sfm D = The diameter of the drill bit in inches When access to a place where drilling is difficult or impossible with a straight drill motor, various types of drill extensions and adapters are used.

Drill Extensions & Adapters

Extension Drill Bits

Extension drill bits are widely used for drilling holes in locations that require reaching through small openings or past projections. These drill bits, which come in 6-to 12-inch lengths, are high speed with spring-tempered shanks. Extension drill bits are ground to a special notched point, which reduces end thrust to a minimum. When using extension drill bits always:

  1. Select the shortest drill bit that will do the job. It is easier to control.
  1. Check the drill bit for straightness. A bent drill bit makes an oversized hole and may whip, making it difficult to control.
  1. Keep the drill bit under control. Extension drills smaller than 1 ⁄ 4-inch must be supported by a drill guard made from a piece of tubing or spring to prevent whipping.

Straight Extension

A straight extension for a drill can be made from an ordinary piece of drill rod. The drill bit is attached to the drill rod by shrink fitting, brazing, or silver soldering.

Angle Adapters

Angle adapters can be attached to an electric or pneumatic drill when the location of the hole is inaccessible to a straight drill. Angle adapters have an extended shank fastened to the chuck of the drill. The drill is held in one hand and the adapter in the other to prevent the adapter from spinning around the drill chuck.

Snake Attachment

The snake attachment is a flexible extension used for drilling in places inaccessible to ordinary drills. Available for electric and pneumatic drill motors, its flexibility permits drilling around obstructions with minimum effort.

Types of Drill Bits

A wide variety of drill bits including specialty bits for specific jobs are available. Figure 4-44 illustrates the parts of the drill bit and Figure 4-45 shows some commonly used drill bits. High speed steel (HSS) drill bits come in short shank or standard length, sometimes called jobbers length. HSS drill bits can withstand temperatures nearing the critical range of 1,400 °F (dark cherry red) without losing their hardness. The industry standard for drilling metal (aluminum, steel, etc.), these drill bits stay sharper longer.

Step Drill Bits

Typically, the procedure for drilling holes larger than 3 ⁄ 16 inch in sheet metal is to drill a pilot hole with a No. 40 or No. 30 drill bit and then to oversize with a larger drill bit to the correct size. The step drill combines these two functions into one step. The step drill bit consists of a smaller pilot drill point that drills the initial small hole. When the drill bit is advanced further into the material, the second step of the drill bit enlarges the hole to the desired size.

Step drill bits are designed to drill round holes in most metals, plastic, and wood. Commonly used in general construction and plumbing, they work best on softer materials, such as plywood, but can be used on very thin sheet metal. Step drill bits can also be used to deburr holes left by other bits.

Cobalt Alloy Drill Bits

Cobalt alloy drill bits are designed for hard, tough metals like corrosion-resistant steel and titanium. It is important for the aircraft technician to note the difference between HSS and cobalt, because HSS drill bits wear out quickly when drilling titanium or stainless. Cobalt drill bits are excellent for drilling titanium or stainless steel, but do not produce a quality hole in aluminum alloys. Cobalt drill bits can be recognized by thicker webs and a taper at the end of the drill shank.

Twist Drill Bits

Easily the most popular drill bit type, the twist drill bit has spiral grooves or flutes running along its working length. This drill bit comes in a single-fluted, two-fluted, three-fluted, and four-fluted styles. Single-fluted and two-fluted drill bits (most commonly available) are used for originating holes. Three-fluted and four-fluted drill bits are used interchangeably to enlarge existing holes. Twist drill bits are available in a wide choice of tooling materials and lengths with the variations targeting specific projects.

The standard twist drill bits used for drilling aluminum are made from HSS and have a 135° split point. Drill bits for titanium are made from cobalt vanadium for increased wear resistance.

Drill Lubrication

Normal drilling of sheet material does not require lubrication, but lubrication should be provided for all deeper drilling Lubricants serve to assist in chip removal, which prolongs drill life and ensures a good finish and dimensional accuracy of the hole. It does not prevent overheating. The use of a lubricant is always a good practice when drilling castings, forgings, or heavy gauge stock. A good lubricant should be thin enough to help in chip removal but thick enough to stick to the drill. For aluminum, titanium, and corrosion-resistant steel, a cetyl alcohol based lubricant is the most satisfactory. Cetyl alcohol is a nontoxic fatty alcohol chemical produced in liquid, paste, and solid forms. The solid stick and block forms quickly liquefy at drilling temperatures. For steel, sulfurized mineral cutting oil is superior. Sulfur has an affinity for steel, which aids in holding the cutting oil in place. In the case of deep drilling, the drill should be withdrawn at intervals to relieve chip packing and to ensure the lubricant reaches the point. As a general rule, if the drill is large or the material hard, use a lubricant.

Reamers

Reamers, used for enlarging holes and finishing them smooth to a required size, are made in many styles. They can be straight or tapered, solid or expansive, and come with straight or helical flutes. Figure 4-48 illustrates three types of reamers:

  1. Three or four fluted production bullet reamers are customarily used where a finer finish and/or size is needed than can be achieved with a standard drill bit.
  1. Standard or straight reamer.
  1. Piloted reamer, with the end reduced to provide accurate alignment.

The cylindrical parts of most straight reamers are not cutting edges, but merely grooves cut for the full length of the reamer body. These grooves provide a way for chips to escape and a channel for lubricant to reach the cutting edge. Actual cutting is done on the end of the reamer. The cutting edges are normally ground to a bevel of 45° ± 5°.

Reamer flutes are not designed to remove chips like a drill. Do not attempt to withdraw a reamer by turning it in the reverse direction because chips can be forced into the surface, scarring the hole.

Drill Stops

A spring drill stop is a wise investment. Properly adjusted, it can prevent excessive drill penetration that might damage underlying structure or injure personnel and prevent the drill chuck from marring the surface. Drill stops can be made from tubing, fiber rod, or hard rubber.

Drill Bushings & Guides

There are several types of tools available that aid in holding the drill perpendicular to the part. They consist of a hardened bushing anchored in a holder.

  1. Tube—hand-held in an existing hole There are four types of drill bushing holder:

Drill Bushing Holder Types

  1. Standard—fine for drilling flat stock or tubing/rod; uses insert-type bushings.
  1. Egg cup—improvement on standard tripod base; allows drilling on both flat and curved material; interchangeable bushings allows flexibility.
  1. Plate—used primarily for interchangeable production components; uses commercial bushings and self-feeding drills.
  1. Arm—used when drilling critical structure; can be locked in position; uses interchangeable commercial bushings.

Hole Drilling Techniques

Precise location of drilled holes is sometimes required. When locating holes to close tolerances, accurately located punch marks need to be made. If a punch mark is too small, the chisel edge of the drill bit may bridge it and “walk off” the exact location before starting. If the punch mark is too heavy, it may deform the metal and/or result in a local strain hardening where the drill bit is to start cutting. The best size for a punch mark is about the width of the chisel edge of the drill bit to be used. This holds the drill point in place while starting. The procedure that ensures accurate holes follows:

  1. Measure and lay out the drill locations carefully and mark with crossed lines.

Note: The chisel edge is the least efficient operating surface element of the twist drill bit because it does not cut, but actually squeezes or extrudes the work material.

  1. Use a sharp prick punch or spring-loaded center punch and magnifying glass to further mark the holes.
  1. Seat a properly ground center punch (120°–135°) in the prick punch mark and, holding the center punch perpendicular to the surface, strike a firm square blow with a hammer.
  1. Mark each hole with a small drill bit ( 1 ⁄ 16-inch recommended) to check and adjust the location prior to pilot drilling.
  1. For holes 3 ⁄ 16-inch and larger, pilot drilling is recommended. Select a drill bit equal to the width of the chisel edge of the final drill bit size. Avoid using a pilot drill bit that is too large because it would cause the corners and cutting lips of the final drill bit to be dulled, burned, or chipped. It also contributes to chattering and drill motor stalling. Pilot drill at each mark.
  1. Place the drill point at the center of the crossed lines, perpendicular to the surface, and, with light pressure, start drilling slowly. Stop drilling after a few turns and check to see if the drill bit is starting on the mark. It should be; if not, it is necessary to walk the hole a little by pointing the drill in the direction it should go, and rotating it carefully and intermittently until properly lined up.
  1. Enlarge each pilot drilled hole to final size.

Drilling Large Holes

The following technique can be used to drill larger holes. Special tooling has been developed to drill large holes to precise tolerances.

  1. Pilot drill using a drill bushing. Bushings are sized for 1 ⁄ 8, 3 ⁄ 16, or 1 ⁄ 4 drill bits.
  1. Step drill bits are used to step the hole to approximately 1 ⁄ 64-inch smaller than the final hole size. The aligning step diameter matches the pilot drill bit size.
  1. Finish ream to size using a step reamer. The aligning step diameter matches the core drill bit size. Reamers should be available for both clearance and interference fit hole sizes.

2. 中文翻译

钻孔是机体修理车间的常见操作。一旦掌握了钻头的基本原理及其用法，在轻金属上钻铆钉孔和螺栓孔并不困难。在机体钣金作业中，小型便携式动力钻通常是这类常见操作最实用的工具，但有时台钻反而可能是更适合这项工作的设备。

便携式动力钻

便携式动力钻以电力或压缩空气驱动。在易燃材料附近进行修理时，建议使用气动钻机，因为电钻电机可能产生的火花会成为火灾隐患。

使用便携式动力钻时，应双手握紧。钻孔前，务必在待钻孔的下方垫一块木制垫块，为金属结构增加支撑。钻头应装入钻夹头，并检查其是否正直、有无振动。这可以通过让电机空转进行目视检查。跳动或略有弯曲的钻头不得使用，因为这种情况会造成孔径扩大。无论工件位置或曲率如何，钻头都应始终与工件保持直角。钻孔时进刀或退刀若有任何倾斜，都可能使孔拉长（呈蛋形）。在薄板上钻孔时，孔边缘会形成小的毛刺。必须去除毛刺，以使铆钉或螺栓贴合紧密并防止划伤。毛刺可用轴承刮刀、锪钻或比孔径更大的钻头去除。若用钻头或锪钻去毛刺，应徒手转动。钻孔时务必佩戴护目镜。

气动钻机

气动钻机是飞机修理工作中最常见的钻机类型。它们重量轻，功率充足，转速控制良好。钻机有多种尺寸和型号。用于飞机钣金作业的大多数钻机额定转速为 3,000 rpm，但若钻深孔或钻硬质材料（如耐蚀钢或钛），则应选用扭矩更大、转速更低的钻机，以防止损坏工具和材料。

直角与 45° 钻机

直角钻机和 45° 钻机用于手枪式钻机无法触及的位置。大多数直角钻机使用几种长度规格的螺纹钻头。重型直角钻配有与手枪式钻机相似的钻夹头。

双孔钻机

能同时钻两个孔的特种钻机用于托板螺母的安装。同时钻两个孔可使孔间距固定，并使两孔与托板螺母上的孔完全对齐。

台钻

台钻是一种精密机床，用于钻要求高精度的孔。它能准确地定位并保持待钻孔的方向，还为操作者提供进给手柄，使向工件进给钻头的工作更轻松。立式台钻是各种台钻中最常见的一种。

使用台钻时，要调整工作台的高度以适应待钻孔零件的高度。当零件高度大于钻头与工作台之间的距离时，将工作台调低；当零件高度小于该距离时，将工作台调高。

工作台调整妥当后，把零件放到工作台上，将钻头降下来帮助定位金属，使待钻的孔正好位于钻尖的正下方。然后把零件夹紧在工作台上，以防钻孔过程中滑动。夹紧不当的零件可能卡住钻头并开始旋转，造成操作者手臂或身体严重割伤，甚至失去手指或手。开始钻孔作业前，务必确认待钻零件已正确夹紧在工作台上。

使用台钻所能达到的精度，在一定程度上取决于主轴孔、套筒和钻头柄的状况。因此必须特别小心，保持这些零件清洁，无划痕、凹痕和变形。始终要确保套筒牢固地压入主轴孔。绝不要把折断的钻头插入套筒或主轴孔。注意绝不能用套筒夹紧台钳来拆卸钻头，因为这可能使套筒变形。

台钻的钻速是可调的。务必为待钻材料选择最佳钻速。严格来说，钻头的速度是指其圆周速度，以表面英尺每分钟（sfm）计。钻铝合金的推荐速度为 200 至 300 sfm，钻低碳钢为 30 至 50 sfm。实际使用中，必须针对每种规格的钻头换算成 rpm（每分钟转数）。机械师与技工手册载有钻速 rpm 图表，也可用下列公式计算 rpm：CS × 4 = rpmD，其中 CS = 以 sfm 计的推荐切削速度，D = 以英寸计的钻头直径。当用直柄钻机难以或无法钻到某处时，可使用各种钻头加长杆和转接器。

钻头加长杆与转接器

加长钻头

加长钻头广泛用于需要穿过小孔或绕过凸起部位才能到达的位置钻孔。这类钻头长度有 6 至 12 英寸，为高速钢材质、柄部经弹簧回火处理。加长钻头磨有特殊的缺口钻尖，可将轴向推力减至最小。使用加长钻头时务必：

  1. 选择能完成工作的最短钻头，它更容易控制。
  1. 检查钻头是否笔直。弯曲的钻头会钻出过大的孔，并可能甩动，难以控制。
  1. 控制好钻头。小于 1 ⁄ 4 英寸的加长钻头必须用一段管子或弹簧制成的钻头护套支撑，以防甩动。

直式加长杆

钻头的直式加长杆可用一段普通钻杆制成。钻头通过热套、铜焊或银钎焊连接到钻杆上。

角度转接器

当孔的位置是直式钻头无法到达时，可将角度转接器装在电动或气动钻上。角度转接器有一根加长的柄，固定在钻的夹头上。一手握钻、另一手握转接器，以防转接器绕钻夹头旋转。

蛇形软轴

蛇形软轴是一种柔性加长件，用于普通钻头无法到达的位置钻孔。它有适用于电动钻机和气动钻机的型号，其柔性使人能以最小力气绕过障碍物钻孔。

钻头类型

现有各种钻头，包括用于特定作业的专用钻头。图 4-44 示出钻头的各部分，图 4-45 示出一些常用钻头。高速钢（HSS）钻头有短柄或标准长度（有时称为 jobbers 长度）两种。HSS 钻头能承受接近 1,400 °F（暗樱桃红）临界范围的温度而不失去硬度。作为钻削金属（铝、钢等）的行业标准，这类钻头保持锋利的时间更长。

阶梯钻头

通常，在薄板上钻大于 3 ⁄ 16 英寸的孔，其做法是先用 40 号或 30 号钻头钻一个导孔，然后再用更大的钻头扩到正确尺寸。阶梯钻把这两项功能合为一步。阶梯钻头由一个先钻出小孔的较小导钻尖组成。当钻头进一步进入材料时，钻头的第二级把孔扩大到所需尺寸。

阶梯钻头设计用于在大多数金属、塑料和木材上钻圆孔。它们常用于一般建筑和水暖工程，在较软材料（如胶合板）上效果最好，但也可用于很薄的钣金。阶梯钻头还可用来去除其他钻头留下的孔边毛刺。

钴合金钻头

钴合金钻头是为硬而韧的金属（如耐蚀钢和钛）设计的。飞机技师必须注意 HSS 与钴合金的区别，因为用 HSS 钻头钻钛或不锈钢时磨损很快。钴钻头钻钛或不锈钢非常出色，但在铝合金中不能加工出高质量的孔。钴钻头可通过较厚的钻芯和钻柄末端的锥度来辨认。

麻花钻头

麻花钻头无疑是最流行的钻头类型，其工作长度上开有螺旋沟槽（容屑槽）。这种钻头有单槽、双槽、三槽和四槽等样式。单槽和双槽钻头（最常见）用于开孔。三槽和四槽钻头可互换用于扩大已有的孔。麻花钻头有多种刀具材料和长度可供选择，其差异针对特定项目。

钻铝用的标准麻花钻头由 HSS 制成，具有 135° 分屑尖。钻钛用的钻头由钴钒合金制成，以提高耐磨性。

钻削润滑

薄板材料的常规钻削不需要润滑，但所有较深的钻削都应加润滑。润滑剂有助于排屑，从而延长钻头寿命并保证孔的良好表面质量和尺寸精度。它并不能防止过热。钻铸件、锻件或厚料时，使用润滑剂始终是好做法。好的润滑剂应稀到有助于排屑，又稠到能附着在钻头上。对铝、钛和耐蚀钢，以十六醇（鲸蜡醇）为基的润滑剂最为理想。十六醇是一种无毒的脂肪醇化学品，有液体、膏体和固体三种形态。固体棒状和块状在钻削温度下会迅速液化。对钢，硫化矿物切削油更胜一筹。硫对钢有亲和力，有助于把切削油保持在位。深孔钻削时，应不时退出钻头，以解除切屑堵塞并确保润滑剂到达钻尖。一般规则是：钻头大或材料硬，就用润滑剂。

铰刀

铰刀用于扩大孔并将其铰到所需尺寸的光滑表面，有多种样式。它们可以是直柄或锥柄、整体式或可胀式，并有直槽或螺旋槽。图 4-48 示出三种铰刀：

  1. 三槽或四槽生产型子弹头铰刀，通常用于需要比标准钻头所能达到的更精细表面和/或尺寸的场合。
  1. 标准铰刀（直铰刀）。
  1. 导柱铰刀，其端部缩细以提供准确的对中。

大多数直铰刀的圆柱部分并不是切削刃，而只是沿铰刀体全长切出的沟槽。这些沟槽为切屑提供排出通道，也为润滑剂提供到达切削刃的通道。实际切削在铰刀端部进行。切削刃通常磨成 45° ± 5° 的斜面。

铰刀槽并不像钻头那样用于排屑。不要试图反转铰刀把它退出，因为切屑会被压入表面，划伤孔壁。

钻深限位器

弹簧式钻深限位器是明智的投资。调整得当，它可以防止钻头过度钻入而损坏下层结构或伤及人员，并可防止钻夹头划伤表面。钻深限位器可用管材、纤维棒或硬橡胶制成。

钻套与导具

有几种工具可帮助保持钻头垂直于零件。它们由一个固定在夹持器中的淬硬钻套组成。

  1. 管式——握持在已有的孔中。钻套夹持器有四种类型：

  1. 标准式——适于钻平板料或管材、棒料；使用插入式钻套。
  1. 蛋杯式——对标准三脚底座作了改进；可在平面和曲面上钻孔；可换钻套使其灵活。

钻孔技术

有时要求钻孔位置精确。定位公差要求严的孔时，需要打出位置准确的样冲眼。若样冲眼太小，钻头的横刃可能跨在眼上，在开始钻削前从准确位置“走偏”。若样冲眼过重，可能使金属变形，和/或造成钻头开始切削处的局部应变硬化。样冲眼的最佳尺寸约为所用钻头横刃的宽度。这样可在起钻时把钻尖固定在位。确保孔位准确的操作步骤如下：

  1. 仔细测量并划出钻孔位置，用交叉线标出。

注：横刃是麻花钻头效率最低的工作表面要素，因为它并不切削，而是实际上挤压或挤出工件材料。

  1. 用尖锐的样冲或弹簧中心冲配合放大镜进一步标出各孔。
  1. 把磨好的中心冲（120°–135°）落入样冲眼中，保持中心冲垂直于表面，用锤子敲一记结实方正的重击。
  1. 用小钻头（建议 1 ⁄ 16 英寸）给每个孔做标记，以便在钻导孔前核对并调整位置。
  1. 对于 3 ⁄ 16 英寸及更大的孔，建议先钻导孔。所选钻头应等于最终钻头尺寸的横刃宽度。避免使用过大的导孔钻头，因为它会使最终钻头的刀尖和切削唇变钝、烧损或缺损。它还会导致颤振和钻机失速。在每个标记处钻导孔。
  1. 把钻尖放在交叉线的中心，垂直于表面，轻压慢慢起钻。钻几圈后停下，检查钻头是否从标记处起钻。应当如此；若不是，就需要把孔稍作“走偏”引导，即把钻头朝应该去的方向倾斜，小心地间歇转动，直到正确对中。
  1. 把每个导孔扩大到最终尺寸。

钻大孔

下列技术可用于钻较大的孔。已开发出专用工装，可将大孔钻到精确的公差。

  1. 用钻套钻导孔。钻套按 1 ⁄ 8、3 ⁄ 16 或 1 ⁄ 4 英寸钻头尺寸配制。
  1. 用阶梯钻头把孔逐步扩大到比最终孔径小约 1 ⁄ 64 英寸。对中台阶的直径与导孔钻头尺寸相匹配。
  1. 用阶梯铰刀精铰到尺寸。对中台阶的直径与空心钻头尺寸相匹配。铰刀应备有间隙配合和干涉配合两种孔径。

4. 语法解析

① while 引导的让步状语从句

原句：While a small portable power drill is usually the most practical tool for this common operation in airframe metalwork, sometimes a drill press may prove to be the better piece of equipment for the job. 拆解：While 引导让步状语从句（“虽然……”），从句主语 a small portable power drill，系动词 is，表语 the most practical tool，后接 for this common operation in airframe metalwork 说明适用范围；主句以 sometimes 起首，主语 a drill press，谓语 may prove to be，表语 the better piece of equipment，for the job 为后置定语。 要点：while 既可表“当……时”也可表“虽然”，此处主句有 sometimes 作转折呼应，应读作让步。prove to be 是系动结构，to be 后接名词作表语，不能理解成“证明某物”。

② 被动语态 + where 引导的定语从句

原句：Pneumatic drill motors are recommended for use on repairs around flammable materials where potential sparks from an electric drill motor might become a fire hazard. 拆解：主句为被动语态 are recommended，for use on repairs 表用途场合，around flammable materials 为后置定语；where 引导定语从句修饰 materials，从句主语 potential sparks，后接 from an electric drill motor 作定语，系动词 might become，表语 a fire hazard。 要点：where 在这里等于 in which，先行词是地点性质的名词 materials（易燃材料所在的环境），不是“哪里”的疑问用法。are recommended 后没有 by 短语，是因为重点在“建议这么做”而非谁建议。

③ 定语从句 + since 原因状语从句

原句：A drill bit that wobbles or is slightly bent should not be used since such a condition causes enlarged holes. 拆解：主语中心词 A drill bit，that 引导定语从句（谓语为并列的 wobbles or is slightly bent，后者为被动/系表结构）；主句谓语 should not be used 为带情态动词的被动语态；since 引导原因状语从句，主语 such a condition，谓语 causes，宾语 enlarged holes。 要点：主句谓语被定语从句隔开，读到 should not be used 才回到主干。since 此处表原因（“因为”），与表时间的“自从”区分要看从句时态与逻辑：从句用一般现在时陈述普遍因果。

④ 定语从句嵌套在主语与谓语之间

原句：The degree of accuracy that it is possible to attain when using the drill press depends to a certain extent on the condition of the spindle hole, sleeves, and drill shank. 拆解：主语中心词 The degree of accuracy，that 引导定语从句修饰 degree，从句中 it 为形式主语、真正主语是 to attain（其宾语即先行词 degree），when using the drill press 为时间状语（分词短语）；主句谓语 depends on，宾语 the condition of the spindle hole, sleeves, and drill shank，to a certain extent 为程度状语。 要点：that it is possible to attain 是“介词+关系代词”被省略后的常见结构，that 在从句中作 attain 的宾语。主语后挂了定语从句和状语，谓语 depends 直到第 15 个词才出现，须先抓住“精度取决于状况”这条骨架。

⑤ 过去分词短语作非限制性定语

原句：Reamers, used for enlarging holes and finishing them smooth to a required size, are made in many styles. 拆解：主语 Reamers，两个逗号之间的 used for enlarging holes and finishing them smooth to a required size 为过去分词短语作非限制性定语（相当于 which are used for...），介词 for 后接并列动名词 enlarging 与 finishing；主句谓语 are made 为被动语态，in many styles 为方式状语。 要点：分词短语被逗号隔开，说明它只是补充信息，删去后句子仍完整——这是判断非限制性定语的关键。finish sth. smooth 是“动词+宾语+形容词补足语”结构，smooth 修饰的是 holes。

⑥ 祈使句 + because 原因从句 + 分词结果状语

原句：Do not attempt to withdraw a reamer by turning it in the reverse direction because chips can be forced into the surface, scarring the hole. 拆解：主句为否定祈使句 Do not attempt to withdraw a reamer，by turning it in the reverse direction 为方式状语；because 引导原因状语从句，主语 chips，谓语 can be forced 为带情态动词的被动语态，into the surface 为方向状语；句末 scarring the hole 为现在分词短语作结果状语。 要点：attempt to do 表示“试图做”，否定祈使句用 Don't/Do not + 动词原形。scarring 的逻辑主语是前面整件事（切屑被压入表面），不是 chips 主动去划伤，译成“从而划伤孔壁”即可。
