Identifying Fire Extinguishers

通用 Ch 01 · Identifying Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

1. 原文

Fire extinguishers are marked to indicate suitability for a particular class of fire. The markings on Figure 1-7 must be placed on the fire extinguisher and in a conspicuous place in the vicinity of the fire extinguisher. When the location is marked, however, take extreme care to ensure that the fire extinguisher kept at that location is in fact the type depicted by the marking. In other words, if a location is marked for a Class B fire extinguisher, ensure that the fire extinguisher in that location is in fact suitable for Class B fires.

Markings must be applied by decalcomanias (decals), painting, or similar methods. They are to be legible and as durable as necessary for the location. For example, markings used outside need to be more durable than those in the hangar or office spaces.

When markings are applied to the extinguisher, they are placed on the front of the shell, if one is installed, above or below the extinguisher nameplate. Markings must be large enough and in a form that is easily seen and identifiable by the average person with average eyesight at a distance of at least 3 feet.

When markings are applied to wall panels, and so forth, in the vicinity of extinguishers, they must be large enough and in a form that is easily seen and identifiable by the average person with average eyesight at a distance of at least 25 feet.

2. 中文翻译

灭火器上设有标记，用以表明其适用于特定类别的火。图 1-7 中的标记必须设置在灭火器上，并设置在灭火器附近显眼的位置。不过，在对位置作标记时务必格外小心，确保该位置放置的灭火器确实是标记所指示的类型。换言之，如果某位置按 B 类灭火器作了标记，就要确保该位置的灭火器确实适用于 B 类火。

标记必须采用贴花（decal）、涂漆或类似方法施加。标记应清晰可读，并根据所处位置具备必要的耐久性。例如，室外使用的标记需要比机库或办公区内的标记更耐久。

标记施加在灭火器上时，应位于筒体正面（如装有筒体），在灭火器铭牌的上方或下方。标记必须足够大，其形式应便于视力正常的普通人在至少 3 英尺的距离上看到并辨认。

标记施加在灭火器附近的墙板等位置时，必须足够大，其形式应便于视力正常的普通人在至少 25 英尺的距离上看到并辨认。

4. 语法解析

① 被动语态 + 并列地点状语

原句：The markings on Figure 1-7 must be placed on the fire extinguisher and in a conspicuous place in the vicinity of the fire extinguisher.

拆解：主语 The markings，on Figure 1-7 是后置定语；谓语 must be placed（情态动词 + 被动）；on the fire extinguisher 与 in a conspicuous place in the vicinity of the fire extinguisher 是两个并列的地点状语，共用 must be placed。

要点：两个并列状语一个用 on、一个用 in，是因为「贴在灭火器上」用 on，「在……位置」用 in，介词不能互换。in the vicinity of 是「在……附近」的正式说法，比 near 更适合规范条文。

② ensure that 宾语从句 + 过去分词后置定语

原句：take extreme care to ensure that the fire extinguisher kept at that location is in fact the type depicted by the marking.

拆解：祈使句 take extreme care，to ensure that … 是目的状语；that 引导宾语从句，从句主语 the fire extinguisher，kept at that location 是过去分词短语作后置定语；从句谓语 is，表语 the type，depicted by the marking 又是过去分词短语作后置定语。

要点：take care to do sth 表示「务必做某事」，extreme 加强语气。ensure that 后接完整陈述句，不能接 to do。两个过去分词短语都是「被动定语」，可还原为 which is kept … / which is depicted …；中文习惯把定语前置，译成「放在该位置的灭火器」「标记所指示的类型」。

③ 比较结构 + those 替代

原句：markings used outside need to be more durable than those in the hangar or office spaces.

拆解：主语 markings，used outside 是过去分词短语作后置定语；谓语 need to be more durable than …；those 替代前面出现过的 markings，避免重复，in the hangar or office spaces 是 those 的后置定语。

要点：比较结构中用 that/those 替代前面提到的名词（单数用 that，复数用 those），这是英文避免重复的常规手段。比较对象必须对等：室外标记与室内标记比，不能拿「标记」与「机库」比。used outside 相当于 which are used outside。
