Transmission System

机体 Ch 02 · Transmission System · 原文 PDF 第 105 页

1. 原文

The transmission system transfers power from the engine to the main rotor, tail rotor, and other accessories during normal flight conditions. The main components of the transmission system are the main rotor transmission, tail rotor drive system, clutch, and freewheeling unit. The freewheeling unit, or autorotative clutch, allows the main rotor transmission to drive the tail rotor drive shaft during autorotation. Helicopter transmissions are normally lubricated and cooled with their own oil supply. A sight gauge is provided to check the oil level. Some transmissions have chip detectors located in the sump. These detectors are wired to warning lights located on the pilot’s instrument panel that illuminate in the event of an internal problem. The chip detectors on modern helicopters have a “burn off” capability and attempt to correct the situation without pilot action. If the problem cannot be corrected on its own, the pilot must refer to the emergency procedures for that particular helicopter.

Main Rotor Transmission

The primary purpose of the main rotor transmission is to reduce engine output rpm to optimum rotor rpm. This reduction is different for the various helicopters. As an example, suppose the engine rpm of a specific helicopter is 2,700. A rotor speed of 450 rpm would require a 6:1 reduction. A 9:1 reduction would mean the rotor would turn at 300 rpm. Most helicopters use a dual-needle tachometer or a vertical scale instrument to show both engine and rotor rpm or a percentage of engine and rotor rpm. The rotor rpm indicator normally is used only during clutch engagement to monitor rotor acceleration, and in autorotation to maintain rpm within prescribed limits.

In helicopters with horizontally mounted engines, another purpose of the main rotor transmission is to change the axis of rotation from the horizontal axis of the engine to the vertical axis of the rotor shaft.

Clutch

In a conventional airplane, the engine and propeller are directly connected. However, in a helicopter there is a different relationship between the engine and the rotor. Because of the greater weight of a rotor in relation to the power of the engine, as compared to the weight of a propeller and the power in an airplane, the rotor must be disconnected from the engine when the starter is engaged. A clutch allows the engine to be started and then gradually pick up the load On free turbine engines, no clutch is required, as the gas producer turbine is essentially disconnected from the power turbine. When the engine is started, there is little resistance from the power turbine. This enables the gas producer turbine to accelerate to normal idle speed without the load of the transmission and rotor system dragging it down. As the gas pressure increases through the power turbine, the rotor blades begin to turn, slowly at first and then gradually accelerate to normal operating rpm.

On reciprocating helicopters, the two main types of clutches are the centrifugal clutch and the belt drive clutch.

Centrifugal Clutch

The centrifugal clutch is made up of an inner assembly and an outer drum. The inner assembly, which is connected to the engine driveshaft, consists of shoes lined with material similar to automotive brake linings. At low engine speeds, springs hold the shoes in, so there is no contact with the outer drum, which is attached to the transmission input shaft. As engine speed increases, centrifugal force causes the clutch shoes to move outward and begin sliding against the outer drum. The transmission input shaft begins to rotate, causing the rotor to turn, slowly at first, but increasing as the friction increases between the clutch shoes and transmission drum. As rotor speed increases, the rotor tachometer needle shows an increase by moving toward the engine tachometer needle. When the two needles are superimposed, the engine and the rotor are synchronized, indicating the clutch is fully engaged and there is no further slippage of the clutch shoes.

Belt Drive Clutch

Some helicopters utilize a belt drive to transmit power from the engine to the transmission. A belt drive consists of a lower pulley attached to the engine, an upper pulley attached to the transmission input shaft, a belt or a series of V-belts, and some means of applying tension to the belts. The belts fit loosely over the upper and lower pulley when there is no tension on the belts. This allows the engine to be started without any load from the transmission. Once the engine is running, tension on the belts is gradually increased. When the rotor and engine tachometer needles are superimposed, the rotor and the engine are synchronized, and the clutch is then fully engaged. Advantages of this system include vibration isolation, simple maintenance, and the ability to start and warm up the engine without engaging the rotor.

Freewheeling Unit

Since lift in a helicopter is provided by rotating airfoils, these airfoils must be free to rotate if the engine fails. The freewheeling unit automatically disengages the engine from the main rotor when engine rpm is less than main rotor rpm. This allows the main rotor and tail rotor to continue turning at normal in-flight speeds. The most common freewheeling unit assembly consists of a one-way sprag clutch located between the engine and main rotor transmission. This is usually in the upper pulley in a piston helicopter or mounted on the accessory gearbox in a turbine helicopter. When the engine is driving the rotor, inclined surfaces in the sprag clutch force rollers against an outer drum. This prevents the engine from exceeding transmission rpm. If the engine fails, the rollers move inward, allowing the outer drum to exceed the speed of the inner portion. The transmission can then exceed the speed of the engine. In this condition, engine speed is less than that of the drive system, and the helicopter is in an autorotative state.

2. 中文翻译

在正常飞行条件下，传动系统把发动机的功率传递给主旋翼、尾桨和其他附件。传动系统的主要部件有主旋翼传动装置、尾桨驱动系统、离合器和单向离合器。单向离合器又称自转离合器，它使主旋翼传动装置在自转状态下仍能驱动尾桨传动轴。直升机传动装置通常用自带的滑油系统润滑和冷却，并设有油位观察窗用于检查油量。有些传动装置在油池中装有金属屑检测器。这些检测器与驾驶舱仪表板上的警告灯相连，一旦内部出现问题，警告灯即点亮。现代直升机上的金属屑检测器具有“烧除”能力，能在飞行员不干预的情况下尝试排除故障。若故障无法自行消除，飞行员必须查阅该型直升机的应急程序。

主旋翼传动装置

主旋翼传动装置的主要作用是把发动机输出转速降低到最佳旋翼转速。不同直升机的减速比各不相同。例如，假设某型直升机的发动机转速为2,700转/分，要使旋翼转速达到450转/分，就需要6:1的减速比；若采用9:1的减速比，则旋翼转速为300转/分。大多数直升机使用双针转速表或垂直刻度仪表，显示发动机与旋翼转速，或显示二者转速的百分比。旋翼转速指示器通常只在离合器接合过程中用来监视旋翼加速情况，以及在自转飞行中把转速保持在规定范围内。

发动机水平安装的直升机上，主旋翼传动装置的另一个作用是把旋转轴由发动机的水平轴改为旋翼轴的垂直轴。

离合器

常规飞机的发动机与螺旋桨是直接相连的。然而直升机上发动机与旋翼之间的关系不同。与飞机上螺旋桨的重量和发动机功率相比，旋翼的重量相对发动机功率更大，因此在起动机接合时必须把旋翼与发动机脱开。离合器使发动机能够起动，然后逐渐承接负载。在自由涡轮发动机上不需要离合器，因为燃气发生器涡轮与动力涡轮实质上是脱开的。发动机起动时，动力涡轮几乎没有阻力，这使燃气发生器涡轮能在不被传动装置和旋翼系统负载拖累的情况下加速到正常慢车转速。随着流经动力涡轮的燃气压力升高，旋翼桨叶开始转动，起初很慢，然后逐渐加速到正常工作转速。

在往复式发动机直升机上，离合器的两种主要型式是离心式离合器和带传动离合器。

离心式离合器

离心式离合器由内组件和外鼓组成。与发动机传动轴相连的内组件由若干蹄块构成，蹄块上覆有类似汽车制动蹄片的摩擦材料。发动机转速低时，弹簧把蹄块收拢，使其与外鼓不接触；外鼓与传动装置输入轴相连。随着发动机转速升高，离心力使蹄块向外张开，开始与外鼓滑动摩擦。传动装置输入轴开始转动，带动旋翼旋转，起初较慢，随着蹄块与传动鼓之间摩擦力增大而加快。旋翼转速升高时，旋翼转速表指针向发动机转速表指针靠拢。当两根指针重合时，发动机与旋翼同步，表明离合器已完全接合，蹄块不再打滑。

带传动离合器

有些直升机用带传动把发动机功率传给传动装置。带传动由装在发动机上的下带轮、装在传动装置输入轴上的上带轮、一根或一组V带，以及某种张紧装置组成。带上没有张力时，带松松地套在上下带轮上，这样发动机可以在不承受传动装置负载的情况下起动。发动机运转后，逐渐增大带的张力。当旋翼与发动机转速表指针重合时，旋翼与发动机同步，离合器即完全接合。这种系统的优点包括隔振、维护简单，以及能在不接合旋翼的情况下起动和暖机。

单向离合器

由于直升机的升力由旋转的翼面产生，一旦发动机失效，这些翼面必须能够自由旋转。当发动机转速低于主旋翼转速时，单向离合器自动把发动机与主旋翼脱开，使主旋翼和尾桨能按正常飞行转速继续旋转。最常见的单向离合器组件是装在发动机与主旋翼传动装置之间的单向楔块离合器。在活塞式直升机上它通常位于上带轮内，在涡轮直升机上则装在附件齿轮箱上。发动机驱动旋翼时，楔块离合器中的斜面把滚柱压向外鼓，从而防止发动机转速超过传动装置转速。若发动机失效，滚柱向内退回，允许外鼓的转速超过内圈，传动装置便可超过发动机转速。此时发动机转速低于传动系统转速，直升机处于自转状态。

4. 语法解析

① 同位语 + allow sb. to do

原句：The freewheeling unit, or autorotative clutch, allows the main rotor transmission to drive the tail rotor drive shaft during autorotation. 拆解：主语 The freewheeling unit，逗号间 or autorotative clutch 是同位语，给出同一部件的另一名称；谓语 allows，宾语 the main rotor transmission，宾补 to drive the tail rotor drive shaft；during autorotation 为时间状语。 要点：or 引出的同位语用逗号隔开，作用等于“又称”，不是选择关系。allow 后接“宾语 + 不定式”作宾补，不能用 allow doing sth.；这里传动装置是主动去驱动，故用主动式 to drive。

② 过去分词定语 + that 定语从句

原句：These detectors are wired to warning lights located on the pilot’s instrument panel that illuminate in the event of an internal problem. 拆解：主句为被动 are wired to warning lights；located on the pilot’s instrument panel 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 lights；that illuminate ... 为定语从句，同样修饰 lights，in the event of 引出的介词短语作条件状语。 要点：warning lights 后面挂了两个定语，一个分词短语、一个定语从句，都指向同一个先行词。that 从句的谓语 illuminate 是复数形式，正好与 lights 一致，可据以判断它修饰的不是单数的 panel。

③ 原因状语 + 被动 + when 从句

原句：Because of the greater weight of a rotor in relation to the power of the engine, as compared to the weight of a propeller and the power in an airplane, the rotor must be disconnected from the engine when the starter is engaged. 拆解：Because of 引出介词短语作原因状语，in relation to 表示“相对于”，as compared to 是插入的比较结构；主句 the rotor must be disconnected from the engine 为带情态动词的被动语态；when 引导时间状语从句。 要点：Because of 后只能接名词性成分，不能接句子，这是与 because 的关键区别。as compared to 是独立比较状语，插在主语之前，读时先跳过它抓住主句骨架。

④ enable ... to do + without 复合结构

原句：This enables the gas producer turbine to accelerate to normal idle speed without the load of the transmission and rotor system dragging it down. 拆解：主语 This 指代前句所述“动力涡轮几乎无阻力”；谓语 enables，宾语 the gas producer turbine，宾补 to accelerate to normal idle speed；without + 名词短语 + 现在分词 dragging 构成 without 复合结构作伴随状语。 要点：enable sb./sth. to do 是固定搭配。without 复合结构中 load 与 drag 是主动关系，故用现在分词 dragging；it 指 the gas producer turbine。整句说明“没有负载拖着它”这一有利条件。

⑤ so 结果句 + 非限制性定语从句

原句：At low engine speeds, springs hold the shoes in, so there is no contact with the outer drum, which is attached to the transmission input shaft. 拆解：At low engine speeds 为时间状语；主句 springs hold the shoes in；so 引出结果分句 there is no contact with the outer drum；which 引导非限制性定语从句，修饰 the outer drum。 要点：hold ... in 是“把……收住/保持在里侧”，in 是副词。which 前有逗号，属非限制性定语从句，只作补充说明；它修饰 drum 而不是 contact，因为从句谓语 is 为单数。

⑥ 过去分词后置定语 + consist of

原句：The most common freewheeling unit assembly consists of a one-way sprag clutch located between the engine and main rotor transmission. 拆解：主语 The most common freewheeling unit assembly；谓语 consists of；宾语 a one-way sprag clutch，后接过去分词短语 located between ... 作后置定语。 要点：consist of 不用被动语态，不能说 be consisted of。located 说明楔块离合器的安装位置，是“被装在”的被动含义；between A and B 界定了它串接在发动机与主减速器之间。
