## 1. 原文

Magnets are either natural or artificial. Since naturally occurring magnets or lodestones have no practical use, all magnets considered in this chapter are artificial or manmade. Artificial magnets can be further classified as permanent magnets, which retain their magnetism long after the magnetizing force has been removed, and temporary magnets, which quickly lose most of their magnetism when the external magnetizing force is removed.

The two bar magnets may be held in the hands and the North poles brought near each other to demonstrate the force of repulsion between like poles. In a similar manner, the two South poles can demonstrate this force. The force of attraction between unlike poles can be felt by bringing a South and a North end together.

If the bar magnet is cut or broken into pieces, each piece immediately becomes a magnet itself, with a North and South pole. This feature supports the theory that each molecule is a magnet, since each successive division of the magnet produces more magnets.

Since the magnetic lines of force form a continuous loop, they form a magnetic circuit. It is impossible to say where in the magnet they originate or start. Arbitrarily, it is assumed that all lines of force leave the North pole of any magnet and enter at the South pole.

There is no known insulator for magnetic flux, or lines of force, since they pass through all materials. However, they do pass through some materials more easily than others. Thus, it is possible to shield items, such as instruments, from the effects of the flux by surrounding them with a material that offers an easier path for the lines of force. Consider an instrument surrounded by a path of soft iron, which offers very little opposition to magnetic flux. The lines of force take the easier path, the path of greater permeability, and are guided away from the instrument.

Materials such as soft iron and other ferrous metals are said to have a high permeability, the measure of the ease with which magnetic flux can penetrate a material. The permeability scale is based on a perfect vacuum with a rating of one. Modern permanent magnets are made of special alloys that have been found through research to create increasingly better magnets. The most common categories of magnet materials are made out of aluminum-nickel-cobalt (alnicos), strontium-iron (ferrites, also known as ceramics), neodymium-iron-boron (neo magnets), and samarium- cobalt. Alnico, an alloy of iron, aluminum, nickel and cobalt, is considered one of the very best. Others with excellent magnetic qualities are alloys such as Remalloy™ and Permendur™.

The ability of a magnet to hold its magnetism varies greatly with the type of metal and is known as retentivity. Magnets made of soft iron are very easily magnetized but quickly lose most of their magnetism when the external magnetizing force is removed. The small amount of magnetism remaining, called residual magnetism, is of great importance in such electrical applications as generator operation.

Horseshoe magnets are commonly manufactured in two forms. The most common type is made from a long bar curved into a horseshoe shape, while a variation of this type consists of two bars connected by a third bar, or yoke.

Magnets can be made in many different shapes, such as balls, cylinders, or disks. One special type of magnet is the ring magnet, or Gramme ring, often used in instruments. This is a closed loop magnet, similar to the type used in transformer cores, and is the only type that has no poles.

Sometimes special applications require that the field of force lie through the thickness rather than the length of a piece of metal. Such magnets are called flat magnets and are used as pole pieces in generators and motors.

由于磁力线构成闭合回路，它们就构成一个磁路。无法说清它们在磁铁内部何处起源或起始。人们任意假定所有力线都从任何磁铁的北极离开、从南极进入。

目前还不知道有什么能隔绝磁通（力线）的绝缘体，因为力线能穿过所有材料。不过，它们穿过某些材料确实比穿过另一些材料容易。因此，可以用一种为力线提供较容易路径的材料把仪表等物件包围起来，使其免受磁通的影响。设想一台仪表被一条软铁路径所包围，软铁对磁通的阻碍很小。力线走较容易的路径，即磁导率较大的路径，从而被引导离开仪表。

软铁等含铁金属被称为具有高磁导率，磁导率是衡量磁通穿过某种材料的容易程度的量。磁导率的标度以一个额定值为 1 的理想真空为基准。现代永磁铁由经过研究发现的、能造出越来越好磁铁的特殊合金制成。最常见的磁铁材料类别有铝镍钴（阿尔尼科）、锶铁氧体（铁氧体，又称陶瓷）、钕铁硼（钕磁铁）和钐钴。阿尔尼科是铁、铝、镍和钴的合金，被认为是最好的磁铁材料之一。另一些磁性能优异的材料是 Remalloy™ 和 Permendur™ 等合金。

磁铁保持磁性的能力随金属种类不同而有很大差异，这种能力称为顽磁。软铁制成的磁铁很容易磁化，但在外部磁化力撤去后会很快失去大部分磁性。所剩下的少量磁性称为剩磁，在发电机运行等电气应用中非常重要。

马蹄形磁铁通常制成两种形式。最常见的一种由一根长条弯成马蹄形制成，而这一类型的变体由两根磁条用第三根磁条（即磁轭）连接而成。

磁铁可以制成许多不同的形状，例如球形、圆柱形或圆盘形。一种特殊的磁铁是环形磁铁，即格雷姆环，常用于仪表中。它是一种闭合回路磁铁，类似于变压器铁芯中使用的那种，并且是唯一没有磁极的磁铁类型。

有时特殊应用要求力场穿过金属的厚度方向而不是长度方向。这类磁铁称为扁平磁铁，用作发电机和电动机中的极靴。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Since 原因状语从句 + 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Since naturally occurring magnets or lodestones have no practical use, all magnets considered in this chapter are artificial or manmade.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，主语 magnets or lodestones 带现在分词前置定语 naturally occurring；主句 all magnets 省去关系词的定语从句 considered in this chapter 为过去分词短语作后置定语（= which are considered），谓语 are artificial or manmade 为系表结构。
**要点**：现在分词作前置定语表主动（occurring = which occur），过去分词短语作后置定语表被动，两者方向相反，不要把 considered 误当作谓语；since 在此表原因而非时间。

### ② 两个并列的 which 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：Artificial magnets can be further classified as permanent magnets, which retain their magnetism long after the magnetizing force has been removed, and temporary magnets, which quickly lose most of their magnetism when the external magnetizing force is removed.
**拆解**：主句为被动 can be classified as permanent magnets… and temporary magnets…；两个 which 分别引导非限制性定语从句修饰 permanent magnets 与 temporary magnets；第一句中 long after 引导时间状语从句，第二句中 when 引导时间状语从句。
**要点**：并列结构中两个 which 各管各的先行词，翻译时分别处理成两个分句；retain 与 lose 构成反义对照，是理解「永久/暂时」分类的关键。long after 表示「在……之后很久」。

### ③ 形式主语 it + where 引导的宾语从句
**原句**：It is impossible to say where in the magnet they originate or start.
**拆解**：it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to say…；where in the magnet they originate or start 为 say 的宾语从句，where 在从句中作地点状语，they 指上文 the magnetic lines of force。
**要点**：it is impossible to do 是技术文常用句式，比 we cannot 更客观；where 从句的语序是「连接词 + 状语 + 主语 + 谓语」，不能用疑问句语序。originate or start 近义并列，起强调作用。

### ④ 形式主语 it + shield A from B + by doing
**原句**：Thus, it is possible to shield items, such as instruments, from the effects of the flux by surrounding them with a material that offers an easier path for the lines of force.
**拆解**：it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to shield…；shield 的搭配是 shield A from B，A 为 items，B 为 the effects of the flux；by surrounding… 为方式状语，that offers… 为定语从句修饰 a material。
**要点**：shield／protect／insulate 都可用 from 引出被隔离的对象，介词不能换成 against 以外的其它词；by + 动名词表示手段，与 with + 名词（工具）形成对比，本句两者都用到了。

### ⑤ 主语 + of 短语 + 不定式作后置定语 + 并列谓语
**原句**：The ability of a magnet to hold its magnetism varies greatly with the type of metal and is known as retentivity.
**拆解**：主语中心词 The ability，of a magnet 与 to hold its magnetism 都是后置修饰语；谓语有两个，varies greatly with the type of metal 与 is known as retentivity，由 and 连接；vary with 表示「随……而变化」。
**要点**：ability to do 是固定搭配，to do 不能用 doing 替换；vary with 与 vary from 不同，前者是「随……变化」，后者是「与……不同」。is known as 后接名称而非定义。

### ⑥ 现在分词后置定语 + 过去分词插入语 + be of + 抽象名词
**原句**：The small amount of magnetism remaining, called residual magnetism, is of great importance in such electrical applications as generator operation.
**拆解**：主语 The small amount of magnetism 后接现在分词 remaining 作后置定语（= which remains）；called residual magnetism 为过去分词短语作插入语，说明该磁性的名称；谓语 is of great importance 中 of + 抽象名词 = 形容词；such…as… 引出举例。
**要点**：be of importance = be important，科技文中用来避免形容词重复，类似的还有 be of value/use/interest；such electrical applications as generator operation 中 as 后是同位举例，不是比较。
