# Alternator Constant Speed Drive System

> 通用 Ch 12 · Alternator Constant Speed Drive System · 原文 PDF 第 606 页

## 1. 原文

Alternators are not always connected directly to the airplane engine like DC generators. Since the various electrical devices operating on AC supplied by alternators are designed to operate at a certain voltage and at a specified frequency, the speed of the alternators must be constant; however, the speed of an airplane engine varies. Therefore, the engine, through a constant speed drive installed between the engine and the alternator, drives some alternators.

The following discussion of a constant speed drive system is based on such a drive found on large multiengine aircraft.

The constant speed drive is a hydraulic transmission that may be controlled either electrically or mechanically.

The constant speed drive assembly is designed to deliver an output of 6,000 rpm, provided the input remains between 2,800 and 9,000 rpm. If the input, which is determined by engine speed, is below 6,000 rpm, the drive increases the speed in order to furnish the desired output. This stepping up of speed is known as overdrive.

In overdrive, an automobile engine operates at about the same rpm at 60 mph as it does in conventional drive at 49 mph. In aircraft, this principle is applied in the same manner. The constant speed drive enables the alternator to produce the same frequency at slightly above engine idle rpm as it would at takeoff or cruising rpm.

With the input speed to the drive set at 6,000 rpm, the output speed is the same. This is known as straight drive and might be compared to an automobile in high gear. However, when the input speed is greater than 6,000 rpm, it must be reduced to provide an output of 6,000 rpm. This is called underdrive, which is comparable to an automobile in low gear. Thus, the large input, caused by high engine rpm, is reduced to give the desired alternator speed.

As a result of this control by the constant speed drive, the frequency output of the generator varies from 420 cps at no load to 400 cps under full load. This, in brief, is the function of the constant speed drive assembly. Before discussing the various units and circuits, the overall operation of the transmission should be discussed as follows.

## 2. 中文翻译

交流发电机并不总是像直流发电机那样直接与飞机发动机相连。由于由交流发电机供电的各种用电设备是按一定电压和规定频率设计的，交流发电机的转速必须恒定；然而飞机发动机的转速是变化的。因此，有些交流发电机是通过装在发动机与交流发电机之间的恒速传动装置由发动机驱动的。

关于恒速传动系统的以下讨论，以大型多发飞机上见到的这类传动装置为基础。

恒速传动装置是一种液压传动装置，可以通过电气或机械方式控制。

恒速传动装置组件设计为在输入转速保持在 2,800 至 9,000 rpm 之间时输出 6,000 rpm。如果由发动机转速决定的输入转速低于 6,000 rpm，传动装置就提高转速以提供所需输出。这种转速升高称为超速传动（overdrive）。

在超速传动中，汽车发动机以约 60 mph 行驶时的转速，与常规传动以 49 mph 行驶时的转速大致相同。在飞机上，这一原理的应用方式相同。恒速传动装置使交流发电机在略高于发动机怠速转速时产生与起飞或巡航转速时相同的频率。

当传动装置的输入转速设定为 6,000 rpm 时，输出转速相同。这称为直接传动（straight drive），可以比作汽车的高速档。然而，当输入转速高于 6,000 rpm 时，必须将其降低以提供 6,000 rpm 的输出。这称为减速传动（underdrive），相当于汽车的低速档。因此，高发动机转速造成的大输入被降低，以给出所需的交流发电机转速。

由于恒速传动装置的这种控制，发电机输出频率从空载时的 420 cps 变化到满载时的 400 cps。简言之，这就是恒速传动装置组件的功能。在讨论各单元和电路之前，应按以下方式讨论该传动装置的总体工作。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① since 原因从句与分词短语作定语
**原句**：Since the various electrical devices operating on AC supplied by alternators are designed to operate at a certain voltage and at a specified frequency, the speed of the alternators must be constant; however, the speed of an airplane engine varies.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 devices 由现在分词短语 operating on AC 后置修饰，supplied by alternators 为过去分词短语修饰 AC；主句为 the speed must be constant；分号后用 however 引出转折分句。
**要点**：operating on AC 意为“以交流电工作”；be designed to do 表目的性设计；however 连接两个独立分句时前面用分号。

### ② 插入介词短语分隔主谓
**原句**：Therefore, the engine, through a constant speed drive installed between the engine and the alternator, drives some alternators.
**拆解**：主语 the engine，谓语 drives，宾语 some alternators；through… 介词短语以逗号隔开插入，作方式状语；installed between… 为过去分词短语修饰 drive。
**要点**：长插入语不影响主谓一致；read 时抓住逗号外的主干 the engine drives alternators。

### ③ provided 引导条件从句
**原句**：The constant speed drive assembly is designed to deliver an output of 6,000 rpm, provided the input remains between 2,800 and 9,000 rpm.
**拆解**：主句为被动结构 is designed to deliver；provided 引导条件状语从句，相当于 if/on condition that。
**要点**：provided (that) 是正式书面条件连词，不能与 provide“提供”混用；between A and B 表范围。

### ④ 非限制性定语从句 + in order to
**原句**：If the input, which is determined by engine speed, is below 6,000 rpm, the drive increases the speed in order to furnish the desired output.
**拆解**：If 引导条件从句，从句主语 the input 与谓语 is 被 which 引导的非限制性定语从句隔开；主句 the drive increases the speed；in order to 引导目的状语。
**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，不能用 that；分隔结构下仍按 the input is 保持主谓一致。

### ⑤ with 复合结构作状语
**原句**：With the input speed to the drive set at 6,000 rpm, the output speed is the same.
**拆解**：With + 宾语 the input speed + 宾补 set at 6,000 rpm 构成 with 复合结构，作条件状语；主句 the output speed is the same。
**要点**：with 复合结构中宾补可用过去分词（表被动完成）、现在分词（表主动进行）或形容词；此处 set 为过去分词。

### ⑥ vary from…to… 表变化范围
**原句**：As a result of this control by the constant speed drive, the frequency output of the generator varies from 420 cps at no load to 400 cps under full load.
**拆解**：As a result of… 介词短语作原因状语；主语 the frequency output of the generator；谓语 varies from 420 cps… to 400 cps…；at no load 与 under full load 分别为两个端点的条件状语。
**要点**：vary from A to B 表示“从 A 变化到 B”；at no load / under full load 是固定搭配，注意介词不同。
