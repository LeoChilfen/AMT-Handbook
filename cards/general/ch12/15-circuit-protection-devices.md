# Circuit Protection Devices

## 1. 原文

Perhaps the most serious trouble in a circuit is a direct short. The term “direct short” describes a situation in which some point in the circuit, where full system voltage is present, comes in direct contact with the ground or return side of the circuit. This establishes a path for current flow that contains no resistance other than that present in the wires carrying the current, and these wires have very little resistance.

Most wires used in aircraft electrical circuits are small gauge, and their current carrying capacity is quite limited. The size of the wires used in any given circuit is determined by the amount of current the wires are expected to carry under normal operating conditions. Any current flow in excess of normal, such as the case of a direct short, would cause a rapid generation of heat. If the excessive current flow caused by the short is left unchecked, the heat in the wire could cause a portion of the wire to melt and at the very least, open the circuit.

To protect aircraft electrical systems from damage and failure caused by excessive current, several kinds of protective devices are installed in the systems. Fuses, circuit breakers, thermal protectors, and arc fault circuit breakers are used for this purpose.

Circuit protective devices, as the name implies, all have a common purpose—to protect the units and the wires in the circuit. Some are designed primarily to protect the wiring and to open the circuit in such a way as to stop the current flow when the current becomes greater than the wires can safely carry. Other devices are designed to protect a unit in the circuit by stopping the current flow to it when the unit becomes excessively warm.

Fuses are used to protect the circuit from over current conditions. The fuse is installed in the circuit so that all the current in the circuit passes through it. In most fuses, the strip of metal is made of an alloy of tin and bismuth, which melts and opens the circuit when the current exceeds the rated capacity of the fuse. For example, if a 5-amp fuse is placed into a circuit, the fuse allows currents up to 5 amps to pass. Because the fuse is intended to protect the circuit, it is quite important that its capacity match the needs of the circuit in which it is used.

When replacing a fuse, consult the applicable manufacturer’s instructions to be sure a fuse of the correct type and capacity is installed. Fuses are installed in two types of fuse holders in aircraft. “Plug-in holders” or in-line holders are used for small and low capacity fuses. “Clip” type holders are used for heavy high capacity fuses and current limiters.

The current limiter is very much like the fuse. However, the current limiter link is usually made of copper and will stand a considerable overload for a short period of time. Like the fuse, it opens up in an over current condition in heavy current circuits such as 30 amp or greater. These are used primarily to sectionalize an aircraft circuit or bus. Once the limiter is opened, it must be replaced. The schematic symbol for the current limiter shows two triangles pointing to each other with a line on both sides of the triangles.

The circuit breaker is commonly used in place of a fuse and is designed to break the circuit and stop the current flow when the current exceeds a predetermined value. Unlike the fuse, the circuit breaker can be reset; whereas the fuse or current limiter must be replaced. There are several types of circuit breakers in general use in aircraft systems. One is a magnetic type. When excessive current flows in the circuit, it makes an electromagnet strong enough to move a small armature, which trips the breaker. Another type is the thermal overload switch or breaker. This consists of a bimetallic strip which, when it becomes overheated from excessive current, bends away from a catch on the switch lever and permits the switch to trip open.

Most circuit breakers must be reset by hand. If the overload condition still exists, the circuit breaker trips again to prevent damage to the circuit. At this point, it is usually not advisable to continue resetting the circuit breaker, but to initiate troubleshooting to determine the cause. Repeated resetting of a circuit breaker can lead to circuit or component damage or worse, the possibility of a fire or explosion. Automatic reset type circuit breakers are not allowed in aircraft. Circuit breakers are commonly grouped on a circuit breaker panel that is accessible to the flight crew.

#### Arc Fault Circuit Breaker

In recent years, the arc fault circuit breaker has begun to provide an additional layer of protection beyond that of the thermal protection already provided by conventional circuit breakers. The arc fault circuit breaker monitors the circuit for an electrical arcing signature, which can indicate possible wiring faults and unsafe conditions. These conditions can lead to fires or loss of power to critical systems. The arc fault circuit breaker is only beginning to make an appearance in the aircraft industry and is not widely used like the thermal type of circuit breaker.

### Thermal Protectors

A thermal protector, or switch, is used to protect a motor. It is designed to open the circuit automatically whenever the temperature of the motor becomes excessively high. It has two positions—open and closed. The most common use for a thermal switch is to keep a motor from overheating. If a malfunction in the motor causes it to overheat, the thermal switch breaks the circuit intermittently.

The thermal switch contains a bimetallic disk, or strip, that bends and breaks the circuit when it is heated. This occurs because one of the metals expands more than the other when they are subjected to the same temperature. When the strip or disk cools, the metals contract and the strip returns to its original position and closes the circuit.

Components in the electrical circuits are typically not all intended to operate continuously or automatically. Most of them are meant to operate at certain times, under certain conditions, to perform very definite functions. There must be some means of controlling their operation. Either a switch, or a relay, or both may be included in the circuit for this purpose.

Switches control the current flow in most aircraft electrical circuits. A switch is used to start, to stop, or to change the direction of the current flow in the circuit. The switch in each circuit must be able to carry the normal current of the circuit and must be insulated heavily enough for the voltage of the circuit.

An understanding of some basic definitions of the switch is necessary before any of the switch types are discussed. The number of poles, throws, and positions they have designates the switch type, as well as some other type of switches.

Pole—the switch’s movable blade or contactor. The number of poles is equal to the number of circuits, or paths for current flow, that can be completed through the switch at any one time.

Throw—indicates the number of circuits, or paths for current, that it is possible to complete through the switch with each pole or contactor.

Positions—indicates the number of places at which the operating device (toggle, plunger, and so forth) comes to rest and at the same time open or close one or more circuits.

A toggle switch that is spring-loaded to the OFF position and must be held in the ON position to complete the circuit is a momentary contact two-position switch. One that comes to rest at either of two positions, opening the circuit in one position and closing it in another, is a two-position switch. A toggle switch that comes to rest at any one of three positions is a three-position switch.

A switch that stays open, except when it is held in the closed position, is a normally open switch (usually identified as NO). One that stays closed, except when it is held in the open position is a normally closed switch (NC). Both kinds are spring loaded to their normal position and return to that position as soon as they are released.

Locking toggles require the operator to pull out on the switch toggle before moving it in to another position. Once in the new position, the switch toggle is release back into a lock, which then prevents the switch from inadvertently being moved.

A microswitch opens or closes a circuit with a very small movement of the tripping device ( 1⁄16 inch or less). This is what gives the switch its name, since micro means small.

Microswitches are usually pushbutton switches. They are used primarily as limit switches to provide automatic control of landing gears, actuator motors, and the like. When the operating plunger is pressed in, the spring and the movable contact are pushed, opening the contacts and the circuit.

#### Rotary Selector Switches

A rotary selector switch takes the place of several switches. When the knob of the switch is rotated, the switch opens one circuit and closes another. Ignition switches and voltmeter selector switches are typical examples of this kind of switch.

#### Pushbutton Switches

Pushbutton switches have one stationary contact and one movable contact. The movable contact is attached to the pushbutton. The pushbutton is either an insulator itself or is insulated from the contact. This switch is spring loaded and designed for momentary contact.

#### Lighted Pushbutton Switches

Another more common switch found in today’s aircraft is the lighted pushbutton switch. This type of switch takes the form of a 5⁄8-inch to 1-inch cube with incandescent or LED lights to indicate the function of the switch. Switch designs come in a number of configurations; the two most common are the alternate action and momentary action and usually have a two-pole or four-pole switch body. Other less common switch actions are the alternate and momentary holding coil configurations. The less known holding or latching coil switch bodies are designed to have a magnetic coil inside the switch body that is energized through two contacts in the base of the switch. When the coil is energized and the switch is pressed, the switch contacts remain latched until power is removed from the coil. This type of design allows for some degree of remote control over the switch body. The display optics of the lighted pushbutton switch provide the crew with a clear message that is visible under a wide range of lighting conditions with very high luminance and wide viewing angles. While some displays are simply a transparent screen that is backlit by an incandescent light, the higher quality and more reliable switches are available in sunlight readable displays and night vision (NVIS) versions. Due to the sunlight environment of the flight deck, displays utilizing standard lighting techniques “washout” when viewed in direct sunlight. Sunlight readable displays are designed to minimize this effect.

Lighted pushbutton switches can also be used in applications where a switch is not required and the optics are only for indications. This type of an indicator is commonly called an annunciator.

#### Dual In-Line Parallel (DIP) Switches

The acronym “DIP” switch is defined as Dual In-Line Parallel switch in reference to the physical layout. DIP switches are commonly found in card cages, and line replaceable units (LRU) and are used in most cases to adjust gains, control configurations, and so forth. Each one of the switches is generally an SPST slide or rocker switch. The technician may find this switch in packages ranging in size from DIP2 through DIP32. Some of the more common sizes are DIP4 and DIP8.

Switch guards are covers that protect a switch from unintended operation. Prior to the operation of the switch, the guard is usually lifted. Switch guards are commonly found on systems such as fire suppression and override logics for various systems. A traditional switch with a guard and a pushbutton switch with a guard are shown. The guard needs to be moved before the switch can be pushed.

A relay is simply an electromechanical switch where a small amount of current can control a large amount of current. When a voltage is applied to the coil of the relay, the electromagnet is energized due to the current. When energized, an electromagnetic field pulls the common (C) or arm of the relay down. When the arm or common is pulled down, the circuit between the arm and the normally closed (NC) contacts is opened and the circuit between the arm and the normally open (NO) contacts are closed. When the energizing voltage is removed, the spring returns the arm contacts back to the normally closed (NC) contacts. The relay usually has two connections for the coil. The (+) side is designated as X1 and the ground-side of the coil is designated as X2.

## 2. 中文翻译

电路中可能最严重的故障是直接短路。“直接短路”一词描述的是这样一种情形：电路中存在全系统电压的某一点，直接与电路的接地侧或回流侧接触。这建立了一条电流通路，该通路除承载电流的导线本身的电阻外没有其他电阻，而这些导线的电阻非常小。

飞机电气电路中使用的大多数导线都是细线规，其载流能力相当有限。在任何给定电路中，所用导线的尺寸由导线在正常工作条件下预期承载的电流量决定。任何超过正常值的电流，例如直接短路的情形，都会造成迅速发热。若短路引起的过大电流未被遏止，导线中的热量可能使一段导线熔化，至少会使电路断开。

为保护飞机电气系统免受过大的电流造成的损坏与失效，系统中装有若干种保护装置。为此使用保险丝、断路器、热保护器和电弧故障断路器。

电路保护装置，顾名思义，都有一个共同目的——保护电路中的单元和导线。有些装置主要用于保护线路，并在电流超过导线能安全承载的值时以停止电流流动的方式断开电路。另一些装置则用于在电路中的单元变得过热时停止流向它的电流，从而保护该单元。

保险丝用于保护电路免受过大电流的影响。保险丝接入电路的方式使电路中的全部电流都通过它。在大多数保险丝中，金属条由锡和铋的合金制成，当电流超过保险丝的额定容量时，它熔断并使电路断开。例如，若把 5 安培的保险丝接入电路，该保险丝允许最大 5 安培的电流通过。由于保险丝旨在保护电路，其容量与所用电路的需求相匹配非常重要。

更换保险丝时，应查阅适用制造商的说明，确保装上的保险丝类型与容量正确。飞机上的保险丝装在两类保险丝座中。“插入式座”或直列式座用于小型、低容量的保险丝。“夹持”式座用于大容量保险丝和限流器。

限流器与保险丝非常相似。不过，限流器的熔断片通常由铜制成，短时间内可承受相当大的过载。与保险丝一样，它在 30 安培或更大电流的重载电路中会在过电流情况下断开。这些装置主要用于把飞机电路或汇流条分段。限流器一旦断开，就必须更换。限流器的原理图符号画成两个相对的三角形，三角形两侧各有一条线。

断路器通常用来代替保险丝，其设计是在电流超过预定值时断开电路并停止电流流动。与保险丝不同，断路器可以复位；而保险丝或限流器必须更换。飞机系统中常用几种断路器。一种是磁性式。当电路中流过过大电流时，它使电磁铁产生足够强的磁力，驱动一个小衔铁动作，从而使断路器跳开。另一种是热过载开关或断路器。它由双金属片构成，当双金属片因过大电流而过热时，便从开关手柄的卡扣上弯开，使开关跳闸断开。

大多数断路器必须手动复位。若过载状况仍然存在，断路器会再次跳开以防止电路损坏。此时通常不宜继续复位断路器，而应开始排故以确定原因。反复复位断路器可能导致电路或部件损坏，更严重时可能引发火灾或爆炸。飞机上不允许使用自动复位型断路器。断路器通常集中装在飞行机组可接近的断路器板上。

#### 电弧故障断路器

近年来，电弧故障断路器开始在常规断路器已有的热保护之外提供额外一层保护。电弧故障断路器监测电路中的电弧特征信号，该信号可指示可能的线路故障和不安全状况。这些状况可能导致火灾或关键系统失去供电。电弧故障断路器在航空工业中才刚刚开始出现，尚未像热式断路器那样被广泛使用。

### 热保护器

热保护器（或称热开关）用于保护电动机。其设计是在电动机温度过高时自动断开电路。它有两个位置——断开和闭合。热开关最常见的用途是防止电动机过热。若电动机发生故障导致过热，热开关会间歇地断开电路。

热开关包含一个双金属盘或双金属片，受热时弯曲并断开电路。这是因为两种金属在相同温度下有一种膨胀得比另一种多。当双金属片或盘冷却时，两种金属收缩，双金属片回到原位并重新接通电路。

电路中的部件通常并非都要求连续或自动工作。大多数部件是用于在特定时间、特定条件下工作，以完成非常确定的功能。必须有一些手段来控制它们的运行。为此，电路中可装有开关、继电器，或两者兼有。

开关控制大多数飞机电气电路中的电流。开关用于启动、停止或改变电路中电流的方向。每个电路中的开关都必须能承载该电路的正常电流，并且其绝缘必须足以承受该电路的电压。

在讨论各种开关类型之前，有必要先了解开关的一些基本定义。它们所具有的极数、掷数和位置数决定……以及其他一些类型的开关。

极（Pole）——开关的可动刀片或触头。极数等于通过该开关在同一时间能够接通的电路数，即电流通路数。

掷（Throw）——表示用每个极或触头能够通过开关接通的电路数，即电流通路数。

位置（Positions）——表示操作装置（拨杆、柱塞等）停留、并同时断开或接通一个或多个电路的位置数目。

一种弹簧加载复位到 OFF 位置的拨动开关并且必须保持在 ON 位置才能接通电路，这属于瞬动触点双位开关。在两个位置中任一位置停留、在一个位置断开电路并在另一位置闭合电路的开关，是双位开关。在三个位置中任一位置停留的拨动开关，是三位开关。

除保持在闭合位置外一直断开的开关，是常开开关（通常标为 NO）。除保持在断开位置外一直闭合的开关，是常闭开关（NC）。这两种开关都由弹簧加载到其正常位置，一松开就回到该位置。

锁定式拨动开关要求操作者在把开关拨到另一位置之前先向外拉出拨杆。到达新位置后，拨杆被释放回锁定状态，从而防止开关被误动。

微动开关靠触发装置的极微小移动（1⁄16 英寸或更小）来断开或闭合电路。这正是它得名的原因，因为 micro 意为“小”。

微动开关通常是按钮式开关。它们主要用作限位开关，为起落架、作动筒电动机等提供自动控制。当操作柱塞被压下时，弹簧和活动触点被推动，使触点和电路断开。

#### 旋转选择开关

旋转选择开关可代替好几个开关。转动开关旋钮时，开关断开一个电路并接通另一个电路。点火开关和电压表选择开关就是这类开关的典型例子。

#### 按钮开关

按钮开关有一个固定触点和一个活动触点。活动触点连接在按钮上。按钮本身是绝缘的，或者与触点绝缘。这种开关由弹簧加载，设计用于瞬动接触。

#### 发光按钮开关

当今飞机上另一种更常见的开关是发光按钮开关。这种开关呈 5⁄8 英寸到 1 英寸的立方体形状，带有白炽灯或 LED 灯以指示开关的功能。开关设计有多种构型；最常见的两种是交替动作和瞬动动作，其开关本体通常为双极或四极。其他不太常见的开关动作是交替与瞬动保持线圈构型。较少见的保持或锁定线圈开关本体的设计，是在开关本体内部装有一个磁线圈，通过开关底座上的两个触点通电。线圈通电后按下开关，开关触点便保持锁定，直到线圈断电。这种设计使开关本体具有一定的遥控能力。发光按钮开关的显示光学器件为机组提供清晰的信息，在很宽的光照条件下都可见，亮度很高且视角很宽。虽然有些显示器只是由白炽灯背光的透明屏，但更高质量、更可靠的开关有阳光可读型显示器和夜视（NVIS）版本。由于驾驶舱的阳光环境，采用标准照明技术的显示器在阳光直射下会“发白”。阳光可读显示器就是为尽量减小这种效应而设计的。

在不要求开关功能、光学器件仅用于指示的场合，也可以使用发光按钮开关。这类指示器通常称为信号牌（annunciator）。

#### 双列直插（DIP）开关

缩写“DIP”开关按物理布局定义为双列直插（Dual In-Line Parallel）开关。DIP 开关常见于卡笼和航线可更换单元（LRU）中，多数情况下用于调整增益、控制配置等。其中每一个开关通常是 SPST 滑动或拨动式开关。技术人员可以见到从 DIP2 到 DIP32 各种封装尺寸的此类开关。较常见的尺寸有 DIP4 和 DIP8。

开关护盖是保护开关免遭误操作的外罩。操作开关前通常先掀起护盖。开关护盖常见于灭火等系统以及各种系统的超控逻辑中。图中给出一只带护盖的传统开关和一只带护盖的按钮开关。必须先移动护盖，才能按下开关。

继电器简单地说是一种机电开关，用很小的电流控制很大的电流。当电压加到继电器的线圈上时，电流使电磁铁励磁。励磁时，电磁场把继电器的公共端（C）或衔铁吸下。衔铁或公共端被吸下时，衔铁与常闭（NC）触点之间的电路断开，衔铁与常开（NO）触点之间的电路闭合。励磁电压撤除后，弹簧使衔铁触点回到常闭（NC）触点。继电器通常有两个线圈接线端。正极（+）侧标为 X1，线圈的接地侧标为 X2。

| pole | n. | 极 | 开关的可动刀片或触头，极数等于可同时接通的电路数 |
| throw | n. | 掷 | 每个极可接通的电路数，即电流通路数 |
| microswitch | n. | 微动开关 | 触发装置移动 1⁄16 英寸或更小时即动作的按钮式开关 |
| DIP switch | n. | 双列直插开关 | 按物理布局命名的多路 SPST 滑动或拨动开关组，用于调增益、配置 |
| annunciator | n. | 信号牌 | 不承担开关功能、仅用光学器件指示的发光指示器 |

## 4. 语法解析

### ① in which 与 where 引导的嵌套定语从句
**原句**：The term “direct short” describes a situation in which some point in the circuit, where full system voltage is present, comes in direct contact with the ground or return side of the circuit.
**拆解**：主句 describes a situation；in which 引导定语从句修饰 a situation，从句主语 some point 后接 in the circuit 与 where 引导的定语从句（修饰 some point），谓语 comes in direct contact with…；
**要点**：in which 与 where 都表地点/情形，但 in which 只能用于“名词 + 介词 + which”，where 可单独引导；插入的 where 从句把主语与谓语隔开，阅读时要先跳过插入成分找谓语 comes；come in contact with 是“与……接触”的固定搭配。

### ② such … as to 结构 + 比较状语从句
**原句**：Some are designed primarily to protect the wiring and to open the circuit in such a way as to stop the current flow when the current becomes greater than the wires can safely carry.
**拆解**：主句谓语 are designed 后接两个并列不定式 to protect 与 to open；in such a way as to stop 为“such + 名词 + as to do”结构表结果；when 引导时间状语从句，其中 greater than 引导比较从句 the wires can safely carry（省略宾语 the current）。
**要点**：such…as to do 意为“如此……以致”，比 so as to 多一层“方式”含义；比较从句常省略与主句重复的宾语，the wires can safely carry 后省去了 the current；in such a way as to 可整体译为“以……的方式”。

### ③ it is important that + 虚拟语气 + in which 定语从句
**原句**：Because the fuse is intended to protect the circuit, it is quite important that its capacity match the needs of the circuit in which it is used.
**拆解**：Because 引导原因状语从句，is intended to protect 为被动；主句中 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句；从句谓语 match 用动词原形，属虚拟语气；in which it is used 为定语从句修饰 the circuit。
**要点**：It is important/necessary that… 后的从句谓语用 (should) + 动词原形，match 不加 -s 正是这一规则的体现；in which 指代 in the circuit，不能换成 where 以外的其他关系词；be intended to do 意为“旨在做”。

### ④ Unlike 对比 + 分号连接的 whereas 并列句
**原句**：Unlike the fuse, the circuit breaker can be reset; whereas the fuse or current limiter must be replaced.
**拆解**：Unlike the fuse 为介词短语作对比状语；第一个分句 the circuit breaker can be reset 为被动；分号后 whereas 引导对比从句/并列句，主语 the fuse or current limiter 为选择关系，谓语 must be replaced 为被动。
**要点**：unlike 后只接名词性成分，不能接句子；分号后 whereas 表示前后对比，可译为“而”；must be replaced 用被动是因为施动者（维修人员）不言自明；reset 的过去分词与原形同形，注意不要误判时态。

### ⑤ what 引导的表语从句 + since 原因从句
**原句**：This is what gives the switch its name, since micro means small.
**拆解**：主句 This is what gives the switch its name 中，what 引导表语从句并在从句中作主语；since 引导原因状语从句，说明命名的由来；give sb sth 双宾语结构中 its name 为直接宾语。
**要点**：what 引导名词性从句时不能换成 that（that 只起连接作用，不充当成分）；this is what… 是“这就是……的原因/东西”的高频句式；since 表原因时语气弱于 because，常置于句末补充说明。

### ⑥ 不定式复合结构 + before + 动名词
**原句**：Locking toggles require the operator to pull out on the switch toggle before moving it in to another position.
**拆解**：主句谓语 require 后接 the operator to pull out，构成 require sb to do 的复合结构；before 为介词，后接动名词 moving，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致；in to another position 中 in 为副词，to 表方向。
**要点**：require/ask/tell 等动词后用 to do 作宾补，不能用 that 从句替代；before 后接动名词表示“在……之前”，若接从句则用 before he moves；pull out on 表示“向外拉”，out 为副词，介词 on 引出被拉的对象。
