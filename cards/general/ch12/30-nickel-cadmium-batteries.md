# Nickel-Cadmium Batteries

## 1. 原文

### Chemistry and Construction

Active materials in nickel-cadmium cells (Ni-Cad) are nickel hydroxide (NiOOH) in the charged positive plate (Anode) and sponge cadmium (Cd) in the charged negative plate (Cathode). The electrolyte is a potassium hydroxide (KOH) solution in concentration of 20–34 percent by weight pure KOH in distilled water.

Sintered nickel-cadmium cells have relatively thin sintered nickel matrices forming a plate grid structure. The grid structure is highly porous and is impregnated with the active positive material (nickel-hydroxide) and the negative material (cadmium-hydroxide). The plates are then formed by sintering nickel powder to fine-mesh wire screen. In other variations of the process, the active material in the sintered matrix is converted chemically, or thermally, to an active state and then formed. In general, there are many steps to these cycles of impregnation and formation. Thin sintered plate cells are ideally suited for very high rate charge and discharge service. Pocket plate nickel-cadmium cells have the positive or negative active material, pressed into pockets of perforated nickel-plated steel plates or into tubes. The active material is trapped securely in contact with a metal current collector so active material shedding is largely eliminated. The typical open circuit cell voltage of a nickel-cadmium battery is about 1.25 volts.

### Operation of Nickel-Cadmium Cells

When a charging current is applied to a nickel-cadmium battery, the negative plates lose oxygen and begin forming metallic cadmium. The active material of the positive plates, nickel-hydroxide, becomes more highly oxidized. This process continues while the charging current is applied or until all the oxygen is removed from the negative plates and only cadmium remains.

Toward the end of the charging cycle, the cells emit gas. This also occurs if the cells are overcharged. This gas is caused by decomposition of the water in the electrolyte into hydrogen at the negative plates and oxygen at the positive plates. The voltage used during charging, as well as the temperature, determines when gassing occurs. To completely charge a nickel-cadmium battery, some gassing, however slight, must take place; thus, some water is used.

The chemical action is reversed during discharge. The positive plates slowly give up oxygen, which is regained by the negative plates. This process results in the conversion of the chemical energy into electrical energy. During discharge, the plates absorb a quantity of the electrolyte. On recharge, the level of the electrolyte rises and, at full charge, the electrolyte is at its highest level. Therefore, water should be added only when the battery is fully charged.

The nickel-cadmium battery is usually interchangeable with the lead-acid type. When replacing a lead-acid battery with a nickel-cadmium battery, the battery compartment must be clean, dry, and free of all traces of acid from the old battery. The compartment must be washed out and neutralized with ammonia or boric acid solution, allowed to dry thoroughly, and then painted with an alkali resisting varnish.

The pad in the battery sump jar should be saturated with a three percent (by weight) solution of boric acid and water before connecting the battery vent system.

#### General Maintenance and Safety Precautions

Refer to the battery manufacturer for detailed service instructions. Below are general recommendations for maintenance and safety precautions. For vented nickel- cadmium cells, the general maintenance requirements are:

1. Hydrate cells to supply water lost during overcharging.

2. Maintain inter-cell connectors at proper torque values.

3. Keep cell tops and exposed sides clean and dry.

Electrolyte spillage can form grounding paths. White moss around vent cap seals is potassium carbonate (K 2CO3). Clean up these surfaces with distilled water and dry. While handling the caustic potassium hydroxide electrolyte, wear safety goggles to protect the eyes. The technician should also wear plastic gloves and an apron to protect skin and clothes. In case of spillage on hands or clothes, neutralize the alkali immediately with vinegar or dilute boric acid solution (one pound per gallon of water); then rinse with clear water.

During overcharging conditions, explosive mixtures of hydrogen and oxygen develop in nickel-cadmium cells. When this occurs, the cell relief valves vent these gases to the atmosphere, creating a potentially explosive hazard. Additionally, room ventilation should be such as to prevent a hydrogen build up in closed spaces from exceeding one percent by volume. Explosions can occur at concentrations above four percent by volume in air.

### Sealed Lead Acid (SLA) Batteries

In many applications, sealed lead acid (SLA) batteries are gaining in use over flooded lead acid and Ni-Cad batteries. One leading characteristic of Ni-Cad batteries is that they perform well in low voltage, full-discharge, high cycle applications. However, they do not perform as well in extended standby applications, such as auxiliary or as emergency battery packs used to power inertial reference units or stand-by equipment (attitude gyro).

It is typical during the servicing of a Ni-Cad battery to match as many as twenty individual cells in order to prevent unbalance and thus cell reversal during end of discharge. When a Ni-Cad does reverse, very high pressure and heat can result. The result is often pressure seal rupture, and in the worst case, a cell explosion. With SLA batteries, cell matching is inherent in each battery. Ni-Cads also have an undesirable characteristic caused by constant overcharge and infrequent discharges, as in standby applications. It is technically known as “voltage depression” and commonly but erroneously called “memory effect.” This characteristic is only detectable when a full discharge is attempted. Thus, it is possible to believe a full charge exists, while in fact it does not. SLA batteries do not have this characteristic voltage depression (memory) phenomenon, and therefore do not require scheduled deep cycle maintenance as do Ni-Cads.

The Ni-Cad emergency battery pack requires relatively complicated test equipment due to the complex characteristics of the Ni-Cad. Sealed lead acid batteries do not have these temperamental characteristics and therefore it is not necessary to purchase special battery maintenance equipment. Some manufacturers of SLA batteries have included in the battery packs a means by which the battery can be tested while still installed on the aircraft. Ni-Cads must have a scheduled energy test performed on the bench due to the inability to measure their energy level on the aircraft, and because of their notable “memory” shortcoming.

The SLA battery can be designed to alert the technician if a battery is failing. Furthermore, it may be possible to test the failure detection circuits by activating a Built in Test (BITE) button. This practice significantly reduces FAA paperwork and maintenance workload.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 化学组成与结构

镍镉单体电池（Ni-Cad）中的活性物质是：充电状态正极板（阳极）中的氢氧化镍（NiOOH）和充电状态负极板（阴极）中的海绵状镉（Cd）。电解液是氢氧化钾（KOH）溶液，浓度为按重量计 20–34% 的纯 KOH 溶于蒸馏水中。

烧结式镍镉单体电池具有较薄的烧结镍基体，构成极板骨架结构。该骨架结构高度多孔，并浸渍了正极活性物质（氢氧化镍）和负极活性物质（氢氧化镉）。随后把镍粉烧结到细目金属丝网上形成极板。在这一工艺的其他变型中，烧结基体中的活性物质先经化学或热的方法转变为活性状态，然后再成型。一般来说，这些浸渍与成型的循环包含许多步骤。薄烧结式极板单体电池非常适合于极高倍率的充电和放电使用。袋式镍镉单体电池把正极或负极活性物质压入镀镍穿孔钢板的小袋中，或压入管中。活性物质被牢固地包住并与金属集流体保持接触，因此活性物质的脱落基本上被消除。极板设计按循环使用要求不同而厚度各异。镍镉蓄电池的典型开路单体电压约为 1.25 伏。

### 镍镉单体电池的工作

对镍镉蓄电池施加充电电流时，负极板失去氧并开始生成金属镉。正极板的活性物质氢氧化镍则被进一步氧化。这一过程在充电电流持续期间进行，直到负极板中的氧全部被除去、只剩下镉为止。

在充电周期接近结束时，单体电池会析出气体。单体电池过充时也会出现这种情况。这种气体是由电解液中的水分解产生的：负极板处析氢，正极板处析氧。充电时所使用的电压以及温度决定何时析气。要使镍镉蓄电池完全充电，必须发生一定量的析气，无论多么轻微；因此会消耗一些水。

放电时化学作用反转。正极板缓慢放出氧，而氧又被负极板收回。这一过程使化学能转化为电能。放电时极板吸收一定量的电解液。再充电时电解液液面上升，满充时电解液处于最高液面。因此，只有在蓄电池完全充电时才应加水。

镍镉蓄电池通常可以与铅酸蓄电池互换。用镍镉蓄电池替换铅酸蓄电池时，蓄电池舱必须清洁、干燥，并且没有任何旧蓄电池留下的酸痕迹。舱室必须冲洗，并用氨水或硼酸溶液中和，让其彻底干燥，然后涂上一层耐碱清漆。

在连接蓄电池通气系统之前，蓄电池积液瓶中的衬垫应先用按重量计 3% 的硼酸水溶液浸透。

#### 一般维护与安全注意事项

详细的使用说明请向蓄电池制造商查询。以下是一般性的维护和安全注意事项建议。对于通气式镍镉单体电池，一般维护要求是：

1. 给单体电池补水，以补充过充时损失的水。

2. 保持单体电池间连接件处于规定的拧紧力矩值。

3. 保持单体电池顶部和外露侧面清洁、干燥。

电解液溢流会形成接地通路。通气帽密封处的白色苔状物是碳酸钾（K 2CO3）。用蒸馏水清洗这些表面并擦干。处理苛性氢氧化钾电解液时，应戴护目镜保护眼睛。技术人员还应戴塑料手套和围裙，以保护皮肤和衣物。若手或衣物沾上溢液，应立即用醋或稀硼酸溶液（每加仑水一磅）中和碱液，然后用清水冲洗。

过充情况下，镍镉单体电池内会产生氢与氧的爆炸性混合气。出现这种情况时，单体电池的安全阀把这些气体排到大气中，从而形成潜在的爆炸危险。此外，室内通风应能防止封闭空间内氢的积聚超过体积的百分之一。当浓度高于空气中体积的百分之四时就可能发生爆炸。

### 密封铅酸（SLA）蓄电池

在许多应用中，密封铅酸（SLA）蓄电池的使用正逐渐超过富液式铅酸蓄电池和镍镉蓄电池。镍镉蓄电池的一个突出特点是，它们在低电压、全放电、高循环次数的应用中表现良好。然而，在长时间待机应用中，例如用作给惯性基准组件或备用设备（姿态陀螺）供电的辅助蓄电池组或应急蓄电池组时，它们的表现就不那么好。

在维护镍镉蓄电池时，通常要配组多达二十只单体电池，以防止不平衡，从而防止放电末期发生单体反极。当镍镉单体电池真的反极时，可能产生极高的压力和热量。结果往往是压力密封失效，最坏情况下是单体电池爆炸。对于 SLA 蓄电池，每只蓄电池本身就内在地完成单体配组。镍镉电池还有一个不良特性，是由持续过充和不常放电（如待机应用）引起的。它在技术上称为「电压凹陷」，通常被错误地称为「记忆效应」。这一特性只有在尝试完全放电时才能检测到。因此，有可能以为电量充足，而实际上并非如此。SLA 蓄电池没有这种电压凹陷（记忆）现象，因此不需要像镍镉电池那样安排定期的深循环维护。

由于镍镉电池特性复杂，镍镉应急蓄电池组需要相对复杂的测试设备。密封铅酸蓄电池没有这些易变的特性，因此不必购买专用的蓄电池维护设备。有些 SLA 蓄电池制造商在蓄电池组内设置了这样的手段：蓄电池仍装在飞机上就能进行测试。镍镉电池则必须在台架上按计划进行能量测试，因为无法在飞机上测量其能量水平，而且它们有明显的「记忆」缺陷。

SLA 蓄电池可以设计成在蓄电池性能下降时提醒技术人员。此外，还可以通过按下机内自检（BITE）按钮来测试故障检测电路。这种做法大大减少了 FAA 的文书工作和维护工作量。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 系表结构 + 长并列表语
**原句**：Active materials in nickel-cadmium cells (Ni-Cad) are nickel hydroxide (NiOOH) in the charged positive plate (Anode) and sponge cadmium (Cd) in the charged negative plate (Cathode).
**拆解**：主语 Active materials in nickel-cadmium cells (Ni-Cad)（in 短语为后置定语），系动词 are，表语为两个并列名词短语 nickel hydroxide (NiOOH) in the charged positive plate (Anode) 与 sponge cadmium (Cd) in the charged negative plate (Cathode)。
**要点**：英文定义句常把物质组成与所在极板写在同一个表语里，翻译时宜拆成分句并补「……中的」；the charged positive plate 译「充电状态的正极板」，charged 是过去分词作定语，不可误译为「被充电的板」；Anode 与 Cathode 在本节按原文分别标注正、负极板。
### ② 不定式目的状语 + however + 形容词的让步插入语
**原句**：To completely charge a nickel-cadmium battery, some gassing, however slight, must take place; thus, some water is used.
**拆解**：To completely charge a nickel-cadmium battery 为不定式短语作目的状语，置于句首；主句主语 some gassing，插入语 however slight 补充让步意味（=no matter how slight），谓语 must take place；分号后 thus, some water is used 为结果分句，用被动语态。
**要点**：however + 形容词的插入语相当于「无论多么……」，须用逗号隔开，不能写成 however slightly；must take place 表示「必然发生」，与 must be done 的被动不同，这里 take place 是不及物动词短语；分号连接两个关系密切的独立分句，thus 引出结果。
### ③ When + 现在分词短语的省略状语 + must be 并列表语
**原句**：When replacing a lead-acid battery with a nickel-cadmium battery, the battery compartment must be clean, dry, and free of all traces of acid from the old battery.
**拆解**：When replacing a lead-acid battery with a nickel-cadmium battery 为省略式时间状语从句（When the technician is replacing…），replace A with B 中 with 引出替换物；主句 the battery compartment must be clean, dry, and free of … 为 must be 加三个并列表语。
**要点**：状语从句主语与主句主语不一致时可改用 When + 现在分词，此时该分词短语的逻辑主语必须是主句主语（technician），因此不能照字面理解为「蓄电池舱在替换」；free of 意为「不含、没有」，是固定搭配，不写 free from 亦可；must be clean, dry, and free 三个表语共用 must be。
### ④ 四个并列被动谓语
**原句**：The compartment must be washed out and neutralized with ammonia or boric acid solution, allowed to dry thoroughly, and then painted with an alkali resisting varnish.
**拆解**：主语 The compartment，谓语为四个并列的被动结构 must be washed out、neutralized (with …)、allowed (to dry thoroughly)、painted (with …)，后三个共用 must be；neutralized 与 washed out 由 and 连接，之后用逗号和 and 收尾。
**要点**：英语为避重复只保留第一个 must be，其余用过去分词并列，翻译时每个动作前要补出「必须」；be allowed to dry 中的 allowed 是「让其」而非「被允许」；alkali resisting varnish 为「名词 + 现在分词」复合定语，意为「耐碱清漆」。
### ⑤ it 形式主语 + 不定式真正主语 + in order to 目的
**原句**：It is typical during the servicing of a Ni-Cad battery to match as many as twenty individual cells in order to prevent unbalance and thus cell reversal during end of discharge.
**拆解**：it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to match as many as twenty individual cells；is typical 为系表结构，during the servicing of a Ni-Cad battery 为时间状语；in order to prevent unbalance and thus cell reversal during end of discharge 为目的状语，and thus 连接两个并列宾语。
**要点**：as many as 二十 cells 表示「多达二十只」，强调数量之大；prevent A and (thus) B 中 thus 表明 B 是 A 的后果，翻译用「从而」；cell reversal 指单体极性反转，是镍镉电池特有的故障模式，不宜译成「电池反转」。
