## 1. 原文

After the discovery that an electric current flowing through a conductor creates a magnetic field around the conductor, there was considerable scientific speculation about whether a magnetic field could create a current flow in a conductor. In 1831, Faraday discovered that this could be accomplished.

To show how an electric current can be created by a magnetic field, a demonstration similar to the one in the figure can be used. Several turns of a conductor are wrapped around a cylindrical form, and the ends of the conductor are connected together to form a complete circuit, which includes a galvanometer. If a simple bar magnet is plunged into the cylinder, the galvanometer can be observed to deflect in one direction from its zero (center) position.

When the magnet is at rest inside the cylinder, the galvanometer shows a reading of zero, indicating that no current is flowing.

In the figure, the galvanometer indicates a current flow in the opposite direction when the magnet is pulled from the cylinder.

The same results may be obtained by holding the magnet stationary and moving the cylinder over the magnet, indicating that a current flows when there is relative motion between the wire coil and the magnetic field. These results obey a law first stated by the German scientist, Heinrich Lenz. Lenz’s Law states that the induced current caused by the relative motion of a conductor and a magnetic field always flows in such a direction that its magnetic field opposes the motion.

When a conductor is moved through a magnetic field, an emf is induced in the conductor. The direction (polarity) of the induced emf is determined by the magnetic lines of force and the direction the conductor is moved through the magnetic field. The generator left-hand rule (not to be confused with the left-hand rules used with a coil) can be used to determine the direction of the induced emf. The left-hand rule is summed up as follows:

The first finger of the left hand is pointed in the direction of the magnetic lines of force (North to South), the thumb is pointed in the direction of movement of the conductor through the magnetic field, and the second finger points in the direction of the induced emf.

When a loop conductor is rotated in a magnetic field, a voltage is induced in each side of the loop. The two sides cut the magnetic field in opposite directions, and although the current flow is continuous, it moves in opposite directions with respect to the two sides of the loop. If sides A and B and the loop are rotated half a turn and the sides of the conductor have exchanged positions, the induced emf in each wire reverses its direction, since the wire formerly cutting the lines of force in an upward direction is now moving downward.

The value of an induced emf depends on three factors:

1. Number of wires moving through the magnetic field

2. Strength of the magnetic field

3. Speed of rotation

### Generators of Alternating Current

Generators used to produce an alternating current are called AC generators or alternators.

The simple generator constitutes one method of generating an alternating voltage. It consists of a rotating loop, marked A and B, placed between two magnetic poles, N and S. The ends of the loop are connected to two metal slip rings (collector rings), C1 and C2. Current is taken from the collector rings by brushes. If the loop is considered as separate wires A and B, and the left-hand rule for generators is applied, then it can be observed that as wire A moves up across the field, a voltage is induced which causes the current to flow inward. As wire B moves down across the field, a voltage is induced which causes the current to flow outward. When the wires are formed into a loop, the voltages induced in the two sides of the loop are combined. Therefore, for explanatory purposes, the action of either conductor, A or B, while rotating in the magnetic field is similar to the action of the loop.

The figure illustrates the generation of AC with a simple loop conductor rotating in a magnetic field. As it is rotated in a counterclockwise direction, varying values of voltages are induced in it.

The conductor A moves parallel to the lines of force. Since it cuts no lines of force, the induced voltage is zero. As the conductor advances from position 1 to position 2, the voltage induced gradually increases.

The conductor is now moving perpendicular to the flux and cuts a maximum number of lines of force; therefore, a maximum voltage is induced. As the conductor moves beyond position 2, it cuts a decreasing amount of flux at each instant, and the induced voltage decreases.

At this point, the conductor has made one-half of a revolution and again moves parallel to the lines of force, and no voltage is induced in the conductor. As the A conductor passes position 3, the direction of induced voltage now reverses since the A conductor is moving downward, cutting flux in the opposite direction. As the A conductor moves across the South pole, the induced voltage gradually increases in a negative direction, until it reaches position 4.

Like position 2, the conductor is again moving perpendicular to the flux and generates a maximum negative voltage. From position 4 to 5, the induced voltage gradually decreases until the voltage is zero, and the conductor and wave are ready to start another cycle.

The curve shown at position 5 is called a sine wave. It represents the polarity and the magnitude of the instantaneous values of the voltages generated. The horizontal base line is divided into degrees, or time, and the vertical distance above or below the base line represents the value of voltage at each particular point in the rotation of the loop.

### Cycle and Frequency

A cycle is a repetition of a pattern. Whenever a voltage or current passes through a series of changes, returns to the starting point, and then again starts the same series of changes, the series is called a cycle. The cycle is represented by the symbol of a wavy line in a circle ~ . In the cycle of voltage shown in the figure, the voltage increases from zero to a maximum positive value, decreases to zero; then increases to a maximum negative value, and again decreases to zero. At this point, it is ready to go through the same series of changes. There are two alternations in a complete cycle: the positive alternation and the negative. Each is half a cycle.

The frequency is the number of cycles of AC per second (1 second). The standard unit of frequency measurement is the hertz (Hz). In a generator, the voltage and current pass through a complete cycle of values each time a coil or conductor passes under a North and South pole of the magnet. #### Wavelength Defined

The distance that a waveform travels during a period is commonly referred to as a wavelength and is indicated by the Greek letter lambda (l). The measurement of wavelength is taken from one point on the waveform to a corresponding point on the next waveform.

### Phase Relationships

In addition to frequency and cycle characteristics, alternating voltage and current also have a relationship called “phase.” In a circuit that is fed (supplied) by one alternator, there must be a certain phase relationship between voltage and current if the circuit is to function efficiently. In a system fed by two or more alternators, not only must there be a certain phase relationship between voltage and current of one alternator, but there must be a phase relationship between the individual voltages and the individual currents. Also, two separate circuits can be compared by comparing the phase

#### In Phase Condition

The figure shows a voltage signal and a current signal superimposed on the same time axis. Notice that when the voltage increases in the positive alternation that the current also increases. When the voltage reaches its peak value, so does the current. Both waveforms then reverse and decrease back to a zero magnitude, then proceed in the same manner in the negative direction as they did in the positive direction. When two waves, such as these, are exactly in step with each other, they are said to be in phase. To be in phase, the two waveforms must go through their maximum and minimum points at the same time and in the same direction.

#### Out of Phase Condition

When two waveforms go through their maximum and minimum points at different times, a phase difference exists between the two. In this case, the two wave-forms are said to be out of phase with each other. The terms lead and lag are often used to describe the phase difference between waveforms. The waveform that reaches its maximum or minimum value first is said to lead the other waveform. The figure shows this relationship. V oltage source one starts to rise at the 0° position and voltage source two starts to rise at the 90° position. Because voltage source one begins its rise earlier in time (90°) in relation to the second voltage source, it is said to be leading the second source. On the other hand, the second source is said to be lagging the first source. When a waveform is said to be leading or lagging, the difference in degrees is usually stated. If the two waveforms differ by 360°, they are said to be in phase with each other. If there is a 180° difference between the two signals, then they are still out of phase even though they are both reaching their minimum and maximum values at the same time.

The peak value is the largest instantaneous value. The largest single positive value occurs when the sine wave of voltage is at 90°, and the largest single negative value occurs when it is at 270°. Maximum value is 1.41 times the effective value. These are called peak values.

#### Effective Value

The effective value is also known as the RMS value or root mean square, which refers to the mathematical process by which the value is derived. Most AC voltmeters display the effective or RMS value when used. The effective value is less than the maximum value, being equal to .707 times the maximum value.

The effective value of a sine wave is actually a measure of the heating effect of the sine wave. The figure illustrates what happens when a resistor is connected across an AC voltage source. The value of the DC voltage source can now be adjusted so that the resistor dissipates the same amount of heat as it did when it was in the AC circuit. The RMS or effective value of a sine wave is equal to the DC voltage that produces the same amount of heat as the sinusoidal voltage.

### Opposition to Current Flow of AC

There are three factors that can create an opposition to the flow of electrons (current) in an AC circuit. Resistance, similar to resistance of DC circuits, is measured in ohms and has a direct influence on AC regardless of frequency. Inductive reactance and capacitive reactance, on the other hand, oppose current flow only in AC circuits, not in DC circuits. Since AC constantly changes direction and intensity, inductors and capacitors may also create an opposition to current flow in AC circuits. It should also be noted that inductive reactance and capacitive reactance may create a phase shift between the voltage and current in an AC circuit. Whenever analyzing an AC circuit, it is very important to consider the resistance, inductive reactance, and the capacitive reactance. All three have an effect on the current of that circuit.

在发现流经导体的电流会在导体周围产生磁场之后，科学界对「磁场能否在导体中产生电流」进行了大量推测。1831 年，法拉第发现这是可以做到的。

为了演示电流如何由磁场产生，可以使用一个与图中相似的演示装置。把导体在圆柱形骨架上绕若干圈，再把导体的两端连接起来，构成一个包含检流计的闭合回路。如果把一根条形磁铁迅速插入圆柱体内，可以观察到检流计从零位（中心位置）向一个方向偏转。

当磁铁静止在圆柱体内时，检流计读数为零，表明没有电流流动。

在图中，当磁铁从圆柱体内被抽出时，检流计显示电流沿相反方向流动。

保持磁铁不动、让圆柱体在磁铁上移动，也能得到同样的结果，这说明当线圈与磁场之间存在相对运动时就会产生电流。这些结果遵循德国科学家海因里希·楞次（Heinrich Lenz）首先提出的定律。楞次定律指出：由导体与磁场的相对运动引起的感应电流，其流动方向总是使其产生的磁场阻碍这一运动。

当导体在磁场中运动时，导体内会感应出电动势（emf）。感应电动势的方向（极性）由磁力线以及导体穿过磁场的运动方向决定。发电机左手定则（不要与线圈所用的左手定则混淆）可用来判定感应电动势的方向。左手定则归纳如下：

左手的食指指向磁力线方向（由北到南），拇指指向导体穿过磁场的运动方向，中指指向感应电动势的方向。

当线圈在磁场中旋转时，线圈的每一侧都会感应出电压。两侧切割磁场的方向相反，因此尽管电流是连续的，但相对于线圈的两侧，电流的流动方向相反。如果 A、B 两侧连同线圈转过半圈、两侧导体的位置互换，那么每根导线中的感应电动势都会反向，因为原先向上切割磁力线的导线现在改为向下运动。

感应电动势的大小取决于三个因素：

1. 穿过磁场运动的导线数量

2. 磁场的强度

3. 旋转速度

### 交流发电机

用来产生交流电的发电机称为交流发电机（alternator）。

最简单的发电机是产生交流电压的一种方法。它由一段置于 N、S 两个磁极之间、标记为 A 和 B 的旋转线圈构成。线圈的两端接到两个金属滑环（集流环）C1 和 C2 上，电流由电刷从集流环上引出。如果把线圈看作 A、B 两根独立的导线并应用发电机左手定则，就可以看出：导线 A 向上穿过磁场时，会感应出电动势，使电流向内流动；导线 B 向下穿过磁场时，会感应出电动势，使电流向外流动。把两根导线接成线圈后，线圈两侧感应出的电压相互叠加。因此，为便于说明，可以认为导体 A 或 B 在磁场中旋转时的作用与整个线圈相同。

图中示出了简单线圈导体在磁场中旋转而产生交流电的过程。当它沿逆时针方向旋转时，其中感应出的电压大小不断变化。

导体 A 平行于磁力线运动。由于它不切割任何磁力线，感应电压为零。当导体从位置 1 前进到位置 2 时，感应电压逐渐增大。

此时导体垂直于磁通运动，切割的磁力线数量最多，因此感应出的电压最大。导体越过位置 2 之后，每一瞬间切割的磁通量逐渐减少，感应电压随之减小。

此时导体已转过半圈，再次平行于磁力线运动，导体内不感应电压。当导体 A 通过位置 3 时，感应电压的方向发生反转，因为导体 A 正在向下运动，以相反的方向切割磁通。当导体 A 越过南极时，感应电压沿负方向逐渐增大，直到到达位置 4。

与位置 2 一样，导体再次垂直于磁通运动，产生最大的负向电压。从位置 4 到位置 5，感应电压逐渐减小，直至为零，于是导体和波形都准备好开始下一个周波。

位置 5 处的曲线称为正弦波。它表示所产生电压的极性以及各瞬时值的大小。水平基线按角度或时间划分，基线以上或以下的垂直距离表示线圈旋转到各特定点时的电压值。

### 周波与频率

周波是某种模式的重复。每当电压或电流经历一系列变化、回到起始点、然后再次开始同一系列变化时，这一系列变化就称为一个周波。周波用圆圈中一条波浪线的符号 ~ 表示。在图中所示的电压周波中，电压从零升至正向最大值，再降至零；然后升至负向最大值，再降回零。此时它已准备好重复同一系列变化。一个完整周波中有两个半周：正半周和负半周。每个半周为一个周波的一半。

#### 频率的定义

频率是交流电每秒的周波数（1 秒）。频率测量的标准单位是赫兹（Hz）。在发电机中，线圈或导体每经过磁铁的一个北（N）极和一个南（S）极下方，电压和电流就经历一个完整的周波变化。线圈或导体每转一周的周波数等于磁极对数。因此，频率等于一周的周波数乘以每秒转数（rps）。用公式表示：

F =（极数⁄2）×（rpm⁄60）　式中 P⁄2 为极对数，rpm/60 为每秒转数。若为 2 极发电机，转子以 3,600 rpm 旋转，则每秒转数为：rps = 3,600 ÷ 60 = 60 转/秒　由于有 2 个磁极，P⁄2 为 1，频率即为 60 周波/秒（cps）。对于电枢转速为 1,800 rpm 的 4 极发电机，代入公式：

F =（P⁄2）×（rpm⁄60）　代入如下：F =（4⁄2）×（1,800⁄60）　F = 2 × 30　F = 60 cps

正弦波完成一个完整周波所需的时间称为周期。正弦波的周期与频率成反比：频率越高，周期越短。频率与周期之间的数学关系为：

#### 波长的定义

波形在一个周期内行进的距离通常称为波长，用希腊字母 lambda（l）表示。波长的测量是从波形上的一点量到相邻波形上与之对应的点。

峰值是最大的瞬时值。最大的单一正值出现在电压正弦波处于 90° 时，最大的单一负值出现在 270° 时。最大值是有效值的 1.41 倍。这些值称为峰值。

### 交流电对电流流动的阻碍

在交流电路中，有三种因素会阻碍电子（电流）的流动。电阻与直流电路中的电阻类似，以欧姆计量，无论频率如何都直接影响交流电。另一方面，感抗和容抗只在交流电路中阻碍电流流动，在直流电路中则不会。由于交流电不断改变方向和大小，电感器和电容器也可能在交流电路中形成对电流流动的阻碍。还应指出，感抗和容抗可能在交流电路中造成电压与电流之间的相移。分析交流电路时，考虑电阻、感抗和容抗非常重要。这三者都会影响该电路中的电流。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 同位语从句 + 介词宾语从句
**原句**：After the discovery that an electric current flowing through a conductor creates a magnetic field around the conductor, there was considerable scientific speculation about whether a magnetic field could create a current flow in a conductor.
**拆解**：After 后接名词 discovery，that 引导同位语从句说明 discovery 的内容（从句主语 an electric current，flowing through a conductor 为现在分词短语作后置定语，谓语 creates）；主句为 there was considerable scientific speculation，about 后接 whether 引导的宾语从句。
**要点**：that 在同位语从句中不作成分、只起连接作用，不能换成 which；介词 about 后面只能用 whether 不能用 if；there be 句型中真正的主语是 speculation，翻译时按「有大量推测」处理。

### ② 并列被动分句 + 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：Several turns of a conductor are wrapped around a cylindrical form, and the ends of the conductor are connected together to form a complete circuit, which includes a galvanometer.
**拆解**：主句由 and 连接两个被动分句：Several turns of a conductor are wrapped around a cylindrical form 与 the ends of the conductor are connected together to form a complete circuit，其中 to form a complete circuit 为目的不定式；逗号后 which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词为 a complete circuit，在从句中作主语，谓语 includes a galvanometer。
**要点**：两个分句都用一般现在时被动，描述实验装置时施动者无关紧要；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是前面的名词短语 a complete circuit，不能换成 that；be connected together 中 together 与 connect 语义重叠，但技术文本常保留以强调「接在一起」；wrapped、connected 都是过去分词，与 be 构成被动。

### ③ 时间状语从句 + 被动语态
**原句**：When a conductor is moved through a magnetic field, an emf is induced in the conductor.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 a conductor，谓语 is moved 为一般现在时被动；主句主语 an emf，谓语 is induced 为被动，in the conductor 为地点状语。
**要点**：定义、原理类描述几乎全用一般现在时被动，因为关注的是「发生了什么」而不是「谁做的」；emf 以元音音素开头，故用 an；汉语习惯说「导体中会感应出电动势」，不必译出「被」。

### ④ 现在分词短语作后置定语 + since 原因从句
**原句**：If sides A and B and the loop are rotated half a turn and the sides of the conductor have exchanged positions, the induced emf in each wire reverses its direction, since the wire formerly cutting the lines of force in an upward direction is now moving downward.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，内含两个并列谓语 are rotated 与 have exchanged；主句 the induced emf in each wire reverses its direction；since 引导原因状语从句，其中 formerly cutting the lines of force in an upward direction 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 the wire（相当于 that was formerly cutting…），is now moving 才是从句谓语。
**要点**：分词短语 cutting… 没有 be 动词，绝不能当作谓语，否则会误判句子结构；formerly 与 now 形成时间对照，正是「方向反转」的原因；since 在此表原因，不表时间。

### ⑤ the + 比较级，the + 比较级
**原句**：The period of a sine wave is inversely proportional to the frequency: the higher the frequency, the shorter the period.
**拆解**：冒号前为主句（be inversely proportional to 表反比）；冒号后是 the + 比较级, the + 比较级 结构，前一分句 the higher the frequency 表条件，后一分句 the shorter the period 表结果，两个分句的谓语 is 都被省略。
**要点**：两个 the 都是副词，缺一不可，且必须放在比较级之前；这种句式比 if…then 更简洁，技术文本用来表达正比、反比关系；比较级用 shorter 而非 more short。
