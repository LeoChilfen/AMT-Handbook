# Metric Based Prefixes Used for Electrical Calculations

> 通用 Ch 12 · Metric Based Prefixes Used for Electrical Calculations · 原文 PDF 第 446 页

## 1. 原文

In any system of measurements, a single set of units is usually not sufficient for all the computations involved in electrical repair and maintenance. Small distances, for example, can usually be measured in inches, but larger distances are more meaningfully expressed in feet, yards, or miles. Since electrical values often vary from numbers that are a millionth part of a basic unit of measurement to very large values, it is often necessary to use a wide range of numbers to represent the values of units, such as volts, amperes, or ohms. A series of prefixes that appear with the name of the unit have been devised for the various multiples or submultiples of the basic units. There are 12 of these prefixes, which are also known as conversion factors. Four of the most commonly used prefixes in electrical work are:

Mega (M) means one million (1,000,000). Kilo (k) means one thousand (1,000).

Milli (m) means one-thousandth (1⁄1,000).

Micro (μ) means one-millionth (1⁄1,000,000).

Kilo is one of the most extensively used conversion factors. It explains the use of prefixes with basic units of measurement. Kilo means 1,000, and when used with volts, is expressed as kilovolt, meaning 1,000 volts. The symbol for kilo is the letter “k.” Thus, 1,000 volts is one kilovolt or 1 kV . Conversely, one volt would equal one-thousandth of a kV , or 1 ⁄1,000 kV . This could also be written 0.001 kV .

Similarly, the word “milli” means one-thousandth, and thus, 1 millivolt equals one-thousandth (1⁄1000) of a volt.

## 2. 中文翻译

在任何一种计量体系中，单一一套单位通常不足以完成电气修理与维修中涉及的全部计算。例如，短距离通常可以用英寸来量，但较大的距离用英尺、码或英里表示更有意义。由于电学数值常常从基本计量单位的一百万分之一一直变化到很大的数值，往往需要用范围很宽的数字来表示伏特、安培、欧姆这类单位的量值。人们为基本单位的不同倍数和分数倍数设计了一系列加在单位名称前的词头。这样的词头共有 12 个，又称换算系数。电气工作中最常用的四个词头是：

Mega（M）表示一百万（1,000,000）。Kilo（k）表示一千（1,000）。

Milli（m）表示千分之一（1⁄1,000）。

Micro（μ）表示百万分之一（1⁄1,000,000）。

Kilo 是最常用的换算系数之一。它说明了词头与基本计量单位配合使用的方式。kilo 意为 1,000，与伏特连用时写作 kilovolt（千伏），即 1,000 伏。kilo 的符号是字母「k」。因此，1,000 伏就是 1 千伏，即 1 kV 。反过来，1 伏等于 1 千伏的千分之一，即 1 ⁄1,000 kV 。也可以写成 0.001 kV 。

同样，milli 一词意为千分之一，因此 1 毫伏等于 1 伏的千分之一（1⁄1000）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在完成被动 + 定语从句后置
**原句**：A series of prefixes that appear with the name of the unit have been devised for the various multiples or submultiples of the basic units.
**拆解**：主语 A series of prefixes 后接 that 定语从句（that 作从句主语，appear 为谓语）；主句谓语 have been devised 为现在完成被动，for… 为状语表目的或对象。
**要点**：a series of + 复数名词作主语时，谓语按语义常取复数（have been devised），这是「整体—成员」一致问题里的典型情况；技术定义句常用现在完成被动，强调「已被设计出来并沿用至今」。

### ② since 原因状语从句 + it 形式主语
**原句**：Since electrical values often vary from numbers that are a millionth part of a basic unit of measurement to very large values, it is often necessary to use a wide range of numbers to represent the values of units, such as volts, amperes, or ohms.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句中 vary from…to… 为固定搭配，that are a millionth part… 是修饰 numbers 的定语从句；主句 it 为形式主语，真正主语 is 后的不定式 to use…，necessary 作表语。
**要点**：vary from A to B 表示「从 A 变化到 B」，A、B 都是范围端点；it is necessary to do 是技术说明文的常用句式，比 we must 更客观。such as 引出的三项是对 units 的举例，不是全部列举。

### ③ 状语从句的省略 + 分词短语
**原句**：Kilo means 1,000, and when used with volts, is expressed as kilovolt, meaning 1,000 volts.
**拆解**：and 连接两个谓语：means 1,000 与 is expressed as kilovolt；when used with volts 是时间状语从句 when it is used with volts 的省略（主语与主句一致且含 be，故省略 it is）；meaning 1,000 volts 为现在分词短语作补充说明。
**要点**：状语从句省略必须同时满足「主语与主句一致」和「谓语含 be」两个条件；be expressed as 是「表示为」的固定被动，meaning 引出对前文的解释，相当于 which means。

### ④ would 表推算 + or 引出同义写法
**原句**：Conversely, one volt would equal one-thousandth of a kV , or 1 ⁄1,000 kV .
**拆解**：Conversely 为句首连接副词；one volt 作主语，would equal 为谓语，one-thousandth of a kV 作宾语；逗号后 or 引出同一数值的另一种写法。
**要点**：技术文字里 would 常不表虚拟，而表示「按换算关系应当等于」，语气比 will 委婉、更像推算；or 在此不是选择，而是「或者说、亦即」，用于给出等值表达。

### ⑤ 插入语 + 被动语态对比
**原句**：Small distances, for example, can usually be measured in inches, but larger distances are more meaningfully expressed in feet, yards, or miles.
**拆解**：for example 为插入语，用逗号隔开；but 连接两个并列分句，前句 can be measured 为情态被动，后句 are more meaningfully expressed 为一般现在时被动，more meaningfully 为比较级副词作状语。
**要点**：measure 用 in inches 表示「以英寸计量」，被动时介词 in 保留；be expressed in 表示「用……单位表示」。more meaningfully 修饰整个被动动作，翻译时要还原成「用……表示更有意义」，不能译成「更有意义地被表示」。
