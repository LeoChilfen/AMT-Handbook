# Triangle

## 1. 原文

A triangle is a three-sided figure. The sum of the three angles in a triangle is always equal to 180°. Triangles are often classified by their sides. An equilateral triangle has 3 sides of equal length. An isosceles triangle has 2 sides of equal length. A scalene triangle has three sides of differing lengths.

Triangles can also be classified by their angles. An acute triangle has all three angles less than 90°. A right triangle has one right angle (a 90° angle). An obtuse triangle has one angle greater than 90°. Each of these types of triangles is shown in Figure 3-16.

The formula for the area of a triangle is area = 1⁄2 × (base × height) = 1⁄2 × (b × h)

Example: Find the area of the obtuse triangle shown in Figure 3-17 . First, substitute the known values in the area formula.

a = 1⁄2 × (b × h) = 1⁄2 × (2'6" × 3'2")

Next, convert all dimensions to inches:

2'6" = (2 × 12") + 6" = (24 + 6) = 30 inches 3'2" = (3 × 12") + 2" = (36 + 2) = 38 inches Now, solve the formula for the unknown value:

a = 1⁄2 × (30 inches × 38 inches) = 570 square inches
