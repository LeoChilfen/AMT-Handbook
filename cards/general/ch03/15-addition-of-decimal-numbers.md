# Addition of Decimal Numbers

> 通用 Ch 03 · Addition of Decimal Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 100 页

## 1. 原文

To add decimal numbers, they must first be arranged so that the decimal points are aligned vertically and according to place value. That is, adding tenths with tenths, ones with ones, hundreds with hundreds, and so forth.

Example: Find the total resistance for the circuit diagram shown in Figure 3-5. The total resistance of a series circuit is equal to the sum of the individual resistances. To find the total resistance, RT, the individual resistances are added together.

RT = 2.34 + 37.5 + 0.09 Arrange the resistance values in a vertical column so that the decimal points are aligned and then add.

2.34 37.5 + 0.09 39.93 Therefore, the total resistance, RT = 39.93 ohms.

## 2. 中文翻译

加小数时，必须先排列各数，使小数点竖直对齐，并按位值对齐。也就是说，十分位与十分位相加，个位与个位相加，百位与百位相加，依此类推。

例：求图 3-5 所示电路图的总电阻。串联电路的总电阻等于各分电阻之和。要求总电阻 RT，就把各分电阻相加。

RT = 2.34 + 37.5 + 0.09 把各电阻值排成一竖列，使小数点对齐，然后相加。

2.34 37.5 + 0.09 39.93 因此，总电阻 RT = 39.93 欧姆。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① they must first be arranged so that …（情态被动 + so that 目的从句）

**原句**：To add decimal numbers, they must first be arranged so that the decimal points are aligned vertically and according to place value.

**拆解**：To add decimal numbers 为目的状语；主句 they must first be arranged 是“情态动词 + be + 过去分词”；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句主语 the decimal points，谓语 are aligned，后接两个并列状语 vertically 与 according to place value。

**要点**：so that 在此表目的（“以便”），从句常用 can/may 或直接一般现在时；表结果时后面常跟逗号和既成事实。arrange 常用被动 be arranged，因为强调的是“被排好”这个状态而不管谁来排。according to 是介词短语，后面只能接名词（place value），不能接从句。

### ② adding tenths with tenths, ones with ones（并列省略 + That is 插入语）

**原句**：That is, adding tenths with tenths, ones with ones, hundreds with hundreds, and so forth.

**拆解**：That is 是独立成分（= that is to say，“也就是说”）；其后是动名词短语的并列，后面几组承前省略了重复的 adding 与 with：ones with ones 即 adding ones with ones。

**要点**：英文靠省略避免重复，中文却要补全（“个位与个位相加”），直译成“个位与个位”会读不通。That is 后接逗号，不充当句子成分，可整体删去而不影响句子结构。and so forth（= and so on）用于列举未尽，技术文本里比 etc. 更正式。

### ③ is equal to the sum of …（be equal to + 名词化）

**原句**：The total resistance of a series circuit is equal to the sum of the individual resistances.

**拆解**：主语 The total resistance of a series circuit；系动词 is；表语为形容词短语 equal to the sum of the individual resistances，其中 of 短语作 sum 的定语。

**要点**：equal 作形容词时后接介词 to，不能说 is equal the sum；作动词时则直接跟宾语（A equals B）。the sum of 把“各分电阻之和”名词化，是数学与工程文本压缩句子的常用手段。individual 在此不是“个人的”，而是“各个、每个”的意思，individual resistances 即各分电阻。

### ④ RT = 2.34 + 37.5 + 0.09 的读法（算式与说明句的衔接）

**原句**：To find the total resistance, RT, the individual resistances are added together.

**拆解**：目的状语 To find the total resistance；RT 是 the total resistance 的同位语，用逗号隔开；主句 the individual resistances are added together 为被动，together 是方式状语。

**要点**：符号名（RT）作同位语时前后用逗号包住，朗读时略作停顿；中文习惯写成“总电阻 RT”。add together 是固定搭配，意为“相加”，together 不能省。全句主语是各分电阻而非 RT，被动语态下“谁加”被隐去，这正是手册描述计算步骤的典型笔法。
