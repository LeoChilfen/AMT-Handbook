## 1. 原文

Area is a measurement of the amount of surface of an object.

Area is usually expressed in such units as square inches or square centimeters for small surfaces or in square feet or square meters for larger surfaces.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 层层 of 短语作后置定语：a measurement of the amount of surface of an object

**原句**：Area is a measurement of the amount of surface of an object.

**拆解**：主语 Area；系动词 is；表语 a measurement；of the amount of surface of an object 为三层 of 短语逐层限定（measurement → amount → surface → object）。

**要点**：英文定义句常用「X is a measurement of ...」，of 链从大到小层层限定；中文要倒过来读：「物体表面多少的度量」。a measurement of 表示「对……的度量」，不要译成「一个测量」。

### ② 一般现在时被动 + such ... as ... 举例

**原句**：Area is usually expressed in such units as square inches or square centimeters for small surfaces or in square feet or square meters for larger surfaces.

**拆解**：主语 Area；谓语 is usually expressed（被动，usually 位于 be 之后）；in such units as ... 为方式状语，such units as 引出例举；for small surfaces 与 for larger surfaces 分别限定两组单位；两个 in 短语并列。

**要点**：express A in units 意为「用……单位表示 A」，被动式 A is expressed in ...；表面积的单位前用 in，不用 with。such ... as ... 后直接跟名词，不加 like。

### ③ 图表作主语的主谓一致 + for + 动名词

**原句**：Figure 3-13 summarizes the formulas for computing the area

**拆解**：主语 Figure 3-13（单数）；谓语 summarizes 用第三人称单数；the formulas 为宾语；for computing the area 为「介词 + 动名词」短语作后置定语，说明公式的用途。

**要点**：Figure / Table / Chart 作主语时谓语一律用单数。for + 动名词表用途，等于 used for computing；写成 for compute 是常见错误。
