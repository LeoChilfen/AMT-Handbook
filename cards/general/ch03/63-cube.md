# Cube

> 通用 Ch 03 · Cube · 原文 PDF 第 117 页

## 1. 原文

A cube is a solid with six square sides. [Figure 3-25] A cube is just a special type of rectangular solid. It has the same formula for volume as does the rectangular solid, which is volume = length × width × height = L × W × H. Because all of the sides of a cube are equal, the volume formula for a cube can also be written as:

volume = side × side × side = S3 Example: A large, cube-shaped carton contains a shipment of smaller boxes inside of it. Each of the smaller boxes is 1 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft. The measurement of the large carton is 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft. How many of the smaller boxes are in the large carton? First, substitute the known values into the formula.

v = l × w × h = 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 cubic feet of volume in the large carton Since each of the smaller boxes has a volume of 1 cubic foot, the large carton holds 27 boxes.

## 2. 中文翻译

正方体是具有六个正方形面的立体。[图 3-25] 正方体只是矩形立体的一种特殊类型。它的体积公式与矩形立体相同，即 volume = length × width × height = L × W × H。由于正方体各边相等，正方体的体积公式也可写作：

volume = side × side × side = S3（原文 S3 为 OCR 压平的上标，即 S³）。例：一个大正方体纸箱内装有一批小盒子。每个小盒子为 1 ft × 1 ft × 1 ft。大纸箱的尺寸为 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft。大纸箱内有多少个小盒子？首先，把已知值代入公式。

v = l × w × h = 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft = 27 cubic feet of volume in the large carton。由于每个小盒子的体积为 1 立方英尺，大纸箱可装 27 个盒子。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① A cube is just a special type of rectangular solid（just 限定 + a special type of）

**原句**：A cube is just a special type of rectangular solid.

**拆解**：主语 A cube，系动词 is，表语 a special type of rectangular solid；副词 just 修饰整个表语，表示「只不过是」。

**要点**：a special type of + 名词 表示「一种特殊的……」，技术文本常用它建立术语之间的从属关系。just 在定义句中不表示时间「刚刚」，而是「仅仅、只不过」，强调正方体没有引入新的体积公式。

### ② the same … as does the rectangular solid（the same … as + 倒装）

**原句**：It has the same formula for volume as does the rectangular solid, which is volume = length × width × height = L × W × H.

**拆解**：主句 It has the same formula for volume；as does the rectangular solid 是 as 引导的比较结构，因主语较长而倒装（正常语序为 as the rectangular solid does）；which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代 formula。

**要点**：the same + 名词 + as … 表示「与……相同」。as 分句常用倒装（as does A），也可说 as A does，意思不变。which 从句用逗号隔开，补充说明公式的具体内容；若写成限制性从句，则意味着「只有那个公式」，不合原意。

### ③ Because … are equal, … can also be written as …（原因状语从句 + 情态被动）

**原句**：Because all of the sides of a cube are equal, the volume formula for a cube can also be written as:

**拆解**：Because 引导原因状语从句，从句为主系表结构 all of the sides ... are equal；主句 the volume formula ... can also be written as 为「情态动词 + be + 过去分词」的被动结构。

**要点**：技术推导常先给原因、后给结论，中文也照此顺序即可。can be written as 是引出等价公式的固定说法，译作「也可写作」；注意 write 的过去分词是 written，不是 wrote。同类表达还有 can be expressed as / can be stated as。
