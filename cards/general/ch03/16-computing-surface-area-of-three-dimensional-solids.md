## 1. 原文

The surface area of a three-dimensional solid is the sum of the areas of the faces of the solid. Surface area is a different concept from that of volume. For example, surface area is the amount of sheet metal needed to build a rectangular fuel tank while volume is the amount of fuel that the tank can contain.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 多重 of 结构作定语
**原句**：The surface area of a three-dimensional solid is the sum of the areas of the faces of the solid.
**拆解**：主语 The surface area of a three-dimensional solid 中 of 短语层层限定；系动词 is；表语 the sum of the areas of the faces of the solid 由四个 of 短语逐级收窄：sum → areas → faces → solid。
**要点**：英语靠 of 链表达“整体的部分的部分”，中文要反过来由大到小译（立体各面的面积之和）；of 链超过三层时，翻译可断开重排，避免“的”字连用。

### ② be different from + that 替代
**原句**：Surface area is a different concept from that of volume.
**拆解**：主语 Surface area，系动词 is，表语 a different concept；from that of volume 中 that 是代词，替代前面的 concept，of volume 说明是“体积的那个概念”。
**要点**：比较结构中用 that（单数）/ those（复数）替代前面出现过的名词，避免重复；different from 是固定搭配，不能用 different than（美式口语除外）。

### ③ while 表对比 + 过去分词与定语从句作定语
**原句**：For example, surface area is the amount of sheet metal needed to build a rectangular fuel tank while volume is the amount of fuel that the tank can contain.
**拆解**：while 连接两个并列分句，表示对比（“而”）；前一分句 the amount of sheet metal needed to build … 中 needed 为过去分词短语后置修饰 sheet metal；后一分句 the amount of fuel that the tank can contain 中 that 引导定语从句修饰 fuel。
**要点**：while 在此不是“当……时”而是对比连词，译作“而”；need 与 sheet metal 是被动关系，故用过去分词 needed，不能改成 needing。

### ④ be given as 引出公式
**原句**：The formula for the surface area of a rectangular solid is given as:
**拆解**：主语 The formula for the surface area of a rectangular solid；谓语 is given（被动）；as 后以冒号引出公式本体。
**要点**：手册中“公式给出如下”惯用 be given as，as 不可换成 like；冒号后接公式时不必补完整句子，这是技术文本常见的省略写法。

### ⑤ with 短语作后置定语 + 特殊疑问句
**原句**：Example: What is the surface area of a cube with a side measure of 8 inches?
**拆解**：What 作表语置于句首，is 提前构成特殊疑问句；主语 the surface area of a cube；with a side measure of 8 inches 为 with 短语后置修饰 a cube。
**要点**：a side measure of 8 inches 中 measure 是名词，表示“量得的尺寸”；疑问句中 of 短语仍须紧跟被修饰名词，不能拆到句末。

### ⑥ 动名词作介词宾语 + 被动总结句
**原句**：The formulas for computing the volume and surface area of three-dimensional solids are summarized below.
**拆解**：主语 The formulas；for computing the volume and surface area of three-dimensional solids 为介词 for + 动名词短语作定语，说明公式的用途；谓语 are summarized（被动）；below 为地点副词作状语。
**要点**：介词 for 后接动名词表示用途（for computing = 用于计算），不能接不定式；below 与 above 这类方位副词作状语时不加介词。
