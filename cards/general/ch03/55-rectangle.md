# Rectangle

> 通用 Ch 03 · Rectangle · 原文 PDF 第 114 页

## 1. 原文

A rectangle is a four-sided figure with opposite sides of equal length and parallel to each other. [Figure 3-14] All of the angles are right angles. A right angle is a 90° angle. The rectangle is a very familiar shape in mechanics. The formula for the area of a rectangle is:

area = length × width = l × w Example: An aircraft floor panel is in the form of a rectangle having a length of 24 inches and a width of 12 inches. What is the area of the panel expressed in square inches? First, determine the known values and substitute them in the formula.

a = l × w = 24 inches × 12 inches = 288 square inches

## 2. 中文翻译

长方形（矩形）是有四条边、对边等长且彼此平行的图形。[图 3-14] 它的所有角都是直角。直角即 90° 角。长方形在机械领域是十分常见的形状。长方形的面积公式为：

面积 = 长 × 宽 = l × w 例：某航空器地板板块呈长方形，长 24 英寸、宽 12 英寸。该板块的面积以平方英寸表示为多少？首先，确定已知数值并把它们代入公式。

a = l × w = 24 英寸 × 12 英寸 = 288 平方英寸

## 4. 语法解析

### ① with 复合结构作后置定语

**原句**：A rectangle is a four-sided figure with opposite sides of equal length and parallel to each other.

**拆解**：主语 A rectangle；系动词 is；表语 a four-sided figure；with opposite sides of equal length and parallel to each other 为 with 引导的复合结构，作后置定语，其中 opposite sides 是 with 的宾语，of equal length 与 parallel to each other 是并列的补充说明。

**要点**：with + 名词 + 形容词短语是技术文本定义图形的常用框架，译作「具有……的」。parallel to 中的 to 是固定搭配，不能换成 with 或 than。

### ② 现在分词 having 作后置定语，表示尺寸

**原句**：Example: An aircraft floor panel is in the form of a rectangle having a length of 24 inches and a width of 12 inches.

**拆解**：主语 An aircraft floor panel；谓语 is；表语 in the form of a rectangle（in the form of 表示「呈……形状」）；having a length of ... and a width of ... 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 rectangle。

**要点**：have a length of / have a width of 是尺寸表达的固定搭配，可换成 measuring 或 with a length of。in the form of 后面只接名词，不接句子。

### ③ 疑问句 + 过去分词短语作后置定语

**原句**：What is the area of the panel expressed in square inches?

**拆解**：疑问句，What 作表语；主语 the area of the panel；expressed in square inches 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 the area（相当于 which is expressed in square inches）。

**要点**：过去分词短语后置相当于省略了 which is 的定语从句，手册中大量使用。expressed in units 表示「以……单位表示」，中文说「以平方英寸表示」，不要直译成「被表达在平方英寸里」。
