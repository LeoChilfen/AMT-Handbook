# Reducing Fractions

> 通用 Ch 03 · Reducing Fractions · 原文 PDF 第 99 页

## 1. 原文

A fraction needs to be reduced when it is not in the simplest form or “lowest terms.” Lowest term means that the numerator and denominator do not have any factors in common. That is, they cannot be divided by the same number (or factor).

To reduce a fraction, determine what the common factor(s)

are and divide these out of the numerator and denominator.

For example, when both the numerator and denominator are even numbers, they can both be divided by 2.

Example: The total travel of a jackscrew is 13⁄16 inch. If the travel in one direction from the neutral position is 7⁄16 inch, what is the travel in the opposite direction?

13 – 7 = 13 – 7 = 6 16 16 16 16 The fraction 6⁄16 is not in lowest terms because the numerator (6) and the denominator (16) have a common factor of 2. To reduce 6⁄16, divide the numerator and the denominator by 2.

The final reduced fraction is 3⁄8 as shown below.

6 = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 16 16 ÷ 2 8 Therefore, the travel in the opposite direction is 3⁄8 inch.

## 2. 中文翻译

分数若不在最简形式（即“最低项”）下，就需要约分。最低项是指分子与分母不含任何公因数，也就是说，二者不能被同一个数（或同一个因数）整除。

约分的方法是：先确定分子与分母有哪些公因数，再把这些公因数从分子和分母中约去。

例如，当分子和分母都是偶数时，两者都可以除以 2。

例：一台千斤螺杆的总行程为 13⁄16 英寸。若从中立位置向一个方向的行程为 7⁄16 英寸，则相反方向的行程是多少？

13 – 7 = 13 – 7 = 6 16 16 16 16
分数 6⁄16 不在最低项，因为分子（6）与分母（16）有公因数 2。要把 6⁄16 约分，就把分子和分母都除以 2。

最终约分所得的分数为 3⁄8，如下所示。

6 = 6 ÷ 2 = 3 16 16 ÷ 2 8
因此，相反方向的行程为 3⁄8 英寸。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① need to be reduced（被动不定式 + when 从句）

**原句**：A fraction needs to be reduced when it is not in the simplest form or “lowest terms.”

**拆解**：主语 A fraction；谓语 needs to be reduced，即 need + 不定式被动式；when it is not in the simplest form or “lowest terms” 是时间／条件状语从句，it 回指 a fraction，or 连接两个同义说法。

**要点**：need to be + 过去分词 表示“需要被……处理”，比 must be 语气弱，强调的是客观必要而非强制规定；不能写成 needs to reduced。when 从句用一般现在时表示通常情形。句中的 or 不是“或者”，而是“即、也就是”，前后是同一概念的解释关系。

### ② To reduce a fraction, determine what the common factor(s) are（目的不定式 + 祈使句 + what 从句）

**原句**：To reduce a fraction, determine what the common factor(s) are and divide these out of the numerator and denominator.

**拆解**：句首 To reduce a fraction 是目的状语（= in order to reduce a fraction）；主句为祈使句，两个并列谓语 determine 和 divide；what the common factor(s) are 是 what 引导的宾语从句，作 determine 的宾语，从句用陈述语序。

**要点**：手册讲操作步骤时大量使用“目的状语 + 祈使句”，主语默认是读者。宾语从句必须是陈述语序（what the factors are），写成 what are the factors 是常见错误。divide ... out of ... 意为“把……从……中约去”，out of 不能省，否则句意会变成“除以”。

### ③ they can both be divided by 2（情态动词被动 + both 的位置）

**原句**：For example, when both the numerator and denominator are even numbers, they can both be divided by 2.

**拆解**：when 引导条件状语从句，both ... and ... 连接两个并列主语，从句谓语用复数 are；主句 they can both be divided by 2 是“情态动词 + 副词 + be + 过去分词”结构。

**要点**：both 在被动结构里放在第一个助动词之后（can both be divided），不能丢到句末写成 can be divided both。divide A by B 意为“用 B 除 A”，介词固定用 by，不能说 divided with 2；同理本节的 divided by the same number 也是这个搭配。
