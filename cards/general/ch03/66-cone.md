## 4. 语法解析

### ① A solid with … and with … is called a cone（并列 with 短语 + that 定语从句）

**原句**：A solid with a circle as a base and with sides that gradually taper to a point is called a cone.

**拆解**：主语 A solid，后接两个并列的 with 短语作后置定语：with a circle as a base 与 with sides ...；that gradually taper to a point 是定语从句，修饰 sides；谓语 is called 为被动，a cone 作主语补足语。

**要点**：with a circle as a base 中 as 表示「作为」，译作「以圆为底」。that 从句的先行词是 sides，谓语 taper 用复数，不要误以为主语是 a solid；读长定义句时先找主句谓语 is called，再回头处理定语。

### ② Use the acronym PEMDAS to remember …（祈使句 + 不定式表目的）

**原句**：Use the acronym PEMDAS to remember the order of operation in algebra.

**拆解**：Use 为祈使句谓语（省略主语 you），宾语 the acronym PEMDAS；to remember … 是不定式短语作目的状语。

**要点**：手册指导操作时常用祈使句，直接、简短，译作「用……来记住……」。acronym 指「首字母缩略词」，PEMDAS 由 Parentheses、Exponents、Multiplication、Division、Addition、Subtraction 的首字母组成；order of operation 是固定说法，作「运算顺序」讲。

### ③ is in the shape of a sphere with a diameter of 34 inches（in the shape of + with 定语）

**原句**：A pressure tank inside the fuselage of a cargo aircraft is in the shape of a sphere with a diameter of 34 inches.

**拆解**：主语 A pressure tank，inside the fuselage of a cargo aircraft 为后置定语；系动词 is，表语 in the shape of a sphere；with a diameter of 34 inches 是 with 短语，修饰 sphere。

**要点**：in the shape of = 呈……形状，比 is shaped like 更书面。with a diameter of + 数值 表示「直径为……」，同类还有 with a radius of / with a length of。主语后的 inside … 短语较长，翻译时可拆成「货机机身内的压力罐」。
