# Whole Numbers

## 1. 原文

Whole numbers are the numbers 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and so on. Whole numbers can be thought of as counting numbers.

### Addition of Whole Numbers

Addition is the process where the value of one number is added to the value of another. The result is called the sum. When working with whole numbers, it is important to understand the principle of the place value. The place value in a whole number is the value of the position of each individual digit within the entire number. For example, in the number 512, the 5 is in the hundreds column, the 1 is in the tens column, and the 2 is in the ones column.

When adding several whole numbers, such as 4,314, 122, 93,132, and 10, align them into columns according to place value and then add. 4,314 122 93,132 + 10 97,578 = 97,578. Therefore, 97,578 is the sum of the four whole numbers.

### Subtraction of Whole Numbers

Subtraction is the process where the value of one number is taken from the value of another. The result is called the difference. When subtracting two whole numbers, such as 3,461 from 97,564, align them into columns according to place value and then subtract.

97,564 – 3,461 94,103 = 94,103. The difference of the two whole numbers is 94,103.

### Multiplication of Whole Numbers

Multiplication is the process of repeated addition. For example, 4 × 3 is the same as 4 + 4 + 4. The result is called the product.

Example: How many hydraulic system filters do you have if there are 35 cartons in the supply room and each carton contains 18 filters? 18 × 35 = 630. Therefore, there are 630 filters in the supply room.

### Division of Whole Numbers

Division is the process of finding how many times one number (called the divisor) is contained in another number (called the dividend). The result is the quotient, and any amount left over is called the remainder. 

Example: 218 landing gear bolts need to be divided between 7 aircraft. How many bolts will each aircraft receive?

218 / 7 = 31……1. In this case, there are 31 bolts for each of the seven aircraft with one extra remaining.

## 2. 中文翻译

整数就是 0、1、2、3、4、5 等这样的数。整数可以看作是计数用的数。

### 整数的加法

加法是把一个数的值加到另一个数的值上的过程，结果称为和。处理整数时，理解位值原理很重要。整数的位值是指整个数中每一位数字所处位置所代表的值。例如在 512 这个数中，5 在百位，1 在十位，2 在个位。

把几个整数相加时，例如 4,314、122、93,132 和 10，应按位值把它们对齐成列，然后相加。4,314 122 93,132 + 10 97,578 因此，97,578 就是这四个整数之和。

### 整数的减法

减法是从一个数的值中减去另一个数的值的过程，结果称为差。两个整数相减时，例如从 97,564 中减去 3,461，应按位值把它们对齐成列，然后相减。

97,564 – 3,461 94,103 这两个整数的差是 94,103。

### 整数的乘法

乘法是重复相加的过程。例如，4 × 3 与 4 + 4 + 4 相同，结果称为积。

例：如果库房里有 35 箱滤芯，每箱装 18 个液压系统滤芯，那么共有多少个滤芯？ 18 × 35 90 54 630 因此，库房里有 630 个滤芯。

### 整数的除法

除法是求一个数（称为除数）中包含另一个数（称为被除数）多少倍的过程。结果称为商，余下的部分称为余数。quotient divisor dividend

例：218 个起落架螺栓要分给 7 架飞机，每架飞机分得多少个螺栓？

（原文此处为位值表与竖式除法的抽排数据：位值表标题 Place Value、Ten Thousands、Thousands、Hundreds、Tens、Ones，随后是 35、269、12,749 三个数的位值示例，以及 218 ÷ 7 的竖式。）本例中，七架飞机每架分得 31 个螺栓，还余 1 个。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① where 引导的定语从句
**原句**：Addition is the process where the value of one number is added to the value of another.
**拆解**：主句 Addition is the process；where 引导定语从句修饰 process，从句为被动结构 the value of one number is added to the value of another（of one number、of another 均作后置定语）。
**要点**：where 在此不表地点而表「在这一过程中」，相当于 in which。手册定义术语时常用 the process where / the process in which 的句式，不可误换成 which（which 在此缺介词会成病句）。

### ② it 作形式主语 + 不定式
**原句**：When working with whole numbers, it is important to understand the principle of the place value.
**拆解**：When working with whole numbers 为时间状语，working 的逻辑主语是隐含的 you；it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to understand the principle of the place value；is important 为系表结构。
**要点**：It is important to do sth 中 it 不能换成 that，也不能省；时间状语从句省略了主语和 be（when you are working），这是手册类说明文的常见省略，翻译时补回「处理……时」。

### ③ 祈使句 + 分词状语 + 并列谓语
**原句**：When adding several whole numbers, such as 4,314, 122, 93,132, and 10, align them into columns according to place value and then add.
**拆解**：When adding … 为时间状语（省略 you are）；主句是祈使句，谓语为并列的 align … and then add；such as … 为插入的举例；according to place value 为方式状语；them 指前面列举的几个整数。
**要点**：such as 后列举四项以上时，最后一项前保留 and，前面用逗号分隔；祈使句无主语，译文按汉语习惯补出「应把它们……」，不要译成「你应当」。

### ④ 并列句 + 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：The result is the quotient, and any amount left over is called the remainder.
**拆解**：由 and 连接两个并列分句；第二分句主语 any amount + 后置定语 left over（过去分词短语，相当于 that is left over）+ 被动谓语 is called + 主语补足语 the remainder。
**要点**：left over 是「剩下的」，只能后置，不能写成 leftover amount 之外的顺序；be called + 名词是给术语下定义的固定搭配，注意 called 后不加 as（区别于 be referred to as）。

### ⑤ with 复合结构（独立主格）
**原句**：In this case, there are 31 bolts for each of the seven aircraft with one extra remaining.
**拆解**：there be 存在句，主语 31 bolts；for each of the seven aircraft 为范围状语；with one extra remaining 为 with + 名词 + 现在分词的复合结构，表伴随情况。
**要点**：with 复合结构中，宾语与后面的分词是主动关系用 -ing（one extra remaining），被动关系用过去分词。该结构信息量大、常放在句末补充结果，翻译时宜拆成「还余 1 个」这样的短句，不要硬套「伴随着」。
