# Scientific Notation

## 1. 原文

Scientific notation is used as a type of shorthand to express very large or very small numbers. It is a way to write numbers so that they do not take up as much space on the page. The format of a number written in scientific notation has two parts. The first part is a number greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10 (for example, 2.35). The second part is a power of 10 (for example, 10⁶). The number 2,350,000 is expressed in scientific notation as 2.35 × 10⁶. It is important that the decimal point is always placed to the right of the first digit. Notice that very large numbers always have a positive power of 10 and very small numbers always have a negative power of 10.

Example: The velocity of the speed of light is over 186,000 miles per second (mps). This can be expressed as 1.86 × 10⁵ mps in scientific notation. The mass of an electron is approximately 0.000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,911 grams. This can be expressed in scientific notation as 9.11 × 10⁻²⁸ grams.

### Converting Numbers from Standard Notation to Scientific Notation

Example: Convert 1,244,000,000,000 to scientific notation as follows. First, note that the decimal point is to the right of the last zero. (Even though it is not usually written, it is assumed to be there.)

1,244,000,000,000 = 1,244,000,000,000.0

To change to the format of scientific notation, the decimal point must be moved to the position between the first and second digits. In this case, it is between the 1 and the 2. Since the decimal point must be moved 12 places to the left to get there, the power of 10 is 12. Remember that large numbers always have a positive exponent. Therefore, 1,244,000,000,000 = 1.244 × 10¹² when written in scientific notation.

Example: Convert 0.000000457 from standard notation to scientific notation. To change to the format of scientific notation, the decimal point must be moved to the position between the first and second numbers, which in this case is between the 4 and the 5. Since the decimal point must be moved 7 places to the right to get there, the power of 10 is −7. Remember that small numbers (those less than one) have a negative exponent. Therefore, 0.000000457 = 4.57 × 10⁻⁷ when written in scientific notation.

### Converting Numbers from Scientific Notation to Standard Notation

Example: Convert 3.68 × 10⁷ from scientific notation to standard notation, as follows. To convert from scientific notation to standard notation, move the decimal place 7 places to the right. 3.68 × 10⁷ = 36,800,000. Another way to think about the conversion is 3.68 × 10⁷ = 3.68 × 10,000,000 = 36,800,000.

Example: Convert 7.1543 × 10⁻¹⁰ from scientific notation to standard notation. Move the decimal place 10 places to the left: 7.1543 × 10⁻¹⁰ =.00000000071543. Another way to think about the conversion is 7.1543 × 10⁻¹⁰ = 7.1543 × 0.0000000001 = 0.00000000071543

When converting, remember that large numbers always have positive powers of ten and small numbers always have negative powers of ten.

### Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, and Division of Scientific Numbers

To add, subtract, multiply, or divide numbers in scientific notation, change the scientific notation number back to standard notation. Then add, subtract, multiply or divide the standard notation numbers. After the computation, change the final standard notation number back to scientific notation.

### 从科学记数法换算为标准记数法

例：按以下步骤把 3.68 × 10 7 从科学记数法换算成标准记数法。要从科学记数法换成标准记数法，把小数位向右移 7 位：3.68 × 107 = 36,800,000。另一种理解换算是：3.68 × 107 = 3.68 × 10,000,000 = 36,800,000。

例：把 7.1543 × 10 -10 从科学记数法换算成标准记数法。把小数位向左移 10 位：7.1543 × 10 -10 = .00000000071543。另一种理解换算是：7.1543 × 10 -10 = 7.1543 × 0.0000000001 = 0.00000000071543。

换算时要记住，大数总是带正的 10 的幂，小数总是带负的 10 的幂。

### 科学记数法数的加、减、乘、除

要对科学记数法的数做加、减、乘、除，先把科学记数法的数改回标准记数法。然后对标准记数法的数做加、减、乘、除。算完后，再把最后得到的结果改回科学记数法。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be used as + 不定式作目的状语
**原句**：Scientific notation is used as a type of shorthand to express very large or very small numbers.
**拆解**：主语 Scientific notation；谓语 is used（被动）；as a type of shorthand 为介词短语作方式/身份补充；to express very large or very small numbers 为不定式作目的状语。
**要点**：be used as 表示“被当作……使用”，as 后接名词；说明用途也可用 be used to do，但 as 与 to do 不能混成一个结构写 be used as to do。

### ② so that 引导目的状语从句
**原句**：It is a way to write numbers so that they do not take up as much space on the page.
**拆解**：主句 It is a way to write numbers；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句主语 they 指 numbers，谓语 take up，宾语 as much space，on the page 为范围状语。
**要点**：so that 从句常带情态动词 can/will，但这里直接陈述目的，用一般现在时也可以；take up space 是“占地方”的固定搭配，as much … 与前面的 not 构成同级比较的否定。

### ③ 比较级短语后置作定语
**原句**：The first part is a number greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10 (for example, 2.35).
**拆解**：主语 The first part，系动词 is，表语 a number；greater than or equal to 1 与 less than 10 都是形容词比较级短语，后置修饰 number，由 and 连接。
**要点**：greater than or equal to 是数学里的“大于或等于”，三个成分必须齐全，不能写成 greater than equal to；两个以上后置定语时按数值范围由低到高或由高到低排列均可，但要保持 and 的并列关系。

### ④ It 作形式主语 + that 从句
**原句**：It is important that the decimal point is always placed to the right of the first digit.
**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句里 the decimal point 作主语，is placed 为被动谓语，to the right of the first digit 为方位状语。
**要点**：important、necessary 等形容词作表语时，后面接 that 从句要用形式主语 it，不能写成 That … is important 的倒装；place 在此作动词，意为“放置”，与名词 place（位置）区分。

### ⑤ even though 让步从句 + assume 的被动复合结构
**原句**：(Even though it is not usually written, it is assumed to be there.)
**拆解**：Even though 引导让步状语从句，it 指小数点，is not usually written 为被动谓语；主句 it is assumed to be there 是 assume + 宾语 + to be 的被动形式，to be there 作主语补足语。
**要点**：even though 比 although 语气更强，其后直接跟完整句子；assume A to be B 变被动后是 A is assumed to be B，“假设某物处于某状态”，这是技术文本的常用句式。

### ⑥ since 原因状语从句 + 数量词作状语
**原句**：Since the decimal point must be moved 12 places to the left to get there, the power of 10 is 12.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 the decimal point，谓语 must be moved（含情态动词的被动），12 places 为数量状语，to the left 表方向，to get there 为目的不定式；主句 the power of 10 is 12。
**要点**：since 在此是“既然、因为”，不是“自从”，判断依据是从句与主句是因果关系；move 后可接“距离/位数 + 方向”作状语，如 move 3 places to the right。
