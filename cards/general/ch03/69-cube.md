## 1. 原文

The formula for the surface area of a cube [Figure 3-25] is given as:

Surface area = 6 × (side × side) = 6 × s2 Example: What is the surface area of a cube with a side measure of 8 inches?

Surface area = 6 × (side × side)

= 6 × S2 = 6 × 82 = 6 × 64 = 384 square inches
