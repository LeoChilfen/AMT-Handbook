## 1. 原文

### Conventional (U.S. or English) System

Our conventional (U.S. or English) system of measurement is part of our cultural heritage from the days when the thirteen colonies were under British rule. It started as a collection of Anglo-Saxon, Roman, and Norman-French weights and measures. For example, the inch represents the width of the thumb and the foot is from the length of the human foot. Tradition holds that King Henry I decreed that the yard should be the distance from the tip of his nose to the end of his thumb. Since medieval times, commissions appointed by various English monarchs have reduced the chaos of measurement by setting specific standards for some of the most important units. Some of the conventional units of measure are: inches, feet, yards, miles, ounces, pints, gallons, and pounds. Because the conventional system was not set up systematically, it contains a random collection of conversions. For example, 1 mile = 5,280 feet and 1 foot = 12 inches.

The metric system, also known as the International System of Units (SI), is the dominant language of measurement used today. Its standardization and decimal features make it well-suited for engineering and aviation work.

The metric system was first envisioned by Gabriel Mouton, Vicar of St. Paul’s Church in Lyons, France. The meter is the unit of length in the metric system, and it is equal to one ten-millionth of the distance from the equator to the North Pole. The liter is the unit of volume and is equal to one cubic decimeter. The gram is the unit of mass and is equal to one cubic centimeter of water.

All of the metric units follow a consistent naming scheme, which consists of attaching a prefix to the unit. For example, since kilo stands for 1,000, one kilometer equals 1,000 meters. Centi is the prefix for one hundredth, so one meter equals one hundred centimeters. Milli is the prefix for one thousandths and one gram equals one thousand milligrams.

### Measurement Systems & Conversions

The United States primarily uses the conventional (U.S. or English) system, although it is slowly integrating the metric system (SI). A recommendation to transition to the metric system within ten years was initiated in the 1970s. However, this movement lost momentum, and the United States continues to use both measurement systems. Therefore, information to convert between the conventional (U.S. or English) system and the metric (SI) system has been included in Applied Mathematics Formula Sheet. Examples of its use are as follows:

To convert inches to millimeters, multiply the number of inches by 25.4. Example: 20 inches = 20 × 25.4 = 508 mm To convert ounces to grams, multiply the number of ounces by 28.35.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 英制（美国或英国）计量制

我们所用的英制（美国或英国）计量制是文化传承的一部分，源自十三个殖民地在英国统治下的那段时期。它最初是盎格鲁-撒克逊、罗马和诺曼-法国度量衡的汇总。例如，英寸代表拇指的宽度，英尺则取自人脚的长度。传统上认为，亨利一世国王曾下令，一码应等于从他的鼻尖到他拇指末端的距离。自中世纪以来，多位英国君主委任的委员会为一些最重要的单位设定了具体标准，从而减少了计量上的混乱。英制的一些常用单位有：英寸、英尺、码、英里、盎司、品脱、加仑和磅。由于英制并非系统地建立起来，其中的换算关系是零散拼凑的。例如，1 英里 = 5,280 英尺，1 英尺 = 12 英寸。

公制又称国际单位制（SI），是当今计量的主流语言。它的标准化与十进制特点使其非常适合工程和航空工作。

公制最早由法国里昂圣保罗教堂的牧师加布里埃尔·穆顿（Gabriel Mouton）提出设想。米是公制中的长度单位，等于赤道到北极距离的一千万分之一。升是体积单位，等于一立方分米。克是质量单位，等于一立方厘米水的质量。

所有公制单位都遵循一致的命名方式，即在单位前加一个词头。例如，由于 kilo 表示 1,000，所以一千米等于 1,000 米。centi 是百分之一的词头，因此一米等于一百厘米。milli 是千分之一的词头，一克等于一千毫克。

美国主要使用英制（美国或英国）计量制，尽管它正在缓慢地融入公制（SI）。20 世纪 70 年代曾发起一项建议，要在十年内改用公制。但这一运动后来失去了势头，美国继续并用两种计量制。因此，英制（美国或英国）与公制（SI）之间的换算资料已收入《应用数学公式表》（Applied Mathematics Formula Sheet）。其用法举例如下：

## 4. 语法解析

### ① when 引导的定语从句 + be part of
**原句**：Our conventional (U.S. or English) system of measurement is part of our cultural heritage from the days when the thirteen colonies were under British rule.
**拆解**：主干 Our conventional … system of measurement is part of our cultural heritage；from the days 为介词短语修饰 heritage；when the thirteen colonies were under British rule 是定语从句，修饰 days（不是状语从句）。
**要点**：修饰表示时间的名词（days、time、age）时，when 引导的是定语从句，可译成“……的那段时期”；be part of 表“是……的一部分”，part 前不加冠词。

### ② hold that + 宾语从句 + should 表规定
**原句**：Tradition holds that King Henry I decreed that the yard should be the distance from the tip of his nose to the end of his thumb.
**拆解**：主语 Tradition，谓语 holds，that 引导宾语从句；从句中 King Henry I 是主语、decreed 是谓语，再带一个 that 引导的宾语从句；该从句用 should + 动词原形，表示“下令应当……”。
**要点**：hold 作“认为、坚持说”时后接 that 从句，不用 hold sb. that；decreed 后的 should 属于规定性用法，可译为“应、须”。

### ③ 现在完成时 + by 引导方式状语
**原句**：Since medieval times, commissions appointed by various English monarchs have reduced the chaos of measurement by setting specific standards for some of the most important units.
**拆解**：Since medieval times 为时间状语，决定谓语用现在完成时 have reduced；主语 commissions 被过去分词短语 appointed by various English monarchs 后置修饰；by setting specific standards … 是方式状语（by + 动名词）。
**要点**：since + 时间点必须配现在完成时；by doing 表示“通过做某事”，与 with（用工具）、in（用方式/语言）区分。

### ④ 插入语式过去分词短语
**原句**：The metric system, also known as the International System of Units (SI), is the dominant language of measurement used today.
**拆解**：主干 The metric system is the dominant language of measurement；also known as … 是过去分词短语作插入语，补充说明主语；used today 是过去分词短语后置修饰 measurement。
**要点**：be known as 的过去分词形式去掉 be 后可作为同位插入语，前后各加逗号；used today 中的 used 与 measurement 是被动关系。

### ⑤ make + 宾语 + 形容词补足语
**原句**：Its standardization and decimal features make it well-suited for engineering and aviation work.
**拆解**：主语 Its standardization and decimal features；谓语 make 属复合宾语结构，it 为宾语，well-suited 为形容词补足语，for engineering and aviation work 说明适应的领域。
**要点**：make + 宾语 + 形容词表示“使……变得……”，补足语位置不能用副词；be suited for 是固定搭配，意为“适合”。

### ⑥ although 让步从句 + 现在进行时
**原句**：The United States primarily uses the conventional (U.S. or English) system, although it is slowly integrating the metric system (SI).
**拆解**：主句 The United States primarily uses the conventional … system；although 引导让步状语从句，it 指代 the United States，谓语用现在进行时 is slowly integrating。
**要点**：although 与 but 不能同现；主句用一般现在时陈述惯例，从句用进行时强调“正在逐步”这一过程，体现两种制度并存的现状。
