## 1. 原文

The United States primarily uses the conventional (U.S.

or English) system, although it is slowly integrating the metric system (SI). A recommendation to transition to the metric system within ten years was initiated in the 1970s.

However, this movement lost momentum, and the United States continues to use both measurement systems. Therefore, information to convert between the conventional (U.S.

or English) system and the metric (SI) system has been included in Figure 3-23 , Applied Mathematics Formula Sheet. Examples of its use are as follows:

To convert inches to millimeters, multiply the number of inches by 25.4.

Example: 20 inches = 20 × 25.4 = 508 mm To convert ounces to grams, multiply the number of ounces by 28.35.

Example: 12 ounces = 12 × 28.35 = 340.2 grams

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 不定式作后置定语 + 被动 was initiated

**原句**：A recommendation to transition to the metric system within ten years was initiated in the 1970s.

**拆解**：主语 A recommendation，后接不定式 to transition to the metric system within ten years 作后置定语；谓语 was initiated 为被动；in the 1970s 是时间状语。

**要点**：recommendation / decision / plan / attempt 等名词后常用不定式作定语（a recommendation to do sth.），不用 of doing。was initiated 译「被发起、被提出」，中文更自然说「曾提出」。in the 1970s 表示「20 世纪 70 年代」，年代用复数并加定冠词。

### ② 连接副词 However + and 并列分句

**原句**：However, this movement lost momentum, and the United States continues to use both measurement systems.

**拆解**：However 是连接副词，位于句首并用逗号隔开；两个并列分句由 and 连接：this movement lost momentum 与 the United States continues to use both measurement systems。

**要点**：lose momentum 是固定搭配「失去势头」，不能按字面译「丢掉动量」。continue to do 表示接着做（下一步动作），continue doing 也可，但手册中 to do 更常见。both 后接复数名词，此处 both measurement systems 指传统体系与公制两套。

### ③ To convert …, multiply …：换算说明的固定句式

**原句**：To convert inches to millimeters, multiply the number of inches by 25.4.

**拆解**：To convert inches to millimeters 是不定式短语作目的状语，位于句首；主句是祈使句 multiply the number of inches by 25.4；convert A to B 与 multiply A by B 都是固定搭配。

**要点**：换算说明的典型句式是「To convert X to Y, multiply … by …」，主句动词一律用祈使句原形。convert … to（换算成）与 multiply … by（乘以）的介词不能互换；the number of inches 中的 of 也不能省，否则 the number inches 不成句。
