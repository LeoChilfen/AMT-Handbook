# Scientific Notation

> 通用 Ch 03 · Scientific Notation · 原文 PDF 第 109 页

## 1. 原文

Scientific notation is used as a type of shorthand to express very large or very small numbers. It is a way to write numbers so that they do not take up as much space on the page. The format of a number written in scientific notation has two parts. The first part is a number greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10 (for example, 2.35). The second part is a power of 10 (for example, 106). The number 2,350,000 is expressed in scientific notation as 2.35 × 106. It is important that the decimal point is always placed to the right of the first digit. Notice that very large numbers always have a positive power of 10 and very small numbers always have a negative power of 10.

Example: The velocity of the speed of light is over 186,000 miles per second (mps). This can be expressed as 1.86 × 105 mps in scientific notation. The mass of an electron is approximately 0.000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,911 grams. This can be expressed in scientific notation as 9.11 × 10-28 grams.

## 2. 中文翻译

科学记数法被用作一种简写形式，用来表示非常大或非常小的数。它是一种书写数字的方式，使数字不必在页面上占据太多篇幅。用科学记数法书写的数字，其格式由两部分组成。第一部分是一个大于或等于 1、小于 10 的数（例如 2.35）。第二部分是 10 的幂（例如 106）。数 2,350,000 用科学记数法表示为 2.35 × 106。重要的一点是，小数点必须始终放在第一位数字的右侧。请注意，非常大的数总是带 10 的正幂，而非常小的数总是带 10 的负幂。

例：光速超过每秒 186,000 英里（mps）。用科学记数法可表示为 1.86 × 105 mps。一个电子的质量约为 0.000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,000,911 克。用科学记数法可表示为 9.11 × 10-28 克。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① It is important that …（形式主语 + 主语从句 + 被动）

**原句**：It is important that the decimal point is always placed to the right of the first digit.

**拆解**：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句中 the decimal point 作主语，is placed 为被动谓语，always 位于 be 之后；to the right of the first digit 是方位状语。

**要点**：It is important / necessary / essential that + 从句是规范类文本的常用句式，that 不能省；中文常译成「必须……」「重要的是……」。注意 always 的位置：在 is 之后、过去分词之前。

### ② so that 引导目的状语从句

**原句**：It is a way to write numbers so that they do not take up as much space on the page.

**拆解**：It 指代上句的科学记数法；to write numbers 是不定式作定语修饰 a way；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句主语 they 指 numbers，谓语 take up，宾语 as much space。

**要点**：so that 表目的时从句常带 can / may 或否定式；take up space 是固定搭配，意为占篇幅、占地方。as much space 后省略了比较对象，表示「不必占那么多篇幅」。

### ③ 比较结构 greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10

**原句**：The first part is a number greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10 (for example, 2.35).

**拆解**：表语 a number 后接形容词短语 greater than or equal to 1 and less than 10 作后置定语；greater than 与 less than 并列，共用中心词 a number；or equal to 1 是「大于或等于 1」的固定说法。

**要点**：英语说 greater than or equal to 1，不能写成 greater or equal than；两个比较结构并列时，后一个省略了重复的 a number。中文顺序为「大于或等于 1、小于 10」。
