# Converting Numbers from Scientific Notation to Standard Notation

> 通用 Ch 03 · Converting Numbers from Scientific Notation to Standard Notation · 原文 PDF 第 112 页

## 1. 原文

Example: Convert 3.68 × 10 7 from scientific notation to standard notation, as follows. To convert from scientific notation to standard notation, move the decimal place 7 places to the right. 3.68 × 107 = 36,800,000. Another way to think about the conversion is 3.68 × 107 = 3.68 × 10,000,000 = 36,800,000.

Example: Convert 7.1543 × 10 -10 from scientific notation to standard notation. Move the decimal place 10 places to the left: 7.1543 × 10 -10 =.00000000071543. Another way to think about the conversion is 7.1543 × 10 -10 = 7.1543 × 0.0000000001 = 0.00000000071543 When converting, remember that large numbers always have positive powers of ten and small numbers always have negative powers of ten. Refer to Figure 3-11 to determine which direction to move the decimal point.
