# Converting Numbers from Scientific Notation to Standard Notation

> 通用 Ch 03 · Converting Numbers from Scientific Notation to Standard Notation · 原文 PDF 第 112 页

## 1. 原文

Example: Convert 3.68 × 10 7 from scientific notation to standard notation, as follows. To convert from scientific notation to standard notation, move the decimal place 7 places to the right. 3.68 × 107 = 36,800,000. Another way to think about the conversion is 3.68 × 107 = 3.68 × 10,000,000 = 36,800,000.

Example: Convert 7.1543 × 10 -10 from scientific notation to standard notation. Move the decimal place 10 places to the left: 7.1543 × 10 -10 =.00000000071543. Another way to think about the conversion is 7.1543 × 10 -10 = 7.1543 × 0.0000000001 = 0.00000000071543 When converting, remember that large numbers always have positive powers of ten and small numbers always have negative powers of ten. Refer to Figure 3-11 to determine which direction to move the decimal point.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 句首不定式表目的 + 祈使句

**原句**：To convert from scientific notation to standard notation, move the decimal place 7 places to the right.

**拆解**：To convert from … to … 是不定式短语作目的状语；主句 move the decimal place 7 places to the right 为无主语祈使句，7 places 作距离状语，to the right 表方向。

**要点**：操作说明惯用「目的状语 + 祈使句」，译成中文可作「要……，就把……」。convert from A to B 是固定搭配；move sth 7 places 中数词直接修饰 places，不加介词。

### ② when + 现在分词作时间状语

**原句**：When converting, remember that large numbers always have positive powers of ten and small numbers always have negative powers of ten.

**拆解**：When converting 是「连词 + 现在分词」构成的时间状语，省略了主语和 be（= When you are converting）；主句为祈使句 remember that …；that 从句内由 and 并列两个分句，后一分句省略了相同的谓语 have。

**要点**：when / while + 动名词要求其逻辑主语与主句主语一致，且为主动关系；写成 when convert 即错。第二个分句 small numbers always have negative powers 承前省略 have，读时补回即可。

### ③ 不定式作定语：a way to think about the conversion

**原句**：Another way to think about the conversion is 3.68 × 107 = 3.68 × 10,000,000 = 36,800,000.

**拆解**：主语 Another way，to think about the conversion 是不定式作后置定语；is 后接一串等式作表语，is 在此相当于「就是」。

**要点**：way 后习惯用不定式作定语（a way to do sth），也可用 of doing；表语是数学等式时，系动词后不必强加名词。译作「另一种理解这次换算的方式是……」。
