## 1. 原文

Ratios have widespread application in the field of aviation.

Example: Compression ratio on a reciprocating engine is the ratio of the volume of a cylinder with the piston at the bottom of its stroke to the volume of the cylinder with the piston at the top of its stroke. For example, a typical compression ratio might be 10:1 (or 10 to 1).

Aspect ratio is the ratio of the length (or span) of an airfoil to

its width (or chord). A typical aspect ratio for a commercial airliner might be 7:1 (or 7 to 1).

Air-fuel ratio is the ratio of the weight of the air to the weight of fuel in the mixture being fed into the cylinders of a reciprocating engine. For example, a typical air-fuel ratio might be 14.3:1 (or 14.3 to 1).

Glide ratio is the ratio of the forward distance traveled to the vertical distance descended when an aircraft is operating without power. For example, if an aircraft descends 1,000 feet while it travels through the air for two linear miles (10,560 feet), it has a glide ratio of 10,560:1,000 which can be reduced to 10.56: 1 (or 10.56 to 1).

Gear ratio is the number of teeth each gear represents when two gears are used in an aircraft component. In Figure 3-7, the pinion gear has 8 teeth and a spur gear has 28 teeth. The gear ratio is 8:28. Using 7 as the LCD, 8:28 becomes 2:7.

Speed ratio is when two gears are used in an aircraft component; the rotational speed of each gear is represented as a speed ratio. As the number of teeth in a gear decreases, the rotational speed of that gear increases, and vice-versa.

Therefore, the speed ratio of two gears is the inverse (or opposite) of the gear ratio. If two gears have a gear ratio of 2:9, then their speed ratio is 9:2.

Example: A pinion gear with 10 teeth is driving a spur gear with 40 teeth. The spur gear is rotating at 160 rpm. Calculate the speed of the pinion gear.

Teeth in Pinion Gear = Speed of Spur Gear Teeth in Spur Gear Speed of Pinion Gear 10 teeth = 160 rpm 40 teeth SP (speed of pinion gear)

To solve for SP, multiply 40 × 160, then divide by 10. The speed of the pinion gear is 640 rpm.

Example: If the cruising speed of an airplane is 200 knots and its maximum speed is 250 knots, what is the ratio of cruising speed to maximum speed? First, express the cruising speed as the numerator of a fraction whose denominator is the maximum speed.

Ratio = 200250 Next, reduce the resulting fraction to its simplest form.

Ratio = 200250 = 4 5 Therefore, the ratio of cruising speed to maximum speed is 4:5.

Another common use of ratios is to convert any given ratio to an equivalent ratio with a denominator of 1.

Example: Express the ratio 9:5 as a ratio with a denominator of 1.

R = 9 5 = ? 1 Since 9 ÷ 5 = 1.8, then 9 5 = 1.8 1 Therefore, 9:5 is the same ratio as 1.8:1. In other words, 9 to 5 is the same ratio as 1.8 to 1.

## 2. 中文翻译

比在航空领域应用广泛。

例：往复式发动机的压缩比，是活塞位于行程下端时气缸容积与活塞位于行程上端时气缸容积之比。例如，典型的压缩比可能是 10:1（或 10 比 1）。

展弦比是翼面长度（或翼展）与

其宽度（或弦长）之比。商用客机的典型展弦比可能是 7:1（或 7 比 1）。

空燃比是送入往复式发动机气缸的混合气中，空气重量与燃油重量之比。例如，典型的空燃比可能是 14.3:1（或 14.3 比 1）。

滑翔比是航空器无动力飞行时，前进距离与下降垂直距离之比。例如，若航空器在空中前进两法定英里（10,560 英尺）的同时下降 1,000 英尺，则其滑翔比为 10,560:1,000，可化简为 10.56:1（或 10.56 比 1）。

传动比是指航空器部件中使用两个齿轮时，每个齿轮所具有的齿数。图 3-7 中，小齿轮有 8 齿，直齿轮有 28 齿。传动比为 8:28。以 7 为最小公分母，8:28 化为 2:7。

速度比是指航空器部件中使用两个齿轮时，每个齿轮的转速以速度比表示。齿轮齿数减少时，该齿轮的转速升高，反之亦然。

因此，两个齿轮的速度比是传动比的反比（或倒数）。若两个齿轮的传动比为 2:9，则其速度比为 9:2。

例：一个有 10 齿的小齿轮驱动一个有 40 齿的直齿轮。直齿轮以 160 rpm 旋转。求小齿轮的转速。

`Teeth in Pinion Gear = Speed of Spur Gear Teeth in Spur Gear Speed of Pinion Gear` `10 teeth = 160 rpm 40 teeth SP（小齿轮转速）`（原文为上下分行排写的比例式，转写后两行连排。）

为求 SP，用 40 × 160，再除以 10。小齿轮的转速为 640 rpm。

例：若飞机的巡航速度为 200 节，最大速度为 250 节，则巡航速度与最大速度之比是多少？首先，把巡航速度写成分数的分子，分母取最大速度。

`Ratio = 200250` 然后，把所得分数化为最简形式。

`Ratio = 200250 = 4 5` 因此，巡航速度与最大速度之比为 4:5。

比的另一常见用法，是把任一给定的比换算成分母为 1 的等值比。

例：把比 9:5 表示成分母为 1 的比。

`R = 9 5 = ? 1` 因 9 ÷ 5 = 1.8，则 `9 5 = 1.8 1`。因此，9:5 与 1.8:1 是同一个比。换言之，9 比 5 与 1.8 比 1 是同一个比。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① ratio of A to B：把两个量串起来的固定框架

**原句**：Aspect ratio is the ratio of the length (or span) of an airfoil to its width (or chord).

**拆解**：主语 Aspect ratio，系动词 is，表语 the ratio；of the length (or span) of an airfoil 是两层 of 短语，先限定「哪个翼面的长度」，再与后面的 to its width (or chord) 构成 the ratio of A to B 框架；括号内 (or span)、(or chord) 是并列同义词。

**要点**：A 与 B 相比一律用 the ratio of A to B，不用 of B、也不用 with B；the ratio between A and B 也常见，但语序与介词都不同。本节五个定义（压缩比、展弦比、空燃比、滑翔比、传动比）全是同一框架，读句子时先找到 of … to … 就能分清分子分母。中文对应「A 与 B 之比」，注意别把 A、B 顺序译反。

### ② As … , … and vice-versa：比例关系的正反两面

**原句**：As the number of teeth in a gear decreases, the rotational speed of that gear increases, and vice-versa.

**拆解**：As 引导比例/伴随状语从句，意为「随着……」；主句 the rotational speed of that gear increases；前后两个分句都用一般现在时表示普遍规律；and vice-versa 是独立短语（反之亦然），补出反过来的情形。

**要点**：as 表「随着」时前后两个变化同步发生，中文译「……减少时，……就升高」。vice-versa 在技术文本中极常用，暗示「反过来也成立」，不必展开重写。注意 as 在此不是「因为」；若写成 because，就成了因果而非比例关系。

### ③ 条件句 + while 从句 + which 定语从句：一个长句三层关系

**原句**：For example, if an aircraft descends 1,000 feet while it travels through the air for two linear miles (10,560 feet), it has a glide ratio of 10,560:1,000 which can be reduced to 10.56: 1 (or 10.56 to 1).

**拆解**：if 引导条件状语从句，从句内部又套 while 引导的时间状语从句（表示两件事同时发生）；主句 it has a glide ratio of 10,560:1,000；which can be reduced to 10.56:1 是非限制性定语从句，修饰前面的比 10,560:1,000。

**要点**：技术文本常把一个计算从条件到结果串成一长句：条件（if）→ 同时发生的动作（while）→ 结论（主句）→ 化简结果（which）。翻译时中文宜拆成短句、按时间与因果顺序重排。reduce to 在此是「化简为」，不是「减少到」；linear mile 指法定英里，区别于海里。
