# Positive & Negative Numbers (Signed Numbers)

> 通用 Ch 03 · Positive & Negative Numbers (Signed Numbers) · 原文 PDF 第 107 页

## 1. 原文

Positive numbers are numbers that are greater than zero. Negative numbers are numbers less than zero. Signed numbers are also called integers.

### Addition of Positive & Negative Numbers

The sum (addition) of two positive numbers is positive. The sum (addition) of two negative numbers is negative. The sum of a positive and a negative number can be positive or negative, depending on the values of the numbers. A good way to visualize a negative number is to think in terms of debt. If you are in debt by $100 (or, −100) and you add $45 to your account, you are now only $55 in debt (or −55).

Therefore: −100 + 45 = −55.

Example: The weight of an aircraft is 2,000 pounds. A radio rack weighing 3 pounds and a transceiver weighing 10 pounds are removed from the aircraft. What is the new weight? For weight and balance purposes, all weight removed from an aircraft is given a minus sign, and all weight added is given a plus sign.

2,000 + −3 + −10 = 2,000 + −13 = 1,987

Therefore, the new weight is 1,987 pounds.

### Subtraction of Positive & Negative Numbers

To subtract positive and negative numbers, first change the “–” (subtraction symbol) to a “+” (addition symbol), and change the sign of the second number to its opposite (that is, change a positive number to a negative number or vice versa). Finally, add the two numbers together.

Example: The daytime temperature in the city of Denver was 6° below zero (−6°). An airplane is cruising at 15,000 feet above Denver. The temperature at 15,000 feet is 20° colder than in the city of Denver. What is the temperature at 15,000 feet?

Subtract 20 from −6: −6 – 20 = −6 + (−20) = −26

The temperature is −26°, or 26° below zero at 15,000 feet above the city.

### Multiplication of Positive & Negative Numbers

The product of two positive numbers is always positive. The product of two negative numbers is always positive. The product of a positive and a negative number is always negative.

Examples: 3 × 6 = 18 −3 × 6 = −18 −3 × −6 = 18 3 × −6 = −18

### Division of Positive & Negative Numbers

The quotient of two positive numbers is always positive. The quotient of two negative numbers is always positive. The quotient of a positive and negative number is always negative.

Examples: 6 ÷ 3 = 2 −6 ÷ 3 = −2 −6 ÷ −3 = 2 6 ÷ −3 = −2

## 2. 中文翻译

正数是大于零的数。负数是小于零的数。带符号数也称为整数。

### 正负数的加法

两个正数之和（加法）为正。两个负数之和（加法）为负。一个正数与一个负数之和可能为正也可能为负，取决于这两个数的值。想象负数的一个好办法是用债务来理解。如果你负债 100 美元（或 −100），又往账户里加了 45 美元，那么你现在只欠 55 美元（或 −55）。

因此：−100 + 45 = −55。

例：一架飞机的重量是 2,000 磅。从飞机上拆下一台重 3 磅的无线电支架和一台重 10 磅的收发机（原文此处夹有「80 N」字样，属抽取残留，原文如此）。新的重量是多少？在载重与平衡计算中，从飞机上拆下的重量都取负号，增加的重量都取正号。

2,000 + −3 + −10 = 2,000 + −13 = 1,987 因此，新的重量是 1,987 磅。

### 正负数的减法

要相减正数和负数，先把「–」（减号）改成「+」（加号），再把第二个数的符号改成与它相反的符号（也就是说，把正数改成负数，或反过来）。最后，把两个数相加。

例：丹佛市白天的气温为零下 6°（−6°）。一架飞机在丹佛上空 15,000 英尺处巡航。15,000 英尺处的气温比丹佛市低 20°。15,000 英尺处的气温是多少？

从 −6 中减去 20：−6 – 20 = −6 + (−20) = −26 该市上空 15,000 英尺处的气温是 −26°，即零下 26°。

### 正负数的乘法

两个正数之积总为正。两个负数之积总为正。一个正数与一个负数之积总为负。例：3 × 6 = 18 −3 × 6 = −18 −3 × −6 = 18 3 × −6 = −18

### 正负数的除法

两个正数之商总为正。两个负数之商总为正。一个正数与一个负数之商总为负。

例：6 ÷ 3 = 2 −6 ÷ 3 = −2 −6 ÷ −3 = 2 6 ÷ −3 = −2

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句 + 比较级

**原句**：Positive numbers are numbers that are greater than zero.

**拆解**：主语 Positive numbers；系动词 are；表语 numbers 后接 that 引导的定语从句 that are greater than zero，that 在从句中作主语。

**要点**：定义数类时用「X are numbers that are …」；greater than 是比较级，修饰的数必须是可比的量。定语从句中 that 作主语时不能省略。

### ② 现在分词短语作伴随状语

**原句**：The sum of a positive and a negative number can be positive or negative, depending on the values of the numbers.

**拆解**：主语 The sum of a positive and a negative number；谓语 can be positive or negative；depending on the values of the numbers 是现在分词短语作伴随状语，说明结果由什么决定。

**要点**：depending on … 的逻辑主语是前面的整个判断，属于垂悬分词，但在技术英语中已固定化。情态动词 can 在此表可能性（「可能为正也可能为负」），不是能力。

### ③ 不定式作定语与表语

**原句**：A good way to visualize a negative number is to think in terms of debt.

**拆解**：主语 A good way to visualize a negative number（to visualize 作 way 的后置定语）；系动词 is；表语是 to think in terms of debt（不定式）。

**要点**：way to do sth 用不定式作定语，不用 doing；think in terms of 意为「从……的角度来想」，是解释抽象概念时的常用搭配。

### ④ if 条件句 + 表示债务的搭配

**原句**：If you are in debt by $100 (or, −100) and you add $45 to your account, you are now only $55 in debt (or −55).

**拆解**：If 引导真实条件句，从句内含两个并列谓语 are in debt by $100 与 add $45 to your account；主句 you are now only $55 in debt (or −55)。

**要点**：be in debt by + 金额表示「负债多少」；add A to B（把 A 加到 B 上）。条件句与主句都用一般现在时，表示普遍成立的换算关系；括号内 or 引出等值的负数写法。

### ⑤ 目的状语 + 祈使句并列 + that is 插入语

**原句**：To subtract positive and negative numbers, first change the “–” (subtraction symbol) to a “+” (addition symbol), and change the sign of the second number to its opposite (that is, change a positive number to a negative number or vice versa).

**拆解**：To subtract positive and negative numbers 是目的状语；主句为祈使句，谓语由 change … to … 与 change … to … 并列构成；that is 是插入语，用来换一种说法解释。

**要点**：change A to B 表示「把 A 改成 B」，注意 to 后接改成的目标。括号内 that is 引出释义，与 i.e. 功能相同；vice versa 意为「反之亦然」。

### ⑥ 过去分词后置定语 + 被动语态

**原句**：For weight and balance purposes, all weight removed from an aircraft is given a minus sign, and all weight added is given a plus sign.

**拆解**：For weight and balance purposes 是目的状语；两个分句并列，均为主语 + is given + 双宾语（a minus sign / a plus sign）；removed from an aircraft 与 added 都是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 weight。

**要点**：give sb sth 变被动为 sb is given sth，此处直接保留一个宾语作补足。removed、added 后置是「被拆下的」「被增加的」，单用过去分词即可，不必还原成定语从句。
