# Mixed Numbers

> 通用 Ch 03 · Mixed Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 99 页

## 1. 原文

A mixed number is a combination of a whole number and a fraction.

### Addition of Mixed Numbers

To add mixed numbers, add the whole numbers together. Then add the fractions together by finding a common denominator. The final step is to add the sum of the whole numbers to the sum of the fractions for the final answer. Example: The cargo area behind the rear seat of a small airplane can handle solids that are 4 3⁄4 feet long. If the rear seats are removed, then 2 1⁄3 feet is added to the cargo area. What is the total length of the cargo area when the rear seats are removed?

4 3 + 2 1 = (4 + 2) +(3 + 1) = 6 +( 9 + 4) = 6 13 = 4 3 4 3 12 12 12 7 1 feet of cargo room 12

### Subtraction of Mixed Numbers

To subtract mixed numbers, find a common denominator for the fractions. Subtract the fractions from each other. It may be necessary to borrow from the larger whole number when subtracting the fractions. Subtract the whole numbers from each other. The final step is to combine the final whole number with the final fraction. Example: What is the length of the grip of the bolt? The overall length of the bolt is 3 1⁄2 inches, the shank length is 3 1⁄8 inches, and the threaded portion is 15⁄16 inches long. To find the grip, subtract the length of the threaded portion from the length of the shank.

31⁄8 inches – 15⁄16 inches = grip length To subtract, start with the fractions. Borrowing is necessary because 5⁄16 is larger than 1⁄8 (or 2⁄16). From the whole number 3, borrow 1, which is actually 16⁄16. After borrowing, the first mixed number is now 2 18⁄16. This is because, 3 1⁄8 = 3 2⁄16 = 2 + 1 + 2⁄16 = 2 + 16⁄16 + 2⁄16 = 2 18⁄16.

3 1 – 1 5 = 3 2 – 1 5 = 2 18 – 1 5 = 113 8 16 16 16 16 16 16 Therefore, the grip length of the bolt is 1 13⁄16 inches.

(Note: The value for the overall length of the bolt was given in the example, but it was not needed to solve the problem. This type of information is sometimes referred to as a “distracter,” because it distracts from the information needed to solve the problem.)

## 2. 中文翻译

带分数是整数与分数的组合。

### 带分数的加法

要相加带分数，先把整数部分相加。然后通过求公分母把分数部分相加。最后一步是把整数之和与分数之和相加，得到最终答案。例：一架小型飞机后座后面的货舱可以装载长度为 4 3⁄4 英尺的硬质物件。如果把后座拆掉，货舱长度可增加 2 1⁄3 英尺。拆掉后座后，货舱的总长度是多少？

（原文此处为带分数加法竖式，抽文中分数被抽平、上下位置丢失：）4 3 + 2 1 = (4 + 2) +(3 + 1) = 6 +( 9 + 4) = 6 13 = 4 3 4 3 12 12 12 7 1 英尺的货舱长度 12

### 带分数的减法

要相减带分数，先为分数部分求公分母。然后把分数部分相减。相减时可能有必要从较大的整数部分借位。再把整数部分相减。最后一步是把最终的整数与最终的分数合并起来。例：该螺栓的夹持长度是多少？螺栓的总长是 3 1⁄2 英寸，杆身长 3 1⁄8 英寸，螺纹部分长 15⁄16 英寸。要求夹持长度，就用杆身长度减去螺纹部分的长度。

3 1⁄8 英寸 – 15⁄16 英寸 = 夹持长度 相减时先从分数部分入手。需要借位，因为 5⁄16 大于 1⁄8（即 2⁄16）。从整数 3 中借 1，这个 1 实际上是 16⁄16。借位后，第一个带分数现在是 2 18⁄16。这是因为 3 1⁄8 = 3 2⁄16 = 2 + 1 + 2⁄16 = 2 + 16⁄16 + 2⁄16 = 2 18⁄16。

（原文此处为带分数减法竖式，抽文中分数被抽平：）3 1 – 1 5 = 3 2 – 1 5 = 2 18 – 1 5 = 113 8 16 16 16 16 16 16 因此，螺栓的夹持长度是 1 13⁄16 英寸。

（注：螺栓总长的数值在例题中给出，但解题并不需要它。这类信息有时被称为「干扰项」，因为它会分散注意力，使人偏离解题所需的信息。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 名词短语作表语 + of 结构

**原句**：A mixed number is a combination of a whole number and a fraction.

**拆解**：主语 A mixed number，系动词 is，表语是名词短语 a combination of a whole number and a fraction；of 后为并列的两个名词短语，由 and 连接。

**要点**：定义术语时常用「X is a combination/sum/product of A and B」，注意 of 后面是构成整体的成分。a combination of … and … 中 and 连接的是 of 的宾语，不是主句成分，判断句意时别误读成两个并列的表语。

### ② 目的状语前置 + 祈使句

**原句**：To add mixed numbers, add the whole numbers together.

**拆解**：To add mixed numbers 是不定式短语作目的状语，置于句首；主句是祈使句 add the whole numbers together，其中 together 是状语，表示「加在一起」。

**要点**：说明操作步骤的祈使句前加 To do sth 说明目的，逻辑主语是读者/操作者。add … together 是固定搭配；注意 add A to B（把 A 加到 B 上）与 add … together（把……加在一起）的区别。

### ③ by + 动名词表示方式

**原句**：Then add the fractions together by finding a common denominator.

**拆解**：主干为祈使句 add the fractions together；by finding a common denominator 是介词短语作方式状语，by 后接动名词 finding。

**要点**：表示「通过做某事」用 by doing，介词 by 后不能接动词原形或不定式。此法与步骤描写的祈使句连用，能把手段与动作分开，是技术说明文的常见写法。

### ④ 不定式作表语 + add A to B

**原句**：The final step is to add the sum of the whole numbers to the sum of the fractions for the final answer.

**拆解**：主语 The final step；is 后接不定式 to add … 作表语；add 的宾语是 the sum of the whole numbers，to 引出 the sum of the fractions；for the final answer 是目的状语。

**要点**：The final step is to do sth 是流程描述的高频套式，表语用不定式而非动名词（表示「要做的事」）。add A to B 中 to 是介词，不能换成 with；两个 of 短语层层修饰 sum，注意拆句理解「整数之和」「分数之和」。

### ⑤ 形式主语 it + when + 动名词

**原句**：It may be necessary to borrow from the larger whole number when subtracting the fractions.

**拆解**：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to borrow from the larger whole number；may be necessary 是谓语部分；when subtracting the fractions 是时间状语，when 后省略了主语和 be（when one is subtracting）。

**要点**：It is necessary to do sth 中 it 不可换成 that。when + 动名词是 when 从句省略主语后的常见压缩形式，主语须与主句一致，否则要补全从句。

### ⑥ 被动语态 + 过去分词后置定语 + because 从句

**原句**：This type of information is sometimes referred to as a “distracter,” because it distracts from the information needed to solve the problem.

**拆解**：主句 This type of information is sometimes referred to as a “distracter”，为被动语态，as 引出补足语；because 引导原因状语从句 it distracts from the information needed to solve the problem，其中 needed to solve the problem 是过去分词短语后置修饰 the information。

**要点**：refer to … as … 的被动形式是 be referred to as …，as 不可省。needed 是过去分词表被动（「被需要的」），不能写成 needing。distract from 是「使偏离、分散注意力」的固定搭配。
