## 1. 原文

### General Concepts

All the terminology and concepts that apply to airplane weight and balance also apply generally to helicopter weight and balance. However, there are some specific differences that need to be identified.

Most helicopters have a much more restricted CG range than airplanes. In some cases, this range is less than 3". The exact location and length of the CG range is specified for each helicopter and usually extends a short distance fore and aft of the main rotor mast or centered between the main rotors of a dual rotor system. Whereas airplanes have a CG range only along the longitudinal axis, helicopters have both longitudinal and lateral CG ranges. Because the wings extend outward from the CG, airplanes tend to have a great deal of lateral stability. A helicopter, on the other hand, acts like a pendulum, with the weight of the helicopter hanging from the main rotor shaft.

Ideally, the helicopter should have such perfect balance that the fuselage remains horizontal while in a hover. If the helicopter is too nose heavy or tail heavy while it is hovering, the cyclic pitch control is used to keep the fuselage horizontal. If the CG location is too extreme, it may not be possible to keep the fuselage horizontal or maintain control of the helicopter.

### Helicopter Weighing

When a helicopter is being weighed, the location of both longitudinal and lateral weighing points must be known to determine its empty weight and EWCG. This is because helicopters have longitudinal and lateral CG limits. As with the airplane, the longitudinal arms are measured from the datum, with locations behind the datum being positive arms and locations in front of the datum being negative arms. Laterally, the arms are measured from the butt line, which is a line from the nose to the tail running through the middle of the helicopter. When facing forward, arms to the right of the butt line are positive; to the left they are negative.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 基本概念

适用于飞机重量与平衡的所有术语和概念，一般也适用于直升机重量与平衡。不过，有一些具体差异需要认识清楚。

大多数直升机的重心范围比飞机窄得多。有些情况下，该范围小于 3"。每型直升机都规定了重心范围的确切位置和长度，通常以主旋翼轴为中心向前后各延伸一小段距离，或位于双旋翼系统的两主旋翼之间的中央。飞机的重心范围仅沿纵轴方向，而直升机既有纵向重心范围，也有横向重心范围。由于机翼从重心向外伸出，飞机往往具有很大的横向稳定性。而直升机则像单摆一样，其重量悬挂在主旋翼轴上。

理想情况下，直升机应达到完全平衡，使悬停时机身保持水平。若悬停时直升机头重或尾重，则用周期变距操纵使机身保持水平。如果重心位置过于极端，可能无法保持机身水平，也无法保持对直升机的操纵。

### 直升机称重

给直升机称重时，必须知道纵向和横向称重点的位置，才能确定其空重和空重重心。这是因为直升机既有纵向重心限制，也有横向重心限制。与飞机一样，纵向力臂从基准量起，基准之后的位置为正力臂，基准之前的位置为负力臂。横向力臂则从翼根线量起，翼根线是从机头到机尾、穿过直升机中部的直线。面朝前方时，翼根线右侧的力臂为正，左侧为负。

给直升机称重之前，必须沿纵向和横向将其调平。这可以用气泡水准仪。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① much + 比较级 + than
**原句**：Most helicopters have a much more restricted CG range than airplanes.
**拆解**：主语 Most helicopters + 谓语 have + 宾语 a much more restricted CG range；much 修饰比较级 more restricted；than airplanes 为比较状语，than 后省略了 have。
**要点**：修饰比较级用 much、far、a great deal，不能用 very，这是中国学习者最常见的错点。more restricted 是形容词比较级作前置定语，修饰 CG range。than 后为避免重复省略谓语，阅读时要能补回 than airplanes do。

### ② Whereas 引导对比从句
**原句**：Whereas airplanes have a CG range only along the longitudinal axis, helicopters have both longitudinal and lateral CG ranges.
**拆解**：Whereas 引导对比状语从句，说明飞机只有纵向重心范围；主句 helicopters have both longitudinal and lateral CG ranges，both … and … 连接两个并列定语。
**要点**：Whereas 意为「而、相比之下」，用于正式书面语中的对照，比 while 更书面。both … and … 连接两项后，被修饰的 range 要用复数 ranges。翻译时可用「而……」把对比关系点出来。

### ③ 现在进行被动 + 情态被动
**原句**：When a helicopter is being weighed, the location of both longitudinal and lateral weighing points must be known to determine its empty weight and EWCG.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，谓语为现在进行被动 is being weighed；主句主语 the location（of both longitudinal and lateral weighing points 为后置定语，修饰 location）+ 谓语 must be known；to determine … 为目的状语。
**要点**：is being weighed 强调「在称重进行的过程中」，与一般现在被动 is weighed 有别。主句真正的主语是 the location（单数），of 短语里的 points 只是修饰成分，所以谓语用 must be known 而不是复数形式。译文常把被动转为「必须知道……的位置」。

### ④ such … that 结果状语从句
**原句**：Ideally, the helicopter should have such perfect balance that the fuselage remains horizontal while in a hover.
**拆解**：Ideally 为评注性状语；主句 the helicopter should have such perfect balance；such … that 引导结果状语从句 the fuselage remains horizontal；while in a hover 为时间状语，while 后省略了 it is。
**要点**：such + 名词短语 + that 与 so + 形容词 + that 要分清：本句中心词 balance 是名词，故用 such。while in a hover 是「while it is in a hover」的省略形式，时间状语从句中主语与主句相同时常这样省略。should 在此表示「应当、理应」，不是过去将来时。

### ⑤ 形式主语 + may not + 并列不定式
**原句**：If the CG location is too extreme, it may not be possible to keep the fuselage horizontal or maintain control of the helicopter.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句；主句 it may not be possible to keep … or maintain …，it 为形式主语，真正的主语是并列的两个不定式 to keep … or (to) maintain …；may not 表示可能性上的否定。
**要点**：it is possible to do 结构中，并列第二个不定式常省略 to。keep the fuselage horizontal 是「动词 + 宾语 + 形容词作宾语补足语」结构。may not be possible 应译成「可能无法」，不要误解为「不允许」。
