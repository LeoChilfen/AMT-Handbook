# Aircraft Specifications

> 通用 Ch 02 · Aircraft Specifications · 原文 PDF 第 77 页

## 1. 原文

Specifications were originated during implementation of the Air Commerce Act of 1926. Specifications are FAA recordkeeping documents issued for both type-certificated and non-type-certificated products that have been found eligible for U.S. airworthiness certification. Although they are no longer issued, specifications remain in effect and will be further amended. Specifications covering type-certificated products may be converted to a TCDS at the option of the TC holder. However, to do so requires the TC holder to provide an equipment list. A specification is not part of a TC. Specifications are subdivided into five major groups as follows:

1. Group I—Type Certificate Aircraft, Engines, and Propellers. Covering standard, restricted, and limited types issued for domestic, foreign, and military surplus products.

2. Group II—Aircraft, Engine, and Propeller Approvals.

Covering domestic, foreign, and military surplus products constructed or modified between October 1, 1927, and August 22, 1938. All have met minimum airworthiness requirements without formal type certification. Such products are eligible for standard airworthiness certification as though they are type- certificated products.

3. Group III—Aircraft, Engine, and Propeller Approvals.

Covering domestic products manufactured prior to October 1, 1927, foreign products manufactured prior to June 20, 1931, and certain military surplus engines and propellers. All have met minimum airworthiness requirements of the Air Commerce Act of 1926 and implementing Air Commerce Regulations without formal type certification. Such products are eligible for standard airworthiness certification as though they are type-certificated products.

4. Group IV—Engine Ratings. Covering unapproved engines rated for maximum power and speed only, their use being limited to specific aircraft with maximum gross weights less than 1,000 pounds. Such engines are not eligible for independent airworthiness certification. These ratings are no longer issued.

5. Group V—Engine Approvals. Covering military surplus engines meeting CAR 13 design requirements without formal type certification. Such engines are eligible for airworthiness certification as though they are type-certificated engines.

## 2. 中文翻译

航空器规范（specifications）起源于 1926 年《商业航空法》的实施。规范是 FAA 的一种记录性文件，既为已取得型号合格的产品颁发，也为未取得型号合格、但被认定有资格获得美国适航合格证的产品颁发。尽管已不再颁发，规范仍然有效，并将进一步修订。涵盖型号合格产品的规范，可由型号合格证持有人选择转换为 TCDS；但这样做要求 TC 持有人提供一份设备清单。规范不是 TC 的组成部分。规范分为以下五大组：

1. 第 I 组——型号合格证航空器、发动机与螺旋桨。涵盖为国产、进口及军用剩余物资产品颁发的标准类、限制类和有限类。

2. 第 II 组——航空器、发动机与螺旋桨的批准。

涵盖 1927 年 10 月 1 日至 1938 年 8 月 22 日之间制造或改装的国产、进口及军用剩余物资产品。这些产品均在没有正式型号合格审定的情况下满足了最低适航要求。此类产品有资格取得标准适航证，如同它们是型号合格产品一样。

3. 第 III 组——航空器、发动机与螺旋桨的批准。

涵盖 1927 年 10 月 1 日之前制造的国产产品、1931 年 6 月 20 日之前制造的进口产品，以及某些军用剩余物资发动机和螺旋桨。这些产品均在没有正式型号合格审定的情况下满足了 1926 年《商业航空法》及其实施性《商业航空条例》的最低适航要求。此类产品有资格取得标准适航证，如同它们是型号合格产品一样。

4. 第 IV 组——发动机额定值。涵盖仅按最大功率和最大转速评定的未批准发动机，其使用仅限于最大全重小于 1,000 磅的特定航空器。此类发动机不具备独立取得适航证的资格。这些额定值已不再颁发。

5. 第 V 组——发动机批准。涵盖满足 CAR 13 设计要求、但未取得正式型号合格审定的军用剩余物资发动机。此类发动机有资格取得适航证，如同它们是型号合格发动机一样。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① at the option of 与情态被动

**原句**：Specifications covering type-certificated products may be converted to a TCDS at the option of the TC holder.

**拆解**：主语 Specifications，现在分词短语 covering type-certificated products 作后置定语；谓语 may be converted 为情态被动；at the option of the TC holder 是方式状语，意为「由 TC 持有人选择决定」。

**要点**：at the option of sb 是「由某人选择、听凭某人决定」的固定说法，比 by the choice of 更书面。may be converted 表「可以转换」，不是「必须转换」——转与不转由持有人决定，这在适航文件里是可选项与强制项的分界。

### ② Although 让步从句 + 并列谓语 remain / will be

**原句**：Although they are no longer issued, specifications remain in effect and will be further amended.

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，they 回指 specifications，用被动 are issued；主句为并列谓语 remain in effect 与 will be further amended；in effect 是介词短语作表语。

**要点**：remain in effect（仍然有效）与 come into effect（生效）是一对易混搭配，读适航文件时二者不能互译。will be further amended 用将来时被动，说明修订还会继续，不能理解为「已经定稿」。although 从句与主句之间不加 but。

### ③ as though 引导的方式状语从句

**原句**：Such products are eligible for standard airworthiness certification as though they are type- certificated products.

**拆解**：主句 Such products are eligible for standard airworthiness certification；as though 引导方式状语从句，从句用陈述语气 are type-certificated products；Such products 回指上文的军用剩余物资或早期产品。

**要点**：as though / as if 后接虚拟语气（were）表示与事实相反；此处用 are，是因为文件把它们「视同」型号合格产品，属事实性类比，两种用法都见于适航文本。eligible for + 名词、eligible to do 是固定搭配，不能写成 eligible of。
