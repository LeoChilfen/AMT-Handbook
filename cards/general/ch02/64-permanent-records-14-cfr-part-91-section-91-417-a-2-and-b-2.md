# Permanent Records—14 CFR Part 91, Section 91.417(a)(2) and (b)(2)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Permanent Records—14 CFR Part 91, Section 91.417(a)(2) and (b)(2) · 原文 PDF 第 89 页

## 1. 原文

91.417(a)(2) and (b)(2)

These records must be retained by the owner during the time they operate the aircraft. They are transferred with the aircraft at the time of sale. Typically, these are documents relating to total time in service, current status of life-limited parts, time since last overhaul, current inspection status, current status of applicable AD notes, and major alteration forms as required by 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9.

## 2. 中文翻译

91.417(a)(2) 与 (b)(2)

这类记录必须由机主在其运营该航空器的期间保存，并在出售时随航空器一并移交。这类文件通常涉及：总使用时间、限寿部件的当前状态、自上次翻修以来的时间、当前检查状态、适用适航指令（AD）的当前状态，以及 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 节所要求的重大改装表格。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① during the time + 省略关系词的定语从句

**原句**：These records must be retained by the owner during the time they operate the aircraft.

**拆解**：主句 must be retained by the owner 为情态被动；during the time 引出时间状语，they operate the aircraft 是修饰 the time 的定语从句，相当于 during the time when/that they operate the aircraft，关系副词被省略。

**要点**：the time (that/when) + 从句是「在……期间」的常见省略结构，翻译时不必译出「那个时间」。they 指 the owner，是按「机主一方」来理解的单数主体，不要误以为指记录。

### ② with / at the time of 表随同与时间点

**原句**：They are transferred with the aircraft at the time of sale.

**拆解**：一般现在时被动 are transferred；with the aircraft 表示「随航空器一起」；at the time of sale 是时间状语。

**要点**：with 在这里是「连同、随同」，不是「用（工具）」。这句是永久记录区别于临时记录的核心：记录随航空器走，不随人走，所以译作「随航空器一并移交」。

### ③ as required by 省略结构

**原句**：Typically, these are documents relating to total time in service, current status of life-limited parts, time since last overhaul, current inspection status, current status of applicable AD notes, and major alteration forms as required by 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9.

**拆解**：主句 these are documents；relating to … 是现在分词短语作后置定语，后面跟着六个并列宾语；as required by … 是「as + 过去分词」的省略式状语，完整形式为 as they are required by …，as 意为「按照」。

**要点**：as required by、as specified in、as described above 都是技术文本的固定省略结构，主语和 be 动词一并省掉；不要误写成 as requiring。relating to 这一长串宾语建议先数项再逐项译，避免漏掉 major alteration forms。
