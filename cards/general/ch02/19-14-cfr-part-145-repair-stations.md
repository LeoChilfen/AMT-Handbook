# 14 CFR Part 145—Repair Stations

> 通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 145—Repair Stations · 原文 PDF 第 63 页

## 1. 原文

This regulation underwent a major rewrite released in 2004 and was the most comprehensive change in nearly 20 years.

It may be of interest to note an airframe and powerplant (A&P) certificate is not necessary to be employed at a repair station. The repair station may also employ both repairmen (under 14 CFR part 65, subpart E) and non FAA-certificated personnel. All work that is signed off is done so using the repair station certificate number and must be done only by persons authorized by 14 CFR part 65 to approve an article for return to service (RTS). Just as other certificate holders must have an operations manual, the repair station must have a repair station manual that contains the following:

• An organizational chart • Procedures for maintaining rosters • Description of housing, facilities, and equipment • Procedures for revising the capability list and conducting a self-evaluation (audit)

• Procedures for revising the training program • Procedures governing work done at another location • Procedures for working on air carrier aircraft • Description of the required records and record keeping • Procedures for revising the repair station manual • Description of the system to identify and control the sections of the manual All records from repair station maintenance activity must be kept a minimum of 2 years. Domestic repair station certificates are effective until they are surrendered, suspended, or revoked. The certificates of foreign repair stations expire, usually after 1 or 2 years and must be renewed.

## 2. 中文翻译

该规章在 2004 年发布了重大改写版本，是近 20 年来最全面的一次改动。

值得注意的是，在维修站任职并不要求持有机体与动力装置（A&P）执照。维修站还可以同时雇用修理人员（依 14 CFR part 65 subpart E）和未取得 FAA 证照的人员。所有签署放行的工作，都以维修站合格证编号签署，并且只能由 14 CFR part 65 授权批准产品恢复使用（RTS）的人员实施。正如其他合格证持有人必须备有运行手册一样，维修站必须备有一份维修站手册，其中包含下列内容：

• 组织机构图 • 维持人员名册的程序 • 场房、设施与设备的说明 • 修订能力清单以及实施自我评估（审核）的程序

• 修订培训大纲的程序 • 管控在其他地点实施工作的程序 • 对航空承运人航空器实施工作的程序 • 所需记录与记录保存的说明 • 修订维修站手册的程序 • 识别并控制手册各章节的系统的说明。维修站维修活动的所有记录必须至少保存 2 年。国内维修站合格证持续有效，直至被交回、暂停或吊销。国外维修站合格证则到期失效，通常是 1 年或 2 年之后，必须重新办理。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① It may be of interest to note …（形式主语 + 省略 that 的宾语从句）

**原句**：It may be of interest to note an airframe and powerplant (A&P) certificate is not necessary to be employed at a repair station.

**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正的主语是 to note …；note 后接省略 that 的宾语从句（an A&P certificate is not necessary）；to be employed 为不定式被动，说明「在被雇用这件事上」不必要。

**要点**：it may be of interest to note that … 是手册里引导读者注意的固定开场；正式写法应补出 that，否则两个动词直接相邻，初学者容易读断。be necessary to do 的主语通常是动作，此处译作「受雇并不要求持有 A&P 执照」比直译通顺。

### ② All work that is signed off is done so using … and must be done only by persons authorized …（定语从句 + 双被动 + 过去分词后置定语）

**原句**：All work that is signed off is done so using the repair station certificate number and must be done only by persons authorized by 14 CFR part 65 to approve an article for return to service (RTS).

**拆解**：主语 All work + that is signed off 定语从句；谓语一 is done（被动）+ so（代指签署方式）+ using … 方式状语；谓语二 must be done only by persons …；authorized by 14 CFR part 65 to approve … 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 persons。

**要点**：do so 用来代替前面出现过的动词短语，避免重复；using … 是分词短语作方式状语（= by using）。only by persons 中的 only 限定施动者，强调排他性，是法规限定责任主体的常用手段。persons authorized … to approve … 译作「被授权批准……的人员」。

### ③ The certificates of foreign repair stations expire … and must be renewed（主动与被动并列）

**原句**：The certificates of foreign repair stations expire, usually after 1 or 2 years and must be renewed.

**拆解**：主语 The certificates of foreign repair stations；谓语一 expire（不及物，主动），usually after 1 or 2 years 为时间状语插入；谓语二 must be renewed（情态被动）。

**要点**：同一主语下主动（expire）与被动（be renewed）并列——「到期」是自然发生的，「续期」是人为办理的，语态选择反映动作性质。expire 没有被动式（证书不能「被到期」）；前文 surrendered／suspended／revoked 三个被动连用，则是因为这三个动作都由人（局方）施加。
