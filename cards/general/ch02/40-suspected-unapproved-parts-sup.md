# Suspected Unapproved Parts (SUP)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Suspected Unapproved Parts (SUP) · 原文 PDF 第 74 页

## 1. 原文

There are four types of aircraft parts:

1. Good parts with good paperwork.

2. Good parts with bad paperwork.

3. Bad parts with “good” (bogus) paperwork.

4. Bad parts with bad paperwork.

The first of those listed represents properly authorized parts that, when properly installed, are approved parts, and the aircraft can be returned to service. The last of those listed represent unauthorized and unapproved parts. The technician should be alert for these and must never install them on an aircraft.

The center two categories of parts represent suspected unapproved parts. If either the physical part or the paperwork associated with the part is questionable, it is best to contact the shop foreman, shift supervisor, or the assigned quality individual to discuss your concerns. Suspected unapproved parts (SUPs) should be segregated and quarantined until proper disposition can be determined. Contacting the manufacturer of the product is a good way to start gathering the facts concerning the product in question. Refer to the current version of AC 21-29, Detecting and Reporting Suspected Unapproved Parts, for additional information.

Current contact information for submitting a SUP Notification can be found at www.faa.gov.

## 2. 中文翻译

航空器零件有四类：

1. 文件齐全的正品件。

2. 文件不合格的正品件。

3. 带有「齐全」（伪造）文件的劣质件。

4. 文件不合格的劣质件。

上述第一类是指经过正当授权的零件，正确安装之后即属批准件，航空器可以恢复使用。上述最后一类则属未经授权、未获批准的零件。维修技师应当对此保持警惕，绝不可将其安装到航空器上。

中间两类零件属于可疑非批准件。如果零件实物或与该零件相关的文件存在疑问，最好联系车间工长、值班主管或指定的质量负责人，讨论你关注的问题。可疑非批准件（SUP）应当隔离、封存，直至能够确定妥善的处置方式。联系产品的制造厂，是着手收集该可疑产品相关事实的好办法。更多信息可参见 AC 21-29《可疑非批准件的识别与报告》的现行版本。

提交 SUP 通报的最新联系方式可在 www.faa.gov 上查到。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句中插入 when 省略结构

**原句**
The first of those listed represents properly authorized parts that, when properly installed, are approved parts, and the aircraft can be returned to service.

**拆解**
主语 The first of those listed，谓语 represents，宾语 properly authorized parts 带 that 定语从句；从句中插入 when properly installed（省略形式，相当于 when they are properly installed）；and 之后另起并列分句 the aircraft can be returned to service。

**要点**
when properly installed 是「when + 过去分词」的省略结构，译「在正确安装后」；that 从句只修饰 parts，不修饰 the aircraft，注意 and 的层级；be returned to service 是「恢复使用、批准放行」的固定说法。

### ② If either … or … is questionable, it is best to …

**原句**
If either the physical part or the paperwork associated with the part is questionable, it is best to contact the shop foreman, shift supervisor, or the assigned quality individual to discuss your concerns.

**拆解**
If 引导条件从句，主语由 either A or B 构成（A = the physical part，B = the paperwork associated with the part），谓语用单数 is；主句中 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to contact … to discuss your concerns。

**要点**
either … or … 作主语时谓语与最近一项一致，此处用 is；it is best to 译「最好……」，it 不指代具体事物；contact 后三个并列宾语代表逐级上报路径，译文保留层级。

### ③ should be segregated and quarantined until …

**原句**
Suspected unapproved parts (SUPs) should be segregated and quarantined until proper disposition can be determined.

**拆解**
主语 Suspected unapproved parts（过去分词 suspected 作定语）；谓语 should be segregated and quarantined，两个过去分词共用 should be；until 引导时间状语从句，从句为被动 can be determined。

**要点**
should 在技术文件中表「应当」，语气弱于 must，不要一律译「必须」；segregated 与 quarantined 是「隔离」与「封存」两个动作，不可合并；until 从句含情态 can，译「直至能确定处置方式为止」。
