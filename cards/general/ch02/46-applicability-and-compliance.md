# Applicability and Compliance

> 通用 Ch 02 · Applicability and Compliance · 原文 PDF 第 77 页

## 1. 原文

The AD subject line specifically identifies the TC holder of the aircraft or products affected by the AD. The specific models affected and any special considerations, such as specific installed part numbers or modifications, are listed in the AD applicability section. In order to find all applicable ADs for a specific product, you must search for ADs on the product, aircraft, engine(s), propeller, or any installed appliance. If there are multiple series under the aircraft or engine model, you must also search for ADs applicable to the model, as well as the specific series of that model.

The final determination of ADs applicable to a particular product can only be made by a thorough examination of the ADs and the product logbooks. No person may operate a product that an AD applies to, except in accordance with the requirements of the AD. Furthermore, the owner or operator of an aircraft is required by 14 CFR part 91, section 91.403 to maintain the aircraft in compliance with all ADs. The AD specifies a compliance time that relates to the effective date of the AD. That compliance time determines when the actions are required.

## 2. 中文翻译

AD 的主题行明确标示该 AD 所影响航空器或产品的型号合格证持有人。具体受影响的型号以及任何特殊考虑事项，如已安装的特定件号或改装，列在 AD 的适用性一节中。要找出某一特定产品的全部适用 AD，必须就产品、航空器、一台或多台发动机、螺旋桨或任何已安装附件检索 AD。如果该航空器或发动机型号下还有多个系列，还必须检索适用于该型号的 AD，以及适用于该型号特定系列的 AD。

对某一特定产品适用哪些 AD 的最终判定，只能通过对 AD 和产品履历本的彻底核查作出。任何人不得运行 AD 所适用的产品，但按该 AD 要求行事者除外。此外，14 CFR 第 91 部第 91.403 条要求航空器的机主或运营人使航空器保持符合所有 AD。AD 规定一个与 AD 生效日期相关的符合期限，该期限决定何时必须完成相应措施。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 不定式短语作目的状语 + 情态动词 must

**原句**：In order to find all applicable ADs for a specific product, you must search for ADs on the product, aircraft, engine(s), propeller, or any installed appliance.

**拆解**：In order to find all applicable ADs for a specific product 是不定式短语作目的状语；主句 you must search for ADs；on the product, aircraft, engine(s), propeller, or any installed appliance 是介词短语列举检索对象。

**要点**：in order to 比单用 to 更强调目的，正式文本常用。search for 是「查找、检索」，单独用 search 是「搜查某处」——search the logbook 是「查阅履历本」，search for the AD 是「检索 AD」，两者不可混。must 表强制义务，与后文 may 形成对照。

### ② 否定主语 no person may + except 除外状语

**原句**：No person may operate a product that an AD applies to, except in accordance with the requirements of the AD.

**拆解**：No person may operate … 为否定主句，no 修饰主语 person，谓语仍用肯定形式 may operate；a product 后接 that an AD applies to 定语从句，that 作 applies to 的宾语；except in accordance with the requirements of the AD 是介词短语作除外状语。

**要点**：No person may 是法规禁止句的典型写法，译「任何人不得」，其中不能再加 not（不能说 No person may not operate）。except in accordance with（除按照……外）是给禁止条款留出合法通道的固定句式，读 AD 时要特别注意这半句。

### ③ 名词化主语 + only 限定的情态被动

**原句**：The final determination of ADs applicable to a particular product can only be made by a thorough examination of the ADs and the product logbooks.

**拆解**：主语 The final determination，of ADs applicable to a particular product 是介词短语，其中 applicable to… 是形容词短语后置修饰 ADs；谓语 can only be made 为情态被动，only 限定唯一途径；by a thorough examination of the ADs and the product logbooks 是方式状语。

**要点**：applicable to a particular product 修饰 ADs 而不是 determination，应切分为「针对某产品的 AD 的最终判定」。only 放在情态动词之后、实义动词之前（can only be made），同类还有 can only be determined by。名词化主语（determination）让句子更书面，但读者要自己把动作还原成「判定」。
