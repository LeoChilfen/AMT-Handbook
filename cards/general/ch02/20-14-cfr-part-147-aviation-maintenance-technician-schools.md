# 14 CFR Part 147—Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools

> 通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 147—Aviation Maintenance Technician Schools · 原文 PDF 第 63 页

## 1. 原文

Schools Title 14 CFR part 147 defines the requirements for obtaining a maintenance training certificate. This certificate may be for either airframe, powerplant, or a combination of the two. The minimum number of curriculum hours for conducting either airframe or powerplant training independently is 1,150.

If both A&P ratings are offered, the combined total curriculum hours are 1,900. This is because of the 1,150 hours specified to obtain either the airframe or the powerplant rating, 400 hours are devoted to general studies. Only one set of general studies hours is applicable to the combined total. Therefore, 400 hours can be subtracted from the implied total of 2,300 hours (1,150 × 2) to obtain the reduced figure of 1,900 hours.

Requirements are detailed as follows:

• Appendix A—Curriculum Requirements • Appendix B—General Curriculum Subjects • Appendix C—Airframe Curricular Subjects • Appendix D—Powerplant Curriculum Subjects

## 2. 中文翻译

学校（Schools）。14 CFR part 147 规定了取得维修培训合格证的要求。该合格证可以是机体、动力装置，或二者的组合。单独开设机体或动力装置培训所需的最低课程学时数为 1,150。

若同时开设 A&P 两项等级，合计课程学时为 1,900。这是因为在取得机体或动力装置等级所规定的 1,150 学时中，有 400 学时用于通用课程。合计学时只适用一套通用课程学时。因此，可从推算总量 2,300 学时（1,150 × 2）中减去 400 学时，得到 1,900 学时这一缩减后的数字。

各项要求具体规定如下：

• 附录 A——课程要求 • 附录 B——通用课程科目 • 附录 C——机体课程科目 • 附录 D——动力装置课程科目

## 4. 语法解析

### ① The minimum number of curriculum hours for conducting … is 1,150（介词 + 动名词作后置定语；主谓一致看中心词）

**原句**：The minimum number of curriculum hours for conducting either airframe or powerplant training independently is 1,150.

**拆解**：主语 The minimum number of curriculum hours；for conducting either airframe or powerplant training independently 为介词 + 动名词短语作后置定语，说明「什么的学时」；谓语 is，表语 1,150。

**要点**：主语的中心词是 number（单数），谓语用 is，不受 of curriculum hours 的影响——这是「数量词组 + of + 复数名词」作主语时的固定规则。for + 动名词作后置定语在规章里很常见，等于 for the conduct of。either A or B 在此表示二者之一，与后文 both 相对。

### ② Only one set of general studies hours is applicable to the combined total（主谓一致 + be applicable to）

**原句**：Only one set of general studies hours is applicable to the combined total.

**拆解**：主语 one set of general studies hours，中心词 set 为单数，故谓语用 is；表语 applicable，后接介词 to 引出适用对象 the combined total。

**要点**：one set of … 作主语时谓语取单数，与 hours 的复数形式无关。be applicable to 是「适用于」，比 apply to 更正式，法规文本常用；注意 applicable 后只能接 to，不能接 for。

### ③ 400 hours can be subtracted from the implied total … to obtain the reduced figure of 1,900 hours（情态被动 + 不定式表目的／结果）

**原句**：Therefore, 400 hours can be subtracted from the implied total of 2,300 hours (1,150 × 2) to obtain the reduced figure of 1,900 hours.

**拆解**：主句 400 hours can be subtracted from the implied total of 2,300 hours，为「can be + 过去分词」的情态被动；(1,150 × 2) 是插入的同位说明；to obtain the reduced figure of 1,900 hours 为不定式短语作目的状语。

**要点**：subtract A from B 意为「从 B 中减去 A」，不能颠倒；被动式 can be subtracted from 译作「可从……中减去」。to obtain … 表目的兼结果，中文用「从而得到」最顺。这类算式推导用被动，是因为动作的实施者（编写者）并不重要。
