# 14 CFR Part 65—Certification: Airmen Other Than Flight Crewmembers

> 通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 65—Certification: Airmen Other Than Flight Crewmembers · 原文 PDF 第 60 页

## 1. 原文

Pilots, flight instructors, and ground instructors are certificated under 14 CFR part 61. Flight crew other than pilots are certificated under 14 CFR part 63. However, many other people are also required to be certificated by the FAA for the U.S. aviation fleet to operate smoothly and efficiently. Title 14 CFR part 65 addresses many of those other people.

• Subpart B—Air Traffic Control Tower Operators • Subpart C—Aircraft Dispatchers • Subpart D—Mechanics • Subpart E—Repairmen • Subpart F—Parachute Riggers A more detailed discussion of this chapter with a special emphasis on mechanics is included in Chapter 15, The Mechanic Certificate.

Note: SFAR 100-2. Relief for U.S. Military and Civilian Personnel who are assigned outside the United States in support of U.S. Armed Forces Operations is a good example of the specific nature and limited time frame that are part of a SFAR.

## 2. 中文翻译

驾驶员、飞行教员和地面教员按 14 CFR part 61 取得证照。驾驶员以外的飞行机组成员按 14 CFR part 63 取得证照。然而，为使美国航空器机队平稳、高效地运行，还有许多其他人员同样须由 FAA 发证。14 CFR part 65 涉及的正是其中许多这类人员。

• Subpart B——空中交通管制塔台管制员 • Subpart C——航空器签派员 • Subpart D——维修人员（Mechanics） • Subpart E——修理人员（Repairmen） • Subpart F——降落伞折叠员（Parachute Riggers） 本章更详细的讨论——特别是对维修人员的重点讨论——见第 15 章《维修人员执照》（The Mechanic Certificate）。

注：SFAR 100-2《对因支援美国武装部队行动而派驻美国境外的美国军人及文职人员的豁免》就是一个很好的例子，说明 SFAR 具有针对特定对象、限定有效期的特点。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① are also required to be certificated by the FAA for the U.S. aviation fleet to operate（be required to do + for … to do 复合结构）

**原句**：However, many other people are also required to be certificated by the FAA for the U.S. aviation fleet to operate smoothly and efficiently.

**拆解**：主句被动 are required to be certificated（被要求取得证照），by the FAA 为施动者；for the U.S. aviation fleet to operate smoothly and efficiently 是 for + 名词 + 不定式的复合结构，作目的状语。

**要点**：be required to do 是规章高频句式，强调「被规定要求」，与 must 的差别在于前者点明要求来自规章；注意 to be certificated 是不定式的被动式，与前面的 are required 构成两次被动。for sb./sth. to do 在此表目的，译作「为使……运行」。

### ② Flight crew other than pilots are certificated under 14 CFR part 63（other than 后置定语 + 主谓一致）

**原句**：Flight crew other than pilots are certificated under 14 CFR part 63.

**拆解**：主语 Flight crew other than pilots，其中 other than pilots 是后置定语（= who are not pilots）；谓语 are certificated 为被动，用复数；under 14 CFR part 63 表依据。

**要点**：other than 等于 except／apart from，作后置定语时译作「……以外的」；它不能改写成 other than 加从句。crew 是集合名词，此处按成员个体处理，接复数谓语。under part XX 是「依据某部规章」的固定说法，与 in accordance with 同义。

### ③ the specific nature and limited time frame that are part of a SFAR（定语从句与并列先行词的一致）

**原句**：Relief for U.S. Military and Civilian Personnel who are assigned outside the United States in support of U.S. Armed Forces Operations is a good example of the specific nature and limited time frame that are part of a SFAR.

**拆解**：主语 Relief，后接 for 短语和 who are assigned … 定语从句；谓语 is，表语 a good example；of 后接并列的 the specific nature and limited time frame，由 that are part of a SFAR 修饰，故从句用复数 are。

**要点**：定语从句的谓语看先行词的数量——先行词是并列的两项，所以用 are；若只修饰 time frame 就该用 is，这是判断句子关系的重要线索。a good example of 后接名词短语，说明「……的一个好例子」；SFAR 段与 part 65 的执照类别没有直接对应关系，它是用来举例说明「特别规章」这种立法形式。
