# Structural Repair Manual (SRM)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Structural Repair Manual (SRM) · 原文 PDF 第 83 页

## 1. 原文

As the name implies, this manual carries detail information for the technician concerning an aircraft’s primary and secondary structure, criteria for evaluating the severity of the detected damage, determining the feasibility of a repair, and alignment/inspection information. This manual is usually a separate manual for large aircraft. On small aircraft, this information is often included in the AMM.

## 2. 中文翻译

顾名思义，本手册为技师提供关于航空器一次结构与二次结构的详细资料，包括评定所发现损伤严重程度的准则、判定修理可行性的方法，以及校装／检查信息。对大型航空器而言，本手册通常是独立的一册。在小型航空器上，这些信息往往包含在 AMM 之中。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① As the name implies …（as 引导的方式状语从句）

**原句**：As the name implies, this manual carries detail information for the technician concerning an aircraft’s primary and secondary structure, criteria for evaluating the severity of the detected damage, determining the feasibility of a repair, and alignment/inspection information.

**拆解**：As the name implies 是方式状语从句，as 在从句中作 implies 的宾语（= the name implies this）；主句 this manual carries detail information for the technician；concerning … 是介词短语作后置定语，一直管到句末。

**要点**：as the name implies 是技术写作的固定套语，as 不是「当……时」，而是「正如」。主句用一般现在时陈述手册的常备功能；句子的重心在 carries information，后面全是给 information 划范围的补充成分，翻译时可先译主干再补细节。

### ② criteria for evaluating …, determining …（介词后接动名词的平行结构）

**原句**：concerning an aircraft’s primary and secondary structure, criteria for evaluating the severity of the detected damage, determining the feasibility of a repair, and alignment/inspection information.

**拆解**：concerning 后接四个并列项：名词短语「一次与二次结构」、名词短语 criteria for evaluating …、动名词短语 determining …、名词短语 alignment/inspection information；for evaluating 中 for 是介词，故用动名词。

**要点**：四项并列靠逗号和收尾的 and 串联，读时先数清项数再逐项对应译文。第二项与第三项都以「评定」为动作核心，但一项用名词 criteria + for + -ing，另一项直接用 -ing 与前面并列，中文都可译成「……的准则」「判定……」。detected 是过去分词作前置定语（the detected damage = 被发现的损伤）。

### ③ On small aircraft, this information is often included in the AMM（状语前置 + 被动语态）

**原句**：On small aircraft, this information is often included in the AMM.

**拆解**：On small aircraft 是地点状语，置于句首；主干 this information is often included in the AMM，为一般现在时被动；often 是频率副词，位于 be 之后。

**要点**：把 On small aircraft 提到句首，是为了与上句的 large aircraft 形成对照，中文同样宜用「在小型航空器上」起句。用被动是因为施动者是手册编写者，属于不言自明、无需点明的信息；is included 不能改成 includes（那样主语就变成信息本身了）。
