# on U.S. aeronautical products by certain Canadian persons

> 通用 Ch 02 · on U.S. aeronautical products by certain Canadian persons · 原文 PDF 第 68 页

## 1. 原文

This section was significantly revised in 2005, as the result of a Bilateral Aviation Safety Agreement (BASA) between the United States and Canada. This section of 14 CFR part 43 defines some terms and gives specific limitations as to what an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME is the Canadian equivalent to the U.S. A&P) may do to maintain U.S.-registered aircraft located in Canada. It also provides similar limitations for an Approved Maintenance Organization. (AMO is the Canadian equivalent to the U.S.- certified repair stations.)

## 2. 中文翻译

本条于 2005 年因美国与加拿大之间的一项《双边航空安全协定》（BASA）而作了重大修订。14 CFR 第 43 部的这一条界定了一些术语，并就在加拿大的美国注册航空器上，加拿大航空维修工程师（AME，相当于美国的 A&P）可以实施哪些维修工作，规定了具体限制。该条还对经批准的维修组织（AMO，相当于美国的持证维修站）作出了类似的限制规定。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 一般过去时被动 + as the result of

**原句**：This section was significantly revised in 2005, as the result of a Bilateral Aviation Safety Agreement (BASA) between the United States and Canada.

**拆解**：主语 This section，谓语 was significantly revised 为一般过去时被动，in 2005 为时间状语；as the result of … 为原因状语，between the United States and Canada 是介词短语修饰 a Bilateral Aviation Safety Agreement。

**要点**：as the result of（由于……的结果）比 because of 更书面，常用于说明修订、事故等的成因。in 2005 用 in 而不用 on，因为年份前用 in；若精确到日则用 on。全句被动是因为“本条被修订”，施动者（两国当局）不必点明。

### ② 并列谓语 + as to + what 引导的宾语从句

**原句**：This section of 14 CFR part 43 defines some terms and gives specific limitations as to what an Aircraft Maintenance Engineer (AME is the Canadian equivalent to the U.S. A&P) may do to maintain U.S.-registered aircraft located in Canada.

**拆解**：主语 This section of 14 CFR part 43，谓语为并列的 defines some terms 与 gives specific limitations；as to 引出介词宾语从句 what … may do；从句内 may do 后接不定式 to maintain 表目的；U.S.-registered aircraft located in Canada 中 located 为过去分词后置定语；括号内为插入说明。

**要点**：as to + 从句意为“关于……”，比 about 更正式，法规中常用来引出“限制的具体内容”。括号插入语打断了 what 从句，阅读时先跳过括号找 may do。located in Canada 是过去分词短语作后置定语，译作“位于加拿大的”。

### ③ 括号插入语与 equivalent to 的搭配

**原句**：(AMO is the Canadian equivalent to the U.S.- certified repair stations.)

**拆解**：括号内为独立完整句，主语 AMO，系动词 is，表语 the Canadian equivalent，to the U.S.-certified repair stations 为介词短语限定“与谁对应”；U.S.-certified 是“缩写 + 过去分词”构成的复合形容词。

**要点**：the equivalent to / of sth. 意为“与……相当的事物”，是跨体系比对的固定说法；此处用 to，与 similar to 同源。技术文本常以括号补出“甲国的 X 相当于乙国的 Y”，翻译时保留括号形式即可，不必改写为正文。
