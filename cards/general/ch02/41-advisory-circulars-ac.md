# Advisory Circulars (AC)

## 1. 原文

AC refers to a type of publication offered by the FAA to provide guidance for compliance with airworthiness regulations. They provide guidance such as methods, procedures, and practices acceptable to the Administrator for complying with regulations. ACs may also contain explanations of regulations, other guidance material, best practices, or information useful to the aviation community.

They do not create or change a regulatory requirement. The AC system became effective in 1962. It provides a single, uniform, agency-wide system that the FAA uses to deliver advisory material to FAA customers, industry, the aviation community, and the public.

Unless incorporated into a regulation by reference, the content of ACs are not binding on the public. ACs are issued in a numbered-subject system corresponding to the subject areas of the FARs (14 CFR, Chapter 1, Federal Aviation Administration) and Chapter 3, Commercial Space Transportation, Federal Aviation Administration, Department of Transportation, Parts 400–450. An AC is issued to provide guidance and information in a designated subject area or to show a method acceptable to the Administrator for complying with a related federal aviation regulation.

## 2. 中文翻译

咨询通告（AC）是 FAA 提供的一类出版物，用于就如何符合适航规章提供指导。这类通告给出的指导包括局长可接受的、用以符合规章的方法、程序和做法。AC 还可能包含对规章的解释、其他指导材料、最佳做法，或对航空界有用的信息。

它们不创设、也不改变任何规章要求。AC 体系自 1962 年起生效。它提供一套单一、统一、覆盖全机构的体系，FAA 用以向 FAA 服务对象、业界、航空界和公众发布咨询性材料。

除通过引用并入某项规章的情况外，AC 的内容对公众不具有约束力。AC 按与联邦航空条例（即 14 CFR 第 1 章联邦航空局，以及第 3 章商业航天运输、联邦航空局、运输部、第 400–450 部）的主题领域相对应的编号—主题体系发布。发布 AC 的目的，是就某一指定主题领域提供指导与信息，或表明局长可接受的、符合相关联邦航空规章的某种方法。

由于与规章关系密切，AC 按与 CFR 各主题领域相对应的编号体系编排。在某些系列中，连续编号可能出现缺号。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Unless incorporated into a regulation by reference（unless 引导的省略式条件状语）

**原句**：Unless incorporated into a regulation by reference, the content of ACs are not binding on the public.

**拆解**：Unless incorporated into a regulation by reference 是省略了主语和 be 动词的条件状语（完整形式为 Unless it is incorporated…），因从句主语与主句主语不一致而按习惯省略 it is；主句 the content of ACs are not binding on the public 为系表结构，binding 是形容词作表语。

**要点**：unless 相当于 if not，后接过去分词时表示「除非被……」，是法规文本规定例外的常用开头，译作「除……外」。注意本句内容与主句主语并不相同（被并入的是 AC，不是 content），这种「悬垂省略」在手册中常见，理解时按语义补回即可，抄录原文时不要改动。

### ② They do not create or change a regulatory requirement（否定词 + or 的并列谓语）

**原句**：They do not create or change a regulatory requirement.

**拆解**：主语 They 回指 ACs；谓语为 do not + create or change 的否定并列形式；宾语 a regulatory requirement 用不定冠词表单数泛指，指任何一项规章要求。

**要点**：否定词 not 否定的范围覆盖 or 连接的两个动词，等于 neither create nor change，译作「既不创设也不改变」；若误读为「不创设、但会改变」，就把 AC 的性质搞反了。技术文本中这种「not … or …」结构一律按全否定理解。

### ③ Because of their close relationship to the regulations（原因状语 + 定语从句）

**原句**：Because of their close relationship to the regulations, ACs are arranged in a numbered system that corresponds to the subject areas of the CFRs.

**拆解**：Because of their close relationship to the regulations 是介词短语作原因状语；主句 ACs are arranged in a numbered system 为被动；that corresponds to the subject areas of the CFRs 是定语从句，that 在从句中作主语，谓语 corresponds 用单数。

**要点**：because of 后只接名词或名词短语，because 后才接从句——写成 because of they are closely related 是典型错误。correspond to 表示「与……对应」，correspond with 才是「与……通信」，机务文件中两者都出现，不可混用。
