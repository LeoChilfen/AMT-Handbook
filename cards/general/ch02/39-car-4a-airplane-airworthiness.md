# CAR 4a—Airplane Airworthiness

> 通用 Ch 02 · CAR 4a—Airplane Airworthiness · 原文 PDF 第 73 页

## 1. 原文

This regulation was originated in 1936 and last amended on December 15, 1952. The subparts included in this regulation are:

• A—Airworthiness Requirements • B—Definitions • C—Structural Loading Conditions, General Structural Requirements • D—Proof of Structure • E—Detail Design and Construction • F—Equipment • G—Powerplant Installation • H—Performance • I—Miscellaneous Requirements Initially, this regulation was the basis for establishing the design requirements for virtually all produced aircraft in the 1930s, 1940s, and 1950s. Eventually CAR 3 evolved as the regulatory material specific to small aircraft, and CAR 4a and b focused on regulatory requirements for large aircraft.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 句首副词 + 双重 for 短语

**原句**
Initially, this regulation was the basis for establishing the design requirements for virtually all produced aircraft in the 1930s, 1940s, and 1950s.

**拆解**
Initially 为句子副词，提示时间起点；主干 was the basis for establishing …，establishing 的宾语为 design requirements，再以 for virtually all produced aircraft 与 in the 1930s, 1940s, and 1950s 两层限定；produced 为过去分词作定语。

**要点**
两个 for 功能不同，前者接动名词「作为……的基础」，后者接名词「对……的要求」，译文要分开；virtually 表「几乎」，不可译「实际上」；年代加 s 表「那个年代」，保留 1930s 写法。

### ② evolve as + 大小机型对照并列

**原句**
Eventually CAR 3 evolved as the regulatory material specific to small aircraft, and CAR 4a and b focused on regulatory requirements for large aircraft.

**拆解**
and 连接两个并列分句：第一句 CAR 3 evolved as the regulatory material specific to small aircraft，as 短语作主语补足语，specific to 为后置形容词短语；第二句 CAR 4a and b focused on regulatory requirements for large aircraft。

**要点**
evolve as 译「逐渐演变为」，不译「进化」；specific to 是「专用于……的」，修饰 material 而非 small aircraft；两句构成小型机与大型机的对照，译文保留对称。

### ③ but 后的省略结构

**原句**
For example, The Cessna 140 was certified as a landplane under CAR 3, but under CAR 4a as a ski-plane or seaplane.

**拆解**
For example 引出例证；主句 The Cessna 140 was certified as a landplane under CAR 3 为被动；but 后是省略结构，补全为 but (it) was certified under CAR 4a as a ski-plane or seaplane。

**要点**
but 后省略了主语与 was certified，译文须补全「但作为……是按 CAR 4a 审定的」；as a landplane 与 as a ski-plane or seaplane 是「按何种类别取证」，不是「像」；under CAR 3 指「依据 CAR 3」，与 under 表方位区分。
