## 1. 原文

During the last 25 years, the field of aviation maintenance has seen a significant change in the documentation requirements for aircraft and related parts. Nowhere is that change seen as revolutionary as the introduction of electronic data and record retention. Just as the arrival of the personal computer placed the possibility of the power and versatility of a computer in the hands of the average person, it made it available to the maintenance technician. Initially some technicians developed their own programs for listing data (TCDS, AD notes, and so forth), but soon commercially available programs were developed. Basically, these were developed by either one of the following two groups:

1. Computer literate persons who felt the aviation industry could benefit from the computer 2. Aviation professionals who felt the aviation industry must benefit from the computer Some of those initial programs were either not very user friendly (if developed by computer wizards) or not “very sophisticated” (if developed by the maintenance technician). Many of these programs offer a combination of the data research, such as ADs, SBs, STCs, and TCDSs, required to conduct proper maintenance, inspections, and data recording (logbook entries, AD compliance history, length of component time in service, and so forth) desired to improve the efficiency of the technician.

Although some large shops and certified repair stations may have a separate group of people responsible for “records and research,” the professional maintenance technician must be aware of the benefits of these systems. Some factors to consider when reviewing a system are:

• What is the typical size of the aircraft that maintenance is being done on? (i.e., less than 12,500 pounds, more than 12,000? Mixed?)

• Does the program have built-in templates for the aircraft being worked on?

• What FAA forms (if any) are available in the program?

• Does it have a user-friendly template to enter the data for the form or must data be directly entered onto the form?

• Can it calculate weight and balance data?

• Does it have adequate word search capabilities?

• Is it networkable?

• Are the updates sent via U.S. mail or downloaded from the Internet?

• What is the maximum number of aircraft that the system can handle?

• Can the system handle both single- and multi-engine aircraft? Fixed and rotary wing? Piston and jet?

• Can an item removed from an aircraft be tracked?

• Is the data from this system exportable to other electronic formats?

• Can it forecast items due for maintenance or inspection?

Since no program can be considered the best, the technician must learn all they can about the numerous systems that exist.

Exposure to the pros and cons of these different systems can be one of the benefits of attending various trade shows, maintenance seminars, or IA renewal sessions. Continuous learning and personal improvement is the goal of every professional maintenance technician.

尽管一些大型维修厂和持证修理站可能设有专门的「记录与研究」小组，专业维修技师仍必须了解这类系统的益处。评估一套系统时可以考虑以下因素：

• 所维修航空器的典型尺寸是多大？（即小于 12,500 磅，还是大于 12,000 磅？还是兼而有之？）

• 该程序是否为所修的航空器内置了模板？

• 程序中可提供哪些 FAA 表格（如有）？

• 它是否有友好的模板用于录入表格数据，还是必须直接在表格上录入数据？

• 它能计算重量与平衡数据吗？

• 它具备足够的文字检索能力吗？

• 它可以联网吗？

• 更新是通过美国邮政寄送，还是从互联网下载？

• 该系统最多能管理多少架航空器？

• 系统能否同时处理单发与多发航空器？固定翼与旋翼？活塞式与喷气式？

• 能否追踪从航空器上拆下的某一部件？

• 该系统的数据能导出为其他电子格式吗？

• 它能预测即将到期的维修或检查项目吗？

由于没有哪套程序可以被认定为最好，技师必须尽可能多地了解现有的众多系统。

接触这些不同系统的优缺点，可以是参加各类行业展会、维修研讨会或授权检查员（IA）续期培训的收获之一。持续学习与自我提升，是每一位专业维修技师的目标。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Nowhere 置于句首引起部分倒装

**原句**：Nowhere is that change seen as revolutionary as the introduction of electronic data and record retention.

**拆解**：否定副词 Nowhere 放在句首，引起部分倒装，把 be 动词 is 提到主语 that change 之前；seen as revolutionary as … 是 as … as 同级比较，第二个 as 后接名词短语 the introduction of …。

**要点**：never、seldom、nowhere、not only 等否定或半否定词居首时必须倒装，这是正式书面语的标志，写作中不宜随意模仿。as revolutionary as 意为「像……一样具有革命性」，是比较而非比喻。

### ② either … or … + if 省略结构

**原句**：Some of those initial programs were either not very user friendly (if developed by computer wizards) or not “very sophisticated” (if developed by the maintenance technician).

**拆解**：either … or … 连接两个并列的表语 not very user friendly 与 not “very sophisticated”；每个表语后的括号是 if 引导的条件状语从句的省略形式，完整为 if they were developed by …，主语与 be 动词一并省略。

**要点**：if + 过去分词是「如果是（由）……（做的）」的压缩写法，比从句简洁，技术文本常见。注意句中有两处否定（not…or not…），含义是「两种情况各有一处不足」，不是双重否定。

### ③ Since 引导原因状语从句 + 情态被动

**原句**：Since no program can be considered the best, the technician must learn all they can about the numerous systems that exist.

**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，从句用情态被动 can be considered；主句 the technician must learn all they can about …；that exist 是定语从句修饰 the numerous systems。

**要点**：since 表原因时相当于 because，但语气更轻、更偏书面；表时间时才译「自从」，这里不能译成时间。all they can 后省略了 learn，是「尽其所能了解」的固定说法。
