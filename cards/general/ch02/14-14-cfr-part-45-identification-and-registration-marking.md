## 1. 原文

Marking Title 14 of the CFR part 45 includes the requirements for the identification of aircraft, engines, propellers, certain replacement and modification parts, and the nationality and registration marking required on U.S.-registered aircraft. All type-certificated products must have the following information on a fireproof dataplate or similar approved fireproof method.

1. Builder’s name 2. Model designation 3. Builder’s serial number 4. TC number (if any)

5. Production certificate number (if any)

6. For aircraft engines, the established rating 7. Reference to compliance or exemption to 14 CFR Part 34, Fuel Venting and Exhaust Emission Requirements for Turbine Engine Powered Airplanes 8. Any other information that the FAA determines to be appropriate Replacement and modification parts are produced in accordance with a Parts Manufacturer Approvals (PMA) (14 CFR part 21, section 21.303) and must have the following information permanently and legibly marked:

1. The letters “FAA-PMA” 2. The name, symbol, or trademark of the holder of the PMA 3. The part number 4. The name and model designation for each type certificated product it can be installed on If a part has a specified replacement time, inspection interval, or other related procedure specification in the maintenance manual or ICA, that part must have a part number and a serial number (or the equivalent of each).

The manufacturer of a life-limited part must either provide marking instructions for that part, or state that the part cannot be marked without a compromise to its integrity. Exceptions are made for the identification of parts that are too small to be practical to mark the required data.

Nationality and registration marks (commonly known as the N-number for U.S.-registered aircraft) can vary in size, depending on the year that the aircraft was built and whether or not the aircraft has been repainted. ## 4. 语法解析

### ① must either provide …, or state that …（either … or … 连接两个谓语）

**原句**：The manufacturer of a life-limited part must either provide marking instructions for that part, or state that the part cannot be marked without a compromise to its integrity.

**拆解**：主语 The manufacturer of a life-limited part；情态动词 must 后接 either … or … 连接的并列谓语 provide … 与 state that …；that 从句内 the part cannot be marked 为被动，without a compromise to its integrity 为条件状语（「若不以损害完整性为代价」）。

**要点**：either A or B 的 A、B 须词性一致，本句都是动词原形。without + 名词在此表条件，cannot be marked without a compromise 应译作「一旦加标识就必然损害完整性」，而不是「没有损害」。state that 后接完整从句，是规章里「声明」的固定说法。

### ② must have the following information permanently and legibly marked（have sth. done 结构）

**原句**：Replacement and modification parts are produced in accordance with a Parts Manufacturer Approvals (PMA) (14 CFR part 21, section 21.303) and must have the following information permanently and legibly marked:

**拆解**：两个并列谓语——前者被动 are produced in accordance with …（「按规定生产」），后者 have + 宾语 the following information + 过去分词 marked，即 have sth. done；permanently and legibly 为副词，修饰 marked。

**要点**：have sth. done 表示「使某物处于（被处理过的）状态」，marked 是过去分词作宾补，不能写成 have … marking。in accordance with 是「按照（规定）」，法规文本高频，等于 under／pursuant to。副词 permanently and legibly 放在宾补后，说明标注的方式与质量要求。

### ③ If a part has a specified replacement time …, that part must have a part number and a serial number（条件句 + 并列宾语 + 代词回指）

**原句**：If a part has a specified replacement time, inspection interval, or other related procedure specification in the maintenance manual or ICA, that part must have a part number and a serial number (or the equivalent of each).

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，谓语 has 后接三个并列宾语（replacement time／inspection interval／procedure specification），in the maintenance manual or ICA 为地点状语；主句 that part must have … 中 that 回指前文的 a part，must 表强制。

**要点**：规章常用「同一名词 + that」回指条件从句里的对象（a part … that part），使逻辑指向明确。specification 在此指「规范要求」而非「规格书」。or the equivalent of each 中的 each 指件号和序号两者，译作「或二者的等效标识」。
