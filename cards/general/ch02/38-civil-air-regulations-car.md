# Civil Air Regulations (CAR)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Civil Air Regulations (CAR) · 原文 PDF 第 73 页

## 1. 原文

Prior to 1926, access to flying was uncontrolled. No licensing or certification was required. By the middle of the 1920s, it became obvious that unregulated private and commercial flying was dangerous. There was a growing awareness and acceptance that regulation could improve safety and encourage growth in aviation. Therefore in 1926, the aviation industry requested Congress to enact federal legislation to regulate civil aviation. Thus, the Air Commerce Act of 1926 provided for the:

1. Establishment of airways.

2. Development of aviation aids.

3. Investigation of aviation accidents.

4. Licensing of pilots.

5. Certification of aircraft.

The Civil Air Regulations (CARs) were part of the original certification basis for aircraft first certified in the 1940s, 1950s, and 1960s by the Civil Aviation Authority (CAA).

Therefore, the CARs may still be needed as a reference for these older aircraft or as a standard for minor changes to older aircraft designs. [Figure 2-11]

CAR 3—Airplane Airworthiness—Normal, Utility, Aerobatic, and Restricted Purpose Categories As the name implies, this specific regulation is the basis for the current 14 CFR part 23 regulation [ Figure 2-1]. It has the following subpart categories:

• A—Airworthiness Requirements • B—Flight Requirements—General • C—Strength Requirements—General • D—Design and Construction—General • E—Powerplant Installations—Reciprocating Engines • F—Equipment Some examples of CAR 3 aircraft are Piper PA 22, PA 28, PA 32, and Cessna 182, 195, and 310.

Note: The “CAR” acronym actually has two interpretations:

Civil Air Regulations and Canadian Aviation Regulations.

The technician must clearly understand the difference and recognize when one or the other is appropriate.

## 2. 中文翻译

1926 年以前，从事飞行不受管制，也不需要任何执照或合格证。到 20 世纪 20 年代中期，人们已经明显看出，不受管制的私人与商业飞行是危险的。一种认识逐渐形成并被接受：法规能够提高安全性，并促进航空业发展。因此，1926 年航空业界请求国会制定联邦立法来管理民用航空。于是，1926 年《航空商务法》规定了下述事项：

1. 建立航路。

2. 发展航空助航设施。

3. 调查航空事故。

4. 对驾驶员颁发执照。

5. 对航空器进行合格审定。

民用航空条例（CAR）是 20 世纪 40、50、60 年代由民用航空局（CAA）首次审定的航空器原始审定基础的一部分。

因此，对于这些较老的航空器，CAR 仍可能需要作为参考资料，或作为对较老航空器设计进行轻微更改的标准。

CAR 3——飞机适航性——正常类、实用类、特技类和限用类 顾名思义，这部具体法规是现行 14 CFR 第 23 部规章的基础。它包含下列分部类别：

• A——适航要求 • B——飞行要求——总则 • C——强度要求——总则 • D——设计与构造——总则 • E——动力装置安装——往复式发动机 • F——设备 属于 CAR 3 的航空器实例有派珀 PA 22、PA 28、PA 32，以及塞斯纳 182、195 和 310。

注：「CAR」这一缩写实际上有两种含义：

民用航空条例，以及加拿大航空条例。

维修技师必须清楚理解二者的区别，并能辨认在何种情况下应当用其中的哪一个。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① it became obvious that …（形式主语 + 主语从句）

**原句**
By the middle of the 1920s, it became obvious that unregulated private and commercial flying was dangerous.

**拆解**
By the middle of the 1920s 为时间状语；it 是形式主语，真正主语为 that 从句；表语 obvious，became 表状态转变。

**要点**
it 不译「它」，须把 that 从句提前作主语：「到 20 世纪 20 年代中期，人们已明显看到……」；by + 时间点表「到……时为止」，常与完成意义相配；unregulated 是过去分词作定语「未受管理的」，与 private and commercial 一并修饰 flying。

### ② awareness and acceptance 的同位语从句

**原句**
There was a growing awareness and acceptance that regulation could improve safety and encourage growth in aviation.

**拆解**
There was 存在句，主语为 a growing awareness and acceptance（两个抽象名词共用冠词 a）；that 引导同位语从句，说明 awareness and acceptance 的具体内容；从句谓语 could improve … and encourage …。

**要点**
that 从句不是定语从句，而是解释前面两个抽象名词的内容，译「……的意识与认同」；growing 是现在分词作定语「日益增强的」；could 在此表「有可能」，不是过去的能力。

### ③ 并列谓语 + when 宾语从句

**原句**
The technician must clearly understand the difference and recognize when one or the other is appropriate.

**拆解**
主语 The technician，谓语为 must clearly understand 与 (must) recognize 两个并列动词短语；understand 的宾语是 the difference，recognize 的宾语为 when 引导的宾语从句；one or the other 指两种规章中的任一种。

**要点**
第二个动词 recognize 前省略了 must，译文要补出「必须」；when 从句在此作 recognize 的宾语，译「何时该用哪一种」，不要当时间状语；one or the other 是固定搭配「两者之一」，需回指上文的两套规章。
