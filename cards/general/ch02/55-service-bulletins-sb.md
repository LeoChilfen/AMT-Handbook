# Service Bulletins (SB)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Service Bulletins (SB) · 原文 PDF 第 83 页

## 1. 原文

Throughout the life of a product (whether TC’d or not), manufacturing defects, changes in service, or design improvements often occur. When that happens, the OEM frequently uses an SB to distribute the information to the operator of the aircraft. SBs are good information and should be strongly considered by the owner for implementation to the aircraft. However, SBs are not required unless they are referred to in an AD note or if compliance is required as a part of the authorized inspection program. Refer to section 14 CFR part 39, 39.27.

## 2. 中文翻译

在产品整个寿命期内（无论是否取得 TC），制造缺陷、使用中的变化或设计改进时常出现。一旦出现这类情况，OEM 往往通过服务通告（SB）把信息分发给航空器运营人。SB 是良好的信息，机主应认真考虑在航空器上执行。但是，除非在 AD 通告中被引用，或作为经批准的检查大纲的一部分被要求遵守，否则 SB 并非必须执行。参见 14 CFR 第 39 部第 39.27 节。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① whether TC’d or not（让步状语的省略式）

**原句**：Throughout the life of a product (whether TC’d or not), manufacturing defects, changes in service, or design improvements often occur.

**拆解**：Throughout the life of a product 是时间状语；括号内 whether TC’d or not 是省略的让步状语（= whether it is TC’d or not）；主干是三个并列主语 manufacturing defects, changes in service, or design improvements + 副词 often + 不及物动词 occur。

**要点**：whether … or not 在省略主语和 be 时可只留形容词或分词，是技术文本压缩行文的常见手段。occur 是不及物动词，没有被动式（不能写 is occurred），也常被误拼成 occure；occurs 作谓语时用单数，因为主语是并列整体。

### ② are not required unless … or if …（被动否定 + 两个条件从句）

**原句**：However, SBs are not required unless they are referred to in an AD note or if compliance is required as a part of the authorized inspection program.

**拆解**：主句 SBs are not required 为被动否定；unless … 与 or if … 是两个并列的条件从句；they 指 SBs；are referred to 为固定被动搭配，介词 to 不可省。

**要点**：unless 意为 if not，但比 if not 紧凑，且后面不再加 not（不写 unless they are not referred to）。be referred to 有两层意思：「被提及」与「被引用」，此处指 SB 被 AD 通告引用。compliance is required 又一次用被动，读者自然理解为「由规章要求」。

### ③ uses an SB to distribute … to the operator（不定式作目的状语 + 双宾搭配）

**原句**：When that happens, the OEM frequently uses an SB to distribute the information to the operator of the aircraft.

**拆解**：When that happens 是时间状语从句，that 指代上句「缺陷、变化或改进出现」这件事；主句 the OEM frequently uses an SB；to distribute the information to the operator 是不定式短语作目的状语；distribute sth to sb 为固定搭配。

**要点**：that 在此是代词而非连词，不能与 when 混为一谈。frequently 说明用 SB 分发信息是惯例而非强制。distribute … to 不能换成 distribute … for；information 是不可数名词，前面不加 a，复数为 information 本身。

### ④ should be strongly considered … for implementation（情态被动 + 名词化）

**原句**：SBs are good information and should be strongly considered by the owner for implementation to the aircraft.

**拆解**：两个并列谓语：are good information（系表）与 should be strongly considered（情态动词 + 被动）；by the owner 点出施动者；for implementation to the aircraft 是介词短语，说明「考虑」的目的指向。

**要点**：implementation 是动词 implement 的名词化形式，技术文本偏爱名词化以压缩句子，中文要还原成动词「在航空器上执行」。consider 后接名词或动名词（consider doing），不接不定式；此处因改为被动，动作改为 for + 名词。strongly 修饰 considered，位置在 be 与过去分词之间。
