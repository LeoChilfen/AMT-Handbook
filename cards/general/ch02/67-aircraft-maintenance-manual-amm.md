# Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) · 原文 PDF 第 94 页

## 1. 原文

Although these manuals do not require any FAA approval, the regulations do require that the manual be developed in accordance with industry standards. This ASTM sets that standard by requiring:

• General specifications to be listed, include capacities, servicing, lubrication, and ground handling • An inspection checklist for the annual condition or 100-hour inspection • A description of and the instructions for the maintenance, repair, and overhaul of the LSA engine • A description of and the instructions for the maintenance, repair, and alteration of the aircraft’s primary structure Other items that maintenance procedures must be provided for are:

• Propeller • Utility system • Instruments and avionics • Electrical system • Structural repair • Painting and coatings The Inspection, Repair, and Alterations section must specifically list any special tools and parts needed to complete the task, as well as the type of maintenance action (line, heavy, or overhaul) necessary to accomplish the activity.

Directly associated with that information is the requirement to specify the level of certification needed to do the job (i.e., LSA repairman, A&P, or repair station). The manual may refer to existing FAA ACs.

## 2. 中文翻译

尽管这些手册并不需要任何 FAA 批准，法规确实要求手册按行业标准编制。ASTM 通过以下要求来确立该标准：

• 须列出一般规范，包括容量、勤务、润滑和地面操作 • 年度状态检查或 100 小时检查的检查单 • LSA 发动机维修、修理与翻修的说明及其描述 • 航空器主结构维修、修理与改装的说明及其描述 维修程序还必须涵盖的其他项目包括：

• 螺旋桨 • 公用系统 • 仪表与航电 • 电气系统 • 结构修理 • 涂漆与涂层 「检查、修理与改装」一节必须专门列出完成任务所需的任何专用工具和零件，以及完成该作业所需的维修类别（航线维修、重维修或翻修）。

与该信息直接相关的要求是：须规定完成该工作所需的执照等级（即 LSA 维修人员、A&P，或修理站）。手册可以引用现行的 FAA 咨询通告（AC）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Although 让步 + do 强调 + require that 虚拟语气

**原句**：Although these manuals do not require any FAA approval, the regulations do require that the manual be developed in accordance with industry standards.

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，句中 do not require 用助动词 do 加强否定；主句 the regulations do require 同样用 do 强调；require that the manual be developed 是虚拟语气，should 被省略；in accordance with 意为「按照」。

**要点**：require、insist、demand 等动词后的 that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形，所以是 be developed 而不是 is developed。两个 do 都起强调作用，翻译时可加「确实、的确」体现语气反差。

### ② as well as 连接宾语 + 分词/形容词后置定语

**原句**：The Inspection, Repair, and Alterations section must specifically list any special tools and parts needed to complete the task, as well as the type of maintenance action (line, heavy, or overhaul) necessary to accomplish the activity.

**拆解**：主干是 must specifically list any special tools and parts … as well as the type of maintenance action；needed to complete the task 是过去分词短语作 tools and parts 的后置定语，necessary to accomplish the activity 是形容词短语作 the type 的后置定语。

**要点**：as well as 连接的是 list 的两个宾语，不是两个句子，别读成「列出工具，以及列出类别」两套谓语。needed 与 necessary 都后置，是因为修饰成分较长，这也提示：先锁定 list 的宾语中心词，再挂修饰语。

### ③ 表语前置引起的完全倒装

**原句**：Directly associated with that information is the requirement to specify the level of certification needed to do the job (i.e., LSA repairman, A&P, or repair station).

**拆解**：正常语序为 The requirement to specify … is directly associated with that information；这里把表语 directly associated with that information 提到句首，系动词 is 随之提前，构成完全倒装；to specify … 是不定式作 the requirement 的同位说明，needed to do the job 是过去分词短语修饰 the level。

**要点**：表语较长而主语又带长定语时，为平衡句子常倒装，读法是从 is 后面找回真正的主语 the requirement。翻译时按正常语序重组：「与该信息直接相关的要求是，须规定……」。
