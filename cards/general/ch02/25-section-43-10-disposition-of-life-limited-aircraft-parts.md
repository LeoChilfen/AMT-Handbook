# Section 43.10—Disposition of Life-Limited Aircraft Parts

> 通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.10—Disposition of Life-Limited Aircraft Parts · 原文 PDF 第 66 页

## 1. 原文

Parts (Note the even number again. This regulation became part of 14 CFR part 43 in 2002.)

This section presents two terms not previously defined in 14 CFR:

1. Life-limited part means any part that has specified a mandatory replacement limit.

2. Life status means the accumulated cycles, hours, or any other mandatory limit of a life-limited part.

This section then goes on to specify what to do with parts that are temporarily removed from and then reinstalled on a type-certificated product; what to do with parts that are removed from a type certified product and not immediately reinstalled; and how to transfer life-limited parts from one type-certificated product to another.

When a life-limited part is removed, the person removing it from the type-certificated product must control the part and ensure proper tracking of the life-limiting factor. This is to prevent the installation of the part after it has reached its life limit. There are seven possible methods the technician or repair facility may choose from to comply with this requirement.

1. Recordkeeping 2. Tagging 3. Non-permanent marking 4. Permanent marking 5. Segregation 6. Mutilation 7. Any other method approved or accepted by the FAA When a life-limited part is transferred, the information concerning the life status of that part must be transferred with it. Although regulations already did exist that required the tracking of life-limited parts when they were installed on an aircraft, this regulation was generated to govern the disposition of such parts when they were removed from the aircraft.

Section 43.11—Content, form, and disposition of records for inspections conducted under parts 91 and 125, and sections 135.411(a)(1) and 135.419 of this chapter This section deals exclusively with inspection record entries; however, the requirements are similar to 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9 in that information of what, when, and who is required.

• What—type of inspection, including a brief description • When—date of the inspection and the total time in service • Who—the signature, certificate number, and kind of certificate of the person approving or disapproving the RTS Since this is an inspection write-up and not a maintenance entry, it is quite possible that the inspecting technician could reject or disapprove the item being inspected for the RTS.

When that situation occurs, the regulation states in paragraph (b) that a list of discrepancies must be given to the owner.

A reference to this list and its delivery to the aircraft owner must be reflected in the record entry. Although the regulation neither specifies how those discrepancies can be cleared, nor who may do them, any appropriately-rated repair station or certificated technician can perform the required maintenance actions. When they are completed and the proper maintenance record entries are generated in accordance with 14 CFR part 43, section 43.9, the aircraft is approved for RTS. It is neither necessary to have an additional inspection, nor is it necessary to contact the disapproving inspector.

If the aircraft is on a progressive inspection program, the inspection statement changes slightly from the statement referenced earlier by adding the reference to both a “routine inspection” and a “detailed inspection.” Refer to explanatory text of 14 CFR part 43, section 43.15 for a definition of these terms. Inspections accomplished in accordance with other inspection program requirements must identify that particular program and that part of the program the inspection completed.

## 2. 中文翻译

零件（注意这里又是偶数编号。该规章于 2002 年成为 14 CFR 第 43 部的一部分。）

本条给出两个此前未在 14 CFR 中定义的术语：

1. 寿命件，是指规定了强制更换极限的任何零件。

2. 寿命状态，是指寿命件已累计的循环、小时或任何其他强制极限。

本条接着规定了以下情形的处理办法：从型号合格审定产品上临时拆下、随后又装回原产品的零件；从型号合格审定产品上拆下但未立即装回的零件；以及如何将寿命件从一个型号合格审定产品转移到另一个。

当寿命件被拆下时，从型号合格审定产品上拆下该件的人必须控制该件，并确保对寿命限制因素进行正确跟踪。这样做是为了防止该件在达到寿命极限之后被装机。技师或维修单位可以选用七种方法来满足这一要求。

1. 记录保存　2. 挂牌标示　3. 非永久性标记　4. 永久性标记　5. 隔离存放　6. 毁形　7. FAA 批准或接受的其他任何方法。当寿命件被转移时，有关该件寿命状态的信息必须随之转移。尽管此前已有规章要求在寿命件装于航空器上时对其进行跟踪，但本规章的出台是为了规范这类零件从航空器上拆下之后的处置。

第 43.11 条——依第 91 部、第 125 部以及本章第 135.411(a)(1) 条和第 135.419 条实施的检查记录的内容、格式与保存。本条只处理检查记录条目；不过其要求与 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 条相似，同样要求写明“做了什么、什么时候做、谁做的”等信息。

• 做了什么——检查的类型，并附简要说明；• 什么时候——检查的日期与总使用时间；• 谁做的——批准或不批准恢复使用的人的签名、证书编号与证书种类。由于这是检查记录而非维修条目，检查技师完全可能拒绝或不批准被检查项目恢复使用。

出现这种情况时，本条 (b) 款规定必须向机主提供一份缺陷清单。

记录条目中必须反映对该清单的引用以及清单已交付机主一事。本条既未规定这些缺陷应如何清除，也未规定由谁清除，但任何具备相应等级的维修站或持证技师都可以实施所需的维修动作。当这些工作完成，并依 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.9 条做出适当的维修记录条目后，航空器即获批准恢复使用。既不需要再做一次检查，也不需要联系做出不批准决定的检查员。

如果航空器处于渐进式检查方案之下，检查声明会比前面提到的表述略有变化，即增加对“常规检查”与“详细检查”的引用。这两个术语的定义见 14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.15 条的说明文字。依其他检查方案要求实施的检查，必须指明该特定方案以及该方案中已完成的那一部分。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词短语作后置定语 + ensure 的搭配

**原句**：When a life-limited part is removed, the person removing it from the type-certificated product must control the part and ensure proper tracking of the life-limiting factor.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，用一般现在时被动 is removed 表规章中的一般情形；主句主语 the person，removing it from the type-certificated product 是现在分词短语作后置定语（= who removes）；谓语为并列的 must control 与 ensure；ensure 后接名词短语 proper tracking of the life-limiting factor。

**要点**：ensure 后接名词或 that 从句均可（ensure proper tracking / ensure that it is tracked），但不能接 to do。现在分词作后置定语说明“谁拆谁负责”，是法规里界定责任主体的常见写法，翻译时补出“……的人”。

### ② Although 让步从句 + 定语从句 + 被动谓语

**原句**：Although regulations already did exist that required the tracking of life-limited parts when they were installed on an aircraft, this regulation was generated to govern the disposition of such parts when they were removed from the aircraft.

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，从句主干 regulations already did exist，that required the tracking … 为定语从句（被谓语隔开，属分隔定语），其内 when they were installed on an aircraft 为时间状语从句；主句 this regulation was generated to govern … 为一般过去时被动 + 不定式表目的；when they were removed from the aircraft 是第二个时间状语从句。

**要点**：did exist 用助动词 did 强调“确实早已存在”，与主句“本规章才出台”形成对照。分隔定语（名词与 that 从句之间插入谓语）在法规文本中很常见，读句时先找到 that 再回看它修饰谁。generated 在此意为“制定、产生”，不是“发电”。

### ③ neither … nor 的部分倒装

**原句**：It is neither necessary to have an additional inspection, nor is it necessary to contact the disapproving inspector.

**拆解**：前一分句 It is neither necessary to have an additional inspection 为形式主语 it + 形容词 + 不定式；后一分句 nor 置于句首，引起主谓倒装，恢复为 nor it is necessary to contact …；两个不定式 to have / to contact 作真正主语。

**要点**：nor 位于句首时必须倒装（nor is it / nor does it），这是考试与写作的高频错误点。neither … nor 连接两个并列成分，此处连接两个“不必要做的事”，译作“既不需要……也不需要……”。注意 not necessary to do 与 unnecessary to do 语体差别不大，但规章偏爱前者。
