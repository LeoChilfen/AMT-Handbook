# Aircraft Registration

> 通用 Ch 02 · Aircraft Registration · 原文 PDF 第 87 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft must be registered in the United States if the aircraft is not registered under the laws of a foreign country and is owned by either a citizen of the United States, a foreign citizen lawfully admitted to the United States, or a corporation organized in and doing business under U.S. laws and primarily based in the United States. This registration is accomplished by using FAA Form 8050-1, Aircraft Registration Application. The aircraft registration form is available online at www.faa.gov. The aircraft owner can mail in completed copy, and keep a copy of the form as temporary authority to operate the aircraft after the fee and evidence of ownership have been mailed or delivered to the Registry. When carried in the aircraft with an appropriate current airworthiness certificate or a special flight permit, a copy of this completed application provides authority to operate the aircraft in the United States for up to 90 days.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① is owned by either … or … or …（三项并列 + 过去分词短语后置定语）

**原句**：Aircraft must be registered in the United States if the aircraft is not registered under the laws of a foreign country and is owned by either a citizen of the United States, a foreign citizen lawfully admitted to the United States, or a corporation organized in and doing business under U.S. laws and primarily based in the United States.

**拆解**：主句 Aircraft must be registered；if 引导条件从句，从句内两个并列谓语 is not registered 与 is owned；is owned by 后接 either A, B, or C 三项并列的所有人；C 项 corporation 后带三个后置修饰：organized in and doing business under U.S. laws（过去分词 + 现在分词并列）、primarily based in the United States（过去分词短语）。

**要点**：either … or … or … 表三项择一，须用逗号分隔前两项、or 引出末项。organized / based 是过去分词作后置定语，doing business 是现在分词，区别在于公司是「被组建」的、却「主动经营」，故两者不能互换。翻译时先立骨架「条件是未在外国登记 + 所有人属三类之一」，再补三个修饰成分。

### ② requires that … be on board（require that 后的虚拟语气）

**原句**：14 CFR section 91.203(a)(2) requires that either the pink copy of the application or the actual certificate of registration be on board the aircraft during its operation.

**拆解**：主语 14 CFR section 91.203(a)(2)，谓语 requires，that 引导宾语从句；从句主语是 either the pink copy … or the actual certificate of registration（二者择一），谓语用 be（动词原形），on board the aircraft 为表语，during its operation 为时间状语。

**要点**：require／demand／insist 等表示「要求」的动词后接 that 从句时用 (should) + 动词原形，故为 be 而非 is。either … or 连接的两个主语虽为单数，但按「择一」处理，谓语同样用原形。be on board 是「在机上／随机携带」的固定说法。

### ③ When carried …, a copy … provides authority（省略主语和 be 的状语从句）

**原句**：When carried in the aircraft with an appropriate current airworthiness certificate or a special flight permit, a copy of this completed application provides authority to operate the aircraft in the United States for up to 90 days.

**拆解**：When carried … 是省略了主语和 be 的时间状语从句，完整形式为 When it (a copy) is carried；with an appropriate current airworthiness certificate or a special flight permit 是伴随状语；主句 a copy of this completed application provides authority to operate … for up to 90 days。

**要点**：状语从句主语与主句主语一致且含 be 时可省略为「when + 过去分词」，本句被省略的主语正是主句的 a copy，故用 carried（被动）。for up to 90 days 中 up to 表上限「最长可达」，与 for 连用表示持续期限。authority to operate 用不定式作后置定语。
