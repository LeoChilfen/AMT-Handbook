# Line Maintenance, Repairs, & Alterations

> 通用 Ch 02 · Line Maintenance, Repairs, & Alterations · 原文 PDF 第 95 页

## 1. 原文

The minimum level of certification necessary to accomplish line maintenance is LSA inspection. Some typical tasks considered to be line maintenance are:

• 100-hour/annual condition inspection • Servicing of fluids • Removing and replacing components when instructions to do so are provided in the maintenance manual – Batteries – Fuel pump – Exhaust – Spark plugs and wires – Floats and skis • Repair or alteration of components when specific instructions are provided in the maintenance manual – Patching a hole in the fabric – Installation of a strobe light kit Heavy maintenance, repairs, and alterations must be accomplished by either a certified mechanic (A or P or A&P) or an LSA repairman—maintenance who has received additional “task specific” training. Some examples of this would be the removal and replacement of complete engine, cylinder, piston and valve assemblies; primary flight controls; and landing gear.

Heavy repair of components or structure can be accomplished when instructions are provided in the maintenance manual or other service directed instructions. A few examples of this activity are:

• Repainting of control surfaces • Structural repairs • Recovering of a dope and fabric Heavy alterations of components can be accomplished when instructions are provided in the maintenance manual or other service directed instructions. Examples of this activity are initial installation of skis and installation of new additional pitot static instruments.

Overhaul of components can be performed only by the manufacturer (or someone authorized to perform) of the LSA or the component to be overhauled. An overhaul manual is required and must be a separate manual from the manufacturer’s maintenance manual. Items typically considered for overhaul are engines, carburetors, starters, generators, alternators, and instruments.

## 2. 中文翻译

完成航线维修所需的最低执照等级是 LSA 检查资质。以下是一些被视为航线维修的典型任务：

• 100 小时／年度状态检查 • 液体勤务（加注与补充） • 在维修手册给出说明的情况下拆装部件 —— 蓄电池 —— 燃油泵 —— 排气系统 —— 火花塞与高压导线 —— 浮筒与雪橇 • 在维修手册给出具体说明的情况下修理或改装部件 —— 修补蒙布上的破孔 —— 加装频闪灯组件 重维修、修理与改装必须由持证机务（A 照、P 照或 A&P）或接受过额外「任务专项」培训的 LSA 维修类维修人员完成。此类工作的例子包括：整台发动机、气缸、活塞与气门组件的拆装；主飞行操纵系统；以及起落架。

当维修手册或其他服务指令给出说明时，可以实施部件或结构的重修理。此类作业的几个例子是：

• 操纵面重新喷漆 • 结构修理 • 涂布蒙布结构（dope and fabric）的重新蒙皮 当维修手册或其他服务指令给出说明时，可以实施部件的重大改装。此类作业的例子有：初次安装雪橇，以及加装新的附加空速管静压仪表。

部件翻修只能由 LSA 或待翻修部件的制造厂（或经其授权实施翻修者）进行。必须有一本翻修手册，且该手册必须与制造厂的维修手册分开、单独成册。通常考虑翻修的项目包括发动机、汽化器、起动机、发电机、交流发电机和仪表。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 情态被动 + either … or … + who 定语从句

**原句**：Heavy maintenance, repairs, and alterations must be accomplished by either a certified mechanic (A or P or A&P) or an LSA repairman—maintenance who has received additional “task specific” training.

**拆解**：主语三项并列；must be accomplished by … 为情态被动，by 引出执行者；either … or … 连接两个执行者；who has received … 是定语从句，修饰 an LSA repairman—maintenance。

**要点**：by either A or B 表示「由 A 或 B 之一完成」，两个执行者都要放在 by 之后，不能一个提前作主语、一个留在 by 后。定语从句跟在 B 之后，只修饰 B，不修饰 A——这一点容易读错。

### ② only by 限定 + 被动不定式作后置定语

**原句**：Overhaul of components can be performed only by the manufacturer (or someone authorized to perform) of the LSA or the component to be overhauled.

**拆解**：can be performed 为情态被动；only by 限定唯一执行者；authorized to perform 是过去分词短语作 someone 的后置定语；to be overhauled 是被动不定式作 the component 的后置定语。

**要点**：only 紧贴 by，强调「只能由……」。of the LSA or the component 是限定 the manufacturer 的，读句时容易被 to be overhauled 隔开而误挂，要按「LSA 或待翻修部件的制造厂」还原。to be overhauled 含「尚待翻修」之意，与已翻修的 overhauled 不同。

### ③ 过去分词短语作后置定语（列举前的常见句型）

**原句**：Some typical tasks considered to be line maintenance are:

**拆解**：主语 Some typical tasks；considered to be line maintenance 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which are considered to be line maintenance；谓语 are 后面用冒号引出列举。

**要点**：consider sth. to be … 变被动后为 be considered to be …，作定语时省略 which are，只留 considered to be。这种「名词 + 分词短语 + are:」的句型在手册列举前极常见，翻译时前置成「被视为……的典型任务有：」。
