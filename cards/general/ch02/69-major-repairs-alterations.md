# Major Repairs & Alterations

> 通用 Ch 02 · Major Repairs & Alterations · 原文 PDF 第 95 页

## 1. 原文

Another major difference between LSA maintenance and traditional aircraft maintenance is that FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration, is not required to document major repairs and alterations. Instead, any major repair or alteration that is accomplished after the LSA has gone through production acceptance testing must be evaluated relative to the applicable ASTM requirements. After this evaluation has been accomplished (either by the manufacturer or an entity approved by them), a written affidavit must be provided attesting that the LSA still meets the requirements of the applicable ASTMs.

The manufacturer (or other approved entity) must provide written instructions defining the level of certification necessary to perform the maintenance and also include any ground test or flight testing necessary to verify that the LSA complies with the original LSA acceptance test standards, and is in condition for safe operation. Proper documentation of this maintenance activity is required to be entered in the LSA records and is also defined by the manufacturer.

Task specific training is not required to be FAA approved.

This is solely the responsibility of the manufacturer. Some examples of this are an engine manufacturer’s overhaul school or the EAA Sport Air fabric covering school.

Safety directives are issued against an LSA or component and are not issued by the FAA, but rather by the original aircraft manufacturer. Note: If the LSA includes a product that is TC’d by the FAA, the manufacturer is required to issue a safety directive. Typical instructions within a safety directive include:

• List of tools required for the task • List of parts needed

• Type of maintenance (line, heavy, overhaul)

• Level of certification needed • Detailed instructions and diagrams • Inspection and test methods Safety directives are mandatory, except for experimental use LSAs.

## 2. 中文翻译

LSA 维修与传统航空器维修的另一个重大区别是：重大修理与改装不需要用 FAA 表格 337《重大修理与改装》来记录。取而代之的是，LSA 在通过生产验收试验之后所实施的任何重大修理或改装，都必须对照适用的 ASTM 要求进行评估。评估完成之后（由制造厂或经其批准的机构实施），必须提供一份书面宣誓书，证明该 LSA 仍符合适用 ASTM 的要求。

制造厂（或其他经批准的机构）必须提供书面说明，界定实施该维修所需的执照等级，并包括为核实该 LSA 符合原 LSA 验收试验标准、且处于安全运行状态所必需的任何地面试验或飞行试验。此项维修活动须按规定记入 LSA 记录，具体做法也由制造厂规定。

任务专项培训不要求经 FAA 批准。

这完全是制造厂的责任。此类例子有发动机厂商的翻修学校，或 EAA Sport Air 的蒙布工艺学校。

安全指令是针对 LSA 或其部件发布的，不由 FAA 发布，而由原航空器制造厂发布。注：如果该 LSA 包含由 FAA 颁发型号合格证（TC）的产品，则制造厂必须发布安全指令。安全指令中通常包含的说明有：

• 完成任务所需的工具清单 • 所需零件清单

• 维修类别（航线维修、重维修、翻修）

• 所需执照等级 • 详细说明与图示 • 检查与试验方法 安全指令是强制性的，实验类 LSA 除外。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 表语从句 + 否定被动 + 目的状语

**原句**：Another major difference between LSA maintenance and traditional aircraft maintenance is that FAA Form 337, Major Repair and Alteration, is not required to document major repairs and alterations.

**拆解**：主语 Another major difference between A and B；系动词 is 后接 that 引导的表语从句；从句中 FAA Form 337 作主语，Major Repair and Alteration 是同位语，is not required 为否定被动，to document … 是目的状语。

**要点**：表语从句一律用陈述语序，不能写成 is that is FAA Form 337 not required。document 在此是动词「记录、以文件证明」，与本节主题 maintenance record 直接呼应。

### ② 定语从句套时间状语从句 + must be evaluated

**原句**：Instead, any major repair or alteration that is accomplished after the LSA has gone through production acceptance testing must be evaluated relative to the applicable ASTM requirements.

**拆解**：主语 any major repair or alteration，后接 that 引导的定语从句，从句内又套 after 引导的时间状语从句；主句谓语 must be evaluated；relative to … 是「对照、相对于」的介词短语作状语。

**要点**：主语被长定语从句隔开时，先找到 must be evaluated 再回读主语，否则容易把 testing 误当主语。relative to 在技术文本中常译「对照……（进行评估）」，不要译成「与……有关」。

### ③ 并列被动 + not … but rather … 对比

**原句**：Safety directives are issued against an LSA or component and are not issued by the FAA, but rather by the original aircraft manufacturer.

**拆解**：两个并列的被动谓语 are issued 与 are not issued；against 引出指令针对的对象；not … but rather … 构成「不是……而是……」的对比，but rather 后省略了重复的 are issued。

**要点**：issue a directive against sth. 是「针对……发布指令」的固定搭配，变被动后 against 须保留，不能用 to 代替。but rather 后面只剩 by 短语，读时要补回被动语态，翻译成「而是由原航空器制造厂发布」。注意与 not … but also 区分，后者是递进不是转折。
