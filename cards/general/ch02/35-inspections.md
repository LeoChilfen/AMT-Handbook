# Inspections

> 通用 Ch 02 · Inspections · 原文 PDF 第 70 页

## 1. 原文

This is commonly referred to as “the 413 test.” (Refer to 14 CFR part 91, section 91.413, which requires that no person may use a transponder unless it has had this test completed successfully within the preceding 24 months.)

This section specifies complex sets of tests, which may be accomplished either as a bench test or by using portable test equipment. Major categories of the testing required are radio reply frequency, suppression, receiver sensitivity, radio frequency peak output power, and mode S (when applicable).

Upon completion of testing, proper entries must be made in the maintenance record.

## 2. 中文翻译

这一测试通常被称为「413 测试」。（参见 14 CFR 第 91 部 § 91.413，该条要求：除应答机已在之前 24 个月内成功完成该测试外，任何人不得使用该应答机。）

本节规定了一整套复杂的测试，这些测试既可以按台架试验的方式进行，也可以通过使用便携式测试设备来完成。所要求测试的主要类别有：无线电应答频率、抑制、接收机灵敏度、射频峰值输出功率，以及 S 模式（适用时）。

测试完成后，必须在维修记录中作出相应记录。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 非限制性定语从句 which requires that …

**原句**
Refer to 14 CFR part 91, section 91.413, which requires that no person may use a transponder unless it has had this test completed successfully within the preceding 24 months.

**拆解**
which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 section 91.413（不是 part 91）；从句谓语 requires 后接 that 宾语从句，从句用 no person may use … unless … 结构，it 指应答机。

**要点**
非限制性从句用逗号隔开，译「该条要求……」，不能拿「这」硬绑前一个词；先行词是条号而非部号，译文要落到「第 91.413 条」；unless 从句与 91.411 同构，可对照记忆。

### ② which 从句 + either … or … 选择

**原句**
This section specifies complex sets of tests, which may be accomplished either as a bench test or by using portable test equipment.

**拆解**
主句 specifies complex sets of tests；which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 tests，从句为被动 may be accomplished，方式状语由 either … or … 给出两个选项：as a bench test 与 by using portable test equipment。

**要点**
either … or … 表二选一，译「或……或……」；两个选项介词不同（as / by using），对应「作为台架试验」与「借助便携设备」；may be accomplished 表许可，不是「可能」。

### ③ Upon + 名词表「一经……即」

**原句**
Upon completion of testing, proper entries must be made in the maintenance record.

**拆解**
Upon + 名词短语 completion of testing 作时间状语，相当于「在测试完成后」；主句为被动 must be made，主语 proper entries，地点状语 in the maintenance record。

**要点**
upon 在此等于 on，表「一……就」，比 after 书面；entries must be made 是记录填写的固定表达，译「须作记录」而非「进入」；the maintenance record 用单数，指该机的维修记录（本）。
