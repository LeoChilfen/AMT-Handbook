# Section 43.13—Performance Rules (General)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Section 43.13—Performance Rules (General) · 原文 PDF 第 67 页

## 1. 原文

This section deals with the specific requirements for conducting maintenance. ( Note: This section best reflects the relationship between the FAA’s numbering of ACs and the regulations they are related to.) Paragraph 3 on the cover page of AC 43.13-2B, Acceptable Methods, Techniques, and Practices—Aircraft Alterations, dated March 3, 2008 states:

“Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 43, section 43.13(a) states that each person performing maintenance, alteration, or preventive maintenance on an aircraft, engine, propeller, or appliance must use the methods, techniques, and practices prescribed in the current manufacturer’s maintenance manual or Instructions for Continued Airworthiness prepared by its manufacturer, or other methods techniques or practices acceptable to the Administrator, except as noted in section 43.16.” [Figure 2-9]

Although not all ACs are linked this directly, there is a definite relationship between ACs and companion regulations. Refer to this chapter on ACs for additional information.

Aircraft maintenance technicians (AMTs) are highly skilled personnel, because aviation maintenance work requires great attention to detail. The complexity of technology on today’s aircraft demands a significant level of communication to

properly accomplish maintenance, preventive maintenance, rebuilding, or alteration. This communication frequently comes in written form (i.e., manufacturer’s maintenance manuals or ICA). If neither of these documents provide the guidance the technician needs to perform maintenance, either AC 43.13 (AC 43.13-1 or AC 43.13-2) contain examples of “other methods, techniques, or practices acceptable to the Administrator” that may be sufficient. However, these ACs specifically state that the information is applicable to non-pressurized areas of civil aircraft weighing 12,500 lb gross weight or less.

In addition to the documentation, the technician must also use the proper tools, equipment, and test apparatus that ensures that the work complies with accepted industry practices.

If the test equipment specified by the manufacturer is not available, equipment that is determined to be equivalent and acceptable to the Administrator may be used. The technician should be cautious, however, as “proving” the equivalence of test equipment may not be as simple as it seems.

Air carriers (commercial—“scheduled” airlines operating under 14 CFR part 121, the “commuter/on demand” aircraft operating under 14 CFR part 135, and foreign air carriers and operators of U.S.-registered aircraft under 14 CFR part 129) may use the maintenance manual required by the operations specifications to comply with the requirements of this section. The operator must provide a continuous airworthiness maintenance and inspection program acceptable to the Administrator.

## 2. 中文翻译

本条涉及实施维修的具体要求。（注：本条最能体现 FAA 通告（AC）编号与其所关联规章之间的关系。）2008 年 3 月 3 日发布的 AC 43.13-2B《可接受的方法、技术与操作——航空器改装》封面第 3 段写道：

“14 CFR 第 43 部第 43.13(a) 条规定，凡对航空器、发动机、螺旋桨或机载设备实施维修、改装或预防性维修的人，都必须采用现行制造商维护手册或其制造商编制的持续适航文件中规定的方法、技术与操作，或采用局方可以接受的其他方法、技术或操作，但第 43.16 条另有规定的除外。”

虽然并非所有 AC 都这样直接对应，但 AC 与配套规章之间确实存在明确的关系。更多信息参见本章关于 AC 的论述。

航空维修技师（AMT）是技能水平很高的人员，因为航空维修工作要求高度注重细节。当今航空器技术的复杂性，要求具备相当程度的沟通才能正确完成维修、预防性维修、重造或改装。这种沟通常常以书面形式出现（即制造商维护手册或 ICA）。如果这两类文件都不能提供技师实施维修所需的指导，那么 AC 43.13（AC 43.13-1 或 AC 43.13-2）中列有“局方可以接受的其他方法、技术或操作”的示例，可能足以满足需要。不过这些 AC 明确指出，其信息适用于总重 12,500 lb 及以下民用航空器的非增压区域。

除文件之外，技师还必须使用合适的工具、设备和试验装置，以确保工作符合公认的行业做法。

如果制造商指定的试验设备无法获得，可以使用经认定等效且为局方接受的设备。不过技师应当谨慎，因为“证明”试验设备等效可能并不像看上去那么简单。

航空承运人（依 14 CFR 第 121 部运行的商业“定期”航空公司、依 14 CFR 第 135 部运行的“通勤/按需”航空器，以及依 14 CFR 第 129 部运行美国注册航空器的外国航空承运人与运营人）可以使用运行规范所要求的维护手册来满足本条要求。运营人必须提供局方可以接受的持续适航维修与检查方案。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 过去分词后置定语 + except as noted 省略结构

**原句**：“Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations (14 CFR) part 43, section 43.13(a) states that each person performing maintenance, alteration, or preventive maintenance on an aircraft, engine, propeller, or appliance must use the methods, techniques, and practices prescribed in the current manufacturer’s maintenance manual or Instructions for Continued Airworthiness prepared by its manufacturer, or other methods techniques or practices acceptable to the Administrator, except as noted in section 43.16.”

**拆解**：主语 section 43.13(a)，谓语 states that …；宾语从句中，主语 each person 后接现在分词短语 performing … 作后置定语；谓语 must use；宾语为并列的 the methods, techniques, and practices，后接过去分词 prescribed in … 与 prepared by its manufacturer 作后置定语，or other methods … acceptable to the Administrator 为并列备选；except as noted in section 43.16 为保留状语。

**要点**：except as noted 是“除……另有规定外”的固定省略式，相当于 except as is noted，不能补成 except as noting。prescribed / prepared / acceptable 三个定语都在修饰“方法、技术、操作”，翻译时要按中文习惯把定语前置并适当重复中心词。

### ② neither … either 的条件与主谓一致

**原句**：If neither of these documents provide the guidance the technician needs to perform maintenance, either AC 43.13 (AC 43.13-1 or AC 43.13-2) contain examples of “other methods, techniques, or practices acceptable to the Administrator” that may be sufficient.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，neither of these documents 作从句主语，provide 为谓语（口语中常见复数动词）；the guidance (that) the technician needs to perform maintenance 是省略关系代词的定语从句；主句主语 either AC 43.13 (…)，谓语 contain 亦取复数，because either AC 指“其中任一册”；that may be sufficient 为定语从句修饰 examples。

**要点**：neither of + 复数名词在正式英语中多接单数动词，但手册语体中常按“就近/意义一致”用复数 provide；either AC … contain 同理，把“任一册”当作复数集合。省略关系代词（the guidance the technician needs）是英语定语从句最常见的省略，翻译时须补出“技师所需的”。

### ③ that 定语从句 + ensures that 宾语从句

**原句**：In addition to the documentation, the technician must also use the proper tools, equipment, and test apparatus that ensures that the work complies with accepted industry practices.

**拆解**：In addition to the documentation 为句首状语；主句 the technician must also use …；that ensures … 是定语从句修饰 tools, equipment, and test apparatus（按整体意义取单数谓语 ensures）；ensures 后接 that 引导的宾语从句 the work complies with accepted industry practices。

**要点**：两层 that 从句嵌套（定语从句 + 宾语从句）是技术英语的常见密度来源，读句时先断出第一个 that 的归属。comply with（符合、遵守）是规章高频搭配，主语通常是工作、产品而非人；这里的 ensures 取单数，是因为三者被视为“一套装备”。
