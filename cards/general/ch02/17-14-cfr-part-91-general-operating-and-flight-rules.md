Domestic, Flag, and Supplemental Operations Title 14 CFR part 121 establishes the operational rules for air carriers flying for compensation or hire. A domestic operation is any scheduled operation (within the 48 contiguous states, the District of Columbia, or any territory or possession)

conducted with either a turbo-jet aircraft, an airplane having 10 or more passenger seats, or a payload capacity greater than 7,500 pounds.

A “flag” operation means any scheduled operation (operating in Alaska or Hawaii to any point outside of those states, or to any territory or possession of the United States, or from any point outside the United States to any point outside the United States) conducted with either a turbo-jet aircraft, an airplane having 10 or more passenger seats, or a payload capacity greater than 7,500 pounds.

“Supplemental” operation means any common carriage operation conducted with airplanes having more than 30 passenger seats (if less than 30, the airplane must also be listed on the operations specifications of domestic and flag carriers), with a payload capacity of more than 7,500 pounds.

Part 121 operators are required by 14 CFR part 119 to have the following personnel:

• Director of Safety This “holding out” that makes a person a common carrier can be done in many ways, and it does not matter how it is done.

Signs and advertising are the most direct means of “holding out,” but are not the only ones.

Carriage for hire which does not involve "holding out" is private carriage. Private carriers for hire are sometimes called “contract carriers,” but the term is borrowed from the Interstate Commerce Act and legally inaccurate when used in connection with the Federal Aviation Act. Private carriage for hire is carriage for one or several selected customers, generally on a long-term basis. The number of contracts must not be too great; otherwise, it implies a willingness to make a contract with anybody. A carrier operating pursuant to 18 to 24 contracts has been held to be a common carrier, because it held itself out to serve the public generally to the extent of its facilities. Private carriage has been found in cases where three contracts have been the sole basis of the operator’s business.

Operations that constitute common carriage are required to be conducted under 14 CFR part 121 or 135. Private carriage may be conducted under 14 CFR part 91 or 125.

The term “for hire” is not defined in any of the FAA documents but is generally understood to mean that compensation for both direct and indirect expenses associated with the flight, as well as a profit margin for the operator, are collected from the person or persons benefiting from the flight operation.

The determination of whether the aircraft is large or small is based upon the definition provided in 14 CFR part 1. If the aircraft has maximum certificated takeoff weight of 12,500 pounds or more, it is a large aircraft. All aircraft less than 12,500 maximum certificated takeoff weight are considered to be small aircraft.

不涉及「公开表示」的有偿运输即私用运输。有偿的私用承运人有时被称为「合同承运人」（contract carriers），但该词借自《州际商业法》，在与《联邦航空法》相关使用时在法律上并不准确。有偿私用运输是为一名或数名经选定的客户实施的运输，通常建立在长期基础之上。合同数量不得过多，否则就意味着愿意与任何人订立合同。按 18 至 24 份合同运行的承运人曾被裁定为公共承运人，因为在其设施能力范围内它已向公众普遍表示愿意提供服务。而在仅有 3 份合同构成运营人业务唯一基础的情形中，也曾被认定属于私用运输。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Although it is an operational regulation that is focused toward …, the maintenance technician must have an awareness …（让步从句内套定语从句）

**原句**：Although it is an operational regulation that is focused toward the owner, operator, and/or pilot of the aircraft, the maintenance technician must have an awareness of this regulation.

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，从句内含 that is focused toward … 定语从句修饰 regulation；主句 the maintenance technician must have an awareness of this regulation，must 表义务。

**要点**：让步从句里再套定语从句是手册常见的两层结构，读时先以逗号切出 Although 从句的边界，再读主句，否则容易把主句主语误当成从句成分。be focused toward 意为「面向（某类人）」，与 be focused on（聚焦于问题）有别。have an awareness of 等于 be aware of，规章文本偏爱名词化表达。

### ② Operations that constitute common carriage are required to be conducted under 14 CFR part 121 or 135（定语从句 + 双重被动）

**原句**：Operations that constitute common carriage are required to be conducted under 14 CFR part 121 or 135.

**拆解**：主语 Operations + that constitute common carriage 定语从句；谓语 are required to be conducted 为「be required + 被动不定式」，即双重被动；under 14 CFR part 121 or 135 为状语，说明依据。

**要点**：require sb./sth. to do 的被动式是 be required to do；to be conducted 又用被动，是因为「运行」是被实施的，中文可直接译成「必须按……实施」，不必译出两层「被」。under part XX 是法规里「依某部分」的固定说法，与 per／in accordance with 同义。

### ③ If the aircraft has maximum certificated takeoff weight of 12,500 pounds or more, it is a large aircraft（条件句 + 数值判定句式）

**原句**：If the aircraft has maximum certificated takeoff weight of 12,500 pounds or more, it is a large aircraft.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，主语 the aircraft，谓语 has，宾语 maximum certificated takeoff weight of 12,500 pounds or more（of 短语给出数值）；主句 it is a large aircraft。

**要点**：X pounds or more 表示含端点（≥），而 more than X 不含端点（>）——本节分别用 or more 与 more than 划 12,500 磅和 7,500 磅两条门槛，读条文时不能混。certificated 是过去分词作定语，修饰 takeoff weight，指「经审定的」重量，不是「已取证的航空器」。
