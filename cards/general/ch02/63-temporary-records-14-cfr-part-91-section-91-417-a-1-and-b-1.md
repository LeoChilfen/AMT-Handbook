# Temporary Records—14 CFR Part 91 Section 91.417(a)(1) and (b)(1)

> 通用 Ch 02 · Temporary Records—14 CFR Part 91 Section 91.417(a)(1) and (b)(1) · 原文 PDF 第 89 页

## 1. 原文

91.417(a)(1) and (b)(1)

These are records that must be kept by the owner until the work is repeated, superseded, or 1 year has transpired since the work was performed. These are typically records referring to maintenance, preventive maintenance, alteration, and all inspections. They include a description of the work performed (or reference to the FAA-accepted data); the date of completion; and the name, signature and certificate number of the person doing the RTS.

## 2. 中文翻译

91.417(a)(1) 与 (b)(1)

这类记录必须由机主保存，直至该项工作被重做、被取代，或自工作实施起满 1 年为止。这类记录通常涉及维修、预防性维修、改装以及全部检查。记录内容包括：所完成工作的描述（或对 FAA 认可数据的引用）、完成日期，以及实施恢复使用（RTS）人员的姓名、签名和执照编号。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① until 从句中的三项并列 + since 从句

**原句**：These are records that must be kept by the owner until the work is repeated, superseded, or 1 year has transpired since the work was performed.

**拆解**：主句 These are records；that must be kept by the owner 是定语从句，含情态被动；until 引导时间状语从句，从句内三个并列谓语 is repeated、is superseded、has transpired（第三项主语换成 1 year），后面再套一个 since 引导的时间状语从句。

**要点**：三个并列项是「任选其一到期」的关系，译成「直至被重做、被取代，或满 1 年」；不能理解成三者同时满足。注意第三项的 1 year 是独立主语，与 the work 不共用。

### ② 现在分词短语作后置定语

**原句**：These are typically records referring to maintenance, preventive maintenance, alteration, and all inspections.

**拆解**：主句 These are typically records；referring to … 是现在分词短语作 records 的后置定语，相当于 which refer to …。

**要点**：分词短语后置是手册英语压缩句子的常用手段，翻译时往往要还原成「关于……的记录」。referring to 在此是「涉及、关于」，不是「参考、提及某人」。

### ③ doing 分词修饰人

**原句**：They include a description of the work performed (or reference to the FAA-accepted data); the date of completion; and the name, signature and certificate number of the person doing the RTS.

**拆解**：三个并列宾语 a description…、the date…、the name, signature and certificate number…；of the person 作后置定语，doing the RTS 是现在分词短语修饰 the person；work performed 中的 performed 则是过去分词后置定语。

**要点**：doing 表主动、当时正在实施，若写成 the person to do the RTS 就变成「将要去做的人」，语义不同。同句中 performed 用过去分词，因为工作是被做的——两个分词方向相反，正好对照记忆。
