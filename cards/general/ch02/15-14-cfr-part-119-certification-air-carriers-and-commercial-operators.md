# 14 CFR Part 119—Certification: Air Carriers and Commercial Operators

> 通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 119—Certification: Air Carriers and Commercial Operators · 原文 PDF 第 60 页

## 1. 原文

In order to better understand the next three regulations discussed here (14 CFR parts 121, 125, and 135) a brief overview of 14 CFR part 119 is beneficial. [ Figure 2-8]

There are more than 50 Advisory Circulars (ACs) in the 120 series alone providing additional non-regulatory information concerning the variety of procedures involved with these operations. There are basically three different criteria that must be analyzed in order to properly determine the regulation that applies. These are:

1. Is the service provided for Private Carriage or Common Carriage?

2. Is the aircraft For Hire or is it Not for Hire?

3. Is it a large or small aircraft?

AC 120-12, as revised, provides the following definition regarding this criterion: A carrier becomes a common carrier when it “holds itself out” to the public, or to a segment of the public, as willing to furnish transportation within the limits of its facilities to any person who wants it. There are four elements in defining a common carrier:

1. A holding out of a willingness to 2. Transport persons or property 3. From place to place 4. For compensation years ago, or replicas of these, or experimental exhibition or amateur-built aircraft may use letters at least 2 inches in height.

Only a few aircraft are authorized to display registration markings of at least 3 inches. Note that this regulation sits directly on the vertical line in Figure 2-5 indicating that it applies to both original and recurrent airworthiness.

## 2. 中文翻译

为了更好地理解下面接着讨论的三部规章（14 CFR part 121、125 和 135），先对 14 CFR part 119 作一个简要概述是有益的。

仅在 120 系列中就有 50 多份咨询通告（AC），就这些运行所涉及的各种程序提供补充性的非规章信息。要正确判定适用哪一部规章，基本上必须分析三项不同的判据。这三项判据是：

1. 所提供的是私用运输（Private Carriage）还是公共运输（Common Carriage）？

2. 该航空器是有偿（For Hire）还是无偿（Not for Hire）？

3. 该航空器是大型还是小型航空器？

经修订的 AC 120-12 就这一判据给出如下定义：当承运人向公众或公众中的某一部分「公开表示」（holds itself out）愿意在其设施能力范围内向任何有此需求的人提供运输时，该承运人即成为公共承运人。界定公共承运人有四项要素：

1. 公开表示愿意 2. 运送人员或财产 3. 从此地到彼地 4. 收取报酬（本段末尾接入了另一页的残片：……年前制造的，或其复制品，或实验类展览用或业余制造的航空器，可使用高度至少 2 英寸的字母。）

只有少数航空器被批准使用高度至少 3 英寸的登记标志。请注意，该规章正好落在图 2-5 的竖线上，这表明它同时适用于初始（original）适航与重复（recurrent）适航。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① There are basically three different criteria that must be analyzed …（there be + 定语从句 + 目的状语）

**原句**：There are basically three different criteria that must be analyzed in order to properly determine the regulation that applies.

**拆解**：There be 句型（There are … criteria）表「存在」；that must be analyzed 为定语从句修饰 criteria，内含情态被动；in order to properly determine … 为目的状语；the regulation that applies 中 that applies 又是定语从句。

**要点**：criteria 是 criterion 的复数，承接复数谓语 are；而 the regulation that applies 的从句谓语用单数 applies，因为先行词是单数 the regulation——定语从句的动词要与先行词一致，不能顺着前面的复数名词走。in order to 比单用 to 更强调目的。

### ② when it "holds itself out" to the public … as willing to furnish transportation（when 从句 + as 引出的主语补足语）

**原句**：A carrier becomes a common carrier when it "holds itself out" to the public, or to a segment of the public, as willing to furnish transportation within the limits of its facilities to any person who wants it.

**拆解**：主句 A carrier becomes a common carrier；when 引导时间状语从句；holds itself out to the public … as willing to furnish … 中，as willing … 是说明 held out 内容的主语补足语；within the limits of its facilities 为状语；who wants it 为定语从句修饰 any person。

**要点**：hold oneself out as … 是法律固定表达「对外自称／表示为……」，反身代词必须与主语一致（it → itself）。as willing to do 不能改写成 as willing doing。定语从句 who wants it 中的 it 指 transportation，译者要补出「有此需求的人」才能让中文通顺。

### ③ Only a few aircraft are authorized to display registration markings of at least 3 inches（only a few + be authorized to do）

**原句**：Only a few aircraft are authorized to display registration markings of at least 3 inches.

**拆解**：主语 aircraft（单复数同形），only a few 作限定；谓语 are authorized to display 为「be authorized to do」结构；of at least 3 inches 为介词短语作后置定语，修饰 markings。

**要点**：only a few 位于句首但句子不倒装，因为它限定的是主语而不是状语（对比 Only then did he …）。be authorized to do sth. 是批准类条文的典型句式，与 be approved for／be permitted to 同类；aircraft 单复数同形，只能靠限定词和谓语判断数量。
