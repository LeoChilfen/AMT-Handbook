# Operations and Rules Governing Persons on Board Such Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 02 · Operations and Rules Governing Persons on Board Such Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 62 页

## 1. 原文

This regulation applies to private and noncommon carriage when such operations are conducted in airplanes having 20 or more seats (excluding crewmembers) or having a payload capacity of 6,000 pounds or more. There must also be “operations specifications” issued to the operator that include the following information:

• Kinds of operations authorized • Types of aircraft and registration numbers of the airplanes authorized for use • Approval of the provisions of the operator’s manual relating to airplane inspections, together with the necessary conditions and limitations • Registration numbers of the airplanes that are to be inspected under an approved airplane inspection program (AAIP) under 14 CFR part 125, section 125.247 • Procedures for the control of weight and balance of airplanes • Any other item that the administrator determines is necessary Just as in 14 CFR part 121, subpart E identifies special airworthiness requirements dealing mostly with the mechanical devices of the aircraft.

14 CFR Part 135—Operating Requirements:

Commuter and On-Demand Operations and Rules Governing Persons on Board Such Aircraft As the title of this section states, this regulation is applicable to short distance commercial aircraft operations or “commuters” and nonscheduled carriers that operate “on- demand.” These aircraft are frequently referred to as air taxi or air charter aircraft.

Aircraft operated under 14 CFR part 135 must be operated and maintained in accordance with the certificate holder’s operations manual. This manual, when accepted by the FAA, specifies how the flight crew, ground personnel, and maintenance technicians conduct their operations.

A pivotal portion of this regulation is the first section in subpart J, 14 CFR part 135, section 135.411, Application.

This section specifies that having a type certificated passenger seating configuration of nine or less may be maintained in accordance with the maintenance manual provided by the aircraft manufacturer. Those aircraft having a type certificated passenger seating configuration of 10 or more seats must be maintained in accordance with a maintenance manual written by the air carrier and must then be submitted to the FAA for approval. The requirements for the maintenance manual are specified in 14 CFR part 135, section 135.427. 14 CFR part 135, sections 135.415 through 135.417 and 135.423 through 135.443 specify additional maintenance requirements. 14 CFR part 135, sections 135.415 and 135.417 are applicable regardless of the number of seats in the aircraft.

A major change in the “nine or less” aircraft maintenance requirements occurred in February of 2005 when section 135.422, Aging Aircraft, was incorporated into 14 CFR part 135. This new subpart (note the even number) to 14 CFR 135 specifically prohibits a certificate holder from operating certain aircraft unless the Administrator has completed the aging aircraft inspection and records review. This inspection requires the certificate holder to show the FAA that the maintenance of age sensitive parts and components has been adequate to ensure safety.

This section only applies to multi-engine aircraft in scheduled operation with nine or fewer passenger seats. It does not apply to aircraft operating in Alaska. The required record review start date varies depending on the age of the aircraft.

However, once initiated, the repetitive inspection intervals are not to exceed 7 years.

The certificate holder must make both the aircraft and the records available to the FAA for inspection and review. The certificate holder must notify the Administrator at least 60 days in advance of the availability of the aircraft and the records for review.

The records must include the following information:

1. Total years in service of the airplane 2. Total time in service of the airframe 3. Date of the last inspection and records review required by this section 4. Current status of life-limited parts 5. Time since the last overhaul of all structural components required to be overhauled on a specific time basis 6. Current inspection status of the airplane, including the time since the last inspection required by the inspection program that the airplane is maintained under 7. Current status of applicable ADs, including the date and methods of compliance, and, if the AD involves recurring action, the time and date when the next action is required 8. A list of major structural alterations 9. A report of major structural repairs and the current inspection status of those repairs

## 2. 中文翻译

当此类运行使用设有 20 座及以上座位（不含机组成员）或商载能力为 6,000 磅或以上的飞机实施时，本规章适用于私用运输及非公共运输。此外还必须向运营人签发「运行规范」，其中包含下列信息：

• 获准实施的运行种类 • 获准使用的飞机型号与登记号 • 对运营人手册中与飞机检查有关条款的批准，以及必要的条件与限制 • 应按 14 CFR part 125 第 125.247 节下经批准的飞机检查大纲（AAIP）接受检查的飞机登记号 • 飞机重量与平衡控制程序 • 局长认为必要的任何其他项目。正如 14 CFR part 121 一样，subpart E 规定了「特殊适航要求」，主要涉及航空器的机械装置。

14 CFR Part 135——运行要求：

通勤与按需运行，以及管理此类航空器上人员的规则。正如本节标题所述，本规章适用于短程商业航空器运行（即「通勤」运行）以及实施「按需」运行的非定期承运人。这类航空器常被称为空中出租（air taxi）或包机（air charter）航空器。

按 14 CFR part 135 运行的航空器，必须按照合格证持有人的运行手册运行和维修。该手册经 FAA 接受后，规定飞行机组、地面人员和维修技师如何实施各自的工作。

本规章的一个关键部分，是 subpart J 中的第一条，即 14 CFR part 135 第 135.411 节「适用性」（Application）。

该节规定，型号合格审定的旅客座位配置为 9 座及以下的航空器，可按航空器制造人提供的维修手册进行维修。型号合格审定的旅客座位配置为 10 座及以上的航空器，则必须按照由航空承运人编写的维修手册进行维修，并须随后提交 FAA 批准。维修手册的要求规定于 14 CFR part 135 第 135.427 节。14 CFR part 135 第 135.415 节至第 135.417 节以及第 135.423 节至第 135.443 节规定了附加维修要求。14 CFR part 135 第 135.415 节和第 135.417 节无论航空器座位数多少均适用。

「9 座及以下」航空器维修要求的重大变化发生于 2005 年 2 月，当时第 135.422 节「老龄航空器」并入 14 CFR part 135。14 CFR 135 的这一新分部（注意其节号为偶数）明确禁止合格证持有人运行某些航空器，除非局长已完成老龄航空器检查与记录审查。该检查要求合格证持有人向 FAA 证明，对易受时效影响（age sensitive）的零件与部件的维修已足以保证安全。

该节仅适用于在定期运行中使用、载客 9 座及以下的多发航空器。它不适用于在阿拉斯加运行的航空器。要求的记录审查起始日期随航空器机龄不同而不同。

不过，一旦启动，重复检查的间隔不得超过 7 年。

合格证持有人必须向 FAA 提供航空器和记录，供其检查和审查。合格证持有人必须在航空器与记录可供审查之日前至少 60 天通知局长。

记录必须包括下列信息：

1. 飞机的累计服役年数 2. 机体的累计使用时间 3. 本节所要求的上一次检查与记录审查的日期 4. 寿命限制件的当前状态 5. 自上一次按特定时间间隔必须翻修的所有结构部件翻修以来的时间 6. 飞机的当前检查状态，包括自上一次按该机所依据的检查大纲要求的检查以来的时间 7. 适用 AD 的当前状态，包括符合性日期与符合方法，以及若该 AD 涉及重复性措施，下一次措施应予实施的时间与日期 8. 重大结构改装清单 9. 重大结构修理的报告以及这些修理的当前检查状态

## 4. 语法解析

### ① specifically prohibits a certificate holder from operating … unless …（prohibit sb. from doing + unless 条件）

**原句**：This new subpart (note the even number) to 14 CFR 135 specifically prohibits a certificate holder from operating certain aircraft unless the Administrator has completed the aging aircraft inspection and records review.

**拆解**：主句 prohibits a certificate holder from operating certain aircraft，即 prohibit sb. from doing sth.；unless 引导否定条件状语从句（= if … not），从句用现在完成时 has completed，强调「已完成」这一前提。

**要点**：prohibit sb. from doing 不能写成 prohibit sb. to do，同类还有 prevent／bar sb. from doing。unless 本身已含否定，从句里不能再用 not。用完成时而非一般现在时，是把「检查已经完成」当作放行的前提条件。

### ② requires the certificate holder to show the FAA that … has been adequate（require sb. to do + 宾语从句用完成时）

**原句**：This inspection requires the certificate holder to show the FAA that the maintenance of age sensitive parts and components has been adequate to ensure safety.

**拆解**：主语 This inspection，谓语 requires，宾语 the certificate holder，to show … 为宾语补足语；show 后接间接宾语 the FAA 和 that 引导的宾语从句；从句谓语 has been adequate，用现在完成时。

**要点**：require sb. to do sth. 的被动形式是 sb. is required to do sth.；show sb. that … 译作「向某人证明……」。has been adequate 用完成时而非 is adequate，强调「一段时间以来一直足够」，译成「已足以保证安全」最贴切。

### ③ once initiated, the repetitive inspection intervals are not to exceed 7 years（be not to do 表规定 + 过去分词作状语）

**原句**：However, once initiated, the repetitive inspection intervals are not to exceed 7 years.

**拆解**：once initiated 为过去分词短语作时间状语（= once they are initiated），逻辑主语是 the repetitive inspection intervals；主句 are not to exceed 7 years 中，be to do 表「按规定应当／不得」，其否定形式表禁止。

**要点**：be not to do 是规章文本里表「不得、不得超过」的正式结构，比 must not 更偏条文语气。once + 过去分词是常见省略写法，恢复后为 once they are initiated，注意过去分词的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，否则会成悬垂分词。
