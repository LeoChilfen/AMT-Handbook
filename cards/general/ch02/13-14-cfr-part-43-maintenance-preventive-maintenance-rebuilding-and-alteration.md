# 14 CFR Part 43—Maintenance, Preventive Maintenance, Rebuilding, and Alteration

> 通用 Ch 02 · 14 CFR Part 43—Maintenance, Preventive Maintenance, Rebuilding, and Alteration · 原文 PDF 第 58 页

## 1. 原文

This regulation represents the heart of aviation maintenance and is one of the three major regulations previously identified.

The 13 rules and 6 appendices contained within 14 CFR part 43 provide the standard for maintaining all civilian aircraft currently registered in the United States. Note that 14 CFR part 43 has a significant relationship with part 91 and other parts in maintaining continued airworthiness. [Figure 2-6] A more detailed explanation of this regulation is presented later in this handbook.

## 2. 中文翻译

这部规章是航空维修的核心，也是前文所述三大规章之一。

14 CFR part 43 所含的 13 条规则和 6 个附录，为维修目前在美国登记的所有民用航空器提供了标准。请注意，在保持持续适航方面，14 CFR part 43 与 part 91 及其他部分有着重要的关系。本手册后文将对该规章作更详细的说明。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① The 13 rules and 6 appendices contained within 14 CFR part 43 provide …（过去分词短语作后置定语）

**原句**：The 13 rules and 6 appendices contained within 14 CFR part 43 provide the standard for maintaining all civilian aircraft currently registered in the United States.

**拆解**：主语 The 13 rules and 6 appendices，后接过去分词短语 contained within 14 CFR part 43 作后置定语（= which are contained）；谓语 provide，宾语 the standard；for maintaining … 为介词 + 动名词作后置定语，其中 currently registered in the United States 又是过去分词短语修饰 civilian aircraft。

**要点**：技术文本常用「名词 + 过去分词短语」代替定语从句，句子更紧凑；阅读时先把中心词和谓语抓住，再回头处理两处分词定语。contained within 意为「包含在……之中」，不是「被容纳」；provide the standard for doing sth. 是「为做某事提供标准」。

### ② This regulation represents the heart … and is one of the three major regulations previously identified（并列谓语 + 省略的定语从句）

**原句**：This regulation represents the heart of aviation maintenance and is one of the three major regulations previously identified.

**拆解**：主语 This regulation，两个并列谓语 represents 和 is；previously identified 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 the three major regulations（= which were previously identified earlier）。

**要点**：第二个谓语前的 is 不能省，因为两个谓语的结构和表语性质不同。previously identified 译作「前文已经指出的」，这种省略定语从句的写法在手册里用来避免重复。one of the three … 后接复数名词，谓语仍用单数 is。

### ③ Note that 14 CFR part 43 has a significant relationship with part 91 …（祈使句 + that 宾语从句）

**原句**：Note that 14 CFR part 43 has a significant relationship with part 91 and other parts in maintaining continued airworthiness.

**拆解**：祈使句 Note 的主语 you 省略，后接 that 引导的宾语从句；从句主语 14 CFR part 43，谓语 has，宾语 a significant relationship with part 91 and other parts；in maintaining continued airworthiness 为状语，表示「在……方面」。

**要点**：手册常用祈使句 Note that／Remember that 直接提醒读者，比 It should be noted that 更直接。have a relationship with sb./sth. in doing sth. 是固定搭配，in 不能换成 on。continued airworthiness 是法规固定术语「持续适航」，不要译成「继续适航性」。
