# Appendix B—Recording of Major Repairs and Major Alterations

> 通用 Ch 02 · Appendix B—Recording of Major Repairs and Major Alterations · 原文 PDF 第 70 页

## 1. 原文

Alterations In most cases when a major repair or alteration is accomplished, FAA Form 337, Major Repair or Alteration, is completed at least in duplicate with the original going to the aircraft owner and a copy sent to the FAA Aircraft Registration Branch in Oklahoma City where all civil aircraft information is compiled and retained. Note: Historically, the second copy was sent to the local FAA FSDO within 48 hours after RTS. This copy is reviewed by an ASI and then forwarded by the FSDO to FAA records in Oklahoma City.

However, in the fall of 2005, the FAA made a significant change to this submittal process and now requires the technician to submit the Form 337 directly to the Aircraft Registration Branch in Oklahoma City. Although a third copy is not required, it makes good business sense for the technician or certified repair station to keep a copy of the work that was accomplished.

However, if a certificated (part 145) repair station completes a major repair, it may provide the customer with a signed copy of the work order and a maintenance release signed by an authorized representative of the repair station, instead of the FAA Form 337. If the major repair or alteration was done by an AME or AMO, the copy normally provided to the FAA-FSDO is sent directly to the FAA Aircraft Registration Branch.

However, if extended range tanks are installed in either passenger or cargo compartments, the technician must generate a third FAA Form 337 for the modification. This copy must be placed and retained in the aircraft. (Refer to 14 CFR part 91, section 91.417(d).)

## 2. 中文翻译

改装 多数情况下，完成重大修理或重大改装时，FAA 表格 337《重大修理或改装》至少要填写一式两份，正本交航空器所有人，副本寄往位于俄克拉何马城的 FAA 航空器登记处，所有民用航空器的信息都在那里汇总保存。注：从历史做法看，第二份副本曾在恢复使用（RTS）后 48 小时内寄往当地的 FAA 飞行标准地区办公室（FSDO）。该副本由航空安全监察员（ASI）审阅后，再由 FSDO 转寄俄克拉何马城的 FAA 档案部门。

然而，2005 年秋，FAA 对这一报送流程作了重大改变，现在要求维修技师将表格 337 直接报送到俄克拉何马城的航空器登记处。虽然不再要求提供第三份副本，但对维修技师或持证维修站而言，留一份所完成工作的副本在经营上是明智之举。

不过，如果由持证的（第 145 部）维修站完成重大修理，该维修站可以向客户提供一份签署过的工作单副本，以及一份由维修站授权代表签署的维修放行文件，以代替 FAA 表格 337。如果重大修理或改装是由 AME（Aircraft Maintenance Engineer，飞机维修工程师）或 AMO（Approved Maintenance Organization，经批准的维修组织）完成的，则通常提交给 FAA-FSDO 的那份副本直接寄往 FAA 航空器登记处。

但是，如果在客舱或货舱内加装增程油箱，维修技师必须为该项改装另外出具第三份 FAA 表格 337。该副本必须放置在航空器上并随机保存。（参见 14 CFR 第 91 部 § 91.417(d)。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① with 复合结构交代正副本去向

**原句**
with the original going to the aircraft owner and a copy sent to the FAA Aircraft Registration Branch in Oklahoma City where all civil aircraft information is compiled and retained

**拆解**
with + 名词 + 分词构成 with 复合结构：the original going to the aircraft owner 与 a copy sent to … 两部分并列，going 为现在分词、sent 为过去分词；Oklahoma City 后接 where 定语从句修饰该登记处。

**要点**
with 复合结构在此表伴随状态，译成「正本……，副本……」比「随着」贴切；going 主动、sent 被动，分别对应「交给」与「寄往」；where 从句修饰 Branch 而非 City，译文要落到「该处」。

### ② must be + 过去分词并列

**原句**
This copy must be placed and retained in the aircraft.

**拆解**
主语 This copy，谓语为 must be placed and retained，两个过去分词共用 must be；in the aircraft 为地点状语。

**要点**
must be + 过去分词是适航文件最常见的强制句式，译「必须……并……」；placed 与 retained 共用助动词，翻译补出两个动词即可，不必重复「必须」；in the aircraft 指随机保存，不是「在飞机内部」。

### ③ Although 让步从句 + it 形式主语

**原句**
Although a third copy is not required, it makes good business sense for the technician or certified repair station to keep a copy of the work that was accomplished.

**拆解**
Although 引导让步状语从句；主句中 it 是形式主语，真正主语为 to keep a copy of the work that was accomplished，for the technician or certified repair station 说明「对谁而言」；that was accomplished 为定语从句修饰 the work。

**要点**
Although 从句与主句是让步关系，译「虽然……但……」；it 不译「它」，须还原为后面的不定式；make good business sense 是固定说法「从经营角度是明智的」，不可逐字硬译。
