# Supplemental Type Certificates (STC)

## 1. 原文

When an aircraft is designed and that design is formally approved for manufacturing, the manufacturer is issued a Type Certificate (TC). The TC is issued by the FAA to signify the airworthiness of an aircraft design and may not be changed except by formal authorization of the FAA.

This formal authorization supplements the original TC and is called the Supplemental Type Certificate (STC).

Therefore, the STC issued by the FAA approves a product (aircraft, engine, or propeller) modification. [Figure 2-14]

The STC defines the product design change, states how the modification affects the existing type design, and lists serial number effectivity. It also identifies the certification basis listing specific regulatory compliance for the design change.

## 2. 中文翻译

当航空器完成设计、且该设计被正式批准用于制造时，制造商会获得型号合格证（TC）。TC 由 FAA 颁发，用以表明某一航空器设计的适航性，非经 FAA 正式批准不得更改。

这种正式批准是对原 TC 的补充，称为补充型号合格证（STC）。

因此，FAA 颁发的 STC 批准的是对产品（航空器、发动机或螺旋桨）的改装。

STC 界定产品的设计更改，说明该改装对现有型号设计的影响，并列出适用的序列号范围。它还标明合格审定基础，列出该设计更改所需符合的具体规章。

合格审定基础中所含的信息，对拟提出后续产品改装、以及评估合格审定基础与其他 STC 改装兼容性的申请人很有帮助。TC 与 STC 的编号方式，见图 2-15 中的列表。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① issue sb sth 的被动式 + 时间状语从句

**原句**：When an aircraft is designed and that design is formally approved for manufacturing, the manufacturer is issued a Type Certificate (TC).

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，内含两个并列的被动分句 is designed 与 is formally approved for manufacturing；主句 the manufacturer is issued a Type Certificate (TC) 是人作主语的被动式（由 issue sb sth 变来）。

**要点**：issue sb sth（发给某人某物）转被动时把人提到主语位置，得到 sb is issued sth；不要写成 sb is issued to sth 或 sth is issued sb。同类动词还有 grant、award、assign。for manufacturing 意为「用于制造」，不是「为了制造业」。

### ② may not be changed except by 的禁止 + 例外结构

**原句**：The TC is issued by the FAA to signify the airworthiness of an aircraft design and may not be changed except by formal authorization of the FAA.

**拆解**：两个并列谓语：is issued by the FAA to signify the airworthiness of an aircraft design（被动 + 目的不定式）与 may not be changed except by formal authorization of the FAA（情态被动 + except by 短语表唯一例外）。

**要点**：may not be changed except by X 意为「非经 X 不得更改」，except by 后接动作的发出者；中文习惯把否定提前，译成「非经……不得……」。注意此处的 may not 是「不得（禁止）」，不是「可能不」。

### ③ 三项并列谓语概述文件内容

**原句**：The STC defines the product design change, states how the modification affects the existing type design, and lists serial number effectivity.

**拆解**：主语 The STC；三个并列谓语 defines、states、lists；states 后接 how 引导的宾语从句，说明「改装如何影响」现有型号设计。

**要点**：三个动词依次对应 STC 的三项功能——界定、说明、列出，是技术文件概述自身内容的典型三项并列句。states 后的宾语从句用 how 而不用 that，因为要表达的是方式，不是事实陈述。

### ④ 过去分词作后置定语 + 并列动名词修饰 applicants

**原句**：Information contained in the certification basis is helpful for those applicants proposing subsequent product modifications and evaluating certification basis compatibility with other STC modifications.

**拆解**：主语 Information，contained in the certification basis 是过去分词短语作后置定语（表被动）；表语 helpful；for those applicants proposing … and evaluating … 中介词 for 引出受益对象，proposing 与 evaluating 是并列的现在分词，修饰 applicants。

**要点**：proposing 与 evaluating 并列修饰 those applicants，相当于 who propose and evaluate；读时要给第二个分词补上宾语——evaluating 的宾语是 certification basis compatibility with other STC modifications。contained 用过去分词，因为信息是「被收录」在基础文件里的。
