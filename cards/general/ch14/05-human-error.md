# Human Error

> 通用 Ch 14 · Human Error · 原文 PDF 第 637 页

## 1. 原文

Human error is defined as a human action with unintended consequences. When you couple error with aviation maintenance and the negative consequences that it produces, it becomes extremely troublesome. Training, risk assessments, safety inspections, etc., should not be restricted to an attempt to avoid errors but rather to make them visible and identify them before they produce damaging and regrettable consequences. Simply put, human error is not avoidable but it is manageable.

An unintentional error is an accidental wandering or deviation from accuracy. This can include an error in your action (a slip), opinion, or judgment caused by poor reasoning, carelessness, or insufficient knowledge (a mistake). For example, an AMT reads the torque values from a job card and unintentionally transposed the number 26 to 62. They did not mean to make that error but unknowingly and unintentionally did. An example of an unintentional mistake would be selecting the wrong work card to conduct a specific repair or task. Again, it is not an intentional mistake but a mistake nonetheless.

In aviation maintenance, an intentional error should really be considered a violation. If someone knowingly or intentionally chooses to do something wrong, it is a violation, which means that one has purposely deviated from safe practices, procedures, standards, or regulations.

#### Active & Latent

An active error is the specific individual activity that is an obvious event. A latent error is the company issues that lead up to the event. For example, an AMT climbs up a ladder to do a repair knowing that the ladder is broken. In this example, the active error was falling from the ladder. The latent error was the broken ladder that someone should have replaced.

## 2. 中文翻译

人为差错定义为具有非预期后果的人的行为。当把差错与航空维修以及它所产生的负面后果联系起来时，它变得极其麻烦。培训、风险评估、安全检查等不应仅限于试图避免差错，而应使差错可见，并在它们产生破坏性和令人遗憾的后果之前识别它们。简言之，人为差错不可避免，但可以管理。

非故意差错是对准确性的意外偏离或偏差。这可能包括因推理不佳、粗心或知识不足而导致的行为差错（疏忽，a slip）、意见差错或判断差错（错误，a mistake）。例如，一名维修技术员从工卡上读取力矩值，无意中把数字 26 的顺序颠倒成了 62。他们并不想犯这个错误，却在不知情、无意中犯了。非故意错误（mistake）的一个例子是为某项特定修理或任务选错了工卡。同样，这不是故意的错误，但仍然是错误。

在航空维修中，故意差错实际上应被视为违章。如果有人明知或故意选择做错事，那就是违章，意味着此人有意偏离了安全做法、程序、标准或法规。

#### 主动差错与潜伏差错

主动差错是具体的个人活动，是显而易见的事件。潜伏差错是导致该事件的公司层面问题。例如，一名维修技术员知道梯子坏了，仍爬上去进行修理。在这个例子中，主动差错是从梯子上摔下来。潜伏差错是那架本应有人更换的坏梯子。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① when 引导时间状语从句与 couple A with B
**原句**：When you couple error with aviation maintenance and the negative consequences that it produces, it becomes extremely troublesome.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 you，谓语 couple，宾语 error，with 引出并列宾语 aviation maintenance and the negative consequences；that it produces 为修饰 consequences 的定语从句；主句主语 it（指把差错与维修联系在一起这件事），系动词 becomes。
**要点**：couple A with B 意为「把 A 与 B 联系起来」；主句 it 指代前面整件事，不是 error。

### ② not…but rather 的对比结构与被动语态
**原句**：Training, risk assessments, safety inspections, etc., should not be restricted to an attempt to avoid errors but rather to make them visible and identify them before they produce damaging and regrettable consequences.
**拆解**：主语为三项并列名词；谓语 should not be restricted to；not…but rather 连接两个 to 短语：to an attempt to avoid errors 与 to make them visible and identify them；before 引导时间状语从句。
**要点**：be restricted to 中 to 是介词，后接名词 attempt，而 but rather 后接不定式，形成不对称并列，阅读时要抓住「不是为了……而是为了……」的对比。

### ③ 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：This can include an error in your action (a slip), opinion, or judgment caused by poor reasoning, carelessness, or insufficient knowledge (a mistake).
**拆解**：谓语 include 后接并列宾语 an error…, opinion, or judgment；caused by… 为过去分词短语，修饰前面的 error/opinion/judgment；括号内 a slip、a mistake 是对应术语。
**要点**：caused by 与被修饰名词构成被动关系；三个并列名词共用同一个分词定语，不要误以为只修饰 judgment。

### ④ which 引导非限制性定语从句
**原句**：If someone knowingly or intentionally chooses to do something wrong, it is a violation, which means that one has purposely deviated from safe practices, procedures, standards, or regulations.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句；主句 it is a violation；which 指代整个主句内容，在从句中作主语，从句谓语 means，后接 that 宾语从句。
**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，which 不能换成 that；deviate from 意为「偏离」，是正式书面搭配。

### ⑤ 动名词短语作表语
**原句**：In this example, the active error was falling from the ladder.
**拆解**：主干 the active error was falling from the ladder；falling from the ladder 是动名词短语作表语，说明主动差错的具体内容。
**要点**：此处 falling 不是过去进行时（那样需要主语是人），而是动名词；判断依据是主语 the active error 不能主动「正在摔落」。
