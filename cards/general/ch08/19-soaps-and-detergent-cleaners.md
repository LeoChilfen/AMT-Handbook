# Soaps & Detergent Cleaners

> 通用 Ch 08 · Soaps & Detergent Cleaners · 原文 PDF 第 353 页

## 1. 原文

A number of materials are available for mild cleaning use. In this section, some of the more common materials are discussed.

### Cleaning Compound, Aircraft Surfaces

Specification MIL-C-5410 Type I and II materials are used in general cleaning of painted and unpainted aircraft surfaces for the removal of light to medium soils, operational films, oils, or greases. They are safe to use on all surfaces, including fabrics, leather, and transparent plastics. Nonglare (flat) finishes are not to be cleaned more than necessary and must never be scrubbed with stiff brushes.

### Nonionic Detergent Cleaners

These materials may be either water-soluble or oil-soluble. The oil-soluble detergent cleaner is effective in a 3 to 5 percent solution in dry cleaning solvent for softening and removing heavy preservative coatings. This mixture’s performance is similar to the emulsion cleaners mentioned previously.

## 2. 中文翻译

有多种材料可用于轻度清洁。本节讨论其中一些较为常用的材料。

### 飞机表面清洁剂

MIL-C-5410 规范的Ⅰ型和Ⅱ型材料用于涂漆和未涂漆飞机表面的一般清洁，去除轻至中度污物、使用中形成的薄膜、油或润滑脂。它们可安全用于所有表面，包括织物、皮革和透明塑料。无光（平光）漆层不得过度清洁，且绝不能用硬毛刷擦洗。

### 非离子洗涤剂清洁剂

这些材料可以是水溶性的或油溶性的。油溶性洗涤剂清洁剂在干洗溶剂中配成 3% 至 5% 的溶液，可有效软化和去除厚重的防腐涂层。这种混合液的性能与前面提到的乳化清洗剂相似。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 目的状语
**原句**：Specification MIL-C-5410 Type I and II materials are used in general cleaning of painted and unpainted aircraft surfaces for the removal of light to medium soils, operational films, oils, or greases.
**拆解**：主语 Specification MIL-C-5410 Type I and II materials，谓语 are used，in general cleaning… 为范围状语，for the removal of… 为目的状语，of 后四个并列宾语。
**要点**：主语中心词是 materials，Specification… 作前置修饰；for the removal of 可译为“用于去除”，比 to remove 更正式，符合规范文体。

### ② be safe to use + including
**原句**：They are safe to use on all surfaces, including fabrics, leather, and transparent plastics.
**拆解**：主语 They，系动词 are，表语 safe；to use 为形容词后的不定式补充说明；including… 为介词短语举例。
**要点**：形容词 + 不定式结构中，不定式的逻辑宾语就是句子主语（材料被使用），故用主动形式 to use 而非 to be used；including 后接名词，不接句子。

### ③ be not to be done + never
**原句**：Nonglare (flat) finishes are not to be cleaned more than necessary and must never be scrubbed with stiff brushes.
**拆解**：主语 Nonglare (flat) finishes，并列谓语 are not to be cleaned 与 must never be scrubbed；more than necessary 与 with stiff brushes 分别为程度和方式状语。
**要点**：be not to do 表示“不得”，语气接近 must not；never 已含否定，不能再与 not 连用；more than necessary 是“超过必要程度”，不是“比需要的多”。

### ④ be effective in + for doing
**原句**：The oil-soluble detergent cleaner is effective in a 3 to 5 percent solution in dry cleaning solvent for softening and removing heavy preservative coatings.
**拆解**：主语 The oil-soluble detergent cleaner，系动词 is，表语 effective；in a 3 to 5 percent solution… 为条件/范围状语，for softening and removing… 为目的状语。
**要点**：be effective in 后接溶液浓度表示“在……浓度下有效”；for 后两个动名词并列，第二个 removing 前的 and 不可省。
