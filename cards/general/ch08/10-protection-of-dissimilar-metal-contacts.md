# Protection of Dissimilar Metal Contacts

> 通用 Ch 08 · Protection of Dissimilar Metal Contacts · 原文 PDF 第 345 页

## 1. 原文

Certain metals are subject to corrosion when placed in contact with other metals. This is commonly referred to as electrolytic or dissimilar metals corrosion. Contact of different bare metals creates an electrolytic action when moisture is present. If this moisture is salt water, the electrolytic action is accelerated. The result of dissimilar metal contact is oxidation (decomposition) of one or both metals. The separating materials may be metal primer, aluminum tape, washers, grease, or sealant, depending on the metals involved.

### Contacts Not Involving Magnesium

All dissimilar joints not involving magnesium are protected by the application of a minimum of two coats of zinc chromate or, preferably, epoxy primer in addition to normal primer requirements. Primer is applied by brush or spray and allowed to air dry 6 hours between coats.

### Contacts Involving Magnesium

To prevent corrosion between dissimilar metal joints in which magnesium alloy is involved, each surface is insulated as follows:

At least two coats of zinc chromate or, preferably, epoxy primer are applied to each surface. Next, a layer of pressure sensitive vinyl tape 0.003" thick is applied smoothly and firmly enough to prevent air bubbles and wrinkles. To avoid creep back, the tape is not stretched during application. When the thickness of the tape interferes with the assembly of parts, where relative motion exists between parts or when service temperatures above 250 °F are anticipated, the use of tape is eliminated and extra coats (minimum of three) of primer are applied.

## 2. 中文翻译

某些金属与其他金属接触放置时会发生腐蚀。这通常称为电解腐蚀或异种金属腐蚀。不同的裸金属相接触，在有水分存在时就会产生电解作用。如果这种水分是盐水，电解作用便会加剧。异种金属接触的结果是其中一种或两种金属发生氧化（分解）。分隔材料可以是金属底漆、铝箔胶带、垫圈、润滑脂或密封胶，视所涉及的金属而定。

### 不涉及镁的接触

所有不涉及镁的异种金属接头，除正常的底漆要求外，都至少加涂两层锌铬酸盐底漆，或最好加涂两层环氧底漆，以得到保护。底漆用刷涂或喷涂施工，每层之间需空气干燥 6 小时。

### 涉及镁的接触

为防止涉及镁合金的异种金属接头发生腐蚀，每个表面都按下列方法进行绝缘处理：

每个表面至少涂两层锌铬酸盐底漆，或最好涂两层环氧底漆。随后平整而牢固地贴上一层厚 0.003 英寸的压敏乙烯基胶带，贴得足以防止气泡和皱褶。为防止胶带回缩，粘贴时不得拉伸。当胶带的厚度妨碍零件装配、零件之间存在相对运动，或预期使用温度高于 250 °F 时，则不用胶带，而改涂额外层数（至少三层）的底漆。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be subject to + 名词：表示「易受……影响」
**原句**：Certain metals are subject to corrosion when placed in contact with other metals.
**拆解**：主语 Certain metals + 系动词 are + 表语 subject to corrosion；subject to 是形容词性短语，后接名词 corrosion 表示所遭受的作用；when placed in contact with other metals 为过去分词短语作时间状语，相当于 when they are placed…。
**要点**：be subject to 后接名词时意为「易遭受、受……支配」，接动词时要用 be subject to doing；不能写成 be subjected to corrosion（那表示「被人为地施以腐蚀」，含被动动作义）。同类表达还有 be prone to、be liable to。

### ② when + 过去分词：状语从句的省略
**原句**：Certain metals are subject to corrosion when placed in contact with other metals.
**拆解**：when placed in contact with other metals 的完整形式是 when they are placed in contact with other metals；从句主语与主句主语一致且谓语含 be 动词，因此省略主语和 are，只留过去分词 placed。
**要点**：状语从句省略必须同时满足「主语一致」和「谓语含 be」两个条件；in contact with 是固定搭配，表示「与……接触」。手册中这类省略很常见，如 when heated、if damaged，阅读时要能还原出完整从句。

### ③ be referred to as：被动语态 + as 补语
**原句**：This is commonly referred to as electrolytic or dissimilar metals corrosion.
**拆解**：主语 This（指前句所述现象）+ 被动谓语 is referred to + as 引出的补语 electrolytic or dissimilar metals corrosion；commonly 作状语修饰整个被动结构。
**要点**：refer to A as B 意为「把 A 称为 B」，变被动后 as B 仍留在动词之后，不能写成 is referred to electrolytic corrosion；同类还有 be known as、be called、be termed。

### ④ 条件状语从句 + 被动语态的强调
**原句**：If this moisture is salt water, the electrolytic action is accelerated.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，主语 this moisture，系动词 is，表语 salt water（不可数名词，不加冠词）；主句为被动语态 the electrolytic action is accelerated，accelerate 在此用作及物动词。
**要点**：科技英语常用被动语态把动作的承受者放在句首，突出「作用被加剧」这一结果；accelerate 既可作及物动词（accelerate the action）也可作不及物动词（the action accelerates），此处为及物用法。

### ⑤ 不定式作目的状语 + in which 定语从句
**原句**：To prevent corrosion between dissimilar metal joints in which magnesium alloy is involved, each surface is insulated as follows:
**拆解**：句首不定式短语 To prevent corrosion between dissimilar metal joints 作目的状语；in which magnesium alloy is involved 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，修饰 joints；主句 each surface is insulated 为被动语态，as follows 是固定短语，引出下文的做法。
**要点**：in which = in these joints，介词提前是书面语特征，口语中可拆成 which…in；as follows 中的 follows 永远带 -s，不管前面列举几项；冒号后接具体步骤，是技术手册的典型写法。
