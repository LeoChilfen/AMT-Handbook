# Processes & Materials Used in Corrosion Control

> 通用 Ch 08 · Processes & Materials Used in Corrosion Control · 原文 PDF 第 346 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft parts are almost always given some type of surface finish by the manufacturer. The main purpose is to provide corrosion resistance; however, surface finishes may also be applied to increase wear resistance or to provide a suitable base for paint.

In most instances, the original finishes described in the following paragraphs cannot be restored in the field due to unavailable equipment or other limitations. However, an understanding of the various types of metal finishes is necessary if they are to be properly maintained in the field and if the partial restoration techniques used in corrosion control are to be effective.

### Surface Preparation

Original surface treatments for steel parts usually include a cleaning treatment to remove all traces of dirt, oil, grease, oxides, and moisture. This is necessary to provide an effective bond between the metal surface and the final finish. The cleaning process may be either mechanical or chemical. In mechanical cleaning, the following methods are employed: wire brush, steel wool, emery cloth, sandblasting, or vapor blasting.

Chemical cleaning is preferred over mechanical since none of the base metal is removed by cleaning. There are various chemical processes now in use, and the type used depends on the material being cleaned and the type of foreign matter being removed.

Steel parts are pickled to remove scale, rust, or other foreign matter, particularly before plating. The pickling solution can be either muriatic (hydrochloric) or sulfuric acid. Cost wise, sulfuric acid is preferable, but muriatic acid is more effective in removing certain types of scale. The pickling solution is kept in a stoneware tank and is usually heated by means of a steam coil. Parts not to be electroplated after pickling are immersed in a lime bath to neutralize the acid from the pickling solution.

Electrocleaning is another type of chemical cleaning used to remove grease, oil, or organic matter. In this cleaning process, the metal is suspended in a hot alkaline solution containing special wetting agents, inhibitors, and materials to provide the necessary electrical conductivity. An electric current is then passed through the solution in a manner similar to that used in electroplating.

Aluminum and magnesium parts are also cleaned by using some of the foregoing methods. Blast cleaning, using abrasive media, is not applicable to thin aluminum sheets, particularly Alclad. Steel grits are not used on aluminum or corrosion resistant metals.

Polishing, buffing, and coloring of metal surfaces play a very important part in the finishing of metal surfaces. Polishing and buffing operations are sometimes used when preparing a metal surface for electroplating, and all three operations are used when the metal surface requires a high luster finish.

## 2. 中文翻译

制造厂几乎总会对飞机零件做某种表面处理。主要目的是提供耐腐蚀性；不过，表面处理也可用于提高耐磨性，或为涂漆提供合适的底层。

在大多数情况下，由于设备不可得或其他限制，以下各段所述的原始表面处理无法在外场恢复。但是，如果要在外场正确维护这些表面处理，并且要使腐蚀控制中使用的部分恢复技术有效，就必须了解各种金属表面处理。

### 表面预处理

钢件的原始表面处理通常包括一道清洁处理，以去除所有残留的污垢、油、润滑脂、氧化物和水分。这对于在金属表面与最终表面层之间形成有效结合是必要的。清洁过程可以是机械的，也可以是化学的。机械清洁采用以下方法：钢丝刷、钢丝棉、砂布、喷砂或蒸汽喷砂。

化学清洁优于机械清洁，因为清洁过程不会去除任何基体金属。目前使用多种化学工艺，所用类型取决于被清洁的材料以及要去除的异物种类。

钢件要经酸洗以去除氧化皮、锈或其他异物，尤其是在电镀之前。酸洗液可以是盐酸（氢氯酸）或硫酸。从成本考虑，硫酸更可取，但盐酸去除某些类型的氧化皮更有效。酸洗液盛放在陶瓷槽中，通常用蒸汽盘管加热。酸洗后不电镀的零件要浸入石灰槽，以中和酸洗液带来的酸。

电化学清洁是另一种化学清洁，用于去除润滑脂、油或有机物。在该清洁过程中，金属悬挂在热的碱性溶液中，溶液含有特殊的润湿剂、缓蚀剂以及提供必要导电性的物质。然后使电流通过溶液，方式与电镀时类似。

铝件和镁件也可采用上述某些方法清洁。使用磨料的喷丸清洁不适用于薄铝板，尤其是包铝板。钢质磨粒不得用于铝或耐腐蚀金属。

金属表面的抛光、布轮抛光和着色在金属表面精饰中起着非常重要的作用。在为电镀做准备时，有时会进行抛光和布轮抛光；而当金属表面需要高光泽精饰时，三种操作都会用到。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 双宾语被动语态
**原句**：Aircraft parts are almost always given some type of surface finish by the manufacturer.
**拆解**：主语 Aircraft parts，谓语 are given（give sb sth 的双宾结构变被动，保留直接宾语 some type of surface finish），by the manufacturer 引出动作执行者。
**要点**：give、offer、allow 等双宾动词变被动时，通常把间接宾语提前作主语、保留直接宾语；almost always 位于 be 动词之后、实义动词之前。

### ② 分号连接并列分句 + 不定式作目的状语
**原句**：The main purpose is to provide corrosion resistance; however, surface finishes may also be applied to increase wear resistance or to provide a suitable base for paint.
**拆解**：前一分句主系表，to provide… 作表语；however 连接后一分句，主语 surface finishes，谓语 may be applied，两个 to do 短语并列作目的状语。
**要点**：分号用于连接两个关系紧密的独立分句，其前不用逗号；两个目的状语用 or 连接时，第二个 to 不可省，否则会被误读成并列谓语。

### ③ 条件从句中的 be to 结构
**原句**：However, an understanding of the various types of metal finishes is necessary if they are to be properly maintained in the field and if the partial restoration techniques used in corrosion control are to be effective.
**拆解**：主句 an understanding… is necessary；两个 if 从句并列，均用 be to 结构表“要想、若要”；used in corrosion control 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 techniques。
**要点**：be to do 在此表目的/条件中的“将要、要想”，比 will 更正式；两个 if 从句共享主句，翻译时宜前置为“如果要……并且要……”。

### ④ since 引导原因状语从句
**原句**：Chemical cleaning is preferred over mechanical since none of the base metal is removed by cleaning.
**拆解**：主句 Chemical cleaning is preferred over mechanical（prefer A over B 的被动式）；since 引导原因状语从句，none of the base metal 作主语，is removed 为被动谓语。
**要点**：since 表原因时语气比 because 弱，常译为“因为、既然”；none of + 不可数名词作主语时谓语用单数，此处 is removed 即为例证。

### ⑤ 不定式被动作后置定语
**原句**：Parts not to be electroplated after pickling are immersed in a lime bath to neutralize the acid from the pickling solution.
**拆解**：主语 Parts，not to be electroplated after pickling 为不定式被动作后置定语；谓语 are immersed；to neutralize… 为目的状语。
**要点**：not to be done 作定语表示“不需要被……的”，注意否定词放在 to 之前；be immersed in 是“浸入”的固定搭配，不要与 be immersed by 混淆。

### ⑥ that 替代前文名词 + 过去分词后置定语
**原句**：An electric current is then passed through the solution in a manner similar to that used in electroplating.
**拆解**：主句 An electric current is passed through the solution（被动）；in a manner similar to that… 为方式状语，that 替代前文的 manner，used in electroplating 为过去分词短语修饰 that。
**要点**：that 在此不是从句引导词，而是替代名词的指示代词，避免重复 manner；similar to 后接名词性成分，不能直接接句子。
