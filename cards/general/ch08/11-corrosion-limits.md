# Corrosion Limits

> 通用 Ch 08 · Corrosion Limits · 原文 PDF 第 345 页

## 1. 原文

Corrosion, however slight, is damage. Therefore, corrosion damage is classified under the four standard types, as is any other damage. These types are negligible damage, damage repairable by patching, damage repairable by insertion, and damage necessitating replacement of parts.

The term “negligible” does not imply that little or nothing is to be done. The corroded surface must be cleaned, treated, and painted as appropriate. Negligible damage, generally, is corrosion that has scarred or eaten away the surface protective coats and begun to etch the metal. Corrosion damage extending to classifications of “repairable by patching” and “repairable by insertion” must be repaired in accordance with the applicable structural repair manual. When corrosion damage exceeds the damage limits to the extent that repair is not possible, the component or structure must be replaced.

## 2. 中文翻译

腐蚀无论多么轻微，都是损伤。因此，腐蚀损伤与其他任何损伤一样，按四种标准类型分类。这四类是：可忽略损伤、可补片修理的损伤、可镶嵌修理的损伤，以及必须更换零件的损伤。

“可忽略”一词并不意味着几乎不用做或什么都不用做。腐蚀表面必须视情清洁、处理并涂漆。可忽略损伤通常是指已经破坏或侵蚀掉表面保护涂层、并开始侵蚀金属的腐蚀。扩展到“可补片修理”和“可镶嵌修理”类别的腐蚀损伤，必须按照适用的结构修理手册进行修理。当腐蚀损伤超出损伤极限、以致无法修理时，必须更换该部件或结构。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 插入语 however + 形容词的省略式让步
**原句**：Corrosion, however slight, is damage.
**拆解**：主语 Corrosion；插入语 however slight（相当于 however slight it may be）作让步补充；谓语 is，表语 damage。
**要点**：however + 形容词是 no matter how + 形容词的省略式让步，意为“无论多么……”，不能译成表转折的“然而”；插入语用逗号与主句隔开，翻译时移到句首。

### ② as 引导的方式从句倒装
**原句**：Therefore, corrosion damage is classified under the four standard types, as is any other damage.
**拆解**：主句 corrosion damage is classified（被动）+ under the four standard types；as is any other damage 为 as 引导的方式从句，表语 any other damage 后置、is 前移。
**要点**：as + be + 主语 的倒装表示“……也是如此”，避免重复主句的谓语；同类结构 as is the case with… 在技术文本中很常见。

### ③ 宾语从句中的 be to 结构
**原句**：The term “negligible” does not imply that little or nothing is to be done.
**拆解**：主语 The term “negligible”，谓语 does not imply，宾语从句 that little or nothing is to be done（little or nothing 作从句主语，is to be done 为 be to + 被动，表“应当做”）。
**要点**：little or nothing 意为“几乎没有或完全没有”，与 not imply 连用是“并不意味着做得很少”，不要误读成双重否定；be to be done 在此表义务。

### ④ 定语从句中现在完成时的并列省略
**原句**：Negligible damage, generally, is corrosion that has scarred or eaten away the surface protective coats and begun to etch the metal.
**拆解**：主语 Negligible damage，插入语 generally，系动词 is，表语 corrosion；that 引导定语从句，谓语为 has scarred or eaten away… and (has) begun to etch…，第二个 has 省略。
**要点**：两个现在完成时并列时，助动词 has 可只保留第一个；scar、eat away 均为及物动词短语，宾语是 the surface protective coats。

### ⑤ to the extent that 引导程度结果从句
**原句**：When corrosion damage exceeds the damage limits to the extent that repair is not possible, the component or structure must be replaced.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；to the extent that… 为程度状语从句，that 后说明达到的程度；主句 the component or structure must be replaced 为情态被动。
**要点**：to the extent that 表示“达到……的程度”，此处引出结果“以致无法修理”；主句用 must be + 过去分词，强调强制处置，不点明执行者。
