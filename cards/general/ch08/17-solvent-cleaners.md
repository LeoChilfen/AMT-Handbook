# Solvent Cleaners

> 通用 Ch 08 · Solvent Cleaners · 原文 PDF 第 352 页

## 1. 原文

In general, solvent cleaners used in aircraft cleaning must have a flashpoint of not less than 105 °F, if explosion proofing of equipment and other special precautions are to be avoided. Chlorinated solvents of all types meet the nonflammable requirements, but are toxic. Safety precautions must be observed in their use. Use of carbon tetrachloride is to be avoided. The SDS for each solvent must be consulted for handling and safety information.

AMTs must review the SDS available for any chemical, solvent, or other materials they may come in contact with during the course of their maintenance activities. In particular, solvents and cleaning liquids, even those considered “environmentally friendly,” can have varied detrimental effects on the skin, internal organs, and/or nervous system. Active solvents, such as methyl ethyl ketone (MEK) and acetone, can be harmful or fatal if swallowed, inhaled, or absorbed through the skin in sufficient quantities. Particular attention must be paid to recommended protective measures including gloves, respirators, and face shields. A regular review of the SDS keeps the AMT updated on any revisions that may be made by chemical manufacturers or government authorities.

### Dry Cleaning Solvent

Stoddard solvent is the most common petroleum base solvent used in aircraft cleaning. Its flashpoint is slightly above 105 °F and can be used to remove grease, oils, or light soils. Dry cleaning solvent is preferable to kerosene for all cleaning purposes, but like kerosene, it leaves a slight residue upon evaporation that may interfere with the application of some final paint films.

### Aliphatic and Aromatic Naphtha

Aliphatic naphtha is recommended for wipe down of cleaned surfaces just before painting. This material can also be used for cleaning acrylics and rubber. It flashes at approximately 80 °F and must be used with care. Aromatic naphtha must not be confused with the aliphatic material. It is toxic, attacks acrylics and rubber products, and must be used with adequate controls.

Safety solvent, trichloroethane (methyl chloroform), is used for general cleaning and grease removal. It is nonflammable under ordinary circumstances and is used as a replacement for carbon tetrachloride. The use and safety precautions necessary when using chlorinated solvents must be observed. Prolonged use can cause dermatitis on some persons.

### Methyl Ethyl Ketone (MEK)

MEK is also available as a solvent cleaner for metal surfaces and paint stripper for small areas. This is a very active solvent and metal cleaner with a flashpoint of about 24 °F. It is toxic when inhaled, and safety precautions must be observed during its use. In most instances, it has been replaced with safer to handle and more environmentally-friendly cleaning solvents.

Kerosene is mixed with solvent emulsion-type cleaners for softening heavy preservative coatings. It is also used for general solvent cleaning, but its use must be followed by a coating or rinse with some other type of protective agent. Kerosene does not evaporate as rapidly as dry cleaning solvent and generally leaves an appreciable film on cleaned surfaces that may actually be corrosive. Kerosene films may be removed with safety solvent, water emulsion cleaners, or detergent mixtures.

### Cleaning Compound for Oxygen Systems

Cleaning compounds for use in the oxygen system are anhydrous (waterless) ethyl alcohol or isopropyl (anti-icing fluid) alcohol. These may be used to clean accessible components of the oxygen system, such as crew masks and lines. Fluids must not be put into tanks or regulators.

Do not use any cleaning compounds that may leave an oily film when cleaning oxygen equipment. Instructions of the manufacturer of the oxygen equipment and cleaning compounds must be followed at all times.

## 2. 中文翻译

一般来说，如果要避免对设备采取防爆措施和采取其他特殊预防措施，飞机清洁所用溶剂清洁剂的闪点就不得低于 105 °F。各类氯化溶剂都满足不可燃要求，但有毒。使用它们时必须遵守安全预防措施。应避免使用四氯化碳。必须查阅每种溶剂的安全数据表（SDS），以获取搬运和安全信息。

航空维修技术人员（AMT）必须查阅其维修活动中可能接触的任何化学品、溶剂或其他材料的 SDS。特别是溶剂和清洁液，即使是那些被认为“环保”的产品，也可能对皮肤、内脏和/或神经系统产生各种有害影响。活性溶剂，如甲基乙基酮（MEK）和丙酮，如果吞入、吸入或经皮肤吸收足够量，可能有害甚至致命。必须特别注意建议的防护措施，包括手套、呼吸器和面罩。定期查阅 SDS 可使 AMT 随时了解化学品制造商或政府主管部门可能作出的任何修订。

### 干洗溶剂

Stoddard 溶剂是飞机清洁中最常用的石油基溶剂。其闪点略高于 105 °F，可用于去除润滑脂、油或轻污物。就所有清洁用途而言，干洗溶剂比煤油更可取；但与煤油一样，它在蒸发后会留下轻微残留物，可能妨碍某些最终漆膜的应用。

### 脂肪族和芳香族石脑油

脂肪族石脑油推荐用于涂漆前对已清洁表面的擦拭。这种材料也可用于清洁丙烯酸塑料和橡胶。它在大约 80 °F 时闪燃，使用时必须小心。不得将芳香族石脑油与脂肪族材料混为一谈。芳香族石脑油有毒，会侵蚀丙烯酸塑料和橡胶制品，必须采取充分的控制措施使用。

安全溶剂三氯乙烷（甲基氯仿）用于一般清洁和除脂。它在通常情况下不可燃，被用作四氯化碳的替代品。使用氯化溶剂时必须遵守相应的使用和安全预防措施。长时间使用可能使某些人患皮炎。

### 甲基乙基酮（MEK）

MEK 也可用作金属表面的溶剂清洁剂和小面积脱漆剂。这是一种活性很强的溶剂和金属清洁剂，闪点约为 24 °F。吸入时有毒，使用期间必须遵守安全预防措施。在大多数情况下，它已被更易安全操作、更环保的清洁溶剂所取代。

煤油与溶剂乳化型清洁剂混合，用于软化厚重的防腐涂层。它也用于一般溶剂清洁，但使用后必须用其他类型的防护剂涂覆或冲洗。煤油不像干洗溶剂那样蒸发得快，通常会在清洁过的表面留下明显的油膜，该油膜可能实际上具有腐蚀性。煤油膜可用安全溶剂、水乳化清洁剂或洗涤剂混合液去除。

### 氧气系统清洁剂

用于氧气系统的清洁剂是无水乙醇或异丙醇（防冰液）。它们可用于清洁氧气系统中可接近的部件，例如机组氧气面罩和管路。不得将液体倒入氧气瓶或调节器。

清洁氧气设备时，不要使用任何可能留下油膜的清洁剂。必须始终遵循氧气设备制造商和清洁剂制造商的说明。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 条件从句中的 be to 被动 + not less than
**原句**：In general, solvent cleaners used in aircraft cleaning must have a flashpoint of not less than 105 °F, if explosion proofing of equipment and other special precautions are to be avoided.
**拆解**：主句主语 solvent cleaners（used in aircraft cleaning 为过去分词短语作后置定语），谓语 must have；if 从句用 are to be avoided（be to 被动）表“若要避免”；not less than 为闪点下限。
**要点**：not less than 是“不低于”，not more than 是“不高于”，切勿混用；be to be avoided 在此表目的条件，译作“如果要避免”。

### ② such as 同位语 + 省略被动的条件从句
**原句**：Active solvents, such as methyl ethyl ketone (MEK) and acetone, can be harmful or fatal if swallowed, inhaled, or absorbed through the skin in sufficient quantities.
**拆解**：主语 Active solvents，such as… 为同位语举例；谓语 can be harmful or fatal；if 从句省略了主语和 be（if they are swallowed, inhaled, or absorbed…），三个过去分词并列。
**要点**：省略的条件从句中，过去分词与主句主语的被动关系必须成立；in sufficient quantities 修饰三个动作，翻译时置于“吞入、吸入、吸收”之前。

### ③ preferable to + 定语从句
**原句**：Dry cleaning solvent is preferable to kerosene for all cleaning purposes, but like kerosene, it leaves a slight residue upon evaporation that may interfere with the application of some final paint films.
**拆解**：前一分句主系表，preferable to kerosene 为比较；but 转折，like kerosene 为插入状语，it leaves a slight residue；that 引导定语从句修饰 residue。
**要点**：preferable to 后接比较对象，不用 than；interfere with 是“妨碍”，不要与 interfere in（干涉）混淆。

### ④ 并列被动语态
**原句**：It is nonflammable under ordinary circumstances and is used as a replacement for carbon tetrachloride.
**拆解**：主语 It（指三氯乙烷），并列谓语 is nonflammable 与 is used as…；under ordinary circumstances 为条件状语。
**要点**：be used as 表示“被用作”，as 后接身份/用途；两个谓语共用主语，第二个 is 不可省，否则会被当成主动的 used。

### ⑤ 否定祈使句 + 定语从句 + when doing
**原句**：Do not use any cleaning compounds that may leave an oily film when cleaning oxygen equipment.
**拆解**：Do not use 构成否定祈使句，宾语 any cleaning compounds；that 引导定语从句，when cleaning oxygen equipment 为时间状语（when you are cleaning… 的省略）。
**要点**：when + 现在分词的逻辑主语是祈使句隐含的 you，不是 compounds；leave an oily film 是“留下油膜”，不是“离开”。

### ⑥ not as…as 同级比较
**原句**：Kerosene does not evaporate as rapidly as dry cleaning solvent and generally leaves an appreciable film on cleaned surfaces that may actually be corrosive.
**拆解**：主句主语 Kerosene，谓语 does not evaporate；as rapidly as 为同级比较；and 连接第二个谓语 leaves；that 引导定语从句修饰 film。
**要点**：否定同级比较 not as…as 意为“不如……那样”，不能译成“和……一样不”；that 从句的谓语 may be corrosive 与 film 保持数的一致。
