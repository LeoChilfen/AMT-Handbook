# Powerplant Cleaning

> 通用 Ch 08 · Powerplant Cleaning · 原文 PDF 第 351 页

## 1. 原文

Cleaning the powerplant is an important job and must be done thoroughly. Grease and dirt accumulations on an air-cooled engine provide an effective insulation against the cooling effect of air flowing over it. Such an accumulation can also cover up cracks or other defects.

When cleaning an engine, open or remove the cowling as much as possible. Beginning with the top, wash down the engine and accessories with a fine spray of kerosene or solvent. A bristle brush may be used to help clean some of the surfaces.

Fresh water, soap, and approved cleaning solvents may be used for cleaning propeller and rotor blades. Except in the process of etching, caustic material must not be used on a propeller. Scrapers, power buffers, steel brushes, or any tool or substances that mar or scratch the surface must not be used on propeller blades, except as recommended for etching and repair.

Water spray, rain, or other airborne abrasive material strikes a whirling propeller blade with such force that small pits are formed in the blade’s leading edge. If preventive measures are not taken, corrosion causes these pits to rapidly grow larger. The pits may become so large that it is necessary to file the blade’s leading edge until it is smooth.

Steel propeller blades have more resistance to abrasion and corrosion than aluminum alloy blades. Steel blades, if rubbed down with oil after each flight, retain a smooth surface for a long time.

Examine the propellers regularly, because cracks in steel or aluminum alloy blades can become filled with oil that tends to oxidize. This can readily be seen when the blade is inspected.

Keeping the surface wiped with oil serves as a safety feature by helping to make cracks more obvious.

Propeller hubs must be inspected regularly for cracks and other defects. Unless the hubs are kept clean, defects may not be found. Clean steel hubs with soap and fresh water or with an approved cleaning solvent. These cleaning solvents may be applied by cloths or brushes. Avoid tools and abrasives that scratch or otherwise damage the plating.

In special cases where a high polish is desired, the use of a good grade of metal polish is recommended. Upon completion of the polishing, all traces of polish must be removed immediately, the blades cleaned, and then coated with clean engine oil. All cleaning substances must be removed immediately after completion of the cleaning of any propeller part. Soap in any form can be removed by rinsing repeatedly with fresh water. After rinsing, all surfaces must be dried and coated with clean engine oil. After cleaning the powerplant, all control arms, bellcranks, and moving parts must be lubricated according to instructions in the applicable maintenance manual.

## 2. 中文翻译

清洁动力装置是一项重要工作，必须彻底完成。气冷发动机上积聚的润滑脂和污垢会形成有效的隔热层，阻碍流经其表面的空气的冷却作用。这种积聚还会掩盖裂纹或其他缺陷。

清洁发动机时，应尽可能打开或拆下整流罩。从顶部开始，用煤油或溶剂的细雾喷洗发动机及附件。可使用硬毛刷帮助清洁某些表面。

可使用淡水、肥皂和经批准的清洁溶剂清洁螺旋桨和旋翼桨叶。除蚀刻工序外，不得在螺旋桨上使用苛性材料。除蚀刻和修理所建议者外，不得在螺旋桨桨叶上使用刮刀、电动抛光机、钢丝刷或任何会损伤或划伤表面的工具或物质。

水雾、雨水或其他空气中的磨蚀性物质以很大的力冲击旋转的螺旋桨桨叶，在桨叶前缘形成小凹坑。如果不采取预防措施，腐蚀会使这些凹坑迅速扩大。凹坑可能大到必须锉平桨叶前缘的程度。

钢制螺旋桨桨叶比铝合金桨叶更耐磨损和腐蚀。钢制桨叶如果每次飞行后都涂油擦拭，能长时间保持光滑表面。

要定期检查螺旋桨，因为钢制或铝合金桨叶中的裂纹可能充满趋于氧化的油。检查桨叶时很容易看到这种情况。

保持表面涂油擦拭具有安全作用，有助于使裂纹更明显。

必须定期检查螺旋桨桨毂有无裂纹和其他缺陷。如果桨毂不保持清洁，缺陷可能无法被发现。用肥皂和淡水或经批准的清洁溶剂清洁钢制桨毂。这些清洁溶剂可用抹布或刷子施加。避免使用会划伤或以其他方式损坏镀层的工具和磨料。

在需要高度抛光的特殊情况下，建议使用优质金属抛光剂。抛光完成后，必须立即清除所有抛光剂痕迹，清洁桨叶，然后涂上清洁的发动机油。任何螺旋桨零件清洁完成后，必须立即清除所有清洁物质。任何形式的肥皂都可通过反复用淡水冲洗去除。冲洗后，所有表面必须干燥并涂上清洁的发动机油。清洁动力装置后，所有操纵臂、双臂摇臂和运动部件必须按照适用维修手册中的说明进行润滑。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 动名词主语 + 情态被动
**原句**：Cleaning the powerplant is an important job and must be done thoroughly.
**拆解**：主语 Cleaning the powerplant（动名词短语），谓语为并列的 is 与 must be done；thoroughly 为方式状语。
**要点**：动名词短语作主语视为单数；第二个谓语省略了相同的主语，翻译时补出“这项工作”。

### ② 介词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Grease and dirt accumulations on an air-cooled engine provide an effective insulation against the cooling effect of air flowing over it.
**拆解**：主语 Grease and dirt accumulations，on an air-cooled engine 为后置定语；谓语 provide，宾语 an effective insulation；against… 与 flowing over it 分别作定语修饰 insulation 和 air。
**要点**：accumulation 后常接 of/on 短语说明积聚物与位置；insulation against 是“对……的隔绝”，不能译成“绝缘材料”。

### ③ such…that 结果状语从句
**原句**：Water spray, rain, or other airborne abrasive material strikes a whirling propeller blade with such force that small pits are formed in the blade’s leading edge.
**拆解**：主语为 or 连接的三个并列名词，谓语 strikes，宾语 a whirling propeller blade；with such force 为方式状语，that 引导结果状语从句，从句用被动 are formed。
**要点**：such + 名词 + that 与 so + 形容词/副词 + that 对应，本句 force 是名词，故用 such；结果从句常译为“以致……”。

### ④ cause sb/sth to do 复合宾语
**原句**：If preventive measures are not taken, corrosion causes these pits to rapidly grow larger.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句（被动 are not taken）；主句主语 corrosion，谓语 causes，宾语 these pits，to rapidly grow larger 为不定式作宾语补足语。
**要点**：cause 后接“宾语 + 不定式”，不能写成 cause these pits grow；rapidly 置于不定式 to 与动词之间（分裂不定式），在技术英语中可接受。

### ⑤ 并列被动中省略 must be
**原句**：Upon completion of the polishing, all traces of polish must be removed immediately, the blades cleaned, and then coated with clean engine oil.
**拆解**：Upon completion of the polishing 为时间状语；主句三个并列被动 must be removed、[must be] cleaned、[must be] coated，后两个省略了 must be。
**要点**：并列谓语省略相同助动词时，读者须自行补全；coated with 是“涂覆”，与 coated by（被……涂覆）不同。

### ⑥ unless 引导否定条件从句
**原句**：Unless the hubs are kept clean, defects may not be found.
**拆解**：Unless 引导条件状语从句（相当于 if…not），从句谓语 are kept（被动）；主句 defects may not be found（情态被动否定）。
**要点**：unless 本身含否定，不能再加 not；may not be found 是“可能找不到”，不是“禁止被找到”。
