# Corrosion Prone Areas

> 通用 Ch 08 · Corrosion Prone Areas · 原文 PDF 第 336 页

## 1. 原文

Discussed briefly in this section are most of the corrosion problem areas common to all aircraft. These areas should be cleaned, inspected, and treated more frequently than less corrosion prone areas. This information is not necessarily complete and may be amplified and expanded to cover the special characteristics of the particular aircraft model involved by referring to the applicable maintenance manual.

### Exhaust Trail Areas

Both jet and reciprocating engine exhaust deposits are very corrosive and give particular trouble where gaps, seams, hinges, and fairings are located downstream from the exhaust pipes or nozzles. Deposits may be trapped and not reached by normal cleaning methods. Pay special attention to areas around rivet heads and in skin lap joints and other crevices. Remove and inspect fairings and access plates in the exhaust areas. Do not overlook exhaust deposit buildup in remote areas, such as the empennage surfaces. Buildup in these areas is slower and may not be noticed until corrosive damage has begun.

### Battery Compartments and Battery Vent Openings

Despite improvements in protective paint finishes and in methods of sealing and venting, battery compartments continue to be corrosion prone areas. Fumes from overheated electrolyte are difficult to contain and spread to adjacent cavities, causing a rapid corrosive attack on all unprotected metal surfaces. Battery vent openings on the aircraft skin should be included in the battery compartment inspection and maintenance procedure. If aircraft batteries with electrolytes, sulfuric acid, or potassium hydroxide are in use, their leakage will cause corrosion. Regular cleaning and neutralization of acid deposits minimizes corrosion from this cause. Consult the applicable maintenance manuals for the particular aircraft to determine the type of battery installed and the recommended maintenance.

Residual oil quite often masks small quantities of water that settle to the bottom and set up a hidden chemical cell. Instead of using chemical treatments for the bilge water, current float manufacturers recommend the diligent maintenance of the internal coatings applied to the float’s interior during manufacture. In addition to chemical conversion coatings applied to the surface of the sheet metal and other structural components and to sealants installed in lap joints during construction, the interior compartments are painted to protect the bilge areas. When seaplane structures are repaired or restored, this level of corrosion protection must be maintained.

### Lavatories, Buffets, & Galleys

These areas, particularly deck areas behind lavatories, sinks, and ranges, where spilled food and waste products may collect if not kept clean, are potential trouble spots. Even if some contaminants are not corrosive in themselves, they attract and retain moisture and, in turn, cause corrosive attack. Pay attention to bilge areas located under galleys and lavatories. Clean these areas frequently and maintain the protective sealant and paint finishes.

### Wheel Well and Landing Gear

More than any other area on the aircraft, this area probably receives more punishment due to mud, water, salt, gravel, and other flying debris. Because of the many complicated shapes, assemblies, and fittings, complete area paint film coverage is difficult to attain and maintain. A partially applied preservative tends to mask corrosion rather than prevent it. Due to heat generated by braking action, preservatives cannot be used on some main landing gear wheels. During inspection of this area, pay particular attention to the following trouble spots:

1. Magnesium wheels, especially around bolt heads, lugs, and wheel web areas, for the presence of entrapped water or its effects

2. Exposed rigid tubing, especially at B-nuts and ferrules, under clamps and tubing identification tapes

3. Exposed position indicator switches and other electrical equipment

4. Crevices between stiffeners, ribs, and lower skin surfaces that are typical water and debris traps

5. Axle interiors

6. Exposed surfaces of struts, oleos, arms, links, and attaching hardware (bolts, pins, etc.)

### Water Entrapment Areas

Design specifications require that aircraft have drains installed in all areas where water may collect. Daily inspection of low point drains is a standard requirement. If this inspection is neglected, the drains may become ineffective because of accumulated debris, grease, or sealants.

Low point drains should be inspected more frequently than other areas.

### Engine Frontal Areas & Cooling Air Vents

These areas are being constantly abraded with airborne dirt and dust, bits of gravel from runways, and rain erosion, leading to removal of the protective finish. Furthermore, cores of radiator coolers, reciprocating engine cylinder fins, etc., may not be painted due to the requirement for heat dissipation. Engine accessory mounting bases usually have small area of unpainted magnesium or aluminum on the machined-mounted surfaces. Inspection of these areas must include all sections in the cooling air path, with special attention to places where salt deposits may be built up during marine operations. It is imperative that incipient corrosion be inhibited and that paint touchup and hard film preservative coatings are maintained on seaplane and amphibian engine surfaces at all times.

### Wing Flap & Spoiler Recesses

Dirt and water may collect in flap and spoiler recesses unnoticed, because they are normally retracted. For this reason, these recesses are potential corrosion problem areas. Inspect these areas with the spoilers and flaps in the fully deployed position.

### External Skin Areas

External aircraft surfaces are readily visible and accessible for inspection and maintenance. Even here, certain types of configurations or combinations of materials become troublesome under certain operating conditions and require special attention.

Relatively little corrosion trouble is experienced with magnesium skins if the original surface finish and insulation are adequately maintained. Trimming, drilling, and riveting destroy some of the original surface treatment and can never be completely restored by touchup procedures. Any inspection for corrosion must include all magnesium skin surfaces with special attention to edges, areas around fasteners, and cracked, chipped, or missing paint.

Piano-type hinges are prime spots for corrosion due to the dissimilar metal contact between the steel pin and aluminum hinge. They are also natural traps for dirt, salt, and moisture. Inspection of hinges must include lubrication and actuation through several cycles to ensure complete lubricant penetration. Use water-displacing lubricants when servicing piano hinges.

Corrosion of metal skins joined by spot welding is the result of the entrance and entrapment of corrosive agents between the layers of metal. This type of corrosion is evidenced by corrosion products appearing at the crevices where the corrosive agents enter. More advanced corrosive attack causes skin buckling and eventual spot weld fracture. Skin buckling in its early stages may be detected by sighting along spot welded seams or by using a straightedge. The only technique for preventing this condition is to keep potential moisture entry points, including seams and holes created by broken spot welds, filled with a sealant or a suitable preservative compound.

### Electronic & Electrical Compartments

Electronic and electrical compartments cooled by ram air or compressor bleed air are subjected to the same conditions common to engine and accessory cooling vents and engine frontal areas. While the degree of exposure is less, because a lower volume of air passing through and special design features incorporated to prevent water formation in enclosed spaces, this is still a trouble area that requires special attention. Circuit breakers, contact points, and switches are extremely sensitive to moisture and corrosive attack, thus inspection is required for these conditions as thoroughly as design permits. If design features hinder examination of these items while in the installed condition, inspection is accomplished after component removal for other reasons.

### Miscellaneous Trouble Areas

Helicopter rotor heads and gearboxes, in addition to being constantly exposed to the elements, contain bare steel surfaces, many external working parts, and dissimilar metal contacts. Inspect these areas frequently for evidence of corrosion. The proper maintenance, lubrication, and the use of preservative coatings can prevent corrosion in these areas.

All control cables, whether plain carbon steel or corrosion-resistant steel, are to be inspected to determine their condition at each inspection period. In this process, inspect cables for corrosion by random cleaning of short sections with solvent soaked cloths. If external corrosion is evident, relieve tension and check the cable for internal corrosion. Replace cables that have internal corrosion. Remove light external corrosion with a nonwoven abrasive pad lightly soaked in oil or, alternatively, a steel wire brush. When corrosion products have been removed, recoat the cable with preservative.

## 2. 中文翻译

本节简要讨论了所有飞机共有的多数腐蚀问题区域。这些区域应比不易腐蚀的区域更频繁地清洁、检查和处置。这些信息未必完整，可参阅适用的维护手册，对本机型特有的情况加以补充和扩展。

### 排气尾迹区域

喷气发动机和往复式发动机的排气沉积物都极具腐蚀性，在排气管道或尾喷管下游的缝隙、接缝、铰链和整流罩处尤其容易出问题。沉积物可能滞留其中，常规清洁方法无法触及。要特别注意铆钉头周围、蒙皮搭接缝及其他缝隙处的区域。应拆下并检查排气区域内的整流罩和检查口盖。不要忽视尾翼面等远处区域的排气沉积物堆积。这些区域的堆积较慢，可能直到腐蚀损伤开始后才被注意到。

### 电瓶舱与电瓶通气口

尽管防护漆层以及密封和通气方法都有改进，电瓶舱仍然是易腐蚀区域。过热电解液产生的酸雾难以抑制，会扩散到相邻的空腔，对所有未受保护的金属表面造成快速腐蚀侵蚀。飞机蒙皮上的电瓶通气口应纳入电瓶舱的检查和维护程序。如果使用带硫酸或氢氧化钾电解液的飞机电瓶，其渗漏会引起腐蚀。定期清洁并中和酸性沉积物，可将由此引起的腐蚀降到最低。应查阅该型飞机适用的维护手册，确定所装电瓶的类型和推荐的维护方法。

这些地方是废液压油、水、污物和零星碎屑的天然聚集点。残留的油常常掩盖少量沉到底部的水，从而形成一个隐蔽的化学电池。对于舱底水，现在的浮筒制造商不建议采用化学处理，而建议认真维护制造时涂在浮筒内部的内部涂层。除制造时对钣金及其他结构件表面施加的化学转化膜，以及接缝中安装的密封胶之外，内部隔舱还涂有漆层以保护舱底区域。修理或翻修水上飞机结构时，必须保持这一防护水平。

### 盥洗室、餐室与厨房

这些区域，特别是盥洗室、水槽和炉灶后面的地板区域，如果不保持清洁，溢出的食物和废弃物就会在那里积聚，是潜在的问题点。即使有些污染物本身不具腐蚀性，它们也会吸湿并保持潮湿，进而引起腐蚀侵蚀。要注意厨房和盥洗室下方的舱底区域。应经常清洁这些区域，并维护防护密封胶和漆层。

### 轮舱与起落架

飞机上大概没有哪个区域比这里遭受更多的泥、水、盐、砾石和其他飞溅碎屑的侵袭。由于形状、组件和接头复杂多样，完整的区域漆膜覆盖难以达到并维持。部分涂覆的防腐剂往往会掩盖腐蚀，而不是防止腐蚀。由于刹车产生热量，某些主起落架机轮上不能使用防腐剂。检查该区域时，要特别注意下列问题点：

1. 镁制机轮，特别是螺栓头、凸耳和轮辐区域周围，查看是否有积水及其影响

2. 外露的刚性导管，特别是 B 型螺母和套圈处、卡箍和导管识别胶带下面

3. 外露的位置指示器开关和其他电气设备

4. 加强件、翼肋和下蒙皮表面之间典型的积水和碎屑滞留缝隙

5. 轮轴内部

6. 支柱、缓冲筒、臂、连杆及连接件（螺栓、销等）的外露表面

### 积水区域

设计规范要求飞机在所有可能积水的区域安装排水孔。每日检查低点排水孔是标准要求。如果忽视这项检查，排水孔就可能因积聚的碎屑、润滑脂或密封胶而失效。

低点排水孔应比其他区域更频繁地检查。

### 发动机前部区域与冷却空气道

这些区域不断受到空气中的污物和灰尘、跑道上的砾石碎屑以及雨水的磨蚀，从而导致防护涂层被去除。此外，散热器芯、往复式发动机气缸散热片等可能因散热要求而不能涂漆。发动机附件安装座在机加工安装面上通常有一小片未涂漆的镁或铝表面。检查这些区域时必须包括冷却空气通路中的所有部位，特别注意海上运行期间可能形成盐沉积的地方。必须抑制初期腐蚀，并始终维持水上飞机和水陆两栖飞机发动机表面上的补漆和硬膜防腐涂层。

### 襟翼与扰流板凹槽

污物和水可能在不被注意的情况下积聚在襟翼和扰流板凹槽中，因为这些部件通常处于收起状态。因此，这些凹槽是潜在的腐蚀问题区域。检查这些区域时，应把扰流板和襟翼置于完全展开位置。

### 外蒙皮区域

飞机外表面易于看到，也便于接近检查和维护。即使在这里，某些类型的构型或材料组合在某些使用条件下也会成为麻烦，需要特别注意。

如果原始表面涂层和绝缘得到充分维护，镁蒙皮上的腐蚀问题相对较少。修剪、钻孔和铆接会破坏部分原始表面处理，且永远无法通过补漆程序完全恢复。任何腐蚀检查都必须包括所有镁蒙皮表面，特别注意边缘、紧固件周围以及开裂、剥落或缺失漆层的地方。

钢琴式铰链是腐蚀的高发部位，因为钢销与铝铰链之间存在异种金属接触。它们也是污物、盐和湿气的天然滞留处。检查铰链时必须包括润滑，并使铰链往复活动若干次，以确保润滑剂完全渗透。维护钢琴式铰链时应使用驱水型润滑剂。

用点焊连接的金属蒙皮腐蚀，是腐蚀性介质进入并滞留在金属层之间造成的。这种腐蚀表现为在腐蚀性介质进入的缝隙处出现腐蚀产物。进一步发展的腐蚀侵蚀会造成蒙皮鼓胀，并最终导致焊点断裂。初期鼓胀可通过沿点焊焊缝目视或用直尺检查来发现。防止这种情况的唯一办法，是让可能出现的水分进入点（包括接缝和断焊点形成的孔）始终填充密封胶或合适的防腐剂。

### 电子与电气设备舱

由冲压空气或压气机引气冷却的电子电气设备舱，会经历与发动机及附件冷却风道、发动机前部区域相同的条件。虽然受侵害的程度较轻（因为通过的空气量较少，并且采用了防止封闭空间内形成水的特殊设计），这仍是一个需要特别注意的问题区域。断路器、触点和开关对湿气和腐蚀侵蚀极为敏感，因此必须在设计允许的范围内尽可能彻底地检查这些情况。如果设计特点妨碍在安装状态下检查这些项目，则在因其他原因拆下部件后进行检查。

### 其他问题区域

直升机旋翼头和齿轮箱除了持续暴露于环境之中，还含有裸钢表面、许多外部运动件和异种金属接触。应经常检查这些区域有无腐蚀迹象。适当的维护、润滑和使用防腐涂层可以防止这些区域发生腐蚀。

所有操纵钢索，不论普通碳钢还是耐蚀钢，都应在每个检查周期检查以确定其状况。在此过程中，用蘸有溶剂的布随机清洁短段，检查钢索有无腐蚀。如果外部腐蚀明显，应放松张力（原文此处夹有图注碎片，非正文）并检查钢索有无内部腐蚀。更换有内部腐蚀的钢索。用轻蘸油的无纺磨料垫，或者改用钢丝刷，清除轻微的外部腐蚀。清除腐蚀产物后，给钢索重新涂覆防腐剂。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 表语前置引起的完全倒装
**原句**：Discussed briefly in this section are most of the corrosion problem areas common to all aircraft.
**拆解**：正常语序为 Most of the corrosion problem areas common to all aircraft are discussed briefly in this section；这里把过去分词短语 Discussed briefly in this section 提到句首作表语，主语 most of the corrosion problem areas 后置，形成完全倒装；common to all aircraft 是形容词短语后置修饰 areas。
**要点**：这种倒装多用于承接上文、强调「本节要讲的是……」，常见于技术文献的段首；倒装后谓语仍与真正的主语一致（are 对应复数 areas），不能因为句首是单数 section 就用 is；be common to 意为「为……所共有」。

### ② 三个并列过去分词 + 比较级
**原句**：These areas should be cleaned, inspected, and treated more frequently than less corrosion prone areas.
**拆解**：主语 These areas，谓语 should be cleaned, inspected, and treated 为「情态动词 + be + 三个并列过去分词」；more frequently than less corrosion prone areas 是副词比较级结构，than 后接比较对象 less corrosion prone areas。
**要点**：并列的过去分词共用一个 be，第二个、第三个前不再重复 be；比较对象须对等（区域与区域比），不能写成 than less corrosion prone；less corrosion prone 是「less + 形容词」的降级比较，意即「不太容易腐蚀的」。

### ③ 并列复合谓语 + 过去分词后置定语
**原句**：This information is not necessarily complete and may be amplified and expanded to cover the special characteristics of the particular aircraft model involved by referring to the applicable maintenance manual.
**拆解**：主语 This information 带两个并列谓语：is not necessarily complete（系表结构）与 may be amplified and expanded（情态动词 + 被动）；to cover… 是目的状语；involved 是过去分词后置修饰 the particular aircraft model；by referring to… 是方式状语。
**要点**：not necessarily 表示「未必」，不是「一定不」；involved 作定语时后置，意为「所涉及的」，the model involved 即「该机型」；by + 动名词表示手段，与前面的被动谓语呼应，说明「通过查阅手册来补充」。

### ④ because of + 名词短语表原因
**原句**：If this inspection is neglected, the drains may become ineffective because of accumulated debris, grease, or sealants.
**拆解**：if 引导条件状语从句，主语 this inspection，谓语 is neglected 为被动；主句 the drains may become ineffective 为主系表结构（become 为系动词）；because of accumulated debris, grease, or sealants 是介词短语作原因状语，三个名词并列。
**要点**：because of 后接名词或动名词，because 后接句子，二者不可混用；become 后接形容词表示状态变化；accumulated 是过去分词作定语，修饰 debris。

### ⑤ It is imperative that + 并列的两个 that 从句
**原句**：It is imperative that incipient corrosion be inhibited and that paint touchup and hard film preservative coatings are maintained on seaplane and amphibian engine surfaces at all times.
**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正主语是两个并列的 that 从句：that incipient corrosion be inhibited 与 that paint touchup and hard film preservative coatings are maintained…；第一个从句用虚拟语气（should be 省略 should，故为 be inhibited），第二个从句用陈述语气 are maintained；at all times 为时间状语。
**要点**：imperative 后的 that 从句要求虚拟语气，谓语用动词原形；同一句中并列的第二个 that 从句若表示事实性要求，可用陈述语气，这是手册中常见的混用；incipient 意为「初期的」，incipient corrosion 指刚出现的腐蚀，须尽早抑制。
