# Protective Paint Finishes

> 通用 Ch 08 · Protective Paint Finishes · 原文 PDF 第 348 页

## 1. 原文

A good, intact paint finish is the most effective barrier between metal surfaces and corrosive media. The most common finishes include catalyzed polyurethane enamel, waterborne polyurethane enamel, and two-part epoxy paint. As new regulations regarding the emission of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) are put into effect, the use of waterborne paint systems have increased in popularity. Also, still available are nitrate and butyrate dope finishes for fabric-covered aircraft. In addition, high visibility fluorescent materials may also be used, along with a variety of miscellaneous combinations of special materials. There may also be rain erosion resistant coatings on metal leading edges and several different baked enamel finishes on engine cases and wheels.

## 2. 中文翻译

完好无损的漆层是金属表面与腐蚀性介质之间最有效的屏障。最常见的漆层包括催化型聚氨酯瓷漆、水性聚氨酯瓷漆和双组分环氧漆。随着有关挥发性有机化合物（VOC）排放的新法规生效，水性漆系统的使用日益普及。此外，仍有用于织物蒙皮飞机的硝酸酯和丁酸酯涂布漆（dope）可供使用。还可使用高可见度荧光材料，以及各种特殊材料的其他组合。金属前缘上还可能有抗雨蚀涂层，发动机机匣和机轮上则有几种不同的烘干瓷漆。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 主系表 + 最高级比较范围
**原句**：A good, intact paint finish is the most effective barrier between metal surfaces and corrosive media.
**拆解**：主语 A good, intact paint finish（两个前置定语），系动词 is，表语 the most effective barrier；between…and… 为介词短语作后置定语，限定比较范围。
**要点**：最高级后常接 in/of/between 短语交代范围；good, intact 并列作定语，intact 意为“完好无损的”，不能译成“完整的”而丢掉“未受损”的含义。

### ② as 引导时间状语从句 + 现在完成时
**原句**：As new regulations regarding the emission of volatile organic compounds (VOCs) are put into effect, the use of waterborne paint systems have increased in popularity.
**拆解**：As 引导时间状语从句，主语 new regulations，regarding… 为介词短语作后置定语，谓语 are put into effect（被动）；主句主语 the use of waterborne paint systems，谓语 have increased。
**要点**：原文主句谓语用 have 与单数主语 the use 不一致，属原文笔误，阅读时按 has increased 理解；as 在此表“随着”，不是“因为”。

### ③ 表语前置倒装
**原句**：Also, still available are nitrate and butyrate dope finishes for fabric-covered aircraft.
**拆解**：正常语序为 nitrate and butyrate dope finishes… are still available；为避免主语过长、保持句子平衡，把表语 still available 提到句首，形成完全倒装。
**要点**：倒装后谓语 are 与真正主语 finishes 一致；also 与 still 连用表示“此外仍然有”，翻译时还原为正常语序。

### ④ 情态被动 + along with 伴随状语
**原句**：In addition, high visibility fluorescent materials may also be used, along with a variety of miscellaneous combinations of special materials.
**拆解**：主语 high visibility fluorescent materials，谓语 may also be used（情态被动）；along with… 为介词短语作伴随状语，说明同时使用的其他材料。
**要点**：along with 连接的成分不影响主谓一致；a variety of 后接复数名词，miscellaneous 意为“各种各样的”，不要与 various 混用。

### ⑤ There be + 情态动词
**原句**：There may also be rain erosion resistant coatings on metal leading edges and several different baked enamel finishes on engine cases and wheels.
**拆解**：There be 存在句，may 表可能性；真正主语为并列的 rain erosion resistant coatings 与 several different baked enamel finishes，on… 为地点状语。
**要点**：There be 后的真正主语决定 be 的单复数，此处因并列主语用复数而用 are 的变体 be；rain erosion resistant 是“抗雨蚀的”复合定语，中心词是 coatings。
