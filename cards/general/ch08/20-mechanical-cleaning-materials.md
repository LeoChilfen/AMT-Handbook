# Mechanical Cleaning Materials

> 通用 Ch 08 · Mechanical Cleaning Materials · 原文 PDF 第 353 页

## 1. 原文

Mechanical cleaning materials must be used with care and in accordance with directions given, if damage to finishes and surfaces is to be avoided.

### Mild Abrasive Materials

No attempt is made in this section to furnish detailed instructions for using various materials listed. Some “do’s and don’ts” are included as an aid in selecting materials for specific cleaning jobs.

The introduction of various grades of nonwoven abrasive pads has given the AMT a clean, inexpensive material for the removal of corrosion products and for other light abrasive needs. The pads can be used on most metals (although the same pad should not be used on different metals) and are generally the first choice when the situation arises. A very open form of this pad is also available for paint stripping when used in conjunction with wet strippers.

Powdered pumice can be used for cleaning corroded aluminum surfaces. Similar mild abrasives may also be used. Impregnated cotton wadding material is used for removal of exhaust gas stains and polishing corroded aluminum surfaces. It may also be used on other metal surfaces to produce a high reflectance.

Aluminum metal polish is used to produce a high luster, long lasting polish on unpainted aluminum clad surfaces. It must not be used on anodized surfaces, because it removes the oxide coat.

Three grades of aluminum wool, coarse, medium, and fine are used for general cleaning of aluminum surfaces. Impregnated nylon webbing material is preferred over aluminum wool for the removal of corrosion products and stubborn paint films and for the scuffing of existing paint finishes prior to touchup.

Lacquer rubbing compound material can be used to remove engine exhaust residues and minor oxidation. Avoid heavy rubbing over rivet heads or edges where protective coatings may be worn thin.

Abrasive papers used on aircraft surfaces must not contain sharp or needlelike abrasives that can imbed themselves in the base metal being cleaned or in the protective coating being maintained. The abrasives used must not corrode the material being cleaned. Aluminum oxide paper, 300 grit or finer, is available in several forms and is safe to use on most surfaces. Type I, Class 2 material under Federal Specification P-C-451 is available in 1 1⁄2" and 2" widths. Avoid the use of carborundum (silicon carbide) papers, particularly on aluminum or magnesium. The grain structure of carborundum is sharp and the material is so hard that individual grains penetrate and bury themselves, even in steel surfaces. The use of emery paper or crocus cloth on aluminum or magnesium can cause serious corrosion of the metal by imbedded iron oxide.

## 2. 中文翻译

如果要避免损坏漆层和表面，必须小心并按照所给说明使用机械清洁材料。

### 温和磨料

本节并不试图提供所列各种材料使用的详细说明，只列入一些“注意事项”，以帮助为具体清洁作业选择材料。

各种等级的无纺研磨垫的问世，为 AMT 提供了一种清洁、廉价的材料，用于去除腐蚀产物和满足其他轻度磨蚀需求。这种研磨垫可用于大多数金属（但同一块研磨垫不应先后用于不同金属），在遇到此类需求时通常是首选。还有一种孔隙很开放的形式，可与湿式脱漆剂配合用于脱漆。

粉状浮石可用于清洁腐蚀的铝表面。类似的温和磨料也可使用。浸渍棉絮材料用于去除排气烟渍和抛光腐蚀的铝表面。它也可用于其他金属表面，以获得高反射率。

铝金属抛光剂用于在未涂漆的包铝表面获得高光泽、持久的抛光效果。不得用于阳极氧化表面，因为它会去除氧化膜。

粗、中、细三种等级的铝丝棉用于铝表面的一般清洁。在去除腐蚀产物和顽固漆膜，以及在补漆前打磨现有漆层时，浸渍尼龙编织材料比铝丝棉更受推荐。

硝基漆擦光剂材料可用于去除发动机排气残留物和轻微氧化。避免在铆钉头或保护涂层可能已磨损变薄的边缘处用力擦磨。

用于飞机表面的砂纸不得含有会嵌入被清洁基体金属或所维护保护涂层的尖锐或针状磨料。所用磨料不得腐蚀被清洁的材料。氧化铝砂纸，300 目或更细，有几种形式，可安全用于大多数表面。联邦规范 P-C-451 中的Ⅰ型 2 类材料有 1 1⁄2 英寸和 2 英寸宽度。避免使用碳化硅（金刚砂）砂纸，尤其是在铝或镁上。碳化硅的磨粒结构尖锐，材料很硬，单个磨粒会刺入并埋入表面，即使在钢表面也是如此。在铝或镁上使用金刚砂纸或氧化铁抛光布会导致嵌入的氧化铁使金属严重腐蚀。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 条件从句后置 + 被动
**原句**：Mechanical cleaning materials must be used with care and in accordance with directions given, if damage to finishes and surfaces is to be avoided.
**拆解**：主句 Mechanical cleaning materials must be used，with care 与 in accordance with directions given 为方式状语（given 为过去分词后置修饰 directions）；if 从句用 is to be avoided（be to 被动）表目的条件。
**要点**：条件从句后置时用逗号与主句隔开；directions given 等于 directions that are given，过去分词单独作定语表被动完成。

### ② 双宾语 + 现在完成时
**原句**：The introduction of various grades of nonwoven abrasive pads has given the AMT a clean, inexpensive material for the removal of corrosion products and for other light abrasive needs.
**拆解**：主语 The introduction（of… 为其后置定语），谓语 has given（现在完成时），间接宾语 the AMT，直接宾语 a clean, inexpensive material；for…and for… 两个目的状语并列。
**要点**：give sb sth 是双宾结构，变成被动时两种宾语都可作主语；主语中心词是 introduction，不是 pads，故谓语用 has。

### ③ 括号插入的让步从句 + 并列谓语
**原句**：The pads can be used on most metals (although the same pad should not be used on different metals) and are generally the first choice when the situation arises.
**拆解**：主语 The pads，第一个谓语 can be used，括号内 although 引导让步从句；and 连接第二个谓语 are the first choice；when the situation arises 为时间状语从句。
**要点**：括号中的让步从句不改变主句结构，翻译时可前置或保留括号；be the first choice 是“首选”，不是“第一个选择”。

### ④ be preferred over + for 动名词
**原句**：Impregnated nylon webbing material is preferred over aluminum wool for the removal of corrosion products and stubborn paint films and for the scuffing of existing paint finishes prior to touchup.
**拆解**：主语 Impregnated nylon webbing material，谓语 is preferred over（被动比较），for the removal… and for the scuffing… 两个目的状语并列，prior to touchup 为时间状语。
**要点**：prefer A over B 的被动式为 A is preferred over B；for 后接动名词短语，scuffing 意为“打磨、擦毛”，不是“划伤”。

### ⑤ so…that 结果从句
**原句**：The grain structure of carborundum is sharp and the material is so hard that individual grains penetrate and bury themselves, even in steel surfaces.
**拆解**：前一分句主系表；后一分句 the material is so hard that…，so 修饰形容词 hard，that 引导结果从句，从句谓语 penetrate 与 bury 并列，themselves 指 grains。
**要点**：so + 形容词 + that 与 such + 名词 + that 对应；反身代词 themselves 与主语 individual grains 保持一致。

### ⑥ must not be used + because 原因从句
**原句**：It must not be used on anodized surfaces, because it removes the oxide coat.
**拆解**：主语 It（指铝金属抛光剂），谓语 must not be used（情态被动否定）；because 引导原因状语从句，it 仍指抛光剂。
**要点**：must not 表“禁止”，不是“不必”（need not）；because 从句说明禁用的原因，翻译时通常后置为“因为……”。
