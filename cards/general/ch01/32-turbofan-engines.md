# Turbofan Engines

> 通用 Ch 01 · Turbofan Engines · 原文 PDF 第 37 页

## 1. 原文

Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft, the turbine-powered aircraft does not require a preflight run-up unless it is necessary to investigate a suspected malfunction.

Before starting, all protective covers and air inlet duct covers are removed. If possible, head the aircraft into the wind to obtain better cooling, faster starting, and smoother engine performance. It is especially important that the aircraft be headed into the wind if the engine is to be trimmed.

The run-up area around the aircraft is cleared of both personnel and loose equipment. The turbofan engine intake and exhaust hazard areas are illustrated in Figure 1-17 . Exercise care to ensure that the run-up area is clear of all items, such as nuts, bolts, rocks, shop towels, or other loose debris. Many very serious accidents have occurred involving personnel in the vicinity of turbine engine air inlets. Use extreme caution when starting turbine aircraft.

Check the aircraft fuel sumps for water or ice. Inspect the engine air inlet for general condition and the presence of foreign objects. Visually inspect the fan blades, forward compressor blades, and the compressor inlet guide vanes for nicks and other damage. If possible, check the fan blades for free rotation by turning the fan blades by hand. All engine controls must be operational. Check engine instruments and warning lights for proper operation.

## 2. 中文翻译

与活塞发动机飞机不同，涡轮动力飞机不需要在飞行前试车，除非需要查明某个可疑的故障。

起动前，所有保护盖和进气道堵盖都要取下。条件允许时，应让飞机机头迎风，以获得更好的冷却、更快的起动和更平稳的发动机工作。如果要对发动机进行试车调整（trim），机头迎风就尤其重要。

飞机周围的试车区域要清空人员和松散设备。涡扇发动机进气道与排气危险区见图 1-17。要格外小心，确保试车区内没有任何杂物，例如螺母、螺栓、石块、抹布或其他松散碎屑。许多非常严重的事故都发生在涡轮发动机进气道附近的人员身上。起动涡轮飞机时必须极其谨慎。

检查飞机各燃油沉淀槽（sump）有无水或冰。检查发动机进气道的总体状况和有无外来物。目视检查风扇叶片、前几级压气机叶片和压气机进口导流叶片有无缺口和其他损伤。条件允许时，用手转动风扇叶片，检查其能否自由转动。所有发动机操纵机构都必须工作正常。检查发动机仪表和警告灯工作是否正常。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Unlike 对比状语 + unless 条件从句

**原句**：Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft, the turbine-powered aircraft does not require a preflight run-up unless it is necessary to investigate a suspected malfunction.

**拆解**：Unlike reciprocating engine aircraft 是介词短语作对比状语（Unlike + 名词，不用 like 表对比）；主句主语 the turbine-powered aircraft，谓语 does not require，宾语 a preflight run-up；unless 引导否定条件状语从句，从句用 it 作形式主语，真正主语是 to investigate a suspected malfunction。

**要点**：unless = if not，但语气更「除非」；从句里 it is necessary to do 是形式主语句型。Unlike 必须接名词/名词短语，不能接从句（接从句要用 whereas 或 while）。

### ② It is important that + 虚拟语气（be 型）

**原句**：It is especially important that the aircraft be headed into the wind if the engine is to be trimmed.

**拆解**：It 是形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句；从句主语 the aircraft，谓语 be headed 是虚拟语气（省略 should 的 be 型虚拟），into the wind 是方向状语；if the engine is to be trimmed 是条件状语从句，is to be trimmed 表示「打算/需要被调整」。

**要点**：It is important/necessary/essential that… 之后用 (should) + 动词原形，be 动词一律用 be，不用 is；这是考试高频点，别写成 is headed。is to do 表示按计划或按需要「要……」，工程文本中常见。

### ③ be cleared of 被动搭配

**原句**：The run-up area around the aircraft is cleared of both personnel and loose equipment.

**拆解**：主语 The run-up area，around the aircraft 是后置定语；谓语 is cleared 是被动语态，of both personnel and loose equipment 是 of 短语表示「清除掉的对象」，both…and… 连接两个并列名词。

**要点**：clear A of B = 「把 B 从 A 中清除」，被动形式 A is cleared of B 是手册里描述现场准备的固定说法；不要与 be clear of（「没有……的」形容词短语）混淆，后者是状态而非动作。

### ④ 现在完成时 + 分词短语后置修饰

**原句**：Many very serious accidents have occurred involving personnel in the vicinity of turbine engine air inlets.

**拆解**：主干 Many very serious accidents have occurred 是现在完成时（强调到现在为止已发生的事故）；involving personnel… 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 accidents（事故涉及人员），in the vicinity of… 是地点定语修饰 personnel。

**要点**：现在分词短语作定语时与被修饰名词是主动关系，accidents involving personnel = accidents that involve personnel；若写成 involved 则变成「被卷入的」，语义反转。现在完成时在这里表示「历来已经发生过多起」，与一般过去时（某一次事故）不同。
