## 1. 原文

The following procedures are typical of those used to start many turbine engines. There are, however, wide variations in the starting procedures used for turbine engines, and no attempts are to be made to use these procedures in the actual starting of an engine. These procedures are presented only as a general guide for familiarization with typical procedures and methods. In the starting of all turbine engines, refer to the detailed procedures contained in the applicable manufacturer’s instructions or their approved equivalent.

Most turbofan engines can be started by either air turbine or electrical starters. Air-turbine starters use compressed air from an external source as discussed earlier. Fuel is turned on either by moving the start lever to “idle/start” position or by opening a fuel shutoff valve. If an air turbine starter is used,

Most turbofan engine controls consist of a power lever, reversing levers, and start levers. Newer aircraft have replaced the start levers with a fuel switch. [Figure 1-18] Turbofan engines also use all the normal instruments speeds, (percent of total rpm) exhaust gas temperature, fuel flow, oil pressure, and temperature. An instrument that measures the amount of thrust being delivered is the engine pressure ratio. This measures the ratio between the inlet pressures to the outlet pressure of the turbine.

The following procedures are useful only as a general guide and are included to show the sequence of events in starting a turbofan engine.

1. If the engine is so equipped, place the power lever in the “idle” position.

2. Turn the fuel boost pump(s) switch on.

3. A fuel inlet pressure indicator reading ensures fuel is being delivered to engine fuel pump inlet.

4. Turn engine starter switch on. Note that the engine rotates to a preset limit. Check for oil pressure.

5. Turn ignition switch on. (This is usually accomplished by moving the start lever toward the “on” position.

A micro switch connected to the leveler turns on the ignition.)

6. Move the start lever to “idle” or “start” position, this starts fuel flow into the engine.

7. Engine start (light off) is indicated by a rise in exhaust gas temperature.

8. If a two-spool engine, check rotation of fan or N1.

9. Check for proper oil pressure.

10. Turn engine starter switch off at proper speeds.

11. After engine stabilizes at idle, ensure that none of the engine limits are exceeded.

12. Newer aircraft drop off the starter automatically.

大多数涡扇发动机的操纵机构由功率杆、反推杆与起动杆组成。较新的航空器已用燃油开关取代起动杆。（原文此处有图 1-18）涡扇发动机同样使用全部常规仪表：转速（以总转速的百分比表示）、排气温度、燃油流量、滑油压力与温度。测量所输出推力的仪表是发动机压力比（EPR），它测量涡轮进口压力与出口压力之比。

以下程序仅作通用指南使用，列出它是为了说明起动涡扇发动机的事件顺序。

1. 如发动机有此装备，把功率杆置于「idle」位。

2. 打开燃油增压泵开关。

3. 燃油进口压力表有读数，即可确认燃油已供到发动机燃油泵进口。

4. 接通发动机起动机开关。注意发动机将转动到某一预设限值。检查滑油压力。

5. 接通点火开关。（这通常通过把起动杆向「on」位移动来实现。

与杆相连的微动开关随即接通点火。）

6. 把起动杆移到「idle」或「start」位，燃油开始流入发动机。

7. 发动机起动（点着火）表现为排气温度上升。

8. 若是双转子发动机，检查风扇或 N1 的转动。

9. 检查滑油压力是否正常。

10. 在规定的转速下关闭发动机起动机开关。

11. 发动机在慢车稳定后，确认未超过任何发动机限制值。

12. 较新的航空器会自动脱开起动机。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be to + 被动不定式：no attempts are to be made

**原句**：There are, however, wide variations in the starting procedures used for turbine engines, and no attempts are to be made to use these procedures in the actual starting of an engine.

**拆解**：and 连接两个分句。前一分句是 there be 句型 There are … wide variations，however 是插入语，in the starting procedures 带过去分词短语 used for turbine engines 作后置定语；后一分句主语 no attempts，谓语 are to be made（be to + 被动不定式），to use these procedures in the actual starting of an engine 是补足说明。

**要点**：be to do 在手册里表「应当／不得」，no attempts are to be made 相当于 must not be attempted，是极正式的禁止表述，比祈使句 do not attempt 更像条文。注意此处不是将来时。attempt 在此作名词，no attempts 是复数主语，故谓语用 are。

### ② 插入的省略条件结构 if exceeded

**原句**：This time interval, if exceeded, indicates a malfunction has occurred and the start must be discontinued.

**拆解**：主语 This time interval 与谓语 indicates 之间插入 if exceeded，是 if it is exceeded 的省略（主语 it 与 be 一起省去）；indicates 后接省略 that 的宾语从句 a malfunction has occurred，and 再连接第二个宾语从句 the start must be discontinued。

**要点**：if + 过去分词 的省略结构（if exceeded / if required / if necessary）在技术文本里极常见，省略的主语必须能从上下文找回，此处即 this time interval。indicate 后省略 that 很常见，但并列两个宾语从句时第二个从句须自带主语 the start。must be discontinued 是被动，主语是 the start——「起动必须被中止」。

### ③ 定语从句 + 现在分词被动式 being delivered

**原句**：An instrument that measures the amount of thrust being delivered is the engine pressure ratio.

**拆解**：主语 An instrument 带定语从句 that measures the amount of thrust；being delivered 是现在分词的被动式，后置修饰 the amount of thrust，表示「正在被输出的推力」；谓语是 is，表语 the engine pressure ratio。全句骨架为 A that… is B。

**要点**：being + 过去分词 表示「正在被……」，与单独的过去分词 delivered（已经输出的）不同，此处强调推力是持续输出中的量。这类「A that… is B」的定义句在手册里用于给术语下定义，翻译成「测量……的仪表就是……」最贴合。读长句时先跳过定语从句找到 is，再回头补修饰成分。
