# Types of Fuel and Identification

> 通用 Ch 01 · Types of Fuel and Identification · 原文 PDF 第 47 页

## 1. 原文

Two types of aviation fuel in general use are aviation gasoline, also known as A VGAS, and turbine fuel, also known as JET A fuel.

Aviation gasoline (A VGAS) is used in reciprocating engine aircraft. Currently, there are three grades of fuel in general use: 80/87, 100/130, and 100LL (low lead). A fourth grade, 115/145, is in limited use in the large reciprocating-engine aircraft. The two numbers indicate the lean mixture and rich mixture octane rating numbers of the specific fuel. In other words, with 80/87 A VGAS, the 80 is the lean mixture rating and 87 is the rich mixture rating number. To avoid confusing the types of A VGAS, it is generally identified as grade 80, 100, 100LL, or 115. A VGAS can also be identified by a color code. The color of the fuel needs to match the color band on piping and fueling equipment. [Figure 1-31]

Turbine fuel/jet fuel is used to power turbojet and turbo- shaft engines. Three types of turbine fuel generally used in civilian aviation are JET A and JET A-1, made from kerosene, and JET B, a blend of kerosene and A VGAS. While jet fuel is identified by the color black on piping and fueling equipment, the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored.

Before mixing A VGAS and turbine fuel, refer to the Type Certificate Data Sheet for the respective powerplant. Adding jet fuel to A VGAS causes a decrease in the power developed by the engine and could cause damage to the engine (through detonation) and loss of life. Adding A VGAS to jet fuel can cause lead deposits in the turbine engine and can lead to reduced service life.

## 2. 中文翻译

目前普遍使用的航空燃油有两类：航空汽油（AVGAS）和涡轮燃油（JET A 燃油）。

航空汽油（AVGAS）用于活塞式发动机飞机。目前普遍使用三个牌号：80/87、100/130 和 100LL（低铅）。第四个牌号 115/145 仅在大型活塞发动机飞机上有限使用。两个数字分别表示该种燃油的贫油混合气辛烷值和富油混合气辛烷值。换言之，对于 80/87 的 AVGAS，80 是贫油混合气评级，87 是富油混合气评级。为避免混淆 AVGAS 的类型，通常以牌号 80、100、100LL 或 115 来标识。AVGAS 还可以通过颜色代码识别。燃油的颜色必须与管线和加油设备上的色带一致。[图 1-31]

涡轮燃油/喷气燃油用于驱动涡轮喷气发动机和涡轮轴发动机。民用航空普遍使用的涡轮燃油有三种：由煤油制成的 JET A 和 JET A-1，以及由煤油与 AVGAS 混合而成的 JET B。喷气燃油在管线和加油设备上以黑色标识，而喷气燃油的实际颜色可能是无色或草黄色。

在混合 AVGAS 与涡轮燃油之前，应查阅相应动力装置的型号合格证数据表。向 AVGAS 中加入喷气燃油会降低发动机产生的功率，并可能通过爆震造成发动机损坏和人员死亡。向喷气燃油中加入 AVGAS 会在涡轮发动机内形成铅沉积，并可能导致使用寿命缩短。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① there be 存在句与冒号列举
**原句**：Currently, there are three grades of fuel in general use: 80/87, 100/130, and 100LL (low lead).
**拆解**：Currently 时间状语 + there are 存在句（谓语与后面的 three grades 一致）+ in general use（介词短语作后置定语）+ 冒号引出三项列举。
**要点**：there be 的 be 与紧随其后的名词数一致（three grades → are）；冒号后列举用逗号分隔、最后一项前加 and，是技术文本说明「具体是哪几个」的常见写法。

### ② 同位语插入
**原句**：A fourth grade, 115/145, is in limited use in the large reciprocating-engine aircraft.
**拆解**：主语 A fourth grade + 同位语 115/145（用逗号隔开，说明牌号具体是多少）+ 系动词 is + 表语 in limited use（be in use 的变体）+ in the large reciprocating-engine aircraft（范围状语）。
**要点**：be in use = 在使用中，in limited use = 有限使用；同位语用逗号夹在主语和谓语之间，去掉后句子仍完整，阅读时先跳过。

### ③ 名词叠加修饰
**原句**：The two numbers indicate the lean mixture and rich mixture octane rating numbers of the specific fuel.
**拆解**：主语 The two numbers + 谓语 indicate + 宾语 octane rating numbers（由 lean mixture 和 rich mixture 两个名词短语前置修饰）+ of the specific fuel（后置定语）。
**要点**：英语名词可以直接前置修饰名词（octane rating numbers = 辛烷值数字），中文要展开成「……的……」；lean/rich mixture 是两个并列修饰语，不要误读成一个整体。

### ④ while 引导对比状语从句
**原句**：While jet fuel is identified by the color black on piping and fueling equipment, the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored.
**拆解**：While 引导对比状语从句（谓语 is identified 为被动，by the color black 表方式）+ 主句 the actual color of jet fuel can be clear or straw colored（can be + 形容词/过去分词作表语）。
**要点**：while 在此不表时间而表「而、虽然」的对比，前后是两个相反的事实（标识色 vs 实际颜色）；straw colored 是「名词 + 过去分词」构成的复合形容词。
