# Securing Light Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 01 · Securing Light Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

## 1. 原文

Light aircraft are most often secured with ropes tied only at the aircraft tie-down rings provided for securing purposes. Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut, since this practice can bend a strut if the rope slips to a point where there is no slack. Since manila rope shrinks when wet, about 1 inch (1") of slack needs to be provided for movement. Too much slack, however, allows the aircraft to jerk against the ropes. Tight tie-down ropes put inverted flight stresses on the aircraft and many are not designed to take such loads.

A tie-down rope holds no better than the knot. Anti-slip knots, such as the bowline, are quickly tied and are easy to untie. [Figure 1-10] Aircraft not equipped with tie-down fittings must be secured in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions. Ropes are to be tied to outer ends of struts on high-wing monoplanes and suitable rings provided where structural conditions permit, if the manufacturer has not already provided them.

## 2. 中文翻译

轻型飞机通常只用绳索固定，绳索只拴在飞机专为系留设置的系留环上。系留绳绝不能拴在升力支柱上：一旦绳索滑到没有余量的位置，这种做法就会把支柱拉弯。马尼拉绳受潮会收缩，因此须留出约 1 英寸（1"）的松量供其活动。但松量过大会让飞机反复冲撞系留绳。系留绳绷得过紧会给飞机施加相当于倒飞状态的应力，而许多飞机并非按承受此类载荷设计。

系留绳牢不牢，全看绳结。防滑绳结（例如称人结/bowline）打结快、解开也容易。[图 1-10] 未装系留接头的飞机，必须按制造商的说明固定。上单翼飞机的绳索应拴在支柱外端；若制造商未预先提供合适的系留环，则应在结构条件允许处设置。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be never to be done：手册式禁止
**原句**：Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut, since this practice can bend a strut if the rope slips to a point where there is no slack.
**拆解**：主句 Rope is never to be tied to a lift strut（be to + 被动表禁止/规定）；since 引导原因状语从句；从句内 if 引导条件状语从句，a point 后接 where 引导的定语从句。
**要点**：be never to be + 过去分词是手册里“严禁被…”的书面表达，比 must not be 更强调“规定如此”；since 在此意为“因为”，不是“自从”，读句时先看逗号后的主句。

### ② 省略式时间状语 when wet 与 need to be done
**原句**：Since manila rope shrinks when wet, about 1 inch (1") of slack needs to be provided for movement.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，when wet 是其内部的省略式时间状语（= when it is wet）；主句 about 1 inch (1") of slack needs to be provided for movement，主语是“物”，用 need to be done 表被动。
**要点**：when + 形容词/分词 可省去主语和 be；need 作实义动词、以物作主语时后接 to be done，不能写成 needs provided。

### ③ no better than：比较级的否定
**原句**：A tie-down rope holds no better than the knot.
**拆解**：主语 A tie-down rope + 谓语 holds + 比较状语 no better than the knot；no 是限定词，修饰比较级 better。
**要点**：no better than = “并不比…强”，语气比 not better than 更绝对；别误读成“好得多”，否则整句的安全警告就丢了。

### ④ 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Aircraft not equipped with tie-down fittings must be secured in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.
**拆解**：主语 Aircraft + 过去分词短语 not equipped with tie-down fittings 作后置定语；谓语 must be secured（情态 + 被动）；状语 in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions。
**要点**：后置的过去分词短语相当于 who are not equipped with…，比定语从句简洁；in accordance with 是“按照（规章、说明）”的固定搭配，别写成 according with。
