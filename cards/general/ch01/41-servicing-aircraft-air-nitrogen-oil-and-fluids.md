# Servicing Aircraft Air/Nitrogen Oil & Fluids

> 通用 Ch 01 · Servicing Aircraft Air/Nitrogen Oil & Fluids · 原文 PDF 第 42 页

## 1. 原文

Checking or servicing aircraft fluids is an important maintenance function. Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper type of servicing equipment and procedures. In general, aircraft engine oil is checked with a dipstick or a sight gauge. There are markings on the stick or around the sight gauge to determine the correct level. Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown. Dry sump oil systems tend to hide oil that has seeped from the oil tank into the gearcase of the engine. This oil does not show up on the dipstick until the engine has been started or motored. If serviced before this oil is pumped back into the tank, the engine overfills. Never overfill the oil tank. Oil foams as it is circulated through the engine. The expansion space in the oil tank allows for this foaming (oil mixing with air). Also the correct type of oil must be used for the appropriate engine being serviced. Hydraulic fluid, fuel, and oil, if spilled on clothes or skin, must be removed as soon as possible because Lights Meaning Flashing green Cleared to taxi Steady red Stop Flashing red Taxi clear of runway in use Flashing white Return to starting point Alternating red and green Exercise extreme caution of fire danger and health reasons.

When servicing a hydraulic reservoir, the correct fluid must be used. Normally, this can be determined by the container or by color. Some reservoirs are pressurized by air that must be bled off before servicing. Efforts must be made to prevent any type of contamination during servicing. Also, if changing hydraulic filters, assure that the pressure is off the system before removing the filters. After servicing the filters (if large amounts of fluids were lost) or system quantity, air must be purged and the system checked for leaks. While servicing tires or struts with high-pressure nitrogen, the technician must use caution while performing maintenance. Clean areas before connecting filling hose and do not overinflate.

## 2. 中文翻译

检查或勤务飞机液体是一项重要的维修工作。在对任何飞机进行勤务之前，应查阅该机型的具体维修手册，以确定合适的勤务设备和程序。一般来说，发动机滑油用油尺或油位观察窗检查。油尺上或观察窗周围有刻度标记，用来确定正确的油位。往复式发动机应在发动机停转后检查，而涡轮发动机必须在刚关车后检查。干槽式滑油系统容易把从滑油箱渗入发动机附件机匣的滑油藏起来。这部分滑油要等发动机起动或带转之后才会在油尺上显示出来。如果在这部分滑油被泵回滑油箱之前就进行勤务，发动机就会加注过量。绝不要给滑油箱加注过量。滑油在发动机内循环时会起泡。滑油箱内的膨胀空间就是为这种起泡（滑油与空气混合）留出的。此外，所勤务的发动机必须使用正确牌号的滑油。液压油、燃油和滑油如果溅到衣服或皮肤上，必须尽快清除，因为（原文此处插入一张“灯光／含义”（Lights／Meaning）信号表的图内文字残片：Lights／Meaning、Flashing green Cleared to taxi、Steady red Stop、Flashing red Taxi clear of runway in use、Flashing white Return to starting point、Alternating red and green Exercise extreme caution 等，略）出于火灾危险和健康方面的原因。

勤务液压油箱时，必须使用正确的液压油。通常可以通过油桶或颜色来判断。有些油箱是充气加压的，勤务前必须把气压放掉。勤务过程中必须设法防止任何形式的污染。另外，更换液压滤芯时，要在拆卸滤芯前确认系统已卸压。勤务滤芯之后（如果损失了大量液体），或补充系统油量之后，必须排出系统中的空气，并检查系统有无渗漏。用高压氮气给轮胎或支柱充气时，技师在作业过程中必须小心谨慎。连接充气软管之前先清洁各部位，且不要充气过量。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be to + 被动 与 must 的对照
**原句**：Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown.
**拆解**：两个分句形成对比：are to be checked（be to + 被动，表“应被检查”）与 must be checked（情态被动）；after 从句用现在完成时 has been inactive 表状态的延续；while 在此表对比。
**要点**：be to do 与 must 都表义务，但 be to 更偏“按规程应当”，must 语气更硬；while 在这里不是“当……时”，而是“而”，表两种发动机的对照。

### ② never 引导的否定祈使句
**原句**：Never overfill the oil tank.
**拆解**：Never 引导否定祈使句，动词原形 overfill + 宾语 the oil tank。
**要点**：手册中的禁令常用 Never + 动词原形，语气比 do not 更强；overfill 的 over- 前缀表“过度”，与下文不及物的 overfills 形成及物/不及物对照。

### ③ “if + 过去分词”的省略条件句
**原句**：If serviced before this oil is pumped back into the tank, the engine overfills.
**拆解**：If serviced = If it is serviced（省略主语和 be）；before 引导时间状语从句，用被动 is pumped back into the tank；主句 the engine overfills 为不及物用法，主动表“加注过量”。
**要点**：技术文本里 “if + 过去分词” 的省略条件句很常见，条件是省略的主语与主句主语一致；注意主句不能用被动，否则“发动机被加注过量”就说不通了。

### ④ 名词化主语 + efforts must be made to do
**原句**：Efforts must be made to prevent any type of contamination during servicing.
**拆解**：主语 Efforts + 谓语 must be made（情态被动）+ 不定式 to prevent… 作目的（内容）状语；during servicing 为时间状语。
**要点**：make efforts to do 的被动式 efforts must be made to do，是规章里“必须设法……”的固定说法；用名词 efforts 作主语比用 We must prevent 更客观，符合手册不点明执行者的习惯。
