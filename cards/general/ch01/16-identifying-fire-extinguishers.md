# Identifying Fire Extinguishers

> 通用 Ch 01 · Identifying Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

## 1. 原文

Fire extinguishers are marked to indicate suitability for a particular class of fire. The markings on Figure 1-7 must be placed on the fire extinguisher and in a conspicuous place in the vicinity of the fire extinguisher. When the location is marked, however, take extreme care to ensure that the fire extinguisher kept at that location is in fact the type depicted by the marking. In other words, if a location is marked for a Class B fire extinguisher, ensure that the fire extinguisher in that location is in fact suitable for Class B fires.

Markings must be applied by decalcomanias (decals), painting, or similar methods. They are to be legible and as durable as necessary for the location. For example, markings used outside need to be more durable than those in the hangar or office spaces.

When markings are applied to the extinguisher, they are placed on the front of the shell, if one is installed, above or below the extinguisher nameplate. Markings must be large enough and in a form that is easily seen and identifiable by the average person with average eyesight at a distance of at least 3 feet. When markings are applied to wall panels, and so forth, in the vicinity of extinguishers, they must be large enough and in a form that is easily seen and identifiable by the average person with average eyesight at a distance of at least 25 feet. ## 2. 中文翻译

灭火器上标有标记，用以表明其适用于哪一类火灾。图 1-7 所示的标记必须贴在灭火器上，并贴在灭火器附近显眼的位置。但在标注位置时，必须极其小心，确保放在该位置的灭火器确实就是标记所指示的类型。换言之，如果某位置标注的是 B 类灭火器，就要确保该位置的灭火器确实适用于 B 类火灾。

标记必须采用贴花（decal）、涂漆或类似方法施加。标记应清晰可辨，并具备与所处位置相称的耐久性。例如，室外使用的标记需要比机库或办公区内的标记更耐久。

当标记施加在灭火器上时，应贴在筒体正面（若装有筒体）以及铭牌上方或下方。标记必须足够大，且形式应便于普通视力的人在至少 3 英尺的距离上轻易看见并辨认。当标记施加在灭火器附近的墙板等处时，必须足够大，且形式应便于普通视力的人在至少 25 英尺的距离上轻易看见并辨认。[Figure 1-8]

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 目的不定式
**原句**：Fire extinguishers are marked to indicate suitability for a particular class of fire.
**拆解**：主语 Fire extinguishers + 被动谓语 are marked + to indicate… 为目的状语（不定式）；suitability for a particular class of fire 为宾语。
**要点**：be marked to do 表示“被标上（标记）以表明……”；suitability for 是固定搭配，不能用 suitability of；不定式表目的时逻辑主语是标记的施加者，不是灭火器。

### ② when 从句 + 祈使句 + ensure that 宾语从句
**原句**：When the location is marked, however, take extreme care to ensure that the fire extinguisher kept at that location is in fact the type depicted by the marking.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；however 为插入的转折副词；主句为祈使句 take extreme care，后接不定式 to ensure + that 引导的宾语从句；从句主语 the fire extinguisher 带过去分词短语 kept at that location 作后置定语；in fact 为插入语；depicted by the marking 为过去分词短语修饰 the type。
**要点**：take care to do 是固定搭配；ensure 后接 that 从句时不用 should；两个过去分词短语（kept…、depicted…）都作后置定语，翻译时宜前置为“放在该位置的”“标记所指示的”。

### ③ by 引出手段时的平行问题
**原句**：Markings must be applied by decalcomanias (decals), painting, or similar methods.
**拆解**：主语 Markings + 被动谓语 must be applied + by 短语引出施加手段，by 后并列三项：decalcomanias (decals)、painting、similar methods。
**要点**：by 后可接名词或动名词表示手段；此处三项形式并不完全一致（名词+动名词+名词），属原文表达习惯，翻译时按“贴花、涂漆或类似方法”处理，不必强行统一词性。

### ④ as…as necessary 比较结构
**原句**：They are to be legible and as durable as necessary for the location.
**拆解**：主语 They（指标记）+ be to be 表“应当”；表语为并列的 legible 与 as durable as necessary（as…as 比较结构，necessary 后省略了前面的 durable）。
**要点**：as…as necessary 意为“按需要达到……的程度”，是省略式比较，不能补成 as necessary as；be to do 在技术文件中表规定、要求。
