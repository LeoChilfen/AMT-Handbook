# Turboprop Starting Procedures

> 通用 Ch 01 · Turboprop Starting Procedures · 原文 PDF 第 37 页

## 1. 原文

To start an engine on the ground, perform the following operations:

1. Turn the aircraft boost pumps on.

2. Make sure that the power lever is in the “start” position.

3. Place the start switch in the “start” position. This starts the engine turning.

4. Place the ignition switch on. (On some engines, the ignition is activated by moving the fuel lever.)

5. The fuel is now turned on. This is accomplished by moving the condition lever to the “on” position.

6. Monitor the engine lights of the exhaust temperature.

If it exceeds the limits, shut the engine down.

7. Check the oil pressure and temperature.

8. After the engine reaches a self-sustaining speed, the starter is disengaged.

9. The engine continues to accelerate up to idle.

10. Maintain the power lever at the “start” position until the specified minimum oil temperature is reached.

11. Disconnect the ground power supply, if used.

If any of the following conditions occur during the starting sequence, turn off the fuel and ignition switch, discontinue the start immediately, make an investigation, and record the findings.

• Turbine inlet temperature exceeds the specified maximum. Record the observed peak temperature.

• Acceleration time from start of propeller rotation to stabilized rpm exceeds the specified time.

• There is no oil pressure indication at 5,000 rpm for either the reduction gear or the power unit.

• Torching (visible burning in the exhaust nozzle).

• The engine fails to ignite by 4,500 rpm or maximum motoring rpm.

• Abnormal vibration is noted or compressor surge occurs (indicated by backfiring).

• Fire warning bell rings. (This may be due to either an engine fire or overheat.)

## 2. 中文翻译

在地面起动发动机，执行以下操作：

1. 打开航空器增压泵。

2. 确认功率杆处于「start」位。

3. 把起动开关置于「start」位，发动机随即开始转动。

4. 接通点火开关。（在某些发动机上，点火是由移动燃油杆触发的。）

5. 此时接通燃油，做法是把状态杆移到「on」位。

6. 监控排气温度的发动机指示灯。

若超过限制值，应停车。

7. 检查滑油压力与温度。

8. 发动机达到自持转速后，起动机脱开。

9. 发动机继续加速到慢车。

10. 把功率杆保持在「start」位，直到滑油温度达到规定的最低值。

11. 如使用了地面电源，将其断开。

起动过程中若出现下列任一情况，应关闭燃油与点火开关、立即中止起动、进行调查并记录所发现的问题。

- 涡轮进口温度超过规定的最大值。记录观察到的峰值温度。

- 从螺旋桨开始转动到转速稳定的加速时间超过规定值。

- 转速 5,000 rpm 时，减速器或动力部分都没有滑油压力指示。

- 喷火（torching，排气喷口处可见燃烧）。

- 到 4,500 rpm 或最大冷转转速仍未点火成功。

- 察觉异常振动，或发生压气机喘振（表现为回火）。

- 火警铃响。（这可能由发动机起火或过热引起。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 句首不定式表目的 + 祈使句

**原句**：To start an engine on the ground, perform the following operations:

**拆解**：To start an engine on the ground 是不定式短语置于句首，表目的；主句是祈使句 perform the following operations（动词原形 + 宾语）。

**要点**：句首不定式表目的时，其逻辑主语就是祈使句隐含的 you，不必另加主语。手册程序常以 To do X, do Y 或 When doing X, do Y 开头，先判断它是目的还是条件再动笔。the following operations 后接编号清单，译「以下操作」。

### ② 被动语态 + by + 动名词

**原句**：This is accomplished by moving the condition lever to the “on” position.

**拆解**：主语 This（指上一步「接通燃油」这件事），谓语 is accomplished 为被动；by moving the condition lever to the “on” position 是方式状语，by 后接动名词 moving，to 是介词表方向。

**要点**：be accomplished by + 动名词 意为「通过……来实现」，是手册解释操作方法的固定句式，比 you should move 更客观。注意 to the “on” position 中的 to 是介词，不是不定式符号。this 指代前一句整件事，中文要还原成「接通燃油这件事」才通顺。

### ③ 条件从句 + 四个并列祈使动词

**原句**：If any of the following conditions occur during the starting sequence, turn off the fuel and ignition switch, discontinue the start immediately, make an investigation, and record the findings.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，主语 any of the following conditions，谓语 occur（一般现在时，表将来），during the starting sequence 是时间状语；主句由四个祈使动词并列：turn off、discontinue、make、record，最后以 and 收尾。

**要点**：手册的应急条文常把「关断—中止—调查—记录」四步压进一句，用逗号并列、and 收尾，翻译时按顺序编号更清楚。注意 turn off the fuel and ignition switch 是「燃油开关与点火开关」两个开关，不要读成一个。findings 是「发现的问题、调查结论」，不是「找到的东西」。
