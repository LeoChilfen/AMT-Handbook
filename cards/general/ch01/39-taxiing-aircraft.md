## 4. 语法解析

### ① 一般现在时被动 + only 限定主语
**原句**：As a general rule, only rated pilots and qualified airframe and powerplant (A&P) technicians are authorized to start, run up, and taxi aircraft.
**拆解**：As a general rule 为状语；主语 only rated pilots and qualified … technicians；谓语 are authorized（被动）+ 不定式 to start, run up, and taxi 作主语补足语。
**要点**：be authorized to do 表“被授权做……”，是规章限定资格的标准说法；only 放在主语前限定范围，位置一移意思就变（如 are authorized only to start 变成“只被授权起动”）。

### ② 被动语态 + in accordance with 方式状语
**原句**：All taxiing operations are performed in accordance with applicable local regulations.
**拆解**：主语 All taxiing operations + 谓语 are performed（一般现在时被动）+ in accordance with 引导的方式状语。
**要点**：in accordance with 是规范文本里“按照、依据”的标准表达，比 according to 更正式；被动语态让“作业”作主语，省去执行者。

### ③ 过去分词后置定语 + 目的不定式
**原句**：Figure 1-22 contains the standard taxi light signals used by control towers to control and expedite the taxiing of aircraft.
**拆解**：主语 Figure 1-22 + 谓语 contains + 宾语 the standard taxi light signals；used by control towers 为过去分词短语作后置定语；to control and expedite 为目的不定式。
**要点**：过去分词后置定语表被动（信号“被塔台使用”）；to do 作目的状语说明用途，注意 the taxiing of aircraft 用动名词名词化，比 taxi aircraft 更书面。
