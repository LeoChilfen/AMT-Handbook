# Defueling

> 通用 Ch 01 · Defueling · 原文 PDF 第 50 页

## 1. 原文

Defueling procedures differ with different types of aircraft. Before defueling an aircraft, check the maintenance/service manual for specific procedures and cautions. Defueling can be accomplished by gravity defueling or by pumping the fuel out of the tanks. When the gravity method is used, it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel. When the pumping method is used, care must be taken not to damage the tanks, and the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel.

General precautions when defueling are:

• Ground the aircraft and defueling equipment.

• Turn off all electrical and electronic equipment.

• Have the correct type of fire extinguisher available.

• Wear eye protection.

## 2. 中文翻译

抽油程序因飞机类型不同而异。在给飞机抽油之前，应查阅维修/勤务手册了解具体程序和注意事项。抽油可以用重力放油法，也可以把燃油从油箱中泵出。采用重力法时，必须有一种收集燃油的方法。采用泵抽法时，必须小心不要损坏油箱，而且抽出的燃油不能与合格燃油混合。

抽油时的一般注意事项有：

• 将飞机和抽油设备接地。

• 关闭所有电气和电子设备。

• 备好正确类型的灭火器。

• 佩戴护目镜。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 情态动词被动与并列 by 短语
**原句**：Defueling can be accomplished by gravity defueling or by pumping the fuel out of the tanks.
**拆解**：主语 Defueling + 谓语 can be accomplished（情态动词 + 被动）+ by gravity defueling 或 by pumping the fuel out of the tanks（两个并列的方式状语，介词 by 后分别接名词和动名词）。
**要点**：两个 by 短语并列时第二个 by 一般保留，避免误读；by + 动名词表手段（通过泵抽），pumping… out of the tanks 中 out of 是短语介词。

### ② it 作形式主语
**原句**：When the gravity method is used, it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句（谓语 is used 被动）+ 主句 it is necessary to have a method of collecting the fuel（it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to have…；of collecting 为动名词作定语）。
**要点**：it is + 形容词 + to do 是英语避免头重脚轻的常用句式，中文直接译「必须……」；a method of doing sth 中 of 后接动名词，不用 to do。

### ③ 并列句中的情态被动与不定式否定
**原句**：When the pumping method is used, care must be taken not to damage the tanks, and the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel.
**拆解**：when 从句 + 两个并列主句：care must be taken（被动）+ not to damage the tanks（不定式的否定式作状语，说明「小心」的内容）；the removed fuel cannot be mixed with good fuel（情态被动 + mix with 搭配）。
**要点**：take care not to do = 注意不要……，否定词 not 放在 to 之前而不是谓语前；the removed fuel 中 removed 是过去分词作前置定语，表「已被抽出的」，与动名词 removing 区分。

### ④ 祈使句与并列宾语
**原句**：Ground the aircraft and defueling equipment.
**拆解**：祈使句以动词原形 Ground 开头 + 并列宾语 the aircraft 和 defueling equipment（defueling 为动名词作定语，修饰 equipment）。
**要点**：安全注意事项常用祈使句，主语 you 省略；ground 在此是动词「使接地」，与名词 ground（地面）同形，注意 defueling equipment 是「抽油设备」而不是「给设备抽油」。
