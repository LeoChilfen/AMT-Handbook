# Ground Support Air Units

> 通用 Ch 01 · Ground Support Air Units · 原文 PDF 第 45 页

## 1. 原文

Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air that can be used for starting the engines and heating and cooling the aircraft on the ground (using the onboard aircraft systems). It generally consists of an APU built into the cart that provides bleed air from the APU’s compressor for operating aircraft systems or starting engines. [Figure 1-30]

## 2. 中文翻译

气源车用于提供低压（最高 50 psi、大流量）空气，这种空气可用于起动发动机，以及在地面为飞机加热和冷却（利用机上系统）。它一般由装在车上的辅助动力装置（APU）构成，由 APU 压气机提供引气，用于操作飞机系统或起动发动机。[图 1-30]

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be used to do 与 be used for doing 两种“用于”
**原句**：Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air that can be used for starting the engines and heating and cooling the aircraft on the ground (using the onboard aircraft systems).
**拆解**：主句 Air carts are used to provide…（被动 + 目的不定式）；that 引导定语从句修饰 air；for starting… and heating and cooling… 为介词 + 动名词并列表用途；两个括号均为补充说明。
**要点**：be used to do（被用来做……）与 be used for doing（用于……）意思相近，但介词后只能接动名词；that 定语从句的先行词是 air，不是 carts。

### ② 过去分词后置定语 + that 定语从句叠加
**原句**：It generally consists of an APU built into the cart that provides bleed air from the APU’s compressor for operating aircraft systems or starting engines.
**拆解**：主语 It + 谓语 consists of an APU；built into the cart 为过去分词短语作后置定语；that provides… 为定语从句，先行词是 APU；for operating… or starting… 为介词 + 动名词作目的状语。
**要点**：分词短语与定语从句可以叠加修饰同一名词；判断 that 的先行词要看谓语搭配——provides bleed air 的主语只能是 APU，不是 cart。

### ③ 括号插入语补充参数
**原句**：low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air
**拆解**：中心词 low-pressure air，括号插入 up to 50 psi high volume flow，紧贴被修饰的名词，补充压力上限与流量特性；括号内为省略结构，不构成完整句子。
**要点**：技术文本常用括号塞参数而不打断主干；括号内可省主语和谓语（如 up to 50 psi 前省略 the pressure is），读时按“补充说明”处理即可，翻译时宜用“最高……、大流量”这类短语还原。
