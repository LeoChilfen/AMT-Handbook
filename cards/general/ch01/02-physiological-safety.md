# Physiological Safety

> 通用 Ch 01 · Physiological Safety · 原文 PDF 第 23 页

## 1. 原文

Working with electrical equipment poses certain physiological safety hazards. When electricity is applied to the human body, it can create severe burns in the area of entrance and at the point of exit from the body. In addition, the nervous system is affected and can be damaged or destroyed. To safely deal with electricity, the technician must have a working knowledge of the principles of electricity and a healthy respect for its capability to do both work and damage.

Wearing or use of proper safety equipment can provide a psychological assurance and physically protect the user at the same time. The use of rubber gloves, safety glasses, rubber or grounded safety mats, and other safety equipment contributes to the overall safety of the technician working on or with electrical equipment. Two factors that affect safety when dealing with electricity are fear and overconfidence. These two factors are major causes of accidents involving electricity. While a certain amount of respect for electrical equipment is healthy and a certain level of confidence is necessary, extremes of either can be deadly.

Lack of respect is often due to lack of knowledge. Personnel who attempt to work with electrical equipment and have no knowledge of the principles of electricity lack the skills to deal with electrical equipment safely. Overconfidence leads to risk taking. The technician who does not respect the capabilities of electricity will, sooner or later, become a victim of electricity’s power.

## 2. 中文翻译

使用电气设备会带来某些生理性安全危害。电流施加于人体时，会在电流进入人体的部位以及离开人体的部位造成严重烧伤。此外，神经系统也会受到影响，可能受损甚至被毁坏。要安全地应对电，技术人员必须掌握电学原理的实用知识，并对电既能做功、又能造成伤害的能力保持适度的敬畏。

正确穿戴或使用合适的防护装备，既能给人心理上的保障，又能同时保护使用者的人身。使用橡胶手套、护目镜、橡胶垫或接地安全垫以及其他安全装备，有助于保障从事电气作业或使用电气设备的技术人员的整体安全。处理电时影响安全的两个因素是恐惧和过度自信。这两个因素是电气事故的主要原因。对电气设备保持一定程度的敬畏是有益的，具备一定程度的自信也是必要的，但两者中任何一方走向极端都可能致命。

缺乏敬畏往往源于缺乏知识。试图从事电气设备作业却不懂电学原理的人员，缺乏安全应对电气设备的技能。过度自信会导致冒险。不敬畏电的能力的技术人员，迟早会成为电的威力的受害者。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 时间状语从句 + 被动语态 + 并列地点状语

**原句**：When electricity is applied to the human body, it can create severe burns in the area of entrance and at the point of exit from the body.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句（is applied to 为被动）+ 主句 it can create severe burns（情态动词 + 动词原形）+ 两个并列地点状语 in the area of entrance 与 at the point of exit from the body。
**要点**：apply A to B 是“把 A 施加于 B”，被动为 A is applied to B，介词 to 不能换成 on。本句两个地点状语分别用 in 与 at：in the area of…（在……区域内）与 at the point of…（在……点上），介词不同是英语搭配习惯，不能统一成一个。entrance/exit 在此不译“入口/出口”，而是电流“进入/离开”人体的部位。

### ② 动名词与名词并列作主语 + 情态动词后并列谓语

**原句**：Wearing or use of proper safety equipment can provide a psychological assurance and physically protect the user at the same time.
**拆解**：主语 Wearing or use（动名词与名词并列，of proper safety equipment 为后置定语）+ 谓语 can provide a psychological assurance and (can) physically protect the user（并列谓语，第二个共用情态动词 can，故用原形 protect）+ 状语 at the same time。
**要点**：动名词与名词并列作主语在技术文本里很常见（Wearing or use），此时谓语按“整体一件事”处理，但本句有情态动词 can，动词一律用原形。易错点：第二个 protect 前省略了 can，不能写成 protects；physically 修饰 protect（“在身体上保护”），与前面的 psychological assurance 形成对照。

### ③ while 引导让步状语从句

**原句**：While a certain amount of respect for electrical equipment is healthy and a certain level of confidence is necessary, extremes of either can be deadly.
**拆解**：While 引导让步状语从句（= 尽管），从句由 and 连接的两个并列分句构成（respect… is healthy / confidence… is necessary）+ 主句 extremes of either can be deadly。
**要点**：while 既可表“当……时”，也可表“虽然、尽管”，此处是让步，译作“虽然……但……”最贴切。either 指前面提到的 respect 与 confidence 两者中的任一方，extremes of either = “两者中任何一方走极端”；deadly 是形容词作表语（“致命的”），不是副词。

### ④ 定语从句隔开主谓 + lack 及物用法

**原句**：Personnel who attempt to work with electrical equipment and have no knowledge of the principles of electricity lack the skills to deal with electrical equipment safely.
**拆解**：主语 Personnel + who 引导定语从句（从句内 attempt to work… 与 have no knowledge… 为并列谓语）+ 主句谓语 lack + 宾语 the skills + 不定式 to deal with… safely 作后置定语。
**要点**：who 从句一直管到 electricity，主句真正的谓语是 lack，长定语从句夹在主语与谓语之间最容易读丢主干——遇到 Personnel/The technician who… 开头，先找 who 从句结束的位置再找谓语。lack 作动词直接及物（lack the skills），不能说 lack of the skills（lack of 是名词搭配）。
