# Physiological Safety

> 通用 Ch 01 · Physiological Safety · 原文 PDF 第 23 页

## 1. 原文

Working with electrical equipment poses certain physiological safety hazards. When electricity is applied to the human body, it can create severe burns in the area of entrance and at the point of exit from the body. In addition, the nervous system is affected and can be damaged or destroyed. To safely deal with electricity, the technician must have a working knowledge of the principles of electricity and a healthy respect for its capability to do both work and damage.

Wearing or use of proper safety equipment can provide a psychological assurance and physically protect the user at the same time. The use of rubber gloves, safety glasses, rubber or grounded safety mats, and other safety equipment contributes to the overall safety of the technician working on or with electrical equipment.

Two factors that affect safety when dealing with electricity are fear and overconfidence. These two factors are major causes of accidents involving electricity. While a certain amount of respect for electrical equipment is healthy and a certain level of confidence is necessary, extremes of either can be deadly.

Lack of respect is often due to lack of knowledge. Personnel who attempt to work with electrical equipment and have no knowledge of the principles of electricity lack the skills to deal with electrical equipment safely. Overconfidence leads to risk taking. The technician who does not respect the capabilities of electricity will, sooner or later, become a victim of electricity’s power.

## 2. 中文翻译

使用电气设备作业会带来某些生理安全危害。电流通过人体时，在电流进入人体的部位以及离开人体的部位都可能造成严重烧伤。此外，神经系统也会受到影响，可能受损甚至被毁。要安全地应对电，维修技师必须对电的基本原理具备够用的知识，并对电既能做功又能造成伤害的能力保持适度的敬畏。

穿戴或使用适当的安全防护用品，既能在心理上给人保障，又能在身体上保护使用者。使用橡胶手套、护目镜、橡胶垫或接地安全垫以及其他安全防护用品，有助于全面提高在电气设备上作业或使用电气设备时技师的安全。

与电打交道时，影响安全的两个因素是恐惧和过度自信。这两个因素都是涉电事故的主要原因。对电气设备保持一定的敬畏是有益的，具备一定的自信也是必要的，但两者中任何一种走向极端都可能致命。

缺乏敬畏往往源于缺乏知识。试图接触电气设备却不懂电学原理的人员，不具备安全处置电气设备的技能。过度自信会导致冒险。不把电的能力当回事的技师，迟早会成为电的威力的受害者。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Working with electrical equipment poses …（动名词短语作主语）

**原句**：Working with electrical equipment poses certain physiological safety hazards.

**拆解**：主语是动名词短语 Working with electrical equipment，谓语 poses（第三人称单数），宾语 certain physiological safety hazards；certain 在此表示「某些」，不是「确定的」。

**要点**：动名词短语作主语时，谓语一律按单数处理，所以是 poses 而不是 pose。同类结构在手册中很常见：Wearing safety glasses reduces eye injuries、Welding in the hangar requires extra precautions。写作时若主语过长，可改用 It is … to do 结构避免头重脚轻。

### ② the nervous system is affected and can be damaged or destroyed（并列被动谓语共用主语）

**原句**：In addition, the nervous system is affected and can be damaged or destroyed.

**拆解**：主语 the nervous system 后面跟两个并列谓语：一般现在时被动 is affected，以及情态被动 can be damaged or destroyed；第二个谓语前省略了重复的主语。

**要点**：英语允许一个主语带多个被动谓语，用 and 连接、只保留一次主语，中文则需要重复主语或用「会……乃至……」承接。注意 damaged or destroyed 是「双过去分词并列」，词尾 -ed 不能丢；译成「可能受损或被毁」比「被损坏」更贴合原文的递进。

### ③ While a certain amount of respect … is healthy …（while 引导让步状语从句）

**原句**：While a certain amount of respect for electrical equipment is healthy and a certain level of confidence is necessary, extremes of either can be deadly.

**拆解**：While 引导让步状语从句（「虽然……」），从句内由 and 连接两个并列分句；主句主语 extremes of either（两者中任何一种的极端），谓语 can be deadly。

**要点**：while 既可表时间「当……时」，也可表对比或让步，本句是让步，译作「虽然……但……」最顺，不要译成「当……的时候」。extremes of either 中的 either 指 respect 和 confidence 两者，of either 是后置定语；这类「of + 代词」结构在技术文中常用来避免重复名词。

### ④ Personnel who attempt to work with electrical equipment … lack the skills（定语从句隔开主谓）

**原句**：Personnel who attempt to work with electrical equipment and have no knowledge of the principles of electricity lack the skills to deal with electrical equipment safely.

**拆解**：主语 Personnel，who … electricity 是定语从句（从句内两个并列谓语 attempt 与 have），主句真正的谓语是最后面的 lack；the skills to deal with … safely 中不定式作后置定语修饰 skills。

**要点**：主语与谓语被长定语从句隔开是手册英语的典型难点，读句子时先找 who 从句的结束位置，再找主句谓语，本句的 lack 容易被误当成从句里的动词。lack 在此是及物动词（lack the skills），不用 lack of；lack of 只能作名词短语。
