# Reciprocating Engines

## 1. 原文

The following procedures are typical of those used to start reciprocating engines. There are, however, wide variations in the procedures for the many reciprocating engines. Do not attempt to use the methods presented here for actually starting an engine. Instead, always refer to the procedures contained in the applicable manufacturer’s instructions. Reciprocating engines are capable of starting in fairly low temperatures

Align the blades without the use of engine heating or oil dilution, depending on the grade of oil used.

The various covers (wing, tail, flight deck, wheel, and so forth) protecting the aircraft must be removed before attempting to turn the engine. Use external sources of electrical power when starting engines equipped with electric starters, if possible or needed. This eliminates an excessive burden on the aircraft battery. Leave all unnecessary electrical equipment off until the generators are furnishing electrical power to the aircraft power bus.

Before starting a radial engine that has been shut down for more than 30 minutes, check the ignition switch for off. Turn the propeller three or four complete revolutions by hand to detect a hydraulic lock, if one is present. Any liquid present in a cylinder is indicated by the abnormal effort required to rotate the propeller or by the propeller stopping abruptly during rotation. Never use force to turn the propeller when a hydraulic lock is detected. Sufficient force can be exerted on the crankshaft to bend or break a connecting rod if a lock is present.

To eliminate a lock, remove either the front or rear spark plug from the lower cylinders and pull the propeller through.

Never attempt to clear the hydraulic lock by pulling the propeller through in the direction opposite to normal rotation. This tends to inject the liquid from the cylinder into the intake pipe. The liquid is drawn back into the cylinder with the possibility of complete or partial lock occurring on the subsequent start.

To start the engine, proceed as follows:

1. Turn the auxiliary fuel pump on, if the aircraft is so equipped.

2. Place the mixture control to the position recommended for the engine and carburetor combination being started. As a general rule, put the mixture control in the “idle cut-off” position for fuel injection and in the

“full rich” position for float-type carburetors. Many light aircraft are equipped with a mixture control pull rod that has no detent intermediate positions. When such controls are pushed in flush with the instrument panel, the mixture is set in the “full rich” position.

Conversely, when the control rod is pulled all the way out, the carburetor is in the “idle cut-off” or “full lean” position. The operator can select unmarked intermediate positions between these two extremes to achieve any desired mixture setting.

3. Open the throttle to a position that provides 1,000 to 1,200 rpm (approximately 1⁄8" to 1⁄2" from the “closed” position).

4. Leave the pre-heat or alternate air (carburetor air)

control in the “cold” position to prevent damage and fire in case of backfire. These auxiliary heating devices are used after the engine warms up. They improve fuel vaporization, prevent fouling of the spark plugs, ice formation, and eliminate icing in the induction system.

5. Move the primer switch to “on” intermittently (press to prime by pushing in on the ignition switch during the starting cycle), or prime with one to three strokes of priming pump, depending on how the aircraft is equipped. The colder the weather, the more priming is needed.

6. Energize the starter and after the propeller has made at least two complete revolutions, turn the ignition switch on. On engines equipped with an induction vibrator (shower of sparks, magneto incorporates a retard breaker assembly), turn the switch to the “both” position and energize the starter by turning the switch to the “start” position. After the engine starts, release the starter switch to the “both” position. When starting an engine that uses an impulse coupling magneto, turn the ignition switch to the “left” position. Place the start switch to the “start” position. When the engine starts, release the start switch. Do not crank the engine continuously with the starter for more than 1 minute. Allow a 3- to 5-minute period for cooling the starter (starter duty cycle) between successive attempts. Otherwise, the starter may be burned out due to overheating.

7. After the engine is operating smoothly, move the mixture control to the “full rich” position if started in the “idle cutoff” position. Carbureted engines are already in the rich mixture position. Check for oil pressure.

8. Instruments for monitoring the engine during operation include a tachometer for rpm, manifold pressure gauge, oil pressure gauge, oil temperature gauge, cylinder head temperature gauge, exhaust gas temperature gauge, and fuel flow gauge.

## 2. 中文翻译

以下程序是起动往复式发动机的典型做法。不过，各种往复式发动机的起动程序差异很大。切勿用此处给出的方法去实际起动发动机，而应始终参照适用厂家说明中所载的程序。往复式发动机能在相当低的温度下起动——

（此处为上一页版面残留的断句：Align the blades……）……对齐桨叶。

在试图转动发动机之前，必须拆下保护航空器的各种罩盖（机翼罩、尾翼罩、驾驶舱罩、机轮罩等等）。起动装有电起动机的发动机时，应尽量或按需使用外部电源，这样可以避免给机上电瓶造成过大的负担。在发电机向机上电源汇流条供电之前，所有不必要的用电设备都要保持关闭。

起动一台已停车 30 分钟以上的星形发动机之前，先检查点火开关是否处于关断位。用手转动螺旋桨三至四整圈，以判断是否存在液锁。若气缸内有液体，表现为转动螺旋桨时阻力异常增大，或螺旋桨在转动中突然停住。一旦判断为液锁，绝不可强行转动螺旋桨：若确实存在液锁，作用在曲轴上的力足以使连杆弯曲或折断。

排除液锁的办法是：拆下下方气缸的前或后火花塞，然后把螺旋桨盘转过来（拉过一圈）。

绝不可朝正常旋转的相反方向盘转螺旋桨来排除液锁。这样做容易把气缸内的液体压入进气管，液体会在下一次起动时被吸回气缸，可能造成完全或部分的液锁。

起动发动机的操作如下：

1. 如航空器装有辅助燃油泵，将其打开。

2. 把混合比操纵杆置于针对所起动发动机与化油器组合所推荐的位置。一般规则是：燃油喷射式置于「慢车切断（idle cut-off）」位，而浮子式化油器则置于

「全富油（full rich）」位。（本句因分页断开。）许多轻型航空器的混合比操纵为拉杆式，没有中间定位槽；当这类操纵杆推到与仪表板齐平，混合比即处于「全富油」位。

反之，把操纵杆完全拉出时，化油器处于「慢车切断」或「全贫油」位。操作者可以在两端之间无标记的中间位置中任选，以获得所需的混合比。

3. 把油门开到能提供 1,000 至 1,200 rpm 的位置（约为距「关闭」位 1/8" 至 1/2"）。

4. 把预热或备用空气（化油器空气）

控制置于「冷」位，以防回火时造成损坏与起火。这类辅助加温装置是在发动机暖机之后使用的，它们能改善燃油雾化，防止火花塞积炭、结冰，并消除进气系统的结冰。

5. 按航空器的装备情况，把注油开关间歇置于「开」（起动循环中按下点火开关即注油），或用注油泵打一至三下进行注油。天气越冷，需要的注油量越多。

6. 接通起动机；待螺旋桨至少转过两整圈后，再打开点火开关。对装有进气振荡器（火花喷注式，磁电机内带延迟断电器组件）的发动机，把开关置于「both」位，再把开关转到「start」位接通起动机；发动机起动后，把起动机开关松回到「both」位。起动使用冲击联轴器磁电机的发动机时，把点火开关置于「left」位，把起动开关置于「start」位，发动机起动后松开起动开关。不得用起动机连续盘转发动机超过 1 分钟；两次尝试之间要留 3 至 5 分钟让起动机冷却（起动机工作循环）。否则，起动机可能因过热而烧毁。

7. 发动机平稳工作之后，若原先是在「慢车切断」位起动，则把混合比操纵杆移到「全富油」位（化油器式发动机本来就处于富油位）。检查滑油压力。

8. 运行中用于监控发动机的仪表包括：指示转速的转速表、进气压力表、滑油压力表、滑油温度表、气缸头温度表、排气温度表以及燃油流量表。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① the + 比较级，the + 比较级

**原句**：The colder the weather, the more priming is needed.

**拆解**：The colder the weather 是条件从句，结构为「the + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语」，其中 is 被省略；主句 the more priming is needed 中 the more priming 作主语，is needed 为被动谓语。

**要点**：这个句型表示「越……，越……」，两个 the 都是副词，不能省略也不能换成冠词。常见错误是写成 The more cold the weather。手册里用它表达剂量与条件的关系：The higher the altitude, the lower the power（高度越高，功率越低）。

### ② 被动语态 + 并列的 by 短语（内含两种不同结构）

**原句**：Any liquid present in a cylinder is indicated by the abnormal effort required to rotate the propeller or by the propeller stopping abruptly during rotation.

**拆解**：主语 Any liquid present in a cylinder（present 作后置形容词，in a cylinder 再修饰它）；谓语 is indicated 为被动；or 连接两个 by 短语：一是 by the abnormal effort required to rotate the propeller，其中 required to rotate 是过去分词短语后置修饰 effort；二是 by the propeller stopping abruptly during rotation，即「by + 名词 + 动名词」的复合结构。

**要点**：同一个 by 后面接「名词 + 过去分词」和「名词 + 动名词」两种结构，是本句难点；the propeller stopping 中 stopping 是动名词，不是现在分词。中文宜译成「表现为……，或表现为……」。abnormal effort 指「异常费力」。

### ③ leave + 宾语 + 补语 + until 从句

**原句**：Leave all unnecessary electrical equipment off until the generators are furnishing electrical power to the aircraft power bus.

**拆解**：祈使句，谓语 leave，宾语 all unnecessary electrical equipment，补语 off 表状态；until 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在进行时 are furnishing 表示持续供电的过程。

**要点**：leave sth off / on 意为「让某物保持关闭／开启状态」，off 是补语，不能换成 closed。until 从句用肯定式表示「一直到……为止」，与「在此之前保持关闭」对应；若改为 not…until 则强调「直到……才」。power bus 惯例译「电源汇流条」。
