# Servicing Aircraft Air/Nitrogen Oil & Fluids

> 通用 Ch 01 · Servicing Aircraft Air/Nitrogen Oil & Fluids · 原文 PDF 第 42 页

## 1. 原文

Checking or servicing aircraft fluids is an important maintenance function. Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper type of servicing equipment and procedures. In general, aircraft engine oil is checked with a dipstick or a sight gauge. There are markings on the stick or around the sight gauge to determine the correct level. Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown. Dry sump oil systems tend to hide oil that has seeped from the oil tank into the gearcase of the engine. This oil does not show up on the dipstick until the engine has been started or motored.

If serviced before this oil is pumped back into the tank, the engine overfills. Never overfill the oil tank. Oil foams as it is circulated through the engine. The expansion space in the oil tank allows for this foaming (oil mixing with air). Also the correct type of oil must be used for the appropriate engine being serviced. When servicing a hydraulic reservoir, the correct fluid must be used. Normally, this can be determined by the container or by color. Some reservoirs are pressurized by air that must be bled off before servicing. Efforts must be made to prevent any type of contamination during servicing. Also, if changing hydraulic filters, assure that the pressure is off the system before removing the filters. After servicing the filters (if large amounts of fluids were lost) or system quantity, air must be purged and the system checked for leaks. While servicing tires or struts with high-pressure nitrogen, the technician must use caution while performing maintenance. Clean areas before connecting filling hose and do not overinflate.

## 2. 中文翻译

检查或勤务航空器油液是一项重要的维修工作。在对任何航空器进行勤务之前，应查阅该机型的维修手册，确定合适的勤务设备类型与程序。一般而言，航空器发动机滑油用油尺或观察窗（目视液位计）检查，油尺上或观察窗周围有刻度标记用以判定正确液位。活塞发动机应在发动机停止运转一段时间之后检查，而涡轮发动机必须在关车后立即检查。干槽式滑油系统容易把已从滑油箱渗入发动机齿轮箱的滑油「藏」起来，这部分滑油在发动机起动或带转之前不会显示在油尺上。

如果在这部分滑油被泵回滑油箱之前就加注，发动机就会加注过量。绝不可把滑油箱加得过满。滑油在发动机内循环时会起泡，滑油箱内的膨胀空间就是为这种起泡（滑油与空气混合）留出的。此外，被勤务的发动机必须使用与之相适应的正确牌号滑油。液压油、燃油和滑油如果溅到衣服或皮肤上，必须尽快清除，因为

勤务液压油箱时，必须使用规定的液压油。通常可凭容器或凭颜色加以判定。有些油箱是用空气加压的，勤务前必须把压力放掉。勤务过程中必须设法防止任何形式的污染。另外，如果更换液压滤芯，在拆下滤芯之前要确认系统已卸压。在勤务滤芯（如果流失了大量油液）或系统油量之后，必须排除系统中的空气，并检查系统有无渗漏。用高压氮气为轮胎或支柱充气时，技师在作业过程中必须小心。连接充气软管前要清洁作业区域，且不得充气过度。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be to be done 与 must be done 表规定

**原句**：Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown.

**拆解**：前半句 are to be checked 表规定；after the engine has been inactive 是时间状语从句，用现在完成时强调「已停放一段时间」的状态；while 连接后半句，表对比。

**要点**：while 在此不表「当……时」而表对比，译作「而」。are to be checked 与 must be checked 意思接近，前者更书面；after 从句用现在完成时，突出「停放」这一持续结果的完成。

### ② not…until… 与现在完成时的被动

**原句**：This oil does not show up on the dipstick until the engine has been started or motored.

**拆解**：not…until… 结构：does not show up…until…，译作「直到……才……」；until 从句用现在完成时 has been started or motored（被动），强调动作完成。

**要点**：not…until 的中文语序与英文相反，翻译时把 until 部分提前：「直到发动机被起动或带转之后，这部分滑油才会出现在油尺上」。show up 意为「显现」，技术文本中也常用。

### ③ while + 动名词表「在……过程中」

**原句**：While servicing tires or struts with high-pressure nitrogen, the technician must use caution while performing maintenance.

**拆解**：句首 While servicing… 是状语从句的省略形式（While the technician is servicing…），while 后接动名词；with high-pressure nitrogen 是方式状语；主句为 must use caution；句末 while performing maintenance 是第二个 while 短语。

**要点**：while + 动名词强调「在……过程中」，与 when + 动名词（在……那一刻）略有区别。本句两个 while 作用不同：前者引出作业内容，后者表示「在进行维修时」，翻译时不要重复成一句。

### ④ after + 动名词 + 并列被动句的省略

**原句**：After servicing the filters (if large amounts of fluids were lost) or system quantity, air must be purged and the system checked for leaks.

**拆解**：After servicing… 是介词 after + 动名词，宾语为 the filters…or system quantity；主句 air must be purged and the system checked for leaks 中，第二分句省略了 must be。

**要点**：并列被动句可省略相同的 must be，翻译时要补全「必须排除空气，并检查系统有无渗漏」。括号内的 if 从句用过去时 were lost，是因为把「油液已流失」当作既成事实来假定。
