# Auxiliary Power Units (APUs)

> 通用 Ch 01 · Auxiliary Power Units (APUs) · 原文 PDF 第 38 页

## 1. 原文

APUs are generally smaller turbine engines that provide compressed air for starting engines, cabin heating and cooling, and electrical power while on the ground. Their operation is normally simple. By turning a switch on and up to the start position (spring loaded to on position), the engine starts automatically. During start, the exhaust gas temperature must be monitored. APUs are at idle at 100 percent rpm with no load. After the engine reaches its operating rpm, it can be used for cooling or heating the

cabin and for electrical power. It is normally used to start the main engines.

## 2. 中文翻译

辅助动力装置（APU）通常是较小的燃气涡轮发动机，在地面为起动发动机提供压缩空气、为客舱提供采暖与制冷，并提供电力。其操作通常很简单：把开关拨到接通（ON）位、再向上拨至起动（START）位（该位置由弹簧保持在接通位），发动机即自动起动。起动过程中必须监控排气温度。APU 在空载、转速 100% 时处于慢车状态。发动机达到工作转速后，即可用于冷却或加热

……客舱并提供电力。它通常用于起动主发动机。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 情态动词 + 被动语态：must be monitored

**原句**：During start, the exhaust gas temperature must be monitored.

**拆解**：During start 是时间状语前置；主语 the exhaust gas temperature；谓语 must be monitored 为「情态动词 + be + 过去分词」，执行者被省略。

**要点**：手册条文只关心「哪个参数必须被监控」，不关心谁来监控，所以用被动。同类写法 must be checked / must be observed。注意 must be + 过去分词，不能写成 must monitored 或 must be monitor。

### ② by + 动名词表手段

**原句**：By turning a switch on and up to the start position (spring loaded to on position), the engine starts automatically.

**拆解**：By turning… 是方式状语，介词 by 后接动名词 turning；a switch 是 turning 的宾语，on 与 up to the start position 是两个补充说明开关位置的成分；主句 the engine starts automatically 用主动语态。

**要点**：by + -ing 译作「通过……（的方式）」，是说明书讲操作步骤最常用的结构，如 by pressing the button。括号中的 spring loaded to on position 是形容词性短语作补语，译「由弹簧保持在接通位」，不是被动句。

### ③ 定语从句 + for 的并列宾语

**原句**：APUs are generally smaller turbine engines that provide compressed air for starting engines, cabin heating and cooling, and electrical power while on the ground.

**拆解**：主句 APUs are generally smaller turbine engines；that provide… 是定语从句修饰 engines；for 后并列三个宾语 compressed air、cabin heating and cooling、electrical power；while on the ground 是省略形式（while they are on the ground）。

**要点**：that 从句虽长，谓语只有一个 provide，三个宾语靠逗号和 and 串起来，读时先定位 provide 再清点宾语。while + 介词短语省略了主语和 be，译作「在地面时」，同类还有 when in doubt、if necessary。
