# Hearing Protection

> 通用 Ch 01 · Hearing Protection · 原文 PDF 第 26 页

## 1. 原文

The flight line is a place of dangerous activity. Technicians who perform maintenance on the flight line must constantly be aware of what is going on around them. The noise on a flight line comes from many places. Aircraft are only one source of noise. There are auxiliary power units (APUs), fuel trucks, baggage handling equipment, and so forth. Each has its own frequency of sound. Combined all together, the noise on the ramp or flight line can cause hearing loss.

There are many types of hearing protection available.

Hearing protection can be external or internal. Earmuffs or headphones are considered external protection. The internal type of hearing protection fits into the auditory canal. Both types reduce the sound level reaching the eardrum and reduce the chances of hearing loss.

Hearing protection is essential when working with pneumatic drills, rivet guns, or other loud tools. Even short duration exposure to these sounds can cause hearing loss because of their high frequency. Continued exposure will cause hearing loss.

## 2. 中文翻译

停机坪是一个危险作业频繁的场所。在停机坪上执行维修工作的技师必须时刻留意周围发生的情况。停机坪上的噪声来自许多地方。航空器只是噪声来源之一。还有辅助动力装置（APU）、加油车、行李装卸设备等等。每一种声源都有自己的声音频率。所有这些声音叠加在一起，停机坪或外场的噪声就可能造成听力损伤。

可用的听力防护用品有很多类型。

听力防护可以是外部的，也可以是内部的。耳罩或头戴式耳机属于外部防护。内部型听力防护用品则塞入耳道内。两种类型都能降低到达耳膜的声音强度，降低听力受损的几率。

使用气动钻、铆枪或其他高噪声工具时，听力防护是必不可少的。这些声音频率高，即使短时间接触也可能造成听力损伤。持续接触则会造成听力损伤。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Technicians who perform maintenance … must constantly be aware of what is going on（定语从句 + 宾语从句）

**原句**：Technicians who perform maintenance on the flight line must constantly be aware of what is going on around them.

**拆解**：主语 Technicians 后接 who 引导的定语从句，主句谓语是 must constantly be aware of；of 后面是 what 引导的宾语从句 what is going on around them，作介词宾语。

**要点**：be aware of 是「意识到、留意到」，后接名词或名词性从句；what 在此是连接代词，既引导从句又在从句中作主语，不能用 that 替换。who 定语从句让主语和谓语隔得很远，读句子时先锁定谓语 must be aware of 再看从句。

### ② Hearing protection can be external or internal（can be + 形容词，不是被动语态）

**原句**：Hearing protection can be external or internal.

**拆解**：主语 Hearing protection，谓语 can be，表语 external or internal（形容词并列），这是「情态动词 + 系动词 + 表语」结构。

**要点**：can be + 过去分词才是被动，can be + 形容词是系表结构，初学者容易一看到 be 就往被动上套。判断方法：看 be 后面是形容词还是过去分词，以及主语是不是动作的承受者。可迁移：The noise can be harmful（系表）、The wheel can be damaged（被动）。

### ③ … because of their high frequency（because of 与 because 的区别）

**原句**：Even short duration exposure to these sounds can cause hearing loss because of their high frequency.

**拆解**：主语 Even short duration exposure to these sounds，谓语 can cause，宾语 hearing loss；because of their high frequency 是介词短语作原因状语，their 指代前面的 these sounds。

**要点**：because of 后只能接名词、代词或动名词，because 后必须接完整的从句，写成 because of they are high frequency 是常见错误。even 在此强调「哪怕只是短时间」，与后一句 Continued exposure 形成递进；exposure to 是固定搭配，介词用 to 不用 of。
