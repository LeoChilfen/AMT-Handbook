# Ground Support Air Units

> 通用 Ch 01 · Ground Support Air Units · 原文 PDF 第 45 页

## 1. 原文

Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air that can be used for starting the engines and heating and cooling the aircraft on the ground (using the onboard aircraft systems). It generally consists of an APU

built into the cart that provides bleed air from the APU’s compressor for operating aircraft systems or starting engines.

## 2. 中文翻译

空气车（air cart）用于提供低压空气（最高 50 psi、大流量），这种空气可供在地面起动发动机，以及为航空器加热和制冷（利用机上系统）。

它一般由集成在车上的 APU 构成，该 APU 从自身压气机引出引气，用于操作航空器系统或起动发动机。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Air carts are used to provide …（be used 表用途的被动句）

**原句**：Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air that can be used for starting the engines and heating and cooling the aircraft on the ground (using the onboard aircraft systems).

**拆解**：主语 Air carts + 被动谓语 are used；to provide … air 为不定式表目的／补足说明；air 后接 that 引导的定语从句，从句内 can be used for 后接并列动名词 starting、heating and cooling；on the ground 为地点状语，(using …) 为分词短语作方式状语。

**要点**：be used to do 与 be used for doing 同义，for 是介词，后面必须接 -ing，别与 used to do（过去常常）混淆。手册介绍地面设备时习惯以设备作主语、用「be + 过去分词」点明用途，而不点明由谁操作。

### ② It generally consists of an APU built into the cart …（代词回指 + 分词后置定语）

**原句**：It generally consists of an APU built into the cart that provides bleed air from the APU’s compressor for operating aircraft systems or starting engines.

**拆解**：It 回指上句的 air cart；consist of 表「由……构成」，本身不用被动；built into the cart 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 APU；that provides … 为定语从句，先行词是 APU 而不是 cart；for operating … or starting engines 为介词短语表用途。

**要点**：consist of 没有被动形式，写成 is consisted of 是常见错误；过去分词短语 built into the cart 译成中文要移到名词前：「集成在车上的 APU」。原文这一句被换行误断成两段，APU 与 built 必须连起来读。

### ③ low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air（复合定语与 up to）

**原句**：Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air

**拆解**：low-pressure 是「形容词 + 名词」构成的复合形容词，带连字符，作 air 的前置定语；括号内 up to 50 psi 与 high volume flow 是并列的补充说明，省略了连词。

**要点**：复合形容词作前置定语一般要加连字符（low-pressure air、high-volume flow），原文写 high volume flow 未加连字符属松散写法。译成中文时定语应后置：「低压（最高 50 psi、大流量）空气」。up to 表示「最高不超过」，不是「已达到」。
