# Oxygen Servicing Equipment

> 通用 Ch 01 · Oxygen Servicing Equipment · 原文 PDF 第 46 页

## 1. 原文

Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper types of servicing equipment to be used. Two personnel are required to service an aircraft with gaseous oxygen. One person is stationed at the control valves of the servicing equipment, and one person is stationed where they can observe the pressure in the aircraft system. Communication between the two people is required in the event of an emergency.

Do not service aircraft with oxygen during fueling, defueling, or other maintenance work that could provide a source of ignition. Oxygen servicing of aircraft is to be accomplished outside hangars.

Oxygen used on aircraft is available in two types: gaseous and liquid. The type to use on any specific aircraft depends on the type of equipment in the aircraft. Gaseous oxygen is stored in large steel cylinders, while liquid oxygen (commonly referred to as LOX) is stored and converted into a usable gas in a liquid oxygen converter.

Oxygen is commercially available in three general types: aviator’s breathing, industrial, and medical. Only oxygen marked “Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen” that meets Federal Specification BB-0-925A, Grade A, or its equivalent is to be used in aircraft breathing oxygen systems. Industrial oxygen may contain impurities that could cause the pilot, crew, and/ or passengers to become sick. Medical oxygen, although pure, contains water that can freeze in the cold temperatures found at the altitudes where oxygen is necessary.

## 2. 中文翻译

在对任何飞机进行勤务之前，应查阅该机型的具体维修手册，以确定应当使用的勤务设备类型。用气态氧对飞机进行勤务需要两名人员。一人驻守在勤务设备的控制阀处，另一人驻守在能够观察飞机系统压力的位置。发生紧急情况时，两人之间必须保持联络。

在加油、抽油或其他可能形成点火源的维修作业期间，不得用氧气对飞机进行勤务。飞机的氧气勤务必在机库外完成。

飞机上使用的氧气有气态和液态两种。某一具体机型该用哪一种，取决于机上装备的设备类型。气态氧储存于大型钢瓶中，而液氧（通常简称 LOX）储存于液氧转换器中，并在其中转化为可用的气体。

商用氧气一般分为三种：航空呼吸用氧、工业氧和医用氧。只有标注为“Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen”、符合联邦规范 BB-0-925A A 级或其等效标准的氧气，方可用于飞机的呼吸供氧系统。工业氧可能含有杂质，会使飞行员、机组成员和/或乘客生病。医用氧虽然纯净，但含有水分，在必须使用氧气的高空低温环境中会结冰。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 不定式作目的状语与后置定语
**原句**：Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper types of servicing equipment to be used.
**拆解**：Before servicing any aircraft（介词 + 动名词，作时间状语）+ 祈使句谓语 consult + 宾语 the specific aircraft maintenance manual + to determine the proper types of servicing equipment（目的状语）+ to be used（被动不定式作 equipment 的后置定语）。
**要点**：规范文本里「查阅手册」后面常跟 to do 说明目的；equipment to be used = 将要被使用的设备，不定式被动式表将来或被动，比 equipment used（已用过）含义更准确。

### ② 并列句与 where 引导的地点状语从句
**原句**：One person is stationed at the control valves of the servicing equipment, and one person is stationed where they can observe the pressure in the aircraft system.
**拆解**：由 and 连接的两个并列分句，谓语均为 is stationed（被动）；第二分句中 where 引导地点状语从句（相当于 at the place where），they 指代 one person（单数概念用 they 指代，避免性别）。
**要点**：where 从句在此修饰谓语 stationed 表示处所，不是定语从句（前面没有先行词）；判断是地点状语从句还是定语从句，看 where 前是否紧跟着名词。

### ③ 否定祈使句与定语从句
**原句**：Do not service aircraft with oxygen during fueling, defueling, or other maintenance work that could provide a source of ignition.
**拆解**：否定祈使句 Do not + 动词原形 service（此处 service 为动词）+ with oxygen（方式）+ during…（时间状语，三个并列名词）+ that could provide a source of ignition（定语从句修饰 maintenance work）。
**要点**：service 既可作名词「勤务」又可作动词「对……作勤务」，本句是动词用法；that 从句用 could 表「有可能」，是安全条文提示风险的常用情态。

### ④ be to do 表示规定
**原句**：Oxygen servicing of aircraft is to be accomplished outside hangars.
**拆解**：主语 Oxygen servicing of aircraft（动名词复合结构作主语）+ 谓语 is to be accomplished（be to + 被动不定式，表规定、必须）+ 地点状语 outside hangars。
**要点**：be to do 在手册、规范中表示「必须/应当被……」，语气比 must be done 更书面；主语中心词是动名词 servicing，谓语用单数 is。
