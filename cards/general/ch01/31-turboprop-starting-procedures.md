# Turboprop Starting Procedures

> 通用 Ch 01 · Turboprop Starting Procedures · 原文 PDF 第 37 页

## 1. 原文

To start an engine on the ground, perform the following operations:

1. Turn the aircraft boost pumps on.

2. Make sure that the power lever is in the “start” position.

3. Place the start switch in the “start” position. This starts the engine turning.

4. Place the ignition switch on. (On some engines, the ignition is activated by moving the fuel lever.) 5. The fuel is now turned on. This is accomplished by moving the condition lever to the “on” position.

6. Monitor the engine lights of the exhaust temperature. If it exceeds the limits, shut the engine down. 7. Check the oil pressure and temperature. 8. After the engine reaches a self-sustaining speed, the starter is disengaged.

9. The engine continues to accelerate up to idle. 10. Maintain the power lever at the “start” position until the specified minimum oil temperature is reached.

11. Disconnect the ground power supply, if used.

If any of the following conditions occur during the starting sequence, turn off the fuel and ignition switch, discontinue the start immediately, make an investigation, and record the findings.

• Turbine inlet temperature exceeds the specified maximum. Record the observed peak temperature.

• Acceleration time from start of propeller rotation to stabilized rpm exceeds the specified time.

• There is no oil pressure indication at 5,000 rpm for either the reduction gear or the power unit.

• Torching (visible burning in the exhaust nozzle).

• The engine fails to ignite by 4,500 rpm or maximum motoring rpm.

• Abnormal vibration is noted or compressor surge occurs (indicated by backfiring).

• Fire warning bell rings. (This may be due to either an engine fire or overheat.)

## 2. 中文翻译

在地面起动发动机时，执行下列操作：

1. 打开飞机上的增压泵（boost pumps）。

2. 确认功率杆在「起动」位。

3. 把起动电门置于「起动」位。发动机随即开始转动。

4. 接通点火电门。（在某些发动机上，点火是由移动燃油杆来激发的。）5. 此时接通燃油。做法是把状态杆移到「开」位。

6. 监视排气温度的发动机警告灯（原文此句作 Monitor the engine lights of the exhaust temperature，词序系抽取错讹，实指排气温度超限警告灯）。如果超过限制，就停车。7. 检查滑油压力和温度。8. 发动机达到自持转速后，起动机脱开。

9. 发动机继续加速，直至慢车。10. 保持功率杆在「起动」位，直到滑油温度达到规定的最低值。

11. 如果使用了地面电源，断开地面电源。

如果起动过程中出现下列任一情况，应关断燃油和点火电门，立即中止起动，进行调查，并记录发现的问题。

• 涡轮进口温度超过规定的最大值。记录观察到的峰值温度。

• 从螺旋桨开始转动到转速稳定的加速时间超过规定值。

• 转速达到 5,000 rpm 时，减速器或动力段（power unit）均无滑油压力指示。

• 尾喷管喷火（torching，尾喷口可见燃烧）。

• 转速到达 4,500 rpm 或最大冷转转速时，发动机仍未点燃。

• 发现异常振动，或发生压气机喘振（表现为回火）。

• 火警警铃响。（原因可能是发动机起火，也可能是过热。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 句首不定式表目的

**原句**：To start an engine on the ground, perform the following operations:

**拆解**：To start an engine on the ground 是不定式短语作目的状语，置于句首；主句是祈使句，动词原形 perform 开头，宾语 the following operations。

**要点**：句首不定式表目的时，逻辑主语与主句主语一致（这里是隐含的 you）；改写为 In order to… 意思不变，但手册体例多用光不定式。注意 on the ground 限定「在地面」，与空中起动（air start）相对。

### ② 被动语态 + by doing 说明手段

**原句**：This is accomplished by moving the condition lever to the “on” position.

**拆解**：主语 This（回指上句「接通燃油」），谓语 is accomplished 是一般现在时被动；by moving the condition lever 是方式状语（by + 动名词），to the “on” position 是结果/方向状语。

**要点**：be accomplished by doing 是手册说明「如何完成某步骤」的固定句式；by 后必须用动名词，不能接不定式。注意 to the “on” position 用 to 表移动到某位置，而非 into（into 强调进入内部）。

### ③ 条件从句 + 并列祈使句

**原句**：If any of the following conditions occur during the starting sequence, turn off the fuel and ignition switch, discontinue the start immediately, make an investigation, and record the findings.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，主语 any of the following conditions 是「any of + 复数名词」，谓语用复数 occur，during the starting sequence 是时间状语；主句由四个祈使句并列：turn off…、discontinue…、make…、record…。

**要点**：any of + 复数名词作主语时谓语用复数；主句的并列祈使句按操作先后排列，翻译时保留「关断—中止—调查—记录」的顺序，不要合并成一句。findings（发现的问题/结论）是维修记录里的固定用词。

### ④ until 从句中的被动语态

**原句**：Maintain the power lever at the “start” position until the specified minimum oil temperature is reached.

**拆解**：主句是祈使句 Maintain the power lever at the “start” position（at… 是位置状语）；until 引导时间状语从句，从句主语 the specified minimum oil temperature，谓语 is reached 为被动语态。

**要点**：until 从句用一般现在时表将来（主句是祈使句，不构成「主将从现」的时态呼应问题，但仍不用 will）；「until + 被动」译作「直到……达到为止」，中文习惯把被动转成主动（达到规定的最低滑油温度）。
