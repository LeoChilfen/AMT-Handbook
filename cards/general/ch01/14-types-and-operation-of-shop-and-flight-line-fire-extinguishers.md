# Types and Operation of Shop and Flight Line Fire Extinguishers

> 通用 Ch 01 · Types and Operation of Shop and Flight Line Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 27 页

## 1. 原文

Water extinguishers are the best type to use on Class A fires. Water has two effects on fire. It deprives fire of oxygen and cools the material being burned. Since most petroleum products float on water, water-type fire extinguishers are not recommended for Class B fires. Extreme caution must be used when fighting electrical fires (Class C) with water-type extinguishers. All electrical power must be removed or shut off to the burning area. Additionally, residual electricity in capacitors, coils, and so forth must be considered to prevent severe injury or possibly death from electrical shock. Never use water-type fire extinguishers on Class D fires. The cooling effect of water causes an explosive expansion of the metal, because metals burn at extremely high temperatures.

Water fire extinguishers are operated in a variety of ways. Some are hand pumped, while others are pressurized. The pressurized types of extinguishers may have a gas charge stored in the container with the water, or it may contain a “soda-acid” container where acid is spilled into a container of soda inside the extinguisher. The chemical reaction of the soda and the acid causes pressure to build inside the fire extinguisher, forcing the water out.

Carbon dioxide (CO2) extinguishers are used for Class A, B, and C fires, extinguishing the fire by depriving it of oxygen. [Figure 1-4] Additionally, like water-type extinguishers, CO2 cools the burning material. Never use CO2 on Class D fires. As with water extinguishers, the cooling effect of CO 2 on the hot metal can cause explosive expansion of the metal. When using CO 2 fire extinguishers, all parts of the extinguisher can become extremely cold, and remain so for a short time after operation. Wear protective equipment or take other precautions to prevent cold injury, such as frostbite. Extreme caution must be used when operating CO2 fire extinguishers in closed or confined areas. Not only can the fire be deprived of oxygen, but so too can the operator.

CO2 fire extinguishers generally use the self-expelling method of operation. This means that the CO2 has sufficient pressure at normal operating pressure to expel itself. This means that once the seal or disk is broken, pressure in the container is released and the fire extinguisher is spent, requiring replacement. [Figure 1-5]

Halogenated hydrocarbon extinguishers are most effective on Class B and C fires. They can be used on Class A and D fires, but they are less effective. Halogenated hydrocarbon, commonly called Freon™ by the industry, are numbered according to chemical formulas with Halon™ numbers.

Carbon tetrachloride (Halon 104), chemical formula CCl 4, has an Underwriters Laboratory (UL) toxicity rating of 3. As such, it is extremely toxic. [Figure 1-6] Hydrochloric acid vapor, chlorine, and phosgene gas are produced whenever carbon tetrachloride is used on ordinary fires. The amount of phosgene gas is increased whenever carbon tetrachloride is brought in direct contact with hot metal, certain chemicals, or continuing electrical arcs. It is not approved for any fire extinguishing use. Old containers of Halon 104 found in or around shops or hangars should be disposed of in accordance with Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations and local laws and ordinances.

Methyl bromide (Halon 1001), chemical formula CH 3Br, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 2. It is very toxic and corrosive to aluminum alloys, magnesium, and zinc. Halon 1001 is not recommended for aircraft use. Chlorobromomethane (Halon 1011), chemical formula CH2ClBr, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 3. Like methyl bromide, Halon 1011 is not recommended for aircraft use. Dibromodifluoromethane (Halon 1202), chemical formula CBr2F2, has a UL toxicity rating of 4. Halon 1202 is not recommended for aircraft use.

Bromochlorodifluoromethane (Halon 1211), chemical formula CBrClF2, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 5. It is colorless, noncorrosive, and evaporates rapidly leaving no residue. It does not freeze or cause cold burns and does not harm fabrics, metals, or other materials it contacts. Halon 1211 acts rapidly on fires by producing a heavy blanketing mist that eliminates oxygen from the fire source. More importantly, it interferes chemically with the combustion process of the fire. Furthermore, it has outstanding properties in preventing re- flash after the fire has been extinguished.

Bromotrifluoromethane (Halon 1301), chemical formula CF3Br, is also a liquefied gas and has a UL toxicity rating of 6. It has all the characteristics of Halon 1211. The significant difference between the two is Halon 1211 forms a spray similar to CO2, while Halon 1301 has a vapor spray that is more difficult to direct.

Note: The EPA has restricted Halon to its 1986 production level due to its effect on the ozone layer.

Dry powder extinguishers, while effective on Class B and C fires, are best for use on Class D fires. The method of operation of dry powder fire extinguishers varies from gas cartridge charges, stored pressure within the container that forces the powder charge out of the container, to scooping pails or buckets of the powder from large containers or barrels to toss on the fire.

## 2. 中文翻译

水型灭火器是扑救 A 类火灾的最佳选择。水对火有两种作用：使火缺氧，并冷却正在燃烧的材料。由于大多数石油产品浮在水面上，水型灭火器不推荐用于 B 类火灾。用水型灭火器扑救电气火灾（C 类）时必须极其谨慎：燃烧区域的所有电源都必须切断或关闭。此外，还必须考虑电容器、线圈等部件中的残余电荷，以防触电造成重伤甚至死亡。绝不要用水型灭火器扑救 D 类火灾。因为金属燃烧温度极高，水的冷却作用会使金属发生爆炸性膨胀。

水型灭火器有多种操作方式：有些靠手动泵压，另一些则是加压式。加压式灭火器可能把压缩气体与水泥同储在容器内；也可能装有一个“酸碱”（soda-acid）内筒，酸液被倒入灭火器内部的苏打液筒中。苏打与酸发生化学反应，使灭火器内部建立压力，从而把水压出。

二氧化碳（CO2）灭火器用于扑救 A、B、C 类火灾，通过使火缺氧来灭火。[Figure 1-4] 此外，与水型灭火器一样，CO2 也冷却燃烧物。绝不要用 CO2 扑救 D 类火灾：与水型灭火器相同，CO2 对高温金属的冷却作用会使金属发生爆炸性膨胀。使用 CO2 灭火器时，灭火器的各部件都可能变得极冷，并在喷射后短时间内保持低温。应穿戴防护装备或采取其他预防措施，以防冻伤。在封闭或受限区域内操作 CO2 灭火器时必须极其谨慎：不仅火可能缺氧，操作者也可能缺氧。

CO2 灭火器一般采用自喷（self-expelling）方式工作，即 CO2 在正常工作压力下具有足以自行喷出的压力。〔原文此处夹入图内文字残片：「It takes three things to start a fire: OXYGEN, HEAT, FUEL」「Oxygen Friction (heat) Fuel」，与本句无关，略〕这种压力由某种密封件或易碎膜片封在容器内，由击发机构（通常是一根销）击破或刺穿。也就是说，一旦密封件或膜片被击破，容器内的压力即被释放，灭火器随之用尽，需要更换。[Figure 1-5]

卤代烃灭火器对 B 类和 C 类火灾最为有效；也可用于 A 类和 D 类火灾，但效果较差。卤代烃在业内通称 Freon™（氟利昂），其 Halon™（哈龙）编号按化学式编排。

四氯化碳（Halon 104），化学式 CCl4，在 Underwriters Laboratory（UL，美国保险商实验室）的毒性分级中为 3 级，因此毒性极强。[Figure 1-6] 只要用四氯化碳扑救普通火灾，就会产生氯化氢蒸气、氯气和光气。当四氯化碳直接接触高温金属、某些化学品或持续电弧时，光气的生成量还会增加。它未被批准用于任何灭火用途。在车间或机库内外发现的 Halon 104 旧容器，应按美国环境保护署（EPA）法规及当地法律、条例处置。

溴甲烷（Halon 1001），化学式 CH3Br，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性分级为 2 级。它毒性很强，且对铝合金、镁和锌有腐蚀性。Halon 1001 不推荐用于飞机。氯溴甲烷（Halon 1011），化学式 CH2ClBr，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性分级为 3 级。与溴甲烷一样，Halon 1011 也不推荐用于飞机。二溴二氟甲烷（Halon 1202），化学式 CBr2F2，UL 毒性分级为 4 级。Halon 1202 不推荐用于飞机。

溴氯二氟甲烷（Halon 1211），化学式 CBrClF2，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性分级为 5 级。它无色、无腐蚀性，蒸发极快，不留残渣。它不会结冰，也不会造成冻伤，并且不损伤所接触的织物、金属或其他材料。Halon 1211 通过产生厚重的覆盖性雾幕排除火源处的氧，从而迅速灭火；更重要的是，它还能在化学上干扰燃烧过程。此外，它在火焰扑灭后防止复燃（re-flash）方面性能优异。

三氟溴甲烷（Halon 1301），化学式 CF3Br，同样是一种液化气体，UL 毒性分级为 6 级。它具备 Halon 1211 的全部特性。两者最显著的差别是：Halon 1211 喷出类似 CO2 的雾状射流，而 Halon 1301 的蒸气射流较难定向。

注：由于对臭氧层的影响，EPA 已将 Halon 的生产限制在 1986 年的水平。

干粉灭火器虽然对 B 类和 C 类火灾有效，但最适用于 D 类火灾。干粉灭火器的操作方式各不相同：有气体储压筒式的，有靠容器内储存压力把干粉压出的，也有用铲子或桶从大容器、桶中舀取干粉撒向火上的。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 省略主语的时间状语 + 被动情态
**原句**：Extreme caution must be used when fighting electrical fires (Class C) with water-type extinguishers.
**拆解**：主语 Extreme caution + 被动谓语 must be used + when fighting…（省略主语的时间状语，= when you fight…）+ with 短语表示工具。
**要点**：caution must be used 是手册中固定的被动表达，相当于“必须极其小心”；when + 动名词的逻辑主语须与主句一致。

### ② cause + 宾语 + to do 与分词结果状语
**原句**：The chemical reaction of the soda and the acid causes pressure to build inside the fire extinguisher, forcing the water out.
**拆解**：主句 causes pressure to build…（cause sth to do 复合宾语）+ forcing the water out 为现在分词短语作结果状语。
**要点**：cause 后接不定式必须带 to，不能像 make 那样省略；分词状语的逻辑主语是前面整件事（压力升高），不是 pressure 本身。

### ③ 比较级 + but 转折
**原句**：They can be used on Class A and D fires, but they are less effective.
**拆解**：两个并列分句：前句为被动 can be used on…，后句为系表结构，less effective 为比较级作表语，比较对象隐含（与 B、C 类火灾相比）。
**要点**：less effective 是“效果较差”，不是“无效”，不能译成“不起作用”；否定意义的比较级常隐含对比对象，翻译时应补出。

### ④ 否定祈使句 + on 的搭配
**原句**：Never use water-type fire extinguishers on Class D fires.
**拆解**：Never + 祈使句 use water-type fire extinguishers + on Class D fires 表作用对象。
**要点**：Never 置于句首构成绝对禁止，语气强于 do not，手册中译作“绝不要”；use sth on a fire 是“用某物扑救某类火灾”的固定搭配。
