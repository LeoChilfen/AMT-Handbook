# Types and Operation of Shop and Flight Line Fire Extinguishers

> 通用 Ch 01 · Types and Operation of Shop and Flight Line Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 27 页

## 1. 原文

Water extinguishers are the best type to use on Class A fires.

Water has two effects on fire. It deprives fire of oxygen and cools the material being burned.

Since most petroleum products float on water, water-type fire extinguishers are not recommended for Class B fires. Extreme caution must be used when fighting electrical fires (Class C)

with water-type extinguishers. All electrical power must be removed or shut off to the burning area. Additionally, residual electricity in capacitors, coils, and so forth must be considered to prevent severe injury or possibly death from electrical shock.

Never use water-type fire extinguishers on Class D fires. The cooling effect of water causes an explosive expansion of the metal, because metals burn at extremely high temperatures.

Water fire extinguishers are operated in a variety of ways.

Some are hand pumped, while others are pressurized. The pressurized types of extinguishers may have a gas charge stored in the container with the water, or it may contain a “soda-acid” container where acid is spilled into a container of soda inside the extinguisher. The chemical reaction of the soda and the acid causes pressure to build inside the fire extinguisher, forcing the water out.

Carbon dioxide (CO2) extinguishers are used for Class A, B, and C fires, extinguishing the fire by depriving it of oxygen.

[Figure 1-4] Additionally, like water-type extinguishers, CO2 cools the burning material. Never use CO2 on Class D fires.

As with water extinguishers, the cooling effect of CO 2 on the hot metal can cause explosive expansion of the metal.

When using CO 2 fire extinguishers, all parts of the extinguisher can become extremely cold, and remain so for a short time after operation. Wear protective equipment or take other precautions to prevent cold injury, such as frostbite. Extreme caution must be used when operating CO2 fire extinguishers in closed or confined areas. Not only can the fire be deprived of oxygen, but so too can the operator.

Halogenated hydrocarbon extinguishers are most effective on Class B and C fires. They can be used on Class A and D fires, but they are less effective. Halogenated hydrocarbon, commonly called Freon™ by the industry, are numbered according to chemical formulas with Halon™ numbers.

Carbon tetrachloride (Halon 104), chemical formula CCl 4, has an Underwriters Laboratory (UL) toxicity rating of 3. As such, it is extremely toxic. [Figure 1-6] Hydrochloric acid vapor, chlorine, and phosgene gas are produced whenever carbon tetrachloride is used on ordinary fires. The amount of phosgene gas is increased whenever carbon tetrachloride is brought in direct contact with hot metal, certain chemicals, or continuing electrical arcs. It is not approved for any fire extinguishing use. Old containers of Halon 104 found in or around shops or hangars should be disposed of in accordance with Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations and local laws and ordinances.

Methyl bromide (Halon 1001), chemical formula CH 3Br, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 2. It is very toxic and corrosive to aluminum alloys, magnesium, and zinc. Halon 1001 is not recommended for aircraft use.

Chlorobromomethane (Halon 1011), chemical formula CH2ClBr, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 3. Like methyl bromide, Halon 1011 is not recommended for aircraft use. Dibromodifluoromethane (Halon 1202), chemical formula CBr2F2, has a UL toxicity rating of 4. Halon 1202 is not recommended for aircraft use.

Bromochlorodifluoromethane (Halon 1211), chemical formula CBrClF2, is a liquefied gas with a UL toxicity rating of 5. It is colorless, noncorrosive, and evaporates rapidly leaving no residue. It does not freeze or cause cold burns and does not harm fabrics, metals, or other materials it contacts. Halon 1211 acts rapidly on fires by producing a heavy blanketing mist that eliminates oxygen from the fire source. More importantly, it interferes chemically with the combustion process of the fire.

Furthermore, it has outstanding properties in preventing re- flash after the fire has been extinguished.

Bromotrifluoromethane (Halon 1301), chemical formula CF3Br, is also a liquefied gas and has a UL toxicity rating of 6. It has all the characteristics of Halon 1211. The significant difference between the two is Halon 1211 forms a spray similar to CO2, while Halon 1301 has a vapor spray that is more difficult to direct.

Note: The EPA has restricted Halon to its 1986 production level due to its effect on the ozone layer.

Dry powder extinguishers, while effective on Class B and C fires, are best for use on Class D fires. The method of operation of dry powder fire extinguishers varies from gas cartridge charges, stored pressure within the container that forces the powder charge out of the container, to scooping pails or buckets of the powder from large containers or barrels to toss on the fire.

## 2. 中文翻译

灭火器——水型灭火器是扑救 A 类火的最佳选择。

水对火有两种作用：它使火失去氧气，并冷却正在燃烧的材料。

由于大多数石油产品浮在水面上，不推荐用水型灭火器扑救 B 类火。

扑救电气火灾（C 类）时，使用水型灭火器必须格外谨慎。

必须切断或关掉通往燃烧区域的全部电源。

此外，还必须考虑电容器、线圈等部件中的残余电荷，以防触电造成重伤甚至死亡。

绝不能用水型灭火器扑救 D 类火。

水的冷却作用会使金属发生爆炸性膨胀，因为金属的燃烧温度极高。

水型灭火器的操作方式多种多样。

有些是手压式的，另一些则是加压式的。

加压式灭火器有的把气体与水储存在同一容器内，有的采用“酸碱式”结构，让酸液流入灭火器内部的苏打溶液容器。

苏打与酸发生化学反应，使灭火器内部建立起压力，把水压出。

二氧化碳（CO₂）灭火器用于 A、B、C 类火，通过使火失去氧气来灭火。

此外，与水型灭火器一样，CO₂ 也能冷却燃烧中的材料。

绝不能对 D 类火使用 CO₂。

与水型灭火器一样，CO₂ 对高温金属的冷却作用也可能导致金属爆炸性膨胀。

使用 CO₂ 灭火器时，灭火器各部位都可能变得极冷，并在喷射结束后短时间内保持低温。

应穿戴防护装备或采取其他预防措施，以防冻伤一类的低温伤害。

在密闭或狭小空间操作 CO₂ 灭火器必须格外谨慎。

不仅火可能失去氧气，操作者本人也可能缺氧。

CO₂ 灭火器一般采用自喷式操作方式。

这意味着 CO₂ 在正常工作压力下就有足够的压力自行喷出。

容器内的压力由某种密封件或易碎膜片封住，击发机构（通常是一根销子）将密封件或膜片击破或刺穿。

这意味着，一旦密封件或膜片破损，容器内的压力就会释放，灭火器随即用尽，需要更换。

卤代烃灭火器对 B 类与 C 类火最有效；它们也可用于 A 类和 D 类火，但效果较差。

卤代烃（工业上常称为 Freon™）按化学式以 Halon™ 编号。

四氯化碳（Halon 104），化学式 CCl₄，UL 毒性等级为 3，因此毒性极强。

用四氯化碳扑救普通火灾时，会产生盐酸蒸气、氯气和光气；当四氯化碳直接接触高温金属、某些化学品或持续电弧时，光气的生成量还会增加。

它未被批准用于任何灭火用途。

在车间或机库内外发现的 Halon 104 旧容器，应按美国环境保护署（EPA）的法规以及当地法律和条例处置。

溴甲烷（Halon 1001），化学式 CH₃Br，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性等级为 2；它毒性很强，并且会腐蚀铝合金、镁和锌。

Halon 1001 不推荐在航空器上使用。

氯溴甲烷（Halon 1011），化学式 CH₂ClBr，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性等级为 3；与溴甲烷一样，Halon 1011 不推荐在航空器上使用。

二溴二氟甲烷（Halon 1202），化学式 CBr₂F₂，UL 毒性等级为 4；Halon 1202 不推荐在航空器上使用。

溴氯二氟甲烷（Halon 1211），化学式 CBrClF₂，是一种液化气体，UL 毒性等级为 5；它无色、无腐蚀性，蒸发迅速且不留残渣。

它不会冻结或造成冷烧伤，也不会损害其接触到的织物、金属或其他材料。

Halon 1211 对火作用迅速，方法是产生厚重的覆盖雾，把氧气从火源处排除。

更重要的是，它会在化学上干扰火的燃烧过程。

此外，它在火被扑灭后防止复燃方面具有优异性能。

三氟溴甲烷（Halon 1301），化学式 CF₃Br，同样是液化气体，UL 毒性等级为 6，具备 Halon 1211 的全部特性。

两者的显著区别在于：Halon 1211 形成类似 CO₂ 的喷射流，而 Halon 1301 的蒸气喷射较难对准目标。

注：由于对臭氧层的影响，EPA 已将 Halon 的产量限制在 1986 年的水平。

干粉灭火器虽对 B、C 类火有效，但最适用于 D 类火。

干粉灭火器的操作方式各不相同：可用气体药筒充压，可靠容器内储存的压力把干粉压出，也可从大容器或桶中舀出干粉泼向火。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 让步状语：while effective on …

**原句**：Dry powder extinguishers, while effective on Class B and C fires, are best for use on Class D fires.

**拆解**：主语 Dry powder extinguishers，谓语 are best for use on；while effective on Class B and C fires 是省略了 they are 的让步状语从句，插在主语与谓语之间，意为「虽然对 B、C 类火有效」。

**要点**：while 除「当……时」外，还可表让步（＝although），判断依据是：主句结论与从句内容构成转折（有效但不是最好/最适用）。省略主谓的 while 结构只能用于从句主语与主句主语一致时；be best for use on 是「最适合用于……」的固定说法。

### ② 部分倒装：Not only … but so too can …

**原句**：Not only can the fire be deprived of oxygen, but so too can the operator.

**拆解**：Not only 置于句首，引起部分倒装：情态动词 can 提到主语 the fire 之前，构成 can the fire be deprived of oxygen。后半句 but so too can the operator 也是倒装，并省略了重复的谓语部分，完整为 but the operator can also be deprived of oxygen。

**要点**：not only 位于句首时主句必须部分倒装（助动词/情态动词 + 主语 + 其余），这是考试与写作的高频考点。so too + 助动词 + 主语 表示「……也是如此」；注意不要写成 so can the operator too 之类语序。

### ③ 现在分词短语作结果状语

**原句**：The chemical reaction of the soda and the acid causes pressure to build inside the fire extinguisher, forcing the water out.

**拆解**：主干 The chemical reaction … causes pressure to build；cause sth to do 表示「使某物做某事」；逗号后的 forcing the water out 是现在分词短语作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面的整个反应或 pressure。

**要点**：cause sb to do 中的不定式必须带 to，与 make/let sb do 省略 to 的用法不同，不要混用；分词作结果状语常置于句末，用逗号隔开，可改写为 so that 从句或 which forces …。注意 forcing 的逻辑主语要与主句内容一致，否则会成悬垂分词。

### ④ 情态动词 + 被动语态

**原句**：All electrical power must be removed or shut off to the burning area.

**拆解**：主语 All electrical power，谓语 must be removed or shut off（情态动词 + be + 两个并列过去分词），to the burning area 是介词短语，说明断电所针对的区域。

**要点**：安全条文常省略执行者，用「must be + 过去分词」表达强制要求；并列过去分词共用一个 be，避免重复。remove power 与 shut off power 都可译「断电」，前者偏「切除」，后者偏「关闭」；本句 to the burning area 修饰的是断电这一动作的作用范围。
