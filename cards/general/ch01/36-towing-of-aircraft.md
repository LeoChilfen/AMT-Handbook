# Towing of Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 01 · Towing of Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 39 页

## 1. 原文

Movement of large aircraft about the airport, flight line, and hangar is usually accomplished by towing with a tow tractor (sometimes called a “tug”). [Figure 1-19] In the case of small aircraft, some moving is accomplished by hand pushing on the correct areas of the aircraft. Aircraft may also be taxied about the flight line but usually only by certain qualified personnel.

Towing aircraft can be a hazardous operation, causing damage to the aircraft and injury to personnel, if done recklessly or carelessly. The following paragraphs outline the general procedure for towing aircraft. However, specific instructions for each model of aircraft are detailed in the manufacturer’s maintenance instructions and are to be followed in all instances.

Before the aircraft to be towed is moved, a qualified person must be in the flight deck to operate the brakes in case the tow bar fails or becomes unhooked. The aircraft can then be stopped, preventing possible damage.

Some types of tow bars available for general use can be used for many types of towing operations. [Figure 1-20] These bars are designed with sufficient tensile strength to pull most aircraft, but are not intended to be subjected to torsional or twisting loads. Many have small wheels that permit them to be drawn behind the towing vehicle going to or from an aircraft.

When the bar is attached to the aircraft, inspect all the engaging devices for damage or malfunction before moving the aircraft.

Additionally, some aircraft have tow steering turn limits.

Some tow bars are designed for towing various types of aircraft. However, other special types can be used on a particular aircraft only. Such bars are usually designed and built by the aircraft manufacturer.

When towing the aircraft, the towing vehicle speed must be reasonable, and all persons involved in the operation must be alert. When the aircraft is stopped, do not rely upon the brakes of the towing vehicle alone to stop the aircraft. The person in the flight deck must coordinate the use of the aircraft brakes with those of the towing vehicle. A typical smaller aircraft tow tractor (or tug) is shown in Figure 1-21.

The attachment of the tow bar varies on different types of aircraft. Aircraft equipped with tail wheels are generally towed forward by attaching the tow bar to the main landing gear. In most cases, it is permissible to tow the aircraft in reverse by attaching the tow bar to the tail wheel axle. Any time an aircraft equipped with a tail wheel is towed, the tail wheel must be unlocked or the tail wheel locking mechanism may damage or break. Aircraft equipped with tricycle landing gear are generally towed forward by attaching a tow bar to the axle of the nosewheel. They may also be towed forward or backward by attaching a towing bridle or specially designed towing bar to the towing lugs on the main landing gear. When an aircraft is towed in this manner, a steering bar is attached to the nosewheel to steer the aircraft.

The following towing and parking procedures are typical of one type of operation. They are examples and not necessarily suited to every type of operation. Aircraft ground-handling personnel must be thoroughly familiar with all procedures pertaining to the types of aircraft being towed and local operation standards governing ground handling of aircraft.

Competent persons that have been properly checked out direct the aircraft towing team.

1. The towing vehicle driver is responsible for operating the vehicle in a safe manner and obeying emergency stop instructions given by any team member.

2. The person in charge assigns team personnel as wing walkers. A wing walker is stationed at each wingtip, in such a position that they can ensure adequate clearance

of any obstruction in the path of the aircraft. A tail walker is assigned when sharp turns are to be made or when the aircraft is to be backed into position.

3. A qualified person occupies the pilot’s seat of the towed aircraft to observe and operate the brakes as required.

When necessary, another qualified person is stationed to watch and maintain aircraft hydraulic system pressure.

4. The person in charge of the towing operation verifies that, on aircraft with a steerable nosewheel, the locking scissors are set to full swivel for towing. The locking device must be reset after the tow bar has been removed from the aircraft. Persons stationed in the aircraft are not to attempt to steer or turn the nosewheel when the tow bar is attached to the aircraft.

5. Under no circumstances is anyone permitted to walk or to ride between the nosewheel of an aircraft and the towing vehicle, nor ride on the outside of a moving aircraft or on the towing vehicle. In the interest of safety, no attempt to board or leave a moving aircraft or towing vehicle is permitted.

6. The towing speed of the aircraft is not to exceed that of the walking team members. The aircraft’s engines usually are not operated when the aircraft is being towed into position.

7. The aircraft brake system is to be charged before each towing operation. Aircraft with faulty brakes are towed into position only for repair of brake systems, and then personnel must be standing by ready with chocks for emergency use. Chocks must be immediately available in case of an emergency throughout any towing operation.

8. To avoid possible personal injury and aircraft damage during towing operations, entrance doors are closed, ladders retracted, and gear-down locks installed.

9. Prior to towing any aircraft, check all tires and landing gear struts for proper inflation. (Inflation of landing gear struts of aircraft in overhaul and storage is excluded.)

10. When moving aircraft, do not start and stop suddenly.

For added safety, aircraft brakes must never be applied during towing, except upon command by one of the tow team members in an emergency situation.

11. Aircraft are parked in specified areas. Generally, the distance between rows of parked aircraft is great enough to allow immediate access of emergency vehicles in case of fire, as well as free movement of equipment and materials.

12. Wheel chocks are placed fore and aft of the main landing gear of the parked aircraft.

13. Internal or external control locks (gust locks or blocks)

are used while the aircraft is parked.

14. Prior to any movement of aircraft across runways or taxiways, contact the airport control tower on the appropriate frequency for clearance to proceed.

15. An aircraft parked in a hangar must be statically grounded immediately.

## 2. 中文翻译

大型航空器在机场、停机坪和机库之间的移动，通常借助牵引车（有时称为 tug）牵引完成。就小型航空器而言，部分移动是通过在航空器正确的部位徒手推来实现的。航空器也可在停机坪上滑行，但通常只允许特定合格人员实施。

牵引航空器可能是一项危险作业：若操作鲁莽或粗心，会造成航空器损坏和人员受伤。以下各段概述牵引航空器的一般程序。但是，每种机型的专用说明详载于制造商的维修说明中，任何情况下都必须遵守。

在待牵引的航空器移动之前，驾驶舱内必须有一名合格人员操作刹车，以防牵引杆失效或脱钩。这样航空器能够被停住，避免可能的损坏。

一些通用型牵引杆可用于多种牵引作业。这类牵引杆按足以牵引大多数航空器的抗拉强度设计，但不应承受扭转或扭转载荷。许多牵引杆装有小型车轮，使其在往返航空器的途中可被牵引车拖着走。

牵引杆与航空器连接后，在移动航空器之前，要检查所有啮合装置有无损坏或功能失常。

此外，某些航空器对牵引转向角度有限制。

有些牵引杆设计用于牵引多种类型的航空器；而另一些专用牵引杆只能用于某一特定航空器，通常由航空器制造商设计并制造。

牵引航空器时，牵引车的速度必须适中，参与作业的所有人员必须保持警觉。航空器停车时，不得仅依靠牵引车的刹车来使航空器停住；驾驶舱内的人员必须把航空器刹车的使用与牵引车刹车的使用协调起来。图 1-21 所示为典型的小型航空器牵引车（或拖车）。

牵引杆的连接方式因航空器类型不同而异。装有尾轮的航空器通常把牵引杆连接在主起落架上向前牵引；多数情况下，也允许把牵引杆连接在尾轮轴上反向牵引。凡牵引装有尾轮的航空器时，尾轮必须解锁，否则尾轮锁定机构可能损坏或折断。装前三点式起落架的航空器通常把牵引杆连接在前轮轴上向前牵引；也可把牵引索具或专用牵引杆连接在主起落架的牵引耳上，向前或向后牵引。以此种方式牵引时，前轮上要装转向杆以操纵航空器转向。

以下牵引与停放程序属某一类作业的典型做法，仅为示例，未必适用于每一种作业。航空器地面勤务人员必须透彻熟悉与被牵引机型有关的全部程序，以及主管航空器地面搬运的当地作业标准。

航空器牵引小组由经过适当考核的合格人员指挥。

1. 牵引车驾驶员负责以安全的方式操纵车辆，并服从任何小组成员发出的紧急停车指令。

2. 负责人指派小组成员担任机翼观察员。每个翼尖处设一名机翼观察员，其站位应能确保足够的间隙

……以避开航空器行进路径上的任何障碍物。需要做急转弯或需要把航空器倒退入位时，另派一名尾部观察员。

3. 由一名合格人员坐在被牵引航空器的驾驶员座位上，按需观察并操作刹车。

必要时，另派一名合格人员值守，观察并保持航空器液压系统压力。

4. 牵引作业负责人应核实：对装有可操纵前轮的航空器，锁定剪已置于全自由回转位置以便牵引。牵引杆从航空器上拆下后，必须把锁定装置复位。牵引杆连接在航空器上时，机上人员不得试图操纵或转动前轮。

5. 任何情况下都不允许任何人在航空器前轮与牵引车之间行走或乘坐，也不得乘坐在运动中的航空器外侧或牵引车上。为安全起见，禁止在航空器或牵引车运动时上下。

6. 航空器的牵引速度不得超过步行的小组成员的速度。航空器被牵引入位时，通常不运转其发动机。

7. 每次牵引作业前，航空器刹车系统必须充压。刹车有故障的航空器只允许为修理刹车系统而牵引入位，此时必须有人员手持轮挡待命以备应急。整个牵引作业过程中，轮挡必须随时可以取用。

8. 为避免牵引作业中可能发生的人身伤害和航空器损坏，应关闭登机门、收起梯子，并装上起落架放下锁。

9. 牵引任何航空器之前，检查所有轮胎和起落架支柱的充气是否正常。（处于翻修和封存状态的航空器，其起落架支柱充气不在此列。）

10. 移动航空器时，不得突然起动和停车。

为进一步保障安全，牵引过程中绝不得使用航空器刹车，除非在紧急情况下按某名牵引小组成员的指令使用。

11. 航空器停放在指定区域。通常，停放航空器各行之间的距离足够大，既能在发生火灾时让应急车辆立即接近，也能让设备和器材自由通行。

12. 在停放航空器的主起落架前后放置轮挡。

13. 内部或外部操纵面锁（阵风锁或挡块）

……在航空器停放期间使用。

14. 航空器穿越跑道或滑行道之前，须以相应频率联系机场管制塔台，取得放行许可。

15. 停放在机库内的航空器必须立即进行静电接地。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 否定词置于句首引起部分倒装

**原句**：Under no circumstances is anyone permitted to walk or to ride between the nosewheel of an aircraft and the towing vehicle, nor ride on the outside of a moving aircraft or on the towing vehicle.

**拆解**：Under no circumstances 是否定状语前置，主句因此部分倒装：is anyone permitted（系动词提前），正常语序为 anyone is permitted；permitted 后接并列不定式 to walk or to ride…；nor ride… 是 nor 引导的并列否定成分，ride 前省略 to。

**要点**：never、under no circumstances、in no case、not until 等否定成分置于句首时，主句要部分倒装。nor 连接并列成分，前提是前文已是否定，本句的否定由 no circumstances 提供；译作「任何情况下都不得……」。

### ② be to do 表规定 + that of 替代前文名词

**原句**：The towing speed of the aircraft is not to exceed that of the walking team members.

**拆解**：is not to exceed 是「be to do」结构的否定式，表规章性的「不得……」；that of the walking team members 中 that 替代前文的 the towing speed，of 短语作其定语。

**要点**：be to do 在手册里表强制规定，比 must 更书面；否定式即「禁止」。比较句中用 that / those 替代前面出现过的名词，避免重复：the speed of A is not to exceed that of B（不是 the speed of B）。

### ③ 目的状语 + 并列被动句的省略

**原句**：To avoid possible personal injury and aircraft damage during towing operations, entrance doors are closed, ladders retracted, and gear-down locks installed.

**拆解**：To avoid… 是不定式短语作目的状语；主句为三个并列的被动分句 entrance doors are closed、ladders retracted、gear-down locks installed，后两个省略了相同的 are。

**要点**：并列句中相同的 be 动词可以只在第一分句出现，这是技术英语压缩句子的常用手段，翻译时要补回「被关闭／被收起／被装上」。不定式表目的置于句首时，后面通常用逗号与主句隔开。

### ④ 不定式被动作后置定语 + in case 从句

**原句**：Before the aircraft to be towed is moved, a qualified person must be in the flight deck to operate the brakes in case the tow bar fails or becomes unhooked.

**拆解**：the aircraft to be towed 中 to be towed 是不定式的被动式，作 aircraft 的后置定语；该名词短语整体作 before 从句的主语，谓语是 is moved；in case 引导条件状语从句，意为「以防……」。

**要点**：不定式被动（to be + 过去分词）常作后置定语，表「将要被……的」，如 the aircraft to be serviced。in case 后接从句时用一般现在时表将来，不接 will；in case of 则后接名词。
