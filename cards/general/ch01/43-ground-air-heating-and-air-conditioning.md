# Ground Air Heating and Air Conditioning

> 通用 Ch 01 · Ground Air Heating and Air Conditioning · 原文 PDF 第 46 页

## 1. 原文

Most airport gates have facilities that can provide heated or cooled air. The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations that connect to the aircraft’s ventilation system by use of a large hose. Portable heating and air conditioning units can also be moved close to the aircraft and connected by a duct that provides air to keep the cabin temperature comfortable.

## 2. 中文翻译

多数机场登机口设有能够提供加热或冷却空气的设施。对空气进行冷却或加热的机组属于固定安装设备，通过一根大口径软管与航空器的通风系统相连。便携式加热与空调机组也可以移到航空器附近，用风管连接，向机内送风以保持客舱温度舒适。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① facilities that can provide heated or cooled air（定语从句 + 分词定语）

**原句**：Most airport gates have facilities that can provide heated or cooled air.

**拆解**：主句 Most airport gates have facilities；that 引导定语从句修饰 facilities，在从句中作主语；heated or cooled 是过去分词作前置定语修饰 air。

**要点**：英文短定语从句译成中文时一般前置成「……的设施」。heated／cooled 是分词形容词，表「已加热／已冷却的」；写成 heating air 就变成「正在加热空气」，意思不同。

### ② The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations …（双定语从句）

**原句**：The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations that connect to the aircraft’s ventilation system by use of a large hose.

**拆解**：主语 The units 后接定语从句 that cool or heat the air；谓语 are；表语 permanent installations 后又接 that connect to … 定语从句；by use of a large hose 为方式状语。

**要点**：句中有两个 that 从句，分别修饰 units 和 installations，不要误读成一句。by use of = by using／with，后接名词，比 by using 更书面。connect to 是「接到……上」，connect with 侧重「与……连通」。

### ③ can also be moved … and connected by a duct（并列被动 + keep 复合宾语）

**原句**：Portable heating and air conditioning units can also be moved close to the aircraft and connected by a duct that provides air to keep the cabin temperature comfortable.

**拆解**：主语 Portable heating and air conditioning units；谓语为并列被动 can also be moved … and connected；close to the aircraft 地点状语；by a duct 方式状语；that provides air to keep … 定语从句修饰 duct，其中 to keep 为目的不定式，keep + 宾语 + 形容词构成复合宾语。

**要点**：并列被动中第二个 connected 前省略了 can also be，读句时补全即可。keep the cabin temperature comfortable 是 keep + n. + adj. 结构，不能写成 keep … comfortably。that 从句的先行词是 duct，不是 aircraft。
