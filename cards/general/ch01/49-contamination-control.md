# Contamination Control

> 通用 Ch 01 · Contamination Control · 原文 PDF 第 47 页

## 1. 原文

Contamination is anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there. The types of contamination found in aviation fuel include water, solids, and microbial growths. The control of contamination in aviation fuel is extremely important, since contamination can lead to engine failure or stoppage and the loss of life. The best method of controlling contamination is to prevent its introduction into the fuel system. Some forms of contamination can still occur inside the fuel system. However, the filter, separators, and screens remove most of the contamination.

Water in aviation fuels generally take two forms: dissolved (vapor) and free water. The dissolved water is not a major problem until, as the temperature lowers, it becomes free water. This then poses a problem if ice crystals form, clogging filters and other small orifices.

This is the water that is drained after fueling an aircraft. Entrained water is suspended water droplets. These droplets may not be visible to the eye but give the fuel a cloudy look. The entrained water settles out in time.

Solid contaminants are insoluble in fuel. The more common types are rust, dirt, sand, gasket material, lint, and fragments of shop towels. The close tolerances of fuel controls and other fuel-related mechanisms can be damaged or blocked by particles as small as 1⁄20 the diameter of a human hair.

Microbiological growths are a problem in jet fuel. There are a number of varieties of micro-organisms that can live in the free water in jet fuel. Some variations of these organisms are airborne, others live in the soil. The aircraft fuel system becomes susceptible to the introduction of these organisms each time the aircraft is fueled. Favorable conditions for the growth of micro-organisms in the fuel are warm temperatures and the presence of iron oxide and mineral salts in the water. The best way to prevent microbial growth is to keep the fuel dry.

The effects of micro-organisms are:

• Formation of slime or sludge that can foul filters, separators, or fuel controls.

• Emulsification of the fuel.

• Corrosive compounds that can attack the fuel tank’s structure. In the case of a wet wing tank, the tank is made from the aircraft’s structure. They can also have offensive odors.

## 2. 中文翻译

污染是指燃油中任何本不应存在的物质。航空燃油中发现的污染类型包括水、固体颗粒和微生物生长物。控制航空燃油的污染极为重要，因为污染可能导致发动机失效或停车以及人员死亡。控制污染的最好方法是防止其进入燃油系统。有些形式的污染仍可能在燃油系统内部产生。不过，滤芯、分离器和滤网能去除大部分污染物。

航空燃油中的水一般有两种形态：溶解水（蒸气）和游离水。溶解水本身不是大问题，只有当温度降低、它变成游离水时才成为问题。此时若形成冰晶，堵塞滤芯和其他细小孔道，便会造成问题。

（原文此处为颜色代码表格残片：Color—Grade，Red—80、Green—100、Blue—100LL、Purple—115，略）游离水可能以水块或夹带水的形式出现。水块是水的聚集。这是在给飞机加油后放出的水。夹带水是悬浮的水滴。这些水滴可能肉眼不可见，但会使燃油呈现浑浊外观。夹带水会随时间沉降析出。

固体污染物不溶于燃油。较常见的类型有铁锈、污垢、砂粒、垫圈材料、棉绒和擦布碎片。燃油控制器及其他燃油相关机构的紧公差可能被小至人发直径 1⁄20 的颗粒损坏或堵塞。

微生物生长物是喷气燃油中的一个问题。有若干种类的微生物能够生活在喷气燃油的游离水中。这些生物有些通过空气传播，另一些生活在土壤中。飞机每次加油时，其燃油系统都会变得容易引入这些生物。燃油中微生物生长的有利条件是温暖的气温以及水中存在氧化铁和矿物盐。防止微生物生长的最好方法是保持燃油干燥。

微生物的影响有：

• 形成黏液或油泥，会污染滤芯、分离器或燃油控制器。

• 燃油乳化。

• 产生腐蚀性化合物，会侵蚀燃油箱结构。对于整体机翼油箱（wet wing），油箱即由飞机结构本身构成。它们还可能产生难闻的气味。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① anything 与 that 引导的定语从句
**原句**：Contamination is anything in the fuel that is not supposed to be there.
**拆解**：主语 Contamination + 系动词 is + 表语 anything（+ in the fuel 后置定语 + that is not supposed to be there 定语从句，that 作从句主语）。
**要点**：anything、something、all 等不定代词作先行词时关系代词通常用 that；be supposed to 表「按理应当」，not supposed to be there = 本不该出现在那里。

### ② since 引导原因状语从句
**原句**：The control of contamination in aviation fuel is extremely important, since contamination can lead to engine failure or stoppage and the loss of life.
**拆解**：主句 The control of contamination in aviation fuel is extremely important（主系表）+ since 引导原因状语从句 + lead to 后接并列宾语 engine failure or stoppage 和 the loss of life。
**要点**：since 表原因时语气比 because 轻、更书面，且常放在主句之后；lead to 中 to 是介词，后接名词，不能接动词原形。

### ③ 现在分词作结果状语
**原句**：This then poses a problem if ice crystals form, clogging filters and other small orifices.
**拆解**：主句 This then poses a problem + if 条件从句 ice crystals form + 逗号后的现在分词短语 clogging filters and other small orifices 作结果状语。
**要点**：分词短语放句末、前有逗号，常表伴随结果（= and this clogs…）；其逻辑主语是前面从句中的 ice crystals，而不是主句的 this，判断逻辑主语要看语义。

### ④ not … until 结构
**原句**：The dissolved water is not a major problem until, as the temperature lowers, it becomes free water.
**拆解**：主句 The dissolved water is not a major problem + until 引导时间状语从句；从句中又插入 as the temperature lowers（as 引导时间状语从句）。
**要点**：not … until 意为「直到……才……」，翻译时否定要后移到 until 之后；until 从句用一般现在时表将来，符合时间状语从句「主将从现」的规则。
