# Engine Starting and Operation

> 通用 Ch 01 · Engine Starting and Operation · 原文 PDF 第 33 页

## 1. 原文

The following instructions cover the starting procedures for reciprocating, turboprop, turbofan, and APU. These procedures are presented only as a general guide for familiarization with typical procedures and methods. Detailed instructions for starting a specific type of engine can be found in the manufacturer’s instruction book.

Before starting an aircraft engine:

1. Position the aircraft to head into the prevailing wind to ensure adequate airflow over the engine for cooling purposes.

2. Make sure that no property damage or personal injury occurs from the propeller blast or jet exhaust.

3. If external electrical power is used for starting, ensure that it can be removed safely, and it is sufficient for the total starting sequence.

4. During any and all starting procedures, a “fireguard” equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher shall be stationed in an appropriate place. A fireguard is someone familiar with aircraft starting procedures.

The fire extinguisher should be a CO 2 extinguisher of at least 5-pound capacity. The appropriate place is adjacent to the outboard side of the engine, in view of the pilot, and also where they can observe the engine/ aircraft for indication of starting problems.

5. If the aircraft is turbine-engine powered, the area in front of the jet inlet must be kept clear of personnel, property, and/or debris (FOD).

6. These “before starting” procedures apply to all aircraft powerplants.

7. Follow manufacturer’s checklists for start procedures and shutdown procedures.

## 2. 中文翻译

以下说明涵盖往复式、涡桨、涡扇发动机以及 APU 的起动程序。这些程序仅作为通用指南提供，用以熟悉典型的程序与方法。起动某一具体型号发动机的详细说明，见厂家的说明手册。

起动航空器发动机之前：

1. 使航空器机头朝向主导风，以确保流过发动机的气流足以冷却。

2. 确认不会因螺旋桨尾流或喷气尾流造成财产损坏或人员伤害。

3. 若起动使用外部电源，要确认它能安全地撤除，并且容量足以完成整个起动过程。

4. 在任何及所有起动程序中，都必须有一名「消防警戒员」携带适用的灭火器，驻守在适当位置。消防警戒员应是熟悉航空器起动程序的人员。

灭火器应为容量至少 5 磅的 CO₂ 灭火器。所谓适当位置，即紧靠发动机外侧、处于飞行员视野之内，同时又能观察发动机／航空器以发现起动异常迹象的地方。

5. 若航空器为涡轮发动机驱动，喷气进气口前方的区域必须保持无人员、无物品、无碎屑（FOD）。

6. 这些「起动前」程序适用于所有航空器动力装置。

7. 起动程序与停车程序均应遵循厂家的检查单。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① shall + 被动语态，配分词后置定语

**原句**：During any and all starting procedures, a “fireguard” equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher shall be stationed in an appropriate place.

**拆解**：During any and all starting procedures 是时间状语（any and all 双重强调「任何、所有」）；主语 a “fireguard” 带过去分词短语 equipped with a suitable fire extinguisher 作后置定语；谓语 shall be stationed 是「shall + be + 过去分词」，表规章义务；in an appropriate place 是地点状语。

**要点**：手册与法规用 shall 表示强制性要求，语气比 must 更「文件化」，且配被动语态把执行者隐去。equipped with 是形容词性被动短语，译作「配备……的」，不要当成「被装备」这个动作。station 在此是动词「派驻、安置」，不是名词「车站」。

### ② where 引导的表语从句

**原句**：The appropriate place is adjacent to the outboard side of the engine, in view of the pilot, and also where they can observe the engine/ aircraft for indication of starting problems.

**拆解**：主干 The appropriate place is + 表语；表语由三部分并列：形容词短语 adjacent to the outboard side of the engine、介词短语 in view of the pilot、以及 where 引导的表语从句 where they can observe the engine/aircraft…。where 在此引导名词性从句作 is 的表语，不是地点状语从句。

**要点**：be + where… 表示「（位置）就在……的地方」。三个并列表语回答同一个问题「哪里才算合适位置」，中文用「即……，同时……，并且……」串起来最清楚。注意 in view of the pilot 意为「在飞行员视野之内」，不是「鉴于」。

### ③ 祈使句 + 两个功能不同的不定式

**原句**：Position the aircraft to head into the prevailing wind to ensure adequate airflow over the engine for cooling purposes.

**拆解**：祈使句以动词原形 Position 开头，宾语 the aircraft；to head into the prevailing wind 补充说明把航空器摆成什么姿态（position sth to do）；第二个 to ensure adequate airflow over the engine 才是目的状语；for cooling purposes 进一步点明目的。

**要点**：同一句里两个 to do 作用不同——第一个与 position 构成「使某物处于某姿态」，第二个表「为了」，读句时先抓住主句动词再判断不定式归属。手册祈使句惯用「动词原形 + 宾语 + to 说明目的」。prevailing wind 是「主导风、常向风」，不是「正在刮的风」。
