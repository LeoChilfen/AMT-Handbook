# Hydraulic Ground Power Units

> 通用 Ch 01 · Hydraulic Ground Power Units · 原文 PDF 第 44 页

## 1. 原文

Portable hydraulic test stands are manufactured in many sizes and cost ranges. [Figure 1-29] Some have a limited range of operation, while others can be used to perform all the system tests that fixed-shop test stands are designed to perform. Hydraulic power units, sometimes called a hydraulic mule, provide hydraulic pressure to operate the aircraft systems during maintenance. They can be used to:

• Drain the aircraft hydraulic systems.

• Filter the aircraft hydraulic system fluid.

• Refill the aircraft hydraulic system with clean fluid.

• Check the aircraft hydraulic systems for operation and leaks. This type of portable hydraulic test unit is usually an electrically-powered unit. It uses a hydraulic system Take off Go down Move back Move forward Move right Move left Landing direction Go up Swing tail to right Swing tail to left StopEngage rotorStart engine Stop rotor capable of delivering a variable volume of fluid from zero to approximately 24 gallons per minute at variable pressures up to 3,000 psi. Operating at pressures of 3,000 psi or more, extreme caution must be used when operating hydraulic power units. At 3,000 psi, a small stream from a leak can cut like a sharp knife. Therefore, inspect lines used with the system for cuts, frays, or any other damage, and keep them free of kinks and twists. When not in use, hydraulic power unit lines are to be stored (preferably wound on a reel) and kept clean, dry, and free of contaminants.

## 2. 中文翻译

便携式液压试验台有各种尺寸和价位。[图 1-29] 有些作业范围有限，而另一些则能完成固定车间试验台设计用来进行的全部系统试验。液压动力装置（有时称为“液压骡”，hydraulic mule）在维修期间提供液压压力，以操作飞机各系统。它们可用于：

• 排空飞机液压系统。

• 过滤飞机液压系统油液。

• 用清洁油液补充飞机液压系统。

• 检查飞机液压系统的运行情况和渗漏。这类便携式液压试验装置通常是电动装置。它使用一套液压系统（原文此处插入一张地面操作信号表的图内文字残片：Take off、Go down、Move back、Move forward、Move right、Move left、Landing direction、Go up、Swing tail to right、Swing tail to left、Stop、Engage rotor、Start engine、Stop rotor 等，略），能够以可变压力（最高 3,000 psi）输送从零到约每分钟 24 加仑的可变流量油液。在 3,000 psi 或更高的压力下操作液压动力装置时，必须极其谨慎。在 3,000 psi 下，渗漏形成的一小股液流能像快刀一样割伤人。因此，应检查系统所用管路有无割口、磨损或任何其他损伤，并保持管路不扭结、不拧绞。不使用时，液压动力装置的管路应存放起来（最好卷绕在卷盘上），并保持清洁、干燥、无污染物。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① while 表对比 + that 引导的定语从句
**原句**：Some have a limited range of operation, while others can be used to perform all the system tests that fixed-shop test stands are designed to perform.
**拆解**：while 连接两个对比分句（some…, others…）；can be used 为情态被动；that 引导定语从句修饰 all the system tests，从句中 perform 的宾语就是关系代词 that，故末尾不再加 it。
**要点**：while 在此表“而”，不是“当……时”；定语从句末尾的 perform 后不能再补 it，这是中国学生最常见的冗余错误。

### ② 过去分词短语作插入式后置定语
**原句**：Hydraulic power units, sometimes called a hydraulic mule, provide hydraulic pressure to operate the aircraft systems during maintenance.
**拆解**：主语 Hydraulic power units；sometimes called a hydraulic mule 为过去分词短语作插入式后置定语（补充说明别名）；谓语 provide + 宾语 hydraulic pressure；to operate… 为目的不定式；during maintenance 为时间状语。
**要点**：过去分词短语用逗号隔开作同位式补充；to operate 表目的，不能用 for operate（介词后须接动名词）。

### ③ 悬垂分词（dangling participle）
**原句**：Operating at pressures of 3,000 psi or more, extreme caution must be used when operating hydraulic power units.
**拆解**：Operating… 为现在分词短语作状语，其逻辑主语本应是“操作人员”，而主句主语是被动句的 extreme caution，形式上构成悬垂分词。
**要点**：技术手册里这种写法很常见，但严格说是语病；改写可让分词与主句主语一致，如 When operating at 3,000 psi or more, you must use extreme caution。读懂即可，自己写时应避免。

### ④ like 引导的介词短语作方式状语
**原句**：At 3,000 psi, a small stream from a leak can cut like a sharp knife.
**拆解**：At 3,000 psi 为状语；主语 a small stream from a leak（from a leak 为后置定语）；谓语 can cut；like a sharp knife 为介词短语作方式状语。
**要点**：like 后接名词表“像……一样”，不能接句子（接句子要用 as if）；can 在此表能力或可能性，说明高压液流的危险性。
