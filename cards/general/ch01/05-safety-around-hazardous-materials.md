# Safety Around Hazardous Materials

> 通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Hazardous Materials · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

## 1. 原文

Material safety diamonds are important with regard to shop safety. These diamond-shaped labels are a simple and quick way to determine the risk of hazardous material within the associated container and, if used properly with the tags, indicate what personal safety equipment to use.

The most observable portion of the Safety Data Sheets (SDSs)

(formerly known as Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS))

label is the risk diamond. It is a four-color segmented diamond that represents flammability (red), reactivity (yellow), health (blue), and special hazard (white). In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4. Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous. The special hazard segment contains a word or abbreviation to represent the specific hazard. Some examples are RAD for radiation, ALK for alkali materials, Acid for acidic materials, and CARC for carcinogenic materials. The letter W with a line through it stands for high reactivity to water. [Figure 1-2]

The SDS is a more detailed version of the chemical safety issues. These forms have the detailed breakdown of the chemicals, including formulas and action to take if personnel come in contact with the chemicals. All sheets have the same information requirements; however, the exact location of the information on the sheet may vary depending on the SDS manufacturer. These forms are necessary for a safe shop that meets all the requirements of the governing safety body, the U.S. Department of Labor Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA).

## 2. 中文翻译

材料安全菱形标志对车间安全十分重要。这些菱形标签是一种简单快捷的方法，用来判断相应容器内危险材料的风险；若与标签（tags）配合正确使用，还能指示应使用何种个人防护装备。

安全数据表（SDS，原称材料安全数据表 MSDS）上最可观的部分是风险菱形标志。它是一个四色分区的菱形，分别代表可燃性（红色）、反应性（黄色）、健康危害（蓝色）和特殊危害（白色）。

在可燃性、反应性和健康危害三个色块中，标有 0 到 4 的数字。0 表示对使用者几乎没有危害或没有危害，而 4 表示该材料危害极大。特殊危害色块中写有一个单词或缩写，用来表示特定的危害。例如 RAD 表示放射性、ALK 表示碱性材料、Acid 表示酸性材料、CARC 表示致癌材料。带斜杠的字母 W 表示对水具有高反应性。

SDS 是化学品安全问题上的更详细版本。这些表格详细列出化学品的构成，包括化学式，以及人员接触化学品后应采取的处置措施。所有 SDS 的信息要求都相同；但信息在表格上的具体位置可能因 SDS 的编制厂商不同而有所差异。要建成符合主管安全机构——美国劳工部职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）——全部要求的、安全的车间，这些表格是必需的。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4（there be 句型）

**原句**：In the flammability, reactivity, and health blocks, there is a number from 0 to 4.

**拆解**：介词短语 In the … blocks 提到句首作地点状语，真正的主干是 there is a number from 0 to 4；there be 句型中 be 与后面的真正主语 a number 一致。

**要点**：there be 表示「某处存在某物」，不能与 have 混用；地点状语前置是为了承接上文（先提三个色块，再说块里的数字）。写法上要注意 there be 后的主语才是决定单复数的成分：there is a number / there are numbers。

### ② Zero represents little or no hazard …, while 4 means that …（while 表对比）

**原句**：Zero represents little or no hazard to the user, while 4 means that the material is very hazardous.

**拆解**：前半句主谓宾 Zero represents little or no hazard；while 引导对比分句，表示「而……」，means 后接 that 引导的宾语从句。

**要点**：while 在此表对比而非时间，译作「而」；与 whereas 相近，但 whereas 更书面、更强调对照。注意 little or no 是「几乎没有或完全没有」，比 not 更精确地留出中间地带，技术文中常用来描述等级刻度。

### ③ The most observable portion of the Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) (formerly known as …) label is the risk diamond（过去分词短语作插入语）

**原句**：The most observable portion of the Safety Data Sheets (SDSs) (formerly known as Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS)) label is the risk diamond.

**拆解**：主干是 The most observable portion … is the risk diamond；formerly known as Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) 是过去分词短语，放在括号中作插入语，补充说明 SDS 的旧称。阅读时跳过括号即可还原主谓。

**要点**：括号插入语在手册中极常见，作用是补充旧名、缩写或单位而不打断句子；分析句子成分时应先剔除插入语。formerly known as 是「过去称为」的固定说法，now known as 则是「现称为」；known as 与 known for 不同，前者表名称，后者表「以……著称」。
