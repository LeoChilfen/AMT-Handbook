# Towing of Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 01 · Towing of Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 39 页

## 1. 原文

Movement of large aircraft about the airport, flight line, and hangar is usually accomplished by towing with a tow tractor (sometimes called a “tug”). [Figure 1-19] In the case of small aircraft, some moving is accomplished by hand pushing on the correct areas of the aircraft. Aircraft may also be taxied about the flight line but usually only by certain qualified personnel.

Towing aircraft can be a hazardous operation, causing damage to the aircraft and injury to personnel, if done recklessly or carelessly. The following paragraphs outline the general procedure for towing aircraft. However, specific instructions for each model of aircraft are detailed in the manufacturer’s maintenance instructions and are to be followed in all instances.

Before the aircraft to be towed is moved, a qualified person must be in the flight deck to operate the brakes in case the tow bar fails or becomes unhooked. The aircraft can then be stopped, preventing possible damage.

Some types of tow bars available for general use can be used for many types of towing operations. [Figure 1-20] These bars are designed with sufficient tensile strength to pull most aircraft, but are not intended to be subjected to torsional or twisting loads. Many have small wheels that permit them to be drawn behind the towing vehicle going to or from an aircraft. When the bar is attached to the aircraft, inspect all the engaging devices for damage or malfunction before moving the aircraft. Additionally, some aircraft have tow steering turn limits.

Some tow bars are designed for towing various types of aircraft. However, other special types can be used on a particular aircraft only. Such bars are usually designed and built by the aircraft manufacturer.

When towing the aircraft, the towing vehicle speed must be reasonable, and all persons involved in the operation must be alert. When the aircraft is stopped, do not rely upon the brakes of the towing vehicle alone to stop the aircraft. The person in the flight deck must coordinate the use of the aircraft brakes with those of the towing vehicle. A typical smaller aircraft tow tractor (or tug) is shown in Figure 1-21.

The attachment of the tow bar varies on different types of aircraft. Aircraft equipped with tail wheels are generally towed forward by attaching the tow bar to the main landing gear. In most cases, it is permissible to tow the aircraft in reverse by attaching the tow bar to the tail wheel axle. Any time an aircraft equipped with a tail wheel is towed, the tail wheel must be unlocked or the tail wheel locking mechanism may damage or break. Aircraft equipped with tricycle landing gear are generally towed forward by attaching a tow bar to the axle of the nosewheel. They may also be towed forward or backward by attaching a towing bridle or specially designed towing bar to the towing lugs on the main landing gear. When an aircraft is towed in this manner, a steering bar is attached to the nosewheel to steer the aircraft. The following towing and parking procedures are typical of one type of operation. They are examples and not necessarily suited to every type of operation. Aircraft ground-handling personnel must be thoroughly familiar with all procedures pertaining to the types of aircraft being towed and local operation standards governing ground handling of aircraft. Competent persons that have been properly checked out direct the aircraft towing team.

1. The towing vehicle driver is responsible for operating the vehicle in a safe manner and obeying emergency stop instructions given by any team member.

2. The person in charge assigns team personnel as wing walkers. A tail walker is assigned when sharp turns are to be made or when the aircraft is to be backed into position.

3. A qualified person occupies the pilot’s seat of the towed aircraft to observe and operate the brakes as required. When necessary, another qualified person is stationed to watch and maintain aircraft hydraulic system pressure.

4. The person in charge of the towing operation verifies that, on aircraft with a steerable nosewheel, the locking scissors are set to full swivel for towing. The locking device must be reset after the tow bar has been removed from the aircraft. Persons stationed in the aircraft are not to attempt to steer or turn the nosewheel when the tow bar is attached to the aircraft.

5. Under no circumstances is anyone permitted to walk or to ride between the nosewheel of an aircraft and the towing vehicle, nor ride on the outside of a moving aircraft or on the towing vehicle. In the interest of safety, no attempt to board or leave a moving aircraft or towing vehicle is permitted.

6. The towing speed of the aircraft is not to exceed that of the walking team members. The aircraft’s engines usually are not operated when the aircraft is being towed into position. 7. The aircraft brake system is to be charged before each towing operation. Aircraft with faulty brakes are towed into position only for repair of brake systems, and then personnel must be standing by ready with chocks for emergency use. Chocks must be immediately available in case of an emergency throughout any towing operation.

8. To avoid possible personal injury and aircraft damage during towing operations, entrance doors are closed, ladders retracted, and gear-down locks installed.

9. Prior to towing any aircraft, check all tires and landing gear struts for proper inflation. (Inflation of landing gear struts of aircraft in overhaul and storage is excluded.)

10. When moving aircraft, do not start and stop suddenly. For added safety, aircraft brakes must never be applied during towing, except upon command by one of the tow team members in an emergency situation.

11. Aircraft are parked in specified areas. Generally, the distance between rows of parked aircraft is great enough to allow immediate access of emergency vehicles in case of fire, as well as free movement of equipment and materials.

12. Wheel chocks are placed fore and aft of the main landing gear of the parked aircraft.

13. Internal or external control locks (gust locks or blocks) are used while the aircraft is parked.

14. Prior to any movement of aircraft across runways or taxiways, contact the airport control tower on the appropriate frequency for clearance to proceed.

15. An aircraft parked in a hangar must be statically grounded immediately.

## 2. 中文翻译

大型飞机在机场、停机坪和机库之间的移动，通常借助牵引车（tow tractor，有时称为“tug”）牵引完成。[图 1-19] 对于小型飞机，部分移动工作是通过在飞机正确部位上手推完成的。飞机也可以在停机坪上滑行移动，但通常只允许特定的具备资格人员来做。

如果操作鲁莽或粗心，牵引飞机可能是一项危险作业，会造成飞机损坏和人员受伤。以下各段概述了牵引飞机的一般程序。不过，每种机型的具体说明详载于制造商的维修说明中，在任何情况下都必须遵照执行。

在移动待牵引的飞机之前，驾驶舱内必须有一名具备资格的人员操作刹车，以防牵引杆断裂或脱钩。这样就能把飞机停住，防止可能的损坏。

可供一般使用的某些型别牵引杆能用于多种牵引作业。[图 1-20] 这些牵引杆设计得具有足够的抗拉强度，可以拖动大多数飞机，但不适合承受扭转或扭绞载荷。许多牵引杆装有小轮，便于在前往或离开飞机时被拖挂在牵引车后面。牵引杆连接到飞机后，在移动飞机之前应检查所有接合装置有无损坏或失灵。此外，有些飞机还有牵引转向的角度限制。

有些牵引杆是为牵引多种型别飞机而设计的。而另一些专用型别则只能用于某一特定飞机。这类牵引杆通常由飞机制造商设计并制造。

牵引飞机时，牵引车的速度必须合理，参与作业的所有人员都必须保持警觉。飞机停下时，不要只依靠牵引车的刹车来停住飞机。驾驶舱内的人员必须把飞机刹车的使用与牵引车刹车协调起来。图 1-21 所示为一种典型的较小飞机牵引车（tug）。

牵引杆的连接方式因飞机型别而异。装有尾轮的飞机一般将牵引杆连接到主起落架上向前牵引。多数情况下，允许把牵引杆连接到尾轮轴上向后牵引。任何时候牵引装有尾轮的飞机，尾轮都必须解锁，否则尾轮锁定机构可能损坏或断裂。装前三点式起落架的飞机一般将牵引杆连接到前轮轴上向前牵引。也可以把牵引索或专门设计的牵引杆连接到主起落架上的牵引接头，向前或向后牵引。以这种方式牵引飞机时，需在前轮上装一根转向杆来操纵飞机转向。以下牵引和停放程序是某一种作业的典型做法。它们只是示例，未必适用于每一种作业。飞机地面保障人员必须彻底熟悉与所牵引机型有关的所有程序，以及当地管辖飞机地面保障的作业标准。经过适当考核合格的胜任人员负责指挥飞机牵引小组。

1. 牵引车驾驶员负责以安全方式操作车辆，并服从任何小组成员发出的紧急停车指令。

2. 负责人指派小组人员担任翼尖观察员（wing walker）。每个翼尖处都布置一名翼尖观察员，其站位须能确保飞机行进路径上任何障碍物都有足够的间隙（原文此处插入图示残片：Distance in feet、Velocity in knots = K、Temperature in °F 以及 125°/60 K 等换算数值，“30 feet”“25 feet”“12 feet”“Exhaust / Air intake idle / Air intake takeoff”等图内文字，略）。需要急转弯或需要把飞机倒退就位时，应指派一名尾部观察员（tail walker）。

3. 一名具备资格的人员坐在被牵引飞机的驾驶员座位上，按要求观察并操作刹车。必要时，另派一名具备资格的人员守候并维持飞机液压系统压力。

4. 牵引作业负责人核实：对于装有可转向前轮的飞机，转弯锁剪已置于全向转动（full swivel）位，以便牵引。牵引杆从飞机上拆下后，必须将锁定装置复位。牵引杆仍连接在飞机上时，机上人员不得试图操纵或转动前轮。

5. 任何情况下都不允许任何人行走或乘坐于飞机前轮与牵引车之间，也不允许乘坐在移动中的飞机或牵引车外侧。为安全起见，不允许试图上下移动中的飞机或牵引车。

6. 飞机牵引速度不得超过步行小组成员的速度。飞机被牵引就位时，其发动机通常不运转。 7. 每次牵引作业之前，飞机刹车系统都必须充压。刹车有故障的飞机只能为修理刹车系统而牵引就位，且此时人员必须手持轮挡待命，以备紧急使用。整个牵引作业过程中，轮挡必须随时可用。

8. 为避免牵引作业中可能的人身伤害和飞机损坏，舱门应关闭、梯子应收起、起落架下位锁应安装到位。

9. 牵引任何飞机之前，检查所有轮胎和起落架支柱的充气是否正常。（翻修中和封存中的飞机，其起落架支柱的充气不在此列。）

10. 移动飞机时，不要突然起动和停止。为增加安全性，牵引过程中绝不允许使用飞机刹车，除非在紧急情况下由牵引小组某成员发出指令。

11. 飞机停放在指定区域。一般各行停放飞机之间的间距足以让应急车辆在发生火灾时立即进入，也便于设备和材料的自由移动。

12. 轮挡放置在停放飞机主起落架的前方和后方。

13. 飞机停放期间使用内部或外部操纵面锁（阵风锁或锁块）。

14. 飞机穿越跑道或滑行道之前，须用适当频率联系机场管制塔台取得放行许可。

15. 停放在机库内的飞机必须立即进行静电接地。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 动名词作主语 + 省略形式的条件状语
**原句**：Towing aircraft can be a hazardous operation, causing damage to the aircraft and injury to personnel, if done recklessly or carelessly.
**拆解**：主语 Towing aircraft（动名词短语）+ 谓语 can be + 表语 a hazardous operation；causing damage… and injury… 为现在分词短语作结果状语；if done… 为省略的条件状语（= if it is done）。
**要点**：动名词主语把“牵引飞机这件事”整体名词化；if done 是“if + 过去分词”的省略式，条件从句主语与主句一致时常用，手册里比 if it is done 更简洁。

### ② 不定式被动式作后置定语 + in case 从句
**原句**：Before the aircraft to be towed is moved, a qualified person must be in the flight deck to operate the brakes in case the tow bar fails or becomes unhooked.
**拆解**：Before 引导时间状语从句，从句主语 the aircraft to be towed（不定式被动式后置定语）+ 谓语 is moved；主句 a qualified person must be in the flight deck；to operate the brakes 为目的不定式；in case 引导预防性条件从句。
**要点**：to be towed 表“待被牵引的”，是不定式被动式作定语的典型；in case + 陈述句（不用 will）表“以防、万一”，与 in case of + 名词区分。

### ③ 否定祈使句 + alone 的后置限定
**原句**：When the aircraft is stopped, do not rely upon the brakes of the towing vehicle alone to stop the aircraft.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，用被动 is stopped；主句为否定祈使句 do not rely upon…；alone 后置修饰 the brakes of the towing vehicle，意为“仅仅”；to stop the aircraft 为目的不定式。
**要点**：rely upon/on … alone 表示“只依靠……”，alone 必须后置；否定祈使句用 do not + 动词原形，是程序文本里最常见的禁止表达。

### ④ 否定状语前置引起的部分倒装 + nor 的并列
**原句**：Under no circumstances is anyone permitted to walk or to ride between the nosewheel of an aircraft and the towing vehicle, nor ride on the outside of a moving aircraft or on the towing vehicle.
**拆解**：否定介词短语 Under no circumstances 置于句首，引起部分倒装：is anyone permitted（助动词 + 主语 + 过去分词）；to walk or to ride 为并列不定式；nor ride… 与前面的 ride 并列。
**要点**：never、under no circumstances、in no case 等否定状语置句首时必须部分倒装；nor 连接并列谓语时承前省略助动词，这里 ride 与 to ride 共用 permitted 后的结构。
