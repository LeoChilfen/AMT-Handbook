# Safety Around Compressed Gases

> 通用 Ch 01 · Safety Around Compressed Gases · 原文 PDF 第 24 页

## 1. 原文

Compressed air, like electricity, is an excellent tool when it is under control. A typical nitrogen bottle set is shown in Figure 1-1. The following “dos and don’ts” apply when working with or around compressed gases:

• Inspect air hoses frequently for breaks and worn spots.

Unsafe hoses must be replaced immediately.

• Keep all connections in a “no-leak condition.”

• Maintain in-line oilers, if installed, in operating condition.

• Ensure the system has water sumps installed and drained at regular intervals.

• Filter air used for paint spraying to remove oil and water.

• Never use compressed air to clean hands or clothing.

Pressure can force debris into the flesh leading to infection.

• Never spray compressed air in the area of other personnel.

• Straighten, coil, and properly store air hoses when not in use.

• Many accidents involving compressed gases occur during aircraft tire mounting. To prevent possible personal injury, use tire dollies and other appropriate devices to mount or remove heavy aircraft tires.

When inflating tires on any type of aircraft wheels, always use tire cage guards. Extreme caution is required to avoid over inflation of high-pressure tires because of possible personal injury. Use pressure regulators on high-pressure air bottles to eliminate the possibility of over inflation of tires. Tire cages are not required when adjusting pressure in tires installed on an aircraft.

## 2. 中文翻译

压缩空气和电一样，在受控状态下是极好的工具。图 1-1 所示为一套典型的氮气瓶装置。在操作或靠近压缩气体作业时，应遵守下列“该做与不该做”：

• 经常检查空气软管有无破损和磨损部位。

不安全的软管必须立即更换。

• 所有接头应保持“不漏气状态”。

• 已装设的管路注油器应保持可用状态。

• 确保系统装有集水器，并按固定周期放水。

• 喷涂用空气须经过滤，除去油和水。

• 绝不要用压缩空气吹手或吹衣服。

压力会把杂物压入皮肉，导致感染。

• 绝不要朝其他人员所在区域喷射压缩空气。

• 空气软管不用时应理顺、盘好并妥善存放。

• 许多涉及压缩气体的事故发生在飞机轮胎安装过程中。为防止可能的人身伤害，安装或拆卸重型飞机轮胎时应使用轮胎小车及其他适用器具。

给任何类型飞机机轮上的轮胎充气时，务必使用轮胎防护笼。由于可能造成人身伤害，必须格外谨慎，避免高压轮胎充气过度。在高压气瓶上使用减压器，以消除轮胎充气过度的可能。对已装在飞机上的轮胎调整气压时，不需要使用轮胎防护笼。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 插入语 like electricity + when 时间状语从句

**原句**：Compressed air, like electricity, is an excellent tool when it is under control.
**拆解**：主语 Compressed air + 插入语 like electricity（介词短语作比较状语，用逗号隔开）+ 系动词 is + 表语 an excellent tool + when 引导时间状语从句。
**要点**：like electricity 是被逗号隔开的插入成分，不是主语的一部分，去掉后句子仍完整。when it is under control 中的 it 指 compressed air；under control 是“处于受控状态”的固定搭配，反义为 out of control（失控），技术文本里常用于气体、压力、火情。

### ② have + 宾语 + 过去分词（复合宾语）

**原句**：Ensure the system has water sumps installed and drained at regular intervals.
**拆解**：祈使句 Ensure + 省略 that 的宾语从句 the system has water sumps installed and drained at regular intervals；从句谓语 has + 宾语 water sumps + 两个过去分词 installed、drained 作宾语补足语。
**要点**：have sth. done 表示“使某事被做/让某物处于已……的状态”，此处是要求系统“装好集水器并定期排放”，动作由人执行而非 system 自己做。and 连接的两个过去分词共用宾语 water sumps；at regular intervals 是“按固定间隔”的固定搭配，不能写成 in regular intervals。

### ③ 现在分词后置定语 + occur 不及物

**原句**：Many accidents involving compressed gases occur during aircraft tire mounting.
**拆解**：主语 Many accidents + 现在分词短语 involving compressed gases 作后置定语 + 谓语 occur（不及物动词）+ 时间状语 during aircraft tire mounting。
**要点**：involving… 是后置定语（= that involve…），划掉后主谓是 Many accidents occur，结构一目了然。occur 是不及物动词，没有被动语态（× be occurred），同类还有 happen、take place。during + 名词短语表示“在……过程中”，tire mounting 是“轮胎安装”这一作业。

### ④ 状语从句的省略 + 过去分词后置定语

**原句**：Tire cages are not required when adjusting pressure in tires installed on an aircraft.
**拆解**：主句被动 Tire cages are not required + when 引导时间状语（省略了主语和 be，即 when one is adjusting…）+ 过去分词短语 installed on an aircraft 作后置定语，修饰 tires。
**要点**：状语从句的主语与主句主语不一致、但为泛指的人（one/you）时，口语与技术文本常把主语和 be 一并省略，只留 when + 现在分词，翻译时补出“（在）调整……时”。installed on an aircraft 是分词短语作定语，不是谓语，别误读成“轮胎被安装”这一动作发生在主句之后。
