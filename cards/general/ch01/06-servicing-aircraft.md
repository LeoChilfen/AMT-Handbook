# Servicing Aircraft

## 1. 原文

### Servicing Aircraft Air/Nitrogen Oil & Fluids

Checking or servicing aircraft fluids is an important maintenance function. Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper type of servicing equipment and procedures. In general, aircraft engine oil is checked with a dipstick or a sight gauge. There are markings on the stick or around the sight gauge to determine the correct level. Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown. Dry sump oil systems tend to hide oil that has seeped from the oil tank into the gearcase of the engine. This oil does not show up on the dipstick until the engine has been started or motored. If serviced before this oil is pumped back into the tank, the engine overfills. Never overfill the oil tank. Oil foams as it is circulated through the engine. The expansion space in the oil tank allows for this foaming (oil mixing with air). Also the correct type of oil must be used for the appropriate engine being serviced. Hydraulic fluid, fuel, and oil, if spilled on clothes or skin, must be removed as soon as possible because of fire danger and health reasons.

When servicing a hydraulic reservoir, the correct fluid must be used. Normally, this can be determined by the container or by color. Some reservoirs are pressurized by air that must be bled off before servicing. Efforts must be made to prevent any type of contamination during servicing. Also, if changing hydraulic filters, assure that the pressure is off the system before removing the filters. After servicing the filters (if large amounts of fluids were lost) or system quantity, air must be purged and the system checked for leaks. While servicing tires or struts with high-pressure nitrogen, the technician must use caution while performing maintenance. Clean areas before connecting filling hose and do not overinflate.

### Ground Support Equipment

#### Electric Ground Power Units

Ground support electrical APUs vary widely in size and type. However, they can be generally classified by towed, stationary, or self-propelled items of equipment. Some units are mainly for in-hangar use during maintenance. Others are designed for use on the flight line, either at a stationary gate area or towed from aircraft to aircraft. The stationary type can be powered from the electrical service of the facility. The movable type ground power unit (GPU) generally has an onboard engine that turns a generator to produce power. Some smaller units use a series of batteries. The towed power units vary in size and range of available power.

The smallest units are simply high-capacity batteries used to start light aircraft. These units are normally mounted on wheels or skids and are equipped with an extra-long electrical line terminated in a suitable plug-in adapter. Larger units are equipped with generators. Providing a wider range of output power, these power units are normally designed to supply constant-current, variable voltage DC electrical power for starting turbine aircraft engines and constant-voltage DC for starting reciprocating aircraft engines. Normally somewhat top-heavy, large towed power units are towed at restricted speeds, and sharp turns are avoided. Self-propelled power units are normally more expensive than the towed units and, in most instances, supply a wider range of output voltages and frequencies.

When using ground electrical power units, it is important to position the unit to prevent collision with the aircraft being serviced, or others nearby, in the event the brakes on the unit fail. It must be parked so that the service cable is extended to near its full length away from the aircraft being serviced, but not so far that the cable is stretched or undue stress is placed on the aircraft electrical receptacle.

Observe all electrical safety precautions when servicing an aircraft. Additionally, never move a power unit when service cables are attached to an aircraft or when the generator system is engaged.

#### Hydraulic Ground Power Units

Portable hydraulic test stands are manufactured in many sizes and cost ranges. Some have a limited range of operation, while others can be used to perform all the system tests that fixed-shop test stands are designed to perform. Hydraulic power units, sometimes called a hydraulic mule, provide hydraulic pressure to operate the aircraft systems during maintenance. They can be used to:

• Drain the aircraft hydraulic systems.

• Filter the aircraft hydraulic system fluid.

• Refill the aircraft hydraulic system with clean fluid.

• Check the aircraft hydraulic systems for operation and leaks. This type of portable hydraulic test unit is usually an electrically-powered unit. It uses a hydraulic system capable of delivering a variable volume of fluid from zero to approximately 24 gallons per minute at variable pressures up to 3,000 psi. Operating at pressures of 3,000 psi or more, extreme caution must be used when operating hydraulic power units. At 3,000 psi, a small stream from a leak can cut like a sharp knife. Therefore, inspect lines used with the system for cuts, frays, or any other damage, and keep them free of kinks and twists. When not in use, hydraulic power unit lines are to be stored (preferably wound on a reel) and kept clean, dry, and free of contaminants.

#### Ground Support Air Units

Air carts are used to provide low-pressure (up to 50 psi high volume flow) air that can be used for starting the engines and heating and cooling the aircraft on the ground (using the onboard aircraft systems). It generally consists of an APU built into the cart that provides bleed air from the APU’s compressor for operating aircraft systems or starting engines.

#### Ground Air Heating and Air Conditioning

Most airport gates have facilities that can provide heated or cooled air. The units that cool or heat the air are permanent installations that connect to the aircraft’s ventilation system by use of a large hose. Portable heating and air conditioning units can also be moved close to the aircraft and connected by a duct that provides air to keep the cabin temperature comfortable.

### Oxygen Servicing Equipment

Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper types of servicing equipment to be used. Two personnel are required to service an aircraft with gaseous oxygen. One person is stationed at the control valves of the servicing equipment, and one person is stationed where they can observe the pressure in the aircraft system. Communication between the two people is required in the event of an emergency.

Do not service aircraft with oxygen during fueling, defueling, or other maintenance work that could provide a source of ignition. Oxygen servicing of aircraft is to be accomplished outside hangars.

Oxygen used on aircraft is available in two types: gaseous and liquid. The type to use on any specific aircraft depends on the type of equipment in the aircraft. Gaseous oxygen is stored in large steel cylinders, while liquid oxygen (commonly referred to as LOX) is stored and converted into a usable gas in a liquid oxygen converter.

Oxygen is commercially available in three general types: aviator’s breathing, industrial, and medical. Only oxygen marked “Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen” that meets Federal Specification BB-0-925A, Grade A, or its equivalent is to be used in aircraft breathing oxygen systems. Industrial oxygen may contain impurities that could cause the pilot, crew, and/ or passengers to become sick. Medical oxygen, although pure, contains water that can freeze in the cold temperatures found at the altitudes where oxygen is necessary.

Gaseous oxygen is chemically stable and is nonflammable. However, combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity in an oxygen-rich atmosphere. In addition, oxygen combines with oil, grease, or bituminous material to form a highly-explosive mixture that is sensitive to compression or impact. Physical damage to, or failure of, oxygen containers, valves, or plumbing can result in an explosive rupture with extreme danger to life and property. It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems. In addition to aggravating the fire hazard and because of its low temperature (it boils at −297 °F), liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) if it comes in contact with the skin.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 飞机勤务：空气/氮气、滑油与油液

检查或勤务飞机油液是一项重要的维修工作。在对任何飞机进行勤务之前，先查阅该机型专用的飞机维修手册，确定应使用的勤务设备类型和操作程序。一般来说，飞机发动机滑油用油尺或油位观察窗检查。油尺上或观察窗周围有刻度标记，用来判读正确的油位。活塞式发动机应在发动机停转之后检查，而涡轮发动机必须在停车后立即检查。干槽式滑油系统容易把从滑油箱渗漏到发动机机匣里的滑油藏起来。这部分滑油在发动机启动或被带转之前不会显示在油尺上。如果在这部分滑油被泵回油箱之前就进行勤务，发动机就会加注过量。切勿把滑油箱加得过满。滑油在发动机内循环时会起泡。滑油箱内的膨胀空间就是为这种起泡（滑油与空气混合）留出的。此外，为所勤务的相应发动机必须使用正确牌号的滑油。液压油、燃油和滑油一旦溅到衣物或皮肤上，必须尽快清除，因为存在火灾危险，也有健康方面的原因。

勤务液压储液箱时，必须使用规定牌号的液压油。通常可通过容器标识或油液颜色来判断。有些储液箱是靠空气加压的，勤务前必须先放掉这部分气压。勤务过程中必须设法防止任何形式的污染。另外，更换液压滤芯时，务必确认在拆下滤芯之前系统已卸压。滤芯勤务（若损失了大量油液）或系统补液之后，必须排出系统内的空气，并检查系统有无渗漏。用高压氮气给轮胎或减震支柱充气时，技术人员在作业过程中必须谨慎。连接充气管之前先清洁接口区域，且不要充气过量。

### 地面保障设备

#### 电动地面电源机组

地面保障用的电动 APU 在规格和型式上差别很大。不过，通常可以按设备是拖车式、固定式还是自走式来分类。有些机组主要用于机库内维修时使用；另一些则设计用于停机坪，既可固定设在登机口区域，也可在飞机之间拖行使用。固定式机组可由设施的供电系统供电。移动式地面电源机组（GPU）一般自带发动机，由发动机驱动发电机发电。一些较小的机组使用一组蓄电池。拖车式电源机组在规格和可提供功率的范围上各不相同。

最小的机组其实就是大容量蓄电池，用来启动轻型飞机。这类机组通常装在轮子或滑橇上，并配有一根加长的电缆，末端接有合适的插接式适配插头。较大的机组配有发电机。这些电源机组能提供更宽范围的输出功率，通常设计用来提供恒流、可变电压的直流电，以启动涡轮发动机飞机；以及恒压直流电，以启动活塞发动机飞机。大型拖车式电源机组通常有些头重脚轻，拖行时要限制车速，并避免急转弯。自走式电源机组通常比拖车式机组更贵，而且在多数情况下能提供更宽范围的输出电压和频率。

使用地面电源机组时，机组的摆放位置很重要：万一机组刹车失效，要防止它撞上正在勤务的飞机或附近的其他飞机。机组停放时应使供电电缆朝离开被勤务飞机的方向拉到接近全长，但又不能远到把电缆绷紧，或给飞机电源插座施加过大的应力。

对飞机进行勤务时，要遵守所有电气安全注意事项。此外，当供电电缆还接在飞机上，或发电系统处于接通状态时，绝不能移动电源机组。

#### 液压地面动力机组

便携式液压试验台有多种规格和价位。有些试验台的工作范围有限，另一些则能完成固定车间式试验台所设计的全部系统测试。液压动力机组有时称为「液压骡」（hydraulic mule），在维修时为操纵飞机各系统提供液压压力。它们可用于：

• 排空飞机液压系统。

• 过滤飞机液压系统的油液。

• 用清洁油液为飞机液压系统补液。

• 检查飞机液压系统的工作情况和渗漏。这类便携式液压试验装置通常是电动式的。它使用一套液压系统，能够以最高 3,000 psi 的可变压力，输出从零到约 24 加仑/分的可变流量。在 3,000 psi 或更高的压力下工作时，操作液压动力机组必须格外小心。在 3,000 psi 下，渗漏喷出的一小股油流能像利刃一样割伤人。因此，要检查系统所用的管路有无切口、磨损或其他任何损伤，并保持管路不打死折、不拧劲。不使用时，液压动力机组的管路要收存好（最好绕在卷盘上），并保持清洁、干燥、无污染物。

#### 地面气源机组

气源车用于提供低压（最高 50 psi、大流量）空气，可用来启动发动机，以及在地面为飞机加温和降温（利用飞机自身的系统）。它一般由装在车上的 APU 构成，由 APU 压气机提供引气，用于操纵飞机系统或启动发动机。

#### 地面加温与空调

多数机场登机口都设有可提供加温或降温空气的设施。这些制冷或加热空气的机组是固定安装的，通过一根大软管接到飞机的通风系统。便携式加温机和空调机组也可以推到飞机附近，用风管与之相连，送风使客舱温度保持舒适。

### 氧气勤务设备

对任何飞机进行勤务之前，先查阅该机型专用的飞机维修手册，确定应使用的勤务设备类型。用气态氧对飞机进行勤务需要两人配合。一人守在勤务设备的控制阀处，一人守在能观察飞机系统压力读数的地方。两人之间必须保持联络，以备发生紧急情况。

在加油、抽油或其他可能产生点火源的维修作业进行期间，不得用氧气对飞机进行勤务。飞机的氧气勤务必须在机库外进行。

飞机上使用的氧气分两类：气态氧和液态氧。某一具体机型使用哪一种，取决于该机所装的设备类型。气态氧储存在大型钢瓶里，而液氧（通常简称 LOX）储存在液氧转换器中，并在其中转换为可用的气体。

市售氧气一般有三种类型：航空人员呼吸用氧、工业氧和医用氧。只有标注“Aviator’s Breathing Oxygen”（飞行员呼吸用氧）、符合联邦规范 BB-0-925A A 级或其等效标准的氧气，才可用于飞机的呼吸供氧系统。工业氧可能含有杂质，会使飞行员、机组成员和/或乘客身体不适。医用氧虽然纯净，但含有水分，在必须供氧的高空低温环境下会结冰。

气态氧化学性质稳定，且不可燃。但在富氧环境中，可燃材料会起燃更快、燃烧更剧烈。此外，氧会与滑油、润滑脂或沥青类物质结合，形成对压缩或撞击敏感的高爆炸性混合物。氧气容器、阀门或管路受到机械损伤或发生失效，可能导致爆炸性破裂，对生命和财产造成极大危险。在处理氧气时，必须遵守最高标准的清洁要求；并且只允许合格且经授权的人员对飞机气态氧系统进行勤务。液氧不仅会加剧火灾危险，而且由于其温度很低（沸点为 −297 °F），一旦接触皮肤会造成严重“烧伤”（冻伤）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 祈使句 + before 动名词状语 + 不定式目的状语
**原句**：Before servicing any aircraft, consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual to determine the proper type of servicing equipment and procedures.
**拆解**：句首 Before servicing any aircraft 是介词 before + 动名词构成的状语；主句为祈使句 consult the specific aircraft maintenance manual（主语 you 省略）；to determine… 是不定式作目的状语，说明查手册要达到的目的。
**要点**：手册、工卡类文本大量用祈使句直接下达动作要求，省掉主语后语气更果断。before 后面接动名词而不是从句，且 servicing 的逻辑主语必须与主句隐含的主语（执行勤务的人）一致，否则就是悬垂修饰。

### ② be to do 表示「应当/必须」的规定性义务
**原句**：Reciprocating engines are to be checked after the engine has been inactive, while the turbine engine must be checked just after shutdown.
**拆解**：主语 Reciprocating engines + 谓语 are to be checked（be to be done 的规定性被动）；after 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在完成时 has been inactive，强调「已停转一段时间」；while 引出对比分句，谓语换成 must be checked。
**要点**：be to do 在规章、手册中相当于 should/must，但语气更「制度化」，用于规定流程而不是临时要求。同一句用 while 把两种机型的检查时机对照起来，是技术文本常见的对比写法。易错点：不要把这里的 be to do 理解成将来时。

### ③ 形容词短语作前置状语 + 并列被动分句
**原句**：Normally somewhat top-heavy, large towed power units are towed at restricted speeds, and sharp turns are avoided.
**拆解**：句首 Normally somewhat top-heavy 是形容词短语作状语，说明状态/原因，逻辑主语是 large towed power units；主句由 and 连接两个被动分句：are towed at restricted speeds 与 sharp turns are avoided。
**要点**：形容词短语置于句首作状语时，其逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，否则成悬垂修饰。第二个分句改用 sharp turns 作主语，避免重复 power units，这是被动语态里常见的「换主语」手法。

### ④ it is imperative that + 虚拟语气（动词原形）
**原句**：It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems.
**拆解**：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是两个由 and 并列的 that 从句；两个从句的谓语都用动词原形 be observed、be permitted（省略了 should），构成虚拟语气；in handling oxygen 为介词短语作状语。
**要点**：imperative、essential、necessary、required 等形容词后的 that 从句要用「(should) + 动词原形」，被动式即「(should) be + 过去分词」。氧气系统这类高风险作业常用此句式表达硬性规定，汉译一般处理成「必须……」。

### ⑤ so that 目的/结果从句与 not so far that 的否定对照
**原句**：It must be parked so that the service cable is extended to near its full length away from the aircraft being serviced, but not so far that the cable is stretched or undue stress is placed on the aircraft electrical receptacle.
**拆解**：主句 It must be parked 为被动；so that 引导目的/结果从句，从句用被动 is extended；but not so far that… 是并列的否定补充，so…that 表程度结果，其中 the cable is stretched 与 undue stress is placed 是 or 连接的并列被动。
**要点**：so that 既能表目的也能表结果，要看上下文；so + 形容词/副词 + that 表「到……的程度以至于」。这里用「so that…, but not so far that…」把「要拉够长、但不能绷紧」这对相反要求写进一句，是设备摆放类说明的典型句式。
