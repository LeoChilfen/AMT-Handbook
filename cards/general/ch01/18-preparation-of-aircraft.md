# Preparation of Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 01 · Preparation of Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft are to be tied down after each flight to prevent damage from sudden storms. The direction that aircraft are to be parked and tied down is determined by prevailing or forecast wind direction.

Aircraft are to be headed into the wind, depending on the locations of the parking area’s fixed tie-down points.

Spacing of tie-downs need to allow for ample wingtip clearance. [Figure 1-9] After the aircraft is properly located, lock the nosewheel or the tail wheel in the fore-and-aft position.

## 2. 中文翻译

航空器每次飞行后都应系留，以防突发风暴造成损坏。航空器停放和系留的方向，由主导风向或预报风向决定。

航空器应机头迎风停放，具体还要看停放区固定系留点的位置。

系留点的间距必须留出足够的翼尖间隙。[图 1-9] 航空器停放到位后，应将前轮或尾轮锁定在前后（纵向）位置。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be to do 表示规定与义务

**原句**：Aircraft are to be tied down after each flight to prevent damage from sudden storms.

**拆解**：主语 Aircraft，谓语 are to be tied down（be to + 被动不定式）；after each flight 是时间状语；to prevent damage from sudden storms 是目的状语。

**要点**：be to do 在手册、规章中表示「应当、必须」，语气接近 must，但更书面；变为被动即 are to be + 过去分词。prevent damage from … 表示「防止由……造成的损坏」，from 引出损坏的来源，不能省略。

### ② 定语从句 + 主句被动

**原句**：The direction that aircraft are to be parked and tied down is determined by prevailing or forecast wind direction.

**拆解**：主干 The direction is determined by prevailing or forecast wind direction；that aircraft are to be parked and tied down 是定语从句修饰 direction，that 在从句中作地点/方式状语（相当于 in which），从句本身又是 be to + 被动。

**要点**：先行词是 direction 时，定语从句常用 that 而不用 which，因为 that 在此相当于 in which。主句用被动 is determined by，强调「由……决定」而不点明决定者。forecast 在此是形容词「预报的」，与 prevailing（主导的）并列。

### ③ 现在分词短语作状语：depending on

**原句**：Aircraft are to be headed into the wind, depending on the locations of the parking area’s fixed tie-down points.

**拆解**：主句 Aircraft are to be headed into the wind；depending on the locations of the parking area’s fixed tie-down points 是现在分词短语作状语，表示「取决于……」。

**要点**：depending on 是固定分词短语，逻辑主语可与主句一致，也可泛指；它比 according to 更强调「随……而变化」。parking area’s 是所有格，表示「停放区的」；fixed 是「固定的」，与临时系留点相对。
