# Preparation of Aircraft

> 通用 Ch 01 · Preparation of Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 30 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft are to be tied down after each flight to prevent damage from sudden storms. The direction that aircraft are to be parked and tied down is determined by prevailing or forecast wind direction.

Aircraft are to be headed into the wind, depending on the locations of the parking area’s fixed tie-down points. Spacing of tie-downs need to allow for ample wingtip clearance. [Figure 1-9] After the aircraft is properly located, lock the nosewheel or the tail wheel in the fore-and-aft position.

## 2. 中文翻译

每次飞行后都应对飞机进行系留，以防突发风暴造成损坏。飞机停放和系留的方向，由主导风向或预报风向决定。

飞机应迎风停放，具体取决于停机区固定系留点的位置。系留点间距必须能保证足够的翼尖间隙。[Figure 1-9] 飞机停放到位后，将前轮或尾轮锁定在前后（纵向）位置。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be to do 的被动式 + 目的状语
**原句**：Aircraft are to be tied down after each flight to prevent damage from sudden storms.
**拆解**：主语 Aircraft + be to be tied down（be to do 表义务、规定的被动式）+ after each flight 时间状语 + to prevent… 目的状语。
**要点**：be to do 在手册中表“应当、须”，被动式 are to be tied down 意为“须被系留”；aircraft 单复数同形，此处作复数却用 are，需按上下文判断；prevent damage from… 是“防止由……造成的损坏”。

### ② that 引导的定语从句 + is determined by
**原句**：The direction that aircraft are to be parked and tied down is determined by prevailing or forecast wind direction.
**拆解**：主语 The direction + that 引导的定语从句（that 在从句中作方向状语，aircraft are to be parked and tied down 为从句谓语部分）+ 主句被动谓语 is determined by + 宾语 prevailing or forecast wind direction。
**要点**：direction 后的 that 从句说明“沿哪个方向”，不是同位语从句；主句谓语用单数 is，与主语 The direction 一致；be determined by 是“由……决定”的固定被动搭配。

### ③ 分词短语作伴随状语（含所有格撇号）
**原句**：Aircraft are to be headed into the wind, depending on the locations of the parking area’s fixed tie-down points.
**拆解**：主句 Aircraft are to be headed into the wind + depending on… 为现在分词短语作伴随/条件状语；the parking area’s fixed tie-down points 中 area’s 为名词所有格（印刷体撇号 ’）。
**要点**：be headed into the wind 意为“机头朝向风”；depending on 的逻辑主语是飞机停放这件事，不是 aircraft 本身；抄写原句时所有格撇号必须用印刷体 ’，不能用直撇号 '。

### ④ 主谓一致异常 + allow for 搭配
**原句**：Spacing of tie-downs need to allow for ample wingtip clearance.
**拆解**：主语 Spacing of tie-downs（中心词 Spacing 为单数）+ 谓语 need to allow for（原文用 need，未加 -s）+ 宾语 ample wingtip clearance。
**要点**：按主谓一致规则此处应为 needs，原文写作 need 属手册中的口语化疏漏，翻译按“间距必须能保证”处理，不必改原文；allow for 意为“为……留出余量”，不是“允许”。
