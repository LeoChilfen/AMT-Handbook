# Oxygen Hazards

> 通用 Ch 01 · Oxygen Hazards · 原文 PDF 第 47 页

## 1. 原文

Gaseous oxygen is chemically stable and is nonflammable. However, combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity in an oxygen-rich atmosphere. In addition, oxygen combines with oil, grease, or bituminous material to form a highly-explosive mixture that is sensitive to compression or impact. Physical damage to, or failure of, oxygen containers, valves, or plumbing can result in an explosive rupture with extreme danger to life and property. It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems. In addition to aggravating the fire hazard and because of its low temperature (it boils at −297 °F), liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) if it comes in contact with the skin.

## 2. 中文翻译

气态氧化学性质稳定，且不可燃。然而，可燃材料在富氧环境中着火更快、燃烧更剧烈。此外，氧气会与油、润滑脂或沥青类物质结合，形成对压缩或撞击敏感的高爆炸性混合物。氧气容器、阀门或管路的物理损伤或失效，可能导致爆炸性破裂，对生命和财产造成极大危险。在处理氧气时必须遵守最高标准的清洁要求，并且只有合格且经授权的人员才允许对飞机气态氧系统进行勤务。液氧不仅会加剧火灾危险，而且由于其温度极低（沸点为 −297 °F），一旦接触皮肤就会造成严重的“烧伤”（冻伤）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 比较级并列谓语
**原句**：However, combustible materials ignite more rapidly and burn with greater intensity in an oxygen-rich atmosphere.
**拆解**：主语 combustible materials + 并列谓语 ignite more rapidly（more + 副词比较级作方式状语）与 burn with greater intensity（with + 抽象名词表方式）+ in an oxygen-rich atmosphere（范围状语）。
**要点**：more + 双音节以上副词构成比较级（rapidly → more rapidly），不能写 more rapider；with greater intensity 是「以更大的强度」，用 with 引出抽象名词是手册英语的常见搭配。

### ② 虚拟语气：It is imperative that …
**原句**：It is imperative that the highest standard of cleanliness be observed in handling oxygen and that only qualified and authorized persons be permitted to service aircraft gaseous oxygen systems.
**拆解**：It 作形式主语，is imperative 作表语，真正主语是两个并列的 that 从句；两个从句谓语都用动词原形 be observed / be permitted（虚拟语气，省略 should）。
**要点**：imperative、essential、necessary 等形容词后的 that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形，被动时即 be + 过去分词，不能写成 is observed；两个 that 从句并列时第二个 that 不能省略。

### ③ 不定式表结果与 be sensitive to 搭配
**原句**：In addition, oxygen combines with oil, grease, or bituminous material to form a highly-explosive mixture that is sensitive to compression or impact.
**拆解**：combines with 后接三个并列宾语 oil, grease, or bituminous material；to form… 不定式表结果；that is sensitive to compression or impact 为定语从句，be sensitive to 后接名词。
**要点**：combine with 表示「与……结合」，此处 to form 表示自然结果而非目的；sensitive to 是固定搭配（对……敏感），to 不可换成 for。

### ④ 介词短语前置与条件从句
**原句**：In addition to aggravating the fire hazard and because of its low temperature (it boils at −297 °F), liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) if it comes in contact with the skin.
**拆解**：In addition to aggravating the fire hazard（「除……之外还」，to 为介词 + 动名词）+ because of its low temperature（原因）+ 插入语 (it boils at −297 °F) + 主句 liquid oxygen causes severe “burns” (frostbite) + if 条件状语从句。
**要点**：in addition to 中的 to 是介词，后接名词或动名词，不能接从句或动词原形；come in contact with 是固定搭配，等于动词 contact；括号插入语补充沸点，翻译时单独处理。
