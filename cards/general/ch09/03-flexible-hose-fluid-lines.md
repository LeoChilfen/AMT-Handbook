# Flexible Hose Fluid Lines

## 1. 原文

Flexible hose is used in aircraft fluid systems to connect moving parts with stationary parts in locations subject to vibration or where a great amount of flexibility is needed. It can also serve as a connector in metal tubing systems.

### Hose Materials & Construction

Pure rubber is never used in the construction of flexible fluid lines. To meet the requirements of strength, durability, and workability, among other factors, synthetics are used in place of pure rubber. Synthetic materials most commonly used in the manufacture of flexible hose are Buna-N, neoprene, butyl, ethylene propylene diene rubber (EPDM) and Teflon™. While Teflon™ is in a category of its own, the others are synthetic rubber.

Buna-N is a synthetic rubber compound that has excellent resistance to petroleum products. Do not confuse with Buna-S. Do not use for phosphate ester base hydraulic fluid (Skydrol™).

Neoprene is a synthetic rubber compound that has an acetylene base. Its resistance to petroleum products is not as good as Buna-N, but it has better abrasive resistance. Do not use for phosphate ester base hydraulic fluid (Skydrol™).

Butyl is a synthetic rubber compound made from petroleum raw materials. It is an excellent material to use with phosphate ester base hydraulic fluid (Skydrol™). Do not use with petroleum products.

Flexible rubber hose consists of a seamless synthetic rubber inner tube covered with layers of cotton braid and wire braid and an outer layer of rubber-impregnated cotton braid. This type of hose is suitable for use in fuel, oil, coolant, and hydraulic systems. The types of hose are normally classified by the amount of pressure they are designed to withstand under normal operating conditions: low, medium, and high.

• Low pressure—below 250 psi. Fabric braid reinforcement.

• Medium pressure—up to 3,000 psi. One wire braid reinforcement. Smaller sizes carry up to 3,000 psi. Larger sizes carry pressure up to 1,500 psi.

• High pressure—all sizes up to 3,000 psi operating pressures.

Flexible hoses used for brake systems have sometimes a stainless steel wire braid installed over the hose to protect the hose from damage.

### Flexible Hose Inspection

Check the hose and hose assemblies for deterioration at each inspection period. Leakage, separation of the cover or braid from the inner tube, cracks, hardening, lack of flexibility, or excessive “cold flow” are apparent signs of deterioration and reason for replacement. The term “cold flow” describes the deep, permanent impressions in the hose produced by the pressure of hose clamps or supports.

When failure occurs in a flexible hose equipped with swaged end fittings, the entire assembly must be replaced. Obtain a new hose assembly of the correct size and length, complete with factory installed end fittings. When failure occurs in hose equipped with reusable end fittings, a replacement line can be fabricated with the use of such tooling as may be necessary to comply with the assembly instructions of the manufacturer.

### Fabrication & Replacement of Flexible Hose

To make a hose assembly, select the proper size hose and end fitting. MS-type end fittings for flexible hose are detachable and may be reused if determined to be serviceable. The inside diameter of the fitting is the same as the inside diameter of the hose to which it is attached.

1. Place hose in vise and cut to desired length using fine tooth hacksaw or cut off wheel.

2. Locate length of hose to be cut off and slit cover with knife to wire braid, taking care to not damage underlying materials. After slitting cover, twist off with pair of pliers. (See note below.)

3. Place hose in vise and screw socket on hose counterclockwise.

4. *Lubricate inside of hose and nipple threads liberally.

5. Screw nipple into socket using wrench on hex of nipple and leave .005" to .031" clearance between nipple hex and socket.

NOTE: Hose assemblies fabricated per MIL-H-8790 must have the exposed wire braid coated with a special sealant.

NOTE: Step 2 applies to high- pressure hose only.

*CAUTION: Do not use any petroleum product with hose designed for synthetic fluids (Skydrol™ and/or HYJET product). For a lubricant during assembly, use a vegetable soap liquid.

Disassemble in reverse order.

#### Flexible Hose Testing

All flexible hose must be proof-tested after assembly and applying pressure to the inside of the hose assembly. The proof-test medium may be a liquid or gas. For example, hydraulic, fuel, and oil lines are generally tested using hydraulic oil or water, whereas air or instrument lines are tested with dry, oil-free air or nitrogen. When testing with a liquid, all trapped air is bled from the assembly prior to tightening the cap or plug. Hose tests, using a gas, are conducted underwater. In all cases, follow the hose manufacturer’s instructions for proof-test pressure and fluid to be used when testing a specific hose assembly.

When a flexible hose has been repaired or overhauled using existing hardware and new hose material, and before the hose is installed on the aircraft, it is recommended that the hose be tested to at least 1.5 system pressure. A hydraulic hose burst test stand is used for testing flexible hose. A new hose can be operationally checked after it is installed in the aircraft using system pressure.

### Size Designations

Hose is also designated by a dash number according to its size. The dash number is stenciled on the side of the hose and indicates the size tubing with which the hose is compatible. It does not denote inside or outside diameter. When the dash number of the hose corresponds with the dash number of the tubing, the proper size hose is being used.

To avoid sharp bends in the hose assembly, use elbow fittings, hose with elbow-type end fittings, or the appropriate bend radii. Bends that are too sharp reduce the bursting pressure of flexible hose considerably below its rated value.

The hose assembly must clear all other lines, equipment, and adjacent structure under every operating condition.

Flexible hose should be installed so that it is subject to a minimum of flexing during operation. Although hose must be supported at least every 24 inches, closer supports are desirable. Flexible hose must never be stretched tightly between two fittings. If clamps do not seal at specified tightening, examine hose connections and replace parts as necessary. The above is for initial installation and should not be used for loose clamps. For retightening loose hose clamps in service, proceed as follows:

• Non-self-sealing hose—if the clamp screw cannot be tightened with the fingers, do not disturb unless leakage is evident. If leakage is present, tighten one- fourth turn.

• Self-sealing hose—if looser than finger-tight, tighten to finger-tight and add one-fourth turn.

Flexible hose may be equipped with either swaged fittings or detachable fittings, or they may be used with beads and hose clamps. Hoses equipped with swaged fittings are ordered by correct length from the manufacturer and ordinarily cannot be assembled by the mechanic. They are swaged and tested at the factory and are equipped with standard fittings. The detachable fittings used on flexible hoses may be detached and reused if they are not damaged; otherwise, new fittings must be used.

### Installation of Flexible Hose Assemblies

Hose assemblies must not be installed in a manner that causes a mechanical load on the hose. When installing flexible hose, provide slack or bend in the hose line from 5 to 8 percent of its total length to provide for changes in length that occurs when pressure is applied. Flexible hose contracts in length and expands in diameter when pressurized. Protect all flexible hoses from excessive temperatures, either by locating the lines so they are not affected or by installing shrouds around them.

When hose assemblies are subject to considerable vibration or flexing, sufficient slack must be left between rigid fittings. Install the hose so that flexure does not occur at the end fittings. The hose must remain straight for at least two hose diameters from the end fittings. Avoid clamp locations that restrict or prevent hose flexure.

Hoses must be installed without twisting to avoid possible rupture of the hose or loosening of the attaching nuts. Use of swivel connections at one or both ends relieve twist stresses. Twisting of the hose can be determined from the identification 1. Provide slack or bend in the hose line to provide for changes in length that will occur when pressure is applied.

2. Observe linear stripe. The hose must not be twisted. High pressures applied to a twisted hose may cause failure or loosen the nut.

3. Relieve sharp bends, avoid strain or hose collapse, and make cleaner installations by using Aeroquip elbows or other adapter fittings. Provide as large a bend radius as possible.

Never use less than the recommended minimum bend radius specified for the hose.

4. Provide additional bend radius when lines are subject to flexing and remember that the metal end fittings are not flexible. Place line support clamps so as not to restrict hose flexing.

## 2. 中文翻译

软管用于飞机流体系统，在易受振动或需要很大柔性的部位把运动部件与静止部件连接起来。它也可以在金属导管系统中用作连接件。

### 软管材料与结构

柔性流体管路从不使用纯橡胶制造。为满足强度、耐久性和可加工性等要求，人们用合成材料代替纯橡胶。制造软管最常用的合成材料是 Buna-N、氯丁橡胶、丁基橡胶、三元乙丙橡胶（EPDM）和 Teflon™。Teflon™ 自成一类，其余则都是合成橡胶。

Buna-N 是一种对石油类产品有优异耐受性的合成橡胶。不要与 Buna-S 混淆。不得用于磷酸酯基液压油（Skydrol™）。

氯丁橡胶是以乙炔为基础的合成橡胶。它对石油类产品的耐受性不如 Buna-N，但耐磨性更好。不得用于磷酸酯基液压油（Skydrol™）。

丁基橡胶是由石油原料制成的合成橡胶。它是配合磷酸酯基液压油（Skydrol™）使用的极佳材料。不得与石油类产品同用。

橡胶软管由无缝合成橡胶内管、外包数层棉线编织层和钢丝编织层以及一层浸胶棉线编织外皮组成。这类软管适用于燃油、滑油、冷却液和液压系统。软管通常按其在正常工作条件下设计承受的压力分类：低压、中压和高压。

• 低压——低于 250 psi。织物编织层加强。

• 中压——最高 3,000 psi。一层钢丝编织层加强。较小规格可承压至 3,000 psi，较大规格承压最高 1,500 psi。

• 高压——所有规格的工作压力均可达 3,000 psi。

用于刹车系统的软管有时在软管外面加装不锈钢丝编织层，以保护软管不受损伤。

Teflon™ 是杜邦公司对聚四氟乙烯树脂的商品名称。它的工作温度范围很宽（−65 °F 至 +450 °F）。它几乎与所用的所有物质或介质相容。它对流动的阻力很小；黏稠物料不会附着在它上面。它的体积膨胀比橡胶小，储存寿命和使用寿命实际上不受限制。Teflon™ 软管柔软，是为满足现役飞机系统更高工作温度和压力的要求而设计的。一般来说，它可以像橡胶软管一样使用。Teflon™ 软管经挤出加工成所需尺寸的管状。它的外面包覆不锈钢丝编织层，以提供强度和保护。Teflon™ 软管不受飞机上常用的任何已知燃油、石油基或合成基油、酒精、冷却液或溶剂的影响。Teflon™ 软管的显著优点是储存时间实际上不受限制、工作温度范围更大、用途广泛（液压、燃油、滑油、冷却液、水、酒精和气动系统）。中压 Teflon™ 软管组件有时做成预成形件，以便避开障碍物并用尽可能短的软管长度完成连接。由于预成形允许采用更紧的弯曲，从而省去了对专用弯头的需要。

### 软管检查

每次检查时都要检查软管及其组件有无老化变质。渗漏、外皮或编织层与内管分离、裂纹、硬化、失去柔性或过度的「冷变形」，都是明显的老化迹象，也是更换的理由。「冷变形」一词指软管在卡箍或支架压力下形成的深而永久的压痕。

装有压接式端接头的软管发生失效时，必须更换整个组件。应取得尺寸和长度正确、带出厂安装端接头的新软管组件。装有可重复使用端接头的软管发生失效时，可以用必要的工具、按制造商的装配说明制作一根替换管路。

制作软管组件时，应选择尺寸正确的软管和端接头。软管用的 MS 型端接头是可拆卸的，经判定可用即可重复使用。端接头的内径与所装软管的内径相同。

2. 确定要切去的软管长度，用刀在外皮上划开到钢丝编织层，注意不要损伤下面的材料。划开外皮后，用钳子把外皮拧掉。（见下面的注）

#### 软管试验

所有软管在装配后都必须进行耐压试验，并向软管组件内部施加压力。耐压试验介质可以是液体或气体。例如，液压、燃油和滑油管路一般用液压油或水试验，而空气或仪表管路用干燥无油的空气或氮气试验。用液体试验时，在拧紧堵盖或堵头之前应排出组件内所有滞留空气。用气体进行的软管试验要在水下进行。在任何情况下，试验特定软管组件时都应遵循软管制造商关于耐压试验压力和试验用液的规定。

用原有硬件和新软管材料修复或翻修过的软管，在装到飞机上之前，建议至少按 1.5 倍系统压力进行试验。软管试验使用液压软管爆破试验台。新软管装上飞机后，可用系统压力进行工作检查。

### 规格代号

软管也按其规格用短横线编号表示。该短横线编号喷印在软管侧面，表示与该软管相配的导管规格。它并不表示内径或外径。当软管的短横线编号与导管的短横线编号一致时，说明选用的软管规格正确。

工作温度：

规格 3 至 12：−65 °F 至 +250 °F；规格 16 至 48：−40 °F 至 +275 °F。注：不得同时使用最高温度和最高压力。

识别：软管用规范号、规格号、制造季度与年份以及软管制造商标识来识别。

用途：高压液压、气动、冷却液、燃油和滑油系统。

工作温度：−65 °F 至 +200 °F。护套条纹沿软管长度方向延伸，该条纹不应呈螺旋状缠绕在软管上。

为避免软管组件出现急弯，应使用弯头接头、带弯头型端接头的软管或合适的弯曲半径。过急的弯曲会使软管的爆破压力大大低于其额定值。

在各种工作状态下，软管组件都必须与所有其他管路、设备和相邻结构保持间隙。

软管的安装应使其在工作中受到的弯曲最少。虽然软管至少每隔 24 英寸必须有一个支架，但支架越密越好。绝不能在两个接头之间把软管拉紧。如果按规定的拧紧量拧紧后卡箍仍不密封，应检查软管连接处并视情更换零件。以上内容适用于初始安装，不适用于已松动的卡箍。对于使用中松动软管卡箍的重新拧紧，按以下步骤进行：

• 非自密封软管——如果卡箍螺钉不能用手拧动，只要没有明显渗漏就不要去动它。如果有渗漏，拧紧四分之一圈。

• 自密封软管——如果比手指拧紧还松，先拧到手指拧紧，再加拧四分之一圈。

软管可以配压接式接头或可拆卸接头，也可以与滚压凸缘和软管卡箍配合使用。配压接式接头的软管按正确长度向制造商订购，通常不能由机务人员自行装配。这类软管在厂内压接并试验，配装标准接头。软管上使用的可拆卸接头在没有损伤时可以拆下重复使用，否则必须使用新接头。

### 软管组件的安装

不得以会对软管产生机械载荷的方式安装软管组件。安装软管时，应在管路中留出总长度 5% 至 8% 的松弛量或弯曲，以补偿加压时发生的长度变化。软管加压时长度缩短、直径增大。应对所有软管采取防高温措施，办法是把管路布置在不受影响的位置，或在软管外加装护套。

当软管组件承受较大振动或弯曲时，必须在刚性接头之间留出足够的松弛量。安装时应使弯曲不发生在端接头处。从端接头起至少两个软管直径的长度内，软管必须保持平直。应避免把卡箍装在限制或妨碍软管弯曲的位置。

安装软管时不得使其扭转，以免软管破裂或连接螺帽松动。在一端或两端使用转动式接头可缓解扭转应力。软管的扭转可以从识别标识判断。1. 在软管管路中留出松弛量或弯曲，以补偿加压时将发生的长度变化。

2. 观察纵向条纹。软管不得扭转。对扭转的软管施加高压可能导致失效或使螺帽松动。

3. 使用 Aeroquip 弯头或其他转接接头来消除急弯、避免受力或软管压瘪，并使安装更整洁。应尽可能采用较大的弯曲半径。

绝不要使用小于软管规定最小弯曲半径的弯曲。

4. 当管路受到弯曲时，应额外加大弯曲半径，并记住金属端接头是不可弯曲的。放置管路支架卡箍时，不要限制软管弯曲。

为保证软管连接处密封良好，并防止拧断卡箍或损坏软管，应仔细遵守软管卡箍的拧紧说明。有条件时，应使用软管卡箍扭矩限制扳手。这类扳手的标定限值为 15 in-lb 和 25 in-lb。没有扭矩限制扳手时，采用「手指拧紧再加圈数」的方法。由于卡箍设计和软管结构的差异，所给数值只是近似值。因此，用这种方法拧紧软管卡箍时应酌情判断。由于软管连接会发生「冷变形」或整形过程，安装后数天内应做一次复紧检查。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be used to do + 并列后置定语
**原句**：Flexible hose is used in aircraft fluid systems to connect moving parts with stationary parts in locations subject to vibration or where a great amount of flexibility is needed.
**拆解**：主语 Flexible hose，谓语 is used 为一般现在时被动，in aircraft fluid systems 为地点状语；to connect moving parts with stationary parts 是目的状语，其中 connect A with B 为固定搭配；in locations subject to vibration or where a great amount of flexibility is needed 是并列定语，前者为形容词短语 subject to... 后置，后者为 where 引导的定语从句。
**要点**：be used to do（被用来做）与 be used to doing（习惯于）、used to do（过去常常）三形近义远，技术文本中绝大数是第一种。subject to 作后置定语时省略了 which are，可迁移为 parts subject to wear（易磨损的零件）。or 连接两个不同类型的定语（短语 + 从句）是英文常见的并列方式，翻译时可处理为「或」。

### ② 目的状语前置 + among other factors 插入语 + in place of
**原句**：To meet the requirements of strength, durability, and workability, among other factors, synthetics are used in place of pure rubber.
**拆解**：句首 To meet the requirements of... 为不定式作目的状语，of strength, durability, and workability 是 requirements 的后置定语；among other factors 为插入语，表示「以及其他因素」，插在目的状语与主句之间；主句 synthetics are used 为被动，in place of pure rubber 为介词短语作状语。
**要点**：目的状语置于句首时可与主句用逗号隔开，插入语 among other factors 前后都要有逗号，去掉不影响句子结构。in place of = instead of，后接名词；若接动词须用 instead of doing。翻译时插入语常提到句末（「以及其他因素」）或融入主语。

### ③ 过去分词后置定语
**原句**：The term “cold flow” describes the deep, permanent impressions in the hose produced by the pressure of hose clamps or supports.
**拆解**：主语 The term “cold flow”，谓语 describes，宾语 the deep, permanent impressions；in the hose 为介词短语修饰 impressions；produced by the pressure of hose clamps or supports 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which are produced by...。
**要点**：过去分词后置定语表示被动、已完成，常紧跟被修饰名词以保持平衡；本句若改用定语从句会显得冗长，这正是技术文本偏爱分词的原因。可迁移：damage caused by overtightening（过度拧紧造成的损伤）。翻译时按中文习惯前置为「……压出的……」。

### ④ if 条件从句 + 并列祈使句 + as necessary
**原句**：If clamps do not seal at specified tightening, examine hose connections and replace parts as necessary.
**拆解**：If clamps do not seal at specified tightening 为条件状语从句，at specified tightening 表示「按规定的拧紧量」；主句由 examine hose connections 与 replace parts as necessary 两个祈使句经 and 并列构成；as necessary 为固定短语作状语。
**要点**：规程类文本主句常用祈使句，即使条件从句用一般现在时，主句也不加 will。as necessary（视需要）与 if necessary 语义相近，前者更书面；as required 则偏「按要求」。注意 specified 是过去分词作定语，表示「规定的」。

### ⑤ Although 让步从句 + every + 比较级
**原句**：Although hose must be supported at least every 24 inches, closer supports are desirable.
**拆解**：Although hose must be supported at least every 24 inches 为让步状语从句，其中 must be supported 为带情态动词的被动，at least every 24 inches 表示「至少每 24 英寸」；主句 closer supports are desirable 中 closer 为 close 的比较级作定语，desirable 作表语。
**要点**：although 引导的从句不能再与 but 连用，这是汉语母语者最常犯的错。every + 数量 + 单位 表频率间隔，at least every 24 inches 的语序不能写成 every at least 24 inches。be desirable（是可取的）常用于给出建议而非强制要求，与 must 形成语气对照。

### ⑥ because of + 名词短语 vs because + 从句
**原句**：Because of the variations in hose clamp design and hose structure, the values given are approximate.
**拆解**：Because of the variations in hose clamp design and hose structure 是介词短语作原因状语，in hose clamp design and hose structure 修饰 variations；主句 the values given are approximate 中 given 为过去分词后置定语（= that are given），are approximate 为系表结构。
**要点**：because of 后只能接名词/名词短语/动名词，because 后必须接完整从句，二者不可混用；同样地 due to、owing to 后接名词。单个过去分词作后置定语时可直接紧跟名词（the values given），但若分词带宾语则须整体后置。approximate 是形容词，不要与动词 approximate 或名词 approximation 混淆。
