# Title Blocks

> 通用 Ch 04 · Title Blocks · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

## 1. 原文

Every print must have some means of identification. This is provided by a title block. The title block consists of a drawing number and certain other data concerning the drawing and the object it represents. This information is grouped in a prominent place on the print, usually in the lower right-hand corner. Sometimes the title block is in the form of a strip extending almost the entire distance across the bottom of the sheet.

Although title blocks do not follow a standard form as far as layout is concerned, all of them present essentially the following information:

1. A drawing number to identify the print for filing purposes and to prevent confusing it with any other print.

2. The name of the part or assembly 3. The drawing scale 4. The date 5. The name of the firm 6. The name of the draftsmen, the checker, and the person approving the drawing

### Drawing or Print Numbers

All prints are identified by a number that appears in a number block in the lower right corner of the title block. It may also be shown in other places—such as near the top border line, in the upper right corner, or on the reverse side of the print at both ends—so that the number shows when the print is folded or rolled. The purpose of the number is quick identification of a print. If a print has more than one sheet and each sheet has the same number, this information is included in the number block, indicating the sheet number and the number of sheets in the series.

### Reference and Dash Numbers

Reference numbers that appear in the title block refer you to the numbers of other prints. When more than one detail is shown on a drawing, dash numbers are used. Both parts would have the same drawing number plus an individual number, such as 40267-1 and 40267-2.

In addition to appearing in the title block, dash numbers may appear on the face of the drawing near the parts they identify. Dash numbers are also used to identify right-hand and left-hand parts.

In aircraft, many parts on the left side are like the corresponding parts on the right side but in reverse. The left- hand part is always shown in the drawing. The right-hand part is called for in the title block. Above the title block a notation is found, such as: 470204-1LH shown; 470204-2RH opposite. Both parts carry the same number, but the part called for is distinguished by a dash number. Some prints have odd numbers for left-hand parts and even numbers for right-hand parts.

## 2. 中文翻译

每张复制图都必须有某种识别手段，这由标题栏提供。标题栏由图纸编号以及与图纸和其所表示物体有关的某些其他数据组成。这些信息集中放在复制图上显眼的位置，通常位于右下角。有时标题栏做成横贯图纸底部几乎整个宽度的条带形式。

尽管标题栏在版面布局方面并不遵循统一格式，但所有标题栏基本上都提供以下信息：

1. 图纸编号，用于归档时识别该复制图，并防止与其他复制图混淆。

2. 零件或组件的名称；3. 图纸比例；4. 日期；5. 公司名称；6. 绘图员、校对人和图纸批准人的姓名。

### 图纸（复制图）编号

所有复制图都用一个编号来标识，该编号出现在标题栏右下角的编号栏内。它也可能出现在其他位置——例如靠近上边框线处、右上角，或复制图背面的两端——这样当复制图被折叠或卷起时仍能看到该编号。编号的用途是快速识别一张复制图。如果一张复制图有多页且每页编号相同，此信息会包含在编号栏中，标明页号以及该套图纸的总页数。

### 参考编号与短横编号

标题栏中出现的参考编号会把读者引向其他图纸的编号。当一张图纸上有多个零件时，使用短横编号。两个零件都用同一图纸编号加各自的编号表示，例如 40267-1 和 40267-2。

除出现在标题栏中外，短横编号也可能出现在图纸图面上所标识零件的附近。短横编号还用于识别左手件和右手件。

在飞机上，左侧的许多零件与右侧对应的零件相同，但方向相反。图纸上总是画出左手件，而标题栏中要求的是右手件。在标题栏上方有一条注记，例如：470204-1LH shown; 470204-2RH opposite。两个零件使用同一编号，但所要求的零件用短横编号加以区分。有些图纸用奇数编号表示左手件，用偶数编号表示右手件。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Although 让步从句 + as far as … is concerned
**原句**：Although title blocks do not follow a standard form as far as layout is concerned, all of them present essentially the following information:
**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，从句谓语 do not follow（助动词 do 体现否定）；as far as layout is concerned 为插入的范围状语；主句主语 all of them（复指 title blocks），谓语 present。
**要点**：as far as … is concerned 是“就……而言”的固定框架，中间只能填名词；all of them 中的 them 必须复指前文的复数名词，不能换成 it。

### ② consist of + concerning 分词后置定语 + 省略关系代词
**原句**：The title block consists of a drawing number and certain other data concerning the drawing and the object it represents.
**拆解**：主语 The title block；谓语 consists of（不用被动）；宾语为并列的 a drawing number 与 certain other data；concerning… 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 data；the object it represents 中省略了关系代词 that/which。
**要点**：consist of 无被动形式，不能说 be consisted of；concerning 在此相当于 about，不要误读为动词；省略关系代词时从句必须有自己的主语（it）。

### ③ in the form of + 现在分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Sometimes the title block is in the form of a strip extending almost the entire distance across the bottom of the sheet.
**拆解**：Sometimes 为时间副词；主语 the title block；系表 is in the form of a strip；extending… 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 a strip；almost the entire distance 为 extend 的程度宾语。
**要点**：in the form of 表示“以……的形式”；extend 在此作不及物动词表“延伸”，却带 almost the entire distance 这类程度宾语，翻译宜处理为“几乎横贯图纸底部”。

### ④ that 定语从句作主语 + refer sb to sth
**原句**：Reference numbers that appear in the title block refer you to the numbers of other prints.
**拆解**：主语为 Reference numbers + that appear in the title block（定语从句）；谓语 refer；宾语 you；to the numbers of other prints 为介词短语，构成 refer sb to sth。
**要点**：refer to 在此不是“提到”而是“使……参照”；限定性定语从句用 that 比用 which 更常见于正式书面语。

### ⑤ When 从句 + more than one + 单数谓语 + 被动
**原句**：When more than one detail is shown on a drawing, dash numbers are used.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 more than one detail 形式像复数，但谓语用单数 is shown；主句为被动 dash numbers are used。
**要点**：more than one + 单数名词作主语时谓语用单数，是主谓一致的常考点；主句用被动是因为施动者（绘图员）不言自明。

### ⑥ If 条件从句 + indicating 分词作补充说明
**原句**：If a print has more than one sheet and each sheet has the same number, this information is included in the number block, indicating the sheet number and the number of sheets in the series.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，内部由 and 连接两个并列分句；主句为被动 this information is included in the number block；indicating… 为现在分词短语作伴随或补充说明状语，逻辑主语是 this information。
**要点**：分词短语的逻辑主语须与主句主语一致，此处 indicating 说明“该信息所表明的内容”；in the series 指“该套图纸”，译作“该套图纸的总页数”更清楚。
