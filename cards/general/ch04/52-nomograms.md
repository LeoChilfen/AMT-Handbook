# Nomograms

> 通用 Ch 04 · Nomograms · 原文 PDF 第 147 页

## 1. 原文

A nomogram is a graph that usually consists of three sets of data. Knowledge of any two sets of data enables the interpreter to obtain the value for the third unknown corresponding value. One type of nomogram consists of three parallel scales graduated for different variables, so that when a straight edge connects any two values, the third can be read directly. Other types may use values on the “x” and “y” axes of a graph with the third corresponding value determined by the intersection of the “x” and “y” values with one of a series of curved lines. Figure 4-32 is an example of a nomogram that shows the relationship between aviation fuels, specific weight, and temperature.

## 2. 中文翻译

诺模图（nomogram）是一种通常由三组数据构成的图。只要知道任意两组数据，读图者就能求出对应的第三个未知值。一种诺模图由三条为不同变量刻度的平行标尺组成，用直尺边连接任意两个数值，第三个数值便可直接读出。其他类型则取图的“x”轴和“y”轴上的数值，由“x”值与“y”值同某一族曲线中某一条的交点来确定对应的第三个值。图 4-32 是一例诺模图，表示航空燃油、比重（specific weight）与温度之间的关系。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Knowledge of … enables the interpreter to obtain …（抽象名词作主语 + enable sb. to do）

**原句**：Knowledge of any two sets of data enables the interpreter to obtain the value for the third unknown corresponding value.

**拆解**：主语是抽象名词 Knowledge，of any two sets of data 作其后置定语；谓语 enables 用第三人称单数；宾语 the interpreter 后接不定式 to obtain … 作宾语补足语。

**要点**：enable sb. to do sth. 意为「使某人能够做某事」，主语常是条件、知识、设备一类抽象事物，比 sb. can do 更强调「前者使后者成为可能」。注意 enable 后接不定式，不接动名词，也不接 that 从句。Knowledge 作「了解、掌握」讲时不可数，故用单数谓语。

### ② consists of … graduated for …, so that …（consist of + 分词定语 + so that 结果从句）

**原句**：One type of nomogram consists of three parallel scales graduated for different variables, so that when a straight edge connects any two values, the third can be read directly.

**拆解**：主句 One type of nomogram consists of three parallel scales；graduated for different variables 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 scales；so that 引导结果状语从句，从句内部又套一个 when 引导的时间状语从句。

**要点**：consist of 表示「由……组成」，只用主动语态，不能用 be consisted of。so that 引出的结果从句常带情态动词（can、may、will），译作「从而／因此可以」。读这类双层从句时先切出 so that，再在从句内找 when。

### ③ the relationship between aviation fuels, specific weight, and temperature（between 的并列项）

**原句**：Figure 4-32 is an example of a nomogram that shows the relationship between aviation fuels, specific weight, and temperature.

**拆解**：主句 Figure 4-32 is an example of a nomogram；that 引导定语从句修饰 nomogram，从句谓语 shows，宾语 the relationship，between … and … 引出三项并列。

**要点**：between 后并列三项时，and 只放在最后一项之前（A, B, and C），形式上像「两两之间」，实际表示三者共同的关系。这是英文列举的固定写法，译作「A、B 与 C 之间的关系」，不要译成「A 与 B 之间的关系以及 C」。
