# Extension Lines

> 通用 Ch 04 · Extension Lines · 原文 PDF 第 141 页

## 1. 原文

Extensions are used to extend the line showing the side or edge of a figure for placing a dimension to that side or edge.

They are very narrow and have a short break where they extend from the object and extend a short distance past the arrow of the dimensioning line.

## 2. 中文翻译

尺寸界线用来把表示图形侧面或棱边的那条线延长，以便把尺寸标注到该侧面或棱边上。

尺寸界线很细，在自物体引出处留有一小段间隙，并越过尺寸线的箭头再延伸一小段距离。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① are used to extend … for placing …（被动 + 不定式目的 + 介词 for 引出动名词）

**原句**：Extensions are used to extend the line showing the side or edge of a figure for placing a dimension to that side or edge.

**拆解**：主语 Extensions，谓语 are used 为被动；to extend … 是目的状语（be used to do）；the line 后接现在分词短语 showing the side or edge of a figure 作后置定语；for placing a dimension to that side or edge 是第二层目的状语，for 后接动名词。

**要点**：be used to do 与 be used for doing 都可表用途，但本句两种结构同时出现且不重复，是技术英语避免单调的常见做法。注意区分 be used to doing（习惯于），其中的 to 是介词。showing … 的逻辑主语是 the line，主动关系，故用现在分词。

### ② have a short break where they extend from the object（where 引导的定语从句）

**原句**：They are very narrow and have a short break where they extend from the object and extend a short distance past the arrow of the dimensioning line.

**拆解**：主句有两个并列谓语 are very narrow 与 have a short break；where they extend from the object 是 where 引导的定语从句，修饰 a short break（在界线引出物体之处留出断口）；and extend a short distance past the arrow 是与 have 并列的第三个谓语。

**要点**：where 在此并非引导地点状语从句，而是修饰前面名词的定语从句，可换成 at which。读长句时先数清并列谓语（are / have / extend），再判断 where 从句挂在哪个名词上，否则容易把「断口的位置」误读成「界线延伸的位置」。

### ③ and extend a short distance past the arrow（并列谓语的省略与介词 past）

**原句**：They are very narrow and have a short break where they extend from the object and extend a short distance past the arrow of the dimensioning line.

**拆解**：第二个 extend 前省略了主语 they，与 have 共用一个主语；a short distance 是名词短语作状语，表延伸的长度；past the arrow 是介词短语，past 在此为介词，意为「越过」。

**要点**：英语常用「名词短语 + 介词短语」表距离或程度，如 extend 2 in past the edge、move 5 mm to the left，不必加 by。past 兼有介词、副词、形容词三种词性，此处后接名词 the arrow，只能是介词；不要与 passed（pass 的过去式）混淆。
