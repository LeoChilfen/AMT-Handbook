# Allowances & Tolerances

> 通用 Ch 04 · Allowances & Tolerances · 原文 PDF 第 133 页

## 1. 原文

When a given dimension on a print shows an allowable variation, the plus (+) figure indicates the maximum, and the minus (−) figure the minimum allowable variation. The sum of the plus and minus allowance figures is called tolerance.

[Figure 4-4G] For example, using 0.225 + 0.0025 − 0.0005, the plus and minus figures indicate the part is acceptable if it is not more than 0.0025 larger than the 0.225 given dimension, or not more than 0.0005 smaller than the 0.225 dimension. Tolerance in this example is 0.0030 (0.0025 max plus 0.0005 min).

If the plus and minus allowances are the same, you will find them presented as 0.225 ± 0.0025. The tolerance would then be 0.0050. Allowance can be indicated in either fractional or decimal form. When very accurate dimensions are necessary, decimal allowances are used. Fractional allowances are sufficient when precise tolerances are not required. Standard tolerances of –0.010 or −1⁄32 may be given in the title block of many drawings, to apply throughout the drawing.
