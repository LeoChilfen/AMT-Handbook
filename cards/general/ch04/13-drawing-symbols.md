# Drawing Symbols

> 通用 Ch 04 · Drawing Symbols · 原文 PDF 第 143 页

## 1. 原文

The drawings for a component are composed largely of symbols and conventions representing its shape and material. Symbols are the shorthand of drawing. They graphically portray the characteristics of a component with a minimal amount of drawing.

### Material Symbols

Section line symbols show the kind of material from which the part is to be constructed. The material may not be indicated symbolically if its exact specification is shown elsewhere on the drawing. In this case, the more easily drawn symbol for cast iron is used for the sectioning, and the material specification is listed in the bill of materials or indicated in a note. The illustration shows a few standard material symbols.

Symbols can be used to excellent advantage when needed to show the shape of an object. Typical shape symbols used on aircraft drawings are shown. Shape symbols are usually shown on a drawing as a revolved or removed section.

### Electrical Symbols

Electrical symbols represent various electrical devices rather than an actual drawing of the units. Having learned what the various symbols indicate, it becomes relatively simple to look at an electrical diagram and determine what each unit is, what function it serves, and how it is connected in the system.

## 2. 中文翻译

部件的图纸在很大程度上由表示其形状和材料的符号与约定画法构成。符号是制图的速记手段。它们用最少的线条，以图形方式表现部件的特征。

### 材料符号

剖面线符号表示该零件将用什么材料制造。如果材料的确切规范已在图纸其他位置给出，材料也可以不用符号表示；此时剖面线采用最易绘制的铸铁符号，材料规范则列入材料清单，或在注释中注明。某图给出了几种标准材料符号。

当需要表示物体的形状时，符号可以发挥极大的优势。飞机图纸上使用的典型形状符号见另图。形状符号通常以旋转剖视图或移出剖视图的形式画在图纸上。

### 电气符号

电气符号表示各种电气装置，而不是各装置的实物图。在了解各种符号的含义之后，看一份电气图并判断每个装置是什么、起什么作用、在系统中如何连接，就变得相对简单了。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 现在分词后置定语
**原句**：The drawings for a component are composed largely of symbols and conventions representing its shape and material.
**拆解**：主语 The drawings（for a component 后置定语）+ 被动谓语 are composed + 状语 largely + of 短语说明构成成分（symbols and conventions）；representing its shape and material 为现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 symbols and conventions。
**要点**：be composed of 是「由……构成」的固定搭配，比 be made up of 更正式，介词只能用 of。representing 的逻辑主语是 symbols and conventions（符号与约定「表示」形状与材料），不是 drawings，找分词逻辑主语时要贴近被修饰名词。

### ② with 短语表示方式/程度
**原句**：They graphically portray the characteristics of a component with a minimal amount of drawing.
**拆解**：主语 They（指前句的 symbols）+ 谓语 portray + 宾语 the characteristics of a component + 状语 graphically 与 with a minimal amount of drawing。
**要点**：with + 抽象名词 表示手段或程度，此处「用最少的图线」；a minimal amount of 是不可数名词的量表达，drawing 在此指「图线、绘图量」而非具体某张图。副词 graphically 修饰 portray，位置在主谓之间，翻译时常提到「用图形方式」置于谓语前。

### ③ from which 引导的定语从句
**原句**：Section line symbols show the kind of material from which the part is to be constructed.
**拆解**：主语 Section line symbols + 谓语 show + 宾语 the kind of material；from which 引导定语从句修饰 material，从句中 the part 为主语、is to be constructed 为「be + 不定式」表示将来/应该的被动结构，from which 在从句中作状语（= the part is to be constructed from the material）。
**要点**：介词 + which 引导定语从句时，介词由从句中的动词搭配决定（construct…from…），不能凭语感换成 of。be to be done 表示「将被/应予……」，是技术规范里的强规定语气，翻译为「该零件要用（这种材料）制造」。

### ④ may not 表「可以不」+ if 条件从句
**原句**：The material may not be indicated symbolically if its exact specification is shown elsewhere on the drawing.
**拆解**：主句主语 The material + 情态被动 may not be indicated + 方式状语 symbolically；if 引导条件从句，从句主语 its exact specification + 被动谓语 is shown + 地点状语 elsewhere on the drawing。
**要点**：may not 在此不是「禁止」而是「可以不、不必」，因为主句讲的是允许省略的写法；要表达禁止须用 must not。elsewhere on the drawing 意为「图纸的其他位置」，与 in the drawing 不同，后者指整张图内部。

### ⑤ 现在分词完成式作状语 + it 形式主语 + 三个并列宾语从句
**原句**：Having learned what the various symbols indicate, it becomes relatively simple to look at an electrical diagram and determine what each unit is, what function it serves, and how it is connected in the system.
**拆解**：Having learned… 为现在分词完成式作时间状语，其宾语是 what 从句；主句 it 为形式主语，真正主语是并列的两个不定式 to look at… and (to) determine…；determine 后接三个并列的宾语从句：what each unit is、what function it serves、how it is connected in the system。
**要点**：分词完成式强调先学懂符号、后读图，与主句动作有先后关系；it becomes simple to do 中 become 是系动词，simple 作表语，翻译成「就变得简单了」。三个宾语从句分别用 what（两个）和 how，注意 what function it serves 中 what 作定语修饰 function，语序仍是陈述语序；第二个不定式的 to 可承前省略。
