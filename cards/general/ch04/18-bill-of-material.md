# Bill of Material

> 通用 Ch 04 · Bill of Material · 原文 PDF 第 131 页

## 1. 原文

A list of the materials and parts necessary for the fabrication or assembly of a component or system is often included on the drawing. The list is usually in ruled columns that

AA BB SECTION A-A SECTION B-B provide the part number, name of the part, material the part is to be constructed of, the quantity required, and the source of the part or material. A typical bill of material is shown in Figure 4-4C . On drawings that do not have a bill of material, the data may be indicated directly on the drawing.

On assembly drawings, each item is identified by a number in a circle or square. An arrow connecting the number with the item assists in locating it in the bill of material.

## 2. 中文翻译

制造或装配某一部件或系统所需的材料与零件清单，常常列在图纸上。该清单通常采用划线分栏的形式，

（图内标注：AA、BB、SECTION A-A、SECTION B-B，略）分栏中给出零件号、零件名称、该零件应使用的材料、所需数量以及零件或材料的来源。典型的材料清单见图 4-4C。在没有材料清单的图纸上，这些数据可以直接标注在图上。

在装配图上，每一项都用圆圈或方框内的编号来标识。用箭头把编号与该项连接起来，便于在材料清单中查到它。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① A list of the materials and parts necessary for … is often included on the drawing（长主语 + 后置定语 + 被动）

**原句**：A list of the materials and parts necessary for the fabrication or assembly of a component or system is often included on the drawing.

**拆解**：主语中心词 A list，of the materials and parts 作后置定语；necessary for … 是形容词短语后置修饰 parts；谓语 is often included 为被动；on the drawing 是地点状语。

**要点**：主语后挂了多层修饰，读句时先剥到中心词 A list，再回头找谓语 is included，不要被 materials、parts 误当成主语。be included on the drawing 用 on，因为图纸是平面载体；in the drawing 多指「在图内（内容上）」。necessary for 后接名词或动名词。

### ② On drawings that do not have a bill of material, the data may be indicated directly on the drawing（定语从句 + 情态被动）

**原句**：On drawings that do not have a bill of material, the data may be indicated directly on the drawing.

**拆解**：On drawings … 是地点状语前置，其中 that do not have a bill of material 是定语从句修饰 drawings；主句 the data may be indicated 为「情态动词 + 被动」；directly 是方式副词；on the drawing 为地点状语。

**要点**：介词短语提到句首时常用逗号与主句隔开，属书面语的信息安排，不构成倒装。may be indicated 表示「也可以标注」，与 must be indicated（必须标注）不同。data 是复数名词（单数 datum），但作「数据」总称时谓语常用单数，技术文本中两种都有。

### ③ An arrow connecting the number with the item assists in locating it（现在分词作后置定语 + assist in doing）

**原句**：An arrow connecting the number with the item assists in locating it in the bill of material.

**拆解**：主语 An arrow，connecting the number with the item 是现在分词短语作后置定语；谓语 assists；in locating it 为介词 in + 动名词，构成 assist in doing；in the bill of material 是地点状语。

**要点**：assist in doing 与 help do 都表「有助于做某事」，但 assist 后必须接 in + 动名词，不能写 assist to do。connect A with B 强调把 A 与 B 对应起来，connect A to B 侧重物理连接；此处指用箭头把编号与件项对应，故用 with。
