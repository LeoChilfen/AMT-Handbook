# Drawing Symbols

> 通用 Ch 04 · Drawing Symbols · 原文 PDF 第 143 页

## 1. 原文

The drawings for a component are composed largely of symbols and conventions representing its shape and material.

Symbols are the shorthand of drawing. They graphically portray the characteristics of a component with a minimal amount of drawing.

## 2. 中文翻译

一个部件的图纸，主要由表示其形状与材料的符号和约定画法构成。

符号是图纸的速记。它们以最少的图线，用图形方式描绘出部件的特征。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① are composed largely of symbols and conventions representing …（被动 + 现在分词作定语）

**原句**：The drawings for a component are composed largely of symbols and conventions representing its shape and material.

**拆解**：主语 The drawings for a component；谓语 are composed largely of …（be composed of 的被动结构，largely 作程度状语插在 composed 与 of 之间）；宾语 symbols and conventions；representing its shape and material 是现在分词短语作后置定语，同时修饰 symbols 与 conventions，its 指 component。

**要点**：be composed of 中间插入副词是技术英语的常见语序（is composed mainly/largely/entirely of）。现在分词短语作定语置于句末时，可能同时修饰前面两个并列名词，翻译时应按语义判断，此处「表示形状与材料」既指符号也指约定画法。

### ② Symbols are the shorthand of drawing（名词性表语构成的隐喻）

**原句**：Symbols are the shorthand of drawing.

**拆解**：主语 Symbols，系动词 are，表语 the shorthand of drawing（of 短语作后置定语，drawing 在此为抽象名词，不加冠词）；全句是「A 是 B」的判断句，用隐喻说明符号的功能。

**要点**：of + 抽象名词（of drawing、of speech）表示所属领域，不加 the/a，如 the shorthand of mathematics。手册常用这类简短判断句给概念下定义，随后再用一句展开，形成「定义 + 阐述」的两句结构，阅读时可把第二句看作第一句的注解。

### ③ portray … with a minimal amount of drawing（with 短语表凭借、方式）

**原句**：They graphically portray the characteristics of a component with a minimal amount of drawing.

**拆解**：主语 They（指 symbols），状语 graphically 修饰谓语 portray；宾语 the characteristics of a component；with a minimal amount of drawing 是方式状语，with 表「凭借、使用」。

**要点**：with + 抽象名词短语常表方式或手段：with a minimal amount of drawing（用最少的图线）、with great care（非常小心地）、with ease（轻易地）。注意 amount of 后接不可数名词 drawing，不能写成 a minimal number of drawings——后者指「张数」，语义完全不同。
