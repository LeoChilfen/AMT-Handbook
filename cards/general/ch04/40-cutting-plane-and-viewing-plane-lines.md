# Cutting Plane and Viewing Plane Lines

> 通用 Ch 04 · Cutting Plane and Viewing Plane Lines · 原文 PDF 第 143 页

## 1. 原文

Cutting plane lines indicate the plane where a sectional view of the object is taken. In Figure 4-25, plane line A indicates the plane that section AA is taken. Viewing plane lines indicate the plane from where a surface is viewed.

## 2. 中文翻译

剖切面线表示取物体剖视图时所处的那个平面。在图 4-25 中，平面线 A 表示取得 A-A 剖面的那个平面。观察面（视向面）线表示观察某一表面时所在的平面。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① the plane where a sectional view of the object is taken（where 引导定语从句）

**原句**：Cutting plane lines indicate the plane where a sectional view of the object is taken.

**拆解**：主语 Cutting plane lines，谓语 indicate，宾语 the plane；where 引导定语从句修饰 plane，可用 on which 替换；从句中 a sectional view of the object 作主语，is taken 为被动谓语。

**要点**：where 引导定语从句时，其先行词是表示「地点、位置、场合」的名词，可以是具体的 plane（平面），也可以是抽象的 case、point。take a section / take a view 是制图固定搭配，「取剖面」不能说 make a section。

### ② the plane that section AA is taken（介词省略的定语从句）

**原句**：In Figure 4-25, plane line A indicates the plane that section AA is taken.

**拆解**：In Figure 4-25 是范围状语；主语 plane line A，谓语 indicates，宾语 the plane；that 引导定语从句，从句中 section AA 是主语、is taken 是被动谓语，that 在从句中作状语性成分（相当于 on which / in which），省略了介词。

**要点**：这是手册中常见的省略写法，严格说应为 the plane on which section AA is taken，或用上句的 where。读到这类句子时要靠语义补回介词，不要误以为 that 是关系代词作主语——从句主语已经是 section AA。

### ③ from where a surface is viewed（「介词 + where」引导的定语从句）

**原句**：Viewing plane lines indicate the plane from where a surface is viewed.

**拆解**：主句 Viewing plane lines indicate the plane；from where a surface is viewed 是「介词 from + where」引导的定语从句，修饰 the plane；从句中 a surface 为主语，is viewed 为被动谓语。

**要点**：from where = from the place where，是「介词 + 关系副词」的合法组合，口语与规范文本中均可使用；同类还有 to where、from when。不能写成 from which place is viewed，因为从句主语已有 a surface。三个句子连续用 where / that / from where 表达同一关系，正是技术英语避免重复的手段。
