# Title Blocks

> 通用 Ch 04 · Title Blocks · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

## 1. 原文

Every print must have some means of identification.

This is provided by a title block. [Figure 4-4A] The title block consists of a drawing number and certain other data concerning the drawing and the object it represents. This information is grouped in a prominent place on the print, usually in the lower right-hand corner. Sometimes the title block is in the form of a strip extending almost the entire distance across the bottom of the sheet.

Although title blocks do not follow a standard form as far as layout is concerned, all of them present essentially the following information:

1. A drawing number to identify the print for filing purposes and to prevent confusing it with any other print.

2. The name of the part or assembly 3. The drawing scale 4. The date 5. The name of the firm 6. The name of the draftsmen, the checker, and the person approving the drawing

## 2. 中文翻译

每一份图纸印件都必须有某种识别标识。

这一标识由标题栏（title block）提供。（图 4-4A）标题栏由图纸编号以及与该图纸及其所表示对象有关的其他数据组成。这些信息集中排在印件的显著位置，通常位于右下角。有时把标题栏做成横贯图纸底部、几乎占满整个宽度的条带形式。

尽管就版面布置而言标题栏并不遵循某种标准格式，但所有标题栏基本都给出下列信息：

1. 图纸编号，用于在归档时识别该印件，并避免与其他任何印件混淆。

2. 零件或装配件的名称 3. 图纸比例尺 4. 日期 5. 公司名称 6. 制图员、校对员和图纸批准人的姓名

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Every print must have some means of identification（情态动词 must + means 的单复数）

**原句**：Every print must have some means of identification.

**拆解**：主语 Every print（every + 单数可数名词，谓语用单数形式）；谓语 must have（情态动词 + 动词原形）；宾语 some means of identification，其中 means 单复数同形。

**要点**：means 是单复数同形的名词，a means／two means 都可以，写成 a mean 是典型错误。some 在此不表「一些」而表「某种」，用于肯定句；否定与疑问句中才换成 any。规定性条文常用 must have／must be provided with 表示「必须具备」。

### ② This is provided by a title block（被动语态 + 指代衔接）

**原句**：This is provided by a title block.

**拆解**：主语 This 指代上一句的 some means of identification；谓语 is provided 为一般现在时被动；by a title block 引出动作的执行者。

**要点**：技术文本常用 This／These 承接上文刚提到的抽象名词，译作「这一标识」「这些信息」，不要机械译成「这」。注意 be provided by（由……提供）与 be provided with（配备有）方向相反：前者引出提供者，后者引出被提供的东西。

### ③ Although title blocks do not follow a standard form as far as layout is concerned（although 让步从句 + as far as … is concerned）

**原句**：Although title blocks do not follow a standard form as far as layout is concerned, all of them present essentially the following information:

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，主句为 all of them present essentially the following information；as far as layout is concerned 是插在从句中的限定短语，意为「就版面布置而言」；present 在此是动词「给出、列出」。

**要点**：although 与 but 不能同时出现在一个句子里，把「虽然……但是……」直译成 Although …, but … 是最常见的中式错误。as far as … is concerned 是书面语固定框架，可换成 in terms of layout。present 作动词时重音在后，与名词、形容词 present 读音不同，注意区分。
