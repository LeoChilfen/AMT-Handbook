# Digital Images

> 通用 Ch 04 · Digital Images · 原文 PDF 第 147 页

## 1. 原文

Though not a drawing, a digital image created by a digital camera can be extremely helpful to aviation maintenance technicians in evaluating and sharing information concerning the airworthiness or other information about aircraft. Digital images can be rapidly transmitted over the World Wide Web as attachments to e-mail messages. Images of structural fatigue cracks, failed parts, or other flaws, as well as desired design and paint schemes, are just a few examples of the types of digital images that might be shared by any number of users over the Internet.

To provide information about the extent of the damage, a measurement scale, or other object, such as a coin, can be placed near the area of concern before the picture is taken. Also, within the text of the e-mail, the technician should state the exact location of the damage, referenced to fuselage station, wing station, and so forth.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Though 引导的省略让步状语
**原句**：Though not a drawing, a digital image created by a digital camera can be extremely helpful to aviation maintenance technicians in evaluating and sharing information concerning the airworthiness or other information about aircraft.
**拆解**：Though not a drawing 为让步状语从句的省略形式（= Though it is not a drawing，主语须与主句一致）；主句主语 a digital image（created by a digital camera 过去分词后置定语）+ 谓语 can be + 表语 extremely helpful + 状语 to aviation maintenance technicians 与 in evaluating and sharing information（concerning… 现在分词短语作 information 的后置定语）。
**要点**：Though/While + 名词/形容词 的省略结构在技术文本中很常见，省略的必须是主语 + be；翻译时补出「虽然……」。be helpful to sb in doing 意为「在做某事上对某人有帮助」，in 后必须用动名词。concerning 此处是「关于」而非「涉及」的分词谓语，别误读成谓语。

### ② 情态动词被动 + as 短语
**原句**：Digital images can be rapidly transmitted over the World Wide Web as attachments to e-mail messages.
**拆解**：主语 Digital images + 情态被动 can be transmitted + 状语 rapidly、over the World Wide Web（传输途径）与 as attachments to e-mail messages（以……形式）。
**要点**：as + 名词 表示「作为/以……形式」，to e-mail messages 是 attachments 的定语，说明是谁的附件，整体译「作为电子邮件的附件」。over 在此表示传输所经的媒介（经由），与 through 可换用但 over 更强调网络这一载体。

### ③ 长主语 + as well as 插入 + 定语从句
**原句**：Images of structural fatigue cracks, failed parts, or other flaws, as well as desired design and paint schemes, are just a few examples of the types of digital images that might be shared by any number of users over the Internet.
**拆解**：主语中心词 Images（of structural fatigue cracks, failed parts, or other flaws 后置定语），as well as desired design and paint schemes 为插入的并列成分（as well as 后的成分是补充而非从属）；谓语 are + 表语 just a few examples（of the types of digital images 后置定语，that 引导定语从句修饰 images）。
**要点**：主语被 as well as 插入语拉长时，谓语形式按 as well as 之前的成分决定（此处 Images 为复数，故用 are）；as well as 表「以及」，不改变主谓一致，这是与 and 的关键区别。that 从句中的 by any number of users 说明共享者数量不限。

### ④ 目的不定式前置 + 主语的歧义切分
**原句**：To provide information about the extent of the damage, a measurement scale, or other object, such as a coin, can be placed near the area of concern before the picture is taken.
**拆解**：To provide… 为目的状语；主句是被动结构，真正的主语是 a measurement scale, or other object（such as a coin 为插入的同位说明），谓语 can be placed + 地点状语 near the area of concern + 时间状语从句 before the picture is taken。
**要点**：句首逗号后紧跟名词时，极易误把 measurement scale 当成 to provide 的宾语，实际上是主句主语——判断依据是后面出现 can be placed；翻译时先读「目的 → 做法」，再补齐主谓。or other object 中的 or 是「或（类似的）」，表示举例性选择。

### ⑤ 过去分词短语 referenced to 作后置定语
**原句**：Also, within the text of the e-mail, the technician should state the exact location of the damage, referenced to fuselage station, wing station, and so forth.
**拆解**：Also 为连接副词；within the text of the e-mail 为地点状语；主语 the technician + 谓语 should state + 宾语 the exact location of the damage；referenced to… 为过去分词短语作后置定语/补充说明（= which is referenced to…）。
**要点**：refer to 在此是「以……为参照」，过去分词 referenced to 表示位置是「被参照着（说明）的」，不能换成 referring to（那会变成主动）。fuselage station、wing station 是飞机定位的两个基准系统，and so forth 表示同类项还有更多，翻译作「等」。
