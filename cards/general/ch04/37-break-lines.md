# Break Lines

> 通用 Ch 04 · Break Lines · 原文 PDF 第 142 页

## 1. 原文

Break lines indicate that a portion of the object is not shown on the drawing. Short breaks are made by solid, freehand lines. For long breaks, solid ruled lines with zigzags are used.

Shafts, rods, tubes, and other such parts that have a portion of their length broken out have the ends of the break drawn as indicated in Figure 4-25.
