# Care & Use of Drawings

> 通用 Ch 04 · Care & Use of Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 126 页

## 1. 原文

Drawings should be handled carefully as they are both expensive and valuable. Open drawings slowly and carefully to prevent tearing of the paper. When the drawing is open, smooth out the fold lines instead of bending them backward.

To protect drawings from damage, never spread them on the floor or lay them on a surface covered with tools or other objects that may make holes in the paper. Hands should be free of oil, grease, or other unclean matter that can soil or smudge the print.

Never make notes or marks on a print, as they may confuse others and lead to incorrect work. Only authorized individuals are permitted to make notes or changes on prints, and they must sign and date any changes they make.

When finished with a drawing, fold and return it to its proper place. Prints are folded originally in an appropriate size for filing. Care should be taken so that the original folds are always used.

## 2. 中文翻译

图纸既昂贵又有价值，因此应小心取用。展开图纸时要缓慢、轻巧，以免撕破纸张。图纸展开后，要把折痕抚平，而不是向反方向弯折。

为保护图纸不受损坏，绝不要把图纸摊在地上，也不要放在堆有工具或其他可能把纸戳出洞的物品的表面上。手应洁净，不得带有油、润滑脂或其他会弄脏或蹭污图纸的污物。

绝不要在图纸副本上写字或做记号，因为这会误导他人并导致作业出错。只有经授权的人员才可以在图纸副本上做注记或作更改，而且必须对其所做的任何更改签名并注明日期。

图纸使用完毕后，应折叠好并放回原处。图纸副本出厂时即按适合归档的尺寸折好。要注意始终沿原有的折痕折叠。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as they are both expensive and valuable（as 原因状语从句 + both … and）

**原句**：Drawings should be handled carefully as they are both expensive and valuable.

**拆解**：主句 Drawings should be handled carefully 为情态动词 + 被动；as 引导原因状语从句，说明为何要小心；both … and … 连接两个并列形容词作表语。

**要点**：as 在此是「因为」，不是「当……时」或「如同」；手册里表原因的 as 常置于主句之后。both A and B 连接并列成分，谓语用复数；不能说成 both of them are 后再接表语。

### ② Never spread … or lay … covered with … that …（否定祈使句 + 分词定语 + 定语从句）

**原句**：To protect drawings from damage, never spread them on the floor or lay them on a surface covered with tools or other objects that may make holes in the paper.

**拆解**：To protect … 是目的状语；主句是 never + 祈使句，两个并列动词 spread 与 lay 共用 never；covered with tools or other objects 是过去分词短语修饰 a surface；that may make holes in the paper 是定语从句修饰 objects。

**要点**：否定祈使句用 never 时语气强于 don’t，属规程用语。过去分词 covered 与 surface 是被动关系，不能改成 covering。that 从句紧跟 objects 而非 surface，翻译时要看清修饰范围，否则会把「物件戳洞」误挂到「表面」上。

### ③ Care should be taken so that the original folds are always used（so that 目的状语从句）

**原句**：Care should be taken so that the original folds are always used.

**拆解**：主句 Care should be taken 是 take care 的被动式，care 作主语；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句用一般现在时被动 are always used。

**要点**：take care 变被动后主语是 care，不能写成 Care should be took。so that 表目的时从句常带 can/may/will，但规程中陈述既定要求也可用一般现在时。original folds 指图纸出厂时的原始折痕，不是「原来的倍数」。
