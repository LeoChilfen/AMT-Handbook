# Pictorial Drawings

> 通用 Ch 04 · Pictorial Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 135 页

## 1. 原文

A pictorial drawing is like a photograph. [Figure 4-15]

It shows an object as it appears to the eye, but it is not satisfactory for showing complex forms and shapes. Pictorial drawings are useful in showing the general appearance of an object and are used extensively with orthographic projection drawings. Pictorial drawings are used in Aircraft Maintenance Manuals (AMM), Structural Repair Manuals (SRM), and Illustrated Parts Catalogues (IPC). Four types of pictorial drawings used frequently by aircraft engineers and technicians are: perspective, isometric, oblique, and exploded view.

Perspective Drawings A perspective view shows an object as it appears to an observer. [Figure 4-16A] It most closely resembles the way an object would look in a photograph. Because of perspective, some of the lines of an object are not parallel and therefore the actual angles and dimensions are not accurate.

Isometric Drawings An isometric view uses a combination of the views of an orthographic projection and tilts the object forward so that portions of all three views can be seen in one view.

[Figure 4-16B] This provides the observer with a three- dimensional view of the object. Unlike a perspective drawing where lines converge and dimensions are not true, lines in an isometric drawing are parallel and dimensioned as they are in an orthographic projection.

Oblique Drawings An oblique view is like an isometric view, except for one distinct difference. In an oblique drawing, two of the three drawing axes are always at right angles to each other.

Exploded View Drawings An exploded view drawing is a pictorial drawing of two or more parts that fit together as an assembly. The view shows the individual parts and their relative position to the other parts before they are assembled. [Figure 4-17]

Exploded view drawings are often used in IPCs that are used to order parts. The exploded view drawing has numbers and the numbers correspond to a list of part numbers. Exploded views are also used in Maintenance Instruction Manuals (MIM) for the assembly and repair of aircraft components.

These drawings are often accompanied by notes that explain the assembly process.

## 2. 中文翻译

立体图（pictorial drawing）就像一张照片。

它按物体在眼中呈现的样子来表现物体，但在表示复杂形体与形状时并不理想。立体图适合表现物体的整体外观，并与正投影图配合大量使用。立体图用于航空器维修手册（AMM）、结构修理手册（SRM）和图解零件目录（IPC）。航空器工程师与技师常用的立体图有四种：透视图、等轴测图、斜轴测图和分解图。

透视图　透视图按观察者所看到的样子表示物体。它最接近物体在照片中的样子。由于透视的作用，物体上有些线条并不平行，因此实际的角度和尺寸都不准确。

等轴测图　等轴测图把正投影的几个视图结合起来，将物体向前倾斜，使三个视图中各个部分都能在一个视图里看到。

这样就为观察者提供了物体的三维视图。与线条汇聚、尺寸不真实的透视图不同，等轴测图中的线条相互平行，并按正投影中的方式标注尺寸。

斜轴测图　斜轴测图与等轴测图相似，只有一个明显差别：在斜轴测图中，三个绘图轴中的两个始终相互垂直。

分解图　分解图是把两个或两个以上装在一起的零件画成的立体图。该图表示各单个零件，以及它们在装配之前相对其他零件的位置。

分解图常用于订购零件所用的 IPC。分解图上标有编号，这些编号与零件号清单一一对应。分解图也用于航空器部件装配与修理的维修指示手册（MIM）。

这类图样常附有说明装配过程的注释。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① An isometric view uses … and tilts the object forward so that … can be seen（并列谓语 + so that 从句）

**原句**：An isometric view uses a combination of the views of an orthographic projection and tilts the object forward so that portions of all three views can be seen in one view.

**拆解**：主语 An isometric view 带两个并列谓语 uses 与 tilts；a combination of the views of an orthographic projection 是 uses 的宾语；tilts the object forward 中 forward 为副词；so that 引导目的／结果状语从句，从句用情态被动 can be seen。

**要点**：so that 表「以便、从而」，从句常带 can / may / will。一个主语带两个并列谓语时，第二个谓语前必须有 and，不能只用逗号。注意 tilt（使倾斜）是及物动词，forward 是方向副词而不是形容词。

### ② Unlike a perspective drawing where …, lines in an isometric drawing are parallel and dimensioned as they are …（unlike 对比状语 + where 定语从句 + as 比较从句）

**原句**：Unlike a perspective drawing where lines converge and dimensions are not true, lines in an isometric drawing are parallel and dimensioned as they are in an orthographic projection.

**拆解**：Unlike + 名词短语作对比状语；where 引导定语从句修饰 a perspective drawing；主句主语 lines，系动词 are + 并列的 parallel（形容词）与 dimensioned（过去分词）；as they are in an orthographic projection 是比较从句。

**要点**：unlike 是介词，后接名词或名词短语，不能接从句（从句用 whereas）。where 在技术文本中可指「在……之中」，不必译成地点。are dimensioned 是被动「被标注尺寸」，与 are parallel 并列共用一个 are，读时可还原为 are parallel and are dimensioned。

### ③ Four types of pictorial drawings used frequently by … are: …（过去分词后置定语 + 冒号列举）

**原句**：Four types of pictorial drawings used frequently by aircraft engineers and technicians are: perspective, isometric, oblique, and exploded view.

**拆解**：主语中心词 Four types，of pictorial drawings 为后置定语，used frequently by aircraft engineers and technicians 是过去分词短语进一步后置修饰 drawings；谓语 are + 冒号后四个并列名词。

**要点**：主谓一致看中心词 types（复数），故用 are，与后面 of pictorial drawings 的复数无关。used frequently by … 等于 which are used frequently by …，是手册压缩句长的常用手段。冒号列举前不用逗号，列举项之间用逗号、最后一项前用 and。
