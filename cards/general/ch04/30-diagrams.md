# Diagrams

## 1. 原文

A diagram may be defined as a graphic representation of an assembly or system, indicating the various parts and expressing the methods or principles of operation. There are many types of diagrams; however, those that the aviation mechanic is concerned with during the performance of their job may be grouped into four classes or types: installation, schematic, block, and wiring diagrams.

Installation Diagrams Figure 4-18 is an example of an installation diagram.

This is a diagram of the installation of the flight guidance control components of an aircraft. It identifies each of the components in the systems and shows their location in the aircraft. Each number (1, 2, 3, and 4) on the detail shows the location of the individual flight guidance system components within the flight deck of the aircraft. Installation diagrams are used extensively in aircraft maintenance and repair manuals, and are invaluable in identifying and locating components and understanding the operation of various systems.

Schematic Diagrams Schematic diagrams do not indicate the location of the individual components in the aircraft nor do they show the actual size and shape of the components, but rather locate components with respect to each other within the system. Schematics show the principle of operation of an aircraft system and are often used for troubleshooting and training purposes.

## 2. 中文翻译

示意图（diagram）可以定义为对组件或系统的图示表达，它标示出各个部分，并表达其工作方法或原理。示意图种类很多；不过，航空维修人员在工作中所涉及的示意图可以归为四类：安装图、原理图、方框图和线路图。

安装图　图 4-18 是安装图的一个实例。

这是某型航空器飞行引导控制部件的安装图。它标示出系统中每一个部件，并示出它们在航空器上的位置。图上各编号（1、2、3、4）示出飞行引导系统各部件在航空器驾驶舱内的具体位置。安装图广泛用于航空器维修与修理手册，在识别和定位部件、理解各系统工作原理方面极有价值。

原理图　原理图既不表示各单个部件在航空器上的位置，也不表示部件的实际尺寸与形状，而是表示各部件在系统内相互之间的相对位置。原理图表示航空器系统的工作原理，常用于排故和培训。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① A diagram may be defined as …, indicating … and expressing …（情态被动 + 分词短语补充说明）

**原句**：A diagram may be defined as a graphic representation of an assembly or system, indicating the various parts and expressing the methods or principles of operation.

**拆解**：主语 A diagram；谓语 may be defined as（情态被动，as 引出表语）；indicating … and expressing … 是现在分词短语，逻辑主语为 diagram，对定义作补充说明。

**要点**：be defined as = 被定义为，as 不可换成 for。两个分词共用同一个逻辑主语 diagram，相当于 which indicates … and expresses …，翻译时可独立成小句「它标示出……并表达……」而非硬译为「表示着」。

### ② Schematic diagrams do not indicate … nor do they show …, but rather locate …（否定 + nor 倒装 + but rather）

**原句**：Schematic diagrams do not indicate the location of the individual components in the aircraft nor do they show the actual size and shape of the components, but rather locate components with respect to each other within the system.

**拆解**：第一分句否定 do not indicate；nor 引起部分倒装 nor do they show（助动词 do 提前、主语在后）；but rather 引出转折，后接第三个并列谓语原形 locate。

**要点**：nor 置于句首时主句必须倒装（nor do they / nor can it / nor is it），这是最容易写错的地方。not … nor … but rather … 是三分结构：既不……也不……而是……。locate 在此为及物动词「确定位置」，with respect to each other 译「相互之间的相对位置」。

### ③ those that the aviation mechanic is concerned with … may be grouped into four classes（定语从句 + 介词后置 + 情态被动）

**原句**：There are many types of diagrams; however, those that the aviation mechanic is concerned with during the performance of their job may be grouped into four classes or types: installation, schematic, block, and wiring diagrams.

**拆解**：分号连接两个独立分句；第二分句主语 those（= those types），that … 是定语从句，介词 with 留在从句末尾（be concerned with 的宾语被提到前面）；谓语 may be grouped into 为情态被动。

**要点**：be concerned with 意为「与……有关、涉及」，介词 with 不能丢；它在定语从句中作宾语时前置，with 留在句末。group into 是「归入、分成」。their job 用 their 呼应前面的 the aviation mechanic，是英文避免性别指代的常见写法。
