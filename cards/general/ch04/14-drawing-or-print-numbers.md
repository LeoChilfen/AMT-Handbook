# Drawing or Print Numbers

> 通用 Ch 04 · Drawing or Print Numbers · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

## 1. 原文

All prints are identified by a number that appears in a number block in the lower right corner of the title block. It may also be shown in other places—such as near the top border line, in the upper right corner, or on the reverse side of the print at both ends—so that the number shows when the print is folded or rolled. The purpose of the number is quick identification of a print. If a print has more than one sheet and each sheet has the same number, this information is included in the number block, indicating the sheet number and the number of sheets in the series. [Figure 4-4B]

## 2. 中文翻译

所有印件都用一个编号来标识，该编号出现在标题栏右下角的编号框内。它也可能出现在其他位置——例如靠近图纸上边框线处、在右上角，或出现在印件背面的两端——这样当印件被折叠或卷起时编号仍然可见。编号的用途是快速识别印件。如果一份印件有多张图纸页，且每页的编号相同，则这一信息也列入编号框，标明页号以及全套的总页数。（图 4-4B）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① It may also be shown in other places … so that the number shows（情态被动 + 破折号插入列举 + so that 目的从句）

**原句**：It may also be shown in other places—such as near the top border line, in the upper right corner, or on the reverse side of the print at both ends—so that the number shows when the print is folded or rolled.

**拆解**：主语 It（指图号），谓语 may also be shown 为「情态动词 + 被动」；破折号之间的 such as … 列举三个位置；so that 引导目的状语从句；when the print is folded or rolled 是时间状语从句。

**要点**：may 在手册中多表「可能、可以」，语气弱于 must。be shown 与 be indicated／be displayed 同义，指「（在图上）标出」。so that 后接完整从句，so as to 后接动词原形，二者不可混用；此处 shows 用一般现在时表将来仍会发生的结果。

### ② If a print has more than one sheet … this information is included in the number block（if 条件句 + more than one 的主谓一致）

**原句**：If a print has more than one sheet and each sheet has the same number, this information is included in the number block, indicating the sheet number and the number of sheets in the series.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，内含 and 连接的两个分句；主句 this information is included 为被动；indicating … 是现在分词短语作补充说明，逻辑主语为 this information。

**要点**：more than one + 单数名词作主语时谓语仍用单数（more than one sheet has），而 more than two sheets 就用复数，这是高频陷阱。现在分词 indicating 的逻辑主语必须与主句主语一致，此处说明「所列入的信息具体是什么」。

### ③ The purpose of the number is quick identification of a print（purpose 的搭配与名词性表语）

**原句**：The purpose of the number is quick identification of a print.

**拆解**：主语 The purpose of the number，系动词 is，表语是名词短语 quick identification of a print；quick 修饰 identification 而非 print。

**要点**：「……的用途是」可用 The purpose of X is + 名词／to do；这里用名词短语，与手册标题式的简洁说明风格一致。purpose 后接 of 表所属（the purpose of the number），接 for 表用途且较少用，purpose for identification 不地道；表「目的」的从句则用 so that／in order that。
