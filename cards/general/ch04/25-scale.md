# Scale

> 通用 Ch 04 · Scale · 原文 PDF 第 133 页

## 1. 原文

Some drawings are made the same size as the drawn part; reflecting a scale of 1:1. Other scales may be used. However, when drawings are made on a computer, drawing sizes may be easily increased (zoom in) or decreased (zoom out). Some electronic printers have the same capability. Furthermore, when a 1:1 copy of a print is made, the copy size may differ slightly from that of the original. For accurate information, refer to the dimensions shown on the drawing. [Figure 4-4H]

## 2. 中文翻译

有些图样按所画零件的实际大小绘制，即比例为 1:1；也可以采用其他比例。不过，图样若在计算机上绘制，其图幅尺寸可以很容易地放大（zoom in）或缩小（zoom out）。某些电子打印机也具有同样的能力。此外，即使按 1:1 复制图样，复制件的尺寸也可能与原图略有出入。要获得准确信息，应参照图样上标注的尺寸。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① Some drawings are made the same size as the drawn part（被动 + the same … as 结构）

**原句**：Some drawings are made the same size as the drawn part; reflecting a scale of 1:1.

**拆解**：被动 are made + the same size as the drawn part，其中 the same … as 表示「与……相同」；分号后 reflecting a scale of 1:1 是现在分词短语作补充说明，逻辑主语是前面整句所述做法。

**要点**：the same + 名词 + as 是固定搭配，不能写成 the same size with。分号后接分词短语在技术文本里很常见，相当于 which reflects…；drawn 是过去分词作定语，指「被画出的」零件。

### ② when drawings are made on a computer, drawing sizes may be easily increased or decreased（时间从句被动 + 情态被动）

**原句**：However, when drawings are made on a computer, drawing sizes may be easily increased (zoom in) or decreased (zoom out).

**拆解**：when 引导时间状语从句，用一般现在时被动 are made；主句谓语 may be easily increased or decreased 是情态被动，两个过去分词并列，副词 easily 插在 be 与分词之间。

**要点**：情态被动中副词的位置是「may be + 副词 + 过去分词」。increase / decrease 在此指显示或图幅尺寸的变化，不是零件实际尺寸变化——这正是本节提醒「以图注尺寸为准」的原因。

### ③ the copy size may differ slightly from that of the original（differ from + that 替代）

**原句**：Furthermore, when a 1:1 copy of a print is made, the copy size may differ slightly from that of the original.

**拆解**：when 从句为被动 is made；主句 may differ slightly from that of the original，其中 that 代指 the copy size，避免重复。

**要点**：differ from 表示「与……不同」，程度副词 slightly 放在 differ 之后。单数名词重复出现时用 that（复数用 those）替代；the original 在此是名词化的形容词，指「原图」。
