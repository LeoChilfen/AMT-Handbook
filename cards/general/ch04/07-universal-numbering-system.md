# Universal Numbering System

> 通用 Ch 04 · Universal Numbering System · 原文 PDF 第 127 页

## 1. 原文

The universal numbering system provides a means of identifying standard drawing sizes. In the universal numbering system, each drawing number consists of six or seven digits. The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing. The number 1 indicates a drawing of 8½" × 11"; number 2 indicates an 11" × 17" drawing; number 4 represents a drawing of 17" × 22"; and 5 indicates a width of between 17 and 36 inches but on a continuous roll. Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E. The letter A is 8½" × 11", B is 11" × 17", C is 17" × 22", D is 22" × 34" and E is 34" × 44". There are additional letters, such as D1 at 24" × 36", E1 at 30" × 42" and additional sizes unique to even larger formats but generally reserved for inter-company operations. The remaining digits identify the drawing. Many firms have modified this basic system to conform to their needs. ## 4. 语法解析

### ① provide a means of + 动名词
**原句**：The universal numbering system provides a means of identifying standard drawing sizes.
**拆解**：主语 The universal numbering system；谓语 provides；宾语 a means（单复数同形）；of identifying… 为介词短语作后置定语修饰 means。
**要点**：means 表“手段”时单复数同形（a means / two means），不能写成 mean；a means of 后接动名词。

### ② 介词短语前置 + consist of
**原句**：In the universal numbering system, each drawing number consists of six or seven digits.
**拆解**：In the universal numbering system 为范围状语前置；主语 each drawing number；谓语 consists of；宾语 six or seven digits。
**要点**：consist of 用主动形式表被动意义，没有 be consisted of；each + 单数名词作主语时谓语用单数 consists。

### ③ 并列谓语 + or 引导的择一列举
**原句**：The first digit is always 1, 2, 4, or 5 and indicates the size of the drawing.
**拆解**：主语 The first digit；两个并列谓语 is… 与 indicates…；表语为四个并列数字（最后一项前用 or 表择一）；indicates 后接宾语 the size of the drawing。
**要点**：并列谓语共用同一主语时，第二个谓语前必须有 and；or 用于择一列举，比 and 的取舍意味更明确。

### ④ 插入语 + with 复合结构
**原句**：Letters are also used (and becoming more prevalent) with the most common letters being A through E.
**拆解**：主句为被动 Letters are also used；括号内 and becoming more prevalent 为插入成分（承前省略 are）；with the most common letters being A through E 为 with 复合结构（宾语 + 现在分词），作伴随状语。
**要点**：with 复合结构中，宾语与分词为主动关系用 -ing（being），被动关系用 -ed；括号插入语在技术文本中常补趋势信息，可译作“而且越来越普遍”。

### ⑤ there be + such as 列举 + 分词短语后置定语
**原句**：There are additional letters, such as D1 at 24" × 36", E1 at 30" × 42" and additional sizes unique to even larger formats but generally reserved for inter-company operations.
**拆解**：There be 句型表存在；such as 后并列 D1、E1 与 additional sizes 三项；unique to even larger formats 与 reserved for inter-company operations 为两个过去分词短语并列作后置定语修饰 sizes。
**要点**：such as 后列举应与前面的中心词同类；unique to 表示“……所独有的”，reserved for 表示“保留给……的”，两短语由 but 转折连接。

### ⑥ 被动语态 + as 补语 + 前置过去分词定语
**原句**：In another modification of this system, the part number of the depicted assembly is assigned as the drawing number.
**拆解**：In another modification of this system 为范围状语；主语 the part number of the depicted assembly（depicted 为过去分词作前置定语）；谓语 is assigned 为被动；as the drawing number 为介词短语作主语补足语。
**要点**：assign A as B（把 A 指定为 B）的被动式是 A is assigned as B；单个过去分词作前置定语（depicted assembly）要与分词短语作后置定语区分开。
