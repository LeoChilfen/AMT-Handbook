# Application

> 通用 Ch 04 · Application · 原文 PDF 第 133 页

## 1. 原文

When shown near or in the title block, application may refer to a specific aircraft, assembly, sub-assembly or unique application. For example, in Figure 4-4A the title block indicates the bracket assembly is for a Roll Servo installation for an S-Tec Auto Pilot installation. If this drawing pertained to a B95 Aircraft equipped with an Aero- Tech air conditioning system and the bracket illustrated was unique to that installation, the title block would provide that application information. The title block may indicate Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed.

## 2. 中文翻译

当「用途」（application）标注在标题栏附近或标题栏内时，它可以指某一特定机型、组件、分组件或某种专门用途。例如，在图 4-4A 中，标题栏表明该支架组件用于 S-Tec 自动驾驶仪的滚转伺服机构安装。如果这张图样所对应的是装有 Aero-Tech 空调系统的 B95 航空器，并且图中所示支架为该安装所专用，那么标题栏就会给出这一用途信息。此时标题栏可能标注为：Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed（滚转伺服支架组件，装有 Aero-Tech 空调，型号 AT103-1）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① When shown near or in the title block, application may refer to …（状语从句的省略 + 并列宾语）

**原句**：When shown near or in the title block, application may refer to a specific aircraft, assembly, sub-assembly or unique application.

**拆解**：When shown … 是「连词 + 过去分词」形式，完整说法为 When it is shown near or in the title block，从句主语与主句一致时连同 be 一起省略；主句 may refer to + 四个并列宾语。

**要点**：状语从句省略的条件是从句主语与主句主语一致且谓语含 be。技术文本里 when + 过去分词（when installed、when required、when shown）极为常见，翻译时补出「当……时」即可。refer to 在此意为「指、表示」，不是「参考」。

### ② If this drawing pertained to … , the title block would provide …（与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句）

**原句**：If this drawing pertained to a B95 Aircraft equipped with an Aero- Tech air conditioning system and the bracket illustrated was unique to that installation, the title block would provide that application information.

**拆解**：If 从句用过去式 pertained、was，主句用 would + 动词原形 provide，构成与现在事实相反的假设；equipped with … 是过去分词短语后置修饰 a B95 Aircraft；the bracket illustrated 中 illustrated 为过去分词后置定语。

**要点**：这是「假设性举例」而非真实条件，作者用它说明「如果情况如此，标题栏就会这样写」。注意主句是 would provide，不是 will provide。equipped with、illustrated 都是被动意义的过去分词定语，可还原为 which is equipped with / which is illustrated。

### ③ The title block may indicate Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed（with 复合结构）

**原句**：The title block may indicate Bracket Assy., Roll Servo, with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed.

**拆解**：主语 The title block + may indicate + 宾语（图样标题原文）；with Aero-Tech air conditioner (Model AT103-1) installed 是 with 复合结构，对前面的组件名作补充说明。

**要点**：引用图样标题原文时不改写、不加冠词，只把意思在括号中译出。with + 名词 + 过去分词表示「已装上……的状态」，installed 与 air conditioner 是被动关系，故用过去分词而非 installing。
