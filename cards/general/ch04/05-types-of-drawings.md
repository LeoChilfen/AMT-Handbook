# Types of Drawings

> 通用 Ch 04 · Types of Drawings · 原文 PDF 第 126 页

## 1. 原文

Drawings must give information such as size and shape of the object and all its parts, specifications for material to be used, how the material is to be finished, how the parts are to be assembled, and any other information essential to making and assembling the object. Drawings may be divided into three classes: detail, assembly, and installation.

A detail drawing is a description of a single part, describing bylines, notes, and symbols the specifications for size, shape, material, and methods of manufacture to be used in making the part. Detail drawings are usually rather simple. When single parts are small, several detail drawings may be shown on the same sheet or print.

### Assembly Drawing

An assembly drawing is a description of an object made up of two or more parts. It describes the object’s size and shape. Its primary purpose is to show the relationship of the various parts. An assembly drawing is usually more complex than a detail drawing and is often accompanied by detail drawings of various parts.

### Installation Drawing

An installation drawing is one that includes all necessary information for a part or an assembly in the final installed position in the aircraft. It shows the dimensions necessary for the location of specific parts with relation to the other parts and reference dimensions that are helpful in later work in the shop.

### Sectional View Drawings

A section or sectional view is obtained by cutting away part of an object to show the shape and construction at the cutting plane. The part or parts cut away are shown by using section (crosshatching) lines. Types of sections are described in the following paragraphs.

A full section view is used when the interior construction or hidden features of an object cannot be shown clearly by exterior views. For example, a sectional view of a cable connector shows the internal construction of the connector.

In a half section, the cutting plane extends only halfway across the object, leaving the other half of the object as an exterior view. Half sections are used with symmetrical objects to show both the interior and exterior.

A removed section illustrates parts of an object. It is drawn like revolved sections, except it is placed at one side and often drawn to a larger scale than the view indicated to bring out pertinent details.

## 2. 中文翻译

图纸必须提供诸如物体及其所有零件的大小和形状、所用材料的规定、材料的表面处理方式、零件的装配方式，以及对制造和装配该物体必不可少的其他信息。图纸可分为三类：零件图、装配图和安装图。

零件图是对单个零件的描述，用线条、注释和符号说明制造该零件所需的尺寸、形状、材料的规格以及制造方法。零件图通常相当简单。当单个零件较小时，可以把若干张零件图画在同一张图纸或复制图上。

### 装配图

装配图是对由两个或两个以上零件组成的物体的描述。它描述该物体的大小和形状。其主要目的是表示各零件之间的相互关系。装配图通常比零件图复杂，并且常附有各零件的零件图。

### 安装图

安装图是包含零件或组件在飞机上最终安装位置所需全部信息的图纸。它给出确定特定零件相对于其他零件位置所需的尺寸，以及有助于后续车间工作的参考尺寸。

### 剖视图

剖视图是通过剖去物体的一部分，以显示切割平面处形状和构造而得到的视图。被剖去的部分用剖面线（交叉剖面线）表示。各类剖视图在以下各段中说明。

当物体的内部构造或隐藏特征无法通过外形视图清楚显示时，采用全剖视图。例如，电缆连接器的剖视图显示了该连接器的内部构造。

在半剖视图中，切割平面只延伸到物体的一半，物体另一半仍按外形视图表示。半剖视图用于对称物体，以同时显示内部和外部。

#### 旋转剖视图

#### 移出剖视图

移出剖视图用于说明物体的各个部分。它的画法与旋转剖视图相似，只是被放在视图的一侧，并且常常比所指的视图画得更大，以显示相关细节。

A-A 剖面表示切割平面线 A-A 处物体的横截面形状。B-B 剖面表示切割平面线 B-B 处的横截面形状。这些剖视图按与主视图相同的比例绘制。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① such as 举例 + 并列的名词短语与 how 从句
**原句**：Drawings must give information such as size and shape of the object and all its parts, specifications for material to be used, how the material is to be finished, how the parts are to be assembled, and any other information essential to making and assembling the object.
**拆解**：主语 Drawings；谓语 must give；宾语 information 后接 such as 引导的举例成分，其中并列了名词短语（size and shape of…、specifications for material…）与两个 how 引导的名词性从句；essential to making and assembling the object 为形容词短语作后置定语修饰 any other information。
**要点**：such as 后可并列名词、名词短语甚至从句，但主谓仍由前面的中心词决定；be to be done（is to be finished、are to be assembled）表示“按规定要……”，是手册规定动作的常见表达。

### ② 被动语态 + 冒号列举
**原句**：Drawings may be divided into three classes: detail, assembly, and installation.
**拆解**：主语 Drawings；谓语为情态被动 may be divided into；冒号后 detail, assembly, and installation 为 three classes 的同位列举，省略了 drawings 一词。
**要点**：divide A into B 的被动式是 A be divided into B；冒号后并列三项时，只在最后一项前用逗号加 and；译文习惯补出“图”字，避免只留三个形容词。

### ③ 分词短语 describing 带方式状语
**原句**：A detail drawing is a description of a single part, describing bylines, notes, and symbols the specifications for size, shape, material, and methods of manufacture to be used in making the part.
**拆解**：主句为系表结构；describing… 为现在分词短语作伴随状语，逻辑主语是 a detail drawing；describe 的宾语是 the specifications…，而 bylines, notes, and symbols 是“借以描述”的方式（describe A by B）；to be used in making the part 为不定式被动作后置定语。
**要点**：分词短语前有逗号时多作状语而非定语；describe 的宾语被方式状语隔开，是长句理解的难点，翻译宜按“用线条、注释和符号说明……”重排。

### ④ 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：An assembly drawing is a description of an object made up of two or more parts.
**拆解**：主语 An assembly drawing；系表 is a description of an object；made up of two or more parts 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 an object。
**要点**：be made up of 意为“由……组成”；分词短语紧跟在名词后时优先理解为修饰该名词，此处若误修饰 description，就会译成“由零件组成的描述”。

### ⑤ by + 动名词 + 不定式目的状语
**原句**：A section or sectional view is obtained by cutting away part of an object to show the shape and construction at the cutting plane.
**拆解**：主语 A section or sectional view（or 连接同义并列主语，谓语用单数）；谓语 is obtained 为被动；by cutting away… 表手段；to show… 为目的状语，说明剖切的目的。
**要点**：obtain 的被动式后接 by doing 表示“通过……得到”；cut away 为“动词 + away”型短语动词，宾语 part of an object 置于其后；目的状语要用 to do 而非 for doing。

### ⑥ 时间状语从句 + 副词插入被动结构
**原句**：A full section view is used when the interior construction or hidden features of an object cannot be shown clearly by exterior views.
**拆解**：主句为被动 A full section view is used；when 引导时间状语从句，从句主语为并列的 the interior construction or hidden features（or 连接，谓语用单数 cannot be shown）；clearly 插在 be 与过去分词之间；by exterior views 为方式状语。
**要点**：or 连接两个主语时谓语与最近的主语一致；clearly 的位置体现“副词插在被动结构中间”的规律；by exterior views 用复数泛指“外形视图”。
