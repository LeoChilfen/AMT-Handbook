# Dimension Lines

> 通用 Ch 04 · Dimension Lines · 原文 PDF 第 141 页

## 1. 原文

A dimension line is a light solid line, broken at the midpoint for insertion of measurement indications, and having opposite pointing arrowheads at each end to show origin and termination of a measurement. They are generally parallel to the line that the dimension is given for, placed outside the outline of the object, and between views if more than one view is shown.

All dimensions and lettering are placed so that they read from left to right. The dimension of an angle is indicated by placing the degree of the angle in its arc. The dimensions of circular parts are always given in terms of the diameter of the circle and are usually marked with the letter D or the abbreviation DIA following the dimension. The dimension of an arc is given in terms of its radius and is marked with the letter R following the dimension. Parallel dimensions are placed so that the longest dimension is farthest from the outline and the shortest dimension is closest to the outline of the object. On a drawing showing several views, the dimensions are placed upon each view to show its details to the best advantage.

In dimensioning distances between holes in an object, dimensions are usually given from center to center rather than from outside to outside of the holes. When several holes of various sizes are shown, the desired diameters are given on a leader followed by notes indicating the machining operations for each hole. If a part is to have three holes of equal size, equally spaced, this information is explicitly stated. For precision work, sizes are given in decimals. Diameters and depths are given for counterbored holes. For countersunk holes, the angle of countersinking and the diameters are given. [Figure 4-26]

The dimensions given for tolerances signify the amount of clearance allowable between moving parts. A positive allowance is indicated for a part that is to slide or revolve upon another part. A negative allowance is one given for a force fit. Whenever possible, the tolerance and allowances for desired fits conform to those set up in the American Standard for Tolerances, Allowances, and Gauges for Metal Fits. The classes of fits specified in the standard may be indicated on assembly drawings.

## 2. 中文翻译

尺寸线是一条细实线，在中点处断开以便填入测量数值，两端各有一个方向相对的箭头，用以表示测量的起点与终点。尺寸线通常与被标注的线平行，置于物体轮廓之外；若图上绘出多个视图，则置于视图与视图之间。

所有尺寸与文字均按从左到右可读的方向布置。角度的尺寸，是把角度值标注在该角的圆弧之内来表示的。圆形零件的尺寸一律按其圆的直径给出，通常在尺寸数值之后标以字母 D 或缩写 DIA。圆弧的尺寸按其半径给出，并在尺寸数值之后标以字母 R。并列尺寸的布置原则是：最长的尺寸离轮廓最远，最短的尺寸离物体轮廓最近。在绘出多个视图的图纸上，尺寸标注在各视图上，以便最好地显示该视图的细节。

标注物体上孔与孔之间的距离时，尺寸通常按中心到中心给出，而不是按孔的外缘到外缘给出。当图上示出若干大小不同的孔时，所需直径标注在引出线上，其后附注说明每个孔的加工工序。如果某零件要有三个大小相同、等距分布的孔，则这一信息会被明确写出。对于精密件，尺寸以小数给出。沉孔要给出直径与深度。锥形沉孔要给出锪孔角度与直径。［图 4-26］

为公差给出的尺寸，表示运动件之间允许的间隙量。对于要在另一零件上滑动或转动的零件，标注的是正余量。负余量则用于压配合。只要可能，所需配合的公差与余量都应符合《美国金属配合公差、余量与量规标准》中的规定。标准中规定的配合等级可标注在装配图上。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① broken at the midpoint … and having …（分词短语并列作后置定语）

**原句**：A dimension line is a light solid line, broken at the midpoint for insertion of measurement indications, and having opposite pointing arrowheads at each end to show origin and termination of a measurement.

**拆解**：主干 A dimension line is a light solid line；broken at the midpoint …（过去分词短语，表被动）与 having opposite pointing arrowheads …（现在分词短语，表主动）由 and 并列，共同作 line 的后置定语；for insertion of measurement indications 表目的，to show origin and termination 是 having 的目的状语。

**要点**：同一名词后并列两个分词短语时，选择现在分词还是过去分词取决于它与该名词的逻辑关系：线被「断开」，故用 broken；线「带有」箭头，故用 having。手册常把一个定义句拉成长串后置定语，读时应先复位主干，再逐段挂回修饰成分。

### ② are placed so that they read from left to right（so that 结果状语从句 + 主动表被动）

**原句**：All dimensions and lettering are placed so that they read from left to right.

**拆解**：主句 All dimensions and lettering are placed 为被动；so that 引导结果（目的）状语从句；从句中 they 指代 dimensions and lettering，read 用主动形式表示被动含义（「被读作」）。

**要点**：read 与 write 一类动词常以主动形式表被动：the sign reads "no smoking"、the drawing reads from left to right。so that 后接完整从句，与 so … that（如此……以致）不同，注意不要漏掉 that。

### ③ rather than from outside to outside（rather than 的并列否定）

**原句**：In dimensioning distances between holes in an object, dimensions are usually given from center to center rather than from outside to outside of the holes.

**拆解**：In dimensioning … 是「介词 + 动名词」作状语，表「在……方面」；主句 dimensions are usually given from center to center；rather than 连接两个对等的介词短语 from center to center 与 from outside to outside of the holes，否定后者。

**要点**：rather than 连接的两个成分必须语法对等（此处同为 from … to … 结构）；若写成 rather than outside to outside 就失去了对称。译作「而不是」时可保留后置，不必前置。

### ④ Whenever possible … conform to those set up in …（省略句 + those 替代）

**原句**：Whenever possible, the tolerance and allowances for desired fits conform to those set up in the American Standard for Tolerances, Allowances, and Gauges for Metal Fits.

**拆解**：Whenever possible 是 Whenever it is possible 的省略形式，作条件状语；主语 the tolerance and allowances，谓语 conform to（符合）；those 替代前面出现过的 the tolerance and allowances，set up in … 是过去分词短语作 those 的后置定语。

**要点**：手册中 Whenever possible / When necessary / If required 都是常见的省略结构，一律省略主语和 be 动词。conform to 是「符合（标准、规范）」的固定搭配，宾语用 those 指代前文复数名词，避免重复；同类替代还有 that（单数）、ones。
