# Special Flight Permits

> 通用 Ch 10 · Special Flight Permits · 原文 PDF 第 393 页

## 1. 原文

For an aircraft that does not currently meet airworthiness requirements because of an overdue inspection, damage, expired replacement times for time-limited parts, or other reasons, but is capable of safe flight, a special flight permit may be issued. Special flight permits, often referred to as ferry permits, are issued for the following purposes:

• Flying the aircraft to a base where repairs, alterations, or maintenance are to be performed or to a point of storage

• Delivering or exporting the aircraft

• Production flight testing new production aircraft

• Evacuating aircraft from areas of impending danger

• Conducting customer demonstration flights in new production aircraft that have satisfactorily completed production flight tests Additional information about special flight permits may be found in 14 CFR part 21. Application forms for special flight permits may be requested from the nearest FAA FSDO.

## 2. 中文翻译

对于一架因检查逾期、损伤、时限件更换时限已过或其他原因，目前不符合适航要求但仍能安全飞行的航空器，可以签发特别飞行许可。特别飞行许可常被称为转场许可，签发的用途如下：

• 将航空器飞往将进行修理、改装或维修的基地，或飞往存放地点

• 交付或出口该航空器

• 对新的生产型航空器进行生产试飞

• 将航空器从即将发生危险的地区撤离

• 在已圆满完成生产试飞的新生产型航空器上进行客户演示飞行。有关特别飞行许可的更多信息，见 14 CFR part 21。特别飞行许可的申请表可向最近的 FAA FSDO 索取。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 长定语从句 + 并列谓语 + because of 名词并列
**原句**：For an aircraft that does not currently meet airworthiness requirements because of an overdue inspection, damage, expired replacement times for time-limited parts, or other reasons, but is capable of safe flight, a special flight permit may be issued.
**拆解**：介词短语 For an aircraft that … 提前交代对象；that 从句里有两个并列谓语 does not currently meet … 和 but is capable of safe flight，共用主语 that；because of 后接四个并列名词短语；主句 a special flight permit may be issued 为被动。
**要点**：定语从句中出现「not A but B」式并列谓语时，第二个谓语前可省略 that，阅读时要跨过 because of 的长串宾语找回 but；because of 是介词，只能接名词，接句子要用 because；主句被动把「被签发」的对象提到句首，是本段最典型的法规语序。

### ② 插入语式过去分词短语 + 被动谓语
**原句**：Special flight permits, often referred to as ferry permits, are issued for the following purposes:
**拆解**：主语 Special flight permits；逗号之间 often referred to as ferry permits 是过去分词短语作插入语，说明主语的别称；谓语 are issued 为一般现在时被动；for the following purposes 作目的状语，冒号引出清单。
**要点**：referred to as 是「被称为」的固定搭配，此处省去了 being，不能补成 referring；插入语两边的逗号必须成对；冒号后接清单时，清单各项在语法上须与前面的成分平行（这里是动名词）。

### ③ 动名词平行清单 + where 定语从句 + be to be done
**原句**：• Flying the aircraft to a base where repairs, alterations, or maintenance are to be performed or to a point of storage
**拆解**：动名词短语 Flying the aircraft 作清单项；to a base … or to a point of storage 为两个并列的目的地；where 引导定语从句修饰 base，从句主语 repairs, alterations, or maintenance 为三项并列，谓语 are to be performed 为被动。
**要点**：be to be done 表示「按计划要做」，比 will be done 更带安排意味；where 从句里三项并列主语用 are；并列结构要读到 or to a point of storage 才知道 to 管两个宾语，别把 storage 误当成 base 的同位语。

### ④ 情态动词被动 + request sth from sb
**原句**：Application forms for special flight permits may be requested from the nearest FAA FSDO.
**拆解**：主语 Application forms for special flight permits；谓语 may be requested（情态动词 + 被动）；from the nearest FAA FSDO 作来源状语。
**要点**：request 的固定搭配是 request sth from sb，变成被动后 sb 前的 from 仍保留，不能改成 by；may 在此表「可以」，不是推测；Application forms for … 中 for 表示用途，「……的申请表」。
