# Aircraft Logs

> 通用 Ch 10 · Aircraft Logs · 原文 PDF 第 378 页

## 1. 原文

“Aircraft logs,” as used in this handbook, is an inclusive term that applies to the aircraft logbook and all supplemental records concerned with the aircraft. They may come in a variety of formats. For a small aircraft, the log may indeed be a small 5" × 8" logbook. For larger aircraft, the logbooks are often larger and in the form of a three-ring binder. Aircraft that have been in service for a long time are likely to have several logbooks.

The aircraft logbook is the record where all data concerning the aircraft is recorded. Information gathered in this log is used to determine the aircraft condition, date of inspections, time on airframe, engines, and propellers. It reflects a history of all significant events occurring to the aircraft, its components, and accessories. Additionally, it provides a place for indicating compliance with FAA airworthiness directives (ADs) or manufacturers’ service bulletins (SB). The more comprehensive the logbook, the easier it is to understand the aircraft’s maintenance history.

When the inspections are completed, appropriate entries must be made in the aircraft logbook certifying that the aircraft is in an airworthy condition and may be returned to service. When making logbook entries, exercise special care to ensure that the entry can be clearly understood by anyone having a need to read it in the future. Also, if making a hand-written entry, use good penmanship and write legibly. To some degree, the organization, comprehensiveness, and appearance of the aircraft logbooks have an impact on the value of the aircraft. High quality logbooks can mean a higher value for the aircraft.

## 2. 中文翻译

“飞机履历”（aircraft logs）在本手册中是一个总称，指飞机履历本以及与该飞机有关的全部补充记录。它们可以有多种形式。对于小型飞机，履历可能确实就是一册小小的 5 英寸 × 8 英寸履历本。对于较大的飞机，履历本往往更大，形式是三环活页夹。

服役时间很长的飞机，很可能有多本履历本。飞机履历本是记录该飞机全部数据的载体。这本履历中收集的信息用于确定飞机状态、检查日期，以及机体、发动机和螺旋桨的使用时间。它反映飞机及其部件和附件所发生的全部重要事件的历史。此外，它还提供了一个记载对 FAA 适航指令（AD）或制造商服务通告（SB）符合情况的地方。

履历本越全面，就越容易理解该飞机的维修历史。检查完成后，必须在飞机履历本中做出相应记录，证明该飞机处于适航状态并可重新投入运行。填写履历本记录时，要格外小心，确保记录能被将来任何需要阅读它的人清楚地理解。另外，如果是手写记录，要字迹工整、书写清楚。飞机履历本的组织性、全面性和外观，在一定程度上会影响飞机的价值。高质量的履历本可能意味着飞机有更高的价值。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 插入语 + that 定语从句
**原句**：“Aircraft logs,” as used in this handbook, is an inclusive term that applies to the aircraft logbook and all supplemental records concerned with the aircraft.
**拆解**：主语 “Aircraft logs” 后插入 as used in this handbook（as + 过去分词构成的省略式定语/状语），谓语为 is（主语虽形似复数，但作为一个术语整体按单数处理），表语 an inclusive term 由 that applies to… 定语从句修饰；concerned with the aircraft 为过去分词短语后置修饰 records。
**要点**：as used in this handbook = as it is used in this handbook，省略了主语和 be，是定义术语时的固定说法；术语加引号作单数主语，谓语用 is；inclusive term 意为「涵盖性说法」，即下文所谓「统称」。

### ② the more…, the easier… 比较结构
**原句**：The more comprehensive the logbook, the easier it is to understand the aircraft’s maintenance history.
**拆解**：这是「the + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语, the + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语」结构；前一分句 The more comprehensive the logbook (is) 省略了系动词，后一分句 the easier it is to understand… 中 it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to understand…。
**要点**：两个分句都常省略 be 动词，翻译成「越……就越……」；注意前后比较级必须对称，不能一边用 more comprehensive 一边用 easilier 之类的错误形式。

### ③ 时间状语从句 + 被动语态 + 现在分词作伴随状语
**原句**：When the inspections are completed, appropriate entries must be made in the aircraft logbook certifying that the aircraft is in an airworthy condition and may be returned to service.
**拆解**：when 引导时间状语从句（被动语态）；主句用 must be made 被动结构，地点状语 in the aircraft logbook；certifying that… 为现在分词短语作伴随/补充说明状语，其逻辑主语是 entries，that 从句中 is in an airworthy condition 与 may be returned to service 并列。
**要点**：make an entry（做记录）的被动形式是 entries must be made，注意不要写成 entries must be done；in an airworthy condition 是「in + a/an + 形容词 + condition」表示状态的固定模式；certify that 后接两个并列谓语，第二个谓语承前共用 certify 的语义。

### ④ 形式宾语/形式主语 + 不定式
**原句**：When making logbook entries, exercise special care to ensure that the entry can be clearly understood by anyone having a need to read it in the future.
**拆解**：when making logbook entries 为「when + 动名词」时间状语；主句是祈使句 exercise special care；to ensure that… 为目的状语，that 从句为被动语态 can be clearly understood by anyone，anyone 由现在分词短语 having a need to read it in the future 后置修饰。
**要点**：have a need to do sth 是「有必要做某事」的正式说法，比 need to do 更强调「有这种需要」；by anyone having… 中的现在分词短语相当于 who has…，修饰不定代词 anyone 时后置，不能前置。

### ⑤ if + 动名词的省略式条件状语 + 并列祈使句
**原句**：Also, if making a hand-written entry, use good penmanship and write legibly.
**拆解**：if making a hand-written entry 是 if 引导的省略式条件状语（= if you make…），逻辑主语与主句祈使句一致；主句为 use good penmanship 与 write legibly 两个并列的祈使谓语。
**要点**：if/when/while + 现在分词，条件从句的主语与主句相同时才可省略；hand-written 是「副词/名词 + 过去分词」构成的复合形容词，作前置定语要带连字符；legibly 是副词，修饰 write，不能写成 legible。

### ⑥ have an impact on 固定搭配
**原句**：To some degree, the organization, comprehensiveness, and appearance of the aircraft logbooks have an impact on the value of the aircraft.
**拆解**：to some degree 为程度状语；主语是三个并列名词 the organization, comprehensiveness, and appearance，of the aircraft logbooks 限定这组名词，谓语 have（复数）与并列主语一致；have an impact on 后接宾语 the value of the aircraft。
**要点**：impact 作名词时搭配介词 on，作动词时为 impact on/upon 或及物；主语为并列三项，谓语用 have 而非 has，这是长主语句最易失分的主谓一致点。
