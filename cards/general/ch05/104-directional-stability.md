# Directional Stability

> 通用 Ch 05 · Directional Stability · 原文 PDF 第 192 页

## 1. 原文

Movement of the airplane around its vertical axis, and the airplane’s ability to not be adversely affected by a force creating a yaw type of motion, is called directional stability. The vertical fin gives the airplane this stability, causing the airplane to align with the relative wind. In flight, the airplane acts like the weather vane we use around our home to show the direction the wind is blowing. The distance from the pivot point on a weather vane to its tail is greater than the distance from its pivot point to the nose. So, when the wind blows, it creates a greater torque force on the tail and forces it to align with the wind. On an airplane, the same is true. With the CG being the pivot point, it is a greater distance from the CG to the vertical stabilizer than it is from the CG to the nose. [Figure 5-64]

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机绕其垂直轴的运动，以及飞机不被产生偏航类运动的外力所不利影响的能力，称为方向稳定性。垂直尾翼赋予飞机这种稳定性，使飞机与相对气流对齐。飞行中，飞机的表现就像我们住宅周围用来指示风向的风向标。风向标上从支点到尾部的距离，大于从支点到前端的距离。因此，风吹来时，就在尾部产生更大的扭矩力，迫使尾部与风向对齐。在飞机上也是如此：以重心为支点，从重心到垂直安定面的距离大于从重心到机头的距离。（见图 5-64）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 长并列主语 + is called + 不定式被动否定式

**原句**：Movement of the airplane around its vertical axis, and the airplane’s ability to not be adversely affected by a force creating a yaw type of motion, is called directional stability.

**拆解**：主语是两个并列名词短语（Movement … 与 the airplane’s ability …），由 and 连接，谓语用单数 is called；to not be adversely affected … 是不定式被动否定式，作 ability 的后置定语；creating a yaw type of motion 是现在分词短语修饰 a force。

**要点**：两句并列而指的是同一件事的两面，所以谓语仍用单数 is。不定式的否定式 to not do 在技术文本中常把 not 直接放在 to 之后（比 not to do 更强调「不被」）。adversely affect 是「不利影响」的固定搭配，形近词 adversely／adversarial 要分清。

### ② 比较结构：it is a greater distance … than it is …

**原句**：With the CG being the pivot point, it is a greater distance from the CG to the vertical stabilizer than it is from the CG to the nose.

**拆解**：With the CG being the pivot point 是 with 复合结构作状语（with + 名词 + 现在分词）；主句 it is a greater distance … than it is …，两个 it 都是形式主语，真正的主语是 from … to … 介词短语；greater 是 distance 的比较级。

**要点**：比较的两边必须对等：from the CG to the vertical stabilizer 对 from the CG to the nose。第二个 it is 不能省略，否则比较结构残缺（× than from the CG to the nose 可省但口语句）。with + 名词 + being 表伴随状态，being 不可写成 is。

### ③ so 承接结果 + force sb/sth to do

**原句**：So, when the wind blows, it creates a greater torque force on the tail and forces it to align with the wind.

**拆解**：So 承接上文表结果；when 引导时间状语从句；主句谓语为并列的 creates 与 forces，force sb/sth to do 中 to align 作宾语补足语；it 指 the tail。

**要点**：force … to do（迫使）保留 to，而 make … do 省略 to，两组易混。align with 是「与……对齐」的固定搭配，航空文本中常见 align with the relative wind、align with the runway。so 置于句首时与上句关系紧密，译作「因此」。
