# Bending

> 通用 Ch 05 · Bending · 原文 PDF 第 163 页

## 1. 原文

An airplane in flight experiences a bending force on the wing as aerodynamic lift tries to raise the wing. This force of lift causes the skin on the top of the wing to compress and the skin on the bottom of the wing to be under tension. When the airplane is on the ground sitting on its landing gear, the force of gravity tries to bend the wing downward, subjecting the bottom of the wing to compression and the top of the wing tension. [Figure 5-22] During the testing that occurs prior to FAA certification, an airplane manufacturer intentionally bends the wing up and down to make sure it can take the stress without failing.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞行中的飞机机翼承受弯曲力，因为空气动力升力试图把机翼抬起。这种升力使机翼上表面的蒙皮受压、机翼下表面的蒙皮受拉。当飞机在地面停放在起落架上时，重力试图把机翼向下压弯，使机翼下表面受压、上表面受拉。（图 5-22）在取得 FAA 合格审定之前进行的试验中，飞机制造商会刻意让机翼上下反复弯曲，以确认机翼能承受该应力而不失效。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as aerodynamic lift tries to raise the wing（as 引导状语从句）

**原句**：An airplane in flight experiences a bending force on the wing as aerodynamic lift tries to raise the wing.

**拆解**：主语 An airplane，in flight 为介词短语作后置定语；谓语 experiences；宾语 a bending force on the wing；as 引导状语从句，表「随着／由于」，从句主语 aerodynamic lift，谓语 tries to raise。

**要点**：in flight 是固定短语「在飞行中」，不是「在航班里」。as 在此偏「随着」，比 when 更强调两个动作同步发生。a bending force on the wing 中 on 表示作用部位。

### ② causes the skin … to compress and the skin … to be under tension（cause + 宾语 + 不定式）

**原句**：This force of lift causes the skin on the top of the wing to compress and the skin on the bottom of the wing to be under tension.

**拆解**：谓语 causes 后接两个并列的「宾语 + 不定式」：the skin on the top of the wing to compress 与 the skin on the bottom of the wing to be under tension；第一个不定式主动，第二个被动（be + 介词短语表状态）。

**要点**：cause 后的宾补必须带 to，这一点与 make 不同。两个并列宾语各自带 to，读时不要漏掉第二个 to be。compress 在此是不及物用法，意为「受压」。

### ③ subjecting the bottom of the wing to compression（分词短语作结果状语 + subject A to B）

**原句**：When the airplane is on the ground sitting on its landing gear, the force of gravity tries to bend the wing downward, subjecting the bottom of the wing to compression and the top of the wing tension.

**拆解**：When 从句 + sitting on its landing gear（现在分词短语，说明停放状态）；主句 the force of gravity tries to bend the wing downward；subjecting … 为现在分词短语作结果状语，subject A to B 意为「使 A 承受 B」；and 后的 the top of the wing tension 前省略了 subjecting … to。

**要点**：subject sth to sth 是「使……遭受／承受」，此处 subjecting 与主语是主动关系。省略的 to 要按并列结构补回，否则会误读成「上表面就是拉力」。
