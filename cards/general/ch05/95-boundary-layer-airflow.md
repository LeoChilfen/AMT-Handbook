# Boundary Layer Airflow

> 通用 Ch 05 · Boundary Layer Airflow · 原文 PDF 第 189 页

## 1. 原文

The boundary layer is a very thin layer of air lying over the surface of the wing and, for that matter, all other surfaces of the airplane. Because air has viscosity, this layer of air tends to adhere to the wing. As the wing moves forward through the air the boundary layer at first flows smoothly over the streamlined shape of the airfoil. Here the flow is called the laminar layer.

As the boundary layer approaches the center of the wing, it begins to lose speed due to skin friction and it becomes thicker and turbulent. Here it is called the turbulent layer. The point at which the boundary layer changes from laminar to turbulent is called the transition point. Where the boundary layer becomes turbulent, drag due to skin friction is relatively high. As speed increases, the transition point tends to move forward. As the angle of attack increases, the transition point also tends to move forward. With higher angles of attack and further thickening of the boundary layer, the turbulence becomes so great the air breaks away from the surface of the wing. At this point, the lift of the wing is destroyed and a condition known as a stall has occurred.

## 2. 中文翻译

边界层（boundary layer）是贴近机翼表面的一层非常薄的空气；就此而言，也是贴近飞机其他所有表面的一层薄空气。由于空气具有黏性（viscosity），这层空气倾向于附着在机翼上。当机翼向前运动穿过空气时，边界层起初沿着翼型的流线型外形平滑地流动。这里的流动称为层流层（laminar layer）。

当边界层接近机翼中部时，由于表面摩擦（skin friction），它开始减速，并变得更厚、更湍流。这里的流动称为湍流层（turbulent layer）。边界层由层流转变为湍流的那一点称为转捩点（transition point）。在边界层变为湍流之处，由表面摩擦引起的阻力相对较高。随着速度增大，转捩点趋于前移；随着迎角增大，转捩点也趋于前移。迎角更大、边界层进一步增厚时，湍流变得非常剧烈，以致空气脱离机翼表面。此时机翼的升力被破坏，出现了称为失速的状态。

在图 5-58 中，视图 A 显示正常的迎角，气流保持与机翼接触。视图 B 显示极大的迎角，气流在机翼上表面分离并变为湍流。在视图 B 中，机翼处于失速状态。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词短语作后置定语 + for that matter 插入语

**原句**：The boundary layer is a very thin layer of air lying over the surface of the wing and, for that matter, all other surfaces of the airplane.

**拆解**：主语 The boundary layer；表语 a very thin layer of air；lying over the surface... 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 layer，相当于 which lies over；and, for that matter, all other surfaces 与 the surface of the wing 并列，共用介词 over；for that matter 是插入语。

**要点**：现在分词短语作定语表主动、持续的状态（air lying over 即铺在……之上的空气），改用过去分词就变成被动。for that matter 意为「就此而言、推而广之」，用来把结论从特例推广到一般。并列成分被插入语隔开时，先找 and 两端对应的名词短语，再核对共用的介词与动词。

### ② with 独立结构 + so … (that) 结果从句

**原句**：With higher angles of attack and further thickening of the boundary layer, the turbulence becomes so great the air breaks away from the surface of the wing.

**拆解**：With + 名词短语（higher angles of attack and further thickening of the boundary layer）构成 with 结构作条件／原因状语，其中 thickening 是动名词，与 angles 并列；主句 the turbulence becomes so great；so great (that) the air breaks away... 是 so...that 引导的结果状语从句，that 在手册中常省略。

**要点**：with 短语表示「在……情况下」，比 if 从句紧凑，是技术文本常用的条件表达。so + 形容词 + (that) 从句表结果，省略 that 时不要误读成两个并列句。break away from（脱离）是描述气流分离的固定说法，同义还有 separate from。

### ③ 介词 + which 引导的定语从句

**原句**：The point at which the boundary layer changes from laminar to turbulent is called the transition point.

**拆解**：主语 The point；at which the boundary layer changes from laminar to turbulent 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，修饰 point；谓语 is called 为被动语态，the transition point 作主语补足语。

**要点**：at which 相当于 at the point，把介词提到关系词前是正式书面语的特征，口语中常留在句末（the point which the boundary layer changes at）。change from A to B（由 A 变为 B）是固定搭配；称某物为某名用 be called + 名词，不加 as。
