# Heat Energy Units

> 通用 Ch 05 · Heat Energy Units · 原文 PDF 第 168 页

## 1. 原文

Two different units are used to express quantities of heat energy. They are the calorie and the BTU. One calorie is equal to the amount of heat required to change the temperature of 1 gram of water 1 degree Centigrade.

This term “calorie” (spelled with a lower case c) is 1/1,000 of the Calorie (spelled with a capital C) used in the measurement of the heat energy in foods. One BTU is defined as the amount of heat required to change the temperature of 1 lb of water 1 degree Fahrenheit (1 °F). The calorie and the gram are seldom used in discussing aviation maintenance. The BTU, however, is commonly referred to in discussions of engine thermal efficiencies and the heat content of aviation fuel.

A device known as the calorimeter is used to measure quantities of heat energy. In application, it may be used to determine the quantity of heat energy available in 1 pound of aviation gasoline. A given weight of the fuel is burned in the calorimeter, and the heat energy is absorbed by a large quantity of water. From the weight of the water and the increase in its temperature, it is possible to compute the heat yield of the fuel.

A definite relationship exists between heat and mechanical energy. This relationship has been established and verified by many experiments which show that:

One BTU of heat energy = 778 ft-lb of work As discussed earlier in this chapter under the topic “Potential Energy,” one pound of aviation gasoline contains 18,900 BTU of heat energy. Since each BTU is capable of 778 ft-lb of work, 1 lb of aviation gasoline is capable of 14,704,200 ft-lb of work.

## 2. 中文翻译

有两种不同的单位用来表示热能的数量，即卡（calorie）和 BTU（英热单位）。1 卡等于使 1 克水的温度升高 1 摄氏度所需的热量。

这里的「卡」（calorie，首字母小写 c）是「大卡」（Calorie，首字母大写 C）的 1/1,000，后者用于计量食物中的热能。1 BTU 定义为使 1 lb 水的温度升高 1 华氏度（1 °F）所需的热量。在讨论航空维修时很少用到卡和克。然而，在讨论发动机热效率和航空燃油含热量时，常用的是 BTU。

一种称为量热器（calorimeter）的装置用来测量热能的数量。在实际应用中，它可用来测定 1 磅航空汽油所含的热能。把给定重量的燃油在量热器中燃烧，热能被大量的水吸收。根据水的重量及其温升，就可以算出该燃油的发热量。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动定义句：is defined as

**原句**：One BTU is defined as the amount of heat required to change the temperature of 1 lb of water 1 degree Fahrenheit (1 °F).

**拆解**：主语 One BTU；谓语 is defined as（被动）；as 后接 the amount of heat；required to change … 是过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 heat；to change 是不定式作 required 的状语（「用来改变」）。

**要点**：定义术语用 be defined as + 名词短语，as 不能省，也不能换成 to be。required to do 中的 to do 说明「所需要用来做什么」。change the temperature of … 1 degree 是「使……温度改变 1 度」的及物用法，1 degree 作程度状语。括号里的 (1 °F) 是对 1 degree Fahrenheit 的换算标注，翻译时原样保留。

### ② 形式主语 it + 不定式：it is possible to compute …

**原句**：From the weight of the water and the increase in its temperature, it is possible to compute the heat yield of the fuel.

**拆解**：From … temperature 是方式状语提到句首；it 是形式主语，真正的主语是 to compute the heat yield of the fuel；is possible 是系表结构。

**要点**：it is possible to do 是手册中说明「可以算出／做得到」的常用句式，比 we can compute 更客观。真正主语后置时，谓语用单数 is。the increase in its temperature 中 increase 是名词，介词用 in（increase in temperature），不用 of。

### ③ since 原因状语从句 + be capable of

**原句**：Since each BTU is capable of 778 ft-lb of work, 1 lb of aviation gasoline is capable of 14,704,200 ft-lb of work.

**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句；主句主语 1 lb of aviation gasoline，谓语 is capable of。

**要点**：be capable of 后接名词或动名词，不能接不定式；若要接不定式，应改用 be able to do。数字 14,704,200 是 18,900 × 778 的结果，保留原文的英文千分位逗号写法即可。1 lb of aviation gasoline 作主语时视为一个整体，谓语用单数 is。
