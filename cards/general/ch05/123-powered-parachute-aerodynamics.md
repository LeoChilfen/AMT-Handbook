# Powered Parachute Aerodynamics

## 1. 原文

A powered parachute has a carriage very similar to the weight-shift control aircraft. Its wing, however, has no support structure or rigidity and only takes on the shape of an airfoil when it is inflated by the blast of air from the propeller and the forward speed of the aircraft. In Figure 5-102, a powered parachute is on its approach to land with the wing fully inflated and rising up above the aircraft. Each colored section of the inflated wing is made up of cells that are open in the front to allow air to ram in, and closed in the back to keep the air trapped inside. In between all the cells there are holes that allow the air to flow from one cell to the next, in order to equalize the pressure within the inflated wing. The wing is attached to the carriage of the aircraft by a large number of nylon or Kevlar lines that run from the tips of the wing all the way to the center. The weight of the aircraft acting on these lines and their individual lengths cause the inflated wing to take its shape. The lines attach to the body of the aircraft at a location very close to where the center of gravity is located, and this attachment point is adjustable to account for balance changes with occupants of varying weights.

As in weight-shift control aircraft, the powered parachute does not have the traditional flight controls of a fixed-wing airplane. When the wing of the aircraft is inflated and the aircraft starts moving forward, the wing starts generating lift.

Once the groundspeed is sufficient for the wing’s lift greater than the weight of the aircraft, the aircraft lifts off the ground.

The powered parachute will typically lift off the ground at a speed somewhere between 28 and 30 mph, and will have airspeed in flight of approximately 30 mph.

Once the powered parachute is in flight, control over climbing and descending is handled with engine power. Advancing the throttle makes the aircraft climb, and retarding the throttle makes it descend. The inflated wing creates a lot of drag in flight, so reducing the engine power creates a very controllable descent of the aircraft. The throttle, for controlling engine power, is typically located on the right- hand side of the pilot. [Figure 5-103]

Turning of the powered parachute in flight is handled by foot-operated pedals, or steering bars, located at the front of the aircraft. These bars can be seen in Figure 5-103. Each

foot-operated pedal controls a set of lines, usually made from nylon that runs up to the trailing edge of each wingtip.

When the right foot pedal is pushed, the line pulls down on the tailing edge of the 8 wingtip. As the trailing edge of the right wing drop downs, drag is increased on the right side and the aircraft turns right. When pressure is taken off the foot pedal, the drag in the entire airfoil equalizes and the aircraft resumes its straight-and-level flight.

To land a powered parachute, the first action the pilot takes is to reduce engine power and allow the aircraft to descend.

With the power reduced to idle, the aircraft will descend at a rate of approximately 5 to 10 fps. As the aircraft approaches the ground, the descent rate can be lessened by increasing the engine power. Just before touchdown, the pilot pushes on both foot-operated pedals to drop the trailing edges on both sides of the wing. This action increases the drag on the wing uniformly, causing the wing to pivot aft, which raises the wing leading edge and increases the angle of attack and lift.

In Figure 5-104, the pilot is pushing on both foot pedals and the left and right wing trailing edges are deflected downward.

The aircraft has just touched down and the wing is trailing behind the aircraft, caused by the high angle of attack and the additional drag on the wing. The increase in lift reduces the descent rate to almost nothing, and provides for a gentle landing. If the pilot pushes on the foot pedals too soon, the wing may pivot too far aft before touchdown resulting in an unacceptable descent rate. In that case, it might be a relatively hard landing.

## 2. 中文翻译

动力伞的吊舱与重量操纵式航空器非常相似。但它的伞翼没有支撑结构，也没有刚性，只有在被螺旋桨气流吹胀、并获得航空器前飞速度时，才呈现翼型形状。图 5-102 中，一架动力伞正在进近着陆，伞翼完全充气并高举在航空器上方。充气伞翼的每一个彩色分区都由若干气室组成：气室前部开口让空气冲入，后部封闭把空气留在里面。所有气室之间都开有孔，让空气从一个气室流到下一个气室，使充气伞翼内的压力均衡。伞翼通过大量尼龙或凯夫拉绳索连接到航空器的吊舱上，这些绳索从翼尖一直连到中央。作用在这些绳索上的航空器重量以及各绳索各自的长度，使充气伞翼形成其形状。这些绳索连接在航空器机体上非常靠近重心的位置，该连接点可调，以适应不同体重的乘员带来的平衡变化。

与重量操纵式航空器一样，动力伞没有固定翼飞机的传统飞行操纵装置。当伞翼充气、航空器开始前飞时，伞翼开始产生升力。

当地速足够大、伞翼升力大于航空器重量时，航空器离地升空。

（此处为图内文字，略）与飞机不同——飞机上飞行员通过决定何时后拉驾驶盘而在很大程度上控制飞机何时抬轮——动力伞在达到特定空速之前不会起飞。动力伞通常在 28 至 30 mph 之间的某个速度离地，飞行中的空速约为 30 mph。

动力伞升空后，爬升和下降由发动机功率控制。增大油门使航空器爬升，收小油门使其下降。充气伞翼在飞行中产生很大阻力，因此减小发动机功率可使航空器以很可控的方式下降。控制发动机功率的油门通常位于飞行员右侧。

动力伞飞行中的转弯由位于航空器前部的脚蹬或操纵杆控制。这些杆见图 5-103。每个

脚蹬控制一组绳索（通常为尼龙绳），绳索向上连到每个翼尖的后缘。

踩下右脚蹬时，绳索把右侧翼尖的后缘向下拉。右翼后缘下垂时，右侧阻力增大，航空器向右转弯。松开脚蹬后，整个翼型的阻力恢复均衡，航空器恢复平直飞行。

动力伞着陆时，飞行员采取的第一个动作是减小发动机功率，让航空器下降。

功率减到慢车后，航空器将以约 5 至 10 fps 的速率下降。航空器接近地面时，可通过增大发动机功率来减小下降率。接地之前，飞行员同时踩下两个脚蹬，使伞翼两侧的后缘下垂。这一动作均匀增大伞翼阻力，使伞翼向后偏转，从而抬起伞翼前缘并增大迎角和升力。

图 5-104 中，飞行员正踩着两个脚蹬，左右两侧的伞翼后缘向下偏转。

航空器刚刚接地，由于迎角大、伞翼阻力增加，伞翼拖在航空器后方。升力增大使下降率几乎降为零，从而实现轻柔着陆。如果飞行员过早踩下脚蹬，伞翼可能在接地前向后偏转过甚，造成不可接受的下降率；那样一来，着陆就可能相当重。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① however 插入 + when 从句 + 被动

**原句**：Its wing, however, has no support structure or rigidity and only takes on the shape of an airfoil when it is inflated by the blast of air from the propeller and the forward speed of the aircraft.

**拆解**：主语 Its wing 后插入 however（两侧用逗号隔开）；谓语并列 has no support structure or rigidity 与 takes on the shape of an airfoil；when it is inflated by the blast of air from the propeller and the forward speed of the aircraft 是时间状语从句，用被动。

**要点**：however 插在主语与谓语之间时两侧都要逗号。no … or … 表示「既没有……也没有……」，否定句中用 or 而不用 and。take on 是「呈现、具有」的多义短语动词，take on the shape of 意为「呈现出……的形状」。

### ② 定语从句 + 并列过去分词形容词 + 不定式作目的

**原句**：Each colored section of the inflated wing is made up of cells that are open in the front to allow air to ram in, and closed in the back to keep the air trapped inside.

**拆解**：主语 Each colored section of the inflated wing；谓语 is made up of；宾语 cells，其后 that are open in the front to allow air to ram in, and closed in the back to keep the air trapped inside 是定语从句，其中 are 后并列 open … 与 closed … 两个过去分词形容词；两个不定式 to allow / to keep 都作目的状语。

**要点**：be made up of 是「由……组成」，与 be made of（由……制成）不同。allow air to ram in 用不定式，而 keep the air trapped 用过去分词作宾补（空气被留住），两个搭配要分别记。

### ③ 现在分词短语作定语 + cause sb to do

**原句**：The weight of the aircraft acting on these lines and their individual lengths cause the inflated wing to take its shape.

**拆解**：主语是并列的两项：The weight of the aircraft（后接现在分词短语 acting on these lines 作定语）与 their individual lengths；谓语 cause；宾语 the inflated wing；宾语补足语 to take its shape。

**要点**：主语含 and 连接的两项时，谓语用复数 cause。acting on these lines 是现在分词短语作后置定语，等于 which acts on these lines。cause sb to do 中的 to 不能省，这一点与 make / let 后接原形不同。
