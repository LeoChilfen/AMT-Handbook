# Doppler Effect

> 通用 Ch 05 · Doppler Effect · 原文 PDF 第 182 页

## 1. 原文

When sound is coming from a moving object, the object’s forward motion adds to the frequency as sensed from the front and takes away from the frequency as sensed from the rear.

This change in frequency is known as the Doppler Effect, and it explains why the sound from an airplane seems different as it approaches compared to how it sounds as it flies overhead. As it approaches, it becomes both louder and higher pitched. As it flies away, the loudness and pitch both decrease noticeably.

If an airplane is flying at or higher than the speed of sound, the sound energy cannot travel out ahead of the airplane, because the airplane catches up to it the instant it tries to leave. The sound energy being created by the airplane piles up, and attaches itself to the structure of the airplane. As the airplane approaches, a person standing on the ground will not be able to hear it until it gets past their position, because the sound energy is actually trailing behind the airplane. When the sound of the airplane is heard, it will be in the form of what is called a sonic boom.

## 2. 中文翻译

当声音来自运动中的物体时，该物体向前的运动会使从前方听到的频率升高，使从后方听到的频率降低。

这种频率变化称为多普勒效应，它解释了为什么飞机的声音在接近时听起来与掠过头顶时不同。接近时，声音既更响，音调也更高；飞离时，响度与音高都明显下降。

如果飞机以等于或高于声速的速度飞行，声能就无法到达飞机的前方，因为飞机在声音试图离开的瞬间就追上了它。飞机所产生的声能不断堆积，并附着在飞机结构上。飞机接近时，站在地面上的人要等它飞过自己所处位置之后才能听到，因为声能实际上是拖在飞机后面的。当终于听到飞机的声音时，它是以所谓“音爆”的形式出现的。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as + 过去分词（省略式状语）

**原句**：When sound is coming from a moving object, the object’s forward motion adds to the frequency as sensed from the front and takes away from the frequency as sensed from the rear.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，用现在进行时表持续；主句主语 the object’s forward motion，谓语是并列的 adds to 与 takes away from；as sensed from the front / the rear 相当于 as it is sensed from ...，省略了主语和 be，过去分词短语作状语。

**要点**：as + 过去分词表示「正如（被）……那样」，是省略式状语从句，同类还有 as shown、as required、as measured。add to 是「使增加」，take away from 是「使减少」，两者的介词不能互换，也不能说 add the frequency。

### ② be known as + why 宾语从句 + compared to

**原句**：This change in frequency is known as the Doppler Effect, and it explains why the sound from an airplane seems different as it approaches compared to how it sounds as it flies overhead.

**拆解**：第一分句 This change in frequency is known as the Doppler Effect（被动）；第二分句主语 it，谓语 explains，宾语是 why 引导的从句；从句内 as it approaches 与 as it flies overhead 是两个时间状语从句；compared to how it sounds 是比较状语，to 后接 how 引导的从句。

**要点**：be known as + 名称意为「被称为」，注意与 be known for（因……而闻名）区分。explain 后可接 why / how / that 从句。compared to（或 compared with）后既可接名词短语，也可接从句，如 compared to how it sounds。

### ③ 名词短语作连词：the instant

**原句**：If an airplane is flying at or higher than the speed of sound, the sound energy cannot travel out ahead of the airplane, because the airplane catches up to it the instant it tries to leave.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句；主句 the sound energy cannot travel out ahead of the airplane；because 引导原因状语从句；the instant it tries to leave 中 the instant 起连词作用，引导时间状语从句，相当于 as soon as。

**要点**：the instant / the moment / the minute / the second 都可当连词，意为「一……就……」，后面直接跟主谓、不加 that。ahead of 表示「在……前方」。catch up to（= catch up with）意为「追上」。

### ④ 现在分词被动式作后置定语

**原句**：The sound energy being created by the airplane piles up, and attaches itself to the structure of the airplane.

**拆解**：主语 The sound energy；being created by the airplane 是现在分词被动式作后置定语（正在被飞机产生的）；谓语为并列的 piles up 与 attaches；itself 是 attaches 的宾语，反身代词回指 the sound energy。

**要点**：being + 过去分词 表示「正在被……的」，要与单纯过去分词区分：the energy created（已产生的能量）／the energy being created（正在产生的能量）。attach itself to 意为「附着在……上」，反身代词不能省，因为它表明主语作用于自身。
