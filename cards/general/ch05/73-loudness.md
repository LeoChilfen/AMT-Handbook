# Loudness

> 通用 Ch 05 · Loudness · 原文 PDF 第 182 页

## 1. 原文

When a bell rings, the sound waves spread out in all directions and the sound is heard in all directions. When a bell is struck lightly, the vibrations are of small amplitude and the sound is weak. A stronger blow produces vibrations of greater amplitude in the bell, and the sound is louder. It is evident that the amplitude of the air vibrations is greater when the amplitude of the vibrations of the source is increased. Hence, the loudness of the sound depends on the amplitude of the vibrations of the sound waves. As the distance from the source increases, the energy in each wave spreads out, and the sound becomes weaker.

As the sound wave advances, variations in pressure occur at all points in the transmitting medium. The greater the pressure variations, the more intense the sound wave is.

The intensity is proportional to the square of the pressure variation regardless of the frequency. Thus, by measuring pressure changes, the intensities of sounds having different frequencies can be compared directly.

## 2. 中文翻译

铃响时，声波向各个方向扩散，声音也向各个方向传播。轻敲铃时，振动振幅小，声音就弱。用更大的力敲击，铃内的振动振幅更大，声音也更响。显然，当声源振动的振幅增大时，空气振动的振幅也随之增大。因此，声音的响度取决于声波振动的振幅。随着离开声源的距离增大，每个波中的能量向外扩散，声音便随之变弱。

声波向前传播时，传声介质中各点都会出现压力变化。压力变化越大，声波就越强。

无论频率如何，声强都与压力变化的平方成正比。因此，通过测量压力变化，就可以直接比较不同频率声音的声强。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① it 作形式主语 + that 主语从句

**原句**：It is evident that the amplitude of the air vibrations is greater when the amplitude of the vibrations of the source is increased.

**拆解**：It 是形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句内部为 the amplitude of the air vibrations is greater，when ... 是时间状语从句，其中用被动 is increased。

**要点**：It is evident / obvious / clear that ... 是技术文本引出结论的固定句式，译作「显然……」，不要译成「它是明显的」。注意从句里 the amplitude ... is greater 是主系表，不要被两个 of 短语绕晕：先说空气振动的振幅更大，再说条件声源振幅增大。

### ② the + 比较级，the + 比较级

**原句**：The greater the pressure variations, the more intense the sound wave is.

**拆解**：前一分句 The greater the pressure variations 是省略式（相当于 the greater the pressure variations are）；后一分句 the more intense the sound wave is 为正常语序。两句都把「the + 比较级」提到句首。

**要点**：该句型表示「越……，就越……」，前后都必须用比较级、the 都不能省，且前句常用省略语序。技术文本用它表达比例关系，如 The higher the altitude, the lower the pressure。

### ③ as 引导的时间状语从句 + and 连接的并列分句

**原句**：As the distance from the source increases, the energy in each wave spreads out, and the sound becomes weaker.

**拆解**：As 引导时间／伴随状语从句，意为「随着……」；主句由 and 连接两个并列分句：the energy in each wave spreads out 与 the sound becomes weaker，两个谓语都用第三人称单数。

**要点**：as 表「随着」时后面必须是从句（主谓齐全）；若只想接名词短语，改用 with，如 with the increase of distance。spreads out 与 becomes 各自的主语分别是 the energy 和 the sound，不要把 and 后的内容当成前面动词的宾语。

### ④ regardless of 引导的让步状语

**原句**：The intensity is proportional to the square of the pressure variation regardless of the frequency.

**拆解**：主句 The intensity is proportional to the square of the pressure variation；regardless of the frequency 是介词短语作让步状语，置于句末。

**要点**：regardless of + 名词（或 wh- 从句）意为「不论……」，可与 irrespective of 互换，后接从句时只能用 whether / what 等，不能接 that。这句是典型的「结论 + 限定条件」写法：先给比例关系，再用 regardless of 排除频率这一变量。
