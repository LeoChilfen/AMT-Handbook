# The Atmosphere

> 通用 Ch 05 · The Atmosphere · 原文 PDF 第 182 页

## 1. 原文

Aviation is so dependent upon that category of fluids called gases and the effect of forces and pressures acting upon gases that a discussion of the subject of the atmosphere is important to the persons maintaining and repairing aircraft.

Data available about the atmosphere may determine whether a flight will succeed, or whether it will even become airborne.

The various components of the air around the earth, the changes in temperatures and pressures at different levels above the earth, the properties of weather encountered by aircraft in flight, and many other detailed data are considered in the preparation of flight plans.

## 2. 中文翻译

航空如此依赖被称为气体这一类流体，以及作用于气体的力和压力所产生的影响，因此讨论大气这一课题，对从事航空器维护与修理的人员来说十分重要。

可获得的大气数据，可能决定一次飞行能否成功，甚至决定航空器能否升空。

地球周围空气的各种成分、地球以上不同高度处温度与压力的变化、航空器飞行中遇到的天气特性，以及许多其他详细数据，都要在制定飞行计划时加以考虑。

（此处为图内文字，略）帕斯卡（原文拼作 Pascan）与托里切利被认为发明了气压计，这是一种测量大气压力的仪器。他们的实验结果至今仍在使用，在设计或认识上几乎没有改进。他们确定空气具有重量，而该重量随相对于海平面的高度变化而变化。如今，科学家还关心大气如何影响航空器及其设备的性能。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① so ... that 结果状语从句

**原句**：Aviation is so dependent upon that category of fluids called gases and the effect of forces and pressures acting upon gases that a discussion of the subject of the atmosphere is important to the persons maintaining and repairing aircraft.

**拆解**：主句 Aviation is so dependent upon A and B，其中 A = that category of fluids called gases、B = the effect of forces and pressures acting upon gases；so ... that 引出结果状语从句 a discussion ... is important to the persons ...；maintaining and repairing aircraft 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 persons。

**要点**：so + 形容词（此处 dependent）+ that 表示「如此……以致」。句中 depend upon 的两个并列宾语都很长，读句时应先定位 so dependent upon，再找到 that 之后的从句主干。the persons maintaining and repairing aircraft 相当于 the persons who maintain and repair aircraft。

### ② available 后置定语 + 两个并列 whether 宾语从句

**原句**：Data available about the atmosphere may determine whether a flight will succeed, or whether it will even become airborne.

**拆解**：主语 Data；available about the atmosphere 是形容词短语后置定语；谓语 may determine；宾语是 or 连接的两个 whether 从句。

**要点**：以 -able 结尾的形容词（available、possible、usable）以及修饰不定代词时多后置，如 data available、the best method available。两个 whether 从句结构对称，也可压缩成 whether a flight will succeed or even become airborne，但原文的并列写法更强调两种可能。

### ③ be credited with + 动名词 + 同位语

**原句**：Pascan and Torricelli have been credited with developing the barometer, an instrument for measuring atmospheric pressure.

**拆解**：主语 Pascan and Torricelli；谓语 have been credited with（现在完成时被动）；with 后接动名词 developing；an instrument for measuring atmospheric pressure 是 the barometer 的同位语，其中 for measuring 表用途。

**要点**：credit sb with sth / be credited with doing sth 意为「被认为做出了……」，是科技史叙述的常用句式。同位语直接用名词（不写 which is）是技术定义的标准写法。for + 动名词表用途，不能换成 to measuring。

### ④ 定语从句 + as 引导的时间状语从句

**原句**：They determined that air has weight which changes as altitude is changed with respect to sea level.

**拆解**：主句 They determined；that 引导宾语从句 air has weight；which changes ... 是定语从句修饰 weight；as altitude is changed 是时间状语从句（被动），with respect to sea level 是状语，说明比较基准。

**要点**：with respect to 意为「相对于、就……而言」，技术文本中常用来点明参照面。两个关键词都由关系代词／连词串起：weight ← which，altitude ← as，读句时可先剥掉从句看主干 air has weight。
