# Sound

> 通用 Ch 05 · Sound · 原文 PDF 第 180 页

## 1. 原文

Sound has been defined as a series of disturbances in matter that the human ear can detect. This definition can also be applied to disturbances which are beyond the range of human hearing. There are three elements which are necessary for the transmission and reception of sound. These are the source, a medium for carrying the sound, and the detector. Anything which moves back and forth, or vibrates, and disturbs the medium around it may be considered a sound source.

An example of the production and transmission of sound is the ring of a bell. When the bell is struck and begins to vibrate, the particles of the medium, or the surrounding air, in contact with the bell also vibrate. The vibrational disturbance is transmitted from one particle of the medium to the next, and the vibrations travel in a “wave” through the medium until they reach the ear. The eardrum, acting as detector, is set in motion by the vibrating particles of air, and the brain interprets the eardrum’s vibrations as the characteristic sound associated with a bell.

## 2. 中文翻译

声音曾被定义为物质中一系列人耳能够察觉的扰动。这个定义也可适用于超出人耳听觉范围的扰动。声音的传送与接收需要三个要素：声源、传送声音的介质和检测器。任何来回运动即振动、并扰动周围介质的东西，都可以视为声源。

声音产生与传送的一个例子是铃响。当铃被敲击并开始振动时，与铃接触的介质（即周围空气）的质点也随之振动。振动的扰动由介质的一个质点传给下一个质点，振动以「波」的形式在介质中传播，直到到达耳朵。作为检测器的耳膜被振动的空气质点带动起来，大脑把耳膜的振动解读为与铃相关的特征声音。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① has been defined as … that the human ear can detect（现在完成被动 + 限制性定语从句）

**原句**：Sound has been defined as a series of disturbances in matter that the human ear can detect.

**拆解**：主语 Sound，谓语 has been defined as（现在完成时被动，as 引出定义内容）；a series of disturbances in matter 是定义；that the human ear can detect 是限制性定语从句修饰 disturbances，that 在从句中作 detect 的宾语。

**要点**：be defined as / be known as / be referred to as 都用 as 引出术语的定义，不能换成 to be。用现在完成时（has been defined）而不是一般现在时，是在交代「这个定义是历来形成的」，与后句 This definition can also be applied to… 的评述相衔接。注意 that 从句紧跟 disturbances，因此「人耳能察觉」修饰的是扰动，不是物质（matter）。

### ② Anything which moves …, or vibrates, and disturbs … may be considered a sound source（定语从句含并列谓语 + 情态被动）

**原句**：Anything which moves back and forth, or vibrates, and disturbs the medium around it may be considered a sound source.

**拆解**：主语 Anything，which moves back and forth, or vibrates, and disturbs the medium around it 是定语从句，从句内三个动词短语并列（moves / vibrates / disturbs），or 把前两个视为同一动作的两种说法；主句谓语 may be considered，a sound source 作主语补足语（consider A (as) B 的被动式）。

**要点**：consider A B 变被动后是 A is considered B，不加 to be（不同于 regard A as B）。定语从句里用 or 连接近义动词是手册的宽容写法：moves back and forth 就是 vibrates。around it 中的 it 回指 Anything，译「其周围的介质」。

### ③ The eardrum, acting as detector, is set in motion by …, and the brain interprets … as …（分词插入语 + set in motion + interpret as）

**原句**：The eardrum, acting as detector, is set in motion by the vibrating particles of air, and the brain interprets the eardrum’s vibrations as the characteristic sound associated with a bell.

**拆解**：The eardrum 与 the brain 分别作两个并列分句的主语；acting as detector 是现在分词短语作非限制性定语（= which acts as detector）；is set in motion by … 是被动（set 的过去分词仍是 set）；the vibrating particles of air 中 vibrating 是现在分词作定语；interprets the eardrum’s vibrations as the characteristic sound 是「interpret A as B」结构；associated with a bell 是过去分词短语后置修饰 sound。

**要点**：set … in motion 是固定搭配「使……运动起来」，被动形式 set in motion 里没有 be 之外的助动词。interpret A as B 译「把 A 理解为／解读为 B」，与 regard A as B 同构；associated with 表示「与……相关的」，是被动含义的过去分词，不能写成 associating。
