# Gauge Pressure

> 通用 Ch 05 · Gauge Pressure · 原文 PDF 第 173 页

## 1. 原文

A gauge pressure (psig) is a reading that refers to when an instrument, such as an oil pressure gauge, fuel pressure gauge, or hydraulic system pressure gauge, displays pressure which is over and above ambient. This can be seen on the fuel pressure gauge shown in Figure 5-34. When the oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump is not turning, and there is no pressure being created, the gauge will read zero.

## 2. 中文翻译

表压（psig）是这样一种读数：当仪表（如滑油压力表、燃油压力表或液压系统压力表）显示的压力高于环境压力时，该读数即为表压。这一点可在图 5-34 所示的燃油压力表上看到。当滑油泵、燃油泵或液压泵不转动、没有压力产生时，表的读数为零。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句 + 介词后接名词性从句（refers to when …）

**原句**：A gauge pressure (psig) is a reading that refers to when an instrument, such as an oil pressure gauge, fuel pressure gauge, or hydraulic system pressure gauge, displays pressure which is over and above ambient.

**拆解**：主语 A gauge pressure，括号内 psig 为同位缩写；系动词 is；表语 a reading；that refers to when … 是定语从句修饰 a reading，其中 refers to 的宾语是 when 引导的名词性从句（介词后接从句）；such as … 是插入的同位列举；which is over and above ambient 是修饰 pressure 的定语从句。

**要点**：refer to 后接 when 从句属于「介词 + 名词性从句」，书面语常见，口语多说 refers to the situation when …。阅读时先跳过 such as 的三个并列例子，骨架就是 a reading → refers to when an instrument displays pressure。ambient 此处作名词，等于 ambient pressure。

### ② 过去分词短语作后置定语（shown in Figure 5-34）

**原句**：This can be seen on the fuel pressure gauge shown in Figure 5-34.

**拆解**：主语 This（指前一句所述的表压读数方式）；谓语 can be seen（情态动词 + 被动）；on the fuel pressure gauge 是地点状语；shown in Figure 5-34 是过去分词短语作 gauge 的后置定语（= which is shown in …）。

**要点**：shown / illustrated / listed 引导的过去分词短语是手册引图引表的固定说法，相当于省略了 which is，翻译时常前置为「图 5-34 所示的燃油压力表」。on the gauge 用 on，因为读数显示在表盘的「面上」；by the gauge 则指「由该表（测得）」，两者不可互换。

### ③ or 连接的并列成分与主谓一致 + there be 中的被动分词

**原句**：When the oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump is not turning, and there is no pressure being created, the gauge will read zero.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，内含两个并列分句：the oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump is not turning 与 there is no pressure being created；前者三个并列修饰语共用中心词 pump，谓语用单数 is；后者是 there be + 名词 pressure + 现在分词被动式 being created（作后置定语）；主句 the gauge will read zero。

**要点**：并列成分若共享一个中心词（oil, fuel, or hydraulic pump），谓语按中心词的单复数来定，用 is 而不是 are。being created 是「正在被产生」，相当于 which is being created；若写成 there is no pressure created，则读作「没有已产生的压力」，与「泵不转时表指零」的实时语境不符。read 在此为不及物用法，意为「（仪表）显示为」。
