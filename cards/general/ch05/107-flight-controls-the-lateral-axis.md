# Flight Controls & the Lateral Axis

## 1. 原文

The lateral axis of an airplane is a line that runs below the wing, from wingtip to wingtip, passing through the airplane’s center of gravity. Movement around this axis is called pitch, and control around this axis is called longitudinal control. The flight control that handles this job is the elevator attached to the horizontal stabilizer, a fully moving horizontal stabilizer, or on a v-tail configured airplane, it is called ruddervators. An elevator on a Cessna 182 can be seen in Figure 5-65 . In Figure 5-66 , a fully moving horizontal stabilizer, known as a stabilator, can be seen on a Piper Cherokee Cruiser PA-28-140, and Figure 5-67 shows a ruddervator on a Beechcraft Bonanza.

On the Cessna 182 shown in Figure 5-65, pulling back on the control wheel causes the trailing edge of the elevator to deflect upward, causing an increased downward force that raises the nose of the airplane. Movement of the elevator causes the nose of the airplane to pitch up or pitch down by rotating around the lateral axis. The Cessna 182 control wheel can be seen in Figure 5-68.

On the Piper Cherokee Cruiser PA-28-140 shown in Figure 5-66, pulling back on the control wheel causes the entire horizontal surface, or stabilator, to move, with the trailing edge deflecting upward. The anti-servo tab seen on the Cherokee provides a control feel similar to what would be experienced by moving an elevator. Without this tab, the stabilator might be too easy to move and a pilot could overcontrol the airplane.

The ruddervators shown on the Beechcraft Bonanza in Figure 5-67 are also moved by the control wheel, with their trailing edges deflecting upward when the control wheel is pulled back. As the name implies, these surfaces also act as the rudder for this airplane.

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定义几何概念的句型：is a line that runs …, passing through …

**原句**：The lateral axis of an airplane is a line that runs below the wing, from wingtip to wingtip, passing through the airplane’s center of gravity.

**拆解**：主句为「主语 + is + 表语 a line」；that runs below the wing … 是定语从句修饰 a line；from wingtip to wingtip 是介词短语补充走向；passing through … 是现在分词短语作伴随状语。

**要点**：定义轴、平面等几何概念时，手册惯用「is a line that runs …, passing through …」：定语从句说方向，分词短语说经过点。分词的逻辑主语必须与主句一致，此处是 a line 而不是 an airplane。run 在此不是「跑」，而是「延伸、走向」。

### ② 被动 + 主语补足语：is called

**原句**：Movement around this axis is called pitch, and control around this axis is called longitudinal control.

**拆解**：两个并列的被动分句，均为「主语 + is called + 名词」，该名词是主语补足语。

**要点**：call A B 变被动为 A is called B，B 不加 as、也不是宾语。三组易混搭配：name A B（直接加名词）、refer to A as B、describe A as B。movement 与 control 的对照是本节的主线：运动叫什么、操纵叫什么。

### ③ 动名词作主语 + cause sth to do + 分词结果状语

**原句**：On the Cessna 182 shown in Figure 5-65, pulling back on the control wheel causes the trailing edge of the elevator to deflect upward, causing an increased downward force that raises the nose of the airplane.

**拆解**：On the Cessna 182 shown in Figure 5-65 是地点状语（shown … 为过去分词短语修饰 Cessna 182）；主语是动名词短语 pulling back on the control wheel；谓语 causes，宾语 the trailing edge of the elevator，宾补 to deflect upward；causing … 是现在分词短语作结果状语，其中 that raises the nose 是定语从句修饰 force。

**要点**：cause sb/sth to do 中的 to 不可省。动名词短语作主语视为单数，谓语用 causes。结果状语用「逗号 + causing」引出，逻辑主语是前面整件事，翻译时拆成「从而……」最顺。pull back 是「向后拉（杆）」，与 push forward（前推）相对。
