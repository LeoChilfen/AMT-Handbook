# Second Class Lever

> 通用 Ch 05 · Second Class Lever · 原文 PDF 第 159 页

## 1. 原文

The second class lever has the fulcrum at one end and the effort is applied at the other end. The resistance is somewhere between these points. A wheelbarrow is a good example of a second class lever, with the wheel at one end being the fulcrum, the handles at the opposite end being the applied effort, and the bucket in the middle being where the weight or resistance is placed. [Figure 5-8]

Both first and second class levers are commonly used to help in overcoming big resistances with a relatively small effort.

The first class lever, however, is more versatile. Depending on how close or how far away the weight is placed from the fulcrum, the first class lever can be made to gain force or gain distance, but not both at the same time. The second class lever can only be made to gain force.

Example: The distance from the center of the wheel to the handles on a wheelbarrow is 60 inches. The weight in the bucket is 18 inches from the center of the wheel. If 300 lb is placed in the bucket, how much force must be applied at the handles to lift the wheelbarrow?

Effort (E) × Effort Arm (L) = Resistance (R) × Resistance Arm (l)

E × 60 inches = 300 lb × 18 in E = 300 lb × 18 in ÷ 60 in E = 90 lb The mechanical advantage of the lever in this example would be:

Mechanical Advantage = Force Out ÷ Force In = 300 lb ÷ 90 lb = 3.33, or 3.33 to 1

## 2. 中文翻译

第二类杠杆的支点位于一端，施力作用在另一端。阻力则位于这两点之间的某个位置。独轮手推车是第二类杠杆的一个好例子：一端的车轮是支点，另一端的手柄是所施加的力，而中间的斗体则是放置重物（即阻力）的地方。

第一类和第二类杠杆都常用来以相对较小的力帮助克服很大的阻力。

不过，第一类杠杆的通用性更强。视重物放置得离支点近或远，第一类杠杆可以被做成增力型或增距型，但不能同时兼得。第二类杠杆则只能做成增力型。

例：独轮手推车车轮中心到手柄的距离为 60 in。斗中重物距车轮中心 18 in。若斗中装入 300 lb 的重物，需要在手柄处施加多大的力才能抬起该手推车？

施力（E）× 施力臂（L）= 阻力（R）× 阻力臂（l）

E × 60 inches = 300 lb × 18 in
E = 300 lb × 18 in ÷ 60 in
E = 90 lb
本例中杠杆的机械利益为：

机械利益 = 输出力 ÷ 输入力 = 300 lb ÷ 90 lb = 3.33，即 3.33 : 1

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 并列句中的两种句式：have + 宾语 + 地点 / 被动语态

**原句**：The second class lever has the fulcrum at one end and the effort is applied at the other end.

**拆解**：and 连接两个并列分句；前句 has the fulcrum at one end 是「have + 宾语 + 地点状语」；后句 the effort is applied at the other end 为被动语态，两个分句主谓各自完整。

**要点**：同一个句子里前后换用不同句式，是英文避免重复的手段；汉语可统一处理为「支点在一端，施力作用在另一端」。have sth at somewhere 表示「某物位于某处」，不必译成「拥有」。注意 and 前若少一个主语就成了简单句，语义会变。

### ② with 引导的三重独立主格

**原句**：A wheelbarrow is a good example of a second class lever, with the wheel at one end being the fulcrum, the handles at the opposite end being the applied effort, and the bucket in the middle being where the weight or resistance is placed.

**拆解**：主句 A wheelbarrow is a good example of…；逗号后 with 接三个并列的复合结构（名词 + being + 表语），being 为现在分词；第三个复合结构的表语是 where the weight or resistance is placed，即表语从句。

**要点**：with 复合结构把三个部件与杠杆三要素一一对应，翻译时拆成「车轮是……，手柄是……，斗体是……」最清楚。being + 名词/从句 表示「充当、即为」。英语靠 with 一个词挂住三层关系，汉语必须分层铺开，否则会读成一句话说不完。

### ③ 现在分词状语 + how 从句 + be made to do

**原句**：Depending on how close or how far away the weight is placed from the fulcrum, the first class lever can be made to gain force or gain distance, but not both at the same time.

**拆解**：Depending on… 为现在分词短语作状语（依……而定）；how close or how far away… 是 how 引导的宾语从句，作 depending on 的宾语；can be made to gain force or gain distance 为被动 + 不定式（make 变被动后 to 必须还原）；but not both 是省略式并列否定。

**要点**：make sb do 变被动必须写成 be made to do，to 不能丢，这是中文母语者的高频错误。depending on 后接从句时介词 on 不能省。or gain distance 中的 or 表「或」，与 at the same time 一起表达「不可兼得」，译「既能增力又能增距是不行的」。
