# Subsonic, Transonic, and Supersonic Flight

> 通用 Ch 05 · Subsonic, Transonic, and Supersonic Flight · 原文 PDF 第 197 页

## 1. 原文

When an airplane is flying at subsonic speed, all of the air flowing around the airplane is at a velocity of less than the speed of sound, which is known as Mach 1. Keep in mind that the air accelerates when it flows over certain parts of the airplane, like the top of the wing, so an airplane flying at 500 mph could have air over the top of the wing reach a speed of 600 mph. How fast an airplane can fly and still be considered in subsonic flight varies with the design of the wing, but as a Mach number, it will typically be just over Mach 0.8.

When an airplane is flying at transonic speed, part of the airplane is experiencing subsonic airflow and part is experiencing supersonic airflow. Over the top of the wing, probably about halfway back, the velocity of the air will reach Mach 1 and a shock wave will form. The shock wave forms 90 degrees to the airflow and is known as a normal shock wave. Stability problems can be encountered during transonic flight, because the shock wave can cause the airflow to separate from the wing. The shock wave also causes the center of lift to shift aft, causing the nose to pitch down.

The speed at which the shock wave forms is known as the critical Mach number. Transonic speed is typically between Mach 0.80 and 1.20.

When an airplane is flying at supersonic speed, the entire airplane is experiencing supersonic airflow. At this speed, the shock wave which formed on top of the wing during transonic flight has moved all the way aft and has attached itself to the wing trailing edge. Supersonic speed is from Mach 1.20 to 5.0. If an airplane flies faster than Mach 5, it is said to be in hypersonic flight.

## 2. 中文翻译

当飞机以亚声速飞行时，绕飞机流动的所有空气速度都低于声速，声速即马赫数 1。要记住，空气流经飞机某些部位（如机翼上表面）时会加速，因此一架以 500 mph 飞行的飞机，其机翼上表面的气流速度可能达到 600 mph。一架飞机能飞多快而仍算作亚声速飞行，取决于机翼设计；但用马赫数表示，通常略高于马赫数 0.8。

当飞机以跨声速飞行时，飞机的一部分处于亚声速气流中，另一部分处于超声速气流中。在机翼上表面大约靠后一半的位置，气流速度会达到马赫数 1，并形成一道激波。该激波与气流方向成 90 度，称为正激波。跨声速飞行中可能遇到稳定性问题，因为激波会使气流从机翼上分离。激波还会使升力中心后移，导致机头下俯。

形成激波的速度称为临界马赫数。跨声速通常指马赫数 0.80 至 1.20 之间。

当飞机以超声速飞行时，整架飞机都处于超声速气流中。在这个速度下，跨声速飞行时在机翼上表面形成的激波已一直后移，并附着在机翼后缘上。超声速为马赫数 1.20 至 5.0。如果飞机飞得比马赫数 5 更快，则称为高超声速飞行。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① have + 宾语 + 动词原形

**原句**：Keep in mind that the air accelerates when it flows over certain parts of the airplane, like the top of the wing, so an airplane flying at 500 mph could have air over the top of the wing reach a speed of 600 mph.

**拆解**：Keep in mind 是祈使句，后接 that 引导的宾语从句；从句内 when it flows over certain parts of the airplane 是时间状语，like the top of the wing 举例；so 引出结果分句，其中 an airplane flying at 500 mph（现在分词作定语）是主语，could have air … reach a speed of 600 mph 是 have + 宾语 + 动词原形结构。

**要点**：have + 宾语 + 原形表示「使某事发生」，此处是客观描述而非人为使役；reach 前不加 to，也不加 -s。flying at 500 mph 是现在分词短语作后置定语，等于 that flies at 500 mph。

### ② How fast 引导主语从句

**原句**：How fast an airplane can fly and still be considered in subsonic flight varies with the design of the wing, but as a Mach number, it will typically be just over Mach 0.8.

**拆解**：How fast an airplane can fly and still be considered in subsonic flight 整个是主语从句（how + 副词 + 主语 + 谓语），谓语是 varies with the design of the wing；but 连接并列分句 as a Mach number, it will typically be just over Mach 0.8，as a Mach number 意为「用马赫数表示」。

**要点**：how 引导的主语从句作句子主语时，谓语用第三人称单数（varies）。still be considered 与 can fly 并列，共用情态动词 can，故用 be 而不是 is。just over / just above 是「略高于」，给出工程数据时的模糊限定。

### ③ at which 引导的定语从句

**原句**：The speed at which the shock wave forms is known as the critical Mach number.

**拆解**：主语 The speed，其后 at which the shock wave forms 是「介词 + 关系代词」引导的定语从句；谓语 is known as，表语 the critical Mach number。

**要点**：先行词是 speed 时，从句中的介词 at 要提到 which 之前（= the speed at which…），不能说成 the speed which the shock wave forms at 那样随意。be known as 后接名称，be known for 后接特征，两者不要混用；这句是定义术语的典型句式。
