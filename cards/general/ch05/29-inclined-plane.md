# Inclined Plane

> 通用 Ch 05 · Inclined Plane · 原文 PDF 第 161 页

## 1. 原文

The inclined plane is a simple machine that facilitates the raising or lowering of heavy objects by application of a small force over a relatively long distance. Some familiar examples of the inclined plane are mountain highways and a loading ramp on the back of a moving truck. When weighing a small airplane, like a Cessna 172, an inclined plane, or ramp, can be used to get the airplane on the scales by pushing it, rather than jacking it. A ramp can be seen in Figure 5-17, where a Cessna 172 right main gear is sitting on an electronic scale.

The airplane was pushed up the ramps to get it on the scales.

With an inclined plane, the length of the incline is the effort arm and the vertical height of the incline is the resistance arm. If the length of the incline is five times greater than the height, there will be a force advantage, or mechanical advantage, of five. The Mooney M20 in Figure 5-17 weighed 1,600 lb on the day of the weighting. The ramp it is sitting on is 6 inches tall, which is the resistance arm, and the length of the ramp is 24 inches, which is the effort arm. To calculate

the force needed to push the airplane up the ramps, use the same formula introduced earlier when levers were discussed, as follows:

Effort (E) × Effort Arm (L) = Resistance (R) × Resistance Arm (l)

E × 24 in = 1,600 lb × 6 in E = 1,600 l-b × 6 in ÷ 24 in E = 400 lb Bolts, screws, and wedges are also examples of devices that operate on the principle of the inclined plane. A bolt, for example, has a spiral thread that runs around its circumference. As the thread winds around the bolt’s circumference, it moves a vertical distance equal to the space between the threads. The circumference of the bolt is the effort arm and the distance between the threads is the resistance arm. [Figure 5-18] Based on this analysis, a fine threaded bolt, which has more threads per inch, has a greater mechanical advantage than a coarse threaded bolt.

A chisel is a good example of a wedge. A chisel might be 8 inches long and only 1⁄2 inch wide, with a sharp tip and tapered sides. The 8-inch length is the effort arm and the 1⁄2- inch width is the resistance arm. This chisel would provide a force advantage, or mechanical advantage, of 16.

## 2. 中文翻译

斜面是一种简单机械，它通过在相对较长的距离上施加一个较小的力，使重物的升高或降低变得容易。斜面的一些常见例子有山区公路和厢式货车尾部的装卸坡道。称量小型飞机（如 Cessna 172）时，可以用斜面（即坡道）把飞机推上磅秤，而不必用千斤顶顶起。图 5-17 中可以看到一块坡道，一架 Cessna 172 的右主起落架正停在一台电子秤上。

这架飞机是被推上坡道、从而上到磅秤上的。

对于斜面，斜面的长度就是施力臂，斜面的垂直高度就是阻力臂。如果斜面长度是高度的五倍，就会得到五倍的力优势，即机械利益为 5。图 5-17 中的 Mooney M20 在称重当天的重量为 1,600 lb。它所停的坡道高 6 in，这 6 in 就是阻力臂；坡道长度为 24 in，这 24 in 就是施力臂。要计算……

……把飞机推上坡道所需的力，可使用前面讨论杠杆时介绍的同一公式，如下：

施力（E）× 施力臂（L）= 阻力（R）× 阻力臂（l）

E × 24 in = 1,600 lb × 6 in
E = 1,600 l-b × 6 in ÷ 24 in
E = 400 lb
螺栓、螺钉和楔块也是依据斜面原理工作的装置实例。以螺栓为例，它有一道绕其周向盘旋的螺纹。螺纹绕螺栓周向盘旋一周时，其上升的垂直距离等于两相邻螺纹之间的间距。螺栓的周长是施力臂，两螺纹之间的间距是阻力臂。据此分析，每英寸牙数更多的细牙螺栓，其机械利益大于粗牙螺栓。

凿子是楔块的一个好例子。一把凿子可能长 8 in、宽仅 1/2 in，尖端锋利、两侧逐渐收窄。8 in 的长度是施力臂，1/2 in 的宽度是阻力臂。这把凿子可提供 16 倍的力优势，即机械利益为 16。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句 + 动名词名词化 + by 方式状语

**原句**：The inclined plane is a simple machine that facilitates the raising or lowering of heavy objects by application of a small force over a relatively long distance.

**拆解**：that facilitates… 为限制性定语从句修饰 a simple machine；facilitates 后接名词化的动名词短语 the raising or lowering of heavy objects；by application of a small force over a relatively long distance 为方式状语（by + 名词）。

**要点**：facilitate 后只接名词或动名词，不能接不定式（× facilitate to raise），这是技术写作高频错误。英文倾向名词化（the raising of…），汉语则还原成动词「把重物升高或降下」更自然。by + 动作名词 是「通过……方式」的紧凑表达。

### ② as 引导伴随状语从句 + 形容词短语后置

**原句**：As the thread winds around the bolt’s circumference, it moves a vertical distance equal to the space between the threads.

**拆解**：As… 引导时间（伴随）状语从句；主句 it moves a vertical distance…；equal to the space between the threads 为形容词短语后置修饰 distance（= which is equal to…）。

**要点**：as 表「随着／当……时」，技术文中常配一般现在时表规律。后置的 equal to… 相当于压缩的定语从句，汉语须提到名词前：「上升一段等于螺距的垂直距离」。wind（wound）此处意为「缠绕」，不是「风」；bolt 的直译是螺栓，thread 指螺纹。

### ③ 主谓被定语从句隔开 + 比较级

**原句**：Based on this analysis, a fine threaded bolt, which has more threads per inch, has a greater mechanical advantage than a coarse threaded bolt.

**拆解**：Based on this analysis 为过去分词短语作状语；主语 a fine threaded bolt 与谓语 has 之间插入非限制性定语从句 which has more threads per inch；宾语 a greater mechanical advantage than… 为比较级 + than 从句（than 后省略谓语 has）。

**要点**：插入定语从句造成主谓分离，读句要跨过逗号找到第二个 has。fine / coarse 在螺纹术语里分别是细牙／粗牙，不是「好／粗糙」。比较级 greater 与 than 成对出现，中间夹一个名词短语是常见干扰。
