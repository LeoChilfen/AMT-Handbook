# General Gas Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · General Gas Law · 原文 PDF 第 175 页

## 1. 原文

By combining Boyle’s and Charles’ laws, a single expression can be derived which states all the information contained in both. The formula which is used to express the general gas law is as follows:

=Temperature 1 TemperaturePressure 2 (V olume 2)Pressure 1 (V olume 1)

or P1 (V1) (T2) = P2 (V2) (T1)

When using the general gas law formula, temperature and pressure must be in the absolute.

Example: 20 ft 3 of the gas argon is compressed to 15 ft 3.

The gas starts out at a temperature of 60 °F and a pressure of 1,000 psig. After being compressed, its temperature is 90 °F.

What would its new pressure be in psig?

60 degrees Fahrenheit = 520 degrees Rankine 90 degrees Fahrenheit = 550 degrees Rankine 1,000 psig + 14.7 = 1,014.7 psia P1 (V1) (T2) = P2 (V2) (T1)

1,014.7 (20) (550) = P2 (15) (520)

P2 = 1,431 psia P2 = 1,416.3 psig

## 4. 语法解析

### ① By combining … can be derived which …（方式状语 + 被动 + 分隔定语从句）

**原句**：By combining Boyle’s and Charles’ laws, a single expression can be derived which states all the information contained in both.

**拆解**：By combining Boyle’s and Charles’ laws 是「介词 by + 动名词短语」作方式状语；主句 a single expression can be derived 为被动结构（情态动词 + be + 过去分词）；which states all the information contained in both 是定语从句，先行词 expression 与从句被谓语 can be derived 隔开；contained in both 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 information。

**要点**：技术文本讲「把 A 与 B 合并即可得 C」时，习惯把 By + 动名词放句首、主句用被动，译作「结合……可以导出……」。这里的定语从句属于分隔定语从句，读长句时先抓主谓（expression … can be derived），再回头处理 which；contained in both 是「被包含在两者之中的」，不是谓语动词，别误读成 states … contained 两个并列谓语。

### ② When using …, … must be in the absolute（连词 + 分词的时间状语）

**原句**：When using the general gas law formula, temperature and pressure must be in the absolute.

**拆解**：When using the general gas law formula 是「连词 when + 现在分词」，相当于时间状语从句 When one uses the general gas law formula 的省略形式；主句 temperature and pressure must be in the absolute 中，must be 后接介词短语 in the absolute 作表语。

**要点**：「连词 + 分词」作状语时，分词的逻辑主语应与主句主语一致；严格讲这句应写 When the formula is used，手册采用了技术文献中常见的宽松写法。in the absolute 是固定短语（绝对温标／绝对压力），定冠词 the 不可省——写成 in absolute 就变成副词「绝对地」，句意全失。同类：in the open（露天）、in the dry（干燥处）。

**要点**：be used to do 是「被用来做某事」，to 为不定式符号；be used to doing 才是「习惯于做某事」，to 为介词——这是机务英语里高频的混淆点。as follows 是固定说法，无论后面列举一项还是多项都用 follows，不能写成 as following。
