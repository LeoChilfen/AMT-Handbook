# Aerodynamic Heating

> 通用 Ch 05 · Aerodynamic Heating · 原文 PDF 第 199 页

## 1. 原文

One of the problems with airplanes and high-speed flight is the heat that builds up on the airplane’s surface because of air friction. When the SR-71 Blackbird airplane is cruising at Mach 3.5, skin temperatures on its surface range from 450 °F to over 1,000 °F. To withstand this high temperature, the airplane was constructed of titanium alloy, instead of the traditional aluminum alloy. The supersonic transport Concorde was originally designed to cruise at Mach 2.2, but its cruise speed was reduced to Mach 2.0 because of structural problems that started to occur because of aerodynamic heating. If airplanes capable of hypersonic flight are going to be built in the future, one of the obstacles that will have to be overcome is the stress on the airplane’s structure caused by heat.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机高速飞行的问题之一，是由于空气摩擦而在飞机表面积聚起来的热量。SR-71「黑鸟」以马赫数 3.5 巡航时，其表面蒙皮温度在 450 °F 至 1,000 °F 以上。为承受这样的高温，该机用钛合金而不是传统的铝合金制造。超声速运输机「协和」最初设计为以马赫数 2.2 巡航，但由于气动加热开始引起结构问题，其巡航速度被降到马赫数 2.0。如果将来要制造能作高超声速飞行的飞机，必须克服的障碍之一就是热对飞机结构造成的应力。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① that 定语从句 + because of

**原句**：One of the problems with airplanes and high-speed flight is the heat that builds up on the airplane's surface because of air friction.

**拆解**：主语 One of the problems with airplanes and high-speed flight；谓语 is；表语 the heat，其后 that builds up on the airplane's surface 是定语从句，because of air friction 是原因状语。

**要点**：one of the + 复数名词作主语时谓语用单数（is），因为中心词是 one。build up 意为「积聚、逐渐累积」，在热学语境中指热量累积，不要译成「建造」；because of 是介词短语，后接名词，若接句子要用 because。

### ② 不定式作目的状语 + instead of

**原句**：To withstand this high temperature, the airplane was constructed of titanium alloy, instead of the traditional aluminum alloy.

**拆解**：句首 To withstand this high temperature 是不定式短语作目的状语；主句 the airplane was constructed of titanium alloy 为被动；instead of the traditional aluminum alloy 是介词短语作补充，表示「而不是」。

**要点**：be constructed of / from 表示「用……制造」，of 强调材料本身未改变化学性质（金属），from 多用于经过加工的原料。instead of 是介词，后接名词；instead 是副词，不能这样用。目的状语提到句首，是为了先交代「为什么换材料」这一逻辑重点。

### ③ be going to 被动 + 定语从句 + 过去分词后置定语

**原句**：If airplanes capable of hypersonic flight are going to be built in the future, one of the obstacles that will have to be overcome is the stress on the airplane's structure caused by heat.

**拆解**：If 引导条件从句，主语 airplanes 后接形容词短语 capable of hypersonic flight 作定语，谓语 are going to be built（be going to + 被动）；主句主语 one of the obstacles，其后 that will have to be overcome 是定语从句；表语 the stress on the airplane's structure caused by heat，caused by heat 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

**要点**：capable of… 作后置定语等于 that is capable of…；overcome 的过去分词仍是 overcome（不加 -ed），have to be overcome 意为「必须被克服」。caused by heat 修饰的是 stress 而不是紧挨着的 structure，靠「分词短语修饰最近的中心名词」这一紧邻原则判断。
