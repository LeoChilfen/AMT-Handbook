# Rolling Friction

> 通用 Ch 05 · Rolling Friction · 原文 PDF 第 156 页

## 1. 原文

Resistance to motion is greatly reduced if an object is mounted on wheels or rollers. The force of friction for objects mounted on wheels or rollers is called rolling friction.

This force may be computed by the same equation used in computing sliding friction, but the values of “m” will be much smaller. For example, the value of “m” for rubber tires on concrete or macadam is about 0.02. The value of “m” for roller bearings is very small, usually ranging from 0.001 to 0.003 and is often disregarded.

Example: An aircraft with a gross weight of 79,600 lb is towed over a concrete ramp. What force must be exerted by the towing vehicle to keep the airplane rolling after once set in motion?

F = mN = 0.02 mu × 79,600 lb = 1,592 lb

## 2. 中文翻译

如果把物体装在车轮或滚子上，运动阻力会大大减小。装在车轮或滚子上的物体所受的摩擦力称为滚动摩擦。

该力可用计算滑动摩擦的同一公式计算，但「m」的数值要小得多。例如，橡胶轮胎在混凝土或碎石路面上的「m」值约为 0.02。滚子轴承的「m」值非常小，通常在 0.001 到 0.003 之间，常可忽略不计（原文中该符号为希腊字母 μ，OCR 压平后显示为 m）。

例：一架总重 79,600 lb 的航空器被拖过混凝土道面。一旦起动之后，牵引车必须施加多大的力才能使飞机保持滚动？

F = mN = 0.02 mu × 79,600 lb = 1,592 lb

## 4. 语法解析

### ① if 引导的真实条件句 + 双被动

**原句**：Resistance to motion is greatly reduced if an object is mounted on wheels or rollers.

**拆解**：主句 Resistance to motion is greatly reduced（被动，is reduced 前的 greatly 修饰程度）；if 从句 an object is mounted on wheels or rollers 也是被动；if 在此表真实条件（只要满足就会出现该结果），因此不用虚拟语气。

**要点**：条件句主将从现：从句用一般现在时、主句用一般现在时或将来时。be mounted on 是「安装在……上」的固定搭配，on 不可换成 in。reduced 在此不是「减少到」而是「减小」，程度副词 greatly / substantially 常与之搭配。

### ② 过去分词短语作定语 + used in + 动名词

**原句**：This force may be computed by the same equation used in computing sliding friction, but the values of “m” will be much smaller.

**拆解**：前一分句为情态被动 may be computed by the same equation；used in computing sliding friction 是过去分词短语作 equation 的后置定语（= which is used in computing …）；but 后接并列分句，much smaller 为比较级。

**要点**：used in doing sth = 「用于做某事」，in 后必须接动名词；若写成 used to compute 则侧重目的而非用途，两者都可但语感不同。过去分词作定语时，单个分词前置、短语后置，此处因带 in computing … 只能后置。

### ③ 疑问句中的情态被动 + keep + 宾语 + 现在分词

**原句**：What force must be exerted by the towing vehicle to keep the airplane rolling after once set in motion?

**拆解**：特殊疑问句，疑问词 What force 作主语；谓语 must be exerted（情态被动），by the towing vehicle 引出施动者；to keep the airplane rolling 是目的状语，keep + 宾语 + 现在分词表示「使某物持续保持某状态」；after once set in motion 是省略了 it is 的时间状语。

**要点**：keep the airplane rolling 不能写成 keep the airplane to roll，因为 keep 后接宾语补足语时用分词表持续状态。after once set in motion 中的 once 表「一旦」，与 after 叠加是习惯写法；补全应为 after it is once set in motion。

### ④ 现在分词短语作伴随状语

**原句**：The value of “m” for roller bearings is very small, usually ranging from 0.001 to 0.003 and is often disregarded.

**拆解**：主句主语 The value of “m” for roller bearings；谓语部分由 is very small 与 is often disregarded 并列；usually ranging from 0.001 to 0.003 是现在分词短语作伴随／补充说明（= which ranges from …）。

**要点**：range from A to B 表示数值区间，主语通常是数值、温度、价格等。用分词短语补充数值范围，比另起一句 which ranges … 更紧凑；注意 and 连接的是两个谓语 is … and is …，不要漏掉第二个 is。
