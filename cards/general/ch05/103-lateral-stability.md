# Lateral Stability

> 通用 Ch 05 · Lateral Stability · 原文 PDF 第 192 页

## 1. 原文

Lateral stability of an airplane takes place around the longitudinal axis, which is from the airplane’s nose to its tail. If one wing is lower than the other, good lateral stability will tend to bring the wings back to a level flight attitude.

One design characteristic that tends to give an airplane good lateral stability is called dihedral. Dihedral is an upward wing angle, with respect to the horizontal, and it is usually just a few degrees.

Imagine a low wing airplane with a few degrees of dihedral experiencing a disruption of its flightpath such that the left wing drops. When the left wing drops, this will cause the airplane to experience a sideslip toward the low wing. The sideslip causes the low wing to experience a higher angle of attack, which increases its lift and raises it back to a level flight attitude. The dihedral on a wing is shown in Figure 5-63.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机的横向稳定性绕纵轴起作用，纵轴由机头贯通至机尾。若一侧机翼低于另一侧，良好的横向稳定性会使两翼回到平飞姿态。

有一种有助于飞机获得良好横向稳定性的设计特性，称为上反角。上反角是机翼相对水平面向上翘起的角度，通常仅有几度。

设想一架具有几度上反角的下单翼飞机，其飞行轨迹受到扰动，以致左翼下沉。左翼一旦下沉，飞机便朝低翼一侧发生侧滑。侧滑使低翼一侧的迎角增大，从而增大其升力，将其抬回平飞姿态。机翼上的上反角见图 5-63。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① take place around（不及物动词短语 + 介词）

**原句**：Lateral stability of an airplane takes place around the longitudinal axis, which is from the airplane’s nose to its tail.

**拆解**：主语 Lateral stability of an airplane，谓语 takes place（发生、起作用），around the longitudinal axis 是范围状语；which is from … to … 是非限制性定语从句，补充说明纵轴的走向。

**要点**：take place 是不及物动词短语，没有被动语态（× is taken place）。技术文本里 take place around … 常译作「绕……起作用」，而不是「在……举行」。定语从句里 from … to … 描述的是轴的起止，不是位置。

### ② with respect to 插入语

**原句**：Dihedral is an upward wing angle, with respect to the horizontal, and it is usually just a few degrees.

**拆解**：主句是并列结构：Dihedral is an upward wing angle … and it is usually just a few degrees；with respect to the horizontal 是插入的介词短语作状语，点明角度的基准面。

**要点**：with respect to ＝ relative to，意为「相对于」，用来交代基准，工程文本里极常见。插入语前后用逗号隔开，读句子时先跳过它抓住主句，再回头补上基准。upward 修饰 angle，指「向上翘的」。

### ③ such that 结果状语从句 + imagine … doing

**原句**：Imagine a low wing airplane with a few degrees of dihedral experiencing a disruption of its flightpath such that the left wing drops.

**拆解**：祈使句 Imagine + 宾语 a low wing airplane；with a few degrees of dihedral 是介词短语后置修饰；experiencing … 是现在分词短语作宾语补足语（＝which is experiencing）；such that 引导结果状语从句 the left wing drops。

**要点**：imagine sb/sth doing 是「设想……正在做」，分词表主动、进行，不能换成 to do。such that 表「以致使……」，前面接名词或短语；与 so that（表目的，「以便」）要分清。drop 在此是不及物动词「下沉、掉高度」。
