## 1. 原文

Sound, in reference to airplanes and their movement through the air, is nothing more than pressure disturbances in the air. As discussed earlier in this chapter, it is like dropping a rock in the water and watching the waves flow out from the center. As an airplane flies through the air, every point on the airplane that causes a disturbance creates sound energy in the form of pressure waves. ## 4. 语法解析

### ① nothing more than 强调结构 + 插入语

**原句**：Sound, in reference to airplanes and their movement through the air, is nothing more than pressure disturbances in the air.

**拆解**：主语 Sound 与谓语 is 之间插入 in reference to airplanes and their movement through the air（就……而言）作范围限定；表语是 nothing more than pressure disturbances in the air，nothing more than = 「不过是、无非是」。

**要点**：nothing more than / nothing but 用于把复杂概念还原为简单本质，是科普式定义句的常见写法。in reference to 是「就……而言、在……语境下」，不要译成「参考」。主语与谓语被插入语隔开，读时先抓住 Sound is。

### ② it is like + 动名词并列

**原句**：it is like dropping a rock in the water and watching the waves flow out from the center.

**拆解**：it 指前句的 sound；is like 后接两个并列的动名词短语 dropping a rock in the water 与 watching the waves flow out from the center；watch + 宾语 + 原形（flow）表示观察动作全过程。

**要点**：like 在此是介词，后接名词或动名词，不能接不定式，写成 it is like to drop 是错的。watch / see / hear 后接不带 to 的不定式表示看到、听到动作全过程，接 -ing 则强调动作正在进行，这里用 flow 强调波纹自始至终地扩散。

### ③ as 时间状语从句 + that 定语从句 + 主谓分隔

**原句**：As an airplane flies through the air, every point on the airplane that causes a disturbance creates sound energy in the form of pressure waves.

**拆解**：As 引导时间状语从句（随着……）；主句主语 every point on the airplane，其后 that causes a disturbance 是定语从句，谓语 creates 被定语从句与主语隔开；in the form of pressure waves 是方式状语。

**要点**：主语后带定语从句时，谓语要与主语 every point（单数）一致，用 creates 而不是 create。in the form of 意为「以……的形式」，是说明能量、信号载体的高频搭配，可迁移到 in the form of heat / of a shock wave。
