# Kinetic Energy

> 通用 Ch 05 · Kinetic Energy · 原文 PDF 第 153 页

## 1. 原文

Kinetic energy is defined as being energy that is in motion.

An airplane rolling down the runway or a rotating flywheel on an engine are both examples of kinetic energy. Kinetic energy has the same units as potential energy, namely foot- pounds or inch-pounds. To calculate the kinetic energy for something in motion, the following formula is used:

Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 Mass × Velocity2 To use the formula, we will show the mass as weight divided by gravity and the velocity of the object will be in feet per second. This is necessary to end up with units in foot-pounds.

Example: An Airbus A380 weighing 600,000 lb is moving down the runway on its takeoff roll with a velocity of 200 fps.

How many foot-pounds of kinetic energy does the airplane possess? [Figure 5-3]

Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 Mass × Velocity2 Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 × 600,000 ÷ 32.2 × 2002 KE = 372,670,000 ft-lb

## 2. 中文翻译

动能（kinetic energy）被定义为运动中的能量。

沿跑道滑跑的飞机，或发动机上旋转的飞轮，都是动能的例子。动能与势能具有相同的单位，即英尺-磅或英寸-磅。要计算运动物体的动能，使用下列公式：

Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 Mass × Velocity2（原文上标被压平：Velocity2 即 Velocity²，1⁄2 为二分之一。）使用该公式时，我们将质量表示为重量除以重力，物体的速度以英尺每秒计。这是为了使最终结果具有英尺-磅的单位。

例：一架重 600,000 lb 的空客 A380 正以 200 fps 的速度沿跑道进行起飞滑跑。

该飞机具有多少英尺-磅的动能？（图 5-3）

Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 Mass × Velocity2 Kinetic Energy = 1⁄2 × 600,000 ÷ 32.2 × 2002 KE = 372,670,000 ft-lb（原文上标被压平：2002 即 200²。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词短语作后置定语 + both

**原句**：An airplane rolling down the runway or a rotating flywheel on an engine are both examples of kinetic energy.

**拆解**：主语由 or 连接的两部分构成：an airplane rolling down the runway（rolling 为现在分词后置定语）or a rotating flywheel on an engine（rotating 同样作定语）；are both examples of kinetic energy 中 both 复指前面两项。

**要点**：rolling / rotating 与所修饰名词是主动关系（飞机自己在滑跑、飞轮自己在转），故用现在分词；改成 which rolls / which rotates 意思不变。主语由 or 连接时，谓语通常与最近的一项一致，但此处两项合起来是复数概念，原文用 are both 强调「两者都算」。

### ② 不定式作目的状语 + show … as …

**原句**：To use the formula, we will show the mass as weight divided by gravity and the velocity of the object will be in feet per second.

**拆解**：To use the formula 是目的状语；主句由 and 连接两个分句：we will show the mass as weight divided by gravity（show A as B，divided by gravity 为过去分词短语修饰 weight）和 the velocity of the object will be in feet per second。

**要点**：科技英语用不定式置于句首表目的，比 in order to 简洁。show A as B 意为「把 A 表示为 B」，被动式为 A is shown as B。divided by 是「除以」的固定表达；注意此处两个分句里前一个是动作、后一个是状态，翻译时不必强求结构对仗。

### ③ the same … as … 同级比较

**原句**：Kinetic energy has the same units as potential energy, namely foot- pounds or inch-pounds.

**拆解**：has the same units as potential energy 是 the same … as … 结构，表示「与……相同的……」；namely 引出同位语，具体说明 units 是哪些。

**要点**：the same … as … 后接比较对象，as 不能换成 that；若用 the same … that … 则强调「同一个」而非「同一类」。namely / i.e. 在技术文本中常用来把抽象名词落到具体单位或数值上，译作「即」。
