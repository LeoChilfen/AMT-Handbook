# Second Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · Second Law · 原文 PDF 第 166 页

## 1. 原文

Bodies in motion have the property called momentum. A body that has great momentum has a strong tendency to remain in motion and is therefore hard to stop. For example, a train moving at even low velocity is difficult to stop because of its large mass. Newton’s second law applies to this property.

When a force acts upon a body, the momentum of that body is changed. The rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force. Based on Newton’s second law, the formula for calculating thrust is derived, which states that force equals mass times acceleration (F = MA). Earlier in this chapter, it was determined that mass equals weight divided by gravity, and acceleration equals velocity final minus velocity initial divided by time. Putting all these concepts together, the formula for thrust is:

Force = Gravity (Time)Weight (Velocity final − Velocity initial)

Force = GtW (Vf − Vi)

Example: A turbojet engine is moving 150 lb of air per second through the engine. The air enters going 100 fps and leaves going 1,200 fps. How much thrust, in pounds, is the engine creating?

F = 32.2(1)150 (1200 − 100)

F = 5,124 lb of thrust

Vector C = Movement of airplane Vector A = Velocity of airplane Vector B = Wind

## 2. 中文翻译

运动的物体具有一种称为动量的属性。动量大的物体保持运动的倾向很强，因此难以使其停止。例如，即使速度很低的列车也很难停下，因为它的质量很大。牛顿第二定律阐述的正是这一属性。

当力作用于物体时，该物体的动量就会改变。动量变化的速率与所施加的力成正比。根据牛顿第二定律可以推导出计算推力的公式，即力等于质量乘以加速度（F = MA）。本章前面已经确定：质量等于重量除以重力加速度，加速度等于末速度减初速度再除以时间。把上述概念合在一起，就得到推力的公式：

Force = Gravity (Time)Weight (Velocity final − Velocity initial)（原书此式原为上下排列的分式，OCR 压平成一行；其分子为 Weight (Velocity final − Velocity initial)，分母为 Gravity (Time)。）

Force = GtW (Vf − Vi)（此式是上一式改用符号书写，被压平的斜杠关系读作 F = W(Vf − Vi) ÷ (G × t)。）

例题：一台涡喷发动机每秒使 150 lb 的空气流过发动机。空气以 100 fps 进入，以 1,200 fps 排出。这台发动机产生多少磅的推力？

F = 32.2(1)150 (1200 − 100)（同样是被压平的分式，实为 F = 150 × (1,200 − 100) ÷ (32.2 × 1)。）

F = 5,124 lb of thrust（推力为 5,124 lb。）

矢量 C = 飞机的位移；矢量 A = 飞机的速度；矢量 B = 风。（此处为图内文字）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词短语作状语：Putting all these concepts together

**原句**：Putting all these concepts together, the formula for thrust is:

**拆解**：句首 Putting … together 是现在分词短语作方式状语，逻辑主语应是推导公式的人，而主句主语是 the formula；两者不一致，属技术文本里常见的悬垂分词（dangling participle）。

**要点**：这类「分词短语 + 主句」在手册中用来承接上文、交代推导步骤，可译作「把……合在一起」。但严格来说，分词的动作必须由主句主语发出；正式写作中应改为 When all these concepts are put together, … 或 Put all these concepts together, and the formula is …。读手册时遇到悬垂分词，按「接着上一步」理解即可，不要硬把它译成主语的动作。

### ② 被动语态 + 非限制性定语从句：is derived, which states that …

**原句**：Based on Newton’s second law, the formula for calculating thrust is derived, which states that force equals mass times acceleration (F = MA).

**拆解**：Based on … second law 是过去分词短语作状语（based on = 以……为依据）；主语 the formula for calculating thrust；谓语 is derived 为一般现在时被动；which states that … 是非限制性定语从句，which 指代 the formula，从句内 that 引导宾语从句。

**要点**：科技英语常隐去施动者，用被动突出公式本身。which 前有逗号，说明是补充说明而非限定：若改成限制性从句，意思会变成「只有由第二定律推出的那个公式才……」，与原文不符。for calculating 中 for 后接动名词，表示用途。

### ③ 不定式作定语与主动形式表被动：tendency to remain / hard to stop

**原句**：A body that has great momentum has a strong tendency to remain in motion and is therefore hard to stop.

**拆解**：主语 A body + that has great momentum 定语从句；谓语由 and 连接两部分：has a strong tendency to remain in motion 与 is therefore hard to stop。to remain 是修饰 tendency 的不定式定语；hard to stop 中 stop 用主动形式表被动意义（被停下来）。

**要点**：tendency to do 是固定搭配，不能写成 tendency of doing。hard / easy / difficult to do 结构中，主语就是不定式的逻辑宾语，所以用主动不定式：the train is hard to stop，而不是 hard to be stopped。therefore 是副词，不能像连词那样直接连接两个句子，这里靠 and 连接谓语才成立。
