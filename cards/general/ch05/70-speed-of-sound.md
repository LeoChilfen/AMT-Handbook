# Speed of Sound

> 通用 Ch 05 · Speed of Sound · 原文 PDF 第 181 页

## 1. 原文

In any uniform medium, under given physical conditions, the sound travels at a definite speed. In some substances, the velocity of sound is higher than in others. Even in the same medium under different conditions of temperature, pressure, and so forth, the velocity of sound varies. Density and elasticity of a medium are the two basic physical properties which govern the velocity of sound.

In general, a difference in density between two substances is sufficient to indicate which one will be the faster transmission medium for sound. For example, sound travels faster through water than it does through air at the same temperature. However, there are some surprising exceptions to this rule of thumb. An outstanding example among these exceptions involves comparison of the speed of sound in lead and aluminum at the same temperature. Sound travels at 16,700 fps in aluminum at 20 °C, and only 4,030 fps in lead at 20 °C, despite the fact that lead is much denser than aluminum. The reason for such exceptions is found in the fact, mentioned above, that sound velocity depends on elasticity as well as density.

Using density as a rough indication of the speed of sound in a given substance, it can be stated as a general rule that sound travels fastest in solid materials, slower in liquids, and slowest in gases. The velocity of sound in air at 0 °C (32 °F)

is 1,087 fps and increases by 2 fps for each Centigrade degree of temperature rise, or 1.1 fps for each degree Fahrenheit.

## 2. 中文翻译

在任何均匀介质中，在给定的物理条件下，声音以确定的速度传播。在某些物质中，声速高于另一些物质。即使在同一介质中，温度、压力等条件不同，声速也会变化。介质的密度和弹性是支配声速的两项基本物理性质。

一般来说，两种物质之间的密度差异，足以表明其中哪一种将是声音传播更快的介质。例如，在相同温度下，声音在水中比在空气中传播得快。不过，这条经验法则也有一些令人意外的例外。其中突出的一个例子，是比较相同温度下铅与铝中的声速：在 20 °C 时，声音在铝中的速度为 16,700 fps，而在铅中只有 4,030 fps，尽管铅的密度远大于铝。出现这类例外的原因，就在于上面提到的事实：声速既取决于密度，也取决于弹性。

把密度作为给定物质中声速的粗略指标，可以给出一条通则：声音在固体材料中传播最快，在液体中较慢，在气体中最慢。0 °C（32 °F）时空气中的声速

为 1,087 fps，且温度每升高 1 摄氏度即增加 2 fps，也就是每升高 1 华氏度增加 1.1 fps。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① sound travels faster through water than it does through air（比较从句中用 does 替代）

**原句**：For example, sound travels faster through water than it does through air at the same temperature.

**拆解**：主句 sound travels faster through water；than 引导比较从句 it does through air at the same temperature，其中 does 替代 travels（避免重复），it 指 sound；at the same temperature 是条件性状语，说明比较在同一温度下进行。

**要点**：比较从句里用 do/does/did 替代前面的实义动词，是英语避免重复的标准手段，译时直接把动词补回（「比在空气中传播得快」）。faster 修饰 travels，不能写成 more fast；比较的两端必须同类——这里是 through water 与 through air 两个介词短语相比，不是 water 与 air 相比。

### ② despite the fact that lead is much denser than aluminum（despite the fact that + 同位语从句）

**原句**：Sound travels at 16,700 fps in aluminum at 20 °C, and only 4,030 fps in lead at 20 °C, despite the fact that lead is much denser than aluminum.

**拆解**：主句为两个并列的 travel 分句（第二个省略了 travels，只留 at only 4,030 fps）；despite the fact that … 是让步状语，the fact 后接 that 引导的同位语从句，much 修饰比较级 denser。

**要点**：despite / in spite of 是介词，只能接名词，所以要接从句必须借助 the fact that；写成 despite that 或 despite of 都是错的（although 才直接接从句）。much、far、a lot 修饰比较级，不能加 more（much denser 正确，more denser 错误）。这句的让步与前面的例外呼应：密度大本该声速快，但铅比铝慢。

### ③ the two basic physical properties which govern the velocity of sound（定语从句 + 主谓一致）

**原句**：Density and elasticity of a medium are the two basic physical properties which govern the velocity of sound.

**拆解**：主语 Density and elasticity of a medium 为并列主语，谓语用复数 are；表语 the two basic physical properties；which govern the velocity of sound 是定语从句修饰 properties，从句谓语 govern 用复数（先行词是复数）。

**要点**：由 and 连接的并列主语一律按复数处理（Density and elasticity … are），容易出错的是把 of a medium 误当主语中心词而用 is。定语从句的谓语要跟先行词一致，不能跟主句谓语一致——此处主句 are、从句 govern 都是复数，只是巧合。可迁移：weight and balance are …、stress and strain are …。
