# Absolute Pressure

> 通用 Ch 05 · Absolute Pressure · 原文 PDF 第 173 页

## 1. 原文

A gauge that includes atmospheric pressure in its reading is measuring what is known as absolute pressure, or psia.

Absolute pressure is equal to gauge pressure plus atmospheric pressure. If someone hooked up a psia indicating instrument to an engine’s oil system, the gauge would read atmospheric pressure when the engine was not running. Since this would not make good sense to the typical operator, psia gauges are not used in this type of application. For the manifold pressure on a piston engine, a psia gauge does make good sense. Manifold pressure on a piston engine can read anywhere from less than atmospheric pressure if the engine is not supercharged, to more than atmospheric if it is supercharged. The only gauge that has the flexibility to show this variety of readings is the absolute pressure gauge. Figure 5-35 shows a manifold pressure gauge, with a readout that ranges from 10 "Hg to 35 "Hg. Remember that 29.92 "Hg is standard day atmospheric.

## 2. 中文翻译

若某仪表的读数把大气压力也计算在内，它测量的就是所谓的绝对压力，即 psia。

绝对压力等于表压加大气压力。若把一只 psia 指示仪表接到发动机的滑油系统上，发动机不运转时该表会读作大气压力。由于这对普通操作者来说不合常理，所以这类应用中不使用 psia 表。对于活塞发动机的进气歧管压力，psia 表却非常合理。活塞发动机的进气歧管压力，在发动机不增压时可低于大气压力，在增压时又可高于大气压力，读数范围很宽。能灵活显示这种宽范围读数的仪表，只有绝对压力表。图 5-35 所示为一具进气歧管压力表，其读数范围为 10 "Hg 至 35 "Hg。请记住，29.92 "Hg 是标准日大气压力。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① what is known as …（名词性从句作宾语）

**原句**：A gauge that includes atmospheric pressure in its reading is measuring what is known as absolute pressure, or psia.

**拆解**：主语 A gauge，后接 that includes atmospheric pressure in its reading 定语从句；谓语 is measuring（现在进行时）；宾语是 what is known as absolute pressure 这一名词性从句（what = the thing which），or psia 为同位语。

**要点**：what is known as / what is called 是技术文献给术语下定义的固定句式，译作「所谓的……」。what 在这里既作 known as 的主语，又充当整个从句的连接词，不能换成 that；也不能省掉 as。

### ② 与现在事实相反的虚拟条件句

**原句**：If someone hooked up a psia indicating instrument to an engine’s oil system, the gauge would read atmospheric pressure when the engine was not running.

**拆解**：If 从句用一般过去时 hooked up（虚拟），主句用 would read（would + 动词原形）；when the engine was not running 是主句内的时间状语从句；a psia indicating instrument 中 psia indicating 是名词作定语修饰 instrument。

**要点**：这句是「假如有人这样接线」的假想，事实上并没有这样用，所以从句用过去时、主句用 would；若写成 If someone hooks up …, the gauge will read …，就变成真会发生的事，与后文「所以这类应用不用 psia 表」的逻辑对不上。技术文本用虚拟是为了区分假定情形与实际状况。

### ③ from … to … 表范围，中间插入 if 从句

**原句**：Manifold pressure on a piston engine can read anywhere from less than atmospheric pressure if the engine is not supercharged, to more than atmospheric if it is supercharged.

**拆解**：主语 Manifold pressure；谓语 can read；anywhere 为程度状语；from … to … 构成范围结构，from 的宾语是 less than atmospheric pressure，to 的宾语是 more than atmospheric（后省略 pressure）；中间插入 if the engine is not supercharged，句末 if it is supercharged 与之对举，it 指 the engine。

**要点**：from A to B 中间插入条件从句时，要先把 from … to … 括起来再补中间成分，否则容易把 if 从句误当成 to 的宾语。atmospheric 在此兼作名词用，等于 atmospheric pressure。can read anywhere from … to … 是技术描述中表示「在……到……范围内变化」的常用句型。
