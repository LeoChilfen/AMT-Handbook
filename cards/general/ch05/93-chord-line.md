## 1. 原文

The chord line is an imaginary straight line running from the wing’s leading edge to its trailing edge. The angle between the chord line and the longitudinal axis of the airplane is known as the angle of incidence.

Relative W ind The relative wind is a relationship between the direction of airflow and the aircraft wing. In normal flight circumstances, the relative wind is the opposite direction of the aircraft flightpath.

• If the flightpath is forward then the relative wind is backward.

• If the flightpath is forward and upward, then the relative wind is backward and downward.

• If the flightpath is forward and downward, then the relative wind is backward and upward.

Therefore, the relative wind is parallel to the flightpath, and travels in the opposite direction.

The angle between the chord line and the relative wind is the angle of attack. As the angle of attack increases, the lift on the wing increases. If the angle of attack becomes too great, the airflow can separate from the wing and the lift

## 2. 中文翻译

弦线（chord line）是一条假想的直线，从机翼的前缘（leading edge）延伸到后缘（trailing edge）。弦线与飞机纵轴（longitudinal axis）之间的夹角称为安装角（angle of incidence）。

相对风（relative wind）是气流方向与飞机机翼之间的一种关系。在正常飞行情况下，相对风的方向与飞机航迹（flightpath）的方向相反。

- 若航迹向前，则相对风向后。

- 若航迹向前并向上，则相对风向后并向下。

- 若航迹向前并向下，则相对风向后并向上。

因此，相对风与航迹平行，且方向相反。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 真实条件句 + then 的推论

**原句**：If the flightpath is forward then the relative wind is backward.

**拆解**：If 引导真实条件状语从句，用一般现在时 is；主句 the relative wind is backward 也用一般现在时；then 是副词，起「那么」的承接作用，不是连词。

**要点**：科技英语的条件句遵循「主将从现」或「主现从现」：本句主、从句都用一般现在时，表示普遍成立的关系，不含将来意义。then 可以省略，但三条情形对照并列时保留 then 能让推论关系更清楚；书面语中 If 从句后通常加逗号。

### ② 并列谓语 + be parallel to

**原句**：Therefore, the relative wind is parallel to the flightpath, and travels in the opposite direction.

**拆解**：主语 the relative wind；两个并列谓语 is parallel to the flightpath 与 travels in the opposite direction；be parallel to 是「形容词 + 介词」构成的表语结构；Therefore 是连接副词，承接上文的三种情形。

**要点**：parallel to 是固定搭配（不用 parallel with）；「朝相反方向」用 in the opposite direction，介词是 in。第二个谓语 travels 与 is 共用主语，注意不要漏掉第三人称 -s；therefore 后加逗号，是书面论证的常见标点。

### ③ as 引导「随着」的比例从句

**原句**：As the angle of attack increases, the lift on the wing increases.

**拆解**：As 引导时间／伴随状语从句，表示两个变化同步发生；主句 the lift on the wing increases。前后两个分句都用一般现在时，谓语同为 increase。

**要点**：as 表「随着」时强调同步变化，译作「随着……，……（也）随之……」；与 when（当……时，一次性事件）、while（在……期间，两事并行）有别。原文没有明说因果，译文可补「也」「随之」使语气通顺，但不能添加原文没有的因果词。
