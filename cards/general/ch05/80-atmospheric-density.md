# Atmospheric Density

> 通用 Ch 05 · Atmospheric Density · 原文 PDF 第 184 页

## 1. 原文

Since both temperature and pressure decrease with altitude, it might appear that the density of the atmosphere would remain fairly constant with increased altitude. This is not true, however, because pressure drops more rapidly with increased altitude than does the temperature. The result is that density decreases with increased altitude.

By use of the general gas law, studied earlier, it can be shown that for a particular gas, pressure and temperature determine the density. Since standard pressure and temperatures have been associated with each altitude, the density of the air at these standard temperatures and pressures must also be considered standard. Thus, a particular atmospheric density is associated with each altitude. This gives rise to the expression “density altitude,” symbolized “Hd.” A density altitude of 15,000 ft is the altitude at which the density is the same as that considered standard for 15,000 ft. Remember, however, that density altitude is not necessarily true altitude. For example, on a day when the atmospheric pressure is higher than standard and the temperature is lower than standard, the density which is standard at 10,000 ft might occur at 12,000 ft. In this case, at an actual altitude of 12,000 ft, we have air that has the same density as standard air at 10,000 ft.

Density altitude is a calculated altitude obtained by correcting pressure altitude for temperature.

## 2. 中文翻译

由于温度与压力都随高度下降，看起来大气密度似乎会随高度增加而大致保持不变。然而这并不正确，因为压力随高度增加下降得比温度更快。结果是密度随高度增加而减小。

利用前面学过的通用气体定律可以证明，对某一特定气体而言，压力与温度决定其密度。既然各高度都对应有标准压力与标准温度，那么在这些标准温度与压力下空气的密度也必须视为标准密度。因此，每一高度都对应一个特定的大气密度。由此产生了“密度高度”（density altitude）这一说法，符号为“Hd”（原文 Hd 中的 d 为下标，OCR 压平）。15,000 ft 的密度高度，就是密度与该高度所认定的标准密度相同的高度。但要记住，密度高度不一定就是真实高度。例如，某日若大气压高于标准、温度低于标准，则本应在 10,000 ft 属于标准的密度可能出现在 12,000 ft。此时，在 12,000 ft 的实际高度上，空气的密度与 10,000 ft 处标准空气的密度相同。

密度高度是用温度修正气压高度后得出的计算高度。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① since 原因从句 + it 形式主语 + would 表推测

**原句**：Since both temperature and pressure decrease with altitude, it might appear that the density of the atmosphere would remain fairly constant with increased altitude.

**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句，both ... and ... 连接两个并列主语；主句 it 是形式主语，真正主语是 that 引导的从句；从句中 would remain 表推测判断；with increased altitude 是方式状语。

**要点**：appear / seem 后可接 that 从句或不定式：it appears that ... 等于 it would appear that ...，语气更委婉。with + 过去分词短语（with increased altitude）表示「随着……增加」，比 as altitude increases 更紧凑，也是技术文本里表变量的常用手段。

### ② than 引导的比较从句部分倒装

**原句**：This is not true, however, because pressure drops more rapidly with increased altitude than does the temperature.

**拆解**：主句 This is not true；however 是插入的转折副词；because 引导原因状语从句；从句中 more rapidly ... than does the temperature 为比较结构，than 后采用部分倒装：助动词 does 提前、主语 the temperature 后置。

**要点**：比较从句中为避免头重脚轻或歧义，常倒装为 than does + 主语，等于 than the temperature does。原句省略了重复的谓语动词：than the temperature does (drop)。读句时把 does 还原成 drops，逻辑就清楚了。

### ③ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + obtained by + 动名词

**原句**：Density altitude is a calculated altitude obtained by correcting pressure altitude for temperature.

**拆解**：主语 Density altitude；系动词 is；表语 a calculated altitude；obtained by correcting ... 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which is obtained by ...；by 后接动名词 correcting。

**要点**：correct A for B 是仪表与气象领域的固定说法，意为「对 A 作 B 的修正」，如 correct the altimeter for temperature error。by + 动名词表示手段或方法，介词 by 后不能用不定式；同类结构还有 obtained by measuring、found by testing。

### ④ give rise to + 名词 + 过去分词作后置定语

**原句**：This gives rise to the expression “density altitude,” symbolized “Hd.”

**拆解**：主语 This（指「每个高度都有对应的密度」这一事实）；谓语 gives rise to；宾语 the expression “density altitude”；symbolized “Hd” 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which is symbolized “Hd”。

**要点**：give rise to 意为「引起、产生」，to 是介词，后接名词而非从句；同类还有 lead to、result in。symbolized 用过去分词，是因为术语「被」用符号表示；这种「名词短语 + 逗号 + 过去分词」的写法在定义术语时极为常见。
