# Measurement of Sound Intensity

> 通用 Ch 05 · Measurement of Sound Intensity · 原文 PDF 第 182 页

## 1. 原文

Sound intensity is measured in decibels, with a decibel being the ratio of one sound to another. One decibel (dB)

is the smallest change in sound intensity the human ear can detect. A faint whisper would have an intensity of 20 dB, and a pneumatic drill would be 80 dB. The engine on a modern jetliner, at takeoff thrust, would have a sound intensity of 90 dB when heard by someone standing 150 ft. away. A 110 dB noise, by comparison, would sound twice as loud as the jetliner’s engine. Figure 5-49 shows the sound intensity from a variety of different sources.

## 2. 中文翻译

声强以分贝计量，而分贝是一种声音与另一种声音的比值。

1 分贝（dB）是人耳所能分辨的最小声强变化。微弱耳语的声强约为 20 dB，风动钻则约为 80 dB。现代喷气客机的发动机在起飞推力下，距其 150 ft 处听到的声强约为 90 dB。相比之下，110 dB 的噪声听起来比喷气客机发动机响一倍。图 5-49 给出各种不同声源的声强。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① with 复合结构（with + 名词 + 现在分词）

**原句**：Sound intensity is measured in decibels, with a decibel being the ratio of one sound to another.

**拆解**：主句 Sound intensity is measured in decibels，in 表示计量单位；with a decibel being the ratio ... 是 with 复合结构作伴随状语，a decibel 是 being 的逻辑主语。

**要点**：be measured in + 单位是固定搭配（in decibels / in psi / in inches of mercury）。with 复合结构可在不另起一句的前提下追加解释，技术定义句常这样写；being 不能换成 is，因为 with 后面不是完整从句。

### ② 定语从句省略关系代词

**原句**：One decibel (dB) is the smallest change in sound intensity the human ear can detect.

**拆解**：主语 One decibel (dB)；系动词 is；表语 the smallest change in sound intensity；the human ear can detect 是省略了关系代词 that/which 的定语从句，修饰 change。（原文在 (dB) 与 is 之间因分页断行。）

**要点**：关系代词在定语从句中作宾语时可以省略，这里 change 正是 detect 的宾语。译成中文要把定语从句挪到名词前：「人耳所能分辨的最小声强变化」。若补全则为 that the human ear can detect。

### ③ when + 过去分词（状语从句的省略）

**原句**：The engine on a modern jetliner, at takeoff thrust, would have a sound intensity of 90 dB when heard by someone standing 150 ft. away.

**拆解**：主语 The engine on a modern jetliner；at takeoff thrust 是插入的状态状语；谓语 would have；when heard by someone ... 相当于 when it is heard by someone ...，省略了主语和 be；standing 150 ft. away 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 someone。

**要点**：when / while / if 引导的状语从句，若从句主语与主句主语相同且含 be，可省略主语和 be，只留分词（when heard / when tested / if damaged）。standing 修饰人、是主动关系，故用现在分词。

### ④ 倍数表达：倍数 + as + 原级 + as

**原句**：A 110 dB noise, by comparison, would sound twice as loud as the jetliner’s engine.

**拆解**：主语 A 110 dB noise；by comparison 为插入语；谓语 would sound（此处 sound 作系动词）；表语 twice as loud as the jetliner’s engine。

**要点**：标准倍数句式是「倍数 + as + 形容词原级 + as」，如 twice as loud as、three times as heavy as。不要写成 twice louder than（口语可见，技术文本不用），也不要与「倍数 + 比较级 + than」混用。注意 110 dB 与 90 dB 相差 20 dB，原文按主观响度说「响一倍」，与声强的对数关系并不矛盾。
