# Resonance

> 通用 Ch 05 · Resonance · 原文 PDF 第 182 页

## 1. 原文

All types of matter, regardless of whether it is a solid, liquid, or gas, have a natural frequency at which the atoms within that matter vibrate. If two pieces of matter have the same natural frequency, and one of them starts to vibrate, it can transfer its wave energy to the other one and cause it to vibrate. This transfer of energy is known as resonance. Some piston engine powered airplanes have an rpm range that they are placarded to avoid, because spinning the prop at that rpm can cause vibration problems. The difficulty lies in the natural frequency of the metal in the prop, and the frequency of vibration that will be set up with a particular tip speed for the prop. At that particular rpm, stresses can be set up that could lead to the propeller coming apart.

## 2. 中文翻译

所有类型的物质，无论其为固体、液体还是气体，都有一个固有频率，物质内部的原子以该频率振动。如果两块物质具有相同的固有频率，且其中一块开始振动，它就能把自身的波能量传递给另一块并使之一同振动。这种能量传递称为共振。某些由活塞发动机驱动的飞机有一个被挂牌明令避开的转速范围，因为在该转速下旋转螺旋桨会引起振动问题。难点在于螺旋桨金属的固有频率，以及螺旋桨在某一特定叶尖速度下所激发的振动频率。在那个特定转速下会产生应力，而该应力可能导致螺旋桨解体。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① regardless of whether + at which 定语从句

**原句**：All types of matter, regardless of whether it is a solid, liquid, or gas, have a natural frequency at which the atoms within that matter vibrate.

**拆解**：主语 All types of matter；regardless of whether ... 是插入的让步状语，of 后接 whether 引导的宾语从句；谓语 have；宾语 a natural frequency；at which the atoms within that matter vibrate 是「介词 + 关系代词」引导的定语从句，修饰 frequency。

**要点**：regardless of 后可接名词或 whether / wh- 从句，但不能接 that 从句。at which 等于 at the natural frequency，介词之所以提前，是因为 vibrate at a frequency 这一搭配；同类还有 in which、by which。插入语较长时，读句先抓主语与谓语 have a natural frequency。

### ② 定语从句中关系代词作宾语 + 动名词作从句主语

**原句**：Some piston engine powered airplanes have an rpm range that they are placarded to avoid, because spinning the prop at that rpm can cause vibration problems.

**拆解**：主语 Some piston engine powered airplanes（piston engine powered 为复合形容词作前置定语）；谓语 have；宾语 an rpm range；that they are placarded to avoid 是定语从句，that 作 avoid 的宾语；because 引导原因状语从句，从句主语是动名词短语 spinning the prop at that rpm。

**要点**：定语从句中 that 作宾语时可省略；are placarded to avoid 是「被要求挂牌避开」，主语虽写飞机，实际执行的是机组。动名词短语作主语时谓语用第三人称单数，故为 can cause 而非 can causes。

### ③ 分隔定语从句 + lead to + 动名词

**原句**：At that particular rpm, stresses can be set up that could lead to the propeller coming apart.

**拆解**：At that particular rpm 为条件状语；主句 stresses can be set up（情态动词 + 被动）；that could lead to the propeller coming apart 是定语从句，修饰 stresses，因谓语太短而被后置，避免头重脚轻；lead to 后接动名词 coming apart。

**要点**：英语允许定语从句与先行词被谓语隔开（分隔定语从句），读句时按「stresses ... that」把先行词找回来，不要误以为 that 指 rpm。lead to 的 to 是介词，后接名词或 -ing：lead to the propeller coming apart、lead to fatigue failure；写成 lead to come apart 是常见错误。
