# Wingtip Vortices

> 通用 Ch 05 · Wingtip Vortices · 原文 PDF 第 190 页

## 1. 原文

Wingtip vortices are caused by the air beneath the wing, which is at the higher pressure, flowing over the wingtip and up toward the top of the wing. The end result is a spiral or vortex that trails behind the wingtip anytime lift is being produced. This vortex is also referred to as wake turbulence, and is a significant factor in determining how closely one airplane can follow behind another on approach to land. The wake turbulence of a large airplane can cause a smaller airplane, if it is following too closely, to be thrown out of control. V ortices from the wing and from the horizontal stabilizer are quite visible on the MD-11 shown in Figure 5-59.

Upwash and downwash refer to the effect an airfoil has on the free airstream. Upwash is the deflection of the oncoming airstream, causing it to flow up and over the wing. Downwash is the downward deflection of the airstream after it has passed over the wing and is leaving the trailing edge. This downward deflection is what creates the action and reaction described under lift and Newton’s third law.

## 2. 中文翻译

翼尖涡（wingtip vortices）是由机翼下方压力较高的空气越过翼尖、向上流向机翼上表面所引起的。其最终结果是一个螺旋或涡旋，只要机翼在产生升力，它就会一直拖在翼尖后方。这个涡也称为尾流湍流（wake turbulence），是决定一架飞机在进近着陆时能跟在另一架飞机后面多近的一个重要因素。如果跟得太近，大型飞机的尾流湍流会使较小的飞机失去控制。图 5-59 所示 MD-11 上，来自机翼和来自水平安定面的涡都清晰可见。

上洗（upwash）和下洗（downwash）指的是翼型对自由气流（free airstream）的影响。上洗是迎面气流发生的偏转，使其向上流过机翼。下洗是气流流过机翼、离开后缘之后的向下偏转。正是这种向下偏转，产生了在升力与牛顿第三定律部分所述的作用与反作用。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 定语从句 + 分词补足语

**原句**：Wingtip vortices are caused by the air beneath the wing, which is at the higher pressure, flowing over the wingtip and up toward the top of the wing.

**拆解**：主句 Wingtip vortices are caused by the air beneath the wing（被动，by 引出施动者）；which is at the higher pressure 是非限制性定语从句修饰 the air；flowing over the wingtip and up toward the top of the wing 是现在分词短语，补足说明 the air 的动作，与 are caused 无关。

**要点**：这句的难点在于 by 的宾语 the air 后面挂了三层结构：介词短语 beneath the wing、定语从句、分词短语。阅读顺序是 the air →（压力较高）→（越过翼尖向上流）。分词短语在此说明 the air 的动作，可还原为 which flows over...。

### ② 介词 in + 动名词 + how 引导的宾语从句

**原句**：This vortex is also referred to as wake turbulence, and is a significant factor in determining how closely one airplane can follow behind another on approach to land.

**拆解**：两个并列谓语 is referred to as wake turbulence 与 is a significant factor...；in determining... 是「介词 + 动名词」，determining 后接 how closely one airplane can follow behind another 宾语从句；how closely 是程度疑问短语，修饰 can follow。

**要点**：factor in doing sth（在做某事方面的因素）中的 in 不能省略。how + 副词引导的宾语从句用陈述语序（one airplane can follow），不能写成 how closely can one airplane follow。closely 是副词，修饰 follow（跟得紧），不能用形容词 close。

### ③ 现在分词短语作结果状语

**原句**：Upwash is the deflection of the oncoming airstream, causing it to flow up and over the wing.

**拆解**：主句 Upwash is the deflection of the oncoming airstream；causing it to flow up and over the wing 是现在分词短语作结果状语，it 指 the oncoming airstream。

**要点**：现在分词短语置于句末并用逗号隔开，常表自然结果，相当于 which causes...。cause sb/sth to do（使……做）在这里再次出现；up and over 是「向上并越过」两个方位词并列，译成中文时按先后顺序展开。
