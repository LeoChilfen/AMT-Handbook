# Boyle’s Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · Boyle’s Law · 原文 PDF 第 174 页

## 1. 原文

As previously stated, compressibility is an outstanding characteristic of gases. The English scientist, Robert Boyle, was among the first to study this characteristic that he called the “springiness of air.” By direct measurement he discovered that when the temperature of a combined sample of gas was kept constant and the absolute pressure doubled, the volume was reduced to half the former value. As the applied absolute pressure was decreased, the resulting volume increased.

From these observations, he concluded that for a constant temperature the product of the volume and absolute pressure of an enclosed gas remains constant. Boyle’s law is normally stated: “The volume of an enclosed dry gas varies inversely with its absolute pressure, provided the temperature remains constant.” The following formula is used for Boyle’s law calculations. Remember, pressure needs to be in the absolute.

V olume 1 × Pressure 1 = V olume 2 × Pressure 2 or V1P1 = V2P2 Example: 10 ft3 of nitrogen is under a pressure of 500 psia.

If the volume is reduced to 7 ft3, what will the new pressure be? [Figure 5-37]

V 1P1 = V2P2 10 (500) = 7 (P2)

10 (500) ÷ 7 = P2 P 2 = 714.29 psia The useful applications of Boyle’s law are many and varied.

Some applications more common to aviation are: (1) the carbon dioxide (CO2) bottle used to inflate life rafts and life vests; (2) the compressed oxygen and the acetylene tanks used in welding; (3) the compressed air brakes and shock absorbers; and (4) the use of oxygen tanks for high altitude flying and emergency use.

## 2. 中文翻译

如前所述，可压缩性是气体的一项突出特性。英国科学家罗伯特·玻意耳是最早研究这一特性的人之一，他把该特性称为「空气的弹性」。通过直接测量他发现：当一定量混合气样的温度保持恒定、绝对压力加倍时，体积就减小到原值的一半。随着所施加的绝对压力降低，相应的体积增大。

根据这些观察，他得出结论：在温度恒定的情况下，密闭气体的体积与绝对压力的乘积保持不变。玻意耳定律通常表述为：「在温度保持恒定的条件下，密闭干燥气体的体积与其绝对压力成反比。」玻意耳定律的计算使用下列公式。请记住，压力必须用绝对压力。

体积 1 × 压力 1 = 体积 2 × 压力 2，或 V1P1 = V2P2。例：10 ft3 的氮气处于 500 psia 的压力下。（原文 `V olume 1` 系 OCR 在 Volume 中插入空格所致。）

若体积减小到 7 ft3，新的压力将是多少？［图 5-37］

V1P1 = V2P2
10 (500) = 7 (P2)

10 (500) ÷ 7 = P2
P2 = 714.29 psia
玻意耳定律的实用场合众多且各不相同。

航空上较常见的应用有：（1）用于给救生筏和救生衣充气的二氧化碳（CO2）瓶；（2）焊接用的压缩氧气瓶和乙炔瓶；（3）压缩空气刹车和减震器；（4）高空飞行及应急用氧瓶。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 方式状语前置 + that 宾语从句内嵌 when 从句

**原句**：By direct measurement he discovered that when the temperature of a combined sample of gas was kept constant and the absolute pressure doubled, the volume was reduced to half the former value.

**拆解**：By direct measurement 是方式状语前置（主语是代词 he，故主谓不倒装）；that 引导宾语从句；从句内含 when 引导的时间状语从句，其中 was kept constant 为被动、the absolute pressure doubled 为主动，两者并列；从句主句是 the volume was reduced to half the former value。

**要点**：方式状语提到句首在技术文本中很常见，主语为代词时主谓不倒装。reduce to half 是「减小到一半」（结果），reduce by half 是「减少了一半」（幅度），中文意思相近但介词逻辑不同。a combined sample of gas 指「一定量的混合气样」。

### ② provided (that) 引导条件从句

**原句**：“The volume of an enclosed dry gas varies inversely with its absolute pressure, provided the temperature remains constant.”

**拆解**：主语 The volume，后接 of an enclosed dry gas 作定语；谓语 varies inversely with its absolute pressure（与……成反比变化）；provided the temperature remains constant 是条件状语从句，provided 后省略了 that。

**要点**：provided / providing 作连词，意为「只要、假如」，比 if 更强调「以此为前提」，是定义定律时的标准用法，且从句用一般现在时表一般条件。vary inversely with / vary directly with 是「与……成反比／成正比」的固定搭配，不要写成 inversely proportional with。

### ③ as 引导「随着」的比例变化

**原句**：As the applied absolute pressure was decreased, the resulting volume increased.

**拆解**：As 引导时间／比例状语从句（「随着……」），从句用被动 was decreased；主句 the resulting volume increased；the resulting … 是「由此产生的……」，resulting 为现在分词作前置定语。

**要点**：as 表「随着」时，主从句都用一般过去时或一般现在时，不用 will。resulting（随之产生的，主动）与 resulted（已经导致的，完成）要分清：the resulting volume 指压力改变后「随之出现的体积」。这句与前面「压力加倍、体积减半」构成正反两面的对照。
