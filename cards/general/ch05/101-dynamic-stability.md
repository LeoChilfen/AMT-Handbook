# Dynamic Stability

> 通用 Ch 05 · Dynamic Stability · 原文 PDF 第 190 页

## 1. 原文

The dynamic stability of an airplane involves the amount of time it takes for it to react to its static stability after it has been displaced from a condition of equilibrium. Dynamic stability involves the oscillations that typically occur as the airplane tries to return to its original position or attitude. Even though an airplane may have positive static stability, it may have dynamic stability which is positive, neutral, or negative.

Imagine that an airplane in straight-and-level flight is disturbed and pitches noseup. If the airplane has positive static stability, the nose will pitch back down after the disturbance is removed. If it immediately returns to straight-

and-level flight, it is also said to have positive dynamic stability. The airplane, however, may pass through level flight and remain pitched down, and then continue the recovery process by pitching back up. This pitching up and then down is known as an oscillation. If the oscillations lessen over time, the airplane is still classified as having positive dynamic stability. If the oscillations increase over time, the airplane is classified as having negative dynamic stability.

If the oscillations remain the same over time, the airplane is classified as having neutral dynamic stability.

In view B, the displacement from equilibrium is increasing after two oscillations, and will not return to equilibrium. In view C, the displacement from equilibrium is staying the same with each oscillation.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机的动稳定性，涉及飞机偏离平衡状态之后对其静稳定性作出反应所需的时间长短。动稳定性涉及飞机在力图回到原来位置或姿态的过程中通常出现的振荡。即使一架飞机具有正静稳定性，它的动稳定性也可能是正的、中性的或负的。

设想一架作直线平飞的飞机受到扰动而机头上仰。若该飞机具有正静稳定性，则扰动消除后机头会重新下俯。若它立即恢复为直线（原文此处换页断句：straight- 与下段开头的 and-level flight 应连读为 straight-and-level flight，即「直线平飞」）

（此处为图内文字：Vertical axis（垂直轴）、Longitudinal axis（纵轴）、Lateral axis（横轴）、CG（重心），以及「axis is a matching color」字样，略）……承接上段断句处：下段开头的 and-level flight 与上段末的 straight- 合为「直线平飞」。

平飞，则也称其具有正动稳定性。然而，飞机也可能穿过平飞状态而仍保持下俯，然后再通过上仰继续其恢复过程。这种先上仰、再下俯的过程称为振荡。若振荡随时间减小，该飞机仍被归为正动稳定性；若振荡随时间增大，该飞机则被归为负动稳定性。

若振荡随时间保持不变，该飞机则被归为中性动稳定性。

图 5-61 示出动稳定性的概念。在视图 A 中，偏离平衡的位移经历三次振荡后回到平衡；在视图 B 中，偏离平衡的位移经两次振荡后仍在增大，不会回到平衡；在视图 C 中，偏离平衡的位移在每次振荡后保持不变。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① involve + 省略关系代词的时间定语从句

**原句**：The dynamic stability of an airplane involves the amount of time it takes for it to react to its static stability after it has been displaced from a condition of equilibrium.

**拆解**：主语 The dynamic stability of an airplane，谓语 involves 后接名词短语 the amount of time；it takes for it to react … 是省略了关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 time；after 引导时间状语从句，用现在完成被动 has been displaced，表示扰动先于反应发生。

**要点**：take 表「花费（时间）」的固定句型是 it takes (sb) + 时间 + to do，中间插进定语从句后就成了 the time (that) it takes for it to react，读时先找到 it takes 这个骨架。after it has been displaced 用完成式强调「已经偏离之后」，不能换成 after it is displaced（那会读成同时发生）。

### ② Even though 让步从句 + 主句 may

**原句**：Even though an airplane may have positive static stability, it may have dynamic stability which is positive, neutral, or negative.

**拆解**：Even though 引导让步状语从句，主句 it may have dynamic stability；which is positive, neutral, or negative 是定语从句修饰 dynamic stability。

**要点**：even though（＝although）与主句之间只用逗号，不能再加 but。两个 may 都表「可能」，说明静稳定性与动稳定性是两回事、彼此可以不一致。区分 even though（承认既成事实）与 even if（假设尚未发生）。

### ③ be classified as + 动名词

**原句**：If the oscillations lessen over time, the airplane is still classified as having positive dynamic stability.

**拆解**：If 引导条件从句，主语 the oscillations，谓语 lessen（不及物动词「减小」）；主句为被动 is still classified as having …，as 是介词，后接动名词短语 having positive dynamic stability。

**要点**：classify … as …（把……归为）变被动后 as 仍是介词，后面必须是名词或 -ing，不能接 that 从句。still 在此表「（幅度减小了也）仍然算作」，是分类判断的关键副词。over time（随时间）是固定搭配，不与 with time 混用。
