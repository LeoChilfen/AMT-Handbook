# Flight Controls and the Longitudinal Axis

> 通用 Ch 05 · Flight Controls and the Longitudinal Axis · 原文 PDF 第 193 页

## 1. 原文

The longitudinal axis of the airplane runs through the middle of the airplane, from nose to tail, passing through the center of gravity. Movement around this axis is known as roll, and control around this axis is called lateral control.

Movement around this axis is controlled by the ailerons, and on jet transport airplanes, it is aided by surfaces on the wing known as spoilers.

The ailerons move as a result of the pilot rotating the control wheel to the left or to the right, much the same as turning the steering wheel on an automobile. [Figure 5-68] When a pilot turns the control wheel to the left, the airplane is being asked to turn or bank to the left. Turning the control wheel to the left causes the trailing edge of the aileron on the left wing to rise up into the airstream, and the aileron on the right wing lowers down into the airstream. This increases the lift on the right wing and decreases the lift on the left wing, causing the right wing to move up and the airplane to bank to the left.

In Figure 5-69, an aircraft can be seen doing an aileron roll.

Notice that the left aileron is up and the right aileron is down, which would cause the airplane to roll around the longitudinal axis in a counterclockwise direction.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机的纵轴穿过飞机中部、由机头到机尾，并经过重心。绕该轴的运动称为滚转，绕该轴的操纵称为横向操纵。

绕该轴的运动由副翼控制；在喷气运输机上，还借助机翼上称为扰流板的翼面加以辅助。

副翼的运动，源自飞行员向左或向右转动操纵盘，这与转动汽车的方向盘颇为相似。（图 5-68）当飞行员向左转动操纵盘时，就是在要求飞机向左转弯或向左压坡度。向左转动操纵盘，会使左翼副翼的后缘上翘进入气流，而右翼副翼下偏进入气流。这使右翼升力增大、左翼升力减小，从而使右翼上抬、飞机向左压坡度。

在图 5-69 中，可以看到一架飞机正在做副翼滚转。

注意左副翼在上、右副翼在下，这会使飞机绕纵轴沿逆时针方向滚转。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as a result of + 动名词；much the same as 比较

**原句**：The ailerons move as a result of the pilot rotating the control wheel to the left or to the right, much the same as turning the steering wheel on an automobile.

**拆解**：主句 The ailerons move；as a result of 引出原因，其宾语是动名词复合结构 the pilot rotating the control wheel；much the same as 引出比较对象 turning the steering wheel on an automobile。

**要点**：as a result of 后接名词或动名词，不能接从句（× as a result of the pilot rotates）。the pilot rotating 是「名词 + 动名词」的复合结构，名词用普通格即可。much the same as 意为「与……大体相同」，比 the same as 多了程度上的弹性，技术文本很喜欢用它打比方。

### ② 插入状语 + 过去分词短语作后置定语 known as

**原句**：Movement around this axis is controlled by the ailerons, and on jet transport airplanes, it is aided by surfaces on the wing known as spoilers.

**拆解**：并列被动句；on jet transport airplanes 是插入的地点／条件状语，前后用逗号隔开；第二分句 it 指 movement；known as spoilers 是过去分词短语，后置修饰 surfaces on the wing。

**要点**：known as 是「被称为……的」，作后置定语时等于 which are known as；同理还有 called、referred to as、termed。插入状语放在第二个分句开头，是为了不让读者误以为副翼只在喷气机上才有——副翼人人都有，扰流板才是喷气运输机的加分项。

### ③ 非限制性定语从句 which 指代整个前句 + would

**原句**：Notice that the left aileron is up and the right aileron is down, which would cause the airplane to roll around the longitudinal axis in a counterclockwise direction.

**拆解**：祈使句 Notice 后接 that 宾语从句；逗号 + which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词不是某个名词，而是前面「左副翼上、右副翼下」这一整件事；would 表示按空气动力原理「就会」产生的结果。

**要点**：which 指代整句时只能用非限制性形式（逗号不能省），且不能换成 that。cause sth to do 中 to 不可省。in a counterclockwise direction 也可简写为 counterclockwise，方向介词用 in。
