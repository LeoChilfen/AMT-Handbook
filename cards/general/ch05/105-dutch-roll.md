# Dutch Roll

> 通用 Ch 05 · Dutch Roll · 原文 PDF 第 192 页

## 1. 原文

The dihedral of the wing tries to roll the airplane in the opposite direction of how it is slipping, and the vertical fin will try to yaw the airplane in the direction of the slip. These two events combine in a way that affects lateral and directional stability. If the wing dihedral has the greatest effect, the airplane will have a tendency to experience a Dutch roll. A Dutch roll is a small amount of oscillation around both the longitudinal and vertical axes. Although this condition is not considered dangerous, it can produce an uncomfortable feeling for passengers.

Commercial airliners typically have yaw dampers that sense a Dutch roll condition and cancel it out.

## 2. 中文翻译

机翼的上反角力图使飞机朝与其侧滑相反的方向滚转，而垂直尾翼则力图使飞机朝侧滑的方向偏航。这两种作用以某种方式叠加，从而影响横向稳定性和方向稳定性。若机翼上反角的作用占优，飞机就会有出现荷兰滚的倾向。荷兰滚是绕纵轴与垂直轴同时发生的小幅振荡。虽然这种情况不被认为危险，但它会给乘客带来不适感。

商用客机通常装有偏航阻尼器，能够感知荷兰滚状态并将其抵消。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① in the opposite direction of / in the direction of（对照结构）

**原句**：The dihedral of the wing tries to roll the airplane in the opposite direction of how it is slipping, and the vertical fin will try to yaw the airplane in the direction of the slip.

**拆解**：并列句：前一分句 tries to roll the airplane in the opposite direction of how it is slipping，后一分句 will try to yaw the airplane in the direction of the slip；how it is slipping 是名词性从句作介词 of 的宾语。

**要点**：in the opposite direction of 与 in the direction of 的对照，正是本节逻辑的骨架：上反角反着侧滑滚、垂尾顺着侧滑偏。of 后接 how 从句是规范的用法（译作「朝其侧滑的相反方向」），也可简化为 of the slip。try to do 在这里是「力图」，含拟人色彩。

### ② in a way that + 定语从句

**原句**：These two events combine in a way that affects lateral and directional stability.

**拆解**：主语 These two events，谓语 combine（不及物）；in a way 是方式状语，that affects lateral and directional stability 是定语从句修饰 a way。

**要点**：in a way that + 从句 ＝「以……的方式」，way 后不能用 how（× in a way how）。combine 在此为不及物「叠加、共同起作用」；及物用法 combine A with B 意为「把 A 与 B 结合」，两者不要混。

### ③ Although 让步从句 + consider 的被动

**原句**：Although this condition is not considered dangerous, it can produce an uncomfortable feeling for passengers.

**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，其中 is not considered dangerous 是「be + 过去分词 + 形容词」的主语补足结构；主句 it can produce an uncomfortable feeling for passengers。

**要点**：consider sth (to be) adj. 变被动后形容词直接跟在 considered 后，to be 可省。uncomfortable feeling for sb 用 for 引出感受的主体，不用 to。Although 与 but 不可同句并用。
