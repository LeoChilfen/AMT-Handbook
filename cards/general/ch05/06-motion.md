# Motion

## 1. 原文

The study of the relationship between the motion of bodies or objects and the forces acting on them is often called the study of “force and motion.” In a more specific sense, the relationship between velocity, acceleration, and distance is known as kinematics.

Motion may be defined as a continuing change of position or place, or as the process in which a body undergoes displacement. When an object is at different points in space at different times, that object is said to be in motion, and if the distance the object moves remains the same for a given period of time, the motion may be described as uniform. Thus, an object in uniform motion always has a constant speed.

### Speed and Velocity

In everyday conversation, speed and velocity are often used as if they mean the same thing. In physics, they have definite and distinct meanings. Speed refers to how fast an object is moving, or how far the object will travel in a specific time. The speed of an object tells nothing about the direction an object is moving. For example, if the information is supplied that an airplane leaves New York City and travels 8 hours at a speed of 150 mph, this information tells nothing about the direction in which the airplane is moving. At the end of 8 hours, it might be in Kansas City, or if it traveled in a circular route, it could be back in New York City.

Velocity is that quantity in physics which denotes both the speed of an object and the direction in which the object moves. Velocity can be defined as the rate of motion in a particular direction. Velocity is also described as being a vector quantity, a vector being a line of specific length, having an arrow on one end or the other. The length of the line indicates the number value and the arrow indicates the direction in which that number is acting.

Two velocity vectors, such as one representing the velocity of an airplane and one representing the velocity of the wind, can be added together in what is called vector analysis. This is demonstrated with vectors “A” and “B” representing the velocity of the airplane and the wind, and vector “C” being the resultant. With no wind, the speed and direction of the airplane would be that shown by vector “A.” When accounting for the wind direction and speed, the airplane ends up flying at the speed and direction shown by vector “C.”

Imagine that an airplane is flying in a circular pattern at a constant speed. The airplane is constantly changing direction because of the circular pattern, which means the airplane is constantly changing velocity. The reason for this is the fact that velocity includes direction.

To calculate the speed of an object, the distance it travels is divided by the elapsed time. If the distance is measured in miles and the time in hours, the units of speed will be miles per hour (mph). If the distance is measured in feet and the time in seconds, the units of speed will be feet per second (fps). To convert mph to fps, divide by 1.467. Velocity is calculated the same way, the only difference being it must be recalculated every time the direction changes.

Acceleration is defined as the rate of change of velocity. If the velocity of an object is increased from 20 mph to 30 mph, the object has been accelerated. If the increase in velocity is 10 mph in 5 seconds, the rate of change in velocity is 10 mph in 5 seconds, or 2 mph per second. If this were multiplied by 1.467, it could also be expressed as an acceleration of 2.93 feet per second per second (fps/s). By comparison, the acceleration due to gravity is 32.2 fps/s.

To calculate acceleration, the following formula is used.

Velocity Final (Vf) − Velocity Initial (Vi)

Acceleration (A) = Time (t)

Example: An Air Force F-15 fighter is cruising at 400 mph. The pilot advances the throttles to full afterburner and accelerates to 1,200 mph in 20 seconds. What is the average acceleration in mph/s and fps/s?

A = tVf − Vi A = 201200 − 400 A = 40 mph⁄s, or multiplying by 1.467, 58.7 fps⁄s In the example just shown, the acceleration was found to be 58.7 fps/s. Since 32.2 fps/s is equal to the acceleration due to gravity, divide the F-15’s acceleration by 32.2 to find out how many G forces the pilot is experiencing. In this case, it would be 1.82 Gs.

### Newton’s Law of Motion

When a magician snatches a tablecloth from a table and leaves a full setting of dishes undisturbed, he is not displaying a mystic art; he is actually demonstrating the principle of inertia. Inertia is responsible for the discomfort felt when an airplane is brought to a sudden halt in the parking area and the passengers are thrown forward in their seats. Inertia is a property of matter. This property of matter is described by Newton’s first law of motion, which states:

Objects at rest tend to remain at rest and objects in motion tend to remain in motion at the same speed and in the same direction, unless acted on by an external force.

Bodies in motion have the property called momentum. A body that has great momentum has a strong tendency to remain in motion and is therefore hard to stop. For example, a train moving at even low velocity is difficult to stop because of its large mass. Newton’s second law applies to this property. It states:

When a force acts upon a body, the momentum of that body is changed. The rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force. Based on Newton’s second law, the formula for calculating thrust is derived, which states that force equals mass times acceleration (F = MA). Earlier in this chapter, it was determined that mass equals weight divided by gravity, and acceleration equals velocity final minus velocity initial divided by time. Putting all these concepts together, the formula for thrust is:

Force = Gravity (Time)Weight (Velocity final − Velocity initial)

Force = GtW (Vf − Vi)

Newton’s third law of motion is often called the law of action and reaction. It states that for every action there is an equal and opposite reaction. This means that if a force is applied to an object, the object will supply a resistive force exactly equal to and in the opposite direction of the force applied. It is easy to see how this might apply to objects at rest. In application, as a man stands on the floor, the floor exerts a force against his feet exactly equal to his weight. This law is also applicable when a force is applied to an object in motion.

Forces always occur in pairs. The term “acting force” means the force one body exerts on a second body, and reacting force means the force the second body exerts on the first. When an aircraft propeller pushes a stream of air backward with a force of 500 lb, the air pushes the blades forward with a force of 500 lb. This forward force causes the aircraft to move forward. A turbofan engine exerts a force on the air entering the inlet duct, causing it to accelerate out the fan duct and the tailpipe. The air accelerating to the rear is the action, and the force inside the engine that makes it happen is the reaction, also called thrust.

Circular motion is the motion of an object along a curved path that has a constant radius. For example, if one end of a string is tied to an object and the other end is held in the hand, the object can be swung in a circle. The object is constantly deflected from a straight (linear) path by the pull exerted on the string. When the weight is at point A, due to inertia it wants to keep moving in a straight line and end up at point B. It is forced to move in a circular path and end up at point C because of the force being exerted on the string.

The string exerts a centripetal force on the object, and the object exerts an equal but opposite force on the string, obeying Newton’s third law of motion. The force that is equal to centripetal force, but acting in an opposite direction, is called centrifugal force. Centripetal force is always directly proportional to the mass of the object in circular motion. Thus, if the mass of the object is doubled, the pull on the string must be doubled to keep the object in its circular path, provided the speed of the object remains constant.

Centripetal force is inversely proportional to the radius of the circle in which an object travels. If the string is shortened and the speed remains constant, the pull on the string must be increased since the radius is decreased, and the string must pull the object from its linear path more rapidly. Using the same reasoning, the pull on the string must be increased if the object is swung more rapidly in its orbit. Centripetal force is thus directly proportional to the square of the velocity of the object. The formula for centripetal force is:

Centripetal Force = Mass (Velocity2) ÷ Radius For the formula above, mass would typically be converted to weight divided by gravity, velocity would be in feet per second, and the radius would be in feet.

## 2. 中文翻译

研究物体运动与作用其上的力之间关系的学问，通常称为「力与运动」的研究。更具体地说，速度、加速度和距离之间的关系称为运动学。

运动可以定义为位置或地点持续不断的变化，也可以定义为物体发生位移的过程。当物体在不同时刻处于空间中的不同位置时，就说该物体在运动；如果在给定时间内物体移动的距离保持不变，这种运动就可以描述为匀速运动。因此，做匀速运动的物体总是具有恒定的速率。

### 速率与速度

在日常交谈中，速率（speed）和速度（velocity）常被当作同一个意思使用。在物理学中，它们有明确的而又彼此不同的含义。速率指物体运动得有多快，或者在特定时间内能走多远。物体的速率并不说明物体运动的方向。例如，如果只提供这样的信息：一架飞机离开纽约市，以 150 mph 的速率飞行 8 小时，那么这些信息完全不说明飞机朝哪个方向飞。8 小时之后，它可能在堪萨斯城；如果它绕环形航线飞，也可能又回到纽约市。

速度是物理学中既表示物体速率、又表示物体运动方向的物理量。速度可以定义为在特定方向上的运动率。速度也被描述为矢量，所谓矢量就是一条具有特定长度的线，其一端带箭头。线的长度表示数值大小，箭头表示该数值作用的方向。

两个速度矢量——例如一个表示飞机的速度、一个表示风的速度——可以相加，这种运算称为矢量分析。矢量“A”和“B”分别表示飞机和风的速度，矢量“C”是合成结果，以此来演示这一运算。无风时，飞机的速率和方向就是矢量“A”所示的那样。考虑风向和风速之后，飞机最终以矢量“C”所示的速率和方向飞行。

设想一架飞机以恒定速率做圆周飞行。由于圆形的航迹，飞机不断改变方向，这意味着飞机的速度不断改变。原因在于速度包含方向。

计算物体的速率时，用它走过的距离除以所经过的时间。如果距离以英里计、时间以小时计，速率的单位就是英里每小时（mph）。如果距离以英尺计、时间以秒计，速率的单位就是英尺每秒（fps）。要把 mph 换算成 fps，除以 1.467。速度的计算方法相同，唯一的区别是每当方向改变时必须重新计算。

加速度定义为速度的变化率。如果物体的速度从 20 mph 增加到 30 mph，该物体就被加速了。如果速度在 5 秒内增加 10 mph，那么速度的变化率就是 5 秒内增加 10 mph，即每秒 2 mph。如果把这个值乘以（此处夹有错位的标注文字，略）1.467，也可以表示成 2.93 英尺每秒每秒（fps/s）的加速度。作为对照，重力加速度为 32.2 fps/s。

计算加速度时使用以下公式。

末速度（Vf）− 初速度（Vi）

加速度（A）= 时间（t）

例：一架空军 F-15 战斗机以 400 mph 巡航。飞行员把油门推到全加力位置，在 20 秒内加速到 1,200 mph。以 mph/s 和 fps/s 计的平均加速度是多少？

A = （Vf − Vi）÷ t　A = （1200 − 400）÷ 20　A = 40 mph⁄s，或乘以 1.467 得 58.7 fps⁄s 在上面这个例子中，求得的加速度为 58.7 fps/s。由于 32.2 fps/s 等于重力加速度，把 F-15 的加速度除以 32.2，即可求出飞行员承受多少个 G 力。此例中为 1.82 G。

### 牛顿运动定律

魔术师从桌上猛地抽走桌布，而整套餐具纹丝不动，他施展的并不是什么神秘法术；他实际上是在演示惯性原理。飞机在停机坪上突然刹停、乘客被向前甩离座位时那种不适感，就是惯性造成的。惯性是物质的一种属性。牛顿第一运动定律描述的就是物质的这一属性，该定律表述如下：

静止的物体倾向于保持静止，运动的物体倾向于以相同的速率沿相同的方向继续运动，除非受到外力作用。

运动的物体具有一种称为动量的属性。动量大的物体保持运动的倾向很强，因此很难使它停下来。例如，即使速度不高，火车也很难刹停，因为它的质量很大。牛顿第二定律讲的就是这一属性，该定律表述如下：

当力作用于物体时，该物体的动量就会改变。动量变化率与所施加的力成正比。根据牛顿第二定律推导出计算推力的公式，即力等于质量乘以加速度（F = MA）。本章前面已经确定，质量等于重量除以重力，加速度等于末速度减初速度再除以时间。把这些概念综合起来，推力公式为：

力 = 重量（末速度 − 初速度）÷（重力 × 时间）

力 = W（Vf − Vi）÷（G × t）

例：一台涡轮喷气发动机每秒使 150 lb 空气流过发动机。空气以 100 fps 进入，以 1,200 fps 排出。该发动机产生多少磅推力？

F = 150（1 200 − 100）÷ 32.2（1）

F = 5,124 lb 推力（此处夹有一组标注文字：Vector C = 飞机的运动方向；Vector A = 飞机的速度；Vector B = 风）

牛顿第三运动定律常被称为作用与反作用定律。它指出，每一个作用都有一个大小相等、方向相反的反作用。这意味着，如果对物体施加一个力，物体就会给出一个与所施加的力大小完全相等、方向相反的抵抗力。很容易看出这一定律如何适用于静止的物体。举例来说，人站在地板上，地板对他双脚施加的力恰好等于他的重量。当力作用于运动中的物体时，这一定律同样适用。

力总是成对出现。「作用力」一词指的是一个物体对第二个物体施加的力，而反作用力指的是第二个物体对第一个物体施加的力。当飞机螺旋桨以 500 lb 的力把气流向后推时，空气也以 500 lb 的力把桨叶向前推。这个向前的力使飞机向前运动。涡扇发动机对进入进气道的空气施加作用力，使它加速从风扇涵道和尾喷管排出。向后加速的空气是作用，而发动机内部使之发生的力是反作用，也称为推力。

圆周运动是物体沿半径恒定的曲线路径所做的运动。例如，把绳子的一端系在物体上、另一端握在手中，就可以使物体做圆周摆动。由于绳子施加的拉力，物体不断偏离直线（线性）路径。当重物位于 A 点时，由于惯性，它想继续沿直线运动，最终到达 B 点。由于施加在绳子上的力，它被迫沿圆周路径运动，最终到达 C 点。

绳子对物体施加向心力，物体则对绳子施加大小相等、方向相反的力，这符合牛顿第三运动定律。与向心力大小相等但方向相反的力称为离心力。向心力总是与做圆周运动物体的质量成正比。因此，如果物体的质量加倍，在物体速率保持不变的前提下，绳上的拉力也必须加倍，才能把物体维持在圆周路径上。

向心力与物体运动所在圆的半径成反比。如果缩短绳子而速率保持不变，由于半径减小，绳上的拉力必然增大，绳子必须更迅速地使物体偏离其直线路径。依同样的道理，如果物体在轨道上摆得更快，绳上的拉力也必须增大。因此，向心力与物体速度的平方成正比。向心力的公式为：

向心力 = 质量（速度²）÷ 半径 对于上面的公式，质量通常要换算成重量除以重力，速度以英尺每秒计，半径以英尺计。

向心力 = 质量（速度²）÷ 半径 向心力 = 10（500²）÷ 32.2（3）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① be defined as + in which 定语从句

**原句**：Motion may be defined as a continuing change of position or place, or as the process in which a body undergoes displacement.

**拆解**：主句为 Motion may be defined as A or as B，两个 as 短语并列作方式状语；A 为 a continuing change of position or place（动名词 continuing 作前置定语），B 为 the process in which a body undergoes displacement，in which 引导定语从句修饰 process。

**要点**：be defined as 是定义句的固定搭配，翻译成「定义为」；in which = in the process，介词不能省，因为从句中 undergoes 后不缺宾语，缺的是地点状语。

### ② 关系代词省略的定语从句作后置修饰

**原句**：If the distance the object moves remains the same for a given period of time, the motion may be described as uniform.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，其主语 the distance 后紧跟 the object moves（省略了关系代词 that/which 的定语从句）；主句 the motion may be described as uniform 为被动语态 + 主语补足语。

**要点**：the distance the object moves 直译是「物体移动的距离」，中文习惯把定语前置；这类「先行词 + 主谓」无连接词的排列是手册英语的高频结构，判断靠语义而非形式标记。

### ③ both … and … 与 in which 的双重限定

**原句**：Velocity is that quantity in physics which denotes both the speed of an object and the direction in which the object moves.

**拆解**：主句为 Velocity is that quantity in physics；which denotes … 为定语从句修饰 quantity，从句内 both the speed of an object and the direction … 构成 both … and … 并列宾语；the direction in which the object moves 中 in which 又引入一层定语从句。

**要点**：both … and … 连接的两个成分语法地位必须相同；in which 相当于 in the direction，表示「朝该方向」，不可换成 which。

### ④ in what is called 名词性从句

**原句**：Two velocity vectors, such as one representing the velocity of an airplane and one representing the velocity of the wind, can be added together in what is called vector analysis.

**拆解**：主语 Two velocity vectors 后接 such as 引出的同位举例，其中 one representing … 为独立主格（one + 现在分词）；谓语 can be added together；in what is called vector analysis 中介词 in 后接 what 引导的宾语从句，what 在从句中作主语。

**要点**：what 从句前可加介词（in/from/by 等），构成「在所谓的……中」；one representing … 若写成 which represents 则失去独立主格的简洁性。

### ⑤ with + 宾语 + 现在分词（独立主格补充说明）

**原句**：This is demonstrated with vectors “A” and “B” representing the velocity of the airplane and the wind, and vector “C” being the resultant.

**拆解**：主句 This is demonstrated 为被动结构；with 引出两个并列的复合结构：vectors “A” and “B” + representing …，以及 vector “C” + being the resultant，分词逻辑主语分别是前面的名词短语，与句子主语无关。

**要点**：with 复合结构在技术描述中用来补充「各部分分别是什么」，翻译时常拆成独立短句；being 表示「是、为」，与 representing 的「表示」分工明确。

### ⑥ provided (that) 引出的附加条件

**原句**：Thus, if the mass of the object is doubled, the pull on the string must be doubled to keep the object in its circular path, provided the speed of the object remains constant.

**拆解**：Thus 承上；if 引导条件从句，主句 the pull on the string must be doubled，to keep the object in its circular path 作目的状语；provided the speed of the object remains constant 为附加条件状语从句，provided 相当于 if/on condition that。

**要点**：provided (that) 比 if 更正式，强调「前提是」，多置于句末；逗号不能省略，因为它是后置的补充限制条件。
