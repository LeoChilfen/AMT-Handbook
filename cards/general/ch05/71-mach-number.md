# Mach Number

> 通用 Ch 05 · Mach Number · 原文 PDF 第 181 页

## 1. 原文

In the study of aircraft that fly at supersonic speeds, it is customary to discuss aircraft speed in relation to the velocity of sound, which is approximately 761 miles per hour (mph)

at 59 °F. The term “Mach number” has been given to the ratio of the speed of an aircraft to the speed of sound, in honor of Ernst Mach, an Austrian scientist. If the speed of sound at sea level is 761 mph, an aircraft flying at a Mach number of 1.2 at sea level would be traveling at a speed of 761 mph × 1.2 = 913 mph.

## 2. 中文翻译

在研究以超音速飞行的航空器时，习惯上把航空器速度与声速联系起来讨论；声速在 59 °F 下约为 761 英里每小时（mph）。

“马赫数”（Mach number）这一术语被赋予航空器速度与声速之比，用以纪念奥地利科学家恩斯特·马赫（Ernst Mach）。若海平面处声速为 761 mph，则在海平面以马赫数 1.2 飞行的航空器，其速度应为 761 mph × 1.2 = 913 mph。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句 + it 作形式主语

**原句**：In the study of aircraft that fly at supersonic speeds, it is customary to discuss aircraft speed in relation to the velocity of sound, which is approximately 761 miles per hour (mph) at 59 °F.

**拆解**：In the study of aircraft 为状语；that fly at supersonic speeds 是定语从句修饰 aircraft；主句为 it is customary to discuss ...，it 是形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to discuss ...；in relation to the velocity of sound 为方式状语；which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 the velocity of sound。（原文在 (mph) 与 at 59 °F 之间因分页断行，此处按同一句译出。）

**要点**：it is customary / necessary / important to do sth 是手册开篇交代做法的固定句式，it 不能换成 that。which 前有逗号即非限制性定语从句，只能指物、不能用 that。in relation to 意为「与……相关联」，技术文本中常用来把两个量放在一起讨论。

### ② 现在完成时被动 + the ratio of A to B

**原句**：The term “Mach number” has been given to the ratio of the speed of an aircraft to the speed of sound, in honor of Ernst Mach, an Austrian scientist.

**拆解**：主语 The term “Mach number”；谓语 has been given（现在完成时被动）；to the ratio ... 是 give sth to sth 的介词宾语；the ratio of the speed of an aircraft to the speed of sound 中 of 与 to 各引出一个比较项；in honor of ... 是目的状语；an Austrian scientist 是 Ernst Mach 的同位语。

**要点**：give a name to sth 的被动式是 a name has been given to sth，译成「把……这一名称赋予……」。the ratio of A to B 是固定搭配，两个介词分别管 A 和 B，不要误读成 of ... to ... 三个并列。同位语用逗号引出，是技术文本首次介绍人名时的标准写法。

### ③ 条件句 + 现在分词短语作后置定语

**原句**：If the speed of sound at sea level is 761 mph, an aircraft flying at a Mach number of 1.2 at sea level would be traveling at a speed of 761 mph × 1.2 = 913 mph.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句；主句主语 an aircraft，flying at a Mach number of 1.2 at sea level 是现在分词短语作后置定语；谓语 would be traveling（would + 进行式）；at a speed of ... 作方式状语。

**要点**：手册里的推算示例常用「If ..., ... would ...」，用 would 表示「在此假设条件下应当得出」的结果，比 will 更像推演。flying 修饰 aircraft 而不作谓语，找主句谓语时要越过它看 would be traveling。算式 761 mph × 1.2 = 913 mph 原样保留，只译说明文字。
