# The Lever

> 通用 Ch 05 · The Lever · 原文 PDF 第 158 页

## 1. 原文

The simplest machine, and perhaps the most familiar one, is the lever. A seesaw is a familiar example of a lever, with two people sitting on either end of a board and a pivoting point in the middle. There are three basic parts in all levers. They are the fulcrum “F,” a force or effort “E,” and a resistance “R.” Shown in Figure 5-7 are the pivot point “F” (fulcrum), the effort “E” which is applied at a distance “L” from the fulcrum, and a resistance “R” which acts at a distance “l” from the fulcrum. Distances “L” and “l” are the lever arms.

The concept of torque was discussed earlier in this chapter, and torque is very much involved in the operation of a lever.

When a person sits on one end of a seesaw, that person applies a downward force in pounds which acts along the distance to the center of the seesaw. This combination of force and distance creates torque, which tries to cause rotation.

## 2. 中文翻译

最简单的机械，也许还是最熟悉的机械，就是杠杆。跷跷板是杠杆的常见例子：两个人分别坐在木板两端，中间有一个支点。所有杠杆都有三个基本部分：支点「F」、力或称动力「E」，以及阻力「R」。图 5-7 中标出了支点「F」（fulcrum）、在距支点「L」处施加的动力「E」，以及在距支点「l」处作用的阻力「R」。距离「L」和「l」就是杠杆臂（原文用大写 L 与小写 l 区分两个力臂，OCR 后不易分辨）。

本章前面已讨论过扭矩的概念，而扭矩与杠杆的作用关系极大。

当一个人坐在跷跷板一端时，他施加一个以磅计向下的力，该力沿到跷跷板中心的距离作用。力与距离的这种组合产生扭矩，而扭矩力图引起转动。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 完全倒装（表语前置）+ 两个并列定语从句

**原句**：Shown in Figure 5-7 are the pivot point “F” (fulcrum), the effort “E” which is applied at a distance “L” from the fulcrum, and a resistance “R” which acts at a distance “l” from the fulcrum.

**拆解**：正常语序应为 The pivot point …, the effort …, and a resistance … are shown in Figure 5-7；此处把过去分词 Shown in Figure 5-7 提到句首作表语，主语（三项并列）后置，构成完全倒装；which is applied … 与 which acts … 分别是修饰 the effort 和 a resistance 的定语从句。

**要点**：科技文献常用「Shown in Figure X are …」「Listed below are …」这类倒装来引出图表内容，翻译时按正常语序还原。注意 which is applied 用被动（力是被施加的），而 which acts 用主动（阻力自己起作用），两者不可互换。

### ② 插入语 + 最高级 + one 替代前文名词

**原句**：The simplest machine, and perhaps the most familiar one, is the lever.

**拆解**：主语 The simplest machine；and perhaps the most familiar one 是插入的同位补充，one 替代前面出现过的 machine；系动词 is；表语 the lever。

**要点**：one / ones 用来替代前面提到的可数名词，避免重复（the most familiar one = the most familiar machine）。插入语用一对逗号夹住，去掉后句子仍成立；最高级前必须加 the。

### ③ 非限制性定语从句 which 指代整句重心

**原句**：This combination of force and distance creates torque, which tries to cause rotation.

**拆解**：主句 This combination of force and distance creates torque；which tries to cause rotation 是非限制性定语从句，先行词是 torque（不是 combination）；try to do 表示「力图做某事」。

**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，不能用 that 引导，译作「而它……」。try to do 是「试图做」，try doing 是「试着做做看」，技术语境中描述扭矩的作用应选前者。

### ④ when 从句 + which 定语从句（修饰 force）

**原句**：When a person sits on one end of a seesaw, that person applies a downward force in pounds which acts along the distance to the center of the seesaw.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；主句 that person applies a downward force in pounds，in pounds 说明力的计量单位；which acts along the distance to the center of the seesaw 是定语从句修饰 force（不是修饰 pounds）。

**要点**：定语从句的先行词要按意义判断：acts along the distance 描述的是力，所以 which 指 force。along the distance 与本章扭矩定义的 along 呼应，区别于功的 through a distance。
