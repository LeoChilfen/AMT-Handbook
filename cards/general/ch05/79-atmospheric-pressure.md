# Atmospheric Pressure

> 通用 Ch 05 · Atmospheric Pressure · 原文 PDF 第 184 页

## 1. 原文

The human body is under pressure, since it exists at the bottom of a sea of air. This pressure is due to the weight of the atmosphere. On a standard day at sea level, if a 1-in2 column of air extending to the top of the atmosphere was weighed, it would weigh 14.7 lb. That is why standard day atmospheric pressure is said to be 14.7 pounds per square inch (14.7 psi).

Since atmospheric pressure at any altitude is due to the weight of air above it, pressure decreases with increased altitude.

Obviously, the total weight of air above an area at 15,000 ft would be less than the total weight of the air above an area at 10,000 ft.

Atmospheric pressure is often measured by a mercury barometer. A glass tube somewhat over 30 inches in length is sealed at one end and then filled with mercury. It is then inverted and the open end placed in a dish of mercury.

Immediately, the mercury level in the inverted tube will drop a short distance, leaving a small volume of mercury vapor at nearly zero absolute pressure in the tube just above the top of the liquid mercury column. Gravity acting on the mercury in the tube will try to make the mercury run out. Atmospheric pressure pushing down on the mercury in the open container tries to make the mercury stay in the tube. At some point these two forces (gravity and atmospheric pressure) will equilibrate out and the mercury will stabilize at a certain height in the tube. Under standard day atmospheric conditions, the air in a 1 square inch column extending to the top of the atmosphere would weigh 14.7 lb. A 1-in2 column of mercury, 29.92 inches tall, would also weigh 14.7 lb. That is why 14.7 psi is equal to 29.92 "Hg. Figure 5-50 demonstrates this point.

A second means of measuring atmospheric pressure is with an aneroid barometer. This mechanical instrument is a much better choice than a mercury barometer for use on airplanes.

Aneroid barometers, or altimeters, are used to indicate altitude in flight. The calibrations are made in thousands of feet rather than in psi or inches of mercury. For example, the standard pressure at sea level is 29.92 "Hg, or 14.7 psi. At 10,000 feet above sea level, standard pressure is 20.58 "Hg, or 10.10 psi. Altimeters are calibrated so that if the pressure exerted by the atmosphere is 10.10 psi, the altimeter will point to 10,000 ft. [Figure 5-51]

## 2. 中文翻译

人体承受着压力，因为它处在空气海洋的底部。这一压力来自大气的重量。在海平面的标准日，若把一根截面积为 1 in²（原文写作 1-in2，上标 2 在 OCR 中被压平，下同）、一直延伸到大气顶部的空气柱称重，其重量为 14.7 lb。这就是为什么标准日的大气压被说成 14.7 磅每平方英寸（14.7 psi）。

由于任意高度处的大气压都来自其上方空气的重量，压力随高度增加而减小。

显然，15,000 ft 处某一面积上方空气的总重量，要小于 10,000 ft 处同一面积上方空气的总重量。

大气压常用水银气压计测量。一根长度略大于 30 英寸的玻璃管一端封闭，然后灌满水银。随后将其倒置，把开口端放入盛水银的盘中。

倒置管中的水银面随即下降一小段，在液柱顶面正上方的管内留下一小部分几乎处于零绝压的水银蒸气。作用于管内水银的重力力图使水银流出；压在敞口容器水银面上的大气压则力图使水银留在管内。在某一位置，这两个力（重力与大气压）会达到平衡，水银便稳定在管内的某一高度。在标准日大气条件下，截面积为 1 平方英寸、延伸至大气顶部的空气柱重 14.7 lb；高 29.92 英寸、截面积为 1 in² 的水银柱同样重 14.7 lb。这就是为什么 14.7 psi 等于 29.92 "Hg。图 5-50 说明了这一点。

测量大气压的第二种手段是膜盒式气压计。这种机械仪表在飞机上使用，比水银气压计合适得多。

膜盒式气压计，即高度表，在飞行中用来指示高度。其刻度以千英尺为单位，而不是以 psi 或英寸汞柱为单位。例如，海平面的标准压力为 29.92 "Hg，即 14.7 psi；在海平面以上 10,000 ft 处，标准压力为 20.58 "Hg，即 10.10 psi。高度表经过校准，使得当大气施加的压力为 10.10 psi 时，高度表指向 10,000 ft。[图 5-51]

## 4. 语法解析

### ① since 引导的原因状语从句

**原句**：The human body is under pressure, since it exists at the bottom of a sea of air.

**拆解**：主句 The human body is under pressure（主系表）；since 引导原因状语从句，it 回指 the human body；at the bottom of a sea of air 为地点状语，用「空气海洋」作比喻。

**要点**：since 表原因时语气比 because 弱，常译作「既然、由于」，多用于已知事实；要靠时态与语境把它与表时间的 since（自从）分开。under pressure 是「承受压力」的固定搭配，under + 名词表示处于某种状态（under repair、under test）。

### ② 虚拟条件句 + 现在分词短语作后置定语

**原句**：On a standard day at sea level, if a 1-in2 column of air extending to the top of the atmosphere was weighed, it would weigh 14.7 lb.

**拆解**：On a standard day at sea level 是交代条件的背景状语；if 引导条件从句，was weighed 为被动；extending to the top of the atmosphere 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 column；主句 it would weigh 14.7 lb。

**要点**：if 从句用过去时、主句用 would，构成假设语气，用来描述一个「假想的称重实验」，译作「若……本会……」。extending 与 column 是主动关系（空气柱延伸到大气顶部），故用现在分词；改成 extended 就变成了「被延伸」。

### ③ so that 目的从句中嵌套 if 条件从句

**原句**：Altimeters are calibrated so that if the pressure exerted by the atmosphere is 10.10 psi, the altimeter will point to 10,000 ft.

**拆解**：主句 Altimeters are calibrated（被动）；so that 引导目的状语从句；从句内部又嵌套 if 条件从句；exerted by the atmosphere 是过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 the pressure。

**要点**：so that 后接从句表目的（从句常含 can / will / may），而 so ... that 中间夹形容词或副词表结果，两者不要混。遇到嵌套时先切出 so that 从句的骨架 the altimeter will point to 10,000 ft，再回头处理 if 条件，句子就不乱了。

### ④ much + 比较级 + than 的比较结构

**原句**：This mechanical instrument is a much better choice than a mercury barometer for use on airplanes.

**拆解**：主语 This mechanical instrument；系动词 is；表语 a much better choice；than a mercury barometer 引出比较对象；for use on airplanes 是后置定语或目的状语，说明使用场合。

**要点**：much 只能修饰比较级（much better、much heavier），不能修饰原级（much good 错）；同类修饰词还有 far、a lot、even、still。for use on airplanes 中的 for + 名词表用途，与 for use in the shop 结构相同。
