# Airfoils

> 通用 Ch 05 · Airfoils · 原文 PDF 第 188 页

## 1. 原文

An airfoil is any device that creates a force, based on Bernoulli’s principles or Newton’s laws, when air is caused to flow over the surface of the device. An airfoil can be the wing of an airplane, the blade of a propeller, the rotor blade of a helicopter, or the fan blade of a turbofan engine. The wing of an airplane moves through the air because the airplane is in motion, and generates lift by the process previously described. By comparison, a propeller blade, helicopter rotor blade, or turbofan engine fan blade rotates through the air.

These rotating blades could be referred to as rotating wings, as is common with helicopters when they are called rotary wing aircraft. The rotating wing can be viewed as a device that creates lift, or just as correctly, it can be viewed as a device that creates thrust.

## 2. 中文翻译

翼型（airfoil）是任何一种装置：当空气被引导流过该装置表面时，它能依据伯努利原理（Bernoulli’s principles）或牛顿定律（Newton’s laws）产生一个力。翼型可以是飞机的机翼、螺旋桨的桨叶、直升机的旋翼桨叶，或涡扇发动机的风扇叶片。飞机的机翼之所以能在空气中运动，是因为飞机本身处于运动状态，并按前面所述的过程产生升力。相比之下，螺旋桨桨叶、直升机旋翼桨叶或涡扇发动机风扇叶片是在空气中旋转的。

这些旋转的叶片可以称为“旋转机翼”，直升机上就是这么叫的——直升机也因此被称为旋翼航空器（rotary wing aircraft）。旋转机翼既可以被看作产生升力的装置，也可以同样正确地被看作产生推力的装置。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句与时间状语从句嵌套：that creates a force … when …

**原句**：An airfoil is any device that creates a force, based on Bernoulli’s principles or Newton’s laws, when air is caused to flow over the surface of the device.

**拆解**：主句是 An airfoil is any device；that creates a force 是定语从句，修饰 device；based on Bernoulli’s principles or Newton’s laws 是过去分词短语作依据状语，插在定语从句中间；when air is caused to flow over the surface of the device 是时间状语从句，其中 is caused to flow 为「be + 过去分词 + 不定式」的使役被动结构。

**要点**：给术语下定义时英文惯用「X is any device that + 功能」，that 从句交代它做什么。读长句时先跳过 based on... 这层插入语，抓住 that creates a force，再回头看触发条件。be caused to do 意为「被驱使、被引导去做」，主语 air 是动作的承受者；同类结构还有 be made to do、be allowed to do。

### ② because 引导原因从句 + 共用主语的并列谓语

**原句**：The wing of an airplane moves through the air because the airplane is in motion, and generates lift by the process previously described.

**拆解**：主语 The wing of an airplane；两个并列谓语 moves through the air 和 generates lift，由 and 连接；because the airplane is in motion 是原因状语从句，说明「为什么能在空气中前进」；by the process previously described 是方式状语，previously described 为过去分词短语后置修饰 process。

**要点**：并列谓语共用一个主语时，and 前的逗号提示读者后面还有谓语，不要误当成新句子的开头。previously described（前面已述的）是技术文本回指前文的固定说法，同类还有 as noted earlier、as discussed above。注意 generates 带第三人称 -s，与 moves 保持一致。

### ③ be referred to as + as 引导的定语从句

**原句**：These rotating blades could be referred to as rotating wings, as is common with helicopters when they are called rotary wing aircraft.

**拆解**：These rotating blades 是主语；could be referred to as rotating wings 为「情态动词 + 被动 + as 补语」；as is common with helicopters 是 as 引导的定语从句，as 指代前文「把旋转叶片称为旋转机翼」这件事；when they are called rotary wing aircraft 是时间状语从句，they 指 helicopters。

**要点**：refer to A as B（把 A 称为 B）的被动式 A can be referred to as B 在手册里极常见，同义替换还有 be called、be known as、be termed。as is common with... 中的 as 是关系代词，指代整个主句内容；换成 which 语法上也成立，但 as 带有「正如……那样」的语气，更贴合原意。
