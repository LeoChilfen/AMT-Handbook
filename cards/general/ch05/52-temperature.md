## 1. 原文

Temperature is a dominant factor affecting the physical properties of fluids. It is of particular concern when calculating changes in the state of gases.

The three temperature scales used extensively are the Centigrade, the Fahrenheit, and the absolute or Kelvin scales.

The Centigrade scale is constructed by using the freezing and boiling points of water, under standard conditions, as fixed points of zero and 100, respectively, with 100 equal divisions between. The Fahrenheit scale uses 32° as the freezing point of water and 212° as the boiling point, and has 180 equal divisions between. The absolute or Kelvin scale is constructed with its zero point established as minus 273 °C, meaning 273° below the freezing point of water. The relationships of the other fixed points of the scales are shown in Figure 5-31.

When working with temperatures, always make sure which system of measurement is being used and know how to convert from one to another. The conversion formulas are as follows:

Degrees Fahrenheit = (1.8 × Degrees Celsius) + 32 Degrees Celsius = (Degrees Fahrenheit – 32) × 5⁄9 Degrees Kelvin = Degrees Celsius + 273 Degrees Rankine = Degrees Fahrenheit + 460 For purposes of calculations, the Rankine scale is commonly used to convert Fahrenheit to absolute. For Fahrenheit readings above zero, 460° is added. Thus, 72 °F equals 460° plus 72°, or 532° absolute. If the Fahrenheit reading is below zero, it is subtracted from 460°. Thus −40 °F equals 460° minus 40°, or 420° absolute. ## 4. 语法解析

### ① be of + 抽象名词

**原句**：It is of particular concern when calculating changes in the state of gases.

**拆解**：主语 It（指 temperature）；系动词 is；表语是介词短语 of particular concern（= particularly concerned / of particular importance 型结构）；when calculating changes in the state of gases 是「连词 + 现在分词」构成的时间状语，相当于 when we calculate …。

**要点**：be of + 抽象名词 = be + 同根形容词，是技术文本里提高信息密度的常用手段（be of value = be valuable）。of 不能省略，省略后 of particular concern 就变成名词短语，句子缺表语。when + 分词要求分词的逻辑主语与主句主语一致，此处隐含的是「人们计算时」。

### ② It should be stressed that …（形式主语 + 被动 + 宾语从句）

**原句**：It should be stressed that the Rankine scale does not indicate absolute temperature readings in accordance with the Kelvin scale, but these conversions may be used for the calculations of

**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；should be stressed 为「情态动词 + 被动」；that 从句内部由 but 连接两个并列分句：does not indicate … 与 these conversions may be used for …；in accordance with the Kelvin scale 是方式状语。

**要点**：It should be stressed / noted / remembered that … 是手册提醒读者注意的固定句式，译作「应当强调／注意」。in accordance with 意为「按照、与……相一致」，此处指兰氏温标与开尔文温标的绝对温度读数并不等价。（本段句末被图面内容切断，原句未完。）

### ③ When + 现在分词作时间状语

**原句**：When working with temperatures, always make sure which system of measurement is being used and know how to convert from one to another.

**拆解**：When working with temperatures 是省略了主语和 be 的时间状语从句（= When you work with temperatures）；主句是祈使句，谓语为并列的 make sure 与 know；which system of measurement is being used 是 make sure 的宾语从句（被动进行时）；how to convert from one to another 是「疑问词 + 不定式」作 know 的宾语。

**要点**：状语从句主语与主句主语一致且含 be 时，可省略成「when + 分词」，这是手册祈使句的常见开头。one … another 指三者以上中的「一个……另一个」，此处 one 与 another 分别指两种温标；只有两者时应用 one … the other。
