# Mechanical Advantage of Machines

## 1. 原文

As identified in statements 3 and 4 under simple machines, a machine can be used to multiply force or to multiply speed. It cannot, however, multiply force and speed at the same time.

In order to gain one force, it must lose the other force. To do otherwise would mean the machine has more power going

out than coming in, and that is not possible.

In reference to machines, mechanical advantage is a comparison of the output force to the input force, or the output distance to the input distance. If there is a mechanical advantage in terms of force, there will be a fractional disadvantage in terms of distance. The following formulas can be used to calculate mechanical advantage.

Mechanical Advantage = Force Out ÷ Force In or Mechanical Advantage = Distance Out ÷ Distance In

## 4. 语法解析

### ① however 插入 + 否定情态动词

**原句**：It cannot, however, multiply force and speed at the same time.

**拆解**：主语 It（指机械）；cannot 后插入 however（两个逗号隔开）；谓语 multiply；宾语 force and speed；at the same time 为时间状语。

**要点**：cannot 后用逗号插入转折副词是英语书面语的常见语序，也可写成 However, it cannot …。这里的 and 在否定句中表示「不能同时做到两者」（部分否定），若写成 cannot multiply force or speed 则变成「两者都不能放大」，意思相反。

### ② in order to 引导目的 + one … the other 对举

**原句**：In order to gain one force, it must lose the other force.

**拆解**：In order to gain one force 是目的状语（in order to + 动词原形）；主句 it must lose the other force，it 指机械；one … the other 表示「（两者中的）一个……另一个……」。

**要点**：one … the other 只在指两者时使用，此处两方是力与距离（速度）；三者以上要用 one … another … the third。in order to 比单用 to 更强调目的，可置于句首，否定目的用 in order not to。下一段承接本句补充：若不然就等于输出功率大于输入功率，而这是不可能的（原文此处被插图碎片隔开）。

### ③ 真实条件句「主将从现」+ in terms of

**原句**：If there is a mechanical advantage in terms of force, there will be a fractional disadvantage in terms of distance.

**拆解**：If 引导真实条件从句，用一般现在时 there is；主句用一般将来时 there will be；in terms of force / in terms of distance 是并列对照的状语短语；a fractional disadvantage 与 a mechanical advantage 形成名词层面的对举。

**要点**：条件句「主将从现」是硬规则，从句里不能用 will。in terms of 在技术文本中译作「就……而言／以……来衡量」，比 about / regarding 更强调衡量维度。这句是机械效益的核心结论，可迁移为：若某维度有增益，另一维度必有等比例的损失。

### ④ a comparison of A to B + or 后的省略

**原句**：In reference to machines, mechanical advantage is a comparison of the output force to the input force, or the output distance to the input distance.

**拆解**：In reference to machines 为状语（「就机械而言」）；主语 mechanical advantage；表语 a comparison of A to B；or 后并列省略了 a comparison of，只剩 the output distance to the input distance。

**要点**：a comparison of A to B（把 A 与 B 相比）中的介词用 to，也可用 with；compare A with B 侧重比较差异，compare A to B 侧重比作。or 后的省略结构要靠前半句补全，否则会误读成两个独立名词短语。
