A tube constructed in this manner is called a “venturi,” or “venturi tube.” Where the cross-sectional area is decreasing, the passageway is referred to as a converging duct. As the passageway starts to spread out, it is referred to as a diverging duct.

As a liquid, or fluid, flows through the venturi tube, the gauges at points “A,” “B,” and “C” are positioned to register the velocity and the static pressure of the liquid. The venturi in Figure 5-46 can be used to illustrate Bernoulli’s principle, which states that: The static pressure of a fluid, liquid or gas, decreases at points where the velocity of the fluid increases, provided no energy is added to nor taken away from the fluid. The velocity of the air is kinetic energy and the static pressure of the air is potential energy.

In the wide section of the venturi (points A and C of Figure 5-46), the liquid moves at low velocity, producing a high static pressure, as indicated by the pressure gauge. As the tube narrows in the center, it must contain the same volume of fluid as the two end areas. As indicated by the velocity gauge reading high and the pressure gauge reading low, in this narrow section, the liquid moves at a higher velocity, producing a lower pressure than that at points A and C. A good application for the use of the venturi principle is in a float-type carburetor. As the air flows through the carburetor on its way to the engine, it goes through a venturi, where the static pressure is reduced. The fuel in the carburetor, which is under a higher pressure, flows into the lower pressure venturi area and mixes with the air.

Bernoulli’s principle is extremely important in understanding how some of the systems used in aviation work, including how the wing of an airplane generates lift or why the inlet duct of a turbine engine on a subsonic airplane is diverging in shape. The wing on a slow-moving airplane has a curved top surface and a relatively flat bottom surface. The curved top surface acts like half of the converging shaped middle of a venturi. As the air flow over the top of the wing, the air speeds up, and its static pressure decreases. The static pressure on the bottom of the wing is now greater than the pressure on the top, and this pressure difference creates the lift on the wing. Bernoulli’s principle and the concept of lift on a wing are covered in greater depth in “Aircraft Theory of Flight” located in this chapter.

当液体或流体流经文丘里管时，位于「A」「B」「C」三点的压力表用来记录液体的速度和静压。图 5-46 中的文丘里管可用来说明伯努利原理，该原理指出：流体（液体或气体）的静压，在流速增大的各处都会降低，前提是没有向流体加入能量，也没有从流体中取走能量。空气的速度是动能，空气的静压是势能。

在文丘里管的宽段（图 5-46 的 A、C 两点），液体以较低速度流动，产生较高的静压，如压力表所示。管道在中段收窄时，它必须容纳与两端截面相同的流体体积。此时速度表读数高、压力表读数低，表明在这个窄段液体以更高的速度流动，产生的压力低于 A、C 两点。文丘里原理的一个很好的应用实例是浮子式化油器。空气在流向发动机的途中经过化油器时，要穿过一个文丘里管，静压在此降低。化油器中处于较高压力下的燃油便流入低压的文丘里区，与空气混合。

伯努利原理对理解航空所用的一些系统如何工作极为重要，包括机翼如何产生升力、亚声速飞机的涡轮发动机进气道为什么做成扩张形。低速飞机的机翼上表面弯曲、下表面相对平坦。弯曲的上表面相当于文丘里管收敛中段的一半。当气流流过机翼上表面时，空气加速，静压降低。此时机翼下表面的静压大于上表面，这一压差就产生了机翼升力。伯努利原理与机翼升力的概念在本章「航空器飞行原理」一节中有更深入的讲解。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① was originally stated to explain the action of a liquid flowing …（被动 + 目的不定式 + 动名词复合结构）

**原句**：Bernoulli’s principle was originally stated to explain the action of a liquid flowing through the varying cross-sectional areas of tubes.

**拆解**：主语 Bernoulli’s principle，谓语 was originally stated 为被动；to explain … 是目的不定式作状语；the action of a liquid flowing through … 中，of 后是「名词 + 动名词」的复合结构，a liquid 是 flowing 的逻辑主语（= the action that a liquid has when it flows）。

**要点**：of + 名词 + 动名词 是技术文本的高频结构，译作「（某物）……的作用／过程」；不能把 a liquid 当成 of 的唯一宾语而漏掉 flowing。originally stated 用被动，因为重点在「这条原理当初是怎么被提出的」，施动者（伯努利）不重要。

### ② …, provided no energy is added to nor taken away from the fluid（provided 引导条件 + nor 并列被动）

**原句**：The static pressure of a fluid, liquid or gas, decreases at points where the velocity of the fluid increases, provided no energy is added to nor taken away from the fluid.

**拆解**：主句 The static pressure … decreases at points where the velocity of the fluid increases（where 引导定语从句修饰 points）；liquid or gas 是插入的同位语；provided (that) no energy is added to nor taken away from the fluid 是条件状语从句，其中 nor 连接两个被动谓语，add to 与 take away from 共用宾语 the fluid。

**要点**：provided / provided that 是「只要、条件是」，不是 provide 的过去式，这是手册里最容易被误读的词之一；同类还有 providing、on condition that。nor 用在否定并列中，等于 and not，此处 no energy 已含否定，故用 nor 而不用 or 更严谨。add to 与 take away from 方向相反，译「加入／取走」。

### ③ As the air flows through the carburetor, it goes through a venturi, where …（as 从句 + where 非限制性定语从句）

**原句**：As the air flows through the carburetor on its way to the engine, it goes through a venturi, where the static pressure is reduced.

**拆解**：As 引导时间／伴随状语从句（「当……时／随着……」）；主句 it goes through a venturi；where the static pressure is reduced 是非限制性定语从句修饰 a venturi，where 相当于 in which。

**要点**：as 与 when 的区别在于 as 强调「随着、同时进行」，译「当空气流经……时」最顺。where 引导定语从句时必须指代一个「场所性」的先行词（此处 venturi 是通道），不能指代抽象概念——若要指代情况，须用 in which case。on its way to the engine 是固定短语，比 to the engine 多一层「途中」。
