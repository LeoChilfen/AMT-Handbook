# Radiation

> 通用 Ch 05 · Radiation · 原文 PDF 第 170 页

## 1. 原文

Conduction and convection cannot wholly account for some of the phenomena associated with heat transfer. For example, the heat one feels when sitting in front of an open fire cannot be transferred by convection because the air currents are moving toward the fire. It cannot be transferred through conduction because the conductivity of the air is very small, and the cooler currents of air moving toward the fire would more than overcome the transfer of heat outward. Therefore, there must be some way for heat to travel across space other than by conduction and convection.

The existence of another process of heat transfer is still more evident when the heat from the sun is considered. Since conduction and convection take place only through some medium, such as a gas or a liquid, heat from the sun must reach the earth by another method, since space is an almost perfect vacuum. Radiation is the name given to this third method of heat transfer.

The term “radiation” refers to the continual emission of energy from the surface of all bodies. This energy is known as “radiant energy.” It is in the form of electromagnetic waves, radio waves, or x-rays, which are all alike except for a difference in wave length. These waves travel at the velocity of light and are transmitted through a vacuum more easily than through air because air absorbs some of them.

Most forms of energy can be traced back to the energy of sunlight. Sunlight is a form of radiant heat energy that travels through space to reach the earth. These electromagnetic heat waves are absorbed when they come in contact with nontransparent bodies. The result is that the motion of the molecules in the body is increased as indicated by an increase in the temperature of the body.

The differences between conduction, convection, and radiation may now be considered. First, although conduction and convection are extremely slow, radiation takes place at the speed of light. This fact is evident at the time of an eclipse of the sun when the shutting off of the heat from the sun takes place at the same time as the shutting off of the light. Second, radiant heat may pass through a medium without heating it. In application, the air inside a greenhouse may be much warmer than the glass through which the sun’s rays pass. Third, although heat transfer by conduction or convection may travel in roundabout routes, radiant heat always travels in a straight line. For example, radiation can be cut off with a screen placed between the source of heat and the body to be protected.

## 2. 中文翻译

传导和对流并不能完全解释与热传递有关的某些现象。例如，坐在明火前所感到的热，不可能靠对流传递，因为气流是朝火的方向流动的。这种热也不可能靠传导传递，因为空气的导热率很小，而朝火流动的较冷气流会远远抵消向外传递的热。因此，热要穿越空间，除了传导和对流之外，必定还有别的途径。

考虑来自太阳的热时，另一种热传递过程的存在就更加明显。既然传导和对流只能通过某种介质（例如气体或液体）发生，而空间几乎是完美的真空，那么太阳的热必定是通过另一种方式到达地球的。辐射就是这第三种热传递方式的名称。

「辐射」一词指的是所有物体表面持续不断地发射能量。这种能量称为「辐射能」。它以电磁波、无线电波或 X 射线的形式存在，这些波彼此相同，只是波长不同。这些波以光速传播，且穿过真空比穿过空气更容易，因为空气会吸收其中一部分。

大多数形式的能量都可以追溯到太阳光的能量。太阳光是一种辐射热能，它穿越空间到达地球。这些电磁热波与不透明物体接触时会被吸收。其结果是物体内分子的运动加剧，表现为物体温度升高。

现在可以讨论传导、对流和辐射之间的差别了。第一，传导和对流都极慢，而辐射以光速进行。这一点在日食时表现得很明显：太阳热的中断与光的中断同时发生。第二，辐射热可以穿过某种介质而不加热该介质。例如，温室内的空气可能比阳光穿过的玻璃暖和得多。第三，传导或对流的热传递可能走迂回的路线，而辐射热总是沿直线传播。例如，在热源与被保护物体之间放置一块遮挡板，就可以把辐射切断。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 省略关系代词的定语从句 + 分词短语作状语

**原句**：For example, the heat one feels when sitting in front of an open fire cannot be transferred by convection because the air currents are moving toward the fire.

**拆解**：主干 the heat … cannot be transferred by convection；one feels when sitting in front of an open fire 是省略关系代词 that/which 的定语从句，修饰 the heat（one feels 的宾语就是 the heat）；when sitting … 是「连词 + 现在分词」，相当于 when one sits …；because 引导原因状语从句。

**要点**：one feels 中的 one 是不定代词「人」，不是数词，整句可译「人所感到的热」。when + -ing 要求分词逻辑主语与主句一致，这里指 one。cannot be transferred 是被动，表示热「无法被传递」；can 的否定式 cannot 写作一个词。

### ② 情态动词 must 表推断 + 两个 since 原因状语从句

**原句**：Since conduction and convection take place only through some medium, such as a gas or a liquid, heat from the sun must reach the earth by another method, since space is an almost perfect vacuum.

**拆解**：句首 Since 引导原因状语从句；主句 heat from the sun must reach the earth by another method；句末 since space is an almost perfect vacuum 是第二个原因状语从句，补充说明理由；such as a gas or a liquid 是对 medium 的举例。

**要点**：must 在此不是「必须」而是「必定、一定」，表示逻辑推断，要靠上下文判断是哪种含义。同一句出现两个 since，前一个可译「既然」，后一个译「因为」，都表原因。take place 是不及物动词短语，没有被动形式，写成 is taken place 是常见错误。

### ③ although 引导的让步状语从句

**原句**：First, although conduction and convection are extremely slow, radiation takes place at the speed of light.

**拆解**：First 是列举标记；although 引导让步状语从句；主句 radiation takes place at the speed of light，at the speed of light 是速度状语。

**要点**：although 与 but 不能同时使用，中文的「虽然……但是……」译成英文时只能保留一个。at the speed of light 用介词 at，不用 in；同类还有 at a constant speed。主句用一般现在时陈述普遍事实，不用进行时。本节第二、第三点分别由 Second、Third 引导，读长段时可先抓这三个标记词定位结构。
