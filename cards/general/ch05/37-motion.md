# Motion

> 通用 Ch 05 · Motion · 原文 PDF 第 164 页

## 1. 原文

The study of the relationship between the motion of bodies or objects and the forces acting on them is often called the study of “force and motion.” In a more specific sense, the relationship between velocity, acceleration, and distance is known as kinematics.

## 2. 中文翻译

研究物体（body 或 object）的运动与作用在其上的力之间关系的学问，通常称为「力与运动」的研究。更具体地说，速度、加速度与距离之间的关系称为运动学（kinematics）。

| motion | n. | 运动 | 物体位置随时间的变化，本节的主题词 |

## 4. 语法解析

### ① The study … is often called the study of “force and motion”（被动定义句 + between 框架）

**原句**：The study of the relationship between the motion of bodies or objects and the forces acting on them is often called the study of “force and motion.”

**拆解**：主语中心词 The study，后接 of the relationship between A and B；A = the motion of bodies or objects，B = the forces acting on them（现在分词短语作后置定语）；谓语 is often called 为被动语态。

**要点**：主语长达十余词时，先抓住中心词 study，再一层层往后挂修饰语。between 后有两个并列项，靠 and 切分；be called 的被动句省略了执行者（人们）。

### ② the forces acting on them（现在分词短语作后置定语）

**原句**：the forces acting on them

**拆解**：acting on them 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 forces，相当于 which act on them；act on = 作用于。

**要点**：现在分词定语表主动、进行；改成过去分词 acted on 就变成「被作用」，意思相反。同类结构：the forces acting on the wing（作用在机翼上的力）。

### ③ In a more specific sense, … is known as kinematics（句首状语 + be known as）

**原句**：In a more specific sense, the relationship between velocity, acceleration, and distance is known as kinematics.

**拆解**：In a more specific sense 为句首状语；主语 the relationship between velocity, acceleration, and distance；谓语 is known as 为被动。

**要点**：be known as 意为「被称为」，be known for 则是「因……而闻名」，不要混用。in a more specific sense =「在更具体的意义上」，常用来把前文的宽泛说法收窄到术语定义。
