# First Class Lever

## 1. 原文

In the first class lever, the fulcrum is located between the effort and the resistance. As mentioned earlier, the seesaw is a good example of a lever, and it happens to be a first class lever. The amount of weight and the distance from the fulcrum can be varied to suit the need. Increasing the distance from the applied effort to the fulcrum, compared to the distance from the fulcrum to the weight being moved, increases the advantage provided by the lever. Crowbars, shears, and pliers are common examples of this class of lever.

The proper balance of an airplane is also a good example, with the center of lift on the wing being the pivot point, or fulcrum, and the weight fore and aft of this point being the effort and the resistance.

When calculating how much effort is required to lift a specific weight, or how much weight can be lifted by a specific effort, the following formula can be used.

Effort (E) × Effort Arm (L) = Resistance (R) × Resistance Arm (l)

What this formula really shows is the input torque (effort × effort arm) equals the output torque (resistance × resistance arm). This formula and concept apply to all three classes of levers and to all simple machines in general.

Example: A first class lever is to be used to lift a 500-lb weight. The distance from the weight to the fulcrum is 12 inches and from the fulcrum to the applied effort is 60 inches. How much force is required to lift the weight?

Effort (E) × Effort Arm (L) = Resistance (R) × Resistance Arm (l)

E × 60 in = 500 lb × 12 in E = 500 lb × 12 in ÷ 60 in E = 100 lb The mechanical advantage of the lever in this example would be:

Mechanical Advantage = Force Out ÷ Force In = 500 lb ÷ 100 lb = 5, or 5 to 1 An interesting thing to note with this example lever is if the applied effort moved down 10 inches, the weight on the other end would only move up 2 inches. Third class levers are easily recognized because the effort is applied between the fulcrum and the resistance. The retractable main landing gear on an airplane is a good example of a third class lever. The top of the landing gear, where it attaches to the airplane, is the pivot point. The wheel and brake assembly at the bottom of the landing gear is the resistance. The hydraulic actuator that makes the gear retract is attached somewhere in the middle, and that is the applied effort.

## 2. 中文翻译

在第一类杠杆中，支点位于施力点与阻力点之间。如前所述，跷跷板是一个很好的杠杆例子，而它恰好属于第一类杠杆。重量的大小以及距支点的距离，都可以按需要调整。与支点到被移动重物的距离相比，增大从施力点到支点的距离，就可增大杠杆所提供的优势。撬棍、剪切工具和钳子都是这一类杠杆的常见例子。

飞机的正确平衡也是一个很好的例子：机翼上的升力中心即为枢轴点（支点），而该点前后两侧的重量则分别为施力与阻力。

要计算举起某一特定重量需要多大的力，或者某一特定的力能举起多大的重量，可以使用下面的公式。

施力（E）× 施力臂（L）= 阻力（R）× 阻力臂（l）

这个公式真正说明的是：输入扭矩（施力 × 施力臂）等于输出扭矩（阻力 × 阻力臂）。这一公式与概念适用于全部三类杠杆，并且普遍适用于所有简单机械。

例：现要用一根第一类杠杆举起 500 lb 的重物。重物到支点的距离为 12 in，支点到施力点的距离为 60 in。举起该重物需要多大的力？

施力（E）× 施力臂（L）= 阻力（R）× 阻力臂（l）

E × 60 in = 500 lb × 12 in
E = 500 lb × 12 in ÷ 60 in
E = 100 lb

机械利益 = 输出力 ÷ 输入力 = 500 lb ÷ 100 lb = 5，即 5 : 1

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 动名词作主语 + compared to 插入语

**原句**：Increasing the distance from the applied effort to the fulcrum, compared to the distance from the fulcrum to the weight being moved, increases the advantage provided by the lever.

**拆解**：Increasing… 是动名词短语作主语，谓语 increases 用第三人称单数；compared to… 为过去分词短语作插入语（比较状语）；being moved 是现在分词被动态作后置定语，修饰 the weight；句末 provided by the lever 是过去分词短语后置修饰 the advantage。

**要点**：动名词短语作主语时，中间的插入语会把它和谓语隔得很远，读者容易误判主语，看到句末的 increases（单数）才能确认。compared to / compared with 是技术文献引出比较的固定插入语，位置灵活，汉语通常提前成「与……相比」。可迁移：the weight being lifted（正在被吊起的重物）、the force applied（所施加的力）。

### ② What 引导的主语从句

**原句**：What this formula really shows is the input torque (effort × effort arm) equals the output torque (resistance × resistance arm).

**拆解**：What this formula really shows 是主语从句（what = the thing that）；is 之后是表语，表语从句 the input torque … equals the output torque … 前省略了 that；括号里的 effort × effort arm 是对 input torque 的解释性同位语。

**要点**：what 从句作主语时谓语用单数；表语从句的 that 在技术文体中常省略，但省略后 is the input torque 连续出现，容易读成「等于输入扭矩」，翻译时务必把「是……等于……」的层次还原出来。可迁移：What matters is (that)…（要紧的是……）。

### ③ be to + 不定式：is to be used

**原句**：A first class lever is to be used to lift a 500-lb weight.

**拆解**：is to be used 是「be to + 不定式被动」，表示按题意、按计划「将被使用」；其后的 to lift 为目的状语，与前面的 to 不是同一个成分。

**要点**：例题中 be to do 表示「拟采用、按题意使用」，比 will be used 更强调既定安排。句子连着出现三个 to（is to / be used / to lift），读时要按「结构助动词—被动—目的」分层。同类：The lever is to be positioned…（该杠杆应按位置安放）、The bolt is to be torqued to…（该螺栓应按……力矩拧紧）。
