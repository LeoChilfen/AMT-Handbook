# Simple Machines

> 通用 Ch 05 · Simple Machines · 原文 PDF 第 157 页

## 1. 原文

A machine is any device with which work may be accomplished. For example, machines can be used for any of the following purposes, or combinations of these 5 purposes:

1. Machines are used to transform energy, as in the case of a generator transforming mechanical energy into electrical energy.

2. Machines are used to transfer energy from one place to another, as in the examples of the connecting rods, crankshaft, and reduction gears transferring energy from an aircraft’s engine to its propeller.

3. Machines are used to multiply force; for example, a system of pulleys may be used to lift a heavy load. The pulley system enables the load to be raised by exerting a force that is smaller than the weight of the load.

4. Machines can be used to multiply speed. A good example is the bicycle, by which speed can be gained by exerting a greater force.

5. Machines can be used to change the direction of a force. An example of this use is the flag hoist. A downward force on one side of the rope exerts an upward force on the other side, raising the flag toward the top of the pole.

There are only six simple machines. They are the lever, the pulley, the wheel and axle, the inclined plane, the screw, and the gear. Physicists, however, recognize only two basic principles in machines: the lever and the inclined plane.

The pulley (block and tackle), the wheel and axle, and gears operate on the machine principle of the lever. The wedge and the screw use the principle of the inclined plane.

An understanding of the principles of simple machines provides a necessary foundation for the study of compound machines, which are combinations of two or more simple machines.

## 2. 中文翻译

机械是任何可用以做功的装置。例如，机械可用于下列任一目的，或这些 5 类目的的组合：

1. 机械用于转换能量，如发电机把机械能转换为电能。

2. 机械用于把能量从一处传递到另一处，如连杆、曲轴和减速齿轮把能量从航空器发动机传递到螺旋桨。

3. 机械用于放大（倍增）力；例如，滑轮系统可用来吊起重载荷。滑轮系统使人能以小于载荷重量的力把载荷升起。

4. 机械可用于放大（倍增）速度。自行车就是一个好例子：施加更大的力便可获得更高的速度。

5. 机械可用于改变力的方向。旗杆升降装置（旗绳）就是一例：在绳子一侧施加向下的力，就在另一侧产生向上的力，把旗子升向杆顶。

简单机械只有六种，即杠杆、滑轮、轮轴、斜面、螺旋和齿轮。不过，物理学家在机械中只承认两条基本原理：杠杆和斜面。

滑轮（滑车组）、轮轴和齿轮按杠杆的机械原理工作。楔和螺旋则使用斜面的原理。

理解简单机械的原理，是学习复合机械（由两种或两种以上简单机械组合而成）的必要基础。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 介词 + which 引导的定语从句 + 情态被动

**原句**：A machine is any device with which work may be accomplished.

**拆解**：主语 A machine；系动词 is；表语 any device；with which work may be accomplished 是「介词 + which」引导的定语从句，修饰 device；从句内部为情态被动 may be accomplished。

**要点**：with which 的 with 是 accomplish work with a device 中的介词提前，不能只写 which。这类定义句的模板是 A is any device with which / by which / in which …，译作「A 是任何可以用以……的装置」。

### ② enable + 宾语 + 不定式被动 + by 动名词 + 比较从句

**原句**：The pulley system enables the load to be raised by exerting a force that is smaller than the weight of the load.

**拆解**：主句 The pulley system enables the load to be raised；enable sb/sth to do 中的不定式此处为被动 to be raised；by exerting a force 是方式状语；that is smaller than the weight of the load 是定语从句修饰 force，从句内含比较结构。

**要点**：enable 后接不定式，不能接动名词（不写 enables raising）。to be raised 用被动是因为载荷是「被升起」的；若写成 to raise 则成了载荷去升起别的东西。by + 动名词表示手段，与上一句的 with which 呼应。

### ③ be used to do 与 be used to doing 的区别 + as in the case of

**原句**：Machines are used to transform energy, as in the case of a generator transforming mechanical energy into electrical energy.

**拆解**：主句 Machines are used to transform energy，be used to do 表目的（被用来做某事）；as in the case of … 是举例状语，意为「正如……的情形」；a generator transforming mechanical energy into electrical energy 是「名词 + 现在分词」构成的复合结构，作 of 的宾语。

**要点**：be used to do（被用来做）与 be used to doing（习惯于做）形近义远，靠 to 后是原形还是 -ing 区分。transform A into B 是「把 A 转换成 B」的固定搭配，convert / change A into B 同义。

### ④ however 作插入语

**原句**：Physicists, however, recognize only two basic principles in machines: the lever and the inclined plane.

**拆解**：主语 Physicists；however 是插入的转折副词，用两个逗号隔开；谓语 recognize；宾语 only two basic principles；冒号后 the lever and the inclined plane 是对 principles 的具体列举。

**要点**：however 作「然而」时必须用逗号与句子其余部分隔开，不能像 but 那样直接连接两个分句（不写 Physicists however recognize … 而漏逗号）。冒号后列举与前面 only two 呼应，读句时冒号提示「具体是哪两个」。
