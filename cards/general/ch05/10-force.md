# Force

> 通用 Ch 05 · Force · 原文 PDF 第 154 页

## 1. 原文

Before the concept of work, power, or torque can be discussed, we need to understand what force means. According to the dictionary, force is the intensity of an impetus, or the intensity of an input. For example, if we apply a force to an object, the tendency will be for the object to move. Another way to look at it is that for work, power, or torque to exist, there must be a force that initiates the process.

The unit for force in the English system of measurement is pounds, and in the metric system it is newtons. One pound of force is equal to 4.448 newtons. When we calculate the thrust of a turbine engine, we use the formula “Force = Mass × Acceleration,” and the thrust of the engine is expressed in pounds. The GE90-115 turbofan engine (power plant for the Boeing 777-300), for example, has 115,000 pounds of thrust.

## 2. 中文翻译

在讨论功、功率或扭矩的概念之前，我们需要理解力的含义。按词典的解释，力是推动的强度，即输入的强度。例如，若我们对一个物体施加力，该物体就会有运动的趋势。换一种看法：要使功、功率或扭矩存在，必须有一个启动这一过程的力。

英制中力的单位是磅，公制中则是牛顿。1 磅的力等于 4.448 牛顿。当我们计算涡轮发动机的推力时，使用「Force = Mass × Acceleration」这一公式（力 = 质量 × 加速度），发动机的推力以磅表示。例如，GE90-115 涡扇发动机（波音 777-300 的动力装置）具有 115,000 磅的推力。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① before 从句中的情态被动

**原句**：Before the concept of work, power, or torque can be discussed, we need to understand what force means.

**拆解**：Before 引导时间状语从句，从句主语 the concept of work, power, or torque，谓语 can be discussed（情态被动）；主句 we need to understand what force means，what 引导宾语从句作 understand 的宾语。

**要点**：before 从句用情态被动，是因为「讨论」的执行者泛化、不必点明；中文常译成无主句「在讨论……之前，需要……」。what force means 中 what 作 means 的宾语，不能说 what does force mean（那是疑问句语序）。

### ② that 表语从句 + there be + for … to do

**原句**：Another way to look at it is that for work, power, or torque to exist, there must be a force that initiates the process.

**拆解**：主语 Another way to look at it（不定式作定语）；is that … 中 that 引导表语从句；表语从句内 for work, power, or torque to exist 是「for + 名词 + 不定式」作条件状语，其后 there must be a force that initiates the process 是 there be + 情态 + 定语从句。

**要点**：the point is that … / another way to look at it is that … 用来解释前文，that 不能省。for … to do 在此表「若要……」，相当于 if … is to …。there must be 是 there be 加情态动词，不能写成 there must have。

### ③ 同一句中主动与被动并列

**原句**：When we calculate the thrust of a turbine engine, we use the formula “Force = Mass × Acceleration,” and the thrust of the engine is expressed in pounds.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；主句由 and 连接两个分句，前一分句 we use the formula …（主动），后一分句 the thrust of the engine is expressed in pounds（被动），主语从 we 换成 the thrust。

**要点**：主动、被动并用，是为了把叙述重点从「人做什么」转到「结果用什么单位表示」，这是技术写作的常见切换。be expressed in + 单位表示「用……单位表示」，介词是 in 不是 by。
