# Charles’ Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · Charles’ Law · 原文 PDF 第 175 页

## 1. 原文

The French scientist, Jacques Charles, provided much of the foundation for the modern kinetic theory of gases. He found that all gases expand and contract in direct proportion to the change in the absolute temperature, provided the pressure is held constant. As a formula, this law is shown as follows:

V olume 1 × Absolute Temperature 2 = V olume 2 × Absolute Temperature 1 or V1T2 = V2T1 Charles’ law also works if the volume is held constant, and pressure and temperature are the variables. In this case, the formula would be as follows:

P1T2 = P2T1 For this second formula, pressure and temperature must be in the absolute.

Example: A 15-ft3 cylinder of oxygen is at a temperature of 70 °F and a pressure of 750 psig. The cylinder is placed in the sun and the temperature of the oxygen increases to 140 °F.

What would be the new pressure in psig?

70 degrees Fahrenheit = 530 degrees Rankine 140 degrees Fahrenheit = 600 degrees Rankine 750 psig + 14.7 = 764.7 psia P1T2 = P2T1 764.7 (600) = P2 (530)

P2 = 764.7 (600) ÷ 530 P2 = 865.7 psia P2 = 851 psig

## 2. 中文翻译

法国科学家雅克·查理为现代气体动理论提供了大部分基础。他发现：在压力保持恒定的条件下，所有气体都按绝对温度的变化成正比例地膨胀和收缩。该定律用公式表示如下：

体积 1 × 绝对温度 2 = 体积 2 × 绝对温度 1，或 V1T2 = V2T1。若体积保持恒定、以压力和温度为变量，查理定律同样成立。此时公式如下：

P1T2 = P2T1。对于第二个公式，压力和温度都必须取绝对值（绝对压力与绝对温度）。

例：一只 15 ft3 的氧气瓶，温度为 70 °F，压力为 750 psig。把该瓶置于阳光下，氧气温度升高到 140 °F。

新的压力（以 psig 计）将是多少？

70 华氏度 = 530 兰氏度；140 华氏度 = 600 兰氏度；750 psig + 14.7 = 764.7 psia；P1T2 = P2T1；764.7 (600) = P2 (530)

P2 = 764.7 (600) ÷ 530；P2 = 865.7 psia；P2 = 851 psig。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① in direct proportion to + provided 条件从句

**原句**：He found that all gases expand and contract in direct proportion to the change in the absolute temperature, provided the pressure is held constant.

**拆解**：He found + that 引导的宾语从句；从句主语 all gases，谓语为并列的 expand and contract；in direct proportion to the change in the absolute temperature 是方式状语；provided the pressure is held constant 是条件状语从句，provided 后省略 that。

**要点**：in direct proportion to（与……成正比）后面接名词或名词短语，不接句子；要接句子得改用 in proportion as … 或 the + 比较级结构。provided 条件从句用一般现在时表一般条件，与 if 一致。is held constant 是「被保持恒定」的被动，动作执行者被省略。

### ② would 表假设推算

**原句**：What would be the new pressure in psig?

**拆解**：疑问词 What 作主语，would be 为「would + be」；the new pressure in psig 为表语，in psig 说明读数所用单位；整句是省略了条件从句（若温度升到 140 °F）的虚拟问句。

**要点**：技术例题常用 would 而非 will，因为结果取决于题设条件，并非必然发生。译成中文时不必译出「将会……的话」，直接写「新的压力是多少」即可。同类还有 the formula would be as follows，would 使语气委婉、不把结论说死。

### ③ must + be + 介词短语（must be in the absolute）

**原句**：For this second formula, pressure and temperature must be in the absolute.

**拆解**：For this second formula 是范围状语；主语 pressure and temperature（并列，谓语用复数形式，但 must 无变化）；谓语 must be；表语是介词短语 in the absolute（表状态）；the absolute 是「the + 形容词」名词化，指绝对压力、绝对温度。

**要点**：in the absolute 是手册里的固定说法，意为「用绝对单位（绝对压力、绝对温度）」，与之相对的是 in gauge（用表压）。must be in … 中的 be 不能换成 get；这类「must + be + 状态介词短语」的句子译成中文时，常补出动词「必须取……／必须是……」。

### ④ 被动语态在例题步骤中的省略表达

**原句**：The cylinder is placed in the sun and the temperature of the oxygen increases to 140 °F.

**拆解**：前一分句 The cylinder is placed in the sun 为一般现在时被动（省略了执行者）；后一分句 the temperature of the oxygen increases to 140 °F 为主动，increase to 表示「升高到」（结果），increases by 才是「升高了」（幅度）。

**要点**：例题里常用一般现在时被动描述操作（is placed、is reduced），因为步骤可重复、不限定时间。increase to / increase by、rise to / rise by 的介词差别必须分清：to 后接终值，by 后接增量。
