# Matter

## 1. 原文

Matter is the foundation, or the building blocks, for any discussion of physics. According to the dictionary, matter is what all things are made of; whatever occupies space, has mass, and is perceptible to the senses in some way. According to the Law of Conservation, matter cannot be created or destroyed, although it is possible to change its physical state. When liquid gasoline vaporizes and mixes with air, and then burns, it might seem that this piece of matter has disappeared and no longer exists. Although it no longer exists in the state of liquid gasoline, the matter still exists in the form of the gases given off by the burning fuel.

### Characteristics of Matter

#### Mass & Weight

Mass is a measure of the quantity of matter in an object. In other words, how many molecules are in the object, how many atoms are in the object, or to be more specific, how many protons, neutrons, and electrons are in the object. The mass of an object does not change regardless of where you take it in the universe, or with a change of state. The only way to change the mass of an object is to add or take away atoms. Mathematically, mass can be stated as follows:

Mass = Weight ÷ Acceleration due to gravity

The acceleration due to gravity here on earth is 32.2 feet per second per second (32.2 fps/s). An object weighing 32.2 pounds (lb) here on earth is said to have a mass of 1 slug. A slug is a quantity of mass that will accelerate at a rate of 1 ft. /s^2 when a force of 1 pound is applied. In other words, under standard atmospheric condition, which is that gravity is equal to 32.2 fps/s, a mass of one slug would be equal to 32.2 lb.

Weight is a measure of the pull from gravity acting on the mass of an object. The more mass an object has, the more it will weigh under the earth’s force of gravity. The only way for an object to be weightless is for gravity to go away, because it is not possible for the mass of an object to disappear. When we view astronauts on the space shuttle, it appears that they are weightless. Even though the shuttle is far from the surface of the earth, the force of gravity has not completely gone away, and the astronauts are not weightless. The astronauts and the space shuttle are actually in a state of free fall, so relative to the shuttle the astronauts appear to be weightless. Mathematically, weight can be stated as follows:

Weight = Mass × Gravity

#### Attraction

Attraction is mutual force acting between particles of matter, which tends to draw them together. Sir Isaac Newton called this the “Law of Universal Gravitation.” Newton showed how each particle of matter attracts every other particle, how people are bound to the earth, and how the planets are attracted in the solar system.

#### Porosity

Porosity means having pores or spaces where smaller particles may fit when a mixture takes place. This is sometimes referred to as granular—consisting or appearing to consist of small grains or granules.

#### Impenetrability

Impenetrability means that no two objects can occupy the same place at the same time. Thus, two portions of matter cannot at the same time occupy the same space.

#### Density

The density of a substance is its weight per unit volume. The unit volume selected for use in the English system of measurement is 1 cubic foot (ft³). In the metric system, it is 1 cubic centimeter (cm³). Therefore, density is expressed in pounds per cubic foot (lb⁄ft³) or in grams per cubic centimeter (g⁄cm³).

To find the density of a substance, its weight and volume must be known. Its weight is then divided by its volume to find the weight per unit volume. For example, the liquid which fills a certain container weighs 1,497.6 lb. The container is 4 ft long, 3 ft wide and 2 ft deep. Its volume is 24 ft³ (4 ft. × 3 ft. × 2 ft.). If 24 ft³ of liquid weighs 1,497.6 lb, then 1 ft³ weighs 1,497.6 ÷ 24, or 62.4 lb. Therefore, the density of the liquid is 62.4 lb/ft³. This is the density of water at 4 °C (Centigrade) and is usually used as the standard for comparing densities of other substances. In the metric system, the density of water is 1 g⁄cm³. The standard temperature of 4 °C is used when measuring the density of liquids and solids. Changes in temperature will not change the weight of a substance, but will change the volume of the substance by expansion or contraction, thus changing its weight per unit volume.

The procedure for finding density applies to all substances; however, it is necessary to consider the pressure when finding the density of gases. Pressure is more critical when measuring the density of gases than it is for other substances. The density of a gas increases in direct proportion to the pressure exerted on it. Standard conditions for the measurement of the densities of gases have been established at 0 °C for temperature and a pressure of 76 cm of mercury (Hg), which is the average pressure of the atmosphere at sea level. Density is computed based on these conditions for all gases.

#### Specific Gravity

It is often necessary to compare the density of one substance with another substance. For this purpose, a standard is needed. Water is the standard that physicists have chosen to use when comparing the densities of all liquids and solids. For gases, air is most commonly used, but hydrogen is also sometimes used as a standard for gases. In physics, the word “specific” implies a ratio. Thus, specific gravity is calculated by comparing the weight of a definite volume of the given substance with the weight of an equal volume of water. The terms “specific weight” or “specific density” are sometimes used to express this ratio.

The following formulas are used to find the specific gravity of liquids and solids.

Specific Gravity = Weight of the substance / Weight of an equal volume of water

or

Specific Gravity = Density of the substance / Density of water

The same formulas are used to find the density of gases by substituting air or hydrogen for water.

Specific gravity is not expressed in units, but as pure numbers. For example, if a certain hydraulic fluid has a specific gravity of 0.8, 1 ft³ of the liquid weighs 0.8 times as much as 1 ft³ of water: 62.4 times 0.8, or 49.92 lb.

Specific gravity and density are independent of the size of the sample under consideration and depend only upon the substance of which it is made.

A device called a hydrometer is used for measuring specific gravity of liquids. This device consists of a tubular glass float contained in a larger glass tube. The larger glass tube provides the container for the liquid. A rubber suction bulb draws the liquid up into the container. There must be enough liquid raising the float to prevent it from touching the bottom. The float is weighted and has a vertically graduated scale. To determine specific gravity, the scale is read at the surface of the liquid in which the float is immersed. An indication of 1000 is read when the float is immersed in pure water. When immersed in a liquid of greater density, the float rises, indicating a greater specific gravity. For liquids of lesser density, the float sinks, indicating a lower specific gravity.

An example of the use of the hydrometer is to determine the specific gravity of the electrolyte (battery liquid) in an aircraft battery. When a battery is discharged, the calibrated float immersed in the electrolyte will indicate approximately 1150. The indication of a charged battery is between 1275 and 1310. The values 1150, 1275, and 1310 represent 1.150, 1.275, and 1.310. The electrolyte in a discharged battery is 1.15 times denser than water, and in a charged battery 1.275 to 1.31 times denser than water.

## 2. 中文翻译

物质是讨论任何物理问题的根基，也就是构成万物的基本单元。按词典的定义，物质是一切事物由之构成的东西；凡占据空间、具有质量、并以某种方式能为感官所感知者，皆为物质。根据守恒定律，物质既不能被创造，也不能被消灭，尽管其物理状态可以改变。当液态汽油汽化并与空气混合、随后燃烧时，看起来这部分物质已经消失、不复存在了。虽然它不再以液态汽油的状态存在，但物质仍以燃烧的燃料所释放的气体形式存在着。

### 物质的特性

#### 质量与重量

质量是衡量物体中物质多少的尺度。换言之，就是物体中有多少个分子、多少个原子；更具体地说，就是物体中有多少个质子、中子和电子。无论把物体带到宇宙中的何处，还是其状态发生变化，物体的质量都不会改变。改变物体质量的唯一办法是增加或取出原子。用数学式表示，质量可写成：

质量 = 重量 ÷ 重力加速度

地球上的重力加速度为 32.2 英尺每二次方秒（32.2 fps/s）。在地球上重 32.2 磅（lb）的物体，其质量称为 1 斯勒格（slug）。1 斯勒格是这样一份质量：当施加 1 磅的力时，它以 1 英尺/秒² 的加速度加速。换言之，在标准大气条件下（即重力加速度等于 32.2 fps/s），1 斯勒格的质量等于 32.2 lb。

重量是衡量重力作用于物体质量所产生的拉力的尺度。物体的质量越大，在地球重力作用下就越重。物体要变得没有重量，唯一的办法是重力消失，因为物体的质量不可能消失。我们看到航天飞机上的航天员时，会觉得他们处于失重状态。尽管航天飞机远离地球表面，重力并未完全消失，航天员也并非没有重量。航天员和航天飞机实际上处于自由落体状态，因此相对于航天飞机而言，航天员看起来是失重的。用数学式表示，重量可写成：

重量 = 质量 × 重力

#### 引力

引力是物质粒子之间相互作用的力，它倾向于把粒子拉在一起。艾萨克·牛顿爵士称之为“万有引力定律”。牛顿阐明了每个物质粒子如何吸引其他每一个粒子，人如何被束缚在地球上，行星又如何被吸引在太阳系中。

#### 多孔性

多孔性是指物质具有孔隙或空隙，在形成混合物时较小的颗粒可以嵌入其中。这有时也称为粒状——由细小颗粒构成，或看起来由细小颗粒构成。

#### 不可入性

不可入性是指任何两个物体不能在同一时间占据同一位置。因此，两部分物质不能同时占据同一空间。

#### 密度

物质的密度是其单位体积的重量。英制计量中选用的单位体积是 1 立方英尺（ft³），公制中是 1 立方厘米（cm³）。因此，密度用磅每立方英尺（lb/ft³）或克每立方厘米（g/cm³）表示。

要求出物质的密度，必须知道它的重量和体积。然后用重量除以体积，求出单位体积的重量。例如，装满某一容器的液体重 1,497.6 lb。该容器长 4 ft、宽 3 ft、深 2 ft，其体积为 24 ft³（4 ft × 3 ft × 2 ft）。若 24 ft³ 的液体重 1,497.6 lb，则 1 ft³ 重 1,497.6 ÷ 24，即 62.4 lb。因此，该液体的密度为 62.4 lb/ft³。这是水在 4 °C（摄氏）时的密度，通常用作比较其他物质密度的标准。在公制中，水的密度为 1 g/cm³。测量液体和固体的密度时采用 4 °C 这一标准温度。温度变化不会改变物质的重量，但会因膨胀或收缩而改变物质的体积，从而改变其单位体积的重量。

求密度的方法适用于所有物质；但在求气体密度时，必须考虑压力。测量气体密度时，压力比测量其他物质密度时更为关键。气体的密度与施加于它的压力成正比增加。测量气体密度的标准条件已定为温度 0 °C、压力 76 cm 汞柱（Hg），这也是海平面处大气的平均压力。所有气体的密度均按这些条件计算。

#### 比重

常常需要把一种物质的密度与另一种物质相比较。为此，需要一个标准。物理学家选用水作为比较所有液体和固体密度的标准。对于气体，最常用的是空气，但氢有时也被用作气体的标准。在物理学中，“specific”一词表示比值。因此，比重是通过把一定体积的给定物质的重量与同体积水的重量相比较来计算的。“specific weight”（比重）或“specific density”（比重密度）这两个术语有时也用来表达这一比值。

求液体和固体比重时使用以下公式。

比重 = 物质重量 ÷ 同体积水的重量

或

比重 = 物质密度 ÷ 水的密度。

求气体的相应数值时，以空气或氢代替水，所用公式相同。

比重不带单位，而用纯数表示。例如，某种液压油的比重为 0.8，则 1 ft³ 该液体的重量是 1 ft³ 水的 0.8 倍：62.4 × 0.8，即 49.92 lb。

比重和密度与所考察样品的尺寸无关，只取决于构成该样品的物质。

测量液体比重所用的装置叫比重计。它由一个装在较大玻璃管内的管状玻璃浮子构成。较大的玻璃管用作盛装液体的容器。橡胶吸气球把液体吸升到容器中。液面必须足够高，使浮子浮起而不触及底部。浮子经过配重，并带有竖直刻度。测定比重时，在读数的刻度处读取液面位置。浮子浸在纯水中时，读数为 1000。浸入密度较大的液体时，浮子上浮，读数表示较大的比重。对于密度较小的液体，浮子下沉，读数表示较小的比重。

比重计的一个应用实例是测定飞机电瓶中电解液（电池液）的比重。电瓶放电时，浸在电解液中的标定浮子读数约为 1150。电瓶充足电时的读数在 1275 到 1310 之间。数值 1150、1275 和 1310 分别表示 1.150、1.275 和 1.310。放电电瓶中电解液的密度是水的 1.15 倍，充足电的电瓶则为水的 1.275 到 1.31 倍。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 同位语与定义句骨架
**原句**：Matter is the foundation, or the building blocks, for any discussion of physics.
**拆解**：主语 Matter + 系动词 is + 表语 the foundation；逗号之间的 or the building blocks 是同位语，换一种说法解释 foundation；for any discussion of physics 为介词短语，说明「对什么而言」。
**要点**：or 引导的同位语常译为「也就是……」，书面语中用来给出更通俗的等价说法；X is the … for Y 是下定义的标准骨架，不要把 for 短语误当成 foundation 的定语而译成「物理讨论的基础」。

### ② 现在分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Weight is a measure of the pull from gravity acting on the mass of an object.
**拆解**：主语 Weight + 系动词 is + 表语 a measure of the pull；from gravity 为介词短语修饰 pull；acting on the mass of an object 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 gravity，相当于 which acts on…
**要点**：现在分词作定语表主动、进行，此处重力是施动者；若改成过去分词 acted on 就变成被动，语义颠倒。后置定语紧跟名词，长句里要与谓语区分开，判断依据是看它有没有自己的主语。

### ③ the + 比较级，the + 比较级
**原句**：The more mass an object has, the more it will weigh under the earth’s force of gravity.
**拆解**：前一分句 The more mass an object has 表条件，宾语 mass 被 the more 提前，正常语序是 an object has more mass；后一分句 the more it will weigh 为主句，under the earth’s force of gravity 为状语。
**要点**：这是「越……越……」的固定句式，两个 the 都不能省且必须紧跟比较级。前句因宾语前置而看似倒装，实为比较结构的要求；技术文本用它表达正比关系，比 if…then 简洁。

### ④ 不定式作目的状语 + 情态动词被动
**原句**：To find the density of a substance, its weight and volume must be known.
**拆解**：句首 To find the density of a substance 为不定式短语作目的状语；主句主语 its weight and volume 为并列结构，谓语 must be known 为情态动词加被动语态。
**要点**：目的状语置于句首时用逗号与主句隔开，其逻辑主语是隐含的「我们」；must be known 隐去执行者，强调「必须已知」。易错点是把 To find 误当主语，结果找不到主句谓语。

### ⑤ 介词 + which 引导的定语从句
**原句**：To determine specific gravity, the scale is read at the surface of the liquid in which the float is immersed.
**拆解**：To determine specific gravity 为目的状语；主句 the scale is read 为被动结构，at the surface of the liquid 为地点状语；in which the float is immersed 是「介词 + which」定语从句，修饰 the liquid。
**要点**：in which 相当于 in the liquid，介词不能丢，因为 immerse 的固定搭配是 be immersed in；正式技术文本用「介词 + which」避免把介词留在句末，换成 where 则只表地点、体现不出 in 的搭配。

### ⑥ 让步状语从句 + 并列主句
**原句**：Even though the shuttle is far from the surface of the earth, the force of gravity has not completely gone away, and the astronauts are not weightless.
**拆解**：Even though 引导让步状语从句，主语 the shuttle，系动词 is，表语 far from the surface of the earth；主句由 and 连接两个并列分句：the force of gravity has not completely gone away 和 the astronauts are not weightless。
**要点**：even though 比 although 语气更强，且不能与 but 连用；本句用「重力未消失」与「航天员并非失重」两个并列事实，纠正「远离地球就失重」的直觉，翻译时让步从句常译作「尽管……」。
