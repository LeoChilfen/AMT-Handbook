# Shock Waves

> 通用 Ch 05 · Shock Waves · 原文 PDF 第 197 页

## 1. 原文

Sound coming from an airplane is the result of the air being disturbed as the airplane moves through it, and the resulting pressure waves that radiate out from the source of the disturbance. For a slow-moving airplane, the pressure waves travel out ahead of the airplane, traveling at the speed of sound. When the speed of the airplane reaches the speed of sound, however, the pressure waves, or sound energy, cannot get away from the airplane. At this point the sound energy starts to pile up, initially on the top of the wing, and eventually attaching itself to the wing leading and trailing edges. This piling up of sound energy is called a shock wave.

If the shock waves reach the ground, and cross the path of a person, they will be heard as a sonic boom. Figure 5-76A shows a wing in slow speed flight, with many disturbances on the wing generating sound pressure waves that are radiating outward. View B is the wing of an airplane in supersonic flight, with the sound pressure waves piling up toward the wing leading edge.

Oblique Shock Wave An airplane that is designed to fly supersonic will have very sharp edged surfaces, in order to have the least amount of drag.

When the airplane is in supersonic flight, the sharp leading edge and trailing edge of the wing will have shock waves attach to them. These shock waves are known as oblique shock waves. Behind an oblique shock wave the velocity of the air is lower, but still supersonic, and the static pressure and density are higher. Figure 5-78 shows an oblique shock wave on the leading and trailing edges of a supersonic airfoil.

Expansion Wave Earlier in the discussion of high-speed aerodynamics, it was stated that air at supersonic speed acts like a compressible fluid. For this reason, supersonic air, when given the opportunity, wants to expand outward. When supersonic air is flowing over the top of a wing, and the wing surface turns away from the direction of flow, the air will expand and follow the new direction. An expansion wave will occur at the point where the direction of flow changes. Behind the expansion wave the velocity increases, and the static pressure and density decrease. An expansion wave is not a shock wave.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机发出的声音，是空气随飞机在其中运动而被扰动、以及由此向外辐射的压力波共同造成的结果。对于慢速飞行的飞机，压力波以声速传到飞机前方。然而，当飞机的速度达到声速时，压力波（也就是声能）就无法跑到飞机前面去了。此时声能开始堆积，最初堆积在机翼上表面，最终附着到机翼的前缘和后缘上。这种声能的堆积称为激波。

如果激波到达地面并扫过人的路径，人就会听到声爆。图 5-76A 示出低速飞行中的机翼，机翼上许多扰动产生向外辐射的声压波。B 图是超声速飞行中的机翼，声压波向机翼前缘堆积。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 动名词复合结构：the air being disturbed

**原句**：Sound coming from an airplane is the result of the air being disturbed as the airplane moves through it, and the resulting pressure waves that radiate out from the source of the disturbance.

**拆解**：主语 Sound coming from an airplane（coming 为现在分词作定语）；表语 the result of the air being disturbed（of + 动名词复合结构「名词 + 被动式动名词」）；as the airplane moves through it 是时间状语；and 连接另一个名词短语 the resulting pressure waves that radiate out from the source of the disturbance（that 引导定语从句）。

**要点**：the air being disturbed 中的 being disturbed 是动名词的被动式，逻辑主语 the air 放在其前；写成 the air is disturbed 就变成了从句，句子结构随即崩塌。the resulting pressure waves 的 resulting 是分词形容词化的用法，意为「由此产生的」。

### ② when 从句 + 否定情态 + 同位语

**原句**：When the speed of the airplane reaches the speed of sound, however, the pressure waves, or sound energy, cannot get away from the airplane.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；however 是插入的转折副词，前后用逗号隔开；主语 the pressure waves 后接 or sound energy 作同位语（换一种说法）；谓语 cannot get away from。

**要点**：however 插在句中时两侧都要逗号，不能像 but 那样直接连接两个分句。or 在此不是「或者」而是「即、也就是」，用来给出同义术语，是技术文本解释生僻词的标准手段：the pressure waves, or sound energy。

### ③ 动名词短语作主语 + be called 下定义

**原句**：This piling up of sound energy is called a shock wave.

**拆解**：主语 this piling up of sound energy（this + 动名词 + of 短语）是动名词短语，指代前句描述的过程；谓语 is called 为被动，表语 a shock wave。

**要点**：piling up 由动词短语 pile up 变来，作名词后仍保持「堆积」的动作含义，谓语用单数 is。给概念下定义时手册习惯用 is called / is known as / is referred to as + 名称，三种说法可互换。
