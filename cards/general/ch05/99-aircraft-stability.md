# Aircraft Stability

> 通用 Ch 05 · Aircraft Stability · 原文 PDF 第 190 页

## 1. 原文

When an airplane is in straight-and-level flight at a constant velocity, all the forces acting on the airplane are in equilibrium.

If that straight-and-level flight is disrupted by a disturbance in the air, such as wake turbulence, the airplane might pitch up or down, yaw left or right, or go into a roll. If the airplane has what is characterized as stability, once the disturbance goes away, the airplane will return to a state of equilibrium.

## 2. 中文翻译

当飞机以恒定速度作直线平飞（straight-and-level flight）时，作用在飞机上的所有力都处于平衡（equilibrium）状态。

如果这种直线平飞被空中的扰动（例如尾流湍流）所打破，飞机可能会上仰或下俯、左偏航或右偏航，或者进入横滚。如果飞机具有被称为稳定性（stability）的特性，那么一旦扰动消失，飞机就会回到平衡状态。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词作后置定语 + be in + 抽象名词

**原句**：When an airplane is in straight-and-level flight at a constant velocity, all the forces acting on the airplane are in equilibrium.

**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句；主句主语 all the forces，acting on the airplane 是现在分词短语作后置定语（= which act on the airplane）；谓语 are in equilibrium，其中 in equilibrium 是「介词 + 抽象名词」构成的表语。

**要点**：现在分词短语作定语表主动、经常性动作；若改成过去分词 acted on 就变成被动，语义逆转。be in + 抽象名词（in equilibrium／in motion／in flight）表示状态，是技术英语的高频结构。

### ② 条件从句 + such as 插入 + 共用情态动词的并列谓语

**原句**：If that straight-and-level flight is disrupted by a disturbance in the air, such as wake turbulence, the airplane might pitch up or down, yaw left or right, or go into a roll.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，从句谓语 is disrupted 为被动，by 引出施动者；such as wake turbulence 是插入的同位举例，补充说明 disturbance；主句 the airplane might pitch up or down, yaw left or right, or go into a roll，三个并列谓语共用 might，后两项之间用 or 表示「或」。

**要点**：might 表示可能性，比 may 更不确定、语气更客观，适合描述非必然现象。多个并列动词共用情态动词时不必重复 might。such as 后接具体例子，译作「例如……之类的」放在被说明词之后；三个选项用逗号分隔、以 or 收尾。

### ③ what 引导的宾语从句 + 主将从现

**原句**：If the airplane has what is characterized as stability, once the disturbance goes away, the airplane will return to a state of equilibrium.

**拆解**：If 从句中 has 的宾语是 what is characterized as stability（what 引导的宾语从句，what 在从句中作主语）；once 引导时间状语从句，用一般现在时；主句用 will return。

**要点**：两个从句（if／once）都用一般现在时表将来，主句用 will，是典型的「主将从现」。what is characterized as... 意为「被称为……的那种东西」，characterize as（把……描述为）常用被动。once 兼有「一旦」之意，比 when 更强调条件性。
