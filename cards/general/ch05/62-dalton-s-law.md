# Dalton’s Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · Dalton’s Law · 原文 PDF 第 175 页

## 1. 原文

If a mixture of two or more gases that do not combine chemically is placed in a container, each gas expands throughout the total space and the absolute pressure of each gas is reduced to a lower value, called its partial pressure. This

reduction is in accordance with Boyle’s law. The pressure of the mixed gases is equal to the sum of the partial pressures.

This fact was discovered by Dalton, an English physicist, and is set forth in Dalton’s law: “A mixture of several gases which do not react chemically exerts a pressure equal to the sum of the pressures which the several gases would exert separately if each were allowed to occupy the entire space alone at the given temperature.”

## 2. 中文翻译

如果把两种或两种以上、彼此不发生化学结合的气体混合物装入容器，那么每种气体都会膨胀到整个空间，而每种气体的绝对压力都会降到一个较低的值，这个值称为该气体的分压。

这一降低遵循玻意耳定律。混合气体的压力等于各分压之和。

这一事实由英国物理学家道尔顿发现，并载明于道尔顿定律之中：「几种不发生化学反应的气体的混合物所施加的压力，等于这几种气体在给定温度下各自单独占据整个空间时所施加的压力之和。」

## 4. 语法解析

### ① If a mixture … is placed …（条件从句中嵌套定语从句，注意主谓一致）

**原句**：If a mixture of two or more gases that do not combine chemically is placed in a container, each gas expands throughout the total space and the absolute pressure of each gas is reduced to a lower value, called its partial pressure.

**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，从句主语是 a mixture（核心词），of two or more gases 与 that do not combine chemically 都修饰 mixture，因此谓语用单数 is placed；从句中 that 定语从句的谓语 do not combine 却是复数，因为先行词是 gases。主句由两个并列分句构成：each gas expands … 和 the absolute pressure of each gas is reduced to a lower value；called its partial pressure 是过去分词短语作后置定语。

**要点**：这是技术英语里典型的「主谓被长定语隔开」结构——看到 that do not combine chemically 时容易误以为谓语该用复数，实际谓语跟着 mixture 走。读法：先划掉 of… 和 that… 两段修饰语，剩下的 a mixture is placed 才是骨架。并列主句中前后两个分句一主动一被动（expands / is reduced），是手册描述「一变二」过程的常见写法。

### ② …, called its partial pressure（过去分词短语作非限制性定语）

**原句**：…the absolute pressure of each gas is reduced to a lower value, called its partial pressure.

**拆解**：called its partial pressure 是过去分词短语，逻辑上等于 which is called its partial pressure，补充说明 a lower value，与主句之间用逗号隔开。

**要点**：called / known as / referred to as 引出的过去分词短语，是手册给术语下定义的固定手段，译作「称为……」。它没有主语和时态，不能写成 calling（那会变成主动、指主语在命名）。同类：a tube constructed in this manner is called a venturi。

### ③ if each were allowed to occupy the entire space alone（虚拟语气 + 被动）

**原句**：A mixture of several gases which do not react chemically exerts a pressure equal to the sum of the pressures which the several gases would exert separately if each were allowed to occupy the entire space alone at the given temperature.

**拆解**：主句主语 A mixture of several gases（带定语从句 which do not react chemically），谓语 exerts，宾语 a pressure，equal to the sum of the pressures 作后置定语；which the several gases would exert separately 是定语从句修饰 pressures；if each were allowed … 是条件从句，were allowed 用虚拟语气（与「实际上各自只占一部分空间」这一事实相反），would exert 与之呼应。

**要点**：描述理想化假想条件（实际做不到）时用 were + 过去分词、主句用 would + 动词原形；这里的 each were 不因 each 是单数而写成 was，虚拟语气一律用 were。allowed to do 是被动 + 不定式，「被允许做……」；occupy the entire space alone 是「单独占据整个空间」，正是道尔顿定律的思维实验。
