# Dalton’s Law

> 通用 Ch 05 · Dalton’s Law · 原文 PDF 第 175 页

## 1. 原文

If a mixture of two or more gases that do not combine chemically is placed in a container, each gas expands throughout the total space and the absolute pressure of each gas is reduced to a lower value, called its partial pressure. This

reduction is in accordance with Boyle’s law. The pressure of the mixed gases is equal to the sum of the partial pressures.

This fact was discovered by Dalton, an English physicist, and is set forth in Dalton’s law: “A mixture of several gases which do not react chemically exerts a pressure equal to the sum of the pressures which the several gases would exert separately if each were allowed to occupy the entire space alone at the given temperature.”
