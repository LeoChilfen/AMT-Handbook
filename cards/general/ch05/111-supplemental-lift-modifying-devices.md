# Supplemental Lift-Modifying Devices

> 通用 Ch 05 · Supplemental Lift-Modifying Devices · 原文 PDF 第 195 页

## 1. 原文

If the wing of an airplane was designed to produce the maximum lift possible at low airspeed, to accommodate takeoffs and landings, it would not be suited for higher speed flight because of the enormous amount of drag it would produce. To give the wing the ability to produce maximum low speed lift without being drag prohibitive, retractable high lift devices, such as flaps and slats, are utilized.

Flaps The most often used lift-modifying device, for small airplanes and large, is the wing flap. Flaps can be installed on the leading edge or trailing edge, with the leading edge versions used only on larger airplanes. Flaps change the camber of the wing, and they increase both the lift and the drag for any given angle of attack. The four different types of flaps in use are called the plain, split, slotted, and Fowler. [Figure 5-73]

Plain flaps attach to the trailing edge of the wing, inboard of the ailerons, and form part of the wing’s overall surface.

When deployed downward, they increase the effective camber of the wing and the wing’s chord line. Both of these factors cause the wing to create more lift and more drag.

The split flap attaches to the bottom of the wing, and deploys downward without changing the top surface of the wing. This type of flap creates more drag than the plain flap because of the increase in turbulence.

The slotted flap is similar to the plain flap, except when it deploys, the leading edge drops down a small amount. By having the leading edge drop down slightly, a slot opens, which lets some of the high-pressure air on the bottom of the wing flow over the top of the flap. This additional airflow over the top of the flap produces additional lift.

The Fowler flap attaches to the back of the wing using a track and roller system. When it deploys, it moves aft in addition to deflecting downward. This increases the total wing area, in addition to increasing the wing camber and chord line.

This type of flap is the most effective of the four types, and it is the type used on commercial airliners and business jets.

Leading Edge Slots Leading edge slots are ducts or passages in the leading edge of a wing that allow high pressure air from the bottom of the wing to flow to the top of the wing. Leading Edge Slats Leading edge slats serve the same purpose as slots, the difference being that slats are movable and can be retracted when not needed. ## 2. 中文翻译

如果飞机机翼设计成在低速时产生尽可能大的升力，以兼顾起飞和着陆，那么它就不适合高速飞行，因为这样会产生极大的阻力。为使机翼在不至于因阻力过大而无法使用的前提下具备产生最大低速升力的能力，人们采用了可收放的高升力装置，如襟翼和缝翼。

襟翼　无论小型还是大型飞机，最常用的增升装置都是机翼襟翼。襟翼可装在机翼前缘或后缘，其中前缘襟翼只用于较大的飞机。襟翼改变机翼的弯度，并且在任意给定的迎角下同时增大升力和阻力。目前使用的襟翼有四种，分别称为简单襟翼、分裂襟翼、开缝襟翼和富勒襟翼。

简单襟翼连接在机翼后缘、副翼内侧，构成机翼整个表面的一部分。

当它向下放下时，会增大机翼的有效弯度和机翼的弦线（弦长）。这两个因素都使机翼产生更大的升力和更大的阻力。

分裂襟翼连接在机翼下表面，向下放下而不改变机翼上表面。由于产生的紊流更大，这种襟翼的阻力比简单襟翼更大。

开缝襟翼与简单襟翼相似，区别在于它放下时前缘会向下偏转一小段。前缘稍稍下偏便打开一道缝，让机翼下表面的部分高压空气流到襟翼上表面。这股附加气流流过襟翼上表面，产生附加升力。

富勒襟翼通过导轨和滚轮系统连接在机翼后部。放下时，它除向下偏转外还向后移动。因此，除增大机翼弯度和弦线外，它还增大了机翼的总面积。

在四种襟翼中，这种襟翼效率最高，也是民航客机和公务喷气机所采用的类型。

前缘槽口　前缘槽口是机翼前缘内的管道或通道，允许机翼下方的高压空气流向机翼上方。这股被引导的空气以很高的速度流过机翼上表面，有助于防止边界层气流变为紊流并与

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 虚拟条件句：If the wing was designed …, it would not be suited

**原句**：If the wing of an airplane was designed to produce the maximum lift possible at low airspeed, to accommodate takeoffs and landings, it would not be suited for higher speed flight because of the enormous amount of drag it would produce.

**拆解**：If 引导与现在事实相反的虚拟条件从句，从句用一般过去时 was designed；主句用 would + 动词原形 would not be suited；to accommodate takeoffs and landings 是目的状语，插在主句之前；because of the enormous amount of drag it would produce 是原因状语，其中 it would produce 是省略关系代词 that 的定语从句，修饰 drag。

**要点**：手册解释「为什么必须有增升装置」时用的是反事实假设：机翼若真按低速最大升力设计，就必然不适合高速。注意主句的 would 不能省，写成 it is not suited 就把假设变成了事实陈述；从句用 was 而非 were 属口语化用法，正式文体可写 were designed。

### ② with + 名词 + 过去分词（复合结构作补充说明）

**原句**：Flaps can be installed on the leading edge or trailing edge, with the leading edge versions used only on larger airplanes.

**拆解**：主句 Flaps can be installed on the leading edge or trailing edge 为情态被动；with the leading edge versions used only on larger airplanes 是 with + 名词 + 过去分词构成的复合结构，作伴随说明，逻辑主语是 the leading edge versions。

**要点**：技术文本常用 with 复合结构在句尾补一句限定，避免另起一句。used 是过去分词表被动（versions 是被使用），不能写成 using；同类如 with the slats fully extended（缝翼完全放出时）。

### ③ By + 动名词表方式，which 引导非限制性定语从句

**原句**：By having the leading edge drop down slightly, a slot opens, which lets some of the high-pressure air on the bottom of the wing flow over the top of the flap.

**拆解**：By having the leading edge drop down slightly 是介词 + 动名词短语作方式状语，其中 have + 宾语 + 动词原形（have the leading edge drop）表「使……做」；主句 a slot opens；which 引导非限制性定语从句，指代前面整件事的结果；lets … flow 是 let + 宾语 + 原形。

**要点**：by + -ing 说明「靠什么手段实现」，是解释工作原理的常用句式；which 前有逗号时指代前面整件事，不能换成 that。have / let / make 后接动词原形，不接 to do，这是本节最容易写错的一处。
