# Convection

> 通用 Ch 05 · Convection · 原文 PDF 第 170 页

## 1. 原文

Convection is the process by which heat is transferred by movement of a heated fluid (gas or liquid). For example, an incandescent light bulb will, when heated, become increasingly hotter until the air surrounding it begins to move.

The motion of the air is upward. This upward motion of the heated air carries the heat away from the hot light bulb by convection. Transfer of heat by convection may be hastened by using a ventilating fan to move the air surrounding a hot object. The rate of cooling of a hot electronics component, such as the CPU in a computer, can be increased if it is provided with copper fins that conduct heat away from the hot surface. The fins provide large surfaces against which cool air can be blown.

A convection process may take place in a liquid as well as in a gas. A good example of this is a pan of water sitting on the stove. The bottom of the pan becomes hot because it conducts heat from the surface it is in contact with. The water on the bottom of the pan also heats up because of conduction. As the heated water starts to rise and cooler water moves in to take its place, the convection process begins.

When the circulation of gas or liquid is not rapid enough to remove sufficient heat, fans or pumps are used to accelerate the motion of the cooling material. In some installations, pumps are used to circulate water or oil to help cool large equipment. In airborne installations, electric fans and blowers are used to aid convection.

An aircraft air-cooled piston engine is a good example of convection being used to transfer heat. The engine shown in Figure 5-29 is a Continental IO-520, with six heavily finned air-cooled cylinders. This engine does not depend on natural convection for cooling, but rather forced air convection coming from the propeller on the engine. The heat generated inside the engine finds its way to the cylinder cooling fins by conduction, meaning transfer within the metal of the cylinder.

Once the heat gets to the fins, forced air flowing around the cylinders carries the heat away.

## 2. 中文翻译

对流是受热流体（气体或液体）运动而传递热的过程。例如，白炽灯泡受热后会越来越热，直到周围的空气开始流动为止。

空气的运动方向向上。受热空气的这种向上运动通过对流把热从炽热的灯泡带走。用通风风扇吹动热物体周围的空气，可以加快对流换热。像计算机 CPU 这样的发热电子元件，如果装上铜散热片把热从热表面导出，其冷却速率就可以提高。散热片提供了很大的表面，可以向其上吹冷空气。

对流过程既可以发生在液体中，也同样发生在气体中。放在炉子上的平底锅就是一例。锅底变热，是因为它从所接触的表面导热。锅底的水也因传导而升温。当受热的水开始上升、较冷的水流过来占据它的位置时，对流过程就开始了。

当气体或液体的循环不够快、无法带走足够的热量时，就用风扇或泵来加速冷却介质的运动。在某些装置中，用泵使水或油循环，以帮助冷却大型设备。在机载装置中，则用电风扇和鼓风机来增强对流。

航空器气冷式活塞发动机是把对流用于传热的好例子。图 5-29 所示的发动机是一台 Continental IO-520，有六个带密集散热片的气冷气缸。这台发动机并不依靠自然对流来冷却，而是依靠来自螺旋桨的强迫通风对流。发动机内部产生的热通过传导到达气缸散热片，所谓传导即指在气缸金属内部的热传递。

热一旦到达散热片，流过气缸周围的强迫气流就会把热带走。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 介词 + which 引导的定语从句：the process by which …

**原句**：Convection is the process by which heat is transferred by movement of a heated fluid (gas or liquid).

**拆解**：主语 Convection；谓语 is；表语 the process；by which 引导定语从句修饰 the process，从句内 heat 作主语、is transferred 为被动谓语、by movement of a heated fluid 是方式状语。

**要点**：by which ＝ by the process，即「通过这一过程」。给术语下定义时常用「X is the process by which …」的句式，翻译宜作「X 是……的过程」。句中出现两个 by：第一个属于从句引导词，第二个表手段，不要混为一谈。括号里的 (gas or liquid) 是对 fluid 的解释，翻译时保留括号。

### ② 被动语态 + 介词 by + 动名词

**原句**：Transfer of heat by convection may be hastened by using a ventilating fan to move the air surrounding a hot object.

**拆解**：主语 Transfer of heat by convection；谓语 may be hastened（情态动词 + 被动）；by using a ventilating fan 是方式状语，by 后接动名词短语；to move the air 是目的状语；surrounding a hot object 是现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 the air。

**要点**：by + -ing 表示手段，不能换成 by + 不定式。hasten 是及物动词「加快」，比 speed up 正式。the air surrounding a hot object 中 surrounding 是分词而非谓语，注意与 the air surrounds … 区分；这类「名词 + 分词短语」结构在手册中极常见。

### ③ Once 引导的时间状语从句 + 现在分词作定语

**原句**：Once the heat gets to the fins, forced air flowing around the cylinders carries the heat away.

**拆解**：Once 引导时间状语从句（「一旦」）；主句主语 forced air，flowing around the cylinders 是现在分词短语作后置定语，谓语 carries … away。

**要点**：once 作连词表「一旦……就……」，从句用一般现在时表将来，不用 will get。flowing 用主动分词，因为空气是自己流动的；若用 flowed 就成了谓语，句子会出现两个谓语。carry away 是「带走」的短语动词，代词宾语要放中间：carry it away。
