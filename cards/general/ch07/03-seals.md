# Seals

> 通用 Ch 07 · Seals · 原文 PDF 第 278 页

## 1. 原文

Seals are used to prevent fluid from passing a certain point, as well as to keep air and dirt out of the system in which they are used. The increased use of hydraulics and pneumatics in aircraft systems has created a need for packings and gaskets of varying characteristics and design to meet the many variations of operating speeds and temperatures to which they are subjected. No one style or type of seal is satisfactory for all installations. Some of the reasons for this are:

• Pressure at which the system operates

• Type fluid used in the system

• Metal finish and the clearance between adjacent parts

• Type motion (rotary or reciprocating), if any Seals are divided into three main classes: packings, gaskets, and wipers.

Packings are made of synthetic or natural rubber. They are generally used as “running seals,” that is, in units that contain moving parts, such as actuating cylinders, pumps, selector valves, and so forth. Packings are made in the form of O-rings, V-rings, and U-rings, each designed for a specific purpose.

#### O-Ring Packings

O-ring packings are used to prevent both internal and external leakage. This type of packing ring seals effectively in both directions and is the type most commonly used. In installations subject to pressures above 1,500 psi, backup rings are used with O-rings to prevent extrusion.

When O-ring packing is subjected to pressure from both sides, as in actuating cylinders, two backup rings must be used (one on either side of the O-ring). When an O-ring is subject to pressure on only one side, a single backup ring is generally used. In this case, the backup ring is always placed on the side of the O-ring away from the pressure.

The materials from which O-rings are manufactured have been compounded for various operating conditions, temperatures, and fluids. An O-ring designed specifically for use as a static (stationary) seal, probably will not do the job when installed on a moving part, such as a hydraulic piston. Most O-rings are similar in appearance and texture, but their characteristics may differ widely. An O-ring is useless if it is not compatible with the system fluid and operating temperature.

Advances in aircraft design have necessitated new O-ring compositions to meet changed operating conditions. Hydraulic O-rings were originally established under AN specification numbers (6227, 6230, and 6290) for use in MIL-H-5606 fluid at temperatures ranging from −65 °F to +160 °F. When new designs raised operating temperatures to a possible 275 °F, more compounds were developed and perfected.

Recently, a compound was developed that offered improved low-temperature performance without sacrificing high- temperature performance, rendering the other series obsolete. This superior material was adopted in the MS28775 series. This series is now the standard for MIL-H-5606 systems in which the temperature may vary from −65 °F to +275 °F.

Manufacturers provide color-coding on some O-rings, but this is not a reliable or complete means of identification. The color- coding system does not identify sizes but only system fluid or vapor compatibility and, in some cases, the manufacturer. Color codes on O-rings that are compatible with MIL-H-5606 fluid always contains blue but may also contain red or other colors. Packings and gaskets suitable for use with Skydrol™ fluid is always coded with a green stripe, but may also have a blue, grey, red, green, or yellow dot as a part of the color code. Color codes on O-rings that are compatible with hydrocarbon fluid always contains red but never contain blue. A colored stripe around the circumference indicates that the O-ring is a boss gasket seal. The color of the stripe indicates fluid compatibility: red for fuel, blue for hydraulic fluid.

The coding on some rings is not permanent. On others it may be omitted due to manufacturing difficulties or interference with operation. Furthermore, the color-coding system provides no means to establish the age of the O-ring or its temperature limitations.

Because of the difficulties with color-coding, O-rings are available in individual hermetically-sealed envelopes labeled with all pertinent data. When selecting an O-ring for installation, the basic part number on the sealed envelope provides the most reliable compound identification.

Although an O-ring may appear perfect at first glance, slight surface flaws may exist. These flaws are often capable of preventing satisfactory O-ring performance under the variable operating pressures of aircraft systems; therefore, O-rings U-ring V-ring Male O-ring U-cup Female should be rejected for flaws that affect their performance. Such flaws are difficult to detect, and one aircraft manufacturer recommends using a 4-power magnifying glass with adequate lighting to inspect each ring before it is installed.

By rolling the ring on an inspection cone or dowel, the inner diameter surface can also be checked for small cracks, particles of foreign material, or other irregularities that cause leakage or shorten the life of the O-ring. The slight stretching of the ring when it is rolled inside out helps to reveal some defects not otherwise visible.

Backup Rings Backup rings (MS28782) made of Teflon™ do not deteriorate with age, are unaffected by any system fluid or vapor, and can tolerate temperature extremes in excess of those encountered in high-pressure hydraulic systems. Their dash numbers indicate not only their size but also relate directly to the dash number of the O-ring for which they are dimensionally suited. They are procurable under several basic part numbers, but they are interchangeable; that is, any Teflon™ backup ring may be used to replace any other Teflon™ backup ring if it is of proper overall dimension to support the applicable O-ring. Backup rings are not color-coded or otherwise marked and must be identified from package labels.

The inspection of backup rings should include a check to ensure that surfaces are free from irregularities, that the edges are clean cut and sharp, and that scarf cuts are parallel. When checking Teflon™ spiral backup rings, make sure that the coils do not separate more than 1⁄4 inch when unrestrained.

#### V-Ring Packings

V-ring packings (AN6225) are one-way seals and are always installed with the open end of the “V” facing the pressure. V-ring packings must have a male and female adapter to hold them in the proper position after installation. It is also necessary to torque the seal retainer to the value specified by the manufacturer of the component being serviced, or the seal may not give satisfactory service.

#### U-Ring Packings

U-ring packings (AN6226) and U-cup packings are used in brake assemblies and brake master cylinders. The U-ring and U-cup seal pressure in only one direction; therefore, the lip of the packings must face toward the pressure. U-ring packings are primarily low-pressure packings to be used with pressures of less than 1,000 psi.

Gaskets are used as static (stationary) seals between two flat surfaces. Some of the more common gasket materials are asbestos, copper, cork, and rubber. Asbestos sheeting is used wherever a heat-resistant gasket is needed. It is used extensively for exhaust system gaskets. Most asbestos exhaust gaskets have a thin sheet of copper edging to prolong their life.

A solid copper washer is used for spark plug gaskets where it is essential to have a non-compressible, yet semisoft gasket. Cork gaskets can be used as an oil seal between the engine crankcase and accessories, and where a gasket is required that can occupy an uneven or varying space caused by a rough surface or expansion and contraction.

Rubber sheeting can be used where there is a need for a compressible gasket. It should not be used in any place where it may come in contact with gasoline or oil because the rubber deteriorates very rapidly when exposed to these substances. Gaskets are used in fluid systems around the end caps of actuating cylinders, valves, and other units. The gasket generally used for this purpose is in the shape of an O-ring, similar to O-ring packings.

Wipers are used to clean and lubricate the exposed portions of piston shafts. They prevent dirt from entering the system and help protect the piston shaft against scoring. Wipers may be either metallic or felt. They are sometimes used together, a felt wiper installed behind a metallic wiper.

### Sealing Compounds

Certain areas of all aircraft are sealed to withstand pressurization by air, to prevent leakage of fuel, to prevent Male V-ring adapter Female V-ring adapter V-ring packing Adjustment nuts passage of fumes, or to prevent corrosion by sealing against the weather. Most sealants consist of two or more ingredients properly proportioned and compounded to obtain the best results. Some materials are ready for use as packaged, but others require mixing before application.

#### One Part Sealants

One part sealants are prepared by the manufacturer and are ready for application as packaged. However, the consistency of some of these compounds may be altered to satisfy a particular method of application. If thinning is desired, use the thinner recommended by the sealant manufacturer.

#### Two Part Sealants

Two part sealants are compounds requiring separate packaging to prevent cure prior to application and are identified as the base sealing compound and the accelerator. Any alteration of the prescribed ratios reduces the quality of the material. Combining equal portions, by weight, of base compound and accelerator, mixes two part sealants.

All sealant material should be carefully weighed in accordance with the sealant manufacturer’s recommendations. Sealant material is usually weighed with a balance scale equipped with weights specially prepared for various quantities of sealant and accelerator.

Before weighing the sealant materials, thoroughly stir both the base sealant compound and the accelerator. Do not use accelerator, which is dried out, lumpy, or flaky. Pre-weighed sealant kits do not require weighing of the sealant and accelerator before mixing when the entire quantity is to be mixed.

After determining the proper amount of base sealant compound and accelerator, add the accelerator to the base sealant compound. Immediately after adding the accelerator, thoroughly mix the two parts by stirring or folding, depending on the consistency of the material. Carefully mix the material to prevent entrapment of air in the mixture. Overly rapid or prolonged stirring builds up heat in the mixture and shortens the normal application time (working life) of the mixed sealant.

To ensure a well-mixed compound, test by smearing a small portion on a clean, flat metal, or glass surface. If flecks or lumps are found, continue mixing. If the flecks or lumps cannot be eliminated, reject the batch.

The working life of mixed sealant is from 1⁄2 hour to 4 hours (depending upon the class of sealant); therefore, apply mixed sealant as soon as possible or place in refrigerated storage.

The curing rate of mixed sealants varies with changes in temperature and humidity. Curing of sealants is extremely slow if the temperature is below 60 °F. A temperature of 77 °F with 50 percent relative humidity is the ideal condition for curing most sealants.

Curing may be accelerated by increasing the temperature, but the temperature should never be allowed to exceed 120 °F at any time in the curing cycle. Heat may be applied by using infrared lamps or heated air. If heated air is used, it must be properly filtered to remove moisture and dirt.

Heat should not be applied to any faying surface sealant installation until all work is completed. All faying surface applications must have all attachments, permanent or temporary, completed within the application limitations of the sealant.

Sealant must be cured to a tack-free condition before applying brush top coatings. (Tack-free consistency is the point at which a sheet of cellophane pressed onto the sealant no longer adheres.)

## 2. 中文翻译

密封件用于阻止液体通过某一部位，同时使空气和污物不能进入其所在系统。飞机系统中液压与气压装置使用日益增多，因而需要具有各种特性和设计的填料与垫片，以适应它们所承受的多种工作速度和温度。没有一种样式或类型的密封件能适用于所有安装场合。其原因包括：

• 系统的工作压力

• 系统中使用的液体类型

• 金属表面加工质量及相邻零件之间的间隙

• 运动类型（旋转或往复），如果有的话 密封件分为三大类：填料、垫片和刮尘圈。

填料由合成橡胶或天然橡胶制成。它们一般用作“动密封”，即用于含有运动部件的装置，如作动筒、泵、选择阀等。填料制成 O 形圈、V 形圈和 U 形圈的形式，各有特定用途。

#### O 形圈填料

O 形圈填料用于防止内泄漏和外泄漏。这种填料环在两个方向都能有效密封，是使用最普遍的型式。在压力超过 1,500 psi 的安装部位，O 形圈需配用备用环，以防挤出。

当作动筒中的 O 形圈两侧都受压力作用时，必须使用两个备用环（O 形圈两侧各一个）。当 O 形圈只有一侧受压时，一般使用一个备用环。此时，备用环总是放在 O 形圈背离压力的一侧。

O 形圈所用材料已针对各种工作条件、温度和液体进行过配方设计。专用于静密封的 O 形圈，若装到运动件（如液压活塞）上，很可能无法胜任。大多数 O 形圈外观和质地相似，但特性可能差别很大。O 形圈若与系统液体及工作温度不相容，就毫无用处。

飞机设计的进步要求采用新的 O 形圈配方以适应变化的工作条件。液压 O 形圈最初以 AN 规范号（6227、6230 和 6290）确定，用于 MIL-H-5606 液体中，温度范围为 −65 °F 至 +160 °F。当新设计把工作温度提高到可能达 275 °F 时，更多的配方被研制并完善。

最近研制出一种新配方，在提高低温性能的同时不牺牲高温性能，使其他系列被淘汰。这种更优材料被纳入 MS28775 系列。该系列现已成为温度在 −65 °F 至 +275 °F 之间变化的 MIL-H-5606 系统的标准。

制造厂在某些 O 形圈上提供颜色编码，但这不是可靠或完整的识别手段。颜色编码系统不标识尺寸，只标识系统液体或蒸气的相容性，某些情况下还标识制造厂。与 MIL-H-5606 液体相容的 O 形圈，其颜色代码总含蓝色，但也可能含红色或其他颜色。适用于 Skydrol™ 液体的填料和垫片总是带绿色条纹，但也可能带有蓝、灰、红、绿或黄色圆点作为颜色代码的一部分。与碳氢液体相容的 O 形圈颜色代码总含红色，但绝不含蓝色。圆周方向的彩色条纹表示该 O 形圈为凸台垫片密封件。条纹颜色表示液体相容性：红色为燃油，蓝色为液压油。

有些环上的编码不是永久性的。另一些环上可能因制造困难或影响使用而省略编码。此外，颜色编码系统无法确定 O 形圈的制造年代或温度限制。

由于颜色编码存在困难，O 形圈以单独的气密封袋供应，袋上标注所有相关数据。选择 O 形圈安装时，密封袋上的基本件号是最可靠的配方识别依据。

虽然 O 形圈乍看可能完美无缺，但可能存在细微的表面缺陷。这些缺陷在飞机系统变化的工作压力下往往足以使 O 形圈无法正常发挥作用；因此，凡影响其使用性能的缺陷，O 形圈都应拒收。这类缺陷难以发现，一家飞机制造商建议在安装每个环之前，用 4 倍放大镜在充足照明下检查。

将环在检查锥或圆棒上滚动，还能检查内径表面有无细小裂纹、异物颗粒或其他导致泄漏或缩短 O 形圈寿命的不规则处。环被里外翻转滚动时的轻微拉伸，有助于显露某些原本看不见的缺陷。

备用环（Backup Rings）。由 Teflon™ 制成的备用环（MS28782）不会因老化而劣化，不受任何系统液体或蒸气影响，并能承受超过高压液压系统所遇的极端温度。其代号（dash number）不仅表示尺寸，还直接对应其在尺寸上适配的 O 形圈的代号。它们有若干基本件号可供采购，但可以互换；也就是说，只要总尺寸合适、能支承相应的 O 形圈，任何 Teflon™ 备用环都可替换任何其他 Teflon™ 备用环。备用环没有颜色编码或其他标记，必须根据包装标签识别。

备用环的检查应包括：确认表面无不规则处、边缘切口干净锋利、斜切口平行。检查 Teflon™ 螺旋备用环时，应确认无约束状态下各圈开口不超过 1/4 英寸。

#### V 形圈填料

V 形圈填料（AN6225）是单向密封件，安装时“V”形开口端总是朝向压力。V 形圈填料必须有阳、阴两个转接件，以便安装后将其保持在正确位置。还必须将密封保持件拧到被维护部件制造厂规定的扭矩值，否则密封件可能无法正常工作。

#### U 形圈填料

U 形圈填料（AN6226）和 U 形杯填料用于刹车组件和刹车总泵。U 形圈和 U 形杯只在一个方向密封压力；因此，填料的唇口必须朝向压力。U 形圈填料主要是低压填料，用于压力低于 1,000 psi 的场合。

垫片用作两个平面之间的静密封。较常见的垫片材料有石棉、铜、软木和橡胶。凡需要耐热垫片处即使用石棉板。它广泛用于排气系统垫片。大多数石棉排气垫片都有薄铜包边以延长寿命。

实心铜垫圈用于火花塞垫片，此处必须使用不可压缩而又较软的垫片。软木垫片可用作发动机曲轴箱与附件之间的油封，也用于需要能填充因表面粗糙或膨胀收缩造成的不平整或变化间隙的场合。

橡胶板可用于需要可压缩垫片处。但不得用于可能接触汽油或油的部位，因为橡胶遇这些物质会很快劣化。液体系统中，作动筒端盖、活门和其他装置的周围都使用垫片。此处一般使用 O 形圈形状的垫片，与 O 形圈填料类似。

刮尘圈用于清洁和润滑活塞杆外露部分。它们防止污物进入系统，并有助于保护活塞杆不被划伤。刮尘圈可以是金属的或毛毡的。二者有时一起使用，在金属刮尘圈后面安装毛毡刮尘圈。

### 密封胶

所有飞机上都有某些区域要密封，以承受空气增压、防止燃油泄漏、防止有害气体通过，或通过密封防风雨而防腐。大多数密封胶由两种或多种成分按适当比例配制而成，以获得最佳效果。有些材料按包装即可使用，另一些则在施工前需要混合。

#### 单组分密封胶

单组分密封胶由制造厂调配好，按包装即可使用。不过，某些这类胶料的稠度可以调整，以适应特定的施工方法。若需稀释，应使用密封胶制造厂推荐的稀释剂。

#### 双组分密封胶

双组分密封胶是必须分装以防使用前固化的胶料，分为基料密封胶和促进剂。任何偏离规定比例的调配都会降低材料质量。按重量取等量的基料与促进剂混合，即调成双组分密封胶。

所有密封胶材料都应按密封胶制造厂的推荐仔细称量。密封胶材料通常用天平称量，天平配有专为不同数量密封胶和促进剂准备的砝码。

称量密封胶材料之前，应彻底搅拌基料密封胶和促进剂。不得使用已干涸、结块或片状的促进剂。若要把整份材料全部混合，预称量密封胶套装无需在混合前称量密封胶和促进剂。

确定基料密封胶和促进剂的用量后，将促进剂加入基料中。加入促进剂后应立即充分混合两组分，可搅拌或翻拌，视材料稠度而定。混合时应小心，防止空气卷入混合物中。搅拌过快或时间过长会使混合物升温，缩短混合密封胶的正常适用期（工作寿命）。

为确保混合均匀，可取少量涂在干净平整的金属或玻璃表面上测试。若发现色斑或结块，应继续搅拌。若色斑或结块无法消除，则该批材料作废。

混合密封胶的工作寿命为 1/2 小时至 4 小时（取决于密封胶的类别）；因此，应尽快使用混合好的密封胶，或放入冷藏储存。

混合密封胶的固化速率随温度和湿度变化而不同。若温度低于 60 °F，密封胶固化极慢。温度 77 °F、相对湿度 50% 是大多数密封胶固化的理想条件。

提高温度可加速固化，但固化周期中任何时候都绝不能让温度超过 120 °F。可用红外灯或加热空气加热。若使用加热空气，必须适当过滤以去除水分和污物。

在所有工作完成之前，不应对任何贴合面密封胶施工部位加热。所有贴合面施工都必须在密封胶的施工限制时间内完成全部连接，无论永久性还是临时性。

在涂刷表层涂层之前，密封胶必须固化至不粘手状态。（不粘手状态是指将一张玻璃纸压在密封胶上不再粘附的状态。）

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as well as 并列不定式 + in which 定语从句
**原句**：Seals are used to prevent fluid from passing a certain point, as well as to keep air and dirt out of the system in which they are used.
**拆解**：`are used to do` 中 to 引出目的；`as well as to keep ...` 用并列的第二不定式，说明另一用途；`in which they are used` 是“介词 + which”引导的定语从句修饰 system。
**要点**：`as well as` 连接的两项须保持语法形式一致（此处均为 to do）；`prevent A from doing` 是固定搭配，`from` 不可省；`in which` = `where`，但先行词为 system 时更正式。

### ② no + 单数名词作主语，谓语用单数
**原句**：No one style or type of seal is satisfactory for all installations.
**拆解**：`No` 修饰由 or 连接的两个单数名词 `style or type`，视为单数概念，谓语用 `is`；`for all installations` 表适用范围。
**要点**：`no + 可数名词单数` 作主语时谓语用单数；若为 `no + 复数名词`，谓语用复数。此句也是维修判断的典型表达：没有万能件，须按安装条件选型。

### ③ 主句 + if 条件从句（说明失效条件）
**原句**：An O-ring is useless if it is not compatible with the system fluid and operating temperature.
**拆解**：主句 `An O-ring is useless` 为系表结构；`if` 引导真实条件从句，从句为 `be compatible with` 结构，宾语为并列的 `the system fluid and operating temperature`。
**要点**：`be compatible with` 是“与……相容”的固定搭配，注意介词是 with；条件从句用一般现在时表普遍规律，不用将来时。

### ④ 原文主谓一致的笔误辨析
**原句**：Color codes on O-rings that are compatible with hydrocarbon fluid always contains red but never contain blue.
**拆解**：主语中心词是复数 `Color codes`，`on O-rings that are compatible with hydrocarbon fluid` 为介词短语加定语从句作后置修饰；因此谓语应为 `contain`。原句前半用 `contains`、后半用 `contain`，前后不一致，`contains` 系原文笔误。
**要点**：长主语后谓语要与中心词一致，不被最近的复数名词（O-rings、fluid）干扰；`always ... but never ...` 表示“必有……但绝无……”。

### ⑤ when 从句 + 后置形容词短语作定语
**原句**：The slight stretching of the ring when it is rolled inside out helps to reveal some defects not otherwise visible.
**拆解**：主语 `The slight stretching of the ring`；`when it is rolled inside out` 为时间状语从句，`it` 指 the ring；谓语 `helps to reveal`；`not otherwise visible` 是形容词短语后置修饰 defects。
**要点**：`not otherwise visible`（否则看不见的）这类后置定语在技术文本中很常见，相当于 `which are not otherwise visible`；`help to do` 中 to 可省略，但正式文本常保留。
