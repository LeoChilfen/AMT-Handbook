## 1. 原文

Many of the blind fasteners are manufactured in oversized diameters to accommodate slightly enlarged holes resulting from drilling out the original fastener. When using rivets or even bolts, care must be taken to ensure the hole is not elongated or slanted.

To reduce the chances of an incorrectly drilled rivet or bolt hole, use a slightly smaller drill bit first, then enlarge to the correct diameter. The last step to prepare the hole for the fastener is to deburr the hole using either a very large drill bit or a special deburring tool. This practice also works well when drilling out a previously attached fastener. If the drill bit does not exactly find the center of the rivet, bolt, or screw, the hole can easily be elongated, but when using a smaller drill bit, drill the head only off the fastener. Then the ring and stem that is left can be pushed out with a pin punch of the appropriate diameter. If an incorrectly drilled hole is found, the options are to re-drill the hole to the next larger diameter for an acceptable fastener or repair the hole using an Acres fastener sleeve.

### Repair of Damaged Holes with Acres Fastener Sleeves

Acres fastener sleeves are thin-wall tubular elements with a flared end. The sleeves are installed in holes to accept standard bolts and rivet-type fasteners. The existing fastener holes are drilled 1⁄64 inch oversize for installation of the sleeves. The sleeves are manufactured in 1-inch increments. Along their length, grooves provide a place to break or cut off excess length to match fastener grip range. The grooves also provide a place to hold adhesive or sealing agents when bonding the sleeve into the hole.

Advantages & Limitations The sleeves are used in holes that must be drilled 1⁄64 inch oversize to clean up corrosion or other damage. The oversize hole with the sleeve installed allows the use of the original diameter fastener in the repaired hole. The sleeves can be used in areas of high galvanic corrosion where the corrosion must be confined to a readily replaceable part. Oversizing of holes reduces the net cross-sectional area of a part and should not be done unless absolutely required.

Consult the manufacturer of the aircraft, aircraft engine, or aircraft component prior to repair have damaged holes with Acres sleeves.

Identification The sleeve is identified by a standard code number that represents the type and style of sleeve, a material code, the fastener shank diameter, and surface finish code letter, and grip tang for the sleeve. The basic code number represents the type and material of the sleeve. The first dash number represents the diameter of the sleeve for the fastener installed, and the second dash represents the grip length of the sleeve. The required length of the sleeve is determined on installation and the excess is broken off the sleeve. A JK5512A-05N-10 is a 100° low profile head sleeve of aluminum alloy. The diameter is for a 5⁄32-inch fastener with no surface finish and is 5⁄8 inch in length.

Hole Preparation Refer to the drill-number chart for standard or close fit holes. Inspect hole after drilling to assure all corrosion is removed before installing the sleeve. The hole must also be the correct shape and free from burrs. The countersink must be enlarged to receive the flare of the sleeve, so the sleeve is flush with the surrounding surface.

Installation After selecting the correct type and diameter sleeve, use the 6501 sleeve breakoff tool for final installation length. The sleeve may be installed with or without being bonded in the hole. When bonding the sleeve in a hole, use MIL-S-8802A 1⁄2 sealant. Reinstall original size fastener and torque as required.

Sleeve Removal Sleeves not bonded in the hole may be removed by either driving them out with a drift pin of the same diameter as the outside diameter of the sleeve, or they may be deformed and removed with a pointed tool. Bonded sleeves may be removed by this method, but care should be used not to damage the structure hole. If this method cannot be used, drill the sleeves out with a drill 0.004 to 0.008 inch smaller than the installation drill size. The remaining portion of the sleeve after drilling can be removed using a pointed tool and applying an adhesive solvent to the sealant.

### Control Cables & Terminals

Cables are the most widely used linkage in primary flight control systems. Cable-type linkage is also used in engine controls, emergency extension systems for the landing gear, and various other systems throughout the aircraft.

Cable-type linkage has several advantages over the other types. It is strong and lightweight, and its flexibility makes it easy to route through the aircraft. An aircraft cable has a high mechanical efficiency and can be set up without backlash, which is very important for precise control.

Cable linkage also has some disadvantages. Tension must be adjusted frequently due to stretching and temperature changes.

Aircraft control cables are fabricated from carbon steel or stainless steel.

Cable Construction The basic component of a cable is a wire. The diameter of the wire determines the total diameter of the cable. Several wires are preformed into a helical or spiral shape and then formed into a strand. These preformed strands are laid around a straight center strand to form a cable.

Cable designations are based on the number of strands and the number of wires in each strand. The most common aircraft cables are the 7 × 7 and 7 × 19.

The 7 × 7 cable consists of seven strands of seven wires each. Six of these strands are laid around the center strand. This is a cable of medium flexibility and is used for trim tab controls, engine controls, and indicator controls.

The 7 × 19 cable is made up of seven strands of 19 wires each. Six of these strands are laid around the center strand. This cable is extra flexible and is used in primary control systems and in other places where operation over pulleys is frequent.

Aircraft control cables vary in diameter, ranging from 1⁄16 to 3⁄8 inch.

Cable Fittings Cables may be equipped with several different types of fittings, such as terminals, thimbles, bushings, and shackles.

Terminal fittings are generally of the swaged type. They are available in the threaded end, fork end, eye end, single shank ball end, and double shank ball end. The threaded end, fork end, and eye end terminals are used to connect the cable to a turnbuckle, bell crank, or other linkage in the system. The ball end terminals are used for attaching cables to quadrants and special connections where space is limited.

The thimble, bushing, and shackle fittings may be used in place of some types of terminal fittings when facilities and supplies are limited and immediate replacement of the cable is necessary.

Turnbuckles A turnbuckle assembly is a mechanical screw device consisting of two threaded terminals and a threaded barrel.

Turnbuckles are fitted in the cable assembly for making minor adjustments in cable length and for adjusting cable tension. One of the terminals has right-hand threads and the other has left-hand threads. The barrel has matching right-hand and left-hand internal threads. The end of the barrel with the left-hand threads can usually be identified by a groove or knurl around that end of the barrel.

When installing a turnbuckle in a control system, it is necessary to screw both terminals an equal number of turns into the barrel. It is also essential that all turnbuckle terminals be screwed into the barrel until not more than three threads are exposed on either side of the turnbuckle barrel.

After a turnbuckle is properly adjusted, it must be safetied. The methods of safetying turnbuckles are discussed later in this chapter.

### Push-Pull Tube Linkage

Push-pull tubes are used as linkage in various types of mechanically-operated systems. This type linkage eliminates the problem of varying tension and permits the transfer of either compression or tension stress through a single tube.

A push-pull tube assembly consists of a hollow aluminum alloy or steel tube with an adjustable end fitting and a check nut at either end. The check nuts secure the end fittings after the tube assembly has been adjusted to its correct length. Push-pull tubes are generally made in short lengths to prevent vibration and bending under compression loads.

## 2. 中文翻译

许多盲紧固件都做成加大直径，以适配钻掉原紧固件后略微变大的孔。使用铆钉甚至螺栓时，必须注意确保孔没有被拉长或钻斜。

为降低铆钉孔或螺栓孔被钻错的可能性，先用略小一号的钻头，再扩到正确直径。为安装紧固件而对孔做的最后一道准备，是用很大的钻头或专用去毛刺工具给孔去毛刺。在钻掉原先装好的紧固件时，这种做法同样有效。如果钻头没有准确找到铆钉、螺栓或螺钉的中心，孔很容易被拉长；但若使用较小的钻头，则只把紧固件的头部钻掉。然后用直径合适的销冲（pin punch）把留下的环和杆推出。如果发现孔被钻坏，有两种选择：把孔重新钻到下一个更大直径以配用合格的紧固件，或用 Acres 紧固件套筒修复该孔。

### 用 Acres 紧固件套筒修复损伤孔

Acres 紧固件套筒是带外翻端的薄壁管状件。套筒装入孔中，用来接受标准螺栓和铆钉类紧固件。为安装套筒，现有的紧固件孔要钻大 1⁄64 英寸。套筒按 1 英寸的增量制造。套筒全长上制有环形槽，为折断或切掉多余长度、以匹配紧固件夹持范围提供位置。把套筒粘入孔中时，这些槽还用来容纳胶粘剂或密封剂。

优点与限制　套筒用于必须钻大 1⁄64 英寸以清除腐蚀或其他损伤的孔。装入套筒后的加大孔允许在修复后的孔中使用原直径的紧固件。套筒可用于高电偶腐蚀区域，把腐蚀限制在易于更换的零件上。把孔加大会减小零件的净横截面积，除非绝对必要，不应这样做。

在用 Acres 套筒修复损伤孔之前，应咨询飞机、飞机发动机或飞机部件制造商。

标识　套筒由标准代码编号标识，该编号表示套筒的类型与形式、材料代码、紧固件杆径、表面处理代码字母以及套筒的夹持凸舌。基本代码编号表示套筒的类型和材料。第一个短横线数字表示所装紧固件对应的套筒直径，第二个短横线数字表示套筒的夹持长度。套筒所需长度在安装时确定，多余部分从套筒上折断。JK5512A-05N-10 是 100° 低矮头铝合金套筒。其直径对应 5⁄32 英寸紧固件，无表面处理，长度为 5⁄8 英寸。

制孔　标准配合孔或紧配合孔的钻头号数请查阅钻头号数表。钻孔后应检查孔，确保在安装套筒前已清除所有腐蚀。孔还必须形状正确且无毛刺。埋头孔必须扩大，以容纳套筒的外翻边，使套筒与周围表面齐平。

安装　选定类型和直径正确的套筒后，用 6501 套筒折断工具确定最终安装长度。套筒装入孔中时可粘接，也可不粘接。粘接套筒时使用 MIL-S-8802A ½ 密封胶。重新装上原尺寸紧固件并按规定力矩拧紧。

套筒拆卸　未粘接在孔中的套筒，可用与套筒外径同直径的冲销（drift pin）冲出，也可使其变形后用尖头工具取出。粘接的套筒也可用此法取出，但应注意不要损伤结构孔。若不能用此法，可用比安装钻头尺寸小 0.004 至 0.008 英寸的钻头把套筒钻掉。钻孔后残留的套筒部分，可用尖头工具并对密封胶施加胶粘剂溶剂来清除。

### 操纵钢索与端接头

钢索是主飞行操纵系统中使用最广的传动连杆。钢索式连杆也用于发动机操纵、起落架应急放下系统以及全机其他各种系统。

与其他形式相比，钢索式连杆有几项优点。它强度高、重量轻，其柔性使它在飞机上易于布线。飞机钢索机械效率高，装配时可做到无空行程（backlash），这对精确操纵非常重要。

钢索连杆也有一些缺点。由于伸长和温度变化，必须经常调整张力。

飞机操纵钢索由碳钢或不锈钢制成。

钢索构造　钢索的基本组成部分是钢丝。钢丝直径决定钢索的总直径。若干根钢丝先预成形为螺旋状，然后捻成一股（strand）。这些预成形的股围绕一根直的芯股捻制，构成钢索。

钢索的标记以股数和每股中的钢丝数为基础。最常见的飞机钢索是 7 × 7 和 7 × 19。

7 × 7 钢索由七股、每股七根钢丝组成。其中六股围绕芯股捻制。这是中等柔性的钢索，用于调整片操纵、发动机操纵和指示器操纵。

7 × 19 钢索由七股、每股 19 根钢丝组成。其中六股围绕芯股捻制。这种钢索柔性特别好，用于主操纵系统以及其他需要频繁绕过滑轮的部位。

飞机操纵钢索的直径各不相同，范围为 1⁄16 至 3⁄8 英寸。

钢索接头　钢索可配装几种不同类型的接头，例如端接头、套环（thimble）、衬套和卸扣（shackle）。

端接头一般为压接式。它们有螺纹端、叉形端、眼环端、单杆球端和双杆球端等形式。螺纹端、叉形端和眼环端接头用于把钢索连接到系统中的松紧螺套（turnbuckle）、摇臂或其他传动件上。球端接头用于把钢索连接到扇形盘以及空间受限的特殊连接处。

当设备与器材有限、且必须立即更换钢索时，套环、衬套和卸扣接头可替代某些类型的端接头。

松紧螺套　松紧螺套组件是一种机械螺旋装置，由两个带螺纹的端接头和一个带螺纹的套筒组成。

松紧螺套装在钢索组件中，用于少量调整钢索长度和调整钢索张力。其中一个端接头为右旋螺纹，另一个为左旋螺纹。套筒内制有与之匹配的右旋和左旋内螺纹。带左旋螺纹的套筒端通常可通过该端周围的沟槽或滚花来识别。

在操纵系统中安装松紧螺套时，必须把两个端接头拧入套筒相同的圈数。同样重要的是，所有松紧螺套端接头都应拧入套筒，直到套筒两侧露出的螺纹都不超过三道。

松紧螺套调整好之后必须打保险。松紧螺套的保险方法将在本章后面讨论。

### 推拉管连杆

推拉管在各种机械操纵系统中用作传动连杆。这种连杆消除了张力变化的问题，并允许通过单根管子传递压缩应力或拉伸应力。

推拉管组件由空心铝合金管或钢管组成，两端各有一个可调端接头和一个锁紧螺母（check nut）。管组件调整到正确长度后，锁紧螺母把端接头固定。推拉管一般做得较短，以防在压缩载荷下振动和弯曲。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 目的不定式前置 + 祈使句并列动作
**原句**：To reduce the chances of an incorrectly drilled rivet or bolt hole, use a slightly smaller drill bit first, then enlarge to the correct diameter.
**拆解**：句首 To reduce the chances of an incorrectly drilled rivet or bolt hole 为目的状语（chances 后接 of 短语，incorrectly drilled 为过去分词作前置定语，修饰 rivet or bolt hole）；主句是祈使句，两个并列动词 use… 和 then enlarge…。
**要点**：技术操作步骤常用祈使句，省略主语 you，译成中文时补出「先……再……」，动作顺序由 first、then 标明。目的不定式置于句首时要用逗号与主句隔开，避免与主句主语混淆。

### ② if 条件从句 + 情态被动 + when 分词短语 + 祈使句
**原句**：If the drill bit does not exactly find the center of the rivet, bolt, or screw, the hole can easily be elongated, but when using a smaller drill bit, drill the head only off the fastener.
**拆解**：If 引导条件从句（主语 the drill bit，谓语 does not find，宾语 the center of the rivet, bolt, or screw）；主句 the hole can easily be elongated 为情态被动；but 转折后 when using a smaller drill bit 为「连词 + 现在分词」作时间状语，相当于 when you use…；最后 drill the head only off the fastener 为祈使句。
**要点**：when + 动名词/分词短语是手册里省略主语的常见写法，逻辑主语与主句一致（都是操作者）；off 在此是介词，「drill the head off」＝把头部钻掉，宾语 the head 夹在动词与 off 之间。注意 can easily be elongated 的被动含义是「容易被拉长」，不是「被轻易地加长」。

### ③ 地点状语前置 + 不定式作后置定语
**原句**：Along their length, grooves provide a place to break or cut off excess length to match fastener grip range.
**拆解**：句首 Along their length 为地点状语前置；主语 grooves + 谓语 provide + 宾语 a place；to break or cut off excess length 为不定式作 place 的后置定语；to match fastener grip range 为目的不定式。
**要点**：place to do sth 中的主动不定式表被动含义（= a place at which one can break…），这是名词后接不定式作定语的典型；一句话里两个不定式各司其职，翻译时应拆分层次。groove 在此为「环形槽」，是折断套筒的位置标记。

### ④ 动名词主语 + 并列谓语 + unless 省略从句
**原句**：Oversizing of holes reduces the net cross-sectional area of a part and should not be done unless absolutely required.
**拆解**：动名词短语 Oversizing of holes 作主语（视为单数）；并列谓语 reduces the net cross-sectional area of a part 和 should not be done（被动）；unless absolutely required 为条件状语从句的省略形式（= unless it is absolutely required）。
**要点**：动名词作主语时谓语用单数，故为 reduces；unless 后省略主语加 be 动词是手册常用简省，意思是「除非确有必要」；should not be done 表建议性禁止，比 must not 语气缓和。

### ⑤ with 短语作后置定语 + 情态被动 + by 表手段
**原句**：The end of the barrel with the left-hand threads can usually be identified by a groove or knurl around that end of the barrel.
**拆解**：主语 The end of the barrel + with the left-hand threads 为后置定语，界定「带左旋螺纹的那一端」；谓语 can usually be identified 为情态被动；by a groove or knurl 引出识别手段；around that end of the barrel 为后置定语修饰 groove or knurl。
**要点**：with 短语紧跟名词时作定语，不要误读成伴随状语；被动句中 by 引出的是手段而不是施动者，因为这里的动作主体是操作者，工具是沟槽或滚花。usually 的位置在情态动词之后、实义动词之前，这是中位副词的标准位置。
