# Aircraft Nuts

## 1. 原文

Aircraft nuts are made in a variety of shapes and sizes. They are made of cadmium-plated carbon steel, stainless steel, or anodized 2024T aluminum alloy and may be obtained with either right-hand or left-hand threads. No identifying marking or lettering appears on nuts. Only the characteristic metallic luster or color of the aluminum, brass, or the insert can identify them when the nut is of the self-locking type. They can be further identified by their construction.

Aircraft nuts can be divided into two general groups: non-self-locking and self-locking nuts. Non-self-locking nuts are those that must be safetied by external locking devices, such as cotter pins, safety wire, or locknuts. Self-locking nuts contain the locking feature as an integral part.

### Non-Self-Locking Nuts

Most of the familiar types of nuts, including the plain nut, the castle nut, the castellated shear nut, the plain hex nut, the light hex nut, and the plain check nut are the non-self-locking type.

LC Lockbolt collar C Material C = 24ST aluminum alloy (green color). Use with heat-treated alloy lockbolts only.

The castellated shear nut, AN320, is designed for use with devices, such as drilled Clevis bolts and threaded taper pins, which are normally subjected to shearing stress only. Like the castle nut, it is castellated for safetying. Note, however, that the nut is not as deep or as strong as the castle nut; also, that the castellations are not as deep as those in the castle nut.

The plain hex nut, AN315 and AN335 (fine and coarse thread), is of rugged construction. This makes it suitable for carrying large tensional loads. However, since it requires an auxiliary locking device, such as a check nut or lock washer, its use on aircraft structures is somewhat limited.

The light hex nut, AN340 and AN345 (fine and coarse thread), is a much lighter nut than the plain hex nut and must be locked by an auxiliary device. It is used for miscellaneous light tension requirements.

The plain check nut, AN316, is employed as a locking device for plain nuts, set screws, threaded rod ends, and other devices.

### Self-Locking Nuts

As their name implies, self-locking nuts need no auxiliary means of safetying but have a safetying feature included as an integral part of their construction. Many types of self-locking nuts have been designed and their use has become quite widespread. Common applications are:

• At tachment of antifriction bearings and control pulleys

• Attachment of accessories, anchor nuts around inspection holes, and small tank installation openings

• Attachment of rocker box covers and exhaust stacks Self-locking nuts are acceptable for use on certificated aircraft subject to the restrictions of the manufacturer. Self-locking nuts are used on aircraft to provide tight connections that do not shake loose under severe vibration. Do not use self-locking nuts at joints, which subject either the nut or bolt to rotation. They may be used with antifriction bearings and control pulleys, provided the inner race of the bearing is clamped to the supporting structure by the nut and bolt. Plates must be attached to the structure in a positive manner to eliminate rotation or misalignment when tightening the bolts or screws.

Boots Self-Locking Nut The Boots self-locking nut is of one piece, all-metal construction designed to hold tight despite severe vibration. Note that it has two sections and is essentially two nuts in one: a locking nut and a load-carrying nut. The two sections are connected with a spring, which is an integral part of the nut.

The spring keeps the locking and load-carrying sections such a distance apart that the two sets of threads are out of phase or spaced so that a bolt, which has been screwed through the load-carrying section, must push the locking section outward against the force of the spring to engage the threads of the locking section properly.

The spring, through the medium of the locking section, exerts a constant locking force on the bolt in the same direction as a force that would tighten the nut. In this nut, the load-carrying section has the thread strength of a standard nut of comparable size, while the locking section presses against the threads of the bolt and locks the nut firmly in position. Only a wrench applied to the nut loosens it. The nut can be removed and reused without impairing its efficiency.

Boots self-locking nuts are made with three different spring styles and in various shapes and sizes. The wing type that is the most common ranges in size for No. 6 up to 1⁄4 inch, the Rol-top ranges from 1⁄4 inch to 1⁄6 inch, and the bellows type ranges in size from No. 8 up to 3⁄8 inch. Stainless Steel Self-Locking Nut The stainless steel self-locking nut may be spun on and off by hand as its locking action takes places only when the nut is seated against a solid surface and tightened. The nut consists of two parts: a case with a beveled locking shoulder and key and a thread insert with a locking shoulder and slotted keyway. Until the nut is tightened, it spins on the bolt easily, because the threaded insert is the proper size for the bolt. However, when the nut is seated against a solid surface and tightened, the locking shoulder of the insert is pulled downward and wedged against the locking shoulder of the case. This action compresses the threaded insert and causes it to clench the bolt tightly. Note that the slot is wider than the key. This permits the slot to be narrowed and the insert to be compressed when the nut is tightened.

Elastic Stop Nut The elastic stop nut is a standard nut with the height increased to accommodate a fiber locking collar. This fiber collar is very tough and durable and is unaffected by immersion in hot or cold water or ordinary solvents, such as ether, carbon tetrachloride, oils, and gasoline. It will not damage bolt threads or plating.

The fiber locking collar is not threaded, and its inside diameter is smaller than the largest diameter of the threaded portion or the outside diameter of a corresponding bolt. When the nut is screwed onto a bolt, it acts as an ordinary nut until the bolt reaches the fiber collar. When the bolt is screwed into the fiber collar, however, friction (or drag) causes the fiber to be pushed upward. This creates a heavy downward pressure on the load carrying part and automatically throws the load carrying sides of the nut and bolt threads into positive contact. After the bolt has been forced all the way through the fiber collar, the downward pressure remains constant. This pressure locks and holds the nut securely in place even under severe vibration.

Nearly all elastic stop nuts are steel or aluminum alloy. However, such nuts are available in practically any kind of metal. Aluminum-alloy elastic stop nuts are supplied with an anodized finish. Steel nuts are cadmium plated.

Normally, elastic stop nuts can be used many times with complete safety and without detriment to their locking efficiency. When reusing elastic stop nuts, be sure the fiber has not lost its locking friction or become brittle. If a nut can be turned with the fingers, replace it.

Self-locking nut bases are made in several forms and materials for riveting and welding to aircraft structure or parts. Certain applications require the installation of self-locking nuts in channels, an arrangement that permits the attachment of many nuts with only a few rivets. These channels are track-like bases with regularly spaced nuts, which are either removable or non-removable. The removable type carries a floating nut that can be snapped in or out of the channel, thus making possible the easy removal of damaged nuts. Nuts, such as the clinch-type and spline-type, depend on friction for their anchorage and are not acceptable for use in aircraft structures.

### Sheet Spring Nuts

Sheet spring nuts, such as speed nuts, are used with standard and sheet metal self-tapping screws in non-structural locations. They find various uses in supporting line clamps, conduit clamps, electrical equipment, access doors, and the like and are available in several types. Speed nuts are made from spring steel and are arched prior to tightening. This arched spring lock prevents the screw from working loose. These nuts should be used only where originally used in the fabrication of the aircraft.

### Internal & External Wrenching Nuts

Two commercial types of high-strength internal or external wrenching nuts are available; they are the internal and external wrenching elastic stop nut and the Unbrako internal and external wrenching nut. Both are of the self-locking type, are heat-treated, and can carry high-strength bolt tension loads.

### Identification & Coding

Part numbers designate the type of nut. The common types and their respective part numbers are:

• Plain, AN315 and AN335

• Plain check, AN316

• Light hex, AN340 and AN345

• Castellated shear, AN320 The patented self-locking types are assigned part numbers ranging from MS20363 through MS20367. The Boots, the Flexloc, the fiber locknut, the elastic stop nut, and the self-locking nut belong to this group. Part number AN350 is assigned to the wing nut.

Letters and digits following the part number indicate such NutFiber collar items as material, size, threads per inch, and whether the thread is right or left hand. The letter “B” following the part number indicates the nut material to be brass, a “D” indicates 2017-T aluminum alloy, a “DD” indicates 2024-T aluminum alloy, a “C” indicates stainless steel, and a dash in place of a letter indicates cadmium-plated carbon steel.

The digit (or two digits) following the dash or the material code letter is the dash number of the nut, and it indicates the size of the shank and threads per inch of the bolt on which the nut fits. The dash number corresponds to the first figure appearing in the part number coding of general purpose bolts. A dash and the number 3, for example, indicate that the nut fits an AN3 bolt (10-32); a dash and the number 4 means it fits an AN4 bolt (1⁄4-28); a dash and the number 5, an AN5 bolt (5⁄16-24); and so on.

The code numbers for self-locking nuts end in three or four digit numbers. The last two digits refer to threads per inch, and the one or two preceding digits stand for the nut size in 16ths of an inch.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机螺母有各种形状和尺寸。它们由镀镉碳钢、不锈钢或阳极化的 2024T 铝合金制成，并可提供右旋或左旋螺纹。螺母上没有任何识别标记或字母。只有自锁型螺母才能靠铝、黄铜或嵌件的特征金属光泽或颜色来识别。还可以通过其结构来进一步识别。

飞机螺母可分为两大类：非自锁螺母和自锁螺母。非自锁螺母是必须用外部锁紧装置（如开口销、保险丝或锁紧螺母）来保险的螺母。自锁螺母则将锁紧功能作为其组成部分。

### 非自锁螺母

大多数常见的螺母类型，包括普通螺母、槽形螺母、槽形抗剪螺母、普通六角螺母、轻六角螺母和普通锁紧螺母，都是非自锁型。

LC 锁栓套环 C 材料 C = 24ST 铝合金（绿色）。仅与热处理合金锁栓配合使用。

槽形抗剪螺母 AN320 设计用于通常只承受剪切应力的装置，如钻孔叉耳螺栓和螺纹锥销。与槽形螺母一样，它也开有槽齿以便保险。但要注意，这种螺母的深度和强度都不如槽形螺母；另外，它的槽齿也不如槽形螺母的深。

普通六角螺母 AN315 和 AN335（细牙和粗牙螺纹）结构坚固，因此适合承受较大的拉伸载荷。但由于它需要辅助锁紧装置（如锁紧螺母或锁紧垫圈），在飞机结构上的使用有一定限制。

轻六角螺母 AN340 和 AN345（细牙和粗牙螺纹）比普通六角螺母轻得多，必须用辅助装置锁紧。它用于各种轻拉伸要求的场合。

普通锁紧螺母 AN316 用作普通螺母、紧定螺钉、螺纹杆端及其他装置的锁紧装置。

N = 非填孔（间隙配合）

槽形螺母 AN310 用于带钻孔杆部的 AN 六角头螺栓、叉耳螺栓、吊环螺栓、钻孔头螺栓或螺柱。它坚固耐用，能承受较大的拉伸载荷。螺母上的槽（称为槽齿）用于容纳开口销或保险丝以实现保险。

蝶形螺母 AN350 用于所需紧度可用手获得、且组件需频繁拆卸的场合。

### 自锁螺母

顾名思义，自锁螺母不需要辅助保险手段，其结构中已包含保险功能。已设计出许多种自锁螺母，其使用也已相当广泛。常见应用包括：

• 防摩擦轴承和操纵滑轮的连接

• 附件、检查孔周围的托板螺母以及小油箱安装口的连接

• 摇臂箱盖和排气管的连接 自锁螺母可在制造厂限制条件下用于合格审定飞机。飞机上使用自锁螺母是为了提供在剧烈振动下不会松脱的紧密连接。不得在会使螺母或螺栓旋转的接头处使用自锁螺母。只要轴承内圈被螺母和螺栓夹紧在支承结构上，自锁螺母就可以与防摩擦轴承和操纵滑轮配合使用。板件必须以可靠方式固定到结构上，以消除拧紧螺栓或螺钉时的转动或错位。

目前使用的自锁螺母有两大类型：全金属型和纤维锁紧型。为简便起见，本手册只讨论三种典型的自锁螺母：代表全金属型的 Boots 自锁螺母和不锈钢自锁螺母，以及代表纤维嵌件型的弹性自锁螺母。

F = 61ST 铝合金（本色）。仅与 75ST 铝合金锁栓配合使用。

R = 低碳钢（镀镉）。仅与热处理合金钢锁栓配合，用于高温场合。

Boots 自锁螺母 Boots 自锁螺母为整体式全金属结构，设计用于在剧烈振动下保持紧固。注意它有两个部分，实质上是合为一体的两个螺母：一个锁紧螺母和一个承载螺母。两部分由一根弹簧连接，弹簧是螺母的组成部分。

弹簧使锁紧部分与承载部分保持一定距离，使两组螺纹错开或间隔开，这样，已经拧过承载部分的螺栓必须克服弹簧力将锁紧部分向外推，才能正确与锁紧部分的螺纹啮合。

弹簧通过锁紧部分对螺栓施加恒定的锁紧力，其方向与拧紧螺母的力相同。在这种螺母中，承载部分具有与同尺寸标准螺母相当的螺纹强度，而锁紧部分压紧螺栓螺纹，将螺母牢固锁定在位。只有对螺母施加扳手才能将其松开。该螺母可以拆卸并重复使用而不降低效率。

Boots 自锁螺母有三种不同的弹簧型式和各种形状、尺寸。最常见的翼型尺寸范围从 6 号到 1/4 英寸，Rol-top 型从 1/4 英寸到 1/6 英寸，波纹管型尺寸范围从 8 号到 3/8 英寸。翼型螺母由阳极氧化铝合金、镀镉碳钢或不锈钢制成。Rol-top 螺母为镀镉钢，波纹管型仅由铝合金制成。

不锈钢自锁螺母 不锈钢自锁螺母可用手拧上和拧下，因为只有当螺母贴合在实心表面上并拧紧时，其锁紧作用才发生。该螺母由两部分组成：一个带斜面锁紧台肩和键的壳体，以及一个带锁紧台肩和开槽键槽的螺纹嵌件。在螺母被拧紧之前，它在螺栓上很容易转动，因为螺纹嵌件与螺栓尺寸相配。然而，当螺母贴合在实心表面上并被拧紧时，嵌件的锁紧台肩被向下拉并楔紧在壳体的锁紧台肩上。这一动作压缩螺纹嵌件，使其紧紧咬住螺栓。剖面图显示了壳体的键如何嵌入嵌件的开槽键槽，从而使壳体转动时螺纹嵌件随之转动。注意槽比键宽。这使槽能在螺母拧紧时被收窄，从而使嵌件被压缩。

弹性自锁螺母 弹性自锁螺母是一种标准螺母，其高度增大以容纳纤维锁定圈。该纤维圈非常坚韧耐用，浸入热水、冷水或普通溶剂（如乙醚、四氯化碳、油类和汽油）均不受影响。它不会损伤螺栓螺纹或镀层。

纤维锁定圈没有螺纹，其内径小于螺栓螺纹部分的最大直径或相应螺栓的外径。当螺母拧到螺栓上时，它像普通螺母一样工作，直到螺栓到达纤维圈。然而，当螺栓拧入纤维圈时，摩擦（或阻力）使纤维被向上推。这对承载部分产生很大的向下压力，并自动使螺母和螺栓螺纹的承载侧形成紧密接触。在螺栓被完全推过纤维圈后，该向下压力保持恒定。这种压力即使在剧烈振动下也能将螺母牢固地锁定在位。

几乎所有的弹性自锁螺母都是钢或铝合金的。不过，这类螺母实际上可用几乎任何金属制造。铝合金弹性自锁螺母经阳极化处理。钢制螺母镀镉。

通常，弹性自锁螺母可以多次使用而完全安全，且不损害其锁紧效率。重复使用弹性自锁螺母时，应确认纤维未失去锁紧摩擦力或变脆。若螺母能用手拧动，则应更换。

螺母拧紧后，应确认螺栓、螺柱或螺钉的圆头端或倒角端至少以整个圆头或倒角长度伸出螺母。平头螺栓、螺柱或螺钉应至少伸出螺母 1/32 英寸。直径 5/16 英寸及以上、带开口销孔的螺栓可与自锁螺母配合使用，但仅限于孔周围无毛刺的情况。螺纹损伤、端部粗糙的螺栓不可使用。不得对纤维锁定嵌件攻丝。弹性自锁螺母的自锁作用，来自螺栓螺纹在未攻丝的纤维中自行压出螺纹。

不得将弹性自锁螺母安装在温度高于 250 °F 的部位，因为超过这一温度点自锁作用的效果会降低。当发动机制造厂规定使用时，自锁螺母可用于飞机发动机及其附件。

自锁螺母底座有几种型式和材料，用于铆接或焊接到飞机结构或零件上。某些应用要求将自锁螺母安装在槽道内，这种布置只需少数几颗铆钉即可连接许多螺母。这些槽道是带规则间距螺母的轨道状底座，螺母可以是可拆卸的或不可拆卸的。可拆卸式装有可卡入或脱出槽道的浮动螺母，从而便于更换损坏的螺母。压入式和花键式等螺母依靠摩擦锚定，不得用于飞机结构。

### 板簧螺母

板簧螺母（如快速螺母）与标准自攻螺钉和钣金自攻螺钉配合，用于非结构部位。它们有多种用途，如支承管路卡箍、导管卡箍、电气设备、检查门等，并有几种类型可供选用。快速螺母由弹簧钢制成，拧紧前呈拱形。这种拱形弹簧锁紧可防止螺钉松动。这些螺母只应在飞机原始制造时使用它们的地方使用。

### 内、外扳手螺母

有两种商用的高强度内扳手或外扳手螺母：内、外扳手弹性自锁螺母和 Unbrako 内、外扳手螺母。二者都是自锁型，经过热处理，可承受高强度螺栓的拉伸载荷。

### 识别与编码

件号表示螺母的类型。常见类型及其相应件号如下：

• 普通型，AN315 和 AN335

• 锁紧型，AN316

• 轻六角型，AN340 和 AN345

• 槽形抗剪型，AN320 专利自锁类型的件号范围为 MS20363 至 MS20367。Boots、Flexloc、纤维锁紧螺母、弹性自锁螺母和自锁螺母都属于这一类。件号 AN350 分配给蝶形螺母。

件号后的字母和数字表示材料、尺寸、每英寸牙数以及螺纹是右旋还是左旋等项目。件号后的字母“B”表示螺母材料为黄铜，“D”表示 2017-T 铝合金，“DD”表示 2024-T 铝合金，“C”表示不锈钢，而用短划代替字母则表示镀镉碳钢。

短划或材料代号字母后的数字（或两位数字）是螺母的短划号，表示杆部尺寸以及与之配合的螺栓的每英寸牙数。短划号与通用螺栓件号编码中出现的第一个数字相对应。例如，短划加数字 3 表示该螺母配 AN3 螺栓（10-32）；短划加数字 4 表示配 AN4 螺栓（1/4-28）；短划加数字 5 表示配 AN5 螺栓（5/16-24）；以此类推。

自锁螺母的编码以三或四位数字结尾。最后两位数字指每英寸牙数，前面的一或两位数字表示以 1/16 英寸为单位的螺母尺寸。

其他一些常见螺母及其编码为：

编码 AN310D5R：AN310 = 飞机槽形螺母 D = 2024-T 铝合金 5 = 直径 5/16 英寸 R = 右旋螺纹（通常每英寸 24 牙）

编码 AN320-10：

AN320 = 飞机槽形抗剪螺母，镀镉碳钢 10 = 直径 5/8 英寸，每英寸 18 牙（此螺母通常为右旋螺纹）

编码 AN350B1032：

AN350 = 飞机蝶形螺母 B = 黄铜 10 = 10 号螺栓 32 = 每英寸牙数

## 4. 语法解析

### ① as 引导的非限定定语从句 + need no + but 并列
**原句**：As their name implies, self-locking nuts need no auxiliary means of safetying but have a safetying feature included as an integral part of their construction.
**拆解**：`As their name implies` 是 as 引导的非限定性定语从句，指代整个主句，意为“正如其名称所示”；主句由 `need no ... but have ...` 两个并列谓语构成；`included as an integral part of their construction` 是过去分词短语后置修饰 `a safetying feature`。
**要点**：`as` 引导的非限定从句可置于句首，此时 as 作 implies 的宾语；`need` 在此为实义及物动词，`need no + 名词` 相当于 `do not need any + 名词`；过去分词短语作后置定语含被动义，可扩展为 `which is included ...`。

### ② such ... that 引导结果状语从句
**原句**：The spring keeps the locking and load-carrying sections such a distance apart that the two sets of threads are out of phase
**拆解**：主句 `The spring keeps the ... sections such a distance apart`；`such a distance` 为名词短语作宾语补足语前的修饰，`apart` 为补语；`that` 引导结果状语从句，说明“距离大到什么程度”，从句中 `are out of phase` 为系表结构。
**要点**：`such + a + 形容词 + 名词 + that` 与 `so + 形容词/副词 + that` 对应，前者修饰名词、后者修饰形容词或副词；`keep + 宾语 + 补语` 表示“使……保持某种状态”，补语可为形容词、副词或介词短语。

### ③ provided (that) 引导条件状语从句
**原句**：They may be used with antifriction bearings and control pulleys, provided the inner race of the bearing is clamped to the supporting structure by the nut and bolt.
**拆解**：主句 `They may be used with ...`；`provided (that) ...` 引导条件从句，相当于 `if` 或 `on condition that`；从句为被动语态 `is clamped to ... by ...`。
**要点**：`provided (that)`、`providing (that)`、`on condition that` 都是正式的条件连词，比 `if` 更强调“前提条件”；条件从句中不用 will/would 表将来；`clamp A to B` 为固定搭配“把 A 夹紧到 B 上”。

### ④ until 引导的时间从句 + because 原因从句
**原句**：Until the nut is tightened, it spins on the bolt easily, because the threaded insert is the proper size for the bolt.
**拆解**：`Until the nut is tightened` 为时间状语从句，置于句首；主句 `it spins on the bolt easily`；`because` 引导原因状语从句解释原因；`the proper size for the bolt` 中 for 表示“对于、配”。
**要点**：`until` 从句用一般现在时表将来（主句为一般现在时）；`until` 与 `not ... until` 语义不同，前者表示“直到……为止（一直如此）”，后者表示“直到……才”；`spin on` 意为“在……上转动”。

### ⑤ 否定祈使句 + where 定语从句 + because 原因从句
**原句**：Do not install elastic stop nuts in places where the temperature is higher than 250 °F, because the effectiveness of the self-locking action is reduced beyond this point.
**拆解**：`Do not install ...` 为否定祈使句；`where the temperature is higher than 250 °F` 是定语从句修饰 places；`because` 从句说明禁止的理由，其中 `beyond this point` 指“超过这一温度”。
**要点**：维修手册中 `Do not + 动词原形` 是最典型的禁止句式，与其并列的常是 `Never + 动词`；`places where ...` 中 where = in which，先行词为地点名词；`higher than` 后接数值时用 `than`，不用 `then`。
