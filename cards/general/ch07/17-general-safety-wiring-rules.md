# General Safety Wiring Rules

> 通用 Ch 07 · General Safety Wiring Rules · 原文 PDF 第 325 页

## 1. 原文

When using the safety wire method of safetying, the following general rules should be followed:

1. Pigtail of 1⁄4 to 1⁄2 inch (three to six twists) should be made at the end of the wiring. This pigtail must be bent back or under to prevent it from becoming a snag.

2. The safety wire must be new upon each application.

3. When castellated nuts are to be secured with safety wire, tighten the nut to the low side of the selected torque range, unless otherwise specified, and if necessary, continue tightening until a slot aligns with the hole.

4. All safety wires must be tight after installation, but not under such tension that normal handling or vibration breaks the wire.

5. The wire must be applied so that all pull exerted by the wire tends to tighten the nut.

6. Twists should be tight and even, and the wire between the nuts as taut as possible without over twisting.

7. The safety wire should always be installed and twisted so that the loop around the head stays down and does not tend to come up over the bolt head, causing a slack loop.

### Cotter Pin Safetying

Cotter pin installation is shown. Castellated nuts are used with bolts that have been drilled for cotter pins. The cotter pin should fit neatly into the hole with very little side play. The following general rules apply to cotter pin safetying:

1. The prong bent over the bolt end should not extend beyond the bolt diameter. (Cut it off if necessary.)

2. The prong bent down should not rest against the surface of the washer. (Again, cut it off if necessary.)

3. If the optional wraparound method is used, the prongs should not extend outward from the sides of the nut.

4. All prongs should be bent over a reasonable radius. Sharp angled bends invite breakage. Tapping lightly with a mallet is the best method of bending the prongs.

A snap ring is a ring of metal, either round or flat in cross section, which is tempered to have spring like action. This spring like action holds the snap ring firmly seated in a groove. The external types are designed to fit in a groove around the outside of a shaft or cylinder and may be safety wired. The internal types fit in a groove inside a cylinder and are never safetied. A special type of pliers is designed to install each type of snap ring. Snap rings can be reused as long as they retain their shape and spring-like action.

## 2. 中文翻译

采用保险丝打结法打保险时，应遵循以下一般规则：

1. 打结末端应留出 1⁄4 至 1⁄2 英寸（三至六道扭结）的猪尾（pigtail）。该猪尾必须回弯或向下弯，以防成为刮挂物。

2. 每次打保险都必须使用新的保险丝。

3. 用保险丝固定槽形螺母时，除非另有规定，应把螺母拧到所选力矩范围的下限；必要时继续拧紧，直到某个槽与孔对齐。

4. 所有保险丝安装后必须拧紧，但张力不得大到使正常操作或振动就会把金属丝弄断。

5. 金属丝的走向必须使金属丝产生的所有拉力都趋于拧紧螺母。

6. 扭结应紧密均匀，螺母之间的金属丝应尽可能绷紧，但不得过度扭绞。

7. 保险丝的打结与扭绞方式应始终使绕过头部的环保持在下侧，不会朝上翻过螺栓头、形成松环。

### 开口销打保险

开口销的安装如图所示。槽形螺母与钻有开口销孔的螺栓配合使用。开口销应吻合地装入孔中，侧向活动量很小。以下一般规则适用于开口销打保险：

1. 弯过螺栓端头的那条销脚不得超出螺栓直径。（必要时将其剪短。）

2. 向下弯的那条销脚不得顶在垫圈表面上。（同样，必要时将其剪短。）

3. 如果采用可选的缠绕法，销脚不得从螺母两侧向外伸出。

4. 所有销脚都应弯成适当的圆弧半径。锐角弯折容易导致断裂。用木锤轻敲是弯折销脚的最佳方法。

卡簧是横截面为圆形或扁平的金属环，经回火处理而具有弹性作用。这种弹性作用使卡簧牢固地嵌在槽内。外置式卡簧设计成装入轴或圆柱外侧的槽中，可以打保险丝。内置式卡簧装入圆柱内侧的槽中，从不打保险。每种卡簧都有专用钳子用来安装。只要卡簧保持其形状和弹性作用，就可以重复使用。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① when + 动名词的条件状语 + 被动语态 + following 前置定语
**原句**：When using the safety wire method of safetying, the following general rules should be followed:
**拆解**：When using… 为「连词 + 现在分词」作条件/时间状语，其逻辑主语与主句一致（读者、维修人员），相当于 When you use…；主句 the following general rules should be followed 为情态被动，following 为现在分词作前置定语，修饰 rules。
**要点**：when + 动名词短语常省略与主句相同的主语，用于规则、程序类文本；the following + 复数名词后接冒号，是引出条文的固定句式，翻译作「以下……」。中文可把状语前置成「采用……时」。

### ② but not + such…that 结果从句（省略重复部分）
**原句**：All safety wires must be tight after installation, but not under such tension that normal handling or vibration breaks the wire.
**拆解**：主句 All safety wires must be tight after installation；but not under such tension 为并列的省略结构，其后省略了 must be（= but they must not be under such tension）；such tension that… 为 such…that 结果状语从句，从句主语 normal handling or vibration（并列主语，用 or 连接，谓语取单数 breaks），宾语 the wire。
**要点**：such + 名词 + that 表「如此……以致」，与 so + 形容词 + that 分工不同；并列省略使句子简洁，翻译时要补出「但也不得处于……的张力下」；tension 指拉伸的紧度，与 tight（拧紧、绷紧）搭配使用。

### ③ so that + 过去分词后置定语 + tend to do
**原句**：The wire must be applied so that all pull exerted by the wire tends to tighten the nut.
**拆解**：主干 The wire must be applied（被动）+ so that 引导目的状语从句；从句主语 all pull + exerted by the wire（过去分词短语作后置定语，= that is exerted by the wire）+ 谓语 tends to tighten（tend to do 搭配）+ 宾语 the nut。
**要点**：so that 在此表目的「使得」；exerted 是过去分词作定语，不要误认作谓语，从句真正的谓语是 tends；pull 为名词「拉力」。这句是保险丝走向的根本原则：拉力方向必须指向拧紧。

### ④ 并列句 + as…as possible 省略 + without doing
**原句**：Twists should be tight and even, and the wire between the nuts as taut as possible without over twisting.
**拆解**：前一分句 Twists should be tight and even（两个形容词并列作表语）；and 后 the wire between the nuts (should be) as taut as possible——省略了相同的 should be；as taut as possible 为 as…as possible 结构；without over twisting 为介词 + 动名词，表否定条件。
**要点**：并列句中后一分句省略与前面相同的助动词或系动词是英语的常规省略；as + 形容词 + as possible 表「尽可能……」，taut 指「绷紧的」；without + 动名词表「而不要……」，逻辑主语与主句一致。

### ⑤ 同位语插入 + which 定语从句 + be tempered to do
**原句**：A snap ring is a ring of metal, either round or flat in cross section, which is tempered to have spring like action.
**拆解**：主干 A snap ring is a ring of metal；either round or flat in cross section 为插入的同位成分，说明横截面形状；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词为 a ring of metal，从句谓语 is tempered（被动）+ to have spring like action 为目的/结果不定式。
**要点**：either…or… 连接两个形容词，修饰省略了的 cross-sectional shape；插入成分用逗号隔开，不打断主句结构；be tempered to do 意为「经回火处理而具有……」，temper 是热处理术语，不要译成「调和」。
