# Aircraft Hardware

> 通用 Ch 07 · Aircraft Hardware · 原文 PDF 第 281 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft hardware is the term used to describe the various types of fasteners and miscellaneous small items used in the manufacture and repair of aircraft. The importance of aircraft hardware is often overlooked because of its small size; however, the safe and efficient operation of any aircraft is greatly dependent upon the correct selection and use of aircraft hardware.

An aircraft, even though made of the best materials and strongest parts, would be of doubtful value unless those parts were firmly held together. Several methods are used to hold metal parts together; they include riveting, bolting, brazing, and welding. The process used must produce a union that is as strong as the parts that are joined.

Their specification number or trade name identifies most items of aircraft hardware. Threaded fasteners and rivets are identified by AN (Air Force-Navy) numbers, NAS (National Aircraft Standard) numbers, or MS (Military Standard) numbers. Quick-release fasteners are usually identified by factory trade names and size designations.

### Threaded Fasteners

Various types of fastening devices allow quick dismantling or replacement of aircraft parts that must be taken apart and put back together at frequent intervals. Riveting or welding these parts each time they are serviced would soon weaken or ruin the joint. Furthermore, some joints require greater tensile strength and stiffness than rivets can provide. Bolts and screws are two types of fastening devices that give the required security of attachment and rigidity. Generally, bolts are used where great strength is required, and screws are used where strength is not the deciding factor. Bolts and screws are similar in many ways. They are both used for fastening or holding, and each has a head on one end and screw threads on the other. Regardless of these similarities, there are several distinct differences between the two types of fasteners. The threaded end of a bolt is always blunt while that of a screw may be either blunt or pointed.

The threaded end of a bolt usually has a nut screwed onto it to complete the assembly. The threaded end of a screw may fit into a female receptacle, or it may fit directly into the material being secured. A bolt has a short threaded section and a comparatively long grip length or unthreaded portion, whereas a screw has a longer threaded section and may have no clearly defined grip length. Turning the nut on the bolt generally tightens a bolt assembly; the head of the bolt may or may not be designed for turning. Turning its head always tightens a screw.

When it becomes necessary to replace aircraft fasteners, a duplicate of the original fastener should be used if possible. If duplicate fasteners are not available, extreme care and caution must be used in selecting substitutes.

#### Classification of Threads

, screws, and nuts are threaded in the American National Coarse (NC) thread series, the American National Fine (NF) thread series, the American Standard Unified Coarse (UNC) thread series, or the American Standard Unified Fine (UNF) thread series. There is one difference between the American National series and the American Standard Unified series that should be pointed out. In the 1-inch diameter size, the NF thread specifies 14 threads per inch (1-14 NF), while the UNF thread specifies 12 threads per inch (1-12 UNF). Both types of threads are designated by the number of times the incline (threads) rotates around a 1-inch length of a given diameter bolt or screw. For example, a 1/4-28 thread indicates that a 1⁄4-inch (4⁄16 inch) diameter bolt has 28 threads in 1 inch of its threaded length.

Class of fit also designates threads. The Class of a thread indicates the tolerance allowed in manufacturing:

• Class 1 is a loose fit

• Class 2 is a free fit

• Class 3 is a medium fit

• Class 4 is a close fit Aircraft bolts are almost always manufactured in the Class 3, medium fit.

A Class 4 fit requires a wrench to turn the nut onto a bolt, whereas a Class 1 fit can easily be turned by hand. Generally, aircraft screws are manufactured with a Class 2 thread fit for ease of assembly.

Bolts and nuts are also produced with right-hand and left-hand threads. A right-hand thread tightens when turned clockwise; a left-hand thread tightens when turned counterclockwise.

Aircraft Bolts Aircraft bolts are fabricated from cadmium- or zinc-plated corrosion-resistant steel, un-plated corrosion-resistant steel, or anodized-aluminum alloys. Most bolts used in aircraft structures are either general purpose, AN bolts, NAS internal wrenching or close tolerance bolts, or MS bolts. In certain cases, aircraft manufacturers make bolts of different dimensions or greater strength than the standard types. Such bolts are made for a particular application, and it is of extreme importance to use like bolts in replacement. The letter “S” stamped on the head usually identifies special bolts.

AN bolts come in three head styles: hex head, Clevis, and eyebolt. NAS bolts are available in hex head, internal wrenching, and countersunk head styles. MS bolts come in hex head and internal wrenching styles.

#### Close Tolerance Bolts

Close tolerance bolts are machined more accurately than the general-purpose bolt. Close tolerance bolts may be hex headed (AN-173 through AN-186) or have a 100° countersunk head (NAS-80 through NAS-86). They are used in applications where a tight drive fit is required. (The bolt moves into position only when struck with a 12- to 14-ounce hammer.)

#### Internal Wrenching Bolts

Internal wrenching bolts, (MS-20004 through MS-20024 or NAS-495) are fabricated from high-strength steel and are suitable for use in both tension and shear applications. When they are used in steel parts, the bolt hole must be slightly countersunk to seat the large corner radius of the shank at the head. In Dural material, a special heat-treated washer must be used to provide an adequate bearing surface for the head. The head of the internal wrenching bolt is recessed to allow the insertion of an internal wrench when installing or removing the bolt. Special high-strength nuts are used on these bolts. Replace an internal wrenching bolt with another internal wrenching bolt. Standard AN hex head bolts and washers cannot be substituted for them, as they do not have the required strength.

#### Identification and Coding

Bolts are manufactured in many shapes and varieties. A clear-cut method of classification is difficult. The shape of the head, method of securing, material used in fabrication, or the expected usage can identify bolts.

AN-type aircraft bolts can be identified by the code markings on the bolt heads. The markings generally denote the bolt manufacturer, the material used to make the bolt, and whether the bolt is a standard AN-type or a special purpose bolt.

• AN st andard steel bolts are marked with either a raised dash or asterisk or a single raised dash.

• AN aluminum-alloy bolts are marked with two raised dashes to indicate corrosion-resistant steel.

• Additional information, such as bolt diameter, bolt length, and grip length, may be obtained from the bolt part number.

For example, in the bolt part number AN3DD5A,

• The “AN” designates that it is an Air Force-Navy standard bolt.

• The “3” indicates the diameter in sixteenths of an inch (3⁄16).

• The “DD” indicates the material is 2024 aluminum alloy.

• The letter “C” in place of the “DD” would indicate corrosion-resistant steel, and the absence of the letters would indicate cadmium-plated steel.

• The “5” indicates the length in eighths of an inch (5⁄8).

• the “A” indicates that the shank is undrilled. If the letter “H” preceded the “5” in addition to the “A” following it, the head would be drilled for safetying.

Close tolerance NAS bolts are marked with either a raised or recessed triangle. The material markings for NAS bolts are the same as for AN bolts, except that they may be either raised or recessed. Bolts inspected magnetically (Magnaflux) or by fluorescent means (Zyglo) are identified by means of colored lacquer or a head marking of a distinctive type.

#### Special-Purpose Bolts

Bolts designed for a particular application or use is classified as special-purpose bolts. Clevis bolts, eyebolts, Jo-bolts, and lockbolts are special-purpose bolts.

Clevis Bolts The head of a Clevis bolt is round and is either slotted to receive a common screwdriver or recessed to receive a cross point screwdriver. This type of bolt is used only where shear loads occur and never in tension. It is often inserted as a mechanical pin in a control system.

Eyebolt The eyebolt is a special-purpose bolt used where external tension loads are to be applied. The eyebolt is designed for the attachment of devices, such as the fork of a turnbuckle, a Clevis, or a cable shackle. The threaded end may or may not be drilled for safetying.

Jo-Bolt Jo-bolt is a trade name for an internally threaded three-piece rivet. The Jo-bolt consists of three parts: a threaded steel-alloy bolt, a threaded steel nut, and an expandable stainless steel sleeve. The parts are factory preassembled. As the Jo-bolt is installed, the bolt is turned while the nut is held. This causes the sleeve to expand over the end of the nut, forming the blind head and clamping against the work. When driving is complete, a portion of the bolt breaks off. The high shear and tensile strength of the Jo-bolt makes it suitable for use in cases of high stresses where some of the other blind fasteners would not be practical. Jo-bolts are often a part of the permanent structure of late model aircraft. They are used in areas that are not often subjected to replacement or servicing. (Because it is a three-part fastener, it should not be used where any part, in becoming loose, could be drawn into the engine air intake.) Other advantages of using Jo-bolts are their excellent resistance to vibration, weight saving, and fast installation by one person.

Presently, Jo-bolts are available in four diameters:

• 200 series, approximately 3⁄16 inch in diameter

• 260 series, approximately 1⁄4 inch in diameter

• 312 series, approximately 5⁄16 inch in diameter

• 375 series, approximately 3⁄8 inch in diameter.

Jo-bolts are available in three head styles: F (flush), P (hex head), and FA (flush millable).

Lockbolts Lockbolts are used to attach two materials permanently. They are lightweight and are equal in strength to standard bolts. Lockbolts are manufactured by several companies and conform to Military Standards, which specify the size of a lockbolt’s head in relation to the shank diameter, plus the alloy used in its construction. The only drawback to lockbolt installations is that they are not easily removable compared to nuts and bolts.

The lockbolt combines the features of a high-strength bolt and rivet, but it has advantages over both. The lockbolt is generally used in wing splice fittings, landing gear fittings, fuel cell fittings, longerons, beams, skin splice plates, and other major structural attachments. It is more easily and quickly installed than the conventional rivets or bolts and eliminates the use of lock washers, cotter pins, and special nuts. Like the rivet, the lockbolt requires a pneumatic hammer or “pull gun” for installation. When installed, it is rigidly and permanently locked in place. Three types of lockbolts are commonly used: the pull type, the stump type, and the blind type.

## 2. 中文翻译

航空硬件是指用于飞机制造和修理的各种紧固件及杂项小件。航空硬件因尺寸小而常被忽视；然而，任何飞机的安全高效运行都在很大程度上取决于航空硬件的正确选择与使用。

一架飞机，即使由最好的材料和最强的零件制成，除非这些零件被牢固地连接在一起，否则其价值也是可疑的。连接金属零件有几种方法，包括铆接、螺栓连接、钎焊和焊接。所用工艺必须产生与所连接零件同等强度的连接。

大多数航空硬件项目由其规范号或商品名来识别。螺纹紧固件和铆钉由 AN（空军-海军）号、NAS（国家航空标准）号或 MS（军用标准）号识别。快卸紧固件通常由工厂商品名和尺寸代号识别。

### 螺纹紧固件

各种紧固装置可让必须频繁分解和重新装配的飞机零件实现快速拆卸或更换。若每次维护都对这些零件进行铆接或焊接，接合处很快就会被削弱或损坏。此外，有些接头需要比铆钉所能提供的更高的抗拉强度和刚度。螺栓和螺钉是两种能提供所需连接安全性和刚性的紧固装置。一般来说，需要很大强度的地方使用螺栓，强度不是决定因素的地方使用螺钉。螺栓和螺钉在许多方面相似：二者都用于紧固或固定，一端有头，另一端有螺纹。尽管有这些相似之处，两类紧固件之间仍有几点明显区别。螺栓的螺纹端总是钝的，而螺钉的螺纹端可以是钝的或尖的。

螺栓的螺纹端通常拧上一个螺母以完成装配。螺钉的螺纹端可以装入内螺纹座，也可以直接拧入被固定的材料中。螺栓有一段较短的螺纹和相对较长的夹持长度（光杆部分），而螺钉的螺纹段较长，且可能没有明确的夹持长度。转动螺栓上的螺母通常使螺栓组件拧紧；螺栓头部可以设计成可转动，也可以不设计。转动螺钉头部则总是使螺钉拧紧。

当需要更换飞机紧固件时，应尽可能使用与原紧固件相同的零件。若没有相同的紧固件，选用代用品时必须极其谨慎小心。

#### 螺纹分类

、螺钉和螺母的螺纹分为美国国家标准粗牙（NC）螺纹系列、美国国家标准细牙（NF）螺纹系列、美国标准统一粗牙（UNC）螺纹系列或美国标准统一细牙（UNF）螺纹系列。美国国家标准系列与美国标准统一系列之间有一处差别需要指出。在 1 英寸直径规格中，NF 螺纹规定每英寸 14 牙（1-14 NF），而 UNF 螺纹规定每英寸 12 牙（1-12 UNF）。两种螺纹都以螺旋线（螺纹）在给定直径螺栓或螺钉 1 英寸长度上绕转的次数来表示。例如，1/4-28 螺纹表示直径为 1/4 英寸（4/16 英寸）的螺栓在其螺纹长度 1 英寸内有 28 牙。

配合等级也用来区分螺纹。螺纹等级表示制造中允许的公差：

• 1 级为松配合

• 2 级为自由配合

• 3 级为中等配合

• 4 级为紧密配合 飞机螺栓几乎总是按 3 级中等配合制造。

4 级配合需要用扳手才能把螺母拧上螺栓，而 1 级配合用手就能轻松拧动。一般来说，飞机螺钉按 2 级螺纹配合制造，以便于装配。

螺栓和螺母也分右旋螺纹和左旋螺纹。右旋螺纹顺时针转动时拧紧；左旋螺纹逆时针转动时拧紧。

飞机螺栓 飞机螺栓由镀镉或镀锌的耐蚀钢、未镀覆的耐蚀钢或阳极化的铝合金制成。飞机结构中使用的大多数螺栓是一般用途的 AN 螺栓、NAS 内扳手或紧公差螺栓，或 MS 螺栓。某些情况下，飞机制造厂会制造尺寸不同于标准型或强度高于标准型的螺栓。这类螺栓是为特定用途制造的，更换时使用同类螺栓极为重要。头部打有字母“S”的通常是专用螺栓。

AN 螺栓有三种头部型式：六角头、叉耳（Clevis）和吊环（eyebolt）。NAS 螺栓有六角头、内扳手和沉头型式。MS 螺栓有六角头和内扳手型式。

#### 紧公差螺栓

紧公差螺栓的加工精度高于通用螺栓。紧公差螺栓可以是六角头（AN-173 至 AN-186），也可以是 100° 沉头（NAS-80 至 NAS-86）。它们用于需要紧压配合的场合。（这种螺栓只有用 12 至 14 盎司的锤击打才能就位。）

#### 内扳手螺栓

内扳手螺栓（MS-20004 至 MS-20024 或 NAS-495）由高强度钢制成，适用于拉伸和剪切两种场合。用于钢制零件时，螺栓孔必须略微锪窝，以使螺栓杆部靠近头部的大圆角贴合。在硬铝材料中，必须使用特殊的经热处理垫圈，为头部提供足够的支承面。内扳手螺栓的头部有凹槽，以便安装或拆卸时插入内扳手。这些螺栓使用特殊的高强度螺母。内扳手螺栓只能用另一个内扳手螺栓更换。标准 AN 六角头螺栓和垫圈不能替代它们，因为其强度不够。

#### 识别与编码

螺栓的制造形状和品种繁多，难以采用清晰的分类方法。可通过头部形状、固定方式、制造材料或预期用途来识别螺栓。

AN 型飞机螺栓可通过螺栓头上的编码标记识别。这些标记通常表示螺栓制造厂、制造螺栓所用材料，以及该螺栓是标准 AN 型还是专用螺栓。

• AN 标准钢制螺栓标有一道凸起的短划或星号，或一道凸起的短划。

• AN 铝合金螺栓标有两道凸起短划，以表示耐蚀钢。

• 附加信息，如螺栓直径、螺栓长度和夹持长度，可从螺栓件号中获得。

例如，在螺栓件号 AN3DD5A 中：

• “AN”表示它是空军-海军标准螺栓。

• “3”表示直径，单位为 1/16 英寸（3/16）。

• “DD”表示材料为 2024 铝合金。

• 若用字母“C”取代“DD”，则表示耐蚀钢；若完全没有字母，则表示镀镉钢。

• “5”表示长度，单位为 1/8 英寸（5/8）。

• “A”表示杆部未钻孔。若在“5”之前有字母“H”，而在其后有“A”，则头部会钻有保险孔。

紧公差 NAS 螺栓标有凸起或凹下的三角形。NAS 螺栓的材料标记与 AN 螺栓相同，只是可以凸起或凹下。经磁力检查（Magnaflux）或荧光检查（Zyglo）的螺栓，用彩色漆或独特型式的头部标记来识别。

#### 专用螺栓

为特定应用或用途设计的螺栓归类为专用螺栓。叉耳螺栓、吊环螺栓、Jo 螺栓和锁栓都是专用螺栓。

叉耳螺栓 叉耳螺栓的头部是圆的，或开槽以配普通螺丝刀，或凹下以配十字螺丝刀。这类螺栓只用于承受剪切载荷的场合，绝不用于拉伸。它常作为机械销插入操纵系统中。

吊环螺栓 吊环螺栓是用于施加外部拉伸载荷的专用螺栓。吊环螺栓设计用于连接各种装置，如花篮螺栓的叉头、叉耳或钢索卸扣。其螺纹端可以钻孔以供打保险，也可以不钻。

Jo 螺栓 Jo 螺栓是一种内螺纹三件式铆钉的商品名。Jo 螺栓由三部分组成：螺纹合金钢螺栓、螺纹钢螺母和可膨胀不锈钢套筒。各零件在工厂预装好。安装 Jo 螺栓时，固定螺母并转动螺栓。这使套筒在螺母端部扩张，形成盲端头并压紧工件。铆接完成后，螺栓的一部分会断掉。Jo 螺栓的高抗剪和高抗拉强度使其适用于应力很高、其他某些盲紧固件不实用的场合。Jo 螺栓常常是新型飞机永久结构的一部分，用于不常更换或维护的部位。（由于它是三件式紧固件，不得用于任何部件一旦松动就可能被吸入发动机进气道的地方。）使用 Jo 螺栓的其他优点包括抗振性极好、减轻重量和可由一人快速安装。

目前，Jo 螺栓有四种直径：

• 200 系列，直径约 3/16 英寸

• 260 系列，直径约 1/4 英寸

• 312 系列，直径约 5/16 英寸

• 375 系列，直径约 3/8 英寸。

Jo 螺栓有三种头部型式：F（埋头）、P（六角头）和 FA（可铣埋头）。

锁栓 锁栓用于永久连接两种材料。它们重量轻，强度与标准螺栓相当。锁栓由多家公司制造，符合军用标准，该标准规定了锁栓头部尺寸与杆径的关系以及制造所用合金。锁栓安装的唯一缺点是，与螺母和螺栓相比不易拆卸。

锁栓兼具高强度螺栓和铆钉的特点，但比两者都有优势。锁栓一般用于机翼拼接接头、起落架接头、油箱接头、纵梁、梁、蒙皮拼接板及其他主要结构连接处。它比传统铆钉或螺栓更易于、更快速地安装，并省去锁紧垫圈、开口销和特殊螺母。与铆钉一样，锁栓安装需要气动锤或“拉枪”。安装后，它被刚性、永久地锁定就位。常用锁栓有三种型式：拉式、短杆式和盲式。

拉式 拉式锁栓主要用于飞机主结构和次结构。它们安装非常迅速，重量约为同等 AN 钢栓的一半。

橙色的经磁力检查的 AN 螺栓和螺母。安装这类锁栓需要专用气动“拉枪”。由于不需要顶铆，一人即可完成安装。

短杆式 短杆式锁栓虽然没有带拉槽的加长杆，但它们是拉式锁栓的配套紧固件。它们主要用于间隙不允许安装拉式锁栓的场合。安装短杆式锁栓所需的工具是一把标准气动铆钉锤（配有一个用于将套环压入销锁槽的锤头）和一根顶铁。

拉式 短杆式 盲式 盲式 盲式锁栓以完整单元或组件形式供应。它们具有极高的强度和夹紧板材的特性。盲式锁栓用于只能接触工件一侧的场合，一般用于难以打普通铆钉的地方。这类锁栓的安装方式与拉式锁栓相同。

共同特点 三种锁栓的共同特点是销上有环形锁槽，以及一个套环，套环被压入销的锁槽中，将销以拉伸状态锁定。拉式和盲式锁栓的销为拉式安装而加长，加长部分设有拉槽和拉断槽。

组成 拉式和短杆式锁栓的销由热处理合金钢或高强度铝合金制成。配套套环由铝合金或低碳钢制成。盲式锁栓由热处理合金钢销、盲套筒和填充套筒、低碳钢套环及碳钢垫圈组成。

代用 合金钢锁栓可替代同直径、同头部型式的钢制高抗剪铆钉、实心钢铆钉或 AN 螺栓。铝合金锁栓可替代同直径、同头部型式的实心铝合金铆钉。钢制和铝合金锁栓也可分别替代同直径的钢制和 2024T 铝合金螺栓。盲式锁栓可替代同直径的实心铝合金铆钉、不锈钢铆钉或所有盲铆钉。

编号系统 各种锁栓的编号系统在相关编号说明中解释。

夹持范围 要确定任何应用所需的螺栓夹持范围，可用钩形量规穿过孔测量材料厚度。测出该尺寸后，参照铆钉制造厂提供的图表选择正确的夹持范围。夹持范围图表的示例见相关资料。

安装后，锁栓套环应在其全长上被充分压入。销断端相对套环顶面的公差必须在规定尺寸之内。

当需要拆卸锁栓时，用锋利的冷凿将套环沿轴向劈开取下。注意不要使孔崩裂或变形。建议在劈开套环的对侧使用顶铁。然后可用冲子将销打出。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① even though 省略 + unless 条件 + 虚拟语气
**原句**：An aircraft, even though made of the best materials and strongest parts, would be of doubtful value unless those parts were firmly held together.
**拆解**：`even though made of ...` 是让步状语从句的省略形式，还原为 `even though it is made of ...`；主句 `would be of doubtful value` 用 `be of + 抽象名词` 表性质；`unless those parts were firmly held together` 为否定条件从句，与 `would` 构成虚拟语气，`were` 为虚拟式。
**要点**：`even though` 引导的从句若主语与主句一致且含 be，可省略“主语 + be”；`unless` = `if ... not`，其从句中不用 will/would；`be of value/importance` 是技术文本常见的系表结构。

### ② while 表对比 + that of 替代前文名词
**原句**：The threaded end of a bolt is always blunt while that of a screw may be either blunt or pointed.
**拆解**：`while` 在此不是“当……时”，而是表对比“而”；`that of a screw` 中 `that` 替代前文的 `the threaded end`，避免重复；`either ... or ...` 并列两个形容词作表语。
**要点**：`that/those of` 用于替代前文出现的名词（单数用 that，复数用 those），是技术英语避免重复的常用手段；`while` 表对比时前后分句结构对称。

### ③ whereas 连接的对比并列句
**原句**：A bolt has a short threaded section and a comparatively long grip length or unthreaded portion, whereas a screw has a longer threaded section and may have no clearly defined grip length.
**拆解**：`whereas` 连接两个地位对等的分句，表示“而、然而”，构成螺栓与螺钉的逐项对比；两个分句都用 `have` 作谓语，第二分句另加情态动词 `may`。
**要点**：`whereas` 只用于书面语，必须连接两个完整分句，不能像 `while` 那样省略；对比项在词序上尽量保持平行（threaded section 对 threaded section）。

### ④ only when + 过去分词（省略 it is）
**原句**：The bolt moves into position only when struck with a 12- to 14-ounce hammer.
**拆解**：`only when struck ...` 中 `when` 从句省略了 `it is`，还原为 `when it is struck with ...`；`only` 限定 `when` 从句，强调“只有在……时”；`12- to 14-ounce` 是复合形容词作定语，ounce 用单数。
**要点**：时间状语从句中 `when/while/if + 过去分词` 是常见的省略结构；`only + 状语` 置于句中不引起倒装，只有置于句首时才倒装。

### ⑤ 过去分词短语作后置定语 + by means of
**原句**：Bolts inspected magnetically (Magnaflux) or by fluorescent means (Zyglo) are identified by means of colored lacquer or a head marking of a distinctive type.
**拆解**：`inspected magnetically ... or by fluorescent means` 是过去分词短语后置修饰 `Bolts`，相当于定语从句 `which are inspected ...`；主句谓语 `are identified`；`by means of` 表示“用……手段”。
**要点**：过去分词短语作定语含被动意义，可扩展为被动语态的定语从句；注意区分 `by means of`（凭借手段）与 `by`（被、通过），同一句中 `inspected by fluorescent means` 与 `identified by means of` 两个介词结构含义不同。
