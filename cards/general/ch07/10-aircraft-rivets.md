# Aircraft Rivets

> 通用 Ch 07 · Aircraft Rivets · 原文 PDF 第 296 页

## 1. 原文

Sheets of metal must be fastened together to form the aircraft structure, and this is usually done with solid aluminum-alloy rivets. A rivet is a metal pin with a formed head on one end when the rivet is manufactured. The shank of the rivet is inserted into a drilled hole, and its shank is then upset (deformed) by a hand or pneumatic tool. The second head, formed either by hand or by pneumatic equipment, is called a “shop head.” The shop head functions in the same manner as a nut on a bolt. In addition to their use for joining aircraft skin sections, rivets are also used for joining spar sections, for holding rib sections in place, for securing fittings to various parts of the aircraft, and for fastening innumerable bracing members and other parts together. The rivet creates a bond that is at least as strong as the material being joined.

Two of the major types of rivets used in aircraft are the common solid shank type, which must be driven using a bucking bar, and the special (blind) rivets, which may be installed where it is impossible to use a bucking bar.

Aircraft rivets are not hardware store rivets. Rivets purchased at a hardware store should never be used as a substitute for aircraft quality rivets. The rivets may be made from very different materials, the strength of the rivets differs greatly, and their shear strength qualities are very different. The countersunk heads on hardware store rivets are 78°, whereas countersunk aircraft rivets have 100° angle heads for more surface contact to hold it in place.

### Standards and Specifications

The FAA requires that the structural strength and integrity of type-certificated aircraft conform to all airworthiness requirements. These requirements apply to performance, structural strength, and integrity as well as flight characteristics. To meet these requirements, each aircraft must meet the same standards. To accomplish standardization, all materials and hardware must be manufactured to a standard of quality. Specifications and standards for aircraft hardware are usually identified by the organization that originated them. Some of the common standardizing organizations include:

AMS Aeronautical Material Specifications AN Air Force-Navy AND Air Force-Navy Design AS Aeronautical Standard ASA American Standards Association ASTM American Society for Testing Materials MS Military Standard NAF Naval Aircraft Factory NAS National Aerospace Standard SAE Society of Automotive Engineers When a MS20426-AD4-6 rivet is required, the specifications have already been written for it in the Military Standard (MS) specifications. That information is available to the aircraft manufacturers, the rivet manufacturers and the mechanic. The specifications designate the material to be used as well as the head type, diameter, and length of the rivet. The use of standardized materials in the production of aircraft makes each aircraft exactly the same as the previous one and makes them less expensive to build.

Aircraft rivets are manufactured to much higher standards and specifications than rivets manufactured for general use. When aircraft manufacturers started building all-metal aircraft in the 1930s, different manufacturers had different rivet head designs. Brazier heads, modified brazier heads, button heads, mushroom heads, flatheads, and 78° countersunk heads were used. As aircraft standardized, four rivet head designs almost completely replaced all the others. Rivets exposed to the airflow over the top of the structure are usually either universal head MS20470 or 100° countersunk head MS20426 rivets. For rivets used in internal structures, the roundhead MS20430 and the flathead MS20442 are generally used.

### Solid Shank Rivets

Solid shank rivets are generally used in repair work. They are identified by the kind of material of which they are made, their head type, size of shank, and their temper condition. The designation of the solid shank rivet head type, such as universal head, roundhead, flathead, countersunk head, and brazier head, depends on the cross-sectional shape of the head. The temper designation and strength are indicated by special markings on the head of the rivet.

The material used for most aircraft solid shank rivets is aluminum alloy. The strength and temper conditions of aluminum-alloy rivets are identified by digits and letters similar to those adopted for the identification of strength and temper conditions of aluminum and aluminum-alloy stock. The 1100, 2017-T, 2024-T, 2117-T, and 5056 rivets are the five grades usually available.

The 1100 rivet, which is composed of 99.45 percent pure aluminum, is very soft. It is for riveting the softer aluminum alloys, such as 1100, 3003, and 5052, which are used for nonstructural parts (all parts where strength is not a factor). The riveting of map cases is a good example of where a rivet of 1100 aluminum alloy may be used.

The 2117-T rivet, known as the field rivet, is used more than any other for riveting aluminum alloy structures. The field rivet is in wide demand, because it is ready for use as received and needs no further heat-treating or annealing. It also has a high resistance to corrosion.

The 2017-T and 2024-T rivets are used in aluminum-alloy structures where more strength is needed than is obtainable with the same size 2217-T rivet. These rivets are known as “ice box rivets,” are annealed and must be kept refrigerated until they are to be driven. The 2017-T rivet should be driven within approximately 1 hour and the 2024-T rivet within 10 to 20 minutes after removal from refrigeration.

The 5056 rivet is used for riveting magnesium-alloy structures because of its corrosion-resistant qualities in combination with magnesium.

Mild steel rivets are used for riveting steel parts. The corrosion-resistant steel rivets are for riveting corrosion-resistant steels in firewalls, exhaust stack brackets, and similar structures.

Monel rivets are used for riveting nickel-steel alloys. They can be substituted for those made of corrosion-resistant steel in some cases.

The use of copper rivets in aircraft repair is limited. Copper rivets can be used only on copper alloys or nonmetallic materials, such as leather.

Metal temper is an important factor in the riveting process, especially with aluminum alloy rivets. Aluminum-alloy rivets have the same heat-treating characteristics as aluminum-alloy stock. They can be hardened and annealed in the same manner as aluminum. The rivet must be soft, or comparatively soft, before a good head can be formed. The 2017-T and 2024-T rivets are annealed before being driven. They harden with age.

The process of heat-treating (annealing) rivets is much the same as that for stock. Either an electric air furnace, a salt bath, or a hot oil bath is needed. The heat-treating range, depending on the alloy, is 625 °F to 950 °F. For convenient handling, rivets are heated in a tray or a wire basket. They are quenched in cold water (70 °F) immediately after heat-treating.

The 2017-T and 2024-T rivets, which are heat-treatable rivets, begin to age harden within a few minutes after being exposed to room temperature. Therefore, they must be used immediately after quenching or else be placed in cold storage.

The most commonly used means for holding heat-treatable rivets at low temperature (below 32 °F) is to keep them in a refrigerator. They are referred to as “icebox” rivets. Under this storage condition, they remain soft enough for driving for up to 2 weeks. Any rivets not used within that time should be removed for reheat-treating.

Most metals, and therefore aircraft rivet stock, are subject to corrosion. Corrosion may be the result of local climatic conditions or the fabrication process used. It is reduced to a minimum by using metals that are highly resistant to corrosion and possess the correct strength-to-weight ratio.

Ferrous metals placed in contact with moist salt air rust if not properly protected. Nonferrous metals, those without an iron base, do not rust, but a similar process known as corrosion takes place. The salt in moist air (found in the coastal areas) attacks the aluminum alloys. It is a common experience to inspect the rivets of an aircraft, which has been operated near salt water, and find them badly corroded.

If a copper rivet is inserted into an aluminum-alloy structure, two dissimilar metals are brought in contact with each other. Remember, all metals possess a small electrical potential. Dissimilar metals in contact with each other in the presence of moisture cause an electrical current to flow between them and chemical byproducts to be formed. Principally, this results in the deterioration of one of the metals.

Certain aluminum alloys react to each other and, therefore, must be thought of as dissimilar metals. The commonly used aluminum alloys may be divided into the two groups.

Members within either group A or group B can be considered as similar to each other and will not react to others within the same group. A corroding action will take place, however, if any metal of group A comes in contact with a metal in group B in the presence of moisture.

Avoid the use of dissimilar metals whenever possible. Their incompatibility is a factor that was considered when the AN Standards were adopted. To comply with AN Standards, the manufacturers must put a protective surface coating on the rivets. This may be zinc chromate, metal spray, or an anodized finish.

The protective coating on a rivet is identified by its color. A rivet coated with zinc chromate is yellow, an anodized surface is pearl gray, and the metal sprayed rivet is identified by a silvery gray color. If a situation arises in which a protective coating must be applied on the job, paint the rivet with zinc chromate before it is used and again after it is driven.

Markings on the heads of rivets are used to classify their characteristics. These markings may be either a raised teat, two raised teats, a dimple, a pair of raised dashes, a raised cross, a single triangle, or a raised dash; some other heads have no markings.

The different markings indicate the composition of the rivet stock. As explained previously, the rivets have different colors to identify the manufacturers’ protective surface coating.

Roundhead rivets are used in the interior of the aircraft, except where clearance is required for adjacent members. The roundhead rivet has a deep, rounded top surface. The head is large enough to strengthen the sheet around the hole and, at the same time, resists tension.

The flathead rivet, like the roundhead rivet, is used on interior structures. It is used where maximum strength is needed and where there is not sufficient clearance to use a roundhead rivet. It is seldom, if ever, used on external surfaces. The brazier head rivet has a head of large diameter, which makes it particularly adaptable for riveting thin sheet stock (skin). The brazier head rivet offers only slight resistance to the airflow, and because of this factor, it is frequently used for riveting skin on exterior surfaces, especially on aft sections of the fuselage and empennage. It is used for riveting thin sheets exposed to the slipstream. A modified brazier head rivet is also manufactured; it is simply a brazier head of reduced diameter.

The universal head rivet is a combination of the roundhead, flathead, and brazier head. It is used in aircraft construction and repair in both interior and exterior locations. When replacement is necessary for protruding head rivets— roundhead, flathead, or brazier head—they can be replaced by universal head rivets.

The countersunk head rivet is flat topped and beveled toward the shank so that it fits into a countersunk or dimpled hole and is flush with the material’s surface. The angle at which the head slopes may vary from 78° to 120°. The 100° rivet is the most commonly used type. These rivets are used to fasten sheets over which other sheets must fit. They are also used on exterior surfaces of the aircraft, because they offer only slight resistance to the slipstream and help to minimize turbulent airflow.

The markings on the heads of rivets indicate the material of which they are made and, therefore, their strength. Although there are three materials indicated by a plain head, it is possible to distinguish their difference by color. The 1100 is an aluminum color; the mild steel is a typical steel color; and the copper rivet is a copper color. Any head marking can appear on any head style of the same material.

A part number identifies each type of rivet so that the user can select the correct rivet for the job. The type of rivet head is identified by AN or MS standard numbers. The numbers selected are in series and each series represents a particular type of head. The most common numbers and the types of heads they represent are:

AN426 or MS20426—countersunk head rivets (100°)

AN430 or MS20430—roundhead rivets AN441—flathead rivets AN456—brazier head rivets AN470 or MS20470—universal head rivets There are also letters and numbers added to a part number. There are many places on an aircraft where access to both sides of a riveted structure or structural part is impossible, or where limited space does not permit the use of a bucking bar. Also, in the attachment of many non-structural parts, such as aircraft interior furnishings, flooring, deicing boots, and the like, the full strength of solid shank rivets is not necessary.

For use in such places, special rivets have been designed that can be bucked from the front. Special rivets are sometimes lighter than solid shank rivets, yet amply strong for intended use. These rivets are produced by several manufacturers and have unique characteristics that require special installation tools, special installation procedures, and special removal procedures. That is why they are called special rivets. Because these rivets are often inserted in locations where one head (usually the shop head) cannot be seen, they are also called blind rivets.

## 2. 中文翻译

必须把金属板连接起来构成飞机结构，这通常用实心铝合金铆钉来完成。铆钉是制造时一端已制出铆钉头的金属销。铆钉杆插入钻好的孔中，然后用人力工具或气动工具将铆钉杆镦粗（变形）。用人力或气动设备形成的第二个头称为“铆成头”。铆成头的作用与螺栓上的螺母相同。除了用于连接飞机蒙皮各段外，铆钉还用于连接翼梁段、固定翼肋段、把接头固定到飞机各部位，以及把无数支撑件和其他零件连接在一起。铆钉形成的连接至少与被连接材料本身等强度。

飞机上使用的铆钉主要有两类：普通实心杆铆钉，必须用顶铁压铆；以及特种（盲）铆钉，可用在无法使用顶铁的部位。

航空铆钉不是五金店卖的铆钉。绝不能用五金店购买的铆钉代替航空级铆钉。两者材料可能差别很大，强度相差悬殊，剪切强度特性也大不相同。五金店铆钉的埋头头为 78°，而航空埋头铆钉为 100° 头，接触面积更大，能把铆钉固定在位。

### 标准与规范

美国联邦航空局（FAA）要求型号合格审定飞机的结构强度与完整性符合所有适航要求。这些要求适用于性能、结构强度和完整性，也适用于飞行特性。为满足这些要求，每架飞机都必须符合同样的标准。为实现标准化，所有材料和硬件都必须按某种质量标准制造。航空硬件的规范和标准通常以制定该标准的机构来标识。常见的标准化机构有：

航空铆钉的制造标准与规范比一般用途的铆钉高得多。20 世纪 30 年代飞机制造商开始制造全金属飞机时，各家厂商的铆钉头设计各不相同。当时使用扁圆头、改良扁圆头、圆钮头、蘑菇头、平头以及 78° 埋头头。随着飞机标准化，四种铆钉头设计几乎完全取代了其他所有型式。暴露在结构上方气流中的铆钉通常是通用头 MS20470 或 100° 埋头头 MS20426 铆钉。用于内部结构的铆钉，一般使用圆头 MS20430 和平头 MS20442。

### 实心杆铆钉

实心杆铆钉一般用于修理工作。识别实心杆铆钉要看其制造材料、头型、杆径和状态。实心杆铆钉头型（如通用头、圆头、平头、埋头、扁圆头）的命名取决于头部的横截面形状。状态代号和强度则通过铆钉头上的特殊标记来表示。

大多数航空实心杆铆钉所用的材料是铝合金。铝合金铆钉的强度和状态用数字和字母表示，其方式与铝合金及铝合金原材料强度和状态的标识方式相似。通常可获得的铆钉有 1100、2017-T、2024-T、2117-T 和 5056 五个牌号。

1100 铆钉由 99.45% 的纯铝组成，很软。它用于铆接较软的铝合金，如 1100、3003 和 5052，这些合金用于非结构件（所有强度不成其为因素的零件）。地图盒的铆接就是可以使用 1100 铝合金铆钉的一个好例子。

2117-T 铆钉称为外场铆钉，在铝合金结构铆接中的用量超过其他任何铆钉。外场铆钉需求量很大，因为收到后即可使用，不需要再热处理或退火。它还具有很强的抗腐蚀能力。

2017-T 和 2024-T 铆钉用于对强度要求高于同尺寸 2217-T 铆钉的铝合金结构。这类铆钉称为“冰盒铆钉”，经过退火，必须冷藏保存，直到要压铆时才取出。2017-T 铆钉应在从冷藏中取出后约 1 小时内压铆，2024-T 铆钉应在 10 至 20 分钟内压铆。

5056 铆钉用于铆接镁合金结构，因为它在与镁配合时具有抗腐蚀性能。

低碳钢铆钉用于铆接钢制零件。耐蚀钢铆钉用于铆接防火墙、排气管支架及类似结构上的耐蚀钢。

蒙乃尔（Monel）铆钉用于铆接镍钢合金。在某些情况下，它们可以代替耐蚀钢铆钉。

铜铆钉在飞机修理中的使用有限。铜铆钉只能用于铜合金或非金属材料，如皮革。

金属的状态在铆接过程中是重要因素，尤其是对铝合金铆钉。铝合金铆钉具有与铝合金原材料相同的热处理特性，可以像铝一样被硬化和退火。铆钉必须较软（或相对较软），才能形成良好的铆钉头。2017-T 和 2024-T 铆钉在压铆前要退火，它们会随时间时效硬化。

铆钉热处理（退火）的工艺与原材料的热处理大致相同。需要电热空气炉、盐浴或热油浴。视合金不同，热处理温度为 625 °F 至 950 °F。为便于操作，铆钉装在托盘或铁丝筐中加热。热处理后立即在冷水（70 °F）中淬火。

2017-T 和 2024-T 铆钉属于可热处理铆钉，暴露在室温下几分钟内就开始时效硬化。因此，它们必须在淬火后立即使用，否则就要放入冷藏。

（原文此处夹入一列螺纹规格碎片：5/16–18、3/8–16、7/16–14、9/16–12、5/8–11、5/16–24、9/16–18、5/8–18、7/8–14）冰盒铆钉压铆后约 1 小时可获得其最大强度的一半，约 4 天达到全强度。2017-T 铆钉在室温下暴露 1 小时或更长时间后，必须重新热处理。2024-T 铆钉在室温下暴露超过 10 分钟后同样如此。

冰盒铆钉一旦从冰箱中取出，就不应与仍冷藏的铆钉混放。如果取出的铆钉超过 15 分钟内能用完的量，应把它们放入单独的容器中存放，以备重新热处理。如果操作得当，铆钉的热处理可以重复多次。

大多数金属（因而也包括航空铆钉原材料）都会腐蚀。腐蚀可能是当地气候条件造成的，也可能是加工工艺造成的。采用抗腐蚀性强且具有正确强度重量比的金属，可把腐蚀减到最低。

铁基金属若未妥善保护，与潮湿的含盐空气接触就会生锈。非铁基金属（不含铁基体的金属）不会生锈，但会发生类似的腐蚀过程。潮湿空气中的盐分（在沿海地区常见）会侵蚀铝合金。检查曾在海水附近使用过的飞机铆钉，发现它们严重腐蚀，这是很常见的经验。

如果把铜铆钉装入铝合金结构，就会使两种异种金属相互接触。要记住，所有金属都有微小的电位。异种金属相互接触且有湿气存在时，会使电流在两者之间流动并生成化学副产物。这主要导致其中一种金属劣化。

某些铝合金之间会相互反应，因此必须把它们看作异种金属。常用铝合金可分为两组。

同属 A 组或同属 B 组的构件可以视为彼此相似，不会与同组内的其他金属反应。但是，如果 A 组的任何金属与 B 组的金属在湿气存在下接触，就会发生腐蚀作用。

应尽可能避免使用异种金属。AN 标准被采纳时，已把异种金属的不相容性作为考虑因素。为符合 AN 标准，制造商必须在铆钉上加保护性表面涂层。涂层可以是锌铬酸盐、金属喷涂或阳极氧化处理。

铆钉上的保护涂层可通过颜色识别。涂锌铬酸盐的铆钉为黄色，阳极氧化表面为珍珠灰色，金属喷涂的铆钉呈银灰色。如果现场必须施加保护涂层，应在使用前给铆钉涂锌铬酸盐，压铆后再涂一次。

铆钉头上的标记用于区分其特性。标记可以是单个凸点、两个凸点、一个凹坑、一对凸划、一个凸十字、单个三角形或一道凸划；还有些头部没有任何标记。

不同的标记表示铆钉原材料的成分。如前所述，铆钉还有不同颜色，用来标识制造商的表面保护涂层。

圆头铆钉用于飞机内部，但相邻构件需要间隙的部位除外。圆头铆钉顶部为深圆弧面。头部足够大，可以加强孔周围的板材，同时又能抵抗拉力。

平头铆钉和圆头铆钉一样，用于内部结构。它用于需要最大强度、而间隙不足以使用圆头铆钉的部位。它很少用于外表面。扁圆头铆钉头部直径大，特别适合铆接薄板料（蒙皮）。扁圆头铆钉对流过的气流阻力很小，因此常用于外表面蒙皮的铆接，尤其是机身和尾翼后段。它用于铆接暴露在气流中的薄板。此外还制造改良扁圆头铆钉，它只是直径缩小的扁圆头。

通用头铆钉是圆头、平头和扁圆头的结合体。它用于飞机制造和修理中的内部与外部部位。当凸头铆钉（圆头、平头或扁圆头）需要更换时，可用通用头铆钉代用。

埋头铆钉顶部为平面，向杆部倾斜，以便装入埋头孔或压窝孔，并与材料表面齐平。头部斜面角度可为 78° 至 120°。100° 铆钉是最常用的类型。这种铆钉用于固定其上还要再装其他板件的板件。它们也用于飞机外表面，因为它们对流过的气流阻力很小，有助于把湍流减到最小。

铆钉头上的标记表示铆钉是用什么材料制成的，因而也表示其强度。虽然光面头部可能对应三种材料，但可以通过颜色区分它们的差别。1100 为铝的本色；低碳钢为典型的钢色；铜铆钉为铜色。任何一种头部标记都可以出现在同一种材料的任何一种头型上。

每种铆钉都有件号，以便使用者为该项工作选择正确的铆钉。铆钉头型用 AN 或 MS 标准编号标识。所选编号成系列，每个系列代表一种特定的头型。最常见的编号及其代表的头型如下：

AN426 或 MS20426——埋头铆钉（100°）

AN430 或 MS20430——圆头铆钉；AN441——平头铆钉；AN456——扁圆头铆钉；AN470 或 MS20470——通用头铆钉。件号中还加有字母和数字。字母表示合金成分；数字表示铆钉直径和长度。合金代号中常用的字母有：

A——1100 或 3003 成分的铝合金；AD——2117-T 成分的铝合金；D——2017-T 成分的铝合金；DD——2024-T 成分的铝合金；B——5056 成分的铝合金；C——铜；M——蒙乃尔。AN 标准编号后不带字母，表示该铆钉由低碳钢制造。

材料成分字母后的第一个数字表示铆钉杆直径，以 1/32 英寸为单位。例如 3 表示 3/32，5 表示 5/32，依此类推。

最后一个（或几个）数字与前一个数字用短横线分隔，表示铆钉杆长度，以 1/16 英寸为单位。例如 3 表示 3/16，7 表示 7/16，11 表示 11/16，依此类推。

铆钉识别标记的一个例子是：

AN470AD3-5——完整件号；AN——空军-海军标准编号；470——通用头铆钉；AD——2117-T 铝合金；3——直径 3/32；5——长度 5/16

飞机上有很多部位无法接近铆接结构或结构件的两面，或者空间有限、无法使用顶铁。此外，在连接许多非结构件（如飞机内饰、地板、除冰套等）时，并不需要实心杆铆钉的全部强度。

为用于这类部位，设计了可以从前端压铆的特种铆钉。特种铆钉有时比实心杆铆钉更轻，但对预定用途来说强度足够。这类铆钉由若干制造商生产，具有独特的特性，需要专用的安装工具、专用的安装程序和专用的拆卸程序。正因如此，它们被称为特种铆钉。由于这类铆钉常装在看不到一个头（通常是铆成头）的部位，所以又叫盲铆钉。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 并列句 + 被动 + 不定式目的状语
**原句**：Sheets of metal must be fastened together to form the aircraft structure, and this is usually done with solid aluminum-alloy rivets.
**拆解**：and 连接两个并列分句；前一分句为情态被动 must be fastened together，to form… 为目的状语；后一分句 this is usually done with… 中 this 指代前一分句整件事，with 引出手段。
**要点**：do sth with + 工具/手段 是「用……来做」的固定搭配，不能用 by（by 后接动名词表示方式）。this 作代词回指前面整句内容，是技术英语避免重复的常用手段，翻译时要还原成「这一连接」。

### ② that 定语从句 + as…as 比较 + 分词后置定语
**原句**：The rivet creates a bond that is at least as strong as the material being joined.
**拆解**：主句 The rivet creates a bond；that 引导定语从句修饰 bond；从句为系表结构 is at least as strong as the material…，as…as 比较结构；the material 后接现在分词被动式 being joined 作后置定语。
**要点**：as strong as 第一个 as 是副词、第二个是连词，后面接比较对象；being joined 是现在分词被动式，表示「正在被连接的材料」，等于 which is being joined，不能写成 joined（那样会被读成「已被连好的材料」，与语义不符）。

### ③ 两个非限制性定语从句 + where 从句中 it 作形式主语
**原句**：Two of the major types of rivets used in aircraft are the common solid shank type, which must be driven using a bucking bar, and the special (blind) rivets, which may be installed where it is impossible to use a bucking bar.
**拆解**：主干为 Two of the major types… are A and B；过去分词短语 used in aircraft 后置修饰 rivets；两个 which 引导非限制性定语从句，分别修饰 the common solid shank type 与 the special (blind) rivets；第二个从句中 where it is impossible to use… 为地点状语从句，it 是形式主语，真正主语是 to use a bucking bar。
**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号与主句隔开，起补充说明作用，不能用 that 引导；driven using a bucking bar 中 using 是现在分词表方式，等于 by using。并列的两个表语各带一个定语从句，翻译时宜拆成两句。

### ④ never 与被动语态构成的禁止
**原句**：Rivets purchased at a hardware store should never be used as a substitute for aircraft quality rivets.
**拆解**：主语 Rivets + 过去分词短语 purchased at a hardware store 作后置定语；谓语 should never be used（情态被动 + 否定副词）；as a substitute for… 为方式状语。
**要点**：never 放在情态动词之后、be 之前，是「绝不应」的固定位置，不能挪到句首（那会变成祈使句的 never use）。substitute for 是「代替……的替代品」，注意介词是 for 而不是 of。后置定语 purchased… 可还原为 which are purchased…。

### ⑤ require that + 虚拟语气
**原句**：The FAA requires that the structural strength and integrity of type-certificated aircraft conform to all airworthiness requirements.
**拆解**：主句 The FAA requires + that 宾语从句；从句主语为 the structural strength and integrity of type-certificated aircraft，谓语 conform 用动词原形（虚拟语气，省略 should）；conform to 为固定搭配。
**要点**：require/demand/insist 后的 that 从句用 (should) + 动词原形，所以是 conform 而不是 conforms；type-certificated 是「型号合格审定」的固定译法，作前置定语修饰 aircraft。conform to 意为「符合」，介词不能换成 with。

### ⑥ 非限制性定语从句 + after being + 过去分词
**原句**：The 2017-T and 2024-T rivets, which are heat-treatable rivets, begin to age harden within a few minutes after being exposed to room temperature.
**拆解**：主语 The 2017-T and 2024-T rivets 后接 which 引导的非限制性定语从句；主句谓语 begin to age harden；within a few minutes after being exposed to room temperature 为时间状语，after 是介词，后接动名词被动式 being exposed。
**要点**：after being exposed 相当于 after they are exposed，介词后接动名词被动式是技术文本的常见压缩；age harden 是「时效硬化」的动词化表达，两个词连用不加连字符（作定语时才写 age-hardening）。begin to do 强调动作开始，与 begin doing 语义相近但更常用于客观过程。
