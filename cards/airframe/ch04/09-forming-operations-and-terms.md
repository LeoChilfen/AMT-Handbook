# Forming Operations & Terms

> 机体 Ch 04 · Forming Operations & Terms · 原文 PDF 第 219 页

## 1. 原文

Forming requires either stretching or shrinking the metal, or sometimes doing both. Other processes used to form metal include bumping, crimping, and folding.

### Stretching

Stretching metal is achieved by hammering or rolling metal under pressure. For example, hammering a flat piece of metal causes the material in the hammered area to become thinner in that area. Since the amount of metal has not been decreased, the metal has been stretched. The stretching process thins, elongates, and curves sheet metal. It is critical to ensure the metal is not stretched too much, making it too thin, because sheet metal does not rebound easily.

Stretching one portion of a piece of metal affects the surrounding material, especially in the case of formed and extruded angles. For example, hammering the metal in the horizontal flange of the angle strip over a metal block causes its length to increase (stretched), making that section longer than the section near the bend. To allow for this difference in length, the vertical flange, which tends to keep the material near the bend from stretching, would be forced to curve away from the greater length.

### Shrinking

Shrinking metal is much more difficult than stretching it. During the shrinking process, metal is forced or compressed into a smaller area. This process is used when the length of a piece of metal, especially on the inside of a bend, is to be reduced. Sheet metal can be shrunk in by hammering on a V-block or by crimping and then using a shrinking block.

To curve the formed angle by the V-block method, place the angle on the V-block and gently hammer downward against the upper edge directly over the ”V .” While hammering, move the angle back and forth across the V-block to compress the material along the upper edge. Compression of the material along the upper edge of the vertical flange will cause the formed angle to take on a curved shape. The material in the horizontal flange will merely bend down at the center, and the length of that flange will remain the same.

To make a sharp curve or a sharply bent flanged angle, crimping and a shrinking block can be used. In this process, crimps are placed in the one flange, and then by hammering the metal on a shrinking block, the crimps are driven, or shrunk, one at a time.

Cold shrinking requires the combination of a hard surface, such as wood or steel, and a soft mallet or hammer because a steel hammer over a hard surface stretches the metal, as opposed to shrinking it. The larger the mallet face is, the better.

### Bumping

Bumping involves shaping or forming malleable metal by hammering or tapping—usually with a rubber, plastic, or rawhide mallet. During this process, the metal is supported by a dolly, a sandbag, or a die. Each contains a depression into which hammered portions of the metal can sink. Bumping can be done by hand or by machine.

### Crimping

Crimping is folding, pleating, or corrugating a piece of sheet metal in a way that shortens it or turning down a flange on a seam. It is often used to make one end of a piece of stove pipe slightly smaller so that one section may be slipped into another. Crimping one side of a straight piece of angle iron with crimping pliers causes it to curve.

### Folding Sheet Metal

Folding sheet metal is to make a bend or crease in sheets, plates, or leaves. Folds are usually thought of as sharp, angular bends and are generally made on folding machines such as the box and pan brake discussed earlier in this chapter.

## 2. 中文翻译

成形需要对金属进行拉伸或收缩，有时两者兼用。其他用于使金属成形的工艺包括锤拱（bumping）、起皱（crimping）和折弯（folding）。

### 拉伸

金属的拉伸是通过在压力下锤击或滚压金属来实现的。例如，锤击一块平板金属会使被锤击区域的材料在该处变薄。由于金属总量并未减少，所以金属是被拉伸了。拉伸过程使钣金变薄、伸长并弯曲。必须确保金属没有被过度拉伸而变得过薄，因为钣金不容易回弹。

拉伸金属的某一部分会影响周围的材料，在已成形的角材和挤压角材上尤其如此。例如，在金属垫块上锤击角材水平边的金属，会使其长度增加（被拉伸），从而使该段比靠近弯折处的部分更长。为容许这一长度差，垂直边（它倾向于阻止弯折附近的材料被拉伸）就会被迫朝背离较长一侧的方向弯曲。

### 收缩

收缩金属比拉伸金属困难得多。在收缩过程中，金属被挤压或压缩到更小的面积内。当需要缩短一段金属的长度时，尤其是在弯曲的内侧，就使用这种工艺。钣金可以在 V 形铁上锤击来收缩，也可以先起皱、再用收缩块收缩。

用 V 形铁法使成形角材弯曲时，把角材放在 V 形铁上，正对着 V 形槽，沿上边缘轻轻向下锤击。锤击时让角材在 V 形铁上来回移动，以压缩沿上边缘的材料。沿垂直边上边缘压缩材料，会使成形角材呈现弯曲形状。水平边上的材料只会在中央向下弯，该边的长度保持不变。

要做出急弯或急剧弯折的带弯边角材，可以使用起皱加收缩块的方法。在这一过程中，先在一个弯边上打起皱褶，然后在收缩块上锤击金属，把皱褶一个一个打平（即收缩）。

冷收缩需要硬质表面（如木块或钢块）与软质木槌或锤子配合，因为钢锤在硬表面上锤击会把金属拉伸，而不是收缩。槌面越大越好。

### 锤拱

锤拱是用锤击或轻敲使可锻金属成形，通常使用橡胶槌、塑料槌或生皮槌。在这一过程中，金属由顶铁、砂袋或模具支承。它们各有一个凹坑，被锤击的金属部分可以陷进去。锤拱可以手工进行，也可以用机器进行。

### 起皱

起皱是把一块钣金折起、打褶或压成波纹以使其缩短，或把接缝上的弯边向下翻折。它常用于把一段烟囱管的一端做得稍小一些，以便一段能套进另一段。用起皱钳在直条角铁的一侧起皱，会使它弯曲。

### 钣金折弯

钣金折弯是在薄板、厚板或板片上做出弯曲或折痕。折弯通常被认为是尖锐的、有角度的弯折，一般在折弯机上进行，如本章前面讨论过的盒形与盘形折弯机。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 动名词作宾语 + 破折号补充说明
**原句**：Bumping involves shaping or forming malleable metal by hammering or tapping—usually with a rubber, plastic, or rawhide mallet.
**拆解**：主语 Bumping，谓语 involves，宾语为动名词短语 shaping or forming malleable metal；by hammering or tapping 为方式状语（介词 by + 动名词）；破折号后 usually with... 补充说明所用工具。
**要点**：involve 后接动名词，不能接不定式；by 后同样是动名词，故用 hammering/tapping 而非 hammer/tap。破折号引出的成分不是独立分句，只是对方式的追加说明。

### ② it 形式主语 + 宾语从句 + 分词结果状语 + 原因从句
**原句**：It is critical to ensure the metal is not stretched too much, making it too thin, because sheet metal does not rebound easily.
**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 to ensure... 不定式短语；ensure 后接省略 that 的宾语从句 the metal is not stretched too much（被动）；making it too thin 为现在分词短语作结果状语，逻辑主语是前面的“过度拉伸”这件事；because 引导原因状语从句。
**要点**：making 的逻辑主语不是 the metal 而是整个“拉伸过度”的动作，故用主动形式；宾语从句里又套了一个被动 is not stretched，形成“被动 + 分词”的双层结构，翻译时拆成“如果拉得太薄，会……”。

### ③ 目的状语 + 非限制性定语从句 + 被动谓语
**原句**：To allow for this difference in length, the vertical flange, which tends to keep the material near the bend from stretching, would be forced to curve away from the greater length.
**拆解**：句首 To allow for this difference in length 为目的状语；主语 the vertical flange 后接 which 引导的非限制性定语从句，从句谓语 tends to keep...，keep sth. from doing 表“阻止……”；主句谓语 would be forced（被动），to curve away... 为主语补足语。
**要点**：主句被长定语从句隔断，骨架是 the vertical flange would be forced to curve；keep...from stretching 中的 stretching 是动名词，介词 from 后不能接动词原形。allow for 意为“为……留出余地”，不是“允许”。

### ④ while + 现在分词作时间状语 + 祈使句
**原句**：While hammering, move the angle back and forth across the V-block to compress the material along the upper edge.
**拆解**：While hammering 是“连词 + 现在分词”构成的时间状语，省略了 you are；主句为祈使句，谓语动词原形 move，宾语 the angle，back and forth 为方式状语，across the V-block 为地点状语；to compress... 为目的状语。
**要点**：while 后的分词与主句主语是主动关系（人锤击），故用 -ing；祈使句没有主语，翻译时补“你/操作者”。back and forth 是固定短语，不能写成 back and forward。

### ⑤ 并列被动分句 + 插入语
**原句**：In this process, crimps are placed in the one flange, and then by hammering the metal on a shrinking block, the crimps are driven, or shrunk, one at a time.
**拆解**：In this process 为范围状语；and 连接两个被动分句：crimps are placed... 与 the crimps are driven...；then 表先后，by hammering... 为方式状语；or shrunk 为插入的同义解释；one at a time 为频度状语。
**要点**：两个分句都用被动，动作的施事（操作者）被隐去；or shrunk 用逗号隔开，是对 driven 的换词解释，不是并列谓语。one at a time 意为“一次一个”，修饰 driven。

### ⑥ 介词短语连环作定语 + because 原因从句 + as opposed to
**原句**：Cold shrinking requires the combination of a hard surface, such as wood or steel, and a soft mallet or hammer because a steel hammer over a hard surface stretches the metal, as opposed to shrinking it.
**拆解**：主语 Cold shrinking，谓语 requires，宾语 the combination of A and B，其中 A = a hard surface（such as wood or steel 举例），B = a soft mallet or hammer；because 引导原因状语从句，主语 a steel hammer，over a hard surface 为后置定语，谓语 stretches，宾语 the metal；as opposed to shrinking it 为对比状语。
**要点**：of 后由 and 连接两个并列中心词，such as 插在第一个中心词后，容易误读成“hard surface 与 soft mallet 并列”而漏掉 combination of 的结构。as opposed to 后接动名词，表示“而不是”，与前面的 stretches 形成对比。
