# Description of Sandwich Structures

> 机体 Ch 07 · Description of Sandwich Structures · 原文 PDF 第 347 页

## 1. 原文

Theory A sandwich construction is a structural panel concept that consists in its simplest form of two relatively thin, parallel face sheets bonded to and separated by a relatively thick, lightweight core. The core supports the face sheets against buckling and resists out-of-plane shear loads. The core must have high shear strength and compression stiffness. Composite sandwich construction is most often fabricated using autoclave cure, press cure, or vacuum bag cure. Skin laminates may be precured and subsequently bonded to core, co-cured to core in one operation, or a combination of the two methods. Examples of honeycomb structure are: wing spoilers, fairings, ailerons, flaps, nacelles, floor boards, and rudders.

### Properties

Sandwich construction has high bending stiffness at minimal weight in comparison to aluminum and composite laminate construction. Most honeycombs are anisotropic; that is, properties are directional. Figure 7-18 illustrates the advantages of using a honeycomb construction. Increasing the core thickness greatly increases the stiffness of the honeycomb construction, while the weight increase is minimal. Due to the high stiffness of a honeycomb construction, it is not necessary to use external stiffeners, such as stringers and frames.

### Facing Materials

Most honeycomb structures used in aircraft construction have aluminum, fiberglass, Kevlar®, or carbon fiber face sheets.

Carbon fiber face sheets cannot be used with aluminum honeycomb core material, because it causes the aluminum to corrode. Titanium and steel are used for specialty applications in high-temperature constructions. The face sheets of many components, such as spoilers and flight controls, are very thin—sometimes only 3 or 4 plies. Field reports have indicated that these face sheets do not have a good impact resistance.

### Core Materials

#### Honeycomb

Each honeycomb material provides certain properties and has specific benefits. The most common core material used for aircraft honeycomb structures is aramid paper (Nomex® or Korex ®). Fiberglass is used for higher strength applications.

• Kraft paper—relatively low strength, good insulating properties, is available in large quantities, and has a low cost.

• Thermoplastics—good insulating properties, good energy absorption and/or redirection, smooth cell walls, moisture and chemical resistance, are environmentally compatible, aesthetically pleasing, and have a relatively low cost.

• Aluminum—best strength-to-weight ratio and energy absorption, has good heat transfer properties, electromagnetic shielding properties, has smooth, thin cell walls, is machinable, and has a relatively low cost.

• Steel—good heat transfer properties, electromagnetic shielding properties, and heat resistant.

• Specialty metals (titanium)—relatively high strength-to-weight ratio, good heat transfer properties, chemical resistance, and heat resistant to very high temperatures.

• Aramid paper—flame resistant, fire retardant, good insulating properties, low dielectric properties, and good formability.

• Fiberglass—tailorable shear properties by layup, low dielectric properties, good insulating properties, and good formability.

• Carbon—good dimensional stability and retention, high-temperature property retention, high stiffness, very low coefficient of thermal expansion, tailorable thermal conductivity, relatively high shear modulus, and very expensive.

• Ceramics—heat resistant to very high temperatures, good insulating properties, is available in very small cell sizes, and very expensive.

Honeycomb core cells for aerospace applications are usually hexagonal. The cells are made by bonding stacked sheets at special locations. The stacked sheets are expanded to form hexagons. The direction parallel to the sheets is called ribbon direction.

Bisected hexagonal core has another sheet of material cutting across each hexagon. Bisected hexagonal honeycomb is stiffer and stronger than hexagonal core. Overexpanded core is made by expanding the sheets more than is needed to make hexagons. The cells of overexpanded core are rectangular. Overexpanded core is flexible perpendicular to the ribbon direction and is used in panels with simple curves. Bell-shaped core, or flexicore, has curved cell walls, that make it flexible in all directions. Bell-shaped core is used in panels with complex curves.

Honeycomb core is available with different cell sizes. Small sizes provide better support for sandwich face sheets. Honeycomb is also available in different densities. Higher density core is stronger and stiffer than lower density core.

#### Foam

Foam cores are used on homebuilts and lighter aircraft to give strength and shape to wing tips, flight controls, fuselage sections, wings, and wing ribs. Foam cores are not commonly used on commercial type aircraft. Foams are typically heavier than honeycomb and not as strong. A variety of foams can be used as core material including:

• Polystyrene (better known as styrofoam)—aircraft grade styrofoam with a tightly closed cell structure and no voids between cells; high compressive strength and good resistance to water penetration; can be cut with a hot wire to make airfoil shapes.

• Phenolic—very good fire-resistant properties and can have very low density, but relatively low mechanical • Polyurethane—used for producing the fuselage, wing tips, and other curved parts of small aircraft; relatively inexpensive, fuel resistant, and compatible with most adhesives; do not use a hot wire to cut polyurethane foam; easily contoured with a large knife and sanding equipment.

• Polypropylene—used to make airfoil shapes; can be cut with a hot wire; compatible with most adhesives and epoxy resins; not for use with polyester resins, dissolves in fuels and solvents.

• Polyvinyl chloride (PVC) (Divinycell, Klegecell, and Airex)—a closed cell medium-to high-density foam with high compression strength, durability, and excellent fire resistance; can be vacuum formed to compound shapes and be bent using heat; compatible with polyester, vinyl ester, and epoxy resins.

• Polymethacrylimide (Rohacell)—a closed-cell foam used for lightweight sandwich construction; excellent mechanical properties, high-dimensional stability under heat, good solvent resistance, and outstanding creep compression resistance; more expensive than the other types of foams, but has greater mechanical properties.

#### Balsa Wood

Balsa is a natural wood product with elongated closed cells; it is available in a variety of grades that correlate to the structural, cosmetic, and physical characteristics. The density of balsa is less than one-half of the density of conventional wood products. However, balsa has a considerably higher density than the other types of structural cores.

## 2. 中文翻译

理论 夹层结构是一种结构壁板概念，其最简单的形式由两层相对较薄、平行的面板粘接在相对较厚、轻质芯材的两侧并与之隔开构成。芯材支撑面板防止其屈曲，并承受面外剪切载荷。芯材必须具有高的剪切强度和压缩刚度。复合材料夹层结构最常采用热压罐固化、压机固化或真空袋固化来制造。蒙皮层压板可以预先固化后再粘接到芯材上，也可以在一次操作中与芯材共固化，或采用两种方法的组合。蜂窝结构的例子有：机翼扰流板、整流罩、副翼、襟翼、发动机短舱、地板和方向舵。

### 性能

与铝和复合材料层压结构相比，夹层结构以最小的重量获得很高的弯曲刚度。大多数蜂窝是各向异性的，即性能具有方向性。图7-18说明了采用蜂窝结构的优点。增加芯材厚度会大大增加蜂窝结构的刚度，而重量增加很小。由于蜂窝结构刚度高，因此不必使用桁条和隔框等外部加强件。

### 面板材料

飞机结构中大多数蜂窝结构采用铝、玻璃纤维、凯夫拉®或碳纤维面板。

碳纤维面板不能与铝蜂窝芯材配合使用，因为它会使铝腐蚀。钛和钢用于高温结构的特殊应用。许多部件（如扰流板和飞行操纵面）的面板非常薄——有时只有3或4层铺层。现场报告表明，这些面板的抗冲击性不佳。

### 芯材

#### 蜂窝

每种蜂窝材料都具有特定性能并有各自的优点。飞机蜂窝结构最常用的芯材是芳纶纸（Nomex®或Korex ®）。玻璃纤维用于强度要求更高的应用。

• 牛皮纸——强度相对较低、绝缘性能好、可大量供应且成本低。

• 热塑性塑料——绝缘性能好、能量吸收和/或转向性能好、蜂窝格壁光滑、耐潮湿和耐化学腐蚀、环境相容、外观美观且成本相对较低。

• 铝——比强度和能量吸收最佳、传热性能好、具有电磁屏蔽性能、格壁薄而光滑、可机加工且成本相对较低。

• 钢——传热性能好、具有电磁屏蔽性能且耐热。

• 特种金属（钛）——比强度相对较高、传热性能好、耐化学腐蚀且耐很高温度。

• 芳纶纸——阻燃、防火、绝缘性能好、介电性能低且成型性好。

• 玻璃纤维——可通过铺层调整剪切性能、介电性能低、绝缘性能好且成型性好。

• 碳——尺寸稳定性和保持性良好、高温性能保持性好、刚度高、热膨胀系数极低、导热性可调、剪切模量相对较高，但非常昂贵。

• 陶瓷——耐很高温度、绝缘性能好、可获得很小的格孔尺寸，但非常昂贵。

航空航天用蜂窝芯的格孔通常为六边形。格孔通过把叠置的片材在特定位置粘接而成。叠置的片材被拉伸形成六边形。平行于片材的方向称为带向（ribbon direction）。

对分六边形芯（bisected hexagonal core）是在每个六边形中横切另一片材料。对分六边形蜂窝比六边形芯更刚、更强。过度拉伸芯是通过把片材拉伸到超过形成六边形所需的程度而制成。过度拉伸芯的格孔为矩形。过度拉伸芯在垂直于带向的方向上柔顺，用于具有简单曲面的壁板。钟形芯（bell-shaped core），或称柔性芯（flexicore），具有弯曲的格壁，使其在各个方向上都柔顺。钟形芯用于具有复杂曲面的壁板。

蜂窝芯有不同格孔尺寸可供选择。小尺寸能为夹层面板提供更好的支撑。蜂窝也有不同密度。较高密度的芯材比较低密度的芯材更强、更刚。

#### 泡沫

泡沫芯用于自制飞机和轻型飞机，为翼尖、飞行操纵面、机身段、机翼和翼肋提供强度和形状。泡沫芯在商用型飞机上不常用。泡沫通常比蜂窝重，强度也不如蜂窝。可用作芯材的泡沫有多种，包括：

• 聚苯乙烯（更常称为发泡聚苯乙烯）——航空级发泡聚苯乙烯，具有紧密的闭孔结构，孔间无空隙；抗压强度高、抗水渗透性好；可用热丝切割成翼型形状。

• 酚醛——防火性能很好，可具有很低的密度，但力学性能相对较低 • 聚氨酯——用于制造小型飞机的机身、翼尖和其他曲面零件；价格相对低廉、耐燃油且与大多数胶粘剂相容；不要用热丝切割聚氨酯泡沫；用大刀和砂磨设备易于修整轮廓。

• 聚丙烯——用于制造翼型形状；可用热丝切割；与大多数胶粘剂和环氧树脂相容；不能与聚酯树脂配合使用，会溶于燃油和溶剂。

• 聚氯乙烯（PVC）（Divinycell、Klegecell和Airex）——闭孔中高密度泡沫，抗压强度高、耐久性好、防火性极佳；可真空成型为复合形状，并可用加热弯曲；与聚酯、乙烯基酯和环氧树脂相容。

• 聚甲基丙烯酰亚胺（Rohacell）——用于轻质夹层结构的闭孔泡沫；力学性能优异、受热尺寸稳定性高、耐溶剂性好、抗蠕变压缩性突出；比其他类型泡沫贵，但力学性能更好。

#### 轻木

轻木是一种天然木材产品，具有细长的封闭细胞；它有多种等级，对应于不同的结构、外观和物理特性。轻木的密度不到常规木材产品密度的一半。然而，轻木的密度比其他类型的结构芯材高得多。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① that 定语从句 + 并列过去分词短语
**原句**：A sandwich construction is a structural panel concept that consists in its simplest form of two relatively thin, parallel face sheets bonded to and separated by a relatively thick, lightweight core.
**拆解**：主句为主系表结构，主语 A sandwich construction，表语 a structural panel concept；that 引导定语从句修饰 concept，从句谓语 consists of（被状语 in its simplest form 隔开），宾语 two relatively thin, parallel face sheets；bonded to... and separated by... 为两个过去分词短语并列，作 face sheets 的后置定语。
**要点**：consists 与 of 被 in its simplest form 拆开，容易误读成 consists in；两个过去分词 bonded 与 separated 由一个 and 连接，前者表“粘接到芯材”，后者表“被芯材隔开”，是同一层关系而不是两个动作。

### ② may be + 三个并列过去分词
**原句**：Skin laminates may be precured and subsequently bonded to core, co-cured to core in one operation, or a combination of the two methods.
**拆解**：主语 Skin laminates，谓语 may be 后接三个并列的过去分词（短语）：precured、bonded to core、co-cured to core in one operation；or 引出第三种可能 a combination of the two methods，与前面两个过去分词并列作表语。
**要点**：may be 只出现一次，后两个并列项共用它，是省略结构（co-cured 前省略了 may be）。a combination of the two methods 与前两项不是同一语法形式（名词短语而非分词），但仍是并列表语，翻译时用“或两种方法的组合”衔接。

### ③ 动名词作主语 + while 对比状语从句
**原句**：Increasing the core thickness greatly increases the stiffness of the honeycomb construction, while the weight increase is minimal.
**拆解**：主语为动名词短语 Increasing the core thickness（其中的 the core thickness 是 increasing 的宾语），谓语 increases（第三人称单数），宾语 the stiffness of the honeycomb construction；while 引导对比状语从句，主语 the weight increase，系表结构 is minimal。
**要点**：句首的 Increasing 是动名词而非现在分词，因为它充当主语；两处 increase 词性不同——第一个是动名词（增加），第二个是名词（增加量）。while 在此表对比“而”，不是“当……时”。

### ④ because 原因状语从句 + cause sb. to do
**原句**：Carbon fiber face sheets cannot be used with aluminum honeycomb core material, because it causes the aluminum to corrode.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态，主语 Carbon fiber face sheets，谓语 cannot be used with...；because 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 it（指代前面整件事，即碳纤维面板与铝蜂窝配合使用），谓语 causes，宾语 the aluminum，宾补 to corrode（带 to 的不定式作宾语补足语）。
**要点**：be used with 意为“与……配合使用”，不是“被……使用”；cause sth. to do 表示“使某物发生……”，to corrode 是宾语补足语而非目的状语。it 指代的是“配合使用”这件事，不是 carbon fiber。

### ⑤ Due to 介词短语 + it 形式主语
**原句**：Due to the high stiffness of a honeycomb construction, it is not necessary to use external stiffeners, such as stringers and frames.
**拆解**：Due to the high stiffness of a honeycomb construction 为介词短语作原因状语；主句 it 为形式主语，真正主语是不定式短语 to use external stiffeners；such as stringers and frames 举例说明 stiffeners。
**要点**：due to 是复合介词，后接名词短语，不能接句子（接句子要用 because）；形式主语 it 无实义。such as 引出的是对 stiffeners 的举例，stringers（桁条）与 frames（隔框）都是“外部加强件”的下位概念。
