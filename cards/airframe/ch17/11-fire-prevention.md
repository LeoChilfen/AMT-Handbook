# Fire Prevention

> 机体 Ch 17 · Fire Prevention · 原文 PDF 第 992 页

## 1. 原文

Leaking fuel, hydraulic, deicing, or lubricating fluids can be sources of fire in an aircraft. This condition should be noted, and corrective action taken when inspecting aircraft systems. Minute pressure leaks of these fluids are particularly dangerous for they quickly produce an explosive atmospheric condition. Carefully inspect fuel tank installations for signs of external leaks. With integral fuel tanks, the external evidence may occur at some distance from where the fuel is actually escaping. Many hydraulic fluids are flammable and should not be permitted to accumulate in the structure. Sound-proofing and lagging materials may become highly flammable if soaked with oil of any kind. Any leakage or spillage of flammable fluid in the vicinity of combustion heaters is a serious fire risk, particularly if any vapor is drawn into the heater and passes over the hot combustion chamber.

Oxygen system equipment must be kept absolutely free from traces of oil or grease, since these substances spontaneously ignite when in contact with oxygen under pressure. Oxygen servicing cylinders should be clearly marked so they cannot be mistaken for cylinders containing air or nitrogen, as explosions have resulted from this error during maintenance operations.

## 2. 中文翻译

燃油、液压油、防冰液或润滑油的渗漏都可能成为飞机上的火源。检查飞机各系统时应注意这种情况并采取纠正措施。这些油液的微小压力渗漏尤其危险，因为它们会迅速形成爆炸性的大气条件。应仔细检查燃油箱安装情况，寻找外漏迹象。对整体油箱而言，外漏的证据可能出现在距燃油实际渗出部位有一定距离的地方。许多液压油是可燃的，不应让其积聚在结构内。隔音材料与隔热包覆材料若浸透了任何油液，可能变得高度可燃。燃烧加热器附近任何可燃液体的渗漏或溢出都是严重的火灾风险，尤其是当有蒸气被吸入加热器并掠过炽热燃烧室时。

氧气系统设备必须绝对不残留任何油迹或油脂，因为这些物质在压力氧接触时会自燃。氧气勤务瓶应清楚标示，以免被误认为装空气或氮气的瓶子，因为维修作业中曾因这一错误而发生爆炸。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 并列谓语中的被动省略
**原句**：This condition should be noted, and corrective action taken when inspecting aircraft systems.
**拆解**：主句由 and 并列两个被动结构：This condition should be noted 与 corrective action (should be) taken；when inspecting aircraft systems 为时间状语，when 后省略了主语与 be 动词。
**要点**：第二个分句承前省略了 should be，只留过去分词 taken，这是技术文中避免重复的常见省略，中文仍须译出“应采取纠正措施”。when 后直接接动名词，相当于 when you are inspecting，逻辑主语与主句一致。

### ② for 引导的原因并列分句
**原句**：Minute pressure leaks of these fluids are particularly dangerous for they quickly produce an explosive atmospheric condition.
**拆解**：主语 Minute pressure leaks of these fluids，谓语 are，表语 particularly dangerous；for 引导原因分句，主语 they 回指 leaks，谓语 produce，宾语 an explosive atmospheric condition。
**要点**：for 在此是并列连词，表“因为”，语气比 because 弱，且前面必须有逗号，不能置于句首。minute 在此读 /maɪˈnjuːt/，意为“微小的”，不是“分钟”。

### ③ 介词短语作条件状语 + from where 名词性关系从句
**原句**：With integral fuel tanks, the external evidence may occur at some distance from where the fuel is actually escaping.
**拆解**：With integral fuel tanks 为介词短语作条件状语，意为“对整体油箱而言”；主句 the external evidence may occur，at some distance 为地点状语；from where the fuel is actually escaping 中 where 引导名词性关系从句，作 from 的宾语。
**要点**：from where 相当于 from the place where，不能只用 from that。at some distance from... 表示“距……有一段距离”，说明外漏证据的位置可能与真正渗漏点相距较远，检查时不能只看渗漏处。

### ④ 主句 + particularly if 条件从句 + 并列谓语
**原句**：Any leakage or spillage of flammable fluid in the vicinity of combustion heaters is a serious fire risk, particularly if any vapor is drawn into the heater and passes over the hot combustion chamber.
**拆解**：主语 Any leakage or spillage of flammable fluid，in the vicinity of combustion heaters 为后置定语，谓语 is，表语 a serious fire risk；particularly if 引导条件从句，从句由 and 并列两个谓语 is drawn（被动）与 passes（主动），主语同为 any vapor。
**要点**：leakage or spillage 用 or 连接，谓语仍用单数 is。if 从句中一个主语带两个谓语，一被动一主动，说明蒸气既被吸入又主动掠过燃烧室，翻译时按先后顺序展开。

### ⑤ so (that) 目的从句 + be mistaken for + as 原因从句
**原句**：Oxygen servicing cylinders should be clearly marked so they cannot be mistaken for cylinders containing air or nitrogen, as explosions have resulted from this error during maintenance operations.
**拆解**：主句 Oxygen servicing cylinders should be clearly marked 为被动；so 引导目的/结果从句，谓语 cannot be mistaken for，宾语 cylinders，containing air or nitrogen 为现在分词作后置定语；as 引导原因状语从句，谓语 have resulted from，宾语 this error。
**要点**：be mistaken for 意为“被误认为”，for 不可换成 as（as 在此另起原因从句）。so 后省略了 that，正式文体常见 so that。as 从句用现在完成时 have resulted，说明这一错误过去已多次造成爆炸。
