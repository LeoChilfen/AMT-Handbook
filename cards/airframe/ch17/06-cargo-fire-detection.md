# Cargo Fire Detection

> 机体 Ch 17 · Cargo Fire Detection · 原文 PDF 第 985 页

## 1. 原文

Transport aircraft need to have the following provisions for each cargo or baggage compartment:

1. The detection system must provide a visual indication to the flight crew within 1 minute after the start of a fire.

2. The system must be capable of detecting a fire at a temperature significantly below that at which the structural integrity of the airplane is substantially decreased.

3. There must be means to allow the crew to check, in flight, the functioning of each fire detector circuit.

### Cargo Compartment Classification

#### Class A

A Class A cargo or baggage compartment is one in which the presence of a fire would be easily discovered by a crewmember while at his or her station and each part of the compartment is easily accessible in flight.

#### Class B

A Class B cargo, or baggage compartment, is one in which there is sufficient access in flight to enable a crewmember to effectively reach any part of the compartment with the contents of a hand fire extinguisher. When the access provisions are being used, no hazardous quantity of smoke, flames, or extinguishing agent enters any compartment occupied by the crew or passengers. There is a separate approved smoke detector or fire detector system to give warning at the pilot or flight engineer station.

#### Class C

A Class C cargo, or baggage compartment, is one not meeting the requirements for either a Class A or B compartment but in which:

1. There is a separate approved smoke detector or fire detector system to give warning at the pilot or flight engineer station.

2. There is an approved built-in fire extinguishing or suppression system controllable from the flight deck.

3. There are means to exclude hazardous quantities of smoke, flames, or extinguishing agent from any compartment occupied by the crew or passengers.

4. There are means to control ventilation and drafts within the compartment so that the extinguishing agent used can control any fire that may start within the compartment.

#### Class E

Class E cargo compartment is one on airplanes used only for the carriage of cargo and in which:

1. There is a separate approved smoke or fire detector system to give warning at the pilot or flight engineer station.

2. The controls for shutting off the ventilating airflow to, or within, the compartment are accessible to the flight crew in the crew compartment.

3. There are means to exclude hazardous quantities of smoke, flames, or noxious gases from the flight crew compartment.

4. The required crew emergency exits are accessible under any cargo loading condition.

### Cargo & Baggage Compartment Fire Detection & Extinguisher System

The cargo compartment smoke detection system gives warnings in the flight deck if there is smoke in a cargo compartment. Each compartment is equipped with a smoke detector. The smoke detectors monitor air in the cargo compartments for smoke. The fans bring air from the cargo compartment into the smoke detector. Before the air goes in the smoke detector, in-line water separators remove condensation and heaters increase the air temperature.

#### Smoke Detector System

The optical smoke detector consists of source light emitting diodes (LEDs), intensity monitor photodiodes, and scatter detector photodiodes. Inside the smoke detection chamber, air flows between a source LED and a scatter detector photodiode. Usually, only a small amount of light from the LED gets to the scatter detector. If the air has smoke in it, the smoke particles reflect more light on the scatter detector. This causes an alarm signal. The intensity monitor photodiode makes sure that the source LED is on and keeps the output of the source LED constant. This configuration also finds contamination of the LED and photodiodes. A defective diode, or contamination, causes the detector to change to the other set of diodes. The detector sends a fault message.

The smoke detector has multiple sampling ports. The fans draw air from the sampling ports through a water separator and a heater unit to the smoke detector.

#### Cargo Compartment Extinguishing System

The cargo compartment extinguishing system is activated by the flight crew if the smoke detectors detect smoke in the cargo compartment. Some aircraft are outfitted with two types of fire extinguisher containers. The first system is the dump system that releases the extinguishing agent directly when the cargo fire discharge switch is activated. This action extinguishes the fire.

The second system is the metered system. After a time delay, the metered bottles discharge slowly and at a controlled rate through the filter regulator. Halon from the metered bottles replaces the extinguishing agent leakage. This keeps the correct concentration of extinguishing agent in the cargo compartment to keep the fire extinguished for 180 minutes.

The fire extinguishing bottles contain Halon 1301 or equivalent fire extinguishing agent pressurized with nitrogen. Tubing connects the bottles to discharge nozzles in the cargo compartment ceilings.

The extinguishing bottles are outfitted with squibs. The squib is an electrically operated explosive device. It is adjacent to a bottle diaphragm that can break. The diaphragm normally seals the pressurized bottle. When the cargo discharge switch is activated, the squib fires and the explosion breaks the diaphragm. Nitrogen pressure inside the bottle pushes the Halon through the discharge port into the cargo compartment. When the bottle discharges, a pressure switch is activated that sends an indication to the flight deck that a bottle has been discharged. Flow control valves are incorporated if the bottles can be discharged in multiple compartments. The flow control valves direct the extinguishing agent to the selected cargo compartment.

The following indications occur in the flight deck if there is smoke in a cargo compartment: • Master warning lights come on. • Fire warning aural operates. • A cargo fire warning message shows. • Cargo fire warning light comes on.

The master warning lights and fire warning aural are prevented from operating during part of the takeoff operation.

## 2. 中文翻译

运输类飞机的每个货舱或行李舱都须具备下列条件：

1. 火警开始后 1 分钟内，探测系统须向机组给出目视指示。

2. 系统须能在明显低于飞机结构完整性大幅下降所对应温度的温度下探测到火警。

3. 须有手段使机组在空中检查每个火警探测器电路的功能。

### 货舱分类

#### A 类

A 类货舱或行李舱是指这样一种舱：机组在其工作位置即可轻易发现火情，且舱内各部分在空中都易于接近。

#### B 类

B 类货舱或行李舱是指在飞行中具有足够的通路，使机组能用便携式灭火器的内装物有效到达舱内任何部位。使用该通路时，不得有危险量的烟、火焰或灭火剂进入机组或乘客所在的任何舱室。另设有经批准的独立烟雾探测器或火警探测系统，在驾驶员或随机工程师工作位置给出警告。

#### C 类

C 类货舱或行李舱是指不满足 A 类或 B 类要求、但具备下列条件的货舱：

1. 有经批准的独立烟雾探测器或火警探测系统，在驾驶员或随机工程师工作位置给出警告。

2. 有可从驾驶舱控制的经批准的固定式灭火或抑爆系统。

3. 有手段阻止危险量的烟、火焰或灭火剂进入机组或乘客所在的任何舱室。

4. 有手段控制舱内通风与气流，使所用灭火剂能控制舱内可能发生的任何火情。

#### E 类

E 类货舱是仅用于载运货物的飞机上的货舱，且其中：

1. 有经批准的独立烟雾或火警探测系统，在驾驶员或随机工程师工作位置给出警告。

2. 切断通向该舱内或位于该舱内的通风气流的控制装置，机组在机组舱内即可接近。

3. 有手段阻止危险量的烟、火焰或有害气体进入驾驶舱。

4. 在任一载货状态下，规定的机组应急出口均可接近。

### 货舱与行李舱火警探测及灭火系统

货舱烟雾探测系统在货舱内有烟时向驾驶舱发出警告。每个货舱都装有一个烟雾探测器。烟雾探测器监测货舱内的空气以探测烟雾。风扇把货舱的空气送入烟雾探测器。在空气进入烟雾探测器之前，串联式水分离器除去冷凝水，加热器提高空气温度。

#### 烟雾探测器系统

光学烟雾探测器由光源发光二极管（LED）、强度监测光电二极管和散射探测光电二极管组成。在烟检测室内，空气在光源 LED 与散射探测光电二极管之间流过。通常只有少量 LED 发出的光到达散射探测器。若空气中含烟，烟粒会使更多光反射到散射探测器上，从而产生报警信号。强度监测光电二极管确认光源 LED 处于点亮状态，并使 LED 的输出保持恒定。这种配置还能发现 LED 与光电二极管的污染。二极管损坏或受污染会使探测器切换到另一组二极管，并由探测器发出故障信息。

烟雾探测器设有多个采样口。风扇把空气从采样口经水分离器和加热组件抽到烟雾探测器。

#### 货舱灭火系统

货舱灭火系统由机组在烟雾探测器探测到货舱有烟时启动。有些飞机装有两类灭火瓶。第一种是直接喷放系统，在货舱灭火喷放电门被启动时直接释放灭火剂，该动作即把火扑灭。

第二种是计量式系统。经过一段延时后，计量瓶通过过滤调节器缓慢地、以受控的速率喷放。计量瓶中的哈龙补充灭火剂的泄漏量，使货舱内灭火剂保持正确浓度，从而在 180 分钟内保持火情熄灭。

灭火瓶内装 Halon 1301 或等效灭火剂，用氮气加压。管路把灭火瓶连接到货舱顶部的喷放喷嘴。

灭火瓶装有起爆管。起爆管是一种由电操作的爆炸装置，紧邻一个可破裂的瓶膜片，该膜片平时密封着加压的瓶体。当货舱喷放电门被启动时，起爆管引爆，爆炸击破膜片。瓶内的氮气压力把哈龙经喷放口压入货舱。瓶子喷放时，一个压力电门被启动，向驾驶舱发出某瓶已喷放的指示。若灭火瓶可向多个货舱喷放，则装有流量控制活门，由这些活门把灭火剂导向所选货舱。

若货舱内有烟，驾驶舱会出现下列指示：• 主警告灯亮。• 火警警告音响工作。• 显示货舱火警警告信息。• 货舱火警警告灯亮。

主警告灯和火警警告音响在起飞过程的某一段被抑制，不工作。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① in which 定语从句 + 并列分句 + while 省略结构
**原句**：A Class A cargo or baggage compartment is one in which the presence of a fire would be easily discovered by a crewmember while at his or her station and each part of the compartment is easily accessible in flight.
**拆解**：主句 A Class A cargo or baggage compartment is one，one 后接 in which 引导的定语从句；从句内由 and 并列两个分句：the presence of a fire would be easily discovered by a crewmember while at his or her station 与 each part of the compartment is easily accessible in flight；while at his or her station 是 while he or she is at his or her station 的省略。
**要点**：in which 相当于 in this compartment，从句的两个并列分句共用这一介词短语，不要误以为 and 后面另起一个主句。while + 介词短语是常见的省略形式，省略了与前面相同的主语和 be 动词。

### ② be capable of doing + that 替代名词 + at which
**原句**：The system must be capable of detecting a fire at a temperature significantly below that at which the structural integrity of the airplane is substantially decreased.
**拆解**：主语 The system，谓语 must be capable of，后接动名词 detecting a fire；at a temperature 为条件状语；significantly below that 中 that 是替代词，代替前面的 a temperature；at which 引导定语从句修饰 that。
**要点**：be capable of 后只能用动名词，不能接不定式。that 在此不是连词而是代词，指代前文的名词，避免重复；at which 从句还原为 the structural integrity is substantially decreased at that temperature。

### ③ in which there is + enable sb. to do
**原句**：A Class B cargo, or baggage compartment, is one in which there is sufficient access in flight to enable a crewmember to effectively reach any part of the compartment with the contents of a hand fire extinguisher.
**拆解**：A Class B cargo, or baggage compartment 中逗号间的 or baggage compartment 是同位补充；is one 后接 in which 定语从句；从句为 there be 句型 there is sufficient access in flight；to enable... 为不定式，说明通路足够到什么程度。
**要点**：in which there is 是 there be 句型的定语从句形式，中文常译成“该舱在飞行中具有……”。enable sb. to do sth. 中的 to 不可省，effectively 修饰 reach，with the contents of a hand fire extinguisher 表示凭借便携灭火器的内装物。

### ④ so that 目的状语从句 + 过去分词后置定语
**原句**：There are means to control ventilation and drafts within the compartment so that the extinguishing agent used can control any fire that may start within the compartment.
**拆解**：主句为 There are means，to control ventilation and drafts within the compartment 作后置定语说明 means；so that 引导目的状语从句，从句主语 the extinguishing agent，过去分词 used 后置修饰它，谓语 can control，宾语 any fire，that may start within the compartment 为定语从句修饰 fire。
**要点**：means 单复数同形，此处There are 说明是复数。so that 从句的谓语带情态动词 can，这是目的状语从句的典型标志。used 是“所使用的”，不是谓语，句子真正的谓语是 can control。

### ⑤ 被动语态 + 隔开的 that 定语从句 + that 同位语从句
**原句**：When the bottle discharges, a pressure switch is activated that sends an indication to the flight deck that a bottle has been discharged.
**拆解**：When the bottle discharges 为时间状语从句；主句 a pressure switch is activated 为被动语态；第一个 that 引导定语从句修饰 pressure switch，从句谓语 sends，宾语 an indication；第二个 that 引导同位语从句，说明 indication 的具体内容。
**要点**：一个句子里两个 that 分工不同：第一个在从句中作主语（that sends），是定语从句；第二个从句结构完整（a bottle has been discharged），只起解释作用，是同位语从句。被动谓语 is activated 把定语从句与先行词隔开，读时须回指 pressure switch。
