# Extinguishing Agents & Portable Fire Extinguishers

> 机体 Ch 17 · Extinguishing Agents & Portable Fire Extinguishers · 原文 PDF 第 981 页

## 1. 原文

There must be at least one hand held, portable fire extinguisher for use in the pilot compartment that is located within easy access of the pilot while seated. There must be at least one hand held fire extinguisher located conveniently in the passenger compartment of each airplane accommodating more than 6 and less than 30 passengers. Each extinguisher for use in a personnel compartment must be designed to minimize the hazard of toxic gas concentrations. The number of portable, hand held fire extinguishers for transport aircraft is shown in Figure 17-10.

### Halogenated Hydrocarbons

For over 45 years, halogenated hydrocarbons (Halons) have been practically the only fire extinguishing agents used in civil transport aircraft. However, Halon is an ozone depleting and global warming chemical, and its production has been banned by international agreement. Although Halon usage has been banned in some parts of the world, aviation has been granted an exemption because of its unique operational and fire safety requirements. Halon has been the fire extinguishing agent of choice in civil aviation because it is extremely effective on a per unit weight basis over a wide range of aircraft environmental conditions. It is a clean agent (no residue), electrically nonconducting, and has relatively low toxicity.

Two types of Halons are employed in aviation: Halon 1301(CBrF 3) a total flooding agent, and Halon 1211 (CBrClF2) a streaming agent. Class A, B, or C fires are appropriately controlled with Halons. However, do not use Halons on a class D fire. Halon agents may react vigorously with the burning metal.

Note: While Halons are still in service and are appropriate agents for these classes of fires, the production of these ozone depleting agents has been restricted. Although not required, consider replacing Halon extinguishers with Halon replacement extinguishers when discharged. Halon replacement agents found to be compliant to date include the halocarbons HCFC Blend B, HFC-227ea, and HFC-236fa.

### Inert Cold Gases

Carbon dioxide (CO2) is an effective extinguishing agent. It is most often used in fire extinguishers that are available on the ramp to fight fires on the exterior of the aircraft, such as engine or APU fires. CO2 has been used for many years to extinguish flammable fluid fires and fires involving electrical equipment. It is noncombustible and does not react with most substances. It provides its own pressure for discharge from the storage vessel, except in extremely cold climates where a booster charge of nitrogen may be added to winterize the system. Normally, CO2 is a gas, but it is easily liquefied by compression and cooling. After liquification, CO2 remains in a closed container as both liquid and gas. When CO2 is then discharged to the atmosphere, most of the liquid expands to gas. Heat absorbed by the gas during vaporization cools the remaining liquid to –110 °F, and it becomes a finely divided white solid, dry ice snow.

Carbon dioxide is about 1½ times as heavy as air, which gives it the ability to replace air above burning surfaces and maintain a smothering atmosphere. CO 2 is effective as an extinguishing agent primarily because it dilutes the air and reduces the oxygen content so that combustion is no longer supported. Under certain conditions, some cooling effect is also realized. CO2 is considered only mildly toxic, but it can cause unconsciousness and death by suffocation if the victim is allowed to breathe CO2 in fire extinguishing concentrations for 20 to 30 minutes. CO2 is not effective as an extinguishing agent on fires involving chemicals containing their own oxygen supply, such as cellulose nitrate (used in some aircraft paints). Also, fires involving magnesium and titanium cannot be extinguished by CO2.

### Dry Powders

Class A, B, or C fires can be controlled by dry chemical extinguishing agents. The only all purpose (Class A, B, C rating) dry chemical powder extinguishers contain mono-ammonium phosphate. All other dry chemical powders have a Class B, C U.S – UL fire rating only. Dry powder chemical extinguishers best control class A, B, and C fire but their use is limited due to residual residue and clean up after deployment.

### Water

Class A type fires are best controlled with water by cooling the material below its ignition temperature and soaking the material to prevent re-ignition.

### Flight Deck & Cabin Interiors

All materials used in the flight deck and cabin must conform to strict standards to prevent fire. In case of a fire, several types of portable fire extinguishers are available to fight the fire. The most common types are Halon 1211 and water.

#### Extinguisher Types

Portable fire extinguishers are used to extinguish fires in the cabin or flight deck. Figure 17-11 shows a Halon fire extinguisher used in a general aviation aircraft. The Halon extinguishers are used on electrical and flammable liquid fires. Some transport aircraft also use water fire extinguisher for use on non-electrical fires.

The following is a list of extinguishing agents and the type (class) fires for which each is appropriate.

1. Water—class A. Water cools the material below its ignition temperature and soaks it to prevent reignition.

2. Carbon dioxide—class B or C. CO 2 acts as a blanketing agent. Note: CO2 is not recommended for hand-held extinguishers for internal aircraft use.

3. Dry chemicals—class A, B, or C. Dry chemicals are the best control agents for these types of fires.

4. Halons—only class A, B, or C.

5. Halocarbon clean agents—only class A, B, or C.

6. Specialized dry powder—class D. (Follow the recommendations of the extinguisher’s manufacturer because of the possible chemical reaction between the burning metal and the extinguishing agent.)

The following hand-held extinguishers are unsuitable as cabin or flight deck equipment.

• Dry chemicals (due to the potential for corrosion damage to electronic equipment, the possibility of visual obscuration if the agent were discharged into the flight deck area, and the cleanup problems from their use).

• Specialized dry powder (it is suitable for use in ground operations).

## 2. 中文翻译

驾驶舱内必须至少配一具供飞行员使用的手提式灭火器，位置要在飞行员就座时伸手可及。每架载客 6 人以上、不足 30 人的飞机，其客舱内必须至少便利地配一具手提式灭火器。用于载人舱的每具灭火器，其设计必须能把有毒气体聚集的危害降到最低。运输类飞机的便携式手提灭火器数量见图 17-10。

### 卤代烃

45 年多以来，卤代烃（哈龙）实际上一直是民用运输机唯一使用的灭火剂。然而哈龙是一种消耗臭氧并导致全球变暖的化学品，其生产已被国际协议禁止。尽管世界部分地区已禁用哈龙，但航空业因其独特的运行与消防安全要求而获得豁免。哈龙之所以成为民航的首选灭火剂，是因为按单位重量计，它在飞机各种环境条件下都极为有效。它是一种洁净灭火剂（无残留），不导电，毒性也相对较低。

航空上使用两种哈龙：Halon 1301（CBrF 3），一种全淹没式灭火剂；以及 Halon 1211（CBrClF2），一种喷射式灭火剂。A、B、C 类火用哈龙都能适当控制。但不得在 D 类火上使用哈龙，哈龙灭火剂可能与燃烧的金属发生剧烈反应。

注：哈龙仍在服役，且是上述各类火的适用灭火剂，但这类消耗臭氧的灭火剂的生产已受到限制。虽非强制要求，但在灭火器喷放后，可考虑用哈龙替代型灭火器予以更换。迄今已确认符合要求的哈龙替代灭火剂包括卤代碳 HCFC Blend B、HFC-227ea 和 HFC-236fa。

### 惰性低温气体

二氧化碳（CO2）是一种有效的灭火剂。它最常用于机坪上扑救飞机外部火灾（如发动机或 APU 火）的灭火器中。多年来 CO2 一直用于扑灭易燃液体火灾和涉及电气设备的火灾。它不燃，且不与大多数物质反应。除在极寒冷气候下可加充氮气使系统适应冬季使用外，它靠自身压力从储罐中喷出。正常情况下 CO2 是气体，但通过压缩和冷却很容易液化。液化后，CO2 在密闭容器中以液体和气体两种形态存在。当 CO2 随后喷放到大气中时，大部分液体膨胀成气体。气化时气体吸收的热量把剩余液体冷却到 –110 °F，使其变成细微分散的白色固体，即干冰雪。

二氧化碳的重量约为空气的 1½ 倍，这使它能够置换燃烧表面之上的空气，维持一种窒息性气氛。CO2 之所以是有效的灭火剂，主要是因为它稀释空气、降低含氧量，使燃烧无法继续。在一定条件下还能产生一些冷却作用。CO2 只被认为有轻度毒性，但如果让受害者吸入灭火浓度的 CO2 达 20 至 30 分钟，就可能致其昏迷乃至窒息死亡。对于含自身供氧的化学品（如某些飞机漆中使用的硝酸纤维素）火灾，CO2 不是有效的灭火剂。此外，镁和钛的火灾也不能用 CO2 扑灭。

### 干粉

干粉化学灭火剂可以控制 A、B、C 类火。唯一具有全能（A、B、C 类额定）评级的干粉灭火器装的是磷酸二氢铵。其余所有干粉灭火剂的额定等级仅为美国 UL 的 B、C 类。干粉化学灭火器对 A、B、C 类火的控制效果最好，但由于喷放后残留物多、清理麻烦，其使用受到限制。

### 水

A 类火最好用水控制，办法是把材料冷却到其着火温度以下，并浸透材料以防复燃。

### 驾驶舱与客舱内饰

驾驶舱和客舱内使用的所有材料都必须符合严格标准，以防火灾。一旦发生火灾，有几种便携式灭火器可用来灭火，最常见的是 Halon 1211 和水。

#### 灭火器类型

便携式灭火器用于扑灭客舱或驾驶舱内的火灾。图 17-11 示出一具用于通用航空飞机的哈龙灭火器。哈龙灭火器用于电气火灾和易燃液体火灾。有些运输类飞机还使用水灭火器，用于非电气火灾。

下面列出各种灭火剂及其各自适用的火灾类型（类别）。

1. 水——A 类。水把材料冷却到着火温度以下，并浸透材料以防复燃。

2. 二氧化碳——B 类或 C 类。CO2 起覆盖窒息作用。注：CO2 不推荐用于飞机内部使用的手提式灭火器。

3. 干粉化学剂——A、B 或 C 类。干粉化学剂是这几类火的最佳控制剂。

4. 哈龙——仅限 A、B 或 C 类。

5. 卤代碳洁净灭火剂——仅限 A、B 或 C 类。

6. 专用干粉——D 类。（应遵循灭火器制造商的建议，因为燃烧的金属与灭火剂之间可能发生化学反应。）

下列手提式灭火器不适合作为客舱或驾驶舱设备。

• 干粉化学剂（因为可能腐蚀损坏电子设备，若在驾驶舱区域喷放可能造成视线模糊，以及使用后的清理问题）。

• 专用干粉（它适用于地面作业）。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① there be 句型 + 定语从句 + while 省略结构
**原句**：There must be at least one hand held, portable fire extinguisher for use in the pilot compartment that is located within easy access of the pilot while seated.
**拆解**：There must be 为存在句；主语 one hand held, portable fire extinguisher 带两个前置定语；for use in the pilot compartment 说明用途；that 引导定语从句修饰 extinguisher；within easy access of the pilot 为表语；while seated 是 while he is seated 的省略形式，作时间状语。
**要点**：that 从句修饰的是 extinguisher 而不是 compartment——从句谓语 is located 的单复数与 extinguisher 一致。while seated 省略了主语和 be，因为从句主语与主句一致；译成“就座时”即可。

### ② 让步从句 + 被动语态 + because of 短语
**原句**：Although Halon usage has been banned in some parts of the world, aviation has been granted an exemption because of its unique operational and fire safety requirements.
**拆解**：Although 引导让步状语从句，从句为现在完成时的被动语态 has been banned；主句 aviation has been granted an exemption 为被动语态，aviation 是授予的接受者；because of 引出名词性短语说明原因。
**要点**：grant sb. sth. 变被动后成 sb. is granted sth.，句中未出现 by 短语，因为施动者不重要。because of 后只能接名词或名词短语，若接句子须改用 because——这是本句最容易写错的地方。

### ③ except + where 定语从句
**原句**：It provides its own pressure for discharge from the storage vessel, except in extremely cold climates where a booster charge of nitrogen may be added to winterize the system.
**拆解**：主句 It provides its own pressure，for discharge from the storage vessel 说明压力的用途；except 引出例外状语 in extremely cold climates；where 引导定语从句修饰 climates，从句为被动语态 may be added，to winterize the system 为目的状语。
**要点**：except 是介词，后面接名词性成分，不能直接跟从句，所以要用 where 另起定语从句。winterize 意为“做冬季适用性改装”，这里指加充氮气保证低温下仍能喷放。

### ④ 过去分词后置定语 + 同位语
**原句**：Heat absorbed by the gas during vaporization cools the remaining liquid to –110 °F, and it becomes a finely divided white solid, dry ice snow.
**拆解**：主语 Heat，过去分词短语 absorbed by the gas during vaporization 作后置定语；谓语 cools，宾语 the remaining liquid，to –110 °F 表结果；and 引出并列分句 it becomes a finely divided white solid，逗号后的 dry ice snow 是 solid 的同位语。
**要点**：absorbed 的逻辑主语是 heat（热被吸收），用过去分词；若用 absorbing 就变成“热吸收气体”，语义相反。同位语 dry ice snow 用逗号引出，中文常译成“即干冰雪”。

### ⑤ but 连接的并列句 + 被动
**原句**：Dry powder chemical extinguishers best control class A, B, and C fire but their use is limited due to residual residue and clean up after deployment.
**拆解**：前一分句主语 Dry powder chemical extinguishers，谓语 control，宾语 class A, B, and C fire，best 为状语前置；but 转折后主语 their use，谓语 is limited 为被动语态，due to 引出两个并列的名词原因：residual residue 与 clean up after deployment。
**要点**：due to 在此相当于 because of，后接名词性成分。their 回指 extinguishers，译成“其使用”比“它们的使用”更符合技术文体；clean up 是名词化的短语，与 residual residue 并列作 due to 的宾语。
