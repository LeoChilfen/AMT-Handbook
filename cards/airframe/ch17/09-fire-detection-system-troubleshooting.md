# Fire Detection System Troubleshooting

> 机体 Ch 17 · Fire Detection System Troubleshooting · 原文 PDF 第 990 页

## 1. 原文

The following troubleshooting procedures represent the most common difficulties encountered in engine fire detection systems:

1. Intermittent alarms are most often caused by an intermittent short in the detector system wiring. Such shorts may be caused by a loose wire that occasionally touches a nearby terminal, a frayed wire brushing against a structure, or a sensing element rubbing against a structural member long enough to wear through the insulation. Intermittent faults often can be located by moving wires to recreate the short.

2. Fire alarms and warning lights can occur when no engine fire or overheat condition exists. Such false alarms can be most easily located by disconnecting the engine sensing loop connections from the control unit. If the false alarm ceases when the engine sensing loop is disconnected, the fault is in the disconnected sensing loop, which should be examined for areas that have been bent into contact with hot parts of the engine. If no bent element can be found, the shorted section can be located by isolating the connecting elements consecutively around the entire loop.

3. Kinks and sharp bends in the sensing element can cause an internal wire to short intermittently to the outer tubing. The fault can be located by checking the sensing element with an ohm meter while tapping the element in the suspected areas to produce the short.

4. Moisture in the detection system seldom causes a false fire alarm. If, however, moisture does cause an alarm, the warning persists until the contamination is removed, or boils away, and the resistance of the loop returns to its normal value.

5. Failure to obtain an alarm signal when the test switch is actuated may be caused by a defective test switch or control unit, the lack of electrical power, inoperative indicator light, or an opening in the sensing element or connecting wiring. When the test switch fails to provide an alarm, the continuity of a two-wire sensing loop can be determined by opening the loop and measuring the resistance. In a single-wire, continuous-loop system, the center conductor should be grounded.

## 2. 中文翻译

下列排故程序对应发动机火警探测系统中最常遇到的故障：

1. 间歇性报警最常见的原因是探测器系统线路中的间歇性短路。这类短路可能由下列原因造成：一根松脱的导线偶尔碰到附近的接线端；一根磨损的导线擦碰结构件；或者敏感元件与结构件摩擦的时间之久足以磨穿绝缘层。间歇性故障往往可以通过拨动导线、再现短路来定位。

2. 在没有发动机火警或过热状态时，也可能出现火警报警和警告灯亮。这类虚假报警最容易通过把发动机敏感环的连接从控制组件上脱开来定位。若脱开发动机敏感环后虚假报警停止，则故障在脱开的敏感环内，应检查该环上是否有被弯曲到与发动机热部件接触的部位。若找不到被弯曲的元件，可沿整个环路依次隔离各连接元件来定位短路区段。

3. 敏感元件上的扭结与急弯会使内部导线对外部套管间歇性短路。可用欧姆表检查敏感元件，同时敲击怀疑的部位使短路出现，从而定位故障。

4. 探测系统中的潮气很少引起虚假火警。但若潮气确实引起了报警，则警告会一直持续，直到污染物被清除或蒸发掉、环路电阻恢复正常值为止。

5. 扳动试验电门时得不到报警信号，可能是由于试验电门或控制组件有故障、缺少电源、指示灯泡不工作，或敏感元件或连接线路中存在断口。当试验电门未能给出报警时，可断开环路并测量电阻来确定双线敏感环的连通性。在单线连续环系统中，中心导线应接地。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动谓语 + that 从句 + 三项并列宾语 + enough to
**原句**：Such shorts may be caused by a loose wire that occasionally touches a nearby terminal, a frayed wire brushing against a structure, or a sensing element rubbing against a structural member long enough to wear through the insulation.
**拆解**：主句 Such shorts may be caused by... 为被动语态，by 后接三个并列成分：a loose wire（带 that 定语从句）、a frayed wire brushing against a structure（现在分词作后置定语）、a sensing element rubbing against a structural member long enough to wear through the insulation（现在分词 + enough to 结果）。
**要点**：三个并列成分中只有第一个带 that 从句，后两个改用分词短语，这是英文避免重复从句的常见写法。long enough to do 表示“久到足以……”，enough 须后置修饰形容词或副词。

### ② 双重条件从句 + 非限定定语从句 + that 定语从句
**原句**：If the false alarm ceases when the engine sensing loop is disconnected, the fault is in the disconnected sensing loop, which should be examined for areas that have been bent into contact with hot parts of the engine.
**拆解**：If 引导条件从句，其中又套一个 when 时间从句；主句 the fault is in the disconnected sensing loop；which 引导非限定定语从句补充说明该环，从句谓语 should be examined，for areas 说明检查对象，that have been bent into contact with hot parts of the engine 为定语从句修饰 areas。
**要点**：which 前有逗号，属非限定定语从句，只作补充信息，不限定先行词的范围。be bent into contact with 意为“被弯到与……接触”，into 表结果状态。

### ③ cause sb./sth. to do 结构
**原句**：Kinks and sharp bends in the sensing element can cause an internal wire to short intermittently to the outer tubing.
**拆解**：主语 Kinks and sharp bends，in the sensing element 为后置定语；谓语 can cause，宾语 an internal wire，to short 为宾语补足语；intermittently 为副词修饰 short，to the outer tubing 说明短路的对象。
**要点**：cause A to do B 中 A 是受影响的对象、B 是产生的结果，B 用不定式而非分词。short 在此为动词“短路”，与作名词的 short 同形，须按 to 后位置判断。

### ④ however 插入 + until 引导两个并列分句 + does 强调
**原句**：If, however, moisture does cause an alarm, the warning persists until the contamination is removed, or boils away, and the resistance of the loop returns to its normal value.
**拆解**：If, however, moisture does cause an alarm 为条件从句，however 插在 If 与主语之间表转折；does 为助动词强调谓语 cause；主句 the warning persists；until 引导时间状语从句，从句内由 and 并列 the contamination is removed, or boils away 与 the resistance of the loop returns to its normal value。
**要点**：does 在此不是助动词构成疑问，而是强调“确实引起了”，译作“确实”。until 从句的谓语用一般现在时表将来，且两个并列分句须同时满足，警告才会消失。

### ⑤ when 从句 + 被动语态 + by doing 方式状语
**原句**：When the test switch fails to provide an alarm, the continuity of a two-wire sensing loop can be determined by opening the loop and measuring the resistance.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，主语 the test switch，谓语 fails，to provide an alarm 为不定式作 fails 的补足语；主句 the continuity of a two-wire sensing loop 为主语，can be determined 为被动谓语，by opening the loop and measuring the resistance 为方式状语，两个动名词由 and 并列。
**要点**：fail to do 表示“未能做到”，不是“失败”。by 后接动名词表示手段，两个动名词共用同一个 by，不可只给第一个加 by。determine 在此意为“测定、判定”，不是“决定”。
