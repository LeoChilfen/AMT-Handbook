# Introduction

> 机体 Ch 10 · Introduction · 原文 PDF 第 520 页

## 1. 原文

Since the beginning of manned flight, it has been recognized that supplying the pilot with information about the aircraft and its operation could be useful and lead to safer flight. The Wright Brothers had very few instruments on their Wright Flyer, but they did have an engine tachometer, an anemometer (wind meter), and a stop watch. They were obviously concerned about the aircraft’s engine and the progress of their flight. From that simple beginning, a wide variety of instruments have been developed to inform flight crews of different parameters. Instrument systems now exist to provide information on the condition of the aircraft, engine, components, the aircraft’s attitude in the sky, weather, cabin environment, navigation, and communication. Figure 10-1 shows various instrument panels from the Wright Flyer to a modern jet airliner.

The ability to capture and convey all of the information a pilot may want, in an accurate, easily understood manner, has been a challenge throughout the history of aviation. As the range of desired information has grown, so too have the size and complexity of modern aircraft, thus expanding even further the need to inform the flight crew without sensory overload or overcluttering the flight deck. As a result, the old flat panel in the front of the flight deck with various individual instruments attached to it has evolved into a sophisticated computer-controlled digital interface with flat-panel display screens and prioritized messaging. A visual comparison between a conventional flight deck and a glass flight deck is shown in Figure 10-2.

There are usually two parts to any instrument or instrument system. One part senses the situation and the other part displays it. In analog instruments, both of these functions often take place in a single unit or instrument (case). These are called direct-sensing instruments. Remote-sensing requires the information to be sensed, or captured, and then sent to a separate display unit in the flight deck. Both analog and digital instruments make use of this method.

The relaying of important bits of information can be done in various ways. Electricity is often used by way of wires that carry sensor information into the flight deck. Sometimes pneumatic lines are used. In complex, modern aircraft, this can lead to an enormous amount of tubing and wiring terminating behind the instrument display panel. More efficient information transfer has been accomplished via the use of digital data buses. Essentially, these are wires that share message carrying for many instruments by digitally encoding the signal for each. This reduces the number of wires and weight required to transfer remotely sensed information for the pilot’s use. Flat-panel computer display screens that can be controlled to show only the information desired are also lighter in weight than the numerous individual gauges it would take to display the same information simultaneously. An added bonus is the increased reliability inherent in these solid-state systems.

It is the job of the aircraft technician to understand and maintain all aircraft, including these various instrument systems. Accordingly, in this chapter, discussions begin with analog instruments and refer to modern digital instrumentation when appropriate.

## 2. 中文翻译

自载人飞行之始，人们就已认识到，向飞行员提供有关飞机及其运行状态的信息是有用的，并能带来更安全的飞行。莱特兄弟的“莱特飞行者”上仪表极少，但确实装有一台发动机转速表、一个风速表（测风仪）和一只秒表。显然，他们关心的是飞机发动机的状况和飞行的进展。从这一简陋的开端起步，人们研制出了种类繁多的仪表，向飞行机组通报各种参数。如今已有各种仪表系统，可提供飞机、发动机及部件状况、飞机在空中的姿态、天气、客舱环境、导航和通信等方面的信息。图10-1展示了从“莱特飞行者”到现代喷气客机的各种仪表板。

能够把飞行员可能需要的全部信息以准确、易懂的方式获取并传达出来，这在航空史上一直是个挑战。随着所需信息的范围不断扩大，现代飞机的尺寸与复杂程度也同步增长，从而进一步扩大了这种需求：既要让飞行机组获得信息，又不能造成感官超载或使驾驶舱过于拥挤。其结果是，驾驶舱前方那块挂着各种独立仪表的旧式平板，已演变为由计算机控制、带平板显示屏和分级消息显示的精密数字接口。常规驾驶舱与玻璃驾驶舱的视觉对比见图10-2。

任何仪表或仪表系统通常都包含两个部分：一部分感受状态，另一部分将其显示出来。在模拟仪表中，这两种功能往往由同一个单元或仪表（表壳）完成，这类仪表称为直读式仪表。远距离传感则要求先把信息感测（或采集）下来，再送到驾驶舱内独立的显示单元，模拟仪表和数字仪表都采用这种方法。

重要信息的传送有多种方式。常用的是电，通过导线把传感器信息送进驾驶舱；有时也采用气动管路。在复杂的现代飞机上，这会导致仪表显示板背后汇集大量管路和导线。采用数字数据总线则实现了更高效的信息传递。从根本上说，这些总线就是一些导线，它们把每路信号数字化编码，让许多仪表共用同一套消息传输通道。这就减少了为把远距感测信息提供给飞行员使用所需的导线数量和重量。平板计算机显示屏可以受控只显示所需的信息，其重量也比同时显示同样信息所需的大量独立表头轻。一个额外的好处是，这类固态系统本身具有更高的可靠性。

航空维修技术人员的职责是理解并维护所有飞机，包括这些形形色色的仪表系统。因此，本章的讨论从模拟仪表开始，并在适当之处涉及现代数字仪表。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① it 作形式主语 + that 主语从句
**原句**：Since the beginning of manned flight, it has been recognized that supplying the pilot with information about the aircraft and its operation could be useful and lead to safer flight.
**拆解**：Since 引导时间状语；it 为形式主语，真正的主语是 that 引导的主语从句；从句主语为动名词短语 supplying the pilot with information about the aircraft and its operation，谓语是并列的 could be useful and lead to safer flight；has been recognized 为现在完成时的被动语态。
**要点**：that 从句放到句首会显得头重脚轻，所以用 it 占位，把长主语后移。supplying... 是动名词短语作从句主语，单个动名词短语作主语时谓语用单数，这里与 could 的搭配不受影响。

### ② but 并列转折 + do 表示强调
**原句**：The Wright Brothers had very few instruments on their Wright Flyer, but they did have an engine tachometer, an anemometer (wind meter), and a stop watch.
**拆解**：but 连接两个并列分句。前分句为一般过去时的肯定句；后分句中 did 是助动词，与动词原形 have 构成强调结构，意为“确实装有”；have 的宾语是三个并列名词。
**要点**：“few” 表示“几乎没有”，带否定意味，所以后面用 but 转折。did + 动词原形的强调用法只用于肯定句，且动词要还原为原形，不能再加 -ed。

### ③ As 状语从句 + so too 引起的倒装 + thus doing 结果状语
**原句**：As the range of desired information has grown, so too have the size and complexity of modern aircraft, thus expanding even further the need to inform the flight crew without sensory overload or overcluttering the flight deck.
**拆解**：As 引导伴随/原因状语从句；主句为 so too + 助动词 have + 主语 the size and complexity of modern aircraft 的部分倒装，表示“现代飞机的尺寸与复杂程度也同样增长”；thus + 现在分词 expanding 作结果状语，其宾语 the need... 因过长而后置，正常语序为 expanding the need... even further。
**要点**：so too 置于句首时主句必须倒装，把助动词提到主语前。expanding 的逻辑主语是前面整件事，不是 aircraft；宾语后置是为了避免“动词 + 长宾语 + 短状语”造成的头重脚轻。

### ④ require 的复合宾语（不定式被动式）
**原句**：Remote-sensing requires the information to be sensed, or captured, and then sent to a separate display unit in the flight deck.
**拆解**：主语 Remote-sensing，谓语 requires，宾语 the information，宾补为不定式被动式 to be sensed, or captured, and then sent...；or captured 与 sent 都是与 be 并列的过去分词，共用 to be。
**要点**：require sth. to be done 是“要求某事被做”的固定搭配；信息是“被感测、被发送”的，所以不定式用被动式。and then sent 前面省略了 to be，不能理解为主动的 sent。

### ⑤ that 定语从句 + by doing 方式状语
**原句**：Essentially, these are wires that share message carrying for many instruments by digitally encoding the signal for each.
**拆解**：主句为 these are wires；that 引导定语从句修饰 wires，从句谓语 share，宾语 message carrying；by digitally encoding the signal for each 为方式状语，说明“共用”的实现手段。
**要点**：share 在这里是“共用、分担”，宾语是动名词短语 message carrying，不是“消息携带者”。介词 by + 动名词表示方式，digitally 修饰 encoding。

### ⑥ 定语从句隔开主谓 + it 作形式主语
**原句**：Flat-panel computer display screens that can be controlled to show only the information desired are also lighter in weight than the numerous individual gauges it would take to display the same information simultaneously.
**拆解**：主语中心词 screens 后接 that 引导的定语从句（其中 to show only the information desired 为不定式作目的/结果状语，desired 为过去分词后置定语），谓语 are lighter；than 引出比较对象 gauges；gauges 后是省略了关系词的定语从句 it would take...，it 为形式主语，真正主语是 to display the same information simultaneously。
**要点**：主谓之间插入了十几词的定语从句，读句时要先跳过从句找到谓语 are。it would take... 是“需要花费……”的固定句式，it 无实义，不能译成“它”。
