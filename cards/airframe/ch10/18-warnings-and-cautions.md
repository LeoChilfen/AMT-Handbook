# Warnings & Cautions

## 1. 原文

### Annunciator Panels

An annunciator panel is a panel of warning lights in plain sight of the pilot. These lights are identified by the name of the system they represent and are usually covered with colored lenses to show the meaning of the condition they announce. Annunciator panels are installed for two purposes: to display current conditions and to notify of unsatisfactory conditions. Standardized colors are used to differentiate between visual messages. For example, the color green indicates a satisfactory condition. Yellow is used to caution of a serious condition that requires further monitoring. Red is the color for an unsatisfactory condition which needs immediate attention or action. Whether part of the instrument face or of a visual warning system, these colors give quick-reference information to the pilot.

Most aircraft include annunciator lights that illuminate when an event demanding attention occurs. These use the aforementioned colors in a variety of presentations. Individual lights near the associated flight deck instrument or a collective display of lights for various systems in a central location are common. Words label each light or are part of the light itself to identify any problem quickly and plainly.

On complex aircraft, the status of numerous systems and components must be known and maintained. Centralized warning systems have been developed to annunciate critical messages concerning a multitude of systems and components in a simplified, organized manner. Often, this will be done by locating a single annunciator panel somewhere on the instrument panel. These analog aircraft warning systems may look different in various aircraft and depend on manufacturer preference and the systems installed. EFIS provide for annunciation of advisory and warning messages as part of its flight control and monitoring capabilities, as previously described. Usually, the primary display unit is designated as the location to display annunciations.

Master caution lights are used to draw the attention of the crew to a critical situation in addition to an annunciator that describes the problem. These master caution lights are centrally wired and illuminate whenever any of the participating systems or components require attention. Once notified, the pilot may cancel the master caution, but a dedicated system or component annunciator light stays illuminated until the situation that caused the warning is rectified. Cancelling resets the master caution lights to warn of a subsequent fault event even before the initial fault is corrected. Press to test is available for the entire annunciator system, which energizes all warning circuitry and lights to confirm readiness. Often, this test exposes the need to replace the tiny light bulbs that are used in the system.

### Aural Warning Systems

Aircraft aural warning systems work in conjunction with illuminated annunciator systems. They audibly inform the pilot of a situation requiring attention. Various tones and phrases sound in the flight deck to alert the crew when certain conditions exist. For example, an aircraft with retractable landing gear uses an aural warning system to alert the crew to an unsafe condition. A bell sounds if the throttle is retarded and the landing gear is not in a down and locked condition.

A typical transport category aircraft has an aural warning system that alerts the pilot with audio signals for the following: abnormal takeoff, landing, pressurization, mach airspeed conditions, an engine or wheel well fire, calls from the crew call system, collision avoidance recommendations, and more. ## 2. 中文翻译

### 信号牌面板

信号牌面板是一块位于飞行员视线正前方的警告灯面板。这些灯以它们所代表的系统名称来标识，通常罩有彩色滤光片，以显示其所通报状况的含义。信号牌面板的安装有两个目的：显示当前状况，以及通报不正常的状况。采用标准化的颜色来区分不同的视觉信息。例如，绿色表示状况正常；黄色用于警示需要继续监控的严重状况；红色则表示需要立即注意或处置的不正常状况。无论这些颜色是仪表盘面的一部分，还是属于目视警告系统，它们都能为飞行员提供可快速查阅的信息。

大多数飞机都装有信号灯，当出现需要引起注意的事件时便会点亮。这些灯以多种呈现方式使用上述颜色。常见的形式是：在与相关驾驶舱仪表相邻处设置单个指示灯，或在中央位置集中显示各系统的指示灯组。每盏灯都有文字标注，或文字本身就是灯的一部分，以便快速、清楚地判明问题所在。

在复杂飞机上，众多系统和部件的状态必须被掌握并保持在正常范围。为此研制出集中警告系统，以简化、有条理的方式通报涉及大量系统和部件的关键信息。这通常是通过在仪表板某处设置一块独立的信号牌面板来实现的。这类模拟式飞机警告系统在不同机型上外观可能不同，取决于制造商的偏好和所装系统。如前所述，EFIS 作为其飞行控制与监控能力的一部分，也提供咨询信息和警告信息的通报。通常指定主显示组件作为显示通报信息的位置。

主警告灯用于在描述问题所在的信号器之外，再引起机组对危急情况的注意。这些主警告灯为集中布线，只要参与其中的任一系统或部件需要关注，它们就会点亮。飞行员得到提示后可以取消主警告，但专用的系统或部件信号灯会持续点亮，直到引起该警告的状况被排除。取消主警告会使主警告灯复位，从而在初始故障尚未纠正之前就能对后续的故障事件发出警告。整个信号牌系统都设有按压测试（press to test）功能，可给所有警告电路和灯通电，以确认其处于可用状态。这种测试常常会暴露出需要更换系统中那些小灯泡。

### 音响警告系统

飞机音响警告系统与灯光信号牌系统配合工作。它以声音告知飞行员出现了需要注意的情况。当某些条件存在时，驾驶舱内会响起各种音调或语音，提醒机组。例如，装有可收放起落架的飞机使用音响警告系统来提醒机组存在不安全状况：如果油门被收回而起落架未处于放下并锁定的状态，就会响起铃声。

典型的运输类飞机装有一套音响警告系统，用音频信号就以下情况提醒飞行员：起飞异常、着陆异常、增压异常、马赫空速状况、发动机或轮舱火警、机组呼叫系统的呼叫、防撞咨询等。图 10-130 列出了部分触发音响警告的问题以及为纠正该状况应采取的措施。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① whether 引导的让步状语（省略结构）
**原句**：Whether part of the instrument face or of a visual warning system, these colors give quick-reference information to the pilot.
**拆解**：Whether ... or ... 引出让步状语，完整形式为 Whether they are part of the instrument face or of a visual warning system，主语与系动词被省略；主句 these colors give quick-reference information to the pilot 为主谓双宾结构。
**要点**：whether 从句省略主语和 be 动词后只剩表语，读时要把省略的成分补回来。quick-reference 是复合形容词作定语，意为“可快速查阅的”，不是名词短语。

### ② 定语从句 + 现在分词短语作定语
**原句**：Most aircraft include annunciator lights that illuminate when an event demanding attention occurs.
**拆解**：主句 Most aircraft include annunciator lights；that illuminate... 为定语从句修饰 lights；从句内 when an event demanding attention occurs 为时间状语从句，其中 demanding attention 为现在分词短语作后置定语修饰 event。
**要点**：that 从句的先行词是 lights，不是 aircraft。demanding attention 的逻辑主语是 event，event 主动“要求注意”，所以用现在分词而非过去分词。

### ③ 被动语态 + 不定式目的状语 + concerning 介词短语
**原句**：Centralized warning systems have been developed to annunciate critical messages concerning a multitude of systems and components in a simplified, organized manner.
**拆解**：主语 Centralized warning systems，谓语 have been developed（现在完成时被动）；to annunciate critical messages 为不定式作目的状语；concerning a multitude of systems and components 为介词短语作后置定语修饰 messages；in a simplified, organized manner 为方式状语。
**要点**：concerning 在此是介词而非分词，意为“关于”，修饰紧邻的 messages。in ... manner 是固定搭配，形容词 simplified、organized 并列修饰 manner。

### ④ 动名词作主语 + before 时间状语从句
**原句**：Cancelling resets the master caution lights to warn of a subsequent fault event even before the initial fault is corrected.
**拆解**：动名词 Cancelling 作主语，谓语 resets，宾语 the master caution lights；to warn of a subsequent fault event 为不定式作目的状语；even before the initial fault is corrected 为时间状语从句，用被动语态。
**要点**：动名词短语作主语时谓语用第三人称单数 resets。before 从句用被动，因为 fault 是“被纠正”的对象；even 加强语气，强调“即便初始故障还没排除”。

### ⑤ which 引导的非限制性定语从句
**原句**：Press to test is available for the entire annunciator system, which energizes all warning circuitry and lights to confirm readiness.
**拆解**：主句 Press to test is available for the entire annunciator system；逗号后 which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 the entire annunciator system；从句谓语 energizes，宾语 all warning circuitry and lights，to confirm readiness 为目的状语。
**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，不能用 that 替换 which；它补充说明整个系统的功能，而不是限定“哪一个系统”。energize 在此意为“给……通电”。

### ⑥ 长列举宾语 + 冒号引出清单
**原句**：A typical transport category aircraft has an aural warning system that alerts the pilot with audio signals for the following: abnormal takeoff, landing, pressurization, mach airspeed conditions, an engine or wheel well fire, calls from the crew call system, collision avoidance recommendations, and more.
**拆解**：主句 A typical transport category aircraft has an aural warning system；that alerts the pilot with audio signals for the following 为定语从句；冒号后是 for the following 的列举内容，由多个名词短语并列构成。
**要点**：alert sb. with sth. for sth. 中 with 表手段、for 表原因或对象。冒号后的并列项共用前面的介词 for，翻译时把“就以下情况……提醒”提前更顺。
