# Flight Director Systems

> 机体 Ch 10 · Flight Director Systems · 原文 PDF 第 583 页

## 1. 原文

A flight director system is an instrument system consisting of electronic components that compute and indicate the aircraft attitude required to attain and maintain a preselected flight condition. A command bar on the aircraft’s attitude indicator shows the pilot how much and in what direction the attitude of the aircraft must be changed to achieve the desired result. The computed command indications relieve the pilot of many of the mental calculations required for instrument flights, such as interception angles, wind drift correction, and rates of climb and descent.

Essentially, a flight director system is an autopilot system without the servos. All of the same sensing and computations are made, but the pilot controls the airplane and makes maneuvers by following the commands displayed on the instrument panel. Flight director systems can be part of an autopilot system or exist on aircraft that do not possess full autopilot systems. Many autopilot systems allow for the option of engaging or disengaging a flight director display.

Flight director information is displayed on the instrument that displays the aircraft’s attitude. The process is accomplished with a visual reference technique. A symbol representing the aircraft is fit into a command bar positioned by the flight director in the proper location for a maneuver to be accomplished. The symbols used to represent the aircraft and the command bar vary by manufacturer. Regardless, the object is always to fly the aircraft symbol into the command bar symbol.

The instrument that displays the flight director commands is known as a flight director indicator (FDI), attitude director indicator (ADI), or electronic attitude director indicator (EADI). It may even be referred to as an artificial horizon with flight director. This display element combines with the other primary components of the flight director system. Like an autopilot, these consist of the sensing elements, a computer, and an interface panel.

Integration of navigation features into the attitude indicator is highly useful. The flight director contributes to this usefulness by indicating to the pilot how to maneuver the airplane to navigate a desired course. Selection of the VOR function on the flight director control panel links the computer to the omnirange receiver. The pilot selects a desired course and the flight director displays the bank attitude necessary to intercept and maintain this course. Allocations for wind drift and calculation of the intercept angle is performed automatically.

Flight director systems vary in complexity and features. Many have altitude hold, altitude select, pitch hold, and other features. But flight director systems are designed to offer the greatest assistance during the instrument approach phase of flight. ILS localizer and glideslope signals are transmitted through the receivers to the computer and are presented as command indications. This allows the pilot to fly the airplane down the optimum approach path to the runway using the flight director system.

With the altitude hold function engaged, level flight can be maintained during the maneuvering and procedure turn phase of an approach. Altitude hold automatically disengages when the glideslope is intercepted. Once inbound on the localizer, the command signals of the flight director are maintained in a centered or zero condition. Interception of the glideslope causes a downward indication of the command pitch indicator. Any deviation from the proper glideslope path causes a fly-up or fly-down command indication. The pilot needs only to keep the airplane symbol fit into the command bar.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞行指引仪系统是一种仪表系统，由电子部件组成，这些部件计算并指示为达到并保持某一预选飞行状态所需的飞机姿态。飞机姿态仪上的指令杆向飞行员显示：为达到期望结果，飞机姿态需要改变多少、朝哪个方向改变。经计算得出的指令指示，免去了飞行员在仪表飞行中所做的许多心算，例如切入角、风偏修正以及爬升率和下降率。

本质上，飞行指引仪系统就是一台没有舵机的自动驾驶仪系统。所有的传感与计算都相同，但由飞行员操纵飞机，并按照仪表板上显示的指令实施机动。飞行指引仪系统可以是自动驾驶仪系统的一部分，也可以装在未配备完整自动驾驶仪系统的飞机上。许多自动驾驶仪系统允许选择接通或断开飞行指引仪显示。

飞行指引信息显示在显示飞机姿态的仪表上。这一过程通过目视基准技术实现。代表飞机的符号被置入指令杆内，指令杆由飞行指引仪定位于完成某一机动所需的正确位置。用来代表飞机和指令杆的符号因制造商而异。但无论如何，目标始终是把飞机符号飞进指令杆符号之中。

显示飞行指引指令的仪表称为飞行指引仪指示器（FDI）、姿态指引仪指示器（ADI）或电子姿态指引仪指示器（EADI）。它甚至可被称为带飞行指引仪的人工地平仪。该显示部件与飞行指引仪系统的其他主要部件组合在一起。与自动驾驶仪一样，这些部件包括传感元件、计算机和接口面板。

把导航功能集成到姿态仪中非常有用。飞行指引仪通过向飞行员指示如何操纵飞机以飞向期望航向，增强了这种实用性。在飞行指引仪控制面板上选择 VOR 功能，就把计算机与全向信标接收机连接起来。飞行员选定期望航向，飞行指引仪便显示出切入并保持该航向所需的坡度姿态。风偏修正量的分配和切入角的计算均自动完成。

飞行指引仪系统的复杂程度和功能各不相同。许多系统具有高度保持、高度选择、俯仰保持等功能。但飞行指引仪系统的设计初衷，是在仪表进近飞行阶段提供最大的帮助。ILS 航向道和下滑道信号经接收机传送至计算机，并以指令指示的形式呈现。这使飞行员能够借助飞行指引仪系统，操纵飞机沿最佳进近路径下降至跑道。

接通高度保持功能后，在进近的机动飞行和程序转弯阶段可以保持平飞。当截获下滑道时，高度保持自动断开。一旦沿航向道进近，飞行指引仪的指令信号保持在居中即零位状态。截获下滑道会使俯仰指令指示器出现向下指示。任何偏离正确下滑道的情况都会产生“上飞”或“下飞”的指令指示。飞行员只需让飞机符号保持在指令杆内即可。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 双宾语 + 两个疑问词引导的宾语从句
**原句**：A command bar on the aircraft’s attitude indicator shows the pilot how much and in what direction the attitude of the aircraft must be changed to achieve the desired result.
**拆解**：主语 A command bar + 介词短语 on the aircraft’s attitude indicator 作后置定语；谓语 shows 接双宾语 the pilot 与 how much and in what direction... 从句；从句主语 the attitude of the aircraft，谓语 must be changed 为被动语态，to achieve the desired result 为目的状语。
**要点**：how much 与 in what direction 是两个并列的疑问词成分，共用后面的主谓，不要读成两个从句。show 后接双宾语时，间接宾语在前、直接宾语（从句）在后。

### ② relieve sb. of sth. + 过去分词后置定语
**原句**：The computed command indications relieve the pilot of many of the mental calculations required for instrument flights, such as interception angles, wind drift correction, and rates of climb and descent.
**拆解**：主语 The computed command indications（过去分词作前置定语）；谓语 relieve；宾语 the pilot；of many of the mental calculations 为介词宾语；required for instrument flights 为过去分词短语作后置定语；such as 引出三项举例。
**要点**：relieve sb. of sth. 意为“免除某人某事”，不能按字面译成“减轻”。required 的逻辑主语是 calculations，是被动关系，故用过去分词；such as 后的三项是对 calculations 的举例，不是新成分。

### ③ but 连接的并列句（被动 + 主动）
**原句**：All of the same sensing and computations are made, but the pilot controls the airplane and makes maneuvers by following the commands displayed on the instrument panel.
**拆解**：前一分句为被动语态 are made；but 转折后主语 the pilot，带两个并列谓语 controls 和 makes；by following the commands 为方式状语，displayed on the instrument panel 为过去分词短语修饰 commands。
**要点**：前句省略了 by the system 之类施动者，重点在“传感与计算照做”；后句换成主动，突出“由飞行员操纵”。by + 动名词表手段，不要与被动句的 by 短语混淆。

### ④ 两个分词短语叠加修饰
**原句**：A symbol representing the aircraft is fit into a command bar positioned by the flight director in the proper location for a maneuver to be accomplished.
**拆解**：主语 A symbol + 现在分词短语 representing the aircraft 作后置定语；谓语 is fit into（被动）；宾语 a command bar + 过去分词短语 positioned by the flight director... 作后置定语，其中 in the proper location 为地点状语，for a maneuver to be accomplished 为不定式复合结构表目的。
**要点**：representing 与 positioned 一主动一被动，分别修饰 symbol 和 command bar，逻辑主语不同——分词作定语时先找它紧跟的名词。for a maneuver to be accomplished 中 maneuver 是 to be accomplished 的逻辑主语。

### ⑤ Once + 省略结构作时间状语
**原句**：Once inbound on the localizer, the command signals of the flight director are maintained in a centered or zero condition.
**拆解**：Once inbound on the localizer 是省略了主语和系动词的时间状语从句（= Once the aircraft is inbound on the localizer）；主句主语 the command signals of the flight director，谓语 are maintained 为被动，in a centered or zero condition 为状态状语。
**要点**：once、when、while 等连词后可省略与主句相同的主语和 be 动词，只留表语或分词。inbound 是形容词化的航海/航空用语，意为“进近、向台飞行”。

### ⑥ with 复合结构作条件状语
**原句**：With the altitude hold function engaged, level flight can be maintained during the maneuvering and procedure turn phase of an approach.
**拆解**：With + 宾语 the altitude hold function + 过去分词 engaged 构成 with 复合结构，作条件状语；主句主语 level flight，谓语 can be maintained 为带情态动词的被动语态，during... 为时间状语。
**要点**：engage 与 function 是被动关系（功能被接通），故用过去分词。该结构相当于条件从句 If the altitude hold function is engaged，中文译成“接通……后”最自然。
