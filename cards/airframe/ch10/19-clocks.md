# Clocks

> 机体 Ch 10 · Clocks · 原文 PDF 第 593 页

## 1. 原文

Whether called a clock or a chronometer, an FAA-approved time indicator is required in the flight deck of IFR-certified aircraft. Pilots use a clock during flight to time maneuvers and for navigational purposes. The clock is usually mounted near the flight instrument group, often near the turn coordinator. It indicates hours, minutes, and seconds.

For many years, the mechanical 8-day clock was the standard aircraft timekeeping device largely because it continues to run without electrical power as long as it has been hand wound. The mechanical 8-day clock is reliable and accurate enough for its intended use. Some mechanical aircraft clocks feature a push-button elapsed time feature.

As electrical systems developed into the reliable, highly redundant systems that exist today, use of an electric clock to replace the mechanical clock began. An electric clock is an analog devise that may also have an elapsed time feature. It can be wired to the battery or battery bus. Thus, it continues to operate in the event of a power failure. Electric aircraft clocks are often used in multiengine aircraft where complete loss of electrical power is unlikely.

Many modern aircraft have a digital electronic clock with LED readout. This device comes with the advantages of low power consumption and high reliability due to the lack of moving parts. It is also very accurate. Solid-state electronics allow for expanded features, such as elapsed time, flight time that starts automatically upon takeoff, a stop watch, and memories for all functions. Some even have temperature and date readouts. Although wired into the aircraft’s electrical system, electronic digital clocks may include a small independent battery inside the unit that operates the device should aircraft electrical power fail.

On aircraft with fully digital computerized instrument systems utilizing flat panel displays, the computer’s internal clock, or a GPS clock, can be used with a digital time readout usually located somewhere on the primary flight display.

## 2. 中文翻译

无论称之为时钟还是精密计时器，按仪表飞行规则（IFR）认证的飞机驾驶舱内都必须装有一件经 FAA 批准的时间指示器。飞行员在飞行中使用时钟为机动动作计时，并用于导航目的。时钟通常安装在飞行仪表组附近，往往靠近转弯协调器。它指示小时、分钟和秒。

多年来，机械式 8 天时钟一直是飞机上标准的计时装置，主要原因是只要事先用手上紧发条，它就能在没有电源的情况下持续走时。机械式 8 天时钟可靠，精度也足以满足其预期用途。有些机械式飞机时钟还带有按钮式已用时间功能。

随着电气系统发展成为今天这样可靠且高度冗余的系统，人们开始用电动时钟取代机械时钟。电动时钟是一种模拟式装置，也可能带有已用时间功能。它可以接到蓄电池或蓄电池汇流条上，因此在电源故障时仍能继续工作。电动飞机时钟常用于完全失去电源可能性不大的多发飞机上。

许多现代飞机装有带 LED 读数的数字电子时钟。这种装置功耗低，且因没有运动部件而可靠性高，精度也很高。固态电子技术使其功能得以扩展，例如已用时间、起飞时自动开始的飞行时间、秒表，以及各项功能的记忆。有些甚至还能显示温度和日期。电子数字时钟虽然接入飞机的电气系统，但单元内部可能装有一块小型独立电池，以便在飞机电源失效时维持装置工作。

在采用平板显示器、全数字计算机化仪表系统的飞机上，可以使用计算机内部时钟或 GPS 时钟，数字时间读数通常显示在主飞行显示器的某个位置。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① whether 让步状语 + 被动语态
**原句**：Whether called a clock or a chronometer, an FAA-approved time indicator is required in the flight deck of IFR-certified aircraft.
**拆解**：Whether called a clock or a chronometer 为让步状语，完整形式是 Whether it is called...，省略主语与 be 动词；主句主语 an FAA-approved time indicator，谓语 is required 为被动语态，in the flight deck of IFR-certified aircraft 为地点状语。
**要点**：whether 引出的过去分词短语作让步状语，意为“无论叫……还是……”。主句是被动语态，真正的要求主体（规章）没有出现，翻译时可补“必须装”。

### ② because 原因状语从句 + as long as 条件从句
**原句**：For many years, the mechanical 8-day clock was the standard aircraft timekeeping device largely because it continues to run without electrical power as long as it has been hand wound.
**拆解**：主句 the mechanical 8-day clock was the standard aircraft timekeeping device；largely because 引出原因状语从句 it continues to run without electrical power；as long as it has been hand wound 为条件状语从句，用现在完成时被动。
**要点**：as long as 在此是“只要”，引导条件而非比较。has been hand wound 是现在完成时被动，强调“发条已被上紧”这一状态的持续；hand 是副词修饰 wound。

### ③ due to 介词短语作原因状语
**原句**：This device comes with the advantages of low power consumption and high reliability due to the lack of moving parts.
**拆解**：主语 This device，谓语 comes with，宾语 the advantages of low power consumption and high reliability（of 后两个名词并列）；due to the lack of moving parts 为介词短语作原因状语。
**要点**：due to 是复合介词，后接名词短语，不能接从句。advantages of A and B 中 of 只管到并列的两个名词，due to 修饰的是“可靠性高”的原因。

### ④ Although 让步（省略）+ that 定语从句 + should 倒装虚拟
**原句**：Although wired into the aircraft’s electrical system, electronic digital clocks may include a small independent battery inside the unit that operates the device should aircraft electrical power fail.
**拆解**：Although wired into... 为让步状语，省略了 they are；主句 electronic digital clocks may include a small independent battery；inside the unit 为地点状语；that operates the device 为定语从句修饰 battery；should aircraft electrical power fail 为省略 if 的倒装虚拟条件从句。
**要点**：should 提到句首、省略 if，是虚拟条件句的倒装形式，等于 if aircraft electrical power should fail。that 从句的先行词是 battery，不是 unit。

### ⑤ as 时间状语从句 + 定语从句 + 主语后置
**原句**：As electrical systems developed into the reliable, highly redundant systems that exist today, use of an electric clock to replace the mechanical clock began.
**拆解**：As 引导时间状语从句，从句谓语 developed into，宾语 the reliable, highly redundant systems，that exist today 为定语从句修饰 systems；主句主语 use of an electric clock to replace the mechanical clock，谓语 began。
**要点**：主句主语带两层后置修饰（of an electric clock 与 to replace the mechanical clock），谓语 began 直到句末才出现。翻译时把“人们开始用……”提前，比直译“电动时钟的使用开始了”更自然。
