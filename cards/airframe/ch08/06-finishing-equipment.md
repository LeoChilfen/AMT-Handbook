## 1. 原文

### Paint Booth

A paint booth may be a small room in which components of an aircraft are painted, or it can be an aircraft hangar big enough to house the largest aircraft. Whichever it is, the location must be able to protect the components or aircraft from the elements. Ideally, it would have temperature and humidity controls; but, in all cases, the booth or hangar must have good lighting, proper ventilation, and be dust free.

A simple paint booth can be constructed for a small aircraft by making a frame out of wood or polyvinyl chloride (PVC) pipe. It needs to be large enough to allow room to walk around and maneuver the spray gun. The top and sides can be covered with plastic sheeting stapled or taped to the frame. An exhaust fan can be added to one end with a large air-conditioning filter placed on the opposite end to filter incoming air. Lights should be large enough to be set up outside of the spray booth and shine through the sheeting or plastic windows. The ideal amount of light would be enough to produce a glare off of all the surfaces to be sprayed. This type of temporary booth can be set up in a hangar, a garage, or outside on a ramp, if the weather and temperature are favorable.

Normally, Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) regulations do not apply to a person painting one airplane. However, anyone planning to paint an aircraft should be aware that local clean air regulations may be applicable to an airplane painting project. When planning to paint an aircraft at an airport, it would be a good idea to check with the local airport authority before starting.

### Air Supply

The air supply for paint spraying using a conventional siphon feed spray gun should come from an air compressor with a storage tank big enough to provide an uninterrupted supply of air with at least 90 pounds per square inch (psi) providing 10 cubic feet per minute (CFM) of air to the spray gun.

The compressor needs to be equipped with a regulator, water trap, air hose, and an adequate filter system to ensure that clean, dry, oil-free air is delivered to the spray gun.

If using one of the newer high-volume low-pressure (HVLP) spray guns and using a conventional compressor, it is better to use a two-stage compressor of at least a 5 horsepower (hp) that operates at 90 psi and provides 20 CFM to the gun. The key to the operation of the newer HVLP spray guns is the air volume, not the pressure.

If purchasing a new complete HVLP system, the air supply is from a turbine compressor. An HVLP turbine has a series of fans, or stages, that move a lot of air at low pressure. More stages provide greater air output (rated in CFM), which means better atomization of the coating being sprayed. The intake air is also the cooling air for the motor. This air is filtered from dirt and dust particles prior to entering the turbine. Some turbines also have a second filter for the air supply to the spray gun. The turbine does not produce oil or water to contaminate the air supply, but the air supply from the turbine heats up, causing the paint to dry faster, so you may need an additional length of hose to reduce the air temperature at the spray gun.

### Spray Equipment

#### Air Compressors

Piston–type compressors are available with one-stage and multiple-stage compressors, various size motors, and various size supply tanks. The main requirement for painting is to ensure the spray gun has a continuous supplied volume of air. Piston-type compressors compress air and deliver it to a storage tank. Most compressors provide over 100 psi, but only the larger ones provide the volume of air needed for an uninterrupted supply to the gun. The multistage compressor is a good choice for a shop when a large volume of air is needed for pneumatic tools. When in doubt about the size of the compressor, compare the manufacturer’s specifications and get the largest one possible.

#### Large Coating Containers

For large painting projects, such as spraying an entire aircraft, the quantity of mixed paint in a pressure tank provides many advantages. The setup allows a greater area to be covered without having to stop and fill the cup on a spray gun. The painter is able to keep a wet paint line, and more material is applied to the surface with less overspray. It provides the flexibility of maneuvering the spray gun in any position without the restriction and weight of an attached paint cup. Remote pressure tanks are available in sizes from 2 quarts to over 60 gallons.

### Miscellaneous Painting Tools & Equipment

Some tools that are available to the painter include:

• Masking paper/tape dispenser that accommodates various widths of masking paper. It includes a masking tape dispenser that applies the tape to one edge of the paper as it is rolled off to facilitate one person applying the paper and tape in a single step.

• Electronic and magnetic paint thickness gauges to measure dry paint thickness.

• Wet film gauges to measure freshly applied wet paint.

• Infrared thermometers to measure coating and substrate surfaces to verify that they fall in the recommended temperature range prior to spraying.

#### Spray Guns

A top quality spray gun is a key component in producing a quality finish in any coating process. It is especially important when painting an aircraft because of the large area and varied surfaces that must be sprayed.

When spray painting, it is of utmost importance to follow the manufacturer’s recommendations for correct sizing of the air cap, fluid tip, and needle combinations. The right combination provides the best coverage and the highest quality finish in the shortest amount of time.

All of the following examples of the various spray guns (except the airless) are of the air atomizing type. They are the most capable of providing the highest quality finish.

Siphon-Feed Gun The siphon-feed gun is a conventional spray gun familiar to most people, with a one quart paint cup located below the gun. Regulated air passes through the gun and draws (siphons) the paint from the supply cup. This is an external mix gun, which means the air and fluid mix outside the air cap. This gun applies virtually any type coating and provides a high quality finish.

Gravity-Feed Gun A gravity-feed gun provides the same high-quality finish as a siphon-feed gun, but the paint supply is located in a cup on top of the gun and supplied by gravity. The operator can make fine adjustments between the atomizing pressure and fluid flow and utilize all material in the cup. This also is an external mix gun.

The HVLP production spray gun is an internal mix gun. The air and fluid is mixed inside the air cap. Because of the low pressure used in the paint application, it transfers at least 65 percent and upwards of 80 percent of the finish material to the surface. HVLP spray guns are available with a standard cup located underneath or in a gravity-feed model with the cup on top. The sample shown can be connected with hoses to a remote paint material container holding from 2 quarts to 60 gallons.

Because of more restrictive EPA regulations, and the fact that more paint is being transferred to the surface with less waste from overspray, a large segment of the paint and coating industry is switching to HVLP spray equipment.

Airless spraying does not directly use compressed air to atomize the coating material. A pump delivers paint to the spray gun under high hydraulic pressure (500 to 4,500 psi) to atomize the fluid. The fluid is then released through an orifice in the spray nozzle. This system increases transfer efficiency and production speed with less overspray than conventional air atomized spray systems. It is used for production work but does not provide the fine finish of air atomized systems.

#### Fresh Air Breathing Systems

Fresh air breathing systems should be used whenever coatings are being sprayed that contain isocyanides. This includes all polyurethane coatings. The system incorporates a high-capacity electric air turbine that provides a constant source of fresh air to the mask. The use of fresh air breathing systems is also highly recommended when spraying chromate primers and chemical stripping aircraft. The system provides cool filtered breathing air with up to 200 feet of hose, which allows the air pump intake to be placed in an area of fresh air, well outside of the spraying area.

A charcoal-filtered respirator should be used for all other spraying and sanding operations to protect the lungs and respiratory tract. The respirator should be a double-cartridge, organic vapor type that provides a tight seal around the nose and mouth. The cartridges can be changed separately, and should be changed when detecting odor or experiencing nose or throat irritation. The outer prefilters should be changed if experiencing increased resistance to breathing.

#### Viscosity Measuring Cup

This is a small cup with a long handle and a calibrated orifice in the bottom that allows the liquid in the cup to drain out at a specific timed rate. Coating manufacturers recommend spraying their product at a specific pressure and viscosity. That viscosity is determined by measuring the efflux (drain) time of the liquid coating through the cup orifice. The time (in seconds) is listed on most paint manufacturers’ product/ technical data pages. The measurement determines if the mixed coating meets the recommended viscosity for spraying.

There are different manufacturers of the viscosity measuring devices, but the most common one listed and used for spray painting is known as a Zahn cup. The orifice number must correspond to the one listed on the product/technical data sheet. For most primers and topcoats, the #2 or #3 Zahn cup is the one recommended.

To perform an accurate viscosity measurement, it is very important that the temperature of the sample material be within the recommended range of 73.5 °F ± 3.5 °F (23 ºC ± 2 ºC), and then proceed as follows:

1. Thoroughly mix the sample with minimum bubbles.

2. Dip the Zahn cup vertically into the sample being tested, totally immersing the cup below the surface.

3. With a stopwatch in one hand, briskly lift the cup out of the sample. As the top edge of the cup breaks the surface, start the stopwatch.

4. Stop the stopwatch when the first break in the flow of the liquid is observed at the orifice exit. The number in seconds is referred to as the efflux time.

5. Record the time on the stopwatch and compare it to the coating manufacturer’s recommendation. Adjust the viscosity, if necessary, but be aware not to thin the coating below recommendations that could result in the release of VOCs into the atmosphere above the regulated limitations.

#### Mixing Equipment

Use a paint shaker for all coatings within 5 days of application to ensure the material is thoroughly mixed. Use a mechanical paint stirrer to mix larger quantities of material. If a mechanical stirrer is driven by a drill, the drill should be pneumatic, instead of electric. The sparks from an electric drill can cause an explosion from the paint vapors.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 喷漆房

喷漆房可以是一个用于涂装飞机部件的小房间，也可以是能容纳最大飞机的机库。无论哪种，其位置都必须能保护部件或飞机不受天气影响。理想情况下应有温度和湿度控制；但在任何情况下，喷漆房或机库都必须有良好的照明、适当的通风，并且无尘。

可以为小型飞机搭建简易喷漆房：用木材或聚氯乙烯（PVC）管做框架。它要足够大，以便能绕着飞机走动并操作喷枪。顶部和四侧可用塑料薄膜覆盖，用订书钉或胶带固定在框架上。一端可加装排风扇，另一端放置大型空调滤网以过滤进入的空气。灯具应足够大，能布置在喷漆房外，透过塑料薄膜或塑料窗照明。理想的光量应足以在所有待喷表面上形成反光。这种临时喷漆房可搭在机库、车库，天气和温度适宜时也可搭在室外的停机坪上。

通常，环境保护署（EPA）的法规不适用于只喷一架飞机的人。不过，任何计划给飞机喷漆的人都应知道，当地的清洁空气法规可能适用于飞机喷漆项目。计划在机场给飞机喷漆时，开工前最好先向当地机场管理当局咨询。

### 供气

使用常规虹吸式喷枪喷漆时，气源应来自一台带储气罐的空气压缩机，储气罐要足够大，能以至少90磅/平方英寸（psi）的压力、10立方英尺/分钟（CFM）的流量向喷枪不间断供气。

压缩机需配备调压器、水分离器、空气软管和适当的过滤系统，以确保向喷枪输送清洁、干燥、无油的空气。

如果使用较新型的高流量低压（HVLP）喷枪而配常规压缩机，最好使用至少5马力（hp）、在90 psi下能提供20 CFM的二段式压缩机。较新型HVLP喷枪工作的关键在于空气流量，而不是压力。

如果购买成套的新型HVLP系统，气源来自涡轮压缩机。HVLP涡轮有一系列风扇（即级），在低压下推动大量空气。级数越多，出风量越大（以CFM计），被喷涂料的雾化也越好。进气同时也是电机的冷却风。这些空气在进入涡轮前经过滤除去尘土颗粒。有些涡轮还为通往喷枪的供气设有第二级过滤。涡轮不会产生油或水来污染气源，但从涡轮出来的空气会升温，使油漆干得更快，因此可能需要加长软管以降低喷枪处的空气温度。

### 喷涂设备

#### 空气压缩机

活塞式压缩机有一段式和多段式，配不同功率的电机和不同容积的储气罐。喷漆的主要要求是确保喷枪获得连续供应的空气量。活塞式压缩机把空气压缩后送入储气罐。大多数压缩机可提供100 psi以上的压力，但只有较大的机型才能提供喷枪不间断供气所需的空气量。当车间需要大量空气驱动气动工具时，多段式压缩机是好的选择。拿不准该选多大压缩机时，比较制造商规格，尽量选最大的。

#### 大容量涂料容器

对于大型喷漆项目，例如给整架飞机喷涂，压力罐中混合好的大量涂料带来许多好处。这种配置可以覆盖更大面积而不必停下来给喷枪杯加漆。漆工能保持连续的湿漆边，更多涂料落到表面、过喷更少。它使喷枪可以在任意位置灵活操作，不受附带漆杯的束缚和重量影响。远程压力罐的容量从2夸脱到60加仑以上不等。

#### 系统空气过滤器

使用活塞式空气压缩机喷漆时，供气管路中必须装设过滤器以除去水和油。典型的过滤器组件见图8-3。

### 其他涂装工具与设备

可供漆工使用的一些工具包括：

• 遮蔽纸/胶带分配器，可容纳各种宽度的遮蔽纸。它带有胶带分配器，在纸卷展开时把胶带贴到纸的一边，便于一个人一步完成铺纸和贴胶带。

• 电子式和磁性漆膜测厚仪，用于测量干漆膜厚度。

• 湿膜测厚规，用于测量刚喷上的湿漆。

• 红外测温仪，用于测量涂层和基材表面温度，确认喷涂前处于推荐温度范围内。

#### 喷枪

高品质喷枪是任何涂装工艺中获得优质涂层的关键部件。给飞机喷漆时尤为重要，因为要喷涂的面积大、表面形状多变。

喷涂时，最重要的是遵循制造商关于空气帽、涂料喷嘴和针阀正确配套尺寸的建议。正确的组合能在最短时间内提供最佳覆盖和最高质量的涂层。

下列各种喷枪示例（无气喷涂除外）都属于空气雾化型。它们最能提供最高质量的涂层。

虹吸式喷枪 虹吸式喷枪是大多数人都熟悉的常规喷枪，1夸脱漆杯位于枪体下方。调压后的空气通过枪体，把漆从供料杯中吸出（虹吸）。这是外混式喷枪，即空气与涂料在空气帽外混合。这种枪几乎能喷任何类型的涂料，并能获得高质量涂层。

重力式喷枪 重力式喷枪能获得与虹吸式喷枪相同的高质量涂层，但供料杯位于枪体上方，靠重力供漆。操作者可在雾化压力与涂料流量之间做细微调节，并可用尽杯中全部涂料。这也是外混式喷枪。

HVLP生产型喷枪是内混式喷枪。空气与涂料在空气帽内部混合。由于喷涂时压力低，它能把至少65%、最高80%的涂料转移到表面上。HVLP喷枪有杯在枪体下方的标准型和杯在上方的重力式；所示样品可经软管连接到容量2夸脱至60加仑的远程涂料容器。

由于EPA法规更加严格，而且更多涂料被转移到表面、过喷浪费更少，很大一部分涂料工业正在转向HVLP喷涂设备。

无气喷涂不直接使用压缩空气来雾化涂料。泵以很高的液压（500～4,500 psi）把漆送入喷枪使涂料雾化，涂料随后经喷嘴上的小孔喷出。该系统比常规空气雾化喷涂系统转移效率更高、生产速度更快、过喷更少。它用于生产性作业，但不能提供空气雾化系统的精细涂层。

#### 新鲜空气呼吸系统

喷涂含异氰酸酯的涂料时应使用新鲜空气呼吸系统。这包括所有聚氨酯涂料。该系统包含一台大流量电动空气涡轮，向面罩持续供应新鲜空气。喷涂铬酸盐底漆和对飞机进行化学脱漆时，也非常推荐使用新鲜空气呼吸系统。该系统可提供长达200英尺软管的凉爽过滤呼吸空气，使气泵进气口能置于喷涂区之外很远的新鲜空气区域。

其他所有喷涂和打磨作业应使用活性炭过滤式呼吸器，以保护肺和呼吸道。呼吸器应为双滤盒有机蒸气型，在口鼻周围形成紧密密封。滤盒可分别更换，当闻到气味或感到鼻喉刺激时就应更换。如果感到呼吸阻力增大，应更换外部预滤棉。

#### 黏度测量杯

这是一个带长手柄的小杯，底部有一个校准小孔，让杯中液体以特定的计时速率流出。涂料制造商建议其产品在特定压力和黏度下喷涂。该黏度通过测量液体涂料经杯孔流出的流出时间来确定。该时间（以秒计）列在大多数油漆制造商的产品/技术数据页上。测量可判断混合后的涂料是否达到推荐的喷涂黏度。

黏度测量器有不同厂家，但喷涂中最常列出和使用的一种称为Zahn杯。其孔径编号必须与产品/技术数据表上所列的一致。对大多数底漆和面漆，推荐使用2号或3号Zahn杯。

要进行准确的黏度测量，非常重要的是样品材料的温度应在73.5 °F ± 3.5 °F（23 ºC ± 2 ºC）的推荐范围内，然后按以下步骤操作：

1. 充分搅拌样品，尽量少产生气泡。

2. 把Zahn杯垂直浸入被测样品，使杯体完全没入液面以下。

3. 一手持秒表，迅速把杯从样品中提起。当杯的上边缘离开液面时，启动秒表。

4. 当观察到液体在孔口出口处第一次断流时，停止秒表。这个秒数称为流出时间。

5. 记下秒表上的时间，与涂料制造商的推荐值比较。必要时调整黏度，但注意不要把涂料稀释到低于推荐值，否则可能导致VOC排放超过法规限值。

#### 混合设备

对所有涂料，在使用前5天内应使用涂料振荡器，以确保材料充分混合。混合较大量材料时使用机械搅拌器。如果机械搅拌器由电钻驱动，电钻应为气动而非电动。电钻的火花可能引燃漆蒸气而发生爆炸。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① whoever 式让步从句 + 情态动词被动
**原句**：Whichever it is, the location must be able to protect the components or aircraft from the elements.
**拆解**：Whichever it is 为让步状语从句（whichever 作表语，it 指代喷漆房或机库）；主句主语 the location，谓语 must be able to protect，宾语 the components or aircraft，from the elements 为来源状语。
**要点**：whichever 引导让步从句时意为“无论是哪一个”，与 whatever 的结构类似。protect A from B 中的 B 是“天气/自然力”，the elements 是固定表达，不能直译成“元素”。

### ② with 复合结构作伴随状语
**原句**：An exhaust fan can be added to one end with a large air-conditioning filter placed on the opposite end to filter incoming air.
**拆解**：主句为被动 An exhaust fan can be added to one end；with a large air-conditioning filter placed on the opposite end 为 with 复合结构（宾语 + 过去分词补足语）作伴随状语；to filter incoming air 为不定式作目的状语。
**要点**：with 复合结构中 filter 与 place 是被动关系，故用过去分词 placed。sb. can be added 与 placed 分属两个不同层次：前者是句子谓语，后者只是状语内部成分。

### ③ 被动 + 不定式完成式 + that 宾语从句
**原句**：The compressor needs to be equipped with a regulator, water trap, air hose, and an adequate filter system to ensure that clean, dry, oil-free air is delivered to the spray gun.
**拆解**：主语 The compressor，谓语 needs，宾语为不定式被动式 to be equipped with...；with 后接四项并列宾语；to ensure that... 为目的状语，that 引导宾语从句，从句为被动 is delivered to the spray gun。
**要点**：need to be equipped 是“需要被配备”，主语是受事，汉语可直接说“需配备”。ensure that 从句中用一般现在时被动，表示常规要求而非将来。

### ④ 主语 + 表语 + not 的对比否定
**原句**：The key to the operation of the newer HVLP spray guns is the air volume, not the pressure.
**拆解**：主语 The key to the operation of the newer HVLP spray guns（两个 of 短语层层后置）；系动词 is；表语 the air volume；逗号后 not the pressure 为省略式对比成分，相当于 and not the pressure。
**要点**：the key to sth. 意为“……的关键”，to 是介词不是不定式符号。句末 not the pressure 不是独立分句，不能用句号断开；汉语用“而不是”衔接最自然。

### ⑤ because of + 名词短语前置 + 分数表达
**原句**：Because of the low pressure used in the paint application, it transfers at least 65 percent and upwards of 80 percent of the finish material to the surface.
**拆解**：Because of the low pressure used in the paint application 为原因状语前置，used in... 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 pressure；主句 it transfers... to the surface，it 指 HVLP 喷枪；at least 65 percent and upwards of 80 percent 为两个并列的数量状语。
**要点**：upwards of 意为“高达/超过”，与 at least 连用表示区间下限与上限。transfer... to... 是“把……转移到……”，此处指涂料转移到工件表面的比例（转移效率）。

### ⑥ the fact that 同位语从句 + 现在进行被动
**原句**：Because of more restrictive EPA regulations, and the fact that more paint is being transferred to the surface with less waste from overspray, a large segment of the paint and coating industry is switching to HVLP spray equipment.
**拆解**：Because of 后接两个并列宾语：more restrictive EPA regulations 与 the fact；the fact 后接 that 同位语从句，从句用现在进行被动 is being transferred；主句主语 a large segment of the paint and coating industry，谓语 is switching to。
**要点**：the fact that 是同位语从句的标志性结构，that 在从句中不作成分。is being transferred 是现在进行被动，强调“正在被转移”的过程；a large segment of 表示“很大一部分”。
