# Sequence for Painting a Single-Engine or Light Twin Airplane

> 机体 Ch 08 · Sequence for Painting a Single-Engine or Light Twin Airplane · 原文 PDF 第 407 页

## 1. 原文

As a general practice on any surface being painted, spray each application of coating in a different direction to facilitate even and complete coverage. After you apply the primer, apply the tack coat and subsequent top coats in opposite directions, one coat vertically and the next horizontally, as appropriate.

Start by spraying all the corners and gaps between the control surfaces and fixed surfaces. Paint the leading and trailing edges of all surfaces. Spray the landing gear and wheel wells, if applicable, and paint the bottom of the fuselage up the sides to a horizontal break, such as a seam line. Paint the underside of the horizontal stabilizer. Paint the vertical stabilizer and the rudder, and then move to the top of the horizontal stabilizer. Spray the top and sides of the fuselage down to the point of the break from spraying the underside of the fuselage. Then, spray the underside of the wings. Complete the job by spraying the top of the wings.

The biggest challenge is to control the overspray and keep the paint line wet. The ideal scenario would be to have another experienced painter with a second spray gun help with the painting. It is much easier to keep the paint wet and the job is completed in half the time.

## 2. 中文翻译

在给任何表面喷漆时，一般做法是让每一道涂层都朝不同方向喷涂，以便获得均匀、完整的覆盖。上完底漆之后，粘接层和其后的各道面漆应交替反向喷涂：视情况一道竖喷、下一道横喷。

先喷各操纵面与固定面之间的所有角落和缝隙。喷涂所有表面的前缘和后缘。如适用，喷涂起落架和轮舱，并把机身底部向上喷到一条水平分界（例如一条接缝线）为止。喷水平安定面的下表面。喷垂直安定面和方向舵，然后转到水平安定面的上表面。把机身顶部和两侧向下喷到与喷机身底部时形成的分界处为止。接着喷机翼下表面。最后喷机翼上表面，完成全部工作。

最大的挑战是控制过喷并保持漆线湿润。理想的情况是有另一位有经验的喷漆工拿第二把喷枪帮忙。这样保持漆面湿润要容易得多，活儿也能在一半的时间里干完。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 句首介词短语作状语 + 不定式作目的状语
**原句**：As a general practice on any surface being painted, spray each application of coating in a different direction to facilitate even and complete coverage.
**拆解**：As a general practice ... 是句首状语，交代“按惯例”；其中 being painted 为现在分词被动式作后置定语，修饰 any surface。主句为祈使句 spray，宾语 each application of coating，状语 in a different direction；to facilitate ... 为不定式短语作目的状语。
**要点**：being painted 是“正在被喷”的进行体被动分词，不能换成 painted（已喷完）。目的状语 to facilitate 的逻辑主语就是隐含的 you，译成“以便……”。

### ② 时间状语从句 + 反向指令的同位补充
**原句**：After you apply the primer, apply the tack coat and subsequent top coats in opposite directions, one coat vertically and the next horizontally, as appropriate.
**拆解**：After 引导时间状语从句；主句为祈使句 apply，宾语 the tack coat and subsequent top coats，状语 in opposite directions；逗号后 one coat vertically and the next horizontally 是独立主格式的补充说明，进一步解释“反向”；as appropriate 为评注性状语。
**要点**：one coat ... the next ... 是省略了 being 的独立结构，用来说明两个涂层各自的方向。as appropriate 意为“视情况而定”，位置灵活，不修饰某个具体词。

### ③ 祈使句并列 + 插入条件 + such as 举例
**原句**：Spray the landing gear and wheel wells, if applicable, and paint the bottom of the fuselage up the sides to a horizontal break, such as a seam line.
**拆解**：两个祈使句由 and 并列：Spray the landing gear and wheel wells 与 paint the bottom of the fuselage；if applicable 为插入的条件状语，只修饰前一个祈使句；up the sides to a horizontal break 为方向与终点状语；such as a seam line 举例说明 break。
**要点**：if applicable 夹在两个并列祈使句之间，容易误读成修饰后者，实际只管“如适用则喷起落架和轮舱”。break 在此不是“破裂”，而是涂层分段施工的“分界”。

### ④ 介词短语层层后置 + 动名词作介词宾语
**原句**：Spray the top and sides of the fuselage down to the point of the break from spraying the underside of the fuselage.
**拆解**：祈使句 Spray，宾语 the top and sides of the fuselage；down to the point of the break 为终点状语；from spraying the underside of the fuselage 是介词 from + 动名词短语，说明这个“分界”是先前喷机身底部时形成的。
**要点**：全句连续四个介词短语后置，读的时候要按“喷哪里—喷到哪—分界来自哪”三段切分。spraying 是动名词，作 from 的宾语，不是分词状语。

### ⑤ 表语从句式的虚拟 + have sb. do 结构
**原句**：The ideal scenario would be to have another experienced painter with a second spray gun help with the painting.
**拆解**：主语 The ideal scenario，系动词 would be（虚拟/委婉语气），表语为不定式短语 to have ... help ...；have 的宾语是 another experienced painter，with a second spray gun 为后置定语修饰 painter，help with the painting 为不带 to 的动词不定式作宾语补足语。
**要点**：have sb. do sth. 中补足语省略 to，是“让某人做某事”。with a second spray gun 修饰 painter 而不是 help，指“带着第二把喷枪的另一位喷漆工”。would be 表示设想，不是过去时。
