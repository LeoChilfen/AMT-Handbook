# Introduction

> 机体 Ch 08 · Introduction · 原文 PDF 第 396 页

## 1. 原文

Paint, or more specifically its overall color and application, is usually the first impression that is transmitted to someone when they look at an aircraft for the first time. Paint makes a statement about the aircraft and the person who owns or operates it. The paint scheme may reflect the owner’s ideas and color preferences for an amateur-built aircraft project, or it may be colors and identification for the recognition of a corporate or air carrier aircraft.

Paint is more than aesthetics; it affects the weight of the aircraft and protects the integrity of the airframe. The topcoat finish is applied to protect the exposed surfaces from corrosion and deterioration. Also, a properly painted aircraft is easier to clean and maintain because the exposed surfaces are more resistant to corrosion and dirt, and oil does not adhere as readily to the surface.

A wide variety of materials and finishes are used to protect and provide the desired appearance of the aircraft. The term “paint” is used in a general sense and includes primers, enamels, lacquers, and the various multipart finishing formulas. Paint has three components: resin as coating material, pigment for color, and solvents to reduce the mix to a workable viscosity.

Internal structure and unexposed components are finished to protect them from corrosion and deterioration. All exposed surfaces and components are finished to provide protection and to present a pleasing appearance. Decorative finishing includes trim striping, the addition of company logos and emblems, and the application of decals, identification numbers, and letters.

## 2. 中文翻译

油漆，或者更具体地说，它的整体颜色与喷涂效果，通常是别人第一次看到一架飞机时得到的第一印象。油漆表达了这架飞机以及拥有或运营它的人的某种态度。涂装方案可以体现业余自制飞机项目主人的想法与色彩偏好，也可以是公司或航空公司的飞机用于识别目的的颜色与标识。

油漆不只是美观问题；它影响飞机的重量，并保护机体的完好性。施加面漆是为了保护外露表面免遭腐蚀和劣化。此外，涂装得当的飞机更易清洁和维护，因为外露表面更耐腐蚀、不易沾污，油类也不那么容易附着在表面上。

用于保护和赋予飞机所需外观的材料与涂层种类繁多。“油漆”一词是泛指，包括底漆、瓷漆、硝基漆以及各种多组分涂装配方。油漆有三种组分：作为涂层材料的树脂、提供颜色的颜料，以及把混合料稀释到可施工黏度的溶剂。

内部结构和不外露部件也要涂装，以保护其免遭腐蚀和劣化。所有外露表面和部件都要涂装，既提供保护，又呈现悦目的外观。装饰性涂装包括条带饰线、添加公司徽标与标记，以及粘贴贴花、识别编号和字母。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 插入语割裂主谓 + 定语从句
**原句**：Paint, or more specifically its overall color and application, is usually the first impression that is transmitted to someone when they look at an aircraft for the first time.
**拆解**：主语 Paint，其后 or more specifically its overall color and application 为插入的同位性说明；谓语 is，表语 the first impression，后接 that 引导的定语从句（被动语态 is transmitted to someone），when 引导时间状语从句。
**要点**：两个逗号之间的插入语把主语和谓语隔开，容易误把 application 当成主语。that 从句修饰 impression，是被动结构，译成中文时“第一印象”要还原为“被人获得的第一印象”。

### ② 分号连接的并列句
**原句**：Paint is more than aesthetics; it affects the weight of the aircraft and protects the integrity of the airframe.
**拆解**：分号前为“主系表”结构 Paint is more than aesthetics；分号后为并列句，主语 it 回指 paint，两个并列谓语 affects 与 protects 共用一个主语。
**要点**：分号在此相当于“而且/不仅如此”，两分句是并列递进而非从属关系。more than aesthetics 是“不只是美观”，不是“比美观更多”。

### ③ because 从句内含两个并列分句
**原句**：Also, a properly painted aircraft is easier to clean and maintain because the exposed surfaces are more resistant to corrosion and dirt, and oil does not adhere as readily to the surface.
**拆解**：主句为主系表结构 a properly painted aircraft is easier to clean and maintain；because 引导原因状语从句，从句内部由 and 连接两个并列分句：the exposed surfaces are more resistant... 与 oil does not adhere...。
**要点**：这个 and 连接的是两个完整分句，不是 resistant to 的宾语并列，所以后面另起主语 oil。as readily 是“那么轻易”，与否定连用表示“不那么容易”。

### ④ 被动语态 + 介词短语作定语
**原句**：The term “paint” is used in a general sense and includes primers, enamels, lacquers, and the various multipart finishing formulas.
**拆解**：主语 The term “paint”，谓语为并列的 is used（被动）与 includes（主动），in a general sense 作方式状语，includes 的宾语是四项并列名词。
**要点**：同一主语下被动与主动谓语并列，说明“paint”这个词既“被使用”又“包含”若干品类。翻译时不必死守被动，可译为“‘油漆’一词是泛指，包括……”。

### ⑤ 不定式作目的状语
**原句**：Internal structure and unexposed components are finished to protect them from corrosion and deterioration.
**拆解**：主语为并列的 Internal structure and unexposed components，谓语 are finished 为被动语态，to protect them from corrosion and deterioration 为不定式短语作目的状语，protect A from B 为固定搭配。
**要点**：不定式的逻辑主语是句子的受事（structures/components），但 protect 的宾语用 them 回指主语，形成“被涂装—受保护”的关系。from 后接两个并列名词，表示防护的两类对象。
