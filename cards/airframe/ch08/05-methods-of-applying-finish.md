# Methods of Applying Finish

> 机体 Ch 08 · Methods of Applying Finish · 原文 PDF 第 399 页

## 1. 原文

There are several methods of applying aircraft finish. Among the most common are dipping, brushing, and spraying.

### Dipping

The application of finishes by dipping is generally confined to factories or large repair stations. The process consists of dipping the part to be finished in a tank filled with the finishing material. Primer coats are frequently applied in this manner.

### Brushing

Brushing has long been a satisfactory method of applying finishes to all types of surfaces. Brushing is generally used for small repair work and on surfaces where it is not practicable to spray paint.

The material to be applied should be thinned to the proper consistency for brushing. A material that is too thick has a tendency to pull or rope under the brush. If the materials are too thin, they are likely to run or not cover the surface adequately. Proper thinning and substrate temperature allows the finish to flow-out and eliminates the brush marks.

### Spraying

Spraying is the preferred method for a quality finish. Spraying is used to cover large surfaces with a uniform layer of material, which results in the most cost effective method of application. All spray systems have several basic similarities. There must be an adequate source of compressed air, a reservoir or feed tank to hold a supply of the finishing material, and a device for controlling the combination of the air and finishing material ejected in an atomized cloud or spray against the surface to be coated.

A self-contained, pressurized spray can of paint meets the above requirements and satisfactory results can be obtained painting components and small areas of touchup. However, the aviation coating materials available in cans is limited, and this chapter addresses the application of mixed components through a spray gun.

There are two main types of spray equipment. A spray gun with an integral paint container is adequate for use when painting small areas. When large areas are painted, pressure-feed equipment is more desirable since a large supply of finishing material can be applied without the interruption of having to stop and refill a paint container. An added bonus is the lighter overall weight of the spray gun and the flexibility of spraying in any direction with a constant pressure to the gun.

The air supply to the spray gun must be entirely free of water or oil in order to produce the optimum results in the finished product. Water traps, as well as suitable filters to remove any trace of oil, must be incorporated in the air pressure supply line. These filters and traps must be serviced on a regular basis.

## 2. 中文翻译

涂装飞机涂层有几种方法。最常见的是浸涂、刷涂和喷涂。

### 浸涂

用浸涂法施工涂层一般限于工厂或大型修理站。其过程是把待涂零件浸入装满涂料的槽中。底漆层常以这种方式施工。

### 刷涂

刷涂长期以来一直是各种表面涂装的令人满意的方法。刷涂一般用于小修补，以及不便于喷涂的表面。

待涂材料应稀释到适合刷涂的稠度。太稠的材料在刷子下容易拉丝或成缕。如果材料太稀，则容易流挂或不能充分覆盖表面。适当的稀释和基材温度能使涂层流平，并消除刷痕。

### 喷涂

喷涂是获得优质涂层的首选方法。喷涂用于在大面积上覆盖均匀的一层材料，是最具成本效益的施工方法。所有喷涂系统都有若干基本共同点：必须有充足的压缩空气源；有一个储罐或供料罐盛装涂料；有一个装置控制空气与涂料的混合，把雾化云或雾状涂料喷向待涂表面。

自带压力的罐装喷漆可满足上述要求，在给部件和小面积补漆时能取得满意效果。然而，罐装可用的航空涂料品种有限，本章讨论的是通过喷枪施工混合组分的方法。

喷涂设备主要有两种类型。带一体式漆杯的喷枪适合涂装小面积。涂装大面积时，压力供料设备更可取，因为可以供给大量涂料而不必停下重新装杯。额外的好处是喷枪总重更轻，并且能以恒定压力向任意方向喷涂。

供给喷枪的空气必须完全不含水和油，才能在成品上获得最佳效果。气压供给管路中必须装设水分离器以及能去除任何微量油的合适过滤器。这些过滤器和分离器必须定期维护。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 不定式被动式后置定语
**原句**：The process consists of dipping the part to be finished in a tank filled with the finishing material.
**拆解**：主语 The process，谓语 consists of，宾语为动名词短语 dipping the part...；to be finished 为不定式被动式作后置定语修饰 the part；in a tank 为地点状语，filled with the finishing material 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 tank。
**要点**：consist of 后用动名词，不能用不定式。to be finished 表示“尚待涂装的”，与 filled（已经装满的）形成“待处理—已就绪”的对照。

### ② 定语从句 + 动词化名词搭配
**原句**：A material that is too thick has a tendency to pull or rope under the brush.
**拆解**：主语中心词 A material，that is too thick 为定语从句；谓语 has，宾语 a tendency，to pull or rope 为不定式作后置定语说明 tendency 的内容；under the brush 为地点状语。
**要点**：have a tendency to do 是“有……的倾向”，比 will 更客观。pull 与 rope 在此都作不及物动词，形象地描述稠漆被刷子拖成丝、成缕。

### ③ which 指代整句
**原句**：Spraying is used to cover large surfaces with a uniform layer of material, which results in the most cost effective method of application.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 Spraying is used to cover...；with a uniform layer of material 为方式状语；逗号后 which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词不是 material，而是前面“用均匀薄层覆盖大面积”这件事。
**要点**：which 指代整句时谓语用单数 results。若误判先行词为 material，就会译成“材料导致最经济的方法”，逻辑不通——判断先行词要看语义而非位置。

### ④ there be 结构 + 三项并列 + 分词后置定语
**原句**：There must be an adequate source of compressed air, a reservoir or feed tank to hold a supply of the finishing material, and a device for controlling the combination of the air and finishing material ejected in an atomized cloud or spray against the surface to be coated.
**拆解**：There must be 引出三项并列主语：an adequate source of compressed air、a reservoir or feed tank、a device；to hold a supply... 为不定式作后置定语修饰 tank；for controlling... 为介词短语修饰 device；ejected in an atomized cloud or spray 为过去分词短语修饰 the combination；against the surface to be coated 为方向状语。
**要点**：三项并列的主语都很长，中间还有 or 构成的同位并列（reservoir or feed tank），读时先抓住三个中心词 source / tank / device。ejected 的逻辑主语是 the air and finishing material 这一组合。

### ⑤ since 原因从句 + 介词 + 动名词
**原句**：When large areas are painted, pressure-feed equipment is more desirable since a large supply of finishing material can be applied without the interruption of having to stop and refill a paint container.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句（被动 are painted）；主句为主系表结构 pressure-feed equipment is more desirable；since 引导原因状语从句，从句为被动 can be applied；without the interruption of having to stop and refill... 为介词短语作方式状语，of 后接动名词短语。
**要点**：since 在此是“因为”，不是“自从”。the interruption of having to... 是“不得不……这种中断”，of 后必须用动名词，不能用不定式。
