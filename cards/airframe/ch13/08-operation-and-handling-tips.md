# Operation & Handling Tips

> 机体 Ch 13 · Operation & Handling Tips · 原文 PDF 第 821 页

## 1. 原文

Aircraft tires experience longer life if operated in a manner to conserve wear and minimize damage. The most important factor impinging on tire performance and wear, as well as resistance to damage is proper inflation. Always inflate tires to the specified level before flight for maximum performance and minimal damage. An improperly inflated tire has increased potential to fail upon landing due to the high impact loads experienced. The following sections include other suggestions that can extend the life and the investment made in aircraft tires.

### Taxiing

Needless tire damage and excessive wear can be prevented by proper handling of the aircraft during taxi. Most of the gross weight of an aircraft is on the main landing gear wheels. Aircraft tires are designed and inflated to absorb the shock of landing by deflection of the sidewalls two to three times as much as that found on an automobile tire. While this enables the tire to handle heavy loads, it also causes more working of the tread and produces scuffing action along the outer edges of the tread that results in more rapid wear. It also leaves the tire more prone to damage as the tread compound opens during this flexing.

An aircraft tire that strikes a chuck hole, a stone, or some other foreign object is more likely to sustain a cut, snag, or bruise than an automobile tire due to its more flexible nature. There is also increased risk for internal tire injury when a tire leaves the paved surface of the taxi way. These incidents should be avoided. Dual or multiple wheel main gear should be operated so that all tires remain on the paved surface so the weight of the aircraft is evenly distributed between the tires. When backing an aircraft on a ramp for parking, care should be taken to stop the aircraft before the main wheels roll off of the paved surface.

Taxiing for long distances or at high speeds increase the temperature of aircraft tires. This makes them more susceptible to wear and damage. Short taxi distances at moderate speeds are recommended. Caution should also be used to prevent riding the brakes while taxiing, which adds unnecessary heat to the tires.

### Braking & Pivoting

Heavy use of aircraft brakes introduces heat into the tires. Sharp radius turns do the same and increase tread abrasion and side loads on the tire. Plan ahead to allow the aircraft to slow without heavy braking and make large radius turns to avoid these conditions. Objects under a tire are ground into the tread during a pivot. Since many aircraft are primarily maneuvered on the ground via differential braking, efforts should be made to always keep the inside wheel moving during a turn rather than pivoting the aircraft with a locked brake around a fixed main wheel tire.

### Landing Field & Hangar Floor Condition

One of the main contributions made to the welfare of aircraft tires is good upkeep of airport runway and taxiway surfaces, as well as all ramp areas and hangar floors. While the technician has little input into runway and taxiway surface upkeep, known defects in the paved surfaces can be avoided and rough surfaces can be negotiated at slower than normal speeds to minimize tire damage. Ramps and hangar floors should be kept free of all foreign objects that may cause tire damage. This requires continuous diligence on the part of all aviation personnel. Do not ignore foreign object damage (FOD). When discovered, action must be taken to remove it. While FOD to engines and propellers gains significant attention, much damage to tires is avoidable if ramp areas and hangar floors are kept clean.

### Takeoffs & Landings

Aircraft tires are under severe strain during takeoff and landing. Under normal conditions, with proper control and maintenance of the tires, they are able to withstand these stresses and perform as designed.

Most tire failures occur during takeoff which can be extremely dangerous. Tire damage on takeoff is often the result of running over some foreign object. Thorough preflight inspection of the tires and wheels, as well as maintenance of hangar and ramp surfaces free of foreign objects, are keys to prevention of takeoff tire failure. A flat spot caused on the way to the runway may lead to tire failure during takeoff. Heavy braking during aborted takeoffs is also a common cause of takeoff tire failure.

Tire failure upon landing can have several causes. Landing with the brakes on is one. This is mitigated on aircraft with anti-skid systems but can occur on other aircraft. Other errors in judgment, such as landing too far down the runway and having to apply the brakes heavily, can cause overheating or skidding. This can lead to flat-spotting the tires or blow out.

### Hydroplaning

Skidding on a wet, icy, or dry runway is accompanied by the threat of tire failure due to heat build-up and rapid tire wear damage. Hydroplaning on a wet runway may be overlooked as a damaging condition for a tire. Water building up in front of the tire provides a surface for the tire to run on and contact with the runway surface is lost. This is known as dynamic hydroplaning. Steering ability and braking action is also lost. A skid results if the brakes are applied and held.

Viscous hydroplaning occurs on runways with a thin film of water that mixes with contaminants to cause an extremely slick condition. This can also happen on a very smooth runway surface. A tire with a locked brake during viscous hydroplaning can form an area of reverted rubber or skid burn in the tread. While the tire may continue in service if the damage is not too severe, it can be cause for removal if the reinforcing tread or protector ply is penetrated. The same damage can occur while skidding on ice.

Modern runways are designed to drain water rapidly and provide good traction for tires in wet conditions. A compromise exists in that crosscut runways and textured runway surfaces cause tires to wear at a greater rate than a smooth runway. A smooth landing is of great benefit to any tire. For the most part, aircraft tire handling and care is the responsibility of the pilot; however, the technician benefits from knowing the causes of tire failure and communicating this knowledge to the flight crew so that operating procedures can be modified to avoid those causes.

## 2. 中文翻译

如果以节省磨损、减少损伤的方式操作，航空轮胎的寿命会更长。影响轮胎性能、磨损以及抗损伤能力的最重要因素是充气压力是否正确。飞行前务必将轮胎充至规定压力，以获得最佳性能与最小损伤。充气不当的轮胎在着陆时要承受较大的冲击载荷，因而失效的可能性更高。以下几节给出其他有助于延长航空轮胎使用寿命、保住这项投资价值的建议。

### 滑行

滑行时正确操纵飞机，可以避免不必要的轮胎损伤与过度磨损。飞机的大部分全重落在主起落架机轮上。航空轮胎通过胎侧的变形来吸收着陆冲击，其变形量是汽车轮胎的两到三倍，设计时就按此设定充气压力。这虽然使轮胎能够承受重载，却也让胎面产生更多变形，并在胎面外缘形成擦蹭，导致更快的磨损。而且挠曲时胎面胶料会张开，使轮胎更易受损。

航空轮胎撞上坑洞、石块或其他外来物时，比汽车轮胎更容易被割伤、钩伤或撞伤，因为它更柔软。轮胎一旦驶离滑行道的铺筑面，内部损伤的风险也会增加。这些情况都应当避免。双轮或多轮主起落架应操纵到所有轮胎都留在铺筑面上，使飞机重量在各轮胎之间均匀分配。在机坪上倒车停放时，应注意在主轮滚出铺筑面之前把飞机停住。

长距离或高速滑行会升高航空轮胎的温度，使其更易磨损和受损。建议采用中速、短距离滑行。还应注意避免滑行时带刹车，那会给轮胎带来不必要的热量。

### 刹车与原地转弯

频繁使用刹车会把热量带入轮胎。小半径转弯同样如此，而且会加剧胎面磨损与轮胎的侧向载荷。应提前规划，让飞机在不重踩刹车的情况下减速，并做大半径转弯来避免上述情况。原地转弯时，轮胎下的物体被碾进胎面。由于许多飞机在地面主要靠差动刹车操纵，应尽量在转弯时保持内侧机轮转动，而不是锁住一个刹车、以固定的主轮为轴让飞机原地转过去。

### 着陆场地与机库地坪状况

对航空轮胎状况贡献最大的因素之一，是机场跑道、滑行道表面以及所有机坪区域和机库地坪的良好维护。技术人员虽然对跑道和滑行道表面的维护插不上手，但可以避开铺筑面上已知的缺陷，并在粗糙表面上以低于正常的速度通过，从而减少轮胎损伤。机坪与机库地坪应保持没有任何可能损伤轮胎的外来物。这需要所有航空从业人员持续用心。不要忽视外来物损伤（FOD）。一旦发现，就必须采取措施清除。虽然发动机和螺旋桨的 FOD 受到高度重视，但只要保持机坪区域和机库地坪清洁，许多轮胎损伤本来是可以避免的。

### 起飞与着陆

航空轮胎在起飞和着陆时承受很大的应力。在正常条件下，只要对轮胎进行正确的操纵与维护，它们就能承受这些应力并按设计发挥作用。

大多数轮胎失效发生在起飞阶段，而这可能极其危险。起飞时的轮胎损伤常常是碾过某个外来物造成的。彻底的飞行前轮胎和机轮检查，以及保持机库和机坪表面没有外来物，是防止起飞轮胎失效的关键。在滑往跑道的途中造成的胎面平点，可能导致起飞时轮胎失效。中断起飞时的重刹车也是起飞轮胎失效的常见原因。

着陆时的轮胎失效可能有多种原因。带着刹车着陆是其中之一。装有防滑系统的飞机可以缓解这一问题，但其他飞机仍可能发生。其他判断失误，例如着陆点过于靠跑道末端、不得不重踩刹车，也会造成过热或打滑。这可能导致轮胎出现平点或爆胎。

### 滑水

在潮湿、结冰或干燥的跑道上打滑，都伴随着因热量积聚和轮胎快速磨损而失效的风险。湿跑道上的滑水对轮胎的损伤作用可能被忽视。轮胎前方积聚的水为轮胎提供了一个滑行的表面，轮胎与跑道表面失去了接触。这称为动态滑水。转向能力和刹车作用也会一同丧失。如果踩下并保持刹车，就会发生拖胎。

粘性滑水发生在覆有薄薄一层水的跑道上，水与污染物混合，形成极其光滑的状态。在非常光滑的跑道表面上也可能发生这种情况。粘性滑水过程中刹车锁死的轮胎，会在胎面形成一块返原橡胶或拖胎烧蚀区。如果损伤不太严重，轮胎或许可以继续使用；但如果增强胎面或保护层被穿透，就可能需要拆下。在冰面上拖滑也会造成同样的损伤。

现代跑道设计得能快速排水，并在潮湿条件下为轮胎提供良好的附着力。这里存在一个折中：横向刻槽跑道和有纹理的跑道表面会使轮胎比光滑跑道磨损得更快。平稳着陆对任何轮胎都大有好处。总体而言，航空轮胎的操纵与保养是飞行员的职责；不过，技术人员了解轮胎失效的原因、并把这种知识传达给机组，以便调整操作程序、避开这些原因，也是一件有益的事。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词短语后置修饰主语中心词

**原句**：The most important factor impinging on tire performance and wear, as well as resistance to damage is proper inflation.

**拆解**：主语是 `The most important factor`，`impinging on …` 是现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 `factor`；`as well as resistance to damage` 用插入方式与 `tire performance and wear` 并列；谓语是 `is`，表语是 `proper inflation`。分词短语把主谓隔得很远，阅读时要先把修饰成分括起来。

**要点**：技术英语常用「名词 + 长分词短语 + be + 表语」的句式下定义，先抓主语和谓语，再回头处理中间的定语。

### ② 倍数比较：times as much as + that

**原句**：Aircraft tires are designed and inflated to absorb the shock of landing by deflection of the sidewalls two to three times as much as that found on an automobile tire.

**拆解**：主干是 `Aircraft tires are designed and inflated to absorb the shock`；`by deflection of the sidewalls` 说明吸收冲击的手段；`two to three times as much as that found on an automobile tire` 是倍数比较状语，`that` 替代前面的 `deflection`，`found on …` 是过去分词短语修饰 `that`。

**要点**：`A is N times as much as B` 译作「A 是 B 的 N 倍」；比较从句中用 `that`/`those` 替代前面出现过的名词，避免重复。

### ③ 两个 so that 的不同作用

**原句**：Dual or multiple wheel main gear should be operated so that all tires remain on the paved surface so the weight of the aircraft is evenly distributed between the tires.

**拆解**：主句是 `Dual or multiple wheel main gear should be operated`；第一个 `so that` 引导目的状语从句（「以便所有轮胎都留在铺筑面上」）；第二个 `so`（省略 that）引导结果状语从句（「这样重量就均匀分配」）。两个从句层层递进。

**要点**：`so that` 表目的，`so (that)` 也可表结果，判断依据是语义上是「为了」还是「于是」。

### ④ 动名词短语作主语

**原句**：Taxiing for long distances or at high speeds increase the temperature of aircraft tires.

**拆解**：主语是动名词短语 `Taxiing for long distances or at high speeds`（`or` 连接两个状语），谓语是 `increase`。原句用 `increase` 而非 `increases`，是把 `taxiing` 当作动作概念而非单数名词处理。

**要点**：动名词作主语时谓语一般用单数，但原文这里按「长距离滑行或高速滑行」的并列意味用了复数形式，属可接受的用法。

### ⑤ 时间状语从句 + 被动语态 + 介词短语

**原句**：When backing an aircraft on a ramp for parking, care should be taken to stop the aircraft before the main wheels roll off of the paved surface.

**拆解**：`When backing …` 是省略了主语和 be 的时间状语从句（完整形式为 When you are backing …）；主句 `care should be taken` 为被动，`to stop the aircraft` 作目的状语；`before the main wheels roll off of the paved surface` 是时间状语从句。

**要点**：`When + 现在分词` 是技术手册中常见的时间状语省略结构，逻辑主语与主句一致时才可省略。

### ⑥ while 让步 + if 条件

**原句**：While FOD to engines and propellers gains significant attention, much damage to tires is avoidable if ramp areas and hangar floors are kept clean.

**拆解**：`While …` 引导让步状语从句（「虽然……」），`FOD to engines and propellers` 是主语；主句 `much damage to tires is avoidable` 是主系表结构；`if ramp areas and hangar floors are kept clean` 是条件状语从句，其中 `are kept clean` 为被动。

**要点**：`while` 在句首常表「虽然」，与表时间的「当……时」要靠主从句的语义对比来判断。
