# Cotton-Covered Aircraft

> 机体 Ch 03 · Cotton-Covered Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 162 页

## 1. 原文

You may encounter a cotton fabric-covered aircraft. In addition to other airworthiness criterion, the condition of the fabric under the finished surface is paramount as the cotton can deteriorate even while the aircraft is stored in a hangar. Inspection, in accordance with the manufacturer maintenance manual or AC 43.13-1, should be diligent. If the cotton covering is found to be airworthy, repairs to the fabric can be made under those specifications. This includes sewn-in and doped-in patches, as well as sewn-in and doped-in panel repairs. Due to the very limited number of airworthy aircraft that may still be covered with cotton, this handbook does not cover specific information on re-covering with cotton or cotton fabric maintenance and repair procedures. Refer to AC 43.13-1, Chapter 2, Fabric Covering, which thoroughly References to fiberglass surfaces in aircraft covering STCs, AC 43.13-1, and other maintenance literature address techniques for finishing and maintaining this kind of surface. However, this is typically limited to fiberglass radomes and fiberglass reinforced plywood surfaces and parts that are still in service. Use of dope-based processes on fiberglass is well established. Repair and apply coatings and finishes on fiberglass in accordance with manufacturer data, STC instructions, or AC 43.13-1 acceptable practices. Mildew, moisture, chemicals, or acids have no effect on glass fabric when used as a structure material. For more information on glass fabric, refer to AC 43.13-1(as revised).

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 介词短语 + 系表结构 + as 原因从句 + even while 让步
**原句**：In addition to other airworthiness criterion, the condition of the fabric under the finished surface is paramount as the cotton can deteriorate even while the aircraft is stored in a hangar.
**拆解**：In addition to other airworthiness criterion 为补充状语；主语 the condition of the fabric under the finished surface；系动词 is；表语 paramount；as 引导原因状语从句，从句中 even while the aircraft is stored in a hangar 为让步性的时间状语从句（被动语态）。
**要点**：as 在这里是“因为”，不是“当……时”。even while 叠加了让步意味，译作“即使……也”。主句的 under the finished surface 是后置定语修饰 the fabric，不是状语，说明“精整层下面的”蒙布。

### ② 插入语分隔主语与谓语 + 情态被动
**原句**：Inspection, in accordance with the manufacturer maintenance manual or AC 43.13-1, should be diligent.
**拆解**：主语 Inspection；逗号之间的 in accordance with... 为插入语（方式状语），说明检查的依据；谓语 should be diligent（情态动词 + 系表）。
**要点**：插入语把主语和谓语隔开，should be 不能跟着 AC 43.13-1 走。in accordance with 意为“按照”，其宾语有两个并列项：the manufacturer maintenance manual 和 AC 43.13-1。diligent 是形容词作表语，不是副词。

### ③ 条件从句 + find sth. to be + 被动
**原句**：If the cotton covering is found to be airworthy, repairs to the fabric can be made under those specifications.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，从句为被动 is found，后接不定式 to be airworthy 作主语补足语；主句 repairs to the fabric can be made 为被动语态，under those specifications 为方式/依据状语。
**要点**：find sth. to be + adj. 变被动后成为 sth. is found to be + adj.，to be 不能省。those specifications 指代前句所说的制造厂手册或 AC 43.13-1 中的规范，翻译时要补出“这些规范”。

### ④ due to 短语 + 定语从句 + 并列介词宾语
**原句**：Due to the very limited number of airworthy aircraft that may still be covered with cotton, this handbook does not cover specific information on re-covering with cotton or cotton fabric maintenance and repair procedures.
**拆解**：Due to... 为原因状语，其中 the very limited number of airworthy aircraft 后接 that may still be covered with cotton 定语从句（被动）；主句 this handbook does not cover specific information；on 后接并列宾语 re-covering with cotton 和 cotton fabric maintenance and repair procedures。
**要点**：due to 是介词短语，后接名词性成分，不能接完整从句。or 连接的是 on 的两个宾语，不是两个句子；maintenance and repair procedures 是“维护与修理程序”一个整体概念。

### ⑤ 并列主语 + when + 过去分词状语（悬垂结构）
**原句**：Mildew, moisture, chemicals, or acids have no effect on glass fabric when used as a structure material.
**拆解**：主语为四项并列 Mildew, moisture, chemicals, or acids；谓语 have no effect on；宾语 glass fabric；when used as a structure material 为省略的时间状语从句（when it is used...），过去分词 used 的逻辑主语是 glass fabric。
**要点**：used 的逻辑主语不是前面的“霉、湿气”等，而是 glass fabric——分词状语的逻辑主语要与主句的某个名词对应，这里对应宾语。have no effect on 是固定搭配，effect 前不用冠词。
