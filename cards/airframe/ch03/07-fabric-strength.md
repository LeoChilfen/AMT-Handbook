# Fabric Strength

> 机体 Ch 03 · Fabric Strength · 原文 PDF 第 146 页

## 1. 原文

Deterioration of the strength of the present fabric covering is the most common reason to re-cover an aircraft. The strength of fabric coverings must be determined at every 100-hour and annual inspection. Minimum fabric breaking strength is used to determine if an aircraft requires re-covering.

Fabric strength is a major factor in the airworthiness of an aircraft. Fabric is considered to be airworthy until it deteriorates to a breaking strength less than 70 percent of the strength of the new fabric required for the aircraft. For example, if an aircraft was certificated with Grade-A cotton fabric that has a new breaking strength of 80 pounds, it becomes unairworthy when the fabric strength falls to 56 pounds, which is 70 percent of 80 pounds. If polyester fabric, which has a higher new breaking strength, is used to re-cover this same aircraft, it would also need to exceed 56 pounds breaking strength to remain airworthy.

In general, an aircraft is certified with a certain fabric based on its wing loading and its never exceed speed (V NE). The higher the wing loading and VNE, the stronger the fabric must be. On aircraft with wing loading of 9 pounds per square foot and over, or a V NE of 160 miles per hour (mph) or higher, fabric equaling or exceeding the strength of Grade A cotton is required. This means the new fabric breaking strength must be at least 80 pounds and the minimum fabric breaking strength at which the aircraft becomes unairworthy is 56 pounds.

On aircraft with wing loading of 9 pounds per square foot or less, or a VNE of 160 mph or less, fabric equaling or exceeding the strength of intermediate grade cotton is required. This means the new fabric breaking strength must be at least 65 pounds and the minimum fabric breaking strength at which the aircraft becomes unairworthy is 46 pounds.

Lighter weight fabric may be found to have been certified on gliders or sailplanes and may be used on many uncertificated aircraft or aircraft in the Light Sport Aircraft (LSA) category. For aircraft with wing loading less than 8 pounds per square foot or less, or VNE of 135 mph or less, the fabric is considered unairworthy when the breaking strength has deteriorated to below 35 pounds (new minimum strength of 50 pounds). ### How Fabric Breaking Strength is Determined

Manufacturer’s instructions should always be consulted first for fabric strength inspection methodology. These instructions are approved data and may not require removal of a test strip to determine airworthiness of the fabric. In some cases, the manufacturer’s information does not include any fabric inspection methods. It may refer the IA to AC 43.13-1, Chapter 2, Fabric Covering, which contains the approved FAA test strip method for breaking strength.

The test strip method for the breaking strength of aircraft covering fabrics uses standards published by the American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM) for the testing of various materials. Breaking strength is determined by cutting a 1¼ inch by 4–6 inch strip of fabric from the aircraft covering. This sample should be taken from an area that is exposed to the elements—usually an upper surface. It is also wise to take the sample from an area that has a dark colored finish since this has absorbed more of the sun’s UV rays and degraded faster. All coatings are then removed and the edges raveled to leave a 1-inch width. One end of the strip is clamped into a secured clamp and the other end is clamped such that a suitable container may be suspended from it. Weight is added to the container until the fabric breaks. The breaking strength of the fabric is equal to the weight of the lower clamp, the container, and the weight added to it. If the breaking strength is still in question, a sample should be sent to a qualified testing laboratory and breaking strength tests made in accordance with ASTM publication D5035.

Note that the fabric test strip must have all coatings removed from it for the test. Soaking and cleaning the test strip in methyl ethyl ketone (MEK) usually removes all the coatings.

Properly installed and maintained polyester fabric should give years of service before appreciable fabric strength degradation occurs. Aircraft owners often prefer not to have test strips cut out of the fabric, especially when the aircraft or the fabric covering is relatively new, because removal of a test strip damages the integrity of an airworthy component if the fabric passes. The test strip area then must be repaired, costing time and money. To avoid cutting a strip out of airworthy fabric, the IA makes a decision based on knowledge, experience, and available nondestructive techniques as to whether removal of a test strip is warranted to ensure that the aircraft can be returned to service.

An aircraft made airworthy under an STC is subject to the instructions for continued airworthiness in that STC. Most STCs refer to AC 43.13-1 for inspection methodology. Poly-Fiber™ and Ceconite™ re-covering process STCs contain their own instructions and techniques for determining fabric strength and airworthiness. Therefore, an aircraft covered under those STCs may be inspected in accordance with this information. In most cases, the aircraft can be approved for return to service without cutting a strip from the fabric covering.

The procedures in the Poly-Fiber™ and Ceconite™ STCs outlined in the following paragraphs are useful when inspecting any fabric covered aircraft as they add to the information gathered by the IA to determine the condition of the fabric. However, following these procedures alone on aircraft not re-covered under these STCs does not make the aircraft airworthy. The IA must add their own knowledge, experience, and judgment to make a final determination of the strength of the fabric and whether it is airworthy.

Exposure to UV radiation appreciably reduces the strength of polyester fabric and forms the basis of the Poly-Fiber™ and Ceconite™ fabric evaluation process. All approved covering systems utilize fill coats applied to the fabric to protect it from UV . If installed according to the STC, these coatings should be sufficient to protect the fabric from the sun and should last indefinitely. Therefore, most of the evaluation of the strength of the fabric is actually an evaluation of the condition of its protective coating(s).

Upon a close visual inspection, the fabric coating(s) should be consistent, contain no cracks, and be flexible, not brittle. Pushing hard against the fabric with a knuckle should not damage the coating(s). It is recommended the inspector check in several areas, especially those most exposed to the sun. Coatings that pass this test can move to a simple test that determines whether or not UV light is passing through the coatings.

This test is based on the assumption that if visible light passes through the fabric coatings, then UV light can also. To verify whether or not visible light passes through the fabric coating, remove an inspection panel from the wing, fuselage, or empennage. Have someone hold an illuminated 60-watt lamp one foot away from the exterior of the fabric. No light should be visible through the fabric. If no light is visible, the fabric has not been weakened by UV rays and can be assumed to be airworthy. There is no need to perform the fabric strip strength test. If light is visible through the coatings, further investigation is required.

### Fabric Testing Devices

Mechanical devices used to test fabric by pressing against or piercing the finished fabric are not FAA approved and are used at the discretion of the FAA-certificated mechanic to form an opinion on the general fabric condition. Punch test accuracy depends on the individual device calibration, total coating thickness, brittleness, and types of coatings and fabric. If the fabric tests in the lower breaking strength range with the mechanical punch tester or if the overall fabric cover conditions are poor, then more accurate field tests may be made.

The test should be performed on exposed fabric where there is a crack or chip in the coatings. If there is no crack or chip, coatings should be removed to expose the fabric wherever the test is to be done.

The Maule punch tester, a spring-loaded device with its scale calibrated in breaking strength, tests fabric strength by pressing against it while the fabric is still on the aircraft. It roughly equates strength in pounds per square inch (psi) of resistance to breaking strength. The tester is pushed squarely against the fabric until the scale reads the amount of maximum allowable degradation. If the tester does not puncture the fabric, it may be considered airworthy. Punctures near the breaking strength should be followed with further testing, specifically the strip breaking strength test described above. Usually, a puncture indicates the fabric is in need of replacement.

A second type of punch tester, the Seyboth, is not as popular as the Maule because it punctures a small hole in the fabric when the mechanic pushes the shoulder of the testing unit against the fabric. A pin with a color-coded calibrated scale protrudes from the top of the tester and the mechanic reads this scale to determine fabric strength. Since this device requires a repair regardless of the strength of the fabric indicated, it is not widely used.

Seyboth and Maule fabric strength testers designed for cotton-and linen-covered aircraft, not to be used on modern Dacron fabrics. Mechanical devices, combined with other information and experience, help the FAA-certificated mechanic judge the strength of the fabric.

## 2. 中文翻译

现有蒙布强度下降，是重新蒙布飞机最常见的原因。蒙布的强度必须在每 100 小时检查和年度检查时测定。蒙布的最小断裂强度用于判定飞机是否需要重新蒙布。

蒙布强度是飞机适航性的一个主要因素。在蒙布强度下降到低于该飞机所需新蒙布强度的 70% 之前，蒙布都被视为适航。例如，如果一架飞机取证时使用的是新布断裂强度为 80 磅的 A 级棉布，那么当蒙布强度降到 56 磅（即 80 磅的 70%）时，飞机就不适航了。如果用新布断裂强度更高的聚酯蒙布来重新蒙布这同一架飞机，它同样需要超过 56 磅的断裂强度才能保持适航。

一般而言，飞机取证时采用某种蒙布，依据的是其翼载和最大平飞速度（VNE）。翼载和 VNE 越高，蒙布就必须越强。对于翼载达到或超过每平方英尺 9 磅，或 VNE 达到或超过 160 英里/小时（mph）的飞机，要求蒙布的强度等于或超过 A 级棉布。这意味着新蒙布的断裂强度至少为 80 磅，而飞机变得不适航的最小蒙布断裂强度为 56 磅。

对于翼载为每平方英尺 9 磅或以下，或 VNE 为 160 mph 或以下的飞机，要求蒙布强度等于或超过中级棉布。这意味着新蒙布的断裂强度至少为 65 磅，而飞机变得不适航的最小蒙布断裂强度为 46 磅。

在滑翔机或翱翔机上，可能见到取证时采用更轻的蒙布；许多未取证的飞机或轻型运动飞机（LSA）类别的飞机也可以使用这种蒙布。对于翼载小于每平方英尺 8 磅或以下，或 VNE 为 135 mph 或以下的飞机，当断裂强度下降到 35 磅以下（新布最小强度为 50 磅）时，蒙布即被视为不适航。图 3-10 汇总了这些参数。

### 蒙布断裂强度如何测定

蒙布强度检查方法应始终首先查阅制造厂说明。这些说明是经批准的资料，可能不要求取下试验条就能判定蒙布是否适航。在某些情况下，制造厂资料不包含任何蒙布检查方法，而可能让 IA 参阅 AC 43.13-1 第 2 章“蒙布”，其中载有 FAA 批准的断裂强度试验条法。

测定飞机蒙布断裂强度的试验条法，采用美国材料与试验协会（ASTM）发布的多种材料试验标准。断裂强度的测定方法是：从飞机蒙布上裁下一条 1¼ 英寸 × 4–6 英寸的蒙布条。试样应取自暴露于环境因素的区域——通常是上表面。明智的做法是还从深色精整层的区域取样，因为深色吸收更多太阳紫外线、老化更快。然后去除所有涂层，并将边缘拆散，留出 1 英寸宽。把布条一端夹在固定夹上，另一端夹住，以便在其上悬挂一个合适的容器。向容器中加配重，直到蒙布断裂。蒙布的断裂强度等于下夹、容器及所加配重的重量之和。如果断裂强度仍有疑问，应把试样送到有资质的试验实验室，按 ASTM 出版物 D5035 进行断裂强度试验。

注意，试验条在试验前必须去除所有涂层。将试验条在甲乙酮（MEK）中浸泡并清洗，通常能去除全部涂层。

安装和维护得当的聚酯蒙布，应在明显强度下降之前使用多年。飞机所有者往往不愿意从蒙布上剪下试验条，尤其是在飞机或蒙布还比较新的时候，因为如果蒙布通过试验，取下试验条就破坏了适航部件的完整性，试验条所在区域随后必须修补，既费时又费钱。为避免从适航蒙布上剪下布条，IA 依据知识、经验和可用的无损检测技术，判断是否有必要取下试验条，以确保飞机可以恢复使用。

依据 STC 取得适航的飞机，须遵守该 STC 中的持续适航说明。多数 STC 引用 AC 43.13-1 作为检查方法。Poly-Fiber™ 和 Ceconite™ 重新蒙布工艺的 STC 载有各自的蒙布强度与适航性判定说明和技术。因此，按这些 STC 蒙布的飞机可依据这些资料进行检查。多数情况下，飞机无需从蒙布上剪下布条即可批准恢复使用。

下文概述的 Poly-Fiber™ 和 Ceconite™ STC 程序，在检查任何蒙布飞机时都有用，因为它们补充了 IA 判定蒙布状况所收集的信息。但是，对并非按这些 STC 重新蒙布的飞机，仅照这些程序做并不能使飞机适航。IA 必须加入自己的知识、经验和判断，最终判定蒙布强度及其是否适航。

紫外线照射会明显降低聚酯蒙布的强度，这也是 Poly-Fiber™ 和 Ceconite™ 蒙布评估方法的基础。所有经批准的蒙布系统都使用涂在蒙布上的填充涂层来保护蒙布免受紫外线。如果按 STC 施工，这些涂层应足以保护蒙布免受日晒，并应能无限期使用。因此，蒙布强度评估的大部分工作，实际上是对其保护涂层状况的评估。

经过仔细目视检查，蒙布涂层应均匀一致、没有裂纹，且柔韧而不脆。用指关节用力顶压蒙布不应损坏涂层。建议检查人员在多个区域检查，尤其是最暴露于日晒的区域。通过此项检查的涂层，可进入一项判断是否有紫外光透过涂层的简单试验。

该试验基于这样的假设：如果可见光能透过蒙布涂层，那么紫外光也能透过。为验证可见光是否透过蒙布涂层，从机翼、机身或尾翼上取下一个检查口盖。让人在蒙布外表面一英尺处举一盏亮的 60 瓦灯。透过蒙布不应看到任何光。如果看不到光，说明蒙布未被紫外线削弱，可认为适航，无需再做蒙布条强度试验。如果有光透过涂层，则需要进一步调查。

### 蒙布测试器具

通过顶压或刺穿已精整蒙布来测试蒙布的机械器具未经 FAA 批准，由持 FAA 证的维修人员自行决定使用，以形成对蒙布总体状况的判断。穿刺测试的准确性取决于各器具的校准、涂层总厚度、脆性以及涂层和蒙布的类型。如果用机械穿刺测试仪测得的蒙布强度处于较低范围，或者蒙布覆盖层总体状况不佳，则应做更准确的现场试验。

试验应在涂层有裂纹或剥落的暴露蒙布上进行。如果没有裂纹或剥落，则应去除涂层，在任何需要做试验的部位露出蒙布。

Maule 穿刺测试仪是一种弹簧加载器具，其刻度按断裂强度标定，在蒙布仍装在飞机上时通过顶压蒙布来测试强度。它大致把每平方英寸磅（psi）的抗力等同于断裂强度。测试仪垂直顶住蒙布，直到刻度读数达到最大允许退化量。如果测试仪没有刺穿蒙布，蒙布可视为适航。接近断裂强度的刺穿应继以进一步测试，特别是上述的布条断裂强度试验。通常，一旦刺穿，就说明蒙布需要更换。

另一种穿刺测试仪 Seyboth 不如 Maule 普及，因为当维修人员把测试器具的肩部顶住蒙布时，它会在蒙布上刺出一个小孔。一根带色标校准刻度的销从测试仪顶部伸出，维修人员读取该刻度以判定蒙布强度。由于这种器具无论所显示的蒙布强度如何都需要修补，因而未被广泛使用。

Seyboth 和 Maule 蒙布强度测试仪是为棉布和亚麻蒙布的飞机设计的，不得用于现代 Dacron 蒙布。机械器具与其他信息和经验结合，可帮助持 FAA 证的维修人员判断蒙布强度。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 名词化主语 + 多层 of 定语 + 不定式定语
**原句**：Deterioration of the strength of the present fabric covering is the most common reason to re-cover an aircraft.
**拆解**：主语中心词 Deterioration + of the strength + of the present fabric covering 两层介词短语逐级限定；谓语 is；表语 the most common reason，后接不定式 to re-cover an aircraft 作定语说明是哪一类原因。
**要点**：三个名词套在一起（deterioration / strength / covering），中文要按“现有蒙布的强度下降”逆序展开。reason 后的不定式是同位性定语，说明“原因的内容”，不是目的状语。

### ② consider ... to be + until 从句 + 比较结构
**原句**：Fabric is considered to be airworthy until it deteriorates to a breaking strength less than 70 percent of the strength of the new fabric required for the aircraft.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 is considered，后接不定式 to be airworthy 作主语补足语；until 引导时间状语从句，从句谓语 deteriorates to，宾语 a breaking strength，less than 70 percent... 为后置比较定语；required for the aircraft 为过去分词短语修饰 the new fabric。
**要点**：consider sth. to be + adj. 变被动后成为 sth. is considered to be + adj.，to be 不能省。less than 70 percent of... 是修饰 strength 的后置定语，不是状语，读时不要断开成“下降到 70% 以下”的独立分句。

### ③ the + 比较级, the + 比较级
**原句**：The higher the wing loading and VNE, the stronger the fabric must be.
**拆解**：前一分句 The higher the wing loading and VNE 为条件（越……），后一分句 the stronger the fabric must be 为结果（就越……）；两个 the 都是副词，分别修饰 higher 和 stronger；must be 后省略了表语 strong 的重复部分。
**要点**：这种句型两个分句都不能独立成句，必须成对出现；语序是“the + 比较级 + 主语 + 谓语”，所以 the stronger 提前后主语 the fabric 紧跟。中文习惯译成“翼载和 VNE 越高，蒙布就必须越强”。

### ④ 条件从句 + 定语从句 + 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：For example, if an aircraft was certificated with Grade-A cotton fabric that has a new breaking strength of 80 pounds, it becomes unairworthy when the fabric strength falls to 56 pounds, which is 70 percent of 80 pounds.
**拆解**：if 引导条件状语从句，其中 Grade-A cotton fabric 后接 that has a new breaking strength of 80 pounds 定语从句；主句 it becomes unairworthy，when 引导时间状语从句；逗号后 which is 70 percent of 80 pounds 为非限制性定语从句，指代 56 pounds。
**要点**：which 前有逗号，说明它补充说明整个前面的数字，不能用 that 替换。56 pounds 与 70 percent of 80 pounds 是同一个量的两种表述，译文用括号“即……”最自然。

### ⑤ 动名词并列作主语
**原句**：Soaking and cleaning the test strip in methyl ethyl ketone (MEK) usually removes all the coatings.
**拆解**：Soaking and cleaning 两个动名词并列作主语（共用宾语 the test strip，in MEK 为方式状语）；谓语 removes 用第三人称单数，与并列主语视为一个整体动作；宾语 all the coatings。
**要点**：两个动名词指同一套动作（浸泡并清洗），所以谓语用单数 removes；若视为两件独立的事则用复数。介词 in 在这里表示“在……中”，不是“用……”，因为 MEK 是浸泡介质。
