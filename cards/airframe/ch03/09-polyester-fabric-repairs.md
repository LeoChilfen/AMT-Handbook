# Polyester Fabric Repairs

> 机体 Ch 03 · Polyester Fabric Repairs · 原文 PDF 第 161 页

## 1. 原文

### Applicable Instructions

Repairs to aircraft fabric coverings are inevitable. Always inspect a damaged area to ensure the damage is confined to the fabric and does not involve the structure below. A technician who needs to make a fabric repair must first identify which approved data was used to install the covering that needs to be repaired. Consult the logbook where an entry and reference to manufacturer data, an STC, or a field approval possibly utilizing practices from AC 43.13-1 should be recorded. The source of approved data for the covering job is the same source of approved data used for a repair.

This section discusses general information concerning repairs to polyester fabric. Thorough instructions for repairs made to cotton covered aircraft can be found in AC 43.13-1. It is the responsibility of the holder of an STC to provide maintenance instructions for the STC alteration in addition to materials specifications required to do the job.

### Repair Considerations

The type of repair performed depends on the extent of the damage and the process under which the fabric was installed. The size of the damaged area is often a reference for whether a patch is sufficient to do the repair or whether a new panel should be installed. Repair size may also dictate the amount of fabric-to-fabric overlap required when patching and whether finishing tapes are required over the patch. Many STC repair procedures do not require finishing tapes. Some repairs in AC 43.13-1 require the use of tape up to six inches wide.

While many cotton fabric repairs involve sewing, nearly all repairs of polyester fabric are made without sewing. It is possible to apply the sewing repair techniques outlined in AC 43.13-1 to polyester fabric, but they were developed primarily for cotton and linen fabrics. STC instructions for repairs to polyester fabric are for cemented repairs which most technicians prefer as they are generally considered easier than sewn repairs. There is no compromise to the strength of the fabric with either method.

Patching or replacing a section of the covering requires prepping the fabric area around the damage where new fabric is to be attached. Procedures vary widely. Dope-based covering systems tend toward stripping off all coatings to cement raw fabric to raw fabric when patching or seaming in a new panel. From this point, the coatings are reapplied and finished as in the original covering process. Some polyurethane-based coating processes require only a scuffing of the topcoat with sandpaper before adhering small patches that are then refinished. Still, other processes may remove the topcoats and cement a patch into the sealer or UV blocking coating. In some repair processes, preshrunk fabric is used and in others, the fabric is shrunk after it is in place. Varying techniques and temperatures for shrinking and gluing the fabric into a repair also exist.

These deviations in procedures underscore the critical nature of identifying and strictly adhering to the correct instructions from the approved data for the fabric covering in need of repair. A patch or panel replacement technique for one covering system could easily create an unairworthy repair if used on fabric installed with a different covering process.

Large section panel repairs use the same proprietary adhesives and techniques and are only found in the instructions for the process used to install the fabric covering. A common technique for replacing any large damaged area is to replace all of the fabric between two adjacent structural members (e.g., two ribs, two longerons, between the forward and rear spars). Note that this is a major repair and carries with it the requirement to file an FAA Form 337.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 适用说明

飞机蒙布的修理不可避免。始终要检查损伤区域，确认损伤仅限于蒙布，未涉及下面的结构。需要进行蒙布修理的技术人员，必须首先确认待修蒙布是用哪份经批准的资料安装的。查阅记录本，其中应记录一条记录，并引用制造厂资料、STC 或可能采用 AC 43.13-1 做法的现场批准。蒙布施工所用经批准资料的来源，也就是修理所用经批准资料的来源。

本节讨论聚酯蒙布修理的一般性资料。棉布蒙布飞机的详细修理说明见 AC 43.13-1。STC 持有人有责任提供该 STC 改装的维修说明，以及完成工作所需的材料规范。

### 修理考虑

所进行的修理类型取决于损伤范围以及蒙布安装时所依据的工艺。损伤区域的大小往往是判断用补片是否足以完成修理、还是应安装新幅面的参照。修理尺寸还可能决定打补片时所需的蒙布与蒙布搭接量，以及补片之上是否需要精整带。许多 STC 的修理程序不要求精整带。AC 43.13-1 中的某些修理要求使用宽达 6 英寸的带子。

许多棉布蒙布修理涉及缝合，而聚酯蒙布的几乎所有修理都不缝合。可以把 AC 43.13-1 中所述的缝合修理技术用于聚酯蒙布，但这些技术主要是为棉布和亚麻蒙布制定的。聚酯蒙布修理的 STC 说明针对的是胶接修理，多数技术人员更愿意采用，因为一般认为它比缝合修理更容易。两种方法都不会损害蒙布强度。

打补片或更换一段蒙布，需要对待粘新蒙布的损伤周围区域进行预处理。各工艺差别很大。涂布漆蒙布系统倾向于在打补片或嵌入新幅面时剥除全部涂层，把裸蒙布胶接到裸蒙布上；此后重新涂敷涂层，并按原蒙布工艺完成精整。某些聚氨酯类涂层工艺只要求用砂纸打磨面漆，然后粘上小补片并重新精整。还有一些工艺可能去除面漆，把补片胶接到封闭漆或紫外线阻挡涂层中。某些修理工艺使用预收缩的蒙布，另一些则在蒙布就位后再收缩。收缩和胶接蒙布入修理部位的技术与温度也各不相同。

这些程序上的差异，凸显了确认并严格遵守待修蒙布所依据的经批准资料中正确说明的关键性。把一种蒙布系统的补片或换幅技术用到以另一种蒙布工艺安装的蒙布上，很容易造成不适航的修理。

大面积换幅修理使用同样的专有胶粘剂和技术，只出现在安装该蒙布所用工艺的说明中。更换任何大块损伤区域的常用做法，是更换相邻两个结构件之间的全部蒙布（例如两根翼肋之间、两根长桁之间、前梁与后梁之间）。注意，这属于重大修理，随之要求提交 FAA 337 表。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 祈使句 + 目的不定式 + ensure 的并列宾语从句
**原句**：Always inspect a damaged area to ensure the damage is confined to the fabric and does not involve the structure below.
**拆解**：Always inspect a damaged area 为祈使句；to ensure... 为目的状语；ensure 后接省略 that 的宾语从句，从句主语 the damage，并列谓语 is confined（被动）和 does not involve，共用同一主语。
**要点**：ensure 后的 that 常省略，不能因此把 the damage 看成主句宾语。and 连接的第二个谓语用助动词 does 否定，与前面的 is confined 保持并列；below 作后置定语修饰 the structure，不是副词状语。

### ② 定语从句 + 宾语从句 + 被动语态
**原句**：A technician who needs to make a fabric repair must first identify which approved data was used to install the covering that needs to be repaired.
**拆解**：主语 A technician + who needs to make a fabric repair 定语从句；谓语 must first identify；宾语为 which approved data was used to install the covering... 宾语从句（被动语态 was used）；covering 后接 that needs to be repaired 定语从句。
**要点**：identify 后的 which 引导宾语从句，从句本身是完整的被动句；不能把 which 当成定语从句的关系词。that needs to be repaired 用被动不定式，因为蒙布是“被修理”的对象。

### ③ while 引导的对比 + 被动语态
**原句**：While many cotton fabric repairs involve sewing, nearly all repairs of polyester fabric are made without sewing.
**拆解**：While 引导对比状语从句（许多棉布修理要缝合），主句 nearly all repairs of polyester fabric are made（被动）without sewing 为方式状语。
**要点**：此处的 while 表对比而非时间，相当于“而、然而”。主句用被动 are made，施动者（技术人员）被省略；without sewing 是介词短语，与从句的 involve sewing 形成对照。

### ④ 动名词并列作介词宾语 + 长定语链
**原句**：These deviations in procedures underscore the critical nature of identifying and strictly adhering to the correct instructions from the approved data for the fabric covering in need of repair.
**拆解**：主语 These deviations in procedures；谓语 underscore；宾语 the critical nature，of 后接并列动名词 identifying 和 adhering to（共用宾语 the correct instructions），instructions 又带 from the approved data 和 for the fabric covering in need of repair 两层后置定语。
**要点**：adhere to 的 to 是介词，所以后面接动名词 adhering，与 identifying 并列共用一个 of。in need of repair 是固定短语作后置定语，修饰 covering，不是状语；整句理解为“（确认并遵守）……这件事的关键性”。

### ⑤ 不定式作表语
**原句**：A common technique for replacing any large damaged area is to replace all of the fabric between two adjacent structural members (e.g., two ribs, two longerons, between the forward and rear spars).
**拆解**：主语 A common technique + for replacing any large damaged area 后置定语；系动词 is；表语为不定式 to replace all of the fabric...，between two adjacent structural members 为地点状语，括号内为举例。
**要点**：主语是 technique（方法），表语用不定式说明该方法“就是做……”，不能用动名词替代而不改变语气。for replacing 中的 for 表用途，与表语不定式 to replace 形成“方法—内容”的呼应。
