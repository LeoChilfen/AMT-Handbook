# Propeller Deice System

> 机体 Ch 15 · Propeller Deice System · 原文 PDF 第 902 页

## 1. 原文

The formation of ice on the propeller leading edges, cuffs, and spinner reduces the efficiency of the powerplant system. Deice systems using electrical heating elements and systems using chemical deicing fluid are used.

### Electrothermal Propeller Device System

Many propellers are deiced by an electrically heated boot on each blade. The boot, firmly cemented in place, receives current from a slip ring and brush assembly on the spinner bulkhead. The slip ring transmits current to the deice boot. The centrifugal force of the spinning propeller and air blast breaks the ice particles loose from the heated blades.

On one aircraft model, the boots are heated in a preset sequence, which is an automatic function controlled by a timer. This sequence is as follows: 30 seconds for the right prop outer elements; 30 seconds for the right prop inner elements; 30 seconds for the left prop outer elements; and, 30 seconds for the left prop inner elements. Once the system is turned on for automatic is activated, it cycles continuously. A manual bypass of the timer is incorporated.

### Chemical Propeller Deice

Some aircraft models, especially single-engine GA aircraft, use a chemical deicing system for the propellers. Ice usually appears on the propeller before it forms on the wing. The glycol-based fluid is metered from a tank by a small electrically driven pump through a microfilter to the slinger rings on the prop hub. The propeller system can be a stand-alone system, or it can be part of a chemical wing and stabilizer deicing system such as the TKS™ weeping system.

## 2. 中文翻译

螺旋桨前缘、桨根套（cuff）和桨毂整流罩（spinner）上结冰会降低动力装置系统的效率。除冰采用电加热元件式系统和化学除冰液式系统。

### 电热式螺旋桨除冰系统

许多螺旋桨的除冰，是在每个桨叶上装一条电加热除冰带。该除冰带牢固地用胶粘就位，通过桨毂隔板上的滑环与电刷组件获得电流。滑环把电流输送给除冰带。旋转螺旋桨的离心力和气流冲刷使冰粒从加热的桨叶上脱落。

在某一机型上，各除冰带按预定顺序加热，这是由定时器控制的自动功能。该顺序为：右桨外侧元件 30 秒；右桨内侧元件 30 秒；左桨外侧元件 30 秒；左桨内侧元件 30 秒。系统一经接通自动模式，便持续循环。系统中还设有定时器的手动旁通。

### 化学式螺旋桨除冰

有些机型，尤其是单发一般航空飞机，对螺旋桨采用化学除冰系统。冰通常先在螺旋桨上出现，而后才在机翼上形成。乙二醇基除冰液由一台小型电动泵从储液箱定量抽出，经微滤器送到桨毂上的甩液环。该螺旋桨系统可以是独立系统，也可以是化学式机翼与安定面除冰系统（如 TKS™ 渗流系统）的组成部分。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 过去分词短语夹在主语与谓语之间
**原句**：The boot, firmly cemented in place, receives current from a slip ring and brush assembly on the spinner bulkhead.
**拆解**：主干 The boot receives current；两个逗号之间的 firmly cemented in place 是过去分词短语作后置定语（= which is cemented in place），被移到谓语前作插入成分；from a slip ring and brush assembly 为来源状语，on the spinner bulkhead 修饰该组件。
**要点**：去掉插入成分后主干仍完整，这是识别插入定语的最快方法。cemented 用过去分词而非现在分词，因为除冰带是“被粘”的，逻辑主语是动作承受者。in place 意为“在位、就位”，不是“代替”。

### ② break sth. + 形容词作宾语补足语
**原句**：The centrifugal force of the spinning propeller and air blast breaks the ice particles loose from the heated blades.
**拆解**：主语是 The centrifugal force... and air blast 两部分并列，谓语用单数 breaks，与 air blast 一致；the ice particles 是宾语，loose 为形容词作宾语补足语；from the heated blades 说明脱落位置。
**要点**：break sth. loose from 是固定搭配“把……从……上弄松脱落”，loose 是补语而非副词。主谓一致按就近原则取单数，这类并列主语容易被误判成复数。

### ③ which 引导非限制性定语从句
**原句**：On one aircraft model, the boots are heated in a preset sequence, which is an automatic function controlled by a timer.
**拆解**：主句 the boots are heated 为被动；in a preset sequence 为方式状语；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是“按预定顺序加热”这一整套做法，不在被加热的 boots 上；controlled by a timer 为过去分词短语后置修饰 function。
**要点**：先行词可指代前面整个动作，从句谓语用单数 is。若把先行词当成 boots，就会误读为“除冰带是自动功能”，语义不通。

### ④ 被动语态 + 双重 by/through/to 介词链
**原句**：The glycol-based fluid is metered from a tank by a small electrically driven pump through a microfilter to the slinger rings on the prop hub.
**拆解**：主语 The glycol-based fluid，谓语 is metered 为被动；from a tank 表来源，by a small electrically driven pump 表执行者，through a microfilter 表经过的路径，to the slinger rings 表终点；on the prop hub 修饰甩液环。
**要点**：被动语态里 by 引出的是“施动者”，不是方式；方式在此由 through 引导。四个介词短语按“来源—施动者—路径—终点”排列，读译文时要按这个顺序还原流程，不能把 through 当成施动者。
