# Wing & Stabilizer Anti-Icing Systems

> 机体 Ch 15 · Wing & Stabilizer Anti-Icing Systems · 原文 PDF 第 883 页

## 1. 原文

The wing leading edges, or leading-edge slats, and horizontal and vertical stabilizer leading edges of many aircraft make and models have anti-icing systems installed to prevent the formation of ice on these components. The most common anti-icing systems used are thermal pneumatic, thermal electric, and chemical. Most general aviation (GA) aircraft equipped to fly in icing conditions use pneumatic deicing boots or a chemical anti-ice system. High-performance aircraft may have “weeping wings.” Large transport-category aircraft are equipped with advanced thermal pneumatic or thermal electric anti-icing systems that are controlled automatically to prevent the formation of ice.

### Thermal Pneumatic Anti-icing

Thermal systems used for the purpose of preventing the formation of ice or for deicing airfoil leading edges usually use heated air ducted spanwise along the inside of the leading edge of the airfoil and distributed around its inner surface. These thermal pneumatic anti-icing systems are used for wings, leading edge slats, horizontal and vertical stabilizers, engine inlets, and more. There are several sources of heated air, including hot bleed air from the turbine compressor, engine exhaust heat exchangers, and ram air heated by a combustion heater.

#### Wing Anti-Ice (WAI) System

Thermal wing anti-ice (WAI or TAI) systems for business jet and large-transport category aircraft typically use hot air bled from the engine compressor. Relatively large amounts of very hot air can be bled off the compressor, providing a satisfactory source of anti-icing heat. The hot air is routed through ducting, manifolds, and valves to components that need to be anti-iced. Figure 15-7 shows a typical WAI system schematic for a business jet. The bleed air is routed to each wing leading edge by an ejector in each wing inboard area. The ejector discharges the bleed air into piccolo tubes for distribution along the leading edge. Fresh ambient air is introduced into the wing leading edge by two flush-mounted ram air scoops in each wing leading edge, one at the wing root and one near the wingtip. The ejectors entrain ambient air, reduce the temperature of the bleed air, and increase the mass airflow in the piccolo tubes. The wing leading edge is constructed of two skin layers separated by a narrow passageway. The air directed against the leading edge can only escape through the passageway, after which it is vented overboard through a vent in the bottom of the wingtip.

When the WAI switch is turned on, the pressure regulator is energized, and the shutoff valve opens. When the wing leading edge temperature reaches approximately +140 °F, temperature switches turn on the operation light above the switch. If the temperature in the wing leading edge exceeds approximately +212 °F (outboard) or +350 °F (inboard), the red WING OV HT warning light on the annunciator panel illuminates.

The ducting of WAI systems usually consists of aluminum alloy, titanium, stainless steel, or molded fiberglass tubes. The tube, or duct, sections are attached to each other by bolted end flanges or by band-type V-clamps. The ducting is lagged with a fire-resistant, heat-insulating material, such as fiberglass. In some installations, thin stainless-steel expansion bellows are used. Bellows are located at strategic positions to absorb any distortion or expansion of the ducting that may occur due to temperature variations. The joined sections of ducting are hermetically sealed by sealing rings. These seals are fitted into annular recesses in the duct joint faces.

When installing a section of duct, make certain that the seal bears evenly against and is compressed by the adjacent joint’s flange. When specified, the ducts should be pressure tested at the pressure recommended by the manufacturer of the aircraft concerned. Leak checks are made to detect defects in the duct that would permit the escape of heated air. The rate of leakage at a given pressure should not exceed that recommended in the aircraft maintenance manual.

Air leaks can often be detected audibly and are sometimes revealed by holes in the lagging or thermal insulation material. However, if difficulty arises in locating leaks, a soap-and-water solution may be used. All ducting should be inspected for security, general condition, or distortion. Lagging or insulating blankets must be checked for security and must be free of flammable fluids, such as oil or hydraulic fluid.

#### Leading Edge Slat Anti-Ice System

Aircraft that utilize leading edge slats often use bleed air from the engine compressor to prevent the formation of frost on these surfaces. On a modern transport category aircraft, the pneumatic system supplies bleed air for this purpose. WAI valves control the air flow from the pneumatic system to WAI ducts. The WAI ducts carry the air to the slats. Holes in the bottom of each slat let the air out.

The airfoil and cowl ice protection system (ACIPS) computer card controls the WAI valves, and pressure sensors send duct air pressure data to the computer. The aircrew can select an auto or manual mode with the WAI selector. In the auto mode, the system turns on when the ice detection system detects ice. The off and on positions are used for manual control of the WAI system. The WAI system is only used in the air, except for ground tests. The weight on wheels system and/ or airspeed data disarms the system when the aircraft is on the ground.

WAI Valve The WAI valve controls the flow of bleed air from the pneumatic system to the WAI ducts. The valve is electrically controlled and pneumatically actuated. The torque motor controls operation of the valve. With no electrical power to the torque motor, air pressure on one side of the actuator holds the valve closed. Electrical current through the torque motor allows air pressure to open the valve. As the torque motor current increases, the valve opening increases.

WAI Pressure Sensor The WAI pressure sensor senses the air pressure in the WAI duct after the WAI valve. The ACIPS computer card uses the pressure information to control the WAI system.

WAI Ducts The WAI ducts move air from the pneumatic system through the wing leading edge to the leading-edge slats. Figure 15-9 shows that only leading edge slat sections 3, 4, and 5 on the left wing and 10, 11, and 12 on the right wing receive bleed air for WAI. Sections of the WAI ducting are perforated. The holes allow air to flow into the space inside the leading-edge slats. The air leaves the slats through holes in the bottom of each slat. Some WAI ducts have connecting “T” ducts that telescope to direct air into the slats while extended. The telescoping section attached to the slat on one end, slides over the narrow diameter “T” section that is connected into the WAI duct. A seal prevents any loss of air. This arrangement allows warm air delivery to the slats while retracted, in transit, and fully deployed.

WAI Control System Modern aircraft use several onboard computers to control aircraft systems. The WAI system is controlled by the ACIPS computer card. The ACIPS computer card controls both WAI valves. The required positions of the WAI valves change as bleed air temperature and altitude change. The left and right valves operate at the same time to heat both wings equally. This keeps the airplane aerodynamically stable in icing conditions. The WAI pressure sensors supply feedback information to the WAI ACIPS computer card for WAI valve control and position indication. If either pressure sensor fails, the WAI ACIPS computer card sets the related WAI valve to either fully open or fully closed. If either valve fails closed, the WAI computer card keeps the other valve closed.

There is one selector for the WAI system. The selector has three positions: auto, on, and off. With the selector in auto and no operational mode inhibits, the WAI ACIPS computer card sends a signal to open the WAI valves when either ice detector detects ice. The valves close after a 3-minute delay when the ice detector no longer detects ice. The time delay prevents frequent on/off cycles during intermittent icing conditions. With the selector on and no operational mode inhibits, the WAI valves open. With the selector off, the WAI valves close. The operational mode for the WAI valves can be inhibited by many different sets of conditions.

The operational mode is inhibited if all of these conditions occur:

• Takeoff mode is selected.

• Airplane has been in the air less than 10 minutes.

With auto or on selected, the operational mode is inhibited if any of these conditions occur:

• Airplane on the ground (except during an initiated or periodic built-in test equipment (BITE) test).

• Total Air Temperature (TAT) is more than 50 °F (10 °C) and the time since takeoff is less than 5 minutes.

• Air-driven hydraulic pump operation.

• Bleed air temperature less than 200 °F (93 °C).

The WAI valves stay closed as long as the operational mode inhibit is active. If the valves are already open, the operational mode inhibit causes the valves to close.

The aircrew can monitor the WAI system on the onboard computer maintenance page. The following information is shown:

• WING MANIFOLD PRESS—pneumatic duct pressure in PSIG.

• V ALVE—WAI valve open, closed, or regulating.

• AIR PRESS—pressure downstream of the WAI valves in PSIG.

• AIR FLOW—air flow through the WAI valves in pounds per minute.

WAI System Built-In Test Equipment (BITE) Test BITE circuits in the WAI ACIPS computer card continuously monitor the WAI system. Faults that affect the dispatch of the aircraft cause status messages. Other faults cause central maintenance computer system (CMCS) maintenance messages. The BITE in the WAI ACIPS computer card also performs automatic power-up and periodic tests. Faults found during these tests that affect dispatch cause status messages. Other faults cause CMCS maintenance messages. The power-up test occurs when the card gets power. BITE does a test of the card hardware and software functions and the valve and pressure sensor interfaces. The valves do not move during this test.

The periodic test occurs when all these conditions are true:

• The airplane has been on the ground between 1 and 5 minutes.

• The WAI selector is set to auto or on.

• Air-driven hydraulic pumps are not in intermittent operation.

• Bleed pressure is sufficient to open the WAI valves.

• The time since the last periodic test is more than 24 hours.

• During this test, the WAI valves cycle open and closed. This test makes sure that valve malfunctions are detected.

### Thermal Electric Anti-Icing

Electricity is used to heat various components on an aircraft so that ice does not form. This type of anti-ice is typically limited to small components due to high amperage draw. Effective thermal electric anti-ice is used on most air data probes, such as pitot tubes, static air ports, TAT and AOA probes, ice detectors, and engine P2/T2 sensors. Water lines, waste water drains, and some turboprop inlet cowls are also heated with electricity to prevent ice from forming. Transport category and high-performance aircraft use thermal electric anti-icing in windshields.

In devices that use thermal electric anti-ice, current flows through an integral conductive element that produces heat. The temperature of the component is elevated above the freezing point of water, so ice cannot form. Various schemes are used, such as an internal coil wire, externally wrapped blankets or tapes, as well as conductive films and heated gaskets. A basic discussion of probe heat follows. Windshield heat and portable water heat anti-ice are discussed later in this chapter. Propeller deice boots, which also are used for anti-ice, are also thermal electric and discussed in this chapter.

Data probes that protrude into the ambient airstream are particularly susceptible to ice formation in flight. Figure 15-14 illustrates the types and location probes that use thermal electric heat on one airliner. A pitot tube, for example, contains an internal electric element that is controlled by a switch in the flight deck. Use caution checking the function of the pitot heat when the aircraft is on the ground. The tube gets extremely hot since it must keep ice from forming at altitude in temperatures near -50 °F at speeds possibly over 500 miles per hour. An ammeter or load meter in the circuit can be used to safely determine functionality.

Simple probe heat circuits exist on GA aircraft with a switch and a circuit breaker to activate and protect the device. Advanced aircraft may have more complex circuitry in which control is by computer and flight condition of the aircraft is considered before thermal electric heaters are activated automatically. Figure 15-15 shows such a circuit for a pitot tube. The primary flight computer (PFC) supplies signals for the air data card (ADC) to energize ground and air heat control relays to activate probe heat. Information concerning speed of the aircraft, whether it is in the air or on the ground, and if the engines are running, are factors considered by the ADC logic. Similar controls are used for other probe heaters.

### Chemical Anti-Icing

Chemical anti-icing is used in some aircraft to anti-ice the leading edges of the wing, stabilizers, windshields, and propellers. The wing and stabilizer systems are often called weeping wing systems or are known by their trade name of TKS™ systems. Ice protection is based upon the freezing point depressant concept. An antifreeze solution is pumped from a reservoir through a mesh screen embedded in the leading edges of the wings and stabilizers. Activated by a switch in the flight deck, the liquid flows over the wing and tail surfaces, preventing the formation of ice as it flows. The solution mixes with the supercooled water in the cloud, depresses its freezing point, and allows the mixture to flow off of the aircraft without freezing. The system is designed to anti-ice, but it is also capable of deicing an aircraft as well. When ice has accumulated on the leading edges, the antifreeze solution chemically breaks down the bond between the ice and airframe. This allows aerodynamic forces to carry the ice away. Thus, the system clears the airframe of accumulated ice before transitioning to anti-ice protection. Figure 15-16 shows a chemical anti-ice system.

The TKS™ weeping wing system contains formed titanium panels that are laser drilled with over 800 tiny holes (.0025-inch diameter) per square inch. These are mated with non-perforated stainless-steel rear panels and bonded to wing and stabilizer leading edges. As fluid is delivered from a central reservoir and pump, it seeps through the holes. Aerodynamic forces cause the fluid to coat the upper and lower surfaces of the airfoil. The glycol-based fluid prevents ice from adhering to the aircraft structure.

Some aircraft with weeping wing systems are certified to fly into known icing conditions. Others use it as a hedge against unexpected ice encountered in flight. The systems are basically the same. Reservoir capacity permits 1-2 hours of operation. TKSTM weeping wings are used primarily on reciprocating aircraft that lack a supply of warm bleed air for the installation of a thermal anti-ice system. However, the system is simple and effective leading to its use on some turbine powered corporate aircraft as well.

## 2. 中文翻译

许多机型和型号的机翼前缘（或前缘缝翼）以及水平安定面、垂直安定面前缘都装有防冰系统，以防止冰在这些部件上形成。最常用的防冰系统有热气防冰、电热防冰和化学防冰。大多数具备结冰条件下飞行能力的一般航空（GA）飞机采用气动除冰带或化学防冰系统。高性能飞机可能采用“渗流机翼”。大型运输类飞机装设先进的热气或电热防冰系统，由系统自动控制以防止结冰。

### 热气防冰

用于防止结冰或为翼面前缘除冰的热力系统，通常让加热后的空气沿翼面前缘内部展向输送，并分布到前缘内表面各处。这类热气防冰系统用于机翼、前缘缝翼、水平安定面和垂直安定面、发动机进气口等部位。加热空气有若干来源，包括涡轮压气机的热引气、发动机排气热交换器，以及由燃烧加热器加热的冲压空气。

#### 机翼防冰（WAI）系统

用于公务喷气机和大型运输类飞机的热气机翼防冰（WAI 或 TAI）系统，通常使用从发动机压气机引出的热空气。可以从压气机引出相当大量的高温空气，构成令人满意的防冰热源。热空气经管道、集管和活门被输送到需要防冰的部件。图15-7示出一架公务喷气机的典型 WAI 系统原理图。引气由每个机翼内侧区域的引射器送往各自机翼的前缘。引射器把引气排入笛形管，沿前缘分配。外界新鲜空气由每个机翼前缘上的两个齐平安装的冲压空气进口引入前缘，一个位于翼根，一个靠近翼尖。引射器夹带外界空气，降低引气温度，并增大笛形管内的质量流量。机翼前缘由两层蒙皮构成，两皮之间是一条狭窄通道。通向前缘的空气只能经这条通道逸出，随后通过翼尖底部的排气口排到机外。

当 WAI 开关接通时，压力调节器通电，关断活门打开。机翼前缘温度达到约 +140 °F 时，温度开关点亮开关上方的“工作”灯。若前缘温度超过约 +212 °F（外侧）或 +350 °F（内侧），信号板上的红色 WING OV HT 超温警告灯点亮。

WAI 系统的管道通常由铝合金、钛合金、不锈钢或模压玻璃钢管制成。各段管子（导管）用带螺栓的端部法兰或带状 V 形卡箍相互连接。管道外面包裹耐火隔热材料，如玻璃纤维。在某些安装中采用薄不锈钢膨胀波纹管。波纹管布置在关键位置，用来吸收温度变化可能引起的管道变形或膨胀。各段导管之间用密封圈实现气密密封，密封圈装在导管接合面的环形凹槽内。

安装某一段导管时，务必使密封圈均匀贴合在相邻接头的法兰上并被其压紧。如有规定，管道应按飞机厂家推荐的压力做压力试验。还要做泄漏检查，以发现那些会让热空气外泄的管道缺陷。在规定压力下的泄漏率不应超过飞机维护手册中的推荐值。

空气泄漏往往可以听出来，有时也能从隔热层或保温材料上的孔洞发现。不过，如果难以确定泄漏位置，可以使用肥皂水溶液。应对所有管道检查其固定是否牢靠、总体状况及有无变形。保温层或隔热毯必须固定牢靠，且不得沾染燃油、液压油等易燃液体。

#### 前缘缝翼防冰系统

使用前缘缝翼的飞机，常用发动机压气机引气来防止这些表面上结霜。在现代运输类飞机上，由气压系统为此提供引气。WAI 活门控制从气压系统到 WAI 导管的空气流量，WAI 导管把空气送到缝翼，每个缝翼底部的孔把空气放出。

翼面与整流罩防冰系统（ACIPS）计算机卡控制 WAI 活门，压力传感器把导管空气压力数据送至该计算机。机组可用 WAI 选择器选择自动或手动模式。在自动模式下，冰探测系统探测到冰时系统即接通。关断位和接通位用于手动控制 WAI 系统。除地面测试外，WAI 系统只在空中使用。轮载信号和/或空速数据在地面时使系统解除预位。

WAI 活门 WAI 活门控制从气压系统到 WAI 导管的引气流量。该活门为电控、气动驱动。力矩马达控制活门的动作。力矩马达不通电时，作动器一侧的气压使活门保持在关闭位。电流通过力矩马达后，气压便可打开活门。力矩马达电流增大，活门开度随之增大。

WAI 压力传感器 WAI 压力传感器感受 WAI 活门下游导管中的空气压力。ACIPS 计算机卡利用该压力信息控制 WAI 系统。

WAI 导管 WAI 导管把来自气压系统的空气经机翼前缘送至前缘缝翼。图15-9示出，只有左翼第 3、4、5 段和右翼第 10、11、12 段前缘缝翼接收防冰引气。部分 WAI 导管段是打孔的，孔使空气流入前缘缝翼内部空间。空气从每个缝翼底部的孔排出。有些 WAI 导管带有可伸缩的“T”形连接管，在缝翼伸出时把空气导入缝翼。伸缩段一端连接在缝翼上，可在接入 WAI 导管的细径“T”形段上滑动，密封件防止漏气。这种布置使缝翼在收起、运动过程中和完全放出时都能获得暖空气。

WAI 控制系统 现代飞机用若干机载计算机控制各系统。WAI 系统由 ACIPS 计算机卡控制，该卡同时控制两个 WAI 活门。WAI 活门所需位置随引气温度和高度变化而改变。左右活门同时工作，使两翼受热相同，从而保持飞机在结冰条件下的气动稳定性。WAI 压力传感器向 WAI ACIPS 计算机卡提供反馈信息，用于活门控制和位置指示。若任一压力传感器失效，WAI ACIPS 计算机卡将相应的 WAI 活门置为全开或全关。若任一活门关断失效，WAI 计算机卡使另一个活门也保持关闭。

WAI 系统只有一个选择器，有自动、接通、关断三个位置。选择器置于自动且无工作模式抑制时，只要任一冰探测器探测到冰，WAI ACIPS 计算机卡就发出信号打开 WAI 活门。冰探测器不再探测到冰后，活门延迟 3 分钟关闭。该延时用于防止在间歇结冰条件下频繁通断。选择器置于接通且无工作模式抑制时，WAI 活门打开；置于关断时，活门关闭。WAI 活门的工作模式可被许多不同的条件组合所抑制。

若下列条件全部出现，则工作模式被抑制：

• 已选择起飞模式。

• 飞机空中时间不足 10 分钟。

选择自动或接通时，若出现下列任一条件，工作模式被抑制：

• 飞机在地面（启动的或周期的机内自检设备（BITE）测试期间除外）。

• 总温（TAT）高于 50 °F（10 °C）且起飞后时间不足 5 分钟。

• 空气驱动液压泵工作。

• 引气温度低于 200 °F（93 °C）。

只要工作模式抑制有效，WAI 活门就保持关闭。若活门已经打开，工作模式抑制会使其关闭。

机组可在机载计算机维护页面上监控 WAI 系统，显示的信息如下：

• WING MANIFOLD PRESS——气压导管压力，单位 PSIG。

• VALVE——WAI 活门处于打开、关闭或调节状态。

• AIR PRESS——WAI 活门下游压力，单位 PSIG。

• AIR FLOW——流经 WAI 活门的空气流量，单位磅/分钟。

WAI 系统机内自检设备（BITE）测试 WAI ACIPS 计算机卡中的 BITE 电路连续监控 WAI 系统。影响飞机放行的故障产生状态信息，其他故障产生中央维护计算机系统（CMCS）维护信息。WAI ACIPS 计算机卡中的 BITE 还执行上电测试和周期测试。这些测试中发现的、影响放行的故障产生状态信息，其他故障产生 CMCS 维护信息。上电测试在计算机卡得电时进行，BITE 检测卡的硬件与软件功能以及活门和压力传感器接口。该测试期间活门不动作。

周期测试在下列条件全部满足时进行：

• 飞机已在地面 1 至 5 分钟。

• WAI 选择器置于自动或接通。

• 空气驱动液压泵不在间歇工作状态。

• 引气压力足以打开 WAI 活门。

• 距上次周期测试已超过 24 小时。

• 该测试期间，WAI 活门循环开闭，以确保能检测出活门故障。

### 电热防冰

用电加热飞机上的各种部件，使冰无法形成。由于耗电量大，这类防冰通常只限于小型部件。有效的电热防冰用于大多数大气数据探头，如空速管、静压孔、TAT 和 AOA 探头、冰探测器以及发动机 P2/T2 传感器。水管、废水排放管和某些涡桨发动机进气整流罩也用电加热来防止结冰。运输类和高性能飞机的风挡采用电热防冰。

在使用电热防冰的装置中，电流通过一体化的导电元件产生热量。部件温度被提高到水的冰点以上，冰便无法形成。具体方案有多种，如内部盘绕电阻丝、外部缠绕的加热毯或加热带，以及导电膜和加热垫圈。下面先对探头加温作基本讨论。风挡加温和饮用水加温防冰将在本章后面讨论。同样用于防冰的螺旋桨除冰带也属电热式，在本章中讨论。

伸入外界气流中的大气数据探头在飞行中尤其容易结冰。图15-14示出一架客机上采用电热加温的探头类型和位置。例如，空速管内部装有电加热元件，由驾驶舱内的开关控制。在地面检查空速管加温功能时要小心，管子会变得非常烫，因为它必须在 -50 °F 左右、速度可能超过 500 英里/小时的高空防止结冰。可用电路中的电流表或负载表安全地判断其功能是否正常。

一般航空飞机上的探头加温电路很简单，由一个开关和一个断路器来接通和保护该装置。先进飞机可能有更复杂的电路，由计算机控制，并在自动接通电加热器之前考虑飞机的飞行状态。图15-15示出这样一种空速管加温电路。主飞行计算机（PFC）向大气数据卡（ADC）提供信号，以激励地面和空中加温控制继电器来接通探头加温。飞机的速度、是在空中还是在地面、发动机是否运转等信息，都是 ADC 逻辑所考虑的因素。其他探头加热器采用类似的控制方式。

### 化学防冰

某些飞机采用化学防冰来防止机翼、安定面、风挡和螺旋桨前缘结冰。机翼和安定面的这类系统常称为渗流机翼系统，或按其商品名称称为 TKS™ 系统。其防冰基于降低冰点的原理。防冻液从储液箱泵出，经嵌入机翼和安定面前缘的网屏流出。由驾驶舱开关启动后，液体流过机翼和尾翼表面，在流动过程中防止结冰。该溶液与云中的过冷水混合，降低其冰点，使混合液在未冻结的情况下从飞机上流走。该系统按防冰设计，但也具备为飞机除冰的能力。当前缘已积冰时，防冻液以化学方式破坏冰与机体之间的结合，使气动力能把冰带走。这样，系统在转为防冰保护之前先清除机体积冰。图15-16示出一种化学防冰系统。

TKS™ 渗流机翼系统由成形的钛板构成，钛板经激光打孔，每平方英寸有 800 多个直径 0.0025 英寸的微孔。这些钛板与未打孔的不锈钢背板配对，并粘接到机翼和安定面前缘上。液体从中央储液箱经泵送出后，从这些孔中渗出。气动力使液体覆盖翼面的上、下表面。这种乙二醇基液体可防止冰黏附在飞机结构上。

有些装有渗流机翼系统的飞机被批准进入已知结冰条件飞行，另一些则把它作为应对飞行中意外结冰的备用手段。两种系统基本相同。储液箱容量可支持 1 至 2 小时工作。TKS™ 渗流机翼主要用于没有暖引气可供安装热气防冰系统的活塞式飞机。不过，该系统简单有效，因此一些涡轮动力的公务机也加以采用。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 过去分词短语作后置定语
**原句**：Thermal wing anti-ice (WAI or TAI) systems for business jet and large-transport category aircraft typically use hot air bled from the engine compressor.
**拆解**：主语中心词 systems，前置定语 Thermal wing anti-ice，介词短语 for business jet and large-transport category aircraft 作后置定语；谓语 use，宾语 hot air，过去分词短语 bled from the engine compressor 作后置定语修饰 hot air（相当于 which is bled from...）。
**要点**：bled 是 bleed 的过去分词，此处不是谓语，而是被动含义的定语“被引出的”。主语与谓语之间隔了 8 个词的定语，读句时先抓 systems ... use 这条主干。

### ② after which 引导的非限制性定语从句
**原句**：The air directed against the leading edge can only escape through the passageway, after which it is vented overboard through a vent in the bottom of the wingtip.
**拆解**：主语 The air，过去分词短语 directed against the leading edge 作后置定语；谓语 can only escape，through the passageway 为方式状语；逗号后 after which 引导非限制性定语从句，which 指代前面“空气经通道逸出”这件事，从句为被动语态 it is vented overboard。
**要点**：after which = after the air escapes，which 指代的是整个先行事件而非单个名词，所以从句用 it 复指 air。介词 + which 是书面语中典型的定语从句形式，不能换成 after that 引导定语从句。

### ③ with 复合结构作条件状语
**原句**：With no electrical power to the torque motor, air pressure on one side of the actuator holds the valve closed.
**拆解**：With no electrical power to the torque motor 为 with 复合结构，作条件状语（相当于 When there is no electrical power...）；主句主语 air pressure，后置定语 on one side of the actuator；谓语 holds，宾语 the valve，宾补 closed（过去分词作宾语补足语，表状态）。
**要点**：hold sth. closed 是“使某物保持关闭”，closed 说明 valve 的状态而非被动谓语。with 复合结构中的名词短语前用 no 表否定条件，译为“在……没有通电时”。

### ④ if 条件从句 + 分隔的主谓
**原句**：If the temperature in the wing leading edge exceeds approximately +212 °F (outboard) or +350 °F (inboard), the red WING OV HT warning light on the annunciator panel illuminates.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，从句主语 the temperature，后置定语 in the wing leading edge，谓语 exceeds，宾语为 or 连接的两个温度值；主句主语 the red WING OV HT warning light，后置定语 on the annunciator panel，谓语 illuminates 为不及物动词。
**要点**：主句主语带了三层修饰（red / WING OV HT / on the annunciator panel），谓语 illuminate 是不及物动词，不能加宾语。温度值后的 (outboard)、(inboard) 是补充说明，不是句子成分。

### ⑤ 并列句中的名词化表达
**原句**：The WAI pressure sensors supply feedback information to the WAI ACIPS computer card for WAI valve control and position indication.
**拆解**：主语 The WAI pressure sensors，谓语 supply，间接宾语 feedback information，直接宾语前的 to the WAI ACIPS computer card 为方向状语；for WAI valve control and position indication 为目的状语，其中 control 与 indication 均为名词化的动作。
**要点**：supply sth. to sb. 与 supply sb. with sth. 两种搭配不要混用，本句是前者。for + 名词短语表用途，译成“用于……”，不要误读成原因。

### ⑥ either ... or 作宾语补足语
**原句**：If either pressure sensor fails, the WAI ACIPS computer card sets the related WAI valve to either fully open or fully closed.
**拆解**：If 引导条件状语从句，主语 either pressure sensor（either 作定语，表“任一”）；主句主语 the WAI ACIPS computer card，谓语 sets，宾语 the related WAI valve，介词短语 to either fully open or fully closed 作宾语补足语，说明活门被置成的状态。
**要点**：句中出现两个 either：第一个是形容词“任一”，第二个构成 either...or... 的并列。set sth. to + 状态，与 set sth. in motion 一样属“动词 + 宾语 + 补足语”结构，to 后接形容词性的过去分词短语。
