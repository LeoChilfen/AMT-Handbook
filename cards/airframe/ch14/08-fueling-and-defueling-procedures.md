# Fueling & Defueling Procedures

> 机体 Ch 14 · Fueling & Defueling Procedures · 原文 PDF 第 878 页

## 1. 原文

Maintenance technicians are often asked to fuel or defuel aircraft. Fueling procedure can vary from aircraft to aircraft. Tanks may need to be fueled in a prescribed sequence to prevent structural damage to the airframe. The proper procedure should be confirmed before fueling an unfamiliar aircraft.

### Fueling

Always fuel aircraft outside, not in a hangar where fuel vapors may accumulate and increase the risk and severity of an accident. Generally, there are two types of fueling process: over-the-wing refueling and pressure refueling. Over-the-wing refueling is accomplished by opening the fuel tank cap on the upper surface of the wing or fuselage, if equipped with fuselage tanks. The fueling nozzle is carefully inserted into the fill opening and fuel is pumped into the tank. This process is similar to the process used to refuel an automobile gas tank. When finished, the cap is secured, and subsequent tanks are opened and refilled until the aircraft has the desired fuel load onboard. Pressure refueling occurs at the bottom, front, or rear of the fuel tank. A pressure refueling nozzle locks onto the fueling port at the aircraft fueling station. Fuel is pumped into the aircraft through this secured and sealed connection. Gauges are monitored to ascertain when the tanks are properly loaded. An automatic shutoff system may be part of the aircraft system. It closes the fueling valve when the tanks are full.

Precautions should be used with either type of fueling. First and foremost, it is absolutely essential that the correct fuel be put in the aircraft. The type of fuel to be used is placarded near the fill port on over-the-wing systems and at the fueling station on pressure refueled aircraft. If there is any question about which fuel to use, the pilot in command, other knowledgeable personnel, or the manufacturer’s maintenance/operations manual should be consulted before proceeding. An over-the-wing refueling nozzle for turbine engine fuel should be too large to fit into the fill opening on an aircraft utilizing gasoline.

Clean the area adjacent to the fill port when refueling over the wing. Ensure the fuel nozzle is also clean. Aviation fuel nozzles are equipped with static bonding wires that must be attached to the aircraft before the fuel cap is opened. Open the cap only when ready to dispense the fuel. Insert the nozzle into the opening with care. The aircraft structure is much more delicate than the fuel nozzle, which could easily damage the aircraft. Do not insert the neck of the nozzle deeply enough to hit bottom. This could dent the tank, or the aircraft skin, if it is an integral tank. Exercise caution to avoid damage to the surface of the airframe by the heavy fuel hose. Lay the hose over your shoulder or use a refueling mat to protect the paint.

When pressure refueling, the aircraft receptacle is part of a fueling valve assembly. When the fueling nozzle is properly connected and locked, a plunger unlocks the aircraft valve so fuel can be pumped through it. Normally, all tanks can be fueled from a single point. Valves in the aircraft fuel system are controlled at the fueling station to direct the fuel into the proper tank. Ensure that the pressure developed by the refueling pump is correct for the aircraft before pumping fuel. Although similar, pressure fueling panels and their operation are different on different aircraft. Refueling personnel should be guided through the correct use of each panel. Do not guess at how the panel and associated valves operate.

When fueling from a fuel truck, precautions should be taken. If the truck is not in continuous service, all sumps should be drained before moving the truck, and the fuel should be visually inspected to be sure it is bright and clean. Turbine fuel should be allowed to settle for a few hours if the fuel truck tank has recently been filled or the truck has been jostled, such as when driven over a bumpy service road at the airport. Properly maneuver the fuel truck into position for refueling.

The aircraft should be approached slowly. The truck should be parked parallel to the wings and in front of the fuselage if possible. Avoid backing toward the aircraft. Set the parking brake and chock the wheels. Connect a static bonding cable from the truck to the aircraft. This cable is typically stored on a reel mounted on the truck.

There are other miscellaneous good practices that should be employed when refueling an aircraft. A ladder should be used if the refuel point is not accessible while standing on the ground. Climbing on an expensive aircraft to access the fueling ports is possible but does not give the stability of a ladder and may not be appreciated by the aircraft owner. If it is necessary to walk on the wings of the aircraft, do so only in designated areas, which are safe.

Filler nozzles should be treated as the important tools that they are. They should not be dropped or dragged across the apron. Most have attached dust caps that should be removed only for the actual fueling process and then immediately replaced. Nozzles should be clean to avoid contamination of the fuel. They should not leak and should be repaired at the earliest sign of leak or malfunction. Keep the fueling nozzle in constant contact with the filler neck spout when fueling. Never leave the nozzle in the fill spout unattended. When fueling is complete, always doublecheck the security of all fuel caps and ensure that bonding wires have been removed and stowed.

### Defueling

Removing the fuel contained in aircraft fuel tanks is sometimes required. This can occur for maintenance, inspection, or due to contamination. Occasionally, a change in flight plan may require defueling. Safety procedures for defueling are the same as those for fueling. Always defuel outside. Fire extinguishers should be on hand. Bonding cables should be attached to guard against static electricity buildup. Defueling should be performed by experienced personnel, and inexperienced personnel must be checked out before doing so without assistance.

Remember that there may be a sequence in defueling an aircraft’s fuel tanks just as there is when fueling to avoid structural damage. Consult the manufacturer’s maintenance/ operations manual(s) if in doubt.

Pressure fueled aircraft normally defuel through the pressure fueling port. The aircraft’s in-tank boost pumps can be used to pump the fuel out. The pump on a fuel truck can also be used to draw fuel out. These tanks can also be drained through the tank sump drains, but the large size of the tanks usually makes this impractical. Aircraft fueled over the wing are manufacturer’s procedure for defueling the aircraft.

What to do with the fuel coming out of a tank depends on a few factors. First, if the tank is being drained due to fuel contamination or suspected contamination, it should not be mixed with any other fuel. It should be stored in a separate container from good fuel, treated if possible, or disposed of properly. Take measures to ensure that contaminated fuel is never placed onboard an aircraft or mixed with good fuel. Second, the manufacturer may have requirements for good fuel that has been defueled from an aircraft, specifying whether it can be reused and the type of storage container in which it must be stored. Above all, fuel removed from an aircraft must not be mixed with any other type of fuel.

Good fuel removed from an aircraft must be handled with all precautions used when handling any fuel. It must only be put into clean tanks and efforts must be made to keep it clean. It may be put back in the aircraft or another aircraft if the manufacturer allows. Large aircraft can often transfer fuel from a tank requiring maintenance to another tank to avoid the defueling process.

### Fire Hazards When Fueling or Defueling

Due to the combustible nature of A VGAS and turbine engine fuel, the potential for fire while fueling and defueling aircraft must be addressed. Always fuel and defuel outside, not in a hangar that serves as an enclosed area for vapors to build up to a combustible level. Clothing worn by refueling personnel should not promote static electricity buildup. Synthetics, such as nylon, should be avoided. Cotton has proved to be safe for fuel handling attire.

As previously mentioned, the most controllable of the three ingredients required for fire is the source of ignition. It is absolutely necessary to prevent a source of ignition anywhere near the aircraft during fueling or refueling. Any open flame, such as a lit cigarette, must be extinguished. Operation of any electrical devices must be avoided. Radio and radar use is prohibited. It is important to note that fuel vapors proliferate well beyond the actual fuel tank opening and a simple spark, even one caused by static electricity, could be enough for ignition. Any potential for sparks must be nullified.

Spilled fuel poses an additional fire hazard. A thin layer of fuel vaporizes quickly. Small spills should be wiped up immediately. Larger spills can be flooded with water to dissipate the fuel and the potential for ignition. Do not sweep fuel that has spilled onto the ramp.

Class B fire extinguishers need to be charged and accessible nearby during the fueling and defueling processes. Fueling personnel must know exactly where they are and how to normally drained through the tank sump drains. Follow the use them. In case of an emergency, the fuel truck, if used, may need to be quickly driven away from the area. For this reason alone, it should be positioned correctly on the ramp relative to the aircraft.

## 2. 中文翻译

维护技术人员常被要求给飞机加油或放油。加油程序因飞机而异。油箱可能需要按规定顺序加油，以防机体结构受损。给不熟悉的飞机加油之前，应当确认正确的程序。

### 加油

始终在室外给飞机加油，不要在机库内进行，因为那里燃油蒸气可能积聚，增加事故的风险和严重程度。一般来说，加油过程有两种类型：翼上加油和压力加油。翼上加油是打开机翼上表面（若装有机身油箱，则为机身上表面）的燃油箱盖来完成的。把加油枪小心插入加油口，燃油被泵入油箱。这一过程类似于给汽车油箱加油。完成后盖好油箱盖，再打开并加注后续油箱，直到飞机上达到所需油量。压力加油在燃油箱底部、前部或后部进行。压力加油枪锁定在飞机加油站的加油口上。燃油通过这一固定且密封的连接泵入飞机。通过监视仪表来确定油箱是否正确加满。自动关断系统可能是飞机系统的一部分，它在油箱满时关闭加油活门。

两种加油方式都应采取预防措施。首先也是最重要的，必须绝对确保给飞机加的是正确的燃油。所用燃油类型，在翼上加油系统中标在加油口附近，在压力加油飞机上标在加油站处。若对使用何种燃油有任何疑问，在继续操作之前应咨询机长、其他有知识的人员或制造商的维护/使用手册。用于涡轮发动机燃油的翼上加油枪，应大到无法插入使用汽油的飞机的加油口。

翼上加油时，清洁加油口附近的区域。确保加油枪也清洁。航空燃油枪装有静电搭接线，必须在打开油箱盖之前接到飞机上。只在准备输油时才打开油箱盖。小心地把加油枪插入加油口。飞机结构比加油枪脆弱得多，后者很容易损坏飞机。不要把加油枪颈部插得太深而触底。这可能使油箱凹陷，若是整体油箱还会使飞机蒙皮凹陷。注意防止沉重的加油软管损坏机体表面。把软管搭在肩上，或使用加油垫保护漆层。

压力加油时，飞机上的加油口是加油活门组件的一部分。当加油枪正确连接并锁定后，一个柱塞解锁飞机上的活门，使燃油可以泵过活门。通常所有油箱都可由单一加油点加注。飞机燃油系统中的活门在加油站处控制，把燃油导向相应的油箱。泵油之前，确认加油泵所产生的压力符合该飞机的要求。尽管相似，但不同飞机的压力加油面板及其操作各不相同。加油人员应接受各面板正确使用的指导。不要凭猜测操作面板及相关活门。

用加油车加油时，应采取预防措施。如果加油车不是连续使用，移动之前应排放所有沉淀槽，并对燃油进行目视检查，确认其明亮清洁。如果加油车油罐最近刚加过油，或加油车受到颠簸（例如在机场崎岖的作业道路上行驶），涡轮燃油应静置数小时。正确地把加油车操纵到加油位置。

应缓慢接近飞机。加油车应尽可能与机翼平行停放，并位于机身前方。避免倒车朝向飞机。设置驻车制动并垫好车轮。把加油车上的静电搭接线接到飞机上。该线缆通常收存在加油车上安装的卷盘里。

给飞机加油时，还应采用其他一些良好做法。如果站在地面上够不到加油点，应使用梯子。爬上昂贵的飞机去接近加油口虽然可行，但不如梯子稳定，而且机主可能并不乐意。如果必须走上机翼，只应在指定的安全区域内行走。

加油枪应被视为重要的工具。不应掉落或在机坪上拖拽。多数加油枪带有防尘盖，只在实际加油过程中取下，随后立即装回。加油枪应保持清洁，以免污染燃油。加油枪不应渗漏，一出现渗漏或故障迹象就应尽快修理。加油时让加油枪始终与加油口颈嘴保持接触。绝不要把加油枪无人看管地留在加油口中。加油完成后，务必再次检查所有油箱盖是否牢固，并确认搭接线已取下并收存。

### 放油

有时需要把飞机燃油箱中的燃油放出。这可能是为了维护、检查，或因污染所致。偶尔，飞行计划的变更也可能需要放油。放油的安全程序与加油相同。始终在室外放油。应备有灭火器。应连接搭接线，以防静电积聚。放油应由有经验的人员进行，无经验人员在无人协助下操作之前必须经过考核。

要知道，放油时也可能像加油时一样存在顺序要求，以避免结构损坏。如有疑问，查阅制造商的维护/使用手册。

压力加油的飞机通常通过压力加油口放油。可用飞机上的箱内增压泵把燃油泵出。也可用加油车上的泵抽油。这些油箱也可通过油箱沉淀槽排放口排空，但油箱尺寸大，通常使这种做法不切实际。翼上加油的飞机，其放油按制造商的程序进行。

放出的燃油如何处理，取决于几个因素。首先，如果油箱是因燃油污染或疑似污染而排放，则不应与任何其他燃油混合。应把它与好燃油分开存放在单独的容器中，尽可能加以处理，或妥善处置。要采取措施，确保受污染的燃油绝不会被装上飞机或与好燃油混合。其次，制造商可能对从飞机上放出的好燃油有要求，规定其能否重复使用以及必须存放在何种容器中。最重要的是，从飞机上放出的燃油绝不能与任何其他类型的燃油混合。

从飞机上放出的好燃油，必须按处理任何燃油时的全部预防措施来处理。只能装入清洁的油箱，并应努力保持其清洁。若制造商允许，可以装回该飞机或另一架飞机。大型飞机常常可以把燃油从需要维护的油箱输送到另一个油箱，从而避免放油过程。

### 加油或放油时的火灾危险

由于航空汽油和涡轮发动机燃油具有可燃性质，给飞机加油和放油时必须应对火灾的可能性。始终在室外加油和放油，不要在机库内进行，因为机库是封闭区域，蒸气会积聚到可燃浓度。加油人员所穿服装不应助长静电积聚。应避免尼龙等合成纤维。棉质已被证明是处理燃油时的安全着装。

如前所述，火灾所需三个要素中最容易控制的是点火源。在加油或放油期间，绝对必须防止飞机附近出现任何点火源。任何明火，例如点燃的香烟，都必须熄灭。必须避免操作任何电气装置。禁止使用无线电和雷达。要注意，燃油蒸气会扩散到远超实际油箱口的地方，一个简单的火花，甚至静电引起的火花，就足以点燃。必须消除任何产生火花的可能。

溢出的燃油构成额外的火灾危险。薄薄一层燃油会迅速汽化。少量溢出应立即擦净。大量溢出可用水冲淹，以稀释燃油并降低点燃可能。不要清扫溢到机坪上的燃油。

加油和放油过程中，B 类灭火器必须充装完好并放在附近可取用处。加油人员必须确切知道它们的位置以及使用方法。发生紧急情况时，所用加油车可能需要迅速驶离该区域。仅出于这一原因，加油车就应相对于飞机正确地停放在机坪上。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 情态动词被动态 + 目的不定式
**原句**：Tanks may need to be fueled in a prescribed sequence to prevent structural damage to the airframe.
**拆解**：主语 Tanks + 情态动词 may + need to be fueled（need to 后接被动不定式）；in a prescribed sequence 为方式状语；to prevent structural damage to the airframe 为目的状语。
**要点**：may need to be fueled 是"可能需要（被）加注"，两层情态叠加，不能译成"可以需要"；to prevent 修饰整个动作，说明按顺序加油的目的。

### ② 祈使句 + 否定介词短语 + where 引导的定语从句
**原句**：Always fuel aircraft outside, not in a hangar where fuel vapors may accumulate and increase the risk and severity of an accident.
**拆解**：Always fuel aircraft outside 为祈使句；not in a hangar 为补充否定的地点状语；where 引导定语从句修饰 hangar，从句内 may accumulate and increase 为并列谓语。
**要点**：not in a hangar 不是另起一句，而是对 outside 的进一步排除，译"而不是在机库里"；where 从句的两个动词共用一个主语 fuel vapors。

### ③ 否定祈使句 + enough to do 结构
**原句**：Do not insert the neck of the nozzle deeply enough to hit bottom.
**拆解**：Do not insert… 为否定祈使句；deeply enough 中 enough 修饰副词 deeply，须后置；to hit bottom 为结果不定式，说明"深到何种程度"。
**要点**：enough 修饰形容词或副词时必须放在其后，不能写成 enough deeply；此处 deeply enough to hit bottom 表结果，译"深到触底的程度"。

### ④ 并列被动结构 + before doing so 的指代
**原句**：Defueling should be performed by experienced personnel, and inexperienced personnel must be checked out before doing so without assistance.
**拆解**：由 and 连接两个并列分句，均为被动：Defueling should be performed by…；inexperienced personnel must be checked out…；before doing so 中 doing so 代指前面提到的放油操作，without assistance 修饰 doing so。
**要点**：doing so 是替代前文动作的代词性结构，译时应补出"独自放油"；by experienced personnel 引出动作执行者，中文宜转为主动"由有经验的人员进行"。

### ⑤ 形式主语 it + 真正主语不定式
**原句**：It is absolutely necessary to prevent a source of ignition anywhere near the aircraft during fueling or refueling.
**拆解**：It 为形式主语，is absolutely necessary 为系表结构，真正主语是后面的不定式 to prevent a source of ignition…；anywhere near the aircraft 与 during fueling or refueling 分别为地点和时间状语。
**要点**：it 不指代任何具体事物，不能译成"它是"；中文习惯把不定式提前作主语，译"绝对有必要防止……"。
