# A Brief History of Aircraft Structures

> 机体 Ch 01 · A Brief History of Aircraft Structures · 原文 PDF 第 27 页

## 1. 原文

The history of aircraft structures underlies the history of aviation in general. Advances in materials and processes used to construct aircraft have led to their evolution from simple wood truss structures to the sleek aerodynamic flying machines of today. Combined with continuous powerplant development, the structures of “flying machines” have changed significantly.

The key discovery that “lift” could be created by passing air over the top of a curved surface set the development of fixed and rotary-wing aircraft in motion. George Cayley developed an efficient cambered airfoil in the early 1800s, as well as successful manned gliders later in that century. He established the principles of flight, including the existence of lift, weight, thrust, and drag. It was Cayley who first stacked wings and created a tri-wing glider that flew a man in 1853.

Earlier, Cayley studied the center of gravity of flying machines, as well as the effects of wing dihedral. Furthermore, he pioneered directional control of aircraft by including the earliest form of a rudder on his gliders.

In the late 1800s, Otto Lilienthal built upon Cayley’s discoveries. He manufactured and flew his own gliders on over 2,000 flights. His willow and cloth aircraft had wings designed from extensive study of the wings of birds. Lilienthal also made standard use of vertical and horizontal fins behind the wings and pilot station. Above all, Lilienthal proved that man could fly.

Octave Chanute, a retired railroad and bridge engineer, was active in aviation during the 1890s. His interest was so great that, among other things, he published a definitive work called “Progress in Flying Machines.” This was the culmination of his effort to gather and study all of the information available on aviation. With the assistance of others, he built gliders similar to Lilienthal’s and then his own. In addition to his publication, Chanute advanced aircraft structure development by building a glider with stacked wings incorporating the use of wires as wing supports.

The work of all of these men was known to the Wright Brothers when they built their successful, powered airplane in 1903. The first of its kind to carry a man aloft, the Wright Flyer had thin, cloth-covered wings attached to what was primarily truss structures made of wood. The wings contained forward and rear spars and were supported with both struts and wires. Stacked wings (two sets) were also part of the Wright Flyer.

Powered heavier-than-air aviation grew from the Wright design. Inventors and fledgling aviators began building their own aircraft. Early on, many were similar to that constructed by the Wrights using wood and fabric with wires and struts to support the wing structure. In 1909, Frenchman Louis Bleriot produced an aircraft with notable design differences. He built a successful mono-wing aircraft. The wings were still supported by wires, but a mast extending above the fuselage enabled the wings to be supported from above, as well as underneath. This made possible the extended wing length needed to lift an aircraft with a single set of wings. Bleriot used a Pratt truss-type fuselage frame.

More powerful engines were developed, and airframe structures changed to take advantage of the benefits. As early as 1910, German Hugo Junkers was able to build an aircraft with metal truss construction and metal skin due to the availability of stronger powerplants to thrust the plane forward and into the sky. The use of metal instead of wood for the primary structure eliminated the need for external wing braces and wires. His J-1 also had a single set of wings (a monoplane) instead of a stacked set.

Leading up to World War I (WWI), stronger engines also allowed designers to develop thicker wings with stronger spars. Wire wing bracing was no longer needed. Flatter, lower wing surfaces on high-camber wings created more lift. WWI expanded the need for large quantities of reliable aircraft. Used mostly for reconnaissance, stacked-wing tail draggers with wood and metal truss frames with mostly fabric skin dominated the wartime sky. The Red Baron’s Fokker DR-1 was typical.

In the 1920s, the use of metal in aircraft construction increased. Fuselages able to carry cargo and passengers were developed. The early flying boats with their hull-type construction from the shipbuilding industry provided the blueprints for semimonocoque construction of fuselages. Truss-type designs faded. A tendency toward cleaner mono-wing designs prevailed.

Into the 1930s, all-metal aircraft accompanied new lighter and more powerful engines. Larger semimonocoque fuselages were complimented with stress-skin wing designs. Fewer truss and fabric aircraft were built. World War II (WWII) brought about a myriad of aircraft designs using all metal technology. Deep fuel-carrying wings were the norm, but the desire for higher flight speeds prompted the development of thin-winged aircraft in which fuel was carried in the fuselage. The first composite structure aircraft, the De Havilland Mosquito, used a balsa wood sandwich material in the construction of the fuselage. The fiberglass radome was also developed during this period.

After WWII, the development of turbine engines led to higher altitude flight. The need for pressurized aircraft pervaded aviation. Semimonocoque construction needed to be made even stronger as a result. Refinements to the all-metal semimonocoque fuselage structure were made to increase strength and combat metal fatigue caused by the pressurization-depressurization cycle. Rounded window and door openings were developed to avoid weak areas where cracks could form. Integrally machined copper alloy aluminum skin resisted cracking and allowed thicker skin and controlled tapering. Chemical milling of wing skin structures provided great strength and smooth high-performance surfaces. Variable contour wings became easier to construct. Increases in flight speed accompanying jet travel brought about the need for thinner wings. Wing loading also increased greatly. Multispar and box beam wing designs were developed in response.

In the 1960s, ever larger aircraft were developed to carry passengers. As engine technology improved, the jumbo jet was engineered and built. Still primarily aluminum with a semimonocoque fuselage, the sheer size of the airliners of the day initiated a search for lighter and stronger materials from which to build them. The use of honeycomb constructed panels in Boeing’s airline series saved weight while not compromising strength. Initially, aluminum core with aluminum or fiberglass skin sandwich panels were used on wing panels, flight control surfaces, cabin floor boards, and other applications.

A steady increase in the use of honeycomb and foam core sandwich components and a wide variety of composite materials characterizes the state of aviation structures from the 1970s to the present. Advanced techniques and material combinations have resulted in a gradual shift from aluminum to carbon fiber and other strong, lightweight materials. These new materials are engineered to meet specific performance requirements for various components on the aircraft. Many airframe structures are made of more than 50 percent advanced composites, with some airframes approaching 100 percent. The term “very light jet” (VLJ) has come to describe a new generation of jet aircraft made almost entirely of advanced composite materials. It is possible that noncomposite aluminum aircraft structures will become obsolete as did the methods and materials of construction used by Cayley, Lilienthal, and the Wright Brothers.

## 2. 中文翻译

飞机结构的历史，是整部航空史的底色。用于制造飞机的材料与工艺不断进步，使飞机从简单的木制桁架结构，演变成今天线条流畅的空气动力飞行器。再加上动力装置的持续发展，“飞行器”的结构也随之发生了显著变化。

一项关键发现推动了固定翼与旋翼飞机的发展：让气流流过弯曲表面的上方，就能产生“升力”。乔治·凯利（George Cayley）在19世纪初研制出高效的弯度翼型，并在此后于该世纪后期造出成功的载人滑翔机。他确立了飞行原理，包括升力、重力、推力与阻力的存在。正是凯利最早把机翼上下叠置，并在1853年造出一架载人飞行的三翼滑翔机。

更早的时候，凯利就研究过飞行器的重心，以及机翼上反角的影响。此外，他还在滑翔机上装设了最早的舵面形式，开创了对飞机方向的控制。

19世纪末，奥托·李林塔尔（Otto Lilienthal）在凯利的发现之上继续推进。他制造并亲自试飞自己的滑翔机，飞行次数超过2,000次。他的柳木蒙布飞机，机翼是依据对鸟类翅膀的大量研究设计而成。李林塔尔还率先在机翼和驾驶舱后方常规使用垂直尾翼和水平尾翼。最重要的是，李林塔尔证明了人能够飞行。

奥克塔夫·沙努特（Octave Chanute）是一位退休的铁路与桥梁工程师，19世纪90年代活跃于航空领域。他的兴趣如此浓厚，以至于他出版了名为《飞行机器的进展》（Progress in Flying Machines）的权威著作。这是他搜集和研究当时能获得的全部航空资料所做努力的结晶。在他人协助下，他制造了类似李林塔尔的滑翔机，随后又造出自己的滑翔机。除著述之外，沙努特还造出用钢丝作为机翼支撑的层叠机翼滑翔机，推动了飞机结构的发展。

莱特兄弟在1903年造出成功的动力飞机时，已经了解所有这些人的工作。作为同类中第一架载人升空的飞机，莱特飞行者（Wright Flyer）的机翼纤薄、覆有蒙布，安装在主要为木制的桁架结构上。机翼包含前梁和后梁，并用撑杆和钢丝共同支撑。莱特飞行者还采用了层叠（双副）机翼。

有动力的重于空气航空从莱特的设计发展而来。发明家和初出茅庐的飞行员开始自行制造飞机。早期许多飞机与莱特兄弟所造的相似，用木材和蒙布，以钢丝和撑杆支撑机翼结构。1909年，法国人路易·布莱里奥（Louis Bleriot）造出一架设计上有显著差异的飞机。他成功制造了单翼飞机。机翼仍由钢丝支撑，但一根从机身上方伸出的桅柱使机翼既能从上方、也能从下方得到支撑。这才使单副机翼所需的加长翼展成为可能。布莱里奥采用了普拉特桁架式机身框架。

更强劲的发动机研制出来，机体结构也随之改变，以利用这些好处。早在1910年，由于有了更强的动力装置把飞机向前推入天空，德国人胡戈·容克斯（Hugo Junkers）得以建造采用金属桁架结构和金属蒙皮的飞机。主结构以金属替代木材，免除了外部机翼支撑和张线的需要。他的J-1型也采用单副（单翼）机翼，而不是层叠机翼。

到第一次世界大战（WWI）之前，更强的发动机还使设计师能够研制更厚的机翼和更坚固的翼梁。机翼不再需要钢丝张线支撑。高弯度机翼上更平坦、位置更低的下翼面能产生更大升力。一战扩大了对大量可靠飞机的需求。当时主要用于侦察、以木材和金属桁架为骨架、多为蒙布的层叠翼后三点式飞机主宰了战时的天空，红男爵的福克Dr-1型即为典型。

20世纪20年代，金属在飞机制造中的使用增多。能够载货载客的机身被研制出来。早期飞船（水上飞机）借鉴造船业的船体式构造，为机身的半硬壳式（semimonocoque）构造提供了蓝图。桁架式设计逐渐淡出，更简洁干净的单翼布局成为主流。

进入20世纪30年代，全金属飞机伴随着更轻、更强的发动机出现。更大的半硬壳机身配以应力蒙皮机翼设计。桁架与蒙布飞机造得越来越少。第二次世界大战（WWII）催生了大量采用全金属技术的飞机设计。载油的厚机翼是常态，但对更高飞行速度的追求促使人们研制薄翼飞机，燃油改由机身携带。第一种复合材料结构飞机——德哈维兰“蚊”式（De Havilland Mosquito）——在机身构造中使用了轻木夹层材料。玻璃钢雷达罩也是在这一时期发展起来的。

二战之后，涡轮发动机的发展带来了更高高度的飞行。航空界普遍需要增压飞机。因此半硬壳结构必须造得更加坚固。全金属半硬壳机身结构经过改良，以提高强度并抵抗增压—释压循环引起的金属疲劳。圆角的窗和门开口被开发出来，以避免形成容易开裂的薄弱区域。整体机加工的铜合金铝蒙皮抗开裂，并允许采用更厚的蒙皮和受控的厚度渐变。机翼蒙皮结构的化学铣切提供了高强度和平滑的高性能表面。可变轮廓机翼因而更易制造。喷气旅行带来的飞行速度提高，要求更薄的机翼；机翼载荷也大幅增加。多梁式和盒形梁机翼设计应运而生。

20世纪60年代，为运送旅客而研制出越来越大的飞机。随着发动机技术改进，巨型喷气客机被设计并制造出来。当时的客机仍以铝为主、采用半硬壳机身，其庞大的尺寸促使人们寻找更轻、更强的材料来建造。波音客机系列中采用蜂窝结构板件，在不牺牲强度的前提下减轻了重量。最初，铝芯配铝蒙皮或玻璃钢蒙皮的夹层板被用于机翼壁板、飞行操纵面、客舱地板及其他部位。

从20世纪70年代至今，航空结构的特征是对蜂窝与泡沫芯夹层构件以及各种复合材料的使用稳步增加。先进工艺与材料组合使材料逐渐从铝转向碳纤维及其他高强度轻质材料。这些新材料经过专门设计，以满足飞机上各种部件的特定性能要求。许多机体结构有50%以上采用先进复合材料，有些机体已接近100%。“超轻喷气机”（VLJ）一词已用来描述几乎完全由先进复合材料制成的新一代喷气飞机。正如凯利、李林塔尔和莱特兄弟所用的建造方法与材料一样，非复合材料的铝制飞机结构也有可能会被淘汰。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 主谓被长后置定语隔开
**原句**：Advances in materials and processes used to construct aircraft have led to their evolution from simple wood truss structures to the sleek aerodynamic flying machines of today.
**拆解**：主语中心词 Advances + 介词短语 in materials and processes 作后置定语 + 过去分词短语 used to construct aircraft 再修饰 processes；谓语 have led to（现在完成时）；宾语 their evolution，后接 from A to B 的范围状语。
**要点**：主语后面挂了两层定语（介词短语 + 过去分词），谓语 have led to 一直拖到第 11 个词才出现。读长句时先锁定“中心名词 + 谓语”这条骨架，再回头补定语；processes 是 used to construct 的逻辑宾语，不是主语。

### ② 同位语从句 + set ... in motion
**原句**：The key discovery that “lift” could be created by passing air over the top of a curved surface set the development of fixed and rotary-wing aircraft in motion.
**拆解**：主语 The key discovery + that 引导的同位语从句（说明 discovery 的内容，从句谓语为 could be created 的被动语态，by passing... 表方式）；主句谓语 set，宾语 the development of fixed and rotary-wing aircraft，宾补 in motion（set sth. in motion 固定搭配）。
**要点**：that 从句紧跟在抽象名词 discovery 后，是同位语从句而非定语从句——它不缺成分，that 不作任何句子成分。set ... in motion 意为“使……启动”，宾语很长时 in motion 容易被误读成新分句的开头。

### ③ 强调句 It was ... who
**原句**：It was Cayley who first stacked wings and created a tri-wing glider that flew a man in 1853.
**拆解**：It was + 被强调的主语 Cayley + who 引导的剩余部分（并列谓语 first stacked wings and created a tri-wing glider）；glider 后接 that 定语从句，说明这架滑翔机曾载人飞行。
**要点**：强调句去掉 It was ... who 后仍是完整句子，可据此与 it 作形式主语的结构区分。句中同时出现 who 和 that 两个关系词：who 属于强调句框架，that 才是真正的定语从句，二者管辖范围不同。

### ④ but 连接的并列句 + 现在分词后置定语
**原句**：The wings were still supported by wires, but a mast extending above the fuselage enabled the wings to be supported from above, as well as underneath.
**拆解**：前一分句为被动语态 The wings were supported by wires；but 转折后主语 a mast，extending above the fuselage 为现在分词短语作后置定语，谓语 enabled，宾语 the wings，宾补 to be supported（不定式的被动式），from above, as well as underneath 为方式状语。
**要点**：enable sb./sth. to do 的宾语补足语用不定式；这里机翼是“被支撑”，所以用被动式 to be supported。extending 的逻辑主语是 a mast，不是 the wings——分词作定语时找逻辑主语要看它紧跟的名词。

### ⑤ with 复合结构作伴随状语
**原句**：Many airframe structures are made of more than 50 percent advanced composites, with some airframes approaching 100 percent.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 are made of；逗号后 with + 名词 some airframes + 现在分词 approaching 100 percent 构成 with 复合结构，作伴随状语补充说明程度差异。
**要点**：with 复合结构中，宾语与补足语是主动关系时用现在分词（approaching），被动或完成关系时用过去分词。该结构不构成独立分句，所以不能用逗号写成完整句子；中文常译成“有些机体已接近 100%”，用“而/其中”衔接。
