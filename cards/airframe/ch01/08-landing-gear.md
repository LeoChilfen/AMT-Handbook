# Landing Gear

> 机体 Ch 01 · Landing Gear · 原文 PDF 第 59 页

## 1. 原文

The landing gear supports the aircraft during landing and while it is on the ground. Simple aircraft that fly at low speeds generally have fixed gear. This means the gear is stationary and does not retract for flight. Faster, more complex aircraft have retractable landing gear. After takeoff, the landing gear is retracted into the fuselage or wings and out of the airstream. This is important because extended gear create significant parasite drag which reduces performance. Parasite drag is caused by the friction of the air flowing over the gear. It increases with speed. On very light, slow aircraft, the extra weight that accompanies a retractable landing gear is more of a detriment than the drag caused by the fixed gear. Lightweight fairings and wheel pants can be used to keep drag to a minimum. Figure 1-82 shows examples of fixed and retractable gear.

Landing gear must be strong enough to withstand the forces of landing when the aircraft is fully loaded. In addition to strength, a major design goal is to have the gear assembly be as light as possible. To accomplish this, landing gear are made from a wide range of materials including steel, aluminum, and magnesium. Wheels and tires are designed specifically for aviation use and have unique operating characteristics. Main wheel assemblies usually have a braking system. To aid with the potentially high impact of landing, most landing gear have a means of either absorbing shock or accepting shock and distributing it so that the structure is not damaged.

Not all aircraft landing gear are configured with wheels. Helicopters, for example, have such high maneuverability and low landing speeds that a set of fixed skids is common and quite functional with lower maintenance. The same is true for free balloons which fly slowly and land on wood skids affixed to the floor of the gondola. Other aircraft landing gear are equipped with pontoons or floats for operation on water. A large amount of drag accompanies this type of gear, but an aircraft that can land and take off on water can be very useful in certain environments. Even skis can be found under some aircraft for operation on snow and ice. Figure 1-83 shows some of these alternative landing gear, the majority of which are the fixed gear type.

Amphibious aircraft are aircraft than can land either on land or on water. On some aircraft designed for such dual usage, the bottom half of the fuselage acts as a hull. Usually, it is accompanied by outriggers on the underside of the wings near the tips to aid in water landing and taxi. Main gear that retract into the fuselage are only extended when landing on the ground or a runway. This type of amphibious aircraft is sometimes called a flying boat.

Many aircraft originally designed for land use can be fitted with floats with retractable wheels for amphibious use. Typically, the gear retracts into the float when not needed. Sometimes a dorsal fin is added to the aft underside of the fuselage for longitudinal stability during water operations. It is even possible on some aircraft to direct this type of fin by tying its control into the aircraft’s rudder pedals. Skis can also be fitted with wheels that retract to allow landing on solid ground or on snow and ice.

### Tail Wheel Gear Configuration

There are two basic configurations of airplane landing gear: conventional gear or tail wheel gear and the tricycle gear. Tail wheel gear dominated early aviation and therefore has become known as conventional gear. In addition to its two main wheels which are positioned under most of the weight of the aircraft, the conventional gear aircraft also has a smaller wheel located at the aft end of the fuselage. Often this tail wheel is able to be steered by rigging cables attached to the rudder pedals. Other conventional gear have no tail wheel at all using just a steel skid plate under the aft fuselage instead. The small tail wheel or skid plate allows the fuselage to incline, thus giving clearance for the long propellers that prevailed in aviation through WWII. It also gives greater clearance between the propeller and loose debris when operating on an unpaved runway. But the inclined fuselage blocks the straight-ahead vision of the pilot during ground operations. Until up to speed where the elevator becomes effective to lift the tail wheel off the ground, the pilot must lean his head out the side of the flight deck to see directly ahead of the aircraft.

The use of tail wheel gear can pose another difficulty. When landing, tail wheel aircraft can easily ground loop. A ground loop is when the tail of the aircraft swings around and comes forward of the nose of the aircraft. The reason this happens is due to the two main wheels being forward of the aircraft’s center of gravity. The tail wheel is aft of the center of gravity. If the aircraft swerves upon landing, the tail wheel can swing out to the side of the intended path of travel. If far enough to the side, the tail can pull the center of gravity out from its desired location slightly aft of but between the main gear. Once the center of gravity is no longer trailing the mains, the tail of the aircraft freely pivots around the main wheels causing the ground loop.

Conventional gear is useful and is still found on certain models of aircraft manufactured today, particularly aerobatic aircraft, crop dusters, and aircraft designed for unpaved runway use. It is typically lighter than tricycle gear which requires a stout, fully shock absorbing nose wheel assembly. The tail wheel configuration excels when operating out of unpaved runways. With the two strong main gear forward providing stability and directional control during takeoff roll, the lightweight tail wheel does little more than keep the aft end of the fuselage from striking the ground. As mentioned, at a certain speed, the air flowing over the elevator is sufficient for it to raise the tail off the ground. As speed increases further, the two main wheels under the center of gravity are very stable.

### Tricycle Gear Configuration

Tricycle gear is the most prevalent landing gear configuration in aviation. In addition to the main wheels, a shock absorbing nose wheel is at the forward end of the fuselage. Thus, the center of gravity is then forward of the main wheels. The tail of the aircraft is suspended off the ground and clear view straight ahead from the flight deck is given. Ground looping is nearly eliminated since the center of gravity follows the directional nose wheel and remains between the mains.

Light aircraft use tricycle gear, as well as heavy aircraft. Twin nose wheels on the single forward strut and massive multistrut/ multiwheel main gear may be found supporting the world’s largest aircraft, but the basic configuration is still tricycle. The nose wheel may be steered with the rudder pedals on small aircraft. Larger aircraft often have a nose wheel steering wheel located off to the side of the flight deck. ## 2. 中文翻译

起落架在飞机着陆时以及在地面期间支撑飞机。低速飞行的简单飞机一般采用固定式起落架，即起落架是固定的，飞行时不收上。速度更快、更复杂的飞机采用可收放起落架。起飞后，起落架收进机身或机翼内，脱离气流。这一点很重要，因为放下的起落架会产生相当大的废阻力，降低性能。废阻力由流过起落架的空气摩擦引起，并随速度增大而增大。在很轻、很慢的飞机上，可收放起落架带来的额外重量，比固定式起落架所造成的阻力更为不利。可用轻质整流罩和轮罩把阻力降到最低。图1-82 示出固定式和可收放式起落架的实例。

起落架必须具有足够的强度，以承受飞机满载着陆时的载荷。除强度之外，一个主要的设计目标是让起落架组件尽可能轻。为此，起落架采用多种材料制造，包括钢、铝和镁。机轮和轮胎是专为航空使用而设计的，具有独特的工作特性。主轮组件通常配有刹车系统。为了应对着陆时可能出现的很大冲击，大多数起落架都有某种手段来吸收冲击，或者接受冲击并将其分散，使结构不致损坏。

并非所有飞机的起落架都装有机轮。例如直升机机动性很高、着陆速度很低，因此一套固定式滑橇很常见，功能也很好，维护还更省事。自由气球也是如此，它飞得慢，落在固定在吊舱底板上的木制滑橇上。另一些飞机的起落架装有浮筒，以便在水面起降。这类起落架带来的阻力很大，但在某些环境中，能在水面起降的飞机非常有用。甚至有些飞机机下装有雪橇，用于在雪地和冰面上起降。图1-83 示出这类替代式起落架中的几种，其中大多数属于固定式。

水陆两栖飞机是既能着陆在陆地、也能着陆在水面的飞机。在某些为此种双重用途而设计的飞机上，机身下半部起船体的作用。通常还在机翼下表面靠近翼尖处装辅助浮筒，以帮助水上着陆和滑行。收入机身的起落架只有在地面或跑道上着陆时才放下。这类两栖飞机有时称为飞船（flying boat）。

许多原本为陆上使用而设计的飞机，可以加装带可收放机轮的浮筒，改为水陆两栖使用。通常，起落架在不需要时收进浮筒内。有时在机身尾部下方加装一片背鳍，以在水上作业时提供纵向稳定性。在某些飞机上，甚至可以把这类鳍的操纵连接到飞机的方向舵脚蹬上，使其可以操纵。雪橇也可以装上可收起的机轮，以便在硬地或雪地、冰面上着陆。

### 后三点式起落架布局

飞机起落架有两种基本布局：常规式（即后三点式）起落架和前三点式（tricycle）起落架。后三点式起落架在早期航空中占主导地位，因此被称为常规式起落架。除了位于飞机大部分重量之下的两个主轮之外，常规式起落架飞机在机身尾端还有一个较小的轮子。这个尾轮往往可以通过连接在方向舵脚蹬上的钢索操纵转向。另一些常规式起落架则完全没有尾轮，只在机身尾部下方装一块钢制滑板。小小的尾轮或滑板使机身呈上仰姿态，从而为二战结束前航空界普遍使用的大直径螺旋桨留出间隙。在未铺装跑道上工作时，它还能增大螺旋桨与松散碎屑之间的间隙。但机身的上仰会挡住飞行员在地面操作时的正前方视线。在加速到升降舵足以把尾轮抬离地面的速度之前，飞行员必须把头探出驾驶舱侧面，才能看到飞机正前方。

使用后三点式起落架还会带来另一个困难。着陆时，后三点式飞机很容易发生打地转（ground loop）。打地转是指飞机尾部甩过来、转到机头前方的现象。发生的原因是两个主轮位于飞机重心之前，而尾轮位于重心之后。若飞机在着陆时偏转，尾轮可能甩到预定行进路线的侧面；若偏出得足够远，机尾就会把重心从它应有的位置——主轮稍后、两主轮之间——拉出去。一旦重心不再位于主轮之后，机尾便绕主轮自由转动，造成打地转。

常规式起落架很有用，至今在某些型号的飞机上仍可见到，尤其是特技飞机、农用喷洒机和为未铺装跑道使用而设计的飞机。它通常比前三点式起落架轻，后者需要一个粗壮、能完全吸收冲击的前轮组件。在未铺装跑道上起降时，后三点式布局表现突出。两个强劲的主轮在前，在起飞滑跑中提供稳定性和方向控制，轻巧的尾轮则不过是不让机身尾部擦地而已。如前所述，达到一定速度时，流过升降舵的气流已足以把机尾抬离地面；速度继续增大后，位于重心之下的两个主轮非常稳定。

### 前三点式起落架布局

前三点式起落架是航空界最普遍的起落架布局。除主轮之外，机身前端还有一个起减震作用的前轮。因此重心位于主轮之前。飞机尾部悬离地面，驾驶舱正前方的视野开阔。由于重心跟随可转向的前轮，并保持在两主轮之间，打地转几乎得以消除。

轻型飞机使用前三点式起落架，重型飞机也一样。支撑世界上最大飞机的，可能是装在单根前支柱上的双前轮，以及庞大的多支柱、多轮主起落架，但其基本布局仍是前三点式。小型飞机的前轮可用方向舵脚蹬操纵转向；较大的飞机往往在驾驶舱一侧设有一个前轮转弯手轮。图1-87 示出采用前三点式起落架的飞机。第13章《飞机起落架系统》将详细讨论起落架。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① because 原因从句里再套定语从句
**原句**：This is important because extended gear create significant parasite drag which reduces performance.
**拆解**：主句 This is important 为主系表结构；because 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 extended gear（gear 单复数同形，此处指多组起落架，作复数），谓语 create，宾语 significant parasite drag；which 引导定语从句修饰 drag，从句谓语 reduces 为单数。
**要点**：gear 是不可数外观的集合名词，这里用复数谓语 create，说明作者把“放下的起落架”当作多个部件看待，不能因为拼写没有 -s 就改用 creates。which 的先行词是 drag 而不是 performance——减的是性能，但“降低性能”的是阻力。

### ② more of a A than B 的比较结构
**原句**：On very light, slow aircraft, the extra weight that accompanies a retractable landing gear is more of a detriment than the drag caused by the fixed gear.
**拆解**：句首 On very light, slow aircraft 为范围状语；主语 the extra weight 后接 that 引导的定语从句（谓语 accompanies，先行词 weight 为单数）；谓语 is；表语 more of a detriment，than 引出比较对象 the drag caused by the fixed gear，其中 caused by... 为过去分词短语后置修饰 drag。
**要点**：more of a A than B 不是普通的“比……更 A”，而是“与其说是 B，不如说是 A”，比较的是性质而非程度。that 从句的谓语 accompanies 用单数，可与前面 create 的复数用法对照记忆。

### ③ 目的状语 + either...or 并列动名词 + so that 从句
**原句**：To aid with the potentially high impact of landing, most landing gear have a means of either absorbing shock or accepting shock and distributing it so that the structure is not damaged.
**拆解**：句首不定式短语 To aid with... 作目的状语；主语 most landing gear 为复数，谓语 have；宾语 a means of 后接 either...or... 连接的动名词短语 absorbing shock 与 accepting shock and distributing it；so that 引导目的（结果）状语从句，从句为被动语态 the structure is not damaged。
**要点**：a means of 后必须接动名词，不能接不定式。either A or B 中 A、B 要形式对称，这里并列的都是动名词。so that 从句的主语 the structure 是 damage 的承受者，所以用被动；译为“使结构不致损坏”即可，不必死扣“以便”。

### ④ once 时间从句 + 分词结果状语
**原句**：Once the center of gravity is no longer trailing the mains, the tail of the aircraft freely pivots around the main wheels causing the ground loop.
**拆解**：Once 引导时间状语从句，从句用现在进行时 is trailing 表示持续的状态；主句主语 the tail of the aircraft，谓语 pivots，around the main wheels 为状语，causing the ground loop 为现在分词短语作结果状语。
**要点**：once 在此意为“一旦”，引导的是时间从句而非“一次”。mains 是 main wheels 的省略说法，航空英语中常见这种以形容词复数为名词的简写。causing 的逻辑主语不是 the main wheels，而是主句所述“机尾绕主轮转动”这一整体动作。

### ⑤ with 复合结构作状语 + do little more than
**原句**：With the two strong main gear forward providing stability and directional control during takeoff roll, the lightweight tail wheel does little more than keep the aft end of the fuselage from striking the ground.
**拆解**：句首为 with 复合结构：with + 宾语 the two strong main gear + 方位补足语 forward + 现在分词 providing...，整体作伴随（原因）状语；主句主语 the lightweight tail wheel，谓语 does，宾语 little more than keep...，其中 keep sth. from doing 为固定搭配。
**要点**：with 复合结构里可以同时出现副词补足语（forward）和分词补足语（providing），两者都说明宾语的状态，不构成独立分句。do little more than 后接动词原形、不带 to，意为“不过是……而已”，语气上强调尾轮作用有限。
