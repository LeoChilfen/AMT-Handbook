### Bleed Air Systems

Temperatures at high altitudes in which aircraft operate can be well below 0 °F. Combined with seasonally cold temperatures, this makes heating the cabin more than just a luxury. Pressurized aircraft that use air cycle air conditioning systems mix bleed air with cold air produced by the air cycle machine expansion turbine to obtain warm air for the cabin. This is discussed in the section that covers air cycle air conditioning in this chapter. Aircraft not equipped with air cycle air conditioning may be heated by one of a few possible methods.

Some turbine-powered aircraft not equipped with air cycle systems still make use of engine compressor bleed air to heat the cabin. Various arrangements exist. The bleed air is mixed with ambient air, or cabin return air, and distributed throughout the aircraft via ducting. The mixing of air can be done in a variety of ways. Mixing air valves, flow control valves, shutoff valves, and other various control valves are controlled by switches in the flight deck. One STC’d bleed air heat system uses mini-ejectors in helicopter cabins to of the aircraft’s generator output, which is better dedicated combine bleed air with cabin air. All of these bleed air heating to the operation of other electrical devices. For this reason, systems are simple and function well, as long as the valves, they are not very common. Electricity flowing through a heating element makes the element warm. A fan to blow air over the elements and into the cabin is used to transfer the heat. Other floor or sidewall elements simply radiate heat to warm the cabin.

### Exhaust Shroud Heaters

Most single-engine light aircraft use exhaust shroud heating systems to heat the cabin. Ambient air is directed into a metal shroud, or jacket, that encases part of the engine’s exhaust system. The air is warmed by the exhaust and directed through a firewall heater valve into the cabin. This simple solution requires no electrical or engine power and it makes use of heat that would otherwise be wasted. [Figures 16-96 and 16-97]

A major concern of exhaust shroud heat systems is the possibility that exhaust gases could contaminate the cabin air. Even the slightest crack in an exhaust manifold could send enough carbon monoxide into the cabin to be fatal. Strict inspection procedures are in place to minimize this threat. Most involve pressurizing the exhaust system with air, while inspecting for leaks with a soapy solution. Some require the exhaust to be removed and pressurized while submerged under water to detect any leaks. Frequency of exhaust heat leak detection can be every 100 hours.

Occasionally, the exhaust system is slightly modified in a shroud heat configuration. For example, an exhaust muffler may have numerous welded studs attached, which increase heat transfer to the cabin air. Each weld point is a location for a potential leak.

Regardless of age or condition, aircraft with exhaust shroud heating systems should contain a carbon monoxide detection device in the flight deck.

### Combustion Heaters

An aircraft combustion heater is used on many small to medium sized aircraft. It is a heat source independent from the aircraft’s engine(s), although it does use fuel from the aircraft’s main fuel system. Combustion heaters are manufactured by a few different companies that supply the aviation industry. Most are similar to the description that follows. The most up to date units have electronic ignition and temperature control switches.

Combustion heaters are similar to exhaust shroud heaters in that ambient air is heated and sent to the cabin. The source of heat in this case is an independent combustion chamber located inside the cylindrical outer shroud of the heater unit. The correct amount of fuel and air are ignited in the air-tight inner chamber. The exhaust from combustion is funneled overboard. Ambient air is directed between the combustion chamber and the outer shroud. It absorbs the combustion heat by convection and is channeled into the cabin. Refer to Figure 16-100 for the following descriptions of the combustion heater subsystems and heater operation.

#### Combustion Air System

The air used in the combustion process is ambient air scooped from outside the aircraft, or from the compartment in which the combustion heater is mounted. A blower ensures that the correct quantity and pressure of air are sent into the chamber.

Some units have regulators or a relief valve to ensure these parameters. The combustion air is completely separate from the air that is warmed and sent into the cabin.

#### Ventilating Air System

Ventilating air is the name of the air that is warmed and sent into the cabin. Typically, it comes into the combustion heater through a ram air intake. When the aircraft is on the ground, a ventilating air fan controlled by a landing gear squat switch operates to draw in the air. Once airborne, the fan ceases to operate as the ram air flow is sufficient. Ventilating air passes between the combustion chamber and the outer shroud of the combustion heater where it is warmed and sent to the cabin.

#### Fuel System

As mentioned, fuel for the combustion heater is drawn from an aircraft fuel tank. A constant pressure fuel pump with relief valve pulls the fuel through a filter. A main solenoid valve downstream delivers the fuel to the unit. The solenoid is controlled by the cabin heater switch in the flight deck and three safety switches located on the combustion heater. The first safety switch is a duct limit switch that keeps the valve closed should the unit not have enough ventilating airflow to keep it within the correct operating temperature range. The second is a pressure switch that must sense pressure from the combustion air fan to allow the solenoid to open. Fuel is delivered to the combustion chamber only if there is air there with which it can be mixed. Finally, an overheat switch also controls the main fuel supply solenoid. When an over temperature condition occurs, it closes the solenoid to stop the supply of fuel.

A secondary solenoid is located downstream of the main fuel supply solenoid. It is part of a fuel control unit that also houses a pressure regulator and an additional fuel filter. The valve opens and closes on command from the combustion heater thermostat. During normal operation, the heater cycles on and off by opening and closing this solenoid at the entrance to the combustion chamber. When opened, fuel flows through a nozzle that sprays it into the combustion chamber.

#### Ignition System

Most combustion heaters have an ignition unit designed to receive aircraft voltage and step it up to fire a spark plug located in the combustion chamber. Older combustion heaters use vibrator-type ignition units. Modern units have electronic ignition. The ignition is continuous when activated. This occurs when the heater switch is placed in the ON position in the flight deck, and the combustion air blower builds sufficient air pressure in the combustion chamber. Use of the proper spark plug for the combustion heater is essential. Check the manufacturer’s approved data.

#### Controls

The combustion heater controls consist of a cabin heat switch and a thermostat. The cabin heat switch starts the fuel pump, opens the main fuel supply solenoid, and turns on the combustion air fan, as well as the ventilating air fan if the aircraft is on the ground. When the combustion air fan builds pressure, it allows the ignition unit to start. The thermostat sends power to open the fuel control solenoid when heat is needed. This triggers combustion in the unit and heat is delivered to the cabin. When the preselected temperature is reached, the thermostat cuts power to the fuel control solenoid and combustion stops. Ventilating air continues to circulate and carry heat away. When the temperature level falls to that below which the thermostat is set, the combustion heater cycles on again.

#### Safety Features

Various automatic combustion heater controls prevent operation of the heater when dangerous conditions exist. As stated, a duct limit switch cuts off fuel to the heater when there is not enough airflow to keep the heater duct below a preset temperature. This is usually caused by a lack of ventilating air flow. An overheat switch set at a higher temperature than the duct limit switch guards against overheat of any kind. It is designed to cut fuel to the combustion heater before an unwanted fire occurs. When this switch activates, a light is illuminated in the flight deck and the heater cannot be restarted until maintenance determines the cause. Some heaters contain a circuit to prevent fuel from being delivered to the combustion chamber if the ignition system is not working.

#### Maintenance & Inspection

Maintenance of combustion heaters consists of routine items, such as cleaning filters, checking spark plug wear, and ensuring inlets are not plugged. All maintenance and inspection of combustion heaters should be accomplished in accordance with the aircraft manufacturer’s instructions. Combustion heater manufacturers also produce maintenance guidelines that should be followed. Intervals between the performance of maintenance items and the time between overhauls must be followed to help ensure a properly functioning heater is available when it is needed.

Inspection of the combustion heater should be performed on schedule as provided by the manufacturer or whenever a malfunction is suspected. Inlets and outlets should be clear. All controls should be checked for freedom of operation and function. Close observation for any sign of fuel leaks or cracks in the combustion chamber and/or shroud should be made. All components should be secure. An operational check can also be made. Follow the manufacturer’s inspection criteria to ensure the combustion heater is in airworthy condition.

### 引气系统

飞机运行所在的高空，温度可远低于 0 °F。再叠加季节性低温，客舱加温就远不只是舒适性配置。采用空气循环式空调系统的增压飞机，把引气与空气循环机膨胀涡轮产生的冷气混合，从而得到供客舱使用的暖空气。这一点在本章讲述空气循环空调的一节中讨论。未装空气循环空调的飞机，可用下面几种方法之一加温。

有些未装空气循环系统的涡轮动力飞机，仍利用发动机压气机引气来加温客舱，其具体布置有多种。引气与外界空气或客舱回气混合，经管路分配到飞机各处。混合空气的方式可以多种多样。混合空气活门、流量控制活门、关断活门及其他各类控制活门，均由驾驶舱内的开关控制。有一种取得 STC（补充型号合格证）的引气加温系统，在直升机客舱内用小型引射器把引气与客舱空气混合。只要活门、管路和控制器处于可用状态，这些引气加温系统都简单且工作良好。

### 电加热系统

电加热元件式加热器需要消耗飞机发电机相当一部分输出功率，而这份功率更适合专供其他用电设备，因此这类加热器并不常见。不过，在地面由地面电源供电时使用它们，可在乘客登机前预热客舱，且不会加重机上电气系统负担。电加热装置有时也用来给飞机加温：电流通过加热元件使元件发热，再用风扇把空气吹过元件并送入客舱，以此传递热量；另一些装在地板或侧壁上的元件则直接辐射热量来加温客舱。

### 排气护套式加热器

多数单发轻型飞机用排气护套式加热系统加温客舱。外界空气被引入一个金属护套（jacket），护套包覆发动机排气系统的一部分。空气被排气加热后，经防火墙加热活门送入客舱。这一简单方案既不需要电源，也不需要发动机功率，而且利用了本来会白白浪费掉的热量。[图 16-96 与 16-97]

排气护套式加热系统的一大隐患，是排气有可能污染客舱空气。排气歧管上哪怕最细微的裂纹，也可能把足以致命的一氧化碳送进客舱。为把这一威胁降到最低，已有严格的检查程序：多数做法是给排气系统充气，同时用皂液检查是否渗漏；有些则要求拆下排气系统，浸在水中加压以查找漏点。排气热交换器漏气检测的周期可以是每 100 小时一次。

有时，排气系统在护套加温构型下会做少量改装。例如排气消声器上可能焊有许多焊柱，以增大向客舱空气的传热；而每个焊点都是潜在的漏点。

无论机龄或状况如何，装有排气护套式加热系统的飞机，驾驶舱内都应配有一氧化碳探测装置。

### 燃烧式加热器

燃烧式加热器用于许多中小型飞机。它是一种独立于飞机发动机的热源，尽管它确实使用飞机主燃油系统的燃油。燃烧式加热器由几家为航空业供货的公司制造，产品大多与下面的描述相似。最新型号采用电子点火和温度控制开关。

燃烧式加热器与排气护套式加热器的相似之处在于：都是把外界空气加热后送入客舱。不同之处在于热源——加热器圆柱形外罩内部有一个独立的燃烧室。适量的燃油与空气在气密的内腔中点燃，燃烧产生的废气被引到机外。外界空气则在燃烧室与外罩之间流过，以对流方式吸收燃烧热后被导入客舱。以下关于燃烧式加热器各分系统及加热器工作的描述，参见图 16-100。

#### 燃烧空气系统

燃烧过程所用的空气，是从机外或从安装燃烧式加热器的舱内采集的外界空气。鼓风机确保送入燃烧室的空气量和压力合适。

有些型号用调节器或释压活门来保证这些参数。燃烧用空气与被加热送入客舱的空气完全隔离。

#### 通风空气系统

通风空气指的是被加热后送入客舱的那部分空气。它通常经冲压进气口进入燃烧式加热器。飞机在地面时，由起落架地面开关（squat switch）控制的通风风扇工作，把空气抽入；升空后冲压气流已足够，风扇停止工作。通风空气从燃烧室与加热器外罩之间通过，被加热后送入客舱。

#### 燃油系统

如前所述，燃烧式加热器所用燃油取自飞机燃油箱。一台带释压活门的恒压燃油泵把燃油抽过过滤器，下游的主电磁活门再把燃油送入加热器。该电磁活门由驾驶舱内的客舱加温开关和装在加热器上的三个安全开关控制。第一个安全开关是管道限温开关：当通风气流不足以把加热器温度维持在正确的工作范围内时，它使活门保持关闭。第二个是压力开关，必须感受到燃烧空气风扇的压力，才允许电磁活门打开——只有燃烧室里有了可与燃油混合的空气，燃油才会被送入。最后，过热开关也控制主供油电磁活门：出现过温时，它关闭电磁活门，切断供油。

主供油电磁活门下游还有一个副电磁活门，它属于燃油控制组件，该组件内还装有压力调节器和另一个燃油过滤器。这个活门按燃烧式加热器恒温器的指令开闭。正常工作时，加热器就是靠开闭燃烧室入口处的这个电磁活门来循环通断的。活门打开时，燃油经喷嘴喷入燃烧室。

#### 点火系统

多数燃烧式加热器有一个点火组件，用来接收飞机电压并升压，以点燃位于燃烧室内的火花塞。较老的燃烧式加热器使用振动器式点火组件，现代产品则采用电子点火。点火在激活后是连续的：当驾驶舱内的加热器开关置于 ON 位，且燃烧空气鼓风机在燃烧室内建立起足够气压时，点火即开始。使用与燃烧式加热器相匹配的火花塞至关重要，应查阅制造厂批准的数据。

#### 控制

燃烧式加热器的控制由客舱加温开关和恒温器组成。客舱加温开关启动燃油泵、打开主供油电磁活门、接通燃烧空气风扇；如果飞机在地面，还接通通风风扇。燃烧空气风扇建立压力后，点火组件才得以启动。需要热量时，恒温器给燃油控制电磁阀送电，触发加热器内燃烧，把热量送入客舱。达到预选温度后，恒温器切断燃油控制电磁阀的电源，燃烧停止，通风空气继续循环并把余热带走。当温度降到恒温器设定值以下时，燃烧式加热器再次循环启动。

#### 安全特性

各种自动控制装置会在出现危险工况时阻止加热器工作。如前所述，管道限温开关在气流不足以把加热器管道温度保持在预设值以下时切断供油，这通常由通风气流不足引起。整定温度高于管道限温开关的过热开关，则防范任何形式的过热，其设计目的是在发生意外着火之前切断燃烧式加热器的供油。该开关动作时，驾驶舱内会亮起指示灯，且在维修人员查明原因之前加热器无法重新启动。有些加热器还设有联锁电路：点火系统不工作时，燃油不会送入燃烧室。

#### 维护与检查

燃烧式加热器的维护都是例行项目，例如清洁过滤器、检查火花塞磨损、确认进气口未堵塞。燃烧式加热器的全部维护与检查，都应按照飞机制造厂的说明执行；燃烧式加热器制造厂也会发布应予遵循的维护指南。必须遵守各项维护工作的间隔和翻修时限，以确保需要时有一台工作正常的加热器可用。

应按制造厂规定的周期、或在怀疑有故障时，对燃烧式加热器进行检查。进气口和出气口应通畅；所有控制装置都应检查操作是否灵活、功能是否正常；应仔细观察燃烧室和/或外罩有无燃油渗漏或裂纹迹象；所有部件应固定牢靠。还可以做功能检查。按制造厂的检查标准执行，确保燃烧式加热器处于适航状态。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 主语被定语从句隔开 + 过去分词后置定语 + 目的不定式
**原句**：Pressurized aircraft that use air cycle air conditioning systems mix bleed air with cold air produced by the air cycle machine expansion turbine to obtain warm air for the cabin.
**拆解**：主语 Pressurized aircraft 后接 that 引导的限制性定语从句（use air cycle air conditioning systems）；主句谓语 mix，宾语 bleed air，with 短语引出混合的另一方 cold air；cold air 后由过去分词短语 produced by the air cycle machine expansion turbine 作后置定语；句末 to obtain warm air for the cabin 为不定式作目的状语。
**要点**：定语从句把主语和谓语 mix 隔开，读长句时先抓住 aircraft ... mix ... bleed air 这条主干。produced by 紧跟 cold air，修饰的是冷气而不是引气——分词作定语按紧邻原则确定修饰对象。目的状语不定式的逻辑主语是句子的主语（飞机）。

### ② 被动语态 + or 引出的同位语 + 定语从句
**原句**：Ambient air is directed into a metal shroud, or jacket, that encases part of the engine’s exhaust system.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 Ambient air is directed into a metal shroud；or jacket 用逗号隔开，是 shroud 的同位语，给出同义术语；that 引导定语从句修饰 shroud（jacket），从句谓语 encases，宾语 part of the engine’s exhaust system。
**要点**：or 在这里不是选择连词，而是“或称、即”引出同位语，不要把 jacket 当成与 shroud 并列的另一个宾语。that 从句的先行词是护套，不是空气（air 是单数不可数，逻辑上也说不通）。被动句未出现 by 短语，因为施动者无关紧要，中文宜译成“被引入”。

### ③ enough ... to 结构表结果
**原句**：Even the slightest crack in an exhaust manifold could send enough carbon monoxide into the cabin to be fatal.
**拆解**：主语 the slightest crack，in an exhaust manifold 为介词短语作后置定语；谓语 could send，宾语 enough carbon monoxide（enough 作前置定语修饰不可数名词），into the cabin 为方向状语；to be fatal 与 enough 呼应，作结果状语。
**要点**：enough 修饰名词时前置（enough carbon monoxide），修饰形容词时才后置。to be fatal 的逻辑主语是一氧化碳，不是裂纹——译文“足以致命的一氧化碳”正是这个关系；若译成“裂纹致命”就把主语弄错了。

### ④ 动名词作宾语 + while 引导的分词状语
**原句**：Most involve pressurizing the exhaust system with air, while inspecting for leaks with a soapy solution.
**拆解**：主语 Most（指多数检查程序），谓语 involve，宾语是动名词短语 pressurizing the exhaust system with air；逗号后 while + 现在分词 inspecting 作伴随（同时）状语，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致，相当于 while they are inspecting。
**要点**：involve 后接动名词而不接不定式，这是固定搭配。while 引导的分词状语省略了主语和 be 动词，判断逻辑主语要回到主句主语。with a soapy solution 是方式状语，不是 with 复合结构——它后面没有名词加补足语。

### ⑤ 定语从句 + 省略 if 的虚拟条件倒装
**原句**：The first safety switch is a duct limit switch that keeps the valve closed should the unit not have enough ventilating airflow to keep it within the correct operating temperature range.
**拆解**：主句为主系表结构 The first safety switch is a duct limit switch；that 引导定语从句，谓语 keeps，宾语 the valve，宾补 closed；should the unit not have ... 是省略 if 的条件状语从句倒装（= if the unit should not have ...），to keep it within ... 为目的状语。
**要点**：should 提到句首、if 省略，是技术文件与法规文本里常见的虚拟条件倒装，遇到句首 should 要先按“如果”理解。keeps the valve closed 是 keep + 宾语 + 过去分词作宾补，宾语与补足语是被动关系；代词 it 指 the unit（加热器）。

### ⑥ 时间状语从句 + “介词 + which” 定语从句
**原句**：When the temperature level falls to that below which the thermostat is set, the combustion heater cycles on again.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，falls to that，其中 that 是指示代词，指代前面的温度值；below which the thermostat is set 为“介词 + which”引导的定语从句，修饰 that；主句为 the combustion heater cycles on again。
**要点**：这里的 that 不是关系代词，真正的定语从句由 below which 引出，which 的先行词就是这个 that，等于 the temperature below which ...。cycle on 意为“循环启动”，与下文的 cycles on and off（循环通断）是同一套动作。
