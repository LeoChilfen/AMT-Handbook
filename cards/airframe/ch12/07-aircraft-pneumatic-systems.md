## 1. 原文

Some aircraft manufacturers have equipped their aircraft with a high pressure pneumatic system (3,000 psi) in the past. The last aircraft to utilize this type of system was the Fokker F27. Such systems operate a great deal like hydraulic systems, except they employ air instead of a liquid for transmitting power. Pneumatic systems are sometimes used for:

• Opening and closing doors.

• Driving hydraulic pumps, alternators, starters, water injection pumps, etc.

• Operating emergency devices.

Both pneumatic and hydraulic systems are similar units and use confined fluids. The word confined means trapped or completely enclosed. The word fluid implies such liquids as water, oil, or anything that flows. Since both liquids and gases flow, they are considered as fluids; however, there is a great deal of difference in the characteristics of the two. Liquids are practically incompressible; a quart of water still occupies about a quart of space regardless of how hard it is compressed. But gases are highly compressible; a quart of air can be compressed into a thimbleful of space. In spite of this difference, gases and liquids are both fluids and can be confined and made to transmit power. The type of unit used to provide pressurized air for pneumatic systems is determined by the system’s air pressure requirements.

### High-Pressure Systems

For high-pressure systems, air is usually stored in metal bottles at pressures ranging from 1,000 to 3,000 psi, depending on the particular system. This type of air bottle has two valves, one of which is a charging valve. A ground-operated compressor can be connected to this valve to add air to the bottle. The other valve is a control valve. It acts as a shutoff valve, keeping air trapped inside the bottle until the system is operated. Although the high-pressure storage cylinder is light in weight, it has a definite disadvantage. Since the system cannot be recharged during flight, operation is limited by the small supply of bottled air. Such an arrangement cannot be used for the continuous operation of a system. Instead, the supply of bottled air is reserved for emergency operation of such systems as the landing gear or brakes. The usefulness of this type of system is increased, however, if other air-pressurizing units are added to the aircraft.

#### Pneumatic System Components

Pneumatic systems are often compared to hydraulic systems, but such comparisons can only hold true in general terms. Pneumatic systems do not utilize reservoirs, hand pumps, accumulators, regulators, or engine-driven or electrically driven power pumps for building normal pressure. But similarities do exist in some components.

Air Compressors On some aircraft, permanently installed air compressors have been added to recharge air bottles whenever pressure is used for operating a unit. Several types of compressors are used for this purpose. Some have two stages of compression, while others have three, depending on the maximum desired operating pressure.

Relief Valves Relief valves are used in pneumatic systems to prevent damage. They act as pressure limiting units and prevent excessive pressures from bursting lines and blowing out seals.

Control Valves Control valves are also a necessary part of a typical pneumatic system. Figure 12-72 illustrates how a valve is used to control emergency air brakes. The control valve consists of a three-port housing, two poppet valves, and a control lever with two lobes.

In Figure 12-72A, the control valve is shown in the off position. A spring holds the left poppet closed so that compressed air entering the pressure port cannot flow to the brakes. In Figure 12-72B, the control valve has been placed in the on position. One lobe of the lever holds the left poppet open, and a spring closes the right poppet. Compressed air now flows around the opened left poppet, through a drilled passage, and into a chamber below the right poppet. Since the right poppet is closed, the high-pressure air flows out of the brake port and into the brake line to apply the brakes.

To release the brakes, the control valve is returned to the off position. The left poppet now closes, stopping the flow of high-pressure air to the brakes. At the same time, the right poppet is opened, allowing compressed air in the brake line to exhaust through the vent port and into the atmosphere.

Check Valves Check valves are used in both hydraulic and pneumatic systems. Figure 12-73 illustrates a flap-type pneumatic check valve. Air enters the left port of the check valve, compresses a light spring, forcing the check valve open and allowing air to flow out of the right port. But if air enters from the right, air pressure closes the valve, preventing a flow of air out of the left port. Thus, a pneumatic check valve is a one-direction flow control valve.

#### Restrictors

Restrictors are a type of control valve used in pneumatic systems. Figure 12-74 illustrates an orifice-type restrictor with a large inlet port and a small outlet port. The small outlet port reduces the rate of airflow and the speed of operation of an actuating unit.

Variable Restrictor Another type of speed-regulating unit is the variable restrictor. It contains an adjustable needle valve, which has threads around the top and a point on the lower end. Depending on the direction turned, the needle valve moves the sharp point either into or out of a small opening to decrease or increase the size of the opening. Since air entering the inlet port must pass through this opening before reaching the outlet port, this adjustment also determines the rate of airflow through the restrictor.

Filters Pneumatic systems are protected against dirt by means of various types of filters. A micronic filter consists of a housing with two ports, a replaceable cartridge, and a relief valve. Normally, air enters the inlet, circulates around the cellulose cartridge, and flows to the center of the cartridge and out the outlet port. If the cartridge becomes clogged with dirt, pressure forces the relief valve open and allows unfiltered air to flow out the outlet port.

A screen-type filter is similar to the micron filter but contains a permanent wire screen instead of a replaceable cartridge. In the screen filter, a handle extends through the top of the housing and can be used to clean the screen by rotating it against metal scrapers.

Desiccant/Moisture Separator The moisture separator in a pneumatic system is always located downstream of the compressor. Its purpose is to remove any moisture caused by the compressor. A complete moisture separator consists of a reservoir, a pressure switch, a dump valve, and a check valve. It may also include a regulator and a relief valve. The dump valve is energized and deenergized by the pressure switch. When deenergized, it completely purges the separator reservoir and lines up to the compressor. The check valve protects the system against pressure loss during the dumping cycle and prevents reverse flow through the separator.

Chemical Drier Chemical driers are incorporated at various locations in a pneumatic system. Their purpose is to absorb any moisture that may collect in the lines and other parts of the system. Each drier contains a cartridge that should be blue in color. If otherwise noted, the cartridge is to be considered contaminated with moisture and should be replaced.

#### Emergency Backup Systems

Many aircraft use a high-pressure pneumatic back-up source of power to extend the landing gear or actuate the brakes, if the main hydraulic braking system fails. The nitrogen is not directly used to actuate the landing gear actuators or brake units but, instead, it applies the pressurized nitrogen to move hydraulic fluid to the actuator. This process is called pneudraulics. The following paragraph discusses the components and operation of an emergency pneumatic landing gear extension system used on a business jet.

Nitrogen used for emergency landing gear extension is stored in two bottles, one bottle located on each side of the nose wheel well. Nitrogen from the bottles is released by actuation of an outlet valve. Once depleted, the bottles must be recharged by maintenance personnel. Fully serviced pressure is approximately 3,100 psi at 70 °F/21 °C, enough for only one extension of the landing gear.

The outlet valve is connected to a cable and handle assembly.

The handle is located on the side of the copilot’s console and is labeled EMER LDG GEAR. Pulling the handle fully upward opens the outlet valve, releasing compressed nitrogen into the landing gear extension system. Pushing the handle fully downward closes the outlet valve and allows any nitrogen present in the emergency landing gear extension system to be vented overboard. The venting process takes approximately 30 seconds.

As compressed nitrogen is released to the landing gear selector/dump valve during emergency extension, the pneudraulic pressure actuates the dump valve portion of the landing gear selector/dump valve to isolate the landing gear system from the remainder of hydraulic system. When activated, a blue DUMP legend is illuminated on the LDG GR DUMP V switch, located on the flight deck overhead panel. A dump valve reset switch is used to reset the dump valve after the system has been used and serviced.

2. Red light in the landing gear control handle is 3. EMER LDG GEAR handle is pulled fully outward.

4. Compressed nitrogen is released to the landing gear 5. Pneudraulic pressure actuates the dump valve portion of the landing gear selector/dump valve.

13. Landing gear doors remain open.

### Medium-Pressure Systems

A medium-pressure pneumatic system (50–150 psi) usually does not include an air bottle. Instead, it generally draws air from the compressor section of a turbine engine. This process is often called bleed air and is used to provide pneumatic power for engine starts, engine deicing, wing deicing, and in some cases, it provides hydraulic power to the aircraft systems (if the hydraulic system is equipped with an air-driven hydraulic pump). Engine bleed air is also used to pressurize the reservoirs of the hydraulic system. Bleed air systems are discussed in more detail in the powerplant handbook.

### Low-Pressure Systems

Many aircraft equipped with reciprocating engines obtain a supply of low-pressure air from vane-type pumps. These pumps are driven by electric motors or by the aircraft engine. Figure 12-77 shows a schematic view of one of these pumps, which consists of a housing with two ports, a drive shaft, and two vanes. The drive shaft and the vanes contain slots, so the vanes can slide back and forth through the drive shaft. The shaft is eccentrically mounted in the housing, causing the vanes to form four different sizes of chambers (A, B, C, and D). In the position shown, B is the largest chamber and is connected to the supply port. As depicted in Figure 12-77, outside air can enter chamber B of the pump. When the pump begins to operate, the drive shaft rotates and changes positions of the vanes and sizes of the chambers. Vane No. 1 then moves to the right, separating chamber B from the supply port. Chamber B now contains trapped air.

As the shaft continues to turn, chamber B moves downward and becomes increasingly smaller, gradually compressing its air. Near the bottom of the pump, chamber B connects to the pressure port and sends compressed air into the pressure line. Then chamber B moves upward again becoming increasingly larger in area. At the supply port, it receives another supply of air. There are four such chambers in this pump and each goes through this same cycle of operation. Thus, the pump delivers to the pneumatic system a continuous supply of compressed air from 1 to 10 psi. Low-pressure systems are used for wing deicing boot systems.

### Pneumatic Power System Maintenance

Maintenance of the pneumatic power system consists of servicing, troubleshooting, removal, and installation of components, and operational testing.

The air compressor’s lubricating oil level should be checked daily in accordance with the applicable manufacturer’s instructions. The oil level is indicated by means of a sight gauge or dipstick. When refilling the compressor oil tank, the oil (type specified in the applicable instructions manual) is added until the specified level. After the oil is added, ensure that the filler plug is torqued, and safety wire is properly installed.

The pneumatic system should be purged periodically to remove the contamination, moisture, or oil from the components and lines. Purging the system is accomplished by pressurizing it and removing the plumbing from various components throughout the system. Removal of the pressurized lines causes a high rate of airflow through the system, causing foreign matter to be exhausted from the system. If an excessive amount of foreign matter, particularly oil, is exhausted from any one system, the lines and components should be removed and cleaned or replaced.

Upon completion of pneumatic system purging and after reconnecting all the system components, the system air bottles should be drained to exhaust any moisture or impurities that may have accumulated there.

After draining the air bottles, service the system with nitrogen or clean, dry compressed air. The system should then be given a thorough operational check and an inspection for leaks and security.

## 2. 中文翻译

过去，一些飞机制造商曾为其飞机装备高压气动系统（3,000 psi）。最后一种采用此类系统的飞机是福克 F27。这类系统的工作方式与液压系统非常相似，只是用空气而非液体来传递动力。气动系统有时用于：

• 开、关舱门。

• 驱动液压泵、交流发电机、起动机、喷水泵等。

• 操纵应急装置。

气动系统与液压系统是相似的装置，都使用被约束的流体（confined fluid）。“被约束”一词意为被封闭或完全包围；“流体”一词则指水、油之类可流动的液体。由于液体和气体都能流动，二者都被视为流体；但两者的特性差异很大。液体实际上是不可压缩的：一夸脱水无论被压得多厉害，仍占据约一夸脱的空间。气体则可高度压缩：一夸脱空气可被压进一个顶针大小的空间。尽管有这种差异，气体和液体都是流体，都能被约束起来传递动力。用于为气动系统提供增压空气的装置类型，取决于该系统的空气压力要求。

### 高压系统

对于高压系统，空气通常储存在金属气瓶中，压力依具体系统而定，在 1,000 至 3,000 psi 之间。这类气瓶有两个活门，其中之一是充气活门。可把地面操作的压缩机接到该活门上向气瓶充气。另一个活门是控制活门，起关断活门的作用，在系统工作之前把空气封闭在气瓶内。高压储气瓶虽然重量轻，但有一个明显的缺点：由于系统在空中无法再充气，工作时间受瓶装空气量少的限制。这种配置不能用于系统的连续工作，瓶装空气只留作起落架或刹车等系统的应急操作用。不过，若在飞机上增设其他空气增压装置，这类系统的实用性就会提高。

#### 气动系统部件

气动系统常被拿来与液压系统比较，但这种比较只能在大体上说成立。气动系统不利用储液罐、手摇泵、蓄压器、调压器，也不用发动机驱动泵或电动泵来建立正常压力。但在某些部件上确实存在相似之处。

空气压缩机　有些飞机加装了固定安装的空气压缩机，以便在压力被用于操纵某个装置后为气瓶补充充气。用于此目的的压缩机有几种类型：有的为两级压缩，有的为三级压缩，取决于所要求的最大工作压力。

释压活门　气动系统中使用释压活门以防止损坏。它们起限制压力的作用，防止过高的压力使管路爆裂、把密封件吹出。

控制活门　控制活门也是典型气动系统的必要组成部分。图 12-72 示出一个活门如何用于控制应急气动刹车。该控制活门由一个三通壳体、两个提升活门（poppet valve）以及一个带两个凸轮瓣的操纵手柄组成。

图 12-72A 中，控制活门处于关断位置。弹簧使左侧提升活门保持关闭，因此进入压力口的压缩空气无法流向刹车。图 12-72B 中，控制活门已被置于接通位置。手柄的一个凸轮瓣把左侧提升活门顶开，弹簧则使右侧提升活门关闭。压缩空气此时绕过被打开的左侧提升活门，经一条钻出的通道，进入右侧提升活门下方的腔室。由于右侧提升活门关闭，高压空气从刹车口流出，进入刹车管路，从而施加刹车。

要松开刹车，将控制活门扳回关断位置。左侧提升活门随即关闭，切断通往刹车的高压空气流。与此同时右侧提升活门被打开，使刹车管路中的压缩空气经通气口排入大气。

单向活门　单向活门在液压系统和气动系统中都有使用。图 12-73 示出一种簧片式（flap-type）气动单向活门。空气从单向活门左侧的接口进入，压缩一根轻弹簧，迫使单向活门打开，让空气从右侧接口流出。但若空气从右侧进入，气压会把活门关闭，阻止空气从左侧接口流出。因此，气动单向活门是一种单向流量控制活门。

#### 限流器

限流器是气动系统中的一种控制活门。图 12-74 示出一种孔板式限流器，其进口大、出口小。小出口降低了空气流量和作动装置的工作速度。

可变限流器　另一类速度调节装置是可变限流器。它含有一个可调针阀，针阀上部有螺纹，下端为锥尖。根据旋转方向的不同，针阀把锥尖移入或移出一个小孔，从而减小或增大该孔的流通面积。由于从进口进入的空气在到达出口之前必须经过这个小孔，这一调节也就决定了通过限流器的空气流量。

滤器　气动系统通过各种类型的滤器防止脏物侵入。微粒滤器（micronic filter）由一个带两个接口的壳体、一个可更换滤芯和一个释压活门组成。正常情况下，空气从进口进入，绕纤维素滤芯环流，流向滤芯中心，再从出口流出。如果滤芯被脏物堵塞，压力会顶开释压活门，让未过滤的空气从出口流出。

网式滤器与微粒滤器相似，但内部是永久性的金属丝网，而不是可更换滤芯。网式滤器有一根手柄穿过壳体顶部，可旋转丝网使其与金属刮片摩擦，从而清洁丝网。

干燥剂/水分离器　气动系统中的水分离器总是装在压缩机的下游。其作用是除去压缩机所产生的水分。完整的水分离器由储液罐、压力电门、排放活门和单向活门组成，还可能包括一个调压器和一个释压活门。排放活门由压力电门通电和断电。断电时，它把分离器储液罐及通往压缩机的管路彻底吹除。单向活门在排放循环中保护系统免于失压，并防止气流经分离器倒流。

化学干燥器　化学干燥器装在气动系统的各个位置。其作用是吸收管路和系统其他部位可能积聚的水分。每个干燥器内装有一个滤芯，滤芯应呈蓝色。若颜色不符，即应认为滤芯已被水分污染，应予以更换。

#### 应急备份系统

许多飞机采用高压气动备份动力源，在主液压刹车系统失效时放下起落架或操纵刹车。氮气并不直接用于驱动起落架作动筒或刹车装置，而是用增压氮气推动液压油流到作动筒。这一过程称为气液压（pneudraulics）。下面一段介绍某型公务机上应急气动起落架放下系统的部件与工作。

### 中压系统

中压气动系统（50–150 psi）通常不包含气瓶，而一般从涡轮发动机的压气机段引气。这一过程常称为引气，用于为发动机起动、发动机防冰、机翼防冰提供气动动力；某些情况下还为飞机各系统提供液压动力（如果液压系统装有空气驱动液压泵）。发动机引气还用于给液压系统储液罐增压。引气系统在动力装置分册中有更详细的讨论。

### 低压系统

许多装往复式发动机的飞机从叶片式泵获得低压空气源。这类泵由电动机或飞机发动机驱动。图 12-77 示出其中一种泵的示意图，它由一个带两个接口的壳体、一根驱动轴和两片叶片组成。驱动轴和叶片上都开有槽，因此叶片能在驱动轴中前后滑动。轴在壳体内偏心安装，使叶片之间形成四个大小不同的腔室（A、B、C、D）。在图示位置，B 是最大的腔室，与供气口相通。如图 12-77 所示，外界空气可进入泵的 B 腔。泵开始工作时，驱动轴旋转，改变叶片位置和各腔室大小。1 号叶片随后向右移动，把 B 腔与供气口隔开，B 腔此时容纳的是被封闭的空气。

随着轴继续转动，B 腔向下移动并越来越小，逐渐压缩其中的空气。在泵的底部附近，B 腔与压力口相通，把压缩空气送入压力管路。随后 B 腔又向上移动，面积越来越大，在供气口处再次吸入空气。该泵有四个这样的腔室，每个都经历同样的工作循环。这样，泵就向气动系统连续提供 1 至 10 psi 的压缩空气。低压系统用于机翼防冰带（deicing boot）系统。

### 气动动力系统的维护

气动动力系统的维护包括勤务、排故、部件的拆卸与安装以及操作测试。

应按适用制造商的说明每天检查空气压缩机的润滑油油位。油位由观察窗或油尺指示。给压缩机油箱加油时，加入规定牌号的油（见适用说明书），直到规定油位。加油后，确认加油堵塞已按规定扭矩拧紧，保险丝已正确安装。

应定期吹洗气动系统，以清除部件和管路中的污物、水分或油。吹洗系统的方法是给系统增压，并拆开系统各处部件的管路接头。拆开带压管路会使气流高速通过系统，从而把杂质从系统中排出。如果从任一系统中排出的杂质（尤其是油）过多，就应拆下管路和部件进行清洁或更换。

气动系统吹洗完毕、所有系统部件重新接好后，应排放各气瓶，排出其中可能积聚的水分或杂质。

排放气瓶后，用氮气或清洁干燥的压缩空气对系统进行勤务。随后应对系统进行全面的操作检查，并检查渗漏和连接牢固性。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① since 原因状语从句 + 分号并列 + there be
**原句**：Since both liquids and gases flow, they are considered as fluids; however, there is a great deal of difference in the characteristics of the two.
**拆解**：Since 引导原因状语从句（“既然/由于”），主句为被动语态 they are considered as fluids；分号后 however 另起一个分句，用 there be 句型，真正主语是 a great deal of difference，in the characteristics of the two 为后置定语。
**要点**：since 在此是“由于”而非“自从”，判断依据是后面跟完整句子且主句谈因果。there be 句型中 a great deal of difference 才是主语，谓语用 is 而非 are；the two 指代前文的 liquids and gases。

### ② 分号并列 + regardless of 引导的让步
**原句**：Liquids are practically incompressible; a quart of water still occupies about a quart of space regardless of how hard it is compressed.
**拆解**：分号连接两个并列分句，后者主语 a quart of water、谓语 occupies、宾语 about a quart of space；regardless of 后接 how 引导的宾语从句 how hard it is compressed，作让步状语。
**要点**：regardless of 是介词短语，后面必须接名词性成分，所以不能写 regardless of it is compressed，要加 how 把句子变成从句。it 指 a quart of water，故用被动 is compressed。

### ③ 非限制性定语从句 one of which
**原句**：This type of air bottle has two valves, one of which is a charging valve.
**拆解**：主句主谓宾为 This type of air bottle has two valves；逗号后 one of which is a charging valve 是非限制性定语从句，先行词为 two valves，one of which 在从句中作主语，谓语 is。
**要点**：of which 表示“其中”，one of which 作主语时谓语要与 one 一致，用单数 is。这类结构不能用 one of them is，否则就成了两个独立句子而缺连词。

### ④ 不定式作目的状语 + 被动语态
**原句**：To release the brakes, the control valve is returned to the off position.
**拆解**：句首 To release the brakes 为不定式短语作目的状语；主句为被动语态，主语 the control valve，谓语 is returned，to the off position 为方向/状态状语。
**要点**：目的状语放在句首时用逗号与主句隔开，逻辑主语应与主句主语一致——松刹车这个动作其实由人完成，但句子把控制活门当主语，属于维修手册常见的“以部件为主语”的客观叙述。return to 在这里是“扳回”，不是“归还”。

### ⑤ 现在分词短语作结果状语 + cause ... to do
**原句**：The shaft is eccentrically mounted in the housing, causing the vanes to form four different sizes of chambers (A, B, C, and D).
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 The shaft is mounted，eccentrically 作方式状语，in the housing 为地点状语；逗号后 causing... 为现在分词短语作结果状语，其逻辑主语是前面整件事；cause the vanes to form... 为 cause + 宾语 + 不定式作宾补。
**要点**：现在分词作结果状语时，其逻辑主语是主句所述事实而非主句主语本身，所以不能改写成 which causes 之外的并列句。cause sb./sth. to do 的宾补必须带 to，与 make/let 后省略 to 不同。four different sizes of chambers 是“四种大小不同的腔室”，不是“四个腔室四种尺寸”的重复。
