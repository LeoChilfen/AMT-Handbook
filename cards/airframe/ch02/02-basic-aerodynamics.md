# Basic Aerodynamics

> 机体 Ch 02 · Basic Aerodynamics · 原文 PDF 第 72 页

## 1. 原文

Aerodynamics is the study of the dynamics of gases, the interaction between a moving object and the atmosphere being of primary interest for this handbook. The movement of an object and its reaction to the air flow around it can be seen when watching water passing the bow of a ship. The major difference between water and air is that air is compressible and water is incompressible. The action of the airflow over a body is a large part of the study of aerodynamics. Some common aircraft terms, such as rudder, hull, water line, and keel beam, were borrowed from nautical terms.

Many textbooks have been written about the aerodynamics of aircraft flight. It is not necessary for an airframe and powerplant (A&P) mechanic to be as knowledgeable as an aeronautical engineer about aerodynamics. The mechanic must be able to understand the relationships between how an aircraft performs in flight and its reaction to the forces acting on its structural parts. Understanding why aircraft are designed with particular types of primary and secondary control systems and why the surfaces must be aerodynamically smooth becomes essential when maintaining today’s complex aircraft.

The theory of flight should be described in terms of the laws of flight because what happens to an aircraft when it flies is not based upon assumptions, but upon a series of facts. Aerodynamics is a study of laws which have been proven to be the physical reasons why an airplane flies. The term aerodynamics is derived from the combination of two Greek words: “aero,” meaning air, and “dyne,” meaning force of power. Thus, when “aero” joins “dynamics” the result is “aerodynamics”—the study of objects in motion through the air and the forces that produce or change such motion.

Aerodynamically, an aircraft can be defined as an object traveling through space that is affected by the changes in atmospheric conditions. To state it another way, aerodynamics covers the relationships between the aircraft, relative wind, and atmosphere.

## 2. 中文翻译

空气动力学是研究气体动力学的学科；对本书而言，最关注的是运动物体与大气之间的相互作用。观察水流经过船首的情形，就能看出物体运动及其周围气流对它的反作用。水与空气的主要区别在于：空气是可压缩的，而水是不可压缩的。气流流过物体表面的作用，是空气动力学研究的主要部分。一些常见的航空术语，如方向舵、船体、水线和龙骨梁，都是从航海术语中借用来的。

关于飞机飞行的空气动力学，已有许多教科书。对于机体与发动机（A&P）维修人员来说，不必在空气动力学上达到航空工程师那样的学识水平。维修人员必须能够理解飞机的飞行表现与其对作用在结构件上的力的反应之间的关系。在维护当今复杂的飞机时，理解飞机为什么采用特定类型的主操纵系统和辅助操纵系统，以及为什么这些表面必须气动光滑，就变得至关重要。

飞行理论应当用飞行定律来表述，因为飞机飞行时发生的一切并非基于假设，而是基于一系列事实。空气动力学研究的是一系列已被证明的定律，这些定律是飞机之所以能飞的物理原因。“空气动力学”（aerodynamics）一词由两个希腊词组合而来：“aero”意为空气，“dyne”意为力。因此，当“aero”与“dynamics”结合，结果就是“aerodynamics”——研究空气中运动的物体以及产生或改变这种运动的力。

从空气动力学角度看，飞机可以定义为在空间中运动、并受大气条件变化影响的物体。换一种说法，空气动力学涵盖飞机、相对气流与大气之间的关系。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 独立主格结构补充说明
**原句**：Aerodynamics is the study of the dynamics of gases, the interaction between a moving object and the atmosphere being of primary interest for this handbook.
**拆解**：主句 Aerodynamics is the study of the dynamics of gases；逗号后 the interaction... 为独立主格结构，由名词短语 + 现在分词 being 构成，being of primary interest 是系表结构作补足语，for this handbook 说明范围。
**要点**：being 的逻辑主语是 the interaction，与主句主语 Aerodynamics 不同，所以整体是独立主格而非普通分词状语。把它理解成“其中……令人关注”即可；独立主格前只能用逗号，不能加 and 变成并列句。

### ② 表语从句说明“区别在于”
**原句**：The major difference between water and air is that air is compressible and water is incompressible.
**拆解**：主语 The major difference，between water and air 作后置定语；系动词 is；that 引导表语从句，从句内部由 and 连接两个并列分句（air is compressible / water is incompressible）。
**要点**：that 在表语从句中不作成分、只起引导作用，但不能省略（系动词后的 that 从句通常保留 that）。两个分句共用 is 前的对比框架，中文用“空气……，而水……”来体现对照。

### ③ such as 插入语 + 被动语态
**原句**：Some common aircraft terms, such as rudder, hull, water line, and keel beam, were borrowed from nautical terms.
**拆解**：主语 Some common aircraft terms，such as... 为举例的同位性插入语，被两个逗号隔开；谓语 were borrowed 为一般过去时被动语态，from nautical terms 说明来源。
**要点**：such as 列举的是前面 terms 的具体例子，不能与表示“例如”的 for example 混用句式。插入语去掉后主谓是 Some terms were borrowed，注意别把 rudder 等误当成主语。

### ④ 动名词短语作主语 + 两个 why 从句
**原句**：Understanding why aircraft are designed with particular types of primary and secondary control systems and why the surfaces must be aerodynamically smooth becomes essential when maintaining today’s complex aircraft.
**拆解**：主语是动名词短语 Understanding + 两个并列的 why 宾语从句（第一个从句为被动 are designed with...，第二个从句为 must be smooth）；谓语 becomes essential；when maintaining... 为时间状语，主动词为动名词 maintaining。
**要点**：主语长达二十余词，谓语 becomes 直到最后才出现，阅读时先把 Understanding ... becomes essential 这条骨架拎出来。两个 why 从句由 and 并列，第二个 why 前省略了 understanding。when + 动名词 相当于 when one maintains，逻辑主语与主句一致。

### ⑤ because 从句中套主语从句 + not...but 对比
**原句**：The theory of flight should be described in terms of the laws of flight because what happens to an aircraft when it flies is not based upon assumptions, but upon a series of facts.
**拆解**：主句为被动 should be described in terms of...；because 引导原因状语从句，从句主语是 what 引导的主语从句 what happens to an aircraft，其中 when it flies 为时间状语从句；从句谓语 is not based upon... but upon...，为 not...but... 的并列对比。
**要点**：what happens... 整个作从句主语，谓语用单数 is。not...but... 连接的是两个介词短语 upon assumptions 与 upon a series of facts，第二个 upon 前省略了 is based，不能理解成两个独立分句。

### ⑥ 不定式作独立成分
**原句**：To state it another way, aerodynamics covers the relationships between the aircraft, relative wind, and atmosphere.
**拆解**：To state it another way 为不定式短语作独立成分（插入语），说明说话方式；主干 aerodynamics covers the relationships，between... 介词短语作后置定语，列出三个并列对象。
**要点**：这类不定式独立成分（to put it another way / to be frank）不作句子成分，只修饰整句。relationships between A, B, and C 中 between 后接三项并列，中文习惯译成“A、B 与 C 之间的关系”。
