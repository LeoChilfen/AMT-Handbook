# Primary Flight Controls

> 机体 Ch 02 · Primary Flight Controls · 原文 PDF 第 81 页

## 1. 原文

The primary controls are the ailerons, elevator, and the rudder, which provide the aerodynamic force to make the aircraft follow a desired flightpath. The flight control surfaces are hinged or movable airfoils designed to change the attitude of the aircraft by changing the airflow over the aircraft’s surface during flight. These surfaces are used for moving the aircraft about its three axes.

Typically, the ailerons and elevators are operated from the flight deck by means of a control stick, a wheel, and yoke assembly and on some of the newer design aircraft, a joy-stick. The rudder is normally operated by foot pedals on most aircraft. Lateral control is the banking movement or roll of an aircraft that is controlled by the ailerons. Longitudinal control is the climb and dive movement or pitch of an aircraft that is controlled by the elevator. Directional control is the left and right movement or yaw of an aircraft that is controlled by the rudder.

## 2. 中文翻译

主操纵装置是副翼、升降舵和方向舵，它们提供气动力，使飞机按期望的飞行轨迹飞行。飞行操纵面是铰接的或可活动的翼型，设计用来在飞行中通过改变飞机表面上的气流来改变飞机的姿态。这些翼面用于使飞机绕其三根轴运动。

通常，副翼和升降舵在驾驶舱内通过驾驶杆、驾驶盘与轴架组件操纵，在一些较新设计的飞机上则用侧杆操纵。在多数飞机上，方向舵通常用脚蹬操纵。横向操纵是飞机的倾斜运动即滚转，由副翼控制。纵向操纵是飞机的爬升和俯冲运动即俯仰，由升降舵控制。方向操纵是飞机的左右运动即偏航，由方向舵控制。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 并列表语 + which 非限制性定语从句 + make sb. do
**原句**：The primary controls are the ailerons, elevator, and the rudder, which provide the aerodynamic force to make the aircraft follow a desired flightpath.
**拆解**：主干 The primary controls are the ailerons, elevator, and the rudder，三个名词并列作表语；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词为前面三个操纵面的整体，从句谓语 provide，宾语 the aerodynamic force，不定式 to make... 作后置定语说明该力的用途。
**要点**：which 指三个操纵面全体而非仅 rudder；make the aircraft follow 是 make + 宾语 + 动词原形（不带 to），与 provide ... to make 中的 to 不同——前者是宾补，后者是定语。

### ② 过去分词短语作后置定语 + by doing 方式状语
**原句**：The flight control surfaces are hinged or movable airfoils designed to change the attitude of the aircraft by changing the airflow over the aircraft’s surface during flight.
**拆解**：主干 The flight control surfaces are hinged or movable airfoils，系表结构；designed to change... 为过去分词短语作后置定语修饰 airfoils；by changing the airflow 为方式状语，over the aircraft’s surface 与 during flight 分别修饰 airflow 与 changing。
**要点**：hinged or movable 两个形容词并列修饰 airfoils，不是谓语；designed 的逻辑主语是 airfoils（被动关系），故用过去分词而非 designing。

### ③ 被动语态 + by means of 列举 + 并列省略结构
**原句**：Typically, the ailerons and elevators are operated from the flight deck by means of a control stick, a wheel, and yoke assembly and on some of the newer design aircraft, a joy-stick.
**拆解**：主干 the ailerons and elevators are operated，被动语态；from the flight deck 为地点状语；by means of 引出三个并列手段（a control stick、a wheel and yoke assembly、a joy-stick）；and on some of the newer design aircraft 是第二个状语，与 by means of 并列（省略了 by means of）。
**要点**：句末的 a joy-stick 属于 by means of 的第三个宾语，中间被 and on some... 隔开，容易误读成独立成分；of the newer design 是“较新设计的”，design 作名词修饰 aircraft。

### ④ 系表结构 + that 定语从句
**原句**：Lateral control is the banking movement or roll of an aircraft that is controlled by the ailerons.
**拆解**：主干 Lateral control is the banking movement or roll，of an aircraft 为后置定语；that 引导定语从句修饰 movement or roll，从句为被动 is controlled by the ailerons。
**要点**：that 从句的先行词是 movement/roll（运动），不是 aircraft——是“滚转运动由副翼控制”。banking 与 roll 在此为同义并列，都译“滚转/倾斜”。
