# Airplane Assembly & Rigging

## 1. 原文

The primary assembly of a type certificated aircraft is normally performed by the manufacturer at the factory. The assembly includes putting together the major components, such as the fuselage, empennage, wing sections, nacelles, landing gear, and installing the powerplant. Attached to the wing and empennage are primary flight control surfaces including ailerons, elevators, and rudder. Additionally, installation of auxiliary flight control surfaces may include wing flaps, spoilers, speed brakes, slats, and leading edge flaps.

The assembly of other aircraft outside of a manufacturer’s facility is usually limited to smaller size and experimental amateur-built aircraft. Typically, after a major overhaul, repair, or alteration, the reassembly of an aircraft may include reattaching wings to the fuselage, balancing of and installation of flight control surfaces, installation of the landing gear, and installation of the powerplant(s).

### Rebalancing of Control Surfaces

This section is presented for familiarization purposes only. Explicit instructions for the balancing of control surfaces are given in the manufacturer’s service and overhaul manuals for the specific aircraft and must be followed closely.

Any time repairs on a control surface add weight fore or aft of the hinge center line, the control surface must be rebalanced. When an aircraft is repainted, the balance of the control surfaces must be checked. Any control surface that is out of balance is unstable and does not remain in a streamlined position during normal flight. For example, an aileron that is trailing edge heavy moves down when the wing deflects upward, and up when the wing deflects downward. Such a condition can cause unexpected and violent maneuvers of the aircraft. In extreme cases, fluttering and buffeting may develop to a degree that could cause the complete loss of the aircraft.

Rebalancing a control surface concerns both static and dynamic balance. A control surface that is statically balanced is also dynamically balanced.

#### Static Balance

Static balance is the tendency of an object to remain stationary when supported from its own CG. There are two ways in which a control surface may be out of static balance. They are called underbalance and overbalance.

When a control surface is mounted on a balance stand, a downward travel of the trailing edge below the horizontal position indicates underbalance. Some manufacturers indicate this condition with a plus (+) sign. An upward movement of the trailing edge, above the horizontal position indicates overbalance. This is designated by a minus (–) sign. These signs show the need for more or less weight in the correct area to achieve a balanced control surface, as shown in Figure 2-60 .

A tail-heavy condition (static underbalance) causes undesirable flight performance and is not usually allowed. Better flight operations are gained by nose-heavy static overbalance. Most manufacturers advocate the existence of nose-heavy control surfaces.

#### Dynamic Balance

Dynamic balance is that condition in a rotating body wherein all rotating forces are balanced within themselves so that no vibration is produced while the body is in motion. Dynamic balance as related to control surfaces is an effort to maintain balance when the control surface is submitted to movement on the aircraft in flight. It involves the placing of weights in the correct location along the span of the surfaces. The location of the weights are, in most cases, forward of the hinge center line.

### Rebalancing Procedures

Repairs to a control surface or its tabs generally increase the weight aft of the hinge center line, requiring static rebalancing of the control surface system, as well as the tabs. Control surfaces to be rebalanced should be removed from the aircraft and supported, from their own points, on a suitable stand, jig, or fixture.

Trim tabs on the surface should be secured in the neutral position when the control surface is mounted on the stand. The stand must be level and be located in an area free of air currents. The control surface must be permitted to rotate freely about the hinge points without binding. Balance condition is determined by the behavior of the trailing edge when the surface is suspended from its hinge points. Any excessive friction would result in a false reaction as to the overbalance or underbalance of the surface.

When installing the control surface in the stand or jig, a neutral position should be established with the chord line of the surface in a horizontal position. Use a bubble protractor to determine the neutral position before continuing balancing procedures.

Sometimes a visual check is all that is needed to determine whether the surface is balanced or unbalanced. Any trim tabs or other assemblies that are to remain on the surface during balancing procedures should be in place. If any assemblies or parts must be removed before balancing, they should be removed.

### Rebalancing Methods

Several methods of balancing (rebalancing) control surfaces are in use by the various manufacturers of aircraft. The most common are the calculation method, scale method, and the balance beam method.

The calculation method of balancing a control surface has one advantage over the other methods in that it can be performed without removing the surface from the aircraft. In using the calculation method, the weight of the material from the repair area and the weight of the materials used to accomplish the repair must be known. Subtract the weight removed from the weight added to get the resulting net gain in the amount added to the surface. The distance from the hinge center line to the center of the repair area is then measured in inches. This distance must be determined to the nearest one-hundredth of an inch.

The next step is to multiply the distance times the net weight of the repair. This results in an inch-pounds (in-lb) answer. If the in-lb result of the calculations is within specified tolerances, the control surface is considered balanced. If it is not within specified limits, consult the manufacturer’s service manuals for the needed weights, material to use for weights, design for manufacture, and installation locations for addition of the weights.

The scale method of balancing a control surface requires the use of a scale that is graduated in hundredths of a pound. A support stand and balancing jigs for the surface are also required. Figure 2-64 illustrates a control surface mounted for rebalancing purposes. Use of the scale method requires the removal of the control surface from the aircraft.

The balance beam method is used by the Cessna and Piper Aircraft companies. This method requires that a specialized tool be locally fabricated. The manufacturer’s maintenance manual provides specific instructions and dimensions to fabricate the tool.

Once the control surface is placed on level supports, the weight required to balance the surface is established by moving the sliding weight on the beam. The maintenance manual indicates where the balance point should be. If the surface is found to be out of tolerance, the manual explains where to place weight to bring it into tolerance.

Aircraft manufacturers use different materials to balance control surfaces, the most common being lead or steel. Larger aircraft manufacturers may use depleted uranium because it has a heavier mass than lead. This allows the counterweights to be made smaller and still retain the same weight. Specific safety precautions must be observed when handling counterweights of depleted uranium because it is radioactive. The manufacturer’s maintenance manual and service instructions must be followed and all precautions observed when handling the weights.

## 2. 中文翻译

型号合格审定飞机的初始装配通常由制造厂在工厂内完成。装配包括把机身、尾翼、机翼段、发动机短舱、起落架等主要部件组装到一起，并安装动力装置。与机翼和尾翼相连的是主飞行操纵面，包括副翼、升降舵和方向舵。此外，辅助飞行操纵面的安装还可能包括襟翼、扰流板、减速板、缝翼和前缘襟翼。

在制造厂设施之外对其他飞机进行的装配，通常限于尺寸较小的飞机和实验类业余自制飞机。一般而言，飞机经过大修、修理或改装后重新装配时，可能包括把机翼重新装到机身上、对飞行操纵面进行平衡和安装、安装起落架以及安装动力装置。

### 操纵面的重新平衡

本节仅供了解。操纵面平衡的具体说明见该型飞机的制造商维护手册和大修手册，必须严格遵照执行。

只要对操纵面的修理使铰链中心线之前或之后增加了重量，就必须对该操纵面重新平衡。飞机重新喷漆后，必须检查操纵面的平衡。任何不平衡的操纵面都是不稳定的，在正常飞行中不会保持在流线位置。例如，后缘偏重的副翼会在机翼向上变形时向下偏转，在机翼向下变形时向上偏转。这种情况会使飞机出现意外而剧烈的机动。在极端情况下，抖振和颤振可能发展到使飞机完全损毁的程度。

操纵面的重新平衡涉及静平衡和动平衡两方面。静平衡的操纵面同时也是动平衡的。

#### 静平衡

静平衡是指物体被支承在自身重心处时保持静止的倾向。操纵面失去静平衡有两种情况，分别称为平衡不足和平衡过度。

把操纵面装在平衡台上时，后缘低于水平位置向下偏转表示平衡不足，有些制造商用加号（+）标示这种状态。后缘高于水平位置向上偏转表示平衡过度，用减号（–）标示。这些符号表示需要在正确部位增加或减少重量，才能使操纵面达到平衡，如图2-60所示。

后缘偏重状态（静平衡不足）会导致不良的飞行性能，通常是不允许的。前缘偏重的静平衡过度能获得更好的飞行操作品质，大多数制造商主张操纵面应具有前缘偏重的状态。

#### 动平衡

动平衡是指旋转体内部各种旋转力相互平衡、使物体运动时不产生振动的那种状态。就操纵面而言，动平衡是力求在飞机飞行中操纵面发生运动时仍保持平衡。它涉及在操纵面展向的正确位置配置配重。大多数情况下，配重位于铰链中心线之前。

### 重新平衡程序

对操纵面或其调整片的修理一般会增加铰链中心线之后的重量，因此需要对操纵面系统以及调整片进行静平衡。需要重新平衡的操纵面应从飞机上拆下，以其自身支点支承在合适的台架、夹具或工装上。

操纵面装到台架上时，其上的配平片应固定在中间位置。台架必须水平，并置于无气流扰动的区域。操纵面必须能绕铰链点自由转动而不卡滞。平衡状态由操纵面悬挂在铰链点上时后缘的动向判定。任何过大的摩擦都会使对操纵面平衡过度或不足的判断失真。

把操纵面装到台架或夹具上时，应建立中间位置，使操纵面弦线呈水平。在继续平衡程序之前，用气泡量角器确定中间位置。

有时仅凭目视检查就能判断操纵面是否平衡。平衡过程中需要留在操纵面上的配平片或其他组件都应装到位；若某些组件或零件必须在平衡前拆下，则应拆下。

### 重新平衡方法

各飞机制造商采用多种操纵面平衡（重新平衡）方法，最常见的是计算法、称重法和平衡梁法。

计算法与其他方法相比有一个优点：不必把操纵面从飞机上拆下即可进行。使用计算法时，必须知道修理部位材料的重量和完成修理所用材料的重量。从增加重量中减去去除重量，得出加在操纵面上的净增重量。然后以英寸为单位测量从铰链中心线到修理部位中心的距离，该距离须精确到百分之一英寸。

下一步是把该距离乘以修理的净重量，得到英寸-磅（in-lb）的结果。若计算所得英寸-磅值在规定的公差范围内，即认为该操纵面已平衡。若不在规定范围内，则查阅制造商维护手册，确定所需配重的重量、配重所用材料、制造设计和安装位置。

称重法需要一台以百分之一磅为刻度的秤，还需要支承台架和操纵面平衡夹具。图2-64所示为装好待重新平衡的操纵面。使用称重法必须把操纵面从飞机上拆下。

平衡梁法为赛斯纳（Cessna）和派珀（Piper）飞机公司所采用。该方法需要就地制作专用工具，制造商维护手册给出制作该工具的具体说明和尺寸。

把操纵面放到水平支承上后，通过移动梁上的滑动配重来确定平衡该操纵面所需的重量。维护手册指明平衡点应在何处。若发现操纵面超出公差，手册会说明在何处加配重使其回到公差范围内。

飞机制造商使用不同材料为操纵面配重，最常见的是铅或钢。较大的飞机制造商可能使用贫铀，因为其质量密度比铅大，这样配重可以做得更小却仍保持相同重量。贫铀配重具有放射性，搬运时必须遵守专门的安全预防措施。搬运配重时必须遵循制造商维护手册和使用说明，并遵守全部预防措施。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① any time 引导时间状语从句 + 被动语态
**原句**：Any time repairs on a control surface add weight fore or aft of the hinge center line, the control surface must be rebalanced.
**拆解**：Any time 相当于 whenever，引出时间状语从句，从句主语 repairs，谓语 add，宾语 weight，fore or aft of the hinge center line 说明增重的位置；主句 the control surface must be rebalanced 为带情态动词的被动语态。
**要点**：Any time 在这里不是“任何时间”的名词短语，而是连词性用法，后面直接跟完整从句，中间不加 that。fore or aft of 是“在……之前或之后”，指沿弦向的位置，不要理解成前后两个方向。

### ② 定语从句 + 并列谓语
**原句**：Any control surface that is out of balance is unstable and does not remain in a streamlined position during normal flight.
**拆解**：主语 Any control surface 带 that 引导的定语从句；主句有两个并列谓语 is unstable 和 does not remain in a streamlined position，during normal flight 为时间状语。
**要点**：句中出现三个 is/系动词形式：从句的 is out of balance、主句的 is unstable，容易看串。that 从句到 balance 结束，unstable 才是主句的表语。remain 后接介词短语表状态，不能用 remain 加副词。

### ③ to a degree that 引导的定语从句
**原句**：In extreme cases, fluttering and buffeting may develop to a degree that could cause the complete loss of the aircraft.
**拆解**：In extreme cases 为条件状语；主语 fluttering and buffeting 为两个并列动名词；谓语 may develop；to a degree 表示发展到的程度，that could cause ... 为定语从句修饰 degree。
**要点**：to a degree that 是“发展到……的程度”，that 在从句中作主语，不能换成 what。fluttering（颤振）与 buffeting（抖振）是两个不同的气动弹性/气流现象，并列作主语时谓语用复数 may develop。

### ④ wherein 引导的定语从句 + so that 结果状语
**原句**：Dynamic balance is that condition in a rotating body wherein all rotating forces are balanced within themselves so that no vibration is produced while the body is in motion.
**拆解**：主语 Dynamic balance，系动词 is，表语 that condition；in a rotating body 为后置定语；wherein（= in which）引导定语从句修饰 condition，从句为被动 are balanced；so that 引出结果状语从句，其中又含 while 引导的时间状语从句。
**要点**：wherein 是关系副词，相当于 in which，只能引导定语从句，不能像 which 那样单独作主语或宾语。so that 在此表结果而非目的，可译为“以致”。句末 while the body is in motion 是“在物体运动时”，修饰 no vibration is produced。

### ⑤ in that 引导原因状语从句
**原句**：The calculation method of balancing a control surface has one advantage over the other methods in that it can be performed without removing the surface from the aircraft.
**拆解**：主语 The calculation method of balancing a control surface；谓语 has，宾语 one advantage，over the other methods 表示比较对象；in that 引导原因状语从句，说明这一优点具体是什么，从句为带情态动词的被动语态。
**要点**：in that 是复合连词，意为“在于、因为”，不能拆成介词 in + 代词 that 来理解。have an advantage over 是固定搭配“优于”。without removing 是介词 + 动名词，逻辑主语是句中的 it（即 the calculation method）。

### ⑥ 被动语态 + when 分词结构 + because 从句
**原句**：Specific safety precautions must be observed when handling counterweights of depleted uranium because it is radioactive.
**拆解**：主句 Specific safety precautions must be observed 为被动语态；when handling ... 是 when + 动名词的省略结构，相当于 when one handles ...；because 引导原因状语从句，it 指 depleted uranium。
**要点**：when handling 省略了主语和 be 动词，其逻辑主语与主句主语一致时才能这样省略；这里实际是“人在搬运时”，属约定俗成的无主句用法。because 从句说明原因，与前面的 must 构成“因为放射性强，所以必须”的因果关系。
