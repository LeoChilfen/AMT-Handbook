# Rotorcraft Controls

## 1. 原文

### Swash Plate Assembly

The purpose of the swash plate is to transmit control inputs from the collective and cyclic controls to the main rotor blades. It consists of two main parts: the stationary swash plate and the rotating swash plate.

The stationary swash plate is mounted around the main rotor mast and connected to the cyclic and collective controls by a series of pushrods. It is restrained from rotating by an antidrive link but is able to tilt in all directions and move vertically. The rotating swash plate is mounted to the stationary swash plate by a uniball sleeve. It is connected to the mast by drive links and is allowed to rotate with the main rotor mast. Both swash plates tilt and slide up and down as one unit. The rotating swash plate is connected to the pitch horns by the pitch links.

There are three major controls in a helicopter that the pilot must use during flight. They are the collective pitch control, cyclic pitch control, and antitorque pedals or tail rotor control. In addition to these major controls, the pilot must also use the throttle control, which is mounted directly to the collective pitch control in order to fly the helicopter.

### Collective Pitch Control

The collective pitch control is located on the left side of the pilot’s seat and is operated with the left hand. The collective is used to make changes to the pitch angle of all the main rotor blades simultaneously, or collectively, as the name implies. As the collective pitch control is raised, there is a simultaneous and equal increase in pitch angle of all main rotor blades; as it is lowered, there is a simultaneous and equal decrease in pitch angle. This is done through a series of mechanical linkages, and the amount of movement in the collective lever determines the amount of blade pitch change. An adjustable friction control helps prevent inadvertent collective pitch movement.

### Throttle Control

The function of the throttle is to regulate engine rpm. If the correlator or governor system does not maintain the desired rpm when the collective is raised or lowered, or if those systems are not installed, the throttle must be moved manually with the twist grip to maintain rpm. The throttle control is much like a motorcycle throttle, and works almost the same way; twisting the throttle to the left increases rpm, twisting the throttle to the right decreases rpm.

### Governor/Correlator

A governor is a sensing device that senses rotor and engine rpm and makes the necessary adjustments in order to keep rotor rpm constant. Once the rotor rpm is set in normal operations, the governor keeps the rpm constant, and there is no need to make any throttle adjustments. Governors are common on all turbine helicopters (as it is a function of the fuel control system of the turbine engine), and used on some piston-powered helicopters.

A correlator is a mechanical connection between the collective lever and the engine throttle. When the collective lever is raised, power is automatically increased and when lowered, power is decreased. This system maintains rpm close to the desired value, but still requires adjustment of the throttle for fine tuning.

Some helicopters do not have correlators or governors and require coordination of all collective and throttle movements. When the collective is raised, the throttle must be increased; when the collective is lowered, the throttle must be decreased. As with any aircraft control, large adjustments of either collective pitch or throttle should be avoided. All corrections should be made with smooth pressure.

In piston helicopters, the collective pitch is the primary control for manifold pressure, and the throttle is the primary control for rpm. However, the collective pitch control also influences rpm, and the throttle also influences manifold pressure; therefore, each is considered to be a secondary control of the other’s function. Both the tachometer (rpm indicator) and the manifold pressure gauge must be analyzed to determine which control to use. Figure 2-47 illustrates this relationship.

### Cyclic Pitch Control

The cyclic pitch control is mounted vertically from the flight deck floor, between the pilot’s legs or, in some models, between the two pilot seats. This primary flight control allows the pilot to fly the helicopter in any horizontal direction; fore, aft, and sideways. The total lift force is always perpendicular to the tip-path place of the main rotor. The purpose of the cyclic pitch control is to tilt the tip-path plane in the direction of the desired horizontal direction. The cyclic control changes the direction of this force and controls the attitude and airspeed of the helicopter.

The rotor disc tilts in the same direction the cyclic pitch control is moved. If the cyclic is moved forward, the rotor disc tilts forward; if the cyclic is moved aft, the disc tilts aft, and so on. Because the rotor disc acts like a gyro, the mechanical linkages for the cyclic control rods are rigged in such a way that they decrease the pitch angle of the rotor blade approximately 90° before it reaches the direction of cyclic displacement, and increase the pitch angle of the rotor blade approximately 90° after it passes the direction of displacement. An increase in pitch angle increases AOA; a decrease in pitch angle decreases AOA. For example, if the cyclic is moved forward, the AOA decreases as the rotor blade passes the right side of the helicopter and increases on the left side. This results in maximum downward deflection of the rotor blade in front of the helicopter and maximum upward deflection behind it, causing the rotor disc to tilt forward.

### Antitorque Pedals

The antitorque pedals are located on the cabin floor by the pilot’s feet. They control the pitch and, therefore, the thrust of the tail rotor blades. Newton’s third law applies to the helicopter fuselage and how it rotates in the opposite direction of the main rotor blades unless counteracted and controlled. To make flight possible and to compensate for this torque, most helicopter designs incorporate an antitorque rotor or tail rotor. The antitorque pedals allow the pilot to control the pitch angle of the tail rotor blades which in forward flight puts the helicopter in longitudinal trim and while at a hover, enables the pilot to turn the helicopter 360°. The antitorque pedals are connected to the pitch change mechanism on the tail rotor gearbox and allow the pitch angle on the tail rotor blades to be increased or decreased.

Helicopters that are designed with tandem rotors do not have an antitorque rotor. These helicopters are designed with both rotor systems rotating in opposite directions to counteract the torque, rather than using a tail rotor. Directional antitorque pedals are used for directional control of the aircraft while in flight, as well as while taxiing with the forward gear off the ground. With the right pedal displaced forward, the forward rotor disc tilts to the right, while the aft rotor disc tilts to the left. The opposite occurs when the left pedal is pushed forward; the forward rotor disc inclines to the left, and the aft rotor disc tilts to the right. Differing combinations of pedal and cyclic application can allow the tandem rotor helicopter to pivot about the aft or forward vertical axis, as well as pivoting about the center of mass.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 旋转斜盘组件

旋转斜盘的用途是把总距和周期变距操纵的输入传递给主旋翼桨叶。它由两个主要部分组成：不旋转斜盘和旋转斜盘。

不旋转斜盘套装在主旋翼轴周围，通过一系列推杆与周期变距和总距操纵机构相连。它由防转连杆限制而不能转动，但可以向各个方向倾斜并上下移动。旋转斜盘通过单球套筒安装在不旋转斜盘上，通过驱动连杆与旋翼轴相连，并可随主旋翼轴一起旋转。两个斜盘作为一个整体一起倾斜和上下滑动。旋转斜盘通过变距拉杆与桨距摇臂相连。

直升机上有三种主要操纵装置，飞行员在飞行中必须使用：总距桨距操纵、周期桨距操纵和抗扭脚蹬（尾桨操纵）。除这些主要操纵装置外，飞行员还必须使用油门操纵，它直接安装在总距桨距操纵杆上，用于操纵直升机飞行。

### 总距桨距操纵

总距桨距操纵杆位于飞行员座椅左侧，用左手操纵。顾名思义，总距用于同时（即总距地）改变所有主旋翼桨叶的桨距角。上提总距杆时，所有主旋翼桨叶的桨距角同时、等量增大；下放时，桨距角同时、等量减小。这是通过一系列机械连杆实现的，总距杆的移动量决定桨叶桨距的变化量。可调摩擦控制器有助于防止总距桨距意外移动。

### 油门操纵

油门的作用是调节发动机转速。如果在总距上提或下放时，协调器或调速器系统不能保持所需转速，或者未安装这些系统，就必须用转把手动移动油门以保持转速。油门操纵很像摩托车油门，工作方式几乎相同：向左扭转油门增大转速，向右扭转减小转速。

### 调速器/协调器

调速器是一种传感装置，它感测旋翼和发动机转速，并作出必要调整以保持旋翼转速恒定。在正常使用中，一旦设定旋翼转速，调速器就保持该转速恒定，无需再做任何油门调整。调速器在所有涡轮直升机上都很常见（因为它是涡轮发动机燃油控制系统的功能之一），部分活塞直升机也有使用。

协调器是总距杆与发动机油门之间的机械连接。上提总距杆时功率自动增大，下放时功率减小。该系统使转速保持在接近所需值，但仍需调整油门进行微调。

有些直升机没有协调器或调速器，需要协调所有总距和油门动作。上提总距时必须增大油门，下放总距时必须减小油门。与任何飞机操纵一样，应避免对总距桨距或油门做大幅调整，所有修正都应平缓柔和地施加。

在活塞直升机上，总距桨距是进气歧管压力的主要控制手段，油门是转速的主要控制手段。但总距桨距操纵也影响转速，油门也影响进气歧管压力；因此，每一个都被视为另一个功能的次要控制。必须同时分析转速表（转速指示器）和进气歧管压力表，才能确定该用哪个操纵。图 2-47 示明了这种关系。

### 周期桨距操纵

周期桨距操纵杆从驾驶舱地板竖直安装，位于飞行员两腿之间，某些型号则位于两个飞行员座椅之间。这一主飞行操纵装置使飞行员能向任意水平方向飞行：前、后和侧向。总升力始终垂直于主旋翼的桨尖轨迹平面。周期桨距操纵的用途是把桨尖轨迹平面向所需的水平方向倾斜。周期变距操纵改变该力的方向，并控制直升机的姿态和空速。

旋翼桨盘随周期桨距操纵杆移动的方向倾斜：周期杆前移，桨盘前倾；周期杆后移，桨盘后倾，依此类推。由于旋翼桨盘的作用类似陀螺，周期操纵杆的机械连杆经过校装，使旋翼桨叶在到达周期变距位移方向之前约 90° 处减小桨距角，在通过位移方向之后约 90° 处增大桨距角。桨距角增大则迎角增大，桨距角减小则迎角减小。例如，周期杆前移时，桨叶经过直升机右侧时迎角减小，经过左侧时迎角增大，结果桨叶在直升机前方达到最大向下偏移、在后方达到最大向上偏移，使旋翼桨盘向前倾斜。

### 抗扭脚蹬

抗扭脚蹬位于座舱地板上、飞行员脚部位置。它们控制尾桨桨叶的桨距，从而控制其拉力。牛顿第三定律适用于直升机机身：除非被抵消和控制，机身会沿与主旋翼桨叶相反的方向转动。为使飞行成为可能并补偿这一扭矩，多数直升机设计采用抗扭旋翼（尾桨）。抗扭脚蹬使飞行员能够控制尾桨桨叶的桨距角，从而在前飞时使直升机处于纵向配平，在悬停时使飞行员能够让直升机转 360°。抗扭脚蹬与尾桨减速器上的变距机构相连，可增大或减小尾桨桨叶的桨距角。

采用纵列双旋翼设计的直升机没有抗扭旋翼。这类直升机的两副旋翼系统转向相反，以抵消扭矩，而不是使用尾桨。方向抗扭脚蹬用于飞行中以及前轮离地滑行时对飞机进行方向控制。右舵前蹬时，前旋翼桨盘向右倾斜，后旋翼桨盘向左倾斜；左舵前蹬时情况相反：前旋翼桨盘向左倾斜，后旋翼桨盘向右倾斜。脚蹬与周期变距的不同组合，可使纵列双旋翼直升机绕后垂直轴或前垂直轴偏转，也可绕质心偏转。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 表语用不定式说明功能
**原句**：The purpose of the swash plate is to transmit control inputs from the collective and cyclic controls to the main rotor blades.
**拆解**：主语 The purpose of the swash plate，系动词 is，表语为不定式短语 to transmit...；transmit 的宾语 control inputs 后接 from...to... 双介词短语，分别表示输入来源和作用对象。
**要点**：说明“用途、目的”时常用“The purpose of X is to do...”，比 X is used to do 更书面。from A to B 在这里是“把 A 传递到 B”，不要误读成时间或范围。

### ② 并列被动结构 + by 短语表施动
**原句**：The stationary swash plate is mounted around the main rotor mast and connected to the cyclic and collective controls by a series of pushrods.
**拆解**：主语 The stationary swash plate 带两个并列的被动谓语 is mounted 与 (is) connected；around the main rotor mast 表安装位置，to the cyclic and collective controls 表连接对象，by a series of pushrods 表施动手段。
**要点**：第二个谓语省略了相同的助动词 is，形成 is mounted...and connected...；by 短语只修饰 connected，不修饰 mounted——斜盘不是被推杆“安装”的。翻译时按汉语习惯把 by 短语前置。

### ③ be restrained from doing 与 be able to 的对比
**原句**：It is restrained from rotating by an antidrive link but is able to tilt in all directions and move vertically.
**拆解**：but 连接两个并列谓语：is restrained from rotating（被动结构，介词 from 后接动名词）与 is able to tilt...and move...（形容词 + 不定式）；by an antidrive link 表施动者。
**要点**：restrain sb./sth. from doing 意为“阻止……做某事”，被动式后仍保留 from + 动名词；be able to 后接动词原形，与 tilt 并列的 move 前省略了 to。两个谓语一“被限制”、一“能自由”，语义正相反，靠 but 转折。

### ④ 两个并列条件从句 + 主句被动
**原句**：If the correlator or governor system does not maintain the desired rpm when the collective is raised or lowered, or if those systems are not installed, the throttle must be moved manually with the twist grip to maintain rpm.
**拆解**：句首两个 if 从句由 or 并列，第一个从句内又套 when 引导的时间状语从句（被动 is raised or lowered）；主句为被动结构 the throttle must be moved，manually 与 with the twist grip 作方式状语，to maintain rpm 作目的状语。
**要点**：主句放在两个长条件从句之后，阅读时应先跳过 if 部分锁定 the throttle must be moved；or if... 是“或者如果……”，与前面的 or 并列项层次不同，翻译时用“或者未安装这些系统”单独成句更清楚。

### ⑤ such ... that 引导的结果状语从句
**原句**：Because the rotor disc acts like a gyro, the mechanical linkages for the cyclic control rods are rigged in such a way that they decrease the pitch angle of the rotor blade approximately 90° before it reaches the direction of cyclic displacement, and increase the pitch angle of the rotor blade approximately 90° after it passes the direction of displacement.
**拆解**：Because 引导原因状语从句；主句为主系表结构 are rigged in such a way，that 引导结果（方式）状语从句；从句内两个并列谓语 decrease 与 increase，各带 before / after 引导的时间状语从句，approximately 90° 为程度状语。
**要点**：in such a way that 中的 that 从句说明“校装成什么样子”，不是定语从句；两个时间从句的 it 都指 the rotor blade，而不是 linkages。90° 的提前量正是陀螺进动的相位补偿。

### ⑥ with 复合结构 + while 表对比
**原句**：With the right pedal displaced forward, the forward rotor disc tilts to the right, while the aft rotor disc tilts to the left.
**拆解**：句首 with + 名词 the right pedal + 过去分词 displaced forward 构成 with 复合结构，作条件/伴随状语；主句 the forward rotor disc tilts to the right；while 连接后半句，表示前后旋翼桨盘的对比。
**要点**：with 复合结构中 pedal 与 displace 是被动关系，故用过去分词；while 在此是“而”的对比义，不是“当……时”。前后两句主语不同（forward / aft rotor disc），tilts 都用第三人称单数。
