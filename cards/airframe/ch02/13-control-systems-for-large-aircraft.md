# Control Systems for Large Aircraft

> 机体 Ch 02 · Control Systems for Large Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 84 页

## 1. 原文

### Mechanical Control

This is the basic type of system that was used to control early aircraft and is currently used in smaller aircraft where aerodynamic forces are not excessive. The controls are mechanical and manually operated.

The mechanical system of controlling an aircraft can include cables, push-pull tubes, and torque tubes. The cable system is the most widely used because deflections of the structure to which it is attached do not affect its operation. Some aircraft incorporate control systems that are a combination of all three. These systems incorporate cable assemblies, cable guides, linkage, adjustable stops, and control surface snubber or mechanical locking devices. These surface locking devices, usually referred to as a gust lock, limits the external wind forces from damaging the aircraft while it is parked or tied down.

### Hydromechanical Control

As the size, complexity, and speed of aircraft increased, actuation of controls in flight became more difficult. It soon became apparent that the pilot needed assistance to overcome the aerodynamic forces to control aircraft movement. Spring tabs, which were operated by the conventional control system, were moved so that the airflow over them actually moved the primary control surface. This was sufficient for the aircraft operating in the lowest of the high speed ranges (250–300 mph). For higher speeds, a power-assisted (hydraulic) control system was designed.

Conventional cable or push-pull tube systems link the flight deck controls with the hydraulic system. With the system activated, the pilot’s movement of a control causes the mechanical link to open servo valves, thereby directing hydraulic fluid to actuators, which convert hydraulic pressure into control surface movements.

Because of the efficiency of the hydromechanical flight control system, the aerodynamic forces on the control surfaces cannot be felt by the pilot, and there is a risk of overstressing the structure of the aircraft. To overcome this problem, aircraft designers incorporated artificial feel systems into the design that provided increased resistance to the controls at higher speeds. Additionally, some aircraft with hydraulically powered control systems are fitted with a device called a stick shaker, which provides an artificial stall warning to the pilot.

### Fly-By-Wire Control

The fly-by-wire (FBW) control system employs electrical signals that transmit the pilot’s actions from the flight deck through a computer to the various flight control actuators. The FBW system evolved as a way to reduce the system weight of the hydromechanical system, reduce maintenance costs, and improve reliability. Electronic FBW control systems can respond to changing aerodynamic conditions by adjusting flight control movements so that the aircraft response is consistent for all flight conditions. Additionally, the computers can be programmed to prevent undesirable and dangerous characteristics, such as stalling and spinning.

Many of the new military high-performance aircraft are not aerodynamically stable. This characteristic is designed into the aircraft for increased maneuverability and responsive performance. Without the computers reacting to the instability, the pilot would lose control of the aircraft.

The Airbus A-320 was the first commercial airliner to use FBW controls. Boeing used them in their 777 and newer design commercial aircraft. The Dassault Falcon 7X was the first business jet to use a FBW control system.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 机械操纵

这是用于操纵早期飞机的基本系统类型，目前仍用于气动力不太大的小型飞机。其操纵装置为机械式，由人工操作。

机械操纵飞机的方式可以包括钢索、推拉管和扭力管。钢索系统应用最广，因为其所连接的结构即使发生变形也不会影响其工作。有些飞机的操纵系统是这三种方式的组合。这类系统包括钢索组件、钢索导件、连杆机构、可调限动块，以及操纵面缓冲器或机械锁定装置。这些操纵面锁定装置通常称为阵风锁，用于在飞机停放或系留时限制外部风力，防止飞机受损。

### 液压机械操纵

随着飞机尺寸、复杂程度和速度的增加，飞行中操纵面的作动变得越来越困难。很快就明显看出，飞行员需要助力来克服气动力、操纵飞机运动。弹簧调整片由常规操纵系统操纵，通过偏转使流经其上的气流真正带动主操纵面。这对在高速范围最低端（250–300 mph）使用的飞机已经足够。对于更高速度，则设计了动力助力（液压）操纵系统。

常规的钢索或推拉管系统把驾驶舱操纵装置与液压系统连接起来。系统接通后，飞行员移动操纵装置，机械连杆便打开伺服活门，从而把液压油导向作动筒，由作动筒把液压压力转换成操纵面的运动。

由于液压机械飞行操纵系统效率很高，飞行员感觉不到操纵面上的气动力，存在使飞机结构超应力的风险。为解决这一问题，飞机设计者在设计中加入了人工感觉系统，在较高速度下增大操纵阻力。此外，一些装液压助力操纵系统的飞机还装有抖杆器，向飞行员提供人工失速警告。

### 电传操纵

电传操纵（FBW）系统采用电信号，把飞行员的动作从驾驶舱经计算机传送到各飞行操纵作动器。FBW 系统的发展，是为了减轻液压机械系统的重量、降低维护成本并提高可靠性。电子式 FBW 操纵系统能够对变化的气动条件作出响应，调整操纵面的运动，使飞机在所有飞行条件下的响应保持一致。此外，计算机还可通过编程防止失速、螺旋等不良危险特性的发生。

许多新型军用高性能飞机在气动上并不稳定。这一特性是为提高机动性和响应性能而特意设计到飞机上的。如果没有计算机对不稳定性作出反应，飞行员就会失去对飞机的控制。

空客 A-320 是第一种采用 FBW 操纵的商用客机。波音在其 777 及更新的商用飞机设计中采用了该技术。达索猎鹰 7X 是第一种采用 FBW 操纵系统的公务喷气机。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句与并列谓语共用先行词
**原句**：This is the basic type of system that was used to control early aircraft and is currently used in smaller aircraft where aerodynamic forces are not excessive.
**拆解**：主语 This，系动词 is，表语 the basic type of system；that 引导定语从句修饰 system，从句内由 and 连接两个并列谓语 was used 与 is currently used；句末 where 引导定语从句修饰 smaller aircraft。
**要点**：that 从句里 was used 和 is used 共用一个关系词，第二个谓语前省略了 that，不要误读成主句的并列谓语。where 的先行词是 aircraft（地方/场合类名词），不是 system。

### ② because 原因状语从句中套介词 + which 定语从句
**原句**：The cable system is the most widely used because deflections of the structure to which it is attached do not affect its operation.
**拆解**：主句为主系表结构 The cable system is the most widely used；because 引导原因状语从句，从句主语 deflections of the structure，谓语 do not affect，宾语 its operation；to which it is attached 是“介词 + which”引导的定语从句，修饰 structure。
**要点**：which 指代 structure，it 指代 the cable system，两个代词所指不同，是易错点。attach to 的介词提到 which 前，是因为 which 在从句中作 attached to 的宾语；还原为 the structure which it is attached to。

### ③ 插入的同位语与 limit ... from doing
**原句**：These surface locking devices, usually referred to as a gust lock, limits the external wind forces from damaging the aircraft while it is parked or tied down.
**拆解**：主语 These surface locking devices，逗号间 usually referred to as a gust lock 为过去分词短语作同位补充；谓语 limits，宾语 the external wind forces，from damaging the aircraft 为介词短语表示阻止的对象；while 引导时间状语从句，省略主语与 be 动词（while it is parked...）。
**要点**：limit sth. from doing 表示“限制某物使其不能做某事”，此处 damage 的逻辑主语是 wind forces。主语是复数 devices 而谓语用 limits，属原文主谓不一致，理解时按 devices 处理，引文不改。

### ④ it 作形式主语 + that 主语从句
**原句**：It soon became apparent that the pilot needed assistance to overcome the aerodynamic forces to control aircraft movement.
**拆解**：It 为形式主语，真正主语是 that 引导的主语从句；系动词 became，表语 apparent；从句中 the pilot 为主语，needed 为谓语，assistance 为宾语，to overcome... 与 to control... 为两个目的状语不定式。
**要点**：去掉 It ... that 框架后，句子还原为 That the pilot needed assistance ... became apparent。两个不定式层次不同：to overcome the aerodynamic forces 说明 assistance 的用途，to control aircraft movement 说明克服气动力的目的。

### ⑤ with 复合结构 + thereby 分词结果状语 + 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：With the system activated, the pilot’s movement of a control causes the mechanical link to open servo valves, thereby directing hydraulic fluid to actuators, which convert hydraulic pressure into control surface movements.
**拆解**：句首 With the system activated 为 with 复合结构作条件状语（system 与 activate 为被动关系，用过去分词）；主句主语 the pilot’s movement of a control，谓语 causes，宾语 the mechanical link，宾补 to open servo valves；thereby directing... 为现在分词短语作结果状语；which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 actuators。
**要点**：cause sb./sth. to do 用不定式作宾补；thereby + 分词是书面语中常见的结果衔接，逻辑主语与主句主语一致。which 前有逗号，说明它补充说明 actuators 而非限定，译成“由作动筒把……”。

### ⑥ 介词短语原因状语 + 被动语态 + there be 并列
**原句**：Because of the efficiency of the hydromechanical flight control system, the aerodynamic forces on the control surfaces cannot be felt by the pilot, and there is a risk of overstressing the structure of the aircraft.
**拆解**：Because of + 名词短语作原因状语；第一分句为被动语态，主语 the aerodynamic forces，情态动词否定 cannot be felt，by the pilot 引出施动者；and 连接第二分句 there is a risk of overstressing...，介词 of 后接动名词。
**要点**：Because of 是介词短语，只能接名词，不能接从句（对比 because 连词）。a risk of doing sth. 中动名词 overstressing 的逻辑宾语是 the structure；被动语态 cannot be felt by the pilot 译成主动更顺：“飞行员感觉不到”。
