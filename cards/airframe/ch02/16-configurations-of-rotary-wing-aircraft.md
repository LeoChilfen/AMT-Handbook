# Configurations of Rotary-Wing Aircraft

> 机体 Ch 02 · Configurations of Rotary-Wing Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 87 页

## 1. 原文

### Autogyro

An autogyro is an aircraft with a free-spinning horizontal rotor that turns due to passage of air upward through the rotor. This air motion is created from forward motion of the aircraft resulting from either a tractor or pusher configured engine/propeller design.

### Single Rotor Helicopter

An aircraft with a single horizontal main rotor that provides both lift and direction of travel is a single rotor helicopter. A secondary rotor mounted vertically on the tail counteracts the rotational force (torque) of the main rotor to correct yaw of the fuselage.

### Dual Rotor Helicopter

An aircraft with two horizontal rotors that provide both the lift and directional control is a dual rotor helicopter. The rotors are counterrotating to balance the aerodynamic torque and eliminate the need for a separate antitorque system.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 自转旋翼机

自转旋翼机是一种装有自由旋转水平旋翼的飞机，旋翼因空气向上穿过旋翼而转动。这种空气流动由飞机的前进运动产生，而前进运动来自牵引式或推进式布置的发动机/螺旋桨设计。

### 单旋翼直升机

单旋翼直升机是装有一副水平主旋翼、由该旋翼同时提供升力和飞行方向的飞机。垂直安装在尾部的辅助旋翼用来抵消主旋翼的旋转力（扭矩），以修正机身的偏航。

### 双旋翼直升机

双旋翼直升机是装有两副水平旋翼、由它们同时提供升力和方向操纵的飞机。两副旋翼反向旋转，以平衡气动扭矩，从而无需单独的抗扭系统。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① with 介词短语 + that 定语从句 + due to
**原句**：An autogyro is an aircraft with a free-spinning horizontal rotor that turns due to passage of air upward through the rotor.
**拆解**：主语 An autogyro，系动词 is，表语 an aircraft；with a free-spinning horizontal rotor 为介词短语作后置定语；that 引导定语从句修饰 rotor，从句谓语 turns，due to passage of air... 为原因状语，upward through the rotor 说明气流方向。
**要点**：that 从句的先行词是 rotor，不是 aircraft；判断方法是从句谓语 turns 为单数，与 rotor 一致。due to 后接名词短语，此处 passage of air 意为“空气的通过”。

### ② 被动语态 + resulting from 分词短语 + either...or
**原句**：This air motion is created from forward motion of the aircraft resulting from either a tractor or pusher configured engine/propeller design.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 This air motion is created from forward motion of the aircraft；resulting from... 为现在分词短语作后置定语，修饰 forward motion；either...or... 连接 a tractor 与 pusher configured 两种布局，共同修饰 engine/propeller design。
**要点**：resulting from 的逻辑主语是 forward motion，不是 air motion。tractor 与 pusher 在此不是“拖拉机/推进者”，而是牵引式、推进式两种螺旋桨布置。

### ③ 长后置定语隔开主系表
**原句**：An aircraft with a single horizontal main rotor that provides both lift and direction of travel is a single rotor helicopter.
**拆解**：主语 An aircraft，后接 with 介词短语，其中 main rotor 又带 that 定语从句（provides both lift and direction of travel）；系动词 is 直到从句结束后才出现，表语 a single rotor helicopter。
**要点**：主句骨架是“An aircraft ... is a single rotor helicopter”，中间整段都是主语的定语。that 从句修饰 rotor，provides 的主语是 rotor，故用单数。

### ④ 过去分词短语作定语 + 不定式目的状语
**原句**：A secondary rotor mounted vertically on the tail counteracts the rotational force (torque) of the main rotor to correct yaw of the fuselage.
**拆解**：主语 A secondary rotor，mounted vertically on the tail 为过去分词短语作后置定语；谓语 counteracts，宾语 the rotational force (torque) of the main rotor；to correct yaw of the fuselage 为不定式作目的状语。
**要点**：mounted 与 rotor 是被动关系，故用过去分词；若写成 mounting 则变成主动，语义错误。括号中的 torque 是对 the rotational force 的同义解释。

### ⑤ 不定式并列作目的状语
**原句**：The rotors are counterrotating to balance the aerodynamic torque and eliminate the need for a separate antitorque system.
**拆解**：主语 The rotors，谓语 are counterrotating（现在进行时）；to balance... and eliminate... 为两个并列的不定式作目的状语，第二个省略 to；宾语分别是 the aerodynamic torque 与 the need for a separate antitorque system。
**要点**：eliminate 前省略了 to，仍与 balance 并列，不能读成谓语。the need for sth. 表示“对……的需要”，separate 意为“单独的、额外的”。
