# Helicopter Power Systems

> 机体 Ch 02 · Helicopter Power Systems · 原文 PDF 第 104 页

## 1. 原文

### Powerplant

The two most common types of engines used in helicopters are the reciprocating engine and the turbine engine. Reciprocating engines, also called piston engines, are generally used in smaller helicopters. Most training helicopters use reciprocating engines because they are relatively simple and inexpensive to operate. Turbine engines are more powerful and are used in a wide variety of helicopters. They produce a tremendous amount of power for their size but are generally more expensive to operate.

### Reciprocating Engine

The reciprocating engine consists of a series of pistons connected to a rotating crankshaft. As the pistons move up and down, the crankshaft rotates. The reciprocating engine gets its name from the back-and-forth movement of its internal parts. The four-stroke engine is the most common type, and refers to the four different cycles the engine undergoes to produce power.

When the piston moves away from the cylinder head on the intake stroke, the intake valve opens and a mixture of fuel and air is drawn into the combustion chamber. As the cylinder moves back toward the cylinder head, the intake valve closes, and the air-fuel mixture is compressed. When compression is nearly complete, the spark plugs fire and the compressed mixture is ignited to begin the power stroke. The rapidly expanding gases from the controlled burning of the air-fuel mixture drive the piston away from the cylinder head, thus providing power to rotate the crankshaft. The piston then moves back toward the cylinder head on the exhaust stroke where the burned gases are expelled through the opened exhaust valve. Even when the engine is operated at a fairly low speed, the four-stroke cycle takes place several hundred times each minute. In a four-cylinder engine, each cylinder operates on a different stroke. Continuous rotation of a crankshaft is maintained by the precise timing of the power strokes in each cylinder.

### Turbine Engine

The gas turbine engine mounted on most helicopters is made up of a compressor, combustion chamber, turbine, and accessory gearbox assembly. The compressor draws filtered air into the plenum chamber and compresses it. The compressed air is directed to the combustion section through discharge tubes where atomized fuel is injected into it. The air-fuel mixture is ignited and allowed to expand. This combustion gas is then forced through a series of turbine wheels causing them to turn. These turbine wheels provide power to both the engine compressor and the accessory gearbox. Power is provided to the main rotor and tail rotor systems through the freewheeling unit which is attached to the accessory gearbox power output gear shaft. The combustion gas is finally expelled through an exhaust outlet.

## 2. 中文翻译

### 动力装置

直升机上最常用的两类发动机是往复式发动机和涡轮发动机。往复式发动机又称活塞发动机，一般用于较小的直升机。大多数训练直升机采用往复式发动机，因为其结构相对简单、运行费用低。涡轮发动机功率更大，用于各种直升机；它以较小的体积产生很大的功率，但运行费用通常更高。

### 往复式发动机

往复式发动机由一系列与旋转曲轴相连的活塞组成。活塞上下运动时，曲轴随之旋转。往复式发动机得名于其内部零件的往复运动。四冲程发动机是最常见的类型，“四冲程”指发动机为产生功率所经历的四个不同循环。

活塞在进气行程中离开气缸盖时，进气门打开，燃油与空气的混合气被吸入燃烧室。活塞向气缸盖方向回行时，进气门关闭，油气混合气被压缩。压缩接近完成时，火花塞跳火，点燃被压缩的混合气，开始做功行程。油气混合气受控燃烧产生迅速膨胀的气体，推动活塞离开气缸盖，从而提供使曲轴旋转的动力。随后活塞在排气行程中向气缸盖方向回行，燃烧后的废气经打开的排气门排出。即使发动机以相当低的速度运转，四冲程循环每分钟也要进行数百次。在四缸发动机中，各缸处于不同行程；由于各缸做功行程的精确正时，曲轴得以连续旋转。

### 涡轮发动机

装于大多数直升机上的燃气涡轮发动机由压气机、燃烧室、涡轮和附件齿轮箱组件组成。压气机把经过滤的空气吸入增压室并加以压缩。压缩空气经输气管送至燃烧段，在那里喷入雾化燃油。油气混合气被点燃并膨胀，这些燃气随后被强制通过一系列涡轮轮盘，使其转动。涡轮轮盘为发动机压气机和附件齿轮箱提供动力。动力经单向离合器传给主旋翼和尾桨系统，该部件装在附件齿轮箱动力输出齿轮轴上。燃气最终经排气口排出。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 并列谓语 + 省略关系词的定语从句
**原句**：The four-stroke engine is the most common type, and refers to the four different cycles the engine undergoes to produce power.
**拆解**：主语 The four-stroke engine 带两个并列谓语 is 和 refers to；宾语 the four different cycles 后接省略了 that/which 的定语从句 the engine undergoes，不定式 to produce power 作目的状语。
**要点**：and 连接的是两个谓语而不是两个句子，所以后半句没有再出现主语。cycles 后的定语从句关系词被省略，因为它在从句中作 undergoes 的宾语；补回 that 即 the engine undergoes that。

### ② 时间状语从句 + 并列被动句
**原句**：When the piston moves away from the cylinder head on the intake stroke, the intake valve opens and a mixture of fuel and air is drawn into the combustion chamber.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，on the intake stroke 说明该动作发生在哪个行程；主句由 and 连接两个分句：the intake valve opens（主动）和 a mixture of fuel and air is drawn（被动）。
**要点**：主句两个分句主语不同、语态也不同，and 前后各自独立成句。draw 的过去分词 drawn 在此是“吸入”，不是“绘制”；into the combustion chamber 是方向状语，说明混合气被吸到哪里。

### ③ 长主语 + thus 分词结果状语
**原句**：The rapidly expanding gases from the controlled burning of the air-fuel mixture drive the piston away from the cylinder head, thus providing power to rotate the crankshaft.
**拆解**：主语中心词 gases，前置定语 rapidly expanding，后置定语 from the controlled burning of the air-fuel mixture；谓语 drive，宾语 the piston，宾补 away from the cylinder head；逗号后 thus + 现在分词 providing 作结果状语。
**要点**：主语和谓语之间隔了两层定语，drive 才是谓语。thus doing 表示前面动作带来的结果，逻辑主语是整句所述的过程；providing power to rotate 中的 to rotate 是 power 的定语。

### ④ 过去分词后置定语隔开主谓 + be made up of
**原句**：The gas turbine engine mounted on most helicopters is made up of a compressor, combustion chamber, turbine, and accessory gearbox assembly.
**拆解**：主语 The gas turbine engine，过去分词短语 mounted on most helicopters 作后置定语；谓语 is made up of，后接四个并列名词构成宾语。
**要点**：mounted 修饰 engine，是“被安装”的被动含义，因此用过去分词而非现在分词。be made up of 表示“由……组成”，四个并列项共用最后一个中心词 assembly 的修饰关系需按专业常识理解。

### ⑤ 被动语态 + which 定语从句
**原句**：Power is provided to the main rotor and tail rotor systems through the freewheeling unit which is attached to the accessory gearbox power output gear shaft.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 Power is provided to ... through ...；which 引导定语从句修饰 the freewheeling unit，从句本身也是被动 is attached to。
**要点**：through 引出传递动力的通道，即动力“经由”单向离合器输出。which 的先行词是紧邻的 the freewheeling unit，不是 systems——从句谓语用 is，单复数已经给出提示。
