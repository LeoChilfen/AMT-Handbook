# The Axes of an Aircraft

> 机体 Ch 02 · The Axes of an Aircraft · 原文 PDF 第 79 页

## 1. 原文

Whenever an aircraft changes its attitude in flight, it must turn about one or more of three axes. Figure 2-10 shows the three axes, which are imaginary lines passing through the The axes of an aircraft can be considered as imaginary axles around which the aircraft turns like a wheel. At the center, where all three axes intersect, each is perpendicular to the other two. The axis that extends lengthwise through the fuselage from the nose to the tail is called the longitudinal axis. The axis that extends crosswise from wing tip to wing tip is the lateral, or pitch, axis. The axis that passes through the center, from top to bottom, is called the vertical, or yaw, axis. Roll, pitch, and yaw are controlled by three control surfaces. Roll is produced by the ailerons, which are located at the trailing edges of the wings. Pitch is affected by the elevators, the rear portion of the horizontal tail assembly. Yaw is controlled by the rudder, the rear portion of the vertical tail assembly.

## 2. 中文翻译

每当飞机在飞行中改变其姿态，它都必须绕三根轴中的一根或多根转动。图2-10 示出这三根轴，它们是穿过……的假想直线（原文此处前一句未完即接入下一句）。飞机的轴可以看作是假想的轮轴，飞机像车轮一样绕其转动。在三轴相交的中心处，每一根轴都垂直于另外两根轴。贯穿机身、由机头延伸到机尾的那根轴称为纵轴。由一侧翼尖横向延伸到另一侧翼尖的轴，是横轴，或称俯仰轴。通过中心、由上到下贯穿的轴称为竖轴，或称偏航轴。滚转、俯仰和偏航由三个操纵面控制。滚转由副翼产生，副翼位于机翼后缘。俯仰由升降舵影响，升降舵是水平尾翼组件的后部。偏航由方向舵控制，方向舵是垂直尾翼组件的后部。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 被动语态 + 介词 + which 定语从句 + like 短语
**原句**：The axes of an aircraft can be considered as imaginary axles around which the aircraft turns like a wheel.
**拆解**：主干 The axes can be considered as imaginary axles，被动语态，as 引出主语补足语；around which 引导定语从句修饰 axles，从句主语 the aircraft，谓语 turns，like a wheel 为方式状语。
**要点**：consider A as B 变被动后 as B 保留；around which = around the axles，介词提到关系词前是书面语特征；like 是介词“像”，不是动词。

### ② where 引导的非限制性定语从句插入
**原句**：At the center, where all three axes intersect, each is perpendicular to the other two.
**拆解**：句首 At the center 为地点状语，where all three axes intersect 为非限制性定语从句，补充说明 center；主句为 each is perpendicular to the other two，each 指“每一根轴”。
**要点**：非限制性定语从句用逗号与主句隔开，删除后主句仍完整（At the center, each is perpendicular...）。each 作主语时谓语用单数 is，the other two 指“另外两根”，三者互指必须用 other。

### ③ 主语与谓语被定语从句隔开
**原句**：The axis that extends lengthwise through the fuselage from the nose to the tail is called the longitudinal axis.
**拆解**：主语中心词 The axis，that 引导定语从句（谓语 extends，lengthwise 为副词状语，through the fuselage、from the nose to the tail 为介词短语状语）；主句谓语 is called 直到从句结束才出现，后接主语补足语 the longitudinal axis。
**要点**：主语后挂了 12 个词的定语从句，谓语 is called 容易被漏读；先跳过 that 从句锁定 The axis ... is called 的骨架。lengthwise 是副词“沿长度方向”，不是形容词。

### ④ 被动语态 + 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：Roll is produced by the ailerons, which are located at the trailing edges of the wings.
**拆解**：主句 Roll is produced by the ailerons，被动语态；which 引导非限制性定语从句，先行词是 the ailerons，从句为被动 are located at...。
**要点**：produce 与 locate 都是被动，但主语不同——滚转是“被产生”的，副翼是“被安装”的。非限制性从句的 which 不能换成 that，翻译时另起一句“副翼位于……”更通顺。

### ⑤ 被动语态 + 名词短语作同位语
**原句**：Yaw is controlled by the rudder, the rear portion of the vertical tail assembly.
**拆解**：主句 Yaw is controlled by the rudder 为被动语态；逗号后 the rear portion of the vertical tail assembly 是名词短语作 the rudder 的同位语，进一步解释方向舵在结构上的位置。
**要点**：同位语与被说明的名词指同一事物，中间只用逗号或破折号，不加 of/which；翻译时常译成“方向舵，即垂直尾翼组件的后部”，不要误读成并列的第二个宾语。
