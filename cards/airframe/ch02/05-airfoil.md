# Airfoil

> 机体 Ch 02 · Airfoil · 原文 PDF 第 74 页

## 1. 原文

An airfoil is a surface designed to obtain lift from the air through which it moves. Thus, it can be stated that any part of the aircraft that converts air resistance into lift is an airfoil. The profile of a conventional wing is an excellent example of an airfoil. Notice that the top surface of the wing profile has greater curvature than the lower surface.

The difference in curvature of the upper and lower surfaces of the wing builds up the lift force. Air flowing over the top surface of the wing must reach the trailing edge of the wing in the same amount of time as the air flowing under the wing. To do this, the air passing over the top surface moves at a greater velocity than the air passing below the wing because of the greater distance it must travel along the top surface. This increased velocity, according to Bernoulli’s Principle, means a corresponding decrease in pressure on the surface. Thus, a pressure differential is created between the upper and lower surfaces of the wing, forcing the wing upward in the direction of the lower pressure.

Within limits, lift can be increased by increasing the angle of attack (AOA), wing area, velocity, density of the air, or by changing the shape of the airfoil. When the force of lift on an aircraft’s wing equals the force of gravity, the aircraft maintains level flight.

### Shape of the Airfoil

Individual airfoil section properties differ from those properties of the wing or aircraft as a whole because of the effect of the wing planform. A wing may have various airfoil sections from root to tip, with taper, twist, and sweepback.

The resulting aerodynamic properties of the wing are determined by the action of each section along the span.

The shape of the airfoil determines the amount of turbulence or skin friction that it produces, consequently affecting the efficiency of the wing. Turbulence and skin friction are controlled mainly by the fineness ratio, which is defined as the ratio of the chord of the airfoil to the maximum thickness. If the wing has a high fineness ratio, it is a very thin wing. Athick wing has a low fineness ratio. A wing with a high fineness ratio produces a large amount of skin friction. A wing with a low fineness ratio produces a large amount of turbulence. The best wing is a compromise between these two extremes to hold both turbulence and skin friction to a minimum.

The efficiency of a wing is measured in terms of the lift to drag ratio (L/D). This ratio varies with the AOA but reaches a definite maximum value for a particular AOA. At this angle, the wing has reached its maximum efficiency. The shape of the airfoil is the factor that determines the AOA at which the wing is most efficient; it also determines the degree of efficiency. Research has shown that the most efficient airfoils for general use have the maximum thickness occurring about one-third of the way back from the leading edge of the wing.

High-lift wings and high-lift devices for wings have been developed by shaping the airfoils to produce the desired effect. The amount of lift produced by an airfoil increases with an increase in wing camber. Camber refers to the curvature of an airfoil above and below the chord line surface. Upper camber refers to the upper surface, lower camber to the lower surface, and mean camber to the mean line of the section. Camber is positive when departure from the chord line is outward and negative when it is inward. Thus, high-lift wings have a large positive camber on the upper surface and a slightly negative camber on the lower surface. Wing flaps cause an ordinary wing to approximate this same condition by increasing the upper camber and by creating a negative lower camber.

It is also known that the larger the wingspan, as compared to the chord, the greater the lift obtained. This comparison is called aspect ratio. The higher the aspect ratio, the greater the lift. In spite of the benefits from an increase in aspect ratio, it was found that definite limitations were defined by structural and drag considerations.

On the other hand, an airfoil that is perfectly streamlined and offers little wind resistance sometimes does not have enough lifting power to take the aircraft off the ground. Thus, modern aircraft have airfoils which strike a medium between extremes, the shape depending on the purposes of the aircraft for which it is designed.

### Angle of Incidence

The acute angle the wing chord makes with the longitudinal axis of the aircraft is called the angle of incidence, or the angle of wing setting. The angle of incidence in most cases is a fixed, built-in angle. When the leading edge of the wing is higher than the trailing edge, the angle of incidence is said to be positive. The angle of incidence is negative when the leading edge is lower than the trailing edge of the wing.

### Angle of Attack (AOA)

Before beginning the discussion on AOA and its effect on airfoils, first consider the terms chord and center of pressure (CP) as illustrated in Figure 2-5 .

The chord of an airfoil or wing section is an imaginary straight line that passes through the section from the leading edge to the trailing edge, as shown in Figure 2-5 . The chord line provides one side of an angle that ultimately forms the AOA. The other side of the angle is formed by a line indicating the direction of the relative airstream. Thus, AOA is defined as the angle between the chord line of the wing and the direction of the relative wind. This is not to be confused with the angle of incidence, illustrated in Figure 2-4 , which is the angle between the chord line of the wing and the longitudinal axis of the aircraft.

### Angle of Incidence

On each part of an airfoil or wing surface, a small force is present. This force is of a different magnitude and direction from any forces acting on other areas forward or rearward from this point. It is possible to add all of these small forces mathematically. That sum is called the “resultant force” (lift). This resultant force has magnitude, direction, and location, and can be represented as a vector, as shown in Figure 2-5 . The point of intersection of the resultant force line with the chord line of the airfoil is called the center of pressure (CP). The CP moves along the airfoil chord as the AOA changes. Throughout most of the flight range, the CP moves forward with increasing AOA and rearward as the AOA decreases. The effect of increasing AOA on the CP is shown in Figure 2-6 .

The AOA changes as the aircraft’s attitude changes. Since the AOA has a great deal to do with determining lift, it is given primary consideration when designing airfoils. In a properly designed airfoil, the lift increases as the AOA is increased. When the AOA is increased gradually toward a positive AOA, the lift component increases rapidly up to a certain point and then suddenly begins to drop off. During this action the drag component increases slowly at first, then rapidly as lift begins to drop off.

When the AOA increases to the angle of maximum lift, the burble point is reached. This is known as the critical angle. When the critical angle is reached, the air ceases to flow smoothly over the top surface of the airfoil and begins to burble or eddy. This means that air breaks away from the upper camber line of the wing. What was formerly the area of decreased pressure is now filled by this burbling air. When this occurs, the amount of lift drops and drag becomes excessive. The force of gravity exerts itself, and the nose of the aircraft drops. This is a stall. Thus, the burble point is the stalling angle.

As previously seen, the distribution of the pressure forces over the airfoil varies with the AOA. The application of the resultant force, or CP, varies correspondingly. As this angle increases, the CP moves forward; as the angle decreases, the CP moves back. The unstable travel of the CP is characteristic of almost all airfoils.

### Boundary Layer

In the study of physics and fluid mechanics, a boundary layer is that layer of fluid in the immediate vicinity of a bounding surface. In relation to an aircraft, the boundary layer is the part of the airflow closest to the surface of the aircraft. In designing high-performance aircraft, considerable attention is paid to controlling the behavior of the boundary layer to minimize pressure drag and skin friction drag.

## 2. 中文翻译

翼型是为从流经它的空气中获得升力而设计的表面。因此可以说，飞机上任何把空气阻力转化为升力的部分都是翼型。常规机翼的剖面就是翼型的绝佳例子。注意，机翼剖面的上表面比下表面弯曲程度更大。

机翼上下表面弯曲程度的差异形成了升力。流过机翼上表面的空气，必须与流过下表面的空气在相同时间内到达机翼后缘。为此，流经上表面的空气要比流经下表面的空气速度更快，因为它沿上表面必须经过的路程更长。根据伯努利原理，速度增大意味着该表面上的压力相应降低。这样，机翼上下表面之间就产生了压力差，迫使机翼朝压力较低的方向（即向上）移动。

在一定范围内，增大迎角（AOA）、机翼面积、速度、空气密度，或改变翼型形状，都可以增大升力。当飞机机翼上的升力等于重力时，飞机就保持平飞。

### 翼型的形状

由于机翼平面形状的影响，单个翼型剖面的特性与整个机翼或飞机的特性并不相同。一个机翼从翼根到翼尖可以有多种翼型剖面，并带有渐变弦长、扭转和后掠。

机翼最终的空气动力特性，由沿翼展各剖面的作用共同决定。

翼型的形状决定了它产生多少紊流或蒙皮摩擦，从而影响机翼的效率。紊流和蒙皮摩擦主要受细长比控制，细长比定义为翼型弦长与最大厚度之比。如果机翼细长比高，它就是很薄的机翼。厚机翼的细长比低。细长比高的机翼产生大量蒙皮摩擦，细长比低的机翼产生大量紊流。最好的机翼是在这两个极端之间折中，把紊流和蒙皮摩擦都控制在最小。

机翼的效率用升阻比（L/D）来衡量。这个比值随迎角变化，但在某一特定迎角下达到确定的最大值。在这个迎角下，机翼达到最高效率。翼型的形状是决定机翼效率最高时对应迎角的因素，同时也决定效率的高低。研究表明，通用性最好的翼型，其最大厚度约位于从机翼前缘向后三分之一处。

通过把翼型做成能产生所需效果的形状，人们研制出了高升力机翼和机翼增升装置。翼型产生的升力随翼型弯度的增大而增大。弯度指翼型相对弦线在上方和下方的弯曲程度。上弯度指上表面，下弯度指下表面，中弯度指剖面的中线。当偏离弦线向外时弯度为正，向内时为负。因此，高升力机翼上表面有很大的正弯度，下表面有轻微负弯度。机翼襟翼通过增大上弯度并形成下表面负弯度，使普通机翼接近这种状态。

人们还知道，翼展相对于弦长越大，获得的升力越大。这一比值称为展弦比。展弦比越高，升力越大。尽管增大展弦比有好处，但研究发现，结构和阻力方面的考虑规定了明确的限制。

另一方面，完全流线型、风阻很小的翼型，有时升力不足以使飞机离地。因此，现代飞机的翼型都在极端之间取中，其形状取决于该型飞机的用途。

### 安装角

机翼弦线与飞机纵轴所成的锐角，称为安装角，或称机翼安装角。在大多数情况下，安装角是固定的、在制造时确定的角度。当机翼前缘高于后缘时，安装角称为正安装角。当机翼前缘低于后缘时，安装角为负。

### 迎角（AOA）

在开始讨论迎角及其对翼型的影响之前，先来了解弦线和压力中心（CP）这两个术语，如图 2-5 所示。

翼型或机翼剖面的弦线，是一条从剖面前缘通到后缘的假想直线，如图 2-5 所示。弦线构成一个角的一边，这个角最终形成迎角。该角的另一边由表示相对气流方向的直线构成。因此，迎角定义为机翼弦线与相对气流方向之间的夹角。不要把它与图 2-4 所示的安装角混淆，安装角是机翼弦线与飞机纵轴之间的夹角。

### 安装角

在翼型或机翼表面的每一点上，都作用着一个小的力。这个力的大小和方向，与该点之前或之后其他区域所受的力都不相同。可以把所有这些小的力在数学上相加。其总和称为“合力”（升力）。这个合力具有大小、方向和作用点，并可用矢量表示，如图 2-5 所示。合力作用线与翼型弦线的交点，称为压力中心（CP）。随着迎角变化，压力中心沿翼型弦线移动。在大部分飞行范围内，迎角增大时压力中心前移，迎角减小时压力中心后移。迎角增大对压力中心的影响如图 2-6 所示。

迎角随飞机姿态的变化而变化。由于迎角对决定升力有很大作用，所以在设计翼型时要优先考虑它。在设计良好的翼型中，升力随迎角增大而增大。当迎角逐渐增大到正迎角时，升力分量迅速增大到某一点，然后突然开始下降。在此过程中，阻力分量起初缓慢增大，随后在升力开始下降时迅速增大。

当迎角增大到最大升力对应的角度时，就到达了气流分离点。这被称为临界迎角。达到临界迎角时，空气不再沿翼型上表面平滑流动，而开始产生翻卷或涡流。这意味着空气脱离了机翼上表面的弯度线。原来压力降低的区域，现在被这种翻卷的空气填充。出现这种情况时，升力下降，阻力变得过大。重力起作用，飞机机头下沉。这就是失速。因此，气流分离点就是失速迎角。

如前所述，翼型上压力分布随迎角变化而变化，合力（即压力中心）的作用位置也相应变化。迎角增大时，压力中心前移；迎角减小时，压力中心后移。压力中心的这种不稳定移动，几乎是一切翼型的共同特征。

### 附面层

在物理学和流体力学研究中，附面层是紧邻固体表面的那一层流体。就飞机而言，附面层是最靠近飞机表面的那部分气流。在设计高性能飞机时，要十分重视控制附面层的特性，以尽量减小压差阻力和蒙皮摩擦阻力。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 现在分词作定语 + the same...as 比较
**原句**：Air flowing over the top surface of the wing must reach the trailing edge of the wing in the same amount of time as the air flowing under the wing.
**拆解**：主语 Air，flowing over the top surface of the wing 为现在分词短语作后置定语；谓语 must reach，宾语 the trailing edge；in the same amount of time 为时间状语；as 引出比较对象 the air flowing under the wing（现在分词作后置定语，后省略了谓语 does）。
**要点**：两个 flowing 分别是两处定语，逻辑主语分别是 Air 和 the air，不要混为一句的动作。the same...as 是同级比较，as 后面是省略结构，完整应为 as the air flowing under the wing does。

### ② 被动语态 + 现在分词作结果状语
**原句**：Thus, a pressure differential is created between the upper and lower surfaces of the wing, forcing the wing upward in the direction of the lower pressure.
**拆解**：主句为被动语态 a pressure differential is created，between... 为地点状语；逗号后 forcing... 为现在分词短语作结果状语，其逻辑主语是前面整句所述情况（压力差），宾语 the wing，宾补 upward，in the direction of the lower pressure 说明方向。
**要点**：forcing 前没有连词、也没有独立主语，说明它不是并列谓语，而是伴随/结果状语。in the direction of 意为“朝……方向”，此处指出机翼被推向压力较低的一侧，即向上。

### ③ 定语从句中套“介词 + which”从句 + 分号并列
**原句**：The shape of the airfoil is the factor that determines the AOA at which the wing is most efficient; it also determines the degree of efficiency.
**拆解**：主句主语 The shape of the airfoil，系动词 is，表语 the factor；that 引导定语从句修饰 factor，从句谓语 determines，宾语 the AOA；at which 引导定语从句修饰 AOA；分号后 it 指代 The shape of the airfoil，also determines 为第二个并列谓语。
**要点**：at which = at the AOA，关系词在从句中作状语，介词 at 与先行词 AOA 是固定搭配（at an angle）。分号连接两个独立分句，后句用 it 回指前句主语，避免重复。

### ④ 省略关系词的定语从句 + or 引出同义名称
**原句**：The acute angle the wing chord makes with the longitudinal axis of the aircraft is called the angle of incidence, or the angle of wing setting.
**拆解**：主语 The acute angle，后接省略关系词 that/which 的定语从句 the wing chord makes with the longitudinal axis of the aircraft（make an angle with 意为“与……成角”）；主句谓语 is called，表语 the angle of incidence；or the angle of wing setting 为同位性补充，给出另一名称。
**要点**：定语从句的宾语就是先行词 The acute angle，被提到了句首，所以从句里 make 后面看不到宾语。or 在这里不是“或者”，而是“又称”，用于引出同义术语。

### ⑤ 不定式被动式 + 过去分词插入语 + 非限制性定语从句
**原句**：This is not to be confused with the angle of incidence, illustrated in Figure 2-4 , which is the angle between the chord line of the wing and the longitudinal axis of the aircraft.
**拆解**：主干 This is not to be confused with...，is to be confused 为“be + 不定式被动式”，表示“不应被混淆”；illustrated in Figure 2-4 为过去分词短语作插入语，补充说明前面的 the angle of incidence；which 引导非限制性定语从句，进一步解释该角。
**要点**：be to do 表示“应该/必须”，此处否定式 not to be confused 是提醒读者区分两个角。非限制性定语从句用逗号隔开，which 指代 the angle of incidence，不能用 that 替换。

### ⑥ 时间状语从句 + cease to do 与 begin to do 并列
**原句**：When the critical angle is reached, the air ceases to flow smoothly over the top surface of the airfoil and begins to burble or eddy.
**拆解**：When 引导时间状语从句，为被动语态 the critical angle is reached；主句主语 the air，并列谓语 ceases to flow 与 begins to burble or eddy，两个不定式分别作 cease 和 begin 的宾语；smoothly 修饰 flow，over the top surface 为地点状语。
**要点**：cease to do 与 begin to do 都接不定式，共用主语 the air；and 连接的是两个谓语，不是两个从句。burble or eddy 为近义并列，中文可合译为“翻卷、起涡”。
