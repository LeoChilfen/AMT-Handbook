# Introduction

> 机体 Ch 02 · Introduction · 原文 PDF 第 72 页

## 1. 原文

Three topics that are directly related to the manufacture, operation, and repair of aircraft are: aerodynamics, aircraft assembly, and rigging. Each of these subject areas, though studied separately, eventually connect to provide a scientific and physical understanding of how an aircraft is prepared for flight. A logical place to start with these three topics is the study of basic aerodynamics. By studying aerodynamics, a person becomes familiar with the fundamentals of aircraft flight.

## 2. 中文翻译

与飞机的制造、使用和维修直接相关的有三个主题：空气动力学、飞机装配与校装。这三个学科领域虽然分别学习，但最终相互衔接，共同构成对飞机如何做好飞行准备的科学与物理认识。从这三个主题入手，合乎逻辑的起点是学习基本空气动力学。通过学习空气动力学，人们就能熟悉飞机飞行的基本原理。

## 4. 语法解析

### ① 定语从句隔开主谓，冒号引出列举
**原句**：Three topics that are directly related to the manufacture, operation, and repair of aircraft are: aerodynamics, aircraft assembly, and rigging.
**拆解**：主语中心词 Three topics，后接 that 引导的定语从句（are 为从句谓语，directly related to... 为被动结构作表语）；主句谓语 are 被从句推到第 13 个词之后；冒号后是三个并列名词，作主语的表语。
**要点**：主句是“Three topics ... are: A, B, and C”，中间的 that 从句只是修饰 topics，读的时候要跳过它去找主句谓语。冒号在此起“即”的作用，把三个主题一次列出，不能换成逗号。

### ② 让步状语插入 + how 宾语从句
**原句**：Each of these subject areas, though studied separately, eventually connect to provide a scientific and physical understanding of how an aircraft is prepared for flight.
**拆解**：主语 Each of these subject areas，though studied separately 为让步状语（though + 过去分词，省略 it is），谓语 connect，不定式 to provide 作结果状语；understanding 后接 of + how 引导的宾语从句，从句为被动语态 is prepared for flight。
**要点**：though studied separately 是插入成分，去掉后句子仍完整；connect 在此意为“相互衔接”，不是“连接某物”，所以后面接 to provide 而不带宾语。how 从句作介词 of 的宾语，说明“理解”的具体内容。

### ③ 不定式作后置定语
**原句**：A logical place to start with these three topics is the study of basic aerodynamics.
**拆解**：主语 A logical place，不定式 to start with these three topics 作后置定语修饰 place；系动词 is，表语 the study of basic aerodynamics（of 短语作 study 的定语）。
**要点**：place 后接不定式是英语的习惯搭配（a place to start），不定式与中心词是动宾关系——place 是 start with 的逻辑宾语。表语的中心词 study 后同样带 of 短语，形成“学习……的研究”这样的嵌套。

### ④ 介词短语作方式状语 + become familiar with
**原句**：By studying aerodynamics, a person becomes familiar with the fundamentals of aircraft flight.
**拆解**：By studying aerodynamics 为介词 by + 动名词构成的方式状语，置于句首；主干 a person becomes familiar with the fundamentals，familiar with 为形容词短语作表语，of aircraft flight 修饰 fundamentals。
**要点**：by + 动名词表示“通过做某事”，是说明手段、方法的固定结构，不能换成 to do。become 是系动词，后接形容词 familiar 作表语，with 短语说明熟悉的对象，不要误读成 become 的宾语。
